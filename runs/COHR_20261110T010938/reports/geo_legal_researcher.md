## COHR — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment

**Company:** Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR) | Photonics, optical transceivers, lasers, engineered materials | FY2026 revenue **$7.12B** (FYE 6/30/26), market cap ~$44B, 51,478 employees.

**Critical mismatch — revenue geography ≠ operating geography.** Per the FY2026 10-K, headcount is **44,582 in Asia-Pacific (86.6%)**, 3,177 Europe, 3,719 Americas; ~89% of staff are in manufacturing. Principal non-U.S. production/R&D: **China, Finland, Germany, Malaysia, the Philippines** (plus Vietnam, Thailand, India). Coherent has China plants in **Fuzhou, Shenzhen and Suzhou** and a **$127M Vietnam factory** inaugurated Aug 2025.

**Implication:** ~65% of revenue is billed to U.S. customers, but the *cost base and physical supply* are overwhelmingly Asian. Tariff and export-control shocks hit COGS and delivery capability, not the revenue line — a risk the revenue mix materially understates. Further, a large share of "North America" revenue is to U.S. OEMs/hyperscalers whose systems are assembled in Asia, so true end-demand exposure to Asia exceeds 35%.

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk 1 — US–China export-control re-escalation (Probability: High | Impact: High)**
COHR sells dual-use photonics (transceivers, lasers, InP/GaAs devices) into China and manufactures there. The BIS "Affiliates Rule" was **suspended for one year in late Oct 2025** as part of the Trump–Xi truce — a live cliff edge. China's MOFCOM has announced **export controls effective Nov 10, 2026 against 16 U.S. entities** from its 2025 list and is drafting rules to **sanction foreign firms for infringing Chinese trade secrets** (Oct 26 draft). Mitigant: China is only 11.4% of revenue and at least two named hyperscale/OEM customers (>10% each of revenue) are U.S.-based; management already guides with China excluded in peer narratives.

**Risk 2 — Chinese critical-minerals leverage (Probability: Medium-High | Impact: Medium)**
The 10-K explicitly flags "**reliance on limited and rare-earth suppliers**" and "**Chinese export restrictions on certain minerals**." Beijing has controlled gallium, germanium, antimony, superhard materials and rare earths since 2023–24. Coherent's July 2023 assessment said gallium/germanium curbs would have "**minimal to no impact**" given inventories, non-China sourcing and recycling — a credible mitigant, but that was written before the Dec 2024 total ban on gallium/germanium/antimony exports to the U.S. **China's suspension of REE controls runs only to Jan 10, 2027** — a recurring, dated cliff risk for a company whose materials segment makes GaAs/InP devices and Bi₂Te₃ thermoelectrics.

**Risk 3 — Tariffs, CHIPS conditionality and industrial policy (Probability: High | Impact: Medium)**
COHR holds a **$50M preliminary CHIPS Act memorandum** for its Sherman, Texas facility (announced June 2026). CHIPS funding carries a **10-year guardrail restricting expansion of advanced semiconductor capacity in China** — directly at odds with the Fuzhou/Suzhou/Shenzhen footprint and with any future China capacity decision. Conversely, Section 232/301 tariffs raise landed cost on Asian-made goods. Note the midterm-driven selloff in Nov 2026 shows the market pricing policy risk into the name.

*Note: no material Russia exposure surfaced post the A&D divestiture; Russia/Ukraine is not a quantified risk driver.*

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **Securities class action (active).** Competing securities-fraud complaints with class periods running **Aug 1, 2024 – Aug 13, 2025** (some firms plead Sept 29, 2020 – Sept 29, 2025). Core allegation: misstatements tied to the **Aug 13, 2025 announcement of the $400M sale of the Aerospace & Defense business to Advent**; COHR fell **>19% on Aug 14, 2025**. Rosen Law and Pomerantz both opened investigations (Oct 2025). Materiality to a $44B-cap, well-insured issuer is likely **low**, but disclosure is live.
- **10-K legal proceedings language is boilerplate**: pending matters "will not materially affect the Company's financial position" — i.e., no disclosed material contingencies. Treat the class action as a disclosure/nuisance item, not a balance-sheet event.
- **Commercial litigation:** *Toyo Tanso USA Inc. v. Coherent Corp.* (D. Or., 3:25-cv-00015, filed Jan 3, 2025) — specialty graphite/supply dispute; ordinary-course.
- **IP portfolio** (~3,160 issued patents, 1,280 pending) implies routine patent litigation exposure in photonics; no single bet-the-company case identified.
- **Regulatory stack:** EAR/BIS dual-use export licensing (core), CFIUS/HSR review on the A&D sale to a financial sponsor, CHIPS Act compliance, plus fab-related environmental/EPA-PFAS and FCPA exposure across China/Vietnam/India operations. The 10-K newly flags "**evolving AI regulation**" and cybersecurity as risk factors.

---

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **Dual-use export licensing** on lasers/photonics into China — revenue-at-risk and potential consent-agreement exposure; enforcement posture is tightening post-2025.
2. **CHIPS Act China guardrail** conflicts with the existing China manufacturing base — strategic constraint, not just a compliance line item.
3. **Disclosure controls** — the Aug 2025 A&D-divestiture communications are the subject of securities litigation; watch for restatement or SEC follow-on.
4. **Supplier concentration in Chinese-controlled minerals** with licensing regimes that can be switched off with short notice.

---

### 5. Overall Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale:** Legal risk alone is **Medium** (one ordinary-course consolidated securities action plus supplier litigation, no material disclosed contingencies). The **High** rating is driven by geopolitics: an operating footprint that is ~87% Asia-Pacific against a business whose entire growth narrative rests on U.S. AI datacenter customers, layered on top of (a) suspended-but-live U.S. affiliates-rule exposure, (b) Chinese retaliation measures effective Nov 2026, and (c) date-certain mineral-control cliff edges (Jan 2027). Note the asymmetry: the CHIPS subsidy and NVIDIA agreement *reward* the U.S. pivot while the existing China/Vietnam base *funds* it — the two are on a collision course over a 10-year guardrail window.
