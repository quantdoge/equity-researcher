**Investment Thesis**
Coherent is the highest-quality *scale* asset in the strongest demand cycle in optical networking history — vertically integrated into the scarcest input (InP/EML lasers), growing ~50–60%, expanding gross margin 250bp+/yr, underwritten by NVIDIA's $2B equity stake and a $20B contracted backlog — and it has already de-rated 49% from its June high. The offsetting reality: it is a 3.6-beta, negative-free-cash-flow (−$1.84B TTM), single-partner-concentrated instrument priced off a discount rate the Fed is actively pushing higher. This is a real business with a real problem (cash conversion), not a fraud and not a defensive compounder — it earns a **deliberately small, staged position**, not a core holding.

**Bear Case**

- **Concentration and alignment**: NVIDIA is simultaneously largest customer, $2B shareholder and partner; two customers >10% of revenue; insiders (Chair, CTO) selling discretionary stock with zero buys; 26% dilution in 15 months.
