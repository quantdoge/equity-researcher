# Coherent Corp. (COHR) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $225.19 (Nov 9, 2026) · Market Cap $44.4B · EV $45.6B · Beta 2.23**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Coherent is the volume leader (~25% share) in datacom optical transceivers, and it sits on the single hardest chokepoint in AI infrastructure: as clusters scale past copper's reach, optical interconnect — 800G → 1.6T → 3.2T, and eventually co-packaged optics (CPO) — becomes the binding constraint. The thesis re-rated from "cyclical laser conglomerate" to "AI bottleneck monopoly" when **NVIDIA invested $2B of equity directly into Coherent (March 2026)**, with a framework through 2030 spanning CPO, advanced lasers, and US-based InP manufacturing. Datacenter & Communications is now **82% of revenue growing +79% YoY**, and management has guided FY27 pro forma growth **above 60%**.

This is no longer a story stock — it is a capacity-constrained ramp with sold-out 2027 capacity. The debate is entirely about whether the capex is funded safely.

## 3. TAM Assessment

- **Datacom optical components:** ~$25B in 2025 → **$30B by 2029** (21% CAGR, Cignal AI), with the coherent-optics subset compounding 20–25%. AI cluster optical content per GPU is rising structurally each generation.
- **CPO / advanced packaging:** the incremental market Coherent explicitly flags as new TAM, unlocked by the NVIDIA framework — this is where the multiple expands.
- **Penetration:** Coherent holds ~25% of transceivers and is the only large independent merchant InP laser + ELS supplier post-consolidation. InP capacity is doubling in 2026 and **doubling again in 2027** — supply, not demand, is the limit.
- **Path to guided numbers:** $4B/quarter run-rate by FY28 = $16B annualized. Against a $30B+ datacom component TAM plus CPO, that requires ~45–50% share of a market it already leads. Aggressive but arithmetically reachable — the key swing factor is whether 3.2T/CPO share holds as Lumentum and Chinese module vendors scale.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 3, 2027 — Q2 FY27 results:** guide of $2.55–2.75B already above consensus; another beat/raise extends the pattern.
- **By June 2027 (FY27 end) — first $3B revenue quarter**, the explicit proof point for the capacity ramp.
- **NVIDIA partnership ramp (through 2030):** CPO qualification milestones, US InP fab buildout, and potential follow-on commitments. NVIDIA holds $2B of equity — deeply aligned, and a validation loop for other hyperscalers.
- **45%+ gross margin quarter (before CY2028 end):** the single largest re-rating lever — mix shift to 1.6T plus InP internalization.
- **FY28 FCF inflection to positive:** removes the bear case outright and likely triggers multiple expansion.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, position-sized for volatility)

The setup is a rare combination: **~50–60% revenue growth, 250bps/yr of gross margin expansion, 7 straight beats, a $2B strategic equity partner in NVIDIA, and a stock that has halved.** At 14.9x FY28 earnings for a business compounding 40%+ with a 45% GM target, the market is pricing in failure of the capex program — not the growth.

I stop short of Strong Buy because the funding structure is genuinely uncomfortable: **$3.1B annualized capex, negative operating cash flow, $3.98B long-term debt up $766M in one quarter, and $773M drawn on credit facilities.** This is a leveraged bet on hyperscaler capex continuity. If AI infrastructure spending digests for even two quarters, Coherent owns the fixed cost and the debt without the revenue.

**Fair value:** 25x FY28E EPS of $15.13 = **~$378**; 30x FY27E of $9.90 = ~$297. Blended 12-month target **~$340 (+50%)**, with a credible path to $400+ if FY28 FCF turns positive.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Negative FCF and rising leverage (highest risk).** FY26 FCF -$1.03B, FY27E ~-$700M, capex $775M in Q1 alone. Debt-funded capacity expansion into a demand cycle — if the cycle bends, the equity is levered to it.
2. **Customer/partner concentration.** NVIDIA plus a handful of hyperscalers drive the datacom ramp. The $2B equity stake raises circular-financing questions — NVIDIA is funding its own supplier's capacity.
3. **Competitive intensity.** Lumentum holds ~60% of EML chips for 800G/1.6T and the industry's largest InP capacity; Fabrinet, Applied Optoelectronics, and Chinese module makers (Innolight, Eoptolink) pressure pricing as 1.6T commoditizes. Coherent's 25% share is not structurally protected.
4. **Industrial segment decay.** $418.6M, **-14.8% YoY** and down sequentially — a drag that offsets datacom strength and signals weakness in the legacy laser franchise.
5. **Execution risk on aggressive guidance.** ">60% pro forma growth" and $4B/quarter by FY28 leave no room for yield problems, InP substrate shortages, or CPO qualification delays. Any slip → severe derating given beta 2.23.
6. **Dilution.** Diluted shares 155M → 202M (+30%) in two years, including the NVIDIA equity.
7. **Sentiment/macro.** Already demonstrated: a Financial Times report on OpenAI's revenue alone triggered a sector-wide optics selloff. This name trades with AI-capex narrative, not its own fundamentals.

---
**Bottom line:** Buy the de-rating. The growth engine is real, accelerating, and strategically underwritten by NVIDIA; the balance sheet is the price of admission. Size for 50% drawdowns — they have already happened twice this year.
