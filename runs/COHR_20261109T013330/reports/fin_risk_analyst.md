Leverage is *not* the problem. On annualised Q1 FY27 EBITDA ($2.21B), net debt/EBITDA is **~0.70x** and total debt/EBITDA ~1.6x — solidly investment-grade trajectory. Interest expense is falling every year while EBITDA nearly triples from FY24 ($701M) to FY26 ($1,560M).

**Refinancing cliff: none.** Per Fitch, there are **no meaningful maturities before 2029**, when the $990M senior unsecured notes and the Term Loan B come due. A $4.0B senior secured facility package is in place (Term A maturing July 2027, revolver). This is the single most important mitigant in the entire risk profile: the company cannot be forced to refinance into a bad market for ~3 years.

**Liquidity runway:** cash $1,987M. FY26 FCF was −$1,030M, but only because capex peaked at $1,110M ($522M of which is roughly maintenance/D&A-level) and working capital consumed $1,102M. The company has full discretionary control over the variable that matters most.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

Split the rating explicitly, because the two halves diverge sharply:

- **Credit / liquidity risk: LOW.** Net debt/EBITDA ~1.0x and falling, EBITDA/interest 8.2x, current ratio 2.43x, $2.0B cash, a $4.0B facility package, no maturities before 2029, and two agencies on positive outlook. Solvency risk is genuinely remote.
- **Market / equity risk: HIGH.** 80% volatility, 2.77 beta, 60% max drawdown. The equity is a high-beta AI-capex derivative.
- **Cash conversion risk: HIGH.** This is the true weak link — see red flags.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Operating cash flow collapsed to $79.5M against $805M of net income (FY26).** That is a ~$725M accruals gap — the direct cause of the F-Score accruals failure. Earnings are not converting to cash. CFO fell 87% year-over-year despite revenue +22.5%.
2. **Inventory ballooned 79% to $2,581M while revenue grew 22.5%.** Inventory days stretched from **140 to 212 days**. Either a deliberate pre-build for the AI datacom ramp, or a looming obsolescence write-down risk in a fast-moving optical component market. Unverified — this is the single most important item to monitor.
3. **Working capital was a $1,102M cash drain in FY26**, driven by inventory (+$1,143M) and receivables (+$324M). Receivable days also ticked up 64→69.
4. **Capex tripled to $1,110M** and — based on the Q1 FY27 cash flow row — is annualising far higher (Q1 FY27 capex ~$776M, FCF ~−$868M). FCF yield is **−2.74%**. Growth is being bought with cash, and the market's 7% after-hours drop on the FY26 print shows investors are watching this line.
5. **Share count dilution is severe:** diluted weighted shares went 155.5M → 195.4M → 202.0M in two years, **~30% dilution**. This directly undercuts per-share value creation and fails Piotroski F7.
6. **Revenue concentration in hyperscaler/AI capex.** Management guides FY27 pro forma revenue growth >60% and a first $3B quarter. A guidance profile this steep is exactly what reverses hardest in a capex digestion cycle — and the 2.77 beta confirms the market prices it that way.
7. **Retained earnings only $1,354M** against $7,260M of goodwill and intangibles — the legacy II-VI merger carrying value remains large relative to accumulated profits, leaving impairment exposure if the optical cycle turns.

## Data Gaps & Caveats (explicit)

- **Balance sheet inconsistency:** FY26 PP&E net is reported at **$21,286M against total assets of $18,299M** — internally impossible. This is a feed error and I have **excluded PP&E-derived ratios** from my conclusions. Altman X3/X5 may be distorted as a result.
- **Net debt discrepancy:** the leverage tool reports $1,551M (= total debt $3,538M − cash $1,987M), while the balance sheet row reports `net_debt` $2,376M. I use $1,551M as the standard definition but show ranges where it matters (0.99x–1.52x).
- **Q1 FY27 cash flow row is mis-scaled ~1000x** (shows OCF −$92.7 *billion*). I normalised by dividing by 1,000; the ~−$868M quarterly FCF figure is an estimate, not a reported value.
- **No CDS spread data available.** Credit assessment relies on agency ratings and financials only.
- **Peer comparison unavailable:** the sector peer list returned mega-cap semis (NVDA, AVGO, TSM) that are not meaningful leverage comparables for a mid-cap optical component supplier. No true comparables (LITE, CIEN, FN, MTSI) were retrievable within budget.

**Bottom line:** This is a **solvency-safe, cash-flow-challenged, high-beta** credit. The balance sheet and maturity profile mean the company survives a prolonged downturn with ease. The risk that should drive position sizing is not default — it is a 60%+ equity drawdown on an AI-capex digestion, compounded by a cash conversion profile that currently converts almost none of its earnings into cash.
