# Coherent Corp (COHR) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $192.81 | Market cap ~$38.0B | EV ~$39.2B | Beta 2.23 | FY ends June*

## 1. Growth Story

Coherent is the vertically integrated arms dealer of the copper-to-optics transition in AI datacenters — it owns the indium phosphide (InP) wafer fabs, the EML/DFB lasers, and the 800G/1.6T transceiver assembly, so it captures value at nearly every layer of the optical interconnect stack. After five years of post-merger integration and a brutal FY2024 digestion (-8.8% revenue), the model has inflected violently: Q1 FY2027 revenue grew **49.9% YoY (+56% pro forma)**, Datacenter & Communications is now **82% of sales at +79% YoY**, and management has guided FY2027 pro forma growth **above 60%** with a first $3B quarter by June 2027 and a first $4B quarter in FY2028. This is no longer a cyclical laser company — it is a capacity-constrained AI infrastructure supplier whose order book extends into 2028.

**Growth quality:** Now **organic**, not acquisition-driven (the +55.6% FY2023 spike was the II-VI/Coherent merger; every point since is AI datacom). Gross margin has expanded **+820bps in two years**, and non-GAAP operating margin +368bps YoY — textbook scale leverage. Revenue CAGR: **11.3%** FY2023→FY2026 (merger-distorted), but **~42%** FY2026→FY2029E.

**Rule of 40:** Reported score is a poor **8.0** (22.5% growth − 14.5% FCF margin) — but this is almost entirely a **capex artifact** (capex was $1.11B, ~16% of sales). On a growth + non-GAAP operating margin basis the score is **~73** (49.9% + 23.2%). The correct read: the company is deliberately sacrificing FCF to build InP and transceiver capacity against contracted demand.

**Forward indicators:** 7/7 consecutive EPS beats, average surprise **+6.6%**; FY2030 EPS estimate revised **+18.7%** (upgrade signal, though only 1 analyst on the tail). Guidance has been conservative every quarter.

**Valuation:** 46.8x trailing GAAP P/E collapses to **19.7x FY2027E**, **12.8x FY2028E**, **9.1x FY2029E**. PEG on FY2027 is **~0.25**. EV/EBITDA goes from 25.2x trailing to **~12.1x FY2027E**. P/S 5.3x FY2026 → 3.5x FY2027E → 2.5x FY2028E. At **3.35x book** it trades at a steep discount to optical peer Lumentum (8.65x) and Fabrinet (4.83x) despite faster growth.

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration Opportunity

- **Datacom optics TAM:** 800G/1.6T pluggables + CPO + DCI transponders — roughly $10–12B today, widely modeled at **$25–30B+ by 2030** as AI cluster scale-out (scale-up and scale-across) converts copper links to optics.
- **Coherent's SAM capture:** management is guiding toward ~$28B FY2030 revenue (consensus), which implies capturing a large share of that optics TAM *plus* SiC power and industrial lasers. At FY2026's $7.1B it is at roughly **25–30% of today's optics TAM** — but the TAM itself is compounding 25%+ annually.
- **The moat is physical, not software:** 6-inch InP wafer capacity (management expects InP output to **double in 2026 and more than double again by end-2027**) is the industry's binding constraint. Competitors must buy lasers from Coherent or from a handful of merchant suppliers. That vertical integration is the reason gross margins are expanding rather than compressing in a boom — rare.
- **Penetration risk is the mirror image:** because 82% of revenue is now AI datacenter, the "diversified laser company" ballast is gone. Industrial revenue is already declining.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 3, 2027 — Q2 FY2027 results.** Guidance $2.55–2.75B revenue, non-GAAP EPS $2.28–2.48. The key question: does the company raise the >60% FY2027 pro forma guide again?
- **Through FY2027 — the "$3B quarter."** Management has committed to a first $3B revenue quarter by fiscal year-end (June 2027) and a **first $4B quarter in FY2028**. Delivery would validate the FY2028 consensus of $15.1B.
- **1.6T transceiver ramp + CPO revenue.** 1.6T is in volume ramp; co-packaged optics components (lasers, photonic engines) are approaching first revenue — optionality on the next architecture, where Coherent supplies content even if pluggables lose share.
- **InP capacity milestones.** Each 6-inch InP capacity add is a direct revenue unlock; watch for the "capacity doubled again by end-2027" checkpoint.
- **Non-GAAP gross margin >45% target "before calendar 2028 ends."** Hitting 45% from 39–40% today would drive material EPS upside vs. consensus.
- **1600ZR DCI deployment cycle (2027+)** — a second, less crowded coherent-optics leg.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Hiring (supportive):** Active requisitions concentrated in *manufacturing engineering, equipment engineering, process development, and MCVD technicians* across Sherman TX, Saxonburg PA, Newark DE, and San Jose — exactly the profile of a company staffing up fabs rather than cutting. This corroborates the capacity-expansion narrative.
- **Google Trends (weak/inconclusive):** "Coherent optical transceiver" is too low-volume to be a reliable series — it spiked to 100 in late May 2026 and collapsed to ~0 by July, tracking the stock's blow-off top rather than underlying demand. I would **not** weight this signal; the keyword measures retail/news attention, not transceiver orders.
- **Order book (strongest alt-signal):** Management commentary that customer schedules extend to **2028 and "even to the end of this decade"** is a better forward indicator than any web metric — though it is company-provided and unaudited.
- **Price action:** -56% from the June 2026 high of $440 to $192.81, including a -13% post-earnings drop on Nov 5 (despite a beat) followed by a +14% rebound. Beta 2.23 and ±14% daily swings mean the equity is being traded as a high-beta AI capex proxy, not on fundamentals.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **STRONG BUY**

For a company guiding **>60% pro forma revenue growth**, expanding gross margins toward 45%, with a **100% beat rate**, **rising estimates**, an order book visible to 2028, and a genuine physical moat in InP, **19.7x FY2027E EPS and ~12x forward EBITDA is too cheap**. Analyst targets cluster at **$350–$450** (mean ~$390, ~2x the current price) and were being *raised* into the selloff. The 55% drawdown has compressed the multiple without denting a single fundamental datapoint — the classic dislocation this framework is built to buy.

**However**, I size this as a high-conviction *growth* position, not a core holding: the thesis is a levered bet on hyperscaler AI capex, and the company is currently burning cash at a ~$3B annualized capex rate. Conviction should be expressed through position sizing, not through ignoring the FCF line.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Negative free cash flow and rising leverage — the #1 risk.** FY2026 FCF was **-$1.03B**; Q1 FY2027 operating cash flow was **-$92.7M** against $775.5M of capex, funded by $773.4M of new credit-facility borrowing. If AI capex digests, Coherent is left with a half-built fab, $3B+ of annual commitments, and rising interest expense. **Watch for equity issuance.**
2. **Hyperscaler concentration.** 82% of revenue from AI datacenter, sold to a handful of hyperscalers and NVIDIA. A single capex pause re-rates the stock 40%+ (it already did once, from $440 to $170).
3. **InP execution.** 6-inch InP is both the moat and the bottleneck — yield problems or a slip in the capacity ramp directly caps revenue, and the >60% guide has no slack.
4. **Architecture risk (CPO vs. pluggables).** If co-packaged optics displaces pluggable transceivers faster than Coherent monetizes CPO content, its largest revenue pool compresses. Partially hedged, not fully.
5. **Competition.** Lumentum, Broadcom (CPO), Marvell, Fabrinet, and Chinese vendors (Innolight/Eoptolink) on price; silicon photonics as a longer-term substitute for InP.
6. **Estimate fragility at the tail.** FY2029–FY2030 consensus rests on 4 and 1 analysts respectively — the "+18.7% revision" is a thin signal, and the $28B FY2030 revenue number is a model output, not a contract.
7. **GAAP/non-GAAP gap.** ~$522M annual D&A and $186M SBC mean non-GAAP EPS ($2.09) runs ~10% above GAAP ($1.90); the 45% gross-margin target is a non-GAAP figure.

**Bottom line:** This is the purest large-cap expression of the copper-to-optics disruption, with the margin structure to prove it and a valuation that has de-rated while fundamentals accelerated. **Strong Buy**, sized for a 2.2-beta stock, with FCF inflection and hyperscaler capex as the two variables that matter.

*Data caveats: FY2030 estimates have single-analyst coverage; Lumentum and Fabrinet multiples are trailing and reflect different profitability stages; Google Trends keyword volume was insufficient for a reliable signal.*
