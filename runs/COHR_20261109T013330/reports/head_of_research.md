**Investment Thesis**

Coherent is the only vertically integrated InP/optics supplier at scale in a capacity-constrained AI datacom market growing >40% CAGR, with revenue re-accelerating (+49.9% YoY in Q1 FY27, >60% FY27 pro forma guide), gross margin up 820bps in two years, a $20B contracted backlog anchor, and a balance sheet that cannot be forced to refinance before 2029. The 56% drawdown from the June peak was manufactured by macro (10Y at 5.13%, hyperscaler capex trims) and sector beta, not by any deterioration in company data — 7/7 beats, flat receivables days, expanding margins. The offsetting reality: this is a 95%-volatility, 3.4-beta instrument that converts only ~10% of earnings to cash, has 212 days of inventory, and has diluted holders ~30% in five quarters — so the position must be sized and gated, not chased.

**Bull Case**
- **Structural scarcity + consolidation**: only vertically integrated InP supplier at scale; Lumentum absorbed by Marvell; possible FCC ban on Chinese transceivers (~51% share) — three simultaneous share/pricing tailwinds.
- **Estimate momentum through a de-rated multiple**: >60% FY27 pro forma guide, $3B quarter by Jun-27, 7/7 beats, GM marching toward 45% — 12.8x FY28E EPS with an order book to 2028.
- **Balance sheet survives the bear case**: ~1.0x net leverage, $2B cash, no maturities before 2029, full discretionary control over the $3B capex program (~4.5 years of runway even at current burn).

**Bear Case**
- **Cash conversion failure**: $805M net income produced $80M of operating cash; 212 inventory days in a fast-obsolescence market; a 20% write-down ≈ two quarters of GAAP operating income.
- **Discount-rate + capex double-hit**: 100bp higher required return = −18–30% on the multiple alone; one more hyperscaler capex trim re-rates the stock violently (it fell ~50% in five weeks on a $5B Microsoft trim).
- **Dilution and concentration**: ~30% share count growth in five quarters, insiders selling into strength with zero buys, and FY27–28 visibility resting on a handful of hyperscalers plus one $20B contract and a politicised InP supply line.

**Key Risks**
1. Q2 FY27 (Feb 3, 2027) operating cash flow stays negative with inventory >$4B → thesis downgrades to Short-candidate (the Short analyst's own trigger).
2. China export-licence interruption of InP/gallium/germanium feedstock, or BIS action touching a COHR customer.
3. Hyperscaler capex digestion / AI-spend policy shock (post-midterm Congress, state data-center resistance).
4. Further equity issuance at current prices; PhotonLink termination/repricing/volume-flex.
5. CPO architecture displacement of pluggables accelerating beyond COHR's content capture (2028+ risk).
