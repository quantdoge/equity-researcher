**Investment Thesis**

Cerebras is a genuinely differentiated wafer-scale architecture wrapped in economics that have never worked: negative operating margins every year of its life, a structurally sub-semi gross margin (~40% core vs 64% peer median), a FY2025 "profit" that is a ~$367M non-operating artifact, and ~86% of FY2025 revenue from two related Abu Dhabi entities whose access to the product is gated by a discretionary US export-licence regime. At ~$21–25B EV against ~$885M FY26E revenue, the market is paying for a flawless conversion of the $25.4B backlog while the anchor customer (OpenAI) has already routed one marquee workload to NVIDIA. The price assumes perfection; the evidence shows a company still proving it can sell to anyone else at a margin worth having.

## Bull Case
- **$25.4B contracted backlog (~29x FY26E revenue), anchored by a >$10B OpenAI agreement.** If it converts at contracted economics, today's EV is ~2.8–3.3x FY28E sales — outright cheap against the delivery of even half the consensus ramp.
- **Real architectural differentiation where the market is heading.** Wafer-scale wins on inference latency/throughput and power density — a natural hedge in a power-constrained, energy-inflating world; sovereign AI broadens the buyer base where CUDA's moat is weakest.
- **Fortress balance sheet + squeeze asymmetry.** ~$6–7B net cash and 5+ years of runway mean no solvency path to zero; a benign CPI print, a Fed pause (−50bp real yield), and a cleared lock-up could drive a +40–60% squeeze in weeks on a 3.0 beta.

## Bear Case
- **No margin of safety at any plausible scenario.** Value base case $59, *bull* case $107; quant bottom-decile everywhere; 11/11 desks decline to recommend ownership. Spot prices a decade of ~40% compounding at 25% FCF margins against NVIDIA.
- **Earnings quality and unit economics are broken.** FY25 profit is a ~$367M non-operating item; GAAP GM collapsed to 14.2% in Q2; SBC exceeds revenue; capex runs >2x revenue; FCF −$596M in H1'26 alone; and the FY guide demands a Q4 hockey stick (+38% q/q) that the flat backlog does not corroborate.
- **Concentration × exogenous shocks.** Two UAE-related entities = ~86% of FY25 revenue under a discretionary, thrice-rewritten export-licence regime; OpenAI already routed one marquee workload to NVIDIA (which also acquired Groq, the nearest architectural substitute); single-source TSMC; ~5x float expansion within weeks; insiders selling with zero buys.

## Key Risks
1. **BIS/export action** against MBZUAI/G42 or an adverse Supplement No. 8 determination — a 40%+ revenue step-down, same-day exit event.
2. **Second OpenAI workload loss** or a backlog decline at the 12-Nov print — breaks the conversion narrative.
3. **Lock-up/float expansion** (estimates range 226–290M shares vs ~30M IPO float — desks disagree on schedule, agree on direction) plus continued insider distribution.
4. **Q4 guidance credibility**: FY26 requires ~$297M in Q4; a miss or guide-down compresses a ~28x forward-sales multiple violently.
5. **Accounting/disclosure crystallisation**: first 10-K with admitted ICFR material weaknesses; the unverified nature of the $367M FY25 gain; "core" vs GAAP framing; a filed securities class action (four firms investigating).
6. **Risk to the bear case (be honest)**: the macro desk's surprise scenario — CPI normalisation, December hike priced out, 10Y to ~4.5% — restores 15–20% of duration value and ignites a squeeze; backlog conversion at contracted prices would make $120 look cheap in 24 months.
