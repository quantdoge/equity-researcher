## 1. Consumer Demand Signals — ⚠️ Weakening (with a nuance)

**Google Trends, "GoDaddy" (US, 12m weekly):** interest ran an elevated band of **62–100 from Feb–mid-Aug 2026** (peaks 87 in Feb, 96–100 in Jul/Aug), then broke down hard to **46–55 from September onward**. Latest 4-week avg **49.8 vs 55.5** in the comparable Nov-2025 period = **~10% YoY decline**, but *flat vs the pre-Feb-2026 baseline*. Interpretation: the Feb–Aug surge was event-driven (earnings + a distinct Aug 9 = 100 spike), not a durable demand step-up. **The fade is normalization to trend, not a collapse** — but it also means the "AI-era reacceleration" narrative is not showing up in the top of funnel.

**Google Trends, "domain name registration" (category proxy):** this is the more bearish read. Last 6 weeks avg **21.0** vs **46.2** in the equivalent Nov-2025–Jan-2026 window = **~55% YoY decline**. Industry checks confirm the *direction*: .com/.net registration bases were **slightly declining** in Q3'26 (DNIB: 401.6M total names, ccTLD +2.3M but gTLD soft; .net down ~1.1M QoQ). Two drivers mixed together: (a) genuine category maturity, (b) query migration to AI assistants — which would depress Google-indexed generic queries without implying lost GoDaddy customers. **Treat the -55% as an upper bound on category pain, not a GDDY share-loss estimate.** GoDaddy's own brand query (−10% YoY) is a much better read, and it argues *against* share loss.

**"GoDaddy Airo" (AI product):** 7-week avg **55.6** vs **68.4** Jun–Aug peak (−19%), yet still **~35–45% above** Nov'25–Feb'26 levels. So Airo has stepped up to a structurally higher awareness level, but the marketing burst is decaying. Caution: the series contains two zero-value weeks, indicating search volumes too thin for reliable indexing — **low confidence, directional only**. This supports the bears' framing that "Airo is an ARPU tool, not a growth tool" — it monetizes the existing base rather than pulling new customers in.

**Web traffic:** godaddy.com **−1.66% MoM**, but engagement quality is solid (44.9% bounce, 5.35 pages/visit, 3:56 avg duration) — consistent with a high-intent installed base rather than a shrinking funnel. Adjacent properties are weaker: **godaddysites.com −7.51% MoM** (customer site-builder activity) and **auctions.godaddy.com −9.31% MoM** (aftermarket/domain-auction demand) — the latter corroborates the domain-query softness.

## 2. Business Activity Signals — Neutral-to-positive on margins, negative on growth
**Hiring: sharply constrained.** Indeed shows only **~13 open GoDaddy jobs**; LinkedIn lists a thin set of strategic roles (ISV partnerships AE, developer-platform marketing, support/sales). For a 5,845-employee company this is an unusually low requisition load and points to **continued efficiency-mode operation**. Read-through: supports margin expansion, FCF conversion and buyback-funded EPS growth; **argues against an imminent revenue reacceleration**.

**Patents: DATA GAP.** USPTO PatentsView feed failed twice (parse error). No patent-intensity read available — a genuine hole in this package, though low materiality for a domain/hosting/SaaS business where R&D shows up in Airo feature velocity rather than filings.
