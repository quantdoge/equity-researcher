## 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Transitional (reflationary, hawkish, long-end under pressure)**

The US economy is in an unusual and uncomfortable configuration for long-duration equity: **growth is re-accelerating, inflation is re-accelerating, and the Fed has stopped easing and turned mildly restrictive again — while the long end of the curve sells off on term-premium and fiscal-supply concerns.**

- Real GDP grew at roughly a **3.3% annualised pace in Q3 2026** (GDPC1: $24,611B vs $24,408B in Q2), after ~2.2% in Q2 and ~2.5% in Q1. YoY growth is a solid **+2.1%**.
- Headline CPI (CPIAUCSL) rose to **336.298 in Sept 2026 = +3.72% YoY**, up from ~2.6% YoY in mid-2025 — inflation has re-accelerated through 2026 (3-month annualised pace ≈ **+4.6%**).
- Core CPI (CPILFESL) is **+2.57% YoY** but **~3.4% annualised over the last three months** — the disinflation trend has flattened.
- The Fed **hiked** the target range to **3.75%–4.00% at the 16 September 2026 meeting** (per FOMC minutes) after having cut from 4.33% to ~3.63% through 2025–H1 2026, and **held at 3.75%–4.00% on 28 October 2026**. Effective fed funds: 3.75%.
- Meanwhile the **10Y Treasury has risen from 4.44% (end-June) to 4.98%**, and the **30Y from 4.87% to 5.32%** — the Fed is no longer the marginal price-setter; **term premium and fiscal risk are**. Curve: 2Y 4.61% / 10Y 4.98% / 30Y 5.32% → **2s10s +37bp, 10s30s +34bp: a bear steepener.**
- Labour market is stable but no longer tight: unemployment **4.2%** (Sept), 4.1–4.5% range over the past year.
- Credit is *not* the problem: **Moody's Baa–10Y spread is 1.49%**, near cycle tights (1.39% in late Sept). Financial conditions are easy in spreads, tight in rates.

**Stance for equity risk: risk-neutral / transitional.** Earnings are supported by nominal growth; multiples are capped by a 5% risk-free rate.

## 2. Company-Specific Macro Impact

GDDY is a **SMB-facing subscription software business** (~$5B revenue, ~6% growth, ~1/3 revenue ex-US, ~$1.5B+ FCF, heavily buyback-funded, low beta 0.94). It is *not* a classic long-duration SaaS story on multiples — at ~$117 the stock trades at a **mid-to-high-teens P/E on 2026 GAAP EPS (Q3 EPS $2.08 vs $1.51 a year ago) and roughly a 10% FCF yield** — so it is less exposed to pure discount-rate compression than peers, but more exposed to **small-business health, FX and the cost of its own leverage/refinancing**.

| Macro variable | Impact | Channel / reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **GDP +3.3% ann., U3 4.2%** | **Positive** | Domain registrations, hosting and commerce attach rates are a derivative of **new-business formation and SMB survival**. Solid nominal GDP and full employment keep gross adds and renewals intact. Q3'26 confirmed this: revenue +6%, bookings +6%. |
| **CPI 3.7% / core 2.6%** | **Mildly positive / neutral** | GDDY has real pricing power in renewals and is pushing ARPU via Airo (Airo annualised bookings >$100M, 70%+ of Airo customers hold ≥2 paid products). Input costs are mostly wages, cloud and marketing — no commodity/input inflation exposure, no tariff-sensitive COGS. Offsetting: sustained 3.5–4% CPI squeezes *its customers* — micro-SMBs are the most rate- and cost-sensitive cohort, raising down-tier churn risk. |

## 3. Stock-level read-through

GDDY has been a **macro-and-narrative battleground, not a rates story**:
- Peak Jan 2025 ≈ $207 → trough $75.17 (11 Jun 2026): **–64%**, driven by AI-disruption fear (websites/domains commoditised by LLM agents) plus growth deceleration, not by Fed policy.
- Post-Q3 (30 Oct) +14.3% single day to **$117.24**; **+56% off the June low**, still **–5.5% YTD** (from $124.08 on 31 Dec 2025).
- Note the tape: on **30 Oct the 10Y was 4.98% and GDDY rallied 14%** on company-specific earnings. That is the key empirical point — **idiosyncratic execution currently dominates macro beta**. Correlation to rates is real but second-order versus the AI/ARPU narrative.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL (with an unfavourable tilt)**

**Justification:**
- *Supportive:* nominal GDP ~5–5.5%, unemployment 4.2%, tight credit spreads, an SMB "trade-down" benefit (a $20/month all-in-one platform is a value proposition when SMBs are squeezed), and a genuinely cheap FCF valuation that limits duration risk.
- *Unfavourable:* a **hiking Fed + 5% 10Y + firming dollar** is the worst combination for a mid-single-digit grower with a third of revenue abroad and a buyback-driven EPS story. The 3.7% headline CPI print removes the "Fed put."
- *Net:* the macro backdrop neither drives the stock nor breaks the thesis. It is a **~1–2 turn multiple headwind**, offset by SMB demand resilience. We would move to **Unfavourable** if 10Y >5.5%, USD index >125, or the Fed signals a path to 4.5%+.

## 5. The Scenario That Would Materially Surprise

### **Bear surprise (probability ~25%): "Second inflation wave forces the Fed back to 4.5%+"**
**Mechanism:** Headline CPI at 3.7% and a 3-month annualised pace of 4.6% prove sticky (energy, food, tariff/reshoring pass-through) into a 3.3%-growth economy. The Fed — already having hiked once in September 2026 — hikes again in December 2026 and twice more in H1 2027 to **4.50%**. Long-end supply concerns push the **10Y to 5.75%** and the **30Y through 6%**, and the broad dollar index to **127–128**.

**GDDY impact:** a triple hit — (i) multiple de-rates from ~17x toward 13x as the equity risk premium widens; (ii) a 5–6% USD appreciation clips ~2% off reported international revenue, widening the apparent growth deceleration from 6% to ~3–4%; (iii) micro-SMB churn rises as real incomes and business formation stall. Buybacks at a 10% FCF yield become a value trap rather than support. **Downside: $75–85 (–25% to –35%)**, i.e., a retest of the June 2026 low even with intact operating results.

**Watch triggers:** core CPI 3-month annualised >3.5%, 10Y >5.25%, 30Y >5.75%, FOMC dissents in the hawkish direction, tight Baa spreads finally widening >2.0%.

### **Bull surprise (probability ~15%): "Growth scare without recession → Fed back to 3% and SMB formation boom"**
A Q4 growth slowdown plus a soft CPI (back to 2.5% headline) lets the Fed reverse to 3.00% by mid-2027; 10Y falls to 4.20%, USD softens 4%. GDDY re-rates to ~21x on FX tailwind plus Airo ARPU compounding → **$150–160 (+30% to +35%)**. This is the scenario in which the ~10% FCF yield plus a 4% buyback shrink gets fully capitalised.
