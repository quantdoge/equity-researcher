# GoDaddy (GDDY) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $117.24 (Oct 30, 2026) · Mkt cap ~$14.8B · EV ~$17.4B (net debt $2.69B) · Diluted shares 125.7M**

---

## 1. Growth Story

GoDaddy is executing a controlled transformation from a low-growth domain registrar into an AI-enabled SMB operating platform: **Airo**, its AI builder/agent suite, went from a $50M to a **>$100M annualized bookings run rate in a single quarter**, and Applications & Commerce (websites, payments, commerce) is now ~39% of revenue. But the honest thesis is not "disruptive hypergrowth" — it is **FCF-per-share compounding at ~15%/yr at a deep-value multiple**: revenue is growing only ~6%, yet FCF is ~$1.8B (34% margin) and management retired **12% of the diluted share count in ten months**. The stock has fully round-tripped a 58% AI-driven de-rating (high $181 → low $73 → $117) and is now being repriced as a cash machine with a free AI option.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **Core SAM ~$85B:** domains ~$10B, hosting/website tools ~$25B, SMB marketing & business apps ~$30B, addressable SMB payments/commerce ~$20B. Current revenue = **~6% penetration**.
- **Customer penetration:** 20.5M customers vs. ~330M SMBs globally — **~6%**, with growth in customer count essentially flat. Growth must come from ARPU/attach, not new logos.
- **The critical TAM question:** agentic AI (Lovable, Replit, Wix, Shopify, in-house LLM builders) can compress per-customer spend on domains/hosting. Management's counter — "agents still need real estate on the internet" — is plausible but **unproven in the P&L**: Airo at $100M is only **2.3% of $4.4B ARR**.
- **Multiple signal is category-wide:** Wix trades at **7.8x FCF** (+13% growth, Rule of 40 = 42) while Verisign, the regulated registry, trades at **26.6x FCF / 25.3x EV/EBITDA**. The market is de-rating the *registrar/reseller layer* while paying up for the monopoly registry — GDDY at ~9.7x EV/FCF sits with Wix, not Verisign.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q4'26 earnings — ~Feb 23, 2027.** First look at FY27 guide; street models ~6%. The $4.99 promo and ~200bp CO-registry drag annualize out in H1'27 → setup for the first clean comp in a year.
2. **Airo monetization disclosure.** Run rate $50M → $100M in one quarter; a third doubling (plus paid-subscriber or AI-credit disclosure) is the single most credible re-rating event.
3. **Promo lap / bookings reacceleration (Q1–Q2 2027).** Bookings were the Feb'26 blow-up; un-lapping the 1-year-term shift restores bookings growth above revenue growth.
4. **Buyback cadence.** ~95% of FCF to repurchases, $1.4B YTD at ~$89 avg; another $1.8B in 2027 = another ~10% share shrink (less accretive at $117 than at $75).
5. **Legal overhang clearance** (securities investigations re: promotional pricing disclosure) and no further insider distribution — CFO sold twice in June with zero insider buys in six months.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

From a disruption-growth lens this is **not** a Cathie Wood-style compounder: 6% revenue growth, 5% ARR growth, A&C decelerating 12% → 11% → 9%, flat gross margin (all operating leverage came from SG&A cuts, not scale economics), and a core TAM under genuine AI attack. Growth is decelerating into a structurally contested market, and the "innovation moat" (Airo) is 2% of ARR.

What prevents a Sell is the arithmetic: **~12% FCF yield, 9.7x EV/FCF, Rule of 40 = 40, 12% annual share shrink, 85% beat rate.** At 8x forward FCF the market is pricing permanent decay, and FCF/share compounding at 14–15%/yr supports a mid-teens IRR if the multiple merely holds. But after a **+58% rally off the June low** to above the median price target, the easy money is made and the next leg requires **evidence** — ARR growth >6% or Airo >$200M run rate.

**Add aggressively below ~$98 (≈7x FCF, 12.5%+ yield); trim/avoid above $130.** Upgrade to Buy if Q4'26 FY27 guidance lands ≥7% revenue or Airo run rate clears $175M.

## 7. Key Risks

- **AI disintermediation (existential, medium probability):** LLM/agent builders compress demand for domains, hosting, and templates. Wix at 7.8x FCF shows the market is not isolating GDDY's risk.
- **Execution/credibility risk (highest near-term):** the $4.99 promo pulled bookings forward and produced a self-inflicted -14% day plus securities-law investigations; two guidance-driven -15% gaps in six months.
- **Deceleration in the only growth engine:** A&C from +12% to +9% in two quarters. If A&C converges toward Core's +4%, consolidated growth goes sub-4% and the FCF multiple de-rates further.
- **Buyback dependency:** EPS growth is ~half buyback. At $117 vs. a $89 average repurchase price, the accretion math weakens materially; $3.8B debt (1.5x NEBITDA) limits flexibility if FCF dips.
- **Competition & channel:** Wix/Squarespace/Shopify upmarket, Cloudflare and Amazon on domains/hosting, Verisign's registry pricing power over .com economics.
- **Governance:** persistent insider selling (22 sales, zero buys in six months) into a recovery.
- **Data gaps:** peer multiples are noisy (WIX EV/EBITDA 134x on near-zero EBITDA); the revision-score output was unusable; and only 4 analysts cover 2029–2030, so long-dated consensus (the +38% EPS jump in 2029) is unreliable and should not be used to anchor valuation.
