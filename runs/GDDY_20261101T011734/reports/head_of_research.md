## Investment Thesis
GoDaddy is a narrow-moat FCF compounder — 34% FCF margin, 28% ROIC, ~12% FCF yield at ~8x cash flow — with essentially **zero growth priced in**, while the market over-extrapolates AI-disruption risk into terminal decline. Earnings quality is forensically clean (Beneish −3.03, 184% cash conversion, net debt flat since 2021), buybacks shrink the share count ~10%/yr, and Airo (>$100M annualised bookings, ~2% of ARR) is a free option. The discount is *partly rational* — growth is price-led at ~5–6% on a flat/shrinking customer base — so we buy the cash machine on weakness and do not chase the +10.7% post-earnings gap.

## Bull Case
- **Nothing is priced in**: ~8x FCF / 12% FCF yield with a zero-growth perpetuity (~$126) above the current price; even a bear-skewed weighting yields ~$125. 14% guided FCF growth and Q3 beat-and-reconfirm argue the decay assumption is wrong.
- **Per-share compounding machine**: operating margin 8%→27% over six years; share count 158.8M→~125M; FCF/share compounding ~14–15%/yr supports a mid-teens IRR if the multiple merely holds.
- **Free AI option**: Airo bookings doubled to >$100M in a quarter with >70% multi-product attach; gross margin has held 63–64% for five straight years — AI erosion is a volume risk, not yet a price/margin reality.

## Bear Case
- **Harvest, not compounding**: customer count declining (~−2.4%), all growth from ARPU/price; ARR growing slower than revenue signals further decel; A&C — the only growth engine — decelerating toward Core's +4%.
- **AI disintermediation**: agentic builders compress the websites/presence SKU (~40% of revenue); the CEO conceded "no one is insulated"; the market deliberately prices GDDY with WIX (~8x FCF), not VRSN (26x) — terminal-value risk caps re-rating.
- **Buyback dependency with impaired capital allocation**: EPS growth ~half share-shrink; FY25's $1.6B of buybacks executed at ~$175 avg (~33% underwater); equity now negative (−$139M); at $117 the accretion math is far weaker than at $89.

## Key Risks
1. **Legal tail**: Express Mobile treble (up to ~$510M); class-action discovery into customer-KPI definitions; a second material breach during the FTC order term = licence-to-operate event.
2. **AI terminal scenario**: value-trap path to $55–85 if the websites/domains SKU structurally deflates.
3. **Macro bear (~25% probability)**: second inflation wave → Fed ≥4.5%, 10Y >5.5%, USD >125 → retest of $75–85 even with intact operations.
4. **Execution/disclosure**: a third guidance-driven −14–17% gap; another promotional-pricing surprise converts the securities case into a pattern-of-conduct problem.
