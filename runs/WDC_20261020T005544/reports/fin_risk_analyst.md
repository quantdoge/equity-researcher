---

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Investment Grade (fundamentally) — though agency ratings not verifiable via available tools.**

WDC has transformed its balance sheet. Total debt collapsed from **$7.82B (FY24) → $5.08B (FY25) → $1.05B (FY26)**, and the company now holds **net cash of $527M** (cash $1.579B vs. debt $1.052B). Interest coverage on operating income is **32.0x** ($4,453M EBIT / $139M interest), versus a thin ~3.6x in FY25 and a *negative* coverage in FY24. This is a materially de-risked credit.

The single most important caveat: **earnings quality is poor.** FY26 net income of **$9,424M exceeds operating income of $4,453M** — implying roughly **$5B of non-operating gains** (consistent with revaluation/disposal gains around the post-SanDisk-flash structure). Every quarter shows this pattern (Q3 FY26: net income $3,205M vs. operating income $1,190M; Q4: $3,195M vs. $1,563M). Piotroski's accruals-quality flag (F4) scores **0** for this reason. **Credit metrics should be underwritten on operating income/FCF, not headline net income.** On that basis the story still holds up well.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Earnings quality / non-operating gains (~$5B in FY26).** Net income materially overstates operating profitability. Underwrite off EBIT/FCF. Piotroski F4 = 0. *(Highest-priority flag.)*
2. **Share dilution overhang.** Diluted shares 383M vs. basic 345M (~11% gap) despite $2.59B of buybacks — indicates convertible/option overhang that partially offsets repurchases (F7 = 0).
3. **Peak-cycle valuation risk.** FCF yield of only **2.6%** on an inflated $132B cap; the stock has already de-rated ~53% from its June peak. If HDD/NAND pricing rolls over, both earnings *and* the multiple compress together.
4. **Liquidity is adequate, not strong.** Quick ratio 0.97x and current ratio 1.33x are middling; ~$3.5B of current assets are receivables + inventory exposed to a demand shock.
5. **Extreme equity volatility** (81.5%) — position sizing / stop discipline essential; beta ~2.9 means a market drawdown is amplified ~3x.
