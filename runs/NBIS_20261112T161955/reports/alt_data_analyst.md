### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **BELOW (near-term conversion); ABOVE (demand)**
The two series diverge, and that divergence *is* the thesis:

- **Surprise history: 85.7% beat rate (6/7), avg +40.0%** — but this is a *low-quality* beat record. Beats are measured against a small negative EPS base (−$0.72 est vs +$0.30 actual = "141% beat"), so the metric is an artifact of sign-flipping, not operational upside. Revenue beats are the honest signal: Q3'26 $980.8M vs $907.5M est (+8%).
- **The organic deceleration the headline hides:** Q3 revenue of $980.8M included **~$325M non-recurring** asset-light revenue. **Clean revenue ≈ $656M, only +13% QoQ** versus +68% reported. The "571% YoY" masks a sharp sequential slowdown.
- **Guidance moved the wrong way:** FY26 revenue now at the **low end** of $3.0–3.4B; the $7B ARR milestone slipped from December to **February** ("some third-party capacity now scheduled for 1H27" — notably the New Jersey site serving Microsoft). ARR at 9/30 was **$6.4B** vs a $7–9B year-end target.
- **Q4 hurdle is steep:** reaching the low end needs ~$1.04B in Q4 — **+58% QoQ on a clean basis.** Meanwhile consensus sits at **$1.462B** (FY ≈ $3.43B, *above* the guidance range) with EPS of **−$0.68** vs +$0.30 actual. Consensus is ~29% above guidance-implied revenue → **elevated risk the next print "beats" on the loss line but disappoints on revenue/guide.** Estimate revision risk is skewed down near term.
- **What is genuinely above trend:** 2027 guide of **$12–13B revenue** (~4x FY26), **$17–20B of expected 2027 customer prepayments** (vs $9B+ in 2026), **$40B+ of total commitments**, Microsoft & Meta contracts, and Token Factory contract value **+17x QoQ**. Demand >> current revenue recognition.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (bullish demand fundamentals / bearish near-term estimate risk)
Demand-side alt data is uniformly strong — accelerating buildout hiring, $6.4B→$7B+ ARR, $17–20B of 2027 prepayments, 5 GW contracted power, Token Factory +17x QoQ. But every *conversion* signal (clean QoQ growth of 13%, ARR target slipping two months, FY guide to low end, capex 1.5x Street, 7.4x revenue capex intensity) says the bottleneck has shifted from **demand to delivered capacity and financing**. The market is now pricing Nebius as a levered buildout story, not a demand story — hence −54% from the $287 June peak and the −14% reaction to a revenue beat.

**Where the asymmetry sits:** at $31.3B cap the stock trades ~10.4x FY26 revenue but only **~2.4x the 2027 guide of $12–13B**. If prepayments fund 75–85% of capex as guided, the equity is cheap; if financing markets close, the −54% drawdown is not the floor. Retail sentiment (71% bullish) says the crowd has already chosen a side — which argues for waiting for the Q4 revenue/guide resolution rather than pre-positioning.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
- **High-quality:** hard financial/ARR/prepayment data from the Q3 shareholder letter and results release; 7-quarter surprise series; price/volume.
- **Medium/low:** Google Trends ends **Nov 1** — the post-earnings week is *not* captured, so the attention series lags the event by 1–2 weeks; web traffic and patent data **unavailable**; job postings and social sentiment are **web-search proxies with no quantitative history** (Indeed's "3000"/"30,000+" headers are unreliable artifacts; the ~60-role count is the credible figure).
- **Structural caution:** alt data measures *demand intent*, and in this name demand is demonstrably not the constraint. Alt data cannot see power interconnection queues, third-party colocation delivery, or credit-market access — the three variables that will actually set Q4/Q1 results. Treat the demand signals as a leading indicator on 2027, not on the February print.
