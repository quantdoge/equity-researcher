# Macroeconomic Assessment — Nebius Group N.V. (NBIS)

**As of 13 November 2026. Company:** AI neocloud / GPU infrastructure, Netherlands-domiciled, USD-reporting, $31.3B market cap, beta 1.56, 2026 capex guided $20–25B.

## 1. Macro Environment Summary — **RISK-OFF / TRANSITIONAL (hawkish-hold, term-premium shock)**

The regime is a "no-landing" stagflation-lite: growth is *above* trend while inflation is *re-accelerating*, which has removed the easing cycle that long-duration growth equities had priced. Real GDP rose **+3.4% in Q3 2026** (Q2 +2.2%, Q1 +2.5%) and unemployment sits at **4.1%** — no recession signal anywhere in the activity data. Yet CPI is running **~3.8% YoY** and, critically, **~5.7% annualised over the last three months** (CPI 332.8 in Jul → 337.5 in Oct). The Fed **held at 3.75–4.00% on 28 October with two dissents in favour of a HIKE**, stating inflation "remains elevated." Effective Fed Funds (3.88% in Oct) is *above* its mid-2026 trough of 3.63%.

The bond market has done the tightening: the **30Y yield ran from 4.99% (1 Jun) to 5.67% (7 Oct)** before retracing to 5.26%; the 10Y peaked at 5.31% and sits at 4.88%. With 10Y breakevens *contained at 2.20-2.22%*, the selloff is **almost entirely real yields (10Y real ≈ 2.66%)** — i.e., term premium and fiscal supply, not inflation expectations. That is the most hostile possible mix for a zero-earnings, capital-hungry equity: the discount rate rises while the inflation offset (pricing power in nominal cash flows) does not.

Credit is the tell. HY OAS at **3.30%** is still near cycle tights, but has widened **+67bp from 2.63% on 31 Aug**. Equity volatility confirms the fragility: VIX spiked to **28.18 on 5 Nov** (from 14.8 in early October) around a "neocloud debt scare" and midterm shock, now 17.5. Credit and equity are diverging — the funding window that AI neoclouds depend on is narrowing before spreads have actually blown out.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

Nebius is the single most rate- and credit-sensitive business model in the AI complex, for three simultaneous reasons: (a) **valuation** is almost entirely terminal value (minimal current earnings), so duration is extreme; (b) it is funding **$20–25B of 2026 capex against a $31B market cap** — capex equals 64-80% of equity value, an unprecedented funding requirement; (c) it has already begun borrowing on a **floating-rate, GPU-backed basis ($775M secured in July 2026 at SOFR + 250bp ≈ 6.4% today)**, so every basis point of policy rate is a direct cash cost and every GPU financed this way is collateral marked against a depreciating asset.

**The mechanism, stated plainly:** this is not primarily an earnings story right now, it is a **funding-liquidity story**. On 12 November Nebius beat revenue, posted $0.30 EPS vs a -$0.61 consensus loss, guided FY revenue *down* on third-party capacity delays, and raised capex to $20–25B — and the stock fell ~20% pre-market and closed at $130. The market is not paying for growth; it is discounting the financing of growth at 5%+ long-end yields with widening credit spreads.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

This is the highest-conviction unfavourable rating available in the framework. Three channels point the same way at once: a hawkish-hold Fed with two votes to hike, a real-yield-driven long-end selloff that hits long-duration equity hardest, and a credit market that is tightening exactly at the moment NBIS needs $20-25B. Historical note: the position is not without offset — Q3 EPS of +$0.30 implies a mark-to-market/valuation gain, and the stock carries far less debt than peers (per analyst commentary), so it is not a near-term solvency story. But macro is a **clear headwind**, not a neutral, and it dominates stock-specific good news.

## 5. Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BEAR: the credit window closes (Dec 2026 FOMC hike)**

The two October dissents in favour of a hike are the tell that consensus is too complacent about the next move. My surprise scenario: **the Fed hikes 25bp on 9 December 2026 to 4.00–4.25%** as Q4 core inflation prints 0.4%+ again, the 30Y retests 5.67% (and breaks to 5.9% on heavy Treasury supply), and HY OAS gaps from 3.30% to **4.75%+** as AI-adjacent credit is repriced. Under that path, the secured, GPU-collateralised, floating-rate financing NBIS needs to fund $20–25B of capex becomes either unavailable or punitively priced (~SOFR+350 = ~8.7%), forcing an equity raise at a deeply discounted price. With the stock already down 54% from June and 38% in the last month, a further **35-50% drawdown to the $65-85 range** is a live tail. *Inverse bull surprise (equally tradable): inflation rolls over to 2.5% by spring 2027, the Fed cuts 100bp, the 10Y falls to 4.0%, and NBIS re-rates 100%+ on pure duration — which is precisely why the position must be sized as a rates call, not a fundamentals call.*

**Bottom line for the memo:** NBIS's macro is unfavourable and the January-June 2026 tailwind (Fed cutting from 4.33% to 3.63%) has fully reversed. Treat NBIS as a leveraged short-duration position: it works only if disinflation resumes and the 10Y breaks below ~4.5%. Any thesis built on Q3's revenue beat is fighting a 4.9% 10-year and a hawkish Fed.
