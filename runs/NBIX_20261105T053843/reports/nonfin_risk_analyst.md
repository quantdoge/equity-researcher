# Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. (NBIX)

**Snapshot:** $16.8B market cap, $166.05/share, ~2,500 employees, San Diego. Commercial-stage neuroscience/endocrine biotech. Q3 2026 revenue $1.123B (+41% YoY); first $1B+ product sales quarter. FY26 INGREZZA net sales guidance $2.9–$2.925B. Three marketed products: INGREZZA (valbenazine), CRENESSITY (CAH), VYKAT XR (Prader-Willi, acquired via Soleno, closed 18-May-2026 for $2.9B).

*Note on sourcing gaps: the 10-K and DEF 14A were not retrievable through the filing feed (the 10-Q returned only an exhibit; SEC's annual-meeting notice returned a 403). Governance observations below rely on secondary sources and Form 4 data rather than primary proxy text, and are flagged accordingly.*

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate** (with weak-leaning features)

**Evidence for:**
- Orderly, planned CEO succession: founder Kevin Gorman retired 11-Oct-2024; Kyle Gano (ex-Chief Business Development) appointed President/CEO after a Board-run process. No abrupt departure, no interim chaos.
- No SEC enforcement actions or restatements. Clean EDGAR full-text search for enforcement mentions.
- Broad independent director base (Sherwin, Morrow, Pops, Lyons, Rastetter, Norwalk) with relevant industry expertise.
- No related-party transactions surfaced in available disclosure.

**Evidence against — this is where I'd push back on management:**
- **Classified board.** Only 3 director nominees stand at the 2026 annual meeting. Staggered elections entrench the board and blunt shareholder accountability — relevant because the board just approved a $2.9B cash acquisition.
- **Founder remains on the board** post-CEO tenure (Gorman is a director; director comp ~$470K). Ex-CEOs on the board of their successor are a well-documented source of strategy drag and awkward oversight.
- **New CEO is untested in the seat** — Gano is ~24 months in, and his two defining acts so far are a large M&A deal and a safety controversy inherited with it. Key-person risk on the regulatory/quality side has shifted, not disappeared.
- **Equity plan creep:** ESPP amended and restated by the Compensation Committee on 11-Sep-2026 (outside the annual meeting), with the share reserve raised to 1.7M in 2025 (+800K). Serial dilution via ESPP/PRSU grants without visible shareholder ratification this cycle.
- **Compensation alignment cannot be confirmed** — proxy unavailable. Unverified alignment is itself a flag.

**Insider behavior is the loudest governance signal here** (see §3): universal, heavy, sustained selling with zero conviction buying.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

## 3. Insider Activity Analysis — **Overwhelmingly net selling; effectively zero buying**

67 Form 4 filings on record. Every economically meaningful transaction in the trailing 12 months is a sale (many as exercise-and-sell). **There are no open-market purchases by any officer or director.** The lone "insider bought shares" headline in the press on 4-Nov-2026 is misleading — it reflects the CEO **converting 614 RSUs**, not buying.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Product concentration / single-asset dependency.** INGREZZA at ~$2.9B of guidance is roughly two-thirds of total revenue and essentially all of near-term free cash flow. A label restriction, TD-market pricing reset, or payer pushback hits the P&L directly. Diversification via CRENESSITY and VYKAT is real but the acquired asset is the one now under a safety cloud.
- **M&A integration and inherited quality risk.** The Soleno deal ($53/share cash, 34% premium, $2.9B) closed 18-May-2026. Neurocrine inherited a first-in-class rare-disease launch *plus* an open clinical-integrity lawsuit *plus* an emerging FAERS safety cluster — with only ~6 months of ownership. Integration of pharmacovigilance and medical affairs for a drug with an active withdrawal petition is a live execution risk.
- **Supply chain and CMO dependency for a controlled-substance franchise.** Valbenazine is a Schedule V controlled substance with long-cycle, single-source synthetic supply; diazoxide choline adds a second specialized supply chain. No disclosed disruption, but inventory and CMO redundancy are unverified — a gap, not a clearance. (No cybersecurity incident disclosed for NBIX in available sources; absence of evidence only.)

---

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **Elevated and rising**

- **Active, named-advocate campaign against VYKAT XR.** A former FDA official publicly demanding withdrawal, national press (STAT, Reuters), and a patient-advocacy community (Prader-Willi families) that is small, tightly networked, and unusually effective at amplification. In rare disease, advocacy sentiment is a regulatory input, not just PR.
- **ICER's "up to 90% discount needed" verdict** on TD drugs lands NBIX in the overpriced-CNS-franchise narrative — the same frame driving state-level pricing bills and payer utilization management. Combined with the 2025 FDA misbranding letter, the company's commercial function now has two public integrity marks in ~12 months.
- **Countervailing positive:** the Q3 beat, the raised INGREZZA guide, and a forward P/E near 14x show the market is currently pricing execution, not scandal. Reputational damage is concentrated in the rare-disease/PWS channel and would become enterprise-level only if FDA acts on the withdrawal petition.

---

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Rationale: no fraud indicators, no SEC/DOJ enforcement, and a long-dated IP runway argue against Critical. But three independent, dated, high-severity exposures coexist — an unresolved fatal-event safety signal in a newly acquired growth asset, certain IRA exposure to the dominant product from 2029, and a governance picture where every informed insider is a seller — against a board that is staggered, retains its founder, and just wrote a $2.9B check. That is a High, not a Moderate.

---

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **VYKAT XR withdrawal petition (HIGH).** 7 deaths + ~100 hospitalizations in 18 months post-approval, an ex-FDA official petitioning CMS/FDA, and no disclosed company-initiated label change or REMS. Ask management directly: what FAERS follow-up has been filed, has FDA requested a labeling negotiation or REMS, and what is the revenue contingency if VYKAT is pulled or restricted? A withdrawal would strand a material slice of the $2.9B Soleno purchase price — impairment risk.
2. **Chief Regulatory Officer selling ~47% of her holding while that petition is pending.** Delaet sold 14,571 shares at $178.65 in July 2026. This is the single most information-rich trade in the file. Request 10b5-1 adoption dates and any pre-clearance documentation.
3. **Founder's $37.8M sale coincident with the Soleno close.** Gorman exited a third of his stake within a day of the deal closing. Either benign diversification or a signal about his conviction in the acquisition he helped approve from the board. Demand an explanation.
4. **IRA selection risk for INGREZZA (~Feb-2027).** The small biotech exception expires after IPAY 2028; valbenazine is flagged as IPAY 2029-eligible. Model a 2029 net-price reset now — the Cantor commentary already concedes a revenue "dip" transitioning 2028→2029. This is scheduled, knowable, and apparently under-appreciated in a 14x forward multiple.
5. **FDA Untitled Letter (Sept-2025) for a false/misleading INGREZZA TV ad.** Misbranding exposure plus a signal of degraded promotional review. Confirm whether corrective communications were required and whether OPDP has issued a follow-up.
6. **Soleno clinical-integrity class action was inherited.** Confirm the deal's indemnification/escrow structure and whether the liability is capped or shared with former Soleno holders — an unquantified contingent liability sitting inside a newly consolidated subsidiary.
7. **Classified board + off-cycle ESPP amendment.** Governance structure limits the ability of shareholders to register dissatisfaction with the M&A and safety record; the 11-Sep-2026 Comp Committee ESPP restatement warrants a dilution question at the next meeting.

**What would change my view:** an FDA announcement of a labeling/REMS resolution for VYKAT (downgrade to Moderate); any open-market insider purchase, particularly by Gano or Delaet (meaningfully improves governance read); a clean CMS IPAY 2029 list in Feb-2027 excluding valbenazine (would materially de-risk the largest asset). Conversely, an FDA Safety Communication or withdrawal action, or a securities class action tied to the VYKAT disclosures, moves this to **Critical**.
