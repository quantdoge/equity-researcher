# Neurocrine Biosciences (NBIX) — Equity Growth Analysis

**Price $166.05 | Market Cap ~$16.8B | EV ~$16.0B | Net cash position**
*(Data as of latest reported Q3 FY2026; stock last at $166.05 following a +13.4% post-earnings move on Nov 3)*

---

## 1. Growth Story

Neurocrine is executing the rarest and most valuable transition in biopharma: converting a single-product franchise (INGREZZA in tardive dyskinesia) into a diversified, first-in-class neuroscience/endocrine platform **without a revenue air-pocket**. Q3 FY2026 was the proof point — revenue crossed $1 billion for the first time (+41% YoY), with INGREZZA at $801M and two new launches, CRENESSITY (CAH) and VYKAT XR (Prader-Willi hyperphagia), contributing $318M — **28% of revenue from products that did not exist 24 months ago**. The market is still valuing NBIX as a maturing single-asset TD franchise (4.0x forward sales, 13.7x FY2028 EPS), while the actual business is compounding ~40% with 98% gross margins and an entirely unpriced Phase 3 muscarinic asset in schizophrenia.

**Total addressable >$25B; NBIX captures ~$4B (~16%).** Crucially, the *growth* is coming from the 15%-penetrated assets, not the 80%-penetrated one. CRENESSITY did $337M in H1 2026, up ~400% YoY — a genuine first-in-class CRF1 antagonist with no direct competitor and a chronic, lifelong pediatric-to-adult population. VYKAT XR is the only FDA-approved therapy for PWS hyperphagia.

**The moat:** proprietary molecule plus **deep payer/access infrastructure** — a reusable commercial chassis (neurology + endocrinology + rare disease sales forces, specialty pharmacy relationships) that lets each new asset ramp at incremental rather than full cost. That's why operating margin is *expanding* (29.5% in Q3 FY2026, a record) while two launches are being funded simultaneously.

**INGREZZA durability is better than the market assumes:** ANDA settlements push generic entry to **no earlier than March 1, 2038** — a 12-year runway on 72% of revenue.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **FY2027 guidance (Q4 FY2026 report, ~Feb 2027)** — first framework incorporating full CRENESSITY/VYKAT ramp plus IRA Medicare negotiation impact. The single most likely revision event; consensus +14.2% revenue growth looks beatable.
2. **CRENESSITY trajectory through 2027** — path from ~$1B to $1.5B+ run-rate; two-year growth data in pediatric classic CAH already presented at ENDO 2026 supports label/guideline expansion.
3. **NBI-1117568 Phase 3 (schizophrenia) — the big one.** Only oral M4-selective orthosteric agonist in Phase 3; positive Phase 2 at 20mg. Any enrollment/readout datapoint is a multi-billion-dollar TAM event currently valued at roughly zero.
4. **NBI-'567 Phase 2 in Alzheimer's** (initiated Oct 2026) — early cognition data; free option on a $10B+ indication.
5. **VYKAT XR safety/regulatory resolution** — see risks; a clean FDA review or AdCom removes a live overhang.
6. **Capital deployment** — $168M buyback in FY2025, $300M in FY2024, ~$1B net cash. Continued repurchases at 13x forward earnings are accretive.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (accumulate; Strong Buy below $150)

**Why not Strong Buy:** INGREZZA is still 72% of revenue, the IRA negotiation overhang on that asset is unresolved, VYKAT XR carries a genuine safety question, and consensus already models a sharp deceleration (2027 +14%, 2029 +2.8%). The quality of the Q3 beat has been partly recognized (+13% day).

**Why Buy is still compelling:** you are paying 4.0x forward sales and **12x FY2030 consensus EPS** for a business that just grew 41% with 98% gross margins, a 47.6 Rule of 40, $749M of FCF, net cash, and a patent runway to 2038 on its largest product. The implied 5-year math: holding a 16–18x multiple on $13.88 of FY2030 EPS yields $222–250, or ~11–15% annualized — **before** assigning any value to a Phase 3 M4 agonist in a $10B+ market. The downside is protected by a business that is already profitable, cash-generative, and buying back stock. Asymmetric.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Concentration + IRA pricing (highest probability).** INGREZZA at 72% of revenue is exposed to Medicare Drug Price Negotiation; consensus already bakes in net price erosion that offsets most volume growth. A worse-than-expected "maximum fair price" would compress the 2030 revenue line that the current 12x multiple is discounting.
2. **VYKAT XR safety signal (highest severity, lowest probability).** Oct 13, 2026 reports of deaths in PWS patients and a former FDA regulator publicly calling for withdrawal. Direct revenue at risk is modest (~$0.35–0.4B), but a label restriction, boxed warning, or withdrawal would hit credibility for the entire "acquire-and-scale rare disease" strategy.
3. **NBI-1117568 Phase 3 execution.** The muscarinic M4 class is not de-risked — AbbVie/Cerevel's emraclidine *failed* Phase 2 in schizophrenia despite the KarXT validation. A Phase 3 miss removes the largest piece of unpriced optionality and would likely take 15–25% off the stock.
4. **CRENESSITY payer friction.** ~$300k/yr list price into a chronic, mostly-pediatric population invites utilization management. Gross-to-net deterioration is the quiet killer of rare-disease ramps — watch implied net price per patient, not just revenue.
5. **Consensus deceleration may be right.** FY2029 revenue growth of +2.8% in consensus is essentially a "franchise stalls" model. If CRENESSITY penetration caps near 25% and VYKAT is constrained, the terminal multiple is ~14x earnings, capping upside near $200.
6. **GAAP/non-GAAP gap.** SBC of $218M (7.6% of revenue) plus IPR&D charges explain the large spread between non-GAAP EPS ($3.29 in Q3) and GAAP ($1.96). Valuation on non-GAAP is generous; on GAAP FCF it is fair, not cheap.
7. **Patent tail risk.** The March 2038 generic entry date rests on ANDA settlements; the underlying compound patent (US 8,039,627) runs to April 2031. Litigation setbacks would re-price the INGREZZA terminal value.

**Bottom line:** NBIX is a 40%-growth, 98%-gross-margin, self-funding platform trading at a mature-single-asset multiple. The market is paying for INGREZZA's decay and getting CRENESSITY, VYKAT XR, and a Phase 3 muscarinic franchise for free. Buy, size for the VYKAT and Phase 3 binaries, and add aggressively on any move back toward $145–150.
