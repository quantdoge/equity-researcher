## NBIX — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment
*Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. | Healthcare / Biotechnology | San Diego, CA | Mkt cap ~$16.8B*

---

### 1. Geographic Risk Map

Neurocrine is a **single-segment, effectively US-only commercial business**. Q3 2026 revenue of $1,123M comprised $1,119M of US net product sales (99.6%) and $4M of "Other Revenues" (partner royalties). The company's own 10-Q describes it as operating "as a single global business segment."

| Geography | Revenue exposure | Basis | Risk level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **United States** | **~99.6%** | INGREZZA $801M, CRENESSITY $211M, VYKAT XR $107M (Q3'26) | **Medium** — policy/pricing risk, not geopolitical |
| **Japan / East Asia** | **<0.5%** | Royalty on DYSVAL (valbenazine) sold by Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma; launched Japan June 2022 | Low |
| **Europe / UK** | **~0%** | No commercial presence; BIAL (Portugal) is an in-licensing counterparty (ONGENTYS), not a revenue source | Low |
| **China / Russia / EM** | **0%** | No disclosed operations, manufacturing, or revenue | Nil |

**Key insight:** NBIX carries essentially **zero direct geopolitical, sanctions, currency-control, or expropriation exposure**. The risk is inverted — it is *concentration* in a single payer system (US Medicare/Medicaid/commercial) that drives the risk profile, not foreign jurisdictions.

**Supply chain (the one cross-border vector):** Neurocrine uses an **outsourced manufacturing model** and states it "depend[s] on a limited number of suppliers for the production (including API) of INGREZZA, CRENESSITY" and VYKAT XR (FY2025 10-K). Specific CMO geographies are not disclosed in retrievable filings — a genuine information gap.

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**#1 — IRA Medicare Drug Price Negotiation: INGREZZA selection for IPAY 2029**
- **Probability: High (~70–80%)** | **Impact: High**
- CMS must announce IPAY 2029 selections **by February 1, 2027** — i.e., within ~3 months. Peer-reviewed selection models (JMCP 2026, *"Which drugs will Medicare negotiate next?"*) explicitly list **valbenazine (Ingrezza) as newly eligible because the small-biotech exception has expired**, with ~$2.4B in Medicare Part D spend.
- INGREZZA is **71% of Q3'26 revenue** ($801M/qtr; FY26 guidance $2.9–2.925B). A negotiated price effective 2029 would compress the single largest cash flow in the company. This is the dominant policy risk in the name and is *not* priced as a near-term binary by most models.

**#2 — VYKAT XR safety controversy escalating toward regulatory action**
- **Probability of label change/monitoring mandate: Medium-High (~50%)** | **Probability of withdrawal: Low (~10%)** | **Impact: Medium**
- As of July 31, 2026, **7 deaths and >100 serious adverse events** (edema, respiratory, cardiac) reported to FAERS since the March 2025 approval (MedPage Today, Aug 12, 2026; STAT, Aug 12, 2026).
- In **October 2026, a former FDA medical officer (Karen Vogt, pediatric endocrinologist) publicly petitioned FDA to withdraw VYKAT XR**; other experts urge caution (STAT, Oct 13, 2026). Neurocrine says it is "closely engaged with the FDA."
- VYKAT XR is ~$107M/qtr (~$430M annualized, ~10% of revenue) and is the entire strategic rationale for the **$2.9B Soleno acquisition closed May 18, 2026**. A restricted label or REMS would impair the deal thesis directly.

**#3 — Section 232 pharmaceutical tariffs on imported drug/API supply**
- **Probability of cost impact: Medium** | **Impact: Low-Medium**
- The April 2, 2026 Section 232 proclamation imposed tariffs **up to 100%** on patented pharmaceutical imports, effective **July 31, 2026**, with a second tranche for 17 named Annex III companies from **September 29, 2026** (White House Proclamation; Crowell, WilmerHale client alerts).
- Neurocrine is not a named Annex III company, but its **limited-supplier, outsourced CMO/API chain** creates unquantified pass-through cost and continuity risk. Duty drawback is available, which partially mitigates.

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Positive (risk-reducing):**
- **INGREZZA ANDA litigation fully settled.** Four generic filers hold US launch rights beginning **March 1, 2038**; Sandoz from **February 1, 2040** (or earlier under certain circumstances). This effectively removes any near-term patent cliff on the 71%-of-revenue asset — a materially better position than most specialty pharma peers. INGREZZA is protected by 22 US patents.

**Negative (risk-adding):**
- **Soleno securities class action (successor liability).** *City of Pontiac Police and Fire Retirement System v. Soleno Therapeutics, et al.*, No. **3:26-cv-01979** (N.D. Cal., filed Mar 6, 2026). Class period Mar 26, 2025 – Nov 4, 2025. Alleges Soleno concealed fluid-retention, pulmonary edema, pre-diabetes and CHF risks in the DCCR/VYKAT XR program. Claims predate Neurocrine's May 2026 close and should sit behind D&O/R&W insurance and indemnification — but the *factual overlap* with the current FAERS safety signal is uncomfortable and raises the odds of follow-on claims.
- **Thin VYKAT XR IP moat.** Only **6 US patents, all filed in 2025**, and one patent litigation case — versus 22 for INGREZZA. Newly issued, untested patents on a drug with an active safety controversy is a weak defensive position.
- **CRENESSITY class risk.** Carries a warning for acute adrenal insufficiency/adrenal crisis — a known, label-managed risk in CAH, but one where any serious AE cluster would attract scrutiny given the VYKAT backdrop.

**Not found / unverified:** No DOJ, SEC, FTC or FCPA investigation identified. No 340B or Medicaid rebate dispute identified. No securities class action against Neurocrine itself. (Note: the 10-K risk-factor text and 10-Q "Legal Proceedings" section could not be retrieved — SEC direct fetch returned 403 and the filing tool returned an exhibit rather than the body. This is a documentation gap, not evidence of absence.)

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

**None material.** No sanctions, AML, FCPA, or export-control exposure given the US-only footprint. Two watch items:
1. **Promotional/off-label risk** — a large CNS/psychiatry sales force (INGREZZA in tardive dyskinesia, plus a new obesity/metabolic push) is inherently exposed to off-label promotion and Anti-Kickback scrutiny.
2. **Post-marketing AE reporting adequacy** — the VYKAT XR episode invites FDA scrutiny of whether Neurocrine's pharmacovigilance and label communications were adequate post-close. This is the most plausible route to a formal enforcement action.

---

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale:** Geopolitically this is close to a **Low**-risk asset — ~100% US revenue, no China/Russia/EM exposure, no sanctions surface, and a settled patent estate extending to 2038–2040. The rating is lifted to **Medium** by two *domestic regulatory* concentrations that are idiosyncratic and near-dated:

1. **IRA selection of INGREZZA by February 1, 2027** — a high-probability, high-impact event against 71% of revenue.
2. **VYKAT XR safety/regulatory escalation** — an active, publicly escalating controversy with a formal withdrawal petition before FDA, sitting on a $2.9B acquisition and a thin patent estate.

**What would move this to High:** CMS selecting INGREZZA for IPAY 2029 *combined with* an FDA label restriction or REMS on VYKAT XR. **What would move it to Low:** INGREZZA excluded from the IPAY 2029 list and the VYKAT safety signal resolving as disease-background noise.

**Monitoring triggers:** CMS IPAY 2029 announcement (by Feb 1, 2027); any FDA Drug Safety Communication or label revision on VYKAT XR; Q4'26 10-K legal proceedings disclosure; ruling activity in 3:26-cv-01979.
