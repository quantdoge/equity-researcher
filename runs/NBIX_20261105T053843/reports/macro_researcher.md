### 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional — a hawkish reflation regime. Risk-on for earnings, risk-off for duration.**

The dominant macro fact of 2026 is that the disinflation trade died and the Fed reversed. After cutting from 5.33% (Aug 2023–Sep 2024) to a trough of **3.63% (Jun–Jul 2026)**, the effective funds rate has turned back up: **3.75% (Sep) → 3.88% (Oct 2026)**. This is not a pause; it is a tightening bias, and the front end confirms it — the **2Y at 4.74% sits 86bp above fed funds**, i.e. the market is pricing further hikes, not cuts.

Why: CPI bottomed at **+2.39% YoY in January 2026** and has re-accelerated to **+3.72% YoY in September 2026** (index 336.298 vs 324.245), with a hot **+0.65% m/m** in September. Meanwhile **Q3 2026 real GDP came in at 3.4% SAAR** (Q2 2.2%, Q1 2.5%) and unemployment has *fallen* to **4.2%** from 4.5% in November 2025. With policy at 3.88% against 3.72% CPI, the **real policy rate is barely +16bp while the economy is growing 3.4%** — policy is not restrictive. The Fed has to keep going, and the long end has said so.

The yield curve is **bear-steepening, not inverting**: 2s10s at **+39bp**, but 30Y at **5.47%** and 10Y at **5.13%** (5 Nov). The 10Y ran from **4.44% (30 Jun) to a 5.31% peak (5 Oct) — +87bp in three months**. The steep long end, with 30Y 34bp over 10Y and unemployment at 4.2%, is a term-premium/fiscal signal: a pro-cyclical fiscal stance and heavy long-dated supply, not a growth signal. **No recession signal is present** — the curve is positively sloped and growth is accelerating.

Dollar: the broad USD index rose from **117.88 (9 Sep) to 121.67 (30 Oct), +3.2% in seven weeks**, tracking the yield move.

### 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

NBIX is not a typical clinical-stage biotech. Q3 2026 delivered **revenue of $1.123B, +41% YoY, the first $1B+ net-product-sales quarter, record operating profitability, and EPS of $3.29 vs $2.04 a year ago**, with FY26 INGREZZA guidance raised to **$2.90–2.925B**. At a ~**14.2x forward P/E** with a 0.36 beta, its cash flows are near-term and US-domestic. That materially changes the macro transmission channels.

| Macro Variable | Impact | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Fed Funds / 2Y — hiking cycle** | **Negative (mild)** | NBIX does not have a leverage problem; the rate channel runs through *cost of capital and sector multiple*, plus a higher hurdle for BD/M&A. Its short-duration, profitable profile is the sector's best natural hedge. |
| **10Y at 5.13% / bear steepener** | **Negative (moderate)** | Biotech is the longest-duration equity sector. Empirically visible: **XBI fell 13.6% from 25 Aug (169.55) to 13 Oct (146.80) while the 10Y rose +57bp**; NBIX fell **-17.9% from 30 Jun to 25 Sep (168.54 → 138.40)** as the 10Y went 4.44% → 4.96%. Every 50bp on the 10Y is worth roughly 8–12% of sector multiple. |

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL** *(with a distinct negative policy skew)*

**Justification.** The cyclical macro backdrop is genuinely unfavourable for NBIX's *multiple*: a hiking Fed, a 5.13% 10Y, re-accelerating CPI and a firm dollar are the textbook recipe for biotech de-rating — and 2026 proves it, with NBIX down ~10% from its 30 Jul high of $185.50 despite blowout results. But the *fundamental* macro sensitivity is unusually low: non-cyclical demand, domestic revenue, no FX translation problem, no refinancing wall, and positive earnings at 14x. The stock has outperformed the tape on idiosyncratic execution (+13.4% on 3 Nov, 145.35 → 164.85; now back above its ~$147.60 200-DMA).

The offsetting negative is policy, not the cycle. Section 232 tariffs at 20% now and 100% possible from April 2027, twinned with MFN pricing as the exemption price, is a live, dated, quantifiable threat to gross and net margins in a 2027–2028 investment horizon. That is a bigger deal for NBIX than the entire rate complex.

**YTD context:** NBIX **+18.1%** (140.60 → 166.05) vs **XBI +31.1%** (121.40 → 159.10) — NBIX has lagged a strong biotech year by ~13pp on idiosyncratic (VYKAT XR safety/patent) issues, not on macro.

### 5. The Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise (Bull)

**Consensus is hawkish. The non-consensus outcome is a violent duration rally in H1 2027.**

The current 100% tariff path is a *price-level shock*, not a persistent inflation process. If the September–October 2026 CPI prints prove to be tariff pass-through ahead of the April 2027 escalation — with firms front-running imports — then headline CPI rolls over hard by Q1 2027 as the base effect annualises and goods disinflation resumes. Simultaneously, 3.4% Q3 growth decelerates toward trend as the 87bp rise in the 10Y does the Fed's work for it.

That combination would force the Fed to abandon its hiking bias and the **10Y back from 5.13% toward 4.25–4.50%**. Biotech, having de-rated all through the Sept–Oct yield spike, re-rates fastest of any sector. For NBIX specifically, a 14x multiple on 40%-growth, record-profitability earnings is priced for a policy disaster that may not arrive — a re-rating to 18–20x on a falling discount rate plus realisation that the tariff/MFN fork is negotiable (onshore commitment for 0% duty) is a **+35–45% move** without a single change to 2027 consensus revenue.

**Bear tail (must be flagged):** the 100% rate takes effect April 2027 with no MFN deal and no Annex IV exemption, while IRA selects a VMAT2 inhibitor. That is a gross-margin and terminal-value shock simultaneously, and 14x becomes 20x on collapsed estimates.
