**Investment Thesis**

Nebius is the fastest-growing neocloud (+571% YoY in Q3, $38.7B RPO, customer prepayments >100% of associated capex) inside the strongest demand cycle in tech history — and demand is not the problem. The equity is a **capital-markets derivative**: 2027 capex guidance of $40–45B against a $34.5B market cap forces a $25–30B external raise that arrives either as debt on a CCC-equivalent credit or as ~75%+ dilutive equity issued by a controlled company (52% votes / 11% economics) with no minority recourse. With ROIC negative four years running, reported profits manufactured from revaluation gains and prepayment inflows, and a hostile duration backdrop (10Y 5.13%), current shareholders are underwriting the buildout without a reliable claim on its returns. The team agrees almost unanimously on demand and splits almost entirely on financing — and financing is what sets the price.

**Bull Case**
1. **Contracted, prepaid demand:** $38.7B RPO anchored by Microsoft and Meta, prepayments covering >100% of associated capex, and rising GPU-hour pricing — the strongest possible customer-commitment signal, with FY27 guidance ($12–13B) above consensus.
2. **Best-in-class operations:** fastest growth in the cohort, 74–77% underlying gross margin, first GAAP operating profit, NVIDIA's $2B warrant endorsement and Exemplar Cloud allocation status.
3. **Asymmetric re-rating setup:** financing clarity + a clean Deloitte ICFR opinion + any retrace in the 10Y could trigger a violent re-rating; 20% short interest is accelerant.

**Bear Case**
1. **Dilution and growth are the same event:** $25–30B external need = ~220–260M new shares on a 298M base if equity-funded; the Growth bull IV (~$160 on 380M shares) collapses to ~today's price on the pro-forma cap table. Kelly fraction ≤ 0.
2. **Earnings quality:** 33% of Q3 revenue non-recurring; 9M net income entirely a ClickHouse revaluation gain (ex-gain: −$387M); OCF is prepayments, not earnings; depreciation policy flatters the asset base.
3. **Duration + funding accident:** Fed behind the curve with real rates ~0%; the bear path (10Y >6%, HY normalising to ~5%) shuts the GPU-backed financing market this company depends on.

**Key Risks** *(to the SELL call — upside):* a financing package with <15% equity dilution; Meta/Microsoft expansion or a third anchor; a short squeeze into any de-risking announcement; 10Y retracing below 4.75%. *(Downside accelerants):* a large equity raise at depressed prices; Microsoft deferring/rejecting a late tranche (New Jersey already slipped to Q2'27); adverse Vineland/Birmingham rulings halting capacity; a securities class action following the −61% drawdown; restrictive AI Diffusion successor rule.
