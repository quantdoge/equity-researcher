## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss — fundamental, not just price.** A conventional stop is useless at 62% vol (a −25% move is inside one sigma, and the observed max drawdown of the tool's window is already −25%). Use:
- **Hard price stop: close below $105** (below the 20-Nov pre-data close of $115.31) → exit fully. At that point the market is pricing something the reports have not yet captured.
- **Fundamental stops — exit on any of:**
  1. FDA requires an **additional dose-ranging study**, or Phase 3 is not initiated by **YE-2027** (the non-monotonic dose response is the live risk here).
  2. **52-week OLE shows durability decay or a rising conjunctivitis imbalance** — this is the "cleaner than Dupilumab" narrative breaking.
  3. **BREADTH asthma topline (late 2027) misses or shows a materially diluted effect size** — kills the TAM-expansion leg, which is where 100% of the incremental upside sits.
  4. **A competitor posts comparable/better oral STAT6 or oral IL-23 data** — collapses the first-in-class premium, which is the whole moat.
  5. **Another equity raise below $120** — proves the window shut and resets per-share value again.
  6. **Sustained, non-10b5-1 insider net selling** at these levels (ESG already flags ~$392M/6mo; treat direction confirmation as a live monitor).

**Trim triggers:**
- **$215–235** (upper analyst targets / ~1.4x blended FV) → **trim ⅓ to ½**.
- **Any price above $250** → trim to a residual ≤0.5% "lottery ticket."
- **Mandatory de-risking rule: cut to ≤0.75% before the Phase 3 AD readout (~2029).** A 2% position into a binary readout with a −80% failure mode is a 160bp portfolio event — that is not a holding, it is an unhedged bet, and the whole point of position sizing is to never let one dataset determine a year's return.
