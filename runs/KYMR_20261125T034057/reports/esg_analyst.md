## KYMR — Kymera Therapeutics, Inc. | Full ESG Analysis
*Healthcare / Biotechnology (clinical-stage, targeted protein degradation) · Watertown, MA · Market cap ~$14.8B · 273 FTEs · Single share class · Nasdaq*

**Materiality note up front:** KYMR is a pre-commercial biotech with E&P logic that is the *inverse* of a heavy-industrial company. Environmental factors are close to immaterial to cash flows; Social factors are dominated by **patient safety in clinical trials** and **access/pricing**; Governance is where the real, near-term, financially relevant risk sits — and 2026 has produced multiple live governance signals.

### 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **LOW**

- No Scope 1 industrial emissions; no data-centre or heavy logistics footprint. Eligible for de minimis treatment.
- **Physical climate risk:** Watertown/MA lab — modest flood/storm exposure; no manufacturing plants or water-stressed operations.
- **Regulatory:** Essentially no exposure to EU CSRD (subsidiary-level EU revenue immaterial), CBAM (no exported carbon-intensive goods) or carbon taxes.
- **Residual E issues worth flagging:** (i) outsourced API/drug-substance manufacturing with **single-source suppliers** disclosed as a risk factor — the "environmental" content here is chemical/solvent handling and EHS at *third-party* sites, i.e., an unmanaged Scope 3 governance gap; (ii) preclinical animal use is inherent to the model (3Rs optics only); (iii) no environmental controversies, fines or remediation liabilities found.
- **Verdict:** not a valuation input. No environmental controversy in the period reviewed.

### 3. Social Risk Assessment: **MEDIUM**

**Positives**
- BROADEN2 Phase 2b (16 weeks) reported KT-621 as **well-tolerated**, most TEAEs mild-to-moderate, no dose response in AEs, 45% placebo-adjusted EASI reduction. Safety data through Ph1b/Ph2b has been clean — the strongest possible social/clinical signal at this stage.
- Product profile is genuinely access-relevant: a once-daily **oral** degrader vs. injectable biologics addresses adherence and access barriers in moderate-to-severe AD. This is a durable social-value and commercial advantage.
- Small, high-skill workforce; 90% employee pride (self-reported); no union, strike, or labour-relations incidents found.

**Risks**
- **Safety concentration:** STAT6 mechanism carries a known class risk of **conjunctivitis-related AEs** (flagged in the BROADEN2 readout). A Ph3 imbalance or a liver/tox signal would be reputationally and financially severe — this is the dominant S risk and it is binary in nature.
- **Trial diversity/regulatory:** FDA Diversity Action Plan expectations for the 2027 Ph3 AD programme; enrollment shortfalls are a timeline risk.
- **Pricing/patient access:** as an immunology franchise competitor to established biologics, KYMR will face payer scrutiny; a launch-price controversy post-2028 is the tail risk.
- **Key-person and human capital:** 273 FTE, founder-CEO-centric. No formal ESG/sustainability or human-capital disclosure whatsoever — a transparency gap rather than an operating failure.
- **Verdict:** no current social controversy; risk is prospective and trial-dependent.

### 4. Governance Quality Score: **5 / 10** (Average, with a negative tilt)

**Supports (positives)**
- **Single class of common stock** — no dual-class supervoting structure, no controlled-company status. One share, one vote.
- **No CEO/Chairman duality:** Felix J. Baker, Ph.D. (Baker Bros. Advisors) became **Chairman in June 2026**, succeeding co-founder Bruce Booth (Atlas Venture), who remains a director. Independent, non-executive chair function exists in substance.
- Nine-member board with a majority of non-employee directors; standing audit, comp and nominating/governance committees; Nasdaq-compliant.
- No poison pill or defensive entrenchment devices identified.

**Detracts (negatives)**
- **Chairman is not independent** — Baker is affiliated with a major institutional holder (Baker Bros.); his predecessor was Atlas Venture (also a large holder and co-founder). The chair seat has effectively been allocated to investor-sponsors, not independents.
- **Founder-CEO sits on the board** (Mainolfi) — standard in biotech but reduces board challenge capacity at exactly the moment capital allocation decisions are largest.
- **Comp committee includes Chairman Baker** (with Albers as chair and Maraganore) — a shareholder-affiliated director on the remuneration committee is a meaningful conflict-of-interest flag.
- **Insider selling is heavy and persistent:** roughly **$392M across 161 open-market sales in six months** (up to ~$462M trailing twelve months), including CEO Mainolfi (~$7.65M, Oct 2026), Chief Legal Officer Adams (~$7.07M, Sep 2026), CBO Goodman (~$6.02M, Jul 2026) and director Booth (~$7.4M, Mar 2026). Some of this is plausibly 10b5-1 diversification after a 30%+ data pop and pre-offering strength, but the scale relative to a $14.8B cap is a *behavioural* signal worth flagging to the investment committee.
- **Executive pay skew:** CEO total compensation ~$8.55M with roughly **92% equity/bonus-linked** and only ~8% salary — highly performance-levered (good alignment in theory) but concentrated in a single binary clinical catalyst, which encourages risk-taking rather than long-horizon stewardship.
- **Disclosure thinness:** no sustainability/ESG report, no SASB/GRI-aligned disclosure, no TCFD-style climate statement.
- **Legal:** no securities class action or derivative suit confirmed; see §5.

### 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure

- **US SEC climate disclosure rule / Nasdaq climate reporting:** US-listed; SEC climate rule remains stayed/contested and, in any case, KYMR's emissions are de minimis. **Exposure: negligible.**
- **EU CSRD / EU Taxonomy / CBAM:** no EU-domiciled operations of scale, no carbon-intensive exports. **Exposure: negligible.**
- **California SB 253/261:** revenue thresholds (~$1B) almost certainly not met by a pre-revenue/early-revenue company. **Exposure: low.**
- **FDA (the *actual* regulatory driver):** clinical trial conduct, GCP, Diversity Action Plans for Ph3, and post-approval REMS/labelling risk. This is where "regulatory ESG" becomes financially real for KYMR.
- **HIPAA / GDPR / state privacy (MA 201 CMR 17.00):** clinical-trial and patient data handling; standard for the sector, cyber-incident disclosure exposure under SEC Item 1.05.
- **ESG-linked debt covenants:** none identified; capital structure is equity-funded ($1.5B+ raised, now +$1.75B), not sustainability-linked. **Exposure: none.**

### 7. Overall ESG Rating: **AVERAGE** (governance tilt negative; environmental non-material)

**Not a Leader** — despite strong clinical-trial safety conduct and an access-positive product profile, Kymera publishes no ESG/sustainability disclosure at all, has a non-independent shareholder-affiliated Chairman, a shareholder representative on its compensation committee, and is carrying three active public "fiduciary duty / self-dealing" investigations alongside ~$392M of six-month insider sales.

**Not a Laggard on operations** — single-class share structure, non-executive chair, clean safety record, no environmental or product controversies, no ESG-linked leverage.

Net: an **Average** ESG profile where the only factor that can move the stock is clinical safety (S), and the governance items are a monitoring overlay rather than a thesis input.

### 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEUTRAL** (governance watch item; not a thesis driver)

- **Thesis impact:** ESG factors do **not** alter the KYMR investment case. There is no carbon cost curve, no stranded-asset risk, no social-licence constraint, and no ESG-linked financing cost to model. Valuation remains a probability-weighted read on KT-621 Ph3 execution and the STAT6 franchise.
- **Where ESG *does* bite:** (1) **S — safety.** Any STAT6-class AE imbalance (conjunctivitis, hepatic, or degrader-specific tox) in Ph3 is the sector's characteristic ESG-to-financial transmission channel and would be worth multiples of any other factor here. (2) **G — signalling.** The combination of a $1.75B raise, a 30% data-day pop, ~$392M of insider sales, and plaintiff-bar "self-dealing" notices is the kind of cluster that erodes governance credibility and can compress the multiple independent of fundamentals. Recommend the investment memo carry an explicit **governance-monitoring line item**: track Form 4s, confirm the substance behind the Halper Sadeh/Kuehn/Purcell notices, and pressure-test whether the chairman's dual role (Baker Bros. chair + comp committee member) influences capital-raising timing.
- **Engagement agenda if long:** (i) obtain an independent lead director or independent chair; (ii) remove shareholder-affiliated directors from the compensation committee; (iii) publish a first sustainability/human-capital disclosure aligned to SASB Biotechnology; (iv) disclose the nature of the self-dealing allegations and related-party transactions with Atlas Venture / Baker Bros.

**Data gaps to note honestly:** no proxy (DEF 14A) or 10-K full text was retrievable — SEC.gov, GlobeNewswire and the company IR proxy pages all returned HTTP 403, and the filings tool returned exhibits/no-match only; no MSCI, Sustainalytics or ISS ESG score was surfaced (Kymera is likely thinly covered by index ESG providers). Board independence is therefore *inferred* from disclosed affiliations (Albers, Maraganore, Ridloff, Sandor appear independent → roughly 4–5 of 9), not confirmed line-by-line from the proxy. No controversy severity or dollar quantification was verified for the self-dealing investigations.
