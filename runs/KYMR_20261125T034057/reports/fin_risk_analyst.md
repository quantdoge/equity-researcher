## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Investment Grade equivalent — but only because there is no credit to rate.**

KYMR carries **zero financial debt**. The $76.6M "total debt" on the balance sheet has short-term debt = $0 and long-term debt = $0, which is consistent with **operating lease liabilities, not borrowings**. There are **no convertible notes, no term loans, no revolver draws, no covenants, and no refinancing cliff**. Interest expense runs ~$62K/quarter.

The correct credit lens for a pre-revenue biotech is not leverage but **funding/going-concern risk**, and on that measure KYMR is strong:

| Funding Test | Value | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Cash + investments (Q3 2026) | ~$1.60B | Reported in Q1/Q2/Q3 2026 releases |
| Financial debt | $0 | No covenants, no maturities |
| Net cash | ~$1.52B | Net cash = 15.9% of market cap |
| LTM FCF burn | -$288.9M | Q4'25 + Q1–Q3'26 |
| Runway (reported burn) | ~5.4 yrs (21.6 qtrs) | Into ~2032 |
| Runway (underlying burn, ex-collaboration revenue) | ~3.7 yrs (15 qtrs) | Into ~mid-2030 |
| Company guidance | "Runway into 2029" | Conservative vs. my math |

Interest income (~$60M/yr) is a real but secondary cushion; it reduces net loss but does not fund R&D.

**Partial offset:** the debt-like instrument here is **equity**. KYMR has repeatedly funded via large public offerings (including a $1.5B common stock offering and ~$700M raised in Q1 2026). Diluted shares went **84.4M (Q4'25) → 100.1M (Q3'26), +18.6%**, and +34% since FY2023. This is "dilution risk" substituting for "default risk."

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Accelerating cash burn with no funding inflection.** LTM FCF -$288.9M vs FY2025 -$234.3M vs FY2024 -$207.3M. R&D has stepped up ~64% YoY (Q2'26 $119.5M vs Q2'25 $78.4M) and reached **~$129M in Q3'26**. Nothing in the model bends this curve; it is pure call option on future data.
2. **Revenue is a mirage — Q3 2026 revenue was $0.** LTM revenue of $102M is entirely lumpy collaboration/milestone income (Q1'26 $34.4M, Q2'26 $65.0M, Q3'26 $0.0M). There is no recurring base. FCF margin is **-283%**: each $1 of revenue accompanies $2.83 of burn.
3. **Serial dilution.** Diluted shares 84.4M → 100.1M in nine months (+18.6%), +34% since FY2023, plus a $1.5B public offering and a further $225M underwriter option. SBC of $59.9M in FY2025 (~$75M annualized in 2026) is a real, recurring economic cost embedded in "non-cash" burn.
4. **Single-catalyst concentration at the top of the range.** +51% in two sessions (Nov 23–24, 2026) on KT-579 Phase 1 healthy-volunteer data. Price is at an all-time high with ~$15.4B of enterprise value resting on one early-stage asset's readout. **The asymmetry has inverted: you are now underwriting perfection.**
5. **Long-duration valuation into a 5%+ long bond.** 10Y at 5.13%, 30Y at 5.47%. A 2030s-weight DCF has no margin for rate disappointment.
6. **Piotroski F = 4 and both operating Altman components negative** (x2 -0.61, x3 -0.18) — the "Safe" Z-score is a balance-sheet artifact, not an earnings signal. Do not let it reassure.
