**Investment Thesis**
Kymera owns a genuinely de-risked, first-in-class asset: KT-621, an oral STAT6 degrader with Phase 2b efficacy in Dupixent's neighbourhood (45pt placebo-adjusted EASI) delivered as a once-daily pill, backed by ~$3.2–3.3B pro-forma cash and zero debt. The problem is purely the price: at a ~$13.6–14.6B EV, the market has already capitalised base-case AD success at ~3.4x a $4B peak-sales scenario — the bull case is priced as the base case. Every probability-weighting in the file (Value $85–145, Growth $150–190, Short desk EV ~$146, blended ~$150–165) lands at or below spot; expected edge at $174 is zero-to-negative. This is the best company in the coverage book at one of its worst entry points — those are different decisions.

**Bull Case**
- KT-621 is a validated oral pill with biologic-class efficacy against a ~$25B injectable franchise — a TAM-expansion story into asthma, COPD, EoE, CRSwNP, all behind Fast Track, plus a free second shot on goal in IRF5 (KT-579, lupus).
- Financing risk is eliminated: ~$3.2–3.3B cash, zero debt, funded through Phase 3 and the late-2027 BREADTH asthma readout; the raise was done from strength at $150.37, not distress.
- Strategic scarcity: wholly-owned oral immunology asset in a patent-cliff market where AbbVie paid $10.9B for a Phase-3-ready *injectable*; KYMR is a live M&A target with a clean Beneish/forensic profile.

**Bear Case**
- The price has run past the evidence: +51% in two sessions to $174 vs blended fair value ~$150–165; success, breadth, and minimal competitive erosion are all already required to justify spot.
- Binary Phase 3 risk with real scientific questions: low Phase 2b placebo response (27%) historically compresses in Phase 3; the inverted dose response is unanswered; failure takes the stock to the $22–34 cash floor (−80% to −87%).
- Structural dilution and signalling: share count +~65% in three years, ~13.6% just added, ~$392M of insider sales in six months, burn accelerating toward $450–500M/yr — equity is the operating currency.

**Key Risks**
1. Phase 3 placebo-delta compression or outright miss (~2029) — the single largest value event.
2. FDA requires additional dose-ranging on the non-monotonic response — adds years and capital.
3. 52-week OLE durability decay or rising conjunctivitis — breaks the "cleaner than Dupixent" narrative.
4. Competitive crowding: oral JAKs, oral IL-23/TL1A, AbbVie/Apogee IL-13; payers won't pay biologic prices for a pill, and the IRA 9-year window truncates LOE value.
5. Macro accident: a 50bp Fed hike taking the 10Y to 5.75–6% with MFN escalation is a modelled 40–50% drawdown to $85–105 — a multiple event, not a solvency event.
6. Governance cluster (self-dealing solicitations + insider selling) compressing the multiple independent of fundamentals.
