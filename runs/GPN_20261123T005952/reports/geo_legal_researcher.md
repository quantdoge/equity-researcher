### 1. Geographic Risk Map

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Macro-Political Risks

**A. UK/Europe payments regulation tightening — Probability: High; Impact: Medium**
- UK **APP-fraud mandatory reimbursement** has been live since 7-Oct-2024, making acquiring/PSP economics absorb scam losses; PSR reports **88% (£316M) of APP-scam losses reimbursed in Q1 2026** vs 61% industry-wide in 2024 ([PSR dashboard](https://www.psr.org.uk/information-for-consumers/app-scams-reimbursement-dashboard/)). The PSR is being absorbed into the **FCA** ([analysis](https://www.botminds.ai/solutions/app-fraud-reimbursement/app-fraud-reimbursement-fca-psr-merger)).
- EU **PSD3 / Payment Services Regulation reached provisional agreement in 2026** — expands scam-liability, transparency and access rules across the EEA ([Forson regulatory news](https://www.forson.co.uk/news)).
- Worldpay's UK/Europe merchant book materially increases this exposure versus legacy GPN.

**B. US–China tension and APAC JV/data-sovereignty risk — Probability: Medium; Impact: Medium**
- Asia-Pacific was **28% of legacy revenue** — unusually high for a US acquirer, reflecting China/Hong Kong and other APAC processing, much of it in minority-held JVs. This creates exposure to Chinese data-localisation (PIPL/DSL), cross-border data-transfer restrictions, and any expansion of US outbound-investment/export-control rules to payment technology.
- Partly mitigated: the Worldpay deal dilutes APAC to a low-double-digit share of the combined company.

**C. Trade-policy/tariff shock to merchant volumes — Probability: Medium; Impact: Medium**
- GPN itself names "the **imposition of tariffs and other trade policies** and the resulting impacts on market volatility and global trade" as a specific risk factor in its Q3-2026 forward-looking statements — i.e., management treats tariff-driven volume/consumer-spending risk as live, not theoretical. Acquirers are high-beta to cross-border e-commerce and SMB discretionary spend.

*Watch item (not geopolitical but adjacent):* **GTCR is exploring a take-private of GPN** (SEC filing 20-Nov-2026), with **CD&R reportedly working alongside on a ~$40B LBO**; GPN has **denied** the reports ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/acquiring/2026/gtcr-weighs-buyout-of-global-payments-days-after-earlier-effort-ended/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/global-payments-buyout-cdr-gtcr.html)). GTCR became GPN's largest outside shareholder via **43.3M rolled-over shares** in the Worldpay deal — a structural conflict/governance overlay worth flagging, plus CFIUS/antitrust review risk if a deal is signed.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

- **Merchant-fraud / "know-your-merchant" liability** is the top compliance red flag. The FTC's 2026 knowledge-standard settlements against peer processors, plus GPN's own prior securities litigation over compliance disclosures, show the regulator's theory of the case is now well-formed. Post-Worldpay scale (6M merchants, heavy SMB/high-risk mix) raises statistical exposure.
- **AML/sanctions in a 175-country settlement network** — funds-settlement flows (settlement processing assets $2.8B / obligations $6.0B) across emerging markets create inherent sanctions-screening and AML obligations; no current disclosed OFAC/DOJ action found.
- **Data privacy** — GDPR/UK-GDPR plus China PIPL and an expanding US state patchwork; a breach is explicitly cited as a risk factor in the Q3-2026 release.
- **JV/minority governance** — redeemable and nonredeemable NCI across Asia/LatAm vehicles can complicate FCPA books-and-records compliance at the subsidiary level.
- **No current material DOJ/SEC/CFPB investigation against GPN was identified in this review** — an honest gap rather than a clean bill of health, since the 10-Q legal-proceedings note could not be retrieved.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

Rationale: revenue is overwhelmingly OECD-based (US ~55–60% post-close), there is no Russia/Ukraine or Iran-linked exposure of consequence, and the one sizeable securities class action is settled for a trivial $3.6M. Offsetting this: **material UK/EEA regulatory tightening with direct P&L consequences (APP-fraud reimbursement, PSD3/PSR)**, a **residual high-APAC legacy footprint with China-adjacent JV structures**, an active **FTC enforcement template against payment processors**, and a balance sheet (43% goodwill, $21B debt, nine-month GAAP loss) that leaves limited cushion for a large regulatory hit. Governance overlay: an active PE take-private exploration by the company's own largest shareholder.

**Where I'd upgrade to High:** a DOJ/FTC action against GPN alleging KYM failures at Worldpay scale, or a UK FCA enforcement/skills-test action on APP-fraud reimbursement.
