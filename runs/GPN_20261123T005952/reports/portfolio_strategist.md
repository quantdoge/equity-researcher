## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (structurally, not randomly)

Two coherent, internally-consistent camps — and this is the single most important finding:

- **Camp A (valuation/earnings mechanics): Growth, Quant-value leg, Sector relative, Alt Data.** Cheap on every adjusted metric, revisions up, guidance reaffirmed, margin expanding, sell-side median target $106–115.
- **Camp B (quality/cash/control): Value, Quant-quality leg, Fin Risk, Non-Fin Risk, Short, Macro, Geo-Legal.** The cheap multiple is manufactured by excluding $1.4B/yr of amortization on assets that *do* decay and must be defended with real spend; the cash isn't there yet (9M26 FCF negative); the controller's office lost its two most senior people on day one of a $24B purchase-accounting event; and the whole valuation rests on a PE bid that two separate consortia have already walked away from once.

**Conflict resolution (per mandate, the risk flags take precedence over the valuation argument):**

1. The **$516M negative FCF while buying back $1.24B of stock at 3.9x leverage** is a fact, not an opinion. It is the highest-quality evidence in the entire file and it contradicts the growth camp's "cash machine" framing. Growth's ~12% equity FCF yield is an *estimate*; Fin Risk's cash flow statement is an *observation*.
2. The **PAO/CAO departure on the closing date** is a control-environment flag that survives all pushback. It does not make GPN a fraud (Short Analyst's Beneish/accruals work is genuinely exculpatory) but it materially raises the odds the "adjusted" earnings base is unverifiable for 4+ quarters.
3. **Macro and the deal are the same bet.** Macro is right that the equity is a rate bet in disguise. Any position here is partly a long-US-duration position in a bear-steepening tape — that must be sized as such.
4. **Not disqualifying:** leverage of 3.4–4.6x on ~$4.7B EBITDA with $6.3B liquidity, BBB ratings, tight BAA spreads (1.56%) and a distribution ladder means no refinancing wall. The credit is fine; the *equity* is the levered stub.

**Net conviction: medium-low.** Not a pass, not a core holding. This is an event-driven, levered, deep-value stub with a hard valuation floor and a fat left tail.

| Input | Reading |

| Volatility | 48.4% annualised; ~14% monthly 1σ |
| Beta to SPY | 0.45 · correlation 0.14 — **low beta, but not defensive** (vol is 100% idiosyncratic) |
| Scenario build | Bull $120 (30%) / Base $105 (45%) / Bear $72 (25%) → **E[value] ≈ $101, +10%**, downside −22% |

Naive Kelly says size large (~μ/σ² ≈ full position) — **I reject that** because the outcome distribution is bimodal on an unmodelable binary (the LBO), scenario probabilities are guesses, and the "σ" of terminal outcomes understates the impairment tail (73% of assets are goodwill/intangibles). Applying a 1/8–1/10 fractional-Kelly haircut plus the HIGH-risk cap → **1.5%**.

- 0.75% **now** at ~$91.83 (we are 2 days off a +10% deal-rumor spike — do not buy the whole thing here).
- 0.75% **on confirmation** (see §5).
- **3.0% hard cap**, reachable only if *all three* of: reported net leverage <4.0x, positive reported FCF for two consecutive quarters, and normalized organic growth ≥4%. If those land, re-rate conviction upward and this becomes a 3% position on a re-rating to 8–9x.
