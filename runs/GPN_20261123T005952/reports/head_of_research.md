### Investment Memo: GPN — 23 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY — *tactical/event-driven, strictly small size* | Conviction: **Low–Medium**
**Price Target**: $110 (~+20% from $91.83) | Bear $72 / Bull $140–160 (takeout or 8–9x re-rating)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
GPN is a post-Worldpay, #1-scale merchant acquirer trading at ~6.7x FY26E adjusted EPS ($13.60–13.80 guide) with 42.5% adjusted operating margins (+220bp), a raised $650M synergy target, a 100% beat record, and a live GTCR/CD&R take-private process (~$40–50B vs a $24.3B market cap) that puts a soft floor under the equity. Against that: 3.9–4.6x leverage, **negative reported FCF through 9M26 (−$516M) while buying back $1.24B of stock**, negative tangible equity (−$21.6B), a ~$11/sh GAAP-to-adjusted gap, and a macro tape (10Y 5.04%, USD +4%) moving against both the multiple and the LBO math. This is a 2–3 year re-rating/value-unlock trade with a takeout kicker — **not a core compounder** — and it must be sized like the levered, bimodal event position it is.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is late-cycle — ~3% pool growth, 2–3pt/yr take-rate compression, group de-rating (FI −40%, FIS −32%); GPN is the only name with 200bp+ margin expansion. Macro is mildly **unfavourable**: the two variables that matter most (long rates, USD) are both adverse; the equity is a rate bet in disguise.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium. ~55–60% US revenue, no Russia exposure, prior securities suit settled for a trivial $3.6M. Watch items: UK APP-fraud reimbursement, PSD3, and the **FTC's new processor-liability "knowledge standard"** (Nuvei $4.85M, $12M settlements) — GPN is the largest exposed acquirer.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD. Intrinsic ~$115–120 (range $95–135) but only ~4% above the bear case; adjusted ROIC ~7–8.5% ≈ WACC; the margin of safety depends on a normalized FCF number the company has not yet demonstrated.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (GARP, not disruptive growth). Organic growth is 4–5%, but EPS compounds ~17% off synergies/deleveraging/buybacks; +16.6% estimate revisions; 0.4 PEG; fair value $146–162 on 9–10x FY27E.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL. Cheapest in peer set on value and revisions (rank 1/4 each) — but **P/B 0.93 + ROIC 3.2% + 12-1 momentum −12.4% is the textbook value-trap signature**. The "+47% alpha" is noise (t≈1.05, R²=0.02); GPN is a factor orphan (corr to SPY 0.14).
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. GAAP EBIT/interest ~0.9–1.0x; current ratio 0.82; leverage 3.9–4.6x vs 3.0x target; goodwill+intangibles 73% of assets. Credit survives on a D&A add-back; a −20% revenue scenario makes a sub-IG downgrade the base case.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. **Principal Accounting Officer and Chief Administrative Officer both departed on the Worldpay closing date** — a textbook control-environment flag; merchant attrition live; twice-aborted public PE process. Execution risk, not malfeasance (Beneish −3.06, four years of negative accruals — the accounting is clean).
- **ESG**: Average, mildly negative skew. Genuine board cyber depth; but **GTCR (16.35% holder, Worldpay seller) + a Silver Lake partner on the comp/integration committees + CEO chairing the Integration Committee** = weak minority protection in any buyout; 85% Say-on-Pay is a rebuke.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL. Beat magnitude is collapsing (+6.5% → +0.6% → **0.0%** in Q3); revenue at parity with consensus; developer-portal traffic +394% is the one green shoot. Alt data is second-order to an unmodelable M&A catalyst.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **No edge.** Accounting is exculpatory; the valuation is already the bear case; the short is crowded (11 days to cover). Bear triggers to monitor: leverage >3.5x at end-FY27, second consecutive negative-FCF quarter, Worldpay goodwill impairment.

**Bull Case**
- **Re-rating arithmetic**: hit the reaffirmed $13.60–13.80 and delever toward 3.0x, and 8–9x earnings is $110–125 with no improvement in the underlying business; FY27E $16.23 supports $146+.
- **Takeout optionality**: GTCR/CD&R interest at a reported ~$40–50B (2x market cap) against a seller-turned-largest-shareholder with 43.3M rolled shares; buybacks at 6.7x are highly accretive.
- **Self-help is landing ahead of plan**: synergies raised to $650M, margins +220bp, sequential growth acceleration to >4%, Q4E EPS +16% y/y — the best execution story in a de-rating sector.

**Bear Case**
- **The cash isn't there**: 9M26 FCF −$516M against $1.44B of capital returns = debt-funded buybacks at ~4x leverage mid-integration; deleveraging has not started.
- **The moat was swapped**: high-margin Issuer Solutions sold, low-margin Worldpay bought (FIS's own >$20B value-destruction asset); gross margin −1,150bp y/y; Stripe/Adyen keep taking the growth segments.
- **The deal premium is a rate bet**: if ~80bp of priced hikes lands, LBO math breaks at a 5.5% 10Y, the bid is withdrawn, and GPN de-rates to standalone value **$70–75 — no earnings miss required**.

**Key Risks**
Worldpay integration/merchant attrition; leverage and refinancing into 6.6% BAA yields; goodwill impairment (73% of assets); control-environment risk post-PAO/CAO exit; FTC processor-liability and UK/EU fee regulation; USD translation; GTCR conflict of interest in any negotiated sale. *Data gaps flagged by agents: covenant headroom, insider transaction detail, 10-Q legal note, clean peer multiples — all unobtainable this cycle.*

**Portfolio Construction Note** (Strategist)
**1.5% target, scaled: 0.75% now at $88–93, 0.75% on either a pullback to $83–86 or confirmation at the 17-Feb-27 print** (first clean full-year Worldpay FCF). **Hard cap 3.0%**, only if reported net leverage <4.0x, two consecutive quarters of positive FCF, and organic growth ≥4% all land. **Hard stop $79** (−14%; 0.21% portfolio loss — sized small deliberately to afford a non-noise stop at 48% vol). Do not chase above $100. Do not hold GPN and FIS at size simultaneously (corr 0.47 — one bet, not two). Net the position against existing rate-sensitive holdings; at 3.9x leverage and 6.7x earnings, GPN behaves like levered long duration in a bear-steepening tape.

**Bottom line**: The risk agents are right about the balance sheet, and the growth camp is right about the asymmetry — both are true, which is why this is a small, rules-governed position and not a conviction holding. The single most important monitor over the next four quarters is whether *reported* net leverage breaks below 4.0x with positive FCF. If it does, add; if it doesn't, the bear case owns the stock.
