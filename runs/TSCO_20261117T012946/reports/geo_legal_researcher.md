## TSCO — Geopolitical, Regulatory & Legal Risk Assessment

**Company:** Tractor Supply Company (NASDAQ: TSCO), Brentwood, TN. Largest US rural-lifestyle retailer; ~2,500 Tractor Supply stores + ~180–200 Petsense stores; ~52–54k employees; market cap ~$19.7bn. FY2025 10-K filed 19 Feb 2026.

---

### 1. Geographic Risk Map

Tractor Supply is effectively a **single-country business**: all stores are in the 49 states (no Alaska), with e-commerce and an owned pet-pharmacy (Allivet) layered on top. There are no disclosed foreign operations, no FX translation exposure of consequence, and no sanctioned-country revenue.

| Geography | Revenue exposure | Nature of exposure | Risk level |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States — 49 states + e-comm | ~100% | Store base, DCs, all revenue | **Medium** (regulatory/tariff, not political stability) |
| China (supply chain, not revenue) | 0% revenue; undisclosed % of COGS | Third-party manufacturers; "a portion of domestically purchased merchandise is manufactured abroad" (FY2025 10-K, Item 1A) | **Medium–High** |
| Other foreign (import sourcing) | 0% revenue | Indirect via vendors | **Medium** |
| Russia / sanctioned jurisdictions | None disclosed | — | **Nil** |

*Gap:* the company does not quantify its import share or China concentration, so supply-chain exposure cannot be precisely sized. Gloom's FY2025 10-K parsing notes the tariff risk factor now covers "China **and other countries**," broadened from China-only in FY2024.

---

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk 1 — US tariff regime churn (probability: near-certain; impact: Medium)**
The Supreme Court invalidated the IEEPA tariffs on **20 Feb 2026**, triggering refunds (NRF; Bricker; Troutman). Tractor Supply has already booked **~$100m of tariff refunds** and reinvested ~40% into price on ~50 essential SKUs (Q3'26 call, 22 Oct 2026, via Motley Fool). That is a **one-time, non-recurring tailwind** — the risk is the *replacement* architecture: Section 301 "forced-labor" tariffs of **10–12.5% across ~60 jurisdictions** (USTR, 12 Mar 2026), with Allianz projecting the effective US tariff rate back to **~12.4% by Q4 2026** and Chinese-import effective rates estimated at **~33–36%**. Management's mitigation is alternative sourcing, vendor negotiation and retail price increases — all of which are margin- and traffic-dilutive in a value-sensitive rural customer base.

**Risk 2 — Multi-state AG/regulator enforcement as a recurring operating cost (probability: high; impact: Low–Medium)**
The **$5.13m California stipulated judgment (5 Aug 2026)** — brought jointly by the Sonoma, San Diego, Santa Cruz, Sacramento and San Bernardino DAs — covered **price accuracy (overcharging vs. lowest advertised price), pesticide licensing/recordkeeping, and greenwashing ("biodegradable" labels)**. The template is now public and portable to other states. Individually immaterial (~0.03% of LTM sales) but it is the third distinct consumer-protection theory in 18 months against the same company — an aggregation risk rather than a solvency risk. Directors should note Tractor Supply voluntarily halted pesticide sales, i.e. the compliance failure was systemic enough to require a trading interruption in a core category.

**Risk 3 — Political/reputational polarization of the rural customer base (probability: Medium; impact: Medium)**
Tractor Supply retreated from DEI roles, carbon-emissions goals and Pride sponsorship in **June 2024** after conservative online pressure (NPR; CNN; Fortune) — a decision that alienated part of its employee, customer and investor base in both directions. The FY2025 10-K retains a specific risk factor that "investors, other stakeholders, and regulators may not be satisfied with our ESG efforts including DE&I." Secondary channel: immigration and agricultural-labor policy affecting the rural economy that drives ~all of its demand.

*(Watch item, not top-3: broadened "geopolitical conditions" language added to the macro risk factor in FY2025, plus a **new** AI risk factor covering operational failure and rising privacy/regulatory compliance cost.)*

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **Data privacy — highest severity.** Being CPPA's inaugural enforcement target means TSCO's consent/opt-out and data-sharing practices were the agency's chosen precedent. With the new AI risk factor, expect AI-governance and automated-decision disclosure scrutiny next.
2. **Greenwashing.** "Biodegradable" labeling was successfully prosecuted under California's Greenwashing law — directly compounded by the 2024 ESG retreat, which leaves marketing claims less supervised, not more.
3. **Price accuracy.** Charging above lowest advertised price is a systems/IT control failure that scales across 2,500 stores and every state with a scanner-price law.
4. **Pesticide/regulated-product licensing.** Selling restricted pesticides without license is a criminal-adjacent, license-gated risk in a core farm category.
5. **No OFAC/sanctions, FCPA, AML or export-control exposure identified** — consistent with a purely domestic retailer. This is the clean part of the ledger.

---

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **LOW–MEDIUM**

- **Geopolitical (classical): LOW.** Zero foreign revenue, no sanctions, no expropriation, no currency controls, no EM exposure. The only geopolitical transmission channel is trade policy via imports.
- **Regulatory/Legal: MEDIUM.** Not financially threatening ($5.1m + $1.35m + misc. ≈ well under 0.2% of annual sales), but the pattern — first-ever CPPA action, a five-county coordinated AG action, repeat pesticide findings, chronic wage-and-hour settlements, and an open tobacco-surcharge case — indicates a compliance function that is reactive rather than preventive. Escalation risk is in injunctive relief and category restrictions (pesticides), not fines.
- **Net for the investment memo:** treat TSCO as a domestic-policy risk, not an international one. The two items that can move the P&L are (a) whether post-IEEPA Section 301 tariffs re-raise landed cost after the $100m refund tailwind rolls off, and (b) whether California's price-accuracy/pesticide/greenwashing template spreads to other states. Neither is currently material to enterprise value; both are persistent 20–50bps margin and multiple drags.
