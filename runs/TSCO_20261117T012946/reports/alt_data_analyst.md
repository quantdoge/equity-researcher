## 8. Confidence Level: **LOW-to-MEDIUM**
Reasons: (a) all signals are **public-web proxies**, not licensed data feeds; (b) Google Trends is weekly, index-scaled and seasonally treacherous in Nov–Dec (Black Friday week will distort the next 2 readings); (c) SimilarWeb figures are **second-hand snippets with conflicting m/m prints**; (d) job-posting counts are a single snapshot with **no time series**; (e) **patent API failed**; (f) social data is stale.

**Data lag:** Google Trends T+2 to T+5 days; SimilarWeb monthly (T+30 to T+45); job postings real-time but unnormalised; FMP news ~T+1 day. The read above therefore describes **late-Oct to mid-Nov demand**, which is roughly **6–8 weeks into the fiscal Q4** — directionally useful for the Feb print, but too early to call the quarter.

**What would change the call:** two consecutive weeks of "near me" intent above 55, tractorsupply.com traffic turning positive m/m for two months, and/or a pickup in non-store (corporate/DC) job postings — any two of those would upgrade the signal to **Bullish**. Conversely, a break of brand interest back below 55 in the Black Friday week would flip the earnings preview to **Below**.
