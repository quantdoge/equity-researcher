### Investment Memo: TSCO — 16 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium — *do not initiate new positions at spot; existing holders hold with discipline*
**Price Target**: $35 (-8% from current $37.95) — ~17x FY27E EPS of ~$2.06
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Tractor Supply is a genuinely well-built franchise — local efficient scale across ~2,500 rural stores, a 38M-member loyalty base driving >80% of sales, 36% gross margins, and clean, cash-backed accounting (Beneish −2.37, accruals −4.9%, OCF/NI 1.49x) — that is mid-way through an unresolved margin reset and priced as though it is complete. Earnings are in outright decline (FY26 EPS −11% GAAP; comps guided (1%)–flat; ROIC down from 22.7% to ~14–15% in three years), yet the stock still trades at ~19.5x FY26E adjusted / ~20.7x GAAP — *above* every published analyst target. The bear case is slow earnings decay against an unchanged multiple, not fraud or insolvency; the bull case is one quarter of comp stabilization (Q3 +0.4%, inflecting intra-quarter) plus a rate-cut option the Fed is not yet offering. Neither justifies new capital at $37.95.

**Key Findings by Domain**

Macro UNFAVOURABLE — Fed re-hiking into supply-shock inflation (CPI 3.82%), 10Y at 5.13%, mortgages 7.11%, gasoline +31% YoY hitting a drive-distance-defined customer, sentiment collapsed to 48. The +31% rally off the June low has decoupled from the rate signal — that is fragility, not strength.

Real risks: post-IEEPA Section 301 tariff replacement (~$100M refund tailwind rolls off) and a portable California enforcement template ($5.1M price-accuracy/pesticide/greenwashing judgment; $1.35M first-ever CPPA action). Financially immaterial (<0.2% of sales) but signals a reactive, not preventive, compliance function.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**

- **Capital returns exceed free cash flow**: ~115–170% payout of FCF funded with rising debt while EPS declines; the buyback shield that manufactured EPS growth is exhausted (FY24 $585M → Q3'26 $88.5M).

4. **Regulatory replication** — a fifth state AG/CPPA/EPA action escalates non-financial risk to High; pesticide licensing is safety-adjacent and federally overlappable.
