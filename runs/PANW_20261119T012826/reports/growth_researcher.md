## Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $339.92 (Nov 18, 2026 close, +10.2% on Q1 FY27 print) | Mkt cap ~$278B | EV ~$272B*

---

### 1. Growth Story

PANW has re-ignited itself as a platform consolidator rather than a firewall vendor: FY26 revenue grew 24.5% and Q1 FY27 accelerated to **+39% y/y** as the $25B CyberArk acquisition (identity/privileged access) consolidated, with a forward-bookings metric up **66% y/y to $9.73B**. Stripping out an estimated ~$350M of CyberArk/Chronosphere revenue, Q1 organic growth was still ~**24–26%** — a true re-acceleration from the ~13–18% organic pace of FY26 — driven by platformization ("~170 net new platformizations/quarter"), AI-era threat demand, and share gains against point solutions. The company is now attacking a converged security stack (network, SASE, cloud, SecOps, identity, and — via the $4.5B all-stock Applied Compute deal — secure open-weight AI), which is the widest TAM any pure-play security vendor has assembled.

### 3. TAM and Penetration

Directly served segments — network security, SASE, cloud security, SecOps, and now identity security — are roughly a **$120–150B market by 2028** on industry estimates, with CyberArk's PAM/identity franchise adding ~$25B and the Applied Compute/AI-security layer an early-stage but potentially large adjacent pool (AI workload and model security). Against FY27E revenue of ~$14.6B, PANW is at roughly **10–11% penetration of its own SAM** — implying a decade of low-to-mid-teens growth is achievable without heroic assumptions. The differentiation is the "platformization" flywheel: bundling six products at incremental cost to displace 10–20 point vendors per account, plus the largest installed enterprise firewall base as a captive upgrade funnel. Identity is the one segment where PANW is not the incumbent (Microsoft Entra dominates access; CyberArk leads PAM only).
