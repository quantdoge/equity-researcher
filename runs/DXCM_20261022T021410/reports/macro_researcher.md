### 1. Macro Environment Summary — **RISK-OFF / LATE-CYCLE RE-TIGHTENING**

The regime has inverted versus the 2024–25 consensus. Rather than an easing cycle, the US is in a **re-acceleration episode**: tariff pass-through has pushed headline CPI from 3.4% (Aug) to **3.7% y/y (Sept)**, and the Fed delivered its **first rate hike since 2023 at the September 2026 FOMC**, lifting the target range to **3.75–4.00%** (effective FFR 3.88% as of 20 Oct). Fed communications (Warsh: "inflation still too high") signal more hikes; futures price ~4.1% by January 2027.

Critically, the labour market is softening *into* this inflation impulse — September payrolls added just **29,000** and unemployment rose to **4.2%** — a **stagflation-lite** configuration. The long end is the pressure point: **10Y ~5.09–5.21%, 30Y 5.43%, prime rate 7.00%**. A ~120bp FFR-to-10Y spread is a **bear steepener** driven by term premium and fiscal supply, not growth optimism. Equity indices sit near highs (~7,555 S&P) but leadership is narrow; high-beta, long-duration growth is being de-rated.

**Bottom line for DXCM:** a rising-real-rate, rising-input-cost, firm-dollar backdrop is close to the worst macro mix for a 1.48-beta, ~5.8x EV/sales, tariff-exposed medtech — partially offset by genuinely defensive, reimbursement-funded demand.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Not because demand is at risk, but because **valuation is**. DXCM combines (a) high beta 1.48, (b) long-duration cash flows dependent on 2028–2030 reimbursement expansion, (c) an offshore manufacturing base exposed to tariffs, and (d) ~35% non-US revenue into a strengthening dollar — while the Fed is **hiking**, CPI is **rising**, and the 10Y sits above 5%. Every one of those four channels is currently pointed the wrong way.

**Key mitigants:** needs-based reimbursed demand, structural COGS deflation (15-day sensor), CONNECT-driven coverage expansion, and a fortress balance sheet. This is an **unfavourable macro tape around a favourable micro story** — the risk is de-rating, not earnings collapse. Watch the 10Y: below 4.75% the multiple stabilises; above 5.50% and DXCM compresses toward $65.

### 5. Material Surprise Scenario — **HAWKISH ACCELERATION (bearish)**

**Consensus is underpricing the hike path.** Futures only price FFR to ~4.1% by Jan 2027, while Fed rhetoric signals more. If tariff pass-through keeps CPI above 4% into H1 2027 — compounded by fiscal supply pushing the 30Y through 5.75% and the 10Y to 5.50%+ — the Fed takes the FFR to **4.75–5.00%** by mid-2027.

In that world:
- The equity risk premium compresses further; 5.8x EV/sales de-rates toward 4.0–4.5x → **$58–65/share** (~20–28% downside) even with FY26 revenue on target.
- USD strengthens another 5–8%, cutting ~2–3% off reported revenue.
- Tariff-driven component costs collide with the Q3'26–2027 Malaysia/ramp-up spending, capping the gross-margin trajectory near 64% rather than the 2030 target path.
- Medicaid/coverage retrenchment as fiscal deficits bite would hit the non-insulin T2D ramp — the single most important growth vector.

**Trigger to monitor:** any core CPI print ≥0.4% m/m, or a Fed dot plot median above 4.25%.

*Offsetting positive risk (less likely):* a tariff reversal plus oil-led disinflation would let the Fed cut to 3.25%, and DXCM re-rates to **$105–115** on multiple recovery plus CONNECT reimbursement — consistent with the Street's $97 average target.
