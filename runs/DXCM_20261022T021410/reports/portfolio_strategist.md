## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT**

**This is not a consensus name — it is a cleanly divided one.** Four agents are constructive (Growth, Quant, Alt Data, Sector) and four are cautionary or negative (Value, Geo Legal, Non-Fin Risk, Macro), with ESG and Fin Risk in the middle.

- **Where agents AGREE (high confidence):** (1) The **margin inflection is real** — GM bottomed at 60.1% and is back above 63%, op margin at 24%. Sector, Growth, Value, and Fin Risk all see it. (2) The **balance sheet is fortress-grade** — net cash, ~50x coverage. (3) **Abbott is the #1 competitive risk** — named as top risk by Sector, Value, Growth, and Fin Risk independently. (4) **Valuation is full** — Value, Quant, and Macro all flag multiple compression as the dominant downside channel.
- **Where agents DISAGREE (the crux):** Value says the price already embeds optimistic assumptions (~$70 fair); Growth says 26x FY27E EPS = $81 is fair and 30x = $94. **The entire disagreement is about the terminal margin and the discount rate — not the business.**
- **The precedence call:** The Geo Legal and Non-Fin Risk desks both independently rate DXCM **HIGH risk** on *active, unresolved* matters — an open FDA Warning Letter with no close-out and a live securities class action alleging concealment. Under my framework, an active regulatory/legal flag on the core quality system **takes precedence over growth optionality**, because it gates the very clearances (15-day G7, non-insulin T2D label) that the growth thesis depends on. The bull case and the bear case are not separate — **the bear case is the precondition for the bull case.**

**Net conviction: MEDIUM-LOW.** Good business, improving fundamentals, full price, unresolved regulatory tail.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **0.75% of portfolio (starter), max 2.0%**

**Kelly logic:** Street median target ~$100 (+25%); Value desk fair value ~$70 (−13%). Probability-weighting the growth desk's asymmetric setup (p≈0.6 beat → $92; p≈0.4 miss → $70) gives an expected value of roughly **$83 — an edge of ~+3%**. A ~3% edge against 39% annualized volatility and a demonstrated −40% single-day gap history supports only a **fractional-Kelly** position — well under 1% at inception.

**Sizing rationale by tier:**
- **0.75% starter** — reflects genuine quality (top-quintile ROIC, F-Score 8, 129% FCF conversion) and confirmed momentum, without underwriting an unresolved FDA matter.
- **Why not 3–5%:** those tiers require high conviction *and* low risk. Here conviction is split and risk is rated HIGH by two independent desks. The −40.7% single-day gap (Jul 2024) is the binding constraint — a 4% position gapped 40% costs 160bps of portfolio in one session.
- **Why not 0%:** the margin inflection, 100% beat rate, +16.5% EPS revisions and 18.4% momentum are real and confirmed by four desks. Passing entirely forfeits a legitimate quality-growth compounding story.
- **Risk-equivalent note:** at 39.4% annualized vol, a **1% DXCM position contributes ~2.5x the volatility of a typical medtech 1%**. Size in *risk* units, not notional. 0.75% DXCM ≈ 1.9% of a normal-vol healthcare name.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss: $66.00 hard stop (−17.8%).** Rationale: $66.37 is the 2026 YTD starting point and the base from which the +20.9% run began; a break means the momentum and re-rating thesis are both dead. Given 39.4% vol this is ~0.45x annualized vol — tight enough to cap loss at ~13bps of portfolio on the 0.75% starter, loose enough to avoid routine noise. *Position sizing, not the stop, is the primary risk control.*

**Thesis invalidation conditions (exit regardless of price):**
1. **FDA escalation** — Warning Letter converts to consent decree, import alert, or refuse-to-accept; or a second warning letter at a third site.
2. **Gross margin relapse** — GM falls back below 62%, or the ≥63% recovery fails to hold for two consecutive quarters. The 850bps 4-year compression is the moat test; the recovery is the whole bull case.
3. **Litigation crystallises** — securities class action survives motion to dismiss with a disclosed reserve >$150M, or a derivative action names officers.
4. **Growth breaks** — FY2027 guidance below ~$5.7B (sub-10% growth), or US non-insulin T2D share loss to Abbott/Lingo becomes evident in two consecutive prints.
5. **Safety event** — another Class 1 recall on alerting/accuracy, or a fatality/hospitalisation tied to a missed hypoglycaemia alert. This converts product quality into a DOJ matter.

**Trim triggers:**
- **$100–105** (Street median ~$100; ~30x FY27E EPS): trim one-third.
- **$110+** (~top of Street range, ~35x FY27E): trim to a 0.25% core stub or exit. The Value desk's $82 upper bound is breached well before this — do not let momentum override the valuation ceiling.
- **Time stop:** if the FDA Warning Letter is not closed within 12 months of initiation, halve the position regardless of price. An open enforcement matter that drags is a signal, not a neutral fact.
