## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**

The alt-data picture is internally consistent and points one direction: a **de-risked clinical asset being industrialised for Phase 3**, with hiring mix, IR diligence traffic, analyst PT revisions, and short covering all confirming the readout rather than fading it. The two programs (KT-621 STAT6, KT-579 IRF5) give a re-rating narrative beyond a single binary event, and management choosing a **$1.5B primary raise over a partnership** signals they believe the asset is worth more than the market's licensing comps imply.

**Counterweights I would not dismiss:** (1) the equity deal is **unpriced** — near-term technical pressure and a likely syndicate discount are the highest-probability 30-day risk; (2) retail sentiment at 78% bullish with a stock +253% is a contrarian caution, not a confirmation; (3) the next hard catalyst (Phase 3 start) is **~7 months out** and the next earnings (Feb 25) is low-information, leaving a catalyst vacuum; (4) headline EPS trend is bottom-decile (43% beat rate, two straight misses).

**Net: Bullish on 6–18 month horizon; Neutral-to-cautious on the immediate 30 days given offering overhang and positioning.**

**Why not High:**

- **Web-traffic tool returned an implausible artifact** (kymeratx.com > gilead.com), so absolute values are unusable and only +28%/+31% MoM direction is being relied upon.

- **Job postings are a qualitative web-search proxy** — I can see role titles and rough counts, but have no historical count series to establish velocity, so "acceleration" is inferred from role seniority/mix, not measured.
- Earnings-calendar revenue estimates for this issuer appear unreliable (Q2'26 actual was ~2x estimate), limiting the value of the "below consensus" call.

**What would move this to High:** confirmed offering pricing at a modest discount with a clean greenshoe exercise, plus a post-readout short-interest print showing continued covering, plus a measured monthly job-posting count series.

**Data limitations:** alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. All signals above are subordinate to the binary clinical/regulatory path — no quantity of hiring, traffic, or sentiment data hedges a Phase 3 efficacy failure. Positions in a 2.0-beta single-asset biotech should be sized accordingly.
