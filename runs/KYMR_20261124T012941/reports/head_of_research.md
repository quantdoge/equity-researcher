## Investment Thesis

Kymera produced the best oral immunology dataset of 2026 — BROADEN2 Phase 2b showed biologic-class efficacy (EASI −72% vs −27%; EASI-75 62% vs 22%) from a once-daily pill, validated twice, with a fortress balance sheet (~$3B pro forma cash, runway into 2029) and zero fraud or solvency risk. But the +30% single-day re-rating has already capitalised the eczema win: the stock trades at ~2x a conservative probability-weighted rNPV (~$78), at or above the Street's average target, into a ~12-month catalyst vacuum with an unpriced $1.5B offering overhead. This is now a **venture-style binary asset priced for Phase 3 success before a single Phase 3 patient is dosed** — own it for the asthma option, don't chase it at the highs, don't short a fortress.

## Bull Case
- **Class-defining asset**: oral STAT6 degrader with injectable-biologic efficacy (62% vs 22% EASI-75), no JAK boxed-warning mechanism, addressing a >$25B injectable-monopolised Type 2 market where orals historically drive 2–3x penetration; consensus $4B peak implies only ~10–12% share — a low bar.
- **Fortress balance sheet through every value event**: ~$3.0B pro forma cash funds Phase 3 and the BREADTH asthma readout (late 2027) with no forced dilution; asthma success converts $4B into $8–10B peak and re-rates the stock 40–60%.
- **Strategic scarcity in an M&A tape**: record biopharma M&A ($139B in 2026), MFN-signing pharmas need US-domiciled assets, and KYMR is the only oral, non-JAK, biologic-like STAT6 — a takeout at $200+ is a live possibility; KT-579 (IRF5) provides a second validated pillar.

## Bear Case
- **Priced for perfection pre-Phase 3**: ~$14–17B EV vs probability-weighted rNPV ~$78; Phase 2→3 success rates in immunology are ~55–65%, and a miss implies 60–75% downside to platform-plus-cash value. Dose response was flat-to-inverted (64/72/61%), and 16-week data cannot detect chronic Th2-suppression safety signals.
- **Structural dilution and cost inflation**: share count +85–94% since 2022, opex annualising ~$590M and rising, revenue falling to ~$40M in 2027–28 with first product revenue ~2030 — shareholders fund every year of progress, and the IRA 9-year small-molecule cliff plus biosimilar-dupilumab pricing compress the terminal prize.
- **Technical/macro overhang**: unpriced $1.5B offering (~12–13% dilution) into a +253%/15-month move, 78% retail bullishness, 12-month catalyst vacuum, beta 2.03 into a re-hiking Fed with 10Y at 5.13% — a rates shock or sector risk-off means a 30–40% drawdown regardless of fundamentals.

## Key Risks
1. **Phase 3 failure or safety signal** (highest severity): 1,000+ patients, 52-week durability, conjunctivitis 11–20% already visible.
2. **BREADTH asthma miss (late 2027)** — removes the free option the growth case is paying for.
3. **Offering indigestion / post-catalyst fade** — unpriced deal, momentum formally negative ex-gap; a give-back to $115 is the quant base case on disappointment.
4. **Macro amplification** — 10Y >5.30% or HY OAS >400bp triggers a high-beta de-rating.
5. **Competitive compression** — STAT6 is the most crowded target in immunology; Kymera's lead is 18–24 months, not durable past 2030, and it has never commercialised a drug (BD hiring suggests a partnership that caps economics).
6. **Data gaps to close**: API manufacturing country (BIOSECURE exposure), permanent CMO appointment, final offering pricing, insider ownership detail.
