## KYMR — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment

**Company:** Kymera Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: KYMR) | Clinical-stage biotech, targeted protein degradation (TPD) | HQ: Watertown, MA | Incorporated: Delaware | ~273 FTE | Market cap ~$12.8B | Fiscal year end: Dec 31 | Large accelerated filer ([SEC submissions metadata](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001815442.json))

**Key framing point:** Kymera is a **pre-product, pre-commercial** company. It has no product revenue and no sales by country. Its only revenue is collaboration revenue from two partners — **Sanofi (France)** and **Gilead (US)** — so "geographic revenue exposure" is really **counterparty concentration risk**, not sovereign risk. Its operational geographic footprint is US (R&D, HQ) plus EU clinical trial sites.

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk 1 — US pharmaceutical trade policy (Section 232 tariffs + MFN pricing)**
*Probability: High (~80%) | Impact: Medium*
The April 2026 Section 232 proclamation imposed a 100% headline tariff on patented pharmaceuticals with country caps — EU at 15%, UK at 0%, generics exempt — with duties phasing in July 31 and September 29, 2026, and a carve-out to zero for companies pairing onshoring with an MFN pricing agreement until January 20, 2029 ([White House proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/adjusting-imports-of-pharmaceuticals-and-pharmaceutical-ingredients-into-the-united-states/); [EY tax news](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0786-new-tariffs-imposed-on-pharmaceuticals-following-section-232-investigation); [PharmaSource](https://pharmasource.global/content/regulation/section-232-pharma-tariffs-explained/)). Kymera's own 10-K explicitly flags "geopolitical conflicts or new or increased international tariffs." Impact is **not near-term P&L** — it is (a) higher cost of importing clinical drug supply/API into the US, and (b) a forced manufacturing-footprint decision ahead of a first Phase 3 in AD starting by mid-2027. MFN pricing is the larger long-run threat to KT-621's commercial ceiling in immunology.

**Risk 2 — Two-partner concentration in a fragmenting US–EU environment**
*Probability: Medium (~40% of a materially adverse event over 3 years) | Impact: High*
86% of FY2025 revenue came from Sanofi, a French company, with **up to $975M in remaining IRAK4 milestones** contingent on Sanofi's continued advancement ([Clinical Trial Vanguard](https://www.clinicaltrialvanguard.com/news/kymeras-kt-485-enters-phase-1-trial-under-20m-sanofi-deal/); [Investing.com](https://in.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/kymera-receives-20m-milestone-from-sanofi-for-trial-start-93CH-5446081)). Gilead exercised its KT-200 option in April 2026 for a $45M milestone. If US–EU trade tensions escalate (renunciation or renegotiation of the 15% cap) or Sanofi re-prioritizes its immunology portfolio, Kymera loses both revenue and validation. Note the asymmetry: **Kymera has no China licensing exposure**, which insulates it from the US–China biotech deal scrutiny that hits peers — a relative positive.

**Risk 3 — EU clinical/regulatory dependency and foreign trial data**
*Probability: Medium | Impact: Medium*
BROADEN2 enrolled in the EU under CTIS, and BREADTH (asthma, NCT07323654) is running. The 10-Q risk factors reference "foreign clinical trial sites and/or foreign clinical trial data" and "the U.S. and other countries in the international community." Risks: GDPR-driven data transfer friction, EU Pharma Package revisions, and heightened FDA scrutiny of foreign-sourced data for a registrational package. A trial-site disruption in one EU country would delay the Phase 3 timeline without being a balance-sheet event.

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Litigation: effectively none.** Both the FY2025 10-K and the Q2 2026 10-Q state the company "is not currently a party to any litigation or legal proceedings that, in the opinion of our management, are likely to have a material adverse effect." No securities class action, no DOJ/FTC/SEC investigation, no IP litigation identified in search.

**One open item — Halper Sadeh LLC inquiry (Nov 23, 2026):** the investor-rights firm announced it is investigating "whether certain officers and directors… breached their fiduciary duties to shareholders in connection with potential self-dealing" ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261123031102/en/KYMR-Stock-Alert-Halper-Sadeh-LLC-Investigates-Kymera-Therapeutics-Inc-for-Potential-Breaches-of-Fiduciary-Duties/)). Timing is notable — same morning as the BROADEN2 data and the $1.5B offering. **Assessment: low severity.** Halper Sadeh issues these alerts in bulk across dozens of tickers (the same week it issued alerts on Aytu, Rapid, Trustmark, ADT, Skye Bioscience, Kyverna). No complaint has been filed. Watch item only; monitor for a Form 8-K or 10-Q disclosure of a filed action.

**Regulatory:** FDA is the gating regulator. First Phase 3 AD trial planned by mid-2027; BREADTH Phase 2b asthma topline by late 2027. FDA review-capacity constraints and the administration's pressure on accelerated-approval pathways are a timeline risk, not a legality risk. No EU MDR/device issues. No export-control exposure (small-molecule degraders are not dual-use controlled).

**Disclosure quality:** clean — no restatement, no late filings, no auditor issues identified.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM** (low end of the band)

**Rationale for Medium rather than Low:** 86% single-partner revenue concentration (Sanofi, France) combined with a high-probability US tariff/MFN policy shock and an EU-dependent registrational trial program. Any of these alone would be Low; together they justify Medium.

**Rationale for Medium rather than High:** no emerging-market exposure, no sanctions nexus, no active material litigation, no China supply-chain confirmation, and ~$3B of pro forma US-dollar liquidity. The company is **jurisdictionally boring** — which is exactly what you want in a geopolitical screen. Its beta of 2.03 reflects clinical/regulatory binary risk, not country risk.
