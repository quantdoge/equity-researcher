## KYMR — Kymera Therapeutics: ESG Analysis

**Company context:** Clinical-stage biotech (targeted protein degradation), Watertown MA, 273 FTEs, market cap ~$12.8B, beta 2.03, pre-revenue (collaboration revenue only: Sanofi, Gilead). Re-rated on positive BROADEN2 Phase 2b data for KT-621 (oral STAT6 degrader, 244 patients, 16 weeks, well-tolerated) on 23 Nov 2026; $1.5B equity offering launched the same day. $1.55B cash at 31 Mar 2026.

---

### 1. ESG Materiality Map

| Factor | Materiality for KYMR | Why it hits cash flows / cost of capital |
|---|---|---|
| **S — Product/patient safety** | **HIGH** | Only true binary ESG factor. A single Ph3 safety signal (class effects of systemic STAT6/IL-4Rα suppression; adolescent exposure in a 244-pt dataset) destroys the asset. Not a "score" issue — a going-concern issue. |
| **G — Board/ownership control** | **MEDIUM-HIGH** | Founder-CEO on board, VC (Atlas Venture) chair, chronic insider selling → discount rate & dilution governance. |
| **S — Access & affordability** | **MEDIUM** | Oral drug can *expand* access vs injectable biologics (equity-positive), but pricing at biologic parity invites payer/political friction. |
| **G — Disclosure quality** | **MEDIUM** | $12.8B cap with zero sustainability/GHG/TCFD reporting. Fails institutional ESG screens on data, not behaviour. |
| **E — Environmental** | **LOW** | No manufacturing (fully CMO-outsourced), no commercial product, no EU plant footprint. Climate transition risk immaterial. |
| **S — Human capital** | **MEDIUM-LOW** | 273 FTEs, key-person concentration (founder-scientist, departing CMO). No union/community/supply-chain-labour risk. |
| **E — Supply chain** | **LOW-MEDIUM** | Scope 3 solvent/API waste and CMO environmental standards are the only real vector. |

**Structural note (positive E angle):** an oral small molecule displaces cold-chain biologics and IV infusion — materially lower per-patient carbon and health-system resource intensity *if* approved. This is a genuine, underappreciated E/S co-benefit of the platform.

### 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **LOW**
- No Scope 1/2/3 disclosure, no emissions targets, no SASB/TCFD alignment. Immaterial today because there is no commercial production.
- Sole exposure: CMO-dependent API/small-molecule synthesis (solvent waste, wastewater) and future EU environmental standards at contract sites.
- Physical climate risk: low (US lab/office, no water-stressed manufacturing).
- No spills, fines, or remediation liabilities found.

### 3. Social Risk Assessment: **MEDIUM**
- **Safety (dominant):** Clean to date — BROADEN2 "well-tolerated," FDA **Fast Track** granted for KT-621. But durability/serious-AE data is 244 patients at 16 weeks; Ph3 expansion (and BREADTH asthma, topline late 2027) is the real risk window.
- **Human capital:** CMO **Jared Gollob stepped into a consulting agreement effective 1 Oct 2026** (expires 31 Dec 2026) — i.e., transitioned out ~2 months before the pivotal readout. Key-person/execution risk, not a scandal. CEO pay ratio a modest **21:1** (median comp $409,607), indicating no workforce-equity red flag.
- **Access:** oral administration is equity-positive for under-served/needle-averse and low-infusion-capacity populations.
- No diversity disclosure; no supply-chain labour or community controversies.

### 4. Governance Quality Score: **5 / 10**
**Rationale:** Mostly-independent board and sane comp architecture, undermined by control concentration and disclosure lag.
- **ISS Governance QualityScore: 7** (1=lowest risk, 10=highest) as of 1 Nov 2026 — pillars **Audit 5, Board 6, Shareholder Rights 8**. Shareholder Rights is the clear weak link.
- Board: all directors independent *except* founder-CEO Nello Mainolfi — but chaired by **Bruce Booth (Atlas Venture)**, the founding/largest VC holder. Effective VC-plus-founder control with limited outsider challenge. CEO/Chair roles split (positive).
- Comp: CEO 2025 total **$8.5M, 86% option-based, down 45% from $15.4M in 2024** — heavily performance-linked, which aligns with long-term value; low cash component. Positive.
- **Insider alignment (negative):** director **Bruce Booth: ~40 sales, ~525k shares, ~$50M**; CLO Adams disposed of ~$744k; CEO sales under Rule 10b5-1 plans. Programmatic, plan-based, but effectively zero insider buying at a $12.8B valuation.
- Single share class (no dual-class/poison pill identified). $1.5B offering executed *after* the data pop rather than before — shareholder-friendly sequencing.

### 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure
- **Not in scope:** EU CSRD (no EU subsidiary/turnover threshold), CBAM (pharma not covered), SEC climate disclosure rule (not in force), no ESG-linked debt covenants.
- **In scope and material:**
  - **IRA Medicare negotiation — the key structural issue.** As a *small molecule*, KT-621 faces negotiated pricing **9 years post-approval vs 13 years for biologics** (e.g., dupilumab). This is a real long-term cash-flow haircut on the franchise and argues for pricing strategies that lean on the oral/access value proposition rather than premium pricing.
  - FDA: Ph3 alignment, Fast Track, adolescent/pediatric enrolment obligations; BREADTH asthma readout late 2027.
  - Pricing politics: state transparency laws, MFN/reference-pricing pressure, pharma tariff exposure on imported API via CMOs.
  - FCPA/anti-kickback and Sunshine Act exposure via Sanofi and Gilead collaborations (ex-US).
  - EU Pharma Package data-exclusivity reform would matter only at future EU launch.

### 7. Overall ESG Rating: **AVERAGE**
Structurally sound on E (trivially low), acceptable on S (clean safety record, expanding access), but a **relative governance and disclosure laggard**: ISS 7/10, Shareholder Rights 8/10, and no ESG reporting whatsoever at a $12.8B market cap. Not a "Leader" because the disclosure and shareholder-rights pillars fail institutional screens; not a "Laggard" because there is no misconduct, no environmental liability, and comp is genuinely equity-aligned.

### 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEUTRAL**
ESG is **not** a driver of the KYMR thesis in either direction — the valuation is a pure KT-621 probability-of-success × peak-sales equation.
- **No exclusion risk:** no carbon-intensive operations, no controversial-weapons/gambling/tobacco exposure, no human-rights or corruption record. ESG-mandate funds can hold it.
- **Screening friction:** will fail "ESG disclosure quality" and low-governance-risk filters (ISS 7) — a marginal ownership constraint, not a valuation one.
- **What actually matters, ranked:** (1) Ph3 patient-safety outcome — binary, unhedgeable; (2) IRA small-molecule 9-year pricing cliff vs biologic competitors — a real, under-modelled terminal-value drag; (3) insider-selling/founder-control overhang — small persistent governance discount; (4) law-firm solicitation noise — ignore.
- **Actionable watch items:** any Ph3 SAE or clinical hold (exit signal); a first open-market insider *purchase* would be a meaningful positive governance signal; appointment of a permanent CMO with Ph3 dermatology pedigree.
