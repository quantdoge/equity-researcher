## 1. Consumer Demand Signals — **Strongly improving**

**Google Trends: "Google Gemini" (US, 12-mo weekly)**
Gemini search interest shows a clear two-stage pattern: a spring 2026 peak (100 in mid-April), a summer fade to a **trough of 53 (late July/early Aug)**, then a decisive re-acceleration into the October print.

| Period | Avg interest | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Late Jul–Aug 2026 trough | 54 | — |
| Aug 30–Sep 20 | 83.8 | +55% |
| Sep 27–Oct 18 (latest 4wk) | **91.0** | **+8.6% QoQ, +70% off trough** |

Latest reading is **96 (Oct 18)** — the highest point in the entire 12-month window.

**Competitive read-through (critical):** "ChatGPT" US interest is 82 on the same date. **Gemini has crossed above ChatGPT in US search interest for the first time in the window.** This is the single most bullish alt-data point in the dataset — it contradicts the "Google is losing the AI race" narrative that drove the selloff.

**Web traffic:** google.com down **-1.66% MoM** (SimilarWeb public). Directionally negative but inside normal noise. Notably, gemini.google.com skews 25–34 (younger) vs. google.com's 45–54 core — evidence of a genuine new-user cohort, not just cannibalization. **Caveat: no hard monthly visit series available; this is proxy-quality only.**

**Corroborating datapoint:** Reddit's Q3 disclosure that **Google search referrals are shrinking** and Reddit is testing a login wall. This is a real-time confirmation that Search's outbound traffic posture is changing — consistent with AI Mode substituting for ten-blue-links. It cuts both ways: less referral leakage cost, but signals monetization transition risk.

## 2. Business Activity Signals — **Expansionary, capex-heavy**

- **Cloud backlog: $561B** and Cloud revenue **+103% YoY** — the strongest growth datapoint in the quarter by a wide margin.
- **TPU external sales**: a new revenue line started April 2026 with first revenue in Q2, scaling into Q3. TPU 8t/8i claim large perf-per-dollar gains vs. Ironwood; OpenAI reportedly a prospective buyer. This is a structural new TAM (~$250B AI silicon opportunity per sell-side) that consensus models likely still under-weight.
- **Hiring:** LinkedIn shows ~127,126 open Google roles, but the proxy returned **levels, not a trend**. Directional signal unavailable. No evidence of hiring freeze.
- **Patents:** ❌ **Data unavailable** — USPTO PatentsView API returned errors on two attempts (both "Google" and "Alphabet"). R&D intensity signal is a **gap** in this report.

**Interpretation:** Negative FCF in Q3 was driven by capex and inventory build ahead of TPU/system demand, not demand weakness. Business activity alt-data is consistent with aggressive capacity expansion — a *leading* positive, a *coincident* negative for cash flow.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **Above on demand / Below on monetization — divergent**

**Q3 2026 (Oct 28) outcome:**
- Revenue $127.58B vs. $127.97B est — **slight miss** (+25% YoY)
- EPS $2.12 vs. $3.01 est — **-29.6% miss** (incl. -$0.55 from equity-stake losses; operating income missed by 6%)
- FCF negative; net cash $140B

**Surprise history (7 quarters):** 85.7% beat rate, avg +50.8% magnitude — but this average is badly distorted by two outliers (**+217% in Q2'26, +91% in Q1'26**) that reflect equity-stake mark-to-market gains, not operating beats. The clean operating beats run +6% to +39%. **The Q3 miss breaks a 6-quarter beat streak** and is the first genuine operating shortfall — treat the historic beat rate as a weak prior going forward.

**For the Feb 3, 2027 print (est. EPS $3.26, rev $142.4B):** Demand-side alt-data (Gemini trends, cloud backlog, TPU ramp) is tracking **above** prior-quarter trends. But the Q3 miss was a *monetization* miss, not a demand miss — AI Mode monetizes below traditional Search despite query growth at a 5-year high. **Alt-data cannot yet confirm that gap is closing.** Net: revenue likely in-line-to-above; EPS still at risk from depreciation on the capex step-up.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (with a constructive demand undercurrent)

**Bull case from alt data:** Gemini US search interest at a 12-month high and, for the first time, *above ChatGPT*. Cloud +103% with a $561B backlog. TPU external sales as a genuinely unmodeled revenue stream. Post-crash sentiment is capitulation, not deterioration. Search referral changes are being actively renegotiated (Reddit login wall) rather than passively absorbed.

**Bear case from alt data:** The company just failed the one test alt-data cannot paper over — monetization. Gemini *usage* interest is rising, but Q3 proves that rising AI engagement is not yet converting to ad revenue at the traditional Search rate. Negative FCF plus a capex step-up to ~$300B creates a multi-quarter EPS/estimate-revision headwind irrespective of demand. Lawsuit overhang and a broken beat streak.

**The signal is a divergence, not a direction:** demand alt-data says *buy the dip*; financial-translation alt-data says *estimates still have to come down*. These resolve on the Q4 print (Feb 3, 2027), not before.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Reasons for limitation:**
- **Patent trend: total failure** (2 API attempts) — a whole business-activity channel is missing.
- **Web traffic, job postings, social sentiment are all web-search proxies**, not quantitative series (no SimilarWeb API, no Thinknum/LinkedIn counts, no Reddit/StockTwits scores). Levels present, *trends* largely absent or unsupported.
- Google Trends interest is **self-relative** — Gemini's 96 cannot be compared across keywords in absolute terms without a common baseline; the Gemini>ChatGPT crossover is suggestive but not rigorous.
- News sentiment is a keyword-count model, event-skewed by a single -12% day, and **~3 weeks stale** at report date.
- Last reported quarter is Q3'26; the next catalyst is ~14 weeks out, leaving a long decay window for these signals.

**Bottom line for the desk:** Alt data argues the $500B drawdown is a monetization-timing event, not a demand event — Gemini demand is at an all-time high. But alt data also cannot confirm the cash-flow trough is behind the company. **Not a high-conviction long on alt data alone**; the honest read is that no alt-data channel we can access is yet leading the estimate revisions that matter.
