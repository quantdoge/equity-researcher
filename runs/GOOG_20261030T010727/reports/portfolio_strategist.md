## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation — **2.0% of portfolio (starter 1.0–1.5%, ceiling 3.5%)**

**Kelly framing.** Probability-weighted intrinsic value is **$252** (bear $176 / 20%, base $238 / 55%, bull $343 / 25%) → raw edge vs. $282 is **negative (−11%)**, which on pure Kelly implies **zero**. But the value case is contested (its DCF uses a 9% WACC in a 5.05% 10Y world and applies full capex with no credit for the $561B contracted backlog or the unmodelled TPU external-sales TAM). Adjusting for those, fair value plausibly sits **$250–290** — i.e. *at* price, not above it. That is a coin-flip edge, and Kelly on a coin-flip is a fractional stake.

**Sizing logic:**
- Medium conviction + **high** macro/legal/disclosure risk + 34% vol → framework band is 1–3%.
- 3.5% cap × 34.4% vol = **1.2% standalone portfolio vol contribution**, ×1.39 beta ≈ **1.7% beta-adjusted** — the most a single name of this risk profile should contribute.
- Hard ceiling reduced from the standard 5% because (i) three independent agents rate risk **HIGH**, (ii) there is **no insider buying** into a 25% drawdown, and (iii) the equity — not the credit — is the risk (Fin Risk's own asymmetry point).

**Build plan: 1.5% now → 3.5% only on evidence.** The trigger set is explicit (§6), not discretionary.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Price stop:** hard **$238** (−15.5%) for a trading-oriented mandate — that sits just above Value's probability-weighted IV of $252 and below the base case, so a break means the market is pricing the bear case. For a long-horizon mandate, replace the price stop with the fundamental triggers below and accept the drawdown (the 3.5% cap already bounds the damage to ~3.5% of NAV at the −29% max-DD template).

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any two:**
1. **Search & Other growth <8% for two consecutive quarters** while 2027 capex stays >$300B (Growth and Value both name this as the single most important watch item).
2. **Third consecutive negative-FCF quarter** into 2027 with no credible capex-peak guidance at the 3-Feb-27 print.
3. **EU adopts a structural (divestiture) ad-tech remedy** — the one regulatory tail that is *not* priced (Geo Legal 10–20% probability).
4. **Adverse D.C. Circuit ruling** on search remedies that reopens divestiture or strips default-search distribution economics (~$20B/yr Apple TAC in scope).
5. **Credit deterioration** — any downgrade path from the Aa/AA tier, which would confirm Macro's funding-constraint thesis.

**Trim triggers:**
- **$320–325** (gap fill): trim 25% — mechanical resistance, not a valuation call.
- **$340** (Value's level, ~35% premium to IV): trim 50%.
- **$375–400** (Growth target / analyst mean ~$400): trim to a 1% residual. Do not chase the bull case past 30x.

**Monitoring cadence:** quarterly review of exactly two variables — **(1) Search & Other growth vs. total revenue, (2) FCF sign.** Everything else is noise. Watch *Stewart v. Alphabet* lead-plaintiff consolidation (1-Dec-26) and the Feb-27 capex guide.
