## Geographic Exposure, Geopolitical & Regulatory Risk — UnitedHealth Group (UNH)

**Snapshot (as of Nov 18, 2026):** Price $349.91, market cap ~$313.5B, 390,000 employees, HQ Minnetonka, MN. FY2025 revenue $447.6B (+12% YoY). Q3 2026: revenue $112.7B, earnings from operations $6.6B, net margin 3.6%, adj. EPS $4.69, FY26 adj. EPS guidance raised (third time in 2026) to ≥$20.50. *(UHG Q3 2026 release, 13-Oct-2026; FY2025 results release)*

**Headline conclusion:** UNH has almost no *foreign* geopolitical exposure — it has spent 2024–2026 exiting the Americas — but it carries one of the highest **domestic political and regulatory risk** profiles of any large-cap US company. This is a "sovereign customer" risk story, not an EM/sanctions story.

### 1. Geographic Risk Map

UNH's revenue is now effectively a single-country exposure. The international book was dismantled: Amil (Brazil) sold (a **$8.3B loss booked in 2024, $7.1B of it Brazil**), Peru operations closed, and the last asset — **Banmédica (Chile/Colombia), bought for $2.8B in 2018 — agreed for sale to Patria Investments for ~$1B** (announced 30-Nov-2025). *(Reuters 9-Jun-2025; CNBC 30-Nov-2025; Healthcare Finance News)*

| Geography | Rev. exposure (est.) | Risk level | Driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| **United States** | **~99%+** | **Very High** (political/regulatory, not geopolitical) | CMS/Medicare Advantage, DOJ, FTC, 50-state insurance regulators, Congress |
| Chile / Colombia (Banmédica, held for sale) | <0.5%, being divested | Medium | Deal-execution/regulatory approval; LatAm FX |
| Brazil (Amil) | ~0% (exited) | Low (residual) | Legacy indemnity/tail liability only |
| Peru | 0% (closed) | — | Exit complete |
| India / Philippines / Ireland (Optum Insight, shared services, IP/treasury entities) | <0.5%, not separately disclosed | Low–Medium | Data-privacy regimes, transfer pricing, offshoring political sensitivity |

**Interpretation:** classic geopolitical vectors — Russia sanctions, US–China tensions, tariffs, export controls, expropriation, currency repatriation — are **immaterial** to UNH's earnings. The company's "geopolitical" risk is entirely the risk that the **US federal government, as both payor (~40%+ of revenue via Medicare/Medicaid) and regulator, changes the rules or pursues enforcement**. Segment mix reinforces this: Optum Q3 2026 revenue $66.9B / earnings $3.7B (Optum Health $25.9B, Optum Insight $4.9B, Optum Rx $34.2B); UnitedHealthcare is the remainder of the $112.7B. *(UHG Q3 2026 release)*

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Political Risks

**#1 — CMS payment-integrity crackdown ("CRUSH" rule) and MA overpayment recapture**
- CMS released the **Comprehensive Regulations to Uncover Suspicious Healthcare (CRUSH) proposed rule on 16-Nov-2026**, targeting ~**$90B of taxpayer savings** across Medicare, Medicaid/CHIP and the Marketplace, and applying overpayment-recovery audit policy to MA contracts **dating back to 2018**. CMS estimates ~**$9B of MA overpayment recovery per payment year**. *(CMS fact sheet & press release; Modern Healthcare; MHK analysis)*
- **Probability: High (finalization likely, though lookback will be litigated/softened) | Impact: High.** This is the proximate cause of the **~15% two-day drawdown (17–18 Nov 2026: $412.02 → $349.91)**, with UNH down ~8.4% to $356.89 on the day of the proposal and described as falling >10% intraday. Humana fell 13% — this is a sector-wide repricing, not company-specific, but UNH has the largest MA book.
- Compounding: the **CY2027 MA final rate announcement set a net +2.48% increase (~$13.3B)**, materially better than the +0.09% proposed — a relative positive — but CMS also **proposed excluding chart reviews from MA risk scoring**, which would remove a key revenue lever for UNH's risk-adjustment engine. *(CMS 2027 Rate Announcement; Healthcare Dive)*
- Also live: **CMS proposed broader anti-fraud audits** (per Yahoo Finance coverage) and a **Senate Finance (Grassley) staff investigation concluding UnitedHealth was "aggressively" gaming Medicare Advantage**, based on review of ~50,000 records. *(Healthcare Dive)*

**#2 — Structural/legislative attack on the Optum vertical-integration model**
- **Break Up Big Medicine Act (S.3822)**, introduced Feb 2026 by Warren (D) and Hawley (R): would **prohibit common ownership of an insurer with a PBM and with physician groups/MSOs**, and would give enforcement authority to FTC, DOJ Antitrust, HHS OIG and state AGs. *(congress.gov S.3822; Warren Senate release; Proskauer; AJMC)*
- Companion House effort: **Patients Over Profits Act** (Hoyle/Merkley/Warren/Jayapal) targeting insurers buying clinics.
- **Probability: Low-to-moderate near term (bipartisan sponsor pair is unusual and notable) | Impact: Very High / structural.** Optum Rx + Optum Health are roughly half of Optum's revenue and the core of the growth narrative; a forced separation would impair the "flywheel." Renewed **"Medicare for All" push (Sanders, Nov 2026)** adds tail risk to the entire MA franchise.

**#3 — Medicaid/ACA funding contraction via OBBBA + subsidy expiration**
- **One Big Beautiful Bill Act** imposes more frequent Medicaid eligibility redeterminations and work requirements — direct membership/margin hit to UnitedHealthcare Medicaid. Enhanced **ACA premium tax credits expired end-2025**, and ~**760,000 people were removed from ACA Marketplace coverage on 31-Aug-2026**. *(AMA; KFF; CBPP; healthinsurance.org)*
- **Probability: High (already law/in effect) | Impact: Medium–High** — compresses government-program membership and raises morbidity mix in the commercial/individual book; partially offset by 2026 pricing actions (insurance margin improved to ~3.3%).

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **Risk-adjustment / diagnosis-coding integrity (HCC-RAF)** — the convergence of a DOJ criminal probe, two intervened FCA suits, CMS's decision to exclude chart reviews from risk scoring, and Senate findings of "gaming" points to a systemic, not isolated, compliance issue at the core of MA economics. **Highest-priority red flag.**
2. **AI-based utilization management (nH Predict)** — potential NAIC/state unfair-claims-practice and potentially ADA/beneficiary-harm exposure; documents already ordered produced.
3. **Vertical-integration conflicts of interest** — intercompany steering between UnitedHealthcare and Optum Rx/Optum Health is a standing fiduciary/ERISA and antitrust theory for regulators and plaintiff firms.
4. **Financial reporting/controls** — the surviving securities class action alleging $3.3B of manufactured earnings, plus a prior CFO transition (DeVeydt), warrants scrutiny of reserve-development and MA margin recognition.
5. **Not a red flag:** sanctions/AML/FCPA/export-control exposure is de minimis given the post-divestiture US-centric footprint.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating

> ## **HIGH**
> *(Decomposed: pure geopolitical/foreign — **LOW**; US political & regulatory — **VERY HIGH**; legal/litigation — **HIGH**)*

**Rationale for the investment memo:** UNH is not exposed to the standard geopolitical playbook — it has voluntarily surrendered its international footprint at a cost of ~$9B+ in impairments to refocus on the US. That refocus concentrates risk rather than diversifying it, because the US federal government is simultaneously UNH's largest customer, its rate-setter, its auditor, its antitrust enforcer, and — as of November 2026 — a bipartisan political target. The **CRUSH rule (16-Nov-2026) with its 2018 lookback** is the single most important new item: it converts what had been a one-time DOJ-litigation risk into a recurring, mechanical revenue clawback of ~$9B/year across the MA industry, and it is the direct cause of the 15% two-day selloff. A legislative break-up of Optum remains a low-probability/high-severity tail. Offsetting these: three guidance raises in 2026, margin recovery (net margin 3.6%, insurance margin 3.3%), a better-than-proposed CY2027 MA rate (+2.48%), an FTC insulin settlement in hand, and a stock still up modestly YTD from ~$324 despite the November shock.

Exact dollar reserves for litigation/regulatory matters, and UNH's own MA risk-adjustment revenue sensitivity, are not established here and should be confirmed from the FY2026 10-K legal proceedings note.
