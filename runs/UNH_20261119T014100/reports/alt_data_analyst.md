### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (conflicting, policy-dominated)**

The signals genuinely point in opposite directions, and the split is clean:

- **Fundamental alt-data (earnings preview, Optum trends, hiring):** modestly **BULLISH** — above-consensus operating momentum.
- **Demand alt-data (search interest):** **NEUTRAL-to-BEARISH** — MA shopping soft, brand search structurally down.
- **Sentiment / policy:** **BEARISH** — an **unsized** retrospective clawback against 44% of revenue.

The decisive point is that **the dominant variable is unquantified and unquantifiable with alt data**. A $9B/payment-year industry recovery pool, extrapolated to 2018, against a 4.8% operating margin, is a left-tail item that no Google Trends series or hiring proxy can resolve. The market repriced -15% in two days on ~3.5x volume — that is the single most information-dense signal in this dataset, and it says the risk is real but the size is unknown.

**Positioning implication:** the operating turnaround is real and alt data confirms it; the stock is not a "buy the alt-data dip" until management sizes the audit exposure at the 2027 guidance call. Rule comments close Jan 19, 2027 — one day before the print.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not higher:** two of seven channels (patents, social) returned **no data**; two more (web traffic, job postings) returned **static snapshots with no time series**; and the automated news-sentiment product was **demonstrably wrong** on a day with a 10% decline, which required manual override.

**Why not lower:** the high-weight evidence is high quality and internally consistent — audited price/volume action, three quarters of earnings-surprise history, management guidance revisions, and clean weekly Google Trends series with valid year-over-year comparisons.

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly, normalized to a within-series max, and heavily confounded by news cycles — it is a poor proxy for insurance demand, where purchase decisions are annual and agent/broker-mediated. SimilarWeb proxies carry no historical series. Job postings and social signals are non-quantitative. Earnings data is as-of the Oct 13 Q3 print; **no analyst has yet revised 2027 estimates for CRUSH** (consensus $23.33 predates the Nov 16 proposal), so the estimate-revision channel is **stale by construction**. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one.

**Bottom line:** UNH is a **fundamental beat story wearing a regulatory bear case.** Alt data supports the business; it cannot support the stock until the clawback is sized.
