## 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

1. **Momentum collapse & recent selloff.** Full-year return −41.9% vs 12-1 momentum only −10.2% implies the bulk of the decline occurred in the **final ~21 trading days** — an acute recent drawdown. High momentum-reversal risk in both directions; catching a falling knife is the base case without a stabilization signal.
2. **Beta estimate is statistically meaningless.** Regression beta 0.467 carries **R² = 0.022** (2% of variance explained) and directly conflicts with the reported 0.958 profile beta. Do **not** size this position as "low-beta." True market sensitivity is likely near 1.0.
3. **Sector-wide de-rating, not stock-specific only.** All three restaurant comps (CMG, MCD, SBUX) show ~−10% 12-1 momentum — this is a group/consumer-cyclical rotation, so relative rank flatters CMG while absolute signal remains negative.
4. **Thin analyst coverage on forward estimates.** Revision momentum rests on just 4 analysts for the forward period — insufficient for statistical significance; treat the +2.85% as directional at best.
5. **Valuation not cheap on book.** P/B 13.3× and P/FCF 26× leave little margin of safety; the value "attractiveness" is only relative to an expensive peer set, not absolute.
6. **Factor crowding / quality trap.** Quality score is genuine (high ROE, low accruals), but a quality-only profile in a negative-momentum tape is a classic "quality trap" — cheap-looking but dead money until the trend turns.

**Bottom line:** Genuine quality franchise (ROE 54%, accruals −6.4%, F-Score 6) trapped in a severe momentum and volatility downturn. Composite **Neutral with Sell bias**; risk-adjusted profile (Sharpe −1.02, Sortino −1.57, alpha −42.9%) is unattractive for entry until price momentum inflects.
