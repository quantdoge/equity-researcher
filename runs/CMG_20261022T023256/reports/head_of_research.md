Chipotle is a genuine quality franchise — zero funded debt, $1.45B FCF, 54% ROE, clean forensic accounting, seven straight earnings beats — whose growth algorithm has structurally downshifted from mid-teens to mid-single digits while gross margin compresses. The market has already done half the work (−53% from the Dec-2024 peak), so this is neither a short (Short Analyst: "No Edge") nor a buy: at 26x P/FCF for a ~5% grower there is no margin of safety, and a binary Q3 print lands 28 October. Hold existing positions with tight risk rules; new money waits for confirmation of a comps/margin inflection.

**Bull Case**
- Fortress economics: zero funded debt, $1.45B FCF, 54% ROE, negative accruals — no solvency path to zero; buyback provides an EPS floor.
- Real runway: 7,000+ NA unit potential, Chipotlane/automation margin leverage, international optionality; 2026 revenue growth already re-accelerating.
- Contrarian re-rating setup: −53% drawdown in a quality franchise; Street median $38 (+35%); beef deflation or tariff relief snaps margins back with high operating leverage; crowded shorts create squeeze convexity.

**Bear Case**
- Structural, not cyclical, slowdown: growth downshifted to ~5%, pricing spent defending traffic, gross margin eroding *while revenue grows* — operating leverage now cuts both ways.
- Unresolved cost/policy binaries: record beef costs, Mexico tariff threat, immigration enforcement against a 130,000-employee model; three straight guidance cuts have destroyed management credibility.
- No valuation cushion + spent capital return: 26x P/FCF for a 5% grower, buybacks at 1.68x FCF shrinking the equity base, momentum making fresh 52-week lows.

**Key Risks**
1. **Gross margin below 24%** in any quarter — breaks the 2028 consensus EPS bridge ($1.59) and reopens the short thesis (primary invalidation signal).
2. **28 October Q3 print**: last two prints moved −18% and +12.5%; a fourth guidance cut or negative comps extends the de-rating toward $25.
3. Tariffs on Mexican produce implemented without pricing offset; new FDA/DOJ enforcement beyond the existing subpoena.
4. Food-safety incident — low probability, brand-extinction severity in a company-owned system.
5. Buyback suspension or new funded debt — removes the EPS floor underpinning the bull case.
