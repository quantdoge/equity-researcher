- **The arbiter** (Short Analyst) says **no edge either way** at $28.

**Conflict resolution per framework:** The Geo Legal HIGH rating does *not* override the bull case here — unlike a fraud flag, these are *known, disclosed, and partially priced* liabilities (the $25M fine is settled; the securities action is a portion-size claim, not an accounting restatement). However, the **structural margin + valuation** bear case is not refuted: Q2-26 revenue +9.3% with operating income −8.6% is the single most damaging data point in the entire pack. **Net: no actionable edge in either direction today.**

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **0% now → 1.0% conditional starter (max 2.0%)**

**Framework application:** "Low conviction or multiple risk flags: 0.5–1% or pass." CMG carries **four** independent risk flags — HIGH geopolitical/legal, UNFAVOURABLE macro, negative momentum at a 52-week low, and a binary earnings event in seven days — plus a valuation that offers no margin of safety (26x P/FCF for a 5% grower).

**Kelly check:** Scenario-weighted — 40% recovery to $38 (+35%), 40% range-bound ($28–32, ~0%), 20% retest to $25 (−11%). Expected return ≈ **+11.8%** against ~11% downside, odds near 1:1. A near-even-odds bet with 41% volatility warrants **fractional Kelly (≤¼)**, i.e. 1–2% of portfolio at most — and only once the binary event clears. Sizing this at a "high conviction" 3–5% would be a framework violation.

**Why not a full pass forever:** the balance sheet (zero funded debt, $1.04B cash, $1.45B FCF) means there is no solvency path to zero; a −53% drawdown in a franchise with 54% ROE and negative accruals is precisely where contrarian re-ratings start. The 10/28 print is the gate, not the destination.

Net effect: it adds quality and subtracts momentum/volatility in a tape that is rewarding momentum. Classic **quality-trap risk**.
