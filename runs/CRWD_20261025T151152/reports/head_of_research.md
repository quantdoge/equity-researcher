### Investment Memo: CRWD — 26 October 2026

**Verdict**: BUY — initiate small, staged accumulation only | **Conviction: Low** (business-quality conviction: High — these are deliberately separated)
**Price Target**: $295 (~+15% from $257.57) | Corroborated bear cluster: **$165–200 (−25% to −35%)**
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
CrowdStrike is the highest-quality franchise in a non-discretionary, ~12%-growth market — #1 in modern endpoint, six consecutive quarters of growth reacceleration to ~25.8%, record net-new ARR (+51% YoY), clean accounting, and a net-new AI-agent security TAM. The problem is entirely the price: at ~49–55x trailing sales and ~170–200x FCF with SBC at 22.8% of revenue, the market has pre-paid for a decade of flawless execution, into a hostile rates regime (10Y ~5%, Fed hiking) and an unresolved SEC revenue-recognition probe. Own the compounder, but size and stage it for a multiple with zero cushion.
