## Financial Risk Assessment: CrowdStrike Holdings, Inc. (CRWD)

**Company Overview:** CrowdStrike: CrowdStrike Holdings, Inc. is a leading provider of cloud-native cybersecurity solutions. It operates in the high-growth, highly competitive Software - Infrastructure sector. The company has demonstrated strong revenue growth, though it remains highly valued by the market as a high-growth, high-risk investment.

### 1. Credit Risk Assessment
**Rating: Sub-Investment Grade (with high liquidity buffer)**

CRWD exhibits low leverage, which is a significant strength. However, its recent financial health scores suggest the company is still undergoing significant operational restructuring or scaling, leading to "Distress" flags in purely mechanical models.

*   **Leverage:** The company maintains a conservative debt profile. The Net Debt/EBITDA ratio of -24.17 indicates the company has significant excess capacity to service debt, primarily due to its strong cash position and negative net debt.
*   **Debt Service:** The Interest Coverage is currently negative, reflecting periods of high investment and operational loss, which is typical for hyper-growth-stage SaaS companies.
*   **Financial Scores:**
    *   **Altman Z-Score (0.998):** Falls into the "Distress Zone" ($\text{Z} < 1.81$). This score is likely indicative of the company's aggressive growth and early-stage financial model rather than immediate insolvency risk, given the massive cash reserves and improving gross margins.
    *   **Piotroski F-Score (4):** Categorized as "Weak," reflecting the company's cash flow is still heavily reinvested in scaling its sales and product portfolio.
