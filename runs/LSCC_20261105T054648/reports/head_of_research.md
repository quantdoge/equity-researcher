### Investment Memo: LSCC — November 5, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY — speculative starter position only (1.0% NAV, 2.0% hard cap) | Conviction: **Medium** on the business, **Low** on the price
**Price Target**: $115 scenario-weighted (+7% from $107.25) — bear $70 / base $118 / bull $165
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Lattice is a genuinely excellent niche franchise — the #1 low-power FPGA vendor with the highest gross margin in its peer set (67.5% vs. 57.4% median) — undergoing a real, verifiable AI control-plane inflection (Q3 revenue +118% YoY, Compute & Comms +132%). But management just levered that franchise from net cash to ~$800M net debt (BB-rated, 2.4x interest coverage) to buy AMI at the top of the cycle, and the equity now trades at ~16x FY26 sales with GAAP EPS of $0.08. Every positive finding in this research set is about the *business*; every negative finding is about the *balance sheet, geography, and discount rate*. Own it small, size for the beta, and let the FY27 ramp prove itself.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector **ATTRACTIVE** — bifurcated cycle: AI datacenter boom plus embedded restock; programmable logic has the most resilient margin structure in semis. Macro **UNFAVOURABLE** — 10Y at 5.13%, Fed re-hiking (3.88%), CPI re-accelerating to 3.72%; the −40% June→October drawdown maps almost 1:1 onto +85bp in the 10Y. This stock currently trades as a rates instrument first, semiconductor second.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH** — Greater China is 52%→64% of revenue; 81–94% of sales flow through distributors with no end-user visibility; Taiwan-fab single point of failure; 2017 presidential CFIUS block makes LSCC a formally designated sensitive asset; prior $560K deemed-export penalty. Litigation itself is low, though an AMI-deal-disclosure class action is reported (unverified docket).
- **Value**: **SELL** — owner-earnings DCF ~$58 ($45–70 range); SBC is 22% of revenue against $3.1M FY25 GAAP net income; buybacks merely offset dilution. No margin of safety at $107 — the price discounts a super-bull case.
- **Growth**: **STRONG BUY** — 7/7 beats with *accelerating* magnitude (30% last quarter); organic growth pre-dates AMI (+42%, +62% in Q1/Q2); FY27 consensus $1.61B (+61%); FY27 EV/EBITDA ~21x vs. AMD ~109x; reverse-DCF ~$220 if FY30 consensus lands. Rule of 40 ≈ 119.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral** (mechanical Sell, 33/100 — value/quality factors are broken at a cyclical trough). Beta 2.77 (not the 1.89 profile figure), 58.5% vol, max drawdown −40%; measured alpha is statistically insignificant (t≈0.44). Relative momentum is decaying vs. peers.
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH** — net cash → $797M net debt in one quarter; coverage 58x → 2.4x; goodwill/intangibles 74% of assets against $1.4B equity; a repeat of the FY24 −31% revenue decline implies ~7.4x leverage and likely covenant stress. **Covenant terms and maturity schedule are the single largest unverified gap in the entire research set.**
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **MODERATE** (M&A sub-risk HIGH) — independent chair, refreshed board, clean SEC record; offset by CEO/CFO churn in 2024–25 and integration strain (AMI was loss-making pre-close). Decisive signal: **CEO Tamer bought $1.0M of stock in the open market on Nov 4** — the only insider buy in the dataset, against $6.6M of SVP/director selling at $134–154.
- **ESG**: **AVERAGE / neutral for thesis** — best-in-class shareholder rights (single class, no poison pill, TSR-linked PSUs); thin disclosure (no Scope 3, no SBTi). Underpriced item: EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance on AMI firmware, plus inherited PKfail exposure.
- **Alternative Data**: **BULLISH (medium confidence)** — news sentiment +0.90; guidance above consensus; Google Trends attention capitulated ~80% from peak into the print (contrarian-positive washed-out setup). Patents/social data unavailable — honesty note: this is confirmation of reported results, not an independent edge.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **SHORT, moderate conviction — explicitly valuation/capital-structure, not fraud** (Beneish −3.25 clean, accruals negative, DSO/inventory improving; the analyst corrected his own margin-dilution error). Ex-SBC FCF was $17M in FY25; 84x annualized EBITDA; FY29 analyst EPS estimates span 2.7x. The catalyst is the cycle, not a restatement.

**Agent disagreement flag**: fair-value estimates span **$58 (Value) to $220 (Growth) — 3.8x**. The portfolio strategist's rebuild (~$72–84 on $300M FY27 ex-SBC owner earnings) sits between the camps, closer to Value. That dispersion *is* the sizing decision.

**Bull Case**
- Real AI control-plane inflection, pre-dating the AMI deal: C&C +132% YoY, content-per-server expanding every rack generation, Arm AGI CPU collaboration as evidence of socket expansion; FY27 consensus +61% at ~21x EV/EBITDA — the cheapest growth-adjusted AI-semi compounder in the comp set.
- Franchise quality is proven, not asserted: 67–70% gross margins held through a −31% revenue collapse; sticky 10–15-year design-in sockets; clean Beneish; deeply negative accruals.
- De-rating already happened and insiders confirm the thesis: −40% peak-to-trough, CEO bought $1.0M at $102.52 two days after a 30%-surprise beat.

**Bear Case**
- Balance sheet impairment: $797M net debt, 2.4x coverage, BB, 74% intangibles — the next FY24-style downturn now risks covenant breach and an impairment that could erase >50% of book equity.
- Earnings quality and valuation: GAAP EPS $0.08; SBC 22% of revenue; consensus already embeds the +60% FY27 target — there is no guide-down cushion at 16x sales.
- Unhedgeable geography + hostile rates: ~50–64% China revenue through an opaque channel, Taiwan fab, CFIUS-sensitive identity, and a 5.13% 10Y against a 2.77-beta equity.

**Key Risks** (position-killers, monitored quarterly)
1. FY27 revenue guide below ~$1.35B (<+35% vs. the 60% target) — breaks the valuation single-handedly.
2. Non-GAAP gross margin below 70% for two consecutive quarters (watch the 290bp QoQ GAAP decline to 67.4%).
3. Net leverage not trending to <3.0x by end-FY27, or adverse covenant terms revealed in the FY26 10-K.
4. BIS/Entity-List action touching a distributor, or China revenue exceeding ~65%.
5. AMI goodwill impairment or a second production-fleet firmware CVE.
6. Fed scenario: a second hike pushing the 10Y through 5.50% implies $75–80 even on an earnings beat (macro agent's bear case).
