## SECTOR BRIEF — LSCC (Lattice Semiconductor)
**Sector: Technology / Semiconductors — Programmable Logic (FPGA) & Embedded Control**
*Current price $107.25 · Market cap $15.2B · Beta 1.89 · Fiscal Q3-26 reported 2-Nov-2026*

### 1. Sector Overview
Programmable logic is a ~$11–13B revenue pool growing high-single digits at the baseline, but the 2025–26 cycle has split into two distinct markets. The AI data-center side is in a boom: programmable/control silicon content per server is rising as hyperscalers add security, telemetry and power-management functions, and LSCC's Q3-26 Compute & Communications revenue grew **+132% YoY**. The traditional embedded side (industrial, auto, consumer) is only 2–3 quarters into recovery from a brutal 2023–24 inventory correction — Microchip's revenue fell **-42% in FY25 before +7% in FY26**, Skyworks-class analog peers **-25% then +34%** — so sector revenue growth is now bifurcating sharply between AI-exposed and legacy-exposed names. Concentration is extreme: AMD (Xilinx), Altera and Microchip hold roughly 85–90% of FPGA share (HHI well above 2,500), with Lattice the clear #1 in the low-power/small-FPGA niche (<100K LUT) that serves control-plane, security and edge applications.

### 2. Key Tailwinds
- **AI server attach growth:** Lattice disclosed ~38% of 2026 revenue from Server and ~25% from AI, pre-AMI. Q3-26 revenue hit a record **$291.0M, +118% YoY**, with organic growth (ex-AMI's $39.1M) still ~+89%; segment Compute & Comms +132%.
- **Embedded cyclical recovery + restock:** peer growth inflected positive across the board (ADI +16.9%, TXN +13.1%, SLAB +34.3%, MCHP +7.1%), giving broad-based FPGA demand a second leg beyond AI.
- **Platform/software expansion via AMI (closed 27-Jul-26):** pulls Lattice into BMC firmware/infrastructure manageability, raising content per system; Q3 free cash flow **$77.6M** funded **$50M** of debt paydown in one quarter. New "Lattice Prompt" NL-to-FPGA tooling lowers design-in friction and widens the addressable developer base.

### 3. Key Headwinds
- **Hyperscaler concentration and ASIC substitution:** the AI/server growth is concentrated in a handful of customers who are simultaneously funding custom ASICs (Broadcom/Marvell/Alchip) that displace programmable content at scale — high bargaining power against a $0.8B-revenue supplier.
- **Leverage and margin dilution from AMI:** Q3 interest expense jumped to **$9.6M from ~$0**, gross margin slipped to **67.4% from 70.3%** QoQ, and GAAP operating margin is only **8.2%** — GAAP EPS of $0.08 leaves the valuation entirely dependent on non-GAAP and on delivering the "60% growth in 2027" target.
- **Cyclicality, geography, volatility:** legacy industrial/auto demand proved violently cyclical (FY24 revenue -30.9%); revenue skews to Asia distribution and OEMs, exposing it to export-control/tariff shifts. Beta 1.89 — the stock fell **-10% on 30-Oct** into the print and sits **~31% below its June 2026 high of ~$155**.

### 5. Target Company's Competitive Position — **STRONG (niche leader), AVERAGE as a scaled player**
Lattice earns **the highest gross margin in the peer group (67.5% vs 57.4% median, +10.1pts)** and revenue growth 15pts above peer median — the signature of genuine pricing power in a defensible niche: a proprietary software toolchain (Radiant), multi-year design-in cycles, and switching costs on control-plane/security sockets where customers qualify parts once and never re-spend the engineering cost. It is also the most profitable *per dollar of revenue* of any pure-play small-cap semi here, with gross margin above TI and ADI. The offset: at $0.8B revenue it is subscale, its **operating margin (9.3%) trails the 13.5% peer median** because opex and now $9.6M/quarter of interest are spread over a small base, and it competes against AMD Xilinx and Altera, each 20–40x its R&D budget. Position: a high-quality franchise that has just re-rated its growth profile — execution risk, not competitive risk, is the swing factor.

### 6. Sector Rating: **ATTRACTIVE**
AI-driven programmable silicon demand plus a synchronous embedded-cycle recovery is the best demand backdrop semis have had since 2021, and gross-margin structures in programmable logic (57–68%) are the most resilient in the sector. Downgrade triggers: hyperscaler capex digestion, a shift of server control content into custom ASICs, or a stall in the 2027 growth ramp — the last of which is the specific risk embedded in LSCC's ~15x forward sales multiple.
