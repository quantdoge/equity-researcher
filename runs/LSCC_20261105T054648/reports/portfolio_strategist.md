## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** — but the split is narrow and locatable

**Total agreement (11/11):**
- The **franchise is real**. 67–70% gross margin held for four years through a −31% revenue collapse. Not one agent disputes the moat — the Short Analyst explicitly concedes it ("a genuine bull point and I will not manufacture a bear case against it").
- The **AI/Compute & Comms inflection is real and pre-dates AMI** (Q1-FY26 +42%, Q2 +62%, both pre-consolidation). Growth is not an accounting artifact.
- **The risk is capital structure and geography, not the business.** Short, Fin Risk, Geo Legal, Non-Fin Risk and ESG converge here from five different methodologies.

**The genuine conflict — and my resolution:** Value says $58, Growth says $220. Both are wrong at the margin for the same reason: they anchor on different owner-earnings bases. Value deducts SBC *and* AMI intangible amortization *and* interest from a trough base, double-counting. Growth capitalises consensus FY30 ($3.2B revenue, 4 analysts, EPS range $8.45–9.74) at 10x sales. **Consensus FY27 EBITDA is $774M, which neither camp reconciles to.**

My own rebuild — $300M FY27 ex-SBC owner earnings, 25–30% growth for 5 yrs, 10% WACC, 4% terminal — gives **$72–84/share**. To justify $107 you need ~35% ex-SBC OE growth for five years. So the truth sits between the camps, closer to Value than to Growth.
