## Lattice Semiconductor (LSCC) — Financial Risk Assessment

**Critical framing note:** LSCC's most recent *audited* financials (FY2025, ended 2026‑01‑03) materially misrepresent current risk. In the September 2026 quarter the company completed a transformational debt-funded acquisition: total assets went from **$976M to $2,610M** and net cash of **–$56M** became **net debt of +$797M** in a single quarter. All headline ratios from the ratio tools (interest coverage 57.9x, net debt/EBITDA –0.99x, Z-score 3.85) are computed on the pre-acquisition balance sheet and are **stale**. I report both below and anchor the assessment on the pro forma position.

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis (revenue –20%)

Base case = annualised Q3 FY2026 run rate ($1,164M revenue, 67.4% gross margin, ~$688M opex, ~$99M D&A, ~$38.6M interest).

**The –20% case is not hypothetical — it is observed history.** Revenue fell 30.9% from FY2023 ($737M) to FY2024 ($509M), and 29% peak-to-trough by FY2025, with operating income collapsing 93% ($212M → $15M) on extreme operating leverage. The critical difference: **that cycle was absorbed from a net-cash position; the next one will be absorbed with $797M of net debt.** At 7.4x net debt/EBITDA, a conventional 4.0–5.0x maintenance covenant would likely be breached, forcing an equity raise or asset sale at a depressed price. Covenant terms, revolver capacity and the debt maturity schedule are **not available in my data** — this is the most consequential gap in the assessment.

**Impairment scenario:** goodwill and intangibles of $1,929M sit against $1,417M of equity. A sustained lower valuation or reduced cash-flow projections could trigger a write-down; **an $800M impairment would erase 56% of book equity.** Notably, the FY2025 Z-score's safe reading already depended on pre-deal book equity being 81% of assets — that cushion is gone.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Not "Critical,"** because: operations are genuinely cash generative (FY2025 FCF $132.6M; $198.6M across the last three reported quarters), there is **zero short-term debt** — the entire $925M is long-dated (period-end), the current ratio is 1.92x, and revenue is inflecting sharply upward ($146M → $171M → $201M → $291M across the last four quarters).

**Not "Low" or "Moderate,"** because leverage went from net cash to ~4x in one quarter, interest coverage fell from 58x to ~2.4x, 74% of assets are intangibles, and the equity is priced at ~19x sales with a 2.38 beta.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Leverage transformation in a single quarter** — net cash → $797M net debt; coverage 57.9x → ~2.4x.
2. **Goodwill/intangibles at 74% of assets** ($1,929M vs. $1,417M equity) — acute impairment exposure into a cyclical downturn.
3. **Covenant exposure unknown but likely tight** — 7.4x net debt/EBITDA under a –20% revenue scenario, a decline this company has already delivered twice.
4. **SBC of ~$200M annualised (~19% of revenue)** — in Q3 FY2026, SBC of $50.3M equalled 65% of the quarter's $77.6M FCF. Cash generation is substantially an SBC add-back; diluted shares grew ~4% over 21 months *despite* a $100M FY2025 buyback.
5. **Severe operating leverage** — FY2025 operating margin of 2.9% on $523M revenue; a 10% revenue move swings EBIT by multiples of itself.
6. **Extreme valuation and beta** — ~84x annualised EBITDA, 340x TTM earnings, beta 2.38, 60% vol, –45% drawdown.
7. **Pre-deal profitability had nearly vanished** — FY2025 net income of $3.1M versus $259M in FY2023.
