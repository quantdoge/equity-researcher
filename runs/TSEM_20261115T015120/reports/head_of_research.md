**Bull Case**
1. **Contracted, prepaid structural growth**: $1.3B of signed 2027 SiPho revenue + $290M prepayments + backlog into 2029, in the one foundry niche (AI optical interconnect) that is capacity-constrained, not demand-constrained. GM 34% and guided higher; Q4 run-rate $2.4B annualised.
2. **De-rating already inflicted**: −54% on record results and rising estimates; 20.6x 2027E EPS for ~40% growth (PEG ~0.5); retail/thematic attention fully washed out — the crowding that caused the collapse is gone.
3. **Fortress funding**: ~$1B net cash, zero interest burden, customer-financed capex and ~$1B METI grant — the $3B Japan build is not dependent on credit markets at 5%+ rates.

**Bear Case**
1. **No margin of safety**: IV $135–145 = spot; EV equals flawless 2028 delivery capitalised and discounted; underlying FCF negative with capex at 35% of revenue; justified P/B ~0.6x vs 5.7x actual on 6.3% ROIC.
2. **Dated binaries in the ramp window**: ITC exclusion order (mid/late-2027) could block US imports of Israel/Japan-built product exactly when $1.3B of contracts convert; Israel disruption already cost share once (Mar-26).
3. **Expectations outrun the company**: 2029 consensus exceeds management's own model; ETR ~4% flatters EPS; CEO monetised up to $60M near the high with no offsetting buys; hawkish-Fed surprise could compress the 2028 multiple from ~14x to ~10x (−20–25% with no estimate cut).

**Key Risks**
1. ITC §337-TA-1500 exclusion order or adverse W.D. Tex. finding (mid/late-2027) — the single hard binary.
2. SiPho/AI-capex digestion or architectural shift to co-packaged optics bypassing merchant SiPho; customer concentration (~46% of 2027 revenue, one theme).
3. Capex execution: Japan 300mm ramp, D&A doubling, FCF staying negative ex-prepayments; advance-refund terms unverified.
4. Renewed North District hostilities or customer-mandated non-Israel second sourcing.
5. Multiple compression: 10Y toward 5.75–6% on a hawkish Fed; this stock de-rated 21% in four sessions on a beat.
6. TPSCo restructuring approval slippage past mid-2027; METI/IIA covenant friction as capacity migrates.
