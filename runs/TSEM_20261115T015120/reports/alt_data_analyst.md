## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **TRACKING ABOVE (modestly), with a revenue caveat**

Surprise history is exceptional and the bar is well-calibrated:

- **Beat rate: 7/7 quarters (100%)**, average surprise **+11.7%**
- Streak of magnitude: +18.8% → +6.4% → +1.3% → +16.4% → +15.5% → +16.1% → **+7.5%** (latest, Q3'26)
- **But revenue beats are thin:** Q3'26 rev $526.2M vs $521.8M est (**+0.8%**); Q2 +1.2%; Q1 +0.7%. Q3'25 actually *missed* revenue (−5.8%)

**Read:** TSEM's beats are a **margin story, not a top-line beat story.** Q3 gross margin ~34% ($179M GP, +92% YoY) vs 23.5% a year ago; management targets 45% at 85% utilisation. For Q4 the guide is $600M ±5% vs consensus $601.7M — i.e. **guidance midpoint is fractionally *below* consensus**, and +14% QoQ is already an aggressive sequential step. So: **revenue likely In-Line (~$605–610M, typical ~1% beat); EPS estimate $1.38 is beatable toward ~$1.45–1.50 on gross-margin leverage.** Net: alt data supports a *smaller-than-usual* EPS beat (+3–8%), not another +16%.

**Critical signal — the market rejected a good print.** Q3 came 2 Nov: revenue +33% YoY, profit more than doubled, Q4 guided +36% YoY. The stock fell from $166.48 (30 Oct) to $130.99 (5 Nov), **−21% in four sessions on 8.0M shares (~4x normal volume)**. That is a valuation/positioning rejection, not an earnings rejection. Same pattern at the Q2 print (4 Aug, beat, then $239 → $187 by 29 Jul–29 Aug). **Two consecutive beat-and-fade reactions = the market is trading the multiple, not the EPS.** Consensus may still be too high on 2028 rather than too low on Q4.

## 6. Overall Alternative Data Signal: **BULLISH** (fundamental), with a **positioning/valuation caveat**

- Fundamental alt data (hiring mix, design-in web traffic, contract/pre-payment disclosures, news tone) is **unambiguously accelerating** and points *above* trend into Q4.
- Attention alt data (Google Trends, StockTwits, Reddit) shows a **complete washout** — which removes the crowding risk that caused the −49% de-rating from the $288 May peak.
- The honest counterweight: this is **not** a high-quality alt-data setup for a *tradeable* beat. Revenue is a ~1% beater, guidance landed *at* consensus, and the stock has now faded two straight beats. The alpha here is multi-quarter (2027–28 contracted backlog, margin ramp), not the 10 Feb print.
- Where alt data argues the de-rating has overshot: record gross profit (+92% YoY) and a 100% 7-quarter beat rate alongside dead retail attention and zero negative news.

## 7. Confidence: **MEDIUM**

**Why not high:** (a) Google Trends on a niche B2B foundry keyword is contaminated by investor/news-cycle interest rather than end-demand — directionally useful, not a revenue proxy; (b) web traffic is a low-fidelity snippet with ambiguous column mapping; (c) social and hiring are web-search proxies, not Thinknum/LinkedIn API counts; (d) **patent trend entirely unavailable** (USPTO feed failed twice) — the R&D-intensity leg of the framework is unverified; (e) no satellite/geospatial fab-utilisation data was obtainable, which for a foundry is the highest-value missing signal (utilisation is the direct gross-margin driver behind every EPS beat).

**Lag warning:** news/social data is ~0–10 days stale but aligned with the 13 Nov close; Google Trends is ~1 week lagged; hiring and traffic are monthly-grained and structurally lagging by 2–4 weeks. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one — and in this name the leading indicator (contracts, hiring, SiPho design activity) and the coincident indicator (stock price) are pointing in opposite directions.
