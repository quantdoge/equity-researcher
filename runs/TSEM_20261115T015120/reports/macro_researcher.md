## Macro Assessment — Tower Semiconductor Ltd. (TSEM)

**Data as of 15 Nov 2026. FRED-sourced unless noted. Note: this is Tower Semiconductor (Israel-domiciled analog foundry, $16.7bn cap, $146.89), not Taiwan Semiconductor.**

---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — *Reflationary with a hostile term premium (transitional-to-risk-off for long-duration assets)*

The US economy is **not decelerating** — it is re-accelerating into a supply-constrained, inflation-sticky regime. Real GDP grew **3.4% in Q3 2026** (advance) after 2.2% in Q2 and 2.5% in Q1; unemployment has *fallen* to **4.1%** (Oct 2026) from 4.5% a year earlier. Yet CPI has re-accelerated to **3.82% YoY** (Oct 2026) from a ~2.6% trough in late 2025 — i.e., the Fed's last 200bp of easing (5.33% → 3.63%) landed on an economy that didn't need it.

The policy response is now **a pause with hawkish skew**: the effective Fed Funds rate bottomed at **3.63%** (Jun–Aug 2026) and has since drifted *up* to **3.88%** (Oct 2026). With real GDP above trend and CPI at 3.8%, the market's easing bias is unsupported.

The critical macro variable for TSEM is the **long end — not the policy rate**. The curve has *bear-steepened*: **2Y 4.74%, 5Y 4.93%, 10Y 5.13%, 30Y 5.47%**, with a **+39bp 2s10s spread**. No inversion → no recession signal, but a **~1.3% real 10Y yield** and a rising term premium are the most punitive discount-rate environment for long-duration growth equities since before the GFC.

**The regime is "good economy, bad multiples."** That is precisely TSEM's tape: record Q3 2026 revenue of $526m (+33% YoY), Q4 guided to $600m (+36% YoY), 2028 targets of $3.6bn revenue / $1.2bn net income — and the stock is **down 54% from its $316.85 close on 22 June 2026 to $146.89**. This is a pure multiple event, not an earnings event.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL**

Split the rating explicitly, because a single word hides the tension:

- **Operating macro: FAVOURABLE.** 3.4% GDP, 4.1% unemployment, hyperscaler capex +~80%, and a genuine analog cyclical recovery. TSEM's demand environment is close to the best it has ever been.
- **Valuation macro: UNFAVOURABLE.** CPI 3.8%, Fed paused with hawkish skew, 10Y 5.13%, 30Y 5.47%, real 10Y 1.3%. Long-duration equities are being repriced regardless of fundamentals.

**Net: Neutral.** The macro backdrop does not threaten TSEM's revenue trajectory — it threatens the *multiple* applied to it, and has already done so violently (-54% from June). At ~14x the 2028 $1.2bn net-income target, the discount-rate compression looks substantially, though not fully, discharged. The residual risk is not rates; it is AI-capex durability in 2027, which is a micro/positioning question, not a macro one.

---

### 5. The Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **a Hawkish Fed, not a recession**

Consensus is positioned for a slowing economy and resumed cuts in 2027. The data says otherwise: **3.4% growth, 4.1% unemployment, CPI back to 3.82% and rising, nominal PCE annualising ~5.6%.** The surprise that is under-priced is that the Fed's next move is a **hike**, not a cut — effective fed funds moving from 3.88% toward 4.50% through H1 2027, dragging the 10Y toward **5.75–6.00%** on term premium and inflation expectations.

Given TSEM's beta of 0.95 understates its true rate duration, and given the stock's 54% drawdown occurred *while earnings were beating*, a further 100bp on the 10Y would plausibly compress the 2028 multiple from ~14x to ~10–11x — a further **-20% to -25%** without a single downgrade to estimates. This is the scenario in which TSEM's record fundamentals and its share price decouple for a second consecutive year.

**The mirror-image bull surprise:** if growth cracks just enough to break inflation (payrolls turn negative, CPI rolls to 2.5%) and the 10Y falls to 4.20%, TSEM re-rates sharply off a $1.2bn 2028 earnings base with $1.3bn of 2027 contracts already booked — that is the path to the $263 price targets. I assign the hawkish-Fed scenario higher probability than consensus does, but the asymmetry favours the bull case from $146.89.

---

**Bottom line for the investment memo:** TSEM is a high-quality secular compounder currently being valued by the bond market rather than the semiconductor cycle. Do not underwrite a rate-relief-driven re-rating; underwrite the $1.3bn 2027 contract backlog and the customer-prepayment-funded Japan capacity, and treat the 5.13% 10Y as a persistent ceiling on the multiple rather than a temporary headwind.
