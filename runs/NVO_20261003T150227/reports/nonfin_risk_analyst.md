# NOVO NORDISK A/S (NYSE: NVO) — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**Date of analysis: October 2026 | ADR of Danish issuer (Novo Nordisk A/S, Copenhagen: NOVO B)**

**Method note:** Foreign private issuer — U.S. SEC Form 4/regulatory-action feeds and the 20-F retrieval returned empty, so insider-activity analysis relies on disclosed gaps; governance, regulatory, and litigation findings are triangulated from current news, FDA/CMS actions, and company disclosures.

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate (with concentration caveat)**

**Evidence:**
- **Controlled-company structure:** Novo Holdings A/S — wholly owned by the Novo Nordisk Foundation — holds **~28.1% of share capital but ~77.3% of voting rights** (A shares carry multiple votes). This is a stable, mission-aligned, century-old structure, but it gives minority/ADR holders negligible voting influence and insulates management from activist or ordinary-shareholder pressure — a genuine control-concentration feature that cuts both ways.
- **Recent leadership churn at the top:** CEO **Maziar "Mike" Doustdar** took the reins **7 Aug 2025** (accelerated succession from Lars Fruergaard Jørgensen), and shareholders **approved a new board chair in Nov 2025**. Two top-governance transitions inside ~15 months, both landing in the middle of a pipeline setback and pricing cliff — execution risk is elevated.
- No related-party red flags beyond the foundation structure; Novo maintains separate audit/nomination/compensation committees and follows Danish governance code. Compensation details not independently verified this cycle.
- Insider data unavailable (see §3). No SEC/Danish enforcement actions surfaced.

**Verdict:** Process-level governance is sound; the concentration of voting power and double leadership transition are the weaknesses.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

**Active material items:**
- **FDA Warning Letter (reported 2026):** Reports indicate the FDA issued a **formal warning letter to Novo for failures in reporting serious adverse events** tied to Ozempic/Wegovy. If accurate, this is a serious pharmacovigilance compliance breach — escalation-worthy (see §7). Unverified directly; sourced via legal-industry trackers.
- **Denecimig (hemophilia A) BLA delay (2 Oct 2026):** FDA extended review over **ongoing remediation at a manufacturing facility; no new timeline communicated**. Positive offset: EMA CHMP endorsed the therapy in Sep 2026.
- **IRA Medicare price negotiation:** CMS selected **Ozempic/Rybelsus at a $274 "maximum fair price" (≈71% below ~$959 list)** and announced **~50% list-price cuts for Ozempic and Wegovy effective 1 Jan 2027** (Wegovy to ~$675/mo). This is the structural driver of the "2027 revenue cliff" — direct regulatory impact on ~40%+ of revenue.
- **Compounding crackdown:** FDA actions against compounded semaglutide knockoffs; Novo **filed patent suit against Hims & Hers (Feb 2026)** after terminating a distribution deal in under two months. Supportive of brand integrity but a live legal/commercial dispute.
- **Expansion positives:** China accepted oral Wegovy marketing application (Aug 2026).
- **No SEC/DOJ enforcement** found in available datasets (search returned empty).

## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Manufacturing quality / regulatory compliance strain:** FDA facility-remediation-driven extension of the denecimig BLA (no timeline) plus an alleged adverse-event reporting warning letter, all during capacity-expansion and GLP-1 supply-constraint mode. Recurring FDA inspection/remediation themes at U.S. plants are the single most execution-critical operational risk.
2. **Pipeline/leadership key-person risk:** New CEO (Aug 2025) + new chair (Nov 2025) steering through the CagriSema setback; value now rests on a thin set of assets (oral semaglutide, CagriSema resubmission, denecimig, next-gen obesity candidates). Concentration of strategic bets in a handful of molecules.
3. **Revenue concentration & franchise cliff:** GLP-1 franchise dominance (Ozempic/Wegovy) with Medicare price compression from Jan 2027, CagriSema failing to decisively beat Zepbound in REDEFINE 4, and unresolved post-loss-of-exclusivity strategy — an operational/financial convergence risk (2030s IP expiries ahead). Manufacturing footprint relies on relatively few sites (Denmark, Clayton NC, etc.), adding supply-chain concentration to the same franchise.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment

**Elevated, with a structural theme:** the *GLP-1 safety backlash*.
- **Product-liability media cycle is live and widening:** 2,600+ lawsuits in the GLP-1 MDL alleging gastroparesis, bowel obstruction, pancreatitis, and new **vision-loss (NAION)** claims; third-party estimates put U.S. legal exposure at ~$2B. Even if discovery shows weak causation, the "Ozempic harmed me" narrative persists in consumer media and social channels (muscle loss, "Ozempic face," psychiatric effects — much of it low-quality content but reputationally sticky).
- **Adverse-event reporting warning letter**, if confirmed, converts safety chatter into a "Big Pharma hid risks" story — worse for the brand than the litigation itself.
- **Compounding knockoffs + Hims & Hers dispute** blur product integrity and trust; Novo's February pivot (sue-then-withdraw) played out messily in public.
- **Investor-relations damage:** frosty capital-markets day, guidance cut to flat/down 6% (2026), and the Feb 23 REDEFINE 4 shock (≈20% one-day drop, ~€90B value wiped) have dented management credibility with investors, though patient/physician goodwill and the foundation's public-health mission remain reputational assets.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

No fraud indicators, no corruption/misconduct findings, and no acute compliance scandal confirmed — which keeps this short of **Critical**. But regulatory (FDA warning letter + IRA price compression), operational (manufacturing remediation, leadership churn), and reputational (GLP-1 safety MDL, backlash) risks are **simultaneously elevated** at a strategic inflection point (pipeline setback + 2027 pricing cliff). Stress-test: if the FDA warning letter is confirmed material, or denecimig is further delayed, upgrade to Critical.
