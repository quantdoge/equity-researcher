## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **Low**

- Strong "Circular for Zero" programme: net-zero Scope 1+2 by **2030**, full value-chain (incl. Scope 3) by **2045**; Japanese site already net-zero Scope 1+2; Denmark production runs largely on renewable grids.
- Pharma is a low-carbon sector vs. industrials/energy; NVO's absolute emissions are modest relative to its market cap. CBAM exposure negligible (domestic/regional manufacturing, minimal steel-intensive imports).
- **Watch item:** Scope 3 (~supply chain) is the bulk of footprint → supplier engagement and pharma-API manufacturing decarbonisation will drive future capex; not a current cash-flow constraint.

---

## 3. Social Risk Assessment: **High**

- **Pricing/access is the single largest ESG-driven financial risk.** IRA Medicare negotiation on its three top-selling GLP-1 products (2027) plus US/state insulin price caps compress the US margin engine; amplified by competitive in-roads from Eli Lilly (amylin/combination data beat NVO's).
- **Product quality / patient safety:** repeated FDA 483 citations at the Bloomington (ex-Catalent) fill-finish site — contamination including hair and pests, unresolved complaints dating back years (FDA warning-letter trail). Denecimig BLA delay is a direct consequence.
- **Partially offsetting:** above-average labour standards (Nordic base, ~68,800 employees), patient-assistance/insulin-affordability programmes, UNICEF childhood-obesity partnership, and demonstrably beneficial health outcomes (CV/metabolic) — a genuine "S-positive" product mission.
- Clinical-trial ethics and supply-chain labour monitoring are well-documented relative to peers; not a material risk.

---

## 4. Governance Quality Score: **7 / 10**

**Strengths:** Danish two-tier board with employee-elected representatives; separate Chair/CEO; long-term foundation stewardship (capital locked for philanthropic/patient purposes — constrains short-termism); strong anti-corruption record; high transparency (EU CSRD/ESRS reporting; leading external ESG ratings, e.g., top-decile MSCI-type scores and CDP leadership).
**Deducts:** (+) controlling position gives foundation veto over major decisions → minority shareholders have limited say on pay, M&A (e.g., ~$16bn+ Catalent deal) and strategy; (++) GMP/quality oversight failures (Bloomington) and the delayed hemophilia BLA point to governance blind spots in post-acquisition quality integration.

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure

- **US:** IRA Medicare Drug Price Negotiation (Ozempic/Rybelsus/Wegovy, 2027) — highest impact; state insulin price caps; FDA cGMP enforcement (Bloomington); SEC climate-disclosure rule (currently stayed/litigated — low near-term burden).
- **EU:** CSRD/ESRS full reporting (NVO is an early adopter); EU Taxonomy alignment; EU Pharma Package pricing/access reforms; CBAM (negligible).
- **DK/domestic:** Danish Corporate Governance Recommendations; foundation oversight regime.
- **Net:** The binding constraint is **US price regulation and FDA quality enforcement**, not EU climate rules.

---

## 7. Overall ESG Rating: **Leader (with rising risk flags)**

Novo remains a best-in-class **E** and **G** name and a net-positive **S** product story — but 2026 marks a step-change in financially material ESG risk: IRA-driven price cuts on its core franchise, an unresolved GMP quality scandal at a key fill-finish site, and intensifying competitive/pricing pressure. The gap between its clean ESG profile and its converging regulatory/quality risks is narrowing; we would not underwrite the "clean leader" label without haircutting for the 2027 US pricing step-down.

---

## 8. ESG Investment Implication: **Negative** (for the stated thesis)

ESG factors are transitioning from reputational to **cash-flow material**: the ~70% Medicare MFP cut on its top-selling drugs (2027) and FDA enforcement delays (denecimig) create a tangible, dated earnings overhang that is only partially priced. Countervailing positives — strong governance, low environmental risk, and durable demand — keep this from being a sell signal, but the ESG risk/return skew is **negative-to-neutral over the next 12–18 months**. Key triggers to revisit: final 2027 MFP confirmation, Bloomington remediation sign-off (FDA re-inspection), and insulin-suit settlement quantum.
