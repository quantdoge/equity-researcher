## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Geopolitical-adjacent Risks

**① US drug-pricing regime: Medicare IRA negotiation hits core franchise (US: ~60% of sales)**
- *Status:* Certain — CMS's second negotiation cycle selected **Ozempic, Wegovy and Rybelsus**; final Maximum Fair Prices (announced Nov 2025) take effect **Jan 1, 2027**: Ozempic ~$274/month vs $959 list (≈71% discount headline; ~44% below list across the 15-drug basket), Wegovy $274 (low dose)/$385 (high dose).
- *Probability:* 100% (already negotiated). *Impact:* High — direct top-line pressure on the GLP-1 franchise; amplified by US Senate/Bernie Sanders pricing hearings and political pressure on obesity drug prices.
- *Mitigants:* US manufacturing base (Clayton, NC) inures NVO to tariff risk; volume offset possible at lower prices.

**② US–China decoupling / China VBP + local competition (China: ~6% of sales, high growth)**
- *Status:* Active. National Volume-Based Procurement cut insulin prices ~48% (2021); Wegovy priced −37% vs US to stay competitive (2025); semaglutide patent expiries in China (and Brazil/Canada/India) invite biosimilars. US–China trade/tech tensions could disrupt cross-border clinical supply chains.
- *Probability:* High (pricing/competition ~90%; hard decoupling disruption ~20–30%). *Impact:* Medium — China is now a margin-dilutive growth market rather than a profit driver.

**③ US–Denmark trade friction (Greenland sovereignty dispute)**
- *Status:* Active but low-probability of direct impact. US has threatened tariffs on Denmark over Greenland sovereignty; Danish exporters face headline risk. Pharma is largely excluded from tariff scope and NVO's US manufacturing insulates it.
- *Probability:* Low (~10–15%). *Impact:* Low-Medium (FX/sovereign sentiment; no direct revenue loss expected).

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **GLP-1 product-liability MDL (biggest legal exposure — High severity):** Novo Nordisk (with Eli Lilly) faces thousands of consolidated federal lawsuits (In re: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists Product Liability Litigation, E.D. Pa.) alleging gastroparesis/"stomach paralysis," ileus and bowel obstruction from Ozempic/Wegovy; discovery ongoing through 2026 with continued new filings. Adverse outcomes could produce multi-billion-dollar liabilities; defense rests on adequate labelling/safety data.
- **Medicare IRA negotiation (above)** — the defining regulatory event this decade for NVO.
- **FDA manufacturing/regulatory actions:** FDA extended review of **denecimig** (hemophilia A BLA) due to ongoing facility remediation (Oct 2026) — a regulatory delay red flag at a manufacturing site, echoing well-documented cGMP/inspection concerns; Wegovy oral subset data also under rolling review.
- **UK marketing/compliance sanctions:** ABPI suspended Novo's membership for two years and the PMCPA publicly reprimanded NVO over **undisclosed payments and digital marketing of obesity drugs** (2024) — reputational and compliance-watchdog exposure in a major European market.
- **Intellectual property / exclusivity:** semaglutide exclusivity losses in Brazil, Canada, China, India (2025–2026 window) open the door to biosimilar erosion — the structural growth deceleration driver behind FY26 guidance.
- **Congressional/political scrutiny:** Senate HELP Committee hearings on US GLP-1 pricing (CEO testimony in 2024); ongoing legislative noise around obesity-drug pricing, Medicaid MFN models.
- *Not identified:* Active DOJ/FTC enforcement actions, nationalization, or expropriation risk.

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **UK ABPI suspension + undisclosed-payments reprimand (2024)** — direct evidence of marketing-compliance failures; elevates FCPA/ABAC and promotional-compliance monitoring burden in other markets.
2. **Continued Russia insulin supply** — legally permissible (medicines carve-outs from sanctions) and humanitarian, but Novo is now a conspicuous outlier among Western pharma; carries reputational/secondary-sanctions optics risk under an escalating sanctions regime.
3. **Obesity-drug promotional scrutiny** — aggressive DTC/digital marketing of Wegovy attracts regulator attention (UK, US Senate, EU) and feeds the product-liability narrative.
4. **China operations** — standard anti-bribery/U.S.-China compliance surface; watch for local commercial-bribery enforcement as VBP price pressure squeezes sales-channel incentives.
5. **Manufacturing-site GMP issues** tied to the denecimig BLA delay — quality-compliance risk with FDA/EMA, not a financial-crime issue, but a compliance outlier nonetheless.

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale:** Country/political risk itself is **low** (Denmark is AAA, rule-of-law stable, no expropriation risk; US political stability high). The **High** rating is driven almost entirely by *regulatory-pricing and legal* risk concentrated in NVO's #1 market: (a) the **locked-in 2027 Medicare price cuts** on the core Ozempic/Wegovy franchise in a ~60%-of-sales US market, (b) the **rapidly growing GLP-1 product-liability MDL** with open-ended liability, and (c) **China VBP/competition plus global patent cliffs** compressing growth. These are multi-year, high-conviction overhangs interacting with geopolitical tail risks (US–China, US–Denmark tariffs).
