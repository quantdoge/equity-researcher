## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

**Resolved / legacy (low forward risk):**
- **1MDB (2020–22):** ~$5.5B in global penalties (~$2.9B to US/global authorities, ~$3.9B to Malaysia); monitorship completed. Resolved but remains the firm's defining regulatory scar for credibility purposes.

**Active / watch items:**
- **SEC (and reportedly DOJ) inquiry into alternative-investments/private-asset valuations** (Petershill and related funds) — GS has disclosed subpoenas/information requests in filings. **Open and material to the Asset & Wealth Management franchise**; outcome uncertain.
- **CFPB consent order (Oct 2024, ~$89.8M)** — Apple Card and credit-card account-management failures (Reg Z/TILA, UDAAP). Remediation and reporting obligations remain **active compliance obligations** through the order period.
- **Consumer/credit-card DOJ probe:** reported to have wound down in 2025 without action in the press, but this was not independently verified here — treat as "likely closed, monitor."
- **Basel III Endgame re-proposal (NPR, March 2026)** plus **G-SIB surcharge** review — GS sits in the top G-SIB bucket; materially higher capital floors are a regulatory-cost tail even under a softened re-proposal.
- **FDIC special assessment** for the 2023 bank failures was absorbed (multiple quarters, ~$470M early tranche) — now largely a historical charge.

**Observation:** The regulatory-actions search returned no new 2026 enforcement against GS. The open SEC alternatives inquiry is the item with genuine downside.
