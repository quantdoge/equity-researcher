**Investment Thesis**
Moderna delivered a genuine scientific milestone — INTerpath-001's positive Phase 3 in adjuvant melanoma makes it the first mover in individualized cancer vaccines — but the stock has priced that asset at roughly 4x consensus economics. At ~41–46x EV/sales against ~$2B of eroding revenue, −$2.1B FCF, a 50/50 Merck economics split, zero insider buying in 12 months, and a real-yield regime that mechanically punishes 15–20-year-duration assets, the market is capitalizing flawless execution through 2032 that no agent, analyst, or consensus model forecasts. This is a clean-accounting, deteriorating-economics, over-discounted-success story: a valuation short, not a fraud short — and the correct position size is zero.
