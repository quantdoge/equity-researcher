- Search interest and digital traffic confirm re-engagement rather than apathy.
- The bear narrative (NIM, class action, CEO transition, retail lag) is dated and largely priced.

**What would flip this:** a failure of NIM to inflect in Q3 (management's explicit promise), renewed institutional distribution, or a fresh legal/regulatory disclosure.
