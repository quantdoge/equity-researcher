**Investment Thesis**

Nextpower is the highest-quality franchise in solar balance-of-system — #1 global tracker share (~26–30%), 29% ROIC, net cash of ~$1.0B with zero funded debt, a record >$6B backlog (~1.4x FY27 revenue), and a 7/7 earnings-beat record — trading at 13.6x FY28E earnings after a 52% drawdown. The offset: margin compression is structural (GM 33.7% → 32.6% → 31.1% Q2 FY27; management's own FY30 EBITDA margin target sits ~250bp *below* FY26), roughly $396M annualized of 45X subsidy underpins ~68% of guided FY27 net income with a statutory phase-down, and the macro regime (10Y real yield 2.92% and rising) is actively hostile to its customers' project financing. At ~fair value with broken momentum, the stock offers optionality, not edge — own a starter, and let the January 26 print and April FY28 guide earn the rest.

**Bull Case**

- **Demand visibility is exceptional**: >$6B backlog (1.4x revenue), record bookings across eBOS/foundations/inverters, safe-harboured US pipeline converting through 2028, plus data-center power as an unmodeled adjacent TAM.
- **Fortress optionality**: $1.04B net cash + $1B revolver + 100% beat record + aggressive buybacks ($162M in Q2 FY27) mean downside is a de-rating toward ~$52, not impairment — while policy restoration post-midterms is a live upside tail.

**Bear Case**
- **Structural margin decay**: GM 35.9% → 31.1% in two quarters; FY30 target embeds ~21% EBITDA margin — *below* today. Growth is being bought with price and mix; FY28 is explicitly an "investment year."
- **Subsidy and policy dependence**: ~$396M/yr of 45X credits (~68% of guided FY27 net income) with statutory phase-down from 2030 and FEOC conditions; 77% US revenue into an OBBBA cliff that pulls demand into 2027 and leaves a 2028–29 air pocket; Section 232 stack compresses both margin and customer FIDs.
- **Macro duration risk**: 10Y real yield at 2.92% and rising directly raises customers' cost of capital; at beta ~2.5, a 10% market drawdown implies ~25% NXT downside — and the stock has already demonstrated −51%.

**Key Risks**
1. **Q3 FY27 print (Jan 26, 2027)**: guide implies ~10% revenue growth and high-teens H2 EBITDA margin — a miss confirms structural erosion; a GM ≥31% hold is a re-rating event. Binary either way.
2. **December 2026 tariff determinations** and 45X/FEOC rulemaking — direct gross-margin swing factor.
3. **2028+ US policy air pocket** as safe-harboured projects complete (Wood Mackenzie: 2026 installs flat despite record pipeline).
4. **Receivables/customer credit**: $1.3B AR from leveraged IPPs; a developer failure cycle hits cash conversion.
