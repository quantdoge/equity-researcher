**Bull Case**

- **Fortress economics**: $3.0B net cash (16% of cap), zero debt, ~40% FCF margin, Z-score 7.66 — the downside is bounded, the business self-funds, and a $1B buyback at $92 retires ~5% of shares.

**Bear Case**

- **Deceleration math**: 69% → 53% → ~40% guided; if FY27 lands nearer 20%, today's multiple is fair-to-full on normalized (~24x) earnings, not cheap.
- **Governance and legal drag compound**: ~70% insider voting control, serial insider selling, DSA VLOP deadline, ICO second-bite risk, and a *pattern* of traffic-disclosure litigation that keeps the stock in the penalty box.
