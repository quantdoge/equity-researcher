**Mechanical Q4 headwind:** The announced **logged-in-only experience** (tested internationally first) will *structurally* reduce logged-out web visits and pageviews. SimilarWeb-type panels will show another leg down in Q4/Q1 regardless of underlying engagement — a data-interpretation trap and a real ad-inventory headwind for unlogged impressions.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH**

Rationale: (a) the single highest-quality real-time demand proxy for this business — search/traffic interest — is in a **confirmed, still-worsening downtrend** with no inflection in the latest weeks; (b) the logged-in-only pivot **mechanically depresses** the very traffic metrics that panelled data will show in Q4/Q1, and it is a defensive response to structural AI-Overview disintermediation, not an offensive monetization choice; (c) beat magnitude and US DAU are both decaying; (d) restricted hiring signals no offsetting growth investment; (e) fresh legal-investigation overhang. The bull offsets are real but second-order — app retention +50% YoY, ARPU/monetization strength, data licensing — and cannot yet be seen in any alt-data series.

*Partially priced:* a -30% day discounts much of the Q3 miss. Our data does not support calling a bottom, but it also does not support fresh shorting at $91 on a 2.05-beta name post-capitulation — the risk/reward is a **"estimates still need to come down on users, revenue may hold"** setup.

### 7. Confidence Level: **Medium**
Strengths: Google Trends is a direct, high-frequency, longitudinal proxy for Reddit's actual demand driver (external search referrals), now corroborated verbatim by management. Price/volume and earnings-surprise data are hard data.

**Data limitations & lag:**
- Web traffic is a **SimilarWeb search proxy**, not the API — MoM figure only, no clean YoY or visit-count time series.
- Google Trends measures *external search interest*, not on-platform engagement or logged-in usage; it will **misread** the logged-in pivot (declining external interest could coexist with rising logged-in DAU). This is the key risk to our bearish call.
- Job postings and social sentiment are web-search scrapes with no historical baseline; patent API unavailable.
- No app-download panel (Sensor Tower/App Annie), no advertiser-spend panel, no satellite data. Peer alt-data (META/GOOGL ad-demand share shift, per LikeFolio commentary) corroborates RDDT share loss but was not independently verified.
- **Lag:** Trends data ends Oct 25, 2026 — pre-print and pre-logged-in-test-launch; the Q4 impact of gating is not yet in any series. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one.
