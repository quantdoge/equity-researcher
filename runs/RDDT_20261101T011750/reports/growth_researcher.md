# Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $91.73 | Market cap ~$18.5B | EV ~$15.5B (net cash $3.03B) | 52-wk range ~$89–$263**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Reddit is a one-of-one asset: 515M weekly uniques generating authentic human discourse that AI models cannot synthesize, monetized at only $6.87 ARPU — a fraction of Meta/Google levels — with 91.5% gross margins and 40%+ FCF margins. The company just compounded revenue 69% (FY25) and 53% (Q3'26) into a $3B+ run-rate business while expanding adjusted EBITDA margins to 45%. **But the thesis broke a leg on October 29, 2026**: management disclosed Google search-referral traffic fell "meaningfully" as AI Overviews absorbed clicks, guided Q4 to 38–42% growth (a 15-point deceleration), and announced a test requiring login to access content — effectively abandoning the open-web distribution model that built Reddit's audience. The stock lost 29% in a day (−60% YTD). This is now a bet on whether **per-user monetization can outrun a structurally impaired acquisition funnel.**

## 3. TAM Assessment

| Layer | Size | Reddit position |
|---|---|---|
| Global digital advertising | ~$700–800B | ~$3.4B revenue ≈ **0.45% share** |
| US digital ad | ~$300B | ~$2.9B US revenue ≈ ~1% |
| International ad (underpenetrated) | ~$400B | $176M/qtr — **20% of revenue** vs >50% of users |

The core bull case is not user growth — it's **ARPU convergence**. US ARPU dwarfs international, and international revenue grew 67% vs US 50%. Closing even half the international ARPU gap on a flat user base supports $8–10B of revenue. At 1.5% global ad share, Reddit is a $10–12B revenue company. **Penetration is de minimis; the question is whether the funnel survives.**

Counterpoint: AI Overviews attacks the *supply* side of that TAM — if Reddit loses logged-out search traffic, its user base (the asset being monetized) shrinks before ARPU can compound. The login-wall test is management's attempt to convert anonymous SEO traffic into owned, directly-addressable audience. It could work — logged-in and app DAUq already grew QoQ in both US and RoW — but it is unproven and cuts against 20 years of open-web positioning.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4'26 earnings — Feb 4, 2027.** Consensus EPS $1.81, revenue $1.025B vs guide of $1.00–1.03B. First hard read on holiday ad spend *and* early login-wall impact. Highest-impact event in the next 12 months.
- **Login-wall test readout (H1 2027).** If logged-in DAUq rises with stable total DAUq, the narrative re-rates hard; if DAUq falls further, the de-rating extends.
- **Q4 EBITDA guide of $480–510M (48–50% margin)** — if delivered, proves a durable 45%+ margin structure, supporting a higher FCF-based multiple.
- **S&P 500 inclusion** (announced Q3'26) — passive flow tailwind, partially already absorbed.
- **AI data-licensing expansion beyond Google** — the $60M+/yr licensing line (in "Other revenue," +35%) is call-option value; also potential remedy if AI firms must license training data.
- **International ad product rollout** — the largest multi-year compounding lever.

| PEG (fwd P/E ÷ growth) | ~0.6x | — | — |

For a company growing 38–42% with 45% EBITDA margins, net cash, and 90+ Rule of 40, **4.6x EV/revenue is a deep-value growth multiple** — the market is pricing in terminal decay of the funnel. Two caveats: (1) 2026 net income is flattered by a near-zero effective tax rate (Q3 net income $283M *exceeded* operating income $262M); normalizing to ~22% tax cuts forward EPS ~20%, putting 2027E P/E nearer ~17x. (2) The 2029–2030 consensus estimates (only 9 analysts, +7–10% growth) are stale and shouldn't be relied on; they imply a hard deceleration few would actually underwrite today.

## 7. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high-conviction, position-sized for volatility)

The fundamentals are elite and the multiple is undemanding. At ~$15.5B EV against $4.4B of 2027E revenue and $1B+ of FCF, you are paying roughly 3.5x forward sales for a 30% grower with net cash. The bear case is fully priced; the bull case (ARPU convergence + login-wall stabilization) is not. This is not a "Strong Buy" because the moat's distribution layer has genuinely been impaired by AI search — the durable-growth premise is under active test, and the next two quarters are a binary read.

**What would change the call:** Two consecutive quarters of stable/rising DAUq post-login-wall → upgrade to Strong Buy. DAUq declining double digits through mid-2027 → downgrade to Sell.

## 8. Key Risks

1. **Search dependency / AI Overviews (highest).** Google referral traffic declining "meaningfully" is a structural, not cyclical, threat. Reddit's 20-year growth engine was SEO; that channel is being closed by its largest traffic partner *and* largest data customer. Citizens' downgrade note ("the problem with selling content to Google") captures the strategic trap precisely.
2. **Login-wall execution risk.** Requiring authentication could accelerate DAUq shrinkage, damage community goodwill, and reduce the open-web corpus that feeds AI licensing revenue. Unproven at scale.
3. **Deceleration slope.** Q4 guide of 38–42% is a ~15-point step down in one quarter. If FY27 lands at 20% rather than 29%, the current multiple is not cheap.
4. **Tax normalization.** The 2026 EPS base is inflated by deferred-tax-asset release / valuation-allowance reversal; GAAP EPS growth will optically compress regardless of operations.
5. **Competition for ad dollars.** Meta and TikTok are deploying AI ad tools at far greater scale; Reddit's $6.87 ARPU remains a discount-rate problem as much as an opportunity.
6. **Dual-class control.** Advance Publications retains voting control; shareholder interests are not fully aligned, and the Q3 buyback at $149.81 avg was poor capital allocation.
7. **High beta (2.05)** — expect ±30% swings around print dates; size accordingly.
