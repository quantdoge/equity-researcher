## MACRO ENVIRONMENT ASSESSMENT — REDDIT, INC. (RDDT)
*As of data through Oct 30, 2026. All series: FRED.*

---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Transitional / Hawkish Late-Cycle**

The regime is **not** a classic risk-off: credit is wide open (HY OAS 326bp on Oct 29, near the tightest since 2007) and real GDP is running ~2–3.4% annualized. But for **long-duration, high-beta growth equities** the regime has turned hostile: **inflation re-accelerated to 3.7% YoY in September from 3.35% in August**, the Fed **held rates steady on Oct 28**, and the market has repriced the front end to price **tightening, not easing** — the 2Y at 4.61% sits **~98bp above** effective fed funds of 3.63–3.75%. The 10Y has backed up violently: **4.63% (Aug 5) → 5.31% (Oct 5) → 4.98% (Oct 29)**, a +68bp two-month bear move within a positively-sloped, bear-steepening curve (2s10s +37bp).

Critically, the selloff is **real-rate driven, not inflation-expectation driven**: the 10Y breakeven is contained at 2.34%, while the **10Y TIPS real yield rose from 2.41% to a peak of 2.95% and is still 2.63%**. That is the worst possible mix for a multiple-heavy equity — discount rates up without a commensurate lift in nominal growth expectations. Divergence between euphoric credit and a hawkish rates market is itself a late-cycle marker.

**Reddit-specific context:** RDDT is repricing for idiosyncratic/structural reasons (Q3 DAUq 130.3M vs 134M consensus; Google AI Overviews cutting search-referral traffic "down meaningfully"; Q4 guide $1.00–1.03B vs $1.05B; login-wall test) — **-29.2% on Oct 30 to $91.73, its worst day ever and -60% YTD**. Macro explains the *amplitude* of that move, not the trigger. The macro backdrop is a secondary, unfavourable force layered on a company-specific de-rating.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

The macro configuration is close to a worst-case for this specific equity's factor profile:

1. **Long-end real yields at cycle highs** compress exactly the multiple that carries 100% of Reddit's valuation.
2. **Inflation re-accelerating** at 3.72% forces the Fed to stay on hold / lean hawkish, capping any multiple recovery.
3. **Ad market growing ~5%** while Reddit decelerates from 53% means the growth premium must be re-earned quarter by quarter, with no cyclical tailwind.
4. **Beta 2.05** means every macro wobble is amplified ~2x.

**Two offsets keep this from being outright bearish:** (a) **no debt** — no refinancing or covenant risk, so Reddit survives a high-rate regime intact; (b) **credit spreads at 326bp and GDP at 3.4%** mean we are not entering recession, so the ad cycle has not broken. A macro-rating of "Unfavourable" here is a **duration/valuation** call, not a solvency or demand-collapse call. Note also that after **-65% from the $258.93 January peak**, forward valuation is now roughly **4.3x annualized Q4 guide revenue ($4.06B)** — much of the macro duration risk has already been marked.

### 5. The Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BULL (rates-led re-rating)**

**The setup:** The market reads September's 3.72% CPI as the peak. It is plausibly a **tariff-and-energy pass-through spike** rather than demand-pull inflation — core goods, not services. Watch the breakeven: it is *already* only 2.34%, meaning bond markets themselves do not believe 3.7% is durable. If CPI prints 3.1% and then 2.7% across Q4'26/Q1'27, the two forces that broke this stock reverse simultaneously:

- **Policy:** the 2Y's 98bp premium to EFFR inverts to price cuts; the Fed's next move becomes easing rather than a hike.
- **Discount rate:** the 10Y falls **5.0% → 4.2%** and the **10Y real yield 2.63% → 1.9%**.

**The mechanical payoff:** a ~70bp decline in the real discount rate applied to a 2.05-beta, terminal-value-heavy equity is worth a **+30–45% multiple re-rating before any earnings change**. Layer on the operating catalyst the company just announced — the **login-wall test**, which converts low-ARPU logged-out traffic into targetable logged-in users — and you get a **double-barrelled, macro-plus-micro re-rating**. In that world RDDT at $92 is not a broken growth story; it is a 4x-sales asset with 90%+ incremental margins inflecting on cheap money. That is a **$150–180 stock on macro alone**, and the surprise is that it happens in **one quarter**, not three years.

**The mirror-image bear surprise (state it plainly):** the Fed *hikes* into 2027 on a 4%+ CPI print, the 10Y goes to 5.75%, and the login-wall test suppresses DAUq. Then both multiple **and** estimates compress at beta 2.05 — a plausible **$60–65** path. Because a 60% drawdown has already occurred, the **asymmetry now favours the bull surprise**, but position sizing must respect that the tail is genuinely two-sided.

---

**Bottom line for the memo:** Reddit's October collapse was **company- and AI-disruption-specific, not a macro event** — easy credit (326bp), 3.4% SAAR growth and 4.2% unemployment all say the cycle is intact. But the macro environment **amplified** the damage through the real-yield/duration channel and will **continue to cap any recovery** until inflation breaks below 3%. The correct macro framing is: *no demand-side problem, no balance-sheet problem, a severe discount-rate problem, and a structural distribution problem wearing a macro costume.*
