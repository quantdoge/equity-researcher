## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**Risk 1 — Western age-assurance & minors'-safety regime (UK, EU, Australia, U.S. states)**
- *Probability: Near-certain (already materialising). Impact: Medium–High.*
- The UK ICO fined Reddit **£14.47m on 24 Feb 2026** for unlawfully processing under-13s' data (period May 2018–Jul 2025), despite Reddit's age-verification rollout from 8 Jul 2025 under the Online Safety Act; Reddit says it **will appeal** ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwyx0xggepjo)). The EU designated Reddit a **VLOP on 31 Aug 2026**, giving ~4 months to comply with the DSA's strictest obligations ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/31/eu-places-chatgpt-reddit-and-roblox-under-strictest-digital-safety-rules)). Australia's under-16 ban is being contested by Reddit in the High Court ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/australia-social-media-ban-reddit-court-lawsuit-5d0d55e4f5668f66a5a3eed8f841d1ed), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/reddit-files-lawsuit-against-australias-social-media-ban-2025-12-11/)).
- *Investment implication:* recurring compliance cost, product friction (login/age-gate walls reduce logged-out virality), and precedent risk for U.S. state statutes. Anonymity is Reddit's product DNA; age assurance is structurally in tension with it.

**Risk 2 — AI-scraping / data-licensing legal war and its antitrust blowback**
- *Probability: High. Impact: Medium (two-sided).*
- Reddit is plaintiff against **Anthropic** (contract/privacy; remanded to state court after Anthropic's federal removal), **Perplexity AI** (most of Perplexity's dismissal bid rejected, 31 Jul 2026, [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/perplexity-ai-loses-bid-toss-reddit-lawsuit-over-data-scraping-2026-07-31/)), and **SerpApi, Oxylabs, AWMProxy** (SDNY 25-cv-8736, filed 22 Oct 2025).
- **New adverse front:** on 28 Aug 2026 SerpApi answered with **Sherman Act §2 counterclaims** (trebled damages sought), alleging Reddit served a Google-permissive robots.txt to Google while blocking Bing, DuckDuckGo, Mojeek and Qwant from 1 Jul 2024 — six months before its "SearchGuard" control existed — and that Reddit cut off the Internet Archive in Aug 2025 ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/reddit-faces-antitrust-counterclaims-over-60m-google-only-crawler-deal/)). Reddit's DMCA counts against SerpApi have already been partly dismissed (three counts out, 31 Jul 2026).
- *Why it's geopolitical, not just legal:* the ~$60M/yr Google crawler/AI arrangement sits inside the live U.S. **DOJ v. Google** remedies and EU antitrust actions against Google — a change in Google's default-distribution or AI-summary behaviour transmits directly into Reddit's traffic and "Other revenue." Reddit reportedly may not renew the Google AI deal ([CNBC, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/reddit-stock-google-ai-content-deal.html)).

**Risk 3 — U.S. securities litigation overhang following the logged-in pivot**
- *Probability of amended/consolidated class action: High. Impact: Low–Medium (reputational/legal cost, not solvency).*
- Shares fell **~28–30% on 30 Oct 2026** (worst day since IPO) after weak Q4 guidance, "search traffic down meaningfully," and the announced shift to a primarily **logged-in experience** (tested in select non-U.S. markets from Q4'26), framed partly as a data-protection/anti-scraping measure ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/reddit-stock-q3-earnings.html), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/reddit-posts-search-traffic-declines-will-push-visitor-sign-ins-curb-data-2026-10-29/)).
- **Levi & Korsinsky and SBS Law have opened investigations** into whether investors were adequately warned of the access-change risks ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/reddit-investigation-notice-levi--korsinsky-notifies-investors-of-pending-investigation-into-reddit-rddt-302925172.html), [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261030543777/en/RDDT-Investors-Have-Opportunity-to-Join-Reddit-Inc.-Fraud-Investigation-with-SBS-Law/)). A prior securities class action already exists with a class period ending 15 Jun 2026 and a **lead-plaintiff deadline of 14 Dec 2026**. Materiality of the "logged-out traffic" disclosure is the crux — expect the D&O and disclosure-controls narrative to dominate 2027 filings.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **Active litigation (Reddit as plaintiff):** *Reddit v. Anthropic*; *Reddit v. Perplexity*; *Reddit v. SerpApi/Oxylabs/AWMProxy* — all data-misappropriation/DMCA-based; monetisation optionality if licensing precedent holds.
- **Active litigation (Reddit as defendant):** *SerpApi* antitrust counterclaims (Sherman §2, treble damages); *Reddit v. Australia* (offensive constitutional challenge); UK ICO appeal; U.S. securities class action + new investigations.
- **Regulators:** UK ICO (fined) and **Ofcom** (OSA enforcement — active against Telegram, 4chan precedent); **European Commission** (DSA VLOP since 31 Aug 2026; EU age-verification and child-safety framework); **U.S. FTC** (historic 6(b)-style inquiry into AI data licensing/sale disclosed pre-IPO, Mar 2024); U.S. state AGs and age-assurance statutes.
- **Disclosure note:** Reddit's most recent 10-K is only available as a PDF on EDGAR and returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval; the FY25/Q3'26 financial detail above is sourced from the IR press releases and the 8-K/10-Q certification. I could not independently verify the exact litigation accrual language in the FY2025 risk factors — treat the contingency-quantification gap as an open item.

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **Data privacy (highest severity, confirmed):** adverse final regulatory finding in the UK with a monetary penalty and an unpersuaded regulator ("must do better"); ICO explicitly said it is still assessing the post-Jul-2025 age-assurance controls — i.e., **tail risk of a second tranche of enforcement**. Read-across to GDPR Art. 8 / EU DSA VLOP audits is direct.
2. **Antitrust / self-preferencing optics:** the SerpApi allegation that Reddit ran a **dual robots.txt** (Google-permissive, others-blocked) tied to a ~$60M Google contract is a reputational and legal escalation risk — it invites regulator and press framing of Reddit as gatekeeper, not victim, and parallels the Microsoft/Bing exclusion narrative that Brussels and DOJ have found useful in other matters.
3. **Minors' safety & anonymity conflict:** engineering a "logged-in only" wall reduces scraping but raises the registration funnel — precisely the vector the ICO penalised. Product and compliance objectives are in direct tension here.
4. **FCPA / AML / sanctions: Low.** No physical operations, no state-owned counterparties, no sanctioned-market revenue (Russia/China/Iran effectively blocked). Advertising and app-store payment flows create only standard counterparty/KYC exposure.
5. **Governance (adjacent red flag):** controlled-company structure — Advance Publications plus CEO Steve Huffman reportedly hold a large majority of voting power (Huffman cited at ~70%+ in secondary sources). Minority holders have limited ability to force governance or compliance change, and the dual-class structure is a distraction in securities-litigation optics.
6. **Revenue concentration / single-counterparty dependency:** heavy traffic dependence on Google Search/AI summarisation — a private commercial relationship now entangled in public antitrust remedies in two jurisdictions.

## 5. Overall Rating

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Geopolitical risk (cross-border, sanctions, expropriation, FX) | **Low** |
| Legal & regulatory risk | **High** |
| **Composite geopolitical/legal risk** | **Medium-High** |

**Rationale:** Reddit has essentially no geopolitical exposure in the classic sense — 81% U.S. revenue, no sanctioned or expropriation-prone assets, no FX or repatriation constraints, and a $2.48B cash pile (FY25) in USD. The risk is almost entirely **regulatory and litigation**: a confirmed adverse UK privacy finding under appeal, fresh VLOP obligations with a Q4'26 compliance deadline, an Australian statutory ban being litigated, and a docket where Reddit is simultaneously plaintiff and antitrust defendant. None of these individually threatens solvency; collectively they represent a persistent, compounding drag on product velocity (anonymity → age gates → login walls), on operating cost, and on the reliability of the traffic-to-revenue funnel that the Oct 2026 de-rating was all about. Watch items for the next 2–4 quarters: (i) the ICO appeal and any second enforcement tranche; (ii) DSA VLOP compliance filing / first Commission information request; (iii) outcome of SerpApi's §2 counterclaims (a motion-to-dismiss ruling would be a discrete catalyst); (iv) the consolidated securities class action and whether "logged-out traffic" disclosures are found wanting; (v) any change in Google default-distribution or AI-summary behaviour flowing from DOJ/EU remedies.
