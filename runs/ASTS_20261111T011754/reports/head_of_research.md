### Investment Memo: ASTS — 11 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL — do not initiate; exit existing positions | Conviction: **High on direction, Low on timing**
**Price Target**: $14 (~58% downside from current $33.69) — probability-weighted intrinsic value $12–15
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
AST SpaceMobile has real technology, a genuine FCC de-risking event, and the deepest carrier roster in direct-to-device — but at $33.69 the equity is priced *above* its own bull case (probability-weighted IV $12–15; even the 25–30% bull scenario yields only $25–28/share). The company must execute ~45 satellite launches it doesn't control, convert contracts into *collected* revenue (DSO: 224 days), and fund ~$1.5–1.8B/yr of burn through repeatedly dilutive capital raises — all in the worst macro configuration possible for a long-duration asset (10Y real yield ~2.9% and rising), while its largest competitor is also its launch provider. Eleven of eleven specialist agents independently reach a non-buy conclusion; the consensus is as clean as this process produces.

**Bull Case** *(stated fairly — all conditional)*
- **Distribution is contracted, not hypothetical**: 45+ MNOs / ~3B subscribers, zero CAC, carrier billing and spectrum — a structural advantage almost no frontier company has. Gross margin already inflecting (40.2% Q3'26).
- **Real de-risking has occurred**: full FCC commercial authorization (248 satellites) + FirstNet SCS acceptance; ~$2.8–3.2B cash gives ~20+ months runway; US government/defence revenue is the least cyclical leg.
- **Double re-rating optionality**: if launch cadence delivers 45 BlueBirds by mid-2027 (continuous coverage = carrier payments switch on) *and* a Q1-2027 growth scare compresses real yields 70–90bp (~25% probability per Macro), a move toward $60–80 is plausible. A Tier-1 carrier strategic investment or take-or-pay would remove financing risk overnight.

**Bear Case**
- **Priced above perfection**: probability-weighted IV $12–15; even the bull case ($25–28) sits below spot. To justify $33.69 you must assign ~65–70% probability to the bull case *and* assume no further large dilution — in a company that has issued 5.5x its share count and needs billions more.
- **Execution and earnings quality are broken**: 0/6 beats, −81% average surprise, two timeline slips, a lost satellite with a $96M uninsured loss, 224-day DSO, and irreconcilable gross margins. ~12–13 satellites in orbit vs. 45 needed by mid-2027 implies a launch cadence several times anything ever achieved — on rockets ASTS doesn't control.
- **Structural competitive and governance asymmetry**: SpaceX — competitor, price-setter, *and* launch supplier — is FCC-authorized for 15,000 D2C satellites (60x AST's grant) and self-funds. Meanwhile insiders sold 560:1, the CEO pre-sold $147M near the top, and minority holders have no recourse under 72% founder control.

**Key Risks** *(to the SELL call — i.e., upside risks; and thesis-invalidation triggers)*
1. Tier-1 carrier strategic equity / take-or-pay at a premium → financing risk vanishes (Value's stated risk to its own call).
2. Squeeze risk is severe: 57.7% insider ownership, beta ~3.6, retail-heavy float, $43–65 sell-side targets. **This is a reason to abstain, not to short.**
3. Downside triggers confirming the thesis: Q4 revenue <$61.4M (guidance cut), a third timeline slip, another ≥$1B raise at a discount, DSO >200 days for two more quarters, a Verizon/AT&T exclusive with Starlink.
4. Data gaps: market cap ambiguity ($8.6B basic vs. $13.8B fully diluted) distorts every multiple; SEC EDGAR was inaccessible to all agents (10-K risk factors unverified); FY27 consensus is thin and internally inconsistent. None plausibly flips an 11-for-11 consensus.
