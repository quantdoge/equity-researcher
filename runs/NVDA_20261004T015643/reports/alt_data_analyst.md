### Data Limitations & Lag
- Google Trends is relative interest vs. a 12-month baseline; weekly series lags days and is heavily news-cycle driven — treat the consumer cooling as sentiment decay, not a revenue forecast.
- Job-posting counts are a one-point web-search proxy, not a Thinknum/LinkedIn time series — no MoM acceleration/deceleration can be computed.
- No satellite/geospatial or retail-traffic overlay applies here (NVDA is a fabless designer; the actionable physical signals would be TSMC/co-packaged-memory supply, outside scope).
- The $1T pipeline figure comes from management communications and third-party paraphrases, not audited statements — verify against 10-Q backlog disclosures.
- **Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one:** it favors NVDA's setup into the Nov-18 print, but the stock at ~$5.7T cap already embeds high AI-exposure expectations.
