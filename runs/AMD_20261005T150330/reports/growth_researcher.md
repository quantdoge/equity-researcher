# AMD (Advanced Micro Devices) — Growth Equity Research Memo
**Analyst:** Equity Growth Research | **Date:** Oct 2026 | **Price:** ~$630 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.03T

## 1. Growth Story
AMD has completed the transition from x86 share-taker to the number-two AI compute franchise, with data center revenue compounding at triple digits (~+107% YoY in Q2'26 to $6.7B in a single quarter) and server CPUs growing >80%. The disruptive thesis is a "dual-engine" AI capture: Instinct MI-series accelerators now on annual silicon cadence (MI350 → MI450 → MI400/CDNA Next) plus EPYC server CPUs riding the same hyperscaler AI buildout — giving AMD participation in the fastest-growing line item in enterprise capex without needing to beat NVIDIA on absolute performance, only on price/performance, supply, and open-ecosystem terms.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration
- **AI accelerator TAM:** gen-AI datacenter accelerator spend projected ~$400–500B+/yr by 2030. AMD share today is ~10–15% vs NVIDIA's ~70–85% — even a few points of share gain at these spend levels is a $10s-of-billions revenue swing. AMD's own DC run-rate already implies >$25B annualized (Q2'26 = $6.7B).
- **Server CPU:** ~$60–80B TAM; AMD at low-20s% unit share and climbing (+80% server CPU growth), with Zen 6 ("Venice") and 3rd-gen EPYC ramps extending the share-compounding story — Intel (589B mkt cap) is a fading competitor.
- **Penetration gap:** datacenter GPUs went ~$1B → $4.5B → (est.) $20B+ from 2023→2026. This is a market-penetration phase, not a maturation phase: MI-class products have run-rate upside for at least 3–4 more cadences.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **Buy** (bordering Strong Buy)
The growth machinery is elite: 28.8% 5-yr revenue CAGR accelerating to +50% YoY, expanding gross margins, 100% beat rate, +42% estimate revisions, and a wall of catalysts. Valuation is the constraint — trailing EV/EBITDA of ~141x and trailing P/E of ~238x price in the full AI story. But against FY27E consensus EPS of $15.89, forward P/E is ~40x, **below NVIDIA's 48x**, while AMD's growth run-rate (DC +107%) is comparable or faster. If MI400 bookings land ahead of estimates, this is analytically a Strong Buy; at spot ~$630 with targets clustered $640–720, we hold at Buy and add on strength.

## 7. Key Risks
- **Valuation/perfection risk:** market is discounting a ~47% 5-yr CAGR to FY30 ($240B median); the estimate range is bimodal ($183–306B) — a hyperscaler order pause or share-share loss would compress the multiple violently (beta 2.48).
- **NVIDIA + custom silicon squeeze:** NVIDIA (5.8T mkt cap, 48x P/E) ships Rubin-class silicon in 2027–28 with an unassailable CUDA moat; meanwhile Broadcom/Google TPU/Amazon Trainium custom ASICs attack the same hyperscaler budget from below.
- **Software execution:** ROCm remains the perennial laggard vs CUDA; MLPerf wins help but enterprise AI teams still default to CUDA — AMD's price/perf edge must widen to convert.
- **Customer concentration & lumpiness:** revenue is hyperscaler-driven (Microsoft, Meta, Oracle-class orders); single-customer timing swings can whipsaw quarterly prints.
- **Supply-chain dependence:** TSMC (a 2.5T peer) allocation and HBM supply constrain upside; AMD is perennially capacity-second behind NVIDIA.
- **Margin mix:** at 49.5% gross margin, AMD is structurally ~25pts behind NVIDIA — AI growth at scale will pressure margins before operating leverage fully offsets it.

*Peer context: NVDA P/E 48x / EV-EBITDA ~40x; MU P/E 14x; gap noted: AMD's forward multiples are defensible, trailing multiples are not.*
