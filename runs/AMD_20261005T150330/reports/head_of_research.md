### Investment Memo: AMD — October 6, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (no new capital; existing holders trim ~50%) | Conviction: Medium-High
**Price Target**: Fair-value convergence ~$400 (−36% from current ~$630; scenario range $350–$450); upside scenario to ~$700 only if FY27 consensus validates
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
AMD is an elite operator and the structural #2 in AI compute — re-accelerating revenue (+34% FY25; Data Center +107% YoY to $6.7B in Q2'26), a 7/7 quarterly beat streak, and contractually anchored demand (Meta 6GW MI450, OpenAI/Meta warrants, $45B custom design wins). But at ~$630 the market capitalizes ~$1.03T — roughly **2x intrinsic value** ($210–330 fair-value band), 238x trailing P/E, 0.65% FCF yield — on the premise of a ~47% five-year CAGR and a 30%+ net margin AMD has never printed. **The business is a Buy; the price is a Sell.** Sell into strength, hold at most a small core, and re-enter only on multiple compression.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Favourable macro (Fed easing to 3.75%, ~2.2–2.5% GDP, no curve inversion) atop an early-to-mid-growth AI capex supercycle; accelerator lead times (20–30 wks) and HBM constraints support pricing into 2026–27. Caveats: 10Y at ~5.0–5.2% (cycle highs) is a live de-rating risk for a beta-2.5/3.2, max-duration asset; AMD's growth matches sector median (+34%) but GM 49.5% and OM 10.7% run ~10–29pts below NVDA/AVGO/TSM — share converted to revenue, not yet to leader-class profit.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH risk, already monetized.** Export controls cost ~$440–800M net in 2025; new MI325 rules (Jan 2026) with management guiding near-zero China AI revenue past Q1'26 — and China is 22% of revenue ($7.8B). Taiwan single-point manufacturing (all leading-edge wafers via TSMC) plus Beijing Anti-Sanctions-Law retaliation risk. Litigation (Adeia patent, Llano class action) manageable; BIS compliance red flags (diversion allegations, distributor due-diligence) elevated.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **SELL.** Narrow moat (widening), financial quality 6.5/10, management 9/10 — but valuation embeds a decade of flawless execution. Intrinsic $210–330/share (reverse + scenario DCFs); P/E 238x vs 44.7x peer median (432% premium), EV/EBITDA 141x, P/FCF 153x, FCF yield 0.65%. Implied market pricing: ~$75–80B FCF by year 10 — a hoped-for outcome, not a base case. Zero margin of safety.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **BUY.** Elite machinery: 28.8% 5-yr revenue CAGR accelerating to ~+50%; 100% beat rate (7 qtrs); FY30 EPS consensus +42% in 3 months; MI450/MI400 annual cadence with penetration headroom (~10–15% accelerator share vs NVDA 70–85%). Forward optics defensible (~40x FY27 EPS vs NVDA 48x) — but only if the consensus is accepted, and the consensus *is* the bull case.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **SELL (leaning).** Bottom-decile Value and Low-Vol factors (71% ann. vol, beta ~3.2), momentum rolling over (−10.3% 12-1m despite +171% tape), quality neutral; only Earnings Revision is Strong Buy (+42%, but n=9 and 5-yr out). Factor crowding high — AMD–INTC 0.69, AMD–TSM 0.65 — a crowded copy of the semi complex with no differentiated edge.
- **Financial Risk**: **Moderate** — and split down the middle. Credit risk LOW (Moody's A1, D/E 0.07x, net cash $10.6B vs $4.5B debt, 28x interest coverage, Altman Z 3.74, Piotroski 8/9); a −20% revenue stress compresses EBIT to breakeven but never threatens solvency. Market risk HIGH: 0.65% FCF yield, 71% vol, 3x drawdown beta. This is a growth-risk story, not a credit-risk story.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **Moderate.** Strong governance (CEO/Chair split, refreshed board, clean enforcement record) against structural exposures: sustained one-way 10b5-1 insider selling at all-time-highs (CEO >$100M scheduled sales, zero buys — sentiment overhang, not fraud signal), $8.2B World Labs integration, key-person reliance on Lisa Su, TSMC single-point.
- **ESG**: Neutral (leader on E, nicked on G). Strong climate management (24.5% absolute emissions cut, SBTi targets, 30x efficiency goal = procurement tailwind) vs. the $800M export-control-governance lapse and up to ~16% customer-warrant dilution optics (OpenAI/Meta). Not the swing variable; Taiwan physical/geopolitical concentration is the live ESG-linked tail.
- **Alternative Data**: **Bullish but late in the repricing.** 7/7 beats (magnitudes compressing: +15.9% → +6.2% → +2.5%), Meta 6GW + $45B custom wins, news sentiment +1.00 with zero negative articles in 14 days — a crowding tell. Consumer search flat/off its hype peak; price now far above even freshly-raised sell-side targets. Next test: Nov 3 Q3'26 print.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Tactical SHORT — not fraud, price.** All forensic screens clean (Beneish −2.76, accruals −4.4%, Piotroski 8) — but six years of revenue compounding produced ~zero diluted EPS growth ($2.57 FY21 → $2.65 FY25); inventory at 165 days (+56% since 2022); GAAP GM collapsed to 39.8% with negative operating income in the Q2'25 ramp; margins implied by 2030 consensus (30.5% net) have no historical support. Honest mirror: another +50% revenue quarter with expanding non-GAAP margins could squeeze shorts to $800+.

**Bull Case**
- Elite compounding: Data Center +107% YoY, 7/7 beat streak, +42% FY30 EPS revisions in 3 months; forward P/E ~40x FY27 sits *below* NVDA's 48x with comparable-or-faster growth.
- Contractually anchored demand: Meta 6GW (MI450 named in first gigawatt), OpenAI/Meta warrants, $45B custom wins, Oracle 50k-GPU; supply-constrained (20–30wk lead times) AI accelerator market supports pricing power into 2026–27.
- Structural share shifts + pristine balance sheet: ~34.5% server CPU share (vs 3.2% in 2018) funds the AI transition; net cash, A1 credit, Piotroski 8 — downside risk is multiple, not balance sheet.

**Bear Case**
- Valuation pre-pays success: ~2x intrinsic value; 238x trailing P/E vs 44.7x peer median; 0.65% FCF yield; ~$240B FY30 revenue consensus requires a 47% CAGR and a 30.5% net margin never achieved — the 2x spread in FY30 EPS estimates ($31–61) is the market's own admission of fragility.
- Structural #2 economics: AMD buys accelerator share with price (GM ~49.5% vs NVDA 71%; Q2'25 GAAP GM 39.8% / negative OI); hyperscaler custom ASICs (TPU, Trainium, Maia) attack the "AI #2" opportunity from below; forward-comparing requires accepting a consensus that is itself the bull case.
- Correlated tails on a high-beta name: export controls already clipped ~22% of revenue (China) with zero AI revenue guided past Q1'26; TSMC/Taiwan single-point supply; beta ~3.2 means a sector de-rate or a long-end rate shock (10Y >5.5%) hits this stock ~3x the benchmark's move.

**Key Risks**
- Hyperscaler capex digestion cycle (2023-style inventory correction) — would compress both earnings and the multiple; watch 165-day inventory drawdown + GAAP GM <45%.
- MI450/MI400 slip or NVIDIA's annual-cadence leapfrog (CUDA moat intact).
- Export-control expansion walling off the full $7.8B China line with no license offset.
- Long-end rates >5.5% (macro agent's flagged bear scenario) de-rating the highest-duration AI asset.
- Ratchet of per-share leaks at the margin: ~16% potential warrant dilution, SBC ≈ 38% of net income, $41.8B goodwill (54% of assets) impairment optics.

Momentum is the swing factor — 12-1 momentum is already negative (−10.3%), and the beat streak is compressing.

None of these gaps change the verdict, which is driven by price, not filings.

**Bottom line**: This is a wonderful company at a price that guarantees poor expected returns for a new buyer. The four independent price-driven sell signals (Value, Quant, Short, Fin-Risk market view) plus sector-level valuation caution outweigh the undisputed quality of operations. Sell into strength; re-enter only on a ≥30% drawdown or demonstrable margin-quality breakout (≥$50B revenue at 30%+ FCF margins) with multiple compression.
