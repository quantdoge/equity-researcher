### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (slight negative near-term tilt)

**Bullish evidence:** Generics are **expressly excluded from Section 232 tariffs** (patented drugs face up to 100%; generics duty-free through Aug 1, 2028) — a genuine structural advantage over branded-peer exposure, and a durable one. Branded demand proxies show zero erosion. 5/5 revenue beats, raised guidance, soft Q4 bar, rising price targets. Search data shows **no retail froth** in a stock up 48% off its low.

**Bearish evidence:** Search interest for both the company and the stock is at 12-month lows (-30%/-40% YoY, -71% from the April policy peak) — the "story" is losing mindshare just as the equity re-rates. The market repriced key-man/execution risk in the R&D leadership exit (-8.7% on 2.2x volume), and "legal overhang" is resurfacing. Coverage is thin and event-driven.

**The signal that matters most:** the -8.7% reaction tells you the market is now pricing Teva as an *execution-dependent* story where the innovative-medicines transition is the entire thesis. Alt data confirms underlying volume demand is intact — but alt data cannot see prescription trends, which is where that thesis actually lives.

### 7. Confidence Level: **LOW–MEDIUM**

**Reasons for low confidence:**
- **Wrong instrument class for the toolkit.** Google Trends/web traffic measure *consumer search*, but Teva's revenue is driven by *prescriptions written by physicians and reimbursed by payers*. There is no validated mapping from search interest to TRx for branded CNS/respiratory drugs. AUSTEDO/AJOVY/UZEDY flatness is mildly reassuring but not predictive.
- **Tool failures:** patent filings (2 attempts) and social sentiment both returned nothing usable. Job postings have no baseline.
- **Sentiment score is unreliable** — the naive keyword model scored a -8.7% week as "positive."
- **Lag:** Google Trends final print is **Nov 8** (12 days stale) and does not capture the Nov 19 selloff at all; web traffic is monthly. The most important recent event is entirely outside the alt-data window.
- **Thin coverage** (3 articles/7 days) limits news-sentiment statistical validity.

**What would change the call:** (a) confirmation in early January that Q4 consensus revenue stays at ~$4.27B against a raised full-year range — that makes "Above" the higher-conviction read; (b) any R&D successor announcement, which would remove the key-man discount; (c) resolution of the legal overhang; (d) a re-acceleration in "Teva stock" search interest into the January print, which would signal returning retail flow.
