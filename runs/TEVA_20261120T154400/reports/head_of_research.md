### Investment Memo: TEVA — 21 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $41 (~+5% from $39.23), hard re-decision on 27 Jan 2027
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Teva's turnaround is real — gross margin up ~500bp since 2020 to 51.8%, investment grade regained from all three agencies, innovative brands growing +33% — but it is also *largely priced* after a +91% twelve-month re-rating. The equity now sits between a dated, quantified headwind (AUSTEDO's 38% Medicare price cut, effective 1 Jan 2027, on ~15% of revenue) and a consensus 2027 bridge that assumes +30% EBITDA growth straight through it. That bridge may prove right — management has beaten in 6 of 7 quarters — but with free cash flow down ~80% year-to-date, leverage exactly at the rating threshold, and zero insider buying against ~$60M of selling, the risk/reward at $39 no longer justifies new capital ahead of the January guidance print. Hold existing positions; do not add.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro is a mild drag with no tailwind — 10Y at 5.13% and bear-steepening, CPI re-accelerating to 3.8%, USD +3.9% since September (translation hit lands Q4'26/Q1'27); the 2025 refinancing tailwind has been forfeited, though refinancing *risk* is retired. Sector rated Neutral (deflationary core, hostile buyer structure, but tariff/IRA carve-outs and an LOE/GLP-1 volume runway). Teva is **strong within the cohort**: gross margin 51.8% vs 35.1% peer median, the only consistently profitable scale name — but 9M26 revenue −0.2% while Viatris grew +4.0% and Amneal +8.0%. Teva is winning on margin, not volume.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium-High. AUSTEDO MFP of $4,093 (−38%) from 1 Jan 2027; a signed MFN pricing agreement makes future launch pricing partly a political variable. Serial antitrust record (DoJ $225M criminal penalty, EC €462.6M fine under appeal, Turkey probe). The multistate price-fixing settlement (16 Nov 2026) removed a major overhang **but terms are undisclosed** — reserve adequacy unverified. Opioid ($4.25B) largely provisioned. Israel-domicile tail risk is live but has not impaired operations.

- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (not strong short). The accounting is clean (Beneish −2.35, negative accruals, 117% cash conversion) — this is not a fraud case. The bear case is a *dated valuation arbitrage*: pay ~48x trailing FCF for a shrinking-revenue company whose #1 profit pool takes a 38% price cut in six weeks, against a consensus 2027 EBITDA bridge requiring +30% growth. Downside scenario ~$24.60 (−37%). Short interest only 2.4% — genuinely non-consensus. Invalidation triggers: 2027 revenue guide >$17.0B, AUSTEDO volume offsetting MFP, FY26 FCF >$1.2B.

**Bull Case**
- The margin transformation is proven, not narrative: 51.8% gross margin (+1,670bp vs peers), innovative brands +33%, and WELTRUZA adds a defensible $1.5–2.5B peak asset — at 12.5x 2027E earnings with a ~19% EPS CAGR, delivery alone gets the stock to the ~$48–49 Street consensus (+24%).
- The balance sheet is repaired: IG from Fitch/Moody's/S&P, refi wall cleared, net debt falling — the existential 2023–25 refinancing risk is gone, and reaching 2.0x leverage unlocks a capital-return catalyst.
- Policy is a relative shield: generics/biosimilars are expressly carved out of Section 232 tariffs and IRA negotiation; management has beaten in 6 of 7 quarters with raised FY26 guidance and a soft Q4 bar.

**Bear Case**
- A dated, quantified profit-pool cut: AUSTEDO's MFP (−38%, ~$500M+ gross revenue impact before offsets) lands 1 Jan 2027 — the same year consensus models +30% EBITDA growth. That bridge requires flawless simultaneous cost extraction and mix offset.
- Cash generation is deteriorating faster than the story admits: 9M26 FCF $220M, Q3 FCF −48% YoY, current ratio 0.95, DSO +16% — the GAAP/adjusted chasm (~$1.6B in 2025) demands faith in management's bridge.
- The people with the best information are selling: universal insider distribution at the highs, a key-man exit post-R&D Day, an undisclosed multistate settlement figure, and $19B of goodwill/intangibles (48% of assets) still available for impairment.

**Key Risks**
1. **27 Jan 2027 guidance miss** — a 2027 revenue guide below ~$16.8B or AUSTEDO commentary implying volume won't offset MFP compresses the multiple toward generics peers (−25–35%).
2. **FCF/liquidity squeeze** — working capital already negative; any legal settlement surprise or weak Q4 cash print revives the balance-sheet narrative.
3. **MFN/IRA extension** beyond AUSTEDO to AJOVY/UZEDY would cap the entire growth engine.
4. **Legal/governance** — the undisclosed multistate figure, EC appeal outcome, and the unnamed R&D successor.
5. **Macro term-premium shock** — 10Y to 5.6–5.75% de-rates a terminal-value-heavy franchise and raises refinancing costs on remaining maturities.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
Size TEVA on its volatility (37–44% realised), not its beta (0.76–0.84): a 2% NAV position carries the drawdown contribution of roughly a 3% position in an average healthcare name, and beta-based risk models will understate tail exposure (max drawdown −43.7% over 3Y). **Existing holders: hold** — the momentum, execution record, and WELTRUZA ramp argue against selling into the January print. **New money: wait.** The central contested number resolves on 27 Jan 2027; paying up for confirmation is cheaper than pre-paying for a bridge that insiders themselves are not buying. **Upgrade triggers** (to BUY): 2027 revenue guidance ≥$17.0B, FY26 FCF ≥$1.2B, a credible named R&D successor, a disclosed multistate figure within existing provisions. **Downgrade triggers** (to SELL): revenue guide <$16.8B, FCF <$1.0B, any new material legal charge or impairment, or a break of the $30–31 breakout shelf on negative news. Short interest of 2.4% means no squeeze fuel either way — this position will be decided by the January print, not by positioning.
