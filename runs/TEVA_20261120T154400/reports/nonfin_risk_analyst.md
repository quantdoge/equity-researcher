# TEVA — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**Teva Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. | Healthcare / Specialty & Generic Drugs | HQ Tel Aviv, Israel (NYSE/TASE) | ~$45.9B mkt cap | CEO: Richard D. Francis**

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE** (structure strong, execution turnover concerning)

**Evidence — structural strengths:**
- 11-member board, **10 independent directors**; CEO Richard Francis is the sole executive director.
- **Independent Chairman** (Dr. Sol J. Barer) — no CEO/Chairman duality, a genuine positive versus peers.
- Independent directors carry substantive industry/regulatory depth (Dr. Tal Zaks, former Moderna CMO; Dr. David Brailer, health IT; Dr. Perry Nisen; Chen Lichtenstein; Gerald Lieberman; Roberto Mignone). Board appears to have refreshed substantially since the 2017–2019 crises.
- Dual-listed (NYSE/TASE), files 10-Q and US-style proxy materials alongside Israeli corporate law; Human Resources & Compensation Committee oversees equity plans.

**Evidence — deterioration signals:**
- **"Interim" Chief Legal Officer** (Brian Savage, Form 4s Sept 2026) implies an unresolved CLO vacancy — during the company's most litigation-intensive period in a decade. This is a material control gap, not a cosmetic one.
- **Executive churn at the worst moment:** R&D Chief & CMO Eric Hughes departed Nov 18, 2026 — *one week after* the high-profile R&D Day that staked the equity story on his pipeline.
- Board approved an **+8.5M share increase to the ESPP** (July 28, 2026, extended to 2036) — modest but persistent dilution layered on heavy RSU grants.
- Compensation is equity-heavy; stock is +100% YoY, which mechanically inflates both executive pay and insider sale volumes.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Manufacturing & quality concentration in Israel.** Four Israeli production sites including the **Neot Hovav API (TAPI) plant**, which experienced a missile-related incident nearby (Teva stated no operational impact) — a live demonstration of physical-concentration risk that few global pharma peers carry. Simultaneously, Teva is cutting 250–350 roles at that same API site under a "global optimization plan," raising **institutional-knowledge and validation continuity risk** at a regulated facility. A CGMP failure at a single-source API site would hit multiple finished products at once.
- **Key-person and leadership continuity risk at a pivot-critical juncture.** The entire equity narrative now rests on "Pivot to Growth" ($6–7B innovative revenue by 2030), which is a **small-team, CEO-anchored bet**: the CMO/R&D head just left, the CLO seat is interim, and the CFO is relatively new. Pipeline fragility is already evidenced — the **Phase 2 TOPAS-MSA (emrusolmin) study was discontinued Nov 2, 2026**, weeks before being implicitly leaned on at R&D Day.
- **Third-party dependency in the growth engine.** Biosimilars depend on **Samsung Bioepis**; long-acting injectables (UZEDY, olanzapine LAI) depend on **MedinCell's BEPO/SteadyTeq technology**. Both are narrow, single-counterparty licenses covering the products meant to replace declining generics (Q3 2026 revenue **fell 2% y/y** on generic REVLIMID erosion — a reminder of how fast a single generic cliff can offset an entire branded franchise).

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **ELEVATED / STRUCTURALLY STICKY**

- Teva is, by third-party litigation analysis, **the first-named defendant in ~40% of federal generic-drug price-fixing cases** and holds the record for the largest domestic antitrust cartel criminal penalty. This is a *durable* reputational asset impairment, and it directly conflicts with the current marketing posture as an "innovative biopharma."
- **Legitimacy mismatch risk:** the company is asking investors and payers to re-rate it as a neuroscience innovator while simultaneously closing a multistate collusion case and an anti-kickback whistleblower settlement. Any new disclosure re-opens that gap.
- **Real positives that partially offset:** a genuine approval cadence (WELTRUZA once-monthly olanzapine LAI Oct 2026; IGALMI at-home agitation Nov 2026), investment-grade ratings from all three agencies (a year early), a well-received first R&D Day, and expanded Samsung Bioepis partnership.
- **ESG/geographic overlay:** Israeli domicile exposes TEVA to boycott/exclusion screens and war-related sentiment — a persistent, low-grade discount factor rather than an acute event.

---

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

Rationale: the **largest single tail (multistate antitrust)** moved to resolution in November 2026, the board is genuinely independent, and the balance sheet is now investment grade. That is enough to keep this out of "High." But the residual profile is unbalanced: **regulatory/legal is High, reputational is Elevated, governance is only Adequate, and insider behaviour is negative.** The rating is Moderate *only because the litigation calendar is improving* — not because underlying controls have been demonstrated to be strong.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Undisclosed multistate settlement economics.** An "agreement in principle" announced public-relations-first, with no number, is a disclosure-quality flag. **Action:** tie the eventual figure to the litigation accrual in the 10-Q before relying on management's reserve adequacy.
2. **Interim Chief Legal Officer during peak legal exposure.** Escalate to management/board contact — why is this seat unfilled while the company is negotiating multistate antitrust settlements?
3. **CMO/R&D chief exit one week post-R&D Day.** Escalate: does the pipeline timeline behind the $6–7B 2030 target survive his departure? Press for a named successor and any program re-basing.
4. **Director-level distribution.** Mignone's $14.4M sale plus ~$42M of CEO sales with zero offsetting buys. Not proof of anything, but worth a direct question at the next board engagement.
5. **AUSTEDO concentration + IRA.** Single-asset dependence on a drug now subject to negotiated Medicare pricing from 2027; management itself guided to the impact. Model a bear case where AUSTEDO pricing and TOPAS-MSA-type pipeline attrition compound.
6. **Dilution creep.** ESPP expansion (+8.5M shares to 2036) plus RSU load — small individually, directionally consistent with shareholder-unfriendly equity management.
