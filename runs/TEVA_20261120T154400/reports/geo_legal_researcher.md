### 1. Geographic Risk Map

Teva is Israeli-domiciled (Tel Aviv HQ; US operating HQ Parsippany, NJ), with US-centric revenues. Q3 2026 total revenue **$4.40bn (-2% y/y)**; FY2026 guidance **$16.7–17.0bn**. Segment split below is anchored on reported Q3 2026 figures (US **$1.87bn, -11% y/y**; Europe **$1.32bn, +7% y/y**; International Markets ~**$1.2bn** residual), which I annualise as approximations.

| Geography | Rev. exposure (est.) | Key exposures | Risk level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **United States** | ~42–45% (~$7.3–7.7bn) | IRA Medicare negotiation (AUSTEDO), MFN pricing deal, DoJ/state antitrust, opioid settlement payments, FDA, tariff/API-supply policy | **High (regulatory/pricing)** |
| **Europe (EU/UK)** | ~30% (~$5.0–5.2bn) | EC antitrust fine & appeal, national price-referencing/reimbursement, EU–Israel political friction | **Medium-High** |
| **Israel** | <5% revenue but core mfg/R&D & domicile | Missile/rocket exposure near Neot Hovav site; war risk; shekel; sanctions/divestment pressure on Israeli-domiciled issuers; Israeli corporate/tax law | **Medium-High (tail risk)** |
| **International Markets** (~27%): Japan, Russia/CIS, LatAm, Canada, Turkey, India | ~$4.5–4.8bn | Turkey Competition Authority probe (Copaxone), Russia sanctions/exit complexity, FX & repatriation controls, EM pricing | **Medium-High** |
| **China / Asia-Pac (ex-Japan)** | Small (API sourcing) | US–China trade tension, API supply-chain dependency, tariff exposure | Low-Medium |

Structural point: Teva has **deliberately de-Israelised its capital-markets footprint** — direct listing of ordinary shares on NYSE (Q3 2026) and a disclosed plan for **conversion of ADSs into ordinary shares / termination of the ADS programme** (per 10-Q Exhibit 10.1, July 2026). This reduces friction for US investors but does **not** remove Israeli domicile/jurisdiction risk.

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**① US drug-pricing regime shift (IRA + MFN) — Probability: ~100% (already legislated/contracted); Impact: High**
- AUSTEDO is subject to Medicare negotiation: **maximum fair price of $4,093/30-day supply from 1 Jan 2027, ~38% below the ~$6,623 list** (CMS IAY-2027).
- Teva also **finalised a Most-Favored-Nation agreement with the US Administration (Aug 31, 2026)**, including a prospective MFN pricing commitment for future innovative launches and API donations (metronidazole, amlodipine, tacrolimus) — i.e., pricing is now partly a *political* variable, not just a commercial one.
- Management guides 2027 growth "despite" IRA impact on AUSTEDO (Reuters, 3 Nov 2026) and CEO argues AUSTEDO can grow "way beyond" it (Endpoints). **Watch item:** AUSTEDO is ~$2.55–2.65bn of 2026E revenue (~15% of total), so any MFN extension to in-line products would be material.

**② Middle East conflict / Israel-domicile tail risk — Probability: Medium (recurring); Impact: Medium (operations), High (valuation/narrative)**
- A missile-related incident occurred near Teva's **Neot Hovav** site; Teva stated **no operational impact** (Citeline). Q1-2026 10-Q language: impact of regional conflicts on results "continued to be immaterial."
- Escalatory context: joint US–Israel strikes on Iran ("Operation Epic Fury," Feb 2026); IMF cut Israel 2026 growth to 3.5% on regional tension.
- Secondary channel: **EU/UK/Canada moves against Israeli-linked entities** — UK sanctions on Israeli firms, 11 European states + Canada joint statement (Sept 2026) on restricting settlement trade, European Commission options paper on EU–Israel Association Agreement suspension. These target settlements, not pharma, but raise headline/divestment and procurement risk for an Israeli-domiciled issuer.
- Shekel/currency and repatriation are unhedged tail items; Israeli political/judicial instability adds tax and legal-regime uncertainty.

**③ Antitrust contagion beyond the US — Probability: High; Impact: Medium**
- **EU: €462.6m fine (May 2025)** for misusing divisional patents and disparaging generic Copaxone rivals — the largest such pharma abuse-of-dominance penalty; **under appeal**.
- **Turkey Competition Authority** opened its first-ever novel abuse-of-dominance probe into Teva on the same Copaxone patent-strategy theory (confirmed by Teva as "a legacy antitrust matter concerning Copaxone").
- Template risk: the Commission's patent-thicket/divisional theory is now exportable to other jurisdictions and to other Teva franchises.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags
- 🔴 **Repeat antitrust offender across two jurisdictions** (DoJ criminal penalty 2023; EC infringement 2025; Turkey probe 2026) — this is pattern, not idiosyncratic, and supports a structurally elevated enforcement probability.
- 🟠 **Political pricing commitments (MFN)** create quasi-contractual exposure to US government discretion; breach/non-compliance risk is not litigation-based but relationship-based.
- 🟠 **Sanctions/Russia:** Teva maintains CIS exposure; no disclosed sanctions violations, but wind-down/repatriation mechanics in Russia are an unquantified compliance cost (not detailed in fetched sources — **gap**).
- 🟢 **FCPA/AML:** No disclosed FCPA or AML enforcement actions in reviewed filings/news. Emerging-market distribution (LatAm, CIS, parts of Asia) is the standard third-party-intermediary risk zone; nothing flagged.
- 🟢 **Data privacy:** No material disclosed GDPR/HIPAA enforcement.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM-HIGH**
*(Legal/regulatory = **High** due to serial antitrust enforcement plus a 2027 pricing reset; geopolitical = **Medium**, with a fat-tailed Israel-conflict/EU-political component.)*

**What would move the rating:** *Upgrade to Low/Medium* —successful EC appeal, closure of indirect-purchaser MDL, benign MFN implementation. *Downgrade to Very High* — extension of MFN/IRA to AUSTEDO-adjacent growth products, an EU/UK procurement exclusion of Israeli-domiciled suppliers, direct conflict damage to Israeli manufacturing, or a new DoJ/EU investigation into current-period conduct.
