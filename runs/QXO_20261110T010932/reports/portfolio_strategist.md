## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — leaning negative (6 bearish / 2 bullish / 3 neutral)**

**The disagreement is not about the facts. It is about which facts dominate.**

- **Everything all 11 agents agree on:** the platform is genuinely scaled (~$18–19.6B pro forma), organic growth is **zero-to-negative**, GAAP earnings are negative and structurally so, leverage is ~5–6x adjusted / ~11x GAAP, and the January-2028 outcome depends on synergy capture that has not yet appeared in any P&L.
- **The real fault line:** Growth and Alt Data underwrite a **2028–2030 EBITDA line** ($4B target); Value, Quant, Short and Fin Risk underwrite the **2026–2027 balance sheet**. Both are correct. The question is which one sets the equity price over a 12–24 month horizon — and for a company with ~53% equity/EV and <2x interest coverage, the balance sheet dominates.
- **Where I break the tie against the bulls:** the Growth thesis itself concedes ~0% organic growth, and the Short Analyst's most damaging fact is that **even the Street's own 2030 spreadsheet implies a 5.5% EBITDA margin** — below the margins of the businesses QXO bought. The synergy story, as modelled by its own proponents, does not close the gap.
- **Where the bears overreach:** there is **no fraud**. Accruals are negative (–0.034), M-Score is a consolidation artifact, no SEC/DOJ action, zero discretionary insider selling. The Short Analyst correctly refuses to call this accounting manipulation. I weight the fraud-risk leg at approximately zero — which *raises* fair value relative to a genuine forensic short.
- **Decisive conflict resolution:** the one agent signal that overrides valuation upside is the **9-Nov governance shock** — Jacobs assuming CEO/Chair of CKX Lands with his own PE firm owning ~99.78% while running the largest integration in QXO history. Three independent agents (Value, Non-Fin Risk, ESG) flagged it unprompted. That is a correlated, non-diversifiable, non-quantifiable risk arriving at the worst possible moment in the integration calendar.
