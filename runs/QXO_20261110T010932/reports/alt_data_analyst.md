### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — **END MARKET BEARISH, DECELERATING**

| Proxy | Reading | Direction |

**Read:** The hard volume data (shingle shipments -13%) says the residential roofing recession is real and still running. But consumer *search intent* for roof replacement has stopped deteriorating and is running modestly **above** last year into November — the first non-negative demand datapoint in this series. Non-discretionary, insurance-driven, aging-housing-stock demand appears to be troughing. Treat this as "less bad," not "recovering."

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **TRACKING ABOVE consensus (revenue/EBITDA); GAAP EPS messy**

Prelim Q3 vs. consensus: **Net sales ~$4.895B vs $4.825B est (+1.4% beat)**; Adj EBITDA **~$557M, +85% YoY** (from $301M); Adj EBITDA margin 11.4% (vs 11.1% LY — modest expansion); GAAP net loss ~$123M (vs $139M LY), net margin -2.5%.

- **Surprise history: 85.7% beat rate, +38.7% avg magnitude** — but that average is distorted by tiny denominators in micro-cap era. Last four quarters: +1.7%, **-24.8% (miss, Q2'26)**, +1.9%, +16.7% — beat magnitude is decaying toward coin-flip.
- **Estimate revisions are negative:** Citi cut PT $25→$22 (Buy maintained) on weak residential + storm demand; Zacks on 10/29 explicitly said QXO "doesn't possess the right combination for a likely earnings beat." Consensus unchanged-to-down into the print.
- **Verdict:** Revenue and EBITDA are tracking **above**; adjusted EPS ($0.1348 est) likely beats given the $557M EBITDA, but rising interest expense from the debt refinancing and the -2.5% net margin make GAAP/reported EPS a poor read-through. **Note: the prelim release discloses NO organic growth figure** — with shingle shipments -13%, organic is plausibly negative and buried inside +79% reported. That is the single largest unknown for 11/12.

The signals genuinely bifurcate. End-market volume (-13% shipments) is bearish and unchanged; consumer intent is stabilizing; but everything print-facing — prelim revenue/EBITDA above consensus, call buying +82%, sentiment inflection, +493% retail attention — points **above expectations for the 11/12 report**. The +19% move has already captured much of this.

### 7. Confidence Level — **MEDIUM**
High confidence in the earnings-preview read (company-issued prelim numbers, not estimates). Only medium on the demand read: the bullish case leans on search-intent proxies that have historically diverged from professional reroof volumes, and the bearish case on ARMA shipments that lag by weeks.

**Data limitations & lag:** Patent API failed twice (unavailable); no quantitative web-traffic series for qxo.com/becn.com; job-postings proxy is qualitative SERP text, not counts; "Beacon Roofing Supply" Trends series is unreliable (multiple below-threshold zero weeks); 12-month Trends window lacks a prior-year spring peak, so seasonality cannot be cleanly differenced. All alt data as of 11/9/2026 — **the full 11/12 filing may disclose organic growth and reverse this read.** Alt data is a leading indicator, not a verdict.

**Key watch items for 11/12:** (1) organic/legacy-Beacon volume growth vs the -13% industry shingle benchmark; (2) adjusted EBITDA margin sustainability at 11.4% with TopBuild (installation, structurally lower-margin) now consolidated; (3) interest expense / refinancing cost vs the $0.1348 EPS bar; (4) any commentary on Jacobs' new venture as a management-attention risk.
