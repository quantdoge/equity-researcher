# Analog Devices (ADI) — Value Investment Analysis

**Price $312.95 | Market cap $151.6B | EV ~$158.1B | FY26 (Oct-26) | Dividend $4.40 (1.41%)**

The stock fell 17.4% on 24-Nov-2026 (to $313 from a $445 June high, −28% from October's $433) despite a Q4 *beat* (EPS $4.06 vs $3.90; revenue $4.37B +42% YoY). The cause matters: Q1 guidance of $4.40B ±$150M / adj. EPS $3.82 ±$0.20 implies a **sequential decline** off a record quarter, fabs are at 100% utilization (no upside upside to take orders), DIO rose to 141 days (13 above the 5-yr average), and capex more than doubles in FY27 (+150% YoY) for capacity that ships no chips until 2H 2028. That is a classic de-rating of *peak earnings plus rising capital intensity* — not a broken thesis.

## 1. Business Quality — Moat: WIDE

| Moat source | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Intangible assets / switching costs** | High-performance analog is designed in, not shopped. Design cycles run 5–10 years in industrial, auto, medical, aerospace. Tens of thousands of SKUs, 100k+ customers, no customer concentration worth noting. A $0.40 converter is never worth re-qualifying a $40k system for. |
| **Cost/scale advantages** | Internal fabs (vs. merchant foundry), 24,500 employees, #2 globally in analog behind TXN, leader in data converters, MEMS, and signal chain. |
| **Pricing power** | Gross margin 66.4% (FY21) → 67.4% (FY26); **adjusted gross margin ~73.5%** and adjusted operating margin ~52% guided for Q1 FY27 — above TXN. |
| **Returns on capital** | Reported ROIC 12.9% is distorted by $35.0B of Maxim goodwill/intangibles (68% of assets). **ROIC on tangible operating capital ≈ 95%+.** That gap is the moat, and it's the single cleanest proof of one. |

**Has the moat widened?** Yes, materially on paper: revenue $7.3B (FY21) → $15.2B (FY26), ~16% CAGR, with margins expanding. Much of the recent widening is cyclical/AI-power-driven rather than permanent — treat the 52% adjusted operating margin as a peak, not a floor.

## 2. Financial Quality — 8 / 10

- Revenue: $7.3B → $15.2B (FY21→FY26); but note the cycle: +64% (FY22), +2% (FY23), **−23% (FY24)**, +17% (FY25), +38% (FY26). Predictability is *good through-cycle, poor year-to-year*.
- FCF conversion: FY26 FCF $5.45B / NI $5.00B = **109%** (excellent). 5-yr average FCF $4.0B.
- Balance sheet: net debt $6.5B; **net debt/EBITDA 0.83x**; EBIT/interest 15.8x. Fortress-grade.
- Owner earnings (NI + D&A − capex) FY26: **$6.26B** → 24.2x market cap.
- Deductions: (a) FY26 GAAP operating margin of 38.6% is a record; (b) effective tax rate of ~9% is far below statutory and vulnerable to Pillar 2; (c) dividends $2.09B + buybacks $3.32B = **99% of FCF** returned, with incremental debt funding part of it; (d) FY26 capex of $733M (4.8% of sales) was abnormally low and flattered FCF — it is now normalizing upward.

**Intrinsic value (three methods):**
- **My base DCF** (conservative path: FCF $5.6B → $9.6B by FY31 as capex peaks at ~$2.2B then normalizes; 8.5–9.0% WACC; 3% terminal): **$264–290/share**.
- **Reverse DCF**: at $313, the market is discounting **~10% FCF growth for ten years** at a 9% WACC → implies fair value ~$340. Not demanding, not generous.
- **Tool DCF** (15.3% FCF growth for 10 years — the bull case): **$476/share**.

**Fair value range: Bear $180 (cycle rollover, margins to 30%) / Base $300–330 / Bull $430–470. Probability-weighted ≈ $310.**

At $313 the stock is at **~0% discount to base-case intrinsic value**. The de-rating has removed the excess but has not created a bargain. My buy discipline requires a 20–30% discount: **entry below ~$245**. Note also that 30.7x trailing GAAP is being paid on **record cyclical earnings** — the classic error Buffett warns against.

## 4. Management Quality — Strong

- **Vincent Roche, CEO since 2013**: transformed ADI from a components vendor into a higher-margin signal-chain franchise; the $21B Maxim deal (2021) roughly doubled the revenue base and expanded margins — value-accretive, not empire-building, though it cost ~130M diluted shares.
- **Capital allocation**: 13 consecutive years of dividend increases ($0.62 → $1.10/qtr since 2020, ~11% CAGR); aggressive buybacks ($3.3B FY26, $2.2B FY25) that reduced diluted shares from 506M (FY23) to 489M; 34% dividend payout of adjusted EPS.
- **Candor**: the capex step-up and capacity constraint were disclosed plainly. Guidance is conservative-by-construction (beats estimates consistently).
- **Minus**: modest insider ownership (~1% or less) and a willingness to add debt while buying back stock ahead of a capex super-cycle. Not disqualifying, but not owner-operator economics either.

## 5. Verdict: **HOLD**

A wide-moat, high-return, net-cash-generative franchise — my circle of competence, and a business I'd be happy to own for a decade. But the price already capitalizes roughly 10% annual FCF growth, earnings are at a cyclical and margin peak (revenue +38%, adjusted operating margin ~52%), free cash flow faces a multi-year capex headwind, and roughly 99% of FCF is already committed to shareholders. Wonderful business, **fair price** — and a fair price is not a buy.

- **Buy** below ~$245 (20% discount to base intrinsic value); **aggressive buy** below $210.
- **Trim/Sell** above ~$400, where the bull case is fully priced.
- Existing holders: hold. Selling a 12–15% compounder at intrinsic value to avoid a possible cyclical drawdown is usually the wrong trade.

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Peak-cycle earnings risk (largest).** FY26 revenue is 23% above the prior FY23 peak with record margins. FY24 proved analog revenue can fall 23% in a year; applying a 20x multiple to trough EPS would put the stock far lower.
2. **AI/data-center digestion.** Communications (AI data center power) grew ~63% at one point in FY26 and is now a meaningful demand pillar. A pause in hyperscaler capex hits both growth and the multiple.
3. **Capex super-cycle.** FY27 capex +150% (~$1.8B+) with capacity offline until 2H 2028 — FCF growth will lag EPS for two years, and if demand softens mid-build, returns on that capital compress.
4. **Inventory.** DIO at 141 days, 13 above the 5-year average — either prudent pre-build or a correction waiting to happen. Watch this line each quarter.
5. **China and trade.** Meaningful China/Asia revenue plus new US export-licensing rules and tariff/threat noise create headline and real demand risk.
6. **Tax normalization.** An ~9% effective rate is a meaningful slice of earnings; global minimum tax could take several points of EPS with no operating change.
7. **Rate sensitivity.** Beta 1.25 with the Fed tightening into a high-multiple semiconductor tape — the stock has had 14 moves >5% in a year.
