### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **IN-LINE, with downside skew on margin**
- FQ1'27 guide: revenue **$4.4B ±$150M** (~flat sequentially vs record $4.373B); adj. EPS **$3.82 — below Bloomberg consensus** (the specific miss that triggered the selloff).
- Surprise history: **100% beat rate over 7 quarters, avg +5.09%** — but magnitude is fading: 8.82% → 5.13% → 0.89% → 6.49% → 6.92% → 3.29% → **+4.10%** (latest). Direction of travel is down.
- Seasonality is unfavourable for FQ1: holiday fab shutdowns + elevated component costs compress operating margin sequentially (op. margin was 45.5% in FQ1'26 — the year-ago comp is a high bar).
- Alt-data read-through is **structurally muted**: because supply, not demand, is the binding constraint, even strong end-market signals (AI power, industrial) cannot produce a revenue beat. Channel inventory is normalised — no restock tailwind.
- **Verdict: In-Line.** Base case is a small headline EPS beat (ADI guides conservatively) but flat-to-down sequential revenue and margin compression. No alt-data support for a beat-and-raise.
