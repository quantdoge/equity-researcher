# Analog Devices (ADI) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $312.95 (Nov 24, 2026 close) | Market cap ~$152B | EV ~$159B | FY26 revenue $15.18B**

---

## 1. Growth Story

ADI has completed the transition from a cyclical analog recovery story to a structural AI-infrastructure supplier: FY2026 revenue rose 37.7% to a record $15.2B, led by Data Center and Industrial, with gross margin expanding ~600bps to 67.4% GAAP (69.3% in Q4, 72.9% adjusted) and operating margin nearly doubling to 38.6%. The thesis is that every megawatt of AI compute needs precision power conversion, optical interconnect, and sensing — and ADI's "grid-to-core" portfolio (deepened by the $1.5B Empower Semiconductor acquisition, closed July 7, 2026) lets it sell more content per rack as architectures shift to 800V DC distribution and vertical power delivery. This is a high-margin, high-barrier franchise levered to the least contested part of the AI buildout (power and analog signal chain), not a commodity GPU proxy.

The catch: the Q4 print (Nov 24) came with Q1 FY27 guidance of $4.4B revenue (±$150M) and $3.82 adjusted EPS — essentially **flat sequential revenue** and a ~6% sequential EPS decline versus Q4's $4.06. That triggered a 17% one-day drop. The stock is now 30% below its June 2026 high of $445 yet still +47% year-over-year. The de-rating is about the slope of the curve, not the destination.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **Core analog/mixed-signal SAM:** roughly $90–100B globally, growing mid-to-high single digits. ADI at $15.2B is ~15% penetrated — with the #2 position behind TXN and 75,000+ SKUs across ~125,000 customers.
- **The incremental TAM is AI power.** Rack power is scaling from ~10kW toward >1MW, forcing a re-architecture (800V DC bus, 48V intermediate, vertical power delivery). That converts power from a low-value commodity into a precision-analog problem where ADI's converters, hot-swap controllers, and telemetry have pricing power. ADI frames this as a multi-billion-dollar SAM expansion by 2030; the evidence is Communications revenue +49% YoY, fueled by AI data-center optical and power.
- **Penetration math:** consensus FY2030 revenue of $25.1B implies a 13.4% CAGR from FY26 — above ADI's historical through-cycle rate but well within reach if AI power/optical contributes and Industrial/Auto recover normally. Market-implied 5-year revenue is therefore *not* heroic.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q1 FY27 results, ~Feb 17, 2027** — first quarter against a deliberately reset bar ($4.4B / $3.82). With a 100% beat record and a 90-day-old guidance sandbag, a beat-and-raise is the base case.
- **Empower Semiconductor ramp through FY27** — $1.5B all-cash deal closed July 2026; design-win announcements with AI accelerator/hyperscaler platforms would validate the "grid-to-core" content story.
- **FY27 capex step-up to $1.6–1.9B** (from $733M) — read as a demand-visibility signal for AI power/optical capacity; a confirmed multi-year build would re-rate the growth durability case (at the cost of near-term FCF).
- **Industrial/automotive inventory normalization** — broad-based industrial recovery plus the end of auto destocking; distribution restocking typically adds 2–3 quarters of above-trend growth.
- **Capital return cadence** — dividend growth plus buyback; a raised authorization at the March-ish board meeting would signal confidence.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends ("Analog Devices", 5-yr, US):** search interest spiked to an index 100 in late Feb 2026, then faded from ~26 (April) to 7–9 (Oct–Nov 2026). This is a retail-attention fade, not a demand signal — for a B2B analog supplier with ~125,000 customers, search interest is noise. Mildly consistent with the post-momentum multiple compression.
- **Hiring:** ~6,000 open roles tagged to ADI in Boston (LinkedIn) and 200–356 discrete postings in Wilmington/San Jose/Portland; notable that the flagged San Jose role is "Staff Applications Engineer, **Power Products**" at $151–219K — consistent with a power/AI-focused ramp rather than a broad cost-cutting posture.
- **Analyst/price-target direction:** still net bullish ($425–$675 cluster) despite the reset; the Bernstein upgrade explicitly cited "valuation decoupled from growth." Targets will lag the price reset by one revision cycle.
- **Signal quality note:** no credible app-download or web-traffic proxy exists for ADI; management commentary (data center +49%, record Q4 gross margin) is the highest-quality forward indicator available, and it is confirmed by the actual margin and FCF numbers rather than by sentiment data.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

ADI is a genuine AI-electrification compounder masquerading as a cyclical analog company. The quality metrics are exceptional: 67%+ gross margin, 38.6% operating margin, 36% FCF margin, a Rule of 40 score of 73.7, sub-1x leverage, and a 100% seven-quarter beat record. After a 30% drawdown the stock trades at **24.0x FY26 adjusted EPS, 18.6x FY27E and 15.7x FY28E**, and 8.6x FY27E sales — cheaper on cash flow (28x P/FCF) than TXN (91x), MCHP (35x), or NXPI (27x) despite growing roughly twice as fast as TXN. A 24x multiple on my FY27 EPS build of $16.50–17.00 supports ~$400–420 over twelve months (+28–34%), with a credible bear case at ~$260 (16x FY27E). Risk/reward is roughly 2.5:1 in favor, but the through-cycle growth rate (7–8% peak-to-peak historically) and the Q1 sequential plateau argue for **Buy, not Strong Buy** — this is a high-quality growth-at-a-reasonable-price position, not a hyper-growth moonshot.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **AI capex digestion / concentration.** Q1 FY27 guidance of flat sequential revenue is the first evidence of a pause. If hyperscaler spending decelerates, the highest-growth, highest-margin piece of the mix (data center/communications) rolls over first, and the multiple — currently 24x trailing — would compress toward the analog peer group's cycle multiples (~15–17x).
2. **Cyclicality is not dead.** FY2024 revenue fell 23.4% and gross margin fell ~800bps. Analog inventory cycles are brutal; the FY26 spike (+37.7%) sets up a tougher comparison base in FY28.
3. **Capex/FCF compression.** FY27 capex of $1.6–1.9B roughly triples FY26's $733M; FCF margin likely compresses from 35.9% toward ~30–32%, and with payout already ~100% of FCF, buyback support for EPS will shrink precisely when the multiple is contracting.
4. **Competition.** TXN's 300mm cost advantage and $30B+ US fab build-out, plus Chinese analog entrants (SGMICRO, 3PEAK) attacking commodity SKUs, cap long-run pricing power outside the high-performance/AI tiers.
5. **Execution on Empower and integration risk** — the $1.5B deal adds intangible amortization (a GAAP-vs-adjusted gap the market has been willing to look past) and requires design wins that haven't yet been disclosed.
6. **Sentiment/multiple fragility and China exposure** (~20% of revenue; export controls, tariffs). A 17% single-day drop on a modest guidance reset shows how crowded the AI-adjacent trade remains.
