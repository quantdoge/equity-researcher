**Rationale — the sizing is set by volatility, not by conviction:**

- **Kelly arithmetic gives ≈ zero edge at $313.** Prob-weighted intrinsic value is ~$310 (Value), reverse-DCF fair value ~$340. Expected return to base case is 0% to +9% against a **48.2% annualised volatility** and a **−29.5% drawdown that has already occurred twice**. Kelly on a near-zero edge with a 48% standard deviation says *don't bet*. There is no sizing math that turns a 0% margin of safety into a position.
- **Volatility-equivalent sizing.** A 48% vol name contributes 4x the portfolio risk per dollar of a 12%-vol stock. A 2.0% ADI position carries roughly the risk of an **8% position in a market-vol name**. That alone caps the weight at 2–3% for anything short of high conviction — and conviction here is *medium at best* on price.
- **β of 1.99.** This is a levered semi-beta proxy (R² 0.44, so 56% idiosyncratic — but the 44% that is market is levered 2x). Two percent in ADI adds ~4% of portfolio-level market beta.
- **Asymmetry is genuinely two-sided:** bull $430–470 (+37% to +50%), bear $180–260 (−17% to −42%). At roughly 1:1 odds with a 0% base-case return, the correct size is small and the correct entry is lower.

**Why not zero:** the franchise is genuinely top-decile within analog (67.4% GM, 38.6% OM, 36% FCF margin, 0.83x net leverage, and it *passed* a real −23.4% revenue stress test in FY24). That earns a place in the book — just not at this price.

2. **Long-duration growth** — 24x forward with a 4.2% earnings yield in a bear-steepening regime where the 10Y has risen 69bp in five months. This is the single worst factor to add.
3. **Momentum** — ADI's *only* positive quant factor is +9.3% 12-1 momentum, and it is 2nd of 6 in its peer group. Buying now is buying the last remaining support at the moment the price has broken down 17% on 4x volume. If momentum rolls over, the Quant analyst's own note says the setup "tips to Sell."

**Where ADI *does* help:** it is the least-volatile name in its peer set (48.2% vs AVGO 50%, QCOM 62%, MU 92%, MRVL 97%) and its demand drivers are *less* AI-concentrated than peers — **two-thirds of FY26 growth came from non-AI markets**. Against a pure-AI position (AVGO/NVDA), ADI is a defensive analog complement. That is a real, if modest, portfolio argument — but it argues for funding ADI *out of* AI-beta exposure, not from cash.

**Correlation recommendation:** hold ADI **instead of** increasing TXN; never both at full weight. Cap the combined analog sleeve at 4–5%.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

- **No stop above $300** — a stop that tight on a 48%-vol stock guarantees you're shaken out. Position sizing (1.5–2%), not the stop, is the primary risk control here.

**Thesis invalidation conditions (fundamental — these override price):**

5. **Distributor sell-through confirmed below sell-in** — verifies the Short's channel-loading thesis outright.
6. **Section 301/232 action catching the broad analog catalogue with no price pass-through** — destroys the tariff-exemption relative advantage the Macro analyst credits.
7. **A goodwill impairment charge** against the $35.0B (68% of assets) intangible base — tangible book is already **negative $1.1B**.

**Trim triggers (upside):**

- **$430–450** → trim to a core 1.0% hold. The bull case is fully priced; Baird's $675 is not a base case.

- **Override:** if the stock reaches $400 *with* DSO <55 days and revenue >$4.6B, the margin peak is not a peak — raise the trim level to intrinsic value on FY28E EPS ($19.98 consensus, 10 analysts).

**Event hedges:** the position is unhedgeable via stop-loss across earnings (17% gap risk on 24-Nov). If holding through 17-Feb-2027, reduce to 1.0% or collar it. Do not carry a 2%+ weight into a Fed meeting (9-Dec) *and* an earnings print in a 48%-vol name.
