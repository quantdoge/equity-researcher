**Investment Thesis**

ADI is a top-decile analog franchise at a top-of-cycle price. The business case is proven — #1 in its peer group on gross margin (67.4%), operating margin (38.6%), revenue growth (+37.7%) and 5-yr CAGR simultaneously, with a fortress balance sheet (0.83x net debt/EBITDA) that survived a -23.4% revenue year in FY24 without stress. The price case is not: at ~24x FY26 adjusted earnings with a 4.2% earnings yield below the 5.13% 10Y Treasury, the equity risk premium is negative, and three independent forensic signals (receivables +81% vs revenue +37.7%, DSO +14.9 days, inventory days 153.5 into a +0.6% QoQ guide) indicate the channel — not just end demand — pulled FY26 forward. Own the franchise; do not pay this price for it.

**Bull Case**
- Industrial recovery is less than half-complete (half of sub-segments below historical consumption) with orders exceeding billings — multi-quarter above-trend growth runway into FY27.
- AI power is a structural second engine: data-center revenue doubling, 800V DC architecture ramping 2027, Empower/Alif acquisitions deepen "grid-to-core" content per rack — and two-thirds of FY26 growth was *non*-AI, a defensive mix.
- Margin leadership is structural: 67.4% GM / 38.6% OM on 14% less revenue than TXN earns the same operating income; the FY27 capacity build ($1.6–1.9B) positions ADI to monetise the next upcycle from 2H 2028.

**Bear Case**
- Peak-cycle earnings priced as structural: record margins mean-revert by definition, and ~600bps of the margin expansion is amortisation roll-off, not pricing power. FY24 proved revenue can fall 23% in a year.
- Channel forensics point to pull-forward: receivables growing >2x revenue, DSO +14.9 days, inventory at 153.5 days — into the first flat sequential guide in eight quarters, with a comp plan that rewards sell-in.
- Negative equity risk premium in a hiking cycle: 4.2% earnings yield vs 5.13% 10Y, β ~2.0, SOX de-rating — the macro bear tail (10Y → 6%) alone implies $205–270.

**Key Risks**
1. **Channel-loading confirmation**: DSO >70 days or distributor sell-through verified below sell-in — thesis-invalidation territory.
2. **Policy event window**: Section 301 determination, January critical-minerals cliff, and any Section 232 broadening onto analog — hits ~25% China revenue and the Asian back-end simultaneously.
3. **AI capex digestion**: the highest-margin growth pillar rolls over first if hyperscaler spending pauses.
4. **FCF compression**: capex triples with no output until 2H 2028, while payout is already ~99% of FCF — buyback support shrinks exactly when the multiple is contracting.
5. **Cycle timing**: Q1 FY27 guide (+0.6% QoQ, EPS guided *down*) may be the cycle top; gross margin <64% for two quarters breaks the "structural margin" case.
6. **Data gaps**: FY26 geographic split unverified (SEC/IR 403 errors); insider Form 4 transaction *direction* unknown; no post-print sell-side targets exist (the "~45% upside to median target" is pre-print anchoring — discount it); Nonfin report truncated.
