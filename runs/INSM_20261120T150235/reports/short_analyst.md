## 2. Key Red Flags (ranked by substance)

1. **Single-product, fifth-quarter-of-launch concentration.** BRINSUPRI = $400.1M of $524.4M Q3'26 revenue (76%). A $32B enterprise value now rests on a launch curve with less than five quarters of observable persistence. 2027 is the first year refills, not new starts, must carry growth — unobservable today, and the single largest unpriced risk.
2. **ARIKAYCE US has flatlined.** Q3'26 US $75.5M (+2% YoY); 9M'26 US $208.6M (**+1%**). The "ARIKAYCE continues to grow" narrative is carried entirely by international (+21%). Growth now hinges on the sNDA for all-comers MAC (PDUFA 28 Jan 2027) — a binary event underwriting $460–470M of 2026 guidance.
3. **The 2027 "profit" is a rounding error.** Consensus 2027E EPS $0.68, 2028E $3.20, 2029E $6.48. GAAP breakeven has been pushed to 2028 despite ~$3B of revenue in 2027. Four concurrent Phase 3 TPIP programmes (PALM-ILD only 35% enrolled, completing 1H'28), INS1148 Ph2, INS1033 Ph1, INS1201/INS1202 gene therapy Ph1 = structurally elevated R&D for years. **You are paying today for 2029–30 cash flows.** Long-duration assets re-rate violently on any pipeline miss — exactly what BIRCH did (-16%, 18 Dec 2025).
4. **Mechanism risk is now empirically proven.** BIRCH failure in chronic rhinosinusitis shows DPP1 inhibition is not disease-agnostic. That caps the "platform" premium the bulls were paying and de-risks nothing for TPIP.
5. **Payer friction is documented, not hypothetical.** Both Cigna and UnitedHealthcare publish Brinsupri prior-authorisation policies; the brand runs a "$0 at point of service" copay programme. PA/step-edit churn in a *prevalent* (not orphan) population caps uptake velocity and puts a ceiling on net price as commercial mix grows.
6. **Chronic dilution and SBC.** Shares 112M (2021) → 219M (2026), **+96% in five years**; 213.6M → 218.8M in the last four quarters. SBC $152.7M in 2025 = **25% of revenue**.
7. **Governance/governance-timing.** CFO transition in 2026. Paul Hudson — *former Sanofi CEO* — joined the board in Oct 2026; a Sanofi acquisition report surfaced one month later. Across 40 Form 4s in 2026 there is **not one open-market purchase**, including a 12-filing cluster on 15 May 2026 and 6 on 5 Jun 2026, and no insider bought the $92 June low.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment — **MODERATE, and the real long-term bear case**

First-in-class advantage is genuine (only MHLW-approved NCFB therapy in Japan; first FDA approval Aug 2025). But first-in-class ≠ durable best-in-class. The brensocatib class carries hyperkeratosis/dental adverse events; a competitor with cleaner tolerability could take share at lower net price. The bronchiectasis field is crowded (ensifentrine, eosinophil-targeting biologics, inhaled anti-infectives). Meanwhile ARIKAYCE's US stagnation (+1%) suggests the legacy franchise has already found its ceiling — its boxed warnings limit expansion to sicker patients. Moat: **narrow and time-limited**, not durable.

## 5. Management Red Flags — **MILD, not disqualifying**

**And the decisive point: the current price is an event price.** Unaffected $119.80; today $146.99 = **+22.6% rumor premium**.
