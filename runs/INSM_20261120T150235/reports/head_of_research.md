**Investment Thesis**

Insmed has executed one of the best rare-disease launches in biotech history — BRINSUPRI went from zero to $400M in a single quarter within ~15 months, is only ~10% penetrated against a ≥$7B peak guide, and the company inflects to cash-flow positive in 2027. The problem is the entry price: the stock carries a ~22% unconfirmed Sanofi takeover premium over its unaffected $119.80, leaving a negatively skewed near-term binary (+~10% if a bid lands, −18% if denied, −25–30% to standalone fair value). The franchise is worth owning; the current tape is an event price, not a fundamental one.
