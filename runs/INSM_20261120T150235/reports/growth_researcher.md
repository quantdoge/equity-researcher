# Insmed Incorporated (INSM) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $146.99 | Mkt cap $31.4B | EV ~$30.7B | Sector: Biotechnology (rare/orphan respiratory)**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Insmed has executed one of the most successful rare-disease launches in biotech history. **BRINSUPRI (brensocatib)** — the first-in-class, first-and-only approved oral DPP1 inhibitor for non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB) — went from zero to **$400.1M in a single quarter** (Q3'26, +29% QoQ) roughly one year post-approval, taking total company revenue to $524.4M (**+268% YoY**). Management has raised BRINSUPRI peak sales guidance to **≥$7B** (excluding international and additional patient cohorts) while stating the drug is **only ~10% penetrated** in its initial U.S. target population — a rare combination of proven commercial de-risking and enormous remaining runway.

This is no longer a "will it work" story: it is a **scale-of-the-opportunity** story, with a second leg (TPIP across four Phase 3 pulmonary indications), an early immunology/gene-therapy pipeline, and — as of Nov 19 — **reported Sanofi takeover interest**.

**Valuation:** EV/FY26E sales **16.5x** → FY27E 10.3x → FY28E **7.7x** → FY30E 4.9x. P/E 45.9x on 2028E, **14.7x on 2030E**; PEG <1 on the 2028–30 EPS ramp (~77% CAGR). Reverse-engineering today's EV at a 5x terminal sales multiple requires ~$6.1B of 2030 revenue — **essentially exactly consensus ($6.24B)**, and far *below* management's own >$13B combined peak-sales framing (BRINSUPRI ≥$7B + TPIP >$6B). The market is paying for consensus, not for blue sky.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

| Layer | Size / Status |
|---|---|
| **NCFB drug market** | ~$2.66B in 2026 → $5.3B by 2031 (14.7% CAGR) — but this understates Insmed's pricing/population capture |
| **BRINSUPRI peak sales (company)** | **≥$7B**, *excluding* international and several additional patient groups |
| **Current penetration** | **~10% of initial U.S. target population** after ~1 year; "tens of thousands" of patients |
| **Adjacent expansion** | COPD/asthma patients with underlying bronchiectasis — a materially larger undiagnosed pool; DTC advertising not yet launched |
| **Geography** | Japan approved (Aug '26) and launched Oct '26; international only $2.0M of Q3 BRINSUPRI revenue — **untapped** |
| **TPIP franchise** | 4 Phase 3 indications: PAH, PH-ILD, PPF, IPF — company projects **>$6B** combined peak |
| **Optionality** | INS1033 (DPP1 in RA, Ph1), INS1148 (IPF, Ph2), INS1201 (DMD gene therapy, Ph1 complete), INS1202 (ALS, Ph1) |

**Differentiation:** first-in-class *and* first-to-market in NCFB with no approved competitor and a patent-protected oral mechanism (DPP1 inhibition of neutrophil serine protease activation). ~10% penetration + a nascent DTC campaign + an unlaunched international business = the most durable part of the growth algorithm. **The central debate is no longer TAM — it's speed of penetration and gross-to-net.**

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Sanofi takeover resolution (weeks, per Bloomberg — reported Nov 19, 2026).** Talks said to be ongoing; a deal could land "in weeks." At a typical 40–60% biotech premium this implies $200+/share. Board additions of **Paul Hudson (ex-Sanofi CEO)** and Utpal Koppikar (Oct '26) add credibility to the strategic-interest read.
- **ARIKAYCE sNDA PDUFA — January 28, 2027** (Priority Review). Label expansion to *all* MAC lung disease patients, including newly diagnosed/recurrent — de-risks a decelerating franchise (U.S. ARIKAYCE only +2% YoY).
- **Q4/FY26 results + 2027 guidance — Feb 18, 2027.** Consensus Q4 revenue $603.8M, EPS -$0.30. Watch for: BRINSUPRI exit run-rate ($461M+ implied by guidance), first explicit cash-flow-positive guidance, and 2027 revenue framing.
- **BRINSUPRI DTC launch + EU/Japan ramp (2027).** DTC is the single largest lever on penetration from 10% → 25%+.
- **TPIP readouts (2028+):** PALM-ILD enrollment complete H1'28; PALM-PAH (first patient Jul '26) and PALM-PPF (started Sep '26) enrolling; IPF Phase 3 start H1'27. Long-dated — optionality, not a 2027 catalyst.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends, "Brinsupri" (US, 12-mo):** sustained 60–90 band all year, with a **92 print in late Oct '26 and 80–81 in early Nov** — no post-launch search decay. Branded demand intent is holding near highs a full year in, consistent with continued new-patient starts and supportive of the DTC ramp.
- **Hiring:** ~894 Insmed job listings on Indeed with live field-force requisitions (Regional Sales Director–North Texas; Therapeutic Specialist–Kansas City East) → commercial infrastructure still *expanding*, not saturating.
- **Sequential dollar adds (the cleanest proxy):** BRINSUPRI +$62M in Q1'26, +$114M in Q2, +$90M in Q3 — absolute adds remain large even as percentage growth normalizes (44% → 29% QoQ). This is what a durable launch curve looks like.
- **Counter-signal:** Q3 EPS miss (-$0.46 vs -$0.38) and rising SG&A confirm the P&L is still being spent, not harvested.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction; Strong Buy on any deal-rumor unwind)

The growth quality is elite and *verified*: accelerating revenue, expanding gross margins (76.6% → 79.4%), four straight revenue beats, +54% forward EPS revisions, and a cash-flow inflection arriving in 2027. At 7.7x FY28E sales and 14.7x 2030E EPS against a >30% durable grower with 10%-penetrated TAM, the multiple is defensible — and management's ≥$7B peak guidance versus ~$6.2B of 2030 consensus revenue leaves real room for estimate drift upward.

The reason this is not a Strong Buy *today*: the Nov 19 takeover report (+20.6% in a day) has embedded a **binary M&A premium** into the price, and the stock is already +60% off its June low. If Sanofi walks, $147 likely re-rates toward the ~$120 pre-report level on fundamentals alone. Position accordingly — accumulate on rumor-driven weakness, size for a 20% drawdown.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Deal break risk (highest near-term).** Bloomberg's report is unconfirmed; Sanofi ADRs fell 3% on it. A collapsed process removes ~20% of the current price with no fundamental offset.
2. **DPP1 franchise expansion has failed twice.** Brensocatib missed in **CRSsNP** (discontinued Dec 2025, stock -16–19%) and again in a **hidradenitis suppurativa** trial (2026). The "pipeline-in-a-molecule" multiple-expansion story is now impaired; BRINSUPRI is a one-indication drug until proven otherwise.
3. **Single-product, single-indication concentration.** BRINSUPRI is ~76% of revenue. Any NCFB safety signal, competitive entrant, or payer/gross-to-net deterioration is a direct hit to the entire thesis.
4. **Dilution and funding.** Weighted diluted shares went 140M (FY23) → 164M (FY24) → **214M (FY25)**, +30% YoY. Cash-flow breakeven is a 2027 promise, not a fact; further raises would tax per-share upside.
5. **Estimate dispersion is extreme.** FY28 EPS range **$0.62–$6.05**; FY27 range **-$0.90 to +$2.22**. Consensus "beats" are low-confidence — the margin ramp (28% EBITDA margin by 2028) has never been executed at this scale by this organization (1,664 employees).
6. **TPIP is 2028+ and capital-hungry** across four Phase 3 programs; a miss there would strand >$6B of projected peak sales and much of the long-dated valuation.
7. **Execution/scale risk on a $7B-peak launch**: SG&A of $701M (FY25) still ramping; ARIKAYCE U.S. already decelerating to +2% YoY, making the sNDA approval closer to a necessity than an upside.
