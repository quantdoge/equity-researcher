## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Political Risks

**#1 — U.S. tax-filing policy reversal & political backlash (highest salience)**
The IRS's free **Direct File** program was shut down — "IRS Direct File will not be available in Filing Season 2026. No launch date has been set" (Tax Notes, 11/5/25; Nextgov) — and formally killed after a lobbying campaign. Intuit and H&R Block **"poured more than $7M into federal lobbying in 2025"** (OpenSecrets, 4/2026). Senators Warren, Wyden and Pocan have publicly demanded Intuit explain its efforts to kill the program.
- *Probability of near-term adverse outcome:* **Low (~15%)** — the current administration is aligned.
- *Impact:* **High** — TurboTax is $5.3B (~25% of revenue); a future administration reviving Direct File or state-run free-filing regimes (state AGs and legislators remain hostile) would compress the Consumer segment. Secondary impact: reputational/political-risk premium and Congressional scrutiny.

**#2 — Consumer-protection enforcement cycle around "free" advertising**
Multi-state AG settlement ($141M, 2022) plus FTC administrative action. The **Fifth Circuit vacated the FTC's 20-year cease-and-desist order on 3/20/26** (*Intuit, Inc. v. FTC*, No. 24-60040) on *Jarkesy* separation-of-powers grounds, remanding to the agency.
- *Probability of re-litigation:* **Medium (~50%)** — the FTC can re-file in Article III district court, where it originally lost an injunction bid; state AGs retain independent authority.
- *Impact:* **Low–Medium** — direct monetary exposure modest (<$200M), but remedies constrain marketing, a core conversion channel.

**#3 — India operational concentration**
Bangalore is the company's largest engineering footprint outside the U.S. (principal offices, 478k sq ft). Risks: India DPDP Act data-localization/cross-border rules, Indo-U.S. visa and trade friction, and any India–Pakistan/China border escalation affecting business continuity.
- *Probability:* **Low–Medium (~20% for a materially disruptive event over 3 yrs)**
- *Impact:* **Medium** — product-delivery disruption rather than revenue loss; no inventory or tariff exposure.

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **Advertising substantiation / "free" claims** — the single most repeated enforcement theme against Intuit (FTC, 50-state AGs). Any return to aggressive "free" marketing re-opens both federal and state exposure.
2. **Payments & money movement controls** — expanding QuickBooks Checking/Credit Karma Money plus a 2026 account-security class action means BSA/AML, Reg E error-resolution and safeguarding controls are a genuine red flag, even where liability sits with bank partners.
3. **Data privacy with tax data** — Intuit holds SSNs and full financial lives of ~93M customers; state privacy laws, GDPR/UK GDPR and India DPDP create multi-jurisdiction breach-notification and transfer risk.
4. **AI output liability** — TurboTax/QuickBooks AI advice sits inside IRS Circular 230-adjacent practice and emerging state AI disclosure laws.
5. **FCPA / sanctions / export controls — LOW.** No Russia operations since 2022, negligible China/EM revenue, no disclosed OFAC or DOJ matters. This is a genuinely clean area for INTU.

---

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale:** Geopolitical risk per se is **low** — ~92% U.S. revenue, no sanctioned-market exposure, no export-control or tariff sensitivity, and a dollar-based cost/revenue base with limited FX translation risk. The **Medium** rating is driven by *domestic political and legal* factors: (a) Intuit is a politically visible target on tax-filing policy with a documented lobbying footprint and an active Senate critique; (b) it carries a live federal securities class action covering a 16-month class period and a fresh payments-security class action; and (c) its core TurboTax franchise depends on the absence of a government-provided free-filing alternative — a policy condition that has already flipped once in three years and can flip again.

**Key monitors for the next 12 months:** FTC re-filing in district court; ruling on lead plaintiff in *26-cv-07086*; any state-level free-filing legislation (CA/NY/NJ); IRS Free File terms for the 2027 season; India DPDP implementing rules; and disclosure of any FTC/CFPB inquiry into Credit Karma Money.
