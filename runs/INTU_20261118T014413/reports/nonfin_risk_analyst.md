# INTU — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**Intuit Inc.** | Technology / Software–Application | Mkt cap ~$85.7B | Price $320.79 (17-Nov-26) | 18,600 FTE | FY26 rev ~$21.4B

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE** (trending weaker)

**Evidence:**
- **Board:** 11 directors, majority independent. Credible financial expertise — Vasant Prabhu (Lead Independent Director, designated financial expert), Thomas Szkutak (ex-Amazon CFO, Audit & Risk), Raul Vazquez, Eric Yuan.
- **CEO/Chairman duality — flag.** Sasan Goodarzi holds both Chair and CEO. The Lead Independent Director role (Prabhu, elevated Nov-2025 when Goodarzi took the chair) mitigates but does not neutralize the concentration. This was a deliberate post-2025 governance step *backwards*.
- **Founder presence:** Scott Cook (founder, director since the 1980s) remains on the board — useful continuity, but adds an informal power center outside the independent-director framework.
- **Say-on-pay:** passed at the 22-Jan-26 annual meeting, but ~9% of fund filers opposed (152 of 1,732) — meaningful minority dissent for a mega-cap, consistent with pay-for-performance questions after the FY26 collapse.
- **Incentive alignment:** heavy stock-based compensation is a recurring critique, and FY26 free cash flow was flattered by *unusually low cash taxes* (per sell-side work) — cash conversion quality is not as clean as headline FCF suggests. Not a fraud indicator, but it weakens the read-through from "record FCF" to compensation outcomes.
- **Disclosure credibility:** the sharpest governance issue is not the board's composition but its **oversight of guidance**. The company affirmed FY26 TurboTax growth, then cut it and announced a 17% RIF on the same day (20-May-26). That is a controls/forecasting failure that the board's Audit & Risk Committee owns.

Residual risk, plus the reputational record stands |

## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **AI disintermediation of the core franchise.** Do-it-yourself tax prep is among the most automatable knowledge-work categories in existence. FY27 guidance already concedes the damage: total revenue growth guided to **9–10% (from 14%)** and **TurboTax unit growth of just 2–3%**. Intuit's answer — Intuit Assist plus human "Live" experts — is a higher-cost model than the software-only economics the market paid for. The market has reclassified INTU from AI winner to AI casualty: **-52% over 12 months, -62% peak-to-trough.**
2. **Serial restructuring and talent/key-person risk.** ~**3,000 roles (~17%) cut in May-26**, with **$300–340M** in charges — the *second* major RIF after ~1,800 in 2024. Two large cuts in two years signals strategic drift, not fine-tuning. Compounding: Goodarzi is the architect of the platform strategy *and* Chair *and* CEO — a genuine key-person concentration at precisely the moment institutional knowledge is walking out the door, while the company competes for AI talent against better-paying balance sheets.
3. **Data concentration, cyber and M&A integration.** Intuit sits on extraordinarily sensitive PII and financial data across TurboTax, Credit Karma, QuickBooks payroll, payments and lending SPVs — a breach would be catastrophic and class-action-generative (see Credit Karma Money suit). Separately, the two transformational deals are underperforming: **Credit Karma** is decelerating and the **Mailchimp ($12B)** SMB marketing franchise faces churn; both create integration, impairment and disclosure risk.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **HIGH**

- **TurboTax brand taint is structural.** A federal agency found Intuit engaged in deceptive "free" advertising across six years of marketing. The Fifth Circuit vacated the *remedy*, not the *finding* — the public record is permanent, and "TurboTax = deceptive free-file marketing" is a durable media and political frame.
- **Layoff backlash.** 3,000 job cuts announced alongside an AI investment message — the "AI is replacing us" narrative is the single most reputationally corrosive story a tech employer can generate. Visible employee-community backlash (Reddit, blind channels) and talent-market damage.
- **Narrative risk is now reflexive.** Every AI product launch from OpenAI/Anthropic/Google is read as a TurboTax threat. The company has lost control of its own story; the CMO's push to make the *parent brand* the hero is a defensive response, not an offensive one.
- **Credibility deficit.** Guiding confidently, then missing and cutting 17% of staff in the same quarter, damages the most valuable reputational asset a serial acquirer has: management's word.

---

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Not **Critical** — there is no fraud signal, no accounting restatement, no going-concern or liquidity question, no going-private dynamic; the board is independent-majority with a real lead director, the FTC's harshest remedy was vacated, and the restructuring is fully funded. But the combination of (a) an active securities fraud class action, (b) a same-quarter guidance failure and mass RIF, (c) CEO/Chair duality, (d) an AI-driven structural threat to the largest profit pool, and (e) **no insider buying into a 60% drawdown** places this clearly in the High band.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **⚠️ Securities class action (Aug-25 → May/Jun-26 class period)** alleging FY26 TurboTax guidance was knowingly unreliable. Monitor for **derivative suits and 401(k)/ERISA claims** — these typically follow within 2–3 quarters and directly implicate directors.
2. **⚠️ Disclosure-controls event:** TurboTax guidance affirmed then cut, with a 17% RIF announced simultaneously, driving a **-20% single-day move on 22.4M shares (≈10x normal volume)**. Ask management directly on the 19-Nov-26 call what internal signal was visible before 20-May-26.
3. **⚠️ Insider conviction gap:** no open-market purchases by any officer or director through a historic drawdown. Verify via Form 4 detail (feed limitation) before relying on this in the memo.
4. **⚠️ Governance concentration:** Chair/CEO duality + founder on board + ~9% say-on-pay dissent. Watch whether the board separates the Chair role as a credibility gesture.
5. **⚠️ Earnings-quality/compensation optics:** FY26 FCF aided by unusually low cash taxes, alongside elevated SBC — margin of safety is thinner than reported FCF implies.
6. **⚠️ Strategy credibility:** second major RIF in two years, with Credit Karma and Mailchimp both underperforming. Elevated impairment risk in the acquired goodwill base.
