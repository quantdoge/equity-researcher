### Investment Memo: INTU — 18 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (scaled/starter entry) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $405 (+26% from current $320.79)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months
**Timing note**: FQ1 FY27 prints **tomorrow (19 Nov)**. Post-earnings reaction has decoupled from surprises (Aug: +12.6% beat → −9.5% gap). Initiate *after* the print, not before.

**Investment Thesis**
Intuit is an 80%-gross-margin, 21.5%-ROIC, 40%-FCF-margin franchise trading at ~14x FY27 SBC-inclusive EPS because the market has renegotiated its terminal growth rate around AI disintermediation. The moat has genuinely narrowed at the low end (TurboTax units −2%, growth price-led), but the forensic short case was disproven, the balance sheet survives a −20% revenue shock, and the bear case (~$258) is bounded while the base case sits near $560. This is a favorable ~1:3 asymmetry — sized with respect for the possibility that the moat is narrowing faster than the numbers yet show.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro **unfavourable** — 10Y at 5.13% (+69bp since Jun), CPI re-accelerating to ~3.8%, Fed hiking again (3.88%). Sector demand is intact (2026 software spend +15.1%) but application-software multiples are ~50% below their 5-yr average on agentic-AI terminal-value risk. INTU is the only peer combining >13% growth with >27% GAAP operating margin; the case is company-specific de-rating, not sector tailwind.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium** — ~92% US revenue, zero sanctions/tariff/EM exposure. The risk is domestic: a live securities class action (class period Feb-25–Jun-26), Credit Karma Money suit, and permanent political tail-risk around free filing (Direct File is dead for 2026 — a near-term positive; FTC order *vacated* Mar-26).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **BUY**. Probability-weighted intrinsic ~$407 (bear $258 / mid $400 / base $560). Reverse-DCF implies the market prices ~4% perpetual growth against a 9–10% guided grower with expanding margins. Moat downgraded Wide→**Narrow**: DIY tax has lost pricing power; assisted tax (Live +37%, 53% of TurboTax), mid-market (+39%) and the accountant channel remain defensible.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **HOLD**. The growth mandate is over: +14% → 9–10% guided, consensus ~6.5% EPS CAGR with FY30 EPS *below* FY29. Growth is price-led (ARPU +15% vs customers +3%) — exactly the mix AI attacks first. What remains is a value floor, not a growth story.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Neutral 55.9/100 — but a rare **Value 88 + Quality 80** double (cheapest or 2nd-cheapest of six software peers on every multiple; ROIC rising 13.1%→21.5%; negative accruals). Regression beta is statistically meaningless (R²≈0) — use ~50% realized vol for risk budgeting.
- **Financial Risk**: **Low credit / high market**. Net debt 0.17x EBITDA, 23x interest coverage, $8.66B FCF; a 20% revenue decline is a solvency non-event. The risk is the equity: −68% max drawdown, ~45–54% vol. Largest unassessed item: receivables tripled to $7.3B (loan-book build) — credit quality undisclosed.

Fraud short rejected, overvaluation short rejected at 14x EV/EBIT; short side only becomes interesting above ~$390–400 (>20x GAAP).

**Bull Case**
- De-rating is done: −60% from peak with fundamentals intact; cheapest quality name in the comp set at ~14x FY27 EPS (19.5x trailing GAAP) with a 9.75% FCF yield.
- Assisted/mid-market layers are compounding fast (Live +37%, IES 4x YoY, QBO Accounting +23%) and are structurally harder for AI agents to disintermediate than DIY forms.
- $7.9B buyback authorization (~9% of cap) executed at these levels is materially accretive; management voluntarily folded SBC into non-GAAP — disclosure candour most peers avoid.

**Bear Case**
- AI commoditizes the DIY funnel: units already −2% with price as the #1 churn reason; FY27's +2–3% unit guide is the make-or-break test.
- Growth deceleration is structural, margin expansion is doing the lifting, and a resumed hiking cycle (10Y → 5.75–6% bear scenario) hits SMB seats and Credit Karma volumes simultaneously.
- Governance/credibility overhang: securities class action, zero insider conviction buying, price-led mix vulnerable to any SMB pricing-tolerance break.

**Key Risks**
1. FY27 TurboTax units decline mid-single digits despite the price reset → proves AI substitution, converts 14x into a value trap (thesis-killer).
2. FY27 revenue <8% or Consumer segment negative; EPS guide cut below $21.
3. 1994-style tightening: 10Y to 6% breaks SMB formation and Credit Karma together.
4. Unassessed credit risk in the $7.3B receivables/loan book.
5. Litigation escalation (lead-plaintiff ruling, derivative/ERISA follow-ons; FTC re-filing).
