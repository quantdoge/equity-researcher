### 3. Earnings Preview Signal → **Tracking ABOVE on the quarter, but on low-quality mechanics**

- Beat rate **7/7 (100%)**, average surprise **+10.63%**; last four quarters +12.6%, +1.8%, +12.8%, +8.1%.
- Q1 FY27 consensus: **EPS $2.46** (vs $3.34 actual LY = **−26% YoY**) on **revenue $4.305B (+10.8% YoY)**.
- An EPS estimate down 26% against revenue up 11% implies a margin collapse — and the pre-print coverage attributes much of this to **higher FY27 cash taxes after an unusually low-tax FY26, plus elevated SBC**. That is largely *mechanical*, not operational, which makes the bar unusually beatable.
- Applying INTU's average surprise to Q1 gives ~$2.72 — still −19% YoY, but a ~10% EPS beat.

**Verdict: likely EPS beat, but the beat is not the trade.** Post-earnings price action has decoupled from surprises: on 25‑Aug‑2026 INTU beat by 12.6% and **gapped down −9.5% the next morning** (355.80 → 321.95 open). The market is trading FY27 guidance and Online Ecosystem customer adds, not the quarter. Q1 is also the low-revenue quarter where Intuit typically sets the tone for the year. **Demand alt-data says in-line-to-soft on units; expectations say beat.**

Direction into the print is neutral with elevated guidance risk; the 13x trailing multiple and 100% beat record make this a "beat-and-guide-is-everything" event, not a clean long. **Data lag caveat: all alt signals here are 1–8 weeks stale relative to the 19 Nov print.**
