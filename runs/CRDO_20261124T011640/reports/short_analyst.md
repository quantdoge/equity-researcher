## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **MEDIUM** (not Fraudulent Risk)

My conclusion up front, because it contradicts the headline output of one of my own models: **the accrual data clears this company of manipulation.** CRDO is a genuine, cash-generating, triple-digit-growth business. What is *not* genuine is the durability of its growth or the safety of its earnings stream.

An accruals ratio of 0.35% is in the top decile of clean reporting. FCF conversion of 86% for FY26. Operating cash flow has tracked or exceeded net income in every full year except the Q1 FY27 inflection. There is no revenue-recognition fraud signature here.

**But quality is deteriorating at the margin, and that is the trade.** Q1 FY27 cash conversion fell to 70%, and FCF conversion to 64%, driven by a **-$151.3M working capital drain**.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

**Flag 1 — Sequential growth has collapsed, and this is the whole thesis.**
YoY growth: 273.6% → 273.1% → 201.5% → 157.0% → **114.7%**. More damning, *sequential* growth:

| Q2 FY26 | Q3 FY26 | Q4 FY26 | Q1 FY27 |
|---|---|---|---|
| +20.2% | **+51.9%** | +7.4% | **+9.6%** |

Revenue went $268M → $407M → $437M → $479M. The step-function is over; CRDO is now a high-single-digit sequential grower wearing a hypergrowth multiple.

**Flag 2 — Gross margin broke trend for the first time in six quarters.**
67.4% → 67.6% → 68.5% → 68.2% → **64.5%**. A 370bp QoQ contraction. Annual GM had risen relentlessly (57.7% FY23 → 68.0% FY26), so this is the first evidence of either mix shift (lower-margin optical vs. AEC) or pricing pressure. Operating margin fell even harder: 35.7% → **25.2%** (-1,046bps), as SG&A rose 42.9% on 9.6% revenue growth.

**Flag 3 — Concentration: four customers ≈ 84% of revenue.** This is the single greatest fragility. AEC/DSP sockets are designed-in per platform and per generation — which cuts both ways. A single hyperscaler in-sourcing, or dual-sourcing to Astera Labs/Marvell/Broadcom, removes a fifth of the company overnight.

**Flag 4 — Working capital is absorbing the growth.** DSO rose **48.6 → 54.9 days** QoQ; inventory rose **+24.8%** against **+9.6%** revenue. Not yet alarming, but the direction is wrong.

**Flag 5 — Stock-based compensation is enormous and just doubled.**
FY26 SBC: $182.6M (13.7% of revenue, 38.7% of net income). Q1 FY27 SBC: **$87.98M in a single quarter** — 18.4% of revenue and **68% of net income**. Diluted shares: 181.2M → 188.2M → **194.4M**. Fully-loaded FCF (OCF − capex − SBC) for Q1 FY27:

> $90.2M − $7.3M − $88.0M = **−$5.0M**

The business consumed cash in Q1 FY27 once you treat comp as the expense it is. On a TTM basis, SBC-adjusted FCF is ~$204M, putting the stock at **~113x true free cash flow**.

**Flag 6 — The DustPhotonics deal hollowed out the balance sheet and stuffed it with goodwill.** $750M cash + 0.92M shares (announced 13 Apr 2026, closed 28 May 2026). Goodwill & intangibles: $122M → **$1,365M**. That is **45% of total assets and 50% of book equity**. Cash fell $1,443M → $764M in one quarter. Any disappointment in the silicon-photonics roadmap means impairment directly against book value.

**Flag 7 — Insiders are selling into strength, at prices 35–58% above today.** 25 Form 4s between 16 Sep and 23 Nov 2026, all sales: CTO Chi Fung Cheng sold 27,500 shares at **$192.99** (13 Oct) and 24,500 at **$164.12** (27 Oct); CFO Daniel Fleming sold 8 Oct. The company is simultaneously **buying back stock** (-$25.9M in Q1 FY27) — funding insiders' exits. Net insider signal: decisively negative.

**Flag 8 — Near-zero effective tax rate is flattering EPS.** FY26 operating income $475.4M vs. net income $472.3M — a ~5% effective rate (Cayman domicile). Normalizing to 15–20% cuts FY26 EPS ~10%.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary

**What I can prove:** Growth is decelerating hard on both axes. Gross and operating margins just broke six-quarter uptrends. Cash conversion fell to 70% and is *negative* on an SBC-adjusted basis. 84% of revenue sits in four customers. Half of book equity is acquisition goodwill. Insiders sold ~60% above market. Consensus FY2028 estimates of $9.67 EPS imply a **~49% net margin** — versus 35.4% delivered in FY26 and **27.0% in Q1 FY27**. That is an extraordinary margin assumption embedded in the bull case, and it is the most vulnerable assumption in the stack.

**Why it is no longer a good short:** The stock is already **-67% from its $302.52 June high** and -26% over twelve months, on *record* results. The market has front-run me. Valuation now: 14.5x TTM sales, 42.8x TTM earnings, 52.5x TTM FCF. Versus **Astera Labs at 45.5x sales and 3x the multiple** — CRDO is the cheapest connectivity pure-play. Meanwhile: Altman Z-score **7.78 (Safe)**, net cash **$446M**, total debt $20.7M, gross margins still 64.5%, and clean accruals. There is no balance-sheet or accounting catalyst to force the squeeze — a bad combination for a short seller.

**What kills the short:** Any quarter where sequential growth returns to 15%+ and gross margin stabilizes at 64–68% re-rates this to $180+ on the existing multiple.
