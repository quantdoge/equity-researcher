## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### **Target weight: 2.5% of portfolio at full build. Initial: 1.0–1.25%.**

**Kelly sanity check:** Probability-weighted outcomes (bull +120% / base +20% / bear −55%, roughly 35/40/25) give an expected return of ~+36%. Full Kelly at 106% vol = μ/σ² ≈ 32% of portfolio. That is unusable for a single 106-vol name. **⅛ Kelly ≈ 2.5–3%** — which lands exactly where the qualitative framework does. Use fractional Kelly, not full.

**Why 2.5% max and not the 3–5% "high conviction" band:**
- **Volatility budget is the binding constraint, not conviction.** At 106% annualized vol, a 2.5% position contributes ~2.7% standalone annualized vol. In a book targeting ~12% vol, that is ~22% of the total risk budget in one 807-employee company.
- **Beta budget:** 2.5% × 3.67 = **+0.09 to portfolio beta**. A 1.0% position adds only +0.037 beta; a 5% position adds +0.18, which would silently convert the whole book into a leveraged AI-capex bet.
- **Binary event inside 8 days** (Dec 1 Q2 FY27), then a hawkish FOMC 6 days later (Dec 9). Full sizing *before* a binary is position-sizing malpractice.
- **Value's IV is 4% below spot.** You are paying full price for the privilege of carrying the risk. That caps *initial* size, not eventual size.
