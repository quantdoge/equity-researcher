## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **INVESTMENT GRADE** (unrated; balance sheet equivalent)

CRDO carries essentially **no debt**. Total debt of $25.4M at FY26 year-end fell to **$20.7M** at Q1 FY27 (finance leases — long-term debt line is $0), against **$764M cash** and **$2.73B equity**. Interest expense is **$0**, so interest coverage is not meaningful (infinite). This is a net-cash business with no refinancing cliff, no covenant exposure, and no maturity ladder to manage.

The credit story is not leverage — it is **scale and concentration**. An 807-employee company generating $1.6B TTM revenue from a handful of hyperscalers is structurally fragile at the *revenue* line, not the *balance-sheet* line.

**Post-DustPhotonics balance sheet (Q1 FY27, closed Aug 2026, ~$750M):** goodwill + intangibles jumped from $122M → **$1,365M**, now **45% of total assets and 50% of equity**. Cash fell $1,443M → $764M to fund it. This is the one place credit quality degraded: the asset base is now half acquisition accounting, and a silicon-photonics disappointment would trigger impairment (non-cash, but equity-dilutive to book value and a sentiment shock).

**Key findings:**
- **Operating leverage is ~2.6x.** A 20% revenue decline cuts operating income 52%. This is a high-torque P&L.
- **Operating breakeven is ~$1.16B annualised revenue** — roughly **−40%** from run-rate. That is the true stress threshold, and it is far below current levels.
- **No downside scenario produces a solvency event.** Zero debt service, $764M cash, current ratio 7.4x, and FCF stays positive even at −40%. Cash *accumulates* in the −20% case. There is no covenant, no maturity wall, no liquidity cliff.
- **The risk is entirely in the equity.** At 14.5x TTM sales / 38x EV/EBITDA, a −20% revenue year with margin compression would plausibly compress the multiple to 7–8x sales on $1.5B revenue (~$11B equity value) — a **~50–55% equity decline** with a fully intact balance sheet. Valuation, not viability, is the exposure.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating

# MODERATE
*(Balance sheet: **LOW** risk | Liquidity: **VERY LOW** risk | Market/valuation: **HIGH** risk)*

CRDO is a financially sound company with a dangerous stock. It cannot go bankrupt at current leverage and cash generation — there is no plausible demand scenario in which it breaches a covenant or fails to fund itself. But it is a 100-volatility, 3.1-beta, 14.5x-sales single-cycle business with ~75%+ revenue concentration, and the equity has already demonstrated a 67% peak-to-trough drawdown. **Risk of permanent capital loss is high; risk of credit loss is near zero.**
