## Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd (CRDO)

**Snapshot:** $23.0B market cap · Semiconductors · Cayman Islands holding company, San Jose HQ · 807 employees · Beta 3.29 · FY2026 revenue $1.34B (+206%), net income $472.3M · Price ~$122 vs. 52-week high ~$283 (−57% from peak).

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE** (structurally weak, practically acceptable)

**Concerns:**
- **CEO/Chairman duality confirmed.** William Brennan is Chairman, President *and* CEO — unusual concentration of power for a company this size.
- **Classified board** (Class I/II/III; only 3 Class II directors up for election at the Oct 12, 2026 AGM: Acevedo, Danesh, Sutardja) plus **Cayman Islands incorporation** as an exempted company — structurally limits shareholder ability to remove directors or force change. This matters more than usual given the insider-selling pattern below.
- **Compensation design:** FY2026 bonuses tied primarily to *revenue and non-GAAP net income growth* plus individual objectives — a revenue-growth-weighted scorecard at a company where GAAP-to-non-GAAP gaps are wide (see Red Flag 5) and where "special performance-based equity awards" were granted. Growth-at-any-cost incentives carry margin/accounting-quality risk. Brennan's FY total comp ~$14.4M.

**Mitigants:**
- **Lead independent director in place:** Sylvia Acevedo (board since Dec 2021), appointed April 2025 — a genuine improvement; the 2022 proxy had disclosed *no* lead independent director.
- 94% say-on-pay support; independent compensation committee + independent consultant; **clawback provisions, stock ownership guidelines, anti-hedging rules, double-trigger change-in-control** protections all disclosed.
- Board grade "B" (Boardroom Alpha); auditor Ernst & Young LLP, ratified for FY2027 — no auditor churn flagged.
- Governance is evolving in the right direction; the gaps are structural (domicile/charter) rather than evidence of self-dealing. No related-party transactions surfaced.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: **LOW, with material forward exposure**

- **No SEC enforcement actions.** EDGAR full-text search for enforcement/litigation mentioning CRDO returned nothing. No DOJ, FTC, or antitrust matters identified.
- **No confirmed securities class action against the company.** An FY2026 10-K snippet contains the phrase "shareholders *have instituted* securities class action litigation" — on inspection this is **boilerplate risk-factor language** ("in the past, shareholders have instituted securities class action litigation following periods of market volatility"), *not* disclosure of a pending case. Law-firm alerts (Bronstein Gewirtz, Pomerantz) are **solicitation/investigation stage only**. Treat as noise, but expect fresh filings after a 32% one-week drawdown.
- **IP litigation — a serial pattern, now largely resolved:** Credo has settled AEC patent disputes with **Amphenol (2025)**, **Molex and TE Connectivity (March 2026, confidential, cross-license)**, and **Marvell (announced Nov 17, 2026, covering respective AEC patent rights)**. Shares rose ~3% on the Marvell news. The IP overhang that had been the single largest legal risk has been substantially cleared — a genuine de-risking event.
- **The real regulatory exposure is export control, not enforcement:** China + Hong Kong = **34.4% of FY2026 revenue ($459.2M)**. Combined with a U.S.-nexus product set in AI connectivity, this is direct exposure to shifting BIS rules, tariffs, and outbound-investment restrictions. Pending legislation/enforcement in this area is the tail risk that could impair a third of the revenue base with little notice.

### 3. Insider Activity Analysis: **HEAVILY NET SELLING — the standout red flag**

- **Net insider sales ~$82M by 6 insiders in the trailing 90 days; $0 in open-market purchases.** No insider buying in over 12 months.
- **CEO William Brennan:** 312,302 shares sold (~$47.3M) between Dec 2025 and Oct 2026; **$35.3M of that under a pre-arranged Rule 10b5-1 plan adopted January 12, 2026**. October sales executed at $170–$180. Retains 366,480 shares direct plus 150,000 via trust — residual alignment is meaningful.
- **CTO Chi Fung Cheng:** sold 27,500 shares at $192.99 (~$5.3M, Oct 13) and ~$4.5M at $164.12; a weekly "staircase" of sales from Oct 6 ($211/$226) down through Oct 27 ($155/$168) — selling *into* the decline.
- **CFO Daniel Fleming:** exited Oct 8 at $211–$215. **COO Yat Tung Lam:** 50,000 shares at a weighted $205.22 (Oct 1).
- **CTO + CEO + CFO combined $26.28M in October at $155–$226** vs. a stock now at ~$122.
- 40+ Form 4s since July 2026 — extreme filing density.

**Assessment:** Mostly programmatic (10b5-1 confirmed for the largest blocks, with plan adoption/amendment disclosed in the 10-K), which meaningfully lowers the "informed trading" inference — but the January 2026 plan adoption ahead of a volatile year, and the CTO's price-tracking staircase, are the kind of pattern that invites scrutiny. The absence of *any* insider purchase after a 57% drawdown is a weak-conviction signal from management. **Request the actual plan adoption dates and cooling-off periods before clearing.**

### 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Extreme customer concentration (the dominant risk).** Top 10 customers = ~90% of FY2026 revenue; two customers each ≥10%; some periods show top 3 at ~88% and four hyperscalers each ≥10%. These buyers have both the capability and stated intent to **dual-source or develop SerDes/AEC silicon in-house** — a single design-loss decision at one hyperscaler is a thesis-level event, not a quarter-level one. Diversification into optical and NeoClouds is directionally right but has not yet diluted the concentration.
- **Single-foundry and geographically concentrated supply chain.** **TSMC is the exclusive wafer supplier**, with a small group of Asian assembly/test and cable partners. No second source for leading-edge wafers; exposure to Taiwan geopolitical risk, capacity allocation to larger AI customers, tariffs, and logistics disruption. Compounded by the China/HK revenue concentration — supply and demand risk sit in the same region.
- **Key-person dependency and integration execution.** 807 employees supporting a $23B market cap; the SerDes/DSP IP architecture is closely tied to founder-CTO Cheng, and strategy to Brennan. Simultaneously absorbing the **DustPhotonics acquisition** and standing up a ~$600M optical business in FY2027 — new product category, new margin profile, new integration risk, all with a very thin bench.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **MODERATE**

- No consumer-facing brand, no product recalls, no safety issues, no ESG controversy of consequence — reputational exposure is concentrated in the **investor/customer** sphere rather than the public sphere.
- Active negative narrative: "$26M sold before a 32% plunge," "insiders prescient or fortunate," repeated coverage of customer concentration, and a plainly **eroding EPS-beat margin (34.3% → 2.6% over four quarters)**. The Dec 1, 2026 earnings call is a reputational event risk — guidance is the number that decides whether the "externally driven sell-off" story survives.
- Cayman domicile + heavy China revenue creates an optics/governance-screen penalty with ESG and governance-focused funds. High beta (3.29) amplifies every headline.

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Driven by **operational concentration** (90% top-10 revenue, exclusive TSMC, founder dependency) and **insider-selling asymmetry**, *not* by misconduct — there are no fraud indicators, no enforcement actions, and the IP litigation overhang has just been cleared. This is a structurally fragile business model rather than a dirty one. Absent the concentration issues, the rating would be Moderate.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Combined Chairman/CEO + classified board + Cayman domicile** — accountability structure is weak precisely when insider selling is at a record.
2. **~$82M net insider sales / zero purchases over 90 days**, CTO selling weekly down from $226 to $155, and **no insider buying into a 57% drawdown**. Obtain 10b5-1 adoption dates and cooling-off periods; verify no plan was adopted or amended during a blackout-adjacent window.
3. **90% top-10 customer concentration with active hyperscaler insourcing threat** — model a single-customer loss scenario before sizing the position.
4. **34.4% of revenue from China/HK** in an escalating export-control regime; no visibility into entity-list or tariff exposure in retrieved materials.
5. **Earnings-quality watch item (hand to accounting team, not yet a fraud indicator):** beat magnitude compressed 34.3% → 2.6%, gross margin declining as optical mix grows, and one source notes **cash generation lagging reported earnings** — reconcile non-GAAP net income to operating cash flow and watch receivables/inventory days around the Dec 1 print.
