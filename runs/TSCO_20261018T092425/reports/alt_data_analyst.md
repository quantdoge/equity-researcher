### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **IN-LINE TO BELOW consensus (mild downside skew)**

- **Revenue:** probably in line. Total sales growth is being carried by unit growth (new stores) while comps stay negative — alt data (flat web traffic, decelerating search) is consistent with comps of roughly **-1% to -2%**, effectively a repeat of Q2. In line with +2.3% revenue.
- **EPS:** modest downside risk. Consensus already models -17%, but the Q2 cost structure (SG&A +14.4%) plus ongoing Petsense restructuring and VIP Petcare integration give little cushion. My base case: another small miss, in the **-2% to -6%** range — matching the ex-outlier historical pattern.
- **Surprise history (corrected):** reported 28.6% beat rate / -14.7% avg surprise is distorted by a **data artifact** — the Jan'25 quarter shows estimate $2.28 vs actual $0.44 (-80.7%), a pre-split/pre-adjustment error. Excluding it: **beat rate ~33%, average surprise ~-3.7%**. Three straight misses (Jan -7.0%, Apr -9.2%, Jul -1.0%), with the miss magnitude *narrowing* — so the estimate-reset process is maturing. That argues against a large negative surprise, not for a beat.
- **Critical flag:** search interest in October is running ~5% below last October. If that holds, it pressures **Q4/holiday** — the quarter the stock's recovery thesis actually depends on. Even an in-line Q3 may not repair the FY27 narrative.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH (mild)**

Not a violent bearish call — a *"no inflection yet"* call. Every leading indicator I can measure is flat-to-deteriorating into the print: search interest at the year's low in September, October trailing last year, web traffic -0.88% MoM, social tone negative. Meanwhile Q2 confirmed the damage is *traffic* (-1.7% transactions), and nothing in alt data suggests that reversed in Q3.

**The important nuance:** the bearish read is largely *consensus* — a 39% drawdown and a cut outlook already price in weak comps. The differentiated insight is narrower: **alt data does not validate the "discrete headwinds"/second-half recovery framing management sold on the Q2 call.** April and June were positive; July–September search data shows no such rebound. So the asymmetry sits in **FY27 estimate risk and the Q4 holiday quarter**, not in a Q3 short. Offsetting real positives: +14.6% off the June low ($28.94), volume pickup into the print (~17–19M shares Oct 14–15 vs ~10M avg), a 3% yield, and an ongoing Petsense/VIP Petcare reset that could flatter 2027 margins.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:**
- **No foot-traffic panel** — the single most important dataset for a traffic-driven comp story is missing. Search interest and web visits are imperfect proxies for in-store visits at a 2,400-store rural chain where e-commerce is a small share.
- Google Trends is a **single clean series** but seasonally confounded; I could not build a YoY comparison for Q3 because the series begins Oct 2025.
- Web traffic is a **one-point MoM proxy**, not a time series.
- Hiring and social are **qualitative web-search proxies**, not measured counts.
- Patent tool failed (immaterial here anyway).
- Earnings surprise history contains at least one **split-adjustment artifact** — I corrected for it, but other quarters may carry residual distortion.

**Data lag:** Google Trends is weekly and finalized with a 1–3 day lag through Oct 11 — it captures almost all of Q3 and the first two weeks of Q4. Web traffic is monthly. News window is 14 days. All are timely relative to an Oct 22 print.

**Bottom line:** Alt data says the Q3 print is likely another flat-to-modestly-negative comp quarter with in-line revenue and a small EPS miss — no recovery signal. Positioning is deeply washed out, so I would not press a short into the number; the exploitable view is **downside to the FY27 recovery narrative, sized small, with the honest caveat that I am blind on foot traffic.**
