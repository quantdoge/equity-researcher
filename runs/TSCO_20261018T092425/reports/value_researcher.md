### 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **NARROW** (strong, but narrowing at the edges)

Tractor Supply is the largest rural-lifestyle retailer in the U.S.: **2,463 stores in 49 states**, plus 209 Petsense units. The moat rests on three real pillars:

- **Efficient scale in small markets.** The typical store sits in a county that cannot profitably support a second farm-and-ranch box. This is the textbook efficient-scale moat: local monopoly economics without regulatory protection. 88-year operating history, no credible national competitor.
- **Needs-based demand mix.** Consumable/usable/edible categories (feed, animal health, fencing, hardware) are repeat-purchase and non-deferrable — the reason comps held positive in 9 of the last 11 years. Private-label penetration across ~25 proprietary brands supports gross margin and supplier leverage.
- **Returns on capital consistent with a moat.** ROIC has run **22.7% → 20.6% → 19.1% → 17.2%** (FY22–FY25), comfortably above an ~8% WACC every year. That is a genuine moat signature.

**But the moat is narrowing, not widening.** ROIC has fallen ~550bp in three years. The decisive evidence: **FY26 comps are now guided to (1%) to flat**, the long-term financial framework was **withdrawn** in July 2026, and management is "reinforcing our value position" — i.e., investing price to defend traffic. A moated retailer with pricing power does not concede gross-margin dollars to hold volume.
