### Investment Memo: TSCO — 16 October 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate new positions | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $37 (~+12% from $33.16); probability-weighted value ≈ $35 (+6%)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Tractor Supply is a genuinely good franchise — #1 rural lifestyle retailer, efficient-scale moat, clean cash-backed accounting, FCF-positive — in a genuinely deteriorating earnings cycle: comps negative, gross margin down ~300bp, operating income flat for four years, ROIC off ~550bp from peak, and the long-term framework withdrawn. The macro regime (real 10Y ~2.95%, 7.4% mortgages, sentiment 51.7, Fed hiking) hits all three channels that matter to this name. At ~16x depressed earnings and ~88% of base-case fair value, the de-rating is largely done — but cheap is not a catalyst, and not one of eleven specialists recommends buying here.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Unfavourable. Consumer-led trough; essentials holding, big-ticket frozen. TSCO grows revenue above peer median (+4.3% vs +3.2%) but runs a 9.5% op margin, ~190bp *below* peers — buying growth with margin. Macro bull tail (CPI → 2.5%, 10Y → 4.2%) would re-rate the stock ~+30%.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Low–Medium. ~100% US revenue; risk is imported via COGS. Post-SCOTUS tariff regime in flux; $100M+ refunds are a one-off, not capitalizable. Two state enforcement actions in 12 months ($1.35M CPPA record privacy fine; $5.13M CA pricing/pesticide/greenwashing judgment).
- **Value**: Moat **narrow and narrowing** — ROIC 22.7% → 20.6% → 19.1% → 17.2%. A moated retailer does not concede gross-margin dollars to hold traffic; TSCO is. Framework withdrawal is the credibility break.
- **Growth**: Fails a growth mandate — 3% three-year revenue CAGR, Rule of 40 = 9, three consecutive misses. This is a margin-recovery story, not a growth story. Unit growth (~80 stores/yr) is the revenue floor.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Neutral. Top-quartile value (16.1x, 4.2% FCF yield, 2.9% dividend) vetoed by worst-in-set momentum (−39% 12-1) and 35% vol. **R² = 0.026 — beta/alpha are statistically empty; risk-budget off volatility, not beta.**
- **Financial Risk**: Moderate — trajectory, not level. Net debt/EBITDA 2.93x, interest +26% y/y, **115% of FCF returned to shareholders**, quick ratio 0.16. A −20% revenue scenario → 6.2x leverage and an uncovered dividend. Debt maturity schedule unretrievable — largest genuine unknown.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: Moderate, watch-list to High. CTO, CSCO and CMO all exited within six months, mid-DC-ramp and mid-pet-restructuring; CTO sold $3.4M at $54 months before resigning. Offset: **CEO's first open-market buy since Feb 2020, with the Chairman and a director, at ~$32** — insiders are calling the floor.
- **ESG**: Average with negative momentum. Quantified costs ~$5–6M (<0.05% of revenue); the tail risk is an unmanaged ~26Mt Scope 3 footprint meeting California SB 253 disclosure, plus a live greenwashing template. Not a thesis driver.
- **Alternative Data**: Bearish (mild). Google Trends Q3 −14% sequential, September the lowest week of the year, October ~−5% YoY; web traffic −0.9% MoM. **Alt data does not validate management's second-half recovery framing** — the asymmetry is in Q4/FY27, not the Q3 print. Foot-traffic panel unavailable.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: No edge. Accounting is clean (M-Score −2.37, 149% cash conversion) — a melting ice cube, not a fraud. The short was 2025's trade: −48% drawdown, 8.3% of float short, 4.7 days to cover. Do not short a 0.5-beta, 2.9%-yield clean name at a decade-low multiple. Flat is the position.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**
- Gross profit *dollars* fell 4.4% in FY25 on record revenue; transactions −1.7% — a traffic problem with no measured inflection.
- Structural decay: ROIC down four straight years, framework withdrawn, FY27 consensus EPS ($2.04) below FY25 actual ($2.06).
- Debt-funded buybacks executed at $45–60 into a cut guide; −20% revenue scenario breaks dividend coverage (0.71x) and pushes leverage to 6x+; bear case $23–24 (−28%).

**Key Risks**
1. **Q3 print 22 October (5 days)** — binary event; two prior prints gapped −8.5% and −12%. Alt data skews to a small miss.

3. Execution continuity: technology/supply-chain leadership vacuum during the Nampa DC ramp and Petsense/VIP integration.
4. Unquantified refinancing cliff with interest expense +26% y/y; regulatory recurrence inside the CPPA four-year certification window.
