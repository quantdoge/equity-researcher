### Investment Memo: TSEM — 17 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY — but only as a small, staged satellite position | Conviction: **Low-Medium** (Medium on business quality, Low on entry timing and macro)
**Price Target**: $150 (+12% from current $133.42) | probability-weighted: bear $92 (30%) / base $150 (45%) / bull $220 (25%)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Tower is a forensically clean, net-cash specialty foundry undergoing a genuine — and unusually well-evidenced — inflection into AI silicon photonics: record Q3'26 (+33% YoY, 34% GM), Q4 guided to $600M (+36%), $1.3B of *contracted* 2027 revenue and $290M of customer prepayments. The 58% drawdown from the June peak has removed the bubble but not created a discount: our Value desk puts fair value at $118–125, *below* spot, while the Growth desk sees ~19x 2027E earnings and a sub-0.5 PEG. This is a real business at a fair-to-slightly-rich price carrying an exceptional stack of *correlated* tail risks (ITC exclusion action, Israel operations, negative FCF into a $3B build, $60M of CEO selling) — own a toehold, not a position.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector ATTRACTIVE and inflecting — mature-node pricing +5–15% in 2026, utilization rising, specialty foundry consolidating. But macro is UNFAVOURABLE for the equity: real 10Y yields at 2.91% (+66bp in 4 months), Fed hiking into 3.8% CPI, SOX in a ~20% drawdown. TSEM's fall from $316.85 to $133.42 on *record* results is a pure multiple event. Bear scenario (10Y → 6%): ~$85.

Tex.) seeks exclusion of US imports of the very SiPho/SiGe products driving growth — the single highest-severity item in the file. Active Israel conflict (Fab 2 + HQ), deliberate *increase* in single-country Japan concentration via the $3B METI-backed build, and the 2023 SAMR veto precedent on China. Clean enforcement record otherwise.

**Bull Case**
- **Contracted, prepay-backed demand**: $1.3B of signed 2027 SiPho contracts and $290M of customer deposits is the hardest demand evidence in the peer group — customers are pre-funding Tower's capacity.
- **Operating leverage inflecting now**: GM 23.2% → 34.0% in four quarters with 62% incremental margins; at the 2028 target ($1.2B net income) the stock trades at ~12.6x.
- **Fortress balance sheet + sovereign backing**: ~$1B net cash plus ~$1B of Japanese grants de-risk the capex; the company survives any downturn and self-funds the ramp.

**Bear Case**

- **Correlated tail risks**: an ITC exclusion order hits the largest revenue region and the highest-margin products simultaneously; Israel physical/geopolitical exposure is unhedgeable within the thesis; Japan/METI adds concentration and grant conditionality.
- **Capital-intensity trap**: negative FCF into a $3B build, depreciation compounding 30%+/yr against a GM guided to fade, and the best-informed insider sold 43.6% of his stock near the top.
