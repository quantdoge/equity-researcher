## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (structurally, not emotionally)

- **Near-unanimous on risk (also 8 of 11):** Macro, Geo Legal, Nonfin, Fin Risk, ESG, Quant, Alt Data and Short all flag elevated-to-severe risk. But note *what kind*: discount-rate risk, governance risk, earnings-quality risk, and a **live −29.4% drawdown on capitulation volume.** No agent finds a business problem.
- **The genuine split is 12 days vs 12 months.** Alt Data and Quant say near-term is bearish; Value and Growth say 12-month is materially undervalued. **Both are correct.** There is no contradiction in a stock that is 41% below intrinsic value and still falling.
- **The single most informative datapoint: the Short Analyst declines to short at $168.89.** A forensic bear who built the full M-Score case, found accruals at 8.4%, AQI at 1.567 and a Beneish score of −1.139, still writes "0% position." When the bear won't press, the asymmetry is not against you.

**Rule applied — risk takes precedence over value:** the fraud/accounting risk does *not* override the value case (no agent found fraud). But the **governance and macro risks override position size**, per mandate. Conviction is high; permissible size is not.
