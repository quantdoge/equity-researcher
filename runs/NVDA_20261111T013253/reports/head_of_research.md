**Investment Thesis**
NVIDIA is an exceptional franchise — 106% YoY revenue growth and *accelerating*, 75% gross margins, a $1T+ disclosed backlog, AA-rated net-cash balance sheet — trading at 18x FY27E earnings because of a company-specific governance shock (CEO scandal, board investigation, class actions) layered on a hostile discount-rate regime, not because of any deterioration in orders, margins, or estimates. The market has cut the multiple in half while raising forward EPS ~30%; our Value and Growth teams independently land 21–69% above spot, and our forensic Short Analyst — having built the full Beneish/accruals case — declines to short at this price. The risk is real but it is a *multiple and timing* risk, not a solvency or fraud risk; size accordingly.

**Bull Case**

- **Asymmetry**: bear-case IV ≈ today's price; base case $214–281; bull ~$430. Downside is a flat-to-modest loss absent a genuine capex bust; upside is 25–100%+. When the forensic bear won't short, the asymmetry is not against you.

**Bear Case**
- **Governance tail is unquantifiable**: a foreign-intelligence probe adjacent to the CEO, criminal-adjacent diversion investigation, no succession plan, and $1.35B of well-timed director selling. Huang's forced departure is a non-diversifiable, unpriced event at a $4T cap.
- **Earnings quality is deteriorating**: accruals 8.4% and rising, DSO +15 days, $30.7B working-capital drain, 61% of revenue from four customers who are simultaneously financing counterparties and future ASIC competitors — circular financing pulls demand forward and warehouses credit risk.
- **Macro/credit regime**: real yields +59bp, a hiking Fed, and HY spreads widening against a debt-funded AI buildout. If the December hike lands and HY breaks 4.5%, 2027 capex guidance gets *cut* and NVDA has another −30–40% at beta 2.2.
