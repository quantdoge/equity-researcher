**Bull Case**
- **Compounding franchise with mechanical re-rating:** ARR +25% (SaaS +36%) runs ~7pts above reported revenue; as the IdentityIQ→SaaS migration laps, reported growth converges *up* toward ARR — cRPO +27% and RPO +30% support it.
- **TAM expansion into machine/AI-agent identity:** genuine greenfield; management targets $2.1B ARR by FY29 (~$800M AI-driven) and AI ARR already exceeds $70M. The Horizons data (79% of enterprises run AI agents, 2% secured) is a real demand asset.
- **Scarcity/strategic value:** post-CyberArk, SAIL is the last scaled pure-play IGA asset; the category takeout comp (~2.5x sales) and a $25–30 Street median PT frame 25–35% upside if execution holds.

**Bear Case**
- **Cash quality:** ~$250M SBC vs ~$200M guided FCF → FCF ex-SBC ≈ −$50M; dilution ~2%/yr; every "profitability" metric is definitional. If SBC doesn't normalize toward ~12–15% of revenue, intrinsic value sits well below price.
- **Structural squeeze:** Microsoft Entra bundling caps pricing (published case study displacing a $1.5M/yr IdentityIQ install); PANW/CyberArk consolidates the platform trade SailPoint was counting on; NRR 113% is the leading indicator to watch.
- **Valuation + event risk into a hostile tape:** ~9.9x EV/ARR, ~70x FY27 FCF, beta 2.08, two double-digit guidance gaps in six months, rising real yields, and a print on 8-Dec after a 16% news-less run-up.

**Key Risks**
1. **8-Dec guidance reset** — revenue growth guided to +16% vs consensus FY28 +19%; alt data shows no demand inflection.
2. **SBC never normalizes** — the single largest thesis risk; per-share value creation lags enterprise value indefinitely.
3. **Macro escalation** — Fed hikes into 5.50%+ 10Y compounds ~15% further multiple compression with higher interest cost and a stronger dollar; retest of $13–15 becomes live.
4. **Sponsor overhang / secondary** — 76–86% Thoma Bravo ownership with a liquidity incentive against a thin float; squeeze-prone in both directions.
5. **Second security incident touching customer identity data** — existential, not costly, for this franchise.
6. **Goodwill impairment** if the stock revisits book value (~$12).
