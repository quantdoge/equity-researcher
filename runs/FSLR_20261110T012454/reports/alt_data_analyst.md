## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL, with a negative near-term tilt**

The alt data tells a coherent, unflattering story that consensus is only partially pricing:

- **Demand-side leading indicators are the weakest link.** Brand attention at 5-year lows, bookings thin at 1.4 GW, guide cut, revenue -23% YoY. Three consecutive quarters of *revenue* misses against *EPS* beats means the beat quality is margin/credit-driven, not demand-driven — a decaying source of alpha.
- **Supply-side indicators are strong but not yet monetizable.** Hiring into Toledo/Gaffney/Decatur and the perovskite license are 2027–28 capacity and cost-curve assets. They don't fix a 15-week bookings gap.
- **The stock's drivers have become exogenous.** Section 232 ($0.38/W floor, Dec 4) and state-level policy post-midterms are the swing factors; neither is visible in alt data yet. The +128% call volume and sentiment flip are *positioning around a political event*, not evidence of demand.
- **Asymmetry:** Downside is that Q4 revenue lands below an already-cut bar and 2027 bookings guidance disappoints. Upside is that the Dec 4 price floor converts directly into ASP and the stock re-rates on the first evidence of re-acceleration in bookings. Alt data can't distinguish these — it can only confirm that the **pre-catalyst demand trough is real**.

**Positioning implication:** this is a *policy-catalyst, event-driven* long, not a fundamentally-confirmed growth long. Alt data does not support adding ahead of a Feb 23 print that is 15 weeks out with revenue tracking below the bar. Watch three alt-data triggers: (1) a turn in "First Solar" Trend interest off the ~20 floor, (2) a booking announcement >2 GW in a quarter at ≥$0.36/W, (3) hiring mix shifting from plant/process roles toward **sales/BD** — that last one would be the earliest honest tell that demand is inflecting.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Supporting quality:** Earnings-surprise history is hard data (7 quarters); price/volume and options flow are hard; news sentiment is well-sampled (20 articles) and thematically consistent; Google Trends series are clean and 12-month and 5-year windows agree.

**Limitations and lags:**
- **Patent filing trend unavailable** (API parse error, one attempt). R&D intensity inferred from news only — unverified.
- **Job postings are a single snapshot count (~265), not a time series.** Cannot distinguish acceleration from steady-state; Indeed/LinkedIn proxies are noisy and geographically skewed toward hourly plant hiring.
- **Web traffic is a qualitative SimilarWeb search snippet**, not monthly visit history — effectively non-signal here.
- **Social sentiment is a web-search proxy, not an API feed** — no true message-volume time series, and StockTwits/Reddit sentiment is reflexive to price, not predictive of fundamentals.
- Google Trends on "First Solar" conflates investor attention with commercial intent; the commercial-purpose keyword ("solar panel") is a poor match for a utility-scale module seller.
- No satellite/geospatial, no channel-check, no module price series. **Section 232 pricing effects (Dec 4) post-date all data collected here** — the single largest swing factor in the thesis is entirely unobserved.
