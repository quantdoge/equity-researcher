### Investment Memo: FSLR — November 10, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (Reduce) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $165 (–14% from current $191.61)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
First Solar is a well-run, fortress-balance-sheet manufacturer whose income statement is, in substance, a statute: FY2025 Section 45X credits of $1.56–1.59B versus net income of $1.53B (~100%+), with the credit legislating itself to zero by 2033 and demand facing a 2028 placed-in-service cliff. The operating indicators that matter for a 12–24 month holder — revenue (–22.8% YoY in Q3'26), backlog (54.5→44.0 GW), book-to-bill (~0.35x) — are all deteriorating, while the quant signature (cheap + negative momentum + negative revisions) is the canonical value-trap pattern. At $191.61 the stock trades ~15–30% above our probability-weighted intrinsic value; the residual bull case is real but is a bet on Washington, not on unit economics.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Hostile rates backdrop (10Y ~5.1%, bear-steepening, Fed re-hiking) for a 1.7-beta, long-duration name; offset by the Section 232 module floor ($0.38/W, effective Dec 4, 2026) — a symmetric benefit since CdTe uses no polysilicon. Sector is a globally oversupplied commodity; FSLR is the protected-margin outlier (37.7% TTM op margin vs 8.5% peer median) but a growth laggard (–0.7% TTM). Both agents: NEUTRAL, policy-administered cycle.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. ~96% of revenue inside one policy regime; active securities class action (Feb 2025–Feb 2026 class period, CEO/CFO named) alleging overstated tariff-management capability — well-corroborated by subsequent guidance cuts. Swift Current warranty suit (new). FSLR's offensive TOPCon patent campaign (JA Solar et al.) is moat defense. Dec 4, 2026 is a two-sided cliff.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL. Ex-credit FY26E EBITDA ~$0.4–0.6B vs $2.65B guided; the annuity must be valued as a declining finite stream. Probability-weighted intrinsic ≈ $145 (bear $100 / base $135 / bull $192); buy price <$135. Financial quality 6/10; cumulative 5-yr FCF negative.

- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE. Solvency is not the risk (net cash ~$1.25B, zero LT debt, 73x coverage, Z-score 3.36). The risks are market risk (57% vol, –53% max drawdown, Sharpe 0.125), a 43% cash decline and negative 9M26 FCF (working-capital driven, reversible), and earnings quality — normalized P/E ~15–17x ex-credit, not 11x.

Broad-based insider selling (~>$17M by the CEO in 2026) with zero open-market buys after a 35% drawdown; warranty-reserve adequacy question (only $50M charged against an alleged >$360M defect population); founder-Chair + 16-year lead director; compensation metrics that reward policy outcomes.

Genuine environmental moat (recycling, low water/chemical intensity, non-China chain) and single-class share structure; offset by the governance overhang (the class action targets the exact tariff narrative the bull case rests on), no SBTi target, and tellurium-refining China exposure that FEOC rules will probe.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL, negative near-term. Brand attention at 5-year lows, Q4 revenue tracking below an already-cut consensus, hiring into US capacity (2027 asset, not current demand); the +128% call-volume spike is positioning around politics, not evidence of orders.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT, moderate conviction — explicitly *not* a fraud short (M-Score –2.47, negative accruals; the accounting is clean). The short is policy-and-valuation: ~$0.17/W of credit against a ~$0.36/W ASP, a shrinking contracted book covering only ~60–65% of annual volume, and consensus models that have not confronted the phase-down. Warns against naked shorting into a binary policy catalyst — defined-risk structures only.

**Bull Case**
- Section 232 (Dec 4) puts a $0.38/W import floor under a product FSLR sells at $0.36/W while consuming no tariffed polysilicon — direct ASP umbrella plus restart optionality on 1.8 GW of idled capacity.
- 8.3x 2027E EPS with a 24% EPS CAGR through 2029, 44 GW / ~$13.5B contracted backlog, net cash, a $500M buyback into a 40% drawdown, and a 71% EPS beat rate — if the credit architecture holds, this is simply cheap.
- Free optionality: perovskite-tandem pilot (H1'27), CuRe, a Democratic Congress potentially restoring 45Y/48E, and tellurium/CdTe as the only scaled non-China technology path.

**Bear Case**
- ~100% of net income is a legislated transfer that phases down 2030→2033; ex-credit the module business earns low-single-digit margins (Q4'24 ex-credit GM ~9% is the proof point) — the market is paying ~13.5x for an annuity priced as perpetual.
- The demand trough is now, not later: Q3 revenue –22.8% YoY, four consecutive backlog declines, book-to-bill ~0.35x, Q4 tracking below consensus, and the 2028 air pocket after the 45Y/48E placed-in-service deadline is visible.
- Value-trap mechanics plus governance decay: negative momentum and revisions, insiders selling with zero buys, a credible class action, and a warranty suit alleging concealment — the "cheap" multiple is the market discounting earnings-durability failure, correctly.

**Key Risks**
1. **45X/FEOC adverse guidance** (Treasury reinterpretation or accelerated phase-down) — compresses earnings and multiple simultaneously; the largest single value risk.
2. **Volume, not price, misses**: backlog fails to stabilize post-232 (watch Q4 print, Feb 23) → 2027 EPS cuts of 20%+.
3. **Rates**: 10Y through 5.5% widens project hurdle rates and 45X-monetization haircuts.
4. **Legal/governance**: class-action motion-to-dismiss outcome; warranty-reserve build; 10b5-1 timing verification on insider sales.
5. **To the downside call itself**: squeeze risk — net cash, buyback, policy optionality, and 57% vol. This is a reduce/underweight, not a short recommendation.
