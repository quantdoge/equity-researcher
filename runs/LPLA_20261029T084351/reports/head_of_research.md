### Investment Memo: LPLA — October 29, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (staged accumulation) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $320 (~19% upside from current $269.19)
**Time Horizon**: 18–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
LPLA is the dominant US independent broker-dealer/wealth platform — $2.6T client assets, 97–98% retention, 18% ROIC — trading at ~11x forward adjusted EPS and ~7x EV/adjusted EBITDA after a 33% de-rating from its February 2026 high, a valuation the fundamentals (20%+ adjusted EPS growth, $1.2B annualized buyback) do not justify. The discount is explained by three identifiable risks rather than franchise decay: a pending cash-sweep pricing review (H1-27), Commonwealth retention cut to 83%, and organic growth decelerating to 3–5%. The central unresolved question is earnings quality — three years of negative reported FCF against $3B of net income — which is why this is a sized, staged position and not a full-weight buy.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector rated ATTRACTIVE — secular fee-based mix shift, consolidation economics favoring the scaled buyer; LPLA is the fastest grower (20.2% 5-yr rev CAGR) at the widest discount to peers (~12x adj. fwd vs ~16x median). Macro is transitional and hawkish (fed funds 3.75–4.00%, 10Y ~5.0%, CPI 3.7% rising): higher short rates help sweep revenue (+$40–70M per 25bp) but a 5% long end compresses the multiple. Bear macro scenario (credit repricing while Fed hikes) implies high-$180s/low-$200s.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Geopolitical risk ~nil (US-only revenue). Legal/regulatory is the binding constraint: >$100M in penalties across SEC/FINRA/states (AML $18M, recordkeeping ~$50M, FINRA $5.5M+), with a repeat-failure supervision pattern. Key offset: SEC closed the cash-sweep probe (Jan-26) with no action; the class action survives only on a monetary unjust-enrichment theory.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: BUY. Base IV ~$330 (range $250–400); 11.3x FY26E adj. EPS, 6.5% owner-earnings yield. Crux: ~half of gross profit is the spread on $57B of client cash — rate- and regulation-sensitive. Negative tangible book (–$631M); goodwill 112% of equity.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY. ~22% adjusted EPS CAGR to 2029E ($42), PEG ≈ 0.5, 7/7 EPS beats, ~$390 median target. Caveat: true organic NNA growth is only ~4–5%; headline 20%+ is M&A- and market-assisted.
- **Quantitative Signals**: SELL — the lone outright bear signal. 4 of 5 factors negative: 12-1 momentum –6.8%, Sharpe –0.77, ROE 51%→16% over three years, accruals 6.95%, F-score 3/9, negative FY25 operating cash flow. Sole positive (FY29 revisions) is long-dated and low-reliability.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE. Credit survives a –20% revenue shock (4.3x coverage, 2.5x leverage); no maturity before 2028; IG ratings (BBB/BBB-). Flags: coverage downtrend 9.8x→5.7x, interest expense +220% since 2022, an unexplained ~$600–700M FY25 below-the-line charge, and negative reported FCF 3 of 4 years (distorted by, but not fully excused by, broker-dealer working capital).
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. The mid-November 2026 Commonwealth conversion (~$300B of assets) is the largest operational event in company history, with retention already cut to 83%. Recruited assets –26% YoY. CEO terminated for cause in Oct-2024; insider activity shows zero open-market buys. Chronic supervisory/AML control-debt.
- **ESG**: AVERAGE / NEUTRAL. Strong governance mechanics (independent chair, supermajority elimination, single-class stock) offset by a below-peer conduct record. Monitor, not a thesis driver.
- **Alternative Data**: BEARISH (moderate). Search interest –22% from the Feb peak; recruiting intake –26% YoY; –15.2% post-print on 5x volume. Leading indicators say growth is slowing into the conversion; do not buy the dip on a demand-inflection thesis.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT, moderate conviction — but explicitly a quality-of-earnings/roll-up de-rating short, not fraud. Adjusted EPS estimated ~25–30% ahead of economics; goodwill impairment is a live dated catalyst. Concedes that <12x forward with the SEC sweep probe closed is an uncomfortable short, and would cover on Commonwealth retention holding ≥83% plus FCF normalization.

**Bull Case**
- Cheapest scaled wealth consolidator: ~11x forward adjusted EPS for a ~20% EPS grower with 97–98% asset retention and a genuine scale moat in compliance/technology.
- Commonwealth conversion (Q4-26) adds ~$415M run-rate EBITDA; buyback of ~$1.2B annualized (~5.6% of cap) provides a bid; 100% EPS beat rate into the Feb-27 print.
- Largest federal tail risk removed (SEC sweep probe closed); hawkish rate path actually supports sweep revenue near-term; multiple re-rates if organic NNA holds ≥5%.

**Bear Case**
- Earnings don't convert to cash: 2023–25 net income $3.0B vs FCF –$1.2B, funded by $4.3B of debt/equity; goodwill 112% of equity, tangible book negative — impairment is a real catalyst.
- Cash-sweep economics (~half of gross profit) face a repricing review (H1-27) plus litigation; a 25% haircut ≈ $4.70/share, ~20% of FY26 adjusted EPS — which would *validate* the current price, not discount it.
- Organic engine decelerating (NNA 3–5%, recruited assets –26% YoY, payout rate 88% and rising, ~$640M/yr in transition-assistance costs); a botched November conversion accelerates advisor attrition.

**Key Risks**
1. Commonwealth conversion failure or retention printing below 78% (event window: weeks).
2. Sweep pricing review outcome >20% revenue haircut, or adverse class certification.
3. Goodwill impairment on the $6.2B intangible base at the FY26 test.
4. Hawkish macro tail: 10Y >5.5% with HY widening from 3.18% OAS — equity drawdown hits fees, sweep balances, and recruiting simultaneously.
5. Organic NNA below 3% for two consecutive quarters — the structural-growth-ceiling case.
