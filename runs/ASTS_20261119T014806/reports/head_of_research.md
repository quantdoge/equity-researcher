### Investment Memo: ASTS — 19 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (new capital: PASS) | Conviction: High
**Price Target**: $20 (−22% from current $25.49)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
AST SpaceMobile has real technology, a real regulatory moat-in-progress (248-satellite FCC grant), and a genuinely exceptional distribution footprint (60+ MNOs, 3B subscribers, AT&T/Verizon/Vodafone/Google on the cap table). But the *equity* is priced for a base-case successful build-out while carrying bear-case impairment risk of −70% to −85%, a structural dilution engine (+94% share count in 21 months, $4.1B of converts struck at $96–$120 that are economically straight debt), six straight quarters of execution failure, and close to the worst possible macro regime for a 15–20-year-duration asset. Eleven of eleven specialist lenses — value, credit, quant, forensic, macro, legal — converge independently on the same conclusion: admire the mission, don't own the stock at this price.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: 10Y at 5.13% with real yields ~2.94% (near 40-yr highs) is a −15/−20% multiple headwind for a ~100%-terminal-value equity; D2D sub-sector is pre-commercial ($0.56B), a two-horse race where SpaceX (650+ D2C sats, own launch) is scaling while ASTS slips (45-sat target pushed to mid-2027). Sector rated Neutral; macro Unfavourable.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH risk. Regulatory permission *is* the product — US-only FCC grant, FCC blocking EU pointing, country-by-country licensing treadmill, contested Ligado L-band dependency, plus an active securities class action (class period Mar 2025–Jul 2026) now consolidating.
- **Value**: Intrinsic value $15–30, midpoint ~$22 ≈ today's price. No margin of safety: the *successful* base case gets you to roughly the current quote; bear case is −70/−85%. Verdict: Sell/Avoid.
- **Growth**: TAM math is genuinely compelling (~0.04% of the existing 3B-subscriber partner base at $1/mo justifies the EV), but it's a manufacturing-and-launch execution story that has failed six consecutive quarters. HOLD, explicitly gated on proof.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Strong Sell — bottom of sector on all four classic factors; beta 3.73, 105% vol, −38% in the last month; falling-knife regime, not basing. Caveat: factor model has limited validity for pre-revenue buildouts; quant is a veto, not a thesis.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. Net cash flipped to $1.32B net debt in nine months; ~18-month runway; interest ≈85% of revenue; Z-score in distress zone; $4.1B of out-of-the-money converts = $4.1B of 2032–36 cash repayment against $2.8B cash. Not Critical only because no near-term maturities.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Insider selling ~23:1 with zero open-market buys by any operating executive; CEO/Chair duality with ~71–76% voting control; dual dependence on SpaceX as both launch supplier and principal rival; six straight earnings misses feeding the litigation narrative.
- **ESG**: Laggard. Governance 3/10 (controlled company, counterparty execs on board, no ESG disclosure, live §10(b) action); astronomy/debris licence conditions are the binding environmental risk to revenue timing, not carbon.
- **Alternative Data**: Bearish near-term — retail attention at 12-month lows, launch cadence slipping, and the March 2027 consensus ($75M revenue, +77% QoQ) looks unachievable; revision risk is downward. Offset: 22–36% short float = live squeeze optionality.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: Earnings quality LOW but explicitly *not* fraud — the burn is capex, not accruals. The real forensic flags: 24-year implied depreciation life vs 5–7-year satellite lives (unverified, top item in the K), 266-day DSO with related-party customers, and a Q4 guidance bar of $61.4M+ vs a $54.3M record quarter.

**Bull Case**
- **Macro reversal**: if the Sept 2026 hike proves a policy error and real 10Y yields retrace to ~1.8%, a 15–20-yr duration equity re-rates 60–90% on the discount rate alone — consensus is not positioned for this.
- **Distribution is already built**: the only missing asset is the constellation; $1.3B of revenue needs ~0.04% penetration of the partner base. Commercial activation + 24+ satellites in 2027 converts partners to revenue.
- **Positioning asymmetry**: 22–36% of float short, sentiment at a 12-month extreme — any launch/cadence headline can trigger a violent 30–50% squeeze.

**Bear Case**
- **No margin of safety**: at ~63–147x sales (share-count dependent), the base case ≈ today's price; the bear case is −70/−85%. Unfavourable asymmetry, full stop.
- **Reflexive dilution spiral**: ~$1.5–1.8B annual burn, ~18-month runway, equity/convert markets effectively shut at $25 — the next raise is likely both necessary and severely dilutive.
- **Execution and competitive asymmetry**: 45-satellite target slipped to mid-2027 while SpaceX fields 650+ D2C satellites, owns the launch pad ASTS rides on, and just bought ~65MHz of EchoStar spectrum.

**Key Risks**
1. Q4'26 revenue miss vs the $61.4M low-end bar (March 2027 print) → guidance cut and re-test of lows.
2. Any equity/convert raise priced below $25 → confirms the dilution spiral; thesis-level negative.
3. Further launch slippage (New Glenn/Blue Origin) or SpaceX converting spectrum into carrier-priced D2D.
4. Class-action motion-to-dismiss ruling; Ligado/FCC order; FCC/EU astronomy-and-debris conditions.
5. Data integrity: unresolved market-cap discrepancy ($7.6B on 300M shares vs $10.4B profile implying ~410M — ~37% latent dilution unverified); 10-K/14A inaccessible (SEC 403), so depreciation policy and revenue-recognition detail unconfirmed.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
PASS for new capital. For existing holders: REDUCE to ≤0.5% notional maximum — at beta 3.73, a 0.5% position already contributes ~1.9% beta-equivalent risk; sizing on notional is a hidden 4x concentration. Note ASTS is *not* a telecom exposure (corr to VZ/T/TMUS ≈ −0.1) — it stacks onto any existing space/speculative-growth sleeve and adds a levered short-duration factor bet. Trim into any squeeze rally toward $45 (most recent sell-side targets); do not hold for stale $90+ targets. Re-entry is catalyst-gated, not price-gated: (1) commercial service activation with disclosed paying revenue, (2) Q4 revenue ≥$61.4M with 24+ satellites in orbit and FY27 guide ≥$400M, (3) no sub-$25 raise in between — and for value buyers, only below ~$15.

*Bottom line: the company may well succeed; the stock can still lose you money. The mission is funded by the shareholder, priced at the base case, and one slip away from a dilutive raise. Sell / avoid; revisit on proof, not on hope.*
