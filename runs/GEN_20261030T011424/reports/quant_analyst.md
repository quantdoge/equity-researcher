## 3. Composite Quant Signal: **NEUTRAL**

Latest analyst action is constructive (Morgan Stanley PT raised to **$34** on Oct 29) but sell-side targets were cut repeatedly through 2026 (RBC $30→$26→$24), and the average sits near the current price.
