### Data Limitations & Lag
- Google Trends is a 12-month weekly index, last point wk ending ~Nov 15 (retail attention, not demand).
- Job postings are current-day counts; no trend rate, no role decomposition.
- News sentiment is keyword-based (positive/negative word counts in headlines) — upgrades inflate it.
- 13F data lags one quarter. Guidance figures are drawn from press coverage, not filings.
- **Key risk the alt data cannot see:** customer concentration and inventory build flagged by bearish write-ups. A single hyperscaler program pivot would invalidate the $40B FY27 frame, and no alt feed here would flag it early.

**Bottom line:** Alt data says the business is accelerating into the Jan-27 print while the equity has been repriced by a sector-wide AI de-rating. Signal is for an above-consensus Q4 — but position sizing should reflect that CLS is currently a high-beta proxy on NVDA sentiment rather than a standalone story.
