## SECTOR BRIEF — BSP (Bending Spoons S.p.A.)

**Sector:** Technology / Software – Application (consumer-app & SMB-software portfolio consolidator)
**Mkt cap $29.4bn | Price $46.29 | Beta 1.48 | 2,187 FTE | IPO 1-Jul-2026**

### 1. Sector Overview

BSP sits at the intersection of two markets: the ~$1.43trn enterprise software market (Gartner 2026E, +15.2% YoY) and the ~$330bn mobile/consumer application market growing toward $1.02trn by 2034 (15.1% CAGR). The critical 2026 development is that *spend is growing while valuations are compressing*: the SEG SaaS Index fell 25.7% YTD through March 2026 and roughly $2trn of software market cap has been repriced as AI agents erode per-seat monetisation (per-seat pricing adoption fell from 21% to 15% of contracts in a year). Within that, BSP occupies a narrow, highly specialised sub-niche — the acquisition and AI-assisted re-engineering of mature, cash-generative apps (Evernote, WeTransfer, Meetup, Remini, AOL, Vimeo, Eventbrite, and per Q3 commentary Airtable and Miro). This is a **capital-allocation business dressed as a software business**: the sector's structural problem (cheap, unloved, sub-scale assets) is precisely its raw material. Concentration in the sub-niche is high — Constellation Software, Vista, Thoma Bravo and IAC compete for the same targets — but asset prices are depressed, making it a buyer's market.

### 2. Key Tailwinds

- **A buyer's market in software assets.** Intuit is exiting Mailchimp at $3.68bn EV versus the ~$12bn it paid in 2021 — a ~70% discount. Distressed/strategic divestitures are abundant, and management states it could deploy **$4–5bn on M&A in 2027**. Falling asset prices mechanically raise the return on BSP's core skill.
- **AI-driven cost deflation on the cost side, not just the revenue side.** BSP's full-stack team rewrites acquired codebases in Python/FastAPI/TypeScript using AI tooling — collapsing maintenance headcount on assets it then runs for cash. FY2025 gross margin rose 251bps to 65.6% and operating margin to 21.3% while revenue nearly doubled; Q3-26 adjusted operating income rose 130% to $424m.
- **App-store economics loosening.** Apple's 2026 EU terms cut App Store fees to as low as 5% and permit web billing; Google Play's EEA standard fee fell to 20% + 5% billing. Web billing retains ~38% more margin on a $10 subscription — a direct, structural margin tailwind for a consumer-subscription portfolio.

### 3. Key Headwinds

- **Organic growth is near-zero.** Q3-26 revenue grew 125% but **organic growth was just 3%**, held back by Remini declines and disruption from AOL's adtech transition. The headline 94.7% FY25 growth (83.7% two-year CAGR) is almost entirely acquired. Against a peer median of +4.3%, BSP's *organic* trajectory is in line at best — the entire growth premium is financial engineering.
- **AI substitution risk sits squarely on its own portfolio.** Mailchimp (email marketing), Evernote (notes), WeTransfer (file transfer) and AOL (adtech) are precisely the seat- or storage-based tools most exposed to AI-native substitutes. BSP buys businesses in structural decline and manages the harvest — but the harvest window may be shortening.
- **Leverage and earnings quality.** FY2025 interest expense jumped to $142.6m from $32.6m (+337%), driving net income to **-$0.1m** despite $278m of operating income — a 0.0% net margin vs a 17.7% peer median. FY2023 net income ($160.8m) exceeded operating income ($84.3m), so the earnings line is dominated by non-operating items. An acquisition programme funded at this pace into a rising-rate environment is the key balance-sheet risk, and the IPO lock-up expiry is an overhang.

### 5. BSP's Competitive Position: **AVERAGE** (strong operator, weak franchise)

**Rationale.** BSP is a best-in-class *operator* and a below-average *franchise*. Its cost discipline, AI-rewrite capability and sourcing network are genuinely differentiated — few buyers can absorb a $3.68bn Mailchimp and a $4–5bn 2027 pipeline. But Porter's forces cut against durability: **supplier power is high** (Apple/Google gatekeeping, though easing), **substitute threat is high and rising** (AI-native tools attacking its exact portfolio), **buyer power is high** (consumer subscribers with near-zero switching costs and rising churn on price increases), and **product-level barriers to entry are low** — BSP owns brands, not moats. Its advantage is capital and process, not network effects or switching costs. The 3% organic growth is the tell: absent M&A, the underlying assets shrink. Market performance reflects this — after listing at $31 and closing day one at $40.50, the stock ran to $59.50 (15-Oct) and has since retraced 22% to $46.29, a 1.48 beta and a wide $30–60 range indicating an unsettled shareholder base.

### 6. Sector Rating: **NEUTRAL**

The broad Software-Application sector is **Neutral**: demand is healthy (+15.2% spend growth, 15.1% app-market CAGR) but the monetisation model is mid-transition and multiples are still de-rating as AI compresses seat-based revenue — you are paid to be selective, not to be long the sector. The **consolidation sub-niche BSP occupies is Attractive**, because depressed asset prices plus motivated divestors create an unusually good buying window. The offset is that BSP's own organic engine is idling at 3%, so the attractive part of the sector accrues to *deal flow*, not to *hold-and-hold* returns — and that makes the rating sensitive to financing costs and the 2027 M&A pipeline rather than to sector growth.
