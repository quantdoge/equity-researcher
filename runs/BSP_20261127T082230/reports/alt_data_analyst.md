## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH**
Broad, synchronous demand decay across the portfolio — and specifically in the two assets just paid $5.0B for — argues the 3% organic growth is a ceiling, not a floor. The "harvest" playbook (raise price, cut cost, accept churn) genuinely works on margins and will keep producing adjusted-EPS beats, but it consumes the asset base. Every acquisition now requires *more* capital ($1.3B equity + debt) against *declining* underlying demand.

**Key counter-argument, stated fairly:** BSP's declining search interest is partly the *intended* output of its strategy, not an accident — margin expansion (+130% adj. op income on +125% revenue) is real, and volume decay can coexist with revenue growth for several quarters. This is a terminal-value debate, not a next-quarter miss.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Weakening factors: only 4 months public and **2 quarters** of surprise history; Google Trends is a *volume* proxy that decouples from *priced* revenue under an aggressive price-hike model; web traffic is single-month MoM snapshots, not a time series; job postings and patents both failed/immaterial; social is qualitative web-search proxy, not API counts. Strengthening factors: data is **current to Nov 15–25, 2026**, the demand decay is directionally unanimous across six independently-measured assets, and it independently reproduces the exact drag management disclosed.

**Trade framing:** Alt data does not support paying above the $44 average target for a 3%-organic, debt-funded roll-up whose acquired assets are shrinking faster post-close. The signal argues for *fading rallies* (the $59 Oct high, the +15% Nov 23 pop) rather than shorting into the M&A headline flow. Watch **Remini and Airtable trends weekly into the Feb 11, 2027 print** — if Remini's baseline keeps sliding below 16, organic growth likely turns negative and the "harvest" narrative breaks.
