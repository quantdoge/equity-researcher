### Investment Memo: BSP (Bending Spoons S.p.A.) — 27 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (Avoid for new money; reduce existing positions) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $40 (−14% from current $46.29) | Synthesis range $32–48
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Bending Spoons is a best-in-class *operator* wrapped in a below-average *franchise*: a capital-allocation machine that buys declining consumer software at distressed multiples and harvests it via AI-driven cost surgery and price increases. The arithmetic doesn't work at $46 — organic growth is 3%, ROIC (7.6%) is below cost of capital, ~$4bn of ~9% debt (→ ~$7bn pro forma for Mailchimp) carries only a ~23% covenant cushion, and the price embeds ~22% owner-earnings compounding for a decade. This is not a fraud — the forensic analyst explicitly clears the accounting, and Berkshire Hathaway's ~$930m anchor at ~$33–35 is real validation — which is why conviction is Medium, not High, and why this is a Sell/Avoid rather than an aggressive short.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro **Unfavourable** — real 10Y ~2.8–3.0% (nominal 5.13%, +22bp in 3 sessions), Fed hiking into 3.82% CPI, HY OAS +106bp in two months: the worst configuration for a 1.48-beta, debt-funded acquirer whose CEO has tied a $4–5bn 2027 M&A programme to financing conditions. Sector **Neutral**, but the consolidation sub-niche is attractive — depressed asset prices (Mailchimp at ~70% below Intuit's 2021 price) are a genuine buyer's market; value accrues to deal flow, not hold-and-hold returns.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium**. Cleanest revenue mix in large-cap software (~85% US/UK/EU-4/CA/AU/JP, zero sanctions hotspots). The binding constraint is merger control: 5+ reviews in 18 months, and the 3%-organic model requires repeatedly clearing US/EU antitrust — Mailchimp ($3.68bn) is pending with an outside date of 19-Aug-27. FPI status means no 20-F/10-K yet; litigation contingencies are unverifiable — itself a diligence gap.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **Sell**. ~60x owner earnings (FCF net of ~$250m SBC), ~10x sales, tangible book −$4.9bn, gross margin collapsed 67.9% → 52.9% in two quarters. DCF fair value $12–20 even on generous assumptions; sell-side targets span $36–72 (2x dispersion = no consensus on normalized earnings).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **Buy (the sole bull)**. 20x FY27E EPS of $2.28 against +53% estimate revisions, 100% beat rate (avg +60%), PEG ~0.4; FV $55–59. But note: FY27 consensus is literally FY26 + Mailchimp — the street has modeled *zero* organic growth; the "growth" is one acquisition.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral/conflicted** — Value 12/100, Low-Vol 8/100 vs Revisions 84/100, Momentum 62/100. All price statistics rest on 103 trading days; beta 0.92 is statistically insignificant (95% CI −0.5 to +2.3); Sharpe 0.80 is a two-gap-day artifact (geometric ~0.35). Do not feed these into a factor model at normal weights.
- **Financial Risk**: **High**. BB-flat credit; net debt doubled to $3.98bn in nine months; EBIT/interest 1.86x; net debt/EBITDA ~3.1x vs a 4.0x covenant — a 20% revenue decline breaches it. Interest (~$413m annualized) consumes ~50% of GAAP operating income. Altman Z 0.65 is a book-equity artifact, but the leverage signal is real.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **High**. Weak governance: 82.7% founder voting control, CEO/Chair duality, ~9% float. Founders converted Class A → ordinary (sellable) shares on 20 Nov ahead of the ~28 Dec lock-up — a watch item, not confirmed selling. Integration overload: three mega-deals landing simultaneously with 60–80% headcount cuts; >90% of production code now AI-authored.
- **ESG**: **Laggard / Negative**. The single sharpest point in the file: the growth lever *is* a regulated lever. Documented 2–12x renewal increases sit directly in the path of the EU Digital Fairness Act + CPC Regulation (fines to 6% of global turnover ≈ $180m+), plus AGCM/CMA/FTC subscription enforcement and Remini biometric exposure. No environmental disclosure or CSRD readiness.
- **Alternative Data**: **Bearish**. Synchronous search-demand decay across the portfolio: Airtable −60%, Remini −45%, Evernote −40%, Mailchimp −35%, Vimeo −15% YoY — and decay continued *after* the Q3 print. This independently reproduces management's disclosed 3% organic number and suggests it is a ceiling, not a floor.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Short, moderate conviction — de-rating thesis, not fraud**. Cash conversion is clean (negative accruals, DSRI 0.865); the M-Score flag is an acquisition-accounting artifact. The case: adjusted operating income ($424m) excludes the amortized cost of acquired assets (GAAP: $216m), 2027 consensus growth is 100% one deal, and the 28-Dec lock-up plus lapping comps will force the 3% organic figure into the headline.

**Bull Case**
- **The deal machine is genuinely excellent**: buying Mailchimp at 2.9x sales vs Intuit's 9x, converting ~20% margins to 54% adjusted margins within quarters; a buyer's market in software assets with $4–5bn of 2027 deployment capacity makes each acquisition potentially day-one accretive.
- **Revision momentum is elite**: +53% FY27 EPS revisions in 3 months, 100% beat rate, consensus demonstrably stale-low (excludes full Miro/Airtable contributions); February guidance above $5bn would force another leg of upgrades.
- **Smart-money anchor**: Berkshire added $330m in October at ~$33–35; interest is fully hedged with $3.36bn liquidity and no maturity wall — levered, but not fragile.

**Bear Case**
- **The core is melting**: 3% organic growth *while actively raising prices* implies mid-to-high single-digit volume decline; alt data shows the newest, most expensive assets (Airtable, Mailchimp) decaying fastest. ROIC has fallen 11.6% → 7.6% as deal sizes and entry multiples escalate.
- **The balance sheet has no shock absorber**: 1.86x EBIT coverage, 23% covenant cushion, gross margin down 15pts in two quarters; the bear macro scenario (Dec +50bp, 10Y >5.5%) hits the multiple, the Mailchimp funding cost, and the lock-up simultaneously — a correlated left tail pointing to ~$30 (−35%).
- **Regulation targets the exact profit engine**: EU DFA/CPC, FTC ROSCA and biometric privacy rules all attack the price-hike/renewal mechanics and data assets that produce the margin expansion.

**Key Risks**
1. **Covenant/credit event**: net debt/EBITDA >3.8x or a second quarter of gross margin <50% breaks the credit story the equity is an option on.
2. **Mailchimp failure**: second request, outside-date extension, or a dilutive equity raise to close — the single largest integration ever attempted at 5x prior deal scale.
3. **Lock-up + insider signal**: 28-Dec expiry into a hawkish tape; monitor for 10b5-1 plans following the 20-Nov founder share conversions.
4. **Organic inflection the other way**: Mailchimp at 40%+ margins and organic >8% would invalidate the thesis — watch the organic-growth line every quarter; it is the entire debate in one number.
5. **Data gaps flagged to IC**: no 20-F/10-K/DEF 14A (FPI), no valid peer set retrievable by any agent, Q4'25 feed row corrupt, SBC unreported in Q2/Q3-26.
