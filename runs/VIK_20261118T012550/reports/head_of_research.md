**Investment Thesis**
Viking is the highest-quality franchise in cruise — a 52% share of North American outbound European river, 21.9% ROIC, F-Score 8/9, net leverage 1.07x, and a 2027 book already 68% sold at +8% pricing — but the equity already prices that quality at 24.3x EV/EBITDA and 36.7x earnings versus a 15x / 19.6x peer norm. The franchise debate is settled (even the short desk found no fraud: Beneish −3.30); what remains is a discount-rate dispute, and the current macro regime (Fed hiking into a supply shock, 10Y at 5.13%) is exactly the one that de-rates a 1.6-beta long-duration compounder — it already drove a 29% Aug–Sep de-rating. Recurring European low-water disruption (~$200M Q3 revenue+margin hit) and a 1.7% effective tax rate facing a structural step-up are both under-priced.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**

- Low water is structural, not weather: ~70% of river capacity days hit, ~$200M Q3 impact, vouchers capping 2027 net yield at low-to-mid single digits, Mizuho modelling drag through 2028.
- The 1.7% tax rate is NOL-shielded and finite; normalisation to ~15% removes 13–15% of EPS with zero operational change — unmodeled by the bulls.
