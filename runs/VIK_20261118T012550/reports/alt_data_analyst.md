## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **BELOW consensus (Q4 only)**

Q4'26 consensus: **EPS $0.71, revenue $1.907B (+10.6% YoY)**. Alt-data says that revenue growth is optimistic:
- **~70% of Q4 river capacity days were affected** by low water as of mid-November, with **~20% of guests on impacted itineraries cancelling** (12% per call transcript).
- Q3 already absorbed **~$100M of revenue and ~$100M of gross margin** from water levels; occupancy fell to **91.3%**; adj. EBITDA **−4.3% to $674M** on revenue **+9.2% to $2.2B**.
- **Vouchers/travel credits depress 2027 reported net yield** — guided to **low-to-mid single digits**, an accounting drag independent of demand.

**Surprise history is deteriorating:** 71.4% beat rate / +4.9% avg, but the **last quarter broke the pattern at −12.6%** and 2 of the last 4 were negative. Expect post-print estimate cuts to Q4/FY26 and to FY27 reported yield.

**However — split the signal by horizon.** The 2027 *booked* position (68%/77%/56%, rates +4.1%) plus ~60% of cancelled guests already rebooked (mostly into 2027 river) means the Q4 miss is a **weather-and-voucher optics event, not a demand event.**

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH (6–12 month) / Below-consensus near term**

**Thesis:** Alt data and management disclosure agree that Viking has a **demand problem of zero** and a **weather problem of large** — a distinction the tape validated by buying the miss. Forward-dated search intent (+~80% YoY), a 68%/77% booked 2027 book, +4.1% pricing on 2027 river itineraries, and $350M of buyback all point to a 2027 recovery that consensus has not yet re-rated for. The single most load-bearing alt signal — the forward-year Google Trends keyword — is low-volume and discretized, so the +80% is directional, not precise.

**Why not low:** the demand read is **triangulated against hard booked-position and pricing disclosure** from the Nov 17 call, and the volume/price reversal on earnings day independently confirms the market reached the same conclusion. Sequencing and recency are excellent — all signals are 1–11 days old.

**Data lags to flag:** Google Trends is a 1-week lagged, weekly-bucketed proxy; 13F holdings are up to 45 days stale; the true test of the forward-booking signal is **wave season (Jan–Mar 2027)**, which no data source yet shows. Alt data is a leading indicator here — not a definitive one.
