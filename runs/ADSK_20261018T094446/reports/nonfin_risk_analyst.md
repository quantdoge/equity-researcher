### 1. Governance Quality Rating: ADEQUATE (trending better, with a serious oversight blemish)

Evidence for:
- **CEO/Chair separation confirmed.** FY2025 proxy language: "we separate the positions of CEO and non-executive Chair of the Board," with a Lead Independent Director required if the Chair is not independent. Andrew Anagnost (CEO since 2017) does not hold the Chair.
- **Activist-driven refresh.** Starboard Value built a ~$500M stake (2024), pushed for margin discipline/cost cuts and board change, and signed a **settlement agreement dated 23 April 2025** ahead of the 2025 annual meeting (proxy fight averted). FY2026 targets (revenue + FCF) now anchor the story.
- **Securities class action largely defeated.** The FCF/non-GAAP class period (1 Jun 2023 – 16 Apr 2024) case: court **granted defendants' motion to dismiss the Second Amended Complaint on 26 Jan 2026**; lead plaintiff filed further papers 12 Mar 2026 (appeal/amendment path). A favorable but not final outcome.

Evidence against:
- **2024 accounting-integrity episode is the defining governance event.** Audit Committee investigation (disclosed Apr 2024; results 31 May 2024) found charges were timed in ways that "generally served to reduce reported free cash flow and/or lower reported margin in the current period" — i.e., practices that flattered FCF/margin optics. FY2024 10-K was delayed (NT 10-K), a **material weakness in internal control over financial reporting/disclosure controls** was disclosed, and the **CFO was reassigned** (June 2024). No restatement.
- **Incentives still keyed to the disputed metric.** FY2025 PSU vesting "was based on revenue and free cash flow" — the exact measure at the center of the 2024 episode. Mitigants exist (remediation, new CFO), but this is an incentive-relapse risk, not a closed chapter.
- **Insider-selling culture / no visible alignment buying** (see §3).

**Verdict:** Board structure and activism response are sound; financial-reporting oversight has a documented failure within the last 30 months. Not "Strong."

### 3. Insider Activity Analysis — NET SELLING, ZERO BUYING

From 40 recent Form 4 filings (Jun 2025 → Jun 2026):
- **~35 of 40 are non-issuer filings** across **at least 12 distinct reporting-person CIKs** — i.e., broad-based, multi-executive disposal activity, not one isolated trade.
- **Heavy clustering:** 9 filings on **20 Jun 2025** and 10 on **22 Jun 2026** — consistent with annual RSU/PSU vest-and-sell cycles (equity settlement dates). Follow-on sales and tax-withholding dispositions recur through Jul 2025, Aug–Sep 2025, Dec 2025, Mar–Apr 2026 and Jun 2026.
- **No open-market purchases identified** in the filing set. Not a single Form 4 code "P" appears among the sampled filings.
- **Largest reported single sale:** EVP & COO **Steven Blum, ~$7.3M** (per Yahoo Finance insider coverage); other measured sales include ~917-share blocks at ~$325 and ~202k-share blocks around $498k notional.
- **Critical context:** these sales occurred while the shares fell from ~$316 (Oct 2025) to a **52-week low of $187.72 (22 Jun 2026)** — a **-42% peak-to-trough drawdown** — and now $239.90 (-26% off the high, ~-24% YoY). Insiders sold into a declining market on scheduled plans and **declined to buy the dip**.

**Signal:** mechanically explainable (10b5-1/vesting) but **directionally bearish**. Absence of insider buying at multi-year lows is a genuine vote of no confidence in near-term re-rating, especially alongside the "undervalued growth" sell-side narrative.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Execution instability / serial restructuring.** Workforce cut ~**1,350 (~9%) in Feb 2025**, then a further **~1,000 (~7%) in the FY2026/FY2027 period** — two rounds inside 18 months, plus management churn (CFO reassigned 2024; **AEC EVP Amy Bunszel retiring**, replaced by Diana Colella from 2 Nov 2026). Repeated cuts while guiding to margin expansion raise delivery, morale and institutional-knowledge risk — and increase reliance on a handful of leaders (**key-person risk** concentrated on CEO Anagnost and a thinned bench).
2. **Cloud data durability & business continuity in Autodesk Construction Cloud.** Customer-facing discussion (Oct 2025–2026) that ACC does **not guarantee backups or point-in-time recovery** of project data. For AEC customers, lost/corrupted project data is existential; a single high-profile data-loss or extended-outage event could trigger contractual liability, churn and reputational damage. Trust Center shows a steady stream of security advisories (MITM, cleartext-transmission flaws).
3. **Channel/partner model transition + M&A integration load.** The new "Autodesk buying process" (effective 10 Jun 2026), multi-user adjustments and discount removal (industry commentary cites **15–30% effective price impact**) shift economics abruptly for resellers — creating partner attrition, gray-market/audit friction (third-party "AutodeskAudits" ecosystem), and renewal risk. Simultaneously integrating **MaintainX** (closed 3 Aug 2026) into FY2027 guidance after activist criticism of M&A discipline adds integration/assumption risk.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — ELEVATED

- **Pricing/backlash cycle.** An **8% list increase (Apr 2026; 9–10% on collections)** layered on discount removal and a new buying process is a classic negative-sentiment trigger in a locked-in, high-switching-cost installed base. Community channels (CAD Managers Unite, LinkedIn) show active frustration; this is the most likely source of a viral customer-relations episode and renewal pushback.
- **Accounting-integrity stain persists.** "Autodesk admitted to timing charges to meet free cash flow targets" remains the dominant third-party framing of the company a full two years on. It undermines credibility of every non-GAAP/FCF-based guidance metric and any "beat and raise."
- **Activist/negative-narrative overhang.** A ~25% YTD decline, repeated post-earnings drops (Q3 EPS guide below consensus, Aug 2026), and a widely covered Starboard campaign keep ADSK in the "broken growth story" bucket — amplifying the impact of each new negative datapoint.
- **Positives:** no product recall/safety scandal, no major breach disclosed, strong ESG/brand equity in design & manufacturing, and top-tier engineering goodwill (Autodesk University 2026 agentic-AI launch received constructive coverage).

---

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH** (upper end of Moderate–High)

Rationale: governance structure is sound and improving, and legal exposure has partially de-risked (class action dismissed). But three factors push this above Moderate — (i) **an unresolved SEC/DOJ accounting probe** with no publicly verifiable closure, (ii) **a documented ICFR material weakness and compensation still tied to the manipulated metric**, and (iii) **serial restructuring + broad insider selling + aggressive pricing** creating correlated execution and reputational risk while the equity is in a 26% drawdown. These are largely *unpriced tail risks*: each is low-probability but individually capable of a double-digit re-rating.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **SEC/DOJ probe status unverified.** I could not confirm closure or settlement in any public source, and the FY2026 10-K (filed 3 Mar 2026) legal-proceedings section was not retrievable. **Action: obtain Item 3 (Legal Proceedings) of the FY2026 10-K and the latest 10-Q directly from IR/EDGAR before publishing the memo.** If the language still reads "intends to cooperate," treat as a live, unquantified overhang.
2. **FCF-linked PSUs after an FCF-timing finding.** Compensation design that rewards the exact metric previously manipulated — a control-design red flag for the Compensation Committee.
3. **Two layoff rounds in 18 months while guiding margin expansion** + CFO and AEC leadership turnover = execution/estimate credibility risk.
4. **Insiders sold into a 42% drawdown with zero purchases** — asymmetric information signal that conflicts with the sell-side "25–30% upside" consensus.
5. **Pricing/channel shock (Apr + Jun 2026)** — monitor renewal rates, reseller defections, and any partner/consumer-protection complaints; potential for a rapid sentiment break.
6. **No guaranteed backup/PIT-recovery for ACC data** — an unmitigated business-continuity exposure for both Autodesk and its customers.
7. **Starboard-driven short-termism** — risk that buybacks/margin targets crowd out R&D in a genuine AI platform transition (Forma/Fusion/Flow).

**Monitoring triggers that would move this to CRITICAL:** any SEC Wells notice/settlement; a restatement or new material weakness; a disclosed ACC data-loss incident; a public reseller/antitrust complaint over pricing changes; or CFO/CEO departure.
