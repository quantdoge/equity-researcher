## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.0% now / 2.5% max**

**Framework logic:** Medium conviction + elevated risk ⇒ 1–3% band. I size at the **bottom of that band, with a lower cap than the standard 3–5%**, for three quantified reasons:

1. **Volatility equivalence.** ADSK realized vol is **42.8%** vs ~15% for a typical large cap. A 2.5% ADSK position carries roughly the risk contribution of a **4.5–5% normal-vol holding.** Nominal weight overstates true risk by ~2x.
2. **Correlation destroys the diversification alibi.** ADSK–**ADBE 0.73**, ADSK–**BSY 0.73**, ADSK–PTC 0.59. The SPY correlation of 0.13 is a trap: it says the position is *not* market beta — it is a **single-factor bet that per-seat application software survives AI repricing**, the exact factor the market is repricing.
3. **Zero margin of safety at $240.** Value's $235 FV is now *below* the price. Purchasing at fair value into a binary catalyst is not sizing, it's gambling.
