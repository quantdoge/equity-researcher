**Data as of 16–18 October 2026 | FRED, FOMC, company filings**

## 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Risk-On in Credit, Risk-Off in Rates ("Transitional / Policy-Error Watch")**

This is the most unusual macro configuration of the cycle: **the Fed has restarted hiking, yet credit markets are pricing no distress whatsoever.** The FOMC raised the target range 25bp to **3.75%–4.00% on 16 September 2026** — the first hike since 2023 — and the SEP points to **4.00%–4.25% by year-end 2026**, i.e. one more hike is the base case. The reason: CPI inflation troughed near **2.4% YoY in Jan–Feb 2026** and has re-accelerated to **+3.72% in September 2026**. The Fed's late-2025 easing (5.33% → 3.64% between Sept 2024 and Jan 2026) appears to have been premature.

Crucially, the long end is doing the tightening. The **10Y has risen ~71bp since 1 May 2026 (4.39% → 5.10%)** and the **30Y sits at 5.37%**, producing a **+34bp 2s10s bear steepener**. Meanwhile **10Y breakevens have *fallen* to 2.35%** (from 2.50% in May). That decomposition is decisive: **the entire long-rate move is real yields (~2.75% real 10Y) and term premium — not inflation expectations.** This is a fiscal-supply / credibility steepener, and it is the single most hostile macro force for long-duration equity.

Offsetting that: real GDP is growing **~2.2%** (Q2 2026 annualised), unemployment is a stable **4.2%**, high-yield OAS is an extraordinarily tight **323bp**, and the broad dollar has just firmed **+3.1% in a month**. Bottom line: **activity and credit are fine; the discount rate is the problem.**

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact — ADSK

ADSK is a **~$50.1B market-cap, subscription-model vertical software company** (AEC, Product Design & Manufacturing, Media & Entertainment), with **~55% non-US revenue** and a **1.33 beta**. It sits at the intersection of three macro transmissions: duration risk, construction/financing conditions, and FX.

| Macro Variable | Impact | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Real 10Y yield (~2.75%)** | **NEGATIVE — dominant** | At ~6.2x EV/Sales and ~19x FY27 non-GAAP EPS, ADSK is a long-duration asset. A 71bp real-rate rise mechanically compresses the multiple. Its ~5.2% earnings yield barely exceeds a 5.10% nominal 10Y — the risk premium is thin, which caps re-rating regardless of execution. |
| **Fed path (one more hike signaled)** | **NEGATIVE** | Hits both legs: higher discount rate *and* higher financing costs for ADSK's customers (AEC firms, fabricators), whose project economics are debt-financed. |
| **30Y at 5.37% / CRE & project finance** | **NEGATIVE — but partly offset** | Non-residential construction starts and CRE refinancing are the most rate-elastic part of ADSK's AEC book (its largest vertical). Mitigants: infrastructure/public spending and the data-centre construction boom are powerful counter-cyclical demand drivers for Forma/AutoCAD. |
| **Inflation +3.7%, sticky** | **MILDLY POSITIVE (revenue) / NEGATIVE (policy)** | Subscription and multi-year contracts give good pricing power; software list-price increases outpace input inflation. But it is inflation that is forcing the Fed's hike, so the net effect is negative. |
| **GDP +2.2%, unemployment 4.2%** | **POSITIVE** | ADSK's model is seat/headcount-based. Design and engineering employment drives named-user counts. No recession + full-ish employment = stable seat expansion and low churn. |
| **HY OAS 323bp (tight)** | **POSITIVE — watch item** | SMB and mid-market AEC/manufacturing customers can finance; ADSK itself is investment-grade with modest leverage (~$2–3B notes vs $50B cap) — **refinancing risk is not the transmission channel; the discount rate is.** Note spreads have widened ~60bp since late Aug. |
| **Broad USD +3.1% in one month (121.6)** | **NEGATIVE — timely** | ~55% of revenue is non-US. Q2 FY27 (ended Jul-2026) enjoyed an FX *tailwind* (reported +16% vs +14% cc). | **Tariffs / input costs** | **NEUTRAL to slightly NEGATIVE** | Little direct COGS exposure (software), but tariffs raise customers' material costs and delay project starts, softening seat demand at the margin. |

**Fundamental context (Q2 FY27, reported 27 Aug 2026):** revenue **$2.05B, +16% reported / +14% cc**; non-GAAP EPS **$3.30**; billings **+10% reported / +12% cc**. FY27 guidance *raised*: revenue **$8.295–8.345B**, billings **$8.575–8.65B**, non-GAAP EPS **$12.52–12.60**. This is a beat-and-raise business — the macro headwind is a *multiple* headwind, not an earnings one, so far.

**Market behaviour confirms the rate-sensitivity thesis:** ADSK fell **−24.9%** from $270.58 (27 Aug) to $203.18 (29 Sep) precisely as the 10Y ran from 4.75% to 5.29%, then rebounded **+20.7%** to $245.16 (15 Oct) as the 10Y backed off to 5.10%. The stock is now trading as a levered bet on the long bond. It is **−21% YoY and −19% YTD**, meaning a meaningful portion of the real-yield shock is already discounted at ~19x FY27 EPS.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL** (sub-scores: Rates **Unfavourable** · Activity **Favourable** · Credit **Favourable** · FX **Turning Unfavourable**)

The two dominant force vectors offset. The discount-rate channel is genuinely hostile — a hiking Fed, a bear steepener, 2.75% real yields and a 5.37% 30Y are all working against a 6x-sales, 1.33-beta name, and the dollar has just turned. But the *operating* macro is supportive: 2.2% real growth, 4.2% unemployment, 3.7% nominal pricing power and risk-on credit with 323bp HY spreads. With FY27 guidance raised and the stock already de-rated 21% YoY to ~19x non-GAAP EPS, the macro risk is **priced more than it is incremental**. Not a macro tailwind — but no longer a macro trap.

## 5. The Scenario That Would Surprise: **A Term-Premium Spiral / Hawkish Policy Error (BEAR)**

**Consensus is treating the September hike as a one-and-done clean-up.** The surprise is if it isn't. The Fed's own SEP implies **4.00–4.25% by December**, and long-end supply plus an AI-capex-driven crowding-out of capital is pushing term premium, not inflation expectations. If the **10Y breaks 5.50% and the 30Y 6.00%** — with breakevens *flat* — real yields go to ~3.1% and ADSK's thin equity risk premium evaporates.

The second leg is under-appreciated: **ADSK's FY27 billings guide of $8.575–8.65B implicitly assumes seat expansion.** A 30Y at 6% shuts down marginal non-residential project starts and CRE refinancing, stalling seat growth in AEC — the largest vertical — just as the +3% dollar move drains 1.5–2pts from reported growth. Compounded: **100bp more on the discount rate plus a 2pt cut to billings growth is worth −25% to −35% from $240**, i.e. back toward the $170–180 area.

**The bull mirror image** is equally sharp and worth hedging: if CPI rolls back toward 2.5% by Q1 2027 and the Fed's hike proves to be the top, the 10Y retraces to 4.40%, real yields fall ~50bp, and ADSK re-rates to 24–25x FY27 EPS — **~$300–315 (+25–30%)** — with an added FX tailwind as the dollar unwinds. **Key catalysts to monitor: the 27–28 October FOMC, the December SEP, and ADSK's Q3 FY27 print in late November.**
