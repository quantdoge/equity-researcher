# Autodesk (ADSK) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $239.90 | Market cap ~$50.1B | EV ~$51.7B | Data as of Oct 16, 2026 close*

## 1. Growth Story

Autodesk is converting a 40-year-old perpetually-licensed drafting franchise into a cloud platform that owns the full **design → make → operate** lifecycle, and the flywheel is now visible in the numbers: FY26 revenue +17.5%, Q2 FY27 non-GAAP operating margin of 41%, and FY27 free cash flow guided to ~$2.74B (33% margin). The underappreciated leg of the story is the August 2026 **MaintainX acquisition**, which pushes Autodesk from multi-year construction projects into the multi-decade asset-operation phase — a ~$40B TAM with proprietary real-world asset data that feeds its AI validation engines. The market is pricing this as an AI-disrupted legacy incumbent (~19x forward non-GAAP EPS, 14.7x forward EV/EBITDA, ~26% off its 52-week high) rather than as an AI-armed data monopolist in 3D geometry — that gap is the opportunity.

**The non-GAAP caveat:** FY26 GAAP EPS was $5.23 versus ~$10.43 non-GAAP, with $788M of stock-based comp (11% of revenue). The 19x multiple is on non-GAAP; true GAAP earnings power is roughly half that headline.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

Autodesk's core design-and-make SAM is generally framed in the **$60–70B** range across AEC, product design/manufacturing, and media & entertainment. MaintainX adds a management-identified **$40B facility and asset operations TAM**, taking the addressable pool to roughly **$110B+**.

- **Penetration:** $8.3B of FY27 revenue against ~$110B = **~7.5% penetration**. Even doubling revenue by FY31 (consensus $12.1B) leaves penetration near 11%.
- **Where the growth is:** construction/AECO growing >20% (AECO $1.03B in Q2, +17%), Fusion-driven Make product type +26%, and now Operations at **>$135M ARR growing >50%** inside MaintainX.
- **Differentiation:** three defensible assets — decades of 3D geometric data, embedded workflow context (how firms actually design and permit), and **deterministic validation** (parametric engines that check AI-generated designs against physics and manufacturability). Generative models propose; Autodesk's engines certify. That is a genuine moat against horizontal LLMs, though not against well-funded AI-native CAD startups.
- **Precedent for the MaintainX playbook:** management built a ~$600M+ Construction segment out of ~$1.8B of M&A, and is explicitly repeating that playbook in Operations. This is the highest-upside part of the thesis and the least proven.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Nov 24, 2026 — Q3 FY27 results.** First quarter with a full MaintainX contribution (~$60M in H2 FY27 revenue). Watch RPO for any stabilization, billings against the $8.575–8.65B guide, and whether FCF guidance ($2.725–2.75B) moves again.
- **Dec 2026–Jan 2027 — MaintainX cross-sell evidence.** Any disclosed attach rate into the AECO installed base validates the "Operate" TAM expansion.
- **Feb–Mar 2027 — FY28 guidance (the real test).** This is the first clean read on organic growth *without* the 1.5pt transaction-model lift, plus the first step up in non-GAAP margin toward the 41% FY29 target.
- **Ongoing — AI monetization.** Seven agents previewed at AU 2026 (Forma Building Layout Explorer, Fusion AutoConstrain, agentic construction intelligence). Any premium SKU or consumption-based AI pricing is a direct ARPU lever.
- **Ongoing — capital returns.** $1.4B of buybacks in FY26 with no dividend; continued repurchase plus balance-sheet capacity supports EPS growth independent of operations.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

This is a Rule-of-40 score of ~50, 92% gross margins, 33% FCF margins, a 41% margin roadmap, and a TAM expansion into operations — available at **19x FY27E and 17x FY28E non-GAAP EPS**, roughly half its five-year average multiple and at a discount to slower-growing peers. The revenue the market is implicitly pricing (consensus gets to only $12.1B by FY31, a ~11% CAGR) is not a demanding bar.

**Scenario framework (12–18 months):**
- **Bear ($200, -17%):** RPO +2% proves to be demand erosion, FY28 organic growth decelerates to 8%, multiple stays compressed at ~15x.
- **Base ($298, +24%):** FY28 EPS ~$14.17 at 21x as AI fear fades and MaintainX scales.
- **Bull ($363, +51%):** Operations ramps on the Construction playbook, AI agents lift ARPU, multiple re-rates to 25x.
- Probability-weighted fair value ≈ **$290 (+21%).**

I stop short of Strong Buy for one reason: RPO growth collapsing from +24% to +2% removes the forward visibility that this multiple is meant to pay for, and I cannot yet distinguish "deliberate shift away from multi-year discounting" from "demand softening." Two more quarters of billings and RPO data resolve that. Position sizing should reflect a stock that is trading on sentiment (AI fear) as much as fundamentals.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **AI disruption (the dominant de-rating force).** If AI-native design tools compress the value of CAD seats — the Adobe precedent is the market's template — the multiple stays structurally lower regardless of execution. Autodesk's counter (geometric data + deterministic validation) is credible but unproven at scale.
2. **RPO deceleration.** +2% YoY is a genuine forward-revenue warning. If it persists through FY28 guidance, the growth thesis needs rebuilding.
3. **MaintainX integration and margin drag.** Largest acquisition to date, unprofitable, and the $40B TAM figure is management's own framing. M&A-driven TAM expansion carries write-down and culture risk.
4. **Go-to-market friction.** Partner-incentive changes (renewals → new business) are creating disruption, particularly in EMEA where labor laws slow the restructure.
5. **Non-GAAP dependence.** $788M stock-based comp means the headline multiple flatters true economics; GAAP P/E is ~46x.
6. **End-market cyclicality.** AEC and industrial capex are rate- and tariff-sensitive; oil-price spikes and rising Treasury yields hit the stock (as seen in the September selloff).
7. **Competition.** Siemens NX/SolidWorks and Dassault in manufacturing, Nemetschek in AEC, plus aggressive Fusion pricing that may cap ARPU even as seat counts grow.
