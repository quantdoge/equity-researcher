**Investment Thesis**

JFrog is a genuinely good business at a genuinely bad price. Execution is top-decile — revenue re-accelerating (+29% Q2'26), cloud +53% and now 53% of revenue, Rule of 40 ≈ 51, net cash ~$686M, 7/7 beat record — but the stock trades ~55% above the DCF midpoint (~$54) and at 1.8x the peer-median revenue multiple on in-line growth. With a live product-security tail (three exploited Artifactory CVEs in CISA KEV), persistent insider selling, and a +87bp real-rate shock compressing long-duration multiples, the risk/reward favours patience. It is not a short — no fraud (Beneish −3.04, negative accruals) and revenue is accelerating.
