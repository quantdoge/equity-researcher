### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **In-Line to modestly Above (revenue); low earnings-quality confidence**

- Beat rate **6/7 (85.7%)**, avg surprise +54.1% — but **distorted by Q2's +215.9%** ($5.75 vs $1.82 est). Ex that quarter, avg surprise ~+27%.
- **Four consecutive revenue beats**, all narrow (+0.9% to +2.4%) — a reliable, low-variance "modest beat" pattern.
- Q3 consensus: **EPS $1.96 / revenue $202.2B**. Consensus EPS is essentially unchanged vs the $1.97 that missed in Feb-26 — estimates have been de-risked.
- **Critical flag:** a cited FY26 consensus EPS of $7.76 versus **$8.53 already booked in H1** implies negative H2 EPS — mathematically impossible without a large **non-recurring item inside Q2's $5.75**. Q2's beat was almost certainly non-operating (mark-to-market on an investment stake). **Do not extrapolate the Q2 beat rate into Q3.**
- Alt data vs prior-quarter trend: **retail demand tracking below** prior-quarter trend; **AWS/AI demand tracking in-line to above**. Net → in-line, with operating income/FCF risk (TTM FCF reportedly **~$1.2B** after a **$59.3B YoY capex surge**) more likely to drive the reaction than revenue.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (bifurcated)
Two clean offsetting forces:
- **Bullish:** AI/cloud contract momentum, capex commitment, overwhelmingly positive 14-day news flow, high retail attention, 85.7% beat rate, four straight revenue beats.
- **Bearish:** consumer search interest down 12–18% YoY with a 12-month low in September; **AWS search interest has collapsed ~60% from its June peak (26 → 9, a 12-month low)**; FCF near zero; stock already **+15% off the June low** and only 8% below its August high, so the set-up is not washed out.

**The AWS Trends collapse is the most interesting divergence — and I'd weight it low.** "AWS" is a generic acronym, its search interest is event-driven (re:Invent is in December, and the 12-month peak of 100 sits at a news spike), and enterprise cloud consumption is contracted years ahead — Google searches have near-zero correlation with it. What it *does* tell you is that **AI/cloud attention and narrative heat are cooling even as fundamentals accelerate** — matching the stock's -12% drift since the August $284 high. Attention, not demand.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Genuinely quantitative: Google Trends (3 keywords), news sentiment (20 articles), earnings/consensus history (7 quarters), price/volume. **Proxy-only, non-time-series:** job postings, social. **Missing entirely:** patent data (tool failure ×2), web traffic (source blocked), app-store rankings, satellite/geospatial.

**Data limitations & lag:** Google Trends are weekly, normalized to each keyword's own peak (not cross-comparable) and heavily contaminated by news-cycle effects; a 5–10 day reporting lag plus 12-month normalization means recent moves are provisional. Social/hiring figures are single snapshots with no baseline, so no trend claim is defensible. **Bottom line: alt data supports an in-line-to-slightly-better Q3 top line, but the consumer is measurably softer than a year ago and the cash-flow line — not demand — is the real swing factor on 10/29.**
