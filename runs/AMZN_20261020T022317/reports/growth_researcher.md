## Amazon.com (AMZN) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $262.45 | Market Cap $2.83T | EV $2.87T | Sector: Consumer Cyclical (disguised hyperscaler)**

---

### 1. Growth Story

Amazon has stopped being a retail company that owns a cloud business and become an **AI infrastructure compounder with a $700B retail distribution arm attached**. AWS re-accelerated to **+36.7% YoY in Q2'26 ($42.2B, $169B run rate)** — the fifth consecutive quarter of acceleration and fastest growth in 18 quarters — while its **backlog (RPO) hit $496B, ~2.5x a year ago**. The under-appreciated leg is custom silicon: Trainium has crossed a **$25B annualized run rate growing triple digits**, with multi-year, multi-gigawatt commitments from Anthropic and OpenAI — Amazon is now a credible merchant-silicon competitor to NVIDIA while owning the demand side of the equation.

**Moat:** Trainium cost advantage is *self-reinforcing* — cheaper tokens win AI workloads, which attracts anchor tenants (Anthropic, OpenAI), whose multi-year commitments de-risk the very capex that builds the next cheaper chip. That is a genuine data/scale network effect, not a narrative.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q3'26 earnings — Oct 29, 2026 (~10 days):** the single largest event. Watch AWS growth holding >35%, Q4 guide, and 2027 capex framing. Guidance of $197–202B looks soft only because Prime Day shifted into Q2 (ex-Prime Day growth ~400bps higher) and ~80bps FX drag — this is a **setup for an easy beat**.
2. **AWS backlog conversion:** $496B RPO converting to revenue over 2–4 years provides rare multi-year visibility; any disclosure on Trainium3 ramp timing re-rates the silicon story.
3. **Capex peak → FCF inflection (2027):** Jassy's framing — data centers monetize for 30+ years, servers break even in <3 years on 5-year contracts. The market is currently paying nothing for this; the FCF turn is the re-rating trigger.
4. **Advertising margin mix:** ads growing 2x retail's rate at ~50%+ incremental margins pushes consolidated operating margin toward mid-teens.
5. **Regulatory overhang clearing:** FTC Prime settlement payments (Sept '26) remove a tail risk that has capped the multiple.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **STRONG BUY**

Amazon is mispriced on its most important axis. The market is applying a **retail-plus-capex-penalty multiple (17.4x EV/EBITDA)** to a company where the fastest-growing, highest-margin piece (AWS, 37% growth, ~39% operating margins, $496B backlog) is now the *majority of operating profit*. Against MSFT at 19.4x and GOOGL at 22.9x with slower cloud growth, the discount is unjustifiable. With an 85.7% beat rate, +23.5% estimate revisions, a deliberately conservative Q3 guide, and a median PT of ~$338 (+29%), the risk/reward is asymmetric into the Oct 29 print.

### 7. Key Risks

1. **The Rule of 40 problem is real (score 13.4):** $200B+ of 2026 capex against $7.7B of 2025 FCF. If AWS growth decelerates below ~30% before the 2027 FCF inflection arrives, the "capex with clear line of sight to returns" thesis loses credibility fast — and the stock has no FCF support to catch it.
2. **Component cost inflation:** Memory, HDD, and SSD prices are elevated (management flagged this explicitly and it *drove the capex raise*). Same dollars buy less compute — a direct hit to ROIC on the largest capital program in corporate history.
3. **AI-capacity digestion risk:** The $496B backlog is heavily concentrated in a handful of model labs (Anthropic, OpenAI). A single anchor tenant renegotiating or insourcing silicon would be a material negative — and Amazon's equity stake in Anthropic cuts both ways.
4. **Earnings quality:** FY26 EPS is flattered by ~$53B of Anthropic marks. If that stake marks *down*, reported EPS compresses even as operations improve — a mechanical derating risk independent of the business.
5. **Retail/consumer & tariff exposure:** Fuel inflation, driver-capacity constraints, Middle East-driven transportation costs, and tariff policy sit directly on the ~$700B low-margin base.
6. **Regulatory:** FTC settlement/antitrust overhang on Prime and marketplace practices remains an unresolved headline risk.

**Position note:** This is a high-conviction core growth holding, not a low-volatility one (beta 1.46). Size for the capex-driven FCF drought through 2027 rather than for the AI narrative — the thesis needs two more quarters of AWS >30% growth to be fully self-funding in the market's eyes.

**Data gaps:** Peer multiples limited to MSFT/GOOGL (FMP's Consumer Cyclical peer set returns irrelevant comparables like HD, MCD, TM). FY26 consensus EPS could not be cleanly normalized for Anthropic marks — treat all P/E figures as approximate.
