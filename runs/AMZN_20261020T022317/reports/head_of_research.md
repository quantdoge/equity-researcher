**Investment Thesis**

Amazon is simultaneously the highest-quality business in our coverage and one of the hardest to underwrite at today's price. The franchise is accelerating — AWS +36.7% (fifth straight quarter of acceleration) on a $169B run-rate with a $496B backlog, record 52.3% gross margin, ads +26% — but the equity has crossed from self-funding to debt-funding a $220B capex program (TTM FCF −$11.6B) into a 5.09% 10-year, with a live FTC monopolization trial and a headline P/E flattered ~2x by Anthropic mark-to-market gains. At $262 there is zero margin of safety against a ~$205–240 probability-weighted intrinsic value; the correct action is a small, staged position — not a core holding, and not a pass.
