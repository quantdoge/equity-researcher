### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk 1 — US antitrust and consumer-protection litigation (Probability: Very High / Impact: Medium-High)**
- **New (31 Aug 2026):** FTC + **22 state AGs** sued Amazon alleging a "secret ad surcharge" — an undisclosed "soft reserve price" in Sponsored Products auctions that caused advertisers to pay their own max bid ~80% of the time by 2024, allegedly overcharging **1.2M+ advertisers by >$20B since 2019** (FTC; The Guardian, 31 Aug 2026; Law.com, 2 Sep 2026). Amazon calls it "misguided." This directly attacks the highest-margin growth engine (advertising +22% in 2025).
- **FTC monopolist case** (W.D. Wash., Judge John H. Chun; 18 states + Puerto Rico): Sherman Act claims survived dismissal; trial moved from Oct 2026, with pretrial conference **15 Mar 2027** and trial briefs 22 Mar 2027 (MLex; Courthouse News). Related *De Coster* class action set for **14 Jun 2027**.
- **Prime "dark patterns"** matter resolved Sept 2025: **$1B civil penalty + $1.5B consumer redress** ($2.5B total); >$845M disbursed by Sept 2026 (FTC). Precedent for aggressive FTC posture toward Amazon's subscription/ad mechanics.
- DC AG antitrust case repeatedly delayed (Law360).

**Risk 2 — EU digital-regulation escalation, now reaching AWS (Probability: High / Impact: Medium-High)**
- The Commission opened market investigations on **18 Nov 2025** and on **25 Jun 2026** reached a preliminary position that **AWS should be designated a DMA gatekeeper** as an important gateway for EU business cloud, citing lock-in, high switching costs, and AI portfolio/partnerships as decisive (European Commission IP/26/1444). Final designation would impose interoperability, data-portability and non-discrimination duties on AWS architecture and pricing in the EU — the highest-value segment (AWS = 57% of 2025 operating income at $45.6B).
- The Commission's first DMA review (**28 Apr 2026**) concluded the regime is "fit for purpose," and enforcement is intensifying (Google fined €890M under DMA in July 2026). Amazon remains under DMA scrutiny for its marketplace and faces a Commission probe into self-preferencing; DSA obligations add notice/transparency liability.
- Fines run to **10–20% of global turnover** — for a $717B-revenue company, a theoretical nine-figure-to-tens-of-billions tail.
- **Italy:** Q4 2025 included a **$1.1B charge** for resolution of tax disputes tied to the Italian stores business — evidence of EU tax-authority aggressiveness.

**Risk 3 — US–China trade regime, tariffs, and Taiwan supply-chain concentration (Probability: Medium / Impact: High)**
- CEO Jassy confirmed in Jan 2026 that tariffs are now **"creeping into prices"** as sellers exhaust pre-tariff inventory (Reuters/CNBC, 20 Jan 2026); tariff-driven goods inflation was ~2.9% from early 2025 to Feb 2026. Amazon's third-party marketplace — heavily China-sourced — transmits this into unit economics and demand.
- The 2026 capex plan of **~$200B** (AI, chips, Leo satellites) deepens dependence on TSMC-fabricated Trainium/Graviton silicon and on Nvidia GPUs — a Taiwan-strait and export-control exposure that is not diversifiable quickly. Tariff/export-control escalation on semiconductors is the key transmission channel.
- AWS China operates only through local partners (Sinnet/NWCD) under PRC data law, with no global-backbone connectivity — reputational and compliance tail risk, negligible revenue.

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights
- **Active matters:** FTC monopoly case (trial 2027); FTC + 22-state ad-auction surcharge suit (filed 31 Aug 2026, >$20B alleged); DC AG antitrust; *De Coster* class action (Jun 2027); residual Prime consent-order compliance through 2026+; Italian tax disputes (resolved with $1.1B charge).
- **Regulators of record:** FTC (Khan-era cases now in litigation), 22+ state AGs (a growing, politically independent enforcement channel), European Commission DG COMP & DG CNECT (DMA/DSA), UK CMA, Italy AGCM, India CCI.
- **Structural drivers:** advertising (~$70B+ run-rate) and AWS are the two profit pools most exposed to legal/regulatory attack; the Prime settlement establishes a template for "dark pattern" liability across subscriptions and ad auctions.
- **Positive offsets:** no disclosed DOJ criminal probe, FCPA matter, or sanctions violation; Amazon has been expanding *sovereign* offerings (AWS European Sovereign Cloud now GA) which partially defuses EU data-sovereignty risk.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags
1. **Ad-auction disclosure** — alleged concealment of pricing mechanics; if proven, implies disclosure-control and consumer-protection weaknesses, not just antitrust.
2. **AI/export controls** — frontier-model hosting (Bedrock: Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen, DeepSeek-class models) and chip distribution create entity-list and compute-export-control screening obligations that are evolving fast.
3. **Data privacy/sovereignty** — DMA/AI Act/GDPR interplay in the EU; AWS government-cloud work (e.g., U.S. Air Force deal) raises conflict-of-law exposure with EU sovereignty rules.
4. **Tariff/customs** — third-party seller import flows create customs-valuation and de-minimis compliance exposure.
*No evidence of FCPA/AML/sanctions violations found in the sources reviewed.*

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**
Diversified across the world's highest-rule-of-law markets, with no meaningful revenue in sanctioned or expropriation-prone jurisdictions and no nationalisation risk. The rating is held up by a **dense and escalating US/EU enforcement stack** — three separate FTC actions, 22 state AGs, and a DMA designation now reaching AWS — where the plausible loss range is large in absolute dollars (multi-billion) but modest relative to $80B operating income and $2.83T market cap. The genuine tail risk is not legal but **geopolitical supply chain**: Taiwan-fabricated AI silicon plus a tariff regime that is already passing through to consumer prices.
