Edge at $262 is **roughly zero to modestly negative** on a full-cycle DCF, and **positive** on relative multiples (+25–33% to Street PTs). Blended expected edge ≈ **+3 to +5%**, but with a 25% probability of a −35% outcome. Raw Kelly on that is small; a **half-Kelly with a variance haircut** lands at **1.5–2.5%**.

**Why the bottom of that range rather than the top:**
- **Beta 1.38 / vol 35.0% / max DD −19.4% in the last 12 months** — this is not a defensively-sized position. A 2% weight contributes ~0.7% of portfolio beta on its own.
- **Negative FCF removes the valuation floor.** There is no dividend or buyback to catch a falling price; the only support is the multiple, which the macro report shows is mechanically levered to the 10-year.
- **Live binary legal calendar** (trial began 13-Oct-26) is a headline-risk position through year-end, per Non-Fin Risk's explicit escalation.
- **Zero insider bid** in 12 months against $4.1B of sales. Not a fraud signal; is an alignment asymmetry that argues against a full-weight position.

**Why not smaller / not a pass:** unanimous business-quality consensus, AWS accelerating for five consecutive quarters, a $496B backlog, and the cheapest EV/EBITDA among hyperscalers growing cloud fastest. Passing on the best franchise in the book because the entry price is 10% too high is a worse error than owning 2% of it.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss — $195 close (≈ −26% from entry).**
Rationale: below the Feb-2026 low of $199.60, and below Value's base-case intrinsic value of $196 — meaning at $195 the market is pricing the bear case nearly in full and the *reason to own it* has changed. Use a **fundamental confirmation**, not a mechanical stop: a close below $195 **with** AWS decelerating below 30% is an exit; a close below $195 on a pure rate shock (10Y >5.5%) is a *re-entry candidate*, not an exit.

**Thesis invalidation (exit regardless of price) — any two of:**
1. **Two consecutive quarters of AWS growth below 28–30%** (currently 36.7%). This is the single cleanest read on whether the capex cycle is being managed.
2. **2027 capex guidance above ~$250–260B without commensurate backlog/revenue acceleration** — the exact condition Short Analyst named as converting "No Edge" into a live short.
3. **FCF still negative through FY2027 with incremental debt >$50B/yr** (debt already +$70B in six months, net debt $30B → $100B).
4. **A binding structural remedy in the FTC case** (divestiture or mandated marketplace/Prime separation) — the tail Non-Fin Risk escalated and the market is not pricing.
5. **Anthropic mark reversal >$30B** booked through the P&L — this unwinds ~40% of reported TTM net income with no cash offset, and mechanically re-rates the headline multiple *up*.
6. **EU DMA final AWS gatekeeper designation with mandated interoperability/portability** on AWS architecture or pricing (currently preliminary only).

**Trim triggers:**
- **First trim (⅓ of position) at $300–310** — ≈90% of Value's bull-case IV ($326) and the low end of Street PTs.
- **Second trim at $338–350** — Street median / Morgan Stanley top-pick target; ≈100% of Value's bull case. Hold only a residual 0.5% "franchise" position above this absent an upward revision to intrinsic value.
- **Re-underwrite, don't trim, if AWS >40% and backlog >$600B** — that would justify lifting the bull-case IV.

**Monitoring cadence:** monthly on **AWS growth vs. capex growth** (Sector's single cleanest signal), **the 10-year yield** (Macro: 5.50% = downgrade to Unfavourable; 4.50% = upgrade to Favourable), **TTM FCF and net debt/EBITDA** (Fin Risk: watch the capex/OCF gap, not the leverage level), and **the FTC docket** through the Mar-2027 pretrial conference.

The truth the 11 reports jointly reveal is uncomfortable and worth stating plainly: **AMZN is simultaneously the highest-quality business in the coverage universe and one of the hardest to underwrite at today's price.** Every agent agrees on the franchise — only the short analyst tried to argue otherwise and conceded defeat.
