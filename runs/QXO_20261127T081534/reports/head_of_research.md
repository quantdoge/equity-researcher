### Investment Memo: QXO — 27 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL / AVOID | Conviction: Medium

**Investment Thesis**

QXO is a genuinely well-assembled, share-leading distribution platform (#1 insulation/waterproofing, #2 roofing) wrapped in an uninvestable financial structure: $10.3bn of debt, ~$650m annualized interest, negative free cash flow, 76% share-count dilution in nine months, and goodwill equal to 73% of assets — assembled at cyclical premiums and now levered into the worst possible macro regime (7.2% mortgages, 5.13% 10Y, Fed hiking, HY spreads widening). The equity is a levered option on Brad Jacobs' integration execution plus a 2027 macro turn; our probability-weighted intrinsic value of ~$9 sits ~25% below the price, which already capitalizes full delivery of a plan the sell side has stopped believing. The correct action is to decline the bet at $11.97, run a live watchlist at $8.50–9.50, and let the 24 Feb 2027 FY27 guidance print gate any entry.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro is UNFAVOURABLE — re-accelerating inflation (CPI 3.82%), two 2026 Fed hikes, mortgage rates +123bp to 7.21%, housing starts –14% from peak, HY OAS +107bp. Sector is NEUTRAL: $800bn fragmented TAM with proven scale economics, but at a cyclical trough. QXO's organic daily sales (+5.4% Oct, +9.1% roofing) genuinely beat a +1.8% peer median — real share gains in a shrinking market — while its ~0% GAAP operating margin vs. the 9.6% peer median is the gap the entire thesis must close.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM. Pure US/Canada footprint (no China/sanctions nexus). The elevated item is cumulative antitrust — serial HSR filings, local market concentration, and DOJ precedent having killed TopBuild/SPI on the same adjacency. Inherited Beacon/Allied long-tail product liability is unquantified (10-K legal note not retrievable — an evidence gap, not a clean bill).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL, high conviction. ROIC ~4% vs. ~8–9% WACC on ~$30bn invested capital; probability-weighted IV ≈ $9 (range $6–14). Issuing equity at ~11x to buy assets at ~18x (TopBuild) is value-destructive arbitrage. Buyable only at ~$6.50–7.00.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (speculative) — the lone bull. Gross margin 23.0%→28.2% in four quarters is the real proof point; 14.6x FY28E and 6.8x FY30E EPS vs. peers at ~25x. Concedes it is a bet on the operator, not the business.
- **Quantitative Signals**: SELL, composite 21/100. Bottom quintile on Value (tangible P/B ~9.4x), Momentum (12-1 = –37%), Quality (ROIC –1.8%, F-score 4/9) and Low-Vol (beta 2.44, vol 68%, max drawdown –62%). The +18% bounce off the October low is a bear-market-reversal pattern, not accumulation.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. Net debt/adj. EBITDA 4.4x (5.7x incl. preferred); 17.3x on GAAP EBITDA; GAAP interest coverage –1.11x; Altman Z 1.10 (distress); cash down 83% in two quarters to $528m; TTM FCF –$98m. Mitigant: maturities appear laddered to 2030–31 and capex is <1% of sales.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Two *interim* CAOs in four months plus a CFO change during the largest integration in company history; **zero open-market insider buying** through a 43% collapse while a 10% holder exited ~$117m; founder-controlled (~36%) with Chair/CEO duality and Jacobs now dual-CEO of CKX.
- **ESG**: LAGGARD on disclosure, NEUTRAL on thesis. Governance warrants a cost-of-capital discount, not exclusion; the 2030 relative-TSR PSU structure is a genuine mitigant. TopBuild's insulation exposure is a mild E-linked demand tailwind.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL. End-market demand flat (roof-replacement interest slightly above last Nov), brand attention fading post-catalyst, revenue-beat magnitude collapsing (+2.1%→+0.7%), tape shows distribution on rallies; retail anchors at $18–24 are overhead supply.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: NO EDGE. Forensic red flags are real — ~$550–650m/yr of recurring "one-time" costs, SBC excluded from adjusted metrics, ~0% EBITDA-to-FCF conversion — but no fraud, and the naive short has already been paid out at ~0.8x book with a $500m buyback live.

**Agent disagreement (flagged)**: Growth's BUY relies on consensus FY28–30 EPS that implies a ~24% EBITDA margin no distributor has ever earned; consensus 2030 EBITDA ($1.63bn) sits 59% below management's $4bn target. Where Growth collides with Value/Fin-Risk/Non-Fin-Risk on cash conversion and governance, the risk evidence takes precedence — eleven agents converge independently on leverage as the swing variable.

**Bull Case**
- Operator + margin inflection: Jacobs' roll-up track record (United Rentals, XPO) is the best in 30 years; if even half the 2030 plan lands, the stock is worth $16–25.
- 2027 disinflation snapback: a sub-3.2% core CPI print → Fed cuts, 10Y ~4.25%, mortgages ~6.3% — QXO is the highest-beta expression of that scenario in Industrials ($16–18 in 2–3 quarters).
- Support optics: ~0.7x book, $500m buyback, 2.2% dividend, no near-term refinancing cliff, and an asset-light model where working-capital release can flip FCF mechanically in 2027.

**Bear Case**
- Cash reality: $2.2bn "adjusted EBITDA" converts to ~negative FCF; GAAP coverage –1.1x; Z-score in distress; a –20% revenue scenario takes leverage to ~6.8x and into covenant stress with $25.3bn of goodwill exposed.
- Core erosion at the wrong point in the cycle: legacy Beacon –2.5% YoY, roofing volumes –7–8%, and TopBuild added new-construction beta precisely as mortgage rates spiked.
- Governance/complexity discount: controlled company, dual-CEO conflict, accounting-function churn, zero insider buying, SBC-addback accounting, and a demonstrated willingness to dilute at 11x to acquire at 18x.

*Downside*: refinancing/covenant event (maturity schedule unverified — the single most important open data point); integration slippage across three simultaneous deals; goodwill impairment; CPI >4.5% forcing 8%+ refis (a $7–8 stock); antitrust remedies on the next deal; key-man/attention risk. *Upside (to a sell call)*: 2.4-beta short squeeze into buybacks and 19/20 sell-side Buys; synergy beat at the Feb print; macro turn. **Data gaps carried**: debt maturity/covenant detail, revenue-by-geography, 10-K legal/contingency notes, segment-level EBITDA.
