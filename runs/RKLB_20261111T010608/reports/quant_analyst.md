## 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

1. **Negative alpha / beta underperformance** — beta 3.61 delivered a 1-year return ~20pp *below* CAPM expectation. Rising beta is not being rewarded; sensitivity cuts both ways.
2. **Momentum reversal risk (highest-conviction flag)** — shares +36.4% in the last month off a −69% drawdown. 12-1 momentum (−8.8%) is negative while the tape looks strong; that divergence is the classic short-term-reversal setup, and 71% idiosyncratic variance means there is no factor hedge.
3. **Factor crowding — the wrong kind** — not crowded in the "smart money owns it" sense; crowded in **retail/momentum/growth-beta flow**. Correlation of 0.537 to SPY vs 0.14–0.27 to actual A&D peers means it sells off with risk appetite, not with defense budgets.
4. **Liquidity is adequate, volatility is not** — 12–80M shares/day (13.4M on 2026-10-23, 80M on 2026-05-08), ~$0.8–5B notional: no liquidity constraint. But 96% annualised vol and a −69% drawdown mean position sizing at normal A&D weights implies 3–4x the portfolio risk contribution.
5. **Dilution drag (structural)** — +13.8% share count in 3 years, F-Score component F7 = 0. Growth in market cap is partly share issuance, not value creation; expect financing risk to recur while operating CF is negative (−$166M FY25).
6. **Estimate quality / data integrity** — the revision score is built on **2 analysts** and an FY2030 target. FY26–27 consensus is *still a loss*. Any "revision momentum" claim on RKLB should be treated as **not statistically significant** until rebased to near-year estimates with adequate coverage.
7. **Analyst dispersion** — $58 to $122 targets on the same stock. Low consensus informational content; do not treat the $92.50 median as a price signal.
8. **Sample-size disclosure** — peer ranks (n=4), the revision score (n=2), and the 1-year statistics (252 obs is adequate, but RKLB's regime changed in May-2026, so **1-year vol/beta/alpha estimates are not stable across sub-periods**; the pre/post-May-2026 drawdown break makes full-window Sharpe figures of limited forward-looking value).

**Data gaps:** true sector-wide factor percentiles (peer feed served 12 names, only 4 economically comparable); prior-vintage FY26/FY27 consensus for a clean revision test; no institutional/ownership or short-interest data for crowding analysis.
