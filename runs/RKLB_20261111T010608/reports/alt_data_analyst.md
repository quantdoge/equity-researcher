## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (mildly constructive on fundamentals, negative on near-term EPS and attention)

**The setup:** every *hard* operating signal — backlog +52% QoQ, positive OCF, gross margin beat, production-role hiring, ESCAPADE execution — points **up**. Every *soft* signal — Google Trends at −32% vs. year avg and −83% on Neutron, StockTwits mentions −44%, EPS misses of −37% and −80% back-to-back, social chatter explaining away dilution — points to **waning marginal demand for the equity** and a still-unresolved cash-burn/dilution problem.

That divergence is the finding: **the business is inflecting faster than the stock's ability to re-rate, because the 2027 Neutron slip removed the catalyst that justified a ~35x P/S (annualized $1.07B revenue vs. $37.5B cap).** Alt data does not support chasing the +36% bounce off the Nov 5 low, and it argues against a bullish call into Q4 prints on EPS. It *does* support the view that revenue-side estimates are too low and that the backlog is real, not aspirational.

**Actionable read:** alt-data direction is *improving fundamentals into a de-rated multiple* — a set-up for accumulation on weakness rather than momentum buying. Watch for (a) production-technician headcount continuing to climb (Neutron rate readiness), (b) "Neutron rocket" search interest turning up off the 8–17 floor (would flag a genuine catalyst re-pricing), and (c) StockTwits mentions bottoming — the last two are the cheapest early-warning signals available here.
