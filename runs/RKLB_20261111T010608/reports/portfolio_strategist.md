## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector — it is NOT a defence diversifier. This is the most actionable finding in my report.**
Verified correlations destroy the natural assumption: RKLB vs **LMT 0.14, GD 0.18, BA 0.27**. Adding RKLB to an Aerospace & Defence sleeve does **not** add defence-budget exposure — it adds growth-beta exposure that happens to be labelled "defence." It will widen active risk against a defence benchmark while adding a *different* risk factor entirely. If the portfolio already holds LMT/GD/RTX, RKLB is a **factor bet, not a sector completion**.

**Factor exposure — negative on four of five factors.** It worsens portfolio Value (5/100), Low-Vol (2/100), Quality (30/100), and adds **negative alpha (−19.5%/yr)**. Only Earnings Revision is positive, and that rests on **n=2 analysts at an FY2030 horizon** — statistically unverifiable. In a quality-tilted book this is a direct contradiction of the mandate.

**Correlation / crowding — the hidden concentration.** **RKLB↔ASTS = 0.75.** Any book holding other space pure-plays already owns this risk. RKLB↔SPY = 0.54 with only 29% R² — meaning **~71% of the variance is idiosyncratic**, so index shorts cannot hedge it, and correlation to *risk appetite* rather than to fundamentals means it sells off in tech/AI derating events unrelated to space. It is a **levered growth-sentiment proxy**.

**Liquidity — no constraint.** 12–80M shares/day, $0.8–5B notional. No position-size constraint below ~3%.

**Balance-sheet fit — genuinely additive.** Net cash $762M–$4.3B with $120–254M debt and no maturity wall. It does *not* import credit risk into the book. Post-Iridium (mid-2027) that changes materially — the $1.775B assumed facility converts this into a leveraged integrator, which is a **portfolio-level event to pre-position for**, not a current one.

**Macro fit — the binding constraint.** In a hawkish-Fed/rising-real-rate regime, this is the highest-duration name available. It is a **negative-duration hedge in reverse**: owning it is a bet that the 10Y falls. If the house macro view is "higher for longer," RKLB is a deliberate contradiction of it and must be sized as such.
