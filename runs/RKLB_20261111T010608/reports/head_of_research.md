**Investment Thesis**

Rocket Lab's operating business is genuinely inflecting — record Q3'26 revenue of $267.8M (+73% YoY), backlog +52% QoQ to $3.6B (4.1x TTM coverage), gross margin up from −3% (FY21) to ~34%, and positive operating cash flow for the first time — but at ~42x TTM sales, ~112x EV/gross profit, and ~19x book, the price has already capitalised a decade of flawless execution. Five independent methodologies (value, quant, financial risk, macro, short) converge on the same conclusion: **the balance sheet is safe; the valuation is not.** With the real 10-year yield at a two-decade high (~2.7%), the Fed re-tightening, and insiders executing 100 sales against zero purchases (~$390M) through a 65% drawdown, expected value at $62.71 is flat-to-negative. This is not a fraud and not a short — the forensic work is exculpatory and the Neutron catalyst is real and dated — it is a disciplined pass, with defined re-entry triggers at lower prices or on a resolved binary.

**Bull Case**
- The only funded Western medium-lift challenger to SpaceX: Electron dominant in dedicated small lift, $3.6B backlog (+52% QoQ) anchored by government programs (SDA >$1.3B, $266M USSF, Golden Dome architecture), with Neutron's first 16 missions already contracted at a raised $70M price — a potential $1.75B annual revenue line, ~2x current company revenue.
- Fundamentals inflecting faster than the multiple: revenue growth accelerating to 73%, gross margin −3%→34% (38% non-GAAP), OCF positive, capex moderating — the "story" is becoming an operating business, with $4.7B cash removing financing risk.
- Convex catalyst stack: a successful 2027 Neutron first flight plus any rate relief (5y5y inflation forwards already falling to 2.15%) could re-rate the stock violently — the Dec'25–May'26 precedent took it from $40 to $150 on a 70bp rate decline. Iridium, if it closes, adds ~$872M of 57%-EBITDA-margin revenue diversification.

**Bear Case**
- Valuation has no margin of safety: ~42x TTM sales, ~20x FY28E sales, ~330x FY28E earnings, negative ROIC for five years. Value desk's central estimate ($18–30) implies the stock trades at 2–3x fair value.
- Neutron is a binary with a poor prior: three public slips (2024→2027, now undated) and two distinct composite-manufacturing failures in ten months (Jan 2026 tank rupture; Oct 2026 dome voids). Industry base rates for maiden orbital launches are failure-prone; a failure from 42x sales plausibly halves the multiple.
- Structural per-share erosion in an adverse macro regime: share count +44% in five years (~11%/yr), the $1.94B Iridium raise already underwater ($66 issue vs $62.71), a $3B ATM still live, and insiders liquidating $390M with zero offsetting buys — all while the binding macro variable (real long-end yield) sits at a two-decade high with the Fed hiking.

**Key Risks**
1. Neutron execution — a fourth slip or first-flight failure is the dominant single-name risk (valuation bridges entirely on this vehicle).
2. Rate regime — two further hikes and 30Y toward 6% likely retests the $37–46 zone (−25% to −40%); conversely the bull case is substantially a Fed call.
3. Gross margin trajectory — GAAP GM has fallen 38.2%→36.1%→33.6% over three quarters; a break below 33% confirms the pricing-power-loss thesis.
4. Iridium — FCC conditions/delay (adversarial docket), integration overstretch, conversion of a net-cash balance sheet into a leveraged integrator, and Starlink direct-to-cell threat to Iridium's consumer franchise.
5. US Government budget concentration — continuing resolution or Golden Dome re-scoping directly hits backlog conversion (only ~33% of backlog recognizable within 12 months).
6. Dilution without delivery — +15%/yr share count with no Neutron flight structurally impairs per-share value.
7. Data gaps: no segment/country revenue splits disclosed; earnings-revision signal based on n=2 analysts; insider aggregates from secondary trackers (10-K/DEF 14A not directly retrievable).
