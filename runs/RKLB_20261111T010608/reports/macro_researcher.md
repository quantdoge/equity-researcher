## 1. Macro Environment Summary — Stance: **Risk-Off for Long-Duration Growth / Reflation-Driven Policy Tightening**

The US is in a **"re-acceleration with a hawkish Fed"** regime — the mirror image of the environment that drove RKLB's May 2026 highs. Growth is running above trend (Q3 real GDP +3.4% annualized, +2.1% YoY), unemployment has fallen to 4.1% from 4.5% a year ago, and headline CPI has re-accelerated to **3.82% YoY** — a "red-hot" print on 10 November. The Fed **ended its easing cycle and hiked 25bp on 16 September 2026** to 3.75–4.00%, with the effective rate now 3.88% (vs. a 3.63% trough in April–July 2026); sell-side houses (BofA) have flagged two further hikes.

Critically, **the long end is not moving on inflation expectations — it is moving on real rates and term premium.** 10Y breakevens are 2.20% and 5y5y forwards 2.15% and *falling*, meaning the market believes the CPI spike is not persistent. Yet the 10Y sits at ~4.9–5.1% (5.13% in the current curve snapshot; 4.88% on 9 November FRED, off a 5.31% peak on 5 October). That implies a **real 10Y yield near 2.7%** — the highest sustained real cost of capital in two decades, with 30Y at 5.47% reflecting fiscal/term-premium pressure. The 2s10s curve has un-inverted to **+39bp**, but the steepening has been driven by the long end, not by Fed cuts — the least supportive flavour of steepening for high-multiple equities.

**Transmission to RKLB:** ~$37.5B market cap against a roughly $1.0–1.1B 2026 revenue run-rate (~33x sales), GAAP-loss-making, with the value concentrated in Neutron cash flows now pushed to a 2027 first flight. This is the definition of a long-duration asset. RKLB peaked at **$150.23 on 27 May 2026** as the Fed cut to 3.63%; as the Fed pivoted hawkish and the 10Y broke 5%, the stock fell to **$45.99 on 5 November (−69%)**, before rebounding to $62.71. **The macro regime, not company fundamentals — Q3 revenue was a record $267.8M with backlog +52% q/q to $3.6B — is the dominant driver of the share price.**

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

**Rationale.** RKLB is the highest-duration, highest-beta (2.61) name in its peer set, with valuation driven by 2028+ Neutron economics rather than current cash flow, and its single most important macro variable — the real long-end yield — is at a two-decade high and rising. The policy path has flipped from tailwind to headwind mid-year, and the company's debt-free, cash-rich, government-contract-anchored model is a genuine but *secondary* mitigant. Offsets (strong nominal GDP, expanding defense budgets, high interest income, tight launch supply) protect the top line but cannot offset a 100–150bp de-rating in the discount rate. **Macro is currently the binding constraint on RKLB's multiple, and it is the reason a record Q3 (revenue $267.8M, backlog $3.6B, operating cash flow positive) produced a −9% reaction.**

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BULL (contrarian): The "Term-Premium Snap-Back"**

The market and the Fed are both leaning hawkish (two more hikes priced/forecast), yet **5y5y inflation forwards are already falling to 2.15%** — the bond market is quietly saying the CPI re-acceleration is a lagging-shelter/oil artefact. If Q1 2027 CPI base effects bring headline back toward 2.5%, the Fed's hikes look like a policy error.

**The surprise (probability ~20–25%, heavily under-priced):** Fed pauses in December 2026, cuts 75–100bp through H1 2027; the 10Y falls from ~5.1% to **~4.2%**, real 10Y compresses from 2.7% to ~1.9%; the broad dollar reverses. RKLB, at 33x sales, re-rates violently — historical internal evidence is Dec'25–May'26, when a 70bp decline in fed funds plus a 10Y at 4.45% took the stock from $40 to $150. **A 60bp–90bp decline in the 10Y, delivered alongside Neutron's maiden flight in 2027, is worth +80–120% from $62.71.**

**The bear mirror (probability ~30%, better recognised):** Two more hikes into a labour market already softening from 4.5% → 4.1% on weak hiring, 30Y pushing 6.0% on deficit financing, and a 2027 earnings recession. High-beta space names lead the drawdown — RKLB retests the $37–46 zone (−25% to −40%), which is exactly what the January-2026 and June-2026 de-rating episodes demonstrated.
