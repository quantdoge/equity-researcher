# Investment Memo: GIS — 22 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (accumulate ≤$31) | **Conviction**: Medium
**Price Target**: $38 (~6% upside from current $35.74; ~13% total return incl. the 6.8% yield)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
General Mills is a narrow-moat, cash-generative packaged-food franchise with best-in-class gross margins (33.6%, +280bp vs peers) — but a structurally shrinking topline (worst-in-class: −5.45% FY26 vs peer median +1.55%), 3.9x net leverage, −$13.5bn tangible equity, and a 6.8% dividend covered only 1.24x by FCF. At 11.3x forward earnings the market has already priced the decline (zero-growth perpetuity ≈ $36); what it has *not* priced is a live dividend-cut catalyst and an uninsured UPF litigation tail. This is a yield/value hold, not a buy: the 25%+ margin of safety only appears at ~$30–31.

## Bull Case
- **Valuation floor with income**: 11.3x forward, 8.3–10.4% FCF yield, 6.8% dividend; the price already assumes zero growth forever, with fair value $38–42 and sell-side high target $43–48.
- **Operational stabilisation**: FQ1 FY27 organic sales flat (best in a year), gross margin holding at 33.9%, four consecutive EPS beats, Cheerios momentum, $3bn savings programme, and portfolio refocused on Pet/International after yogurt/Brazil exits.
- **Macro pivot optionality**: if unemployment breaks and the Fed cuts, staples re-rate as the earnings-visible sector (counter-scenario ~$45); sector M&A (Mars–Kellanova) and 8x take-private math put a bid floor under brand portfolios.

## Bear Case
- **Dividend cut**: 79% payout of FY27E EPS, 1.24x FCF cover and falling, frozen seven quarters, 3.9x leverage — a ~35% cut is now published sell-side math; forced income-fund selling takes the stock toward $23–27 (−25% to −36%).
- **Structural topline erosion**: worst-in-class revenue, negative volumes in every segment, GLP-1 and private label secular; savings are spent defending a shrinking perimeter — consensus shows ≤2.5% EPS growth to FY2030.
- **Balance-sheet/impairment tail**: −$13.5bn tangible equity with $20.8bn of goodwill/intangibles (69% of assets) exposed to elevated discount rates; a second $2bn+ impairment is plausible, against 0.68 current ratio and revolver-dependent liquidity.

## Key Risks
1. **Dividend cut >25%** — the single binary event; not in consensus; decision window Dec 2026–Jan 2027.
2. **Hawkish overshoot**: 10Y through 5.31% collapses the yield-spread support → de-rate to 9x ≈ $27.
3. **UPF litigation**: a quantified reserve or multi-state AG coalition converts the uninsured tail from unquantified to real (rating → High).
4. **Second impairment ≥$2bn** or Pet/Blue Buffalo further deterioration.
5. **FQ2 FY27 print (17 Dec)**: toughest comp of the year (EPS est. $0.95, −14% YoY) into a contested dividend narrative.
6. **Execution risk**: new CEO + new operating model + restructuring all live on 1 Jan 2027.
7. **Data gaps** (stated plainly): Growth report missing; 10-K Item 3/1A, PFAS/tax contingencies, debt maturity ladder, covenants and revolver terms unverified (SEC access blocked); quant output truncated.
