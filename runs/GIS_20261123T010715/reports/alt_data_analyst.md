| "General Mills" (corporate) | 16.3 | 16.3 | **0%** | No new consumer interest; flat |

- **Cheerios is the one genuine positive.** Interest has held a 33–45 band for nine months vs a 25–32 band the prior year — consistent with the heavy 2026 innovation/renovation slate (Honey Nut Protein, Confetti Cheerios, packaging partnership). This supports the "organic sales flat" trajectory reported in FQ1.
- **Blue Buffalo is the warning.** Interest spiked to 96–100 in early April 2026, then fully mean-reverted to **14–17, below both its pre-spike base and prior year.** I could find **no active recall** (Blue Buffalo's own site and FDA lists confirm none) — so this was a media/promotional spike fully unwound. Search interest now sits at multi-year lows for the highest-margin segment.
- **Category headwind is structural, not cyclical.** Industry sources describe ~3 years of weak packaged-food volumes with GLP-1 adoption as a persistent drag; peer-reviewed work puts grocery spend down ~5.3% within six months of GLP-1 adoption in affected households. GIS is targeting GLP-1 users with reformulated products, but this is defence, not growth.

**Web traffic:** SimilarWeb direct pull returned **403 Forbidden**. Search-layer proxy indicates generalmills.com **trailing both annies.com and kraftheinz.com** in total visits in Sep and Oct 2026, while generalmills.ca rose +17.8% MoM. **Low-confidence, directionally soft** — corporate domain traffic is a weak proxy for a company that sells through retailers anyway.

*Note on the Feb 2026 "General Mills" spike to 53:* coincides with the Feb 17, 2026 price break (−7.3% on 22M shares, ~4x normal volume). Event-driven, not demand-driven, and fully faded.

Restructuring + divestiture + cost programs is a **defensive posture**, and it's the correct context for interpreting every other signal here.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **IN-LINE (EPS) / BELOW (revenue quality)**

**Setup for Dec 17:** consensus **EPS $0.946**, revenue **$4.713B** (vs $4.861B LY, −3%). Implies EPS −14% YoY against the toughest seasonal comp of the year.

Supporting the in-line call:
- **FQ1 FY27 (Sep 23) beat:** EPS $0.75 vs $0.717 (**+4.6%**); revenue $4.389B vs $4.351B. Critically, **organic net sales were flat** — the best organic print in over a year after FY26 organic of −2%.
- **Guidance reaffirmed:** FY27 EPS $3.00–3.20, organic sales −1.5% to +0.5% (guidance was set *below* the $3.41 consensus at initiation — expectations are already reset).
- **Beat history is strong:** 6 of 7 quarters beat, avg surprise **+4.63%**; four consecutive beats with magnitude ≥4.4%, including a +19.2% blowout in Jul 2026.
- Cheerios trend improvement corroborates the "stabilisation" narrative management will lean on.

Working against it:
- **Blue Buffalo search interest at multi-year lows** argues the pet segment is still losing consumer pull — a revenue/mix risk in the highest-margin unit.
- **The only miss in seven quarters (−12.1%, Mar 2026)** came in this same FQ3-style volume-sensitive window, showing the model can break when volumes roll.
- GLP-1 structural drag is unchanged sequentially.

**Net: I expect a modest EPS beat (low-single-digit, in line with the +4% pattern) on a still-shrinking revenue base. The print itself is not the risk.**

## 4. News Sentiment Score and Summary — **+0.455 (20 articles, 21 days) — positive headline score that is misleading**

Mechanically positive (8 positive / 3 negative), but the composition matters enormously:
- **The positive tail is yield-marketing, not operational:** "Which food dividend is better for retirees," "how much $2,500 could pay you in 2027," "4 high-yield staples to buy before December," "3 food & beverage dividend stocks." Plus the routine dividend declaration ($0.61/qtr, 128 consecutive years).
- **The negative tail is the emerging bear thesis:** **Piper Sandler flagged a potential ~35% dividend cut** (Nov 12) to free cash for debt paydown and growth; a separate piece ranks GIS among "7%+ yielders that could cut payouts"; another notes a $1,000 investment a decade ago is now worth $831 with dividends reinvested.
- **Tone shift:** 7 of the last 20 headlines are explicitly **dividend-sustainability** framed. This is a rising-frequency narrative (a new bear vector that did not exist a quarter ago).
- Valuation framing now standard in coverage: **~10.1x forward earnings, ~11.9x trailing FCF, ~7% yield.**

**Interpretation:** headline sentiment is positive; *narrative momentum* is negative. That divergence — a high yield attracting income buyers while sell-side begins questioning the payout — is a classic late-stage de-rating setup. A dividend reset is a **stock event, not an EPS event**, and it is not in consensus numbers.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (with a bearish skew)**

The operating stabilisation is real — organic sales flat, four straight beats, reaffirmed guidance, Cheerios interest up double digits YoY, and a stock already down ~40% from its Mar-2025 high with guidance pre-reset. That earns it above "Bearish."

But nothing in the alternative data says the *turn* has arrived: corporate search interest is dead flat YoY, Blue Buffalo brand pull has decayed to multi-year lows, hiring is plant-floor and cost-oriented, patents are unavailable, and the news narrative has quietly rotated from "cheap dividend" to "**can they keep the dividend**" (Piper Sandler's 35% cut scenario). Alt data is consistent with **a low-quality EPS beat on a shrinking top line** — which historically is the least rewarding kind of beat for a 7%-yielder.

**Positioning read:** the asymmetric risk at this print is not the EPS line — it is the dividend/guidance commentary, and neither is captured in consensus. I'd treat a headline EPS beat as a sell-into-strength event unless management explicitly defends the payout.

## 7. Confidence Level: **LOW-MEDIUM**

1. **Price history ends June 30, 2026** — a ~5-month gap. All pricing/yield math relies on the profile quote ($35.74) and November web snippets (~$34.48), not verified OHLCV.
2. **Patents: unavailable** (API failure, 2 attempts). No R&D intensity read.
3. **Web traffic: unavailable** (SimilarWeb 403); only a weak search-layer proxy.
4. **Job postings: qualitative proxy only** — no longitudinal counts, so no acceleration/deceleration measurable.
5. **Social: no data** for a name with no retail participation.
6. **Google Trends is a 0–100 relative index** and is contaminated by news events in both series (Feb-2026 corporate spike; Apr-2026 Blue Buffalo spike). The Blue Buffalo April spike remains **unexplained** — no recall, no identifiable catalyst — so treat that series with caution.
7. **Peer multiples not obtained:** the sector tool returned mega-cap staples (WMT, COST, KO, PG) rather than packaged-food comps (KHC, HRL, CAG, SJM, MKC). **Relative valuation gap — my 10.1x forward figure comes from secondary coverage, not my own screen.**
8. **Trends data ends week of Nov 15, 2026**; news through Nov 22. Two to three weeks of lag into a Dec 17 event.

Alt data is a leading indicator, not a verdict — and here the leading indicators are *flat*, not improving.
