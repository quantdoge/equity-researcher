This is a non-cash write-down, not an operating cash loss (OCF was +$2.17B). I therefore value GIS on **EV/EBITDA and P/FCF**, not earnings.
2. **The reported "earnings upgrade" is a methodology artifact.** The revision tool returned +3.02% by comparing the **FY2030** consensus ($3.431) against the **FY2029** consensus ($3.330) — *two different forward periods*, not a same-period revision. Worse, FY2030 has **n=1 analyst**. This is not a revision signal.
3. **Beta has almost no explanatory power.** R² = **0.0322** → ~97% of GIS variance is idiosyncratic. The −0.378 beta is statistically significant (t ≈ −2.9) but economically unusable as a hedge or risk input.
