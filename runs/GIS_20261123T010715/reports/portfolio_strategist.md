**Conflict resolution (my mandate):** where the Short Analyst and Value Researcher disagree, I weight the Short Analyst on *risk* and Value on *price*. ## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

**Derivation (Kelly-adjusted):** Base fair value $40 vs $35.74 spot = ~12% edge; downside to bear $25 = −30%; upside to bull $47 = +31%. With roughly even odds (p≈0.48), Kelly f* = p − (1−p)/b ≈ **+0.03, i.e. ~3% of an idiosyncratic risk budget**. Scale that by realized vol: 3% × (16% typical staples vol / 28.5% GIS vol) ≈ **1.7%**, then haircut for (a) the fat left tail of a dividend cut, (b) 0.76 correlation with CPB/KHC so the position is not truly independent, and (c) the Dec 17 binary → **0.75%, cap 1.25%**.

**Tiering logic from my framework:** *Low conviction + multiple risk flags (Fin Risk HIGH, dividend cut live, negative momentum, uninsured litigation) = 0.5–1.0% or pass.* GIS sits at the top of that band only because the 8.3% FCF yield and 33.6% gross margin put a genuine floor under the franchise.

**If the mandate is income:** cap at 0.75% and fund it from the *lowest-yielding* staples holding, not incremental sector exposure. Do not let a 6.8% coupon talk you past the 1.24x coverage.
