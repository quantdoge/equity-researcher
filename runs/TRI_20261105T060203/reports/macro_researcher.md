## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional — "late-cycle reflation colliding with a term-premium shock."** This is not a classic risk-on or risk-off tape; it is a regime where earnings are fine and discount rates are hostile.

The defining feature of the last six months is that **long-end yields have risen for the wrong reason**. The US 10Y went from 4.44% (30 Jun 2026) to a peak of 5.31% (5 Oct 2026) and sits at 4.95% (3 Nov 2026) — but 10Y breakevens *fell* over the same window, from 2.40% (late May) to **2.26%** (4 Nov 2026). Inflation expectations are anchored and drifting down; therefore essentially the entire rise is **real yields and term premium** (10Y real ≈ 4.95 − 2.26 = **2.69%**, versus ~2.04% at end-June, +65bp in four months). The 30Y at 5.47% points to fiscal supply/term-premium stress, not growth optimism.

Meanwhile the Fed has **pivoted back to tightening**: after cutting the funds rate from 4.33% (Jan 2025) to 3.63% (May–Aug 2026), the effective rate rose to 3.75% (Sep) and **3.88% (Oct 2026)**. With the 2Y at 4.74% versus an effective 3.88%, the market is pricing roughly **+85bp of further hikes over two years**. So the forward curve is *not* the friend of long-duration equities.

Mitigants: the curve is positively sloped (2s10s **+39bp**, no inversion, no recession signal), unemployment is a low and stable 4.2%, real GDP growth re-accelerated to ~3.4% annualised in Q3 2026, and credit is wide open (HY OAS **3.40%**, ~1pp tighter than a year ago despite widening ~40bp off the September low of 2.66%). This is a "high nominal growth, high real rates, tight credit" world — earnings-supportive, multiple-hostile.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact (TRI)

TRI is a ~**$50.8bn** Toronto-domiciled, USD-reporting information-services compounder (Legal Professionals, Corporates, Tax & Accounting, Reuters News; Global Print now 49% retained after the 51% sale to KKR for ~$500m, closed 1 Oct 2026). Q3 2026 (reported 3 Nov): **organic revenue +10%**, "Big 3" +10%, recurring +8%, transactions +23%; FY26 guidance raised to **organic 8.5–9.0%**, adj. EBITDA margin ~39%. Beta is **0.17** — the P&L is almost macro-insensitive. The macro transmission is through the **multiple**, not the earnings.

| Macro variable | Impact | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **10Y real yield +65bp / term premium** | **Strongly negative** | ~30x forward adjusted EPS (est.), high recurring revenue = long-duration equity. A theoretical 50bp WACC increase on a 25-year cash-flow stream is worth ~10–12% of value. TRI is **−22.8% over 12 months ($151.86 → $117.23)** even as organic growth *accelerated* from 8% to 10% — an implied de-rating of roughly 12 P/E turns. This is the single most important macro force on the stock. |
| **CPI +3.7% / Fed hiking** | **Positive on revenue, mildly negative on cost** | Subscription contracts carry annual escalators; with US law-firm billing rates up **+7.3%** and demand +3–4.2% in H1 2026, TRI has unusual pricing power — inflation is *monetisable*. Wage inflation (~half of opex) is the offset. Note Q3 adjusted EPS of $0.81 missed $0.89 consensus on software amortisation/margin pressure — so the pricing-vs-cost balance is no longer a free lunch. |
| **USD +3.2% since 9 Sep** | **Negative** | ~40% of revenue is non-USD (EUR, GBP, CAD, JPY, emerging markets) but reporting/guidance is USD. A 3%+ move in seven weeks is roughly a **1–1.5pp translation drag** on Q4 revenue growth — material against a ~8% Q4 organic guide. Offsets: CAD cost base and some hedging. |
| **Unemployment 4.2% / law-firm demand +3–4%** | **Positive** | Seat-count growth and customer P&L health are the real drivers; law firm profits +13% in 2026 support renewals and upsell. **The crucial interaction:** TRI sits at the intersection of the two things the market currently dislikes — long duration (real-rate exposure) and AI-disruption narrative. On 3 Feb 2026 Anthropic's legal-sector plugins triggered a record one-day **−16%** move in TRI and ~$285bn of legal-tech market cap loss; the stock bottomed at **$78.53 on 23 Feb 2026 (−48% from $151.86)** before recovering to $123.26 by 22 Oct. Macro did not cause that crash, but the high-multiple, long-duration profile is precisely what made it so violent. Macro and the AI narrative are *correlated vulnerabilities*, not independent ones.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **Neutral (with negative skew)**

Not "Unfavourable" because the operating environment is genuinely good for TRI: 10% organic growth, a +7% billing-rate end-market, monetisable inflation, tight credit, full employment, and a defensively low beta. Not "Favourable" because the **discount rate is doing more damage than fundamentals are creating**: ~2.7% real 10Y, a Fed that has resumed hiking, a market pricing +85bp more, and a strengthening dollar. For a ~30x subscription compounder, that is a 15–20% headwind to fair value for every 100bp of sustained real-yield increase. Net: **the business is fine; the valuation regime is not.**

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **Bear: "The un-relieved hike cycle"**

The market's complacency is in breakevens at 2.26% — it assumes the Fed wins. The surprise is that **CPI (3.7% and accelerating, +0.65% MoM in September) forces the Fed to keep going** — effective funds rate to 4.50%+ through 2027 — while GDP holds near 3%. That combination means *no rate relief and no recession to force it*. In that world the 10Y tests 5.75–6.00%, the 30Y through 6%, and high-multiple information-services names suffer a second de-rating leg. TRI at 25x an unchanged ~$3.85 adjusted EPS implies roughly **$85–95**, i.e. re-testing the February low despite mid-single-digit EPS growth. The trigger to watch is a hot October/November CPI print that pushes the 2Y above 5.0%; the tell would be breakevens *rising* alongside nominal yields (de-anchoring), which has not happened yet.

**The mirror-image bull surprise** is a growth crack (a payrolls print sub-50k, HY spreads widening through 4.5%) that flips the Fed back to cuts: the 10Y falls to 4.0%, and TRI re-rates to 35x — **~$135–140** — on the same earnings. Given beta 0.17 and 8–10% organic growth, TRI is a highly convex way to express a "term premium normalises" view.
