### Investment Memo: TRI — 5 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (constructive — initiate a small position) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $130 (~+11% from current $117.23); base intrinsic value $135, range $124–145
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months (decisive checkpoint: Q4/FY26 print, 4 Feb 2027)

**Investment Thesis**
TRI is a wide-moat, 88%-recurring-revenue information franchise (ROIC 14–16% vs ~8% WACC; A− credit; 75.8% gross margin held through the entire AI panic) trading at ~87% of intrinsic value after a −62% peak-to-trough AI-disruption repricing. The demand evidence has inverted the bear narrative — organic growth +10% for two consecutive quarters, gen-AI at 34% of contract value — but operating margin is compressing (−7.7pp since 2023) and the market now prices margin durability, not growth. This is a fairly-priced quality business with a genuinely contested terminal value: the debate is what the franchise is worth in 2030, not whether it survives, and that debate resolves on the margin line.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector ATTRACTIVE (non-discretionary, regulation-underwritten demand; e-invoicing mandates). TRI has the highest gross margin in its true comp set (+11pp vs median) but is the only large cap with a negative operating-margin trajectory. Macro is Neutral with negative skew: 10Y real yield +65bp to 2.69%, Fed re-hiking (3.88%), market pricing +85bp more — a 15–20% fair-value headwind per 100bp of sustained real-yield rise on a ~30x long-duration multiple. The P&L is macro-insensitive (beta 0.17); the multiple is not.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: LOW risk. Developed-market, USD, recurring revenue; no sanctions exposure. The Third Circuit ROSS win (Sep 2026) *monetises* the content moat by establishing an AI-training licensing market. Escalation paths: TRI named as an AI-copyright *defendant*, or a CLEAR data-privacy enforcement action.
- **Value**: Moat WIDE on current evidence (ROIC, switching costs, stable gross margin) but unproven through a full AI-native cycle. IV ≈ $135; bear $78 (precisely the Feb 2026 low); bull $160. At $117 the market capitalises ~4–5% perpetual FCF growth — mildly pessimistic, not a margin of safety. FCF has been flat for four years on +18% revenue.
- **Growth**: The strongest bull input. 10% organic, Big 3 +10% twice running, transactions +23% organic, FY26 guide raised to 8.5–9.0%. FV $135–150 on 18–19x 2027E EBITDA; Thomson LLM licensing is free optionality not in consensus. Caveats: Rule of 40 only ~34; 2026 adj. EPS +3% only; Q3 broke a five-quarter beat streak (−9%).

The bear case is directionally right but already half-paid-out after a 62% drawdown. **Bull Case**
- **The moat is monetising AI, not being eaten by it**: 10% organic growth, gen-AI at 34% of ACV, usage-based revenue +23%, gross margin flat at 75.8% — the single hardest refutation of disruption, since pricing erosion would show there first.
- **Fortress economics with legal validation**: 88% recurring revenue, 0.77x net leverage, and the ROSS appellate precedent converting the AI threat into a licensing revenue stream for Westlaw/Reuters content.
- **Convexity**: at 87% of IV with bull case $160+, a 0.17-beta long-duration compounder re-rates violently if term premium normalises; Thomson LLM commercial licensing (2027) is unpriced upside.

**Bear Case**
- **Growth without operating leverage**: operating margin −7.7pp (34.3%→26.6%), Legal EBITDA margin 46.6% vs 49.0%, Q3 operating income −32% on +2.9% revenue; AI cost is arriving before AI profit, and FY27 consensus (+18% EPS) is a steep bar off a +3% base.
- **Terminal value is genuinely contested**: seat-based pricing is attacked simultaneously by Harvey/Legora and by foundation-model suppliers who are both partners and captors; bear DCF is $78, and goodwill+intangibles are 112% of equity (negative tangible book) — impairment is the bear case's transmission mechanism.
- **Hostile valuation regime + governance discount**: real 10Y at 2.69% with a hiking Fed, debt-funded buybacks at $200+, repeat data-breach pattern, and zero insider buying at the lows.
