# Forensic Short-Seller Analysis: Thomson Reuters (TRI)

**Price $117.23 | Mkt cap $50.8B | Net debt $1.61B | EV ≈ $52.4B | FY25 rev $7.48B**

---

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **HIGH**

This is the least fraudulent large-cap I have reviewed. The cash does not merely corroborate the earnings — it exceeds them materially.

| Metric | FY25 | FY24 | FY23 | FY22 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operating CF / Net income | **1.80x** | 1.06x | 0.88x | 1.41x |
| FCF / Net income | **1.37x** | 0.80x | 0.68x | 0.98x |
| Accruals ratio (NI−OCF)/assets | **−6.7%** | −0.7% | +1.7% | −2.5% |

Every year negative accruals. There is no evidence of revenue recognition games, expense deferral, or working-capital manipulation. I went looking for a fraud thesis and did not find one.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

**A. Receivables: clean — the opposite of channel stuffing.** AR fell 3.7% while revenue rose 3.0% (FY25), and fell 3.4% vs +6.8% revenue in FY24. DSRI 0.935. Days sales outstanding is *shortening*. In a channel-stuffing case you see the reverse. **Not a red flag.**

**B. Gross margin: stable, no pricing-power crack *yet*.** 75.1% → 76.1% → 77.0% → 75.3% → **75.8%**. There was 170bp of compression in FY24 that has partially reversed. GMI 0.994 confirms no deterioration. **The single strongest refutation of the AI bear case** — if generative AI were already eroding Westlaw pricing, it would show here first. It doesn't.

**C. The revenue reconciliation gap — my most serious finding.** Reported total revenue grew **+9.8% (Q1'26), +9.5% (Q2'26), then +2.9% (Q3'26)**. Management simultaneously publicises "Big 3 organic growth +10%" and "Legal Professionals +9% at constant currency, *all organic*." Yet FY26 consensus revenue is **$7.73B = +3.4%**, consistent with the *reported total*, not with 9–10% organic. A persistent 600bp+ wedge between the promotional metric and the number you actually own implies ~$250–275M of annual drag from Global Print/Reuters News (a >15% decline in that pool). Arithmetic: if Big 3 (~$5.9B) grows 10%, that is +$590M — against total revenue growth of only $254M. **This gap is disclosed nowhere clearly and is the highest-value item to reconcile before shorting.** It also means the celebrated 2026 "re-acceleration" is a segment story, not a company story.

**D. FCF is flattered by underinvestment.** Capex $645M vs D&A $930M = **0.69x**. FCF of $2.05B benefits from ~$285M of depreciation not being reinvested — roughly **14% of stated FCF**. TRI spends "$200M+ annually" on AI integration; if competitive pressure forces capex toward D&A parity, FCF falls to ~$1.77B.

**E. Shareholder returns exceed free cash flow.** FY25: dividends $1,057M + buybacks $1,018M = **$2,075M = 101% of FCF** — before ~$1.1B of M&A (goodwill +$811M). Funded by drawing cash from $1,888M to $605M (−68%) and lifting net debt $385M. Leverage is still trivial (0.53x EBITDA), but the era of cash-funded buybacks plus acquisitions is over.

**F. Adjusted EPS overstates GAAP by ~17%.** FY25 consensus "EPS" was $3.99 vs **GAAP $3.40**. Serial-acquirer intangible amortisation add-backs (~$400M+ of the $930M D&A) are treated as non-economic, though the capital deployed to earn them was entirely real. Standard for the sector, but it means every headline multiple understates cost.

**G. Goodwill & intangibles = 70.6% of assets.** $12.66B against equity of $11.91B → **tangible book value is −$746M**. An AI-driven impairment to the Legal franchise would wipe out reported book value. This is the bear case's real transmission mechanism — slow, but enormous.

## 3. Beneish M-Score & Accruals

**M-Score: −2.788 → "Low risk"** (comfortably below the −2.22 soft and −1.78 hard thresholds). Component indices are uniformly benign: DSRI 0.935, GMI 0.994, AQI 1.083, SGI 1.030, DEPI 0.945, SGAI 0.992, LVGI 0.963, TATA **−0.067**. There is no discernible manipulation signature.

**Piotroski F-Score: 7/9** (missing only on ROA improvement and equity change). **Altman Z: 2.63 "grey"** — but this is an artefact of negative working capital (current ratio 0.64x), which is structural for a prepaid subscription business with deferred revenue, not a distress signal. Ignore it.

*Tool caveat: the composite earnings-quality score returned 46/100, which directly contradicts its own inputs (180% cash conversion, 137% FCF conversion, −6.7% accruals). I regard the raw ratios as authoritative and the composite as unreliable.*

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment: **REAL, STRUCTURAL, NOT YET IN THE NUMBERS**

- Anthropic's Claude Cowork, upgraded with legal-task plugins (contract review, NDA triage, compliance workflows), was explicitly cited as the catalyst for the Feb 2026 collapse. Harvey, Legora, Spellbook attack the workflow layer above Westlaw.
- TRI's defence is genuine but narrower than bulls claim: KeyCite citator, editorial classification, and workflow integration. LLMs commoditise *retrieval*, which is where the seat-based value sits. The moat defends a shrinking share of perceived value.
- **Counter-evidence bulls are right about:** CoCounsel passed **1 million users** (Feb 2026), driving +11.4% and +10.3% two-day rallies; Big 3 organic growth stayed 9–10%; gross margin held. Caveat: "users" is a bundled/free-tier metric, not paid seats — precisely the kind of promotional KPI I distrust.
- **Truth:** disruption is a 2027–2030 terminal-value question, not a 2026 earnings question. Churn in legal research moves at contract-renewal pace, not at model-release pace.

## 5. Management & Governance

- **Woodbridge / Thomson family control (~66–69%).** No takeover optionality, structurally no minority voice. Historically not abusive, but it removes every external check.
- **Historical GAAP EPS was flattered by LSEG stake disposal gains, now exhausted.** FY21 NI $5,689M on operating income of only $1,242M; FY23 NI $2,695M on $2,332M OI. The FY25 −30% EPS decline is a base-effect artefact, *not* operating deterioration — don't misread it as a red flag, but do note that sell-side models anchored on that CAGR.
- **No evidence of auditor churn or related-party abuse.**
- **Data gaps (honest disclosure):** SEC Form 4 data returned only two filings (2017, 2020) — insider selling cannot be assessed from this tool. Stock-based comp is reported as zero across all five years, which is a data-provider omission, not a company disclosure. Woodbridge's periodic block sales should be verified independently.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary

**The accounting short does not exist.** Beneish −2.79, accruals −6.7%, 180% cash conversion, receivables shrinking against rising revenue, stable gross margin, fortress balance sheet. Anyone shorting TRI on a fraud or manipulation premise is wrong.

**The valuation/terminal-value short is real but already half-paid-out:**

| | Now | Bear | Bull |
|---|---|---|---|
| EBITDA | $3.05B | $2.6–2.8B | $3.4B |
| Multiple | 16.3x EV/EBITDA | 12x | 18x |
| Implied equity | $50.8B | −22% to −39% | +13% |

Downside:upside ≈ 2:1 — modestly favourable, not compelling. And the entry is hostile: the stock is **−44% from its $208 July 2025 peak** and **−62% peak-to-trough** (Feb 2026 low $78.53), having de-rated from ~45x to ~28x P/E and ~16x EBITDA. Every bear point above — AI, Anthropic, "why own Westlaw" — is now the consensus. There is no informational edge in being bearish on Thomson Reuters in November 2026. Meanwhile ROIC is ~12.2% against a ~8% WACC; the spread is thin but positive, and a 400bp gross-margin collapse is required to break it — which has not begun.

**What would make me short, with conviction and a position:** gross margin breaking below 74%, net sales retention disclosure deteriorating, Big 3 organic growth dropping below 4%, or a goodwill impairment charge. **Trigger-watch, not a position.**

## 7. Contrarian Rating: **NO EDGE — Reject Short Thesis**

**Position: no short. No long either.**

The bear case is directionally correct and quantitatively unsatisfying. You are asked to short a business with 180% cash conversion, falling receivables, stable 76% gross margins, 0.5x leverage, and a 2.1% dividend yield, at 28x earnings that are genuinely cash-backed — purely on a terminal-value hypothesis that the P&L has not yet begun to validate, after a 62% drawdown that has already transferred most of that hypothesis into the price.

**Where I concede to the bulls:** on every forensic axis — manipulation probability, accruals, revenue recognition, receivables discipline, balance-sheet integrity — TRI passes cleanly. My toolkit is built to catch frauds. This is not one. If the AI disruption does arrive, it will show up as slow margin compression over 6–8 quarters, and I would rather pay up for that signal than underwrite a multi-year thesis short in the meantime.

**Single highest-priority verification:** reconcile the >600bp wedge between reported total revenue (+2.9% Q3'26) and the "Big 3 organic +10%" narrative against the actual segment tables. I could not retrieve the Q3'26 release body (page returned navigation boilerplate only). If that gap turns out to be aggressive organic-definition or a non-Big-3 segment deteriorating far faster than disclosed, my rating moves to **Short**. It is the only place in this file where the numbers and the narrative diverge — and that, not the AI headlines, is where the next real bear case will be found.
