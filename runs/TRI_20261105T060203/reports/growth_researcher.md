# Thomson Reuters (TRI) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $117.23 | Market cap ~$51B | EV ~$54B | Data as of early Nov 2026*

## 1. Growth Story

Thomson Reuters is the most compelling "AI-disrupted incumbent becomes AI beneficiary" re-rating in information services. On Feb 2–3, 2026, Anthropic's launch of a Claude legal plug-in triggered a ~$1T software/services selloff and took TRI from $150 to a $76 low (−50%) on fears that frontier LLMs would commoditize legal research. Eight months later the evidence has inverted: **Q3 2026 organic revenue grew 10%** — with the "Big 3" segments (Legal, Corporates, Tax/Audit/Accounting) also +10% and usage-based transaction revenue +23% organically — and management *raised* full-year organic guidance to 8.5–9.0%. The moat did not break; it monetized. TRI's proprietary content corpus, workflow embedment (Westlaw/Practical Law → CoCounsel), and now the **Thomson LLM** (a domain-tuned legal model benchmarked against Anthropic, OpenAI and DeepMind) are converting an existential threat narrative into an accelerating growth algorithm.

Big 3 addressable pool ≈ **$65–70B**, and TRI captures ~$7B ≈ **11% penetration**. Two points make this a *growth* TAM rather than a mature one: (1) the pool itself is compounding high-single digits as compliance/regulatory complexity rises; (2) **AI expands the buyer set** — the "prosumer"/small-law and non-lawyer-corporate segments that were never economic to serve at $200+/seat can now be served by agentic product at lower price points. Transactions revenue +23% organic (Q3) is the tell that TRI is capturing new usage-based demand, not just renewing seats.

**Differentiation: proprietary content + workflow + a proprietary model.** General-purpose LLMs hallucinate on legal citations; TRI's Thomson LLM is trained on validated case law and statutory content with lower hallucination rates than frontier models on legal benchmarks. That is the "innovation moat" — data network effects from 150+ years of curated primary law, embedded in the system of record where the work actually gets done.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends, "CoCounsel" (5-yr):** interest held ~50 through late 2025, inflected to a 100 peak in early April 2026, and remains elevated at **77–84 in Oct 2026** — no post-hype decay. Sustained brand pull for the AI product, not a launch spike.
- **Hiring (job-posting proxy):** heavy AI-specific requisitions — Thomson Reuters Labs research engineers, Staff Platform Engineer (Materia AI), and notably **"Legal AI FDE (forward-deployed engineer)"** roles. FDE hiring is a leading indicator of enterprise AI deployment revenue, not R&D theater.
- **Internal monetization signal:** recurring organic +8%, transactions organic **+23%** — consumption/agentic pricing is inflecting ahead of seats. This is the strongest evidence the AI products are generating incremental revenue.
- **Contradicting signal:** segment adj. EBITDA margin compression (Legal Professionals adj. EBITDA +4% to $368M, margin **46.6% vs 49.0%**) and Q3 FCF −23%. Growth is being bought with technology spend — the bear's best data point.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, not yet Strong Buy)

**Why Buy:** TRI now delivers 8.5–9% organic growth — above RELX (~7%) and Verisk (~7%) — while trading at a **discount**: 17.8x trailing EV/EBITDA and ~24x 2027E adj. EPS vs. RELX 22.1x, Verisk 20.5x, MSCI 30.0x (FactSet, the low-growth comp, is 15.1x). On 2027E EBITDA of ~$3.55B at 18–19x, fair value is **$135–150 (+15% to +28%)**, with a bull case of $160+ if Thomson LLM licensing materializes. Consensus PT ~$131.

**Why not Strong Buy:** Rule of 40 is 30.5 today and only ~34 in 2026 — below the 40 threshold — because AI investment is compressing margins and 2026 EPS grows just +3%. Gross margin is flat-to-down (75.8% vs 77.0% in 2023). And the disruption question is *deferred, not resolved* — Anthropic remains both partner and predator.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Existential/competitive AI risk (the big one).** A frontier lab shipping a genuinely good, cheap legal agent — bundled into a general assistant — could compress legal research pricing faster than TRI's content moat defends. The Feb 2026 −16% day is a live demonstration of how violently the market will price this tail.
2. **Execution risk on affordability.** TRI's own AI must not cannibalize high-priced seats faster than transaction revenue replaces them. If organic recurring decelerates below ~6% while margins compress, the bull case breaks.
3. **Margin/earnings quality.** Q3 2026 adj. EPS missed by 9% and FCF fell 23%; guidance implies FY2027 EPS +18%, a steep acceleration off a +3% base — high execution bar.
4. **M&A integration and pricing.** Serial bolt-ons (SafeSend, Materia, cPaperless) plus $2B new debt add acquisition-amortization noise and integration risk; the 2025 revenue mix already includes inorganic contribution.
5. **Concentration/thin float and Woodbridge control.** The Thomson family's Woodbridge stake limits float and can mute governance responsiveness; beta of 0.17 belies the true event risk.
