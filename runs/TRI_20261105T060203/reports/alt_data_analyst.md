### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (demand-tilted Bullish, earnings-line Bearish)

Two coherent but opposing reads:
- **Bullish case:** Every demand-side alt signal is *green and improving* — traffic +12% MoM, AI-focused FDE hiring, 34% AI share of ACV, 10% organic growth sustained. The stock has de-rated ~40% from its ~$195–208 mid-2025 highs and −25% over 12 months on AI-disruption fear that **the demand data does not corroborate**.

The market has stopped paying for growth and started pricing margin durability and terminal-value risk. Alt data can confirm *demand*; it cannot confirm *margin*.

**Net: Neutral.** If the Feb 4, 2027 print clears the ~39% EBITDA margin bar, the demand alt-data supports a genuine re-rating toward the $130–131 consensus target. Until then, demand strength is necessary but not sufficient.
