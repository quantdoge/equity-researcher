## 2. Consensus Assessment: **MEDIUM-LOW — constructive on the business, split on the price**

**Genuine consensus (high agreement, high weight):**
1. **The moat is real and it is not a fraud.** Value (WIDE moat, ROIC 14–16%), Short (Beneish −2.79, 180% cash conversion), Sector (highest gross margin in comp set), Non-Fin (ROSS win) all converge. Gross margin has held at 75–77% through the entire AI panic — the strongest single refutation of the bear case.
2. **The balance sheet is not the risk; terminal value is.** Fin Risk, Short, Value agree. Credit was *upgraded* into the equity collapse.
3. **AI disruption is a 2027–2030 terminal-value question, not a 2026 earnings question.** Sector, Short, Growth, Alt Data all say churn moves at contract-renewal pace.
4. **Operating margin is the swing variable.** Sector (−7.7pp), Growth (46.6% vs 49.0% Legal EBITDA margin), Fin Risk (op. income −32% on +2.9% revenue), Alt Data, Macro all independently nominate the same metric. **This is the position's single KPI.**

**Contested (weight in the sizing decision):**
- *Price*: Growth says BUY at 17.8x EV/EBITDA; Value says HOLD until $101. Both are internally consistent — Growth capitalises a 2027 recovery, Value refuses to pay for it. I weight Value higher on **entry discipline** and Growth higher on **direction**.
- *Momentum*: Quant says SELL; Short says the bear case is "already half-paid-out" after −62% peak-to-trough. Quant's own inputs are partially corrupted and its Sharpe (+1.06) is a window artifact. I discount Quant to a *timing* signal, not a thesis signal.

**Conflict resolution (per framework: the risk flag governs):** Non-Fin's HIGH rating and Fin Risk's capital-allocation finding take precedence over Growth's BUY and Value's HOLD-for-quality. A business with a repeat data-breach pattern and management buying back stock at $200 with borrowed money does **not** earn a full-size position, however good the franchise. → **Reduced size, staged entry.**

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector.** GICS files TRI under *Industrials / Specialty Business Services* — a misclassification that will make any sector-exposure report understate information-services concentration. Economically it belongs with RELX/VRSK/SPGI/MSCI/FDS/WKL. **Test before adding: if the book already owns RELX or VRSK, TRI is a fourth bet on the same AI-terminal-value question, at 0.69–0.70 correlation.** At that correlation, 1.5% TRI plus 3% RELX plus 2% VRSK is one ~4% factor bet, not three diversifiers. **Hard cap: aggregate information-services exposure (TRI + RELX + VRSK + SPGI + MSCI + FDS) ≤ 8% of portfolio.**

**Factor exposure — net negative additions on three axes:**
- **Momentum:** adds a 12-1 ≈ −27%, 2nd-percentile name. If the book has a momentum or quality-momentum tilt, this is an active anti-momentum bet. Acceptable only as a deliberate value/contrarian sleeve.
- **Duration:** long-duration, real-rate-sensitive. At 2.69% real 10Y and a hiking Fed, **do not fund this from cash while also holding other 25x+ long-duration compounders** — that is a doubled bet on term premium.
- **Low-vol / defensive:** the label is a trap. Beta 0.13, but 46% realized vol and a −63.5% drawdown. **This will not behave defensively in a growth scare** despite the beta suggesting it will.
- **Offsetting positive:** near-zero correlation to broad equity (idiosyncratic), so it *does* diversify market beta at the portfolio level even though it is volatile standalone. That is the whole case for holding 1.5% rather than 0%.

**Correlation.** Primary risk is intra-sleeve, not vs. SPY.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss discipline — price *and* fundamental, with the fundamental dominant.** At 46% annualised vol, a mechanical 15% stop is ~5 weeks of ordinary noise and would almost certainly be triggered by nothing. Therefore:

- **Hard review trigger: $90** (−23%, near the Feb-2026 $78.53 low zone). At $90 the stock is ~2/3 of base IV and inside Value's bear-case band ($78–95). *Trigger a full re-underwrite, not an automatic exit* — the bear case may already be in the price. Cut to 0.5% if the review finds any fundamental trigger also hit.
- **Position size is the primary risk control**, not the stop. 1.5% × −33% bear case = **−50bp of portfolio** — a loss the book absorbs without a decision. That is the design.

**Thesis invalidation (any one → exit fully, regardless of price):**
1. **Organic revenue growth <6% for two consecutive quarters** (from 8.5–9.0% guided). Value, Short, Growth and Sector all independently nominate this.
2. **Gross margin breaks below 74%** (currently 75.8%; four-year range 75.3–77.0%). Short Analyst's designated short trigger. This is the earliest hard evidence that AI is eroding Westlaw pricing.
3. **Disclosed net revenue retention deterioration, or explicit seat-count/ARR decline** in Legal Professionals.
4. **A goodwill impairment charge** (goodwill + intangibles = 112% of equity; tangible book −$1.3bn) — the bear case's actual transmission mechanism.
5. **A state AG enforcement action or injunction against CLEAR/PeopleMap data sourcing**, or confirmation the C-Track breach exposed sealed records at scale. Non-Fin's escalation item #2 (repeat privacy failure) is the one governance flag I will not tolerate through a second cycle.
6. **The revenue-reconciliation gap the Short Analyst flagged** — if the ~600bp wedge between reported total growth and "Big 3 organic +10%" turns out to be a promotional organic definition, thesis is void.

**Trim trigger:**
- **$135 (base intrinsic value) → trim one-third.** Rule: never let a 1.5% position become a 3% position on re-rating; that converts a value trade into an accidental momentum bet.
- **$145–150 (upper IV / Growth's bull case) → reduce to 0.5%.** The franchise is priced for success; recycle into names still on the wrong side of the margin trade per the Sector Researcher's preference ranking (RELX, SPGI, VRSK).
- **Trim 0.25% into the Feb 4 print** if the position is at full 1.5% and the stock is above $125 — the outcome distribution around that EPS line is genuinely wide.

**Monitoring calendar:** Feb 4 2027 (Q4/FY26 + first FY27 guide — the decisive event); quarterly operating margin and Legal segment EBITDA margin; any AI-copyright suit naming TRI as defendant.
