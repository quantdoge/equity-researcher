**Price $170.15 (Nov 24, 2026 close, +14.1% on 79M shares / ~35x normal volume) | Mkt Cap $37.3B | EV ~$41.0B at deal price**

> **⚠️ The single most important fact first:** on the morning of Nov 24, 2026, **Sanofi (SNY) agreed to acquire Insmed for $175.00/share in cash — $41.0B equity value, ~46% premium to the $119.80 unaffected price, closing expected H1 2027.** Every sell-side desk followed with downgrades to Neutral/Market Perform and $175 targets (UBS, Mizuho, BMO, Morgan Stanley). The trailing consensus "Buy / $180.89 PT" on data feeds is **stale pre-deal**. This is no longer a growth stock — it is a bond proxy with a (very good) underlying business attached.

### 1. Growth Story

Insmed executed the single best rare-disease launch of the decade: **Brinsupri (brensocatib)**, the first and only approved DPP1 inhibitor, went from $0 to a ~$1.35–1.4B annualized run-rate in roughly 15 months, becoming the standard of care for non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB) — a chronic, previously untreated disease with devastating exacerbation burden. Revenue grew **+268% YoY in Q3'26** while gross margin simultaneously expanded to **85.1%**, the classic signature of a fixed-cost pharma platform hitting scale. **But the market has already paid for it:** Sanofi's $175 bid capitalizes the entire remaining ASPEN-to-2030 trajectory — and then some, in Sanofi's favor at only ~6x 2030E revenue.

### 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **NCFB 7MM+China market:** ~$3.5B (2022) before any approved therapy existed; brensocatib is **first-in-class with no competitor** for ~3–4 years.
- **Peak sales consensus: ~$4.3B** for brensocatib across indications.
- **Penetration today is the bull case:** ~$1.4B 2026 sales against a large diagnosed-but-untreated US population implies **low-single-digit penetration** after one year — the growth runway is genuinely long, which is precisely why Sanofi paid up.
- **Label expansion optionality:** EU approval (Dec 2025), **Japan MHLW approval** (launch pending), plus CRSwNP, hidradenitis suppurativa and other neutrophil-mediated indications in the pipeline. **TPIP** (inhaled treprostinil palmitil) is in Phase 3 for PAH and PH-ILD — a second potential franchise Sanofi gets free.
- **Differentiation:** oral, once-daily, mechanism-validated by ASPEN (19.4% exacerbation reduction). No oral competitor; the alternative is nothing.

### 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends ("Brinsupri", US, 5-yr):** sustained interest 51–96, averaging ~70 in recent quarters with **no post-launch decay** — the classic signal of durable refill/persistence rather than a trial-pop demand spike. Supports the persistence assumption behind the $4B+ peak.
- **FCF trajectory (4 consecutive quarters of narrowing losses):** the strongest non-GAAP corroboration that this is real commercial scale, not channel stuffing.
- **Revenue beat streak:** four straight top-line beats — launch tracking *ahead* of modeled consensus, so guidance has been conservative.
- **Refuting signal — price action history:** the stock fell from a **$212.75 high (Nov 2025) to a $90.39 low (June 2026)**, including sharp one-day breaks on Dec 18, 2025 (−16%) and May 7, 2026 (−23%) on heavy volume. The financial data does not identify the trigger; the pattern reads as a sentiment/credibility reset around spend and pipeline perception rather than a demand problem. **Gap noted — not resolvable within budget.** This volatility is also why the arb spread now looks thin rather than fat.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

**The business: A / the stock: fairly valued to slightly rich.**

The disruptive thesis is fully validated — first-in-class mechanism, 85% gross margins, 268% growth, four consecutive FCF improvements, and a $6.2B 2030 revenue path that Sanofi is buying at just **6.6x 2030 sales and ~17.5x 2030 EPS**. Sanofi is getting a bargain; INSM shareholders already collected the re-rating.

Post-deal the economics are arithmetic, not thesis: **$175.00 vs $170.15 = +2.85% gross**, over ~7 months to an H1'27 close → **~4.9% annualized**. Against that sits **~−29% downside to the $119.80 unaffected price** if the deal breaks — a **0.1:1 reward-to-risk ratio**. Probability-weighting at a 90–92% close rate gives fair value of **$169.5–$170.6, essentially today's price.** There is no growth alpha left to capture here; a growth mandate should redeploy into the *next* brensocatib, not pay 98 cents for a dollar that closes in 2027.

- **Action for existing holders:** Hold and collect the $175. Rotating out only makes sense if you find a higher-returning use of capital for the 7-month lock-up; the 4.9% annualized is decent for a low-risk spread but not a growth return.
- **Action for new money: do not initiate.** You are underwriting an antitrust outcome, not an innovation curve.

### 7. Key Risks

1. **Deal-break risk (dominant risk, ~5–10%):** antitrust/HSR second request or ex-US regulatory delay. Break would reset the stock toward ~$120 (unaffected), a −29% move. Any break fee received would only partially cushion.
2. **Financing/macro risk on Sanofi's side:** a $41B all-cash deal is a large draw; deterioration in SNY's credit access or a major adverse event at Sanofi could pressure terms — though the merger agreement makes a re-cut unlikely absent a financing condition failure.
3. **Time risk:** close slips past H1 2027 → annualized arb return compresses toward zero; each quarter of delay costs ~1–1.5% of return.
4. **Fundamental (moot for INSM holders, relevant to Sanofi):** Brinsupri launch durability depends on payer access and persistence; NCFB is a heterogeneous population where real-world adherence often lags trial adherence. Competitive entrants (other DPP1/neutrophil-pathway programs, e.g. developed by large-cap peers) enter the 2028+ window.
5. **Concentration/spend risk:** SG&A still 48% of revenue and DTC spend is about to ramp — margin conversion could disappoint relative to the $10 2030 EPS model that underpins the bid's logic.
6. **Pipeline execution:** TPIP Phase 3 is the largest un-derisked asset Sanofi inherits; a miss there affects Sanofi, not the $175 cash price.
