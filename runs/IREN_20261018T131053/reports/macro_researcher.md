## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

IREN is a **vertically integrated Bitcoin miner pivoting to an AI cloud/neocloud operator** — the most macro-rate-sensitive business model in the market, for three compounding reasons: (a) it is a pure long-duration asset (value is NPV of a 2028–2032 AI capacity build); (b) it is a **serial capital raiser with $25–30B of fiscal 2027 capex against a $10.6B market cap**; (c) it carries a 4.27 beta and a second revenue engine (BTC mining) that is itself a liquidity-sensitive risk asset.

- **Real discount rates — NEGATIVE (dominant).** With real 10Y at ~2.75%, every 50bp of real-rate move is worth a mid-teens percentage change in a long-dated capacity NPV. This is empirically visible: from 1 July (10Y 4.48%) to 16 October (10Y 5.10%), IREN fell **−38%**; from the 5 Nov 2025 close of $76.41 it is **−65%**. The stock is at its 52-week low while GDP grows 2.2% — this is a *discount-rate* de-rating, not an earnings de-rating.
- **Cost of capital / financing access — NEGATIVE.** The near-term catalyst of the October collapse was explicitly macro-financial: CoreWeave bond yields spiking, a shelved AI data-centre IPO, a $850M zero-coupon convert priced at Cipher, and a Gartner warning that >80% of neoclouds may not survive independently to 2030. IREN must fund $25–30B with its equity down 65% and the 30Y at 5.37% — the marginal cost of capital has risen sharply and the dilution risk per dollar of capex has roughly doubled.
- **Inflation on the cost base — NEGATIVE.** 3.7% CPI with sticky services inflation feeds directly into construction labour, transformers/turbines, switchgear and power equipment — the exact bottlenecks for a multi-GW buildout. IREN's PPAs fix some power cost, but the *capex* line is inflating, which cuts return on invested capital precisely when capital is repricing. Mining economics also suffer: hashprice is fixed in BTC terms while opex and hardware inflate.
- **Growth — NEUTRAL / mildly positive.** 2.2% real GDP and 4.2% unemployment mean the AI demand backlog isn't a growth problem. IREN's risk is financing, not demand.
- **USD & trade — MILD NEGATIVE.** Broad USD +3.0% in four weeks. IREN is USD-reporting with CAD/AUD cost bases (BC, Texas, South Australia, Spain), so translation of opex is a small tailwind, but a firming dollar is mechanically hostile to BTC and to global risk appetite — a bigger net negative via the mining revenue line.
- **Fed hike risk — NEGATIVE.** The single most dangerous line in the news flow: "rising expectations for a Fed rate hike." A 2Y at 4.77% against a 3.75% EFFR says the market has already begun to price it.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Growth and credit are fine — that is not the issue. The issue is that IREN's valuation is a levered bet on the *long end of the curve and the credit window*, and both are deteriorating simultaneously: real 10Y ~2.75%, 30Y 5.37%, front end repricing toward hikes, HY spreads widening 55bp in five weeks, and a fat 3.7% CPI that removes the Fed put. A $10.6B company needing $25–30B of capex in this rate environment is structurally exposed. Macro is currently a **headwind large enough to dominate company-specific execution**; the 65% drawdown from the November 2025 peak is a macro discount-rate event, not an operational failure.

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise

**Bear tail (higher probability than the market's 2.35% breakevens imply): a Fed hike becomes consensus.** If Q4 CPI prints stay ≥3.5% and the FOMC moves to a tightening bias, the 10Y clears 5.75% and HY OAS widens past 5%. Neocloud credit windows shut (CoreWeave's already cracking), and IREN — with $25–30B of committed capex, no ability to fund at 5.37%+ long rates, and a cratered equity currency — is forced into deeply dilutive equity issuance. That is a further **−40% to −55%** downside from $26.99 despite unchanged AI demand. The Gartner neocloud-viability warning is, at bottom, a macro-financial-access call, and it is being validated in the bond market before the equity market fully marks it.

**Bull mirror (the asymmetry worth owning):** if October CPI confirms the September spike was tariff/energy noise and CPI rolls back under 3.0%, the 90bp of hike pricing in the 2Y unwinds violently. A 60–80bp rally in the 10Y would compress real rates, reopen the convert market, and re-rate a 4.27-beta long-duration name by **+50–70%** — the same channel that took IREN from $26 to $76 in 2025, run in reverse. Watch the 2Y first: it is the leading indicator for this stock, not the Bitcoin price.
