# Sector Brief — IREN Limited (NASDAQ: IREN)

## 1. Sector Overview

**Classification caveat first:** FMP tags IREN as *Technology / Software – Services*. That is materially misleading and useless for benchmarking against a $5tn NVDA/AAPL cohort. The economically correct sector is **powered data-centre infrastructure** — the convergence of Bitcoin mining (IREN's legacy, ~$700m of FY26 revenue) and AI/HPC colocation + GPU cloud (the "neocloud" trade it is pivoting into). Sub-scale, capital-hungry, and priced off contracted backlog rather than current earnings.

The sector's defining 2026 dynamic is a **scarcity-of-power arbitrage**: miners collectively control roughly 27GW of planned/interconnected power capacity, and Morgan Stanley models 68GW of US data-centre demand between 2026–28 against only ~38GW projected supply — a ~30GW shortfall. That has turned stranded grid interconnects into valuable options, and IREN has converted: **$4bn of contracted ARR for 2026 capacity (~85% contracted), a ~$13bn backlog**, a Microsoft GPU contract, and $9.3bn of funding raised in eight months. But the sector is now in its **capital-cycle stress phase, not early growth**: the gap between signing contracts and collecting cash is being financed with convertibles, prepayments and GPU leases, and October 2026 delivered a violent de-rating — Gartner's warning that **>80% of neoclouds may cease independent operations by 2030**, spiking CoreWeave bond yields, a shelved AI data-centre IPO, and renewed Fed-hike expectations. IREN is down ~65% from its $76.87 high and sits at the low ($26.99 vs 52-wk range $26.55–$76.87).

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **Power scarcity as pricing power.** ~30GW US supply shortfall 2026–28 means energised, liquid-cooled capacity commands rents well above legacy DC economics. IREN's 68.9% gross margin (2nd best in peer set, +27pts vs 41.6% median) is direct proof of the rent capture on powered shells.
- **Prepayment-funded GPU capex reduces equity dilution risk.** $3.6bn tied to the Microsoft contract funds **96%** of associated GPU capex via prepayments; $2.8bn of other GPU financings fund ~90%. Plus a $2.6bn conv. at **1.00% coupon due 2033** — genuinely cheap, long-dated money.
- **Demand visibility is contractual, not speculative.** $4bn contracted ARR exiting 2026 and a $13bn multi-year backlog give IREN forward revenue coverage rare in this sector; a frontier-lab customer (reportedly Anthropic, unconfirmed) alongside Microsoft/NVIDIA validates the platform.
- **Liquid cooling expands revenue per MW** on the same power footprint — the highest-ROIC lever available, needing no new interconnect.

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **The financing gap is now the whole investment case.** FY27 capex guidance of **$25–30bn against a $10.6bn market cap** (2.4–2.8x). In a rising-rate tape with CoreWeave's credit spreads widening, the marginal dollar of build-out sets the equity value, not the backlog.
- **Bitcoin mining is in a post-halving margin trough.** MARA gross margin **–47.7%**, RIOT **–15.6%** (FY25). Mining cash flow can no longer self-fund the AI build — the legacy business is a melting ice cube being deliberately harvested.
- **Neocloud viability / customer concentration.** Gartner's >80% failure forecast, single-customer concentration, and construction timelines mean contracted ARR ≠ collected cash. Sell-side has IREN as Moderate Buy with an **$81 target against a $26.99 price** — an 3x dispersion that itself signals how unresolved the debate is.
- **Depreciation intensity is under-appreciated.** FY26 D&A of $417.7m = **59% of revenue**; CRWV's D&A is 48% of revenue with interest at 24%. GPU/ASIC obsolescence shortens asset lives faster than DC shell lives.

## 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG (on unit economics) — with a financing-dependent qualification**

**Rationale for strong:**
- **68.9% gross margin, +27pts above peer median and second only to CRWV** — the clearest evidence that IREN owns genuinely scarce assets (energised, liquid-cooled capacity) rather than just GPUs.
- **Vertically integrated** — owns land, power, shell, and compute hardware. Few peers control the full stack; most rent or lease (APLD at 25.8% GM is the tell for lease-heavy models).
- **Contractual coverage:** $4bn ARR, $13bn backlog, ~85–90% of associated GPU capex pre-funded — a structurally better funded position than CIFR (zero-coupon converts) or CORZ (revenue down 37.5%).
- **Only profitable-on-an-operating-basis mining operator in the group** on clean-year data.

**Why not unambiguously strong:** revenue growth of +41.1% is *below* the +48.0% peer median at exactly the moment peers are accelerating — IREN is lapping a huge FY25 and monetising power slower than APLD/CRWV/NBIS. And the $25–30bn FY27 capex against a $10.6bn cap means **the equity is a financing instrument first and an operator second**; a widening credit spread or a Fed hike compresses IREN's value more than its contracted backlog protects it.

## 6. Sector Rating: **NEUTRAL**

Demand-side fundamentals are exceptional — a ~30GW power shortfall is a genuine multi-year tailwind, and IREN holds best-in-class unit economics on scarce assets. But the sector has moved from *scarcity pricing* to a **capital-cycle auction where returns are being bid away toward the cost of capital**: a 27GW pipeline chasing 30GW of demand, financed with convertibles and prepayments into a rising-rate tape, with >80% of neoclouds forecast to fail as independents. The October 2026 de-rating (IREN –65% from highs, CoreWeave bonds widening) is the market repricing exactly that. **Attractive for operators with pre-funded capex and investment-grade-adjacent customers; unattractive as a group.** IREN screens as one of the better-positioned ways to own the theme — but it is a leveraged bet on the financing window staying open, not a defensive sector exposure.
