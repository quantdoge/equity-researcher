### Investment Memo: IREN — October 20, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $22 (-18% from current $26.99)
**Time Horizon**: 12 months

**Investment Thesis**

IREN owns genuinely scarce, well-located powered assets with Tier-1 commercial validation (a $9.7bn Microsoft contract, an NVIDIA warrant struck at $70), but the equity is a financing instrument, not an operating business: revenue has fallen 43% from its Sep-25 peak while debt rose 8x, and the company must raise $25–30bn of FY27 capex against a ~$10.6bn market cap into a rising real-rate, narrowing-credit-window tape. Our target sits below the Value team's ~$32 probability-weighted enterprise value because 12 months of expected dilution (share count +41% in the past year) plus a stale $81 sell-side consensus facing a likely Nov 5 reset push per-share value toward the low $20s.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector:** Unfavourable. Real 10Y ~2.75%, 2Y at 4.77% pricing Fed *hikes*, HY spreads widening; IREN (beta 4.27) is the most rate-sensitive model in the market. Sector holds a genuine ~30GW power-shortfall tailwind, but has shifted from scarcity pricing to a capital-cycle auction — Gartner's >80% neocloud-failure call is being validated in credit first.
- **Geopolitical & Legal:** Medium-High. No sovereign risk (all OECD), but the two growth jurisdictions are actively re-regulating: Texas ERCOT interconnection audit/pause and BC's discretionary power allocation gate the pipeline the equity is priced on. Live EDNY securities class action targeting the core AI-readiness claim; dual-class/FIRB/ACCC structure limits optionality.
- **Value:** Sell/Avoid. IV ~$32 (range $2–$80); no margin of safety at $27. ROIC has never exceeded WACC; cumulative 4-yr FCF −$3.9bn; Adjusted EBITDA overstates owner earnings given GPU depreciation reality.
- **Growth:** The lone BUY — 2.6x contracted ARR and ~1.5x FY28E revenue with Microsoft/NVIDIA/developers signed. But only ~$1bn of the $4bn ARR was operating in August, revisions are −60.9%, and consensus itself models a 1.7% FY30 net margin.
- **Quant:** Sell. Sharpe −0.36, Sortino −0.68, vol 107%, worst drawdown in miner peer set; the positive 12−1 momentum is a stale artefact that excludes the crash.
- **Financial Risk:** HIGH. Net debt/EBITDA 56x, interest coverage −17.7x, Altman Z 0.497 (distress), Piotroski 4/9, FCF −$2.23bn — but $5.9bn cash and 1% converts due 2033 mean no solvency event within 24 months. Risk is dilution and multiple compression, not default.
- **Non-Financial Risk:** HIGH. 15-vote founder B-shares, $66.7m of insider sales with zero open-market buys (including through the drawdown), co-CEO key-person concentration, 685 employees executing a multi-GW build.
- **ESG:** ESG Risk, governance-driven (4/10). Environmental exposure is regulatory/cost (ERCOT curtailment, AASB S2 disclosure), not footprint; governance penalises the very capital raises the thesis depends on.
- **Alternative Data:** Bearish into Nov 5. Consensus FQ1 revenue of $229.5m requires +67% QoQ after three consecutive misses; search/news/social signals are fear-driven and deteriorating.
- **Short / Contrarian:** SHORT, moderate conviction. Earnings quality is low — FY26's $2.1bn OCF is substantially customer prepayments, and the reported Beneish "low risk" reading flips into warning territory (~−1.97) once adjusted. Critically: **not a fraud** — the funded balance sheet and real Microsoft contract cap conviction.

**Bull Case**
- Contracted demand is real and partly prepaid: $9.7bn Microsoft (20% cash up front), NVIDIA's $3.4bn contract plus a warrant at $70, $4bn contracted ARR / $13bn backlog on scarce energised capacity — best-in-class 68.9% gross margin, +27pts above peer median.
- Funded for ~24 months: $5.9bn cash, long-dated 1% converts, no maturity cliff — the bear case is dilution, not bankruptcy.
- Violent upside asymmetry if the macro turns: a CPI rollover unwinding the 90bp of hike pricing could re-rate a 4.27-beta long-duration name +50–70%, and the stale $81 consensus creates squeeze fuel.

**Bear Case**
- The operating engine is shrinking into a leverage explosion: quarterly revenue $240m → $137m (−43%), Q4 EBITDA negative, SG&A +253%, $639m impairment admitting prior capital was mis-sized.
- Financing dependency at the worst moment: $25–30bn FY27 capex (2.4–2.8x market cap) with the 30Y at 5.37% and the neocloud credit window narrowing — further dilutive issuance at depressed prices is the base case.
- Governance, regulatory and catalyst risk are correlated: founder control with insider selling, a live class action on the core claim, Texas/BC gating the pipeline, and a binary Nov 5 print with the bar set ~3x above the operating trend.

**Key Risks**
- **To our SELL call (upside):** Nov 5 confirms operating ARR >$2.5bn and first positive AI-Cloud EBITDA; the unconfirmed Anthropic deal is formalised; non-dilutive asset-level financing lands; CPI rolls under 3.0% → short squeeze toward $45–65.
- **Downside accelerants:** Fed-hike consensus + HY OAS >5% closes the financing window; Microsoft acceptance slippage or contract penalty; further impairments on $6.76bn PP&E; a raise at >10% dilution below $30.
- **Data integrity flag (Head of Research challenge):** FY26 OCF of $2.1bn contains a ~$2.6bn unreconciled gap in Q4; a ~$0.66bn operating charge remains unexplained; market-cap feeds disagree ($8.5bn vs $10.6bn); FY26 financials post-date part of the price series. These gaps weaken confidence in any precise valuation and argue for sizing conservatively.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
Core/diversified portfolios: **0% — pass.** IREN is a risk-aggregator, not a diversifier (crypto beta + AI-capex beta + long-duration rates exposure, correlated downside). Existing holders: reduce/exit into strength; do not average down — the 65% drawdown is a discount-rate event, not a valuation floor. Speculative sleeves only: max 0.25–0.5%, and only after Nov 5 confirms (i) operating ARR >$2.5bn, (ii) positive AI-Cloud EBITDA, (iii) committed non-dilutive FY27 funding. Hard stop $22; trim any speculative position at $45 and $65–72. Revisit constructively below ~$20 *with* proof of positive owner earnings and ROIC >10%.
