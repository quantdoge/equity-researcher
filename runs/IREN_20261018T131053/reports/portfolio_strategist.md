## 2. Consensus Assessment

**Agreement level: LOW / SPLIT — with a pronounced negative skew.**

- **Negative / risk-flag views (8 of 11):** Macro, Value, Fin Risk, Non-Fin Risk, Quant, Short, ESG, Alt Data.
- **Neutral / qualified (1):** Sector Researcher — strong unit economics, but neutral on the sector and explicit that IREN is “a leveraged bet on the financing window staying open.”
- **Constructive (1):** Growth Researcher — the only outright Buy, based on contracted ARR and forward revenue multiples.
- **Near-term bearish (1):** Alt Data — explicitly into the Nov 5 print.

**The core contested issue:** Growth argues the market is pricing failure at 2.6x contracted ARR even though Microsoft, NVIDIA and other AI developers have signed multi-year, partly prepaid contracts. Value and Short argue those contracts are not yet collected cash, the build requires $25–30bn of new capital against a $10.6bn equity base, and the accounting presentation flatters the underlying economics.

**How the conflict resolves:** the Growth case is real but *conditional on financing and execution*. The Value/Fin Risk/Short case identifies the conditions that must be met before the growth is worth owning. Because the downside is permanent capital impairment through dilution — not merely a bad quarter — the risk flags take precedence over the forward revenue multiple. The Short Analyst’s own conclusion is important: this is **not a fraud short**, which removes the most severe tail risk but does not create a buy signal. The Sector Researcher’s “strong on unit economics, financing-dependent” verdict is the correct framing: a good asset inside a fragile capital structure.

**Net consensus:** the business has genuine strategic assets; the equity does not offer an adequate risk-adjusted return at $26.99. Consensus is not “short the fraud” — it is **“do not own this before the financing and operating-ARR questions are answered.”**

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### Core diversified portfolio: **0% — Pass**

IREN fails the position-sizing framework on multiple dimensions:

- **Conviction:** low-to-negative across the majority of agents.
- **Risk flags:** leverage, dilution, governance, regulatory, customer concentration, and a binary Nov 5 catalyst.
- **Kelly criterion:** using the Value Researcher’s scenario weights — bear $2 (−93%, 30%), base $27 (0%, 45%), bull $80 (+196%, 25%) — expected return is positive (~+21%), but the Kelly fraction is **negative** because the probability of loss is 75%. Even using more generous growth weights, Kelly supports only a token position, not a core allocation.
- **Beta-adjusted sizing:** with beta ~4, a 1% IREN position contributes roughly 4% of market beta and ~1.1% of portfolio volatility. A 3–5% “high conviction” position would add 12–20% beta and 3–5% portfolio vol — unacceptable for a name with this risk profile.

### Recommended sizing by mandate

| Mandate | Position Size | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Core / diversified** | **0%** | Risk flags dominate; no margin of safety; binary catalyst. |
| **Opportunistic / high-risk sleeve** | **0.25–0.5% maximum** | Only after Nov 5 confirmation; treat as a venture-style lottery ticket, not a core AI-infrastructure holding. |
| **Existing holder** | **Reduce to ≤0.5% or exit** | Do not average down. Use strength to reduce; the 65% drawdown is a discount-rate event, not a valuation floor. |
| **Growth-mandate, forced exposure** | **0.5% starter, max 1.0%** | Only if the fund must own the theme; pair with stricter stop and catalyst conditions. |

**Why not 1–3%:** the framework reserves 1–3% for medium conviction or elevated risk. IREN has *multiple* elevated risks that are correlated with each other — a Fed hike hurts the valuation, widens credit spreads, raises dilution risk, and compresses the AI-infrastructure multiple simultaneously. Correlated risks should be sized smaller than their standalone probability suggests.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

### Sector concentration
IREN is misclassified by FMP as “Technology / Software – Services.” Economically it is **powered data-centre infrastructure / neocloud** — a hybrid of Bitcoin mining and AI colocation. If the portfolio already owns AI-infrastructure exposure (NVDA, MSFT, AMD, CRWV, NBIS, APLD, CIFR, power/utility names), IREN adds **concentration to the same AI-capex factor**, not diversification. If the portfolio already owns Bitcoin miners or BTC, IREN adds a second layer of crypto beta.

### Factor exposure
Adding IREN increases unintended tilts:

- **High beta:** 3.9–4.3 vs SPY. A 1% position is equivalent to ~4% market beta.
- **High volatility:** 106–108% annualised; worst in the miner peer set.
- **Negative quality:** negative ROE, negative FCF, Altman distress, Piotroski 4/9.
- **Negative earnings revision:** FY30 EPS revisions −60.9%; three consecutive EPS misses.
- **Small/mid-cap illiquidity risk:** ADV is adequate, but volume spikes coincide with gap-downs, i.e. distribution.

### Correlation
- **vs SPY:** ~0.49.
- **vs miners:** 0.63–0.74 (MARA, RIOT, CLSK, HUT).
- **vs AI-infrastructure peers:** likely high, given the shared AI-capex/rates factor, though a clean matrix was not available.
- **R² vs SPY:** only 0.24 — ~76% idiosyncratic. That sounds diversifying, but the idiosyncratic risk is precisely the financing/execution/regulatory risk we are trying to avoid. High idiosyncratic vol is not the same as diversification.

**Portfolio-fit conclusion:** IREN is a **risk-aggregator**, not a diversifier. It correlates with crypto beta, AI-capex beta, and long-duration rates exposure simultaneously. For most portfolios, the correct weight is zero.

## 5. Entry Strategy

### Base case: **no entry now**

Do not initiate before the Nov 5 FQ1’27 print. The event is binary: consensus requires +67% QoQ revenue after three consecutive misses, and the sell-side price target (~$81) is ~3x the current price, indicating unrevised models and high downgrade risk. Buying ahead of that is gambling on a beat, not investing.

### If the fund requires exposure, use a conditional scale-in

**Gate 1 — Nov 5 print (0.25% initial):**
- Operating AI-cloud ARR **>$2.5bn** (vs ~$1bn in August).
- First positive EBITDA quarter from AI Cloud.
- FY27 capex guidance **≤$30bn with committed, non-dilutive financing** disclosed.
- No new equity/convertible issuance at a discount to $30.

**Gate 2 — FQ2’27 (add 0.25%):**
- Operating ARR **>$3.0bn** and on track for the $4bn year-end target.
- Capex per MW stable or falling; interest expense covered by EBITDA.
- Share count growth **<5%** sequentially.

**Execution:** use limit orders, not market orders — 11–17% single-day moves are documented. Consider a **put-spread hedge** or a **long-CLSK/short-IREN pair** if the mandate permits, rather than a naked long. Avoid sizing up on a post-earnings gap; wait for the first pullback that holds above the pre-print low.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

### Stop-loss
- **Hard stop:** $22.00 (−18.5% from $26.99), closing basis. A tighter stop at $25 would be triggered by normal volatility (107% annualised; 11–17% single-day moves are common).
- **Technical overlay:** a close below the 52-week low of $26.55 on above-average volume is a warning; a close below $25 confirms.
- **Gap-risk caveat:** stops may not fill in a high-beta drawdown. This is why position size must be small enough to absorb a 30–40% gap without portfolio-level damage.

### Thesis invalidation — exit immediately if any occur
1. **Operating ARR <$2.0bn** at the Nov 5 print, or the **$4bn year-end 2026 ARR target is missed**.
2. **Microsoft contract delayed, renegotiated, or terminated**, or prepayment terms materially altered.
3. **FY27 capex >$30bn without committed financing**, or a new equity/convertible raise at **>10% dilution below $30/share**.
4. **Additional impairment >$250m** on PP&E or mining assets.
5. **Texas PUCT/ERCOT suspends Childress or Sweetwater beyond contracted delivery dates**, or BC impairs existing capacity.
6. **Fed hike becomes consensus and HY OAS >5%** — the financing window closes; the equity is a financing instrument first.
7. **Covenant breach or cross-default** on the Blue Owl GPU facility or converts.

### Trim trigger

- **Valuation-based:** the Value Researcher’s probability-weighted intrinsic value is ~$32; 90% of that is ~$29 — essentially today’s price. There is no margin-of-safety trim level that justifies holding.

### Monitoring dashboard

## 7. Final Portfolio Action

### **PASS — do not initiate.**

For existing holders: **REDUCE / EXIT** into strength; do not average down.

**Rationale in one paragraph:** IREN owns genuinely scarce powered assets and has commercial validation from Microsoft and NVIDIA, but the equity is priced as a financing instrument rather than an operating business. The company must raise and deploy $25–30bn against a $10.6bn market cap while revenue has fallen 43% from its peak, EBITDA is negative in the latest quarter, interest coverage is −17.7x, and the macro backdrop is repricing real rates and closing the neocloud credit window. The growth case is real but entirely conditional on execution and financing; the value case offers no margin of safety; the quant and alt-data signals point to further near-term downside; and the governance/regulatory structure penalizes minority capital. A 0.25–0.5% speculative position is defensible only for mandates that explicitly permit venture-style risk, and only after the Nov 5 print confirms operating ARR, AI-cloud EBITDA, and non-dilutive financing. For a diversified portfolio, the correct weight is zero.

**What would change the recommendation:** operating ARR >$2.5bn with positive AI-cloud EBITDA, committed non-dilutive capex funding, a credible path to ROIC >10%, and a stabilization in real rates/credit spreads. Until then, IREN is a watchlist name, not a portfolio holding.
