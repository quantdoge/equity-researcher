**Bear Case**

- Structurally impaired core: the only major telecom with falling revenue (-5.2% FY25; consensus -5.7% CAGR through 2030), margins ~22pp below peers, negative ROIC and FCF, serial guidance misses.
- Non-diversifiable risk stack: CRITICAL financials, VERY HIGH legal/regulatory exposure with license value hostage to politics, 2/10 governance with no minority recourse — in a security with -41% realized drawdown.
