# Viasat, Inc. (NASDAQ: VSAT) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price ~$74 | Market cap ~$10.2B | EV ~$15.0B | Net debt $4.8B | 7,000 employees**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Viasat is **not** a growth stock that has re-rated — it is a **declining consumer satellite ISP that has been re-labelled as a spectrum landlord**. The 8x move from ~$9 (June 2025) to ~$90 (peak May 2026, +123% YTD) was driven almost entirely by *asset-value optionality*: Oppenheimer's Timothy Horan called VSAT "the last major global satellite spectrum play left" and valued its L-/S-band MSS holdings at **~$15B conservative**, after Rocket Lab's $8B EV acquisition of Iridium removed the last comparable spectrum asset from the market.

The genuine operating growth engine is **Defense & Advanced Technologies (DAT)**, up 20% YoY in Q2 FY27 with segment EBITDA +57%, riding Space Force awards, the USMC $307M-ceiling IDIQ, and Golden Dome-adjacent demand. The problem: DAT is only ~31% of revenue, while **Communication Services (69% of revenue) is shrinking 3% with EBITDA down 13%** — Viasat has lost **81–83% of its US fixed broadband subscribers** since Starlink's 2020 launch. Viasat is the *disrupted*, not the disruptor, in its largest segment.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

**Two very different TAMs:**

- **D2D / MSS spectrum (the bull case):** Harmonized L-band + S-band, **>100 MHz across 160+ markets** — genuinely scarce, globally coordinated spectrum. This is the largest addressable prize and Viasat's only true "moat" asset. But Viasat is monetizing it as a *wholesaler*, not a platform owner: the **Equatys JV with Space42** (up to $1B combined equity commitments, ~60 MHz S-band ex-North America) is a capital-light licensing structure. **Penetration: effectively 0%.** Value is real but unrealized and binary.
- **Aviation IFC + Defense (the operating case):** ViaSat-3 F2 and F3 are now in service (F3 on a SpaceX Falcon, Asia-Pacific), expanding global capacity. DAT TAM is expanding via Space Force/Golden Dome. **Penetration: low but so is the growth rate** — DAT is $1.5B annualized against a defense satcom market measured in tens of billions, yet backlog grew only 7%.
- **Fixed broadband (the melting ice cube):** Viasat has structurally lost this TAM to Starlink. 81–83% US subscriber decline is not cyclical — it is terminal.

**Critical caveat:** Viasat is not the disruptive platform in D2D. SpaceX/Starlink and AST SpaceMobile own the direct-to-consumer relationship and capital. Viasat's realistic outcome is a **spectrum sale or licensing royalty**, which is an M&A trade, not a compounding growth thesis.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Equatys JV closing — Q3 FY27 (Oct–Dec 2026):** Formation with Space42 subject to regulatory approval; **prime technology contractor appointment** is the value-validation event. Slip risk is real.
- **Spectrum monetization / M&A — 2027:** Post-Iridium/Rocket Lab ($8B EV, closing mid-2027), VSAT is the last scalable harmonized MSS block. A sale, JV, or D2D partnership is the single largest re-rating lever (~$15B Oppenheimer mark vs $15B total EV).
- **Q3 FY27 earnings — Feb 4, 2027:** Must show DAT mid-to-high-teens growth *and* FCF tracking the ~$180M guide after the cut. Consensus $1.23B rev / −$0.15 EPS.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

This is a **speculative asset-value / deleveraging story wearing a growth-stock multiple**, and it has already been paid for. Against my growth framework, VSAT fails on nearly every operating criterion: **2–3% revenue growth, Rule of 40 of 15.5, gross margins compressing 550bp, widening net losses, a guidance cut from mid- to low-single-digit, awards down 24%, and guided FCF of ~$180M against a $10.2B market cap (~57x)**. The headline 19% CAGR is an Inmarsat accounting artifact; organic growth is ~4%.

The bull case is not wrong — the L-band/S-band spectrum is genuinely scarce, Equatys is a clever capital-light structure, DAT is compounding at 20%, and deleveraging from 3.2x toward 2.5x would re-rate equity. But after an **8x move**, the market has already capitalized a meaningful portion of that spectrum optionality, and the core cash engine (Communication Services, 69% of revenue) is in structural decline with EBITDA falling 13%.

**Hold, do not add.** I would become constructive on: (a) an announced spectrum transaction or D2D partnership with a real counterparty, or (b) two consecutive quarters of DAT growth ≥15% *with* FCF tracking above $250M and leverage under 3.0x. I would turn negative on a break below ~$60 (the Q2 gap level) or a further FCF guide cut, since at 57x guided FCF there is no valuation support for execution slippage.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Structural disruption in the core (highest severity):** Communication Services = 69% of revenue, EBITDA −13%, US fixed broadband subs down 81–83% vs Starlink. This segment funds the whole company and is shrinking.
2. **Leverage / balance sheet:** $4.8B net debt, 3.2x, S&P B+, ~$400M annual interest — nearly 1.5x guided FCF. Refinancing risk if credit spreads widen; equity is a levered call option on the satellites.
3. **Capex treadmill:** $850–900M FY27 capex (incl. $250–300M Inmarsat-related). FCF is a residual, not a certainty — FY27 guide already cut to ~$180M.
4. **Execution & guidance credibility:** Revenue miss, EPS miss ($−0.93 vs −$0.42), beat rate 42.9%, guide cut, awards −24%. Management's forward visibility is weak.
5. **Spectrum value is illiquid and binary:** The $15B Oppenheimer mark is a private-market appraisal with no committed buyer. Barclays' $49 target implies the market disagrees with the SOTP by ~35%. Equatys could slip or be funded on worse terms.
6. **Competitive intensity:** SpaceX/Starlink and AST SpaceMobile are better-capitalized D2D platform owners. Viasat risks being a spectrum supplier to its own competitors rather than capturing the D2D economics.
7. **Cost ramp ahead of revenue:** ViaSat-3 F2/F3 in-service costs hit P&L before utilization revenue — margins likely worsen near term.
