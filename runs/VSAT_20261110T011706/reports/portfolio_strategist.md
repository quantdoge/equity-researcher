## 2. Consensus Assessment — **SPLIT, with a bearish operational consensus and a live bullish binary**

**Consensus (High agreement, 9 of 11 agents):** the *operating* business is deteriorating and does not support the price.
- Revenue growth 2–3% with **gross margin compressing ~560bp** — all agents agree this is pricing-power loss, not mix.
- Core consumer franchise is destroyed: US fixed broadband ~603k → ~100k (−83%), corroborated independently by the Alt Data search series.
- The FY26 "FCF inflection" is **structurally one-time**: Value ($568M Ligado), Short ($420M Ligado + $227M WC), Growth (guide cut to ~$180–225M) all reach the same conclusion from different angles.
- Balance sheet is the binding constraint: Fin Risk and Non-Fin Risk both flag 0.30x interest coverage, Altman distress zone, and a 2029 wall.

**Contested (genuine split):** *what the spectrum is worth.*
- **Bear side (Value, Short):** Oppenheimer's ~$15B L-band/S-band mark is a private appraisal with **no committed buyer**; Viasat's MSS L-band is encumbered by federal incumbents and ITU/Ligado coordination, unlike EchoStar's deployable US terrestrial AWS-4. Management **explicitly declined** to commit to a spectrum sale or DAT separation in Nov 2026.
- **Bull side (Growth, Alt Data's "respect the asset story"):** the board adopted a comp plan with **$150–300/share milestones** tied to the strategic review; Iridium ($8B EV to Rocket Lab) and Globalstar transactions have repriced the comp set; Barclays' $49 vs Oppenheimer's $140 target is a 3x dispersion that itself signals the market has no earnings anchor.

**Conflict resolution:** the framework says fraud/risk overrides value. Here there is **no fraud override to apply** — the Short Analyst's forensic work is unusually clean (Beneish −2.83, negative accruals every year, OCF/EBITDA 87%, DSRI 1.03). So the *value* view survives intact: base intrinsic ~$40. What does override, however, is **leverage plus refinancing**: because 3.2x net debt sits ahead of equity in any spectrum monetization, a large share of the bull case accrues to bondholders, not shareholders. That caps how much of the $90–110 bull case I will capitalize.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation — **0% new capital; reduce existing to ≤0.5%**

| Sleeve | Recommendation |

| **Existing position** | **Trim to ≤0.5% of portfolio** — and treat that residual purely as a **dated, defined-risk call option on a spectrum/DAT transaction**, not as an equity investment. Size it to a **total loss**; the bear case is $10–15, i.e. −80% from here. |
| **Absolute ceiling if a transaction is announced** | 1.5%, and only on a confirmed counterparty + executed definitive agreement (not a review, not an MoU). |

**Rationale — why not 1–3%.** The framework's 1–3% band assumes medium conviction *or* elevated risk. VSAT is low conviction **and** elevated risk **and** at a premium to intrinsic value simultaneously. Three additional sizing suppressors:

1. **Volatility-adjusted sizing.** At 85.7% annualised volatility, a 1% position contributes roughly **5x the risk of a 1% S&P position**. On a risk-parity basis, a 1% VSAT weight is the equivalent of a ~4–5% index weight. A 1% nominal position is already a meaningful, top-quartile risk allocation.
2. **Beta-adjusted exposure.** Beta 3.00 (1y daily; 2SE band 2.38–3.66 — decisively > 1). A 2% position adds **~6% of market beta** to the book in a bear-steepening regime. Unacceptable unless deliberately taken as a macro-short-duration hedge, which is not the mandate here.
3. **Asymmetric payoff geometry.** Bear −80%, bull +35–45% to $100–110. To make a 1% position worth the monitoring cost you need the transaction probability to be ≥50%; the evidence (Equatys still pending approvals, management declining to commit, $400M of equity going *into* the JV rather than out to shareholders) suggests well below that.

## 5. Entry Strategy

**Primary: no entry.** If the desk insists on maintaining optionality on the strategic-review catalyst, the only defensible structure is:

- **Instrument:** defined-risk only — long-dated calls or a small common position capped at 0.5%. **Do not** use a naked short (the Short Analyst's own caveat: +780% in a year, live M&A optionality, three >20% single-day gaps in the trailing year, April 2026 +19% on a peer's takeover speculation). A short here can be right on fundamentals and still be stopped out by a headline.
- **Trigger-based, not price-based.** Add only on: (a) a **definitive** spectrum transaction or D2D partnership with a named, creditworthy counterparty; (b) **DAT separation filed** with terms; or (c) two consecutive quarters of DAT growth ≥15% **with** FCF >$250M and leverage <3.0x. None of these are in hand — Equatys is "anticipated," not closed, and management declined to commit to separation.

Retail mentions are **down 82%**; the move is not accumulation.
- **If a fundamental long is ever warranted,** the price is **$45–55**, where EV/underlying FCF approaches ~20x and the deleveraging case starts to carry the equity on its own. Below ~$45 the Short Analyst himself calls the thesis void.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss (for any residual long):**
- **Hard price stop: $56 on a closing basis.** Rationale: 252d max drawdown low was $57.38; the Q2 FY27 gap low was $59.38. A close below $57 breaks both and confirms the momentum reversal Quant flagged. That is ~−25% from $74 — wide in percentage terms, but this is an 86%-vol name where a 2-sigma daily move is ~11%; a tighter stop is noise-harvesting.
- **Volatility stop:** if realised 20-day vol exceeds 110%, halve the position regardless of price — that regime means an idiosyncratic event (satellite anomaly, Equatys rejection, Ligado adverse ruling) is in progress.

**Thesis invalidation (fundamental — exit on any of these):**
1. **Equatys fails to close, or closes on terms requiring incremental Viasat equity** beyond the $400M committed. Non-Fin Risk is right that committing equity *before* collecting the Ligado receivable into a 'B'-rated balance sheet is the sequencing error.
2. **Ligado damages awarded against Inmarsat**, or the escrowed $100M is not released. This underwrites the entire deleveraging narrative.
3. **FY27 FCF guide cut below $180M**, or FY27 revenue guide re-based again.
4. **Additional goodwill impairment** — Goodwill is $3.49B = 76% of equity and already fell $569M in six months. Further impairment erodes both the covenant and the equity cushion with no cash offset.
5. **Refinancing of the $400M 2027 notes at a coupon materially above 7.625%**, or a draw on the revolver. HY OAS is +77bp in nine weeks; a 'B' issuer does not control this market.
6. **Commercial aviation contract loss disclosure** beyond the 15–20% of aviation revenue already flagged — this is the highest-margin engine of Communication Services.

**Trim trigger:**
- **Trim 50% at $88–90** (the $89.81 high / momentum-reversal zone and the top of the recent distribution range). Note the framework's standard "trim at 90% of intrinsic" is inoperative here: at $74 the stock is already at **~185% of base-case intrinsic** and at the short analyst's stated "thesis-void-if-covered-above-$95" line.
- **Trim the balance at $95–105** absent a named counterparty. The 11-analyst average is ~$90.78 and the dispersion ($49 Barclays → $140 Oppenheimer) is an admission that nobody is anchoring to earnings.
- **Do not trim on the way down** into a binary catalyst — that is how you sell the option at the moment it becomes valuable. The stop and the invalidation list do that job.

**Structural discipline:** calendar-review the position at each of the three dated catalysts — **Equatys closing (Q3 FY27)**, the **~Feb 4, 2027 Q3 FY27 print**, and the **mid-2027 Iridium/Rocket Lab close** (which re-marks the spectrum comp set). If none has produced a definitive transaction by the FY28 capex guide, close the residual: you are then paying 68x underlying FCF for a guided aviation decline with no catalyst left.

A short is a bet on multiple normalisation that can be stopped out by a headline.

**The synthesis in one line:** *VSAT is a levered, low-quality, capital-intensive GEO operator whose equity has been re-rated ~10 notches on an operating turnaround that is worth ~1 notch — the entire premium to intrinsic value is a binary option on spectrum that bondholders have a prior claim on. Own it as a sized-to-zero event position with a hard stop, or not at all. At $74.37, "not at all" is the correct portfolio answer for new capital.*

**Recommended book representation:** 0% new; existing holdings trimmed to **≤0.5%**, defined-risk, hard stop $56, trim at $88–90, three dated catalyst reviews. Revisit as a **fundamental long at $45–55**.
