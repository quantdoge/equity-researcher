### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — **Sharply Negative**

| Google Trends (US, weekly, 0–100) | Jun–Jul '26 avg | Latest 8-wk avg | Δ |

- **"Viasat internet" is the cleanest read and it is deteriorating badly.** It printed 24 (wk of Oct 25) and 34 (Nov 1) — the two lowest readings in the trailing 12 months, vs. a 12-month average of ~66 and a Nov-2025 comparable of ~65. That is roughly **−48% YoY** and −59% off the summer peak. This is the search footprint of someone shopping for residential satellite broadband, and it is in structural decline — consistent with the reported fixed satellite services revenue decline and the long-running subscriber-attrition narrative (Viasat has reportedly lost ~57% of US fixed broadband subs since Starlink's debut).
- **The competitive gap has widened ~2x.** Starlink/Viasat-internet interest ratio went from ~1.0x in Jun–Jul to **2.1x** now. Starlink has recovered off its Sept lows (61–83 range) while Viasat internet sits at 12-month lows.
- **"Viasat" brand search bottomed at 39 (Oct 4) and recovered to 52–54** in late Oct/early Nov — but that recovery tracks the Oct 28 +11% and Nov 9 +16% stock moves, not product demand. Brand search is now a **stock-narrative proxy, not a demand proxy**. Do not read it as improving fundamentals.
- **Web traffic:** SimilarWeb shows viasat.com visits **−1.81% MoM**. Directionally corroborative but low magnitude and low precision (search-proxy only; wifi1.viasat.com in-flight portal data returned an unusable comparison).

### 2. Business Activity Signals — **Mixed, tilting to defense**

- **Hiring: stable-to-modest, and skewed away from consumer.** ~300–400 Viasat listings visible on Indeed, with LinkedIn showing a clear tilt toward **Embedded Rust / software engineers, "Viasat Government," RF/antenna engineering technicians**. Read: investment is flowing into the **DAT (Defense & Advanced Technologies)** and government franchise, not into consumer broadband. Consistent with DAT +20% YoY and Communications Services −3%.
- **Patents: DATA GAP.** The USPTO PatentsView feed failed twice (malformed response) under both "Viasat Inc" and "Viasat." No R&D-intensity trend available. Flag as an unverified gap.
- **Corporate activity is the real signal here:** the board adopted a compensation plan tied to **stock-price milestones of $150–$300/share**, explicitly aligned to an ongoing **strategic review** of (a) the DAT segment's value and (b) the company's **spectrum** value. William Blair interprets the 8-K as the board believing equity is worth **≥$150/share** based on recent Iridium and Globalstar spectrum transactions. This is an asset-value/sum-of-the-parts catalyst, not an operating one.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **BELOW consensus (revenue); EPS uninformative**

**Revenue miss pattern is persistent and the bar for H2 is now implausible:**

| Quarter | Actual | Consensus | Surprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| FQ4'26 (May '26) | $1,171.3M | $1,195.6M | −2.0% |
| FQ1'27 (Aug '26) | $1,156.5M | $1,199.7M | −3.6% |
| FQ2'27 (Nov '26) | $1,180.0M | $1,201.8M | −1.8% |

- **Three consecutive revenue misses, avg −2.5%.**
- **The arithmetic is the problem.** FY27 guidance is "mid-single-digit" revenue growth. H1 FY27 revenue grew only **+1.1% YoY** ($2,336.5M vs $2,312.0M). Hitting mid-single digits requires **H2 FY27 growth of ~+7–8%** (~$1,235–1,255M/qtr). Consensus FQ3 of **$1,232.5M (+6.5% YoY)** sits at the very low end of that requirement.
- **The mix cannot deliver it.** DAT (+20%, $365M) contributes ~+$61M YoY; Communications Services (−3%, $815M) subtracts ~$25M. Net ≈ **+3%**. Management explicitly guided: **aviation revenue to *decline* in FY28**, continued **slight sequential declines in installed aircraft**, IFC backlog 660 commercial aircraft, business aviation flat YoY/QoQ (2,080 aircraft), FS&O service revenue **−27% YoY**, maritime and fixed satellite declining. CFO flagged that recent aviation customer announcements affect **15%+ of aviation revenue**.
- **EPS surprise history is noise, not signal:** beat rate 42.9% (3 of 7), avg surprise −132% (range −1,250% to +280%). Adjusted EBITDA guided to **~flat YoY (cut from "flat to slightly up" at FQ1)**, capex $950M–$1B, FCF ~$180M. Consensus FQ3 EPS of −$0.15 is a coin flip on interest/tax/impairment lines.
- **Verdict: Below consensus on revenue.** Alt-data (consumer search −48% YoY) and company guidance (aviation down, FS&O −27%) both point to the same conclusion: the +6.5% consensus revenue acceleration is not supported.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal — **BEARISH** (on operations), with a non-alt-data caveat

The operating alt-data is unambiguously negative and *corroborates* the last three revenue misses: consumer search demand −48% YoY at 12-month lows, competitive gap to Starlink widening 2x, aviation guided into decline, FS&O −27%, and hiring tilted to defense rather than growth segments. Consensus still models +6.5% revenue growth next quarter. **The gap is real and it is negative.**

**Critically, the recent +26% eight-session rally is not an alt-data event and should not be underwritten as one:**
- **Nov 9 (+16.3% on 4.5M shares, ~3x avg):** catalyst was a **NASA NEXUS *Phase 1* award under the NextSTEP-3 BAA** — a study-phase vehicle, materially smaller than the separate ~$4.82B NASA Near Space Network IDIQ Viasat won earlier in 2026. It coincided with a **broad risk-on tape** (SPY +2.5%, QQQ +3.5% in the session), so a large part of the move is beta + a thin headline.
- **Oct 28 (+11.3%, 4.65M shares):** tied to the Terrestar/Equatys shared direct-to-device infrastructure announcement — a spectrum/D2D narrative event.
- **Bearish tell:** SVP Craig Miller **sold 22,500 shares at $70.19 on Nov 4, one day before the print** (~$1.58M, retained 41,570 shares).

The genuine bull case is a **sum-of-the-parts / spectrum / DAT strategic-review story validated by the $150–$300 comp plan** — an M&A/asset-value thesis. That is outside alt-data's lens and I cannot confirm or deny it with these tools. What I *can* say is that **the fundamental demand data does not support the operating narrative the tape is implying.**

### 7. Confidence Level — **MEDIUM**
- **High confidence:** Google Trends "Viasat internet" decline (large magnitude, consistent across 6+ weeks, triple-corroborated by reported fixed-satellite and FS&O declines); the H1-vs-H2 revenue arithmetic gap; three straight revenue misses; guidance language on aviation.
- **Medium:** web traffic (−1.8% MoM, proxy-only); hiring read (qualitative listing count, no Thinknum time series); social sentiment (search-proxy, stale Reddit corpus, contradictory Stocktwits readings).
- **Low / missing:** **patent filings (feed failed twice — no R&D trend)**; no SimilarWeb monthly time series; no app-store, satellite-imagery, or credit-card data; no quantitative job-posting counts.

**Data limitations:** Google Trends is a relative index, not absolute volume, and is contaminated by stock-related search during event weeks (visible in the "Viasat" brand series recovering alongside the share price). Trends is weekly and lags ~2–5 days. Job postings, web traffic, and social sentiment here are **web-search proxies, not API-grade time series** — treat direction, not magnitude. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one; the strategic-review/spectrum optionality could overwhelm operating weakness in either direction.

**Bottom line for the memo:** Alt data says **fade the operating story, respect the asset story.** Expect revenue to miss again in FQ3'27 (~$1.15–1.19B vs $1.23B consensus) and expect FY27 mid-single-digit revenue guidance to be cut or quietly re-based. Do not extrapolate the Nov 9 NASA pop — it was a Phase 1 BAA award amplified by a beta rally and an 80/100 retail sentiment spike on mentions that are down 82%.
