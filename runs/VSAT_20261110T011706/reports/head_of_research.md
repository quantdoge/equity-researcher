### Investment Memo: VSAT — November 10, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (reduce; no new capital) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: **$45** (−39% from current $74.37) — probability-weighted; base-case intrinsic ~$40, bear $10–15, bull $90–110
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Viasat is a levered, sub-investment-grade GEO satellite operator whose equity re-rated roughly 10x (from ~$7–9 in late 2024 to a $93 peak) on two things: a genuine but *one-notch* operating turnaround, and spectrum-monetization optionality. The operating business — 2–3% organic growth, gross margin down ~560bp, an 83% destroyed consumer base, and ~$225M of underlying FCF against a $15.4B enterprise value — supports roughly $40/share. Everything above that is a binary call option on spectrum/DAT transactions in which $5.1B of net debt holds the senior claim. Not one of eleven specialist agents is a buyer at $74; the honest disagreement is about the spectrum's value, not the operations' weakness.

**Bull Case**
1. **Spectrum/DAT monetization**: the board's own comp plan is struck at $150–300/share milestones tied to the strategic review; post-Iridium ($8B EV) and EchoStar ($17B) transactions, an EchoStar-like outcome implies $90–110/share.
2. **DAT defense engine**: +20% growth, +57% segment EBITDA, $4.2B backlog, ~$2.0B guided FY27 awards — largely rate-insensitive and margin-accretive.
3. **Self-help financials**: capex cliff ($1.54B → ~$875M) mechanically lifts FCF; $568M Ligado receivable plus deleveraging from 3.2x toward 2.5x would re-rate the equity sharply.

**Bear Case**
1. **Core erosion**: Communication Services (69% of revenue) shrinking, aviation revenue guided *down* in FY2028 with 15–20% of aviation revenue at risk to LEO; consumer franchise already lost.
2. **Phantom inflection**: strip the Ligado one-off and working capital and FY26 FCF was ~zero; FY27 guide ~$180–225M against a $15.4B EV.
3. **Balance sheet in a tightening tape**: 0.30x interest coverage, 'B' credit, $400M 2027 and ~$1.4B 2029 maturities refinancing into HY OAS +77bp — plus $3.49B goodwill (76% of equity) exposed to impairment.

**Key Risks** (both directions)
- Equatys fails to close, or closes requiring incremental Viasat equity / restrictive Team Telecom conditions.
- Ligado damages awarded against Inmarsat or the escrowed $100M not released — undermines the deleveraging narrative.
- FY27 FCF guide cut below $180M; further goodwill impairment; aviation contract losses beyond those flagged; refinancing materially above 7.625%.
- *To the short/underweight*: a definitive spectrum sale, DAT separation, or foreign/strategic bid — violent squeeze risk (three >20% single-day gaps in the past year).
