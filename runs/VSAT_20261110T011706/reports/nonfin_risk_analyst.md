# Viasat, Inc. (VSAT) — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**As of: ~November 9, 2026** | Sector: Technology / Communication Equipment | Mkt Cap ~$10.2B | Price $74.37 | Beta 1.76 | ~7,000 FTE | CEO: Mark Dankberg (founder)

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: ADEQUATE (with meaningful flags)

**Supporting strengths:**
- **Majority-independent board** with a **Lead Independent Director (Sean Pak, since Feb 2019)** — a genuine counterweight to founder control.
- Dedicated **Audit, Compensation, Nominating/Governance, Banking & Finance, and Strategic Review** committees; the existence of a Banking & Finance committee plus a Strategic Review committee is telling — it institutionalizes oversight of the debt stack and strategic alternatives.
- Executive pay is **~91% incentive-based** in FY2026, with **clawback and anti-hedging policies** and stock ownership guidelines. Board refreshment ongoing (Shekar Ayyar appointed independent Class II director, May 2026).
- Engagement with holders of ~57% of shares outstanding; PwC fees $8.2M in FY2026.

**Flags:**
- **Combined Chairman & CEO** — Dankberg is founder, Chairman, and CEO (resumed both roles July 2022). Founder-led dual-role concentration at a company executing a complex post-Inmarsat integration.
- **Classified/staggered board** — 2026 nominees (three Class III seats) would serve until **2029**. Entrenchment feature that limits shareholder ability to force change.
- **Low insider alignment: directors and officers as a group own just 1.4%** of shares.
- **Pay-for-performance slippage:** the FY2026 corporate bonus pool funded at **121%** while **total revenue missed target ($4,640M actual vs. $4,719M objective)**. The beat came from Adjusted EBITDA ($1,550M vs. $1,525M) and contract awards — i.e., non-GAAP, management-defined metrics. A revenue miss still producing above-target bonus is a board-discipline question.
- 2026 say-on-pay outcome (Sept 3, 2026 advisory vote) **not obtained — gap**.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: MODERATE-HIGH (elevated)

- **SEC/DOJ enforcement: none found.** EDGAR full-text search for enforcement actions mentioning VSAT returned zero results. No restatement, no disclosed material weakness, and goodwill was flat as of June 30, 2026 (no impairment taken).
- **FCC — Ligado/AST SpaceMobile dispute (the key regulatory flashpoint).** Inmarsat (a Viasat subsidiary) signed a binding settlement under which Ligado would pay **$535–568M** for Inmarsat's support of the AST/Ligado L-band plan. Inmarsat then filed a **petition to deny** that application on Feb 27, 2026. In response, the Delaware bankruptcy court (Judge Horan, Mar 30, 2026) let Ligado **escrow the remaining $100M**, finding a "colorable argument" Inmarsat breached its contract and warning that **"the quantum of damages here could really be large."** $420M had already been received. Viasat/Inmarsat now sits in an **adversarial FCC posture with a litigation counterparty in Chapter 11** over spectrum the company itself uses.
- **Equatys JV regulatory approvals pending.** The binding Space42 (UAE) agreement (Sept 14, 2026) remains **subject to regulatory approvals and closing conditions**, with each side committing up to **$400M at formation**. Multijurisdictional review (incl. potential CFIUS-adjacent scrutiny of a UAE sovereign-linked partner holding spectrum infrastructure) is an unresolved gating item.
- **Export controls / ITAR:** material and growing US defense revenue (Space Force, USMC $307M-ceiling contract, NASA NEXUS/NextSTEP-3, NASA CSP) raises compliance-surface and appropriations risk.
- **Cyber/privacy regulation:** two nation-state compromises (below) invite FCC/Congressional scrutiny of critical-infrastructure security; GDPR exposure across European Inmarsat operations.
- **IP:** Viasat **lost** its Federal Circuit appeal against Western Digital (opinion Jan 7, 2026) — PTAB's finding that all challenged patent claims were unpatentable was affirmed, eroding patent leverage.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Spacecraft single points of failure and insurance dependency.** The **ViaSat-3 F1 reflector anomaly** (2023) crippled a $700M+ asset, driving a **~$421M insurance claim** and a multi-year capacity shortfall. ViaSat-3 F2 only entered service **Sept 17, 2026**. The GEO fleet is small, capital-intensive, and non-redundant — one anomaly resets the growth plan. Insurance market capacity/cost for GEO is deteriorating.
- **Technology obsolescence and customer flight to LEO.** US satellite broadband subscribers reportedly collapsed from **~603,000 to ~100,000 (>80% loss)**; **American Airlines** is installing Starlink across 500+ narrowbodies; management guided **commercial aviation revenue to *decline* in FY2028**; IFC contracts are shortening. Mitigants: Jetstar/AMARA win (Apr 2026) and stated hope to "re-engage Starlink adopters," but the franchise erosion is real and partly irreversible.
- **Leverage, refinancing, and a contested receivable.** S&P has cut VSAT to **'B+'** (from BB-); Fitch carries a Negative Outlook; the revolver carries a **max total leverage covenant starting at ~6.5x and stepping down**. The deleveraging plan leaned on the Ligado proceeds — now **partially escrowed and subject to a threatened "large" damages claim**. Meanwhile the company is committing **$400M+ into Equatys**. Cash-flow fragility and covenant headroom are the binding constraints on the whole strategy.
- **Key-person risk.** Dankberg is founder, Chair, and CEO of a company mid-integration of a transformational (and leveraged) Inmarsat acquisition. No clear publicly communicated succession architecture.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: MODERATE-HIGH

- **Repeat nation-state compromise — the standout reputational issue.** Viasat suffered the **2022 KA-SAT wiper attack** (AcidRain), which disabled Ukrainian military communications on the eve of the invasion and remains a canonical case study in cyberwarfare; more recently it was **breached by China's "Salt Typhoon"** actors via a compromised device. Two high-profile intrusions of a critical-infrastructure provider in roughly four years is a durable reputational and regulatory liability, not a one-off.
- **Consumer franchise retreat.** Losing >80% of US subscribers invites "also-ran vs. Starlink" framing and weakens the consumer brand even as the enterprise/defense narrative strengthens.
- **Deal-speculation narrative.** Barron's (Sept 2026) noted the stock more than doubled on sector takeover speculation while the company "has other ideas" (pursuing Equatys). Managing expectations around M&A optionality is a communications risk.
- **Valuation skepticism.** Third-party models publish stark gaps (e.g., GF Value $18.75 vs. ~$70 price; Weiss D- rating), signaling that a segment of the market views the equity story as unsupported — which amplifies drawdown risk on any negative disclosure.
- **ESG:** orbital-debris/space-sustainability exposure is inherent to GEO/LEO operations; spectrum-sovereignty controversies (Ligado) carry political salience.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Rationale: no fraud indicators and no SEC enforcement (which keeps this out of "Critical"), but the combination of (i) aggressive, one-sided insider selling into strength by the founder/CEO and former CEO, (ii) a **B+** credit profile with step-down covenants that depends on a **contested, escrowed receivable from a Chapter 11 counterparty**, (iii) proven single-point-of-failure spacecraft risk, (iv) structural share loss to LEO competition with a guided FY2028 aviation decline, and (v) two nation-state cyber compromises, produces a non-financial risk profile materially worse than the peer median.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Ligado contract-breach exposure.** Bankruptcy court permitted escrow of the remaining $100M after finding a "colorable argument" Inmarsat breached; judge flagged potentially very large damages. Escalate for **cash-flow timing and contingent-liability quantification** — this underwrites the deleveraging story. (Also note an extraordinary reported **$50B Court of Federal Claims takings award to Ligado** on Sept 21, 2026 — unverified in primary sources, but if sustained it changes Ligado's solvency and Viasat's recovery odds in both directions.)
2. **Cluster insider selling with zero buys.** ~$37M (CEO) + $20M (director/ex-CEO) sold near 52-week highs. Escalate to the investment committee as a **conviction signal**; request 10b5-1 plan adoption dates to test whether plans were amended toward sale.
3. **Bonus funded at 121% despite a revenue miss.** Board compensation discipline concern; pairs badly with the 1.4% aggregate insider ownership.
4. **Repeat critical-infrastructure cyber compromise** (KA-SAT 2022; Salt Typhoon). Escalate for **disclosure-control adequacy and latent liability/regulatory-action risk**.
5. **$400M+ Equatys capital commitment into a UAE joint venture while rated B+ with stepping-down covenants.** Strategic logic may be sound, but the **capital-allocation and governance sequencing** (committing equity before regulatory approval, before collecting the Ligado receivable) is a legitimate escalation item.
6. **Single-signature risk:** founder as Chair + CEO + public face during a leveraged integration, with no stated succession plan.
