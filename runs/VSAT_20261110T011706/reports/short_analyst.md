## FORENSIC SHORT-SELLER ANALYSIS — Viasat, Inc. (VSAT)

**Price $74.37 (Nov 9, 2026) | Mkt cap $10.24B | Net debt $5.19B | EV ≈ $15.4B**
**Context: +~780% since spring 2025 (~$7.50 → $89 peak intraday Jun 2026); 2026 range ~$32–$90**

---

### 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **LOW** (not fraud — decay)

This is **not** an accounting-fraud case. The manipulation models are clean: Beneish M-Score **-2.83** ("low risk"), accruals ratio **-0.107** (negative accruals = conservative), OCF/EBITDA **87%**. I want to be explicit about that up front, because a reflexive bear would overreach here.

The problem is different and, for equity value, arguably worse: **the headline cash-flow "inflection" is manufactured by one-time items and working capital, while unit economics are deteriorating fast.**

| Forensic reframe of FY2026 "FCF inflection" | $M |
|---|---|
| Reported FCF (FY26) | +597 |
| Less: one-time Ligado lump-sum receipt (per company's own Q1 FY27 letter) | (420) |
| Less: working-capital inflow | (227) |
| **Underlying FCF** | **≈ (50)** |

Management's own Q1 FY27 letter concedes TTM FCF was **$189M excluding the Ligado payment** — and guides FY27 FCF to ">$225M" on capex of $850–900M. Against a $15.4B EV, that is **~68x underlying free cash flow** for a company consensus says will not earn a positive dollar of net income through **FY2030** (EPS est. -$1.61, -$1.71, -$1.14, -$1.08).

**Cash conversion ratio is structurally fine but economically poor:** five-year cumulative FCF (FY22–FY26) is **-$1.66B**, funded by $4.16B of new debt and ~61M new shares (73.4M → 134.7M). The equity has been asked to pay $10.2B for assets built with other people's money and shareholders' dilution.

### 2. Key Red Flags Found

1. **Gross margin collapse — the single most important number.** FY26 gross margin **27.4%** vs **33.0%** FY25 (-560bp). Gross profit fell **-$217M (-14.6%)** on revenue **+2.7%**. This is the classic pricing-power-loss signature: fixed-cost capacity being filled at lower prices. Beneish GMI **1.202** flags it. (Peer reference: Starlink est. ~54% GM.)
2. **Losses re-widening after the "turn."** Q2 FY27 (Sept 2026): revenue $1,180M (+3%), **net loss $128.7M vs $61.4M a year earlier** — the loss more than doubled YoY.
3. **Free broadband franchise is gone.** US fixed-broadband subs: **603k (Q3 CY20) → ~100k (Q3 CY26), -83%**; Communication Services revenue -3% YoY. New ViaSat-3 F2 capacity (>1 Tbps, in service 2026) is landing into a market where the retail customer has already churned to Starlink (13.6M subs, +1.6M in Q3 alone).
4. **Revenue growth ~2–3%, not the 5–8% the multiple needs.** Quarterly YoY: 4.0%, 1.7%, 3.0%, 2.1%, **-1.2%**, 3.4%.
5. **The operating-income swing is an impairment artefact.** Op income went -$97M → +$108M while gross profit fell $217M. SG&A was flat ($1,012M → $999M). The residual non-COGS/SG&A charge fell from ~$576M to ~$165M — i.e. the improvement is prior-year impairments not repeating, not operations improving.
6. **Goodwill = 86% of equity.** $4.06B intangibles vs $4.73B equity; retained earnings now **-$360M** (from +$249M in FY24). Tangible book ≈ $670M ≈ **$4.9/share** vs a $74 stock. One aviation/enterprise impairment resets book value.
7. **Balance sheet is sub-investment-grade.** $6.94B debt; Fitch 'B' (secured 'BB-'/RR2, **unsecured 'CCC+'**), S&P 'B+'. Altman Z **0.79 = distress** (still only ~1.1 even using market cap for x4). Capex of $850–900M plus ~$350M cash interest consumes ~80% of EBITDA.
8. **Dilution:** F-Score fails on share count (F7=0); shares +15% in two years.
9. **FY23 data inconsistency:** cash-flow statement shows net income **+$1,090M** vs income statement **-$577M**. Unexplained in the feed — I discount that year's OCF, and flag it as a data-quality caveat rather than evidence of manipulation.

### 4. Competitive Threat Assessment: **SEVERE — the moat is being commoditised**

- **Consumer broadband: already lost.** 83% subscriber destruction is not cyclical churn; it is a technology-generation loss. Management blamed "seasonally high churn," which does not explain a six-year, five-sixths decline.
- **Aviation/maritime/government: under attack.** Aviation (+15% YoY in Q3 FY26) and DAT (+20%) are the entire bull narrative — and they are precisely where Starlink IFC and direct-to-device are winning. Viasat is defending share with price, which is exactly what the 560bp gross-margin compression tells you.
- **Capacity is not scarcity anymore.** Viasat's answer to Starlink is more GEO throughput (VS3 F2/F3). But the marginal buyer of bandwidth now has a cheaper LEO alternative; incremental GEO capacity is a depreciating asset competing against a deflationary one.
- **What is genuinely defensible:** government/defence, secure mobility, spectrum licences, Inmarsat's maritime safety (GMDSS) obligations, and the fleet itself. That is real — but it is a *strategic asset* argument, not a growth-equity argument.

### 6. Short Thesis Summary

**Credible, but valuation-driven, not fraud-driven.**

The market has re-rated VSAT on (a) a sector-wide momentum/AI-adjacent comms rally, (b) an **April 2026 +19% move on M&A speculation about a peer**, and (c) a genuinely improving capex trajectory. What it has paid for is a business that:
- grows revenue 2–3% with **gross margin down 560bp**,
- lost **83% of its US retail base** to a competitor that added 16x that base in one quarter,
- generates **~$225M of real FCF on $15.4B of EV (~68x)** after you strip a one-time $420M receipt,
- carries **$6.9B of B-rated debt** with capex still ~48% of EBITDA,
- has **negative tangible book value ex-goodwill** and a **-$360M** accumulated deficit,
- and whose losses **re-widened to $128.7M** in the latest quarter.

Shorting here is a bet on multiple normalisation, not on insolvency. Every new satellite enters service and adds D&A, so GAAP losses persist even as cash improves — the "profitless deleveraging" regime can last years while the equity is priced as if it ends tomorrow.

**Catalysts:** Q3 FY27 print (Feb 2027); ViaSat-3 F3 in-service D&A step-up; FY28 capex guide; 2028–29 refinancing at 'B' spreads; M&A premium deflating; any aviation IFC contract loss disclosure.

**What kills the short (said plainly):**
1. **Squeeze.** +780% in a year, retail/quant momentum, and takeover optionality. The April 2026 move proves strategic interest in satcom assets exists. Size accordingly.
2. **Post-capex satellite economics are genuinely explosive.** If maintenance capex settles near $700M against $1.9B EBITDA, owner earnings could reach $800M+, and a strategic buyer values the fleet and spectrum, not the FCF multiple.
3. **Real deleveraging** ($7.64B → $6.94B debt) plus $1.75B liquidity means no near-term solvency cliff.

**Honest verdict on the bull case:** the operational turn is real in *direction* (capex down, FCF positive, 3 satellites in orbit) but far too small in *magnitude* to support the price. Fundamentals improved ~1 notch; the stock re-rated ~10 notches.

### 7. Contrarian Rating: **SHORT** (moderate conviction, risk-managed)

- **Not "Strong Short":** no fraud, clean accruals, real liquidity, live M&A optionality, and extreme squeeze risk in a momentum name.
- **Not "No Edge":** at ~68x underlying FCF, ~15x tangible book, with gross margin contracting 560bp, insider distribution, and the core consumer franchise destroyed, the asymmetry favours the bear side at $74.
- **Entry discipline:** this is a *scale-in-on-strength* short, not a chase. Reduce/cover if it clears ~$95 with an actual takeout bid; the thesis is void below a ~$45–50 price (where EV/underlying FCF nears ~20x and the deleveraging case starts to carry the equity).
- **Position construction:** prefer options/defined-risk or pair against a capex-light peer — the borrow and squeeze dynamics, not the thesis, are the main risk of ruin.

**One-line summary:** *Honest accounting, deteriorating unit economics, a one-time cash receipt dressed as an inflection, insiders selling into a 10x narrative rally — and a $15.4B enterprise value resting on ~$225M of real free cash flow.*
