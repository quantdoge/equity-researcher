## 2. Consensus Assessment: **MEDIUM (unusually coherent for a contested name)**

1. **Valuation is genuinely cheap** (Value, Growth, Quant, Short, Sector): ~9x EV/EBITDA, ~10–13x FCF, ~14x FY26E EPS, 8.9% FCF yield, against a 60–65% de-rating on every multiple.

3. **The core growth engine is decaying** (Sector, Growth, Short, Quant, Nonfin): three consecutive quarters of *revenue decline*, CV growth of 2.5% carried by renewal escalation, **new business negative**, 4% exit rate conceded "unlikely."
4. **AI is an unresolved binary, not a priced-in known** (all).
5. **Governance/disclosure credibility is impaired** (Nonfin, ESG, Geo/Legal, Short): and notably, *management credibility is discounted even by the bull camp.*
6. **No near-term re-rating catalyst** — next print 2-Feb-27, and Alt Data expects a third beat-and-fade.

**Genuinely split:** *Is a 26%-ROIC franchise at 9x EBITDA a gift, or a melting ice cube at 9x on peak margins?* Value says buy (31% discount to IV); Growth says hold (Rule of 40 fails, new business shrinking); Quant says neutral (no momentum, no revision confirmation). This is a **price-versus-durability** disagreement, not a factual one — no amount of additional diligence resolves it. It resolves only on CV data over 2–4 quarters.

**Conflict resolved — fraud risk vs. value case.** The framework says fraud/forensic flags override a bullish value view. **They do not here**, and this is the single most important judgment in the memo:
- The **Short analyst went looking and found nothing** — no receivables inflation, no inventory games, no capitalisation abuse, no revenue recognised ahead of collection. Beneish −2.88.
- Nonfin Risk rates HIGH but explicitly on **governance/disclosure *credibility***, not on fraud: no restatement, no material weakness, clean certifications, no SEC enforcement.
- Correct treatment: the litigation is a **$30–100M contingent liability (0.2–0.8% of market cap) plus a discount-rate/overhang penalty** — it *reduces sizing and widens the margin of safety*. It does **not** override the thesis. **But** an adverse class-certification ruling, an SEC informal inquiry, or any restatement instantly falsifies the Short analyst's clean finding and is an **immediate exit regardless of price** — that is the one path from value-trap to permanent capital loss.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.5% initial → 2.5% target → 3.0% hard cap**

**Framework band:** Medium conviction or elevated risk = 1–3%. I land at the **low end of the band**, deliberately.

**Why not 3–5% (the high-conviction band), despite Value's BUY and a ~46% expected return?**
- **Kelly logic caps it.** Value's distribution gives EV = 0.30($149) + 0.45($307) + 0.25($478) = **$302** (+46%) against −28% to the bear case — a ~1.6:1 payoff. Raw Kelly at those odds implies a stake far above any prudent portfolio limit; standard practice is ¼-Kelly, then cap by *risk budget*. With forward vol of ~40–45% (63.6% realized is gap-inflated), a 2.5% weight contributes ~1.1% of portfolio annual volatility — the outer edge of what a single-name idiosyncratic bet should cost.
- **The bear case is not a tail — it is a 30% probability at only −28%.** Kelly sizing presumes you survive being wrong. Here the downside is *shallow but likely*, which argues for a full position built slowly, not a large one built fast.
- **Momentum is absent (−18.6% 12-1).** Value names without momentum keep getting cheaper for quarters. There is no cost to patience; there is a large cost to being early at size.
- **Governance + legal overhang** (Nonfin HIGH, Geo/Legal MEDIUM, ESG credibility deficit) is a persistent discount-rate penalty that caps multiple expansion even if fundamentals hold.

**Why not 0.5–1% (the pass band)?**
- 26% ROIC vs ~8% WACC, 98–99% wallet retention, 67.7% gross margin *unchanged for six straight years*, 3% pricing power, and 8.9% FCF yield at 9x EBITDA is a genuinely rare combination. The Short analyst — the most adversarial reader in the pack — explicitly **rejects** the short and concedes there is "no informational edge" and "$400M/quarter of buyback still bids the stock." When the bear capitulates on valuation, you do not pass.
- Expected return of ~12%/yr if the franchise merely *persists*, with 28% of downside cushion to the bear case, clears a 20–30% margin-of-safety bar at 31%.

**Formal sizing: 1.5% starter, sized up to 2.5% on evidence, hard-capped at 3.0%.**
