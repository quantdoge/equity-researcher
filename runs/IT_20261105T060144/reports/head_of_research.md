### Investment Memo: IT (Gartner, Inc.) — 5 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $290 (+40% from current $207.19); scenario range: bear $149 / base ~$300 / bull $478

**Investment Thesis**
Gartner is a 26%-ROIC, 98–99% wallet-retention subscription franchise trading at ~9x EV/EBITDA and ~9% FCF yield because the market fears generative AI renders syndicated IT research obsolete. The de-rating (−62% from the Nov-2024 peak) has already priced a severe outcome, while the operating data — three consecutive quarters of contract-value re-acceleration, expanding gross margins, an ~11% shrinking share count — describe stabilization, not collapse. This is a **show-me deep-value position on the category leader surviving AI**, sized accordingly, not a growth compounder.

**Bull Case**
- Rare quality-at-a-discount: 26% ROIC, 78% contribution margins, 98–99% wallet retention and 3% pricing power at ~9x EBITDA / ~9% FCF yield — ~12%/yr expected return if the franchise merely *persists*, with 28% downside cushion to the bear case.
- Stabilization is visible: CV growth re-accelerated three straight quarters (+1% → +2.5%), 9M-26 operating margin recovered to ~20.8%, and buybacks shrank the share count ~11–15% — per-share value compounds even at low organic growth.
- The AI threat is unproven in the data: AI is the top client-inquiry topic, enterprises burned by failed AI ROI are returning for guidance, and gross margins *expanded* in all three FY26 quarters. A disinflation-driven 10Y move back to ~4.25% re-rates the stock to $270–290 on multiple alone.

**Bear Case**
- AI disintermediation is a live binary: LLMs attack exactly the synthesis layer Gartner sells; revenue has declined three consecutive quarters and new business is negative at both segments — the installed base renews while the funnel narrows.
- Growth quality is poor: EPS growth is buyback- and cost-driven; FY27 consensus +3.7% revenue is arithmetically inconsistent with 2.5% CV growth, and management conceded a 4% CV exit rate is "unlikely."
- Credibility overhang plus hostile tape: a class action alleging five quarters of misstatement on the company's headline KPI, combined Chair/CEO, and a 5.13% 10Y mean a third consecutive post-beat selloff on Feb-2-27 is the base case.

**Key Risks**
1. **CV relapse**: growth <2% for two quarters, wallet retention <97%, or annual price increase <2% — validates the melting-ice-cube case; bear anchor $149.
2. **Legal escalation**: adverse MTD/class-certification ruling, SEC informal inquiry, or any restatement — falsifies the clean forensic finding; **immediate exit regardless of price**.
3. **Rate shock**: 10Y to 5.75–6.0% compresses the multiple to ~10x FCF (~$155–160, −25%).
4. **Key person**: unplanned departure of the 22-year Chairman-CEO with no disclosed successor.
5. **Data debts**: no Fin Risk report; net debt, 10-K legal-proceedings detail, and proxy/10b5-1 status unverified.
