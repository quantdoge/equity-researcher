## SECTOR BRIEF — Gartner, Inc. (NYSE: IT)
**Sector:** Technology / Information Technology Services — subsector: *Syndicated IT Research & Advisory / Information Services*
**Price $207.19 | Mkt cap $13.1B | Beta 0.95 | 20,244 employees | FY25 revenue $6.50B**

---

### 1. Sector Overview

The relevant sector is not "Technology" (the FMP peer list — AAPL, NVDA, MSFT — is meaningless here) but syndicated **IT research and advisory**, a subscription information business selling benchmark data, analyst access and best-practice frameworks to enterprise CIOs. It is a structurally high-margin oligopoly: Gartner is roughly **75–80% of the pure-play market by revenue** ($6.50B vs Forrester's $397M and private IDC), with top-3 concentration implying an HHI well above 2,500, gross margins of 57–76%, and low capital intensity (capex <4% of sales; content is labour, not plant). The sector is **mature and mid-to-late cycle on its own adoption curve, yet in an acute structural-disruption phase**: enterprise IT budgets are booming (Gartner's own forecast: worldwide IT spending **+14.2% to $6.37T in 2026**), but generative AI is simultaneously deflating the price of synthesised information — the exact product a subscription research seat sells. The result is a stark bifurcation: proprietary/regulatory **data** franchises (Verisk +6.6%, Morningstar +7.5%) are compounding, while pure **opinion-and-report** franchises are shrinking (Forrester –8.2%, Clarivate –4.0%).

---

### 2. Key Tailwinds

- **Record enterprise IT spending = rich subject matter.** Gartner's Feb-26 forecast of $6.15T was twice revised upward to **$6.37T (+14.2%)** by July 2026, driven by AI infrastructure. Complexity, vendor churn and AI governance anxiety are precisely what advisory subscriptions monetise — and Gartner research showing only **13% of CIOs see transformational AI value** is itself a sales asset (it argues *more* guidance is needed).
- **AI is expanding inquiry demand, not erasing it (so far).** FY26 contract value re-accelerated to **$5.3B, +2.5% YoY FX-neutral and +1.1% sequentially** (Q3-26), versus roughly +1% in Q4-25/Q1-26 — the classic signature of a stabilising, not collapsing, renewal base. Management raised FY26 Insights revenue guidance to $5.18B.
- **Self-help is visibly working into margins.** 9M-26 operating margin is **20.8%** (op. income $975M / revenue $4.69B) vs **15.8%** for FY25, and diluted shares fell from 72.1M (Q4-25) to **63.9M** (Q3-26), an **~11% shrink** — Q3-26 GAAP EPS $3.35 vs $0.47 a year earlier. Consensus-beating prints and a raised outlook have lifted the stock **~65% off the June-26 low of $125.73**.

---

### 3. Key Headwinds

- **Generative AI is a direct substitute at the seat level.** Gartner research seats run roughly $20–25k per user per year; an LLM plus public 10-Ks/benchmarks now produces a passable first draft of "what should my IT strategy be." This compresses both seat counts and renewal pricing power — the reason revenue has now declined for **three consecutive quarters** (-1.5% Q1-26, -0.6% Q2-26, **-1.3% Q3-26**) even with CV growing.
- **Buyer power is rising and budgets are being reallocated.** CIO discretionary advisory spend is being raided to fund AI infrastructure; procurement is scrutinising multi-year subscriptions, and consolidated "enterprise agreements" trade volume for price. Consulting revenue is the most cyclical piece and is exposed to deferred transformation projects.
- **Competitive flanking from free/cheap alternatives and vendors.** Hyperscalers, SaaS vendors and consultancies (Accenture, Big-4, ISG, boutiques) distribute research free as demand-gen; AI-native research startups attack at a fraction of price. Forrester's **–8.2% revenue and 0.5% operating margin** is the clearest evidence that the second-tier model is already breaking — a share-gain opportunity for Gartner, but also a live read on category pricing.

### 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **AVERAGE (strong franchise, deteriorating economics)**

*Rationale.* Gartner's **moat remains the strongest in its niche** — brand ("the Gartner Magic Quadrant" is a category institution), a proprietary benchmark dataset no AI can scrape, a 20k-person analyst/conference network, and a 2.5%-growing $5.3B contract-value book that is *re-accelerating*. Switching costs are real but seat-level, not enterprise-level, and the buyer universe is only ~ tens of thousands of CIO-adjacent executives, capping TAM. Its pricing power is being tested for the first time in two decades: three straight quarters of revenue decline alongside CV growth means **price/mix and seat count are leaking even as the customer base holds** — a classic pre-disruption pattern. Financially the company is compounding per-share value through buybacks (share count –11%) and cost discipline (9M-26 op margin 20.8%), and the **balance sheet is conservative** (est. net debt ~$0.5B, ~0.3x EBITDA). On valuation, at ~$207 the shares trade near **~15x FY26E EPS (~$13.4) and ~9x FY26E EBITDA (~$1.5B)** — versus Verisk/Morningstar at roughly 20–30x — i.e., the market has already priced substantial disruption. That is a *valuation* cushion, not an *operating* one. **Verdict: average — a wide-moat incumbent with eroding incremental returns, and the single largest open question in the sector.**

---

### 6. Sector Rating: **UNATTRACTIVE** *(for pure-play syndicated IT research)*

The sector is a mature, highly concentrated, high-gross-margin oligopoly **whose core product is directly exposed to generative-AI substitution** — the first genuine threat to the subscription-research model since its invention. Aggregate profit pools are being redistributed *within* information services from "opinion and report" (Forrester, Clarivate — both shrinking) toward "proprietary and regulatory data" (Verisk, Morningstar — both compounding 6–8% at 40%/21% margins), and syndicated research is on the wrong side of that line. The offsetting fundamentals — 14.2% IT spending growth, re-accelerating contract value at Gartner, and a ~11% shrinking share count — make this an **unattractive *industry* containing one of the more interesting *contrarian* equities**. For the memo: treat IT as a **show-me turnaround / deep-value option on the category leader surviving AI**, not as a growth compounder; the proof point to monitor is FX-neutral contract value sustaining >2.5–3% with revenue inflecting positive in FY27, against the risk case of a Forrester-style secular reset in seat pricing.
