**Investment Thesis**

AppLovin is a genuinely exceptional economic machine — 88% gross margins, ~77% operating margins, 73% ROIC, 102% FCF conversion, effectively zero net leverage — trading at ~17x FY26E earnings and a 5% FCF yield after a 64% drawdown. Forensic review **refutes the fraud thesis** (Beneish −2.52, negative accruals three years running, OCF/NI 1.19x, receivables growing slower than revenue), which removes the fat left tail. What remains is a legitimate but already largely priced debate about margin *duration*, sitting alongside a correlated legal overhang (SEC probe, securities class action, Dutch GDPR action — all one fact pattern: how AXON sources data) and the most hostile macro regime possible for a 2.2-beta, 76%-vol equity. The discount is real but not yet wide enough for full size.

**Bull Case**
- 73% ROIC, 84% EBITDA margins and ~$4.5B TTM FCF at ~17x forward earnings / 5% FCF yield — a growth multiple that has round-tripped to a value multiple while the business still compounds ~30%+.
- Self-serve (opened June 2026) + e-commerce expand TAM from ~$10B mobile gaming to a $100B+ performance-ad pool; alt data confirms record advertiser demand and rising wallet share into Q4.
- Forensically clean accounting + fortress balance sheet (0.08x net leverage) + Q3 guide implying sequential *re*acceleration (+7.8%) — a clean 4-Nov print re-rates the stock violently given beta and positioning capitulation.

**Bear Case**
- Growth is hostage to AXON release cadence: one "lighter than normal" quarter already cost ~20% of market cap; R&D +127% and the first margin guide-down suggest the arbitrage is getting more expensive to defend against Meta/Google's superior first-party data.
- Correlated legal risk with dated catalysts: SEC enforcement (40–50% probability within 24 months per Geo-Legal) or platform-partner ToS enforcement would impair the core data flywheel — binary and undiversifiable.
- Macro duration trap: Fed hiking into 4.5–6% annualised CPI momentum with the 10Y at 5.10%; a 5.50% 10Y implies a further ~10–15% de-rating regardless of fundamentals.

**Key Risks**
1. SEC Wells notice / platform-partner (Apple, Meta, Google) enforcement of attribution terms — *thesis-level; exit trigger*.
2. Margin-duration break: two consecutive quarters of EBITDA-margin guide-downs with sequential growth <5%.
3. Earnings-quality drift: cash conversion <0.80x or DSO >110 days for two quarters (Q2 already printed 0.69x / 103d).
4. 4-Nov Q3 print: consensus sits at the *top* of guidance; Q2 precedent was −19.7% on a 1% miss.
5. Further C-suite turnover (CTO and CLO already gone mid-probe); EU injunction restricting profiling (~20%+ of revenue).
