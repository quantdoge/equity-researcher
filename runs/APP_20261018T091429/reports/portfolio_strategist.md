## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** — but a *resolvable* split

This is not the usual noise-around-a-mean dispersion. The agents cluster into three coherent and **mutually exclusive at this price** camps:

**Camp A — Own it for the machine (Growth, Value-to-a-point, Sector, Alt Data demand leg):** ~$4.5B TTM FCF, 73% ROIC, 84% EBITDA margins, 17x forward. The sell side is modelling a plateau (2029E EPS +2.9%) and the price has already discounted one.

**Camp B — Don't own it into a binary legal event at a hostile macro point (Geo Legal, Non-Fin, ESG, Macro, Short):** an active federal probe into the *data provenance* underpinning the 88% gross margin, a class action with a 16-Nov consolidation deadline, insider distribution with no bid, and a Fed that restarted hiking into a 76%-vol, β-2.16 equity.

**Camp C — Neutral on price action (Quant):** refuses both directions because momentum is worst-in-class and alpha is statistically insignificant (t ≈ −1.6, R² 0.138 — 86% idiosyncratic).

**How I resolve it:**

1. **The Short's fraud thesis is REFUTED — and this matters enormously.** Beneish −2.52, negative accruals three years running, OCF/NI 1.19x, AR growth (28.7%) far *below* revenue growth (70.0%), capex 0.5% of revenue. Four subsequent short reports have been followed by beats. **I remove the "accounting fraud" leg from the risk stack entirely.** This is the single largest piece of information in the entire packet, because it removes the fat left tail that would justify a pass.
2. **But the Short's *residual* thesis — margin duration — is validated by the bull camp's own data.** R&D +127% YoY, first-ever guided EBITDA margin *decline* (84% → 83%), sequentials 18.0 → 11.1 → 4.4. The growth camp reads these as "investment"; the bear reads them as "the margin line breaking last." Both are describing the same fact. I treat this as an **elevated but not thesis-breaking risk**, and size accordingly.
3. **Value's discipline outranks Growth's enthusiasm on price.** Growth is right about the three-year math; Value is right that a business whose revenue is hostage to a model-training cadence does not earn a 20% margin of safety. **Buy threshold $242, not $266.**
4. **Macro's duration math governs the timing, not the direction.** Every 25bp on the long end ≈ 3–5% on this name. A 10Y at 5.50% implies another 10–15% de-rating *all else equal*. This is the reason not to take a full position today.
5. **Legal's "correlated risks" finding is the key structural insight:** the SEC probe, the securities suit and the Dutch action are **one fact pattern** (how AXON sources data), not three diversifiable exposures. That is a genuine single point of failure and caps size regardless of how good the business is.

**Net conviction: MEDIUM-HIGH on the business, MEDIUM on the price, LOW on the next six weeks.** That is a starter position, not a target position.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

**Kelly logic.** Edge: FV $345 vs $266 = ~30% expected return on a ~23% MoS, but the distribution is wide ($175 bear → $470 bull). With win probability ~60% and a bear leg of −35%, full Kelly would imply a large allocation — **but full Kelly is inappropriate for a β-2.16, 76%-vol, single-point-of-failure name.** I apply a **one-third Kelly fractional** and then a hard risk-contribution cap.

**Risk-contribution math (why 3% and not 5%):**
- 2.5% × β 2.16 = **5.4% beta-equivalent** — already equivalent to a 5.4% position in a market-beta name.
- Position contribution to portfolio vol at 2.5%: 2.5% × 76.5% = **1.91% standalone**, ~0.92% portfolio-level given the 0.37 SPY correlation — acceptable, but only at starter size.
- At 5% (the "high-conviction" band), standalone vol contribution is 3.8% and a repeat of the realized −64% drawdown costs **320bp of portfolio** — an unacceptable single-name loss for a name carrying an unresolved federal probe. **That is the binding constraint, not valuation.**

**Why not zero / why not wait for $242:** the Value camp's threshold sits only 9% below spot on a stock that moves ±4.8% daily. Waiting has a real cost: the Alt Data demand leg (record advertiser spend, +140bp e-comm wallet share) and the 7/7→Q2 break pattern suggest the Q3 bar is achievable, and a clean print at 17x forward gaps this away. A 1% starter buys information and thesis-tracking at a trivially small portfolio risk.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss: $195 hard (−27%).**
Not tighter. At 76.5% annualised vol, daily σ ≈ 4.8%; a 15–20% stop would be triggered by ordinary noise (the stock has had five >12% single-day moves in nine months, two of them **up**). $195 also sits below the 52-week low ($260.59) and below the −40% revenue-shock valuation implied by Fin Risk's stress case. A close below $195 on volume invalidates the "23% discount to FV" premise and the position should be exited, not averaged.

**Fundamental thesis-invalidation triggers — exit regardless of price:**
1. **SEC issues a Wells notice or formal enforcement action** on data-collection / platform-partner agreements. This is the binary tail. *Exit fully.*
2. **Any platform partner (Apple, Meta, Google) publicly enforces attribution or SDK terms against APP.** This converts a regulatory question into immediate product impairment — the single most damaging structural outcome.
3. **Two further consecutive quarters of EBITDA margin guide-downs** *with* sequential growth below +5%. That confirms the margin-duration short thesis (84% is rent on an arbitrage, not a software margin) and breaks the FV math.
4. **Cash conversion below 0.80x for two consecutive quarters, or DSO above 110 days.** Q2 already printed 0.69x and 103 days. One quarter is timing; two is a trend.
5. **Any further CTO/CFO/CLO departure.** Two of the three executives needed for the defense have already turned over mid-probe.
6. **Dutch GDPR action produces an injunction restricting profiling in the EU** (~20%+ of revenue).

**Trim triggers:**
- **$380–400** (Quant's 200-day reclaim area): trim one-third. Thesis is now consensus.
- **$440+** (top of Value's range, ~28x FY26E): trim to a core 1.0% residual. Above this you are paying for the optimistic DCF case, which requires 30% growth for five years *and* a legal all-clear.
- **Position drift >4.0% of portfolio** from appreciation: mechanical trim to 3.0%.

**Portfolio-level monitors:** total portfolio beta; aggregate AI/duration sleeve weight; monthly check that the 4-Nov print did not produce a fundamental (vs price) deterioration.
