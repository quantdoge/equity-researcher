Google Trends therefore measures *retail investor attention*, not product demand, and that attention has gone into full capitulation. Treat the Trends decline as a **flow/crowding signal (contrarian), not a revenue signal.** The genuine demand proxy is advertiser wallet share, which is still inflecting upward.

### 2. Business Activity Signals

Weak signal, small base. *Direction: neutral-to-slightly-positive.*

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **In-Line to Slightly Above (revenue), with fat-tailed model risk**
- Q3 guide: **$2.055–2.085B**, adj. EBITDA $1.71–1.74B (~83%). Consensus **$2.0756B / $4.04 EPS** — sitting at the **upper end** of the guide, so the bar is not sandbagged this quarter.
- Alt data supports the top half: advertiser spend at records into Q3, post-quarter AXON upgrades live for a full quarter, e-comm share of wallet +140bp YoY, 8% sequential growth required vs. 7.9% implied.
- **The catch:** the Q2 miss was explicitly *not* a demand problem — it was AXON model-improvement pace ("lighter than normal"). **No alternative data source can validate model-efficacy pace.** Q2 is the cautionary precedent: revenue $1.924B vs $1.942B est (a 1% miss) produced a **-19.7% single-day gap** (5 Aug close $417.80 → 6 Aug $335.67).
- Surprise history: **7 consecutive beats through Q1'26 (100% beat rate, +14.75% avg)**, but magnitude decayed monotonically — +54% → +16% → +15% → +3% → +10% → +4.7% → **0.0%**. The beat engine has been normalising for four quarters.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **Neutral, with a modest contrarian-positive demand tilt**
The two signal families point opposite ways and should be weighted differently:
- **Demand-side (leading, hard):** advertiser spend at record, share-of-wallet gaining, checks steady → **supports the quarter.**
- **Sentiment/attention (coincident, contaminated):** deeply negative but dominated by legal spam and already fully reflected in a **-57% drawdown** from the $613 May peak → **poor forward predictor; and capitulation is a contrarian tell.**

Net: fundamentals-facing alt data is **in-line-to-above**, but the one variable that actually broke the stock (AXON improvement cadence) is **not observable in alt data**. I would not call this Bullish on alt data alone.

### 7. Confidence Level: **Medium**
**Supporting:** multiple independent demand proxies agree (channel checks, share-of-wallet, spend records); clean earnings-date anchor; price/estimate data high quality.
**Limiting:** (a) **patent data unavailable** (two failed pulls); (b) **social sentiment unavailable** — no quantitative cashtag feed; (c) SimilarWeb access blocked (403), leaving only a scraped MoM snippet with no visit history; (d) job-posting proxy is a search-engine artifact with an unreliable count base; (e) news sentiment is **materially contaminated by litigation notice spam**; (f) Google Trends for a B2B ad platform is an attention—not demand—gauge.

**Data lag note:** Trends/SimilarWeb/headlines run 1–4 weeks stale; Q3 advertiser-spend reads are Sep-quarter snapshots. Alt data is a *leading* indicator here only for revenue — **it is blind to the model-efficacy and litigation-overhang variables that will dominate the 4 Nov reaction.** Position sizing should reflect that asymmetry, not the alt-data read.

**Watch into the print:** (1) whether Q3 revenue lands above $2.075B consensus (validates the demand proxies); (2) any Q4 e-commerce share commentary vs. Jefferies' 8.1%; (3) language on AXON cadence — the only thing that matters and the one thing not in this dataset.
