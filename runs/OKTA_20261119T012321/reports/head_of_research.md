**Investment Thesis**
Okta is a genuinely good, fortress-balance-sheet business (net cash ~$2.1B, 77% gross margin, ~30% FCF margin, clean accounting — Beneish −2.65, negative accruals) that has become a priced business. Growth has decelerated monotonically for five years (55.7% → 10.6% in Q2 FY27), SBC at ~18% of revenue means true owner earnings are ~$315M (a ~1% economic FCF yield on EV), and the stock trades above the most generous intrinsic value any of the eleven reporting agents could construct ($188). The April→October +269% re-rating was narrative, S&P 500 inclusion and flow — not estimate revision (FY27 guide moved just $3.19B → $3.22B all year). The one genuine bull pillar is backlog: RPO +17% / cRPO +14% vs revenue +11%. That claim resolves at the **1 December print, nine days away** — optionality is worth more than the position right now.

**Bull Case**
- **Backlog acceleration is real**: RPO +17% / cRPO +14% against +11% revenue — if it holds, revenue re-accelerates and the FY28 guide bends to ≥13%, justifying a re-rate toward $235–260.
- **AI-agent identity is a greenfield TAM** with no consensus revenue in it; Okta's Cross App Access standard and Permiso acquisition are a credible land-grab, and margin structure (79.6% GM, ~30% FCF) means any growth surprise drops straight to cash flow.
- **Fortress balance sheet + clean accounting** (net cash $2.1B, Beneish −2.65, buybacks offsetting dilution) removes downside solvency risk entirely — the bear case is multiple, not impairment.

**Bear Case**
- **Valuation**: $195 sits above every DCF constructed (max $188; blended ~$160); a 10–11% grower at ~10x EV/S and 74x owner earnings has no valuation floor nearby — a routine de-rating to 6–7x sales is $115–135.
- **Structural growth decay**: five consecutive years of deceleration against Microsoft Entra's bundling economics and a $25B Palo Alto/CyberArk platform; the honest Rule of 40 ex-SBC is ~23, not 41.
- **Macro regime**: rising real yields (TIPS 2.89%), hawkish Fed with room to hike, and a software de-rating already in motion — every channel except growth works against the multiple.

**Key Risks**
1. **cRPO <12% on 1 Dec** — kills the only genuine bull pillar; Auth0 search collapse becomes the leading indicator. (High impact, binary in 9 days)
2. **Margin-quality reversal** — cost of revenue resuming growth faster than revenue, or D&A snapping back to $24M/qtr, unwinds the Q2 margin inflection that justified the re-rating.
3. **Microsoft Entra price compression** on the core workforce identity franchise; seat-based pricing also exposes revenue to AI-driven headcount reduction.
4. **Repeat breach event** — fourth iteration of the support/helpdesk failure mode, or worse, a core-platform breach (uninsurable-by-analysis tail; exit on disclosure).
5. **Goodwill impairment** — $5.58B = 80% of book equity (Auth0); any write-down admits the acquisition price was wrong.
6. **Governance/dilution** — SBC ~18% of revenue, ~29% equity-plan opposition; watch for dilution >3%/yr.
