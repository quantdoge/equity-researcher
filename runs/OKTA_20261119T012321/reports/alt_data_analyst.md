## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **TRACKING ABOVE consensus**

Consensus for Dec 1: **EPS ~$0.93–0.95, revenue ~$816–834M** (vs. $742M in FQ3 FY26 = **+10% y/y**).

Supporting evidence:
- **Perfect 7/7 beat rate, avg surprise +7.9%** — and unusually *stable*, not decaying: 5.98%, 11.69%, 7.44%, 8.18%, 6.38%, 6.68%, 8.81%. Q2 FY27 beat by 8.8% on EPS and $12M on revenue.
- **Backlog accelerating into the print:** FQ2 FY27 RPO +17% to $4.858B (vs +16% in FQ1), cRPO +14%. **cRPO growing 14% against revenue guided at +10%** is the single most important alt-anchored datapoint — it structurally supports revenue upside over the next 1–3 quarters and argues the guide is conservative.
- Search interest +9% vs. the equivalent pre-Q2 window.
- AI-agent identity narrative is broadening (TrueFoundry joining Okta's Cross App Access ecosystem, Nov 4) — an emergent TAM expansion story with no consensus revenue in it yet.

Tempering the call: consensus already embeds only +10% vs. Q2's +11% delivered, so a beat is *expected*, not differentiated. Given the Auth0 decay, I'd size it at **modest**: revenue beat of ~1–2% ($820–830M plausibly toward the top of consensus), EPS beat ~5–8% (~$1.00–1.02). The bigger risk is **FY28 guidance** framed around Auth0/CIC softness rather than the Q3 print itself.

The stock is ~16% below its Oct 9 high of $232 and the recent drawdown was macro/software-de-rating plus AI-trade unwinding — *not* company-specific.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

Why not High: web traffic is ~2 months stale; patent data missing entirely; job-posting data is non-quantitative and measures the ecosystem, not Okta's own hiring; social sentiment has no numeric feed; News sentiment score is saturated and structurally unreliable.

**Data limitations & lag:** Google Trends is a 0–100 *relative* index with strong day-of-week artifacts (weekend troughs of ~38 vs. weekday ~78) — I averaged over weeks to control for this. All these are coincident-to-leading proxies on a 1–2 quarter horizon, not definitive. Google Trends also cannot separate investor/analyst attention from genuine buyer intent, and Aug 23's reading of 100 was earnings-driven, not demand-driven.

**What would flip me to Bearish:** FQ3 cRPO growth decelerating below +12%, or FY28 guidance implying Auth0/CIC growth <10% — that would retrospectively validate the Auth0 search collapse as a leading indicator rather than a rebranding artifact.
