# Okta, Inc. (NASDAQ: OKTA) — Value Investment Analysis
*Price $195.61 (Nov 18, 2026) · Market cap ≈$35.0B (178.8M diluted sh.) · EV ≈$34.4B*

---

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **Narrow**

**What Okta does:** cloud identity — single sign-on, MFA, lifecycle management, Universal Directory — plus the Auth0 developer platform. It is the "front door" that authenticates every employee, customer and, increasingly, every AI agent into enterprise systems.

**Moat sources (real but narrower than the price implies):**
- **Switching costs (strong):** identity is mission-critical infrastructure. Ripping out an IdP means re-federating hundreds/thousands of applications and re-certifying access controls — typically a 12–24 month, career-risking project. Renewal/retention is correspondingly high.
- **Network effects (moderate):** the integration catalog (thousands of pre-built app connectors) improves with scale and creates a two-sided flywheel with ISVs.
- **Cost advantage: none.** This is the problem. Identity is a feature Microsoft bundles into M365/Entra ID at near-zero incremental price. Okta's 77% gross margin is respectable but it must spend ~50% of revenue on S&M to keep pace.

**Moat direction over 5 years: slightly narrowed.** FY2022→FY2026 revenue growth decelerated from 56% → 43% → 22% → 15% → **11.8%**, and FY2027 guidance is **$3.216–3.226B (+10.4%)**. A widening moat does not decelerate to low-double-digits at $3B of revenue while a bundled competitor takes share. The bull case now rests on a *new* TAM — securing non-human/AI-agent identities (Okta for AI Agents) — which is promising but unproven and, notably, is not yet visible in guidance.

**ROIC = 2.0% (FY26), 3.4% ROE** — far below any reasonable WACC. Operationally the business is capital-light and cash-generative, but ~$5.58B of goodwill and intangibles (largely the $6.5B Auth0 deal struck at the 2021 peak) sits on the balance sheet, and returns on *total* capital deployed are dismal. This is the single clearest violation of my "ROIC > WACC consistently" test.

## 3. Valuation Analysis — **Intrinsic value ≈ $90/share base case; price is ~2x that**

At $195.61 the multiples are not merely full, they are narrative-driven:

| Multiple | OKTA today | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| P/E (GAAP TTM) | **118x** | vs. peer-median ~31x per tool |
| P/E (non-GAAP fwd, ~$3.9 EPS) | ~50x | for a 10% grower |
| EV/EBITDA (TTM) | **82x** | |
| P/FCF (TTM) | 36x | 2.8% FCF yield |
| **P/owner earnings (FCF–SBC)** | **~74x** | **1.3% yield** |

**My DCF (owner earnings = FCF less SBC, WACC 9%, terminal 3%):** $470M base growing 9–10% fading to 6%, margins expanding to ~22% of revenue by FY32, gives EV ≈$14B → **~$80–90/share**.

**Generous case** (ignore SBC entirely — bullish), $972M FCF compounding 12% for ten years, 3% terminal: **~$188/share** — still *below* today's price.

**Reverse DCF — what the market is paying for:** to justify $195.61 at a 9% hurdle, Okta must compound per-share owner earnings at roughly **20–22% annually for a decade** (with SBC expensed), or ~12%+ for a decade if you pretend SBC is free. That is an acceleration from 11% revenue growth to sustained 20%+ earnings growth, i.e., the AI-agent identity story must land almost perfectly *and* Microsoft must not compress pricing.

**Verdict on price:** the stock tripled from ~$63 (April 2026) to $233 (October 2026) — a pure re-rating, since guidance was only nudged. Analyst targets of $215–245 posted in Sept/Oct 2026 sit at or below the current price, meaning even the sell side sees no upside from here.

## 4. Management Quality — **Good, with one large blemish**

- **Founder-led:** Todd McKinnon (co-founder, CEO) and Frederic Kerrest (co-founder) — owner-operator DNA, long tenure, clear technical vision.
- **Capital allocation:** mixed-to-poor historically. Auth0 at $6.5B in 2021 was struck at peak SaaS multiples and is the direct cause of today's 2% ROIC. Mitigating: they have since repaid $1.95B of debt, built net cash, and initiated buybacks ($248M in Q1 FY27, $124M in Q2). Honest accounting — no aggressive capitalization games.
- **The tell to watch:** issuing ~$500M/yr of stock while buying back ~$445M/yr at 36x FCF is not obviously value-accretive; it is dilution management dressed as shareholder returns. No dividend, and none appropriate.
- **Guidance credibility: high** — beat-and-raise in every one of the last eight quarters (Q2 FY27 EPS $1.05 vs. $0.965 est.).

## 5. Investment Verdict: **SELL** (avoid; watchlist at $90–110)

This is a decent-to-good business at an indefensible price. Okta has genuine switching costs and excellent gross margins, but it fails the two tests I care about most: **ROIC (2%) is far below WACC**, and the moat is narrowing against a bundled competitor. Meanwhile the market has assigned it a multiple that requires a decade of 20%+ owner-earnings compounding to justify.

> A business that trades at a fair price is not a buy. OKTA trades at roughly **double** my base-case intrinsic value and above even my no-SBC, 12%-growth bull case. There is no margin of safety here — there is a margin of *optimism*.

- **Action:** Sell / do not initiate. Existing holders with large gains should trim aggressively into the AI-agent enthusiasm.
- **Where I would get interested:** ~$90–110 (≈20–25x owner earnings ex-SBC, ~3.5–4.5% true FCF yield), and only if revenue growth stabilizes at ≥10% with non-GAAP operating margin breaking above 25%. That is a ~50% drawdown — entirely plausible given this stock has already traded at $63 this year.
- **What would change my mind (upgrade to Buy at a higher price):** two consecutive quarters of *accelerating* revenue growth driven by demonstrable AI-agent identity revenue (not pilot announcements), plus SBC falling below 10% of revenue.

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

**Risks to owning it (i.e., to the bear case being wrong):**
1. **AI-agent identity becomes a genuine second act** — a large, high-priced, low-competition TAM that re-accelerates growth to 20%+. This is the entire bull case, and it is not in guidance.
2. **Operating leverage surprise** — non-GAAP margins are ~22%; runway to 30% would lift owner earnings faster than revenue.
3. **Takeout optionality** — a $35B net-cash-rich, founder-led strategic asset is a perennial M&A candidate for a large platform player.

**Risks to the business (the more probable set):**
1. **Microsoft Entra ID bundling** — structural price compression on Okta's core workforce identity franchise.
2. **Growth deceleration to high-single digits** — at 10% growth, a 36x FCF multiple reverts toward 20x; that arithmetic alone implies a 40–50% downside.
3. **SBC-driven dilution** persisting near 2%/yr, silently eroding per-share value while buybacks at 36x FCF transfer wealth from buyers to sellers.
4. **Sentiment reversal risk** — a stock that tripled in five months on a theme can halve on a single guidance miss; the $63 April 2026 print is the recent proof.
5. **Goodwill impairment risk** — $5.58B of goodwill/intangibles against $7.0B of equity; an impairment would be non-cash but would confirm the Auth0 price was a mistake.
