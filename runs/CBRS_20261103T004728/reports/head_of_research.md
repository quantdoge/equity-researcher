### Investment Memo: CBRS (Cerebras Systems) — 3 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL / DO NOT INITIATE | Conviction: Medium-High
**Price Target**: $55 (−43% from current $96.36); fair-value range $45–65; bear floor ~$26–31
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Cerebras owns genuinely differentiated wafer-scale inference technology and a $25.4B RPO backlog (1.17x market cap), but the financial evidence contradicts the franchise claim: GAAP gross margin collapsed to 14.2% in Q2'26 (hardware gross profit just 1.8%), revenue declined sequentially for the first time, ~86% of FY25 revenue came from two Abu Dhabi-linked entities, and the backlog is concentrated in a single counterparty (OpenAI) that is simultaneously customer, lender, and warrant holder. Probability-weighted intrinsic value is ~$55–63 — the bull case is required merely to break even at $96. With a hawkish real-rate shock compressing long-duration multiples and two binary events in nine days (lockup expiry ~Nov 9–10, Q3 print Nov 12), the risk/reward is decisively negative.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector attractive (~$800B 2026 hyperscaler capex; inference shifting toward CBRS's strength) but decelerating into 2027–28. Macro is the worst possible regime for this asset: 10Y at 5.13% driven entirely by *real yields* (breakevens −20bp), Fed hiking again, beta 1.85–3.0. Macro sensitivity currently exceeds fundamental sensitivity.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. Majority of revenue UAE-headquartered (G42 historically 83–87%; exact FY25 figure contested at 24%/62%/86% — unresolved); revenue rests on a revocable, entity-whitelisted BIS export privilege; active securities class action with Dec 8, 2026 lead-plaintiff deadline; Culper short report alleges 1.5–2.5yr chip field life vs 3–5yr disclosed.
- **Value**: SELL. No demonstrated moat — 14.2% GM is dispositive evidence of absent pricing power; FY25 "+$238M net income" is a fair-value artifact (operating income −$145M); IV ~$63 vs $96 price. Financial quality 2/10, propped up only by the $8.6B cash pile.
- **Growth**: BUY (staged) — the lone dissent. 0.81x market-cap/RPO and 6.8x EV/FY27E sales look cheap *if* the backlog converts at margin; even this camp refuses a full position before the Nov 12 print.
- **Quantitative Signals**: STRONG SELL. Bottom quintile on value, momentum, quality, and low-vol; negative FCF, ROE −41%, high accruals; R² vs SPY 0.11 (89% idiosyncratic — unhedgeable with sector instruments).
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH, but not solvency-driven: ~$5.9B net cash, no maturity wall, 3–4yr runway even under stress. The risk is earnings quality (Q2 opex $503M incl. $377M SBC; FCF −$477M) and concentration, not default.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. 99.2% insider voting control; no DEF 14A yet; circular OpenAI/G42 related-party web; alleged depreciation-life extension (1.5–3yr → 3–5yr) between draft and final S-1 is the highest-severity accounting flag; zero insider buying at −75%.

- **Alternative Data**: BEARISH (low confidence). Search interest at 7% of IPO peak, X mentions −47%, and the last two earnings *beats* produced average −16% one-day reactions — the reaction function is hostile heading into Nov 12.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (downgraded from Strong at $300+). Gross profit *dollars* fell 20% while revenue rose 74%; Beneish M-Score 5.37 is largely artifact but margin-deterioration and accrual signals survive independently; fair value $31–47 at 8–12x forward sales. Not calling fraud — calling poor economics at a rich price.

**Bull Case**
- $25.4B contracted RPO at 0.81x market cap; 600MW capacity under contract supports the FY27 triple and FY28 consensus of $7.5B — arithmetically possible on execution alone.
- $8.6B cash (~40% of market cap) funds the buildout with no near-term dilution; three hyperscaler channels (OpenAI, Oracle, AWS) won in six months.
- If Q3 proves 14.2% GM was transitional pass-through mix and core GM holds ≥40%, the de-rating reverses violently — beta 3 cuts both ways (Growth's staged-buy scenario).

**Bear Case**
- 14.2% GAAP GM with 1.8% hardware GM, sequential revenue decline, and gross-profit dollars shrinking — the challenger trades at a *higher* P/S than NVIDIA at one-fifth the gross margin.
- Binary customer concentration: ~86% of FY25 revenue from two UAE entities; the $25.4B RPO is essentially one counterparty, with ~$1.1B of customer warrants set to *reduce* reported revenue.
- Live fraud litigation + an unrefuted product-durability allegation that attacks warranty economics and the depreciation schedule simultaneously.

**Key Risks** (to the sell call, and to any holder)
1. **Nov 12 Q3 print** — the single swing variable: core GM vs the 38–40% guide and whether GAAP GM recovers. Either outcome is a 20–40% move.
2. RPO quality: any disclosure that backlog is contingent on Cerebras-provided financing is immediately disqualifying.
3. BIS export-policy rollback or G42 delisting — existential, not tail, risk.
4. Lockup supply through Nov 9 colliding with 8–15% short interest.
5. *Upside risk to a sell:* takeout/strategic premium for the IP, or a Fed pivot rallying the stock 50–70% on flat fundamentals.

**Data integrity flag**: Reports conflict on Q2 revenue ($180.1M GAAP vs $209.9M core), G42 share of FY25 revenue (24/62/86%), beta (1.85/3.04), and net cash ($5.9B vs $7.1B). Contested inputs are themselves a reason to own nothing.
