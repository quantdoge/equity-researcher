Supporting evidence is unusually strong for a leading indicator:

**Why the stock still fell 18%:** the market fixated on that QoQ deceleration versus prior quarters' +40% and +36.7% sequential data-center growth (retail commentary on the call thread: "isn't a guide of 15% a bit low?"). The bar was set on *rate of change*, not level. Alt data says the level and backlog support the annual claim.
