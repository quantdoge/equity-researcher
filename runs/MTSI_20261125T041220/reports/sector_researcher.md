# SECTOR BRIEF — MACOM Technology Solutions (MTSI)
**Sector:** Technology / Semiconductors — Analog & RF/Microwave, III-V photonics
**Price:** $200.20 | **Market cap:** $15.37B | **Beta:** 1.83 | **FY end:** late Sept/early Oct

---

## 1. Sector Overview

MACOM competes in the specialised analog/RF-and-photonics slice of the ~$750B global semiconductor market (forecast ~9% CAGR to $2.56T by 2040), rather than in logic or memory. Its addressable niches are three: **RF/microwave power** (RF power semiconductor market ~$29.7B in 2026, with GaN RF devices at ~$1.75B and growing mid-teens), **datacom optics** (800G shipments more than doubling in 2026 as the industry transitions to 1.6T), and **defence/industrial radar, SATCOM and EW**. The sector is extraordinarily concentrated at the top (NVDA $3.74T, TSM $1.81T, AVGO $1.44T) but fragmented in MACOM's own niches, where ADI, Qorvo, Skyworks, NXP, Lumentum and Coherent compete on design wins rather than price. Critically, the sector is now **bifurcated**: AI-networking and defence are in a capacity-constrained upcycle with book-to-bill above 1 and lead times extending, while handset RF and carrier telecom are structurally shrinking. Management pegs MACOM's combined serviceable addressable market at **~$15B in 2027**.

---

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **AI data-centre optical interconnect — the dominant driver.** FY2026 data-centre revenue rose **+74.0% to $509.7M**, and Q4 alone was **$188.1M, +37% sequentially**. The 800G→1.6T transition plus InP laser/transimpedance amplifier content gains puts MACOM on the AI-capex treadmill; the CW laser entry targeted for 2028 opens a multi-billion-dollar incremental market.
- **Defence and industrial rearmament.** Industrial & Defense reached **$536.4M, +27.8% YoY** and a record in Q4 ($164.6M, +23% QoQ). GaN-on-SiC mmWave process upgrades for SATCOM/defence and Western budget expansion give a multi-year, less cyclical runway than consumer RF.
- **Violent operating leverage.** Adjusted gross margin expanded to **61.3% in Q4 from 57.1%** a year ago; adjusted operating margin to **38.2% from 25.6%**. On an annualising ~$1.9B FY2027 revenue base, each 100bp of gross margin is worth ~$19M of gross profit — the margin story is now as powerful as the revenue story.

---

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **Multiple compression and AI-capex scepticism, not demand.** The stock has **halved from its ~$400 May 2026 peak** — down 19.2% on an analyst downgrade on 5 Nov despite a Q4 beat and raise, and another 13.5% on 24 Nov as the semiconductor group fell 8.3%. Fundamentals are accelerating while the multiple de-rates; beta of 1.83 amplifies both directions.
- **Architectural substitution risk in optics.** Silicon photonics and co-packaged optics (CPO) threaten the pluggable InP-laser model; LPO/CPO ports are forecast at >30% of 800G/1.6T ports deployed 2026–28. A shift in this mix would compress MACOM's optical content per port.
- **Telecom and handset RF are structurally soft.** Telecom grew only **+1% QoQ** in Q4 (~$281M for FY2026, ~+10% YoY). Peer evidence is stark: Skyworks FY2026 revenue **-3.1%** with operating margin collapsing to **5.5%** (from 12.2%), and Qorvo **-1.1%** with an 11.2% operating margin. Concurrent capacity expansion across three wafer fabs and two US hybrid sites adds fixed-cost risk if the AI cycle rolls over.

## 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG**

**Rationale.** MACOM is gaining share in exactly the two sub-markets that are growing — data-centre optics and defence RF — while its closest handset-exposed peers are shrinking. Growth is nearly double the peer median, gross margin is above median and expanding ~640bp in a year, adjusted operating margin has inflected from 25.6% to 38.2%, and Q4 book-to-bill of **1.4x** with record backlog and only 7% of revenue booked-and-shipped in-quarter signals genuine supply-constrained demand rather than channel stuffing. The balance sheet carries trivial interest expense ($6.3M) and FY2026 net income of $335M reversed FY2025's $54M loss (driven by a one-off non-operating item).

**Qualification:** MACOM is **sub-scale** — $1.33B revenue is ~9% of ADI's — with meaningful customer concentration in hyperscalers and defence primes, and its $15B SAM claim depends on winning the CW laser and 1.6T sockets against far larger incumbents. The 50% drawdown from May 2026 shows the market is no longer paying for growth at any price.

---

## 6. Sector Rating: **ATTRACTIVE** (narrowly — a bifurcated call)

The AI-networking optics and Western defence/industrial RF niches are in a genuine capacity-constrained upcycle with pricing power and visible backlog — that is the attractive half. The broad analog, handset RF and carrier telecom complex is neutral-to-declining (NXPI, SWKS, QRVO all flat-to-down with margin erosion), and the whole group is exposed to a violent AI-capex sentiment cycle evidenced by the 8.3% single-day sector selloff in November 2026. **Net: attractive for MTSI's specific SAM, but the rating is conditional on AI data-centre spending holding through FY2027 and on the CPO transition not cannibalising pluggable-laser content.**
