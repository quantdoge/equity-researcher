The critical test for a short: **is the growth real?** It is. Goodwill and intangibles *fell* from $414.9M to $393.6M, so the 37.3% revenue increase is **organic, not roll-up**. Accruals are negative in every year, cash conversion exceeds 1.0x, and FCF conversion is 0.87x. There is no evidence of revenue-recognition games.

The two deductions from quality:
- **Earnings quality score is only 51/100**, and the reason is real: FY26 OCF of $357.1M includes an add-back of **$87.8M of stock-based compensation**. Cash-based FCF is $204.9M, not $292.7M — a 30% haircut, and just a **1.3% FCF yield** on the current market cap.
- **Net income ($335.3M) exceeded operating income ($305.5M)** despite $6.3M of interest expense, implying a roughly **zero-to-negative effective tax rate**. This is consistent with release of a valuation allowance on deferred tax assets. That is a one-time-quality tailwind that will not repeat and ultimately becomes a headwind as the normalized tax rate resets higher — a mechanical drag on FY27 EPS that consensus may be under-modelling.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

**1. Customer concentration — the single largest defect (Severity: High)**
- **Customer A = 20% of FY26 revenue, up from 12%.** In **Q3 FY26, Customer A reached 26% of revenue** (from 11% a year earlier).
- Customer B = 14%. **Top-10 customers = 58% of revenue.**
- The trajectory (11% → 12% → 19% → 26% → 20%) is the signature of a business whose growth is increasingly a bet on one counterparty's order book. A single sourcing change is a ~20-26% revenue hole.

**2. China = 37.7% of revenue, up 930bps from 28.4% (Severity: High)**
Disclosed on a *billing address* basis, which likely understates true end-demand exposure. Simultaneous escalation of Customer A concentration *and* China concentration is not a coincidence — it strongly suggests Customer A is a China-based optical-module vendor shipping into US hyperscalers. **The "AI data center" growth engine may be routed through the most geopolitically exposed link in the chain.** (Caveat: identity of Customer A is unconfirmed — this is the single most important diligence item remaining.)

**3. Data-center concentration in a single capex cycle (Severity: Medium-High)**
Data center revenue $509.7M, **+74%**, now 38.4% of FY26 and **44% of Q4**. This is a levered bet on one customer cohort's capex cadence, not a diversified analog franchise.

**4. A 24% sequential revenue jump plus $302M of near-term purchase commitments (Severity: Medium-High)**
Q4 revenue rose **+24.1% QoQ** in a business carrying **186 inventory days**. Simultaneously, the 10-K discloses **$328.8M of purchase commitments, ~$301.7M due within one year**. In semiconductor history, a >20% sequential step-up paired with take-or-pay supply commitments and record margins is the classic setup for a subsequent digestion quarter. This is the mechanism by which negative operating leverage arrives.

**5. Margin at an all-time peak, 1,600bps above the nearest peers (Severity: High — see §4)**

**6. Insider selling cluster (Severity: Medium)**
Roughly **55 reported transactions totalling >$42M of sales**, with the CEO showing **19 sales and 0 purchases**, CEO sales on 11-Nov-2026 at **$240.29–$252.61**, and at least one director reducing holdings to ~39,239 shares after a ~$14.7M sale. **Zero insider purchases into a 51% drawdown.** (Caveat: the Form 4 tool returned filing metadata only, not transaction line items — dollar figures are from secondary sources.)

**7. Dilution and zero buybacks (Severity: Medium)**
Piotroski **F7 = 0** (share count rose). $87.8M SBC (6.6% of revenue, 28.7% of operating income) offset by **$0 of repurchases in FY26** (vs $43.1M in FY25). The 10-K records **1,262K shares issued to settle convertible notes** plus $161.2M cash repaid. The **2029 Convertible Notes become convertible at holder option during Q1 FY27**, and $340.7M is now classified as current — a live dilution overhang with a "stock price trigger" feature.

**8. Receivables outgrew revenue in FY25 (40.6% vs 32.6%)** — a mild flag, but it *normalized* in FY26 and **DSO improved from 51.8 to 44.9 days**. This is *not* channel stuffing.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

| | P/Sales | Gross margin | Operating margin | P/E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MTSI** | **11.6x** | **58.0%** | **23.0%** | 46x GAAP / 36x adj |
| COHR (Coherent) | 5.4x | 37.5% | 12.7% | 48x |
| LITE (Lumentum) | 14.7x | 41.7% | 17.4% | n.m. (impairment) |

This table *is* the bear case. MACOM earns **2,050bps more gross margin than Coherent and 1,630bps more than Lumentum** on functionally comparable photodetector / TIA / driver content. Q1 FY27 guidance is for **62–63% adjusted gross margin**. Either that is a durable technological moat in high-speed detection, or it is **scarcity rent in a temporary 1.6T bottleneck** — and 63% gross margin in a merchant component business is precisely the price signal that recruits capacity, accelerates customer dual-sourcing, and pulls forward silicon-photonics/co-packaged-optics substitution that integrates the discrete photodetector away.

The threat vector is not a competitor beating them today; it is **architectural disintermediation** (CPO/SiPh moving detection on-package) and hyperscaler-mandated second sourcing. Management's own guidance implies margins go *up* from here, which leaves no cushion for either.

**Bear-case arithmetic (margin reversion):** FY26 gross profit $770.4M on $464.9M of opex. A reversion to **50% GM** — still 800–1,200bps above peers — removes $106.6M of gross profit and cuts operating income **35%** to $198.9M, taking EPS to roughly **$2.90**. On a de-rated 30x that is **~$87/share**. Modestly: revenue $1.6B (vs $1.94B consensus) at 54% GM yields ~$4.00–4.50 EPS, or **$100–112 at 25x — a 45% decline.**

## 5. Management Red Flags

- **Insider selling, no buying, into weakness** (§2.6). CEO with 19 sales / 0 purchases is a behavioural signal, not a legal one.
- **Zero buyback** with $792M cash while issuing $87.8M/yr of stock — capital allocation that transfers value to employees, not holders.
- **Promotional framing**: the "net income of $335.3M vs a $54.2M loss" headline reverses on inspection — FY25's loss was a **$193.1M non-cash loss on debt extinguishment**. GAAP FY26 still required walking from GAAP $4.31 to **adjusted $5.60** EPS (a 30% uplift), and Q4 adjusted operating margin of 38.2% is the highest in company history. Aggressive non-GAAP framing at a cyclical peak is a classic tell.
- **No auditor change, no restatement, no related-party transactions, no aggressive capitalisation** (capex $64.4M ≈ D&A $62.6M) identified. Governance red flags are behavioural, not accounting.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary — is there a credible bear case?

**Yes, but it is an operational/duration thesis, not a fraud thesis.**

*The bear case:* MTSI is being priced off a single record quarter ($424.8M revenue, +24% QoQ, 38.2% adjusted operating margin) that reflects a **temporary bottleneck in 1.6T optical components**. The market extrapolates 62–63% gross margins — 1,600bps above the nearest peers — from a business with **26% single-customer concentration, 37.7% China revenue, and $302M of take-or-pay supply commitments due within 12 months** while carrying 186 inventory days. Consolidation of Customer A and China exposure into the same counterparty means the AI growth vector and the geopolitical risk vector may be the *same node*. Consensus FY27 revenue of $1.94B (+46%) and EPS of $9.80 requires peak margins to become the permanent base. Any order-cadence pause, export-control action affecting Chinese module vendors, or 300-500bps of margin compression produces a $90–120 stock.

*The bull case, stated fairly — and it is strong:* Everything forensic checks out. **M-Score warning is growth-driven; DSRI is 1.03; accruals are negative; cash conversion is 1.065x; DSO improved 7 days; inventory days fell 13 days; growth is organic with goodwill declining; F-Score is 8/9; Z-Score is 3.23 safe; net debt is trivial; guidance is accelerating into Q1 FY27.** The company is taking real share in AI optical interconnect and margins are expanding on genuine mix and operating leverage. And critically, **the stock has already fallen 51% and sits 7% above its 52-week low at ~20x FY27E adjusted EPS** — the multiple compression that a short would be betting on has already been delivered.

## 7. Contrarian Rating

### ⚖️ NO EDGE — do not initiate a short here (short thesis: systematically harvested; fraud case: rejected)

**Reasoning.** My job is to find the failure mode, and I found the *risk* without finding the *lie*. On every test that distinguishes fraud from quality — DSRI, accruals, cash conversion, DSO, inventory days, organic vs. acquired growth, auditor continuity — MTSI passes. A short built on "the accounting is aggressive" would be wrong and would lose money against a company generating $357M of real operating cash flow with a 3.23 Z-Score. The residual bear case is a **margin-durability and concentration argument**, and that argument was worth 50 points of downside at $409 — it has already been paid out. Shorting a name at 20x forward earnings, 7% off its low, with guidance still accelerating, is a poor risk/reward for a contrarian.

**Where I would re-engage short (concrete triggers):**
1. A re-rating back above **$290–310** (≈30x FY27E) on no upgrade to the durability of 62%+ gross margins.
2. **Any guidance implying gross margin below 58%**, or a quarter where data-center sequential growth decelerates below ~5%.
3. **Evidence that Customer A is a China-domiciled module vendor** and/or China revenue crossing 45% — that would convert a valuation short into a genuine structural short.
4. Purchase commitments rising further while inventory days tick up and DSO extends — the trio that signals a demand air-pocket.
5. Failure of the 2029 convertible to convert smoothly / capped-call unwind that forces equity issuance.

**Watch items for the long side too:** the $88M SBC load with no buyback is a permanent value leak that makes the "adjusted $5.60" a fiction; size any long using the 1.3% SBC-adjusted FCF yield, not the 1.9% headline.
