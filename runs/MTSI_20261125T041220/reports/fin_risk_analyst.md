## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Valuation is the dominant risk, not leverage.** 32.6x EV/EBITDA, 46.4x P/E, 1.88% FCF yield on record-cycle margins. Multiple compression alone can erase half the equity value — and already has.
2. **Peak-cycle margins with extreme operating leverage.** Gross margin 58.0% and EBITDA margin 34.6% are likely all-time highs. The −20% revenue scenario cuts EBITDA 52%. Operating leverage works both directions.
3. **Share dilution.** Basic shares +2.3% and diluted shares +5.1% YoY; SBC of $87.8M equals **30% of free cash flow** and 6.6% of revenue. This is the sole Piotroski miss (F7 = 0) and a persistent, recurring transfer of value from shareholders.
4. **Repayment concentration.** $340.7M (91.8% of debt) is current. Cash-covered 2.3x, so not a crisis — but if any of this is convertible debt, settlement could occur in shares, compounding flag #3. Terms were not available in the data.
5. **Working capital is a growing cash drag.** FY2026 consumed $92.7M of cash via working capital (vs. $37.8M in FY2025), driven by receivables +40.9% and inventory +46.5%. DSO drifted from 56.1 to 57.6 days. Manageable, but a reversal signal if growth decelerates.
6. **Acute event/tail risk in recent tape.** Three sessions of −18.4%, −11.0% and −9.6% within four weeks, with the stock 52% below its high despite record reported results. This indicates forward-guidance sensitivity that the trailing financials do not reveal.
7. **Goodwill and intangibles of $393.6M** equal 23% of equity — a residual acquisition-quality exposure, though well covered.
