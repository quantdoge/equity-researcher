FY26 (ended 10/2/26): revenue $1,327.6M (+37.3%), gross margin 58.0%, net income $335.3M, operating cash flow $357.1M. ## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary — **Moderate, concentrated in geopolitics**

- **SEC/DOJ enforcement: none found.** EDGAR full-text search for SEC actions naming MTSI returned zero results.
- **No current material litigation identified.** Historical items are resolved/ordinary-course: the 2016 Applied Micro Circuits merger class action (settled), GigOptix patent litigation (settled), MACOM v. Infineon/AG patent dispute (favorable injunction upheld by Fed. Cir. 2018). Routine IP disputes are endemic to the sector.
- **China export-control exposure is the dominant regulatory risk: China = 37.7% of FY26 revenue; Asia Pacific a further 11.6%** (US only 38.1%). This is a >$500M annual revenue line sitting inside an escalating US–China tech control regime, and MACOM also sells into **defense applications (radar, RF jamming, ECM)** which carry ITAR/EAR licensing obligations. No disclosed consent agreements or violations, but any Entity List action, end-use restriction, or Chinese retaliatory/critical-materials control (GaN/GaAs substrates, rare earths) hits revenue and input costs simultaneously.
- **Pending approvals:** none material identified (no FDA/FCC-type gating).
- **Convertible note mechanics:** 2029 notes became convertible at holder option in Q1 FY27; 1.262M shares already issued to settle notes and $161.2M repaid in cash. Dilution overhang, not a regulatory matter.

## 3. Insider Activity Analysis — **Strongly net selling; zero open-market buys**

This is the most important finding in the file.

- **Reported aggregate of >$355M in executive insider sales over the trailing year** (third-party aggregation; a narrower recent-window count shows ~55 transactions / >$42M). Treat the $355M figure as directionally reliable, not audited.
- **Cluster of sales in Nov 2026, immediately after record Q4 results (11/5/26):** CEO Daly sold 59,939 sh at weighted-average $240.29–$252.61 (11/11); CFO Kober sold 22,009 sh (~$4.56M) on 11/6–11/9 following 43,793 PRSUs vesting on 11/5; COO Dennehy, SVP Hwang (48,026 sh), and GC Roth also filed.
- **CEO also sold 15,828 sh at ~$357.82 on 5/29/26** under a Rule 10b5-1 plan — i.e., sold near the all-time high.
- **No Form 4 open-market purchases by any officer or director in the period reviewed.** Sales are characterized as 10b5-1 pre-planned (CFO's plan adopted Aug 14, 2025), which mitigates opportunistic-trading optics but does not change the signal: management monetized a 419% five-year run while retaining trivial residual stakes.
- **Minor disclosure-controls blemish:** GC Ambra Roth filed a **Form 4/A** correcting a reported sale (522 sh at $250, 11/12/26).

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Supply-chain and capacity constraint is now the binding constraint on the growth story.** MTSI fell **−15.9% in a single session on 10/22/26** — not on its own results, but on Lam Research's warning of cleanroom space and component bottlenecks through 2027. MACOM's III-V (GaAs/InP/GaN) manufacturing is capacity-inelastic and split between internal fabs and external foundries; **$328.8M of purchase commitments ($301.7M due within 12 months)** locks in spend against a demand forecast that is itself the risk.
- **Geographic concentration (China 37.7% + APAC 11.6%)** is an operational-continuity issue as much as a regulatory one — dual-sourcing and re-routing capability is unverified.
- **Key-person and technical concentration.** CEO Daly is the architect of the current strategy with no publicly named successor; ~2,100 employees supporting a $1.33B revenue base means thin bench depth in photonics/compound semi engineering, and the Lowell, MA fab carries single-site disruption risk.
- *Mitigant:* **no direct customer represented ≥10% of revenue in FY26/FY25/FY24**, which materially reduces the classic hyperscaler-insourcing/one-customer cliff risk that plagues peers.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **Low-to-Moderate**

- No consumer-facing exposure, recalls, or brand-driven backlash vectors. End markets are carriers, hyperscalers, defense primes, and industrial OEMs — reputational damage travels through **reliability of supply and delivery credibility** rather than public sentiment.
- The live reputational thread is the **"AI-bubble / overvaluation" narrative**: MTSI fell ~33% in a month, and GuruFocus-type fair-value screens flagged it at $200.50 fair value versus a $273 price in October. High beta (1.83) means every peer's capex or cleanroom comment becomes a MACOM headline.
- Defense/China dual-use optics invites future press and political scrutiny but has not produced an incident.
- Social/ESG controversy scores: no flagged controversy found. **Minor indirect exposure:** the Lowell, MA site operates under a Massachusetts Ch. 21E Activity and Use Limitation (historical contamination) — a legacy environmental encumbrance, not an active controversy.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH** (lower end of High)

Rationale: the *controllable* risk set is genuinely clean — no enforcement actions, no material litigation, no restatements, no customer concentration, stable CFO. What pushes the rating to High is the *uncontrollable* set stacking up at once: **37.7% China revenue inside an escalating export-control regime**, a **supply-constrained ramp into a stock priced for perfection**, a **44% drawdown at 1.8 beta**, and **>~$355M of insider sales with zero insider buying and de minimis residual ownership** at the cycle peak. Non-financial risk here is not about misconduct — it is about geopolitical and alignment fragility at a valuation that has no cushion.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Management alignment gap (highest conviction):** CEO/Chairman duality + single-digit-thousand-share director holdings + >$355M trailing insider sales + zero open-market buys. Ask the board, in writing, for the ownership guidelines, pledging policy, and whether the comp committee has clawback/retention mechanics forcing meaningful residual ownership.
2. **China 37.7% of revenue** with defense end-use exposure and no disclosed quantification of Entity-List/end-use/rare-earth sensitivity. Stress-test a 25–50% China revenue impairment.
3. **$328.8M purchase commitments ($301.7M within one year)** committed against a demand forecast that the market just repriced −16% in a day on a supplier's capacity commentary. Watch for inventory/commitment build ahead of an order air-pocket.
4. **Valuation-narrative fragility:** −15.9% on a peer's warning, ~−33% in a month, 419% five-year run. Beta 1.83 makes headline risk a shareholder-value risk independent of fundamentals.
5. **Form 4/A amendment by the General Counsel** — low severity, but a disclosure-controls data point worth monitoring in combination with #1.
6. **Succession:** no named successor to a founder-era CEO who is simultaneously Chairman and the largest individual seller.

**Data gaps (declared, not hidden):** the filings tool could not retrieve a DEF 14A or 10-Q for MTSI, so board independence percentage, exact compensation metrics, and the full Legal Proceedings note were not read directly — governance conclusions rely on IR-site board listings and press. The $355M insider-sale aggregate is a third-party figure and should be reconciled to Form 4 line items before use in the memo. No peer-multiple benchmarking of governance was performed.
