### Investment Memo: SSNC — 26 October 2026

**Verdict**: BUY | Conviction: Medium

**Investment Thesis**
SS&C is a narrow-moat, mission-critical financial-infrastructure franchise — 90% recurring revenue, ~48% gross margins, top-2 global alternatives administrator riding an ~11–12% AUM CAGR — trading at 11.6x FY26 adjusted EPS and ~11x FCF, a 25–40% discount to financial-software peers. The forensic screen is clean (negative accruals four years running, no manipulation signal), so this is a genuine value+momentum setup, not a value trap. What caps conviction: the real balance sheet is levered ~2.6–3.2x (not the 1.9x the naive screen shows), organic growth is guided *down* to 4.0% for Q4, and the easy +34% off the June low has been made.

**Bull Case**
- 11.6x forward adjusted EPS / ~9% advertised FCF yield for a 40%-margin, 90%-recurring-revenue franchise — market is pricing near-zero growth against 5%+ organic and expanding margins.
- Alternatives franchise momentum: GlobeOp redemptions 1.64% vs 2.37% 5-yr avg, AUA +$581bn; AI as margin lever (39.9% record EBITDA margin, targeting 41%).
- Deleveraging + $1.5bn buyback + record capital return is directly accretive; multiple paths to re-rate toward 13–14x ($99–106).

**Bear Case**
- Real leverage ~3.2x, sub-IG, $2.0bn refi into a 5%+ 10Y world; a term-premium accident (10Y→5.75%) de-rates the stock to ~$68 even if operations are fine.
- Organic growth decelerating (5.2% → 4.0% guided); if it prints <3%, an 11.6x multiple is fair, not cheap — the entire value thesis rests on this one variable.
- AI disintermediation of labour-arbitrage fund administration is the live narrative risk; GAAP earnings flat for five years means the roll-up treadmill must keep running.

**Key Risks**
1. Organic growth <3% for two consecutive quarters (thesis invalidation — every agent flagged this variable).
2. 2027 refinancing above ~7% all-in, or HY OAS >450bp freezing the M&A engine.
3. AI-driven price compression showing up in gross margin (<46%) before it shows up in revenue.
4. Goodwill impairment against −$7.1bn tangible equity; India/DORA operational event.
