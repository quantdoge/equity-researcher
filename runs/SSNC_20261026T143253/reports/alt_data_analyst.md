### 7. Confidence Level: **LOW–MEDIUM**
Rationale: (a) the company's B2B model defeats most consumer alt-data methods — Google Trends, app and social signals are structurally low-relevance; (b) the patent tool failed twice (no data); (c) the web-traffic proxy returned no quantitative time series, leaving the +22.9% sscmaster fragment unverified; (d) job-posting data is a level, not a trend, and the two sources disagree by 7x; (e) news sentiment rests on 8 headline-scored articles. **The highest-quality signals here are hard facts, not alt data: the 8-quarter surprise-decay pattern, the Q4 organic-growth guide-down to 4.0%, and the -7.3% post-print price reaction.** Those are what I would weight.

**Key limitation and lag:** Google Trends and social data lag by 1–4 weeks and are pre-earnings (data ends Oct 18 / Oct 26); web traffic is monthly and lags ~4–6 weeks. None of these feeds cleanly reflects Q4-to-date (Oct–Nov) demand. A genuine read on Q4 requires fund-administration AUM levels and equity-market levels through December, which are not in this data set. **Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one — and here it is barely leading at all.**
