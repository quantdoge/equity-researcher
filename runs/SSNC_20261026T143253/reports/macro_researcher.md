## 1. Macro Environment Summary — **TRANSITIONAL, tilting risk-off**

As of 26 October 2026 the US is in a **re-tightening cycle at the long end**, not a cutting cycle. The Fed cut through 2025 (effective funds 4.33% → 3.63%) but inflation re-accelerated: headline CPI rose from a 2.4% YoY trough in Feb 2026 to **3.7% YoY in Sept (+0.65% m/m)**, core PCE to **3.0% YoY**. The Fed hiked 25bp in Sept 2026 to **3.75%–4.00%**, and markets price ~85% odds of at least one more hike by December (BIS, 13 Oct). The 2Y at 4.65% embeds a short hiking *sequence*, not a one-off.

The curve has **un-inverted, but for the wrong reason**: 2s10s is +34bp, driven by a long-end term-premium repricing (10Y **4.99%**, peaked 5.31% on 5 Oct; 30Y **5.37%**) — yields last seen pre-pandemic. That is a bear steepener, not a growth signal. Meanwhile credit is still priced for perfection: HY OAS 338bp (23 Oct), though **+70bp since 21 Sept (266bp)** — early stress worth monitoring. The broad USD has firmed 2.9% since early May to **121.4**. Growth is fine (~2.2% saar) and labour is stable (4.2%). Net: a "hot nominal, high-long-end, thin-risk-premium" regime — the most hostile configuration for long-duration and leveraged financial assets.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL** (with an unfavourable tilt)

The transmission channels to SSNC are **real but second-order**: refinancing and floating-rate debt cost ~2-3% of EPS in aggregate, USD translation ~1-2%, offset by demonstrated pricing power, 5%+ organic growth that is not GDP-dependent, 90%-style recurring revenue, 2.63x leverage and an 11.6x multiple that already discounts a lot. SSNC is a **levered-value business, not a long-duration growth asset** — the correct macro frame is credit and AUM, not the discount rate. Downgrade to **Unfavourable** if (a) the 10Y clears 5.5%, (b) HY OAS exceeds ~450bp, or (c) the Fed delivers two more hikes.

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BEAR: a term-premium accident**

**The setup:** 30Y at 5.37% and 10Y near 5% with HY OAS at 338bp and equities near highs is a complacent combination. A weak long-end auction or a foreign-demand strike pushes the **10Y to 5.75% and the 30Y through 6%** in a quarter, with HY OAS to 550bp.

**Transmission to SSNC (quantified):**
- The **$2B 2027 refi** prices at ~7.5% instead of ~6.5% → **+$20M/yr** incremental interest, exactly at the wrong moment.
- Rolling a 5% discount rate to 6.5% takes a 11.6x multiple to ~9.5x on unchanged EPS → **~$68/share (-18%)**, *even though operations are fine*.
- A concurrent 15% equity drawdown cuts AUM-linked fund admin fees and freezes fund launches → organic growth from 5.2% toward ~2%, compounding the de-rating → downside toward **$55–60**, retesting the June low.
- Mitigant: buyback capacity ($155M/quarter already) becomes *more* accretive at that price, and 9% FCF yield puts a floor. That asymmetry — cheap equity + fixed-rate-able debt — is why this is a **de-rating surprise, not a solvency event**.

**The mirror-image bull:** if the CPI re-acceleration proves tariff-driven and transient, the Fed signals it is done, the 10Y falls to 4.2% and the USD rolls over — SSNC re-rates to 14x $7.60 = **~$106**, with FX becoming a tailwind again. I weight the bear scenario more heavily on positioning (thin risk premia) but the bull more heavily on fundamentals.
