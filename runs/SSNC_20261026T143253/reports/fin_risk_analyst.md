## SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. (SSNC) — Financial Risk Assessment
*Analysis date: 26-Oct-2026. Financials: FY2021–FY2025 annual; P&L trailing-twelve-months (TTM) through Q3-2026. Balance sheet is FY2025 (Dec-2025) — a 9-month mismatch forced by data availability.*

---

### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **SUB-INVESTMENT GRADE (crossover quality)**

Both agencies rate SSNC below investment grade: **Moody's Ba3 CFR** (stable) and **S&P BB-** — S&P *lowered* the rating from BB and rates the proposed senior secured debt BB-. This is the single most important fact in the file: the market's own credit committee classifies this balance sheet as speculative.

**Leverage is materially higher than the reported figures suggest.** Total debt is $7,647M against $3,574M of reported cash, giving an apparent net debt/EBITDA of only 1.93x. **I do not believe that cash balance is corporate cash.** Cash tripled from $567M to $3,574M in FY2025, but nothing in the cash flow statement funds it: FCF of $1,664M less $1,036M buybacks, $254M dividends and ~$1,350M of goodwill/intangibles added leaves cash *down* roughly $1.0B. Total equity rose only $327M, so there was no equity raise. Either the line includes client/segregated funds, or it is a data error. On a normalised ~$500M cash balance (the FY2022–FY2024 run-rate), **net debt is ~$7.1B and net leverage is 3.2–3.4x, not 1.9x.** This is the largest single uncertainty in the analysis and must be verified against the 10-K liquidity footnote.

Even on the company's own basis, gross debt/EBITDA is 3.4–3.6x — 2–3x the level carried by investment-grade software peers.

### 4. Downside Scenario — Revenue −20%

Applied to TTM ($6,686M revenue, $2,238M EBITDA), assuming ~55% variable COGS, ~45% variable SG&A, fixed D&A:

| Revenue | $6,686M | $5,349M | −20.0% |
| EBITDA | $2,238M | **$1,266M** | **−43.4%** |
| EBITDA margin | 33.5% | 23.7% | −9.8pts |
| EBIT | $1,511M | $539M | −64.3% |
| **EBIT / interest** | 3.51x | **1.25x** | Near breach |
| EBITDA / interest | 5.19x | 2.94x | Weak |
| **Net debt / EBITDA (normalised)** | ~3.2x | **~5.65x** | Above typical 4.5x covenant thresholds |
| FCF | ~$1,664M | ~$630M (11.8% margin) | −62% |
| Dividend ($254M) cover | 6.5x | 2.5x | Still covered |

Operating leverage is ~2.2x: a 20% revenue decline destroys 43% of EBITDA. The company would **remain free-cash-flow positive and able to service interest** — the recurring, contract-based revenue base and 1.3% capex intensity are real protections. But leverage at 5.65x would freeze the M&A engine, suspend buybacks, and likely trigger a downgrade to B-category. At 9x stressed EBITDA ($11.4B EV) less $7.1B net debt, equity value falls to roughly $4.2B, or **~$18/share (−78%)** — the equity is a levered option on the EBITDA line. Add a goodwill impairment (>$14B of intangibles supporting negative tangible equity), and book solvency would visibly deteriorate.

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE** (with a High tail)

SSNC will not fail in a normal downturn: 26% FCF margins, 3.9x FCF/interest coverage, capital-light operations, and $1.3B+ of annual free cash flow make default a remote near-term outcome. But the balance sheet is levered 3.2–3.4x on a normalised basis, rated sub-investment-grade, carries a $2.0B 2027 maturity, and is structurally levered to a fixed-cost base with ~2.2x operating leverage. **Credit risk: elevated but serviceable. Liquidity risk: low on cash flow, thin on balance sheet. Market risk: above average.**

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **$3.0B unexplained cash increase with no funding source in the cash flow statement** — reported 1.9x net leverage is likely understated by ~1.3–1.5 turns. Highest-priority verification item.
2. **$2.0B of 5.5% notes mature in 2027** — refinancing at higher coupons inside 15 months.
3. **Negative tangible book equity (−$7.1B)**; goodwill/intangibles are 68% of total assets — impairment risk in a downturn.
4. **S&P downgrade to BB-**; both agencies sub-investment grade.
5. **EBITDA margin erosion**, 37.8% (FY21) → 33.5% (TTM), ~1pt/year, while debt rose $1.43B.
6. **Shareholder-first capital allocation**: $1.29B returned in FY2025 (78% of FCF) plus ~$1.35B of M&A, partly debt-funded; share count down 5%/yr. Credit is subordinated to buybacks.
7. **Thin liquidity cushion**: current ratio 1.07x, working capital 0.7% of revenue, normalised cash ratio ~0.10x against $7.6B of debt.
8. **Altman Z of 1.13** (distress zone; ~1.7x market-adjusted) — I weight this *low* because the goodwill-heavy, high-turnover-penalty balance sheet of an acquisitive roll-up structurally depresses Z, but it is not a source of comfort.
9. **Recurring double-digit earnings gap risk** (−10.4% on 3-Feb-2026) means equity drawdowns arrive abruptly.
