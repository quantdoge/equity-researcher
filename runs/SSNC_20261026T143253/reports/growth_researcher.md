# SS&C Technologies Holdings (SSNC) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $83.13 (26-Oct-26) · Market cap ~$20.5B · Net debt ~$4.0B · EV ~$24.5B*

## 1. Growth Story

SS&C is the operating system for the global "money management" back office — the embedded software *and* the outsourced workforce that administers ~$3.0T of alternative fund assets, retirement recordkeeping, and healthcare claims. Roughly 90% of revenue is recurring, switching costs are existential for clients (SS&C literally holds the books and records), and the company is now bolting agentic AI onto a 28,800-person service base to lift adjusted EBITDA margins to ~40% while converting >28% of revenue into free cash flow. This is not a hyper-growth story but a **durable 5–7% organic grower with a 9%+ FCF yield trading at ~11.6x forward adjusted EPS — a growth-at-a-value setup rather than a frontier-disruption moonshot.**

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration

- **Financial services software + fund/asset administration outsourcing:** ~$60–70B globally, growing high-single digits; alternatives administration is the fastest pocket (+10–12%) as private credit/PE allocations rise.
- **Healthcare payer/provider administration & claims software:** ~$40B+.
- **Serviceable market ≈ $90–110B vs. $6.8B revenue → <8% penetrated.** Even in its strongest niche, SS&C administers **$2.98T of alternative AUA** against an estimated $15–20T global alts AUM — only ~15–20% share, and that AUA grew **+$581B** recently.
- **Differentiation:** the only scaled vendor combining front-to-back software (Black Diamond, Advent, Geneva, GlobeOp) *with* the regulated operations staff to run it. Competitors sell tools (SEI, State Street, Citco, Gen II) or labor — SS&C sells the integrated stack, which is why displacement risk is low and pricing is annuity-like.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4'26 results & FY27 guidance — ~4 Feb 2027.** Q4 revenue guided to $1.72B (+4%), 1.5% *below* consensus, which triggered the post-print slide from $89.54 to $83. An in-line/beat-and-raise with FY27 organic ≥5% is the single biggest re-rate catalyst.
- **The "leave at 40%" margin milestone (YE2026).** Adjusted EBITDA hit 39.9% in Q3; management targets ~50bp/yr of expansion. Proof that AI/agentic automation is a *margin* lever supports the "software multiple" argument.
- **$1.5B renewed buyback + deleveraging.** FY25 capital return was $1.29B (~6% of cap; $1.04B buyback). Net debt ~$4.0B (~1.5x EBITDA) with $426M interest expense — every 100bp of refi savings and every $500M of debt paydown is direct EPS accretion.
- **Alternatives franchise momentum.** GlobeOp Forward Redemption Indicator at **1.64% in Aug-2026 vs. 2.37% 5-yr average** — low redemptions plus AUA inflows drive fund-admin revenue with little incremental cost.
- **AI product monetization (Sept-2026 "Portfolio of AI-Enabled Enhancements" at SS&C Deliver).** If agentic tools shift from cost-saver to separately priced revenue, the narrative shifts from BPO to software. A new Analyst Day (last was Sept-2024) with 2028 targets would crystallize this.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

A high-quality, deeply embedded franchise with 48% gross margins, 40% adjusted EBITDA margins, 28% FCF margins, and 90% recurring revenue, available at **11.6x forward adjusted EPS / ~9x EV/EBITDA / ~11x FCF** versus 14–15x EBITDA for slower-marginning peers. Deleveraging, a $1.5B buyback, +16% EPS growth in 2026, and AI optionality provide multiple paths to a re-rate toward $99–104 (analyst targets cluster $85–$104, average ~$99, +19%). It falls short of **Strong Buy** on two counts: Rule of 40 is only ~36 and *falling organic growth* (7.8% reported vs. 5.2% organic, Q4 guided +4%) means ~30% of growth is acquired — that is a roll-up, not a disruptor. Position as a core value-compounder, not a frontier-innovation bet.

## 7. Key Risks

- **AI disintermediation (the bear case that drove the Feb–Jun 2026 drawdown from ~$88 to $62):** a large share of revenue is labor-based fund administration and healthcare BPO. Agentic AI could let clients in-source or let nimble AI-native administrators undercut on price. SSNC's margin expansion story and its disruption risk are two sides of the same coin.
- **Organic growth deceleration:** Q4 guided to +4% total. If organic slips to 3%, an 11.6x multiple is fair, not cheap — the entire "value" thesis rests on organic holding ≥5%.
- **Acquisition dependence and balance sheet:** reported 7.8% vs. 5.2% organic; $704M of D&A is largely acquired intangibles; goodwill-heavy history. ~$4B net debt and $426M interest expense make EPS sensitive to rates and to refinancing.
- **Segment/client concentration:** alternatives redemptions, a single large healthcare client or regulatory change in claims adjudication could dent a segment quickly.
- **Founder/key-man and governance:** Bill Stone is Chairman/CEO and controls the dual-class structure — capital allocation (M&A vs. buyback) is his call, and large deals have historically been the main source of downside surprise.
- **Guidance credibility whipsaw:** shares fell ~7% the day after a Q3 *beat* purely on soft Q4 revenue guidance — expect continued headline volatility around prints.
