## 1. Business Quality Assessment

**Moat rating: Narrow — and narrowing in quality, even as revenue accelerates.**

Oracle is now two businesses wearing one ticker, and they have opposite economics.

**(a) Legacy database + enterprise applications — genuinely moated.** Oracle Database carries the highest switching costs in enterprise software; Fusion/NetSuite are sticky. This annuity (software support ~$19.6B, cloud apps $15.9B in FY26) is the crown jewel: ~80%+ gross margins historically, minimal capital intensity. But it is no longer growing — software revenue fell 3.0% in Q1 FY27, license revenue fell 14%, support fell 1%. This is a melting ice cube being harvested to fund the buildout.

**(b) OCI / AI infrastructure — no moat.** This is rented compute. Management itself discloses **30–40% gross margins** on AI infrastructure versus 80%+ in software. OCI holds **5.2%** of cloud infrastructure spend versus AWS 28%, Azure 20%, Google 15%. Growth of 121% is impressive execution (850MW delivered in Q1, 97.9% utilization, 1.2GW+ in FY26), but it is capacity-race competition, not a franchise: no network effects, no pricing power, no customer captivity worth the name. The proof is in the consolidated margin line — **gross margin has fallen from 80.6% (FY21) to 65.8% (FY26)** and is still sliding (cloud+software segment margin 60% → 55%).

**Moat trend over 5 years: deteriorating.** The business is trading a high-return, capital-light annuity for a low-return, capital-devouring commodity. ROIC has gone **14.8% (FY24) → 14.1% (FY25) → 10.3% (FY26)**, roughly equal to my estimated WACC (~9%). The hallmark of a moated business — ROIC comfortably above cost of capital, sustained — is absent.

**Circle-of-capability verdict:** the legacy assets I understand and like. The OCI economics — residual value of GPUs, depreciation lives, counterparty credit of AI lab customers, and $288B of leases signed but not commenced — I cannot underwrite with confidence. That alone disqualifies it from a concentrated Buffett-style position.
