### Investment Memo: ORCL — 25 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (Speculative) | Conviction: **Low**
**Price Target**: $120 (~+16% from $103.88) — probability-weighted: 30% bull ~$167 / 40% base ~$115 / 30% bear ~$75. Stale sell-side median of ~$207 was set before the November leg down and should be treated as likely to be cut, not as support.
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Oracle has executed a real, contracted business-model pivot — $664B of RPO (~7.4× forward revenue), OCI +121% YoY, revenue accelerating to +30% — but it is funding that growth with ~$156B of debt plus ~$288B of signed-but-uncommenced leases, negative FCF (−$23.7B FY26), and a BBB− balance sheet the CDS market already prices as high yield (356bp, record). At ~12.8× FY27E non-GAAP EPS and −68% from peak, the market has de-rated the equity to capitalise the entire AI build at roughly zero — which is why even our short analyst refuses to short it here. The equity is now a levered call option on the credit tape, not a software multiple: positive expected value, fat left tail, sized accordingly.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro is close to the worst feasible mix — 10Y 5.13% (real ~3.0% and rising), Fed hiking 9-Dec (97% priced), firm USD; ~85% of ORCL's value sits beyond year 3, so +100bp ≈ −15–20% equity. Sector demand is intact ($664B RPO, hyperscaler capex still revised up), but returns are accruing to suppliers (NVDA, HBM, power), not leveraged compute lessors. ORCL is the only large-cap peer converting growth into *lower* gross margin (−2,060bp since FY21; 60.0% in Q1 FY27).
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH.** The growth engine is the risk — 87% of FY26 growth from the Americas, Gulf capacity hostage to US export-licence discretion, TikTok USDS role politically contingent, EU antitrust info-gathering plus CADA sovereignty rules, US state data-centre moratoriums. Active securities class action (AI-capex disclosure) and a parallel **bondholder** suit — corroborated by S&P's cut to BBB− and Moody's negative outlook.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **3/10 financial quality; pass.** FCF −$23.7B, capex 105% of revenue, ROIC 10.3% ≈ WACC, legacy software annuity (−3% YoY) being harvested to fund a ~30–40% gross-margin compute business. Legacy franchise alone plausibly worth $375–425B — the market prices OCI at ≤ zero.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **BUY, speculative.** RPO is the most under-appreciated asset in mega-cap tech; FY28E EPS $11.10 ⇒ 9.4×; 71% EPS beat rate, four straight beats. Bear case is credit, not demand.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral — value-trap signature.** Cheapest in peer set (17.8× P/E, 12.8× EV/EBITDA) but −49% annualised alpha, 63% vol, β ~2.3, Sharpe −0.37; ROIC deteriorating. Earnings-revision input invalid (tool artifact). Cheap-and-falling-with-declining-ROIC — do not add on valuation alone.
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH, approaching Critical.** Debt +50% in one year ($104B→$156B); interest coverage rolled over (4.94x→4.48x); Altman Z ~1.4 even on market cap; ~1.25 years cash runway without market access; CDS 356bp vs BBB− rating — the bond market is pricing the downgrade the agencies haven't delivered. Depreciation cliff (~$13B annualised D&A vs ~$114B annualised capex) is the under-modelled P&L risk.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Founder-controlled governance (8/13 independent, co-CEOs, no independent chair); **Ellison's 413M pledged shares (~$54B, undisclosed loan terms)** — a mechanical forced-seller risk in a 53% drawdown; 20M-patient Cerner breach with delayed notification; $117.8M option grant into a −41% year. Mitigant: Ellison cancelled a $7.5B 10b5-1 sale with zero shares sold.
- **ESG**: **Laggard / negative modifier.** 2030 absolute emissions target quietly re-engineered to an intensity metric while adding gigawatts; no CDP/SBTi; permitting/community opposition is the ESG→revenue transmission channel ($130B of US projects blocked in Q1 2026). Not disqualifying; removes any quality premium.
- **Alternative Data**: **BEARISH near-term.** Search interest −66% off peak; the +8% Investor-Day rally fully reversed in four weeks; news sentiment negative but crowded. Key insight: this is a *financing* problem, not demand — every market-facing signal is negative while contract data runs above trend. **First sustained CDS compression is the highest-conviction buy trigger; not yet present.**
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Reject short at $104.** Accounting is clean (Beneish −2.56, accruals −5.7%, OCF/NI 187%) — not a fraud. The depreciation/lease thesis is directionally right but already paid; the better instrument is Oracle **credit**, not equity.

**Bull Case**
- $664B contracted backlog (~7–8.6× revenue coverage) converting physically: 300k GPUs and 850MW delivered in one quarter; even 70% delivery of consensus supports $160–200.
- Capex capped in FY27, self-funding targeted FY28 — management's FY30 FCF target implies an 8.5% FCF yield at $104; any credible FCF-inflection narrative re-rates the stock violently.
- Valuation already prices failure: ~12.8× FY27E EPS, legacy annuity alone arguably covers the market cap; an OpenAI funding confirmation or CDS compression is a +50–70% catalyst.

**Bear Case**
- December credit accident (~30%): Fed hike + higher dots + capex guide raised + downgrade to high yield → forced IG-index selling, equity re-rates to 8–10× earnings = $70–80.
- Single-counterparty fragility: ~45–54% of RPO tied to OpenAI, a pre-profit entity that must raise to pay; lease obligations (+$288B) are compounding slightly faster than backlog.
- Structural economics: incremental gross margin ~35%, D&A must converge toward a $100B+ capex run-rate, interest annualising to ~$5.7B+, 3–6%/yr dilution — consensus FY28–31 EPS is not compatible with the asset base being built.

**Key Risks**
1. High-yield downgrade or CDS >500bp (mechanical, non-fundamental selling)
2. OpenAI funding shortfall / contract renegotiation
3. Ellison margin-call dynamics on pledged shares (lender-decided, not thesis-decided)
4. Refinancing ~$35–45B/yr funding gap into record-wide spreads; **debt maturity ladder is the single most important missing data point**
5. Gross margin <58%; RPO sequential adds turning negative

**Portfolio Construction Note**
**Initiate 0.5% now, hard cap 1.5%, funded from the high-octane AI-capex sleeve (total levered AI-capex exposure ≤2.5%).** Half-Kelly sizing; at 63% vol / β 2.27, 1.5% of ORCL consumes the risk budget of a 3% normal position. Scale only on *credit* or *gross-margin* confirmation — never on price weakness: +0.35% post-print if Q2 delivered with capex ≤$85B and close >$120; +0.40% on CDS sustained <300bp or GM ≥60%. Stops: close <$92, CDS >500bp, any HY downgrade, or new equity issued <$100. Trim ⅓ at $160 and ⅓ at $180. Where the mandate permits, the **9% senior bonds are the better risk-adjusted expression** of this thesis — seniority, contractual coupon, same view. Own this one in ounces, not pounds.
