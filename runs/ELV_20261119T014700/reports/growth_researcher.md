## ELV — Elevance Health, Inc. | Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $400.84 (Nov 18, 2026) · Market cap $86.6B · EV ~$112.7B · Healthcare / Managed Care*

---

### 1. Growth Story

ELV is a $199B-revenue health benefits incumbent running a two-engine model: a mature, membership-challenged Health Benefits core (Commercial/Medicaid/Medicare) and **Carelon**, the fastest-growing asset — a pharmacy-services (CarelonRx) + care-delivery + behavioral platform growing high-single digits with a stated low-double-digit revenue CAGR target and mid-single-digit margin target. The disruptive thesis is not top-line hyper-growth; it is the **2026–2028 earnings re-acceleration off a washed-out base**: benefit-expense-ratio repair (MLR 90.6% in Q3'26, −110bps YoY), Medicaid rate catch-up in 2027, ACA marketplace repricing, and Carelon attach — all against a stock at ~13x forward earnings that prices in ~zero growth.

### 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration

- US national health expenditure ≈ **$5.3T, growing ~5%/yr, ~18% of GDP**. ELV's ~$200B revenue is roughly 4% of that — and its medical membership (~46–47M) covers ~14% of the US population.
- **Carelon is the real TAM story:** addressable spend spans US pharmacy (~$700B drug spend), care delivery/ASC/clinics, behavioral health and digital utilization management. Carelon revenue run-rate is now ~$19B/quarter (Q2'26 +6% YoY; Q1'26 +7.9%), annualizing near $75B including CarelonRx product revenue.
- **Penetration lever:** Carelon's captive internal base (ELV's own members) versus **external/unaffiliated clients**. Every point of external mix shift raises Carelon's margin (target: mid-single-digit op margin) without requiring ELV membership growth — this is the highest-quality growth available to the company.
- Competitive differentiation: state-level Blue-licensed scale (14 states), Carelon's integrated pharmacy + clinical data on ~47M members, and a digital-first member platform. Against UNH/Optum, ELV is the clear #2 in a two-player integrated-payer race — a real moat, but a follower position.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q4 2026 results + formal 2027 guidance — Jan 27, 2027.** Company has already committed to **≥12% adjusted EPS growth off a ≥$26.75 baseline (≈$30)**, and raised FY26 adj EPS to **≥$29.00** with OCF **≥$6.5B**. Consensus Q4 sits at just $2.08 — a low bar.
2. **CMS final Medicare Advantage rate notice (typically April).** Mid-November's **near-flat rate proposal, far below consensus, drove a −7.6% single-day drop in ELV (and −5.4% UNH, −8.5% HUM)**. A favorable final rate, or a 2028 rate cycle that reflects acuity, is the largest binary re-rating event.
3. **Medicaid rate relief in 2027** — states resetting rates to catch up with acuity; the clearest margin tailwind in the model (management framed 2027 Medicaid improvement explicitly).
4. **ACA marketplace open enrollment (Nov 2026–Jan 2027)** — post-subsidy-expiration repricing is already lifting Individual margins; enrollment durability is the swing factor.
5. **Carelon scaling + capital return:** new Carelon president installed, external-client wins, and **$5.0B of buyback authorization** being deployed at ~$399 average — accretive at 13x earnings.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (moderate conviction — GARP, not frontier innovation)

Scored honestly against a disruption framework, ELV **fails the growth screen**: 0.5% 2026 revenue growth, ~2.3% 2025–28 revenue CAGR, Rule of 40 of 14, and five consecutive years of operating-margin erosion. It is not a Cathie Wood compounder and should not be sized like one.

But the forward setup is genuinely asymmetric: a **13.3x 2027E / 11.6x 2028E multiple** discounts essentially no recovery; the company has **beaten by ~17% on average for seven quarters**, guided 2027 EPS growth to ≥12%, and drew 11 target-price raises plus an upgrade in the last month. **Growth is inflecting from −(negative) to +12% EPS, not from +40% to +30%** — that is a re-rating trade, and a 21% gap to consensus targets plus a 1.7% dividend and a $5B buyback gives a plausible ~17–20% annualized total-return path. Upside case: MLR holds ~90%, Medicaid rates reset, 2027 EPS $30–31, multiple re-rates to 16x → ~$490. Downside: near-flat MA rates persist and 2027 EPS growth lands in the 5–8% range → 12x $27.5 ≈ $330 (−18%).

### 7. Key Risks

- **Regulatory/rate risk (highest):** the near-flat MA rate proposal plus a proposed Medicare fraud rule (the "CRUSH" rule that hit UNH 8%) show a hostile rate environment. Final rates below consensus would break the ≥12% 2027 EPS growth commitment.
- **Medical cost trend:** Q3 improvement was driven by MA and Individual ACA; a respiratory/flu season or acuity miss resets the whole thesis.
- **Structural margin erosion:** 6.45% → 4.07% operating margin over five years. If 2027 does not reverse it, ELV is a value trap, not a growth recovery.
- **Membership attrition:** MA −18% (~400k) and Medicaid declines cap revenue growth; Carelon must outgrow the shrinkage.
- **Policy/legislative:** PBM reform (delinking), drug-pricing MFN, and ACA subsidy politics each target a piece of the Carelon/Individual economics.
- **Execution & balance sheet:** Carelon integration and one-time investment spend (opex ratio +90bps); ~$26B net debt limits flexibility; DCP-driven cash-flow quality.
- **Valuation floor risk:** at 13x the stock is cheap but not distressed — there is no deep-value cushion if EPS estimates reset again as they did in July 2025 (−$67/share in two sessions).

**Downgrade trigger:** final 2027 MA rates near-flat **and** 2027 guidance below +10% EPS growth → cut to Hold. **Upgrade trigger:** favorable final rates + MLR sustained ≤90.5% + Carelon external revenue disclosure → add, target $520+.
