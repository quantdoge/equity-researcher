## 7. Confidence: **MEDIUM** (low end of medium)

**Data limitations and lag:**
- The single most important variable for a payer — **medical loss ratio / cost trend** — has **no alt-data proxy** in this toolkit. Nothing here observes claims, utilization, or acuity. That is the whole thesis risk.
- Web traffic, job postings and social sentiment are **qualitative web-search proxies with no time series** — direction cannot be computed, only level.
- Patent feed unavailable; Google Trends is weekly with ~3-day lag (last obs 11/8/26); news through 11/18/26; prices through 11/12/26.
- Google Trends on "Anthem Blue Cross" conflates Medicare, individual and employer shopping — the ACA-led inference is an interpretation, not a measurement.

**Bottom line:** Alt data aligns with the "2026 is the trough, 2027 is the recovery" setup — strong consumer shopping interest, clean sentiment, persistent positive estimate surprises, and an apathetic retail base. It does not, and cannot, confirm the MLR trajectory that determines whether the 12% 2027 growth target is real. Size conviction accordingly; treat the late-October fade in price action as a warning that easy money has been made.
