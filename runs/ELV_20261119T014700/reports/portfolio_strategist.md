## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration — the binding constraint.** Measured correlations (252d) to the managed-care complex are *moderate, not tight*: CNC **0.60**, UNH **0.58**, CI **0.53**, HUM **0.53**, CVS **0.28**. **Factor tilts this position adds:**

- **Quality: −** (5/10 and decaying; ROIC/ROE compressing four straight years, F-score 6/9 failing on ROA trend, accruals, gross margin). This is *not* a quality-compounder; do not let it be counted as one in a quality sleeve.
- **Low-vol: −** (3/10; 33.7% annualised, 2-yr Sharpe 0.32, Sortino 0.36). The low beta (structural ~0.71, measured 0.16) is **not** downside protection — R² is ~0.005, i.e., SPY explains <1% of variance. **Budget this as a high-idiosyncratic-risk holding, not a defensive one.**
- **Duration: −** In a bear-steepening regime (10Y 5.13%, 30Y 5.47%) a 13x long-duration cash-flow asset has a mechanical headwind; higher reinvestment income only partly offsets.
- **Momentum/revisions: mixed** — 12-1m +11.7% (positive), but FY2026E EPS is −3.1% vs FY2025E and the stock has given back the entire post-Q3 beat ($450.69 → $400.84).

**Bottom line on fit:** acceptable as a small, deliberately value-tilted, policy-beta-tolerated holding. **Unacceptable** as a defensive/low-vol ballast position, which is how a 0.71 beta would tempt you to classify it.
