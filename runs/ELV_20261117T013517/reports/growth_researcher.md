# Elevance Health (ELV) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $420.07 (close 10/30/26) · Market cap ~$93B · EV ~$117B · Beta 0.71 · Div yield 1.6%**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Elevance is **not** a disruptive-innovation compounder — it is a $199B-revenue, low-beta managed-care utility executing a **cyclical margin recovery** while building a genuinely differentiated second engine in **Carelon** (PBM + care delivery/services). The bull case is not "big TAM, take share" — it is: *2026 is the trough Medicaid/MA margin year, benefit ratio just improved 70bp, and management has guided to ≥12% adjusted EPS growth in 2027 off a raised baseline.* That is a re-rating/recovery thesis dressed as growth, and the multiple (14.5x forward) is priced accordingly. My framework rewards TAM capture and durable growth; ELV fails that test but passes a GARP test.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

- **US commercial health insurance ~$900B, ~4.5% CAGR**; total US national health expenditure ~$5T. ELV's ~$199B is a mid-teens share of the addressable pie.
- **Carelon is the real TAM story**: Q3'26 revenue **$19.3B (+5%)**, now **~38% of enterprise revenue**, spanning CarelonRx (pharmacy) and Carelon Services (behavioral, complex care, care delivery) against a multi-hundred-billion PBM + care-delivery pool.
- **The penetration problem is the thesis-killer for a growth mandate**: consensus has ELV growing *at or below* market (4.4% vs 4.5%), meaning **zero net share gain** over five years. Carelon is still predominantly an internal "make vs. buy" ingredient business; external client revenue is not yet large enough to bend the enterprise curve. Until Carelon Services' third-party book inflects, ELV's TAM is theoretical, not capturable.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Jan 27, 2027 — Q4/FY26 results + formal 2027 guidance.** Guide is ≥12% adj EPS growth off a ≥$26.75 baseline → **≥$29.96**, above the $30.25 consensus's implied bar in spirit; FY26 adj EPS already raised to **≥$29.00**, OCF raised to **≥$6.5B**. Highest-variance event on the calendar.
- **2027 Medicare Advantage footprint execution (now → Q1'27).** ELV cut to **308 plans from 345 (−11%)** — margin over membership. AEP-to-enrollment conversion is the read on whether they cut too deep.
- **State Medicaid rate updates (Jan 1 and Jul 1, 2027 effective dates).** Management calls 2026 the Medicaid margin low; rate actions are the single biggest swing factor to the 2027 EPS bridge.
- **Carelon Services external wins + new Carelon president** (named with Q3). Any disclosed third-party booking acceleration re-rates the segment from "captive PBM" to "platform."
- **Washington**: ACA enhanced-subsidy expiry, Medicaid eligibility policy, PBM reform (direct threat to CarelonRx economics).
- **Buyback**: **$5.0B authorization remaining (~5% of market cap)**, repurchasing at ~$399 avg in Q3 — a mechanical EPS tailwind.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

*(Buy for a value/GARP mandate; **pass** for a pure disruptive-growth mandate.)*

Justification: the forward math is real — **≥12% EPS growth in 2027, 14.5x forward earnings, PEG 1.2, unanimous positive revision breadth, 1.6% dividend, $5B buyback, and a benefit ratio improving 70bp** off a genuine cyclical trough. But by my own framework ELV **fails on the four things that matter**: revenue growth decelerating to 4.4% (below market), **Rule of 40 of 14**, an operating margin that has eroded 240bp in four years, and **FCF that has fallen 56% since 2022 with only 56% net-income conversion**. There is no innovation moat — Carelon is a cost-optimization vertical, not a platform with data network effects. This is a well-run, cheap, mean-reverting insurer — not a growth compounder. Hold at $420; I would add meaningfully below ~$380 (≈13x '26E) where the recovery is priced with a real margin of safety, and trim into $480+ where the consensus PT is reached.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Medical cost trend re-acceleration (highest risk).** A 90.6% benefit ratio means a 100bp adverse move wipes out ~25% of operating income. Utilization spikes, GLP-1/specialty pharmacy inflation, or a severe flu season re-compress margins instantly — and the 240bp of erosion since 2021 proves how fast and how persistently this happens.
2. **Medicaid rate-update slippage.** The entire 2027 bridge rests on states granting rate increases. State budget stress or delayed effective dates push the recovery out a year and break the ≥12% EPS guide.
3. **Medicare Advantage over-cutting.** −11% plan footprint may surrender membership and scale faster than it improves margin, ceding share to UNH and Humana at exactly the wrong time.
4. **Cash conversion / earnings quality.** FCF $3.17B on $5.66B net income; working capital drag of −$3.3B in 2025 and DCP up **3.5 days to 44.9** — higher OCF guidance partly reflects payable timing that reverses. At **29x P/FCF**, a single year of weak conversion compresses the multiple hard.
5. **Guidance credibility.** The Jan 2026 −14% and Jul 2026 −8.5% sessions show a "beat and de-rate" pattern; management has repeatedly set expectations it later resets.
6. **Regulatory/competitive.** ACA subsidy expiry shrinks the Individual market; PBM reform targets CarelonRx directly; UNH ($448B revenue, 2.2x ELV's scale) and Cigna/Evernorth's integrated PBM are structurally better-positioned to take the Carelon opportunity.
