## 2. Consensus Assessment

**Overall agreement: MEDIUM.** Not split on direction (nobody wants to short it), but genuinely divided on whether ~$446 is a buy.

**Four high-consensus views (weight these heavily):**
1. **No accounting fraud.** Short, Quant, Nonfin and ESG independently converge. Beneish −2.25, F-Score 6/9, no SEC enforcement, 165% cash conversion in 9M-26. Per my framework the fraud risk would override the value case — **it doesn't exist here.** That is what keeps this in the book at all.
2. **The stock is fairly valued, not cheap.** Value ($475 FV), Growth (PEG 1.2), Quant (mid-pack value factor), Short (13.6x '26E FCF) and the tape (PT median **$472 = +5.8%**; 13 of 15 targets above spot) all land within a few percent of each other. **This is the single most reliable signal in the packet: ~+6% upside to consensus.**
3. **Legal/regulatory risk is the dominant idiosyncratic driver, not medical trend.** Five agents flag it. This is why the stock has 32.5% vol with a **0.058 correlation to SPY**.
4. **The trough is behind us, but only by two quarters.** Q3-26 EBIT +22.5% YoY, benefit ratio −70bp, two guidance raises. The recovery is *real but young*.

**Three genuinely contested points:**
- **Cash quality.** Fin Risk/Quant/Value weight FY2025's $3.17B FCF and 130% payout; Short/Alt Data weight 9M-26's $6.72B. I weight **both** — hence normalized FCF ~$7B (7.1% yield, cheap) *conditional on* conversion holding.
- **Beta/defensiveness.** Resolved above: it is a **low-correlation, high-idiosyncratic-vol** name, not a low-risk name.
- **2027 growth.** Growth Researcher flags 2027E $30.25 vs 2026E $29.05 = **only +4.1%**.

⚠️ **My own finding, and the most important correction in this memo:** management's headline **"≥12% adjusted EPS growth in 2027"** is measured off the *initial, lowballed* $26.75 2026 guide — not off the raised ≥$29.00. Against the raised base, consensus 2027 growth is **~4%**. The recovery narrative's headline **overstates the true 2027 step-up by roughly 3x.** This alone justifies refusing to pay above ~15x forward.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss — use fundamental triggers, not tight price stops.** At 32.5% annualized vol, a −20% move is ~0.6σ: a normal-noise event, not a thesis break. A conventional stop would whipsaw us out of a cheap, non-fraudulent franchise.

- **Re-underwrite trigger: $335 (−25%).** At that point the position is re-underwritten from scratch, not auto-sold (this is roughly Value's $330–350 buy zone — a level at which we would more likely *add* than exit).
- **Hard price stop: close below $361** (July-2026 intraday low $363.79 / $361.41). That level marked the last policy shock; a break means the market is pricing a new impairment.

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any *one* of these:**
1. **FY2027 MLR guided >91%** at the 27-Jan-2027 print (Short's flip trigger; the MLR guide has already been raised once, 90.2%→90.6%).
2. **FY2026 operating cash flow <$6.5B** — i.e., the 165% conversion reverses; this kills the normalized-FCF bull case outright.
3. **Net debt/EBITDA >3.0x or interest coverage <4.5x** (Fin Risk's upgrade-to-HIGH threshold). Current: 2.68x / 5.1x.
4. **2027 Medicaid rate increases not granted or delayed past 1-Jul-2027** — this is the entire 2027 EPS bridge (Sector, Growth, Nonfin all agree).
5. **CMS Medicare Advantage enrollment/marketing sanction reinstated** — Nonfin's largest unverified item; confirm in writing whether the 7/1/26 restriction was lifted.
6. **Adverse US v. Anthem development:** damages quantified, reserve taken, or settlement >$500M. Nine-figure treble-damages exposure with **no disclosed accrual.**
7. **Goodwill impairment** on the $39.5B balance (90% of equity; tangible equity only $4.5B).
8. **Buybacks resume above FCF** (they were $4.13B vs $3.17B FCF in FY2025).

**Trim triggers:**
- **$472 — trim ⅓** (consensus PT median; upside largely realized).
- **$500 — trim to half** (approaching Value's $520 trim zone and UBS's $523 street high).
- **$520+ — exit to a tracking position.** At $520 the stock is 19.5x 2026E on a 4%-growth 2027 — no longer defensible.

**Monitoring cadence:** quarterly MRR/MLR by segment (Medicaid is the swing), monthly CMS/state rate notices, and docket checks on both star-ratings cases and the FCA suit.
