# ESG Analysis — Elevance Health, Inc. (NYSE: ELV)

**Sector:** Healthcare / Managed Care (Health Benefits + Carelon services) | **Market cap:** ~$96B | **Employees:** ~96,600

---

## 1. ESG Materiality Map — Managed Care

For a health insurer, the financially material factors are heavily weighted toward **Social (S)** and **Governance (G)**; Environmental (E) is comparatively minor (low-carbon business, but procurement/physical-climate exposure via facilities and care network).

| Factor | Materiality | Why it hits cash flows |
|---|---|---|
| **Product/consumer safety & quality** (claims denial, prior auth, risk-adjustment coding) | **High** | Direct driver of DOJ/FCA liability, medical-loss-ratio and reputational damage |
| **Access & affordability of coverage** | **High** | Core social licence; ties to Medicaid/ACA enrollment, rate-setting, and political risk |
| **Business ethics / anti-corruption** | **High** | FCA suits, CMS audits, state AG actions |
| **Data privacy & cybersecurity** | **High** | 79M-record breach history; HIPAA fines; class actions |
| **Governance / board & comp** | **Medium-High** | Independent chair, comp alignment; shareholder rights |
| **Labour practices** | **Medium** | 96k workforce, clinician shortages, turnover |
| **Environment / carbon** | **Low** | Low Scope 1/2 intensity; procurement Scope 3 and physical climate (facility/network) only |

---

## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment — **LOW**
Health insurance is asset-light with modest direct emissions. Principal environmental exposure is **indirect** (Scope 3 procurement, facilities, travel) and **physical climate risk** to the provider network and members (heat, extreme weather affecting care access). No material spills, pollution liabilities, or stranded-asset risk. Carbon taxes / CBAM have negligible direct effect. *Rating: Low.*

## 3. Social Risk Assessment — **HIGH**
This is the central ESG exposure.
- **Medicare Advantage risk-adjustment (FCA litigation):** DOJ False Claims Act suit alleges ELV submitted inaccurate diagnostic codes 2014–2018; court found the alleged cost to the government "well over $100 million and potentially tens of millions per year." A judge denied Elevance's motion to dismiss; active deposition disputes continue. Tail risk of treble damages/penalties.
- **Medicaid profitability trough:** Elevance posted the largest absolute Medicaid membership losses among the Big Five (~3.4–3.8M members lost since March 2023 per Georgetown CCF), pressuring margins and affordability narrative.
- **Access/affordability & political exposure:** Prior-authorization and claims-denial scrutiny is a live regulatory and reputational theme.
- **Positives:** Substantial community investment — **$640M in affordable housing over 5 years, >$1B total** — addressing social drivers of health, which supports social licence and cost-of-care.

## 4. Governance Quality Score — **7.5 / 10**
- **Board independence:** Strong — **independent Chair (Ramiro Peru)** separate from CEO (Gail Boudreaux) → **no CEO/Chairman duality**. Audit, Compensation & Talent, Finance, and Governance committees all fully independent.
- **Board refreshment:** Adding directors (e.g., Steve Collis, Aug 1) signals refreshment; industry expertise (DeVore, Collis) appropriate for sector.
- **Compensation:** Pay tied to adjusted EPS and long-term metrics; watch that guidance-driven EPS framing (raised 2026 adj. EPS to ≥$29) doesn't over-reward short-term optics vs. durable margin recovery.
- **Deduction:** Ongoing DOJ compliance matter and the 2015 breach history indicate residual ethics/risk-oversight gaps historically. No dual-class structure; reasonable shareholder rights.
- *Score: 7.5/10.*

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure
- **US:** CMS risk-adjustment audits, DOJ/FCA enforcement, state insurance regulators, HIPAA/OCR, Medicare/Medicaid rate setting.
- **SEC climate disclosure rule:** Limited direct exposure (low-emissions business); Scope 3 procurement reporting only.
- **EU CSRD / CBAM:** Minimal — ELV is US-centric; CSRD applies only if EU subsidiaries exceed thresholds (Carelon/global ops negligible).
- **Medicaid managed-care rate-setting uncertainty** (2025 reconciliation law) — profitability risk.

## 7. Overall ESG Rating — **AVERAGE**
Structurally solid environmental profile and above-average governance (independent chair, no duality); but governance quality is offset by a **live DOJ fraud matter** and **high social/regulatory exposure** in its core Medicare Advantage and Medicaid businesses. Third-party data (Sustainalytics/KnowESG) suggests a low headline ESG risk score (~10), but I weight the **unresolved FCA litigation and Medicaid margin stress** as under-appreciated social/governance risk that headline scores understate.

## 8. ESG Investment Implication — **NEUTRAL (with governance overhang)**
ESG factors are **not a primary thesis driver** for ELV and do not create a structural valuation discount. The material ESG risk is **concentrated in the DOJ Medicare Advantage FCA case** — a discrete, quantifiable tail liability (>$100M alleged harm, treble-damages potential) rather than an existential license-to-operate threat. Environmental and governance fundamentals are sound. Net: **ESG is neutral to the investment case**; monitor (1) DOJ settlement/monitoring outcomes, (2) Medicaid margin recovery into 2026, and (3) any escalation in prior-authorization/claims-denial regulatory scrutiny. A DOJ resolution materially above reserve would be the key negative catalyst.
