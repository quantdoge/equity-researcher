### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH (moderate conviction)**

**Why:** (1) Consumer-facing brand search at a 12-month high, +35% YoY, into the peak selling season, with elevated site engagement quality — supportive of the enrollment/retention base. (2) The most reliable pattern in the dataset — an 85.7% beat rate with accelerating magnitude — points to Q4 EPS **above** the $2.08 consensus. (3) Price is confirming: $446.28, **+20.8% since May 1**, +3.8% on Nov 16 on ~2.3M shares (~2x average) to a fresh high. (4) Relative strength is stark: **ELV +17.1% vs UNH -0.2%** since Aug 3 — the market is paying for ELV's Medicaid/Carelon mix over pure MA exposure.

**Why not high conviction:** the post-Q3 tape rejected a 48% beat (445→414), insider selling hit the bid at $440, operating gain is down 25.7% YTD, MA open-enrollment search interest is not accelerating, and the careers/hiring proxies show no growth step-up.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Gaps and lags to disclose:**
- **Patent data unavailable** (API failure, 2 attempts) — not estimated.
- **Job postings: qualitative only** — no time series, so "hiring trend direction" is unsupported.
- **Web traffic is a search-proxy**, not SimilarWeb API monthly series; only one directional datapoint (+3.3% MoM).
- **Sentiment score** is keyword-based on a 7-article sample; social data is a search proxy with no post counts.
- **Google Trends is brand search, not enrollment** — intent (demand vs. friction/complaints) is not separable, and the November spike is seasonally confounded (can't be fully de-seasonalized with one prior year).
- **Q4 is ELV's seasonally weakest quarter** and the guidance-reset quarter; alt data has ~2–6 week lag and is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. A single Medicaid rate decision or a large-group contract loss would swamp every signal above.
