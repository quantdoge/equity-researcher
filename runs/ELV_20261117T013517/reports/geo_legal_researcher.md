# Elevance Health (ELV) — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment

**Prepared:** November 2026 | **Data through:** Q3 2026 10-Q (filed 21-Oct-2026), FY2025 10-K, litigation tracker updates to 16-Nov-2026

## 1. Geographic Risk Map

Elevance is effectively a **single-country business**. The FY2025 Form 10-K states: *"An immaterial amount of our total consolidated revenues is derived from activities outside of the U.S. and Puerto Rico."* This is the single most important framing fact: **classic geopolitical risk (sanctions, EM currency, expropriation, US-China trade) is near-zero**. The real geographic risk is *sub-national* — 50-state insurance and Medicaid regulation.

| Geography | Revenue exposure | Risk level | Driver of risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **United States — total** | ~100% | Medium | Federal/state regulatory, FCA enforcement, ACA & Medicaid policy |
| California (Blue Cross of California / Wellpoint entities) | Largest single state concentration; 10-K lists CA first among states with "largest concentrations of revenues" (CA, FL, NY, OH, IN, GA, VA, etc.) — individual state % not disclosed | Medium | Largest DOI, aggressive rate review, Covered California exposure, provider consolidation |
| Medicaid states — **21 states** with MCOs (KFF, as of 1-Jan-2026) | Medicaid is a major margin drag; ~44.9M total medical members | Medium-High | Capitation rate adequacy, 2025 reconciliation law, work requirements, procurement re-bids (FL, IN) |
| Medicare Advantage — national | Large; MA membership guided **down ~18%** in 2026 | High | Star Ratings litigation, risk-adjustment enforcement, CMS rate notices |
| **Puerto Rico** | Small but operationally real | Medium | Fiscal/PROMESA stress, hurricane exposure, Medicaid federal match caps |
| **India & Philippines** (captive back-office/BPO sites) | Nil revenue; cost/ops only | Low-Medium | Data privacy/offshoring vendor risk, geopolitical disruption is immaterial to earnings |

**Note on Puerto Rico:** the 10-K explicitly carves out PR alongside the US, confirming it is the only non-CONUS operating jurisdiction of consequence. Wikipedia corroborates operating sites in India, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and the US. No material European, LatAm, or Asian revenue exposure — **no Russia sanctions, no China revenue, no tariff exposure.**

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**Risk 1 — Medicare Advantage Stars & risk-adjustment enforcement (Probability: High / Impact: High)**
This is the dominant risk vector, and it has already crystallized into litigation. Elevance is suing CMS **twice**: *Elevance I* (S.D. Ga., No. 2:26-cv-00061, filed 1-Jul-2026) over CMS declining to recalculate 2026 star ratings for five MA contracts — **~$115M** in 2027 quality bonus impact per Healthcare Finance News — and *Elevance II* (D.D.C., filed 6-Nov-2026) over 2027 ratings, alleging 11 measures were not adopted through notice-and-comment; Modern Healthcare reports a **~$516M** impact. Elevance is one of **11 payers** suing over 2027 stars (Becker's). In Elevance I, both the motion to dismiss *and* the motion for preliminary injunction were **denied** (Opinion, 27-Aug-2026); cross-motions for summary judgment are fully briefed as of 6-Nov-2026. Combined, ~$630M of bonus revenue is in dispute — roughly 1.5% of annual premium, but a much larger share of MA segment margin.

**Risk 2 — Medicaid rate-setting dislocation from the 2025 reconciliation law (Probability: High / Impact: Medium-High)**
KFF (21-Sep-2026) documents that the 2025 budget reconciliation law — work requirements, more frequent eligibility redeterminations for expansion adults, provider-tax and state-directed-payment caps — is creating acute capitation-rate-setting uncertainty. Elevance management has already **exited DC's Medicaid market** and expects further exits; **Louisiana** confirmed an Elevance plan exits end-2026. Medicaid MLRs rose from 88% (2023) to 91% (2024), the highest of any insurance market in a decade. Bear case: programmatic retreat from states, forfeiting scale, amid rebid risk (Indiana re-bid $68B of contracts in 2026; Florida award cycle). Bull case: management guides to **2027 Medicaid margin recovery** as rates reset (Zacks, 16-Nov-2026).

**Risk 3 — ACA individual market / premium tax credit cliff (Probability: Medium / Impact: Medium)**
The enhanced premium tax credits **expired**, driving 2026 enrollment declines and 2027 premium spikes (KFF; Commonwealth Fund; CRS R48290). Healthier enrollees dropping coverage raises acuity and morbidity risk on the Individual book. Mitigant: Q3 2026 benefit expense ratio *improved* 70bps on better Individual ACA and MA performance — the company is currently managing this well.

**Non-risk to state explicitly:** trade policy, tariffs, currency controls, expropriation, and sanctions. Elevance is a domestic premium-collection business. Even the "geopolitical" label is largely a category error here — this is a **regulatory-state risk**, not an inter-state risk.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Active litigation (most material first):**

- **United States v. Anthem Inc.**, S.D.N.Y. No. 1:20-cv-02593 (filed 26-Mar-2020), Judge Andrew L. Carter Jr. DOJ False Claims Act suit alleging Anthem submitted unsupported/false diagnosis codes ("upcoding") for MA enrollees to obtain millions in overpayments. Anthem's motion to dismiss was **denied** (Opinion & Order, 30-Sep-2022). Case remains in **fact discovery/briefing** — 2026 docket is dense (multiple motions to compel, motions to seal, position statements filed 13-Nov-2026; conference set 17-Nov-2026). **No damages figure is public.** This is the largest unquantified contingent liability: FCA treble damages over a multi-year MA book can run into nine figures. Peer context is adverse — The Villages Health System settled MA risk-adjustment allegations for **$541.5M** (Aug-2026), and DOJ settled a separate MA plan for $22.5M (30-Sep-2026). DOJ has made MA risk adjustment its single clearest FCA priority.
- **Elevance I & II (star ratings)** as above — Elevance is *plaintiff* here, which is a favorable posture, but the outcome is binary and the money is real.
- **DOJ MA kickback complaint (May 2025)** naming Aetna, Humana and **Elevance** alongside GoHealth over alleged illegal marketing/agent compensation arrangements; company stated it "will defend itself in the legal proceedings" (Fierce Healthcare). Status not confirmed in this review — **gap**.
- **WebTPA data breach class action** (Elevance subsidiary): **$13.75M** settlement agreed Sept-2025 (ClassAction.org). Small financially, but establishes a privacy-liability precedent for the TPA/Carelon Services perimeter.
- **PBM/antitrust perimeter:** CarelonRx is a top-tier PBM. The FTC's ongoing §5 suit against Caremark, Express Scripts and OptumRx does **not** name Elevance, but the sector is under active legislative attack — the **Patients Before Monopolies Act** (banning common ownership of PBM + pharmacy) was reintroduced in 2026. This directly threatens the vertical-integration logic of combining Health Benefits and Carelon (the explicit strategic thesis in the Q3-2026 release).

**Regulatory posture:** CMS (MA/PDP), state DOIs (rate review), state Medicaid agencies (21 states), HHS-OIG/DOJ (FCA), FTC (antitrust/§5), state AGs (privacy, PBM transparency), DOL (ERISA/FEP). Elevance is a serial defendant in provider "underpayment" and out-of-network reimbursement suits — routine, individually immaterial.

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating

### **MEDIUM**

**Geopolitical risk: LOW** (~100% US + Puerto Rico revenue; no sanctions, tariff, currency or expropriation exposure; offshore presence is captive cost centers, not revenue).

**Legal / regulatory risk: MEDIUM-HIGH** — and this is what the rating reflects. Three live federal lawsuits (~$630M of MA bonus revenue in dispute across the two star-ratings cases plus an unquantified FCA exposure with a denied motion to dismiss), 21 simultaneous state Medicaid rate negotiations with confirmed market exits (DC, Louisiana), and an expired ACA subsidy regime are all unresolved within a 12-month window.

**The offsetting consideration:** management has *raised* FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to ≥$29.00 and guides ≥12% adjusted EPS growth in 2027 off a ≥$26.75 baseline (Q3-2026 release, 21-Oct-2026); the benefit expense ratio improved 70bps and Carelon operating gain rose 17%. The company is winning the *operating* argument while losing ground in the *legal/regulatory* channel. For an equity thesis, the key asymmetry is that the FCA case (US v. Anthem) is **not** in guidance and has no disclosed reserve — that is the tail.
