# Axon Enterprise (AXON) — Value Investment Analysis
**Price: $390.29 | Market cap ~$31.7B | EV ~$32.5B | Date of data: 16-Oct-2026**

---

## 1. Business Quality Assessment

**What it is:** Axon sells a vertically integrated public-safety ecosystem — TASER conducted-energy devices, body cameras, in-car/fixed video, drones/counter-drone (Dedrone), and the Axon Evidence cloud platform, increasingly layered with AI (Draft One, Brief One, Axon Assistant). Sold overwhelmingly on multi-year, bundled subscription contracts to ~18,000+ law-enforcement agencies.

**Moat rating: NARROW (and widening) — with a credible path to Wide**

Evidence for the moat:
- **Extreme switching costs.** Evidence files, chain-of-custody, prosecutor integrations and officer workflow live inside Axon Evidence. Ripping that out is an operational and legal risk no police chief wants. This shows up in **net revenue retention of 126%** (Q2'26) and **ARR of $1.3B, +35%**.
- **Contracted backlog.** 2025 bookings $7.4B (+46%); **total future contracted bookings $14.4B (+43%)** — roughly 4x current annual revenue. This is about as close to predictable revenue as industrials get.
- **Scale/brand + regulatory intangibles.** TASER is the de facto standard in US CEDs; Axon is one of only two credible integrated suppliers (with Motorola Solutions). Gross margins held at ~60% for six years through a 4x revenue increase — real pricing power.
- **Ecosystem flywheel.** Hardware lands the account; software and AI expand the wallet. 500+ customers live on Axon Assistant in year one.

Evidence *against* a Wide rating — and this is the honest part:
- **ROIC has never cleared WACC on a reported basis:** 4.7% (2022), 6.9% (2023), 1.6% (2024), **-1.2% (2025)** vs. WACC ~9.5–10.5%. Buffett's acid test — "consistently ROIC > WACC" — **fails**.
- Gross margin has slipped ~2 pts (61.5% → 59.7%) as lower-margin hardware, Dedrone and international mix grow.
- The moat is partly *manufactured* by aggressive bundling and stock-comp-funded selling, not purely by customer captivity.
- Motorola Solutions is larger (3x revenue), highly profitable, and attacks the same accounts; the hardware layer is commoditizing toward the software/AI layer where the real rent sits.

**Bottom line:** a genuinely great franchise — one of the best in Industrials — but the economic moat has not yet been converted into superior *returns on capital*. A wide moat should show up in ROIC, not just in revenue growth.
