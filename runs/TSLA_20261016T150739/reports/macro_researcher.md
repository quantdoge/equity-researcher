# Macroeconomic Assessment — Tesla, Inc. (TSLA)

## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: TRANSITIONAL, with a distinct risk-off tilt for long-duration assets.**

The US is mid-re-pricing of interest rates: the 10-year Treasury has broken decisively above 5% (5.31% on Oct 5; ~5.0–5.2% mid-October after a pullback), the effective Fed funds rate *rose* to 3.75% in September 2026 after bottoming at 3.63% in mid-2026, and FOMC dot plots imply **another hike in 2026 and no cuts in 2027** under new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh. CPI is re-accelerating (3.7% YoY in September). This is the worst possible macro setup for a $1.4T, beta-1.9 growth stock whose valuation is dominated by far-dated optionality (robotaxi, Optimus, FSD): rising discount rates compress that optionality, and rising consumer loan rates pressure the core auto demand function. The steep positive curve (2s10s +34bp) with 30Y at 5.37% signals term-premium/fiscal stress rather than recession — supportive of growth, but corrosive to long-duration equity multiples.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating

**UNFAVOURABLE**

High rates at 5%+ with a hawkish Fed tilt, re-accelerating inflation (3.7% CPI), a strengthening dollar, and tariff cliffs (US–China January 2027, EU rules-of-origin) form a hostile backdrop for the highest-beta, longest-duration name in the S&P 500. Risk-reward skews negative at the macro level, even though Q3 earnings (due imminently) and the SpaceX-merger narrative (56% odds by March '27) provide idiosyncratic upside.

## 5. One Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise (Bear)

**The term-premium spiral / "higher-for-longer reset":** Deficits + supply-driven inflation force the 10Y toward **5.5–6%**, the Fed hikes in December 2026 (bets on a hawkish 2027), and the broad dollar pushes past 124. Long-duration equity de-rates wholesale: TSLA — already at −21% YoY with compressed margins and negative FCF from the robotaxi capex cycle — could re-test the $300–310 zone (−15–20%) while Shanghai margins absorb both a stronger dollar and a January US–China tariff resumption. This is the "growth stock multiple mean-reversion" scenario and is the primary macro tail risk.
