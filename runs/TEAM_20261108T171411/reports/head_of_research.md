**Investment Thesis**

Atlassian is the highest-quality franchise in a violently de-rating software sector — 26% FY26 growth at $6.6B scale, 84.8% and expanding gross margins, cloud +31%, RPO +44%, net cash — now trading at ~5.3x FY27E sales, its cheapest multiple as a public company. The November 6 selloff (−16% on a beat) reflects a guidance reset (FY27 ~13.75% vs 24% delivered) driven by the deliberate Data Center sunset, not demand loss. The genuine open question — whether agentic AI structurally compresses per-seat revenue — is unresolved, and cash quality is poor (SBC-adjusted FCF is negative). The asymmetry favors owning a small position now and letting the Feb 4, 2027 cloud print decide sizing.

**The one finding that matters most (five desks converged independently)**: SBC of $1.607B = 24.4% of revenue; **SBC-adjusted FCF = −$288M**; operating cash flow *fell* 6.6% over two years while revenue rose 51%; DSO stretched 54.5 → 70.5 days. Atlassian's "$1.3B FCF" is less than the stock it pays employees. This is the binding constraint on conviction, not growth.

**Where agents disagree — and my resolution**: Value (HOLD, wait for $140) vs Growth (BUY at $150–160) is a timing dispute, not a thesis dispute — both accept IV ≈ $185. **Bull Case**
1. **Deceleration is mechanical, not demand**: cloud +31%, RPO +44%, subscription ARR $6.8B; as the ~$1B+ Data Center base runs off, reported growth converges back to the mid-20s cloud rate in FY28–29.
2. **AI monetization is live, not theoretical**: Rovo/Teamwork Collection customers up 7x YoY, usage-based pricing from Dec 3, 2026, GPT-6 partnership — converts AI from seat threat to consumption layer.
3. **Low bar**: 5.3x FY27E / 4.7x FY28E sales, ~25x FY27E non-GAAP EPS, with the widest EPS beat cushion in four quarters into Feb 4; margin inflection (25.5% non-GAAP op margin guided) plus ~4%/yr buyback shrinkage compounds EPS regardless.

**Bear Case**
1. **Cash quality**: SBC-adjusted FCF is −$288M; FCF margin down 12.4pp in two years; the $1.8B buyback was a dilution-recycling treadmill funded from the cash pile.
2. **AI seat compression is structural, not narrative**: if agentic tooling shrinks customer engineering headcount, the exact unit Atlassian monetizes erodes — and the FY27 guide may be the first print of that, not the last.
3. **Credibility + fragility**: two guidance-driven gap-downs in three months, a CTO/CFO turnover during a 10% RIF and dual pricing-model migration, and a CVSS 9.3 exploited in hours — execution slack is thin, and insiders only sell.

**Key Risks**
- Cloud growth <25% for two consecutive quarters (discriminates runoff from impairment)
- FY27 OCF <$1.5B, DSO >75 days, or SBC/revenue rising above 25% — confirms the cash-conversion reset is permanent
- GDPR/EU AI Act complaint on the Aug 17 default-on training change; confirmed customer breach traced to an Atlassian CVE; FedRAMP continuity loss
- Macro: a third hike and 10Y retest of 5.5%+ implies ~$120–125 even at constant fundamentals
