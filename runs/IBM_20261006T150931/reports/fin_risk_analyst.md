## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Leverage is the outlier** — Net debt/EBITDA 3.05x vs ~0.0–0.7x for direct peers; Moody's explicitly conditions the stable outlook on leverage declining from 3.2x.
2. **Liquidity ratios < 1.0** — current 0.93, quick 0.90; negative working capital (−$2.8B) means IBM funds ongoing operations partly from payables timing rather than internally.
3. **Capital allocation tension** — FY25 dividends took ~52% of FCF ($6.26B/$12.1B) and buybacks resumed in H1'26 (~$0.47B) *while* leverage is still >3x; debt reduction would be the lower-risk priority.
4. **Earnings quality volatility** — Net income swung $7.5B (23) → $6.0B (24) → $10.6B (25), with Q4'25 alone contributing $5.6B (one-off gains); trendline is positive but noisy.
