**Investment Thesis**
IBM is a cash-clean, investment-grade, 3%-yielding company whose re-acceleration narrative broke in Q2'26: revenue growth collapsed to +1.1% YoY, the Z mainframe shortfall triggered the worst one-day crash in IBM history (−25%, $68bn erased), and organic software growth is ~0% with headline growth acquisition-driven (HashiCorp ~25x revenue, Confluent). At ~18x FY26E EPS, the market still prices re-acceleration the operating evidence contradicts; the downside zone is $185–205.

**Bull Case**
- Genuine earnings quality and a hard floor: cash-backed (124% cash conversion), Piotroski 7/9, IG credit, $14.5bn cash, 3.0% dividend with 31 consecutive increases, 5.7% FCF yield — none of this is at risk.
- Real franchise assets: mainframe/hybrid-cloud lock-in with regulated enterprise workloads, Red Hat, >$12.5bn gen-AI book of business; IT services remains the primary channel for regulated-enterprise AI deployment, and FY25 margin leverage (GM 58.2%, OM 18.5%) proved operating leverage works even at mid-single-digit growth.
- Mean-reversion optionality: stock already −25% one-day / −32% off June peak; sell-side targets cluster $230–270; a clean Q3 print (Oct 21) with reaffirmed 4–5% CC guidance, or disinflation taking the 10Y back toward ~4.3% with a dollar reversal (~3–4%), would re-rate toward the high-$200s.

**Bear Case**
- Growth engine stalled: Q2'26 revenue +1.1% vs +12.2% in Q4'25; software organic flat; FY26 guidance cut to 4–5% CC; post-HashiCorp/Confluent comp cliff lands in 2027. FY25 "record" EPS flattered by one-time/non-operating gains.
- Macro double-squeeze: ~100bp 10Y backup + rising dollar compress both the multiple and reported USD revenue for a high-duration, ex-US name; ~20–25% probability (per Macro) of 10Y → 5.75–6.0% and USD → 125, implying high-$180s/low-$200s.
- Structural fragility: Z concentration (one product, −42% in a quarter), open securities probe, receivables +26% vs +7.6% revenue, buybacks suspended while debt rose $8.7bn to $67bn, goodwill 52% of assets.

There is **no independent intrinsic-value anchor, no growth build, and no regulatory-legal verification** on the open probe. This materially limits conviction; do not initiate on this basis without closing them.
