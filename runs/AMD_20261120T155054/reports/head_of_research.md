### Investment Memo: AMD — 20 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL / REDUCE | Conviction: Medium-High

---

**Investment Thesis**

AMD is an excellent business at an uninvestable price. All eleven substantive agents — including the forensic short analyst who explicitly cleared it of fraud — agree the fundamentals are elite: revenue +45%, Data Center +97%, gross margin inflecting to 54%, fortress net-cash balance sheet. But the stock prices a decade of flawless execution (168x trailing / ~75x FY26E GAAP earnings, 0.92% FCF yield) while four independent risk domains — macro (real yields ~2.9%, Fed hiking), geopolitical (Dec congressional hearing, Entity List allegations), non-financial (three securities investigations, >$320M CEO selling, zero insider buying), and valuation — all point negative with a dated, imminent catalyst calendar. This is a multiple-and-durability short, not an integrity short: the downside is bounded by a clean balance sheet but the central estimate is 15–35% lower.

---

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Semis are in a historic upcycle (WSTS 2026 market ~$1.5T, +90% YoY) with a clear peak signature — 400% DRAM inflation taxing AMD's fabless gross margin, memory tightness forecast to break in 2027. AMD is the *only* major semi that is both slower-growing (44.7% vs 64.3% peer median) and less profitable (21.2% vs 53.9% op margin) than median, yet on the highest multiple. Macro regime is the worst mix for a 2.5–3.6-beta duration asset: 10Y real yield ~2.91% (highest in a generation), Fed guiding to *more* hikes, HY OAS +89bp in 11 weeks threatening debt-financed hyperscaler capex.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH risk. ~50% of billings in exposed jurisdictions (China/HK 22.4%, Singapore 12.4%, Taiwan 15%). Hunterbrook alleges post-blacklisting firmware support to Entity-Listed IEIT Systems; new BIS licensing rules hit the Singapore line; 50%-ownership rule returns 10-Jan-27; December congressional hearing; $800M MI308 charge proves P&L transmission. 40–50% odds of formal inquiry.
- **Value**: Base-case DCF $280–305/share; *bull* case ~$420 — both below spot. Reverse DCF requires ~$70B FCF by 2036 (~10x today). 97x TTM P/E vs 27.6x peer median. Negative margin of safety.
- **Growth**: Genuine and accelerating — FY27 guide >+80%, Rule of 40 near 70, five hyperscalers in production, 7/7 beats. At 25x FY27E the growth is arguably underpriced *if* estimates land; but FY2028 consensus revenue range of $106.7B–$207.2B (2x spread) means nobody can underwrite the out-years the multiple rests on.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **No report submitted — flagged as a gap.** Alt-data proxies partially fill in: product search interest at 12-month lows (EPYC −45%, MI350 −85% YoY) while ticker searches spike — a positioning unwind, not demand collapse.
- **Financial Risk**: LOW credit risk — net cash $6.1B, A1 (upgraded), Altman Z 3.74, Piotroski 8/9, survives a −20% revenue shock with positive EBITDA. The risk is equity, not solvency: 66% realised vol, beta 2.46–3.56, max drawdown −54.7%.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Three securities-fraud investigations opened within 24 hours of the short report; CEO sold >$320M over 11 months (last tranche three sessions before the report) with zero insider buying; CEO/Chair duality; $8.2B all-stock World Labs deal closed into a drawdown.
- **ESG**: Average and deteriorating. Strong environmental disclosure, but fails typical export-control controversy screens; ~$100B of market cap lost in 48 hours on an allegation its own author concedes may not be a violation. Governance 6.5/10; compensation design weights no compliance metrics.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL with bearish skew, LOW confidence. Consensus ($17.08B) now sits *above* the Q4 guide midpoint — the bar has been reset to require a raise. December hearing is the dominant, un-modelable driver.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT on valuation/structure, *not* fraud. Beneish −2.76, DSO 98→67 days, OCF/NI 1.78x — accounting is clean. The under-modelled structural issue: the ~$85B OpenAI warrant (~10% of the company) is equity-for-revenue that must surface as contra-revenue or dilution; inventory days at 165 echo the pattern preceding the 2022–23 −66% collapse.

**Bull Case**
- Data Center +97% with five hyperscalers in production; FY27 guide >+80% looks conservative given a 100% beat rate; at 25x FY27E the market embeds only ~60% of management's 2030 path.
- Sole credible merchant second source to NVIDIA, with NVIDIA's leadership vacuum and hyperscaler de-risking actively donating share; rack-scale Helios maximises content per cabinet.
- Clean legal resolution (no BIS action, detailed firmware rebuttal) unwinds ~$100B of governance discount quickly — the asymmetry cuts both ways.

**Bear Case**
- Priced above the *bull-case* intrinsic value; every 25bp of real yield costs 6–9% of fair value, and the Fed is hiking into it.
- December hearing / BIS escalation converts a sentiment problem into a revenue problem across ~35% of billings; precedent charge was $800M in one quarter.
- AI capex financing stall (S&P flags negative hyperscaler FCF; HY OAS widening): a 2028 digestion at the low end of estimates yields ~$290–345/share; a hard landing ~$180.

**Key Risks**
1. December congressional hearing names AMD or a BIS subpoena lands (→ thesis breaks downward immediately).
2. Hyperscaler 2027 capex guided below ~$950B (from ~$1.2–1.3T).
3. Warrant accounting disclosed as a material contra-revenue charge.
4. HBM cost inflation caps gross margin below the 56% non-GAAP ceiling.
5. Inventory days cross 180 (currently 165).
6. Bull-side risk to a sell call: a forced Fed easing pivot would violently re-rate a 3.6-beta name 32% off its high.
