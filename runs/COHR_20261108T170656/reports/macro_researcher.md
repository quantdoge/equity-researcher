### 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Transitional, tilting risk-off in duration assets**

The US is in a **reflation / rates-shock regime**, not a landing or easing regime. Three forces define it:

- **The Fed is hiking again.** After cutting from 4.33% (mid-2025) to a 3.63% trough (Jun–Aug 2026), the FOMC **raised 25bp in September 2026 — the first hike in three years — to 3.75–4.00%**, held in October, and markets price **~77% odds of another 25bp in December** with **98% odds of zero cuts in 2026**.
- **Inflation is re-accelerating.** Headline CPI **3.7% y/y (Sep 2026)** vs 2.65% in Dec 2025 — a ~105bp re-acceleration. Core 2.6%, edging up. Oct CPI prints **10 Nov** — a live, imminent catalyst.
- **The long end is repricing, not the front end.** 10Y peaked at **5.31% (5 Oct)** and sits ~4.84–5.13%; 30Y at **5.47%**; 2s10s at **+39bp** — a **bear steepener** driven by term premium/fiscal supply, not growth optimism.

The striking divergence: **credit is euphoric while rates are hostile.** HY OAS is 3.48%, only ~83bp off its September cycle low of 2.65%, and real GDP growth accelerated to **~3.4% annualized in Q3 2026** with unemployment at **4.1%**. Equity risk appetite is intact; the discount rate is what's breaking.

### 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

COHR is the **purest high-beta expression of AI-datacom capex** in the market (beta **2.23**, $38.0B cap). Q1 FY27 (to 30 Sep) was excellent: revenue **$2.37B, +50% y/y (+56% pro forma)**, GAAP GM **39.1% (+254bp)**, non-GAAP EPS **$2.09 vs $1.16**, with guidance of **>60% FY27 growth** and a **$20B five-year PhotonLink agreement** ramping to $250M/quarter by end-2027.

| Macro variable | Impact | Transmission mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **10Y / 30Y yield at 5%+** | **Negative (dominant)** | COHR's value is back-end-loaded: heavy capacity capex now (InP/optical), PhotonLink cash flows 2027–2031. Each 50bp on the long end materially cuts the PV of that stream. Demonstrated: 10Y +40bp from Jun (4.44%) to Nov coincided with the stock falling **–56% from its $440 June peak** to $169.76 on 5 Nov. |
| **Fed hiking / no cuts in 2026** | **Negative** | Raises refinancing cost on merger-era debt and, more importantly, the hurdle rate for the capacity build. Interest expense already fell to **$16M/qtr** (from $62M y/y) and coverage is **7.83×** — leverage is no longer the acute risk it was, which is a genuine improvement. |
| **Growth +3.4%, unemployment 4.1%** | **Positive** | Hyperscaler and industrial demand are pro-cyclical; a no-landing economy supports capex budgets and pricing. |
| **CPI 3.7% / sticky core** | **Neutral-to-positive** | Supply-constrained InP market gives real pricing power — GM +254bp y/y shows pass-through exceeding wage/input inflation. This is an inflation *beneficiary*, unusually. |
| **USD +3.0% since September** | **Mildly negative** | Legacy Coherent ran ~76–77% foreign sales; the combined entity is majority non-US (China, Korea, Japan, Europe). Translation headwind, partly offset by SE-Asian manufacturing cost base (natural hedge). |
| **Tariff / export controls** | **Negative (tail risk)** | FY26 10-K flags trade-control exposure; China revenue and China-based customers are the vulnerable node. Midterm election outcome (3 Nov) adds policy uncertainty. |
| **CHIPS / industrial policy** | **Positive** | Company has cited CHIPS support for US capacity — a fiscal tailwind largely insulated from the rates cycle. |
| **HY OAS +83bp in 8 weeks** | **Watch item** | If credit widens further, the marginal AI-capex project gets deferred — the single fastest route from a rates problem to a demand problem. |

**The pivot point:** macro is currently hitting COHR through the *denominator* (discount rate, risk appetite), not the *numerator* (demand, margins). Fundamentals are accelerating while the stock de-rates — a classic high-duration squeeze. The Nov 4–6 tape shows the violence: $195 → $170 (–13% on a beat-and-raise) → $193 (+13.6%) in three sessions.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

*(with an explicit split: **end-demand environment = Favourable; macro-financial environment = Unfavourable**)*

The rating turns on the discount rate, not the economy. With the Fed hiking into 3.7% CPI, 30Y at 5.47%, a December hike ~77% priced, a CPI print on 10 Nov, and a stock already down 56% from its high on a **2.23 beta**, COHR offers no macro cushion. Its operating momentum is the best in its history; its macro-financial backdrop is the worst in three years. That combination caps the multiple regardless of the revenue line, and leaves the shares hostage to every long-end data point.

---

### 5. The Surprise Scenario (Bear)

**"The December hike plus a 3.9% October CPI takes the 10Y through 5.50%."**

On 10 Nov, October CPI (consensus-sensitive; September came in 3.7% vs 3.6% expected and pushed the 10Y +9bp to 5.30%). If it prints ≥3.9%, the Fed's December hike becomes near-certain and the long end breaks to new cycle highs on term premium. In that world, every high-multiple, cash-flow-deferred AI infrastructure name is marked down simultaneously — and COHR, with the highest beta in photonics and a build-out whose payoff sits in FY2028–31, is the marginal seller's first stop. A re-test of the **$160s (5 Nov low $160.23)** and potentially the $130s (Nov 2025 levels) is achievable on macro alone, with zero change to the PhotonLink thesis.

**The mirror-image bull case is equally violent:** if October CPI lands at 3.4–3.5% and the Fed signals the hiking cycle is complete, the 10Y retreats toward 4.30%, and a company guiding >60% revenue growth re-rates hard. Given the demonstrated 13.6% single-day snap-back, the asymmetry here is real — but the *macro* is currently the adversary, not the ally.
