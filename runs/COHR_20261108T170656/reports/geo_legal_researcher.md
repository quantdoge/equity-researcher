### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk #1 — China's indium phosphide (InP) export-control regime (HIGHEST SEVERITY)**
- China imposed InP/indium-related export restrictions in **February 2025**; 6-inch InP wafer prices have since risen **~250% to ~$5,000**, with licensing delays acting as a rolling bottleneck on AI datacenter optical buildouts (Reuters, 2026-06-11; Mexico Business News, 2026-06-12).
- **Coherent is named directly.** It warned of an InP shortage on its early-May earnings call; CEO Jim Anderson then joined a US business delegation to China specifically to raise **export-license delays**, and the issue reached US–China trade negotiator level ahead of the May 14–15 Trump–Xi summit.
- AXT (a major Coherent supplier) stated "InP export permits represent the most significant challenge we currently face." AXT and Sumitomo control ~80% of global InP substrate manufacturing; China produces ~70% of global indium.
- **Probability of recurrence/persistence: ~85%.** **Impact: High** — directly gates the highest-growth Datacenter & Communications segment propped up by the Nvidia relationship (Nvidia made a reported **$2B investment** in Coherent in March 2026).
- *Investor framing:* this is a **volume/execution risk, not a demand risk** — the most important thing to monitor each quarter is InP license cadence and wafer pricing, not order books.

**Risk #2 — US–China tariff and export-control whiplash (BIDIRECTIONAL)**
- Coherent has an **established history** of tariff damage: it previously cut costs to offset "ongoing negative impact of the US–China trade war and associated tariffs" (optics.org).
- Currently operating under a partial détente — China–US trade officials held a video call in Nov 2026, and effective average US tariff rates had fallen to ~6.7% by July 2026 after a Supreme Court ruling in February 2026 (Wharton Penn Wharton Budget Model, 2026-09-09). That is a *tailwind*, but an explicitly reversible one.
- **Probability of renewed escalation within 12 months: ~40%.** **Impact: Medium-High** (margin compression on China-sourced components and China-destined sales).
- Note also China's expanding **Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law / trade-secret toolkit**, which creates extraterritorial risk for a company with dual-use photonics IP.

**Risk #3 — Export-control and dual-use technology scrutiny on photonics/AI hardware**
- Coherent sits precisely at the intersection of AI infrastructure and defense photonics. It **divested its Aerospace & Defense business to Advent for $400M** (announced 2025-08-13; expected close Q3 CY2025, advised by A&O Shearman), which reduces — but does not eliminate — defense-end-market export licensing exposure.
- Sector-wide, US export controls on advanced AI hardware remain the key policy lever; peer chipmakers have already excluded China from forward guidance, a path Coherent may be pushed toward.
- **Probability: ~35%** of a new license requirement or entity-list-adjacent disruption. **Impact: Medium.**

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Securities litigation — active but low materiality (in my assessment)**
- A **securities class action** has been filed, class period **Sept 29, 2020 – Sept 29, 2025** (Levi & Korsinsky / ZLK). The pleaded theory is notably *weak*: the complaint alleges Coherent's Aug 13, 2025 press release announcing the A&D sale (stating the deal was "expected to close in Q3 CY2025 subject to customary closing conditions") was misleading, after the stock fell 19% on Aug 14, 2025.
- Multiple firms (Rosen, Pomerantz, Bronstein Gewirtz & Grossman, Shamis & Gentile) have issued **investigation notices**, which are typically solicitor-driven and not indicative of merit.
- **Assessment:** these notices appear triggered by stock-price declines and a deal-timing disappointment, not by an accounting restatement or restatement-adjacent disclosure. I find **no evidence of an SEC enforcement action, DOJ inquiry, FCPA matter, or restatement** against Coherent in retrieved materials. Expect nuisance-level defense costs; reserve probability of material (>$100M) outcome as **low, ~10–15%**.
- *Caveat:* I could not retrieve the FY2026 10-K legal-proceedings note, so an unlisted contingency cannot be fully excluded.

**Regulatory / antitrust**
- The A&D divestiture required customary regulatory clearance (CFIUS-adjacent considerations given defense photonics); counsel (Covington, Weil, A&O Shearman) described it as a standard conditions package. No disclosed impediment.

**Antitrust / market position**
- Coherent is a top-tier supplier in InP photonics alongside Lumentum; both are sold out, and Nvidia capital is flowing into both. Consolidation + scarcity invites **pricing scrutiny**, though no investigation is disclosed.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM-HIGH**

**Rationale:** Legal/litigation risk is genuinely **low** (solicitor-driven class action with a thin theory, no government investigation, defense business divested). The rating is driven almost entirely by **supply-chain geopolitics**: Coherent is a named, board-level participant in the China InP export-control dispute, with a manufacturing base materially located in China and a growth engine (AI datacenter optics) that is precisely the target of Beijing's "materials chokepoint" strategy. Geographic diversification (US, Germany, Malaysia, Finland) and the Nvidia capital injection mitigate, but do not offset, the single-jurisdiction InP chokepoint.

**What would change the rating:**
- *Downgrade to HIGH:* a formal InP allocation cut to Coherent, entity-list action, or renewed 2025-style tariff escalation.
- *Upgrade to MEDIUM:* durable China licensing normalization, or evidence Coherent has qualified non-China InP substrate supply (Sumitomo/JX) at scale.

**Monitoring checklist (next 2 quarters):** (1) InP export-license cadence and 6-inch wafer spot pricing; (2) Datacenter & Communications segment gross margin — the cleanest read-through on input cost pass-through; (3) any 10-K legal-proceedings disclosure on the class action; (4) US–China trade posture post-Nov 2026 talks; (5) China trade-secret rules implementation.
