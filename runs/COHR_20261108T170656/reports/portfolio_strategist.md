## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.5% initial → 3.0% hard maximum**

### Framework application
The framework's bands put this at "medium conviction or elevated risk → 1–3%," and the "multiple risk flags → 0.5–1% or pass" clause is live (five distinct flags: negative FCF, thin liquidity runway, 15–26% dilution, InP chokepoint, 95% vol). I land at the **top of the 1–3% band for the starter**, justified by genuinely high conviction on the fundamental leg — but with a **hard 3% cap**.

### Volatility-adjusted risk budget (the binding constraint)
| | Value |
|---|---|
| Position (initial) | 1.5% of portfolio |
| COHR annualised vol | 95.5% |
| **Vol contribution to portfolio** | **≈1.43%** |
| Equivalent size in a 30%-vol stock | **≈4.8%** |

**A 1.5% COHR position is already a full-size risk position.** A 3% holding contributes ~2.87% of portfolio volatility from a single name — roughly 6x the risk budget of a normal 5% core holding. This, not the notional percentage, is why I cap at 3%.

### Kelly criterion (honest answer: it argues for smaller)
Probability-weighted on my 3-scenario distribution:
- **Bull (45%)** — FY28 EPS ~$15 at 20x = **$300 (+56%)**
- **Base (35%)** — FCF inflects late, multiple stays compressed = **$230 (+19%)**
- **Bear (20%)** — cycle turns, writedowns, forced dilution = **$100–130 (−35% to −48%)**

Expected return **≈+23%**; win/loss odds ratio b≈1.24; p=0.45 → **Full Kelly f ≈ 0.7%**, half-Kelly ≈ 0.3%.

Pure Kelly says this is a **sub-1% position**. I am overriding it upward to 1.5% for one reason: Kelly is maximally punitive on fat left tails and is highly sensitive to my probability inputs, which carry large error bars. I am *not* overriding the direction of the answer — Kelly's message is that **this must be a small position, and 1.5% already exceeds full Kelly.** That is the correct lens, and it is why I refuse 4–5% regardless of the 2x analyst-target upside.

**Net fit verdict: POOR as a standalone add; ACCEPTABLE only as a small, correlation-budgeted, counter-trend tactical position that displaces — not supplements — other optical exposure.**
