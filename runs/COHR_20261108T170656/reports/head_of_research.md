### Investment Memo: COHR — November 8, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY | Conviction: **Medium**

**Investment Thesis**

Coherent is the vertically integrated optical engine of the AI datacenter — owning the scarce InP laser supply that gates the 800G→1.6T transition — trading at 19.7x FY27E / 12.8x FY28E after a 56% de-rating, with contracted demand ($20B PhotonLink LTA, NVIDIA $2B stake + supply agreement) and accelerating growth (+50% LQ, >60% guided FY27). But the income statement and the cash flow statement are telling opposite stories: three independent forensic reviews converged on a ~$710–725M accrual gap, negative and accelerating FCF, and 212 inventory days. This is a buy the *earnings* and *size for the cash* situation — a staged, small, counter-trend position, not a core holding.

**Bull Case**
- Cheapest forward exposure to contracted AI-optics demand: 12.8x FY28E with a $20B LTA, NVIDIA equity validation, and a 100% beat rate; FY27 estimates look structurally low (guide implies ~$3.2B/qtr H2 run-rate).
- Margin inflection is real: operating margin 2.6% → 15.9% in two years, 37.5% gross at peer median with vertical integration protecting it; FCF inflects as capex normalizes into FY28.
- The de-rating is done or nearly so: −56% from peak, forward multiple below Lumentum's, capitulation-reversal volume signature, and COHR is now the last independent Western laser maker (strategic scarcity/target optionality post Marvell–Lumentum).

**Bear Case**
- Cash conversion is broken: ~$1.9B FCF consumed over five quarters, funded by customer equity and new debt simultaneously; the company structurally cannot self-fund the ramp — terminal multiple capped regardless of EPS.
- 212 inventory days (+72 YoY) into an 800G→1.6T node transition is a writedown wager; one hyperscaler capex-pause quarter hits estimates and multiple together on a 2.2–3.5 beta.
- Macro is the active adversary: December hike + a hot CPI takes the 10Y through 5.50% and retests $160s/$130s with zero change to fundamentals — demonstrated once already this quarter.

**Key Risks**
1. FCF fails to inflect by FQ2–FQ3'27 → forced dilution/leverage spiral and credit downgrade path (net debt $5.5–6.5B at current burn).
2. China InP export-license disruption — volumes, not demand, are what get cut.
3. Inventory obsolescence on node transition; DC&C gross-margin compression as the tell.
4. Macro: long-end rates vs. a cash-flow-deferred asset; CPI Nov 10 and December FOMC.
5. Dilution (+14.5–30% share count in 6 quarters) continuing at depressed prices.
6. Data-quality caveats: corrupted annual PPE feed, missing Value/Non-Fin reports, ambiguous beta (2.23–3.47).
