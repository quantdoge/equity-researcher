### Investment Memo: ADBE — October 18, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $330 (~35% upside from current $245.20)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months, tranche entry

**Investment Thesis**
Adobe is a 89%-gross-margin, ~48%-ROIC franchise trading at ~10x forward earnings and a ~10% FCF yield because the market has priced it as an AI casualty. The forensic evidence rejects that framing: accounting is clean, cash conversion is 1.41x, the balance sheet is effectively net-debt-free, revenue is *re-accelerating* (+13% Q3 FY26), and the buyback retires ~10% of shares annually — meaning per-share value compounds even with zero growth. The bear case (seat-model disruption) is legitimate but is a 2027–2030 terminal-value question already more than reflected in a multiple that has compressed ~60%; the burden of proof has shifted to the bears. Conviction is capped at Medium by genuinely weak governance, a hostile real-rate path, and deteriorating near-term demand signals.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro is *unfavourable* — 10Y real yield 2.78% (+87bp in 5 months), ~50% October hike odds; this pressures the multiple, not the business (net cash, negligible refinancing risk). Sector is in an AI-driven de-rating with extreme dispersion: ADBE is #1 on gross margin (89.1%) and operating margin (35.7%) among peers, #2-last on growth (12.5% vs ~17.5% median), and cheapest at 3.6x sales — priced for stagnation.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium risk, low materiality. DOJ/ROSCA settled ($150M + injunction on cancellation flows); **UK CMA probe into the same conduct is live**; EU AI Act obligations now apply to 27% of revenue. Worst case is a low-single-digit EPS drag — an irritant, not thesis-defining.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Financial quality 9/10; FCF conversion 138%; blended intrinsic value ~**$392** (25/45/30 weighting of $605/$385/$225 scenarios) — a ~37% discount. Price implies 0–1% perpetual FCF growth. Moat rated **Narrow (contested)**, down from Wide — the key honest uncertainty.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: Rule of 40 = **52** at 10.0x forward P/E; FY26 guide raised twice; AI-first ARR +150% to >$650M (but only ~2.4% of base). Fair value $300–330. Deceleration to ~9% by FY27 and negative long-dated revisions cap this at a value-plus-optionality call, not a compounder call.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Value #1/6 and Quality #1/6** simultaneously in the software comp set — a rare double. But momentum middling (+11.8% vs CRM +46.7%), Sharpe −0.29, MDD −42%, revisions −6.5% (thin 6-analyst sample). R² vs SPY of 0.0075 means returns are essentially pure idiosyncratic risk.
- **Financial Risk**: Split rating. Credit **LOW** (0.01x net leverage, 33x coverage, A+, Z-score 4.35); equity **HIGH** (37–44% vol, −67% 3y max drawdown). Watch: FY25 buybacks ($11.28B) exceeded FCF ($9.85B); goodwill/intangibles of $13.35B = 115% of book equity is a Digital Experience impairment risk.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH** — the binding constraint. Say-on-pay passed at just **50.65%**; CEO *and* CFO seats simultaneously in transition (Narayen → Chakravarthy Dec 1, interim CFO); Narayen retains Board Chair; ~$45M of insider selling with **zero insider buying** at multi-year lows; AI training-data class actions active.
- **ESG**: Average. E a sector leader (SBTi net-zero); S damaged by the regulator-confirmed consumer-protection failure (the injunction permanently removes friction-based retention — model consumer net adds conservatively); G weak in practice. Firefly's commercially-safe training data and C2PA provenance work is an under-appreciated ESG *asset* supporting pricing power.
- **Alternative Data**: **Bearish near-term.** Creative Cloud/Photoshop search interest −21% to −25% sequentially with a counter-seasonal failure to recover into fall; Canva captured the recovery (+61% off July low) while Firefly mindshare fell −62% from peak; EPS beat magnitude compressing (3.24% → 2.41% → 0.82%). Revenue/ARR quality tracking below consensus into the Dec 9 print; EPS roughly in-line.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Short thesis rejected at $245.** M-Score −2.85, negative accruals four years running — one of the cleanest large-cap profiles reviewed; "anyone shorting ADBE on an accounting thesis is shorting the wrong thing." The short was right at $440–500; it has been paid. Downside from here requires an actual *revenue decline*, not deceleration. Notable forensic flags: ARR growth (10.9%) now trails revenue growth (12.0%), and five quarters of ~10.5% growth *through price increases* implies weak underlying unit growth.

**Bull Case**
- Valuation dislocation: ~10x FY26E EPS / ~10.6x FCF for 89% gross margins, 41% FCF margins, 47.7% ROIC — even the slow-erosion DCF scenario is worth ~$385.
- Self-correcting mechanism: a ~10%-of-market-cap annual buyback at ~10x cash flow compounds per-share value at low-double-digit rates with *no* growth and *no* re-rating required.
- AI optionality is free: AI-first ARR +150% YoY, NVIDIA partnership, Firefly's commercially-safe moat, and Q3's +13% re-acceleration directly refute "AI is already eating Adobe."

**Bear Case**
- Seat-model disruption: AI collapses the prosumer/casual creation tier (Canva, Figma, OpenAI); alt data shows Adobe losing the demand-funnel recovery to Canva *now*, and per-seat pricing is structurally mismatched with AI-driven seat compression.
- Quality-of-growth deterioration: ARR < revenue growth, price increases masking unit weakness, beat magnitude decaying, FY27 consensus already below FY26 — the leading indicators all point the wrong way.
- Governance + macro compound: a dual C-suite vacuum with a 50.65% say-on-pay vote executes the AI pivot while real yields rise 87bp-per-five-months; two hikes + 3% real yield puts the $195 June low back in play.

**Key Risks**
1. Sustained ARR deceleration below 8% or gross margin below 88% — the two hardest thesis breaks (currently 10.2% guided / 89.3%).
2. Real-rate shock: Fed hikes Oct + Dec → 10Y real >3% → $195 retest, $170s below.
3. Digital Experience impairment of the $13.35B goodwill/intangible stack.
4. Capital allocation: debt-funded buybacks at poor prices (~$5B destroyed buying at ~$400 avg in FY25), or an outsider CEO overpaying for an AI asset (Figma 2.0).
5. UK CMA adverse finding; AI copyright litigation escalation; negative estimate revisions broadening.

Hard stop $188 (max loss 0.46% of NAV at target size). Exit regardless of price on: deferred revenue growth < revenue growth, ARR <8%, GM <88%, DX impairment, or a second senior finance departure. Trim one-third at $350, another at $390–400. ADBE correlates 0.72–0.77 with CRM/NOW/INTU — it counts against the **software-cluster bucket, not as a diversifier**; cut sizing 25% if the book already holds SaaS peers. **Do not beta-hedge** (1y beta 0.29 vs vendor 1.42, R² 0.0075 — the hedge won't track); size on 44% total vol. Cheap and improving are different things — ADBE is the former and not yet the latter; own it small, get paid to wait via the buyback, and let the ARR line dictate whether this becomes 3% or an exit.
