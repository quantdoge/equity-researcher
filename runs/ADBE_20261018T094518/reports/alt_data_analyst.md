**Caveats:** The Trends window returned only ~52 weeks, so a true year-over-year comparison is unavailable — the −21% to −25% figures are sequential, not YoY. Keyword indices are individually scaled, so cross-keyword *levels* aren't strictly comparable (directions are). "Adobe Creative Cloud" also likely loses volume to "Adobe," "Firefly," and Adobe Express / "Adobe Express" queries, and Adobe's top-15 ranking in Semrush's 2026 AI Visibility Index suggests some of this is **AI-answer absorption of clicks rather than pure demand destruction**. Management reports freemium users >100M and Firefly ARR +40% QoQ, which supports a partial channel-shift read. This is the single biggest ambiguity in the dataset.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:** Three of the six core business-activity feeds failed (SimilarWeb traffic, LinkedIn job postings, USPTO patents) — the entire hiring/R&D-investment dimension is unmeasured. The Google Trends pull returned only ~52 weeks, so the −21%/−25% declines are **sequential, not year-over-year**, and are contaminated by July seasonality; the sequential framing partially overstates the deterioration. Keyword-indices are relative and cross-keyword levels aren't strictly comparable.

**Why not Low:** The Trends signal is directionally consistent across three independent keywords and survives seasonal adjustment in the key respect (Aug–Oct should recover; it didn't). Earnings-surprise history and consensus/guidance figures are hard data. Price-reaction data is clean and unambiguous.

**Known data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is a 1–3 month leading proxy at best and is being structurally distorted economy-wide by AI-answer absorption of search — a genuine confound here. SimilarWeb/Thinknum/Reddit-API integrations would materially upgrade this read. Alt data is a leading indicator, **not** a definitive one; nothing here resolves whether Adobe's AI-first ARR is truly offsetting core cannibalization — only that the legacy funnel is softening now.
