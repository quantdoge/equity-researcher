### 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **LOW**

- **Targets (company-disclosed):** 100% renewable electricity globally; **net zero by FY2050**; **42% absolute Scope 1+2 reduction by FY2030 vs FY2022**; earlier SBTi-validated targets of −35% Scope 1+2 and −30% business travel vs FY2018. SBTi validation confirmed via KnowESG coverage metadata (2023).
- **Exposure:** asset-light software; emissions concentrated in purchased cloud/compute (Scope 3) and business travel. No manufacturing, no spills, no remediation liabilities, no carbon-tax-exposed heavy assets. **No environmental fines or enforcement actions found.**
- **Watch item:** generative-AI inference load (Firefly, agentic workflows, expanded NVIDIA collaboration announced Oct-2026) is the only credible upward pressure on energy intensity. Adobe does **not** disclose CDP Climate (KnowESG: "Not disclosed") and publishes **no absolute Scope 3 figure** in what I could verify — that is the main disclosure gap and a growing investor question given AI compute.
- **EU CSRD** will eventually force Scope 3 disclosure regardless; low financial risk, moderate disclosure risk.

### 3. Social Risk Assessment: **MEDIUM** (escalated from Low by a regulator-confirmed consumer-protection failure)

**Negative — product/consumer protection (the material one).** In June 2024 the DOJ (on FTC referral) sued Adobe **and two named executives**, alleging hidden Early Termination Fees on "Annual, Paid Monthly" plans and a cancellation process designed to obstruct. On **13 March 2026** Adobe settled: **$75M cash to the DOJ + $75M in free services = $150M**, plus an injunction on disclosure and cancellation practices. Adobe "denies any wrongdoing." Two things matter more than the cash:
1. **Named-executive liability** is unusual and signals a governance/oversight failure, not a clerical one.
2. **The injunction removes a revenue lever.** Friction-based retention in consumer Creative Cloud is now legally constrained — a structural, not one-off, margin headwind of low-single-digit % on consumer subscription revenue.

**Negative — DEI retrenchment.** April 2025: Adobe's CPO told staff it was discontinuing "Aspirational Goals" diversity hiring targets, joining Google and Meta. Reverses a prior public target (30% female leadership by 2025). Reputationally negative with talent and the creative community; **not** a near-term cash-flow item.

**Negative — creator trust / ToS.** Adobe's 2024 terms-of-service update triggered a creator backlash over fears of AI training on customer work, forcing a clarifying rewrite. **[unverified — could not confirm via tool; widely reported].** Directly relevant because Firefly's "commercially safe" claim is a competitive moat against OpenAI/Canva/Midjourney.

**Positive.** Adobe-founded **Content Authenticity Initiative / C2PA Content Credentials** is genuine, industry-leading AI-transparency work (TikTok and others joined) — a rare case of a company pre-empting its own regulatory risk. Workforce safety risk negligible (office-based).

### 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEUTRAL** (mildly negative tilt)

**Not an ESG-driven thesis.** The ESG overlay neither creates nor destroys the investment case here; the case turns on **AI cannibalisation of Creative Cloud** (stock −32% YTD 2026, −17% from the late-August $294 high, despite Q1 FY26 revenue of $6.40B beating guidance).

**Three ESG factors that do transmit to cash flow:**
1. **The DOJ injunction is a permanent, if small, revenue-quality haircut** — friction-based retention is now legally off the table. Model consumer Creative Cloud net adds slightly more conservatively. Cash cost already taken ($150M).
2. **Governance is the live risk, not the settled one.** A Chair/CEO running his own succession search with no named internal candidate and no timeline is a genuine value-destroying risk in a moment requiring decisive strategic repositioning. Watch for: internal vs external hire, the new severance policy's cost, and whether Narayen retains the Chair.
3. **Firefly's "commercially safe" licensing position is an under-appreciated ESG asset** — it is the one AI-governance credential that supports pricing power against OpenAI/Canva. Continued C2PA leadership protects it; a second ToS misstep would not.

**Risks that would change the call to Negative:** any follow-on state-AG or class action beyond the $150M; EU AI Act enforcement tied to provenance/provenance-labeling failures; Scope 3 disclosure revealing materially worse AI-compute intensity; a contested or externally imposed CEO appointment that splits the board.

**What would not move the call:** DEI target reversal (no cash-flow link), CBAM, physical climate, or carbon pricing.
