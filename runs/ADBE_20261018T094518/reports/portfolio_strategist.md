## 2. Consensus Assessment: **MEDIUM (fundamentals) / SPLIT (timing)**

There is unusually strong agreement on *what* Adobe is: **four independent agents (Value, Quant, Short, Fin Risk) converge on "statistically cheap, genuinely high-quality, clean accounting."** The short-seller — whose job is to find fraud — explicitly rejected the short at this price. That is the highest-value signal in the file and it removes the one category of risk that would override a valuation call.

There is equally strong agreement on *near-term direction*: Alt Data, Quant (revisions −6.5%), Short (ARR 10.9% < revenue 12.0%), and Growth (alt-data) all say the leading indicators are deteriorating **now**.

These are not contradictory. **The market is pricing the deterioration, and more than pricing it.** Where agents genuinely split is the one question that determines terminal value: *is AI compressing Adobe's seat economics permanently?* Sector says eroding; Short says a 2027–2030 problem, not a 2026 one; Value says honestly unresolved. Record **89.3% gross margin** is the strongest single fact against the bear case — if pricing power were cracking, it would show there first.

**Conflict resolution applied:** Non-Fin's HIGH governance rating is the binding constraint, not the valuation. I discount the bull case for a 50.65% say-on-pay vote, a dual C-suite vacuum, and active IP litigation — none of which the three bull agents weight at all. That discount is what caps sizing below what the value math alone would support.
