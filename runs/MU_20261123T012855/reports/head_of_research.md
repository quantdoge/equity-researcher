**Investment Thesis**
Micron is a fortress-balance-sheet cyclical at the most extreme earnings peak in its history (FY26 revenue +256% to $133.2B, 86.8% Q4 gross margin), trading at 8x peak EPS after a −51% drawdown driven almost entirely by a +70bp move in real yields, not by deteriorating fundamentals — credit spreads are calm and >75% of FY27 output is contracted at fixed prices. The entire debate is FY28 normalized earnings power: at $594, the market capitalizes roughly $40 of sustainable EPS, which is either a bargain (if the HBM oligopoly dampens the cycle) or 30–40% too high (if memory mean-reverts as it always has). The asymmetry is modestly positive (+10% probability-weighted), but the risk vectors are correlated, not diversifying — so the position must be small.

**Bull Case**
1. **The bust is priced; the contract book says it hasn't arrived.** >75% of FY27 shipments committed at fixed prices, 7/7 beats, HBM supply agreements signed at higher prices — at $594 the market implies ~$40 sustainable EPS, ~70% below the Q4 run-rate. The 23-Dec print is a 70%-probability positive catalyst.
2. **This cycle is structurally better than prior ones.** Three-player DRAM oligopoly, HBM physically cannibalizing conventional supply (23% of wafers 2026 → 30% 2027), HBM TAM $35B→$100B by 2028E, and $38B net cash to fund any downturn without dilution — unlike FY23.
3. **Rate-driven re-rating optionality.** If December is the last hike and real yields retrace ~60bp, the observed drawdown relationship implies 40–60% multiple expansion *without* any improvement in AI sentiment. Breakevens at 2.16% say inflation expectations are anchored.

**Bear Case**
1. **Peak margins meet a depreciation cliff.** Gross margin is asymptoting at ~87%; capex/D&A of 3.2x means $13–18B of annual D&A headwind lands by FY29, precisely as 2028 industry capacity arrives and the FY27 book rolls off. Normalized EPS of $15–40 makes today's price 15–40x reality.
2. **Correlated risk stack, all hitting the same variable.** Antitrust treble-damages tail + BIS export rules on the AI demand that is >60% of revenue + Taiwan strike/fab suspension — three "independent" risks that all transmit through delivery assurance and pricing.
3. **Insiders and the tape disagree with the bull case.** ~$182M of CEO selling bracketing the top, zero insider buys at "record value" prices, management retaining 97% of an $85B profit rather than buying stock at 4x forward — plus a −13.9% down-day on 2.4x volume and a historical prior of a further 42–68% decline in the year after 3 of 5 prior 40%+ drawdowns.
