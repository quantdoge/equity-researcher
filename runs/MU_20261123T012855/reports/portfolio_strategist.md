**SPLIT — and this is the whole investment question:**

| | Growth / Quant | Value / Macro / Sector | Resolution |

**My own test of the estimates — this resolves the split.** Consensus FY28 revenue $325.7B with net income $227.8B = **70.0% net margin**; FY29 $333.3B / $217.5B = **65.3%**. No memory company in history has earned a 65–70% net margin through a cycle. **The "3.4x FY27 / 2.8x FY28" multiple is not a valuation signal — it is consensus assuming margins never normalize.** Growth's own exit rule (first negative contract print) is an implicit concession to this.

**Conflict resolution applied:** where Value (bearish on price) meets Short Analyst (no fraud) and Fin Risk (fortress credit), the fraud/credit risk *does not* override the valuation concern — they're orthogonal. But Nonfin Risk's **surviving antitrust class action** does override Growth's bullishness, because it is an unquantified treble-damages tail that cannot be diversified within the position and is *correlated* with the "RAM price gouging" narrative that record margins create. It caps size; it does not kill the trade.
