# COST (Costco Wholesale) — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment

**Scope:** Geographic revenue map, geopolitical risks, litigation/regulatory exposure, compliance flags. Data reflects FY2025–FY2026 filings, company releases, court records and press reporting as of the research date.

## 1. Geographic Risk Map

**Structural note:** Costco's China exposure is two-sided — a small growing retail footprint *and* a very large import base (Chinese-sourced merchandise is a key cost input), which is what drags it into tariff litigation.

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

1. **US-China tariff escalation (import-cost shock).** *Probability: High. Impact: Medium-High.* Costco sued the Trump administration seeking a "full refund" of Section 232 and Section 301 duties (NBC News, BBC) and has already recovered ~$184M in refunds after courts ruled companies are entitled to refunds on unlawfully imposed tariffs (Yahoo Finance). It simultaneously faces customer class actions (e.g., Stockov v. Costco) seeking a share of up to ~$170B in aggregate refunds (Reuters, 3/2026) on claims costco "illegally" raised prices. New tariff rounds would hit gross margins and pricing optics directly.

2. **Taiwan Strait conflict / PRC retaliation.** *Probability: Low. Impact: High.* 14 Taiwan warehouses (~2% of sales) sit in the highest-risk geopolitical theater; Taiwan income is deliberately quarantined as a non-controlling interest, signaling Costco itself treats it as contingent. A blockade or conflict would also sever regional logistics (Taiwan/China/Southeast Asia sourcing) well beyond the retail P&L.

3. **North America trade renegotiation (US- Mexico-Canada).** *Probability: Medium. Impact: Medium.* Canada (~13% of sales) and Mexico (~7%) make ~20% of revenue hostage to USMCA 2026 renegotiation, steel/aluminum tariff spillovers, and counter-tariffs (Costco's October sales comps already show Canada growth lagging the US — Globe and Mail). Peso/CAD currency swings compound margin pressure on imported goods.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **Tariff refund litigation (both sides):** Plaintiff against the administration for 232/301 refunds (received $184M; case tied to SCOTUS review — BBC) **and** defendant in consumer "share of refunds" class actions (Reuters).
- **DOJ / state antitrust probes — food pricing:** DOJ opened an egg-price antitrust probe (Bloomberg, 3/2025); three major egg suppliers were subsequently charged and settled with DOJ + 17 states (2026); the beef-price investigation has been **expanded to Costco, Walmart and Amazon** (reported). Retailer-side exposure remains under investigation.
- **Employment litigation:** $8M settlement of California sex-bias class action (Law360); $200K jury award in disability discrimination case; active NLRB unfair-labor charge (Case 07-CA-383757); $868K worker-misclassification penalty (delivery affiliates).
- **Product liability / food safety:** Salmonella class actions tied to Nebraska processing plant (Yahoo Finance); PFAS "baby wipes" class action allowed to proceed on deceptive-marketing claims; LA-class Protein powder heavy-metals (Prop 65) suit; ~1M-bottle Prosecco recall on laceration risk (CPSC). These are recurring, quantifiable but manageable exposures for a staple-goods retailer.
- **Privacy:** Suit alleging Costco's pharmacy site leaked consumers' medical data to Meta via tracking pixels — a growing state-privacy-law (and potential HIPAA-adjacent) exposure class.
- **Political/regulatory pressure:** Multistate Republican AG letter (led by Texas) challenging Costco's DEI policies; shareholders rejected the anti-DEI proposal 98%–2% (NPR), but political pressure is ongoing.

---

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

- **Antitrust scrutiny of food pricing:** DOJ/state probes into egg and beef pricing name retailers; Costco's size makes it a litigation magnet regardless of merit.
- **Health-data privacy:** pharmacy tracking-pixel claims could trigger state AG/consumer-privacy actions and reputational damage.
- **Prop 65 / consumer-protection class actions:** recurring California exposure on heavy metals, PFAS, and promotional claims.
- **Customs valuation & tariff compliance:** active disputes over 232/301 duties invite CBP scrutiny of its import declarations.
- **FCPA/AML/sanctions:** No Russia/Iran/North Korea/Cuba operations and no known FCPA enforcement — genuinely low exposure here; China and Mexico operations are the jurisdictions to watch for corruption/AML risk, but no public allegations found.
- **Foreign-exchange/transfer controls:** repatriation of China and Mexico earnings is subject to local capital controls — immaterial today given scale.

---

## 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM** (tilt toward Medium-High)

**Rationale:** Rule-of-law jurisdictions anchor ~95% of revenue, there are no expropriation or sanctions exposures, and litigation is routine for a consumer-staples giant. Offsetting factors: (i) the tariff fight makes Costco simultaneously plaintiff and target in venues that could shift materially with the Supreme Court outcome; (ii) ~20% of revenue sits in USMCA renegotiation crossfire; (iii) active federal antitrust probes into retail food pricing; and (iv) a small but strategically growing China footprint plus 14 Taiwan warehouses in the world's highest-risk flashpoint. None of these is existential, but the combination — plus dense consumer, employment and privacy litigation — justifies a Medium rating with an upward tilt if tariff adjudication or North America trade talks turn adverse.
