### Investment Memo: COST (Costco Wholesale) — October 6, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (accumulate on weakness; starter for new money) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $1,030 (~12% upside from current $920.65)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Costco is an elite, wide-moat compounder — the only discount-retail comp-set name growing revenue double-digit (+10.1% FY26) with five straight years of operating-margin expansion, a ~92% membership-renewal annuity, ~26% ROIC, and a net-cash balance sheet. The debate is purely price: at 44× trailing EPS with a ~5% 10-year discount rate and negative 12-month momentum, the market pays full freight for flawless execution. Own the franchise; respect the entry.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Neutral sector, operationally favourable regime — sticky ~3% CPI and trading-down favour the club format; but a 10Y at 4.99% (5.24% intraday) is the swing risk, and the ~5% long end already drove a ~14% derating from the Feb-2025 peak. COST is the sector's best-positioned asset within a mature, low-margin industry (WMT steady, TGT in decline).
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium (tilt Medium-High). ~95% of revenue in rule-of-law markets, but Costco is simultaneously plaintiff (suing for 232/301 duty refunds, ~$184M recovered) and defendant (consumer share-of-refund class actions); ~20% of sales sit in USMCA crossfire; DOJ/state antitrust probes into retail food pricing; 14 Taiwan warehouses (non-controlling JV); China is a small sales base but a large import-cost source.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD. Wonderful business, unwonderful price: DCF intrinsic ~$666 (9% WACC) vs $920; 44.4× P/E, 43.6× P/FCF, 2.3% FCF yield, zero margin of safety. Financial quality 9/10; net cash ~$11.6B; buyable zone low-$30s P/E (~$625–725).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY. ~10% revenue CAGR, 92.2% US/Canada renewal, record 33 new warehouses in FY27, fee-step flow-through at ~90% margin, +9.5% consensus EPS revision (FY31E $32.63) → ~9.7% EPS CAGR. Alpha must come from beats, not rerating.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL — quality top-quintile (ROIC 26.3%, negative accruals −7.4%) vs value bottom-quintile and 12-1 momentum −3.6% vs SPY +15%; β≈0.86 (5y), 1y R² vs SPY ≈ 0.01 (near-zero market beta, idiosyncratic tape); vol 20.4%.
- **Financial Risk**: LOW. Net cash $12.7B, S&P AA-/Aa2, 80.6× interest coverage, Altman Z 4.73; survives a −20% revenue shock for 2+ years with zero cash generation. FCF yield 2.3% is the only real flag — a valuation, not solvency, signal.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: Moderate (low end). Governance and fraud clean; recurring food-safety events at owned Lincoln Premium Poultry (listeria/salmonella), Teamsters strike-cycle risk, no open-market insider buying at $920 (scheduled 10b5-1 sales only).
- **ESG**: Leader, net supportive — best-in-class labour (a moat input, not a cost drag), independent chair, SBTi-validated targets; watch-item: Class I recall frequency and DEI state-AG pressure.

Sept-2026 monthly sales **+15.8%** to $19.5B — a decisive acceleration above the FY26 ~10% run-rate in the quarter the Dec-10 print covers; news sentiment +0.90; 71.4% beat rate. **Bull Case**
- Elite compounding economics: +10.1% revenue growth with rising margins, 92% renewal annuity, 26% ROIC vs sub-10% WACC — a genuine core compounder, unanimously clean on credit, governance, and ESG.
- Demand accelerating into FY27 ahead of consensus: Sept sales +15.8%, +9.5% estimate revisions, record 33-unit expansion, and broker mean target ~$1,074 (trim-worthy only ~14% above).
- Defensive fortress + idiosyncratic tariff advantage: net cash, AA credit, refunds returned to members (loyalty-plus), and a low-beta staple that benefits from sticky inflation and a trading-down consumer.

**Bear Case**
- Full price, no cushion: 44× earnings / 43.6× FCF at a ~5% discount rate; value DCF ~$666; a comps miss below ~5% or fee-cycle gap re-rates the multiple fast (stock already −16% from May-2026 peak on steady fundamentals).
- Rate/term-premium rout: sustained 10Y >5.25% with sticky CPI blocking Fed cuts → second leg to ~$820–850; duration-multiple compression is the dominant risk to a long-duration staple.
- Input and model pressures: new tariff rounds re-stick Chinese-sourced COGS, ~20% of revenue in USMCA crossfire, USD >122 FX drag, thin (3.9%) operating margins amplifying wage shocks, and a recession lap could stall membership *growth* even as renewals hold.
