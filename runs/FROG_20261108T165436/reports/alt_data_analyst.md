## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**

The fundamentals-adjacent alt-data cluster is uniformly strong and mutually confirming: a 7-for-7 beat record, the *widening* revenue beat, a stale-below-actual consensus, a guidance raise, positive news tone, and euphoric social sentiment. Volume confirmation is emphatic — 7.03M shares on 6 Nov vs. ~1.5M average (~4.7x).

Two offsets keep this from being a "high conviction" bull:

- **Positioning whiplash:** the stock fell from $100.47 (28 Oct) to $74.62 on the 5th (**-26% into the print**) before gapping +35%. That violent reversal indicates crowded short/underweight positioning, not a stable re-rating — it can unwind as fast as it occurred.
- **Valuation:** ~$12.2B cap on a ~$717M annualized revenue run-rate ≈ **16–17x EV/S** (derived, cash-adjusted) for 31% growth. Priced for continued acceleration; the bear case is multiple, not model.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:** Only one quantitative demand series (Google Trends) is available, and it points the opposite way to the price and to the earnings data. Web traffic and patent channels both failed and are genuine gaps. Job-posting and social signals are headline-level proxies with no historical series, so no trend direction can be computed. Peer benchmarking is unavailable — the sector-peer endpoint returned mega-cap semis/hardware (AAPL, NVDA, MSFT) rather than DevOps/infra-software comparables, so relative multiple work is not possible here. Finally, the highest-quality signal (Q3 results) is **one day old**; alt data is largely confirming the print rather than leading it.

**Data limitations & lag:** Google Trends is weekly, normalized to its own 12-month peak, and reflects developer/job-seeker interest more than enterprise procurement. News sentiment is keyword-count based and event-skewed. Job-posting and web-traffic figures are search-snippet proxies, not Thinknum/SimilarWeb API series. All alt data here should be treated as corroborating evidence, not an independent forecast.

**Net:** Lean long into the 11 Feb 2027 print on a demonstrably sandbagged consensus and widening beats — but size for the fact that top-of-funnel demand intent is fading and the multiple already discounts flawless execution.
