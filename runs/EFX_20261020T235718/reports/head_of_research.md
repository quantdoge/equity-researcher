**Bull Case**
- **Workforce Solutions moat intact**: 44% margins, +7% growth, $550M state-agency ACV — counter-cyclical, contracted, effectively un-replicable payroll data.
- **Mortgage optionality un-priced**: mortgage revenue +15% in Q3 into a shrinking market (share gain); a 100bp rate retrace flips the biggest headwind to a 300–500bp tailwind at high incremental margin.
- **Cheapest quality asset in the cohort**: 12.8x EV/EBITDA vs. 16.3x peer median, 20.8x forward EPS, FCF $1.1B — with $150M AI cost savings cushioning the cycle.

**Bear Case**
- **FHFA bi-merge / $1 VantageScore pricing** structurally cuts revenue-per-origination in the highest-margin product line — a policy decision outside management control, now in formal rulemaking.
- **Margin recovery is promised, not proven**: operating margin 18.0% vs. 23.1% in 2021; consensus needs ~380bp of one-year expansion to a record. EPS growth is partly buyback arithmetic (share count −5.5%) with returns running at 128% of FCF.
- **Macro tape hostile**: 7.40% mortgage (post-quarter-end), Fed hiking, USD 121.4 — three of four transmission channels negative and intensifying.

**Key Risks**
1. FHFA finalizes bi-merge/two-bureau rule (structural, permanent — exit trigger, don't wait for price).

3. Workforce Solutions organic growth <5% or adverse CFPB action against The Work Number — that segment *is* the P&L.
