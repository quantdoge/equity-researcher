## 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **In-Line to Slightly Above, but Decelerating**

Surprise history is exceptional: **7/7 beats, avg +4.83%**, corroborated by an unbroken string of revenue beats (Q1'26 +$31.8M, Q2'26 +$3.9M, Q3'26 +$4.5M vs. consensus).

The caution is in the **shape** of the trend:
- Surprise magnitude is **compressing**: +10.06% (Q1'26) → +2.27% (Q2'26) → **+2.73% (Q3'26)**, below the 4.83% average.
- Revenue growth is **decelerating**: +14.3% (Q1'26) → +10.6% (Q2'26) → +9.6% (Q3'26) → **+7.9% implied** by the Feb 3, 2027 consensus ($1,672.7M vs. $1,550.6M).
- Consensus Q4 EPS of $2.22 (+6.2%) grows *slower* than revenue (+7.9%), implying margin compression — a normally conservative setup that EFX has historically beaten.

**Verdict: In-Line to modestly Above** on beat propensity, but the magnitude of outperformance should be assumed small (~2–3%) rather than the 5% historical average. The five months of alt-data since the July guide (Sept mortgage deterioration warning) argue against a large positive surprise.

## 7. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL**

**Confidence: LOW**

The genuine positives — a 100% seven-quarter beat rate, positive and broadening news sentiment (+0.714), elevated consumer search interest (+20% y/y), an obvious share-gain catalyst in VantageScore 4.0 mortgage pricing, and mortgage revenue growing 15% into a declining market — are offset by three real negatives: **decelerating revenue growth (14.3% → 9.6% → ~7.9% implied)**, **shrinking surprise magnitude**, and a **28.6% three-week rally that has already priced in the beat**. Social sentiment sits at neutral, providing no edge in either direction.

**Data limitations (material):**
- EFX is a **B2B data utility** — consumer-facing alt data (Trends, apps, web traffic) has structurally low explanatory power for its revenue. The high-value signals (MBA mortgage application volumes, FICO/VantageScore mix shift, e-commerce/credit-inquiry volumes) were **not available** through this toolset.
- **Patent feed failed twice**; **web traffic and app data returned no numerics**; **job postings returned no quantitative counts or baseline**; two Google Trends calls hit **HTTP 429**.
- Article-level dates from the news proxy are unreliable (several "Q4"/"Q3" headlines are mislabeled across years); I anchored all conclusions to the verified earnings calendar and OHLCV series instead.
- Google Trends lags ~5–7 days (latest point 2026-10-11) and is not seasonally adjusted.

**Actionable read:** Alt data does not support an aggressive directional position ahead of the Feb 3, 2027 report. The signal is "quality franchise executing through a weak end market, at an already-recovered price." A beat remains the base case on history, but the *asymmetry has deteriorated* post-rally — consistent with neutral, not bullish.
