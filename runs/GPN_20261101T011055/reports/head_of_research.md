### Investment Memo: GPN — 1 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — initiate small, accumulate on weakness | **Conviction**: Medium
**Price Target**: $110 (~+14% from $96.84, 30-Oct close) | Bear $78 / Bull $125
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
GPN is now a pure-play merchant acquirer after closing the Worldpay acquisition ($24.25B) and Issuer Solutions divestiture ($13.5B) in January 2026. It is statistically cheap — 7.1x FY26E adjusted EPS ($13.60–13.80 guide), ~6.9x EV/adjusted EBITDA, ~12% normalized FCF yield — with a live CD&R/GTCR take-private report and a $7.5B capital-return program. But the cheapness is substantially *earned*: ROIC (~3.2% GAAP, ~10% adjusted) sits at or below WACC, pro forma leverage is ~3.5x (4.25x S&P-adjusted) at a BBB−/Baa3 floor, GAAP EPS is ~zero against the $13.70 adjusted figure, and the stock just popped 13% on deal speculation. Probability-weighted fair value (~$99) is barely above spot — this is a hold-and-harvest price, not a build-a-position price.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Mature, consolidating processor layer growing 4–6% with structural fee compression; GPN is an above-median-margin, mid-pack-growth #3–4 acquirer in a "prove-it" integration year. Macro is two-sided: 3.4% GDP and 326bp HY spreads help volumes and LBO financing; a re-hiking Fed, 5% 10Y, and a firm dollar hurt the multiple and the enlarged international book. **Q3 print on 5 Nov dwarfs every macro variable near-term.**
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM. Quantified Middle East travel headwind (~100bp of revenue, drove the guidance cut); $99M of $101M lifetime penalties are consumer-protection — ISO/agent sales conduct is the most likely next legal event; regulatory perimeter just doubled across 40+ jurisdictions.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD; base IV ~$110 (range $80–140). Narrow moat, 73% of assets goodwill/intangibles, unproven post-deal FCF year. Buy below $82–85; trim above ~$120.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (moderate) on catalysts, not innovation: 4–5% organic growth, +150bp margin expansion, $600M synergies, 86% beat rate. Rule of 40 ~31 normalized — solid, not disruptive.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Neutral (54/100). Value 78, Revisions 80 (eight consecutive PT raises) vs Quality 32 (ROIC veto), Momentum 35 (6-1 momentum −15%), 45% vol. Alpha statistically meaningless (R² 5.9%).
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. One notch above HY at two of three agencies; interest coverage 2.3–3.1x; ~$2.5B tangible equity vs $22.4B debt; H1'26 operating cash flow down 73%. Solvency is not the question — cost of capital and re-rating are.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. $1.7B tax hit on non-deductible goodwill (a board-level structuring miss); goodwill at 117% of equity with provisional purchase accounting; no insider open-market buying through a guidance cut; guidance cut three months post-close.
- **ESG**: Average. Environmentally benign; governance discount is real (17% one-day deal reaction, 16.4% GTCR block, adjusted-metric compensation); cyber/DORA is the true ESG tail risk ($93.9M 2012 breach precedent).
- **Alternative Data**: Cautiously positive, low confidence. Retail attention at 12-month lows (Trends −62% QoQ), institutional IR traffic +24%, hiring tilted to SMB/POS go-to-market — an under-owned re-rating, but the signal predates the 30-Oct pop.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **No edge — short thesis rejected.** Clean forensics (M-score −3.06, negative accruals, 1.9x cash conversion). The bear case is capital allocation ($8.9B bought back at ~$166 avg; ~$4.1B destroyed), not accounting — and it is already in the price.

**Bull Case**
- Deep value with a cash-flow floor: 7.1x earnings, ~12% FCF yield, 42% adjusted operating margins, >$2B/yr of buybacks retiring ~2% of shares annually.
- Catalyst stack: 5 Nov Q3 (86% beat rate), synergy milestones (~$0.30–0.35 EPS per $100M), deleveraging to ~3.0x unlocking a re-rate to 8–9x FY27E → $115–130, plus a take-private option held by the party (GTCR) that knows the asset best.
- Broad-based sell-side upgrade cycle with median PT ~$106–110; FY26→28 consensus EPS CAGR ~17%.

**Bear Case**
- Leverage at the rating floor with thin coverage; a downgrade to BB triggers forced index selling and re-prices $22B of debt. The LBO itself is rate-sensitive — if credit normalizes, the floor under the stock evaporates.
- Earnings quality: GAAP EPS ≈ $0 vs $13.70 adjusted; 4% organic growth in a ~7–9% TAM is share loss to Adyen/Shift4/Toast; a second guidance cut on 5 Nov breaks the deleveraging narrative.
- Deal premium unwind: corrected for ~$17B net debt, the reported ~$40B bid figure implies only ~$89/share — *below* spot. If the process dies, the stock reverts to the low $80s or worse.

**Key Risks**
1. Formal withdrawal/collapse of the CD&R/GTCR process (stock to $75–85).
2. Q3 miss or organic growth <4% on 5 Nov — four days away.
3. S&P/Moody's downgrade; interest coverage <2.5x.
4. Goodwill impairment in *continuing* operations — breaks the adjusted-EPS construct.
5. Bear steepener persists: multiple compression plus refinancing headwind on ~$1.1B/yr interest.
