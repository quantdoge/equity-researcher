## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — and the split is decisive**

**Where all agents agree (this is the load-bearing consensus):**
1. **No forensic or fraud problem.** Short, Fin Risk, Quant and Non-Fin independently converge: clean accruals, no revenue-recognition games, no auditor change, no SEC/DOJ action. *This clears the one veto that would have outranked everything else.*
2. **The business is genuinely cheap on cash flow** — 7.1x FY26E adjusted EPS, ~11x P/FCF, ~12% FCF yield, P/B 0.98.
3. **Deleveraging is the whole thesis.** Every agent independently lands on the same fulcrum: ~3.0–3.5x net leverage within 18–24 months.
4. **The Nov 5 print is the dominant near-term variable** (Macro, Alt Data, Growth, Geo Legal, Non-Fin all say so).
5. **The M&A premium is binary and uncontrollable.**

**Where they genuinely conflict — and how I resolve it:**

- **Quality (Quant 32/100, ROIC 3.2%) vs. Value (78/100, P/B 0.98).** Not a real conflict: both are right. Goodwill-adjusted ROIC of ~10% ≈ 9% WACC means GPN earns *roughly its cost of capital* — so book value at 0.98x is a **rational** price, not a mispricing. **Resolution: the P/B "cheapness" is an artefact; value the company on FCF, not book.** Quant wins this point on quality, and it caps the multiple.
- **Growth (Buy) vs. Value (Hold, don't buy here).** Resolution: Value's discipline governs entry price; Growth's catalyst set governs the *reason to own*. Net: own it, but not at $96.84 with full size.
- **Cheapness vs. leverage risk.** Resolution: **Fin Risk and Non-Fin Risk outrank Growth and Alt Data.** Two independent HIGH risk ratings, one notch above high yield at two of three agencies, and negative H1 free cash flow cannot be offset by a 7x multiple. **Size is set by the risk agents, entry by the value agent, catalysts by the growth agent.**
- **The one place I overrule multiple agents:** the LBO upside. Growth, Value and Alt Data all treat the take-private as free upside. Corrected for net debt, it is an option with a **$89 floor-side outcome**, not a $125 payday.

**Agreement level: Split.** Bullish on cash flow and estimate revisions; bearish on quality, leverage and entry price.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

**Target position: 3.0% maximum. Initiate at 1.0% today.**

Rationale — this is a "medium conviction, elevated risk, thin margin of safety" profile, which per framework maps to 1–3%, and I land at the **bottom of that band** for three reasons:

**a) The edge is real but thin at $96.84.** Probability-weighted, 12-month fair value:
- 25% — LBO/competitive auction completes at $45–48bn EV → **$108–120**
- 45% — standalone execution: 8x FY27E EPS $16.11 → **$105–112**
- 30% — deal dies + organic growth slips to <4% → **$75–85** (back to the 28-Oct level at the top of that range)

Expected value ≈ **$99 vs. $96.84 spot — a ~2% expected return.** That is *not* a position you build at full size. It becomes one at $85, where the same math yields ~19%.

**b) Two independent HIGH risk ratings and ROIC below WACC** cap the ceiling at 3%, never the 5% a "high conviction" name would earn. The equity is ~$25bn supporting ~$2.5bn tangible equity against $22.4bn of debt — one impairment or one stalled synergy program re-prices it.

**c) Volatility-adjusted sizing.** 45% annualised vol means a 3% position carries ~1.35% of portfolio volatility — equivalent to ~3.5% in a 38%-vol name or ~9% in a 2.6%-vol bond proxy. Sizing on capital rather than on risk would systematically over-bet this name.

**Kelly check.** With ~25% downside / ~15% upside probability-weighted, the full-Kelly fraction is small; quarter-Kelly (standard institutional prudence for a levered single name with binary event risk) lands at ~2.5–3%. **A 3% cap with a 1% entry is consistent with quarter-Kelly.**

**Ladder:**
| Tranche | Size | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **T1 — now** | 0.5% | Market, pre-print |
| **T2 — post-print** | 0.5% | After 5 Nov Q3, *if* organic growth ≥4% and leverage commentary confirms ≤3.5x |
| **T3 — value add** | +1.0% | Price ≤$85 with thesis intact (≈25% discount to base IV) |
| **T4 — max** | +1.0% | Price ≤$78 *and* FY26 FCF positive *and* no impairment |
| **Hard cap** | 3.0% | Trim mechanically above 3.5% |

**Why not zero:** the forensic screen is clean, the stock has a PE sponsor as its largest holder who knows the asset, $7.5bn of buyback support retires ~2%/yr of shares, the 12% FCF yield provides a valuation floor, and the analyst bear case was explicitly rejected. **Passing entirely would be an overreaction to leverage risk that is already in the price at 6.9x EV/EBITDA.**

**Why not more:** the price already contains the deal.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector.** Financials / Credit Services. **This is an addition to financials, so net exposure must be checked first.** Any existing payments-processor weight is the binding constraint: combined GPN+FI+FIS+PYPL+XYZ should not exceed **6–7%**, of which GPN is at most 3%.

**Correlation (252d, verified — this is the most useful new data in the report):**

| | FIS | PYPL | XYZ | MA | V |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GPN** | **0.58** | 0.43 | 0.46 | 0.47 | 0.47 |

GPN is **moderately, not highly, correlated** to the processor complex — 0.58 to FIS is the tightest link. That is genuinely useful diversification, but it also means **GPN + FIS is close to one trade**. Note also that FIS is on the *other side* of GPN's Issuer sale — owning both partially hedges the integration, and doubles the leverage bet against the same capital-markets backdrop. **Do not own GPN, FI and FIS simultaneously at full weight.**

**Factor exposure — three unintended tilts this position adds:**
1. **Short quality.** ROIC 3.2% GAAP / ~10% adjusted, 90% goodwill equity, adjusted-vs-GAAP gap of 2.3x. If the book already tilts cheap-and-levered, GPN is correlated *expression* of that, not a diversifier.
2. **Long credit-spread / short duration.** GPN's LBO optionality is a direct function of HY OAS at 326bp. A portfolio that already owns rate-sensitive, leveraged financials is doubling a macro bet it may not have intended. **Underwrite GPN as a credit-spread position, not an equity position.**
3. **Positive estimate revisions / negative momentum.** An unusual pairing — factor-neutral funds will see offsetting active risk (12-1 momentum is only +0.95%, 6-1 is −15.25%).

**What it does NOT add:** market beta. Beta of 0.84 and **R² of just 5.9%** mean 94% of GPN's variance is idiosyncratic. This is single-name risk, not market risk — which is precisely why it is sized small, not why it is safe.

**Liquidity/crowding: clean.** ~3–8M shares/day, no short interest crowding, no retail participation. **Do not size on this basis — but do note the exit door is narrow on a 45%-vol event reversal.**

2. **0.5% on/after 5 Nov (Q3).** Pre-defined conditions: normalized organic growth ≥4%, FY26 adjusted EPS guidance maintained or raised, and no deterioration in leverage commentary. If the print shows organic <4% or a second guidance cut, **stop at 0.5% and do not complete T2.**

4. **If the CD&R/GTCR process is formally confirmed with a per-share price:** treat as deal-arb, not fundamental — size to the spread, not the thesis, and tender rather than hold for a bump.

**This is the crux: GPN at $96.84 is a "hold and harvest" price, not a "build a position" price. The only genuinely attractive entries are ≤$85 (the pre-event level) or a confirmed higher bid.**
