## GLOBAL PAYMENTS INC. (GPN) — FINANCIAL RISK ASSESSMENT
**Price $81.66 (2026-02-20) · Market cap ≈$19.6–25.6B · Sector: Financial Services / Credit Services**

---

### ⚠️ CRITICAL REPORTING CAVEAT (read first)

GPN's capital structure is **not** what FY2025 statements show. Two events break comparability:

1. **Issuer Solutions was sold to FIS for $13.5B**, completed **Jan 12, 2026**. FY2025 income statement reflects **continuing operations only** — revenue falls to $7,706M from $10,106M, and goodwill drops $35.2B → $21.3B. Cash jumps to $8,336M and current assets to $12.6B largely from held-for-sale/proceeds reclassification.
2. **Worldpay was acquired for $24.25B**, closed **the same day (Jan 12, 2026)**. **None of the FY2025 balance sheet reflects the acquired debt.** S&P cited ~$22.5B reported debt post-notes issuance; company guides **pro forma net leverage ≈3.5x at close**, with **S&P-adjusted pro forma leverage ≈4.25x**.

Every ratio below is therefore a **pre-Worldpay** snapshot. Pro forma figures are flagged separately. This is the single most important limitation of this assessment.

### 1. CREDIT RISK ASSESSMENT

**Rating: Investment Grade — but at the bottom rung and under pressure.**

| Agency | Rating | Outlook |
|---|---|---|
| S&P | BBB- (issuer & senior unsecured) | Stable |
| Moody's | Baa3 | Stable |
| Fitch | BBB (senior unsecured, affirmed Jan 16, 2026) | Stable |

GPN sits **one notch above sub-investment grade at S&P and Moody's**. The BBB-/Baa3 floor is the entire ballgame: any downgrade triggers a crossover into high yield, raising refinancing costs on ~$22B+ of debt and forcing index-driven selling.

**The tension:** management's own 3.5x target is *comfortably* consistent with BBB-. But **S&P's adjusted 4.25x is not** — the ~0.75-turn gap is their differential treatment of pensions, leases, and operating-lease-adjusted EBITDA. At 4.25x, GPN has essentially **zero headroom** to the ~4.0–4.5x net leverage threshold that IG frameworks tolerate for this sector. The rating is being held up by the *commitment* to delever to 3.0x in 18–24 months, not by current metrics.

A credit-rating upgrade is unlikely; a downgrade is a live risk if Worldpay integration underperforms or the macro backdrop deteriorates.

**On the Z-Score (1.02, "Distress"):** I do **not** treat this as a genuine bankruptcy signal. The score is distorted by three structural factors inherent to GPN's business model: (a) **x4 uses book equity**, understating the market-based version; (b) **x5 (asset turnover) is structurally depressed** because $21.3B of goodwill sits on a $7.7B revenue base — an artifact of 20 years of M&A, not operating weakness; (c) working capital is inflated by held-for-sale classification. The **Piotroski 6/9** is the more reliable read. Flagging this explicitly rather than passing through a misleading headline.

**On interest coverage:** the 2.27x tool output vs. 3.12x on a raw EBIT/interest basis is a meaningful divergence. Either way, **coverage is thin** — interest expense of ~$650M consumes a large share of EBIT. This is the weakest point in the credit profile.

**Beta divergence:** the profile beta of 0.805 and my computed 1.135 conflict. The realized 3-year beta is the more decision-relevant figure — GPN has behaved **more** sensitively to market moves than its stated beta implies, amplified by its own M&A event risk. I would not rely on the 0.805 figure for downside sizing.

**Price action is itself a risk signal.** GPN fell ~51% peak-to-trough, and in the last twelve months shows extreme event-driven dispersion: **–16% on Feb 3, 2026** (integration/deleveraging concern), then **+16.5% on Feb 18, 2026** (earnings). A Sharpe of 0.11 on 41% volatility means investors have taken enormous risk for essentially no return. High FCF yield (10%) plus deep drawdown plus poor Sharpe is the classic signature of a **value trap / de-rating candidate** — the market is explicitly discounting the deleveraging story.

### 4. DOWNSIDE SCENARIO: –20% REVENUE

**A. Pre-Worldpay (FY2025 continuing ops, $7,706M revenue)**
Assuming ~45% contribution margin (high operating leverage from fixed processing infrastructure):
- EBITDA: $3,445M → **~$1,905M** (–45%)
- **Net debt/EBITDA: 13,529 / 1,905 = 7.1x** ❌ far above IG tolerance
- EBITDA/interest: **2.9x**; EBIT coverage turns **below 1.5x**
- FCF: roughly $1,905M – $618M capex – $650M interest – ~$300M tax ≈ **$335M**
- Dividend ($239M) barely covered; **buyback ($1,231M/yr) suspended entirely**
- **Verdict: covenant stress, probable downgrade to BB+/Ba1**

**B. Pro forma post-Worldpay (combined revenue ~$12.5–13B, combined EBITDA ~$7B, net debt ~$24–25B)**
- Revenue –20% → EBITDA –~$1.2B → **~$5.8B**
- **Net leverage: ~4.3–4.4x** — at or slightly beyond S&P's stated 4.25x
- Deleveraging to 3.0x becomes unreachable inside the 18–24 month window → **rating downgrade to sub-IG highly likely**
- FCF still positive (~$1.5B+), so **liquidity crisis is not the risk — a ratings/cost-of-capital spiral is**

**Tangible equity check:** book equity $23.8B less goodwill & intangibles $21.3B = **~$2.5B tangible equity** supporting $21.9B debt. The equity cushion is thinner than headline debt/equity of 0.92x suggests.

### 5. OVERALL FINANCIAL RISK RATING: **HIGH**

Not "Critical" — GPN generates ~$2B of FCF, holds $8.3B cash, has staggered maturities out to 2052, and retains IG access. But the combination of **BBB-/Baa3 floor ratings, 4.25x S&P-adjusted pro forma leverage, 2.3–3.1x interest coverage, a just-closed $24.25B integration, thin tangible equity, and a 41%-volatility/0.11-Sharpe equity profile** places this at the high end of investment grade and squarely in "High" financial risk.

The company's solvency is not in question. Its **cost of capital and re-rating potential are** — and both hinge on delivering 3.0x leverage within 18–24 months.

---

### 6. KEY RED FLAGS

1. 🚩 **Rating floor risk.** BBB-/Baa3/BBB = one notch from high yield at two of three agencies. A downgrade raises interest on $22B+ of debt and triggers forced index selling.
2. 🚩 **S&P-adjusted pro forma leverage ~4.25x vs. management's 3.5x** — a 0.75-turn credibility gap with no covenant cushion.
3. 🚩 **Interest coverage of 2.27–3.12x** is the weakest metric in the profile; well below 4x.
4. 🚩 **$24.25B Worldpay integration** — largest M&A event in company history, executed while already leveraged, with a hard 18–24 month deleveraging clock.
5. 🚩 **Thin tangible equity (~$2.5B)** against $21.9B debt; goodwill is 90% of book equity.
6. 🚩 **Equity market is voting against the story:** –51% peak-to-trough, 41% vol, Sharpe 0.11, ~10% FCF yield. High FCF yield + deep drawdown is a de-rating pattern, not a bargain signal, until deleveraging is proven.
7. 🚩 **Refinancing concentration:** Fitch notes maturities 2026–2052 with **most debt due 2027 and thereafter**. Notes due **Mar 2026 and Apr 2026** are imminent and will be refinanced into a higher-rate environment.

### DATA GAPS & LIMITATIONS (disclosed)
- **Post-Worldpay pro forma financials are not in the dataset.** All pro forma leverage figures (3.5x, 4.25x) are sourced from company guidance and an S&P research headline, **not** from audited statements. This is the material gap in this analysis.
- **Peer benchmarking unavailable.** `get_sector_peers` returned Vanguard index funds and ETFs rather than payments comparables, so no relative-multiple or relative-leverage positioning could be computed. Flagging rather than fabricating.
- **FCF yield discrepancy:** tool used ~$23.3B market cap; at the $81.66 close the yield is ~10.4%. Both shown.
- **Market cap inconsistency:** profile lists $25.6B at $96.84, but the 2026-02-20 close was **$81.66** — the profile price is stale. Calculations here use the price history series.
- **Beta conflict (0.805 vs 1.135)** unresolved — I have used both and leaned on the realized 1.135.
- **Covenant thresholds are not publicly specified** in available data; the ~4.0–4.5x reference is an industry norm for IG payment processors, not a confirmed GPN covenant level.
