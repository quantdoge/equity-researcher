## 2. Key Red Flags Found

1. **The Q4 guide is a sequential-growth air pocket — the first in company history.** Q4-26 revenue guided $1.19–1.20B vs Q3's $1.197B = **flat QoQ**, after Q4 sequential gains of +7% to +8% in every prior year. Implied Q4 YoY growth: **+25.4% vs +35.2% reported in Q3** — a ~10-point deceleration in one quarter.
2. **Single-customer concentration is now disclosed in the 10-Q.** Management disclosed its largest customer (RBC links to **OpenAI**) reduced usage in Q3 and is expected to fall back to its **minimum contractual commitment** in Q4. That one account was ~**7% of YoY revenue growth**. The AI cohort is the growth engine and the fragility.
3. **The Q3 "beat" was not repeatable.** Q3 revenue beat by 4.6% ($1.197B vs $1.14B) and GAAP operating income jumped 13x QoQ ($5.5M → $74.7M), with gross margin leaping 160bp — in the same quarter the largest customer "spent well above its contractual commitment." The beat and the margin inflection are the same event, and it reverses in Q4.
4. **Guidance buffer is gone.** FY26 revenue guide raised to $4.52B — an increase equal to only the size of the Q3 beat, versus the historical pattern of raising by 2–3x the beat. When the sandbagging stops, demand has changed.
5. **Dilution is accelerating, not fading.** Diluted WAS: 364.7M (Q1-26) → 371.0M (Q2) → **375.0M (Q3)** — +2.8% in two quarters, ~+4.5% annualised. Diluted 375M vs. basic 361M: a 14M-share, ~$2.2B cumulative claim sitting in the options/RSU pool.
6. **Gross margin is not expanding.** FY25 COGS +33.2% vs revenue +27.7% (GM 80.8% → 80.0%); 9M26 GM 79.35% vs 79.79%. In Q2-26, COGS grew +44.7% against +35.6% revenue. This is what pricing pressure and inference-heavy AI workloads look like on a usage-billed model.
