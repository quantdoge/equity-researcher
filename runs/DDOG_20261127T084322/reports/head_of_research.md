**Investment Thesis**

Datadog is a genuinely excellent franchise — 35% growth, 80% gross margin, net cash, share leader in a consolidating ~20%-CAGR observability market — trading after a 46% de-rating at a price that is cheap *relative to peers* (12.1x FY26E EV/S vs CRWD at ~42x) but expensive *in absolute owner-earnings terms* (~135x FCF-ex-SBC; intrinsic value ≈$105). The drawdown was driven by a real-yield shock and a guidance air pocket (first flat-sequential quarter in company history, caused by the largest AI-native customer dropping to minimum commitment), not by fundamental deterioration — earnings beats are 7/7 and FY2030 EPS revisions are +15%. The stock is now a bimodal macro option on the 10Y real yield (roughly +58% bull / −30% bear), and the correct posture is to respect both the quality and the absence of margin of safety: hold, don't add at full size, and let the February 9 print resolve the concentration question.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro is **Unfavourable** but largely priced — Fed re-tightening (Dec hike priced), 10Y at 5.13%, real yields +68bp since July; this rate shock, not fundamentals, caused most of the $293→$158 collapse. Asymmetric tail: a disinflationary rollover could re-rate the stock +40–60% with no change in the story. Sector is **Attractive but late-cycle**: ~20% CAGR, AI workloads additive to telemetry, vendor consolidation favouring platforms; DDOG is the only name in its peer set accelerating (34% YTD vs ~25% peer median) with best-in-class margins — but consumption-model deceleration (Q4 guide +26% vs Q3 +35%) is the dominant sector risk.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium risk.** No classical geopolitical exposure; risk is regulatory-regime risk in friendly jurisdictions — EU data/AI stack (GDPR, AI Act, DPF fragility) over ~26% of revenue, ~15% of headcount in France, Tel Aviv R&D hub, unresolved April 2025 source-code breach, and an open 90-day securities-litigation window post-sell-off. No SEC/DOJ/FTC/antitrust matters identified — notably clean.

SBC at ~22% of revenue consumes ~75% of reported FCF; GAAP profit is mostly interest income. Buy trigger ~$80–85. Quality of the operating business itself is superb (~70% ROIC on operating assets).

**Bull Case**
- **Share gainer in a consolidating, AI-tailwinded market**: 34% YTD growth vs ~20% market CAGR, unique acceleration in the peer set, 80% GM + 27.9% non-GAAP op margin funding unmatched R&D breadth; 16% of customers on 10+ products is the moat compounding.
- **The de-rating is done and it was macro, not fundamentals**: 7/7 beats, FY30 EPS revisions +15%, mean sell-side PT ~$270 (+71%); at 12.1x FY26E EV/S the multiple already embeds a re-rating — a 50bp real-yield decline plausibly drives +40–60%.
- **Fortress balance sheet + real cash generation**: net cash $2.94B, ~$1.4B FY26E reported FCF, zero solvency risk — downside is a valuation event, not a business failure.

**Bear Case**
- **The AI mega-customer is the growth engine and the fragility**: largest customer (widely identified as OpenAI) cut to minimum commitment; early renewals by other AI-native accounts at *lower unit prices* — the first clean evidence of customer pricing leverage. Q4 guide is the first flat-sequential quarter in company history.
- **Owner economics are far thinner than the headline**: SBC ~22% of revenue, ~4.5%/yr dilution, GAAP profit dependent on interest income; at ~135x owner earnings there is no cushion if 2027 growth lands in the low-20s.
- **Broken momentum in a hostile rate regime, with insiders unanimously selling**: trend broken, 74.5% vol, zero insider buying through the drawdown, and a December hike plus 10Y >5.50% compresses the stock toward ~8x sales ≈ $110.

**Key Risks**
1. **Customer concentration (top risk)**: a second usage step-down from the largest customer converts tail risk into base case — the single highest-impact unknown; concentration % is undisclosed and the 10-K was unretrievable by three agents.
2. **Deceleration**: Q4 +26% → 2027 low-20s would make even 9.8x forward sales look fair; consumption model transmits customer optimisation to revenue within a quarter.
3. **Rates**: the stock is a real-yield derivative; December hike + hawkish 2027 dots = ~−30%.
4. **SBC/dilution permanence**: if SBC stays >22% of revenue, per-share value compounds ~3pts slower than enterprise value.
5. **Governance/alignment**: insider selling pattern, Nevada domicile, dual class — caps position size and institutional sponsorship.
6. **Commoditisation at the edges**: OpenTelemetry, Grafana, hyperscaler bundling; gross margin <78% for two quarters is the tell.
