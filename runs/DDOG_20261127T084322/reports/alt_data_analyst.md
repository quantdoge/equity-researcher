**As of:** Nov 25, 2026 · **Price:** $158.25 · **Mkt cap:** $57.1B · **Next catalyst:** Q4'26 print, Feb 9, 2027

**Context that frames every signal below:** DDOG closed at $158.25, **-46% from its Oct 9 peak of $293.26** and near the low end of its 52-week range ($135–$301). The Q3'26 print (Nov 4) was a clean beat — revenue $1,197M vs $1,146M est (+35.1% YoY), EPS $0.81 vs $0.647 (+25.2%) — yet the stock fell ~14–16% and has kept sliding. So the question for alt data is narrow: **is Datadog-specific demand breaking, or is this multiple compression on a still-growing franchise?**

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (with a negative demand-momentum tilt)

The signals genuinely conflict, and the conflict is informative:

- **Bearish:** brand and pricing-intent search deteriorating for two straight quarters; the largest AI-native customer's usage cut is now confirmed in the filing, not just guided; retail dip-buying looks crowded.
- **Bullish:** hiring +25.6% YoY with a GTM-heavy requisition mix; 7/7 beat record with accelerating EPS surprises; a sandbagged guide; two Gartner leadership placements.
- **Important correction to the obvious bear read:** the search decay is **category-wide (-28% to -34% across Datadog, Dynatrace, and "observability")**, so it does *not* support the thesis that Datadog is losing share or that observability demand is collapsing — it supports the thesis that the 2026 AI-hype search cycle unwound. And the -46% drawdown is occurring inside a sector-wide SaaS de-rating, which multiple compression explains better than fundamental deterioration.

Net: I cannot call this Bullish while high-intent commercial search and the largest customer's consumption are both deteriorating. I will not call it Bearish, because the company's own hiring behavior and the beat record contradict a demand break. **Neutral.**

**What would flip me Bullish:** "datadog pricing" search re-basing off zero; evidence the AI mega-customer's usage has stabilized; web traffic confirmation that trial/console activity held in Q4.
