### Investment Memo: CLS (Celestica Inc.) — 28 October 2026

**Verdict**: BUY — Initiate small (1.5% starter) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $355 (~+28% from $276.54) — ≈17x management's FY27 EPS guide of $21.50 / 18x consensus $19.74; bear-case floor ~$150, street median $415
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months, with a hard re-underwrite at the Q4'26 print on 27-Jan-2027

**Investment Thesis**
Celestica is the best operator in a bifurcating EMS sector — the only large-cap name pairing +28% revenue growth with expanding margins, now guiding $12.4B (FY25) → $21B (FY26) → $40B (FY27) with a 7/7 beat record and $3.0B of net cash. The equity has de-rated 42% from its June high on capital-intensity and AI-capex-financing fears, not demand deterioration, leaving it at 12.9x the FY27 guide. The offset is real: ~66% of revenue sits in three hyperscaler customers, growth is currently margin-dilutive, and the stock is a beta-3.1, 79%-volatility proxy on the single most macro-sensitive capex line in the market. This is a position you size for the volatility, not the conviction.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector ATTRACTIVE but narrowly so — AI-infrastructure EMS (>$600bn 2026 hyperscaler capex, +36% YoY; white-box share shift) vs. structurally ex-growth legacy EMS. CLS holds the best growth-and-margin combination in the peer set (+28.5% growth vs +8.1% peer median; 8.6% op margin vs 5.0%). Macro is the counterweight: 10Y at 5.00% (+56bp in 4 months), 2Y pricing *hikes*, CPI re-accelerating to 3.72%, HY OAS +70bp off the lows — directly pressuring the ~⅓ of 2027 AI capex that is debt-funded. Macro rating: neutral, tilted unfavourable.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM-HIGH risk. ~80% of revenue is produced outside North America against ~60% North American demand — maximal tariff transmission (Section 122 Mexico duties, Section 232 metals/semiconductor tiers), plus a live BIS export-control/China-manufacturing nexus. Two plaintiff-firm securities *investigations* (Pomerantz, Portnoy) are open but unfiled; no material litigation; legal risk itself is contained.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD/do-not-initiate at $277. Narrow, cycle-dependent moat; peak-of-cycle 12% gross margin; FCF conversion only 55% (70x P/FCF, 1.4% FCF yield); intrinsic value ~$245 (range $200–330); buy threshold $185. Management quality (Mionis) is the best feature — disciplined, no empire-building.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: STRONG BUY. Guidance raised 3x in 12 months; FY26 +69% / FY27 +90% growth; FY27 guide sits *above* consensus; 12.9x guide EPS, PEG ~0.14, ~8.5x 2027E EV/EBITDA — cheaper than FLEX/JBL while growing 4–6x faster. TAM tailwinds: 800G→1.6T refresh, custom-silicon shift.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL, momentum-biased. 12-1 momentum +19%, ROIC 34%, F-Score 7/9 — but beta 3.09 (daily), vol 79%, alpha −41%, Sharpe 0.21, max drawdown −48%. Returns are beta, not skill; size accordingly. The +23% revision signal rests on one analyst (2030) — no weight.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE and bifurcated. Credit/liquidity risk LOW (net cash ~$3.0B post-raise, 21x interest coverage, BB+/Ba1, negligible bankruptcy probability — market-cap Altman Z ≈ 6.75). Market/valuation risk HIGH. A −20% revenue scenario is survivable for the balance sheet but cuts operating income 45–69% — leverage is violently symmetric at a 12% gross-margin base.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Top-10 customers = 84% of revenue; CEO/Chair duality created in 2026 with no confirmed lead director; ~404k shares of net insider selling (13 transactions, zero buys) into the most aggressive guidance raise in company history; $1B receivables facility is *uncommitted*; Section 232 semiconductor tariff in effect Jan-2026.
- **ESG**: AVERAGE — disclosure leader, performance laggard. Scope 3 has roughly doubled against an SBTi target that will be badly missed; this is a *commercial* liability (hyperscaler procurement gating), not a reputational one. Forced-labour (UFLPA) tail is low-probability but thesis-breaking severity. Nothing in ESG currently touches cash flow.
- **Alternative Data**: BULLISH. Manufacturing hiring ramp (Richardson/Plano TX), 100% beat rate with *expanding* surprise magnitude, news sentiment maximally positive, retail attention at a trough into the print — an attention/fundamentals dislocation that began resolving with the +12% post-earnings gap.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: NO forensic edge. Accruals, receivables and cash conversion (113% of NI) are clean; Beneish M-score is a growth artifact; no channel stuffing. The one genuine flag is quality-of-growth: gross margin 13.0% → 11.0% on +83% revenue, inventory days 61 → 79, capex quadrupled to ~$862M TTM. This is a valuation bear case, not a fraud case — and shorting an accelerating, net-cash name is structurally dangerous.

**Bull Case**
- Guide delivery: $40B FY27 revenue / $21.50 EPS is above consensus, backed by 7/7 beats (avg +8.6%), a visible hiring/capex ramp, and structural share shift to merchant AI networking.
- Forward valuation is undemanding: 12.9x FY27 guide, ~10x 2028 consensus, a discount to slower-growing EMS peers.
- Fortress balance sheet ($3.0B net cash) + clean accounting + operating leverage: a downturn is self-funding; upside re-rates toward the $415 street median (+50%).

**Bear Case**
- Concentration: three customers ≈ 66% of revenue; one insourcing or second-source decision is a step-function revenue event with ~50% operating-income sensitivity.
- Growth is being bought: GM −204bp YoY at +83% revenue, FCF conversion 44–55%, capex 4x, ~8–10% dilution from the August raise at $310 (above today's price).
- Macro accident: 10Y >5.25% / HY OAS >4% triggers an AI-financing credit event; at beta 3.09 the equity falls 35–45% on estimate cuts plus EMS-multiple re-rate (toward $100–150).

**Key Risks**
1. AI-capex digestion / credit event in H1-2027 (macro agents' surprise scenario — monitor HY OAS >4.00%, 10Y >5.25%).
2. Margin quality: GM <10.5% for two consecutive quarters with inventory days >85 = growth being purchased with price.
3. Customer action: any top-3 customer dropping toward/below ~28% of revenue, or announced insourcing.
4. Policy: Mexico tariff renewal / USMCA review, Section 232 scope expansion, BIS chip-rule tightening.
5. Governance/disclosure: Pomerantz/Portnoy converting to a filed complaint tied to FY27 guidance; continued insider distribution.
