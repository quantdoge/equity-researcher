### 2. Consensus Assessment — **SPLIT on valuation, HIGH on everything else**

- **Unanimous (near-zero dissent):** demand is real and accelerating; balance sheet is fortress-grade; **there is no accounting fraud** (Short Analyst explicitly rejects the fraud thesis — M-score driven purely by sales growth, DSRI 0.987, accruals 0.024).
- **Unanimous (negative):** customer concentration is the dominant un-hedgeable risk — three customers at 35%/17%/14% of Q3'26 revenue.
- **The genuine split — valuation.** Growth says 12.9x 2027E; Value says 38x trailing and 13% *above* intrinsic. **Both are arithmetically correct.** I verified the forward base: consensus 2027 is $19.74 EPS on $36.0B revenue (14.0x spot), 2028 is $27.63 on $49.0B (10.0x). But consensus sits **below** management's $40B/$21.50 guide, and the 2030 number rests on **one analyst**. The entire bull case reduces to: *does the $40B guide get delivered, and at what margin?*
- **Conflict resolution:** no fraud/accounting override applies. I resolve the growth-vs-value conflict by concluding it is **not resolvable analytically at this price** — it is a delivery question with a binary answer arriving 27-Jan-2027. The correct portfolio response to an unresolvable binary is **small size with a scale-in ladder**, not a full position or a pass.

**Net conviction: MEDIUM-HIGH on business quality, MEDIUM on 12-month risk-adjusted return.**

### 3. Position Sizing Recommendation — **1.5% initial, 3.0% hard cap**

| Band | Size | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Initiate now** | **1.5%** | Current price, low end of range |
| Scale to 2.25% | +0.75% | Q4'26 (27-Jan-27) confirms ≥$6.4B rev **and** GM ≥11.0% |
| Scale to 3.0% cap | +0.75% | FY27 guide reiterated at $40B **with** FCF guidance raised off $460M |
| Do not exceed | 3.0% | Regardless of price action |

**Rationale — Kelly with a volatility haircut.** Bull case to consensus target (~$415 median) is +50%; base bear case (2028 EPS $20 on 12x) is −13%; severe bear (AI-credit event, revenue plateau at $20B, EMS re-rate) is −55%. At p≈0.55, b≈1.1, full Kelly = 14.5%; half-Kelly ≈ 7%. That is then reduced by **four** independent risk haircuts: (1) 79.1% annualized vol and 3.09 beta; (2) 66% revenue concentration in three customers — a single-point, un-hedgeable failure mode; (3) CEO/Chair duality plus net insider selling into a ~2x guidance raise; (4) macro configuration is levered to the exact variable under pressure. Those take 7% → 1.5–2%.

**Why not smaller/pass:** risk-adjusted stats are poor (Sharpe 0.21, alpha −40.9%), but those are *backward* measures on a stock that has de-rated 42% while fundamentals inflected up. Passing entirely on a name where 8 of 11 agents are constructive on demand would be an error of omission.

**Why not larger:** at beta 3.09, a 3.0% position carries **~9.3% of SPY-equivalent market beta** and 2.4% standalone vol contribution. That is already a large risk budget for one idiosyncratic, 66%-concentrated customer base.

### 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector/factor concentration — the key warning.** CLS must be booked as **AI-capex exposure, not diversified tech**. Measured correlations (252d): AVGO **0.58**, JBL **0.61**, FLEX **0.52**, NVDA 0.43, SPY 0.51. If the book already holds semis or AI-infrastructure names, CLS compounds a single factor bet — hyperscaler capital budgets — and should be funded **out of** those positions, not added alongside them. If the book is underweight AI infrastructure, CLS is a reasonable vehicle because it is *less* correlated with NVDA (0.43) than with its own EMS peers.

**Factor tilts introduced:** + high momentum (12-1 return +19.2%), + high beta, + negative value (bottom quintile), − low-vol. This is a pro-cyclical, high-beta addition. **R² of 0.26 vs SPY means 74% of variance is idiosyncratic** — index hedging will not protect this position; only sizing will.

**Macro overlap (under-priced by the bulls):** the 10Y at 5.00%, 2Y pricing hikes, HY OAS widened 70bp in eight weeks, and ~⅓ of 2027 AI capex expected debt-funded. CLS's order book is levered to the *financing* of its customers' capex, not just the demand. This is why I size below what the growth case alone would justify.

**Correlation note:** CLS is a genuinely poor diversifier — it correlates more with the market (0.51) than with its own peer group median, and its downside beta (3.09 daily) is far worse than its stated 1.46 monthly beta.
