## Genuine Parts Company (GPC) — Value Investment Analysis
*Price $123.52 · Market cap $17.0B · EV ~$25.0B · Beta 0.68 · Dividend yield 3.4%*

**Situation:** This is no longer a "steady compounder" story. GPC missed badly in Q4 2025 (adjusted EPS $1.55 vs $1.82 est.), took a ~$1.0B non-cash charge (EBIT $216M vs operating income $1,215M), and on Feb 17, 2026 announced a tax-free separation into **Global Automotive (NAPA, >$15B sales, ~$1.2B 2025 EBITDA)** and **Global Industrial (Motion, ~$9B sales, >$1.1B EBITDA)**, targeted for Q1 2027 (~$100M of dis-synergies), following a Sept 2025 Elliott settlement. Operational momentum has since improved: Q1–Q3 2026 adjusted EPS of $1.77 / $2.15 / $2.07 all beat, and 2026 guidance of $7.50–$8.00 is reaffirmed (consensus ~$7.73).

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: NARROW (trend: narrowing)

**What's genuinely good.** Non-discretionary, replacement-driven demand (average US fleet age ~12.8 years); NAPA's ~10,000-location network with 20,000+ NAPA Auto Care centers and an independent owner-operator model gives real local density and same-day availability — a legitimate *efficient scale / distribution-density* advantage. Motion (Industrial) is the better business: 180,000+ customers, 14 end markets, 10M+ SKUs, value-added services (fluid power, automation, repair) that create genuine switching costs and **12%+ EBITDA margins vs ~8% at Automotive**.

**Why the moat is narrowing, not widening.**
- **Operating margin 7.57% (2023) → 6.14% (2024) → 5.0% (2025).** SG&A grew +8.2% against revenue +3.5% — negative operating leverage at a company that should have positive leverage.
- **Gross margin −171bp in 2025** (36.29% → 34.58%): mix shift toward lower-margin acquired/wholesale business plus tariff cost pressure.
- **EPS has gone nowhere for four years:** $8.31 GAAP (2022) → $9.33 (2023) → ~$8.0 adj. (2024) → $7.38 adj. (2025) → ~$7.73E (2026). That is *negative* nominal growth after four years, before inflation.
- Automotive's ~8% EBITDA margin sits far below AutoZone/O'Reilly (~20%), reflecting a structurally lower-margin jobber/wholesale model that is losing DIFM share to competitors' aggressive commercial programs.

**Verdict: Narrow moat, and it has eroded over five years.** Industrial has the more durable franchise; Automotive has the better brand but weaker economics.

- **Goodwill & intangibles $5.04B = 24% of assets against only $4.44B of equity.** The $1.0B charge is direct evidence of past overpayment; further impairment risk is live.
- Offsets: FCF conversion of *adjusted* earnings remains decent (~$900M normalized owner earnings on ~$1.08B adjusted NI); gross margin still 34.6%; revenue is recurring and non-discretionary.
