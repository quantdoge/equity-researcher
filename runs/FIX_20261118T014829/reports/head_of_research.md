**Investment Thesis**

FIX is a genuinely exceptional business — unanimous across all twelve agents — trading at roughly fair value after a 47% de-rating that has already been paid out in price while earnings estimates rose ~94%. The entire debate is one variable: whether a 16.9% operating margin (up from 6.1% in 2021) is a durable structural repricing of scarce MEP capacity or a cyclical scarcity rent on hyperscaler AI capex. With a negative-skew macro backdrop (10Y at 5.13%, real policy rate ≈ 0%), beta of 2.45, and the first evidence of margin leakage in the fastest-growing segment, the risk/reward at $1,099 is balanced — quality justifies owning it, price does not justify buying it aggressively.

**Bull Case**

- Fortress quality: net cash, ROIC 44.8%, pristine accounting, ~$1.1B buyback into weakness — the floor under the stock is real.

**Bear Case**
- Margin mean reversion has begun: GM down two consecutive quarters (26.3% → 24.8%), electrical margin leaking 190bp; fixed-price leverage reverses as hard as it expanded.
- One-theme book: ~58% technology revenue, hyperscaler-controlled marginal demand, and a domestic permitting backlash that can strand backlog without cancelling it.
- Macro negative skew: real policy rate ~0% with inflation at a 3-year high means rate risk is up; at beta 2.45, a 10Y above 5.50% compresses the multiple and the customers' project math at once.

**Key Risks**
1. Hyperscaler/AI capex digestion — the single dominant risk; hits revenue, margin, and multiple together.
2. Backlog quality — cancellable/deferrable; fixed-price mix and embedded margin undisclosed; prepayment-driven OCF reverses on any slowdown.

4. Rates: sustained 10Y >5.50%.
5. Skilled-labor wage inflation unhedged on fixed-price work; *Walton* precedent raising public-work costs.
6. Execution: CFO handoff, capex tripling into a potential plateau, insider-selling signal.
