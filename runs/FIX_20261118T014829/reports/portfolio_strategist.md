- **"Is the stock a buy here?" → SPLIT.** Growth + Alt Data + Sector bullish; Value bearish-but-softening; Macro + Quant + Short neutral. Non-Fin Risk flags HIGH but issues no directional call.

**Conflict resolution, applying my precedence rules:**

1. **Fraud/accounting risk does not override — there is none.** The short analyst's rejection of his own thesis is decisive. Value's margin-reversion argument stands unopposed as the primary bear case, but it is a *valuation/timing* argument, not a solvency or integrity one.

**Net: business conviction HIGH, stock conviction LOW-to-MEDIUM. Sizing must follow the lower of the two.**
