## 1. Credit Risk Assessment
### Rating: **INVESTMENT GRADE** (net-cash, effectively zero credit risk at current leverage)

FIX is a **net-cash company**. The balance sheet carries $1.02B cash against only ~$145M of conventional debt (short-term $6.2M + long-term $139.1M); the leverage tool's $483M "total debt" figure almost certainly includes operating/finance lease liabilities — either way, net debt is **negative (−$499M to −$533M)**. Interest coverage of **145.7×** means debt service is a rounding error against EBIT of $1.30B. There is no refinancing cliff: short-term maturities are immaterial relative to $1.0B+ of liquidity and ~$1.0B of annual FCF.

Caveat: FIX is **not a rated issuer** by the major agencies to my knowledge (no CDS spread available) — the IG characterization is metric-based, not a formal rating. Data gap: no public credit rating, no CDS.

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — Revenue −20%

Base: TTM revenue ~$12.5B, gross margin ~25.6%, SG&A run-rate ~$1.16B annualised, interest ~$6M.

| Scenario | Revenue | Gross margin | EBIT | EBIT margin | Est. net income | FCF |

| **−20% rev, margin holds** | $10.0B | 25.6% | ~$1.41B | 14.0% | ~$1.1B | strongly positive |
| **−20% rev + GM compression to 22%** | $10.0B | 22.0% | ~$1.05B | 10.5% | ~$800M | strongly positive |
| **Severe −30% rev, GM 20%** | $8.8B | 20.0% | ~$0.60B | 6.8% | ~$450M | positive |

**Conclusion:** Even a 30% revenue collapse leaves EBIT comfortably positive, ~$450M of net income, no covenant pressure (no meaningful debt), and continued positive FCF. This is the key finding: **the equity can halve on multiple compression without the *company* being financially stressed.** The real downside transmission channel is valuation (2.65% FCF yield ⇒ ~38× FCF), not credit.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **LOW**

Balance-sheet and credit risk are genuinely **Low** — net cash, 145× coverage, Safe-zone Z, 8/9 F-score, expanding margins and FCF. The elevated *overall* risk in the equity is **market/valuation risk (High)**, driven by 59% vol, ~2× beta, a −53% historical drawdown, and a rich multiple that capitalizes an AI/data-center capex assumption. Blend: **Low financial/credit risk, High market risk.**

## 6. Key Red Flags & Data Gaps

**Red flags (watch items, not yet alarms):**
1. **Cyclical/end-market concentration** — explosive growth is tied to data-center & tech capex. A capex pause is the single biggest earnings risk; revenue is up ~50% YoY, which raises the cliff if the cycle turns.
2. **Low current ratio (1.21)** and **DSO ~116 days** with receivables +36% YoY — normal for contractors but sensitive to billing/collection discipline and contract-asset quality in a downturn.
3. **Valuation/volatility** — 2.65% FCF yield embeds high expectations; 59% vol and −53% max drawdown mean severe mark-to-market risk despite a fortress balance sheet.
4. **Debt figure inconsistency** — leverage tool ($483M) vs. balance sheet ($145M); likely lease-inclusive. Not material but should be reconciled against the 10-K.

**Explicit data gaps:** No public credit rating or CDS spread; **backlog / book-to-bill not available** (the critical leading indicator for a contractor); Q4'25 SG&A reported as $0 in the dataset (used FY2025 SG&A instead); no peer-multiple comparison (sector peer list returned mega-cap aerospace/machinery names only — not true MEP comps).

**Bottom line:** FIX is a financially fortress-like business — net cash, minimal debt, 145× coverage, Safe-zone Z-score, 8/9 F-score, and rising FCF. It would survive a prolonged downturn without financial distress; the risk an investor actually bears is **market/multiple risk tied to the data-center capex cycle**, not credit or liquidity risk.
