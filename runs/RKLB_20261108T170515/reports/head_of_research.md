**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium

**Investment Thesis**
Rocket Lab is a genuinely improving franchise — 62% revenue growth, gross margin up ~39pts in four years to 36%, a $2.36B backlog (+137% YoY), and a net-cash balance sheet — wrapped in a valuation that still prices near-flawless execution at ~33–41x sales while its central catalyst (Neutron) has slipped three times and carries no firm date. Two-thirds of the multiple compression has already occurred (−65% from $150), the books are forensically clean, and the short case is exhausted — but a re-hiking Fed, structural dilution (+35% shares since 2022, live $3B ATM), and zero insider buying versus ~$400M of selling argue against paying up here. The stock is fairly valued on a probability-weighted basis; the right posture is patience, with entry gated on physical Neutron evidence, not price.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector demand is early-cycle and attractive (defense space budgets growing ~2x the broader budget; Golden Dome $17.9B; launch supply constrained below medium-class) — but SpaceX sets industry price and captures the profit pool. Macro is a clear headwind: Fed re-hiking (3.75–4.00%), 10Y at 4.84% (peak 5.31%), CPI 3.7%, December hike priced. The −65% drawdown tracked ~85bp of yield rise; 10Y → 5.5% alone implies $30–38. RKLB grows ~4x the A&D prime median with the cohort's best margin trajectory — best franchise, worst macro setup.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM risk, no current legal overhang. Securities class action dismissed with prejudice (Apr-26); no DOJ/SEC/OFAC actions. Exposure concentrated in US-allied jurisdictions. Two binary watch items: FCC/Team Telecom approval of Iridium (filed Aug-26, SpaceX lobbying for scrutiny; close mid-2027) and a Neutron first-flight mishap. Customer concentration rising — 77% of backlog in five customers.

Negative ROIC and owner earnings every public year; FCF conversion 0%. **Bull Case**
- Neutron flies successfully in 2027 into a structurally supply-short medium-lift market, validating $1.8B+ 2028 revenue and collapsing the risk premium toward the $90–110 range.
- Defense space supercycle (Golden Dome, SDA, NSSL Lane 1, HASTE) provides a government-funded growth floor; backlog already +137% YoY with Electron cadence at 19 missions and ~100-mission backlog.
- Iridium's spectrum/constellation proves strategically scarce in a direct-to-device world, and the core business (ex-Neutron spend) is already near EBITDA breakeven — Q2'26 adj. EBITDA loss just $8.8M.

**Bear Case**
- A fourth Neutron slip (or first-flight failure) removes the vehicle the valuation is capitalised on; 2027E/2028E revenue lines fall and the 33x sales multiple compresses toward 15–20x (~$30 or below).
- Macro: December hike becomes a cycle, 10Y through 5.5% → 25–35% further multiple compression plus repriced Iridium financing — $30–38 with no change to fundamentals.
- Dilution compounds: +35% share count since 2022, $3B ATM live, ~$3B Iridium cash component plus Neutron capex against −$371M annual FCF burn; insiders at every level are sellers, not buyers.

**Key Risks**
1. Neutron execution — carbon-composite tank defect unresolved; no new date until Q1'27 (probability: meaningful; impact: severe, binary).
2. FCC/Team Telecom conditions or delay on Iridium, extending financing and dilution exposure into a 5% long end.
3. Rates — the single largest near-term driver of the multiple on a 30x+ sales stock with 2.6–3.6 beta.
4. Working-capital quality (AR growing 3x revenue) and Mynaric/Iridium integration and impairment risk ($619M goodwill/intangibles).
5. SpaceX/Starship resetting medium-lift pricing before Neutron reaches cadence.
