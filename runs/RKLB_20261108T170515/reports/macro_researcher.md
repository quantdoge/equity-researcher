## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional → Risk-Off for long-duration growth. Regime: "Reflation with a hawkish Fed."**

The US economy is *not* landing — it is re-accelerating into a tightening cycle. Real GDP growth bottomed at a near-stall in Q4 2025 (+0.2% annualised) and has sequentially accelerated to **+3.4% annualised in Q3 2026** (+2.1% YoY). Unemployment has fallen to **4.1%** from 4.5% a year ago. But headline CPI has re-accelerated to **3.72% YoY** (Sept 2026), nearly double the 2.65% pace of December 2025, with core at 3.1% — a three-year high.

The Fed's response has been to reverse course. After cutting to 3.63% by mid-2026, it **hiked** to 3.75% (Sept) and 3.88% effective (Oct), then held at a 3.75–4.00% target range in late October. Multiple FOMC participants are openly arguing for further hikes, and futures price a **25bp hike to 4.00–4.25% on 10 December**. This is the single most important macro fact for RKLB: the easing cycle it was priced against in 2025–early 2026 is over.

The long end has repriced violently. The 10Y hit **5.31% on 5 October** before rallying to **4.84% by 5 November** — a 47bp round trip in a month. The curve is positively sloped (2s10s ≈ **+38bp**) but the steepening is **bear steepening** driven by term premium and fiscal supply (30Y ~5.4–5.5%, with the Fed's balance sheet expanding again to $6.75T in a de facto reserve-management QE that sits awkwardly alongside hikes). No inversion, therefore **no recession signal** from the curve — but also no discount-rate relief for long-duration equities.

**Implication for RKLB:** equity markets repriced duration hard in 2026. RKLB fell from a $150.23 close (27 May 2026) to $53.07 — **−65%** — and printed a 2026 closing low of $45.99 on 5 November before rebounding 15% the next day. That drawdown coincided with the 10Y rising ~85bp and with the Neutron slip to 2027. The macro multiple compression and the company-specific catalyst were additive, not alternative, explanations.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

RKLB is a **30–36x sales, pre-profit, long-duration asset** (TTM revenue ~$871M; FY2026E ~$1.0B vs $31.8B cap). Roughly all of its equity value derives from 2029+ cash flows, which makes it among the most rate-sensitive names in Industrials. Beta is **2.61**. It is simultaneously levering up: a **pending ~$7.6B acquisition of Iridium** (merger agreement 28 June 2026) is expected to take total debt to roughly $4B, versus ~$555M in Q1 2026 — a wholesale shift from net-cash to levered.

**The key macro insight:** RKLB faces a *fiscal tailwind and a monetary headwind simultaneously*. Defense space appropriations are booming while the cost of capital needed to fund the Iridium acquisition and Neutron's 2027 ramp is rising. Near-term cash needs are being financed into the most expensive long end since 2007.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Probability-weighted, the macro backdrop subtracts from RKLB. A hiking Fed, 5% long yields, sticky 3.7% inflation, a firm dollar and rising interest expense on acquisition-related debt all press on the same variable — the discount rate applied to 2030+ cash flows — while fixed-price backlog exposure erodes the margin bridge to profitability. These are only partly offset by record defense-space budgets and solid nominal growth. **The macro is a headwind, not a thesis-breaker**; the company-specific Neutron timeline and Iridium integration are larger swing factors, but macro removes the multiple-expansion cushion that a 30x-sales stock requires.

## 5. The Surprise Scenario (Bear)

**"December hike becomes a cycle."** If the 10 December FOMC delivers +25bp to 4.00–4.25% and core inflation prints 3.3%+ in the following months, the market is forced to abandon the "one-and-done" view. Term premium pushes the **10Y back through 5.5% and the 30Y above 6.0%**. Three things happen to RKLB simultaneously: (1) the equity multiple compresses another 25–35% on pure duration; (2) the Iridium acquisition financing reprices, raising interest expense and diluting the deal's accretion narrative; (3) risk appetite for pre-profit space names collapses in favour of defense primes trading on near-term earnings. Given the stock already fell 65% on ~85bp of yield increase, a further 65bp to 5.5% on the 10Y is plausibly a **$30–38 share price** — a further 30–40% downside, entirely from macro, with no change to the Neutron or Iridium story.

*The bull mirror-image:* if the Fed signals a December pause and the 10Y retraces to ~4.2%, RKLB re-rates 40–60% as the discount-rate headwind reverses, amplified by a maiden Neutron flight in 2027.
