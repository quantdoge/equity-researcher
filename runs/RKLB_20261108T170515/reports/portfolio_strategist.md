## 2. Consensus Assessment — **SPLIT (with a specific shape)**

**Agreement level: HIGH on the facts, LOW on the price.**

Six things are effectively unanimous and should be treated as established:
1. **Business quality is improving, not deteriorating** — GM 9%→36% in four years, backlog +137%, Electron cadence 19 missions, share gains vs RDW/LUNR.
2. **No accounting problem.** The Short Analyst's forensic test (cumulative book losses ≈ cumulative cash losses within $14M over five years; negative accruals in all four years) is the most important single finding in the packet and closes the fraud branch of the tree.
3. **The balance sheet is not the risk** — $2.0B+ net cash, no maturity wall, ~6yr runway even in a −20% revenue scenario.
4. **Dilution is structural** — +35% share count since 2022, a live $3B ATM, and consensus 2030 EPS computed on *fewer* shares than exist today.
5. **Neutron is the swing factor** and has now slipped three times, with no new date until Q1'27.
6. **Insider behaviour is one-sided** — zero open-market buys, ~$400M sold, clustered at $118–149 and continuing at $52.

**Where they split is the discount rate, not the forecast.** Value and Growth are both right about different variables: Growth is right about the trajectory; Value is right about the price paid per unit of trajectory. The tie-breaker is the Macro Researcher, and it cuts against the bulls for the next 3–6 months: with the Fed re-hiking and the long end at 4.84%, the multiple that a 33–41× sales stock earns is more likely to fall than rise, independent of Neutron.

**Conflict resolution.** The Value–Growth conflict does not resolve toward fraud (Short Analyst clears it) but *does* resolve toward caution on entry price. The Nonfin Risk insider-selling finding carries **above-weight precedence** in this framework: management with a decade-long horizon is not buying its own equity at a 65% discount. The Short Analyst's "no edge" verdict is read as *permission, not encouragement* — the asymmetry that existed at $150 has largely been paid out.

**Net:** no agent in the packet recommends a full-size long. Even the Growth BUY is conditional on evidence; even the Short says don't short. That convergence points to **a small, staged, risk-budgeted starter — not a conviction position.**

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation — **0.5–0.75% initial; 1.5% maximum**

| Method | Output |
|---|---|
| **Volatility targeting** (risk budget ~50bp of portfolio vol) | 0.50% ÷ 0.958 = **0.52%** |
| **Kelly** (p≈0.70, b≈1.4 on a 12–18mo horizon) | Raw f ≈ 48% — *not usable*; scenario range spans $20–$110, so the edge is not estimable. Apply an ~85–90% uncertainty haircut → **0.5–1.0%** |
| **Risk contribution** (1% weight × 96% vol × 0.5 ρ ≈ 48bp) | A 1% weight consumes **~4% of a 12%-vol portfolio's total risk — 4× its capital weight** |

**Recommended: initiate 0.60% (call it 0.5–0.75%), cap at 1.5%.**
- 1.5% × 96% × 0.5ρ = 72bp of portfolio vol ≈ **6% of total portfolio risk** — that is the hard ceiling for a single pre-profit, unflown-rocket name.
- At 0.60%, a −50% drawdown costs **30bp of portfolio return**. That is the correct maximum tolerable loss for a position whose central case is a 7×-wide valuation range.
- Conviction grade on the framework: *low-medium with multiple risk flags* → the 0.5–1% band, not the 1–3% band.

- **It does not diversify the market bet.** β 3.55 with R² 0.28 means ~72% idiosyncratic variance: it will not hedge a drawdown (a −20% market = ~−50% on RKLB) and it adds 96% standalone vol. 0.60% adds only ~2bp of portfolio beta but ~29bp of portfolio vol.

**Factor exposure.** This is a *deliberately* anti-factor position: 5th percentile Value, 4th Quality, 2nd Low-vol, offset by 95th Momentum. In a value/quality-tilted book it is a large active bet dressed up as a small position. If the book already runs a momentum tilt, RKLB **doubles** the most fragile factor in the model (12-1 momentum is currently +5.46% *only* because the A&D peer set is down 10–14%; one bad quarter erases it).

**Sector concentration.** Industrials/A&D is a fine place to be. But note the profit-pool point the Sector Researcher raises: the *revenue* pool is attractive, the *profit* pool is not — SpaceX captures it and sets price. RKLB is a revenue-growth exposure to space, not an earnings exposure.
