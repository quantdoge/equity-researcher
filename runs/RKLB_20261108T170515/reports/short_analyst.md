# Rocket Lab USA (RKLB) — Forensic Short-Seller Review
**Price $53.07 · Mkt cap ~$31.8B · Beta 2.61 · 6 Nov 2026**

**Headline conclusion: clean books, dirty multiple.** The accounting holds up under forensic stress. The problem is price and a binary engineering catalyst — and at $53 after a 65% drawdown, most of that short is already in the tape.

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **MEDIUM** (not fraudulent)

The earnings-quality score comes back **27/100** and the annual figures look ugly, but both are artefacts of a loss-making company, not manipulation. The forensic tells that *matter* are all pointing the right way:

| Test | Reading | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Accruals ratio (2025) | **−0.0141** | Negative accruals = conservative, not aggressive |
| Accruals 4-yr history | −0.0297 / −0.0889 / −0.1193 / −0.0141 | Consistently negative — cash losses ≥ book losses |
| Receivables vs revenue (2025) | AR **+10.3%** vs rev **+38.0%** | No channel stuffing; collections outpaced sales |
| Inventory days | 175→204→136→**147** | Falling/stable — no demand-weakness build |
| Cash conversion (OCF/NI) | **0.835** | Below 1.0, but the gap is working capital, not accrual inflation |
| Cumulative 2021–25 | Net income **−$824M** vs FCF **−$838M** | Book losses ≈ cash losses. Rare honesty for a story stock |

That last line is the single most important forensic fact about RKLB: over five years, its cumulative GAAP losses and cumulative free cash burn differ by ~$14M. There is no gap for a fraud to hide in. Gross margin is also *genuinely* expanding — −3.0% (2021) → 9.0% → 21.0% → 26.6% → 34.4% (2025) → 36–38% in H1 2026. That is real operating leverage, not a reclassification.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

**1. Cash burn inflected sharply negative — the $105M working-capital swing.** FY25 operating cash flow deteriorated to **−$165.5M** from −$48.9M, a $116.6M worsening. Change in working capital flipped from **+$45.8M to −$58.8M**. Some of this is legitimate Neutron build (inventory +$39M, capex up to **$156.3M** from $67.1M). But it means the reported margin improvement is being partly financed off the balance sheet, and FY25 FCF hit **−$321.8M**. Burn is *accelerating into* the biggest capital program in company history.

**2. Serial dilution — the most underweighted bear point.** Weighted diluted shares: 452M (2021) → 466M → 482M → 496M → 531M (2025) → 605M (Q1'26) → **629.7M (Q2'26)**. That is **+24.5% in five quarters** and +39% since 2021. SBC alone ran **$71.1M in FY25 (11.8% of revenue)**; cumulative SBC 2021–25 ≈ $270M. Then, in 2026, management established a **$3B at-the-market program** — roughly 57M more shares (~9%) at today's price, and potentially far more at lower prices. The stock fell 14.5% on the dilution/insider-selling disclosure.

**3. Consensus EPS is arithmetically inconsistent with the ATM.** The 2030 street EPS of **$0.838** on $444.7M net income implies ~531M shares — i.e. *fewer* shares than RKLB has today, despite a live $3B equity program. Re-base 2030 EPS on ~700M shares and it drops to ~$0.64. Investors are buying an EPS number that assumes the company stops issuing stock. It won't.

**4. Goodwill/intangibles tripled — impairment risk on a distressed asset.** Goodwill & intangibles jumped from **$129.7M to $430.5M** (+$300.8M) in FY25, driven by the **Mynaric** laser-comms acquisition — a target that was in insolvency proceedings. That is now **25% of equity and 18.5% of total assets**, sitting in a business that has never earned a profit.

**5. Neutron: third delay in a year, pushed to 2027, no new date.** Announced 14–15 Oct 2026, citing a manufacturing issue with the carbon composite structure. The stock broke from ~$59 to **$45.99** by 5 Nov. This is the entire bull case (medium-lift, 8-tonne class) and it has slipped repeatedly. Interim revenue growth of 60%+ is coming from Electron and Space Systems — not from the vehicle the valuation is capitalised on.

**6. Beneish "moderate risk" is a false positive — but two components deserve monitoring.** M-Score **−2.193** (inside the −2.22 soft band, above the −1.78 manipulation line). Decomposed, the drivers are benign: **LVGI 0.383** (leverage collapsed *because* of an equity raise — good, not bad), **SGI 1.38** (real growth), **GMI 0.773** (margins improving), **DSRI 0.799** and **SGAI 0.911** (both favourable), **TATA −0.014** (negative accruals). The only components I'd put on a watch list are **AQI 1.192** and **DEPI 1.208** — a rising depreciation index during a capex ramp can signal asset-capitalisation or useful-life extension. Flag it; don't over-claim it.

## 3. Beneish M-Score and Accruals Analysis

M-Score **−2.193** → "MODERATE RISK — possible manipulator" by the model's threshold, but the underlying indices exculpate the company. A model flag driven by *high growth plus a large equity raise* is exactly the false-positive pattern Chanos-style analysts are supposed to see through rather than headline.

Accruals analysis is the cleanest part of the file: balance-sheet accruals ratio **−0.0141** in FY25, negative in all four years, accruals-to-revenue **−5.4%**. There is no earnings inflation. If anything, RKLB under-reports book earnings relative to cash.

**Altman Z of 1.613 ("Distress") is mechanically misleading and I will not use it as a bear point.** X2 (retained earnings/assets) is −0.4353 and X3 (EBIT/assets) −0.0858 purely because of accumulated losses and a pre-profit P&L. Against that, X1 (working capital/assets) is a strong **+0.4436**, X4 is **+2.857**, and the company held **$1.018B of cash/short-term investments** against $254M of debt — a **net cash position of roughly $575M** at YE25, larger today. Liquidity is not the risk. Piotroski F-Score **5/9** (failing: positive ROA, positive OCF, no dilution) is the more honest summary.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

This is where I have to depart from the reflexive bear. RKLB's competitive position is **improving, not eroding**:

- **Backlog rose from $1.847B (Dec-25) to $2.356B (Jun-26)**, with $1.416B tied to longer-cycle contracted work. That is contracted, not pipeline.
- Gross margin expansion of 2,700bp in four years is the *opposite* of pricing-power loss.
- It is the only credible Western small/medium-lift alternative to SpaceX, and Western defence demand for sovereign launch access is a structural tailwind, not a cyclical one.
- Q1'26 revenue **+63.5%**, Q2'26 **+62.0%** — accelerating, and the volume comes from Electron and components, not Neutron.

The genuine competitive threat is **SpaceX**, now visibly a listed $1.77T entity with vastly superior cadence and unit economics, plus Chinese launch pricing on the commercial side. Neutron succeeds into a market where the incumbent is cutting price. That is a real margin ceiling — but it's a 2028+ problem, not a fraud.

Form 4 activity is dense and clustered (25 filings in the recent window, heavy in Jun 2026 and Aug 2026), consistent with systematic programme selling rather than a single opportunistic dump. ## 6. Short Thesis Summary: is there a credible bear case?

**Yes — but it is a multiple-and-execution short, not an accounting short.**

The bear case in one line: a $31.8B equity value on ~$769M of trailing revenue (**41x TTM sales**) that has *never* generated positive operating cash flow and is now burning **$322M/yr**, funding a rocket that is now on its **third delay**, while issuing stock at a 24% five-quarter dilution rate and a $3B ATM.

Valuation really is extreme on any near-term lens:
- **41x** TTM sales; **23x** 2027E revenue ($1.36B); **17x** 2028E ($1.83B)
- Even on **2030** consensus — four years out — the stock trades at **~63x** that year's EPS ($0.838), or **~84x** if you correct the share count for dilution
- For scale: RKLB is worth **~23% of Boeing's market cap on roughly 1% of Boeing's revenue**, and ~24% of Lockheed Martin

**But the other side is stronger than the bears admit.** The stock already peaked at **$150.23 (27 May 2026)** and now sits at **$53.07 — down 64.7%**. Roughly two-thirds of the multiple compression has occurred. Accounting is clean, backlog is growing 28% in six months, gross margins are up 2,700bp, revenue is compounding at 62%, the balance sheet is net cash, and the addressable market is being re-rated upward by defence budgets. To a 15x 2027E revenue multiple the stock is ~$32 (−40%); but a successful Neutron flight in 2027 plausibly supports $90–110. At $53, that is roughly **1.3:1 reward-to-risk with a 2.6-beta, retail-crowded, heavily-shorted tape** — poor short math.

## 7. Contrarian Rating: **NO EDGE** (short thesis was right at $150; largely priced at $53)

To be explicit about my own discipline: this is **not** a Hindenburg situation. There are no phantom revenues, no receivables-inflated growth, no accrual games, no going-concern risk. Anyone pitching RKLB as an accounting fraud is wrong, and I'd reject that thesis outright.

The legitimate short was a **valuation and dilution short at $100–150**, and it has paid. From here I would not initiate a fresh directional short. The only high-conviction, event-driven trade in this name is a **defined-risk position around the Neutron first-flight milestone** — the company has given no firm date, and a fourth slip or a test failure is the one catalyst capable of re-breaking the valuation with no offsetting fundamental support.

**What would make me bearish again:**
1. A fourth Neutron schedule slip, or any stated 2028 first-flight date.
2. FY26 operating cash flow worse than −$250M, or FCF beyond −$400M.
3. Inventory days re-rising above ~170 with revenue decelerating.
4. Any goodwill impairment on Mynaric (watch the Q3'26 10-Q).
5. Gross margin stalling below 34% as Electron volumes scale.
6. Additional equity issuance beyond the $3B ATM being drawn at depressed prices.

**What would validate the bull case and keep me away:** a dated Neutron launch commitment with a clean static-fire, backlog conversion above $2.8B, and operating cash flow inflecting toward breakeven through 2027.
