# AECOM (ACM) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $80.53 | Market cap $10.36B | EV $9.21B (≈$1.1B net cash) | FY ends Sept 30*

---

## 1. Growth Story

AECOM is the largest pure-play infrastructure design and consulting franchise on earth (~51,000 people), and it is monetizing the three biggest capex cycles of the decade — AI/data-center buildout, grid and energy transition, and water/resilience — through a **record $28.0B backlog (+13% YoY) and a 1.3x book-to-burn ratio**. The growth engine is not headline revenue (which is deliberately shrinking as low-margin pass-through construction management rolls off) but **net service revenue (NSR), the fee-based "design" business growing 3–6% organic at record segment margins of 18.2%**, with management guiding FY2027 to 17.6% segment margin and $6.15–6.35 adjusted EPS. The stock has de-rated ~39% from its October 2025 peak on a $337M fixed-price project charge; the Q4 FY2026 print (Nov 16) beat, guided FY2027 above consensus, and the shares re-rated +11% on Nov 17 — the setup is now a **quality compounder priced like a distressed contractor**.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **Global engineering services:** ~$2.0T (IBISWorld 2026) to ~$3.76T (Grand View 2026), growing **4–6.3% CAGR** to $5.8T by 2033.
- **Addressable slice** (large-scale infrastructure design/advisory/program management): roughly **$350–450B**, growing faster than nominal GDP due to AI-infrastructure and grid spend. PwC (Nov 2026) sizes **$3.2T of data-center-driven construction spend** flowing into the sector.
- **Penetration:** AECOM's ~$7.5B NSR equals **~0.2–0.4%** of total engineering services and **~2%** of its true SAM. Backlog of $28.0B covers **~3.7x annual NSR** — visibility is exceptional for a services model.
- **Differentiation:** #1 global position in transportation, water and environmental design; peerless institutional knowledge on mega-projects; growing **Advisory** practice (higher multiple, consulting-like economics); dedicated hyperscale data-center/fab/cleanroom practice. Moat = scale + win-rate + reference portfolio, not patents.
- **Verdict:** TAM is enormous and demand is policy- and AI-capex-supported, but AECOM's realistic capture is low-single-digit share gains. **The bull case is margin + mix + buyback, not TAM capture.** That matters for how you underwrite the multiple.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q1 FY2027 results — Feb 8, 2027** (consensus $1.38 EPS, $1.90B NSR): first proof point on the 4–6% organic NSR guide and 17.6% margin target.
2. **Second CM project delivery, ~end of Q2 FY2027 (March 2027):** management says both fixed-price projects are on timeline/budget, with phase-one substantial completion already achieved. Clean resolution removes the single largest overhang and the "is the next shoe going to drop" discount.
3. **FY2027 record-margin delivery:** every 100bps of segment margin at ~$7.7B NSR is worth ~$0.55–0.60 of EPS — the highest-leverage lever in the model.
4. **Capital return:** dividend raised **+10% to $0.34/qtr (1.7% yield)** with $388M of FY2025 buybacks; ~$1.1B net cash funds further repurchase or tuck-in M&A in Advisory/data-center design.
5. **Data-center / grid award announcements** — backlog conversion from AI-infrastructure and utility capex; watch book-to-burn staying ≥1.1x.
6. **Estimate revision trough-and-turn:** 12 consecutive price-target cuts (Aug–Oct 2026) suggest the downgrade cycle is mature; FY2027 guide above consensus is the first upward datapoint.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (not Strong Buy)

**Why Buy:** At $80.53 the market is paying **12.9x FY2027E adjusted EPS ($6.25 mid)** and **6.7x FY2027E EBITDA ($1.37B mid)** for the #1 global infrastructure design franchise with a record $28B backlog and record margins. Peer comps (trailing): **STN 11.2x EV/EBITDA, TTEK 15.8x, J 22.1x, PWR 37.5x** — ACM trades at a *steep* discount to the cheapest quality peer despite comparable or better backlog visibility. Normalized FCF of ~$700M against a $10.4B cap is ~15x. A 5-year path to ~$9 EPS (FY2029E, one analyst) implies 9x that number today; even 15x FY2029 EPS argues for a $135+ stock. Risk/reward is roughly 2.5:1 with a clean CM outcome.

**Why not Strong Buy:** (a) Rule of 40 is only ~13 on adjusted numbers — this fails a pure high-growth mandate; (b) organic growth is 4–6%, not disruptive hypergrowth; (c) the stock already sits **exactly at the $80.5 consensus price target** after a +38% bounce, so the easy money was made on Nov 17; (d) FY2026's $337M charge is proof that fixed-price execution risk is real, and the second project isn't delivered until ~March 2027.

**Entry discipline:** accumulate on pullbacks toward $70–73 (where the pre-earnings gap and the Baird/Barclays/Truist targets cluster); a decisive close above $90 with book-to-burn >1.1x in Q1 FY2027 would justify upgrading.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Fixed-price execution risk (highest severity):** The $337M pre-tax CM charge wiped out ~$2.00/share of FY2026 EPS and halved net income. A repeat on the second project would destroy credibility in the 17.6% margin guide.
2. **Americas margin deterioration:** Americas adjusted operating income fell **-37% to $566M** in FY2026 while International rose +14% to $393M. Margin recovery is currently an International story — the US is where the data-center upside lives, and it's the weak link.
3. **Revenue optics / pass-through confusion:** Gross revenue -6.5% while NSR grows; some investors and screens (including raw Rule of 40) will read this as contraction. Also note the data inconsistency: consensus revenue estimates ($7.33B FY26 → $8.44B FY28) are on an **NSR basis** versus $15.1B reported gross revenue — do not interpret the "47% revenue cut" in estimates as real.
4. **Policy and funding risk:** US federal infrastructure appropriations, permitting reform and European defense/infrastructure budgets are swing factors; a continuing-resolution or cut scenario hits backlog conversion.
5. **Labor inflation / talent scarcity:** 51,000-person model; wage inflation above billing-rate escalation compresses margins quickly.
6. **Valuation optics:** GAAP P/E is 35.6x and P/FCF 15.3x (FY2025 basis) or ~30x on depressed FY2026 FCF — the "cheap" case depends entirely on the FY2027 guide being met. If FY2027 EPS lands at the low end ($6.15) and margins stall, the stock is a 13x no-growth contractor with a 1.7% yield.
7. **Sentiment overhang:** 12 straight target cuts ($103 → $65 range) and a shareholder base that has been whipsawed twice in 12 months will cap multiple expansion until two clean quarters print.
