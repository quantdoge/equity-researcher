## Bull Case
1. **Backlog conversion + margin delivery:** record $28.0B backlog (~22 months coverage) and a FY27 guide above consensus imply ~53% adjusted EPS growth to $6.25; each 100bp of segment margin adds ~$0.55–0.60.
2. **Cheapest quality franchise in the group:** 12.9x FY27E EPS / ~8.9x EBITDA vs peers at 11–22x; ~$700M normalized FCF (≈6.5% yield) funding ~4–5% annual buybacks plus a 1.7% dividend — ~10–12% per-share value growth even without a re-rating.
3. **Scheduled de-risking:** the second legacy CM project closes ~Mar-2027 and the FY26 10-K will resolve the claims/SEC question; clean outcomes remove the two overhangs that explain the entire discount.

## Bear Case
1. **Earnings quality:** GAAP FY26 EPS is $2.26 vs adjusted ~$4.05; two ~$0.3B charges in four years in the same business line are a recurring operating cost dressed as one-offs, and the adjusted number converts to cash at 0.92x.
2. **The risk stack is correlated and scheduled:** BB credit + $1.0B 2027 maturity (refinanced post-earnings-collapse) + filed class action + $680M claims + alleged breach — three independent agents rated this HIGH for non-overlapping reasons.
3. **Wrong-place growth:** revenue −6.5% vs peers +4.6%; Americas op income −37%; the demand tailwind is concentrated where ACM is weakest (US private capex) and geopolitical risk where it is growing (Gulf). FY27 requires a ~25% EBITDA step-up on a shrinking revenue base.

## Key Risks
1. **Second CM project (~Mar-27):** any further charge destroys the "outlier" narrative and the 17.6% margin guide — highest-probability negative catalyst.
2. **2027 refinancing:** failure to roll the $1.0B notes at acceptable cost, or a covenant waiver request.
3. **Claims accounting escalation:** SEC subpoena, formal investigation, or restatement — thesis-breaking.
4. **Middle East disruption** to the International growth engine (project delays, receivable impairment).
5. **Cash confirmation:** FY27 FCF <$300M, conversion <0.9x, or DSO >110 days would prove backlog is being financed on the balance sheet.
6. **Goodwill impairment** ($3.9B, 144% of book equity) if Americas underdelivers ex-charge.
7. **Macro/fiscal:** FY27 advance-appropriations non-renewal ($36.8B) would hit the public-funded book directly.
