## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — leaning constructive, capped by risk**

There is **no consensus on direction**, but there is unusually strong consensus on *what kind of stock this is*:

- **Agreement (high confidence):** the asset is good (record backlog, #1 global design franchise, real margin progress), the price is cheap on adjusted numbers (12.9x fwd EPS, 8.9x EBITDA, 15.3x FCF, 6.5% FCF yield), and the **quality of those adjusted numbers is poor** (two ~$0.3B "one-off" charges in four years, 0.92x adjusted cash conversion, GAAP EPS $2.26 vs adjusted ~$4.50).
- **Agreement (high confidence):** three independent agents — Geo Legal, Fin Risk, Non-Fin Risk — land on **HIGH risk** for *different, non-overlapping* reasons (claims accounting, refinancing cliff, governance/litigation). That is the single most important fact in this file. Three uncorrelated High ratings is not one risk; it is three.
- **Genuine disagreement:** Value says the discount is insufficient (needs ≤$65); Growth says the discount is excessive (2.5:1 upside). The gap is entirely a **margin-of-safety preference**, not a factual dispute — Value's base IV ($88) and Growth's implied path ($135 by FY29) are reconcilable.
- **The bear is not a bear.** The forensic short analyst explicitly rejects the short at $80.53. That removes the "fraud / accounting manipulation" tail and materially narrows the left tail.

**Resolution rule applied:** where the value case and the risk flags conflict, risk flags take precedence for *sizing*, not for *direction*. The stock is a legitimate long at a small weight; it is not a legitimate full-weight position.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration — acceptable, with one caveat.** ACM is Industrials/E&C. Critically, its correlation to the electrification winners is essentially nil: **PWR 0.07, EME 0.05, DY 0.05**. If the book is overweight the AI/grid capex trade via PWR/EME/DY, ACM adds a *different* exposure, not more of the same. **The caveat:** ACM correlates **0.55 with Jacobs and 0.53 with Tetra Tech** — the "government-services consultant" factor. If the portfolio already owns J or TTEK, ACM is largely redundant and should be sized as an *addition to that bucket*, not a new one.

**Factor exposure — this is the real cost.** Adding ACM increases the portfolio's **value tilt** (good, if intended) while worsening **momentum (bottom quintile, 12-1 of −19.1%), quality (bottom quintile, F-score 4) and volatility (bottom quintile, 41.2% annualized)**. If the book is already value-tilted or already carries high-vol idiosyncratic names, this position is marginal and should be cut to 0.5%.

**Correlation / hedging — do not use beta.** Beta is 0.71 but **R² vs SPY is 0.06** and realized vol is 41.2% (2x the market). Index or beta hedges provide almost no protection; the risk is entirely idiosyncratic (four single-day drops of −8% to −14% in twelve months). **Size to 41% vol, not to 0.71 beta.** A 1% position in ACM carries roughly the risk of a 1.4% position in a market-beta name.

**Credit bucket.** ACM is S&P **BB** with a **$1.0B 2027 maturity**. If the portfolio has a credit-sensitive or high-yield-adjacent sleeve, this position belongs partly there — it is not a pure equity-risk exposure.

**Liquidity.** ~1.3M shares/day, $10.4B cap. No constraint at a 1–2.5% weight.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any one of these, regardless of price:**
1. **A third** construction-management or fixed-price charge. The "non-recurring" narrative dies; the 12.9x forward multiple becomes ~35x on GAAP.
2. **SEC formal investigation/subpoena or DOJ probe** into claims/contract-asset accounting, or any restatement.

4. **FY27 FCF < $300M**, or adjusted-EPS-to-cash conversion below 0.9x, or DSO extending past ~110 days (backlog being financed on the balance sheet).

**Trim triggers:**
- Trim one-third at **$88–92** (base IV to the top of consensus) unless FY27 estimates have revised *up*.
- Trim another third at **$98** (bull IV).

**Monitoring calendar:** Q1 FY27 results **8-Feb-2027** (consensus $1.38 EPS, $1.90B NSR) — first proof of the 4–6% organic guide; second CM project substantial completion **~Mar-2027**; FY26 10-K for the claims footnote and any SEC subpoena disclosure.
