## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: SUB-INVESTMENT GRADE (speculative)**

S&P rates AECOM's senior unsecured notes **'BB'** — the top rung of speculative grade, two notches below investment grade. S&P's stated rationale is that leverage will remain "comfortably below 3x," i.e. the rating is constrained by business/segment risk and the debt stack rather than by current leverage. S&P has also revised the **secured debt recovery rating to '2'**, indicating meaningful subordination of the unsecured notes behind secured term debt.

The paradox here is that **leverage is genuinely low but the rating is not**. That combination — low leverage, junk rating — tells you the market and agencies are pricing *volatility and execution risk*, not balance-sheet insolvency. FY2026 validated that concern.

**The refinancing cliff is the single most important credit fact:** approximately **$1.0 billion of senior unsecured notes matures in 2027** (the Feb-2017 $1.0B 2027 Senior Notes). That is inside ~12 months of the FY2026 balance sheet date. A BB-rated issuer refinancing $1.0B into the current curve, immediately after a 40% operating-income decline and a quarterly operating loss, faces elevated spread and potentially restrictive covenant terms. The Term Loan A facility was increased by ~$334M, which reads as pre-emptive liquidity-building — a mildly defensive signal.

## 4. Downside Scenario: Revenue −20%

**Base (FY2026):** Revenue $15,096M, operating income $620M (4.1% margin), EBITDA $691M, interest expense $192M.

AECOM's cost of revenue runs ~94% of revenue, but much of that is reimbursable pass-through and subcontractor cost that flexes with volume. The genuinely fixed cost is SG&A plus overhead. Using a **20% contribution margin** on lost revenue (consistent with segment operating margins in the 8–9% range and limited near-term cost flex):

| Line | Base FY2026 | Revenue −20% |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $15,096M | $12,077M |
| Operating income | $620M | **~$16M** (breakeven) |
| EBITDA | $691M | **~$90M** |
| Interest expense | $192M | $192M |
| **Interest coverage** | 3.2x | **~0.1x** |
| Net income | $294M | **~−$180M** |
| **Net debt / EBITDA** | ~2.6–2.9x | **~20x** |
| FCF | ~$685M | **negative** |

**Sensitivity:** at a 15% contribution margin, operating income goes to **−$139M**; at 25%, to **+$171M** (coverage 0.9x). In every case, **EBIT no longer covers interest** — the company would be reliant on cash and revolver capacity to service debt.

**Covenant breach:** S&P's framing of "comfortably below 3x" implies a leverage covenant in the 3.5–4.0x region. A 20% revenue decline takes net debt/EBITDA to a double-digit multiple — **a covenant breach is near-certain** in this scenario, triggering a waiver negotiation at exactly the moment the 2027 notes need refinancing.

**Mitigants that materially soften the scenario:**
- **Backlog of $27.8B** (~1.8x annual revenue) — revenue visibility is unusually high for a cyclical; a 20% single-year decline is a low-probability tail, not a base case.
- **Working-capital release:** receivables of $4.36B. A 20% volume decline releases roughly **$850M of cash** over the following 12 months — a genuine liquidity buffer.
- **Cash of $1.24B** plus an undrawn revolver (size not disclosed in the feed).
- Asset-light model: capex is only ~0.8% of revenue, so there is almost no discretionary capex to defend.

**Liquidity verdict under stress:** survivable for 12–18 months on cash + working-capital release + revolver, but **not survivable without a covenant reset and an equity-friendly refinancing**. The binding constraint is the 2027 maturity, not near-term solvency.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating

# **HIGH** (low end of the band)

The rating is driven by **credit and refinancing risk, not by leverage**. On pure balance-sheet arithmetic ACM is a moderate-risk credit: 1.25x debt/equity, 1.4x net debt/EBITDA, ~6x interest coverage, five years of positive FCF, and a 6.6% FCF yield. On every forward-looking measure it is high risk: a **BB (junk) rating**, a **$1.0B 2027 maturity**, a **46% EBITDA decline**, a **quarterly operating loss**, an **F-score collapse from 7 to 4**, a **Z-score of ~2.0 in the grey zone**, and a stock that has lost 39% from its high with a negative Sharpe ratio.

Sub-rating: **Credit risk — High. Liquidity risk — Moderate. Market risk — High (idiosyncratic). FCF sustainability — Moderate.**

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Sub-investment-grade rating (S&P 'BB')** with secured recovery rating revised to '2' — unsecured holders are structurally subordinated.
2. **~$1.0B senior notes mature in 2027** — a refinancing cliff inside 12 months, at junk spreads, immediately post-earnings-collapse. *This is the #1 risk.*
3. **FY2026 operating income −40% to $620M; EBITDA −46% to $691M; net income −48% to $294M** on only a 6.5% revenue decline — severe negative operating leverage.
4. **Q3 FY2026 operating loss** driven by a **Construction Management charge** (company reported an operating loss of $139M and an adjusted operating loss of $130M; the financial-statement feed shows −$76M — a definitional gap worth resolving). This is an **execution/risk-control failure**, the most dangerous kind for an engineering & construction firm, and it undermines confidence in project bidding discipline.
5. **Piotroski F-Score 4/9 (Weak)**, down from ~7 — broad-based deterioration across margins, leverage, equity, and turnover.
6. **Altman Z-Score 1.73 on book equity (distress zone)**; only market-cap substitution lifts it to 2.21. Equity valuation is load-bearing for the credit picture.
7. **Accumulated deficit of −$1.22B** in retained earnings — no organic equity cushion; growth has been debt- and acquisition-funded.
8. **Thin liquidity:** current ratio 1.14x, cash ratio 0.27x. Adequate, not comfortable.
9. **Negative Sharpe (−0.14) with 31.9% volatility and a 56% max drawdown** — investors are bearing idiosyncratic risk without compensation.
10. **Data gaps:** FY2026 balance sheet (debt, current liabilities, retained earnings, goodwill, PP&E) and FY2026 cash flow are **absent from the feed**. FY2026 net debt/EBITDA (~2.6–2.9x) and cash ratio are **my estimates**, not reported figures. Revolver size and covenant thresholds are **not disclosed** in available data. FY2026 FCF is unverified.

**What would change the rating:** a clean refinancing of the 2027 notes at reasonable cost, restoration of segment operating margins above 8%, and evidence that the Construction Management charge is contained rather than a systemic project-risk problem. Absent those, the trajectory is toward **Critical** if FY2027 shows a second consecutive year of margin erosion.
