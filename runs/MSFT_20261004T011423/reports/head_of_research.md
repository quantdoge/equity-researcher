**Investment Thesis**
Microsoft is the highest-quality mega-cap compounder in the market — a widening enterprise moat, accelerating ~18% revenue growth on a $332B base, 46.8% operating margin and ~30% ROIC. But at $517 the market pays full price for flawless execution (28.8× P/E, 57× reported P/FCF) while a once-in-a-generation capex cycle ($116B FY26 → ~$190B guided FY27) suppresses cash conversion and 5%+ long-dated yields punish exactly this stock's duration. Verdict: own it at disciplined size, add only on 10–25% drawdowns of an intact franchise, and exit only on demonstrated thesis invalidation, not volatility.
