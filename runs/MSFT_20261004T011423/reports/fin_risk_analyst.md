# MSFT — Financial Risk Assessment
**Microsoft Corporation | Software–Infrastructure | FYE June 30, 2026 | Price $517.53 | Mkt Cap ~$3.84T**

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment: **Investment Grade (AAA)**

Microsoft carries the highest possible credit ratings (S&P/Moody's **AAA/Aaa**, confirmed via secondary sources). The quantitative picture fully supports this: leverage is trivial, interest coverage is ~51x, and there is no near-term solvency threat. The Altman Z-score prints 2.68 ("grey zone") **only because the tool uses book equity for x4** — substituting market cap (~$3.84T) for book equity (~$442B) yields a Z-score north of ~4.9, squarely in the safe zone. This is a measurement artifact, not a credit signal.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **FCF conversion is deteriorating at the margin.** FY26 FCF fell to $67.0B (from $71.6B FY25) despite revenue +18% — capex doubled to **$115.9B** (AI/datacenter buildout). FCF margin slipped from ~25% to ~20%.
2. **Cash balance declining while debt rises.** Cash dropped $94.6B→$76.8B YoY; total debt up to $128.8B — the buildout is partly debt-funded.
3. **Rising capital intensity.** PP&E tripled in 3 years ($110B→$337B); gross margin ticked down (68.8→67.9%) and asset turnover fell — the two Piotroski points MSFT lost. ROIC on the AI buildout is the key variable to watch.
4. **Rich valuation + rate sensitivity.** 1.7% FCF yield and ~29x earnings give thin cushion if cloud/AI growth decelerates — evidenced by the 34.5% drawdown over the last 3 years despite flawless fundamentals.
5. **Data gaps:** No debt-maturity schedule tool beyond ST/LT split ($9.2B / $31.1B within total debt of $128.8B); no live CDS spreads; credit rating confirmed via secondary sources rather than a direct feed.

**Bottom line:** From a credit and liquidity standpoint MSFT is as safe as large-cap equity gets — comfortably investment grade with no covenant, refinancing, or solvency risk in any realistic stress. The genuine risk is market-side: elevated valuation on a high-duration asset in a ~5% rate environment, with the only fundamental soft spot being FCF compression from the aggressive capex program. Overall risk: **Low**.
