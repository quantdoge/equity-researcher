**Investment Thesis**

CoreWeave is the best *operator* in the AI-neocloud sector and arguably the worst *security* in it. The demand side is exceptional — $104–129bn of contracted backlog (~8–10x FY26E revenue, ~2.3x EV), capacity sold out past 2028, best-in-class technical execution. But the equity is a thin levered residual (~$5bn book equity, ~$31bn market cap) on ~$51bn of debt priced at ~15% into a Fed that is hiking, with a ~$33bn 2027 funding wall, negative and accelerating FCF (−$13.7bn TTM), 3/10 governance, and forensic earnings-quality flags. Base-case intrinsic value ($45–60) merely brackets the $56.32 price: there is no margin of safety, and the asymmetry favours the downside until the capital structure is resolved. The next 60 days — Q3 earnings on 9 November and the 2027 refinancing path — are decisive.
