# CoreWeave (CRWV) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $56.32 (10/26/26 close) · Market cap ~$31B (551M diluted sh.) · EV ~$56B · 70% below $187 ATH**

## 1. Growth Story
CoreWeave is the purest listed play on AI compute scarcity: a purpose-built GPU cloud that has converted itself from a crypto-mining also-ran into the contracted infrastructure layer beneath Microsoft, Meta and Anthropic. The Q2 2026 print validated the demand thesis emphatically — revenue **+112% YoY**, revenue backlog **$104B** (excluding **>$25B** of new Q3 commitments), **1.5GW** active power, and management explicitly stating demand exceeds supply **beyond 2028**. The debate is no longer whether the TAM exists; it is whether the company can fund $35–39B of 2026 capex at bond yields near 15–16% without the capital structure becoming the thesis.

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration Opportunity
The AI infrastructure market is tracking toward **$1T+ of annual data center capex by 2030**; neoclouds are taking a rising share because hyperscalers cannot build fast enough. CoreWeave's **$104B backlog plus >$25B Q3 commitments (~$129B)** equals **2.3x enterprise value** and roughly 10x FY26 revenue — an extraordinary visibility ratio for a $31B-cap company. Backing: Meta's **$21B** incremental commitment through 2032, a multi-year **Anthropic** agreement, and a **$6B** Jane Street commitment. Contracted power at **1.5GW → 1.85GW by YE26** is the physical penetration metric; at ~$8–10B revenue per GW annualized, 1.85GW supports a ~$15–18B run-rate exiting 2026, consistent with FY27 consensus. Penetration is still low single digits of global AI compute. **Moat:** Nvidia allocation priority, "Mission Control" fleet orchestration software, Platinum-tier reliability track record, and the largest GPU footprint among neoclouds (~10x Nebius). That moat is real but *rented* — it rests on Nvidia preference and debt-funded hardware, not on a demand-side network effect.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts
1. **Q3 2026 earnings — Nov 9, 2026.** Guide $3.4–3.6B revenue (+~158% YoY) vs. $3.54B consensus, EPS -$1.28. First test of whether 1.85GW converts on schedule. Binary.
2. **Backlog disclosure / new hyperscaler signings.** Q3 total likely ~$129B+; any Microsoft, Meta, Anthropic or new-enterprise expansion announcement re-rates the multiple instantly.
3. **Financing events (highest-impact).** Bond yields at 15–16% with record CDS; a successful secured/ABS refinancing at tighter spreads — or any drop in the 10Y on softer inflation prints — would compress the discount rate and re-rate the equity. Conversely, a failed raise is the bear case trigger.
4. **Vera Rubin / Blackwell ramp (2027).** Management says "pricing and margins for Blackwell and Vera Rubin SKUs are setting new highs," and prior-gen GPU pricing is at or above multi-year levels. Next-gen deployment lifts ASPs and gross margin.
5. **Power and permitting.** 1.85GW year-end milestone; offset by New York's July 2026 moratorium on large-scale data centers. Favorable new-region approvals would extend the runway.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY (speculative, position-sized)**
Not a Strong Buy, because the framework's own health test fails: Rule of 40 = 26.6, gross margin compressing 8pp YoY, FCF -$13.7B TTM, and a balance sheet carrying **$35B of debt at ~15–16%** that consumes 38% of gross profit in interest alone. But the growth quality — 60–75% forward CAGR, a ~$129B backlog at 2.3x EV, 1.5GW scaling to 1.85GW, demand exceeding supply past 2028 — is exactly the durable-disruption profile that deserves a premium multiple. At **2.1x EV/FY27 revenue** and **1.3x EV/FY28 revenue**, the market is pricing near-zero terminal value beyond backlog conversion. The 70% drawdown was a **rates/debt** de-rating, not a demand de-rating. If Q3 confirms the GW ramp and yields stabilize, the path to the ~$104 median target (+85%) is mechanical. **Treat as a levered call option on AI compute demand plus falling rates — size accordingly.**

## 7. Key Risks
1. **Financing / rate risk (dominant).** $35B debt, 15–16% bond yields, record CDS, $640M/qtr interest expense and rising. The Oct 14 −15.5% day and −25% week were triggered by inflation data and borrowing-cost fears — not by operations. A failed refinancing is an existential-equity risk.
2. **Customer concentration.** Microsoft has historically been the majority of revenue; Meta and Anthropic reduce but don't eliminate concentration — and **Meta is reportedly considering launching its own cloud**, making it simultaneously the largest customer and a future competitor.
3. **GPU depreciation / obsolescence.** D&A is **54% of revenue**. Useful-life assumptions are the key accounting lever; shortening to 4 years or a Rubin-driven write-down would erase the ~8% adjusted operating margin and the FY28+ profit trajectory (FY28E EPS of $0.87 has a range of **-$1.49 to +$3.68** across 15 analysts — an enormous dispersion).
4. **Gross margin compression.** 74.3% → 65.9% in six quarters as depreciation scales. Scale is *not* producing margin leverage — the opposite of the thesis's ideal pattern.
5. **Competition.** Nebius (+240% CAGR), SpaceX selling excess compute, Lambda/Crusoe/Groq, hyperscaler insourcing, and hyperscalers' own capacity buildout. Pricing is currently favorable; that is a cyclical, not structural, comfort.
6. **Dilution and volatility.** Diluted shares 246M → 551M in ~15 months; beta 7.3. Backlog definition includes non-binding estimates and cancellation-flexible hyperscaler contracts — the $129B is not all firm.
7. **Power/regulatory.** New York's large-scale data center moratorium plus rising local opposition could slow the GW roadmap that every estimate depends on.
