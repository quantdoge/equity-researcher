All three series spiked simultaneously in May–June 2026 (event-driven attention bubble), then mean-reverted below prior-year baseline.

**Web traffic — datadoghq.com (SimilarWeb public data): ✅ Mildly Positive**

Traffic is stable-to-up, which argues against a demand air-pocket even as *search interest* fades.

> Interpretation: the divergence matters. **Product usage proxies (traffic, engagement) are holding; attention proxies (search) are fading.** For a consumption-based model, the traffic signal is the more revenue-relevant of the two.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **IN-LINE** (low-end beat bias)

**Historical surprise record — strong and consistent:**
- **7/7 beats**, avg EPS surprise **+12.3%**.
- Revenue beats: Q2'26 +3.9%, Q1'26 +4.8%, Q4'25 +3.75%, Q3'25 +3.9%, Q2'25 +4.5%, Q1'25 +2.7% — a remarkably stable **~+3–5% revenue beat cadence**.
- ⚠️ **Deceleration in surprise magnitude:** EPS surprise fell from **+18.1%** (Q1'26) to **+11.5%** (Q2'26). Revenue YoY decelerated 35.6% → ~29.4% consensus for Q3.

**The bar itself looks undemanding on revenue:**
- Consensus Q3 revenue ≈ **$1.146–1.15B**, which implies only **+2.2% QoQ** (Q2'26 actual $1,121.5M). Last year's Q2→Q3 sequential was **+7.1%**. Either the consensus figure is stale relative to a raised Q2 guide, or the seasonal bar is unusually low. Historical beat cadence (+4%) applied to $1.146B → **~$1.19B**, consistent with normal seasonality.
- Estimates have **not been cut** during October: NTM normalized EPS steady at ~$2.65 on Sep 30 and Oct 30; 2026–28 revenue estimates *edged up*. Street mean PT rose to **~$294** from ~$286 (Wells Fargo to $325 from $295 on Oct 28). The October de-rate was pure multiple compression (**fwd P/E 103x → 89x**).

**Where alt data disagrees with the setup:** demand-attention proxies are tracking *below* prior-quarter trends, so I do not see evidence supporting an *acceleration*-driven blowout. Q2 was a cautionary template: **EPS +11.5% beat, revenue +3.9% beat, stock -19%** on Aug 6 — the beat is priced; **guidance, consumption growth and AI-native customer contribution** are the swing factors.

**Verdict:** In-line to a modest (~3–4%) revenue beat on a soft sequential bar, but the alt-data demand picture does not support upside guidance revision. Asymmetric risk is to the *print vs. reaction* gap.
