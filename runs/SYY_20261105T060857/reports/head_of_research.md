**Investment Thesis**

Sysco is the best operator in foodservice distribution — #1 gross margin (18.5%), #1 operating margin (3.66%), 1.5x cash conversion, four years of negative accruals — trading at roughly 100–105% of its standalone intrinsic value (~$72 midpoint). The $29.1B Jetro Restaurant Depot acquisition has converted a low-beta staple into a levered, binary antitrust bet: ~$21B of new debt into a 5%+ long-end market, an estimated 4.5–5.6x pro forma leverage, and a 35–45% probability of an FTC block in the same product market where the FTC already stopped Sysco in 2015. Eleven of eleven specialists found no reason to buy at $76.94 — and the short side was explicitly rejected too (mispricing already harvested). This is an index-like expected return for above-index risk.
