## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — Revenue −20%

Applied to FY2026 standalone (revenue $67.6B). Assumptions: gross margin −50bps to 18.0% (lost volume leverage, adverse mix); opex −12% (labor and route density are semi-fixed — SG&A derived as $12,544M, 14.8% of revenue); interest at $900M reflecting partial refinancing at 5%+ rates.

| Line item | Base | −20% Revenue | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 84,553 | 67,642 | −20.0% |
| Gross profit | 15,639 | 12,176 | −22.1% |
| Operating expense | 12,544 | 11,039 | −12.0% |
| **Operating income** | **3,095** | **1,137** | **−63.3%** |
| Interest expense | 717 | 900 | +25.5% |
| Pre-tax income | 2,378 | **237** | −90.0% |
| EBITDA | 4,122 | 2,266 | −45.0% |
| **Interest coverage** | 4.32x | **1.26x** | Covenant stress |
| **Net debt / EBITDA** | 3.20x | **5.82x** | Likely breach |
| FCF (incl. WC release) | 1,938 | ~1,000–1,100 | Dividend just covered |

**Verdict on downside:** A 20% revenue shock produces a **~63% operating income collapse** and pushes net debt/EBITDA to **~5.8x**, almost certainly breaching a maintenance covenant in the 4.0–4.5x range. Mitigants: (a) working capital *releases* cash in a downturn — inventory ($5.3B) and receivables ($5.9B) shrink, providing a ~$500–700M liquidity buffer; (b) the dividend would consume essentially all FCF with zero cushion, likely forcing a cut; (c) capex is already near maintenance levels, limiting further defense. **The buyback would be suspended immediately.** Note the structural asymmetry: at a 3.66% operating margin, a 20% revenue decline destroys two-thirds of operating profit — this is a low-margin, high-operating-leverage business with very little room for volume loss.

**Pro-forma overlay:** apply that 5.8x to a ~$30B debt stack and the scenario is not survivable without equity issuance, asset sales, or a covenant waiver. This is the core reason the rating agencies moved.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating

# HIGH

**Standalone (pre-deal): MODERATE.** Defensive sector, positive FCF, manageable 3.2x leverage, long-dated new debt maturities (2029–2066, so **no near-term refinancing cliff**), and only $1.05B of notes maturing in July 2026. On its own this is a BBB business.

**Pro-forma (post-JRD): HIGH.** The rating is driven by four compounding factors: (1) leverage roughly doubling at the same time as (2) interest coverage has already fallen for four consecutive years (5.34x → 4.32x); (3) equity is only 9% of assets and **tangible equity is negative $3.5B**, leaving no loss-absorbing cushion; and (4) all three agencies are on negative/watch, one notch of comfort above sub-investment grade. A defensive revenue base does not compensate for a balance sheet being levered ~2x larger into a 5%+ rate environment.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **$29B debt-and-stock funded acquisition closing into FY2027** — ~19% dilution (91.5M new shares) and roughly a doubling of gross debt. Integration and deleveraging risk is the dominant credit issue.
2. **Three simultaneous negative rating actions** — Moody's cut to Baa2; S&P outlook Negative; Fitch Rating Watch Negative. Consensus direction is one-way.
3. **Interest coverage eroding every year for four years** (5.34x → 4.32x) while interest expense rose **+36%** in three years. Refinancing now occurs with the 10Y at **5.13%** and 5Y at **4.93%** — well above legacy coupons.
4. **Negative tangible book equity (−$3.51B)** against $6.18B of goodwill/intangibles. A deal-related goodwill writedown would hit an already threadbare equity base.
5. **Earnings quality divergence:** revenue +7.2% over two years but net income **−10.1%**, and EBITDA has declined every year since FY2024. Growth is not converting to profit.
6. **FY26 FCF "recovery" is capex-driven, not operational** — capex cut $206M while operating cash flow rose only $128M.
7. **Thin absolute liquidity:** $1.79B cash against a ~$30B pro-forma debt stack; quick ratio 0.77. Liquidity is inventory- and receivables-based, both of which are cyclical.
8. **Q1 FY27 GAAP operating income fell 10.4%** (adjusted +3.2%) — restructuring/acquisition costs are already diluting reported results, with the deal not yet closed.
9. **Poor risk-adjusted equity returns:** ~0.02 true Sharpe; 24% max drawdown driven by company-specific (deal) risk rather than market beta.
