### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (slight bearish tilt on volumes, neutral on EPS)**
The alt-data picture says the *earnings* trajectory is intact (cost program, steady logistics hiring, growing digital order flow, reiterated guidance, strong beat rate) but the *demand* trajectory is softening (restaurant reservation search interest down double-digits YoY into the Dec quarter). Nothing in the alternative data justifies the 9.3% drawdown nor argues against it — that move was a capital-structure and regulatory event (Jetro/FTC, $21B debt) that alt data cannot adjudicate.

**Actionable framing:** alt data is consistent with an *in-line EPS / soft-revenue* pattern repeating on Feb 2 — i.e., risk is asymmetric to the top line and to FY27 volume guidance, not to the EPS line. The softness is already partly discounted after the Q1 reset, so this is not a short signal; it is a "don't underwrite a volume-driven beat" signal.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM-LOW**
**Strengths:** fresh (prices/news through Nov 4–5), multiple independent sources agreeing on the same directional story, seven-quarter surprise history with clean data.

**Limitations to flag to the desk:**
- **Web traffic** is only available via SimilarWeb search snippets (MoM % and engagement metrics, no monthly series) — cannot compute a multi-month trend line.
- **Job postings** are search-result counts, not a normalized index; no baseline or YoY comparison.
- **Patent data unavailable** (API failure, two attempts) — immaterial here but noted.
- **Social sentiment** is a search proxy, not an API; Reddit returns stale content.
- **Google Trends for "Sysco"** is not a consumer demand read for a B2B distributor; the reservation proxy is directional only and confounded by seasonality (holiday booking seasonality Dec–Feb will flatter the next data points, so treat the Feb print read cautiously).
- Biggest swing factors for SYY — FTC clearance timing of Jetro, deal financing terms, food deflation magnitude, fuel — are **not observable in alt data**. This signal should be weighted as a secondary confirmatory input, not a primary thesis driver.
