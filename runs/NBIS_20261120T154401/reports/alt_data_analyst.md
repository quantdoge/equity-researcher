### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Data limitations:**

- **Job postings unquantified** — search proxy only, no time series; cannot measure acceleration.
- **Web traffic is a search-scraped estimate, single snapshot, no monthly series** — the +10.7% MoM figure is directionally useful, not auditable.
- Google Trends on product keywords too sparse to read.
- Q3'26 EPS "beat" of +141% is one-off driven; **do not use EPS surprise as an operating signal.**
- All feeds lag 1–2 weeks; the 11/12–11/20 collapse is post-earnings, so alt data partially predates the de-rating.

**Bottom line for the memo:** Alt data does not support the bear case on Nebius's *demand* — traffic, hiring, ARR and revenue surprise trend all point up. The risk the market is pricing is **financing, and alt data confirms it**: leverage up 12x QoQ on negative operating cash flow. The Q4 print itself is likely a modest revenue beat, but a beat will not resolve the equity until the funding structure is de-risked. Watch Q4 capex guidance and any equity/debt raise as the real catalysts — not the revenue line.
