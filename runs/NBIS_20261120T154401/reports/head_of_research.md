**Investment Thesis**

Nebius is the #2 neocloud globally, with real and accelerating fundamentals — ARR of $6.4B (doubled QoQ), $38.7B RPO, 77.6% gross margin, and the sector's first positive GAAP operating-income quarter. But the equity is the residual claimant on a project-finance structure: $40–45B of planned 2027 capex against a ~$26B market cap, $16.1B of debt, 0.4x EBIT interest coverage, and converts struck at $313–325 (3.6x above spot) that behave as straight debt. At $88 the stock trades at our probability-weighted fair value (~$82) with zero margin of safety — the bull case requires flawless execution, the bear case requires only that the credit window stays shut. This is a financing-liquidity story wearing a growth-stock costume, and the macro tape (10Y 5.13%, HY +87bp, live hiking cycle) is pointed directly at it.

**Bull Case**
1. **Contracted hyper-growth at a de-rated multiple**: $38.7B RPO covers ~3 years of guided 2027 revenue ($12–13B); at ~1.2x 2028E revenue the market prices growth to stall — delivery of ~$25B 2028 revenue at ~29% EBITDA margins supports a $58–73B EV (2–2.5x today's).
2. **Customer-financed build**: prepayments covered >100% of Q3 capex; $17–20B expected for 2027 — growth without proportional equity dilution if the model holds.
3. **Operating inflection is real**: gross margin up 8pts across five quarters, first GAAP operating-profit quarter in the peer group, best-in-class unit economics and engineering (PUE 1.25), with non-correlated optionality (Avride, Toloka, TripleTen).

**Bear Case**
1. **Financing wall**: $40–45B capex = 1.5–2x market cap; converts are 3.6x out-of-the-money (straight debt); equity raises at $88 are 30–40% dilutive; the AI junk window is narrowing. Loss of *access*, not coupon cost, is the kill shot.
2. **No margin of safety, negative carry**: 0.4x EBIT interest coverage, –$12.4B FCF, ROIC below WACC, depreciation policy flattering EBITDA (6.6% annualized on $21.5B PP&E).
3. **Concentration and obsolescence**: Microsoft + Meta ≈ 80% of commitments; hyperscalers are simultaneously customers and competitors; a 20% revenue shock swings EBIT negative and net leverage to ~8.5x, with impairment risk on the PP&E base.

**Key Risks**
- December hike + sticky CPI pushing 10Y toward 5.75–6% → credit window shuts, forced dilutive raise (bear case $48–62, –30–45%)
- Microsoft delivery-milestone slip, scope reduction, or second guidance cut
- BIS licence denial/delay for third-country deployments; strike/force-majeure on Israeli capacity
- Equity/convert issuance at a discount; failure to remediate ICFR weaknesses by end-2026; further insider selling
- Securities class action (drawdown + guidance cut is a textbook predicate; not yet filed)
