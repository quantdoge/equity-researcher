## Nebius Group N.V. (NBIS) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $88.22 (Nov 20, 2026) · Mkt cap ≈$22–26B (share count in flux) · EV ≈$30–35B · Beta 1.56*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Nebius is a pure-play "neocloud" — purpose-built GPU/AI infrastructure (1,543 employees, ~$2M revenue per head) that has gone from near-zero to a **$6.4B annualized run-rate in roughly seven quarters**, with $38.7B of contracted RPO and a ~$100B backlog anchored by Microsoft and a $27B Meta agreement ($12B firm, 5 years, starting early 2027 + $15B optional). Management is now guiding **2027 revenue of $12–13B** — a ~4x on 2026 — against a capex program of $20–25B in 2026 (already tracking toward ~$27B) and reportedly $40–45B in 2027. This is not a software compounder; it is an asset-backed AI-factory buildout being financed by customer prepayments ($17–20B expected in 2027, 40–50% of capex) plus asset-backed debt (25–45%) plus equity. The disruptive thesis: Nebius is one of maybe three credible Western merchant alternatives to hyperscaler compute, and it is scaling into a supply-constrained market where customers pay years in advance for capacity.

### 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration Opportunity

- **TAM:** Global AI infrastructure spend. Hyperscaler capex alone is running >$500B/yr and merchant GPU-cloud demand is supply-constrained, not demand-constrained — Nebius has sold out capacity through 2026.
- **Penetration:** At $6.4B ARR against a multi-hundred-billion-dollar annual AI infra market, Nebius holds **low single-digit share of a market growing 40–60%/yr**. Even the FY2030 consensus of $57.3B revenue implies <5% share. Runway is the single best argument for the stock.
- **How much can they capture?** Capacity is the binding constraint: management plans to add **>1 GW** in 2027, with multi-GW sites across Finland, France, Spain and the US. Power, not demand, sets the ceiling.
- **Differentiation:** Unlike hyperscalers, Nebius is merchant-neutral and Kubernetes-native with a full AI Cloud stack (managed inference, token factory, Soperator); unlike pure crypto-converts (IREN, Cipher), it has multi-year hyperscaler contracts with prepayment terms. Its closest true comp is CoreWeave.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4/FY2026 results — ~Feb 11, 2027.** Q4 revenue est $1.22B (+434%); first read on whether the 2027 $12–13B ramp has slipped. Highest-impact near-term event.
- **2027 capacity deliveries / GW commissioning.** Each new data hall coming online is a revenue step-function; the company has already flagged capacity delays, so any on-time delivery re-rates the stock.
- **New contract announcements beyond Microsoft/Meta.** A third anchor tenant (Oracle, Anthropic, sovereign AI programs) would de-risk customer concentration and validate the ~$100B backlog.
- **Financing execution — the biggest swing factor.** $17–20B of 2027 prepayments + $3.75B converts already raised + asset-backed facilities. If the 75–95% funding coverage holds without a deeply dilutive equity raise, the equity story works; if not, per-share value erodes.
- **Analyst target resets post-drawdown.** Targets are being cut (Baird $340→$200; Piper $237→$185) even as EPS estimates rise — consensus PT ~$220–260 vs $88 spot. A stabilization in target trajectory is itself a catalyst.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high-conviction, high-volatility — size accordingly)

**Valuation math at $88.22:**
| Multiple | NBIS | CRWV | IREN |
|---|---|---|---|
| EV / FY2027E revenue | **~2.5x** | ~3.5x | ~3.0x |
| EV / FY2028E revenue | **~1.2x** | ~1.0x | ~0.85x |
| EV/EBITDA (trailing) | ~47x | 17.7x | 173x |

At 2.5x 2027 revenue and 1.2x 2028 revenue, the market is capitalizing Nebius as if growth stalls and capex destroys capital. If it delivers $25B of 2028 revenue at a 29% EBITDA margin ($7.3B consensus) and the market pays 8–10x EV/EBITDA, EV is $58–73B — **2–2.5x today's enterprise value**. Conversely, the $40–45B 2027 capex against a ~$26B market cap means equity is the residual claimant on a project-finance structure; a funding air-pocket or a GW delay is genuinely value-destructive per share, which is exactly why the multiple collapsed.

**Why not Strong Buy:** the Rule of 40 in its honest, FCF-based form is catastrophically negative (−216% on FY2025, −772% FCF margin in Q3'26 alone). Growth is being purchased with debt and dilution at a share count that has already risen from 236M to 298M in six quarters. This is the highest-beta way to own the AI buildout, not the safest.

**What would make me upgrade to Strong Buy:** two consecutive quarters of on-time GW deliveries, a third anchor tenant, and a financing package that keeps equity issuance below ~10% of market cap.

---

### 7. Key Risks

1. **Financing/dilution risk (highest).** $40–45B of 2027 capex vs ~$26B market cap. The company claims 75–95% is covered by prepayments and asset-backed debt — the uncovered residual is raised in equity, and the stock is 69% off its high. Dilution compounds against per-share value.
2. **Customer concentration.** Microsoft + Meta dominate the ~$100B backlog, and Meta's $15B piece is *optional* beyond the $12B firm commitment. A single scope reduction would gut the 2027–28 ramp.
3. **Capacity execution.** Management has already cited capacity delays; 1 GW+ of new capacity in 12 months is a supply-chain, power-interconnect and construction gamble. Guidance has been trimmed once (FY2026 $3.0–3.4B).
4. **GPU obsolescence / depreciation.** $354.7M quarterly D&A and rising. If Nvidia's annual cadence shortens useful GPU life, 77% gross margins compress hard and the "asset-backed" debt collateral weakens.
5. **Revenue quality.** A third of Q3 revenue was "partnership revenue," and $12B–13B of 2027 guidance depends on converting RPO to billings on schedule.
6. **The reflexive AI-capex cycle.** If hyperscaler capex decelerates in 2027–28, merchant neoclouds are the first thing cut — and this equity has already shown it can lose two-thirds of its value on sentiment alone.
7. **Estimate fragility.** Only 4–7 analysts model 2028–30; the 2027 EPS range spans a sign flip. Treat long-dated consensus ($57B revenue / $21 EPS by 2030) as directional, not bankable.

---

**Bottom line:** Nebius is the highest-torque public pure-play on AI compute scarcity, priced at ~1.2x two-year-forward revenue after a 69% drawdown. The operating data — ARR, RPO, gross margin, estimate revisions, 80% beat rate — is unambiguously inflecting up. The debate has shifted entirely to balance-sheet engineering and construction execution, and that is a risk I would underwrite at this multiple, with position sizing that respects a 1.56 beta and 70% drawdown history.
