### Investment Memo: SITM — 25 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate; trim into strength | Conviction: Low (agents materially split)

**Investment Thesis**
SiTime is a genuinely exceptional business attached to a currently un-investable security. Organic revenue +154% y/y, gross margin at peer parity, and the Renesas deal creating the world's second-broadest timing portfolio confirm real AI-infrastructure demand and pricing power. But the equity is a 3.5-beta, 98%-vol, long-duration asset trading ~40% above base-case intrinsic value, into a hawkish-Fed / 5.13%-10Y regime, carrying negative tangible book, SBC-funded "cash flow," 62% Greater China revenue concentration, and active securities litigation over the exact window of its AI rally. Probability-weighted fair value (~$314) sits below the price: the demand story is bull-grade, the risk-reward is not.

**Key Findings by Domain**

SITM is the growth leader (+61% FY25 vs +20% peer median) with GM at parity (56.2% vs 58.0%); the −20pt operating-margin gap is opex/integration, not product economics. Macro **Unfavourable**: Fed re-hiking (3.88%, real FF ~0%), 10Y 5.13%, bear-steepening term premium — the single largest driver of the −58% drawdown. Tell: HY OAS >4.5% breaks the AI-capex trade.

Section 301/232 tariff vectors live; China's critical-minerals suspension expires Jan 2027. HSR cleared; no disclosed material litigation, but post-close geographic mix undisclosed.
