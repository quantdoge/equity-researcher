The signal is **directionally bullish but not incrementally new** — the market already repriced it on 24-Nov (+11.2% on ~3x normal volume).

**Key divergence to flag:** the stock is **+63% since 1-May-2026** ($94.67 → $154.65) and **+23% in the last month**, yet **Google search interest is down ~10% YoY** and web traffic is only +3% MoM. That is a real, defensible narrative — but it means the re-rating is already well-recognised and the next leg requires *sustained* margin delivery, not volume upside. Watch Q4 for margin give-back from tariff-refund reinvestment and holiday promotional intensity, which the news flow does not price.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:** three of the seven signals are **search-proxy only** (job postings, web traffic, social), with no longitudinal quantitative series; **patent data failed entirely**; Google Trends is directional and seasonally noisy; the social mention base is thin (~54/day); and there is a **revenue-series inconsistency** between the earnings calendar and company reporting. The genuinely high-quality inputs are the **earnings surprise history** and **news sentiment**, both of which are strongly positive.

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly and lagged ~3–5 days; SimilarWeb comparison data is point-in-time and excludes app traffic; Indeed/LinkedIn proxies capture only publicly posted roles and under-represent distribution-centre hiring; news sentiment is a headline keyword count (12/0 split is unusually clean and may reflect a single-day cluster on 24-Nov rather than a durable 14-day tone); social proxies are not real-time APIs. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one — and at a 52-week high with an overbought technical set-up, it is a **confirming** signal here, not an early one.
