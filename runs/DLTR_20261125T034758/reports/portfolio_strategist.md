## 2. Consensus Assessment: **HIGH on quality, HIGH on price, SPLIT on signal**

**Consensus (7 of 10 agents):** DLTR is a *genuinely improving, well-executed* business trading at a *full-to-rich* price with **no valuation cushion**.
- Quality is uncontested: traffic **+3.8%** with ticket only +0.8% (Growth, Value, Alt Data all flag this as the hard-to-fake datapoint); inventory **−3.8% on sales +6.9%**; ROIC up four straight years; F-Score 8/9; Beneish −2.83.
- Price is uncontested: Value ($140 FV), Growth (<$135), Short ($112–114), Alt Data ("late-stage"), Quant (value "neutral," 3rd of 5). **Five independent methods converge on $112–145 fair value against a $154.65 price.**

**Contested (2 of 10):**
- **Quant says BUY** — but self-labels "low conviction," concedes it is "not a factor-alignment buy," and reports its own alpha as statistically insignificant (t=0.63). **I weight this down heavily: it is a quality score masquerading as a recommendation, and quality is already the consensus.**
- **Alt Data says BULLISH** — but explicitly "confirming, not early," with search interest *down 10% YoY* and the +11.2% day already banked. **Not incremental information at $154.65.**

**Conflict resolution (per framework):** The Short analyst's quality-of-earnings work is the tiebreaker. It does not allege fraud — and I confirm that, given Beneish −2.83, negative accruals, F-Score 8/9, and zero receivables (channel stuffing structurally impossible). But it establishes three hard facts that override the bullish execution narrative: **(1)** the reported EPS gain is 100% share-count, on a *shrinking* numerator; **(2)** ~8% of forward guidance is non-recurring; **(3)** the buyback manufacturing that EPS is funded *above* free cash flow while leverage rises. Value's independent owner-earnings work ($798M = 62% of NI) corroborates the cash-conversion problem from a different direction. **Two independent agents reaching the same conclusion from different data = high confidence.**

The single most important number in the entire file: **20.7x normalized earnings for a 6%-operating-margin discounter guiding comps down from 4.6% to 3–4%.**

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss (once a tranche is entered):** hard stop at **−15% from entry cost**, or on a **daily close below $112** — whichever comes first. $112 is the September 2026 base and the level whose break invalidates the entire post-Q3 re-rating structure. Note the Short analyst's $160 is a *squeeze* level, not a long's stop; do not confuse the two.

**Thesis invalidation (exit fully, regardless of price):**
1. **Traffic turns negative** for even one quarter. Traffic +3.8% on ticket +0.8% is the load-bearing pillar of the entire bull case — three agents independently flag it as the one datapoint that cannot be faked. If it breaks, the story is gone.
2. **Gross margin fails to expand ex-tariff through two more quarters of multi-price conversion.** ~7,400 of 9,511 stores are already converted with **zero gross-margin expansion** — the Short analyst's strongest empirical point. The bull thesis is currently unsupported by the P&L.
3. **FY2027 guidance implies normalized EPS flat-to-down** (below ~$8.00 excluding refunds) — confirms the buyback is the only earnings driver.
4. **Section 301 exclusions / US–China truce lapse on 9–10 Jan 2027** and produce a >150bp gross-margin shock without offsetting mitigation.
5. **Fennessy v. DLTR certified as a class**, or a state AG opens on tariff-refund retention.
6. **Buybacks continue above FCF** with lease-adjusted net leverage moving past **2.5x** (currently ~2.6x — already at the practical ceiling).

**Trim trigger (if entered in the $112–$135 zone):**
- **Trim ⅓ at $140** (Value's base FV; ~19x normalized) — take the round trip off the table.
- **Trim ½ of the remainder at $160** (top of Value's range and above every published PT).
- **Exit fully at $175** (bull case).
- **Also trim on any quarter where more than half of the EPS beat is attributable to tariff refunds or share-count reduction** rather than operating income.

**Monthly monitoring checklist:** multi-price % of sales (target >20%), gross margin ex-tariff, SG&A growth vs revenue growth (currently +9.5% vs +6.9% — deleveraging), FCF vs buyback dollars, lease-adjusted net leverage, and any Section 301 / CBP action.
