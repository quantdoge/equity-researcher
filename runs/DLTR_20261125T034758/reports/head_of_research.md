### Investment Memo: DLTR — November 25, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate at $154.65 | Conviction: **Medium-High**
**Price Target**: $140 (−9.5% from current $154.65) | Fair value range $125–160
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Dollar Tree is the best-executing operator in discount retail — traffic-led comps (+3.8% traffic, +4.6% comp), four straight years of ROIC improvement (11.1% → 14.8%), a clean post-Family-Dollar balance sheet, and genuine self-help runway via multi-price conversion. The problem is arithmetic, not operations: five independent valuation methods converge on $112–145 fair value, the stock trades at ~20.7x *normalized* EPS (stripping ~$0.65 of one-time tariff refunds) for a 6–8% operating-margin discounter, and the headline EPS growth is entirely buyback arithmetic on a shrinking earnings numerator. This is an excellent business at a poor entry — a Kelly-negative bet today, an attractive accumulation candidate below ~$135.

**Bull Case**
- Traffic +3.8% on ticket +0.8% is the hardest datapoint in retail to fake; inventory −3.8% on sales +6.9% confirms merchandising is working.
- Multi-price (18% of sales, 78% of stores converted, ~2,100 stores of runway) plus tariff normalization is a ~200bp operating-margin tailwind into FY2027.
- $2.3B buyback (~7.6% of market cap) against $1.4B FCF mechanically retires ~3–5% of shares annually; ROIC 14.8% and rising.

**Bear Case**
- 20.7x normalized EPS / ~24x FCF for ~6% growth, priced above every independent fair-value estimate — no cushion against one disappointing quarter.
- Earnings quality: EPS growth is share-count engineering funded above free cash flow while leverage rises; ~8% of guidance is one-time.
- Jan-2027 tariff regime expiry + SNAP cuts + discretionary comps of only +0.4% expose the low-income consumer pillar that three agents could not independently verify (macro gap).

**Key Risks**
1. **Traffic turns negative** — the load-bearing pillar of the entire thesis (primary monitoring metric).
2. **Jan 9–10, 2027** Section 301/truce expiry producing a >150bp gross-margin shock.
3. **Buyback-funded EPS model hits its leverage ceiling** (~2.6x lease-adjusted, already at the practical IG limit), exposing the −5% underlying net income trend.
4. **Fennessy class certification or state AG action** on tariff-refund retention — financially small, brand-damaging for the "value champion" franchise.
5. Multi-price conversion dilutes the value perception before margin expansion shows (currently zero GM expansion after 7,400 conversions).
