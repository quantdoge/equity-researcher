## Astera Labs (ALAB) — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment
*As of mid/late November 2026 (latest data: Q3 2026 10-Q filed 11/04/2026; FY2025 10-K filed 02/20/2026)*

**Company snapshot:** Fabless semiconductor connectivity (PCIe/CXL/Ethernet fabric — Aries, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio) for AI/cloud data centers. HQ Santa Clara, CA; 756 employees; market cap ~$28–33B. Q3 2026 revenue $584.9m (+154% YoY), Q4 guide $730–750m; FY2026 implied ~$2.0B.

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**R1 — US–China export controls & tariff escalation (PROBABILITY: HIGH / already materialised; IMPACT: MEDIUM-HIGH)**
- Section 232 Proclamation 11,002 (14 Jan 2026) imposed a **25% tariff on advanced logic semiconductors effective 15 Jan 2026**, with a two-phase plan for derivative products (White House; CRS LSB11409; Covington Global Policy Watch).
- BIS **15 Jan 2026 final rule on AI-chip exports** and a **19 Nov 2026 interim final rule on remote access to advanced "super-intelligence" chips/servers** (BIS; Str Trade; Pilsburry) — each narrows or shifts licence pathways.
- China retaliated with **blocking rules** shielding entities from extraterritorial sanctions and rare-earth export linkage (Taipei Times 25 Nov 2026; Digitimes).
- Company-specific: 10-K risk factor No. 22 states outright that *"adverse changes in the political, regulatory, and economic policies of governments in connection with trade with China and Chinese customers **have reduced the demand for our products and damaged our business**"* — this is a realised, not hypothetical, risk. China billed revenue still rose to 30% of FY2025, so the exposure is growing alongside the restriction regime.

**R2 — Taiwan Strait / East Asia supply-and-shipping concentration (PROBABILITY: LOW-MEDIUM over 12m; IMPACT: SEVERE)**
- 10-K risk factor No. 10: manufacturing partners, distributors and *"the majority of our revenue, are concentrated primarily in Singapore, China, Taiwan, South Korea... areas that are or may be subject to geopolitical uncertainty, trade disputes and restrictions, environmental disasters."* Fabless model ⇒ dependence on TSMC wafers and Taiwanese OSAT (assembly/test/packaging).
- Taiwan is tightening **AI-chip and server export controls toward China** (CommonWealth 20 Oct 2026), aligning with US rules — adds a second, non-US licensing regime over the same flows.
- Taiwan was 68% of FY2024 billed revenue and 29% in FY2025 — even as it declines in mix, physical supply (not billing) remains heavily Taiwan-anchored. A blockade/quake scenario is low-probability/high-severity and largely unhedgeable.

**R3 — Tariff/country-of-origin & transhipment compliance on ODM flows (PROBABILITY: MEDIUM; IMPACT: MEDIUM)**
- With 53% of Q3 2026 revenue billed through Singapore and 30% through China, and 25% Section 232 duties plus Section 301 China tariffs in force, the **classification and origin documentation** of ALAB's boards/modules (often shipped as part of a third-party rack) becomes a live cost and enforcement question. Risk factor No. 22 and No. 9/19 (no long-term supply contracts) mean tariff cost cannot be reliably passed through.

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **Litigation: clean.** FY2025 10-K: *"We are not currently a party to any material pending legal proceedings."* Q1 2026 10-Q and Q3 2026 10-Q repeat this; Q3 10-Q reports **no material change to risk factors and no material legal proceedings**. No securities class action, derivative suit, or ITC action against ALAB surfaced in search (the 2026 securities class actions in search results are AST SpaceMobile, not ALAB).
- **No disclosed government investigations** (DOJ/FTC/SEC/EU) in 10-K/10-Q/proxy.
- **Regulatory posture:** 64 risk factors in FY2025 10-K (vs 65), 12 substantially reworded, none new — a stable disclosure profile. **Prior material weaknesses remediated**; now a **large accelerated filer** (Section 404 auditor attestation, higher compliance cost). ERP implementation still unscoped — internal-control execution risk.
- **IP risk:** fabless connectivity is a patent-dense field (Marvell, Broadcom, Credo, Montage, Renesas). 10-K carries standard IP/trade-secret litigation risk; trademark registrations held in US, EU, Japan, Singapore, Taiwan. No pending IP suit found.
- **Data privacy:** 10-K newly specifies **CCPA** obligations for processing/storing personal data (employee, supplier, partner data) — low materiality, but a stated change.
- **Antitrust/commercial:** Amazon warrant (contra-revenue) plus >70% single-end-customer dependence invites scrutiny of exclusivity/pricing terms if hyperscaler procurement is ever investigated.
- **Insider selling:** $786.7m sold over 12 months with no open-market purchases (Form 4 cadence heavy in Nov 2026) — governance sentiment negative, not a legal exposure.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **China revenue at 30% of FY2025 with AI-infrastructure adjacency** — deemed-export, end-use/end-user screening and diversion diligence on Chinese ODM customers is the single largest compliance exposure. No detail on export-compliance program, licences, or China revenue that is licence-dependent is disclosed — an information gap, not a proven violation.
2. **Billing-address geography obscures true end-use** (Singapore 53% of Q3 2026) — raises transhipment/origin-substantiation risk under Section 232/301 and potential future outbund-investment-style rules.
3. **Conflicting legal regimes:** China's new **blocking rules** vs US export controls create a genuine "comply-with-one, breach-the-other" dilemma for any China-based support or data flows.
4. **Israel R&D center** — mobilisation/force-majeure and export-controls-on-defense-adjacent-tech considerations; small financial materiality today.
5. No FCPA/AML/sanctions enforcement history disclosed; no entity-list or BIS enforcement action found.

---

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale:** Legal risk is genuinely **Low** (no material litigation, no investigations, clean 10-Q legal proceedings, remediated controls). Geopolitical risk is **Medium-High at the revenue-line level** — 59% of FY2025 billed revenue sits in China + Taiwan, and the 10-K concedes China policy has *already* reduced demand — but two mitigants keep the blended rating at Medium: (i) end-demand is overwhelmingly US hyperscaler, so billing geography overstates true political exposure; and (ii) the company is asset-light and can re-route ODM flows. The rating would move to **High** on any of: Taiwan quarantine/blockade headlines, a BIS action touching ALAB products or customers, disclosure of a China licence requirement, or a single-end-customer (>70%) capex cut.

**Monitoring triggers:** BIS Federal Register actions (esp. the Nov 2026 remote-access IFR scope), Section 232 phase-two derivative-product list, Taiwan MOEA export-permit implementation, Amazon/Nvidia procurement disclosures, and any 8-K Item 3.01/legal-proceedings update.
