## 1. Growth Story

Astera Labs is the pure-play merchant supplier of the "nervous system" inside AI racks — PCIe retimers (Aries), PCIe fabric switches (Scorpio), and CXL memory controllers — and it is in the steepest part of an S-curve where content per AI server is rising *faster* than unit volumes. Revenue compounded **106% annually from FY2024 to the FY2027 consensus** ($396M → $3.46B), and Q4-26 guidance of $730–750M implies the company will exit 2026 at a **~$3.0B annualized run-rate** — already 88% of the entire FY2027 consensus print in the rear-view mirror. The disruptive thesis is that rack-scale AI (scale-up fabrics via UALink/NVLink Fusion, PCIe 6→7) requires a merchant connectivity layer that hyperscalers cannot economically insource and that a general-purpose vendor (Broadcom/Marvell) cannot optimize as tightly — converting ALAB from a component vendor into a *platform* with software-defined fabric management.
