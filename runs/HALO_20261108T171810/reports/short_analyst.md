## 2. Key Red Flags Found

**RF-1: Negative shareholders' equity.** Q3'26 total equity **−$55.2M**; total liabilities $3,528.6M > total assets $3,473.3M. *Tangible* book value ≈ **−$1.53B** ($1,474.7M goodwill/intangibles). Equity was $363.8M at FY24 and $48.8M at FY25 — a three-year destruction via buybacks, IPR&D write-offs and debt-funded M&A. This is a capital-structure flag, not a solvency flag (see RF-6).

**RF-2: Debt has more than doubled while the royalty estate runs off.** Total debt: $876.7M (2021) → $1,505.8M (2024) → $2,142.6M (FY25) → **$3,250.3M (Q3'26)** after a **$1.5B convertible** issuance. Cash $1,074.4M, net debt $2,220.3M.

**RF-3: Serial, debt-funded M&A into a patent cliff.** Goodwill + intangibles: $0 (2021) → $955.7M (2022) → $1,561.8M (FY25) → $1,474.7M (Q3'26). The **Surf Bio** deal immediately produced **$284.9M of expensed IPR&D** — an admission that a large slice of acquisition spend created no balance-sheet asset and no revenue stream. With $1.07B cash *and* $1.5B of fresh converts, another deal is clearly being pre-funded. Buying revenue to offset a dated royalty run-off is the single most reliable destroyer of value in specialty pharma.

**RF-4: Buybacks at escalating prices, funded partly with debt.** Repurchases: $350.1M (2021) + $200.0M (2022) + $402.4M (2023) + $250.0M (2024) + $342.4M (2025) + $400M (2026) = **~$1.94B**, shrinking diluted shares from 146.8M (2021) to 113.8M basic (Q3'26), **−19%**. Q3'26 buybacks averaged **$79.47**; the stock then ran to $134 and has fallen back to $103.63. This mechanically manufactures per-share growth on top of a finite royalty stream — the core of the bear case.

**RF-5: GAAP-to-non-GAAP gap is enormous and widening.** FY25 GAAP EPS $2.64 vs FY26 non-GAAP EPS guidance of **$8.75–$9.05 (+111–118%)**. Management headlines "Adjusted EBITDA +89–96%" against revenue growth of only 33–38%. The entire wedge is IPR&D, intangible amortisation and SBC. Aggressive-but-legal framing; note FY25 GAAP diluted EPS *fell* to $2.64 from $3.50.

**RF-6: Altman Z-Score 1.537 → "Distress" zone.** Mitigant: net debt/FY26 guided EBITDA ≈ **1.76x**, $1.07B cash, converts carry minimal cash coupon ($6.0M quarterly interest), and management states a "clear path to retire our remaining 2027 and 2028 notes at maturity." I do not believe HALO faces a liquidity event. The Z-score is an artefact of book equity, not cash.

**RF-7: Receivables growing faster than revenue — two consecutive years.** FY24: AR +31.7% vs revenue +22.4%; FY25: AR +43.1% vs revenue +37.6%. DSO 103 → 111 → 115 days. **Honest caveat: this is not channel stuffing.** Cash conversion of 206% and accruals of −13% of assets make stuffing impossible — you cannot recognise revenue without collecting it and still post those ratios. Explanation is royalty true-up timing from partners. Flagged and largely dismissed.

**RF-8: Inventory-days "rising" signal is a false positive.** 242 days (2023) → 324.8 (2024) → **281.6 (2025)** — the trend is *improving* off a 2024 peak. Dismissed.

**RF-9: Revenue lumpiness.** YoY growth by quarter: 29.6% → 35.2% → 40.8% → **22.1%** (Q3'25) → 51.6% → 42.2% → 47.7% → 39.2%. The 22.1% trough shows how a single partner true-up or milestone can halve reported growth. This is a *volatility* flag on consensus modelling, not a demand flag.

**RF-10: Consensus itself models the cliff.** Street revenue: **$2,102M (2027) → $2,324M (2028) → $2,165M (2029) → $2,023M (2030)**. EPS: $10.42 → **$12.16 (2028) → $10.81 → $10.01**. Even the bulls' own model has HALO shrinking from 2029. That is the whole thesis in one line.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment — this is where the real bear case lives

**The moat is a patent estate with a printed expiry date, not a durable franchise.**

- ENHANZE base rHuPH20 patents began expiring **2024 in Europe and 2027 in the US**; the core licensed estate runs to **March 2029**. Royalty terms are a patchwork: HyQvia 10% to **09/2030**; Tecentriq SC royalties to **12/2040** *only if* the pending patent grants; Ocrelizumab SC on a 10-year term; DARZALEX FASPRO defended in the US/EU through **2032** via Janssen's own co-formulation patents — i.e., **HALO's revenue durability is now hostage to partners' IP, not its own.**
- **Concentration is severe.** Royalty revenue $341M/quarter is dominated by J&J (Darzalex Faspro) and Roche (Phesgo, Ocrevus SC, Tecentriq Hybreza). Two partners largely determine the equity story. Any partner designing around rHuPH20, or litigating a royalty reset, is existential rather than incremental.
- **Royalty-rate renegotiation risk.** Halozyme has pushed partners to *raise* rates at renewal; this invites the 2024-style European co-formulation patent challenge and partner intransigence. The moat is being monetised harder precisely as its legal life shortens — classic end-of-life harvesting behaviour.
- **The swing factor is the MDASE patent family.** HALO is winning: German preliminary injunction (4-Dec-2025), and the **Dutch court of The Hague (Oct-2026)** found Merck infringing an MDASE patent, enjoining Keytruda Qlex across Belgium, Denmark, France, Ireland, Italy, Sweden, Switzerland and the Netherlands, and **rejecting Merck's invalidity arguments**. But a **US PTAB post-grant review (PGR2025-00004)** targets the '400 patent. If the US patent falls, the "durable revenue well into the next decade" claim loses its legal foundation and consensus 2029–2030 numbers get cut hard.
- **Gross margin is holding at 83.6%** and SBC is contained ($51.6M, 16% of net income) — no pricing-power erosion yet. Small positive.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary — is there a credible bear case?

**Yes, a credible *valuation/duration* bear case. No, not a fraud or solvency bear case.**

**The bear case in one paragraph:** HALO is a finite royalty annuity whose core patents run off from 2027–2029, dressed up as a growth compounder via $1.94B of buybacks (−19% share count) that have pushed book equity **negative**, funded increasingly by debt now totalling **$3.25B**, and supplemented by debt-funded M&A whose largest component (Surf Bio) was **immediately written off** as $284.9M of IPR&D. Consensus already shows revenue peaking at $2.32B in 2028 and EPS at $12.16, then *declining* to $10.01 by 2030. At $103.63 you pay **EV/FY26 adj. EBITDA of 11.0x** and **11.6x FY26 non-GAAP EPS** for a stream that begins shrinking in 36 months — and roughly **9x that already-declining 2030 EBITDA**. A melting-ice-cube asset deserves 6–8x, implying fair value **$65–85**. The 6-Nov-2026 breakdown on a guidance midpoint miss is the first crack in the growth premium.
