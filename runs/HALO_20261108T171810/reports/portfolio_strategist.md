**The split (Growth vs. Short/Value) is entirely a multiple argument.** All three use the *same* consensus curve — revenue peaking $2.32B in FY28, EPS peaking $12.16, then declining to $10.01 by FY30. Growth applies 14–15x; Short applies 6–8x EBITDA. **Neither can be adjudicated with available information**, because terminal value is 45% (Value) to 66% (Growth) of IV and hinges on 12 undecided PGRs. I therefore **weight the dispersion, not the midpoint**: professional IV estimates span **$65–$156**, and the sell-side median (~$101) is *below* spot.

**Conflict resolution applied:** Non-Fin Risk's [CRITICAL] US patent-invalidation flag and the Value researcher's "no margin of safety" both take precedence over Growth's upside case, per framework. The Merck MDASE dispute is correctly *excluded* from base-case value by Geo Legal and Value (ENHANZE licenses contractually insulated; Keytruda Qlex ≈$40M 2025 sales) — so I treat the litigation as **free optionality, not as base-case upside to underwrite.**

### 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **2.0% initial, 3.0% maximum**

**Recommendation: 1.5% at market, scaling to 2.25% at ≤$92 or on a Merck US resolution, hard cap 3.0%.**

Rationale, applying Kelly logic with a parameter-uncertainty haircut:

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Probability-weighted 3-year return (25% bull +40% / 45% base +15% / 30% bear −28%) | **≈ +9.6%** |
| Excess over 10Y (4.84%) | ≈ +4.8% |
| Realised vol (252d) | **43.5%** |
| Naïve Kelly f* = μₑ/σ² | ~25% — **absurd; rejected** |

Kelly on a *binary-legal-risk* asset with a $65–$156 IV dispersion systematically overbets. The correct adjustments:
- **÷5 fractional Kelly** for parameter uncertainty on terminal value → ~5%.
- **Further haircut to 2–3%** for the three High-risk flags (Non-Fin, Fin Risk market risk, Short duration) and the absence of any margin of safety at $103.63 vs. $117 IV.
- **Not 1%**, because: (a) zero agents flag fraud/solvency; (b) 8.5% FCF yield and ~12x FY26E for 33–38% growth is a real discount; (c) **correlation to everything in the portfolio is 0.08–0.36** — the diversification math (below) makes this position cheap to hold.

**Bucket rule:** HALO counts against a **6–8% aggregate "binary/single-asset biotech" cap.** Do not add a second high-vol single-name biotech alongside it.

**Marginal risk math:** adding 2% HALO to a 15%-vol, 0.20-correlated portfolio raises portfolio vol by only **~20bp** (15.00% → 15.20%) while lowering portfolio beta slightly. You are buying ~90bp of standalone volatility per 1% of weight at a cost of ~10bp of portfolio volatility. This is the rare high-vol name that is *genuinely diversifying* rather than a hidden factor bet.

- **Sector:** Healthcare. Critically, HALO is **not additive to large-cap pharma exposure** — it is a partial *hedge* against it. It monetizes the 2027–2031 LOE cliff that threatens MRK/AMGN/ABBV: every IV→SC conversion extends a partner's franchise and pays HALO a perpetual royalty. If the portfolio is overweight LOE-exposed pharma, HALO is a logical long. Caveat: it is *also* a supplier to those companies, so partner-level stress (Section 232 tariffs, IRA, MFN) transmits to the royalty line — the hedge is partial, not clean.
- **Factor:** Adds value (P/FCF 19.9x), momentum (+32.6% 12-1) and quality (ROIC 36.6%) tilts with **no beta increase**. However, momentum-crowding reversal is the flagged risk: the stock ran $85.76 → $134.00 (Aug 7 gap **+20% in one session**) then fell **−21% in five sessions**. Expect this position to *lead* portfolio variance in any given month.
- **Rates/duration:** HALO is a long-duration royalty annuity, −7–8% NPV per +100bp. With 30Y at 5.47% and real 10Y at 2.60%, **do not stack this against other long-duration growth exposure** — size it against total duration, not just sector.
- **Hidden structural note:** $3.25B of 0%-coupon converts (2031–33) + negative book equity mean the equity behaves like a levered claim on the royalty stream. If the portfolio runs convertible-arb or high-yield, treat HALO as overlapping exposure.

### 6. Risk Management Rules

**Hard stop: $80 (−23%).** Below the August-2026 breakout base ($85.76) and consistent with the name's 24–32% realised max drawdown. Do *not* use a tight stop on a 43.5%-vol stock (2.74% daily σ — a −15% day is a normal event, not a signal). A 2.25% weight at the stop costs ~0.5% of portfolio — acceptable.

**Thesis invalidation (exit on any two, regardless of price):**
1. **PTAB final written decisions invalidating the majority of the 12 instituted PGRs** — a US genus-level loss removes the exclusivity argument beyond 2029 *and* the Merck optionality. This is the single break point.

3. **FY27 revenue guidance below $2.0B** (consensus $2.10B) — implies the cliff is arriving early.
4. **Gross margin below 80%, or cost of revenue growing >2x revenue for two quarters** (already flagged: +43.5% vs +37.5% in FY25; Q3'26 cost of sales $82.5M vs $55.2M).
5. **A debt-funded acquisition of a pre-revenue asset** (Surf Bio precedent: $284.9M instantly written off as IPR&D) or a buyback authorisation executed above $120.
