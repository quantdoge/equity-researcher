# Halozyme Therapeutics (HALO) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price ~$103.63 | Market cap ~$12.8B | EV ~$14.8B | Net cash-flow royalty engine trading at ~11–12x 2026E non-GAAP EPS**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Halozyme is not a drug developer — it is a **toll booth on the subcutaneous conversion of the world's best-selling IV biologics**. Its ENHANZE platform (rHuPH20) and newer MDASE enzyme estate let partners convert blockbuster infusions (Darzalex, Vyvgart, Phesgo, Ocrevus, Opdivo, Tecentriq, Keytruda) into fast subcutaneous injections, earning a perpetual royalty at near-100% incremental margin. That model is now inflecting violently: FY2025 revenue +37.6% to $1.40B, FY2026 guidance of +33–38%, and **adjusted EBITDA guided to grow 89–96%** — the purest operating-leverage compounder in biotech. The entire investment debate is not *whether* the next three years are explosive, but what survives the 2027–2029 core patent expiries.

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration

- **The royalty base:** 2026 royalty guidance of ~$1.245B, at a typical 1.5–3.5% effective royalty rate, implies **$35–60B of partner product sales currently ride on Halozyme's enzyme**. Anchor programs: J&J's Darzalex Faspro (~2/3 of the franchise now SC), argenx's Vyvgart Hytrulo (fastest-growing royalty driver, +44% royalty growth), Roche's Phesgo/Rituxan Hycela/Ocrevus Zunovo/Tecentriq Hybreza, BMS's Opdivo Qvantig, J&J's Rybrevant Faspro, Lilly's Ebglyss.
- **The white whale:** Merck's **Keytruda Qlex** (~$30B IV franchise). There is *no license* here — Halozyme is monetizing through patent litigation instead (German injunction Dec 2025; Dutch court found infringement and rejected Merck's invalidity arguments in 2026; UK patent revoked by consent in May 2026). A settlement royalty on even $10–20B of SC Keytruda sales would be transformational.
- **Addressable pool:** global biologics ~$500B, of which IV-administered large molecules plausibly $150–250B. At 2.5% blended royalty on 10% penetration of a $200B pool, that is **$500M/year of incremental royalty** above today's base — with essentially zero COGS.
- **Penetration today: low.** Roughly 10 commercial products and **12 in development** (three new Phase 1 starts with Pfizer, Incyte and argenx in Q3 2026, plus expanded collaboration agreements with argenx, Incyte and J&J). Subcutaneous conversion of the IV biologic universe is arguably <15% penetrated. **TAM is not the constraint — patent life is.**

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4/FY2026 results + initial 2027 guidance — Feb 16, 2027** (consensus EPS $2.56; management has raised guidance at every print in 2026). Watch for a 2027 royalty framework that quantifies the post-2027 patent transition.
- **Merck/Keytruda Qlex IP resolution (2027).** US district court and EU appeal rulings; management has signaled it will "address the 2027 and 2028" patent expirations. A settlement royalty or a US injunction are both violent re-rating events — in opposite directions.
- **Core US rHuPH20 patent expires Sept 23, 2027; EU ENHANZE/collaboration patents to March 6, 2029.** Any disclosure of MDASE-based patent estate extending exclusivity into the 2030s is the single largest swing factor in the terminal value.
- **Pipeline conversion:** 12 ENHANZE programs in development; Phase 1 data/advancement from Pfizer, Incyte and argenx programs over 2027.
- **Capital return:** $400M of buybacks completed in 2026 at an average of ~$79/share plus $1.5B convertible notes issued — a new authorization at ~11x earnings would be highly accretive.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction on 12–24 months; sized for 2029 terminal risk)

The setup is unusually asymmetric for a company growing 35% with an 84% Rule-of-40 score. At $103.63 you pay **~11.6x 2026E non-GAAP EPS of $8.90** and roughly **12–13x 2026E EV/FCF**, for a business where incremental royalty dollars carry ~100% margin and where guidance implies **EBITDA nearly doubling this year**.

The market is doing something quite specific: it is paying almost nothing for 2026–2028 and instead pricing a steep post-2028 decline ($2.32B → $2.02B revenue). My read is that this is **too punitive**. Cumulative 2027–2030 FCF of roughly $3.5–3.8B equals ~28% of today's market cap, all of which can be retired at 11x earnings — mechanically manufacturing per-share growth even with flat absolute royalties. Add Keytruda Qlex optionality (currently valued at ~zero), 12 development programs, and an MDASE estate that has already proven enforceable in Dutch and German courts, and you are getting the post-2029 franchise for free.

**Where I stop short of Strong Buy:** this is a *patent-life* asset, not a perpetual-growth asset. Cathie Wood-style durability requires a TAM that compounds for a decade; HALO's royalty stream has a visible contractual end date in the US in September 2027. That structurally caps multiple expansion — you get paid in cash flow and buybacks, not in re-rating. Hence **Buy with a value-plus-optionality framing**, not a "hold forever" compounder.

**Fair-value frame:** 14–15x 2027E EPS of ~$10.40 (still a discount to the S&P for a 2x-growth business) = **$145–156**, roughly 40–50% upside, plus a $1.5B+ annual buyback compounding the per-share math.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **The 2027/2029 patent cliff (the dominant risk).** The core US rHuPH20 patent runs to Sept 23, 2027; EU and collaboration patents to March 6, 2029. If partners can switch to non-infringing enzymes or biosimilar hyaluronidases at expiry, royalty revenue could compress far faster than consensus's ~7%/yr decline. Equity book value is only ~$0.39/share after years of buybacks — there is no balance-sheet cushion, and $1.5B of new convertible notes adds leverage plus potential dilution.
2. **Litigation binary risk.** Merck is litigating hard and Halozyme has already lost the UK patent (May 2026). A US loss on the MDASE patent family would remove the Keytruda optionality *and* the argument for exclusivity beyond 2029. This is the likely driver of the recent ~16% drawdown and will keep the stock headline-sensitive.
3. **Partner concentration.** Three products (Darzalex SC, Vyvgart Hytrulo, Phesgo) drive the majority of royalty growth. argenx's Vyvgart growth decelerating or Darzalex losing share to bispecifics would hit the highest-margin line directly.
4. **Revenue quality is royalty-dependent, not recurring-subscription-dependent.** Royalties are legally finite. Post-2029 the company must replace ~$1.2B+ of high-margin revenue with new deals — an unproven at-scale capability.
5. **Execution/M&A risk.** Halozyme has shown appetite for large inorganic moves (the 2025 Evotec approach), and with $1.5B of converts raised, capital allocation discipline is not guaranteed. A dilutive deal at the wrong price would destroy the buyback thesis.
6. **Terminal-value math dominates.** Because two-thirds of intrinsic value sits beyond 2028, small changes in assumed post-2029 erosion swing fair value enormously — expect 30%+ drawdowns on single headlines.
