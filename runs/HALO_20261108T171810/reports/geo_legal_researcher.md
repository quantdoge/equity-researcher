# HALO (Halozyme Therapeutics) — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment
**As of November 2026 | Market cap ~$11.8B | FY26E revenue $1.860–1.925B, royalties $1.235–1.255B**

---

## 1. Geographic Risk Map

HALO is a **royalty-collection business with a US manufacturing/legal core**. ~66% of FY26E revenue is royalty revenue earned on *partner* end-market sales in 100+ countries, so geographic exposure follows counterparties, not HALO's own footprint. Note: an explicit revenue-by-geography table was **not retrievable** from the 10-K/10-Q excerpts available (the filing tool returned only an ESPP exhibit; the FY25 10-K text was not accessible in-page). Shares below are therefore qualitative weights inferred from partner disclosures and management commentary.

| Country/Region | Nature of exposure | Revenue weight | Risk level |
|---|---|---|---|

Three counterparties in three jurisdictions (US/Belgium/Switzerland) drive the majority of growth — this is concentration risk dressed as geographic diversification.

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

### Risk #1 — Multi-jurisdictional IP war with Merck over MDASE/Keytruda SC (Probability: High / Impact: Medium)
The single most legally levered item in the story, and the only one where outcomes are genuinely adversarial rather than administrative.

- **Europe is going HALO's way.** Munich Regional Court granted a preliminary injunction on **4-Dec-2025** blocking Keytruda SC in Germany; on **7-Oct-2026** the specialised Dutch patent court of The Hague held MSD BV infringed **EP 2,797,622 (EP622)**, rejected Merck's invalidity arguments, and ordered MSD BV to refrain from manufacturing, marketing or importing Keytruda SC in **Belgium, Denmark, France, Ireland, Italy, Sweden, Switzerland and the Netherlands** — and barred MSD from facilitating infringement via its European Marketing Authorisation (Halozyme IR release, 7-Oct-2026; Reuters, 7-Oct-2026). This is a meaningful chokepoint: it targets Merck's *central EU packaging/distribution hub*, not just one market.
- **The US is going the other way.** On **12-May-2026** the PTAB found all challenged claims of US 11,952,600 unpatentable (lack of written description/enablement); on **18-May-2026** the same for US 12,152,262 (Venable BiologicsHQ, 22-May-2026). One institution denial (PGR2025-00087, '685 patent, 15-May-2026) is a partial offset, but **12 instituted PGRs remain awaiting final written decisions** and Merck has logged at least five PTAB invalidation wins. The only US forum left is **Halozyme, Inc. v. Merck Sharp & Dohme Corp., No. 2:25-cv-03179 (D.N.J.)**, asserting 15 patents.
- **Why it matters less than headlines suggest:** MDASE patents are **distinct from ENHANZE** and are not part of the licensing program — HALO states the outcome "will not impact the ability of any licensee to use ENHANZE, or the license fees." Keytruda Qlex generated only ~$40M of 2025 sales (~$35M in Q4-25). So this is optionality/annuity protection, not base-business risk. SG&A is already rising on litigation costs (Q3-26).

**Watch item:** if US courts/PTO continue invalidating the MDASE genus, Merck's US launch proceeds unimpeded and the deterrent value of HALO's patent estate against *future* subcutaneous entrants erodes — a strategic rather than near-term P&L risk.

### Risk #2 — US Section 232 pharmaceutical tariffs (Probability: High that tariffs persist / Impact: Low–Medium, indirect)
Section 232 tariffs on patented pharmaceutical imports took effect **31-Jul-2026**: a **100% headline rate with reduced tiers** — ~15% for qualifying imports from the EU, Japan, South Korea and Switzerland; zero for the UK and for Annex IV-listed products/ingredients (White House proclamation, Apr-2026; Crowell, Mohawk Global, RSM summaries).

HALO has **no direct tariff line** — it is not an importer of finished branded drugs, and its rHuPH20 API is US-manufactured and *exported*. Exposure is second-order: partner products manufactured in the EU/Switzerland/Japan and sold into the US (Roche's Phesgo supply chain; argenx's VYVGART Hytrulo) face tariff-inflated landed costs. Because HALO's royalty is a percentage of **partner net sales**, any tariff-driven price concession, US supply re-routing, or volume loss compresses the royalty base. HALO also books "product sales from API" — cross-border API shipments into partner supply chains carry their own classification exposure and possible retaliatory treatment.

Probability of *material* (>2–3% royalty) impact in FY27: **low-to-medium**. It is a slow-burn margin risk to partners, not a HALO-specific cliff.

### Risk #3 — US drug-pricing policy: IRA Medicare negotiation / OBBBA royalty framework (Probability: Medium / Impact: Low near-term, tail-heavy long-term)
On **6-Nov-2026** HALO reaffirmed it projects **zero-to-minimal royalty revenue impact through at least 2035** based on CMS's final rule for IPAY 2029 issued 5-Nov-2026 and the statutory framework under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (PRNewswire, 6-Nov-2026). This is credible and well-argued (royalties are computed on net sales, so negotiated price cuts transmit partially).

The residual tail: HALO's royalty durations extend well into the 2030s, and its "IRA Commentary" IR page shows this is a core investor debate. Any future change to how royalties are treated on negotiated products, or expansion of negotiation to a high-royalty product like **DARZALEX** (a J&J blockbuster and a top-3 royalty driver), would hit the duration value of the annuity. Do not underwrite 2035 zero-impact as permanent.

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

**None material.** Explicitly:

- **FCPA/UK Bribery Act:** Low. 423 employees, no emerging-market manufacturing, no direct sales force in high-corruption jurisdictions; commercialization runs through large-cap partners.
- **Sanctions/export controls:** Very low. No Russia, Iran, or China manufacturing/sales exposure disclosed. rHuPH20 is a US-origin biologic — a theoretical export-control/DEA-style licensing sensitivity, but no issue identified.
- **AML:** Not applicable at scale (B2B licensing, royalty receipts from investment-grade counterparties).
- **Data privacy (GDPR/CCPA):** Low. Limited patient-level data; Hylenex/XYOSTED commercial activity is partner-operated (Teva, Viatris/McDermott).
- **One structural flag worth noting:** HALO sits on **Irish and Swiss legal entities** with material ex-US royalty flows. OECD **Pillar Two global minimum tax** and evolving transfer-pricing scrutiny of IP-holding structures are a live (if unquantified) regulatory risk to the tax rate. I could not verify HALO's effective tax rate or Pillar Two disclosure from available filings — **treat as unverified**.

## 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale.** On classic country risk HALO is unusually clean: no China, no Russia, no emerging-market manufacturing, no expropriation or currency-control exposure, and its revenue geographies (US, Switzerland, EU, Japan) are all high-rule-of-law jurisdictions with functioning IP enforcement. That alone would justify a Low rating.

It is upgraded to **Medium** on two grounds: (1) a genuinely adversarial, multi-front IP conflict with Merck in which the **US forum is trending against the company** while Europe trends in its favor — jurisdictional fragmentation is itself the risk, and 12 pending PGRs create a rolling news-flow overhang; and (2) **counterparty concentration** in three partners across three jurisdictions, which means HALO's "geographic diversification" is thinner than it appears and transmits US tariff and drug-pricing policy directly into its royalty line.

**Bottom line for the memo:** geopolitical risk is *not* the reason to own or avoid HALO — this is a duration-of-royalty and IP-enforceability story. The EU injunction victories are a genuine, underappreciated asset (they block a competitor's SC rollout across Merck's own EU distribution hub); the US PTAB record is the offsetting negative. Neither is likely to move the base business, which is contractually insulated via ENHANZE and explicitly reaffirmed as unaffected. The under-monitored risk is **tax/transfer-pricing under Pillar Two** across the Irish/Swiss entities, not any country's politics.
