### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:**
- **Web traffic, job postings, and social sentiment are all web-search proxies**, not API-sourced (SimilarWeb/Thinknum/LinkedIn/Reddit). The +2.38% MoM traffic figure comes from a search snippet, not a monthly time series — no trend, only a snapshot.
- **Patent data entirely missing** (two API failures) — no R&D intensity read.
- **No app-store, satellite/geospatial, retail foot-traffic, or reservation-backlog data available** for this name; no direct reservation count is public, so order intent is inferred from invitation-mention social chatter (single source, unverifiable).
- **Google Trends keyword bias:** the "Rivian R2" series is distorted by discrete launch events (89 on Feb 8, 98 on Mar 8, 100 on Jun 7), so level comparisons are unreliable — only direction is usable.
- **Caveat on timing:** the Q3 beat and UBS upgrade are **already public and largely priced**. The informational edge has shifted forward to Q4 (Feb 2027) and to the R2 Premium trim launch in late 2026.

**Data lag:** News/social real-time (Nov 2). Google Trends ~1 week. Web traffic 1 month. Job postings ~1–2 weeks stale and qualitative. Delivery figures one month lagged (Q3 reported early October).

**Bottom line:** Alt data is directionally bullish and was conspicuously *right* against price in October — but this is a leading indicator on demand only. It says nothing about whether Rivian can fund the ramp. Weight it as a demand confirmation, not a solvency endorsement.
