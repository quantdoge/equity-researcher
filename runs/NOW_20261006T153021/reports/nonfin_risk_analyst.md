# Non-Financial Risk Assessment — ServiceNow, Inc. (NASDAQ: NOW)

**Company:** ServiceNow, Inc. — enterprise SaaS workflow automation ("Now Platform"), Santa Clara, CA. CEO William R. McDermott; ~29,200 employees; market cap ≈ $143B. Sells mission-critical ITSM/HR/security/GRC workflows to ~85% of the Fortune 500, incl. government, healthcare, financial services.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: **Low-Moderate**

- **No active investigations, fines, or enforcement actions** found (EDGAR search empty; regulatory news feed clean over 180 days).
- **Emerging AI regulation:** Rapid roll-out of gen-AI products (Flow "AI service desk," AI Assist/consumption-based "assist packs") creates forward regulatory exposure — EU AI Act, sector-specific AI rules in healthcare/finance, and customer-data processing under GDPR/CCPA. AI ACV is projected at ~$1.5B by YE2026 (~30% of ACV by 2030), meaning AI is becoming a *core* revenue line, not a pilot — raising the stakes of AI-governance failures.
- **Government exposure:** FedRAMP-authorized federal business carries procurement/compliance risk (policy shifts, export-control-adjacent scrutiny). No adverse actions found in window.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Single-platform tail-risk:** The Now Platform is the operational backbone of customers' IT/HR/security workflows; a multi-hour outage (historical precedent: the Sept-2021 AWS-linked outage) propagates directly to customers' P&L, reputational damage, and SLA/contractual liability. No incident surfaced in the last 12 months — but none is tolerated at this scale.
2. **AI-product integrity risk:** Gen-AI features now embedded in regulated-vertical workflows (health, finance, government) create hallucination/bias/security liability concentrated in the fastest-growing revenue line; a high-profile AI failure would hit both trust and the AI-ACV growth narrative that anchors the stock.
3. **Enterprise-deal & partner-channel concentration:** Growth depends on large enterprise renewals and integrator partners; the 25% stock drawdown and sector-wide software de-rating raise churn/elongation risk into a fundamental business risk, not just a market story.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **Moderate, improving**

- **Financial press sentiment is mixed-to-positive on substance:** "ServiceNow crashed 25% in one year" headlines sit alongside bullish AI-adoption coverage (Deutsche Bank top pick, Seeking Alpha "Strong Buy," $1B+ AI contract value milestone, Nvidia CEO Huang calling NOW "destined to be the best platform" for enterprise AI). The Fortune AIQ partnership is a positive brand halo.
- **No consumer/product/ESG controversy** found in the window — no recalls, no labor or privacy scandal, no social-media backlash.
- **Primary reputational friction is investor-confidence:** multiple single-day drawdowns of 10–20% (Nov 2025 volume ~48M, Dec 15 ~148M, Apr 10 ~58M, Jul 23 ~49M) show how violently the name is traded; narrative whiplash is a real, if soft, reputational cost.
