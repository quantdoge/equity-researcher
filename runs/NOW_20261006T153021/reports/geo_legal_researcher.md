## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

1. **EU AI Act & data-sovereignty pressure — Prob: High | Impact: Medium-High.** ServiceNow's platform now embeds generative AI (Now Assist, Flow, AI-native ITSM). The EU AI Act (GP-AI obligations in force, high-risk provisions phasing 2026–27) plus GDPR and EU cloud-sovereignty procurement (e.g., public-sector "Energy/DIAMOND" sovereignty inclinations; data residency in EU datacenters) will raise compliance cost, constrain AI feature availability per-market, and could slow federal/regulated-industry rollouts in EMEA (~a quarter of revenue).
2. **US-China technology decoupling — Prob: Medium | Impact: Medium.** As a US SaaS/AI platform, ServiceNow faces no direct US export-controls ban today, but heightened US-China friction (AI model licensing, chip controls, potential forced localization for foreign AI services in China) can disrupt multinational customers' deployments and any indirect China-channel revenue. China direct exposure is minimal, muting impact.
3. **Sanctions & geopolitical escalation in MENA / Eastern Europe — Prob: Medium | Impact: Low-Medium.** Gulf-region customers (government-linked entities) create OFAC/export-controls diligence surface; Iran-war escalation noted in current news flow could dent MENA enterprise spending. Russia/Belarus exposure is already exited; residual risk is enforcement/compliance, not P&L.
