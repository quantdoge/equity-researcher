# Sector Brief — ServiceNow (NOW)

## 1. Sector Overview
ServiceNow operates in **Enterprise Application Software (Software – Application)**, the $600B+ category spanning CRM, ERP/HCM, creative, and IT workflow platforms. The sector is in a **late-growth phase for legacy modules but in an AI-driven renewal cycle**: CIO budgets are rerouting toward agentic-AI platforms and workflow automation, while legacy seat-based vendors (CRM, creative) have converged to high-single-digit growth. The category's leaders are splitting into two archetypes — mature compounders (CRM, ADBE, WDAY growing ~10–13%) and AI-reaccelerators (NOW, TEAM growing 21–26%). Consolidation and platform wars (Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce) are intensifying, but barriers remain high: deep enterprise switching costs, multi-year contracts, and network effects from installed workflow ecosystems.

## 2. Key Tailwinds
- **Agentic AI / workflow automation is the sector's primary growth engine.** ServiceNow has surpassed **$1B in AI-related contract value** (Now Assist, AI agents) and beat/raised guidance through Q3 2025 on "AI-driven demand"; it exited FY2025 with FY revenue of **$13.28B (+20.9%)**, comfortably above its own raised guide ($12.78–12.80B).
- **IT spend consolidation toward "system of action" platforms.** Enterprises are collapsing point tools into integrated workflow platforms, favouring NOW's ITSM→employee/customer-workflow land-and-expand model; subscription revenue grew ~21% with an expanding enterprise base.
- **Structurally advantaged economics.** Recurring subscription revenue, ~78% gross margins, and operating leverage (op margin 4.9% → 13.7% over four years) fund continued AI R&D while holding growth >20% — rare at $13B+ revenue scale.

## 3. Key Headwinds
- **Growth decay is real**: YoY revenue growth has stepped down 23.8% → 22.4% → 20.9% over FY23–FY25, and FY2026 will face a $14B+ base. Sustainably >20% becomes harder each year.
- **Premium valuation leaves no room for error**: NOW trades at **~10.8x sales (~83x GAAP EPS)** vs a ~5.2x peer median — the richest tape in its peer set, pricing in flawless AI monetisation. Agentic-AI revenue is still nascent (street estimates ~$19M in 2026, scaling toward ~$340M by 2030).
- **Competition and pricing pressure**: Microsoft (Copilot in ITSM/automation), Salesforce (Agentforce) and hyperscaler-native agents are converging on the workflow layer, while enterprises push back on seat-based pricing and demand ROI-justified AI add-ons, which can compress ARPU.

## 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG**
- **Growth is ~1.6x the peer median** (+20.9% vs 13.1%); only Atlassian grows faster, from a one-third smaller base and off near-zero profitability.
- **Margins are at peer median** — gross margin 77.5% and op margin 13.7% sit exactly at the peer median, reflecting deliberate reinvestment (op margin has tripled over four years while growth stayed >20%, a favourable trade-off vs ADBE's high-margin/low-growth model).
- **Valuation reflects the franchise**: ~2x the peer sales multiple is a premium to growth and AI positioning, but leaves it the most crowded long in the group; the stock's premium compresses quickly if growth dips below 18–20% or agentic-AI bookings disappoint.

## 6. Sector Rating: **Attractive**
Enterprise application software is in an AI-driven upcycle with durable double-digit growth among leaders, high switching costs, and an expanding total addressable market in agentic automation. Core sub-segments (CRM, creative) are maturing, but NOW sits on the right side of the bifurcation — the workflow/agentic category with 20%+ growth, best-in-class share gains, and pricing power. The main risks are the premium multiple and crowding, not the underlying sector economics.
