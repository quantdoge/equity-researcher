## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (unusually cleanly split)

The disagreement is **not about the business — it is about the price**, and that is the single most useful fact in this file.

**Consensus FOR (agreement level: High):** Growth, Alt Data, Sector and the operating portions of Fin Risk all independently confirm the same thing — the demand engine is real and accelerating. Three independent corroborations: (i) five consecutive ~12–14% revenue beats, (ii) a guide raised $140M at the midpoint, (iii) hard volume data (+56% MRD, +22.3% total tests). Reimbursement is *expanding* (six Medicare indications; HCC added 3-Nov-26), not contracting. There is no channel-stuffing signature — receivables fell 5.6% while revenue grew 35.9%, and accruals are negative and widening. **This is not a broken company.**

**Consensus AGAINST (agreement level: High):** Value, Short, Fin Risk, Quant-value and Macro converge from five different methodologies on one conclusion — **there is no margin of safety.** Base intrinsic ~$103–155 vs. $310.67 spot; the *bull* case (~$190–200) sits below the current quote; 14.5x FY26 sales against a 6–7x median for scaled diagnostics; 0.26% FCF yield; a 10Y at 4.84% with the Fed still hiking. Five independent routes to "too expensive" is a strong signal.

**Conflicts I am resolving explicitly:**

- **Fraud/accounting override? → NO.** My framework says fraud flags trump a bullish value view. Here the *opposite* applies: the forensics agent (Short) affirmatively rebuts its own tools. Beneish M=1.098 is driven entirely by AQI, which is an artifact of the Foresight acquisition creating $515M of intangibles plus a $1.08B cash inflow — not expense capitalisation. Altman Z=0.82 is a retained-earnings (−$2.78B) artifact on a company with **$862M net cash**. Accruals, DSO, and receivables all point the *conservative* way. Three independent agents (Short, Fin Risk, Nonfin Risk) reached the same "no accounting manipulation" conclusion. **Accounting-manipulation risk: LOW.** I therefore do not apply a fraud override against the growth case.
- **Governance/conduct — partial override.** Nonfin Risk's "HIGH" is not an accounting rating; it is a *conduct and disclosure-quality* rating built on a 72-hour convergence: surprise CEO exit, wider-than-expected loss, and ~$3.0M of insider sales at $410–412 two days before a −29% drawdown. I do **not** treat this as fraud, but I do treat it as a genuine, unresolved overhang that caps sizing until 10b5-1 adoption dates are verified. This is the single highest-priority diligence item.
- **Litigation — quantifiable, absorbable.** $292.5M + $57M ≈ $350M vs. $1.08B cash and a $44.8B cap (≈0.8%). Punitives ($175.5M) are the most appealable component. **Not thesis-breaking; it is a liquidity-flexibility constraint, not a solvency one.** However, counsel sanctions and the $20M remaining revolver availability are governance/practical flags.
- **Value vs. Growth deadlock — resolved by role, not by averaging.** Growth is right that the *franchise* is intact; Value is right that the *security* is not cheap. Both can be true. A portfolio holds securities. So: own a small amount of the franchise, not a large amount of the multiple.

**My synthesis verdict:** high conviction on business quality, **low-to-medium conviction on the entry price**, elevated risk stack. That combination maps to a *starter* position, not a core holding.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.25% initial → 2.5% maximum**

**Sizing logic (Kelly):** Realistic bull case $375–390 (+20–25% to a *post-drawdown* target; the $435 median is stale-high — most notes predate 6-Nov) vs. downside $250 (−20%) or $220–240 (−25%) in the Macro sticky-inflation scenario. Call it p_win ≈ 0.52 at roughly 1:1 odds → full Kelly f\* ≈ 4%; **half-Kelly ≈ 2.0%**. Volatility-adjusting from there (56.8% vol is 2x a normal equity) argues for the lower half of that band.

**Risk-budget cross-check:** a 1.25% position in a 56.8%-vol name contributes ~0.71% of portfolio volatility — economically equivalent to a 2.5% position in a 28%-vol stock. A −50% position drawdown costs ~0.6% of the portfolio. That is a survivable, repeatable loss for a name with this much binary policy exposure.

**Why not larger (3–5%):** twelve years of execution are already in the price; base intrinsic value is roughly half of spot by one credible DCF; the Feb-2027 FY27 guide is unproven under a brand-new CEO; and the governance question is *open*, not closed.
**Why not smaller/pass:** the drawdown is event-driven, not operating-driven; five of six demand indicators are accelerating; and after −29% the entry is ~30% better than it was nine sessions ago. Passing entirely would ignore a genuinely differentiated franchise.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Hard price stop: $235 (−24% from entry).** Deliberately wide — a 20% stop is only 1.2 monthly sigmas at 56.8% vol and would be noise-stopped with near certainty. The stop is sized so total loss ≈ **0.30% of portfolio**. Loss of $250 on volume **without** a fundamental trigger = cut to half, don't wait for $235.

**Thesis invalidation (exit regardless of price) — any one:**
1. **FY27 revenue guide below 20%** (vs. 25–30% consensus) — the multiple has no floor at that growth rate.
2. **Gross margin below 62% for two consecutive quarters** — this is the ASP-compression tell the CFO pre-flagged ("no rule that ASPs improve linearly"). Flat 64.7% GM on 34% growth is already the weakest line in the model.
3. **Adverse MolDX LCD** restricting a major indication, or CLFS cuts beyond the scheduled PAMA path (≤15%/yr from 1-Jan-27).
4. **Combined litigation outflows >$500M**, or 9th Circuit affirming Guardant punitives in full.
5. **🚩 10b5-1 adoption/amendment dates confirm insider plans were modified inside the cooling-off window ahead of the 5-Nov announcement** → immediate exit. This converts a governance concern into a trading-integrity issue. Highest-priority diligence.
6. Kentucky AG adverse finding with multistate contagion, or any accelerated GIPA/state-AG billing action.
7. **SBC still >14% of revenue in FY27** with no operating-margin progress → per-share value creation is not occurring; the Growth thesis is enterprise-value-only.

**Trim triggers:**
- Trim **1/3 at $420–435** (the Oct-26 high zone) — do not re-underwrite a second re-rating on the same estimates.
- Trim if **EV/sales >16x forward** without offsetting gross-margin improvement.
- Trim if a strategic acquirer bids — take the control premium rather than hold for the standalone bull case.

**Review cadence:** formal re-underwrite at the Feb-2027 print; light check on every MolDX/CLFS headline and every quarter's **Signatera ASP disclosure** (the single most important reported line).
