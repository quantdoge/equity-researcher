Capped by: (a) **no patent data**; (b) **no web-traffic time series or visit counts**, only a category ranking; (c) Google Trends is a weak proxy for a physician-ordered test and the 5-year window failed, blocking y/y comparison; (d) **no quantitative social feed**; (e) alt data ends Nov 6–9, 2026 with ~11 weeks of blind spot before the Feb 25 print.

Alt data cannot resolve the two things that actually move NTRA — ASP trajectory and opex discipline — both of which are company-controlled and already flagged as risks. Treat the revenue-beat thesis as high-conviction within a Medium-confidence frame; treat margin recovery as unverified.
