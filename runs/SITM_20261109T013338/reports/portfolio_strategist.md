### 6. Risk Management Rules

*Logic:* that level is the market's own statement of where the "sentiment-only" thesis fails. With 96% of my sizing already constrained to 1%, a stop-out costs ~21bp of portfolio — immaterial, and far cheaper than a 40% drawdown on a larger position.

**Thesis invalidation conditions (exit regardless of price — any two):**
1. Comms/enterprise/DC organic growth decelerates below **+30%** for two consecutive quarters.
2. Non-GAAP gross margin falls below **66%** or GAAP GM fails to recover off 51.6% by mid-2027 → integration/mix problem, not timing noise.
3. Guidance is cut on **wafer allocation**, or ×1–2 quarters of supply disruption (Taiwan/Bosch/TPD) is disclosed.
4. Channel-quality deterioration: DSO or distributor inventory days rising while growth decelerates (this is where a pull-forward would first appear — flagged by both Non-Fin and Alt Data).
5. Any **BIS/Entity-List action touching the distributor network**, or a Renesas share disposal / adverse related-party supply term.
6. A new §10(b) securities class action (none found — but a −54% drawdown normally attracts one; the 10-Q Legal Proceedings note is **unverified**).
7. A quantified ICFR material weakness in the TPD purchase accounting (restatement history: 2023–24).

**Trim trigger:** trim one-third at **$560–600 pre-split** (~$187–200 post-split), ≈90% of a conservative $650 intrinsic value (34x FY27E non-GAAP EPS).
