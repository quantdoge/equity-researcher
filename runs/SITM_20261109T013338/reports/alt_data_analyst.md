### 1. Consumer Demand Signals
SITM is a B2B component supplier — there is no app/download/traffic dataset that maps cleanly. Best available proxies:

- **Google Trends ("SiTime", US, 12-m): deteriorating, but that's investor attention, not product demand.** Interest peaked at 100 (wk of 31 May 2026) and has bled down to a 21–28 trough through Sep–Oct. Latest week (1 Nov) = 38. **Critically, the earnings-week spikes are shrinking: 75 (Feb) → 75 (Apr) → 100 (May) → 89 (Aug) → 38 (Nov).** That is a retail-attention deflation consistent with the derating, not a demand signal.
- **YoY read (the only clean positive):** same weeks in Nov 2025 ran 22–30 (avg ~24.5) vs 38 now = **+~55% YoY**. Direction: mildly up on baseline, down sequentially.
- **Web traffic (sitime.com): NOT AVAILABLE.** SimilarWeb proxy returned only a comparison stub ("rohm.co.kr had more total visits… September 2026") with no visit counts. Direction: unknown — treat as a gap.
- **Best actual demand evidence is qualitative and company-sourced:** wafer supply constraints expected to persist **through 2027**, i.e. revenue is supply-capped, not demand-capped. Third-party commentary (Seeking Alpha, 22 Oct) pegs SITM at ~80% share of AI data-centre timing. Demand direction: **strongly up; visibility capped by foundry allocation.**

Revenue surprise: +11.7% → +7.5% → +7.0% → +11.2% → +9.7% → +7.5% → **+2.1%** (latest, smallest of the streak). Beat rate 7/7, avg surprise +43%, but the trajectory is decay.

Signal remains above consensus but the gap is ~3%, inside the normal beat variance.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**
Demand signals are supply-constrained (the best kind), hiring skews to capacity and product execution, guidance is above consensus, and the analyst/retail attention reset removes froth. This is a fundamentals-up / sentiment-down setup.

**Two things temper it:** (a) surprise magnitude is decaying monotonically — the "beat" is becoming consensus, so the 3 Feb print needs the $341M top end to move the stock; (b) the +257% YoY headline is acquisition-distorted, and organic AI-timing growth is not separately disclosed, so alt data cannot isolate it.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
- **High-quality:** earnings history, guidance figures, price/volume, news flow.
- **Low-quality / gaps:** patent trend (feed broken), web traffic (no visit data), hiring trend (snapshot only, no series), "SiTime" Google Trends (earnings-date noise, 5-y request returned the same 12-m series — no longer history available), social (thin, partly stale).
- **Structural limitation:** for a fabless B2B component maker there is no true independent panel dataset. The most valuable alt signal here is indirect — *supply-chain constraint commentary as proof of demand* — and management is the source of it, so it is not independent verification.
- **Lag:** news sentiment is 14-day; Google Trends weekly with ~3-day lag; job postings undated. Guidance is 9 weeks stale as a demand read by the Feb print.

**Bottom line:** Alt data supports the consensus-beating trajectory into Q4'26, with the caveat that the margin of surprise is compressing toward zero and the stock's next leg depends on 2027 wafer allocation rather than on demand.
