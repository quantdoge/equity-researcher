### Investment Memo: ZS — October 21, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (do not initiate at current price; trim into strength) | Conviction: Medium-High
**Price Target**: $200 (-13.5% from current $231.33); accumulation zone $150–185
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Zscaler is a genuine category leader in Zero Trust/SASE with 77% gross margins, ~25% reported growth, a Rule-of-40 score of 51, and only ~4% penetration of a ~$100B TAM — but the equity case fails on arithmetic, not quality. Reported FCF of $852M collapses to ~$30M once $822M of stock compensation is treated as the real expense it is; ROIC has been negative four straight years; organic growth is decelerating (~21% ex-Red Canary vs. 25.4% reported); and the stock, having doubled off its April low, now trades ~15% above base-case intrinsic value with an unresolved securities investigation overhead. A fine business, a full price, no margin of safety.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector Attractive — cloud security is a mandatory secular spend (9–10% CAGR market) and ZS holds the highest gross margin among peers (76.8%). Macro Favourable — easing rates lift long-duration SaaS multiples. Bear scenario: sticky inflation forces "higher for longer," collapsing the multiple.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium-High risk. **Pomerantz LLP investor claims investigation confirmed active (Oct 2026)** — flagged independently by four agents, quantified by none. Data sovereignty fragmentation adds compliance overhead. No DOJ/FTC/SEC action found.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Base-case IV ~$200 (9.5–10% WACC); price is a premium, not a discount. Buy threshold ~$150. Moat narrow and contested — gross margin drifted down ~90bps over five years; Microsoft/PANW bundling is the structural eroder.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (high conviction, capped below Strong Buy). 7/7 quarterly beats averaging +11%; consensus needs only ~16% CAGR to FY31 ($7.15B). But FY27 guided to ~17% growth with FCF guided *down*, and 47x forward P/E leaves no cushion.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL. Top-quintile momentum (+41% 12-1) and upward revisions vs. bottom-decile value and low-vol (73% annualised). R² vs. SPY = 0.032 — returns are 97% idiosyncratic; beta is meaningless.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE. Solvency safe (net cash ~$1.6B, no near-term maturities, ~6-year stressed runway) but equity risk very high: 65% volatility, −65% max drawdown, repeated −12% to −31% earnings gaps. Altman Z "distress" reading is a book-equity artifact.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Existential single-point-of-failure risk — a breach of Zscaler's own platform would be catastrophic for a trust-based franchise. Insider activity is one-directional selling (routine, but zero open-market buying at the lows).
- **ESG**: Negative implication, driven entirely by the governance/litigation overhang (score 6/10). Product integrity is the critical social factor by nature of the business.
- **Alternative Data**: Bullish but low-confidence — 100% beat rate and +0.867 news sentiment, though job-posting and patent signals both failed (data gap).
- **Short / Contrarian View**: Credible quality-of-earnings case, explicitly *not* fraud. Beneish M-Score −2.70 (clean), accruals −15% (honest accounting). The finding: cumulative 4-year FCF of $2.50B ≈ cumulative SBC of $2.46B — shareholders have funded their own dilution (~3.4%/yr). EV/SBC-adjusted-FCF ≈ 1,240x.

**Bull Case**
- Category-defining platform: inline proxy architecture, ~3.7% TAM penetration, 7/7 beat record, and positive estimate-revision breadth (14 target hikes in one day)
- Rule of 40 = 51 with 25% FCF margins and a net-cash balance sheet — self-funding hyper-growth, rare in software
- Optionality: AI security SKUs, Red Canary SecOps attach, and federal mega-deals could re-accelerate organic ARR above 20%; Q1 FY27 print (Nov 24) is against a reset, low bar

**Bear Case**
- Economic FCF is ~zero after SBC; negative ROIC four consecutive years; per-share growth ~22%, not the 25% headline
- Organic growth decelerating (25.4% reported vs. ~21% ex-M&A) into a bundling war with Microsoft, PANW, and CrowdStrike; gross margin already eroding
- At 10.6x EV/sales and 190x EV/EBITDA, any guidance slip is punished asymmetrically — the May 2026 −31.5% single-day gap is the template, and the next binary event is 34 days away

**Key Risks**
1. **Litigation**: Pomerantz investigation escalating to a filed class action or restatement — unquantified and under-priced by the bull camp
2. **Guidance credibility**: a second reset after May 2026 would exhaust management's forecasting capital
3. **Multiple compression**: stagflationary persistence or a rotation out of high-multiple software compresses this 20–30% independent of fundamentals
4. **M&A impairment**: ~$1.43B goodwill (~55% of book equity) from acquisitions made during a growth scare
5. **Data gaps to disclose**: no formal credit rating, no debt maturity ladder, no peer EV/S multiples retrieved, no board-independence detail, litigation claims unquantified

**Portfolio Construction Note**
**PASS at $231.33 — 0.0% target weight.** Negative expected return against base-case IV means Kelly fraction is zero. ZS is a sector bet wearing a ticker (0.70 correlation to CRWD, 0.64 to PANW, 0.18 to SPY) — redundant if the book already holds cyber exposure; cap total cyber sleeve at 4%. **Existing holders: trim to ≤1.0% now; exit remainder at $265–275.** Re-entry: first tranche 0.5% only post-Q1 earnings at ≤$200, scaling to a 1.25% maximum at ≤$185 or upon resolution of the Pomerantz matter. Upgrade to Buy requires SBC <15% of revenue, post-SBC FCF margin >10%, and positive ROIC — evidence the moat converts into owner returns, not just revenue.
