## Non-Financial Risk Assessment: Zscaler, Inc. (ZS)

**Sector:** Technology | **Industry:** Software - Infrastructure | **CEO:** Jagtar Singh Chaudhry
**Date of Analysis:** October 2026 (Based on available data feeds)

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: Adequate

**Evidence:**
*   **Positive:** The company maintains an Investor Relations portal that includes corporate responsibility and CSR information, suggesting an awareness of ESG pressures.
*   **Weakness:** Direct data regarding board independence, specific committee structures (e.g., audit committee composition), and executive compensation alignment is not readily available through automated filings. This limits a deep dive into corporate oversight quality.
*   **Observation:** The recent filings (Form 4s) show active insider trading, though without volume data, direction cannot be definitively confirmed.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

**Summary:** Low to Moderate.
*   **Active Investigations/Fines:** No current, active SEC enforcement actions or Department of Justice (DOJ) investigations were flagged against ZS.
*   **Pending Approvals:** Not specified in available data.
*   **Key Red Flag (Legal):** The most significant regulatory/legal risk is external: Multiple news reports confirm that a law firm (Pomerantz) is investigating claims on behalf of investors of Zscaler. This signals a potential class-action or securities dispute, which could lead to litigation costs, settlements, and reputational damage.

### 3. Insider Activity Analysis

**Analysis:** Moderate Interest/Activity.
*   Recent Form 4 filings are frequent, indicating active trading by corporate insiders (officers and directors) over the past several months.
*   *Note: Without specific volume/direction data from the filings, a definitive Net Buying/Selling conclusion cannot be made. However, the consistent reporting suggests high insider activity, which must be closely monitored.*
*   **Contextual Note:** One news report indicated a private equity firm (Krilogy Financial LLC) was growing its stake in ZS, suggesting external investor confidence.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

*   **Cybersecurity Threat Landscape:** As a core cybersecurity provider, ZS is inherently at the epicenter of the global threat environment. Any successful major breach of ZS's own platform, or a systemic failure in its cloud infrastructure (Enforcement Nodes/Central Authority), would represent a catastrophic operational failure and immediate reputational crisis.
*   **Platform Dependency Risk:** The business is entirely dependent on the reliability and performance of its cloud-based infrastructure. Downtime or performance degradation in ZIA or ZPA services would directly impact its clients' business continuity, leading to contract risks and financial penalties.
*   **Technology Evolution Risk (AI/Zero Trust):** The pace of technological change (e.g., integration of AI into hacking/defenses) requires constant, massive R&D investment. Failure to rapidly adapt the Zscaler platform against novel, AI-driven attack vectors could lead to product obsolescence and competitive disadvantage.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment

**Assessment:** High.
*   **Brand Damage:** The brand is highly valuable and relies entirely on trust (being a gatekeeper of corporate security).
*   **Controversies:** The confirmed investor investigation by Pomerantz Law Firm is a serious reputational liability. Even if unsubstantiated, the existence of such an investigation creates negative market sentiment and forces the company to expend resources on legal defense.
*   **ESG:** While the company has a corporate responsibility page, the intense focus on technology innovation can sometimes overshadow environmental or social governance concerns, which must be monitored for backlash.

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: High

**Justification:**
While ZS has no immediate regulatory fines, the combination of a **High** Reputational/Legal Risk (investor lawsuits) overlaying critical **High** Operational Risk (reliance on cutting-edge, high-stakes cloud infrastructure) elevates the overall profile. The potential for litigation and catastrophic platform failure is high.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

*   **Immediate Escalation:** **Investor Lawsuit Investigation (Pomerantz LLP):** This requires a legal and communications deep dive to understand the claims, the scope, and the potential financial liability.
*   **Monitoring Point:** **Insider Trading Patterns:** Continued surveillance of Form 4 filings is necessary to determine if selling pressure is increasing amongst executives.
*   **Future Monitoring:** **Security Incident History:** Any reported cybersecurity incident, regardless of severity, must be tracked immediately, as it poses the single greatest threat to Zscaler's business model.
