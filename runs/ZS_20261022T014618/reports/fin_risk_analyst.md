### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **Investment Grade (net-cash profile), with a caveat**

ZS carries **no near-term refinancing risk**. Total debt of $1.856B is entirely long-term ($0 short-term debt), and cash & short-term investments of $3.474B cover total debt **1.87x**, producing a **net cash position of ~$1.62B** (net debt/EBITDA **−8.7x**). That is structurally an investment-grade balance sheet.

The caveat: the credit strength comes from the cash pile, not from earnings. The company is **GAAP-loss-making** (FY26 operating loss −$133.3M, net loss −$63.2M), so traditional interest coverage computed off EBIT is **negative (−11.3x)**. Interest expense is only $11.8M, and EBITDA coverage is a healthy **15.8x**, but the operating loss means debt service is funded by the balance sheet, not by operating profit. With no maturities due and net cash, default risk is negligible over the next 2–3 years; a *formal* rating is not available in the data set (gap), but an indicative **BBB-/unrated-with-investment-grade-characteristics** is defensible on liquidity alone, tempered by persistent losses.

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**
Solvency and liquidity risk are **Low** (net cash, no maturities, 1.7x current ratio, positive FCF). The overall rating is lifted to Moderate by **very high equity/market risk** (65% vol, 65% drawdown, zero Sharpe), **structural GAAP unprofitability**, and the fact that reported FCF is almost entirely a stock-compensation add-back. This is a **balance-sheet-safe, cash-flow-quality-questionable, valuation-fragile** credit.

### 6. Key Red Flags
1. **FCF is essentially SBC.** FY26 SBC of $821.9M vs FCF of $852.4M — FCF *ex*-SBC is only ~$30M (yield ≈0.1%). The "25% FCF margin" is an artifact of adding back 24.5%-of-revenue stock comp. This is the single most important adjustment in the analysis.
2. **Persistent GAAP losses** (FY24 −$57.7M, FY25 −$41.5M, FY26 −$63.2M) and **negative EBIT interest coverage (−11.3x)**. Accumulated deficit of $1.25B leaves no retained-earnings buffer.
3. **Dilution:** shares out +3.8% YoY (154.4M → 160.2M); Piotroski F7 = 0. No buyback, no dividend.
4. **Altman Z in distress zone** on a book basis (0.81) — a genuine signal of thin book equity (equity/assets 0.33), even though market-cap-adjusted Z (~4.8) is safe. Worth monitoring if the equity value de-rates further.
5. **Extreme drawdown/volatility** (65.4% vol, −64.9% max DD) and **near-zero Sharpe** — the stock offers no risk-adjusted compensation and has repeatedly gapped −12% to −26% on earnings.
6. **Gross margin erosion** (77.9% → 76.9% → 76.8%) and Piotroski F8 = 0 — possible pricing/mix or hosting-cost pressure.
7. **Valuation-driven equity risk:** ~9–11x EV/Sales on a company with a GAAP operating loss. Any growth deceleration compresses the equity multiple violently; the balance sheet protects creditors, not shareholders.
