### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: Medium-High

**Summary Justification:**
Zscaler operates in a high-growth, strategically essential sector (cybersecurity), which mitigates some general economic risk. However, the presence of an active and confirmed investor-led legal investigation introduces a material legal risk that elevates the rating above Medium. The risks are primarily centered on **legal/litigation exposure** and **global regulatory complexity** rather than outright political instability (e.g., expropriation or war), which is typical for a sophisticated, US-headquartered SaaS provider.
