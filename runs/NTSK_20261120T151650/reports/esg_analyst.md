## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment — **LOW**
- **Carbon intensity:** No Scope 1/2/3 disclosure located in filings or on the CSR page. Revenue $538.3M (FY25) with no heavy-asset base → estimated Scope 1+2 well under 5,000 tCO2e; intensity immaterial relative to $9.8B cap.
- **Transition risk:** Nil carbon-tax/stranded-asset exposure. Indirect exposure only via data-centre power costs (NewEdge network + cloud regions) — a cost line, not a valuation risk.
- **Physical risk:** Low; distributed offices + cloud regions. No water stress, no weather-exposed assets.
- **Controversies:** None found (no spills, fines, remediation).
- **Gap/flag:** Zero public climate targets or TCFD reporting. This is a *disclosure* gap, not a *risk* — but see §6: California SB 261 likely captures them on revenue.

## 3. Social Risk Assessment — **MEDIUM**
**Negatives:**
- **Repeated workforce reductions.** June-2026 RIF confirmed via employee channels; prior RIFs (2024) tied to over-hiring. Employee-review sentiment (Glassdoor/Blind/RepVue) has deteriorated markedly ("3 RIFs… high turnover," "burnout"). One-time severance likely $10–20M — immaterial to P&L, but sales/AE churn feeds ARR deceleration.
- **Founder-CEO culture risk.** Public commentary (Fortune, Jul-2026) on aggressive reference-checking/hiring filters — reputational and litigation surface in a tight security-talent market.
- **No public DEI metrics** post-IPO (was a pre-IPO CSR talking point). EEO-1-type disclosure absent.
- **Concentration of delivery in India** — wage-inflation and attrition exposure.

**Positives:**
- **No product-safety breach identified.** No Netskope platform compromise, customer data-loss event, or privacy class action found in a 24-month controversy sweep. This is the single most important social data point for a security vendor, and it is clean.
- Strong compliance posture as a *compliance enabler*: FedRAMP High authorised, ISO 27001/SOC 2, EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework self-certified (per FY26 10-K), DORA and EU AI Act mapping guides published.
- EcoVadis **bronze** (top ~35%) — weak-positive, self-reported.

## 4. Governance Quality Score — **4 / 10**
**Rationale:**
- **Dual/tri-class control with 20 votes per share.** Per the final 424B4 prospectus and Schedule 13G filings: Class A = 1 vote, **Class B = 20 votes** (2× the Silicon Valley 10-vote norm), plus a Class C. Founder-CEO **Sanjay Beri** (CEO & director since co-founding in Oct-2012) retains outsized voting control on roughly a ~10% economic stake. Minority holders have effectively no ability to influence the board. Empirical literature puts a 3–8% valuation/governance discount on perpetual dual-class structures.
- **Short public-company record** (IPO Sep-2025); first proxy cycle only. As an emerging growth company, disclosure is deliberately thin (no CD&A performance-metric depth, limited ESG reporting).
- **"Controlled company" exemptions** are available to issuers with this ownership profile and may be elected, weakening nominating/compensation independence.
- **Positives (partial offset):** audit committee appears chaired by **Kimberly Alexy**, a recognised financial expert; standing audit committee met five times in FY2026; Sarbanes-Oxley 302 certifications clean in the Q2-FY27 10-Q (no disclosed material weakness or restatement); no related-party or self-dealing flags; no pledging/dual-CEO-Chair confirmation either way.
- **Compensation alignment:** unverifiable — DEF 14A text was not retrievable through my tooling (SEC returned 403 on direct fetch; filing-text tool reported "no DEF 14A found"). Treat as a **gap**; the founder-control structure means pay design is unlikely to be shareholder-constrained.

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure
- **US state climate rules (most concrete):** California **SB 261** (climate-risk reporting, >$500M revenue doing business in CA) — Netskope at $538M FY25 revenue is **likely in scope**; **SB 253** (Scope 1/2/3) becomes relevant as revenue crosses $1B. Compliance cost low (est. <$0.5M/yr) but requires building an inventory they don't have.
- **EU CSRD:** Possible via EU subsidiary thresholds (>€40M EU revenue); the Omnibus "stop-the-clock"/scope-reduction materially lowers the probability of a near-term obligation. **CBAM: not applicable.**
- **SEC climate rule:** Stayed/effectively withdrawn — no exposure.
- **Data-privacy regimes (the real regulatory driver):** GDPR/UK GDPR, EU-U.S. DPF self-certification, US state privacy patchwork, and — critically — **downstream customer regimes**: **DORA** (EU financial-services customers), **EU AI Act** (AgentSkope/AI Guardrails), **FedRAMP High** (US federal). Netskope is a *processor* for regulated customers, so any of their supervisory findings become Netskope's contractual liability. This is the largest un-modeled regulatory risk in the name.
- **ESG-linked debt covenants:** None identified.

## 7. Overall ESG Rating — **AVERAGE (with a governance overhang)**
Low environmental risk and a genuinely clean product-safety/security record support the rating; it is capped by a 20-vote founder-control structure, a thin public disclosure record, and deteriorating human-capital signals. Not a Laggard — the underlying business *sells* security and compliance, which structurally aligns product with social good — but nowhere near Leader status on governance.

## 8. ESG Investment Implication — **NEUTRAL for the thesis**
ESG factors are **not** a driver of the NTSK call and should not be sized as one. Concretely:
- **No ESG disqualifier.** No environmental liability, no breach history, no sanctions/bribery exposure. Nothing here breaks the growth thesis.
- **One real discount-rate input:** the Class B 20-vote structure plus founder control argues for a modest 3–8% governance haircut (~$0.3–0.8B of the $9.8B cap) and will keep NTSK out of some Article 8/SFDR "good governance" and dual-class-screened mandates — a structural, low-grade ownership headwind rather than a thesis breaker.
- **One watch item with real teeth:** a Netskope platform compromise or a DORA-driven customer exodus would be a step-change event, not a gradual ESG score drift. Monitor breach disclosures and 8-Ks, not ESG ratings.
- **Where ESG *helps* the thesis:** AI governance and DORA/compliance-mapping are demand drivers for SASE/AI-security spend — regulatory burden on customers is Netskope's TAM.

**Action:** No position change on ESG grounds. Engage on Scope 1/2 disclosure and Class B sunset; flag governance discount in the target multiple.
