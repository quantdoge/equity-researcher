## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional — "late-cycle re-acceleration." Risk-on for earnings and credit quality, risk-off for duration.**

As of 20 November 2026 the US economy is *not* slowing. Q3-2026 real GDP grew at a **3.4% annualized rate** (24,611bn vs 24,408bn chained 2017$, +2.1% YoY), unemployment has *fallen* to **4.1%** (from 4.5% in Nov-2025), and nominal PCE is running ~+6.3% YoY. That is a reaccelerating, no-landing economy.

The problem is on the price side. CPI fell to a **2.4% YoY trough in January 2026**, then re-accelerated every quarter to **3.7% (Sep) and 3.8% (Oct)** — a ~140bp round trip. The Fed cut ~70bp through H1-2026 (EFFR 4.33% → 3.63%), then had to reverse: **3.75% in September, 3.88% in October**, with the FOMC holding at 3.75–4.00% on **28 October 2026**. The September SEP has the median dot at **4.00–4.25% for both end-2026 and end-2027** — i.e. no cuts, mild tightening. Market pricing goes further: **one hike in December 2026 and two more in 2027**.

The critical detail for a long-duration equity is the *decomposition* of the long-end selloff. From 1 July to 20 November 2026:

- 10Y nominal: **4.48% → 5.13%** (+65bp)
- 10Y TIPS real yield: **2.25% → 2.88%** (+63bp)
- 10Y breakeven: **2.23% → 2.16%** (−7bp)

**Essentially all of the yield rise is real rates and term premium, not inflation expectations.** Breakevens are *falling* — the market believes the Fed will contain inflation, and is pricing the real cost of that containment. The 2Y has moved from 4.05% (4 June) to 4.80% (18 Nov), ~90bp *above* the effective funds rate. This is a hawkish real-rate shock, and it is the single most important macro variable for Netskope.

Meanwhile credit is benign and only now starting to notice: HY OAS **3.52%** (19 Nov) — up 92bp from the 2.60% August trough, but still historically very tight. Ex-post real Fed funds is **~0.06%** (3.88% − 3.82% CPI) — an effectively zero real policy rate. The Fed is behind the curve and the bond market is doing the tightening at the long end.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

Netskope is a cloud-security SaaS platform (Netskope One: SSE, ZTNA, CASB, DLP, AI security), IPO'd 18 Sept 2025, ~$9.8bn market cap at $24.00, 408m shares, **beta 2.99** (on only ~14 months of trading history — treat as indicative), ARR growth **~27%**. It is a long-duration, largely cash-flow-deferred asset: value sits in terminal years, so it is among the most rate-sensitive instruments in the market. Critically, it carries **no meaningful debt maturity wall** post-IPO, so rate sensitivity here is *valuation-driven, not solvency-driven*.

**Interest rates / real yields — STRONGLY NEGATIVE.** A 63bp rise in the 10Y real yield is a direct haircut to a ~12x EV/Sales, negative-earnings business. Rule of thumb: on a cash-flow stream with 15+ year duration, +65bp real is roughly **−15% to −25%** of intrinsic value. That is the core headwind, and it is mechanical.

**Fed policy path — NEGATIVE.** The 2Y at 4.80% vs EFFR 3.88% prices ~90bp of hikes. Every hawkish repricing raises the hurdle rate for unprofitable growth. Small-cap growth has already felt it: Russell 2500 Growth returned **−10.35%** in Q3-2026 while the 10Y rose 81bp.

**Growth — POSITIVE.** 2–3% real GDP plus 3.8% inflation = ~6% nominal GDP. Security budgets are breach-driven and non-discretionary; identity/network security is among the last lines cut. Solid growth is what lets NTSK sustain 27% ARR growth in a decelerating-spend environment.

**Credit conditions — NEUTRAL-to-POSITIVE.** HY OAS 3.52% and an open IPO window mean financing is available; Netskope doesn't need it. But the +92bp widening since August is an early warning worth watching.

**USD — NEGATIVE but muted.** Broad USD +3.8% since 11 August. Netskope has material EMEA/APAC/Japan revenue (roughly a third of the business — *estimate; not confirmed by a fundamentals feed*), and most SaaS contracts are USD-denominated, so translation exposure is smaller than the reported mix implies. Partially offset: significant R&D and support headcount in India, so a strong dollar is a *cost* tailwind.

**Tariffs/trade — NEUTRAL.** Pure software; no direct tariff exposure. Indirect exposure only, via the CPI re-acceleration that is driving the rate shock.

**AI capex cycle — POSITIVE (with a caveat).** AI-security demand is Netskope's fastest-growing surface, and the AI buildout is clearly intact. The caveat is existential: AI-native security tooling is also the most plausible threat to the 27%-ARR-growth assumption underpinning the multiple.

**Valuation context.** The stock round-tripped violently: **$25.79 (22 Sep 2025) → $7.73 (27 Mar 2026), −70%**, then **+243% to $26.52 (28 Oct 2026)**, and now $24.00. It fell 21% on 12 March 2026 (−20.8% in one session on 20.9m shares) and 19% on 4 June 2026 — company-specific earnings events amplifying an already-hostile duration backdrop. Note the divergence: NTSK rallied 57% from the August low *while* the real 10Y rose ~45bp. Equities are currently behaving as if rates don't matter.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

The company's own execution is not the macro problem; the discount rate is. Four of the six macro channels that matter (real yields, Fed path, USD, growth-duration premium) are pointed against a 12x-sales, 27%-growth, cash-flow-deferred software asset. The offsets — tight credit, no debt wall, non-discretionary security budgets, falling breakevens — are real but defensive rather than catalytic. In a regime where the Fed is hiking into 3.8% CPI and the 10Y real yield is at 2.88% and rising, high-multiple SaaS is structurally the wrong duration.

## 5. The Surprise Scenario: A Hawkish December

**Bear case (primary):** The market is pricing the hikes but *equities have not de-rated for them*. If the Fed hikes on 9 December 2026 and the dots confirm two more in 2027, the 10Y real yield pushes toward **3.25–3.50%** while breakevens stay anchored. On a mechanical duration basis that is another **−20% to −30%** on NTSK's multiple, *independent of earnings*. Combine that with 27% ARR growth decelerating below 25% into the 2 December Q3 print, and the stock retests **$14–15 (−40%)**. The tell would be HY OAS through 4.00% alongside a 2Y above 5.00%.

**Bull counter-scenario:** If the October CPI (3.82%) marks the peak — AI-driven productivity and shelter cooling pull CPI back toward 2.5% by mid-2027 — the Fed's hike path gets priced out, the 2Y falls to 4.00%, and the 10Y real yield retreats to ~2.30%. Long-duration software re-rates **+25% to +35%**; with a 2.99 beta, NTSK could trade **$35–40** on no change in fundamentals. Asymmetric: the bull case needs inflation to cooperate, the bear case only needs the current curve to hold.
