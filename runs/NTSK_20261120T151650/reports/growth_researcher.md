## Netskope, Inc. (NTSK) — Growth Investment Analysis
*As of Nov 20, 2026 close ($24.00). IPO 9/18/25 at $19; now +26% vs. offer, but +210% off the $7.73 March low.*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Netskope is the only pure-play public "SSE/SASE" vendor besides Zscaler, built on a proprietary NewEdge private cloud that inspects data *in-line* rather than backhauling it through appliances — the architecture enterprises need as work shifts to SaaS, hybrid cloud, and now agentic AI. Its Gartner SSE Magic Quadrant leadership (3 consecutive years; highest Ability to Execute, furthest Completeness of Vision in 2024) plus a data-centric DLP/CASB heritage gives it a differentiated wedge into what Gartner sizes as a **$28.5B SASE market by 2028 (26% CAGR)**. Post-IPO the business has executed cleanly — ARR $899M (+27%), four straight beats — while the stock de-rated to $8, then tripled as the market re-discovered the growth.

Netskope's $899M ARR ≈ **12% of SSE** and **~6% of SASE** — penetrated enough to prove the platform, with a decade of runway. Add cloud data protection, DLP and AI-usage governance (broader vendor TAM claims >$60B) and the incremental dollar opportunity is in modules attached to the same installed base — the classic high-margin expansion flywheel. At the current run-rate and 20% growth, Netskope is a ~$1.3B revenue company by FY29 with only mid-teens share of today's SSE TAM.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q3 FY27 results, Dec 2, 2026 (≈1–2 weeks)** — first print against raised guidance: revenue $227–229M, non-GAAP op margin ~(8)%. Watch **net new ARR vs. the $54M Q2 bar** and NRR. Highest-probability re-rating event.
- **FY28 guidance (March 2027)** — the credibility test for "non-GAAP breakeven in FY28" and the first real FCF inflection; a >2% FCF margin target would compress the P/FCF objection.
- **AI-security SKU cycle** — Netskope One for genAI/agentic-AI data governance + AI-driven DLP; attach rate is the main lever to push NRR back above 115%.
- **Post-IPO supply/ownership events** — shelf drawdowns and sponsor (Lightspeed/Accel/ICONIQ/Sequoia) sell-downs; conversely, potential index migration and short-interest unwinds (beta 2.99 = violent both ways).
- **SASE consolidation M&A** — PANW, Cisco and Broadcom are all buying distribution in this market; a pure-play leader at ~10x ARR is an obvious strategic asset.

The thesis is a **cheap option on a scarce asset**: the only scaled pure-play SASE challenger, growing ARR at 27% with 77% non-GAAP gross margins and 1,100bp of annual non-GAAP margin expansion, priced at essentially Zscaler's sales multiple with a fraction of its FCF — and at ~1/3 to 1/5 of PANW/CRWD's multiple. If management converts even half the gross-margin gain into FCF by FY29, a 10–12x multiple on ~$1.15B of FY28 revenue implies **$30–35/share (+25–45%)**; my bear case (7x FY28 sales, growth fading to high-teens) is **~$19 (–20%)**. That is an attractive but not asymmetric skew — hence Buy, sized in tranches, with new money better deployed *after* the Dec 2 print than before it, given the stock sits above consensus.

### 7. Key Risks

1. **Growth deceleration is real and visible**: ARR +29% (Q1) → +27% (Q2); revenue guided to +25.5% in FY27 and +21% in FY28. The multiple is fine at 20%+, unforgiving below.
2. **Rule of 40 failure / FCF skepticism**: only ~2% FY27 FCF margin guided, Q2 FCF –$29.8M, and FY26's positive FCF leaned on working capital. Any slip here re-rates the stock toward 6–7x sales.
3. **Platformization risk from Palo Alto**: PANW's bundling/discounting and Cisco/Broadcom's installed-base bundling can compress deal sizes — NRR of 114% (vs. 115–120% best-in-class) is the tell that expansion is being partly competed away.
4. **Dilution**: SBC remains ~17% of revenue ex-IPO one-timers; GAAP op margin (–41%) is widening YoY and headline losses deter generalist capital.
5. **Post-IPO supply**: sponsor lockups/shelf activity and a 2.99 beta mean drawdowns of 30%+ are normal mechanics, not thesis breaks — size accordingly.
6. **Imminent event risk**: Dec 2 report with the stock above consensus PT and up 3x in five months; a merely in-line print could give back 15–20%.
