**Rating: Sub-Investment Grade**

### 4. Downside Scenario Analysis
**Stress Test: 20% Revenue Drop**

If TMUS experiences a sustained 20% drop in revenue, the impact is severe:

*   **EBIT/EBITDA Impact:** Revenue decline drastically compresses operating margins, leading to a proportional drop in EBITDA.
*   **Interest Coverage:** The already tight Interest Coverage Ratio would likely fall below 1.5x, pushing the company dangerously close to **covenant breaches** and defaulting on debt service payments.
*   **Liquidity:** While current cash buffers may absorb the initial shock, the lack of substantial FCF generation means the company cannot easily liquidate assets to cover shortfalls without triggering covenant triggers.
*   **Conclusion:** A 20% revenue drop constitutes a **High Stress Event**, potentially triggering a credit downgrade and necessitating emergency refinancing efforts.
