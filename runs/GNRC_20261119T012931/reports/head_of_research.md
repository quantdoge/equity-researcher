**Investment Thesis**

Generac is a genuinely good business — a moated residential standby franchise (~75–80% US share, ~9,900 dealers, 38–40% gross margin) now bolted to a real second growth engine: a $4.5B data-center backlog anchored by a $2.4B committed Amazon tranche for 2027–28. The stock, however, already discounts that story: our DCF intrinsic-value range is $190–220, and the current $205.45 price sits at the midpoint with **zero margin of safety**, while FY27 consensus revenue ($6.89B) sits ~30% above what management's own capacity and segment guidance implies (~$5.3B). The asymmetry at market is unfavorable — roughly +8% in the rigorous bull case versus −27% in the bear case, on a 60%-volatility, beta-2.0 instrument — so the correct action is to hold, pre-commit a $170/$160/$150 scale-in ladder, and let the February 10, 2027 print resolve the consensus-versus-guidance gap.

**Bull Case**
1. **Contracted, visible growth step-change**: $4.5B DC backlog (≈ FY25's entire revenue) with $2.4B committed to Amazon for 2027–28; management claims capacity can ~triple to ~$4B with minimal incremental capex; a second/third hyperscaler signature re-rates the stock toward $280+.
2. **A real moat with optionality**: dominant residential share and dealer network at record gross margins, only ~5–6% household penetration, a 1M+ installed-base service annuity — plus free storm-season optionality and a macro mirror-trade (10Y back to ~4.3% → mortgages ~6% → highest-torque re-rating in Industrials).
3. **Self-funding balance sheet**: 1.36x net leverage, ~$450M guided FY26 FCF, EBITDA margin guide raised twice to 20.5–21%; the growth is funded without diluting beyond the warrant or stressing the credit.

**Bear Case**
1. **The consensus number is unreachable**: FY27 $6.89B requires C&I to double in twelve months against CAT/Cummins (combined ~50–60% large-genset share) in a DC genset TAM sized at ~$8.6B growing 2.7% — while management's own capacity tops out near $3.75B by Q3'27 and its segment math implies ~$5.3B.
2. **Growth is margin-dilutive by construction**: C&I EBITDA margin 14.6% vs. residential 34.7%; Q4 gross margin already guided down to 37–38%; the warrant makes the anchor customer a 2.9% equity partner — growth is being purchased with price and equity.
3. **Residential macro + equity-structure risk**: 7.11% mortgages and 3.75M turnover contract the financed, discretionary buyer pool; beta ~2.0 and 60%+ vol transmit any AI-capex or rate shock fully to holders (already −31% from the June high once this year).

**Key Risks**
1. **Execution on the capacity triple** (Sussex/Belvidere ramps; hyperscaler qualification gates) — any slip pushes the 2027 story out a year and compresses the multiple fast.
2. **Customer concentration**: Amazon deferral, re-spec, in-sourcing, or dual-sourcing decision removes the thesis' anchor volume.
3. **Receivables/DSO trend** — two more quarters of AR growing >1.5x revenue is the cleanest channel-stuffing tell.
4. **A second CPSC §15(b) penalty** or a stationary-generator CO clearance rule — governance-credibility and franchise-economics events, respectively.
5. **10Y sustained above 5.25%** — macro regime shifts from Neutral to Unfavourable.
6. **Data gaps flagged**: peer set incomplete (Vertiv, Enphase, Rehlko/Kohler not pulled); 10-K/DEF 14A text inaccessible (403s) so governance detail is partially unverified; definitive debt maturity ladder and segment P&L splits rely on management commentary; alt-data web/patent feeds failed. None change the verdict; all argue for the event gate.
