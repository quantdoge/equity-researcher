### ⚠️ First, a Data Correction (material)

The screening multiples are misleading and must be discarded: **P/E 76x, EV/EBITDA 28x, P/FCF 45x**. These are computed off FY2025 GAAP results, which were depressed by a trough in residential demand (mild 2025 storm season) *plus* restructuring/impairment and clean-energy warranty charges (Q4'25 alone: GAAP EPS **–$0.42** vs. adjusted **+$1.61**).

The business has since inflected sharply. Trailing-twelve-month (Q4'25–Q3'26) figures:

| Metric | TTM | FY2026E |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $4.62B | ~$4.92B (+17%) |
| GAAP diluted EPS | $5.05 | ~$7.70 |
| **Adjusted EPS** | **$8.86** | **~$9.86** |
| Adj. EBITDA | — | ~$1.02B (20.75%) |
| Free cash flow | — | ~$450M (guided) |

**True multiples: 20.8x FY2026E adjusted EPS, 12.9x EV/EBITDA, 3.7% FCF yield.** That is a fair-to-full price for a good industrial — not the absurd one the screen implies.

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **NARROW**

**Revenue mix (Q3'26):** Residential $744M (57%), Commercial & Industrial $553M (43%).

**Where the moat is real (Residential):**
- **~75–80% U.S. home standby share** with roughly **9,900 dealers** — a distribution network built over 20 years that competitors cannot buy.
- **Switching costs:** product is specified by the installing dealer; Mobile Link monitoring creates a service relationship. Homeowners don't swap a $15k installed asset.
- **Trade-up funnel:** >50% of standby buyers previously owned a Generac portable unit — a genuine customer-acquisition cost advantage.
- **Brand/intangibles:** Guardian Series is the default specified product at big-box and dealer channels.
- Structural tailwinds: grid strain from data centers, EVs, and heat pumps ("lower power quality"), plus an aging grid.

**Where the moat is absent (the new data-center leg):**
- Large-megawatt gensets pit Generac against **Caterpillar, Cummins, Rolls-Royce and Kohler** — deep-pocketed incumbents with decades of engineered-spec relationships.
- **Customer concentration and customer power are now explicit:** the Amazon agreement ($2.4B of initial 2027–28 deliveries) came with **warrants on ~1.7M shares vesting at $8B of purchases**. Generac's largest growth customer has equity participation — the economics are shared, not owned.
- Zero switching costs for hyperscalers, who dual-source by policy.

**Moat trend:** Widened in residential (dealer count, record 9.9k, activations returned to growth in Sept '26). Unproven and *narrowing-by-construction* in data centers. Net: the moat protects the franchise but not the earnings stream — **weather, not competition, is the primary driver of year-to-year results**, and a moat does not stop a mild hurricane season. FY2025 residential sales fell 23% in Q4 on exactly that.

**ROIC check (the moat test):** Reported ROIC was **7.3% in 2025**, 13.5% in 2024, 14.3% in 2022 — i.e., it fails the ROIC > WACC test in trough years. Critically, **excluding $2.14B of goodwill/intangibles, ROIC is ~26%**. The *operating business* is genuinely high-return; **acquired goodwill is what drags reported returns down.** That is a capital-allocation verdict, not an operating one.

Quality is improving, but this is not a bond-like compounder — it's a good cyclical with an emerging growth leg.

On EV/EBITDA (12.9x FY26E, ~9.5x FY27E) it is unremarkable for an industrial with this growth profile. **The stock fell ~31% from its June 2026 high of $296 to $205 in the November AI-linked de-rating — sentiment, not fundamentals, did the damage. But it is still not cheap.**

## 4. Management Quality: **Good (B / 7 out of 10)**

- **Aaron Jagdfeld**, CEO since 2008, took the company public in 2010 and built the residential dealer moat. Long-tenured, operationally credible, owner-minded in communication.
- **Guidance credibility is improving but blemished:** missed Q4'25 badly, then beat Q1–Q3'26 by wide margins ($1.80 vs. $1.33; $2.91 vs. $2.01) and raised FY26 EBITDA margin twice. Post-2025 reset, execution has been excellent.
- **Buybacks are real but poorly timed:** $346M repurchased in 2022 at levels well above today's price — value-destructive in hindsight.
- **M&A is the weak spot.** Pramac/Deep Sea (international) worked; **ecobee and PWRcell/clean energy have consumed capital and generated warranty charges with no visible moat.** The $2.14B goodwill pile is the receipt.
- **The Amazon warrants** are a defensible strategic trade (locks in anchor volume) but explicitly transfer value to the customer. Watch this closely.

Honest, capable operators — not exceptional capital allocators.

## 5. Investment Verdict: **HOLD**

**Not a Buy.** By the discipline that matters: the price is fair, and *a business trading at a fair price is not a buy*. My intrinsic-value range ($190–220) brackets the current quote. There is no discount to intrinsic value, let alone the 25% margin of safety I require.

**Not a Sell either.** The residential franchise is durable and genuinely moated, the data-center backlog ($4.5B, with $2.4B contracted to Amazon) is real contracted visibility rather than a PowerPoint, margins are at record highs with a leverage ratio of 1.4x, and FY27E EV/EBITDA of ~9.5x is not a bubble multiple. The November 2026 decline was an AI-sentiment event, not a thesis break.

**Action: hold existing positions; do not add.**
- **Buy zone: $150–165** (≈25% discount to ~$205 midpoint, and near the July–Nov 2026 panic low of $168.79).
- **Trim zone: >$270** (≈9.5x+ on bull-case 2027 numbers).
- This is a **"too hard" pile candidate at today's price** — the outcome depends on hyperscaler capex, which is outside my circle of competence.

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Backlog ≠ revenue.** $4.5B of data-center backlog must convert through a capacity build from ~$1.25B annualized to ~$4B by 2H2027. Any slip — Sussex ramp, Belvidere (opens early 2027), supply chain — pushes the 2027 story out a year and the multiple compresses fast.
2. **AI capex digestion.** If hyperscaler spending pauses, Generac holds ~$4B of dedicated capacity and $2.1B of goodwill. Impairment risk is real; the November 2026 AI selloff already demonstrated the stock's beta (1.86) to that narrative.
3. **Weather dependence remains the core risk.** ~57% of revenue is residential and outage-driven. FY2025 is the template: mild storms → residential –23% in Q4 → GAAP EPS of $2.69. There is no moat against calm weather.
4. **Peak margins.** 39.9% gross and 20.5–21% EBITDA are all-time highs versus a 15–19% historical band. Mean reversion would gut the 2027 EPS estimate even with revenue delivered.
5. **Competitive response.** Caterpillar, Cummins and Rolls-Royce will defend the large-MW market. Generac is the entrant, and entrants discount to win share.
6. **Customer concentration + dilution.** Amazon holds warrants on 1.7M shares (≈2.9%) vesting at $8B of purchases; a second hyperscaler deal likely carries similar terms. Growth is being purchased with equity and price.
7. **Serial-acquirer drag.** Reported ROIC of 7–14% versus ~26% ex-goodwill shows a decade of M&A has diluted returns. Another large, richly-priced deal would be a red flag.
8. **Clean-energy overhang.** Solar/storage is declining under higher rates and adverse policy, with recurring warranty costs.

---

**Bottom line:** Generac is a **genuinely good business with a narrow but real moat, an honest and capable CEO, a conservative balance sheet, and a legitimate second growth engine** — now priced at approximately fair value after a 31% drawdown. It is the kind of company I would like to own *at a price*. That price is not $205; it is closer to $150. **Hold, and wait.**
