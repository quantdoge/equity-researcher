**Investment Thesis**

AMD is a genuinely elite operating franchise — revenue accelerating (+45% YoY in Q3 FY26), gross margin expanding (46%→54%), a record 46.2% x86 server CPU share, and status as the sole credible second source to NVIDIA in AI accelerators — trading at a price that already capitalizes the FY27 ">80% growth" guide. Accounting is clean (fraud is screened *out*, not merely unproven) and the balance sheet is net-cash and A1/A-rated; the problem is purely price: a 0.92% FCF yield against a ~5.1% 10-year Treasury, with a Fed that is hiking again and HY spreads widening. The probability-weighted outcome (bull $625 / base $440 / bear $260 at 30/40/30) is ≈ $441 — flat. This is a franchise to own small, size off the 3.5 one-year beta, and add to only at lower prices or on confirmed fundamentals.

**Agent disagreement flag**: The spread between Growth (Strong Buy, ~$650) and Value (Sell, ~$290) is the widest in the coverage set. Both are internally coherent — the entire gap is the terminal-value assumption (a decade of 25% growth vs. cyclical interruption). Resolution: Growth is right about direction; Value is right about the price paid for it. Six of ten reporting agents independently identify valuation — not the franchise — as the dominant risk variable.

**Bull Case**
1. **Operating leverage inflection**: FY27 guided >80% revenue growth with opex growing at less than half that rate; operating margin already 1.8%→21.2% in three years; FY26–28E EPS CAGR ~79% makes 25.7x FY27E look cheap if the guide lands.
2. **Structural share gains on two fronts**: record 46.2% x86 server CPU share against a supply-constrained Intel (delays through H2-27), plus the only credible merchant AI-GPU alternative with named commitments from OpenAI (6GW), Meta, Microsoft, Oracle and Anthropic; agentic AI raises CPU attach, an underappreciated EPYC tailwind.
3. **Fortress balance sheet + proven execution**: net cash, A1/A, F-score 8/9, no solvency scenario; 7/7 beats; the 31% drawdown from $648 has partially reset the entry — permanent capital loss on fundamentals is highly unlikely.

**Bear Case**
1. **Valuation**: 96x TTM / 56x FY26E earnings, 34x tangible book, 0.92% FCF yield vs. 5.13% risk-free; reversion to the peer-median 38x on FY26E EPS alone implies ~$305 (−32%) with zero operational deterioration; prob-weighted DCF ~$290.
2. **Quality of revenue**: growth is partly self-financed — 320M penny warrants to two customers plus AMD investing $1–5B *into* its own customers — while Q3 cash conversion broke to 0.50x and inventory built to 169 days. This is the sequence that historically precedes de-ratings.
3. **Macro + geopolitical compounding**: a hawkish Fed into 3.7% CPI with HY spreads widening threatens the debt-funded portion of 2027 AI capex (bear path $200–250); China (22.4% of revenue) is one BIS rule or MOFCOM directive from vaporizing; Taiwan is an unhedgeable binary. Insiders are selling the narrative, not buying it.
