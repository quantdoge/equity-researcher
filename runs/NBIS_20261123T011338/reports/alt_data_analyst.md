## Alternative Data Report — Nebius Group N.V. (NBIS)
**Data window:** through 20–21 Nov 2026 | **Price:** $81.31 | **Mkt cap:** $19.5B | **Sector:** Technology / AI cloud infrastructure

**Context for reading every signal below:** NBIS fell from $154.69 (11 Nov) to $81.31 (20 Nov), **−47% in seven sessions** and **−72% from its June record (~$300)**. This was *not* a fundamental miss — Q3 (12 Nov) delivered revenue of $980.8M vs $907.2M consensus (+8.1%) and EPS of +$0.30 vs −$0.72 expected. The de-rating is a **financing/credibility event**: 2026 capex lifted to $27B, 2027 capex guided to **$40–45B** against 2027 revenue of only $12–13B, net debt up **+1,243% QoQ** to ~8x adj. EBITDA, and the $7B ARR target slipped two months. Read the alt data with that lens.

### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — **Cooling sharply (bearish)**

| Signal | Peak | Latest | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Trends "Nebius" (US, 12m) | 100 (wk of 9 Aug) | 43 (wk of 15 Nov) | **−57% from peak** |
| Google Trends "Nebius AI cloud" | 100 (wk of 28 Jun) | 1–2 (Aug–Nov) | **~zero** |
| Google Trends "GPU cloud" (category) | 100 (wk of 7 Jun) | 8–13 (Sep–Nov) | **−87% from peak** |

Three points matter:

- **The attention bubble deflated months before the price did.** Brand search interest peaked at the Q2 print (12 Aug) and decayed to 25–28 through September–October — a full month before the crash. The November re-acceleration (53 then 43) is *news-driven* (Burry, "sinking ship," −16% day), not demand-driven. Negative-flavoured attention.
- **Product-intent search has flatlined.** "Nebius AI cloud" went to literally 0–2 from late August. Low absolute volume makes this noisy, and enterprise GPU capacity is sold through direct contracts rather than search — so I weight this lightly, but the direction is unambiguously negative.
- **The category signal is the most important one here.** "GPU cloud" search collapsing 87% from June says the broad *bottom-up* demand narrative has cooled even as headline contract announcements keep coming. This is the cleanest evidence that the market is repricing the *slope* of AI infrastructure demand, not just multiples.

**Web traffic (nebius.com):** +10.71% MoM — positive, but *slower* than cloud.oracle.com (+12.64%). Engagement quality is poor: avg visit 2:30 vs Oracle 10:02, pages/visit 3.69 vs 12.17, bounce 42.6% vs 25.4%. Traffic is growing modestly but is shallow and non-sticky — consistent with a developer-evaluation site rather than a production console. *(Snippet-derived proxy, not SimilarWeb API — low confidence.)*

### 2. Business Activity Signals — **Expanding (bullish)**

- **Hiring: clearly expansionary, and specifically physical buildout.** ~60 live Indeed listings including 6× Data Center Technician, DC Facilities Engineer and RMA Specialist in **Vineland, NJ** (the Microsoft site), plus Kansas City. LinkedIn shows GPU Performance, HPC/GPU Compute and CloudGate engineering roles. Hiring skewed toward data-centre *operations* rather than R&D implies capacity is being commissioned, not just designed — supportive of the 2027 revenue ramp. *(Qualitative proxy; no time series, so no velocity read.)*
- **Contracted demand is the strongest signal in this entire dataset.** Signed/announced AI cloud agreements exceed **$46B**, including a **$12B Meta** capacity agreement (delivery from early 2027, Vera Rubin platform, plus up to $15B more) and the multi-year **Microsoft** Vineland deal. Management states it has deliberately *not* pre-sold all 2027 capacity and could have sold out the year. Deferred revenue +57.4% QoQ corroborates that customers are genuinely prepaying.
- **Patents: unavailable.** USPTO/PatentsView feed errored twice. Low materiality — Nebius is an infrastructure operator, not a patent-heavy R&D shop — but noting the gap.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **Below consensus (bearish), with low-quality beat history**

- Historical beat rate **85.7% (6 of 7)**, average surprise **+40.0%** — a strong track record, but built on a low, loss-making EPS base and increasingly polluted by one-offs. Q3's headline EPS beat (+$0.30) came alongside **$325M of non-recurring revenue** — organic Q3 was ~$656M, not $981M. Treat the beat record as low-quality.
- Next print **11 Feb 2027**: consensus EPS −$0.85, revenue **$1.215B** (+23.9% QoQ). Note the tension: $1.215B for Q1 implies a violent H2 back-end load to reach $12–13B for the year. **The guidance requires an acceleration that the alt data does not corroborate.**
- My read: **below-trend versus prior quarters.** Every forward-looking alt signal (category search, product-intent search, web engagement depth) is deteriorating QoQ, while the constraint management itself flags is *colocation delivery timing*, not demand. Watch the delivery schedule, not the bookings.

### 4. News Sentiment — **Score +0.444 (20 articles, 30d, 13 pos / 5 neg) — but the score is misleading**

The keyword classifier is mislabelling: "Nebius: Time To Jump Off The Sinking Ship" is tagged *positive*. The actual 7-day tone is **distinctly negative and defensive** — Michael Burry taking a public victory lap ("I told you so") on NBIS/CRWV/NVDA, coverage of the −16% day and 72%-below-record level, and a forensic piece on the +1,243% net debt jump. Structural narrative risk has escalated: Gartner forecasts **80% of neoclouds will "cease independent operations" by 2030**, Bernstein sees only 35% surviving, and the press has moved to "neocloud shakeout" framing (Fortune, BBC, Reuters). Counter-narrative is emerging but thin: two Seeking Alpha notes upgraded NBIS to Buy near/below $100 on the 64% drawdown and 47.5% adj. EBITDA margin.

Bullboard/StockTwits-style feeds show heavy negative price tags (−12.8% sessions) alongside dip-buying curiosity. Reddit (r/NBIS_Stock) remains constructively engaged on the Meta agreement. ### 6. Sector Read-Across — **Idiosyncratic risk is low, sector beta is high**

CRWV fell **−43%** from 15 Oct to 20 Nov; NBIS fell −47% in seven sessions. The magnitude is comparable, confirming this is a **neocloud-wide de-rating**, not a Nebius-specific fundamental break. NBIS carries the highest beta (1.56) of the group, so it takes the heaviest hit on the way down and would snap back hardest on any stabilisation.

### 7. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (bifurcated, with a bearish near-term tilt)**

The alt data does **not** support a −72% drawdown on fundamentals — contracted demand ($46B+ in signed deals, prepayments, deferred revenue +57%), buildout hiring and 47.5% adj. EBITDA margins all point to a business that is *scaling, not deteriorating*. But the alt data also gives **no evidence of a demand inflection** that would call the bottom: category search −87%, product-intent search at zero, web engagement shallow, and the selloff is being driven by a financing/credit narrative that alt data cannot adjudicate. **The binding constraint on this stock is funding and delivery timing, not demand** — and no amount of search or hiring data resolves that.

Practical framing: **fundamental demand intact, valuation now ~1.5x 2027 guided revenue vs $40–45B of capex** — a levered call option on AI compute scarcity, not a demand story.

### 8. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM-LOW**

- **Recency: high** (data through 20–21 Nov, price action current).
- **Data quality: low.** Google Trends is a weak proxy for a B2B enterprise infrastructure company whose revenue comes from a handful of hyperscaler contracts — the very customers who never google "GPU cloud." Web traffic is snippet-derived, not API. Patents unavailable. Job postings and social are qualitative search proxies. The headline news sentiment score is demonstrably mislabelled.
- **Lag:** Trends are weekly and revised; traffic is monthly. Neither can detect a contract cancellation or a capex revision — the two variables that actually matter here.
- **Where alt data is genuinely additive:** the timing observation that brand attention peaked in August and decayed through September–October, *ahead* of the November price collapse. That was the signal.

**Data gaps:** patent filings (API failure, twice); quantitative job-posting time series; real SimilarWeb monthly visits; Reddit/StockTwits quantitative sentiment; no options-implied or short-interest data.

**What would change the call:** (a) "GPU cloud"/"Nebius AI cloud" search inflecting off the floor would be the first genuine bottoming tell; (b) job postings shifting from data-centre ops back to capacity *sizing* roles would flag a demand reassessment; (c) any customer prepayment deferral would invalidate the entire funding structure — the highest-severity risk, and one that would show up in news before it shows up in alt data.
