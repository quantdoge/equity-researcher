## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT (High conviction on both sides)**

This is not a weak-consensus name. It is a **genuinely binary disagreement with high conviction on both tails**, and the split is clean and diagnostic:

**Nobody disputes the demand.** Sector, Growth, Alt Data and even the Short Analyst agree: $38.7B RPO, $46B+ signed agreements, investment-grade counterparties *prepaying*, 76% gross margin, capacity genuinely scarce. The Short Analyst explicitly lists this as the bull case and declines to be aggressive.

**Everybody disputes the funding.** Financial Risk, Macro, Geo/Legal and Value all converge on the same mechanism from four independent angles: **the equity is a levered call option on capital markets staying open for ~$35B of 2027 external funding, and the equity price is itself the funding currency.** A −72% drawdown is therefore self-reinforcing, not self-correcting.

**Resolution per my framework — risk flags dominate the bullish fundamental case.** Value's moat/owner-earnings argument is *unrebutted* by the bulls: Growth concedes the funding math is "unforgiving"; Sector concedes "execution, not demand, is the constraint." The bear case is not a valuation opinion — it is a **dated compliance cliff (mid-Feb 2027)** plus a **dated earnings print (11-Feb-2027)** plus a **dated injunction hearing (8-Mar-2027)**. Three hard catalysts inside 120 days, all capable of permanent capital impairment, none diversifiable.

**Two corrections I am imposing on the underlying reports:**

1. **The EV is understated by the bulls — this is material.** Growth and Sector cite "1.5–1.7x 2027E EV/Sales" using EV ≈ $21–24B, which is the vendor tool's output and **omits $8.65B of net debt**. On the correct EV (~$29–33B), 2027E EV/Sales is **~2.3–2.7x, not 1.5–1.7x**. Roughly 40% of the apparent cheapness evaporates. Value Researcher got this right; I adopt the $33B figure.
2. **Beta: underwrite to ~3.0, not 1.56.** Three reads are in the file — company 1.56 (2yr), Fin Risk 2.97 (730d), my 365d calc **3.96**. With 113% annualized vol and −71.6% realized max drawdown, the low figure is a stale artifact of the pre-collapse melt-up. **Use 3.0.**

**Expected-value math (Value's scenarios, my weights: Bear 35% / Base 45% / Bull 20%):**

> E[return] = 0.35(−67%) + 0.45(+18%) + 0.20(+130%) = **+10.7% over a 4-year horizon ≈ +2.5%/yr**

Against β≈3.0 and 113% vol. **That is a negative Sharpe proposition at $81.** Value's own arithmetic — base-case PV of $39B vs $33B EV — puts the stock at **~89% of base-case intrinsic value**. A 90%-of-intrinsic trim trigger sits at roughly **$89/share, about 10% above spot.** There is no margin of safety; there is a 10% cushion to a trim trigger and a 67% hole beneath it.
