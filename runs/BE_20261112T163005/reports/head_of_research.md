### Investment Memo: BE (Bloom Energy) — November 12, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate at $207; trim into strength | Conviction: **Low**
**Price Target**: $180 (~13% downside from current $206.77)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Bloom is the only scaled, profitable solid-oxide fuel-cell platform in the world and has become the default speed-to-power vendor for the AI data-center buildout — the margin inflection is real (gross margin 14.8% → 33.8% in three years), forensics are clean, and the balance sheet is genuinely de-risked. But at ~16x TTM sales, ~100x EBITDA and a 3.6–4.3 beta, the price already capitalizes four more years of ~50% compound growth: even the *bull* DCF ($190–200) sits at today's price. The residual is a portfolio of unhedgeable binary risks — Chinese scandium, New Mexico air permits, an active securities class action — for which the equity is paying nothing. This is a valuation verdict, not a business verdict.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is **Attractive** — turbine slots sold out to 2029–30, grid queues multi-year, AI power scarcity structural; BE is the #1 screened name on growth/margin vs. VRT, GEV, GNRC, PLUG, FCEL. Macro is **Unfavourable**: real 10Y yields +59bp since June, a hiking Fed, and ~25–30yr equity duration. October's 26.6% drawdown on a 5.31% 10Y print is the transmission proof. Operating backdrop (gas $2.63, grid tariffs +4%/yr) is a genuine tailwind — the problem is the discount rate.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH**. China put scandium under export licensing (Apr 2025); trade data contradicts management's "no China supply chain" claims, spawning an active 10b-5 class action (class period Feb'25–Jul'26), derivative probes, and a 10-Q/A. 81% US revenue is the mitigant; the input chain is the exposure.
- **Value**: **SELL/AVOID**. Base intrinsic $85–90; bull $190–200 ≈ today's price. EV/EBITDA ~99x, P/E ~118x LTM, P/FCF ~139x. Zero margin of safety.
- **Growth**: **BUY** (moderate). +122% FY26E revenue, Rule of 40 ~135, 7/7 beats, PEG ~0.7 on '26–'28; reverse DCF says market prices consensus, not perfection. Entry zone $180–200, half-size.
- **Quantitative**: **SELL**. Bottom quintile on value, quality *and* low-vol simultaneously; momentum-crash signature (+96% 12-1, but –19% 6-mo, –27% 1-mo); beta 4.31 with R² 0.30 — unhedgeable.
- **Financial Risk**: **MODERATE**, split cleanly: credit LOW (net debt/EBITDA 0.48x, coverage ~16x, $2.49B cash, no maturity before 2028, 0% converts) vs. equity risk HIGH (108% vol, –53% max DD). Solvency survives a –35% revenue shock; the multiple does not.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH**. Four CFOs since 2024; ~73% of H1'26 revenue from one customer; 74% of Q4'25 revenue from part-owned Brookfield JVs; $20B unaudited backlog vs. $443–493M audited RPO (>40x gap); $149.6M insider sales vs. $0 buys, including ~$47M by the CEO.

Air permitting is the binding constraint on backlog conversion — a *procedural* permit delay on Oracle's Project Jupiter cut 22% in one session. Governance upgraded (dual-class retired 2023, 9/10 independent board, 36:1 pay ratio) — note conflict with Non-Fin on residual Class B voting control; verify against DEF 14A.

But revenue beat magnitude has compressed three straight quarters (+39%→+29%→+15%) and the market now sells beats.
- **Short/Contrarian**: **Not a fraud** — Beneish –2.52, four years of negative accruals, receivables lagging sales. The bear case is arithmetic: 8% FCF conversion (Q3: +$250M NI, –$181M FCF), contract assets +343% YoY to $793M, and a 2028 policy cliff (48E credit deadlines) meeting peak order cycle.

**Bull Case**
- Speed-to-power monopoly in a capacity-constrained super-cycle: +138% Q3 revenue, 18.2% GAAP operating margin, guidance raised 3x in 2026, $20B backlog, Brookfield's $25B financing arm solving customer capex.
- Consensus path (2027: $7.3B rev/$5.34 EPS; 2030: $15.6B/$13.78) with a 100% beat record — PEG ~0.7 if delivered; sell-side PTs $230–351.
- Fortress balance sheet ($2.49B cash, 0.48x net leverage) plus clean forensics mean the downside is multiple compression, not impairment — dips get bought.

**Bear Case**
- Price = perfection: flawless execution through 2030 earns roughly zero return from $207; any slip against a 60x+ multiple with beta ~4 implies 30–50% drawdowns as the base-case volatility, not the tail.
- Cash quality: 8% FCF conversion, Q3 operating cash flow *negative* into record GAAP profit, contract assets and inventory absorbing the ramp; related-party (Brookfield JV) revenue circularity unresolved.
- Binary event stack: China scandium licence denial, Project Jupiter permit failure, class-action/SEC escalation, 2028 subsidy cliff, insider distribution at $277–298.

**Key Risks**
1. Multiple compression (real rates ↑ + growth deceleration from +122% to +60%)
2. Scandium supply "off switch" + litigation overhang (upgrades to Critical on SEC formal order or restatement)
3. Permitting latency gating backlog → revenue (NMED/Jupiter, AEP slip to 2030)
4. Single-customer and related-party concentration; FCF conversion staying <50%
5. 2028 air pocket: ITC deadlines + turbine supply normalization eroding the speed premium
