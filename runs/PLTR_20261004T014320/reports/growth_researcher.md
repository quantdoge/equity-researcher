## 1. Growth Story

Palantir is the operating system for the AI era. With the Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP), it has moved from government-intelligence data fusion (Gotham) and enterprise data orchestration (Foundry) to becoming the **"logic layer" of agentic AI** — turning proprietary enterprise data into governed, LLM-orchestrated decision workflows. The thesis is simple and powerful: every enterprise that adopts LLMs needs a data layer that is secure, ontology-driven, and deployment-flexible, and Palantir is the only vendor with a decade-plus track record in precisely that problem. Growth has **accelerated from 17% (2023) → 29% (2024) → 56% (2025)**, a near-unprecedented re-acceleration for a company at $4.5B revenue, driven by AIP bootcamps converting into commercial contracts plus a structural US "AI supremacy" defense supercycle (TITAN, Maven, Army battlefield programs).

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **Addressable market:** Management frames TAM in the **$150B–$1T+ range** (global enterprise data/AI software + defense/agency IT modernization). A defensible SAM is the $200–300B figure for AI-infrastructure software across the ~2,000 largest global enterprises and Western allied governments.
- **Penetration:** ~$4.5B revenue implies **<2% penetration of even the conservative $200B SAM** — Palantir is still in the earliest innings of market capture. U.S. commercial revenue is the growth engine (triple-digit growth); the U.K. and new allied markets (Australia, NATO partners) are greenfield.
- **Why Palantir, not a peer:** proprietary ontology/data-integration moat, federal-security-grade deployment (ONLY front-door to classified AI workloads), 100% revenue retention economics via deep embeddedness of AIP, and a private/on-prem/cloud-agnostic posture that hyperscalers' off-the-shelf AI stacks cannot match. Competitors (Databricks, Snowflake, C3.ai) address segments; Palantir addresses the full decision loop.

## 7. Key Risks

- **Valuation/mean-reversion:** at 299x trailing P/E and 55x FY2028 EPS, any guide-down — even a beat-and-lower — could trigger a 30–50% drawdown. Multiple compression is the dominant risk, not fundamentals.
- **Execution & commercial durability:** the 56% growth is increasingly US-commercial-driven; if AIP bootcamp→contract conversion decelerates or net revenue retention slips from ~130%+, the hypergrowth math breaks.
- **Customer concentration & government dependence:** sizable revenue remains tied to USG/defense agencies and a handful of large accounts; budget cycles or procurement reviews (e.g., any DOGE-style audit outcome) create lumpiness.
- **Competitive threat:** hyperscalers bundling AI data stacks, plus Databricks/Snowflake/C3.ai and open-source agent frameworks, could commoditize portions of the logic layer.
- **Valuation disconnect vs. sell-side dispersion:** the $80 Jefferies target vs. $250 UBS illustrates extreme analytical disagreement — expect violent headline-driven volatility around each print.
- **Insider/float dynamics:** heavy historical insider selling and equity-comp dilution (share count +20% since 2021) are a persistent, if modest, overhang.
