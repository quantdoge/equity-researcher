## MACRO IMPACT ASSESSMENT — Astera Labs (ALAB)
**As of 17 November 2026 | Macro regime: No-landing reacceleration + fiscal term-premium bear steepener**

---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary

The US is in a **no-landing, late-cycle reacceleration**, and the transmission is running almost entirely through the discount rate rather than through demand.

Real GDP grew 0.83% q/q in Q3 2026 (~3.4% annualised), +2.1% YoY, with unemployment stable at 4.1% (Oct). There is no recession signal in the activity data, and the 2s10s spread is positive at **+39bp** — the curve is no longer inverted; it has re-steepened. But this is a **bear steepener**: the 30Y is at 5.47%, the 10Y at 5.13% (5.15% on 13 Nov), up ~70bp from 4.44% on 1 July.

The driver is **real yields, not inflation expectations**. The 10Y TIPS yield has risen from **2.07% (2 June) to 2.91% (13 Nov), +84bp in five months**. Ten-year breakevens sit at only ~2.24% — *below* headline CPI of 3.82%. The market is pricing fiscal supply/term premium and a hawkish Fed, not an inflation-expectations spiral.

Inflation itself has re-accelerated: CPI is **+3.82% YoY** (Oct), with the 3-month annualised run-rate at roughly **5.7%** versus ~3.1% on a 6-month basis — momentum has turned back up in the last quarter. The Fed cut the effective rate from 5.33% to a 3.63% trough (June–August 2026), paused, and is now drifting back up to **3.88%**, with futures pricing a **December hike** as the live risk (CNBC, 13 Nov).

Critically, **credit is not stressed**: HY OAS is 3.23%, having widened only to 3.4% in early November from a 2.63% August trough. That matters enormously for ALAB — the AI capex cycle is increasingly debt-funded, and the credit channel is still wide open even as the rate channel has tightened hard.

**Net:** risk-on in credit, risk-off in long-duration equities. That is precisely the mix that hurts a 3.86-beta, high-multiple semiconductor growth stock while its end-market demand stays intact.

### 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

ALAB sells connectivity silicon (Aries/Scorpio/Taurus/Orion) into rack-scale AI infrastructure. Q3'26 revenue was **$584.9m, +154% YoY**, with Q4 guided to **$730–750m** (~27% q/q) at ~72% gross margin and Scorpio X-Series exiting the year above a $1bn run-rate. The demand backdrop is not the problem.

Beta is 3.86 (short post-IPO history, but directionally right). Each 50bp on the real 10Y has plausibly cost 8–12% of multiple. **Adjacent, non-macro shock to flag:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned 12 Nov amid a probe into alleged Chinese intelligence ties (CFO Colette Kress interim CEO), taking NVDA below $4tn and triggering a broad AI selloff. ALAB's content is levered to NVDA rack-scale platforms, so this compounds the rate-driven drawdown. It is a sector-idiosyncratic event, not a macro one — but it is currently the loudest driver of ALAB's tape and should be separated from the macro thesis.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Not because of demand — the operating momentum is exceptional — but because **the macro regime is maximally hostile to ALAB's valuation structure**. High real yields, a hawkish Fed with a live December hike, a bear steepener driven by fiscal supply, and a strengthening dollar form a headwind that overwhelms strong fundamentals. ALAB's 58% drawdown against 154% revenue growth is the cleanest possible proof that this is a discount-rate market, not an earnings market.

The rating is not "bearish on ALAB." It is: **the macro is a persistent valuation headwind, and the offsetting support is credit spreads staying near 3.2%.** Those two facts define the risk.

---

### 5. Surprise Scenario (Bear, materially underpriced)

**"The December hike is not the last one, and the AI debt trade seizes up."**

Consensus treats 3.82% CPI as a temporary re-acceleration and the 5.13% 10Y as near a peak. The genuine surprise is the opposite: CPI's 3-month run-rate at ~5.7% forces the Fed into a *sequence* of hikes back toward 4.50%+, pushing the 10Y through 5.75% and the 10Y real yield above 3.25%. Simultaneously, hyperscaler issuance — roughly a third of 2027's $1.1–1.3tn capex — saturates the IG market, HY OAS gaps from 3.2% to 4.5%+, and 2027 capex plans get trimmed in the February guidance round.

In that world ALAB's Q4 guide of +27% q/q becomes the peak quarterly growth rate, and the multiple compresses another 30–40% *before* estimates are touched — with beta 3.86 amplifying the move. A realistic downside is $110–130.

**The mirror-image bull case, for completeness:** if the CPI re-acceleration proves a shelter/energy artefact and the Fed's hike is a one-and-done, the 10Y real rallies 60–80bp back toward 2.30% and ALAB — already down 58% with estimates rising — re-rates violently. Asymmetric in both directions; the macro variable, not the business, is the swing factor.
