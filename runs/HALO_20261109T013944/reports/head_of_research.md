### Investment Memo: HALO — November 10, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $95 (−8% from current $103.63)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

---

**Investment Thesis**

Halozyme is a genuinely excellent business trading at roughly fair value with a contractual expiry date. It converts IV biologics to subcutaneous delivery via ENHANZE and collects royalties at 84% gross margins, 57% operating margins, 37% ROIC, and >100% FCF conversion — FY26 guidance of $1.86–1.93B revenue (+34%) and $1.24–1.29B adj. EBITDA is real cash, confirmed by every forensic test our short desk ran. But the core US patent expires September 2027, consensus already models revenue declining from 2029, and the stock at ~$104 sits at-or-above our blended intrinsic value of $93–100. The bull case rests on a binary legal option (MDASE enforcement vs. Merck's Keytruda SC) that HALO is winning in Europe and losing in the US. There is no margin of safety here — this is a hold-and-trade-the-catalysts name, not an initiation.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector:** Sector rated attractive near-term, degrading post-2027. HALO is the fastest-growing profitable name in its purpose-fit comp set (+37.6% revenue vs +16% peer median; 57.5% op margin second only to Royalty Pharma). Macro is neutral: cash flows are non-cyclical and inflation/tariff-resilient, but a 4.84% 10Y (peaked 5.31% in Oct) caps the multiple on a long-duration royalty stream. Notably, HALO fell −15.7% on Nov 6 *despite* a beat-and-raise — event risk, not macro, drives this tape.
- **Geopolitical & Legal:** MEDIUM overall (legal sub-rating HIGH). Operating footprint is low-risk (US/W. Europe/Japan, no sanctions/China manufacturing exposure). The dominant item is the multi-front patent war with Merck: injunctions won in Germany and 8 countries via The Hague, but PTAB rulings and a UK revocation (with indemnity costs against HALO) went the other way. CMS IPAY-2029 final rule: management projects zero-to-minimal royalty impact through 2035 — a dated, falsifiable claim, but a management assertion nonetheless.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value:** HOLD; manual DCF base case ~$85, probability-weighted ~$88. At $103.63 the market is capitalizing near-perpetuity of ~$1.1B FCF — approximately the bull case. Buy zone below ~$70; trim above ~$130. Flag: GAAP earnings are unusable (FY25 net income hit by $285M acquired-IPR&D charge); non-GAAP/EBITDA is the correct lens.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth:** BUY, moderate conviction; probability-weighted ~$124. Rule of 40 = 83.7, 38.7% five-year organic revenue CAGR, 6-of-7 quarterly beats. The defining caveat: consensus revenue peaks at $2.32B in 2028 and declines thereafter (−6.8% in 2029E). The MDASE estate is the under-appreciated asset — a royalty on Keytruda SC would be $300–600M+ annually past 2029, but it is legally binary.
- **Quantitative Signals:** BUY (not Strong Buy). Momentum 9/10 (rank 1 of 6, +32.6% 12-1), quality 8.5/10 (accruals −13.3%, ROIC rising four straight years to 36.6%), cheapest P/FCF in peer set. Drags: highest total volatility in the group (43.4%), low-vol factor rank 6/6, and a −24.5% single-session gap on record. The +41.6% alpha has t≈1.6 — directionally real, not statistically proven.
- **Financial Risk:** MODERATE. Credit risk low (45.8x interest coverage, cash covers 2027 converts 3.5x, no maturity wall until 2033, revenue could fall ~44% before coverage hits 1.0x); equity risk high. The single most important flag in the file: 6+ PH20/MDASE patents invalidated in 2026 — four in October, *all challenged claims*, on §112 written-description grounds — a portfolio-wide vulnerability, not a one-off. Insider activity is one-sided: zero open-market buys vs ~$30M sold in six months, including ~60k CEO shares at ~$109 in early October, immediately preceding the −15.7% drawdown. Governance is adequate (independent chair, no enforcement actions) but the board is staggered and CEO tenure is ~13 years.

- **Short / Contrarian View:** Structural SHORT, low conviction on timing, explicit "do not initiate here." Not a fraud short (M-score −2.59, FCF/NI 2.06x — the cash is real). A terminal-value short: bear fair value $55–65 if post-2028 normalized EPS is $5–6 rather than ~$10. Preferred short entry $118–130 or on an adverse US ruling.

**Bull Case**
- FY26 beat-and-raise momentum (+44% royalty growth in Q3), ~$1.25B+ adj. EBITDA, and a $1B buyback retiring ~5%/yr of the float at ~11x earnings — per-share value compounds even if revenue plateaus.
- MDASE option: European injunctions already enforceable in 8 countries against the world's largest drug; a US settlement/license on Keytruda SC adds $300–600M+ of annual royalty revenue and extends earnings duration past 2032 — re-rating to $130–175.
- Balance-sheet reality is far stronger than headline optics: 45.8x interest coverage, ~$886M TTM FCF, and the model absorbs a −20% revenue shock while remaining investment-grade.

**Bear Case**
- The patent clock: US composition-of-matter Sept 2027, EU March 2029; consensus already models revenue declining from 2029 and has cut 2030 EPS by 7.5%. This is a dated annuity, and the market is paying ~11.6x non-GAAP for it.
- §112 written-description invalidations are portfolio-wide: if the PTAB erosion reaches ENHANZE collaboration patents (not just MDASE), the 2035 royalty narrative breaks — and the market may be conflating the Nov 6 IRA reaffirmation with a defence against PTAB losses. They are different risks.
- Concentration and insider signal: Darzalex ≈45% of royalty revenue, royalties ≈66–69% of total; zero insider buys against ~$30M of sales into strength is a weak conviction tell at the top of the range.

**Key Risks**
1. **Binary litigation calendar** — UK EP622 hearing Nov 23, 2026; pending PTAB PGRs; NJ district court. A single adverse US ruling could gap the stock 20–35% (this name has a −24.5% session on record).
2. **Post-2028 royalty duration** — the thesis fails if new ENHANZE programs and MDASE/Elektrofi economics don't replace the expiring base on schedule.
3. **Capital allocation** — $1.1B of fresh debt sitting in cash, a history of large M&A (Evotec approach, $750M Elektrofi, Surf Bio IPR&D charge), and recurring "one-time" charges from a serial acquirer.
4. **Macro multiple compression** — a term-premium shock (10Y → 5.75%) takes 20–25% off the stock *with guidance intact*.
