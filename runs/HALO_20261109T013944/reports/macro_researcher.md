## HALO (Halozyme Therapeutics) — Macro Impact Assessment
*Data as of 9–10 November 2026. FRED series unless noted; company figures from Q3'26 release (5 Nov 2026).*

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Transitional: reflation with a hawkish Fed ("risk-on growth, risk-off duration")**

This is not a classic late-cycle slowdown. Real GDP re-accelerated to **+3.4% SAAR in Q3'26**, unemployment has fallen to **4.1%**, and yet **CPI is running at 3.7% YoY with the last two prints at +0.65% and +0.40% MoM (3-month annualised ≈4.5%)**. Inflation is re-accelerating, not sticky-and-falling. The Fed cut through H1'26 (EFFR 4.33% → 3.63%) then *stopped and reversed*: EFFR 3.75% (Sep) → **3.88% (Oct 2026)** — the effective rate is now above the mid-2026 trough, implying a tightening bias. The long end has repriced violently: 10Y from 4.48% (1 Jul) to **5.31% (5 Oct)**, back to 4.84% (5 Nov); 2Y 4.05% → 4.92% → 4.46%. The resulting curve is **positively sloped (+39bp 2s10s) but bear-steepened, with the 30Y at 5.47%** — a term-premium/fiscal-supply story, not a growth story. Credit is still easy but deteriorating at the margin (HY OAS 2.63% → **3.48%** since 31 Aug), and the broad USD has firmed **+3.0% in seven weeks** (118.08 → 121.67). Verdict: nominal GDP is booming (~6–7%), the discount rate is the binding constraint.

### 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

HALO is a **royalty compounder, not a cyclical drug marketer**: Q3'26 revenue $493M (+39%), royalties $341M (+44%, 69% of revenue); FY26 guidance revenue $1.86–1.925B, adj. EBITDA $1.24–1.29B (≈66% margin), non-GAAP EPS $8.75–9.05. At $103.63 the stock trades ≈**11.7x FY26 non-GAAP EPS** and ≈**10–11x EBITDA**. That low multiple *is* the macro story: the market is discounting long-dated royalties at a 5% risk-free rate.

**Realised evidence of low rate-sensitivity:** HALO is **+56% over 12 months** ($66.20 → $103.63) *despite* the 10Y rising from ~4.5% to ~5.0%, and rallied from $109 to $131 between 14 and 30 Oct while the 10Y probed 5.31%. The −15.7% on 6 Nov (to $103.63, 6.2M shares) came **despite a beat-and-raise** — driven by the Merck patent dispute/event risk, not macro. Conclusion: cash-flow fundamentals dominate the tape; the discount rate sets the *ceiling* on the multiple, not the day-to-day price.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL**
Split the rating honestly. **Cash flows: favourable** — non-cyclical demand, contractual royalty escalators, 40%+ growth, a policy overhang (MFN/IRA) just removed, and a model that is inflation- and tariff-resilient. **Multiple: unfavourable** — a 5%+ long end and a hawkish Fed cap re-rating for long-duration royalty streams and are the likeliest reason HALO sits at 11.7x earnings while growing EPS >100%. These roughly offset. The macro backdrop neither creates nor destroys the thesis; it determines whether 40% growth gets paid for at 11x or 15x.

### 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **Bear: "Term-premium shock / fiscal dominance"**
**Trigger:** CPI holds ≥3.5% into Q1'27 (Sep was 3.7%; 3M annualised 4.5%) while Treasury supply keeps pressuring the long end. The Fed hikes to 4.25–4.50%, the 10Y breaks the 5.31% October high toward 5.75%, the 30Y clears 6%, and the broad dollar runs to 128–130.

**Transmission to HALO:** (i) a long-duration royalty DCF loses 15–20%; (ii) ~$15–25M FX translation drag on ex-US royalties; (iii) multiple compresses from 11.7x to ~9x forward EPS as GARP funds de-rate all royalty/duration names; (iv) HY OAS doubling from 3.48% toward 5% triggers biotech risk-off, and HALO — liquid, profitable, index-weighted — gets sold as a source of funds. Net: **−20% to −25% even with guidance intact.** The critical point is that this drawdown would arrive with *record* operating results, so it cannot be managed by watching the P&L.

**Upside mirror (lower probability):** the 5 Oct 10Y spike at 5.31% marked the term-premium peak; disinflation resumes, the Fed pivots back to cuts, the 10Y returns to 4.3% and the dollar weakens. HALO re-rates to 14–15x FY26 EPS → $125–135, before any Merck resolution or buyback accretion.
