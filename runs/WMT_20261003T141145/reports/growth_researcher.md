# WMT — Walmart Inc. — Growth Investment Analysis
**Sector:** Consumer Defensive / Discount Stores | **Mkt Cap:** ~$830B | **Price:** $104.26 | **Beta:** 0.60

## 1. Growth Story
Walmart is no longer a pure volume retail story — it is converting the world's largest physical retail footprint (4,600+ US stores) into a high-margin flywheel of **retail media (Walmart Connect), membership income (Walmart+), third-party marketplace, and shoppable TV (Vizio)**, while e-commerce grows ~27% and advertising at ~38–53%. The headline grows mid-single digits; the *profit pool* grows double digits as each basket monetizes through ads, deliveries, and subscriptions. The bull case is not revenue acceleration — it is durable mix-shift margin expansion inside a defensive, inflation-resistant giant.

## 3. TAM Assessment
- **Addressable retail space:** ~$30T global retail; US grocery+general merchandise ~$2T+, with Walmart at ~10% share of US retail — headroom exists but is capped at mid-single-digit comps.
- **The real TAM is the emerging profit engines:** US retail media is a ~$50B+ market growing ~20%/yr (Walmart Connect is #2 behind Amazon); Walmart+ membership (~$100M+ members lever) and the marketplace; Vizio adds ~19M TV households of shoppable inventory. Penetration is early — ads/membership are still low-single-digit % of revenue but were the majority of profit growth.
- **Share shift catalyst:** Walmart is deliberately winning higher-income households (>$100K income cohorts) via delivery/omni — this is the classic "share of wallet from the top of the income distribution" growth vector.
- Implied 5-yr market forecast: analysts see ~$890B revenue by FY2031 (~4.5% CAGR) — *achievable* given consistent beats; the upside is if ads/membership compound faster than the topline.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts
- **Q3 FY27 earnings — Nov 19, 2026** (next event; est. revenue $187.3B / EPS $0.63). Beat history (86%) makes another beat the base case; watch guidance raise (already raised 3 consecutive quarters to 4.8–5.1% net-sales growth).
- **Holiday season trading** — Walmart+ net adds were strongest-on-record in Q3 FY26; seasonal traffic spikes visible in Google Trends (100 index at Thanksgiving/Christmas) support a strong Q4 read-through.
- **Walmart Connect / Vizio monetization** — ad growth at 38% global (43% US ex-Vizio) and potential SmartCast OS/CTV expansion is the highest-margin incremental-dollar stream; watch for ad-tier growth disclosures.
- **Membership actions** — Walmart+ price/benefit packaging ahead of the holidays (double-digit member growth already reported).
- **Tariff/import-cost management** — management's proven ability to hold margin through tariff pressure (ads up 46% even during tariff shocks) is a quarterly re-rate driver.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **Buy**
Not a Strong Buy: top-line growth is mid-single-digit and the stock is priced *for* the profit-engine story, not growth in revenue. But the combination of (a) 86% beat rate with conservative guidance, (b) +7.4% upward EPS revisions, (c) double-digit EPS growth from ads/membership/marketplace mix-shift, (d) 10–27% growth in the highest-margin lines, and (e) defensive positioning (0.60 beta, dividend now ~$0.99/yr and raised ~5%) earns a solid Buy for long-horizon growth-conviction investors. Valuation note: 38x P/E, 19x EV/EBITDA, 56x P/FCF are *rich* — at these levels the market is paying ~$56 per $1 of FCF for a 5% grower, so the thesis must be held to the profit-pool shift, with peers bracketing WMT (COST 27.6x EV/EBITDA / 44x P/E — pricier; TGT 10.3x / 19x — cheaper and the value alternative).

## 7. Key Risks
- **Multiple compression:** all six major price targets were **cut in Aug 2026** (Jefferies $150→$120, Evercore $140→$125, BMO $145→$126, etc., consensus ~$122) even after a beat — the market is de-rating the multiple on consumer/tariff concerns, not fundamentals. That repricing pressure is the single biggest near-term headwind.
- **Tariffs & cost inflation** on a 25%-gross-margin, low-ticket model; so far absorbed via supplier leverage, but a fresh shock hits the most price-sensitive customer base.
- **Consumer tape:** if the US consumer weakens, comps and the membership trade-down story stall (TGT is running cheaper on 10x EV/EBITDA for a reason).
- **Competitive:** Amazon (retail media+delivery), Costco (membership moat), Aldi/Lidl and Temu/Shein on price — Walmart's lead in physical-retail media is real but contested.
- **Execution/credibility:** Vizio integration and stores-as-fulfillment-hubs carry integration risk; labor-cost investment (2.1M employees, union pressure, wage inflation) can absorb the margin gains the market is underwriting.
