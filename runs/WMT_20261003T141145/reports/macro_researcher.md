# Macroeconomic Environment Assessment — Walmart Inc. (WMT)

**Analyst:** Macroeconomy Research | **Date:** Oct 3, 2026 | **Price:** $104.26 | **Mkt Cap:** ~$830B | **Beta:** 0.60

## 1. Macro Environment Summary — TRANSITIONAL (risk-off bias at the margin)

The US is in a **muddied middle regime**: the Fed has delivered ~170bp of easing off the 2024 peak (fed funds 5.33% → 3.63% by Aug-2026, drifting back to 3.75% in Sep), but the long end of the curve has *gone the other way* — the 10Y has risen from ~4.4% (June-2026) to **5.24%** (Oct-1). Real growth is positive but decelerating (+2.2% y/y real GDP), unemployment has crept from 4.0% to **4.2%**, and CPI has stalled at **~3.0% y/y** — sticky, tariff-boosted, above target. The yield curve has fully re-steepened (2s10s at +34bp, no inversion → recession signal off), but a 5%-and-rising long end at this point in the cycle is a **term-premium/fiscal-stress signal, not a growth signal**. This is a "soft landing that refuses to finish" environment: good for value-seeking staples demand, uncomfortable for duration-sensitive equities. For a trade-down retailer like WMT, the macro mix is **structurally favourable on volume** and **cautious on valuation**.

**Net read:** demand-side macro is *Walmart's regime* (sticky inflation + squeezed consumer + trade-down). The bear case lives entirely in the balance-sheet channel — a 5%+ 10Y and fiscal-term-premium — and in tariff-cost pass-through into food margins.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **FAVOURABLE** (with two named de-risks)

The macro environment is favourable to WMT's *business model* — value retail is structurally long the exact regime we're in (3% sticky inflation, labour market softening, household budgets under pressure) — and the company is demonstrably converting it into share. The rating is pulled down from a clear "favour-able" by (i) a 10Y that keeps re-rating duration-sensitive staples, and (ii) tariff-cost passthrough risk in food. Unless the 10Y breaks above ~5.5% or tariffs feed uncompensated grocery inflation, the macro backdrop continues to be a tailwind disguised as a choppy tape (stock -22% off peak despite solid fundamentals).

## 5. One Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise (BULL)

**"Disinflation without recession + Fed finishing the job."** If tariff deals and supplier concessions (which WMT is actively extracting) pull CPI from ~3.0% down toward **2.3-2.5% by mid-2027**, the Fed resumes cuts toward ~3.0%, and the 10Y rolls back toward ~4.3-4.5% (term premium unwinds): **WMT re-rates upward** — the multiple compression of 2026 reverses, margins expand as refunds/import concessions layer on, and the trade-down share gains keep compounding through a non-recessionary consumer. In that world the current ~$104 price (street range $110-155) is meaningfully cheap for a share-gaining staples monopoly with ~8%+ FCF. Watch: the 10Y between 4.5% and 5.0%, and whether WMT's holiday-quarter gross margin holds ~30-50bp of the tariff-refund cushion — both are month-ahead tripwires for this trade.
