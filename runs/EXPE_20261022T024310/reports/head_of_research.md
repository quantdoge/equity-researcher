### Investment Memo: EXPE — 27 October 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (staged initiation) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $345 (~21% upside from current $285.64), based on ~14x 2027E adjusted EPS of $24.69
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Expedia is an accelerating margin-recovery story that the market is still pricing as a structurally declining asset. Revenue growth has accelerated for six consecutive quarters (3.4% → 14.0%), the B2B/advertising mix shift is lifting take-rate and margin, and the balance sheet is net cash — yet the stock trades at 11.6x 2027E adjusted EPS, roughly half Airbnb's EV/EBITDA. The offsetting reality: 29x GAAP trailing, a HIGH non-financial risk stack led by antitrust litigation targeting the core hotel-contracting model, a permanent dual-class governance discount, and a hostile rates/FX/energy macro mix. This is a buy, but a half-sized, tranche-built one — not a full position.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector Attractive (~$1T TAM by 2030, high barriers), but EXPE is the slowest grower (7.6% vs 12.1% peer median) at the lowest margin (12.7% vs 23.3%) among the big four OTAs — a margin-recovery story, not a share gainer. Macro is Neutral with negative skew: demand resilient (Q2 bookings +12%, revenue +14%), but 10Y at 5.15% (+113bp), a live Fed-hike debate, DXY +3.5%, and energy CPI +16% all press a long-duration, ~35–40%-international name simultaneously. Macro bear path retests $185–200.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium-High risk. EU DMA/P2B regulatory tightening plus US antitrust scrutiny create persistent, costly model risk; chronic consumer/labor/airline-distribution litigation. No sanctions/FCPA issues.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: ⚠️ **Agent report unavailable (timeout).** Valuation judgment rests on Quant, Growth, and Short: cheap on forward adjusted EPS (11.6x 2027E) and headline FCF (8.25% yield); full on GAAP trailing (29.1x, a 21.7% premium to peer median) and on ex-float FCF (~4.5% yield). **Bull Case**
1. **Operating momentum is undeniable**: six quarters of accelerating revenue growth, 6-of-7 beats (avg +11.5%), Q2 above high-end guidance with FY26 raised — the short desk tested the bear case and failed.
2. **Largest margin runway in the sector**: 12.7% operating margin vs BKNG's 34.5% — every 100bp of catch-up is ~$160M EBITDA; B2B and advertising mix shift is the mechanism, and it's already working.
3. **Per-share compounding machine**: net cash + $3.1B FCF + ~5%/yr buyback shrink + new dividend = ~11% EPS CAGR against a 7.2% revenue CAGR — consensus 2030 estimates look undemanding vs an 11% TAM CAGR.

**Bear Case**
1. **The litigation cluster is existential-adjacent**: an adverse EU price-parity ruling or US class certification changes hotel-contracting economics and the brand trust premium at once — a permanent de-rating, not a one-off charge.
2. **Valuation and macro leave no cushion**: 29x GAAP, 21.7% peer P/E premium, 10Y at 5.15% with a hiking bias and a firm dollar — a miss on Nov 4 compresses the multiple into a demand stall.
3. **Earnings/cash quality is flattered**: 40% of "adjusted" EPS is add-backs; ~26–30% of headline FCF is working-capital float that reverses when bookings stall; receivables growth 4x revenue is the early signature to watch.
