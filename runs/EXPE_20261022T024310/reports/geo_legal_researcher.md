## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Active Litigation & Disputes:**
*   **Monopolization/Antitrust Suits:** EXPE has faced and sought dismissal of lawsuits alleging anticompetitive behavior related to acquisitions (e.g., Trivago).
*   **Consumer Litigation:** There are multiple class action lawsuits focusing on consumer protection, specifically related to refund policies and alleged "unfair or deceptive practices."
*   **Labor Disputes:** The company and its subsidiaries (e.g., Egencia) are involved in labor lawsuits concerning overtime pay and working conditions.
*   **Contract Disputes:** Ongoing legal battles with airlines regarding ticket distribution costs illustrate friction in the core business model.

**Regulatory Environment:**
*   **European Union (High Alert):** EXPE is highly exposed to specific EU legislation, including the Digital Markets Act (DMA), which targets the behavior of large gatekeepers, and the Package Travel Directive (P2B), which demands high levels of transparency and consumer protection for bundled travel arrangements.
*   **US Jurisdiction:** The company is under continuous scrutiny from US competition and consumer protection agencies.

**Justification:**
The rating is **Medium-High** because while there are no immediate, catastrophic geopolitical flashpoints (like direct state sanctions or expropriation risk), EXPE operates in an environment of persistent, high-stakes regulatory change.
