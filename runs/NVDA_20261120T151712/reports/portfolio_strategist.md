## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (agreement level: Low)

This is not a split between "good company / bad company." It is a split between **two different questions**, and the resolution is the whole recommendation:

**Consensus — HIGH agreement on the business and the arithmetic:**
- Demand is real and accelerating (Sector, Alt Data, Fin Risk, Value, Quant all concur).
- Margins are exceptional and structurally above peers (Sector, Value, Short, Fin Risk).
- The balance sheet cannot fail (Fin Risk, Short, Value — Altman Z 6.75, 503x coverage).
- The drawdown is a **multiple/governance event, not an earnings event** (Alt Data, Value, Quant, Short, Macro all explicitly say this).

**Contested — the disagreement is on governance and timing:**
- **Value** says buy at 31% below intrinsic. **Short** says the *quality of the earnings* is deteriorating faster than the multiple is compressing. **Quant** says the price factors are in free-fall and won't buy a −34% gap with no base. **Non-Fin** says CRITICAL and cap the size.
- **Macro** says don't fight a 2.9% real rate. **Alt Data** says the market is repricing key-man risk that alt data cannot resolve.

**Conflict resolution — the fraud/quality flag takes precedence, but only partially.** The Short Analyst explicitly disclaims fraud ("Altman Z safe; solvency/fraud short not supported") and the Non-Fin Analyst's CRITICAL rating is **governance and disclosure**, not accounting fraud. So this is not a "walk away" case — it is a **"size for the possibility that the 10-Q is the beginning of a restatement"** case. The Value Researcher's 3–5% sizing is wrong not because the valuation is wrong, but because **it is sizing on a business-quality thesis while ignoring an unhedged, binary, near-dated disclosure risk.** The Short Analyst's SHORT is also too strong — at 35.7x FCF with 75% growth guided, the downside is a de-rating, not an impairment.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.0% initial, 2.5% hard cap**

**Framework ladder position:** *Low conviction or multiple risk flags → 0.5–1.0% or pass.* I am at the top of that band because the valuation asymmetry is genuinely exceptional and the solvency risk is genuinely nil — but I am capping well below the 3–5% "high conviction" band because **five risk flags are simultaneously live and three of them are binary events inside 10 weeks.**

**Kelly check.** Probability-weighted 12-month value: 20% bull $255 (Fed pause + clean 10-Q + CEO named) / 45% base $185 (~12x FY28, muddle-through) / 35% bear $95 (restatement, capex break, or margin reset) → **EV ≈ $167.50, +19% vs spot.** With 40% annualised vol that is a Sharpe of ~0.47 on a one-year view. Full Kelly ≈ 38%; standard 1/10–1/20 fractional haircut for a single name with unmodeled binary risk gives **2–4%**. I then apply a further discretionary haircut for the **empty Geo Legal report** and the **unfiled 10-Q** → **1.0% now, 2.5% maximum.**

**Why 2.5% is the ceiling, not 3–5%:** at 40.3% vol, a 2.5% weight contributes **~1.0% of portfolio annualised volatility** on its own — before its 0.63 SPY correlation. A 3–5% weight would make one name with a deferred 10-Q and a vacant CEO responsible for ~2% of total portfolio volatility. That is not a portfolio decision; it is a bet.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration — the binding constraint.** NVDA's **highest pairwise correlation is TSM at 0.67**, not SPY. If the book already holds TSM, AVGO (0.47), AMD (0.49) or MU (0.49), NVDA is **not a new position — it is an add to an existing AI-capex risk unit.** Treat **NVDA + TSM + AVGO + AMD + MU as a single sleeve** capped at ~12–15% of the portfolio, with NVDA itself at 2.5%.

**Factor exposure — this is an anti-diversifier, not a diversifier.**
- β 1.74, **R² 0.40** → **60% of variance is idiosyncratic single-name event risk.** It does not hedge anything; it concentrates.
- Momentum is the worst factor state in the market (63d Sortino −6.1). Adding here **increases** the portfolio's negative-momentum tilt.
- Value (70) and Quality (78) are genuinely additive — but the Quality score is capped by **accruals 0.084, the worst in the peer group** (AVGO −0.026, AMD −0.044, TSM −0.073, ASML −0.060).
- **Beta budget:** at 1.74 beta, a 2.5% position adds ~4.4% of portfolio beta-equivalent exposure. If the book's aggregate beta is already >1.1, this position needs an offsetting hedge or a smaller weight.

**The valuation/factor trap to avoid:** at **35.7x trailing FCF (2.8% yield) against a 10Y at 5.13%**, NVDA offers a **negative ~230bp real spread**. The "cheap" case rests entirely on FY28 consensus EPS of $15.72 being delivered — and the Q4 guide (71–72% GM vs 74.9%) does not yet support the margin recovery that number requires. This is a *forward-earnings* bargain, not a *cash-flow* bargain. Own it as a growth position, never as a value position.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Price stop-loss: $110 (−22% from $140.92).** With 40.3% annualised vol, monthly sigma ≈ 11.5%; a 22% stop is ~2 sigma and therefore not noise-triggered. A break below $110 means the market is pricing a restatement or a capex break, not sentiment.

**Fundamental stop — these override price and require immediate exit regardless of P&L:**
1. **Q3 10-Q discloses a material weakness or restatement** → exit in full. This is the categorical change; the thesis does not survive it.
2. **10-Q still unfiled by 31-Dec-2026** → reduce to 0.5% and reassess. A deferred periodic report that becomes a *delinquent* one triggers exchange/rating consequences.
3. **Gross margin guided below 68%** for Q4 or FY28 → cut by half. The 74.9% → 71–72% step-down is already a real crack; sub-68% means the pricing-power thesis is over.
4. **FY28 revenue guidance cut, or the $390B supply commitment disclosed as impaired/renegotiated** → exit. The supply book is the moat; impairment converts it into a liability.
5. **Macro tripwire: HY OAS > 500bp or 10Y > 5.75%** → reduce to 0.5%. This is the Macro Researcher's explicit invalidation condition — it breaks the levered neocloud financing chain that underpins the marginal order.
6. **Loss of a top-3 customer, or merchant-GPU share below 50% of accelerator shipments** → cut by half. ASIC substitution is a structural share shift, not a sentiment wobble.

**Trim triggers:**
- **$190–205** (≈90% of the $203–225 fair-value band, ~12–13x FY28): trim **one-third**.
- **$225+** (median post-earnings PT $227.50, ~14x FY28): trim to a **1.0% core** holding.

**Near-dated binary calendar — do not add into these:** **1 Dec 2026** congressional testimony (Huang + GC Teter — GC testimony creates new disclosure obligations); **8 Jan 2027** securities class-action lead-plaintiff deadline; **24 Feb 2027** Q4 print (consensus $2.84 EPS / $130.79B revenue — note guidance revenue of $130.0B is *below* consensus midpoint and requires the high end of both revenue and margin to clear).
