## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Investment Grade (effectively AAA-caliber balance sheet) — no formal agency rating available in data (gap flagged)**

NVDA carries **net cash of $51.1B** (cash & ST investments $62.56B vs. total debt $11.41B). Interest coverage is **503x** (EBIT $141.7B / interest $0.259B). There is no plausible operating scenario in which NVDA fails to service debt. Altman Z-Score of **6.75** sits deep in the safe zone (>2.99), with book-equity x4 of 3.18 — using market cap instead would push the score far higher. This is a creditor's dream credit: negative net debt, 0.07x debt/equity, and 60% operating margins.

The only credit-relevant concerns are non-solvency in nature: a **Piotroski F-Score of 5 (only "Moderate")** — unusual for a company of this quality — driven by deteriorating accruals quality and working-capital absorption (detailed in Red Flags).

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Extreme customer concentration** — Two direct customers accounted for **36% of FY2026 revenue**; at peak, the top 6 customers represented **85% of quarterly revenue**. A single hyperscaler capex pause is an immediate 15–25% revenue event. *(Source: NVIDIA FY2026 annual report / Reuters)*

2. **Piotroski F-Score only 5/9 — accruals quality failing.** CFO ($102.7B) came in **below net income ($120.1B)** for the first time in the trend, with a **-$15.9B working capital drag**. Receivables grew **+67%** (to $38.5B) and inventory **+112%** (to $21.4B) versus revenue growth of +65%. DSO rose to 65 days (from 60) and inventory days to 125 (from 113). Classic late-cycle growth-quality signal — earnings are outrunning cash collection.

3. **Gross margin compression and falling asset turnover.** Gross margin fell 610bp YoY (74.99% → 71.06%) and asset turnover fell from 1.17x to 1.04x as the balance sheet ballooned (+85% total assets). F-Score components F3, F4, F8, F9 all scored zero.

4. **Goodwill & intangibles jumped 4x** ($6.0B → $24.1B) — acquisition-driven. Impairment risk if acquired assets underperform.

5. **China revenue excluded from guidance.** Export controls have already cost an estimated $2.5B/quarter plus a guided $8B forward hit; Huawei share gains toward 50–60% of China AI chips could cost up to **$15B annually**. Guidance no longer includes China — but the geopolitical tail risk is unresolved and can re-escalate.

6. **Valuation provides no cushion.** FCF yield of **2.8%** against a $3.37T market cap means the price embeds sustained hyper-growth. A 20% revenue decline would compress the multiple violently even though the business remains enormously profitable.

7. **Recent momentum broken** — 6-month Sharpe of -1.876 with 41% realised volatility.
