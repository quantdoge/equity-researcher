**Investment Thesis**
NVDA is the widest-moat franchise in semiconductors — 75% gross margins, 77% ROIC, net cash, revenue +103% YoY — trading at ~15x trailing and ~9x FY2028E earnings after a 41% drawdown driven by a governance crisis (founder-CEO ouster, SEC internal-controls probe, an as-yet-unfiled Q3 10-Q), not by earnings, which are still accelerating and were guided *up*. **Bull Case**
- Demand is real, accelerating and contractually locked: +103% YoY, $390B of supply commitments, FY28 guided *up* to 75%, hiring +115%, rental rates firming — against a price implying a permanent ~30% earnings decline.
- Valuation asymmetry: ~9x FY28E with a $227B buyback authorization remaining and median PT ~$225; the two governance events alone (clean 10-Q + credible CEO) re-rate the stock from ~8.5x toward 15x FY28, ~+60–75%.
- Unpriced optionality: China is zero in guidance, so any licensed resumption is pure upside; the macro surprise (Fed forced to stop by Q1 2027, real 10Y 2.9%→2.3%) implies a 30–50% re-rating on a 2.2-beta name with estimates unchanged.

**Bear Case**
- Earnings quality is deteriorating on every cash metric simultaneously: 67% conversion on 9M, DSO +18 days, 70% of receivables in five counterparties, worst-in-peer-group accruals — the classic late-cycle signature, trending the wrong way three years running.
- NVDA is financing its own demand: $530B of guarantees (2x equity), customer equity stakes, and a levered neocloud tier whose funding chain snaps if HY OAS exceeds 500bp — with hyperscaler capex already at ~93% of operating cash flow, there is no incremental internal cash left to fund acceleration.
- Governance is an open wound: no permanent CEO through a technology transition, an SEC probe into the controls the interim CEO signed off on, and a periodic report unfiled during a disclosure investigation — a restatement or material weakness changes the thesis categorically.
