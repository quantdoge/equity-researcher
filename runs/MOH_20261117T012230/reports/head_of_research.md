**Investment Thesis**
Molina is a high-quality pure-play Medicaid operator at a genuine cyclical trough — the 2027 rate/trend spread has inflected positive (~6% rates vs 5.5–6% trend) for the first time in three years, and management has sketched >$9.50 of 2027 EPS versus a ~$5.25 2026 base. But the stock has already re-rated +92% off the February low and now trades at ~24x that unconfirmed 2027 recovery, ~44x 2026 EPS, 6.3x tangible book, and above its own street median target ($220). Three independent valuation anchors — intrinsic value ($208), street median ($220), and growth-based entry ($180–190) — all sit below the current price. Eleven specialist agents, zero buy recommendations. The business is mending; the price has run ahead of it.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector in late trough/early recovery; 2027 rate resets are the first positive spread since 2023, but OBBBA funding cuts (Oct-2027) and ~6% work-requirement attrition cap durability. Macro skews negative: 10Y at 5.13% (+67bp) compresses a back-end-loaded recovery multiple; 4.1% unemployment is countercyclical headwind to Medicaid membership. Bear macro path: $110–130.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Risk is legislative, not geopolitical — 100% of revenue is government-priced. Work requirements effective 1/1/27; ACA subsidy expiry already biting. Recurring FCA/AG settlement pattern (TX $40M, MA $4.6M, FL $0.26M); securities class action dismissed Sep-2026. Rating: Medium.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Narrow-to-no moat; zero pricing power (134% revenue growth 2020–25 delivered −30% net income). ROIC collapsed 25.8% → 9.7%. FCF −$636M. IV ≈ $208; required entry $165. Price is ~12% above IV with no margin of safety.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: Not a growth stock — revenue contracting (−3.5% YoY), 4.4% CAGR to 2030, Rule of 40 = 10. EPS recovery is arithmetic mean-reversion, not market capture. Buyer only at $180–190 or on a formal 2027 guide >$10.50.

**Bear Case**
