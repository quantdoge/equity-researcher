### 6. Short Thesis Summary

**The credible bear case is valuation and expectations, not accounting.**

Molina trades at **44x FY2026E EPS ($5.26)**, **24x FY2027E ($9.65)**, **3.0x book** and **6.5x tangible book** — for a business with a **0.6% adjusted pre-tax margin**, **negative trailing GAAP earnings**, **declining revenue**, a negative rating outlook, and $2.2B of unimpaired goodwill.

Timing a valuation short into sector-trough momentum is how shorts lose money.
