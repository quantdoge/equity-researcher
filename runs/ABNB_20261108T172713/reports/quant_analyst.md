### 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

1. **⚠️ SEVERE MOMENTUM-REVERSAL / OVERBOUGHT RISK — highest-priority flag.** Total 1-yr return +68.5% but 12-1 momentum only +9.1%. Decomposing: 1.6845 / 1.0907 = **+54.4% concentrated in the last ~21 trading days.** The entire Sharpe/Sortino/return record rests on a one-month vertical move (analyst PTs posted at $170 on 2026-11-06 vs $190 now). Standard 12-1 momentum *excludes* this window precisely because it tends to reverse. Expect high probability of near-term give-back.

2. **Factor crowding / one-sided positioning.** 7 of 8 published targets raised in a single week (range $180–$245, median **$200.50**, mean $206.4 → only **+5.5% / +8.6%** upside). Implied upside is thin relative to realised 1-month volatility of ~15–20%; the risk/reward on new entry is poor even with a bullish factor composite.

3. **Alpha is statistically indistinguishable from zero** (t ≈ 1.24, R² 0.145). Low market correlation (0.379) means ABNB is largely idiosyncratic — that cuts both ways: high active risk, and benchmark-relative performance will be dominated by company-specific news, not market direction.

4. **Max drawdown −21.5% inside a +68% year**, with 41.8% vol and beta 1.12 — this is a high-beta, high-vol name that has already drawn down ~22% once in the window. Position sizing should reflect that, not the headline Sharpe.

5. **Valuation offers no cushion.** EV/EBITDA 40.6 and P/E 47.1 leave zero multiple support if momentum breaks. P/FCF 25.5 is the sole valuation anchor.

6. **Fragility in quality sub-scores.** Piotroski F-Score **5/9** (moderate) — fails on ROA improvement, leverage reduction, equity issuance (dilution), and gross-margin improvement; only asset turnover improved. FY2025 net income fell to $2.511bn from $2.648bn despite higher FCF ($4.646bn vs $4.518bn) — GAAP earnings are decelerating while cash flow grows. Watch for margin compression.

7. **Sample-size / methodology caveats:** (a) Sector rank uses n=8 large-cap Consumer Cyclical names, not the full sector — ranks are indicative, not precise quintiles; (b) revision score uses an FY2030 estimate, not forward-12m; (c) two internal Sharpe values (1.66 vs 1.40) and two market caps ($113.8bn vs $118.4bn) disagree — likely geometric vs arithmetic annualisation and basic vs diluted shares; (d) ~6 years of trading history post-2020 IPO limits long-horizon factor stability testing.

---

**Bottom line:** Quality and relative momentum are real and quantifiable; value and low-vol are outright fails; the 47% alpha and 3.3 Sortino are artifacts of a 54% one-month spike with no statistical support. **Buy the name, not the price — accumulate on a pullback toward the $170 area rather than at $190.**
