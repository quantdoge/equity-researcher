## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (near-term bearish skew)**

**The alt data splits cleanly along time horizon:**

- **Demand is intact but decelerating.** Bookings +25% YTD, AI bookings +50%, software +43% — these are real, accelerating forward signals, and the guide cut is genuinely a *timing* event on three named federal deployments, not lost demand. Search interest has stopped falling (+5% QoQ) and hiring is active. This is not a broken franchise.
- **But the estimate-momentum alt signals are unambiguously negative.** Beat magnitude has decayed to +0.6% → +2.2% → −9.0%; product-specific search is −23% QoQ; consensus sits at the top of a freshly cut guide; PTs down 17% with $380–$700 dispersion; a securities-fraud solicitation is live; and consensus's **+33% FY27 revenue** requires a re-acceleration that no dataset here corroborates.

**Net:** I would **not** add into the Feb 23 print. The stock has already de-rated 48% and the rebound is technical, but the guidance cadence (two consecutive guide-downs and two consecutive November misses) plus the razor-thin headroom above the Q4 guide leaves the risk/reward on the *print itself* skewed negative. Constructive only on a longer horizon, and only if Q4 bookings re-accelerate and the federal Dedrone revenue reappears in 2027 guidance.
