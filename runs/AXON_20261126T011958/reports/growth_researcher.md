## AXON — Axon Enterprise, Inc. | Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $343.05 · Mkt cap ~$28.3B · EV ~$28.5B · 82.4M diluted shares*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Axon is no longer a TASER company — it is becoming the operating system for public safety. It has compounded revenue **32.5% annually since 2021** (to $2.78B in 2025) by wrapping an evidence-management SaaS layer (Axon Evidence, Fusus, Axon Records, Draft One AI) around a hardware annuity (body cameras, Fleet, TASER 10, Dedrone counter-drone), and it is now extending into the two largest new fronts in the space: **AI copilots for first responders** (AI ARR >$100M, AI bookings +50% YTD) and **Drone-as-First-Responder**, backed by a $1.2B investment taking its **Skydio stake to 37%** with exclusive US/Canada public-safety distribution. The stock is down ~55% from its Aug-2025 high (~$867) on a *timing* problem — $130M of federal Dedrone deployments slipped out of 2026 — not a demand problem, which is precisely the kind of dislocation a growth mandate should be buying.

### 3. TAM and Penetration

Axon does ~$3.6B of revenue against a public-safety technology spend pool that management frames as expanding from a ~$20–25B core (US law-enforcement evidence, cameras, less-lethal, CAD/records) toward **$60–100B+** as the definition of the market widens to include federal, international, drones/DFR, and — critically — **non-law-enforcement verticals** (healthcare, schools, enterprise security, corrections; Q3 included a $10M enterprise healthcare win). Treat the outer number as a management framework, not a measured SAM.

Penetration evidence that the runway is real, not narrative:
- **International is still a minority of revenue**, yet produced a $21M TASER 10 win with an international police agency — the least-penetrated, longest-duration leg.
- **New 5-year bookings +25% YTD**, with Q3 bookings ~$1B and deals of $258M / $115M / $40M — deal *size* is compounding faster than deal count, the signature of platform consolidation rather than unit sales.
- **Software & Services +43% to $437M** (accelerating, now ~48% of revenue) while **Connected Devices +16%**. Newer software (real-time ops, Axon Records, AI, Axon 911, Dedrone software) hit a ~$500M run-rate, **more than doubling YoY**.
- **AI ARR crossed $100M**; AI Era Plan bookings reportedly +~140% YoY early 2026.

Penetration is still single-digit-to-low-teens of the expanded TAM. The moat is the **switching cost of the evidence file** — once a decade of case video, chain-of-custody and records live in Axon Evidence, ripping it out is an evidentiary liability, not an IT decision. That is why gross margins hold ~59% despite hardware mix and why bookings durations stretch to five years.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4 2026 earnings — ~Feb 23, 2027** *(highest-impact, near-term)*. Consensus $1.98 EPS / $1.005B revenue against guidance of $990M–$1.01B and adj. EBITDA $245–250M. The bar has been reset; a clean print plus credible 2027 framework is the single biggest re-rating lever. Watch whether any of the **$100M of deferred federal Dedrone revenue** is pulled forward.
- **Body 5 + TASER Ion ramp (launched Oct 2026, revenue through 2027).** A genuine hardware refresh cycle that historically pulls the whole ecosystem (docks, Fleet 3, AI Era Plan attach) — the mechanism by which hardware growth re-accelerates from 16% toward 20%+.
- **$130M of federal Dedrone deployments re-entering 2027.** Structurally outside consensus; three signed-but-delayed federal deployments landing = pure upside to the 26.9% 2027 growth number.
- **Skydio / Drone-as-First-Responder.** 37% stake + exclusive US/Canada public-safety distribution. Any Skydio capital event, federal DFR program award, or first material DFR revenue disclosure forces the market to value a business it currently assigns zero.
- **Axon Guardian / "Axon Gravity" superintelligence platform.** If AI ARR compounds from $100M toward $300M+, the narrative shifts from "hardware cyclical" to "AI software multiple" — the classic Cathie Wood re-rate.

Trailing GAAP multiples (P/E 227x, EV/EBITDA 145x, P/FCF 376x) are noise — 2025 GAAP earnings were crushed by $634M SBC, $83M D&A and $94M interest expense. The relevant frame: **36x 2027E EPS for a 27%+ three-year revenue compounder with accelerating software mix.** PEG on 2026–28 EPS CAGR (32.6%) is **1.46x on 2026, 1.11x on 2027** — cheap for this quality tier. A 40x multiple on 2028E EPS of $12.64 gives ~$506; 40x on 2027E gives $378. What the market implies at 4.9x 2028E sales is deceleration to high-teens growth and a sustained ~20x terminal multiple — i.e., near-total write-off of the drone/AI optionality.

### 7. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, accumulate)

Not a Strong Buy, and the distinction is honest: this is a superb 25–30% compounder trading at a de-rated multiple with a fully intact demand signal, but the *quality of earnings* story is not yet repaired. Two straight Q3 misses, a 310bp gross-margin decline since 2021, and an FCF line that hasn't yet proved it can convert 25%+ EBITDA margins into cash keep this out of the top bucket. The setup, however, is exactly what a five-year growth horizon wants: **estimates flat-to-up, multiple down 55%, and a $130M revenue deferral — not loss — sitting outside consensus for 2027.**

### 8. Key Risks

1. **Federal lumpiness / customer concentration.** This is the risk that already bit. $130M slipped in a single quarter across three deals; a federal budget continuing resolution or procurement protest can repeat this at will. Do not model federal smoothly.
2. **Gross-margin compression.** Hardware mix + **elevated memory costs (~50bp 2026 drag, more in 2027)** + Dedrone counter-drone hardware. If gross margin breaks below ~58%, the "software platform" multiple thesis weakens materially.
3. **Stock comp and dilution.** 22.8% of revenue, diluted shares 66.2M → 82.4M since 2021 (+25%). Non-GAAP EPS flatters the story; GAAP net income was $125M on $2.78B revenue.
4. **FCF quality / working capital.** $482M WC outflow in 2025. If inventory is being built into a demand air-pocket rather than ahead of memory inflation, this reverses badly.
5. **Capital allocation concentration.** $1.2B into Skydio (37%), plus Dedrone integration — large, illiquid, private-mark asset, likely debt/cash funded (2025 interest expense jumped to $94M from $7M). Write-down or a Skydio funding down-round hits both book value and sentiment.
6. **Competition.** Motorola Solutions (end-to-end public-safety incumbent), **Flock Safety** (fast-growing, well-capitalized ALPR/network competitor), DJI on drone price, and well-funded AI-native startups attacking Draft One-style report automation. The moat is real but not unassailable.
7. **Multiple risk.** At 36x 2027E the stock is no longer expensive, but it is not statistically cheap either; a deceleration print below 25% growth would compress it further toward 25x.
8. **Reputational/political.** Exposure to immigration-enforcement and controversial deployments can create ESG-mandate selling unrelated to fundamentals — a persistent source of the volatility seen since Jan-2026.
