## 1. Geographic Risk Map

Axon discloses revenue only as **United States** vs. **all other countries** — no country-level breakdown is provided, so sub-US splits below are estimates and flagged as such.

| Geography | Revenue exposure | Basis | Risk level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **United States** | **~82–83%** (FY2025: $2,305.0M of $2,779.5M; TTM to 6/30/26: $2,630M of $3,222M = 81.6%) | 10-K FY2025; stockanalysis.com geographic data | **Medium** — not country risk but *federal procurement/political* risk |
| **Canada** | Est. ~2–3% | Skydio exclusive public-safety distribution in U.S. **and Canada**; no disclosure | Low |
| **UK / Western Europe** | Est. ~5–7% (largest international bloc) | Axon UK entity; **NSPA (NATO) Counter-UAS framework** selection; EU AI Act/GDPR exposure | **Medium** (regulatory, not political) |
| **Middle East / Turkey / Africa** | Est. ~3–4%, growing | Dedicated Managing Director for Middle East, Turkey & Africa; silver sponsor + product launches at **World Police Summit, Dubai (Nov 16–18, 2026)**; "Axon Arc" partner program launched there | **Medium-High** — human-rights/export-control and FCPA channel risk |
| **Ukraine / NATO eastern flank** | Immaterial revenue today, strategic | Dedrone counter-UAS deployed in Ukraine; Axon strategic investment in Ukraine's **The Fourth Law** (AI military drones); NATO counter-drone marketplace (Nov 5, 2026) | **High** (conflict/end-use risk) |
| **Taiwan / China (supply chain, not revenue)** | ~10–25% of certain components at risk per CFO | Q3'26 call: geopolitical conflict could disrupt "access to critical components like batteries and chips… roughly 10% to 25% depending on the component" | **Medium-High** |
| **Australia / LatAm / India** | Est. ~2% combined | Reseller/distributor model; no disclosure | Medium |

**Key structural point:** Axon is a **domestically-anchored revenue story with a globally-sourced supply chain**. ~82% of revenue is USD-denominated U.S. state/local and federal public safety budgets, which materially insulates it from EM currency, expropriation, and sanctions-driven revenue loss — but concentrates risk in the *U.S. political/budget cycle*, the *U.S. legal system*, and *Asian component supply*.
