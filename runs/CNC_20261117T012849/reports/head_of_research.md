### Investment Memo: CNC — 17 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate new capital at spot | **Conviction**: Medium
**Price Target**: $78 (+4% from current $74.77) | Intrinsic value range: $59–$102
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Centene's margin recovery is real — repricing is landing (2026 adjusted EPS guidance raised to >$5.20; HBR guided to ~90.75% from 94.3%), the balance sheet is net cash (~$2.2B), and buybacks have cut the share count 17% since 2021. But the market has already paid for it: the stock has nearly tripled off the July 2025 low and trades at ~14x 2026E adjusted EPS with effectively zero upside to street targets (mean ~$75). At this price you are underwriting a live, unaccrued DOJ False Claims Act suit, junk-rated credit, a 92.7% HBR with ~100bp of tolerance, and two binary 2027 policy cliffs (ACA subsidies, Medicaid work requirements) — for a ~5% expected return against 47.6% volatility. That is a watchlist entry at $60, not a portfolio position at $75.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector Neutral — the 2026–27 repricing cycle is genuine, but volume is shrinking everywhere and the re-rating has already occurred; remaining upside requires earnings delivery. Macro skews unfavourable: ~9–11% medical cost trend against administered rates (CY2027 MA finalized at +2.48% — better than the proposed +0.09% but below trend), a bear-steepening curve (10Y 5.13%), and a Fed hiking into 3.8% CPI. CNC is short-duration value, a relative shelter, but macro is the risk to the trade, not the support.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **High risk.** Zero cross-border exposure, but the highest domestic policy/legal risk tier in large-cap managed care. DOJ intervened in the FCA suit (*US v. Centene*, M.D. Tenn., 27-Oct-2026) alleging four years of ineligible MA enrollments — treble-damages exposure on a novel, portable theory, unresolved and unaccrued. Layered on a $1B+ multi-state PBM settlement record and a pending securities class action.
- **Value**: **Hold / do not initiate.** IV ~$80 (range $59–105) = only ~7% discount to spot, and the stock trades above the median analyst target. No moat — ROIC below WACC four consecutive years; required entry $55–60 for a true margin of safety.
- **Growth**: **Buy** (the lone bull, and self-labeled "value/turnaround, not growth") — PEG 0.72, ~12% FCF yield, positive revision momentum; FV $83–89. But fails its own TAM, growth-durability, and Rule-of-40 tests; forward revenue CAGR is just 1.8% with 2027 negative.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral.** Value rank 1/6 (P/FCF 8.5x, cheapest in group) cancels against quality rank 5/6 (negative ROE/ROIC) and momentum rank 5/6 (6-month return −1.1% into a 52-week high). Analyst targets have converged to price — ~1% implied upside. Regression beta 0.36 is statistically meaningless (R² 1.2%); treat as high-beta, 47%-vol with a documented −66.5% three-year drawdown.
- **Financial Risk**: **High.** S&P BB+ (junk, downgraded July 2025), Fitch Negative; ~$17B refinancing at ~6%+. Margin convexity is the real fragility: each 100bp of HBR ≈ $2.0B pretax ≈ ~60% of adjusted EPS. Offset: net cash, ~$4B TTM FCF, no near-term maturity cliff, deleveraging.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **High.** Zero insider open-market buys in 12 months vs. ~$14.6M of sales (GC sold ~$6.9M in two weeks; 10b5-1 status unverified); Georgia Medicaid contract loss with a pending protest; July 2025 guidance withdrawal remains a disclosure-credibility marker. Governance architecture improving (independent chair, ~85% say-on-pay) but outcomes unproven.
- **ESG**: **Neutral.** Environmentally immaterial. The one sharp transmission channel: Medicare Star Ratings — only ~12% of MA members in 4★+ plans for 2027 (down from 16%), with CNC suing CMS; quality-bonus revenue at risk inside the Medicare margin build.
- **Alternative Data**: **Neutral, leading signals bearish.** Open-enrollment search intent for "Ambetter" running −20–25% below last year while healthcare.gov traffic rises — shoppers defecting on price into a +15% premium year. Coincident data (6/7 beat rate, Medicare-channel hiring) still positive.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Short, moderate conviction** — an earnings-and-policy short, not a fraud short (Beneish/accruals clean; fraud thesis explicitly rejected). Bear case: 2027 EPS $4.00–4.50 at 10x = $40–45 (−40%+). Watch items: Q3'26 operating cash outflow of $3.7B and DSO jumping to 36.5 days — seasonal defence is legitimate until the Q4 print adjudicates.

**Bull Case**
- Operating leverage to recovery: every 50bp of HBR on ~$195B revenue ≈ $1B of operating income; three consecutive large beats and upward revisions across the street support the 2027 bridge ($5.54 consensus, ~20% EPS CAGR).
- Cheapest large-cap managed-care asset on cash: 8.5x FCF (~12% yield), 1.83x book, net cash, active buyback — with a multiple gap to ELV/UNH if delivery holds.
- Political optionality: a Democratic Congress raises the odds of restoring enhanced ACA subsidies — high-margin re-expansion of a Marketplace book deliberately shrunk 42%; December funding package is the live catalyst.

**Bear Case**
- Policy cliff is here, not hypothetical: subsidies lapsed, 2027 premiums +15%, Ambetter demand signals down ~20–25%, Medicaid work requirements effective 1-Jan-2027, membership already −8% total.
- Legal tail is unpriced and unhedgeable: DOJ-intervened FCA with treble damages and a theory portable to 30+ state Medicaid plans; a marketing-suspension or program-integrity channel is the unmodelable downside.
- Structural fragility: no-moat, sub-WACC returns, goodwill/intangibles at 67–77% of book equity (tangible book ~$15/sh — stock at ~5x tangible), junk credit, and a business where a 2-point HBR swing is the difference between $4B profit and $2B loss.

**Key Risks**
1. **HBR >93.5% for two consecutive quarters** — the falsification test flagged independently by Growth and Short; would confirm 2025 was a pattern.
2. **FY2027 guidance below $5.25** at the 5-Feb-2027 print (the guide is the trade; the seasonally negative Q4 EPS is noise).
3. **December double-whammy** (Macro): hawkish Fed + failed subsidy package → 20–25% de-rating toward $55–60.
4. **DOJ expansion** to state Medicaid plans or a qui tam cascade; second goodwill impairment ($14.9B remains).
5. Q4 cash-flow adjudication: thesis supportive if OCF ≥$2.5B and DSO reverts below 33 days.
