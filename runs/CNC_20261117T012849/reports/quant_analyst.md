## 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

1. **Impairment contamination (highest severity).** FY25 operating income −$7.6B, net income −$6.67B, EPS −$13.62. The accruals ratio of **−0.153 looks like pristine earnings quality but is purely an artifact** of non-cash charges against positive operating cash flow (+$5.09B). Systematic value/quality screens will either reject CNC outright (negative P/E, negative EBITDA) or mis-rank it on accruals. Any screen output on CNC must be hand-verified.
2. **Momentum reversal / crowded recovery.** +85% in 12 months, yet 6-month return **−1.12%** and rank 5/6 vs. peers. The classic post-crash re-rating phase appears to be decelerating. High risk of further mean reversion.
3. **Beta instability breaks risk models.** 0.359 (regression) vs. 1.079 (published) vs. 0.108 SPY correlation. Position sizing, hedging, and factor-neutral construction based on short-window beta will be wrong by ~3x.
4. **Tail risk is documented, not hypothetical.** 47.6% annualised vol; a **−54.9% drawdown occurred within the last 18 months** and sits *outside* the current 252-day window, meaning trailing max drawdown (−29.3%) understates true downside.
5. **Thin outer-year coverage.** 2–4 analysts on FY29/FY30E; near-term estimates unavailable. The revision signal has an effective sample size of essentially zero.
6. **Peer-set contamination.** Sector peers returned by the screen are dominated by pharma (LLY, JNJ, ABBV, MRK); I substituted the true managed-care comps manually. Cross-sectional ranks are against 6 names — **statistically descriptive, not inferential**.

**Data gaps (honest disclosure):** no quarterly analyst estimates (plan restriction); no peer volatility/beta to complete the Low-Vol cross-section; no P/B or P/FCF for some peers; no FY2026E consensus, so forward P/E is anchored on FY2027E (12 analysts).
