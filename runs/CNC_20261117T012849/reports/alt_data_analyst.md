Centene's own brand search intent is running ~20–25% below last year while the federal exchange is seeing *rising* traffic — consistent with shoppers window-shopping but defecting from branded plans on price. **Important offset:** Centene has been *deliberately* shrinking and repricing its marketplace book since 2025. Falling enrollment here is partly a chosen mix shift toward margin, not pure demand destruction — which is why this signal hits 2027 revenue more than near-term EPS.
