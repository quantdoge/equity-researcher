**Bull Case**
- Order book (+100% organic, $15B backlog) plus a sandbagged Q4 guide mechanically supports 25–30%+ 2027 growth; consensus FY30 EPS of $16+ at 22–25x = $350–400 stock.
- Content-per-MW rises with every architecture turn (liquid, 800VDC); NVIDIA reference-design lock-in and capacity lead defend share through the transition.
- Fortress balance sheet (net cash, 65x coverage) and elite cash conversion mean the equity can de-rate violently without the business ever being impaired — drawdowns are buying opportunities, not impairment events.

**Bear Case**
- Consensus 2027 requires a 30.4% EBITDA margin no large electrical peer has ever achieved, in the year end-market capex growth halves — estimate risk is one-sided to the downside.
- Duration/rate shock: ~1/3 of customer capex is debt-financed; a 30Y through 5.75% hits the discount rate and the order book at once. The stock has already demonstrated −48% on sentiment alone.
- Inventory at 148 days + working-capital reversal + insider distribution (~40% of chairman's stake at peak) + discontinued orders disclosure = the classic late-cycle seam, visible now.

**Key Risks**
1. Hyperscaler capex digestion or a single large-customer pause — revenue air-pocket with a short backlog cycle; first shows in inventory/cash conversion.
2. Multiple compression from long rates (30Y 5.37% and rising) on a 2.8-beta, 34x-forward name.
3. Liquid-cooling commoditisation (cold plates already seeing price pressure; Schneider/Motivair, Eaton, Asian entrants adding capacity) — monitor gross margin; sub-35% is the cycle-turn signal.
4. Governance/disclosure: repeat guidance-reset pattern, active 2026 securities probe, insider selling, orders disclosure withdrawn.
5. Tariff/trade escalation (Mexico USMCA qualification, US–China export controls) and US state-level data-centre moratoria deferring backlog conversion.
6. Hyperscaler in-housing of power/cooling — the most credible long-term threat to the independent-supplier model.
