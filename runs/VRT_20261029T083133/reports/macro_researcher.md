## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional / risk-on with a hawkish policy undertow.**

The dominant macro fact of the last six weeks is that the **Fed has reversed course**. Having cut the effective funds rate from 4.83% (Oct-24) to 3.63% (mid-2026), the FOMC **raised 25bp on 16 Sep 2026 to a 3.75–4.00% target range** and then **held on 28 Oct 2026 by a divided 10–2 vote**, citing elevated inflation. The easing cycle is over; the debate is now whether more hikes are needed.

Inflation has re-accelerated rather than settled: **CPI +3.7% y/y in Sep-26** (336.3 vs 324.2), up from ~2.6% y/y in Dec-25, with PCE +3.4% y/y (Aug-26). Goods/tariff pass-through and record copper are the likely culprits. Growth is decelerating but not weak — **Q2-26 real GDP +2.2%** after +2.5% in Q1 — while labour remains tight at **4.2% unemployment**.

The critical signal is the **curve**. With the 2Y at 4.65% against a 3.875% midpoint policy rate, the front end is pricing a **further ~75–80bp of tightening, not cuts**, while the 10Y (4.99%) and 30Y (5.37%) carry a large term premium. This is a **bear-steepener driven by inflation and fiscal/term-premium risk, not by growth optimism** — the most hostile possible rate backdrop for a high-multiple, long-duration equity with cyclical beta of 2.0.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

Vertiv's revenue is **not GDP-levered — it is AI-capex-levered**, which is why the macro read is unusually two-sided.

**POSITIVE — Growth/capex supercycle.** Hyperscaler capex is widely forecast **above $600bn in 2026 (+36% y/y)**, with JPMorgan putting 2027 near **$1.1tn**. VRT's Q3-26 confirmed the transmission: **revenue $4.3bn, adj. EPS $2.21, organic orders more than doubled**, FY guidance raised and 2027 above-market growth signalled — the stock **+16.5% on 28 Oct**. Q1-26 was already +30% reported / ~23% organic. This is a boom decoupled from a 2.2% GDP economy.

**POSITIVE — Inflation/pricing power.** 51% operating-profit growth on 30% sales growth in Q1-26 proves **pass-through capability**. Electricity/grid cost inflation is a *demand driver* for its UPS and thermal-management products, not a threat.

**NEGATIVE — Long-end rates / valuation duration.** At $93.5B cap, VRT trades near **~27–30x annualised Q3 adjusted EPS (~$8.84)**, a large premium to Industrials. Each 50bp on the 10Y compresses a long-duration multiple disproportionately. Worse, the *second-order* channel: **Goldman estimates ~one-third of 2027 hyperscaler capex will be debt-funded**, and S&P warned on 19 Oct 2026 that AI-linked liabilities are understated. A 5.4% 30Y raises customer hurdle rates and raises the probability of marginal project deferrals — VRT's order book is directly exposed to that financing spread.

**NEGATIVE — USD strength.** Q1-26 enjoyed a **~+3% FX tailwind**; the broad dollar has since rallied **+2.9% off the 9-Sep low**. With **EMEA already down 20% organically in Q1-26** and roughly half of revenue outside the US, FX flips from tailwind to translation headwind into 2027.

**NEGATIVE — Tariffs & copper.** A **50% copper tariff** with COMEX at record highs, plus Section-232 exposure on Mexico/China/India-manufactured electrical equipment, is a genuine COGS drag. Manageable in a shortage market with pricing power, but it caps incremental margin and creates a lag risk if pricing power ever softens.

**NEUTRAL — Labour.** 4.2% unemployment means wage pressure on manufacturing/service technicians, offset by volume leverage.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL**

Split verdict: **revenue macro is Favourable** (capex supercycle, pricing power, secular electrification); **valuation/rates macro is Unfavourable** (Fed hiking, 10Y at 5%, term premium expanding, dollar turning). These roughly offset. The offset is *not* stable, however — it depends on hyperscaler capex continuing to be funded irrespective of a ~5% long bond, which is precisely the assumption most vulnerable to a rates shock.

## 5. Material Surprise Scenario — **BEAR (the one to hedge)**

**"Term premium breaks the capex curve."** If the 30Y pushes through **5.75–6.0%** on fiscal-supply concerns rather than growth, the market re-rates the entire debt-funded AI complex simultaneously on two axes: (i) discount rate on ~30x multiples, and (ii) actual 2027 order deferrals as hyperscalers face higher project IRRs on the ~1/3 of capex that is debt-funded. VRT's **beta of 2.05** and its already-demonstrated 48% drawdown from the May-26 peak ($376 → $194 on 23 Oct) show exactly how violently this de-rates — and note the stock fell *despite* flawless fundamentals before the 28 Oct beat. A 10Y at 5.5% with a hawkish Fed, in a name whose customers borrow to buy its products, is the asymmetric risk.

*Upside mirror case:* goods disinflation resumes, the Sep hike proves to be the last, the 10Y retreats to ~4.2%, and 2027 capex guidance beats $1.1tn — a high-multiple, order-book-exploding name re-rates hard in that world.
