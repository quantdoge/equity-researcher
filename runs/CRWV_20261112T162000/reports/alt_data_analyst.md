## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH (moderate, contrarian)**

The alt-data composite says **the demand side is not the problem** — and the market is pricing it as if it is.
- **Confirmed bullish:** 10.6% headcount hiring rate concentrated in data center ops; revenue beat 5/5; QoQ growth accelerating to +42%; $132.5B backlog; sold out through 2027; positive news tone.
- **Not confirming:** Google Trends flat, web traffic flat, retail sentiment negative — no *new* demand acceleration beyond what's already contracted. The demand upside is largely **disclosed**, so it is at least partly in consensus.
- **The un-modelable overhang:** ~$47B debt, $846M quarterly interest, discounted bonds, capex $35–39B, a **Texas data center moratorium** threatening the 4.6 GW pipeline, and 2028 profitability. Alt data cannot resolve solvency — only price can.

At $58.33 the stock trades ~**2.4x FY26 revenue** and ~**1.6x** the $20–21B run-rate guide — cheap for 168% growth, but ~**6x on an EV basis** (~$79B EV). The alt signal is a **demand confirmation**, not a "buy the balance sheet" call.
