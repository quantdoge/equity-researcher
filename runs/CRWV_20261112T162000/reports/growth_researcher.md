## CoreWeave (CRWV) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $58.33 | Market cap ≈ $32.6B | Est. EV ≈ $65–70B | Data through Q3 FY2026 (reported 11/10/26)*

---

### 1. Growth Story

CoreWeave is the purest listed proxy on the buildout of AI compute: a purpose-built GPU cloud that grew revenue 168% YoY to $3.66B in Q3'26 and exited the quarter with a **$132.5B revenue backlog** (10x FY26E revenue) against **4.6 GW of contracted power**, only 1.7 GW of which is live. The disruptive thesis is not "cheaper cloud" — it is that frontier AI labs and enterprises need *power-dense, NVIDIA-dense, purpose-built* capacity that hyperscalers cannot deliver fast enough, and CoreWeave has locked the scarce inputs (power, GPUs, land) under multi-year contracts. Critically, the next doubling of revenue is **already sold**: deploying contracted-but-unbuilt capacity alone lifts run-rate revenue from ~$20.5B to roughly $51B at current revenue-per-MW economics, without a single new booking.

**Rule of 40 — the whole debate in two numbers:**
- Growth + Adj. EBITDA margin: **156% + 62% = 218** (spectacular)
- Growth + FCF margin: **156% + (~228%) = −72** (catastrophic)

Both are "true." The gap is capex: FY26 capex guided to **$37–39B against $13.0–13.3B of revenue = 2.9x capex/revenue**, up from 2.0x in FY25. D&A is also outrunning revenue (+200% YoY vs +168%), now **51.6% of revenue** vs 46.2% a year ago — the amortization burden of the fleet is compounding faster than the top line.

**Guidance / revisions:** Management *raised* FY26 revenue to $13.0–13.3B, raised adj. operating income 26% at the midpoint, and raised exit-2026 ARR to **$20–21B**. Revenue estimates have held up all year (revenue beat every one of the last 5 quarters). But the sell-side *price targets* have been slashed: Mizuho $115→$68, Morgan Stanley $99→$70, UBS $120→$105, Piper $153→$124, BNP $169→$117, MoffettNathanson $107→$89. This is a **multiple derating, not an estimate cut** — an important distinction. Median PT ≈ **$100** (range $45–$200, a 4.4x spread) vs. $58.33, implying ~72% upside but reflecting near-total disagreement on terminal economics. *(Note: the automated revision score reads +159% "upgrade"; the prior-period value exactly matches the current 2029E, indicating a period-mismatch artifact. The genuine revision signal is the PT cuts above.)* Earnings surprise: 60% beat rate, avg +12.5%; but 2 of the last 4 quarters missed, and revenue beat magnitude decelerated (+5.4% → +0.8% → +3.3%).

### 3. TAM and Penetration

The right unit here is **revenue per megawatt**, not market share. At the guided $20.5B exit ARR on 1.85 GW, CoreWeave monetizes **~$11M of annual revenue per MW**. Apply that to 4.6 GW contracted → **~$51B run-rate capacity already under contract**. The $132.5B backlog is ~2.6 years of coverage at that level.

- **Deployment penetration:** 1.7 GW active / 4.6 GW contracted = **37%**. This is the single most important number in the thesis — it is contracted, financeable, non-speculative growth.
- **2030 consensus ($89.7B revenue)** requires roughly 8+ GW — i.e., contracted power must nearly double again. Feasible only if global AI datacenter power grows as forecast; power interconnect and permitting, not demand, is the binding constraint.
- **Differentiation:** first-to-production NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 (4.8x token throughput vs GB200), 3x consecutive SemiAnalysis ClusterMAX Platinum, MLPerf Inference v6.1 top server throughput among cloud providers, plus proprietary software (Mission Control, Tensorizer, Forge). Against hyperscalers, CoreWeave's edge is *speed and focus*; against other neoclouds (Nebius, Crusoe, Lambda), it is *scale and financing access*. Both edges are eroding as GPU supply loosens.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **~Feb 25, 2027 — Q4'26 results.** Consensus revenue $4.84B (+208% YoY); first audited print against the $20–21B exit-ARR guide. Q4 interest expense guided to $1.05–1.15B (~$4.4B annualized) — watch coverage.
- **~$9B of customer prepayments and deposits expected within three months** (per CEO). Non-dilutive funding that directly de-risks the capex program.
- **1.85 GW active at YE26 → ~2.5GW+ through 2027.** Every incremental 1 GW deployed ≈ **$11B of incremental ARR**. Quarterly MW additions are the cleanest leading indicator.
- **Vera Rubin ramp.** Improves revenue per MW and is the main lever to stabilize gross margin (67.7% vs 73.0% YoY).
- **Financing cadence:** follow-on converts/ATM (35M shares authorized), DDTL-type facilities, and any ABS/securitization milestones or credit-rating actions.
- **Customer ramp:** OpenAI and enterprise/sovereign wins (DARPA, Leidos, Canva, Jane Street, Perplexity).

### 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Contracted power (strongest):** +200 MW active in Q3 alone; +900 MW contracted to 4.6 GW. This is physical, verifiable, and hard to fake. **Supports the thesis.**
- **Job postings (supports buildout):** ~233 open roles on Indeed, concentrated in data center technicians, commissioning/quality managers and hardware platform engineering (Plano TX, Marble NC, Miami FL, Livingston NJ). Hiring profile is *physical infrastructure*, not sales — consistent with a company capacity-constrained rather than demand-constrained. 2,189 FTEs against $13B revenue = **~$6.0M revenue/employee**, extraordinarily capital-rather-than-labor-driven.
- **Google Trends (negative/neutral):** search interest peaked at 100 in Feb 2026, has fallen to ~40 (off ~60% from peak). The Oct 2026 spike (74→68) coincided exactly with the 45% six-session drawdown — i.e., interest is being driven by the *stock*, not by customer adoption. Consistent with narrative fatigue ahead of fundamentals.
- **Price action (negative):** $137.98 high (5/6/26) → $40.58 low (11/5/26), now $58.33. **-58% from the high**, rallying +44% off the low post-Q3. Beta 7.67 — this is a high-duration, high-volatility instrument.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY (speculative — size-constrained)**

The demand side of this thesis is not in question: $132.5B of backlog, 3x more contracted power than deployed, 168% growth, 62% adjusted EBITDA margins, and an analyst curve pointing to $90B of revenue by 2030. At ~2.6x FY27E and ~1.6x FY28E EV/revenue, the growth is genuinely cheap *if* it is fundable. But this is not a clean growth story — it is a **levered call option on AI compute demand**. The equity sits behind **$46.9B of debt**, FY26 capex of $38B against $13B of revenue, trailing-twelve-month FCF of **−$20.4B**, and GAAP net loss *widening* YoY (−$709M vs −$110M) because interest expense tripled to $846M/quarter. I cannot underwrite "Strong Buy" on a company whose solvency depends on continuous capital-markets access, and whose depreciation burden (51.6% of revenue and rising) may be understated if GPU useful lives prove shorter than assumed. **Buy with strict position sizing** — the asymmetry is real (2030 consensus EPS of $18 implies 3.2x today's price; even a 50% haircut implies ~5x), but the downside in a credit event is permanent capital loss.

### 7. Key Risks

1. **Refinancing / leverage (highest severity).** $46.9B debt; Q4 interest expense $4.4B annualized vs. FY26 adjusted operating income of only $1.3B. Coverage is thin and the maturity ladder requires perpetual refinancing. A credit-market closure is existential, not merely dilutive.
2. **Capex intensity.** 2.9x capex/revenue and *worsening* (2.0x in FY25). Growth is being purchased, not generated. Negative FCF of ~$30B in FY26 alone.
3. **Depreciation assumptions.** D&A at 51.6% of revenue and growing faster than revenue. If GPU economic life is 4–5 years rather than ~6, reported gross margins are materially overstated and the path to GAAP profitability pushed out indefinitely.
4. **Customer concentration.** Microsoft and OpenAI dominate backlog. CoreWeave is effectively a supplier to companies that are also its potential competitors and its landlords' largest tenants. Concentration cuts both ways: Microsoft has been insourcing capacity.
5. **Competitive erosion.** Hyperscaler self-build (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, ORCL) plus Nebius/Crusoe/Lambda. CoreWeave's premium exists because capacity is scarce; scarcity is easing. Gross margin −530bp YoY is the first evidence of this.
6. **Dilution.** $4.2B convertible issuance, $2.6B DDTL facility, and an ATM for up to 35M Class A shares (~6% of shares) — plus SBC. Per-share economics lag revenue growth.
7. **Power and execution.** 4.6 GW contracted requires permitting, interconnect and construction across multiple countries; a slip in MW deployment defers revenue recognition on a fixed-cost base.
8. **Volatility / estimate dispersion.** Beta 7.67; 2028 EPS estimates span −$1.67 to +$3.85. Position sizing is the risk control here — nothing else works.
