### Investment Memo: CRWV — November 12, 2026

**Investment Thesis**
CoreWeave is the largest pure-play AI cloud ("neocloud") operator, with demand that is contracted rather than speculative — $132.5B of backlog (~10x FY26E revenue), capacity sold out through 2027, and revenue growing +168% YoY. It is also a broken capital structure: $66B of debt against 4.4% equity-to-assets, ~0.0x EBIT interest coverage, and −$20.4B TTM free cash flow, making equity a levered option on continuous capital-markets access. All eleven specialists agree on both facts; the investment question is not direction but sizing — how much capital to expose to a binary outcome whose resolution (credit conditions, not demand) no equity analysis controls.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector Neutral; CRWV rated Strong *relative* to peers (only neocloud near operating breakeven; 7–10x peer revenue). But macro is Unfavourable: Fed re-hiking into 3.8% CPI, 10Y ~4.9–5.1% bear-steepening, and AI/data-center credit bifurcating (DC bond yields <5% → ~7%; half of 2026 issuance junk). The discount rate and secured-credit availability — not demand — are the marginal drivers of this equity.

Preferred expression: long-dated puts or credit, not naked equity (7.7 vendor beta, violent squeezes).

**Bull Case**
- **Contracted, financeable growth**: $132.5B backlog + $9B imminent customer prepayments; QoQ growth *re-accelerated* to +42%; power/permits are the real moat and CRWV holds them.
- **De-rating is a financing story, not demand destruction**: −58% from peak while revenue grew 168%; at ~1.6x FY28E revenue the growth is priced for failure. If the 10Y retraces toward 4.25% and AI credit normalizes, a 3.5–7.7 beta stock re-rates violently.
- **Best operator in the sector**: only neocloud with positive quarterly operating income (+5.7% Q3'26), cheapest funding access (7.9% WACD), scale 7–10x peers.

**Bear Case**
- **Cash-flow insolvency**: coverage ~0.0x, −$20.4B FCF and worsening every year, ~$4B/month new debt issuance; one closed high-yield window or failed syndication is existential, not dilutive.
- **Depreciation mismatch**: 6-year accounting life vs plausibly 3–4-year economic life means the celebrated first operating profit may be a ~$500M quarterly loss on realistic assumptions; a GPU-fleet impairment of just ~$4B wipes out book equity.
- **Conflicted concentration**: Microsoft is ~2/3 of revenue, a competitor, and under antitrust scrutiny; gross margin −530bp YoY says scarcity rents are already being conceded.

**Key Risks**
1. Refinancing/credit event (highest severity): $10.6B current maturities, cross-defaults tied to customer contracts, unlimited equity-cure window already closed.
2. Texas/permitting: ERCOT report Dec 10, 2026 fails to restart permits; second state moratorium.
3. Microsoft deferral or insourcing of contracted capacity.
4. Depreciation restatement or GPU-life impairment.
5. Class action survives motion to dismiss; material weaknesses persist.
6. Dilution: shares 435M → 559M in nine months (+29%).
