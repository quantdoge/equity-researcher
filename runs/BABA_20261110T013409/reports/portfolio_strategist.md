**Where agents genuinely disagree — and how I resolve it:**

**Net conviction: MEDIUM at best, with a fat, non-diversifiable left tail.** That maps to a small position, not a core holding.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

**Recommended target size: 1.0–1.5% of portfolio (max ceiling 2.5%).** For ESG/UNGC-screened or US-taxable mandates with exclusion policy risk: **0.5% or Pass.**

**Kelly logic.** Expected value, probability-weighted: bull $160 (+52%) at 30%, base $120 (+14%) at 45%, bear $80 (−24%) at 20%, NS-CMIC impairment (−40%) at 8% (overlapping, not additive): EV ≈ +13%. With 40% vol, continuous Kelly = μ/σ² ≈ 0.13/0.16 ≈ 0.8 — **a mathematically absurd number** precisely because Kelly assumes no absorbing barrier. The NS-CMIC designation *is* an absorbing barrier (forced sale at a distressed price, no ability to hold through). Applying fractional Kelly (¼) and then overriding for the binary tail gives **~1.0–1.5%**. This is consistent with the framework's "elevated risk, multiple flags → 1–3%" band, at the low end.

**Why not larger:** (a) no margin of safety at $105 — Value's buy level is $88, 16% lower; (b) negative FCF and ROIC below WACC mean the downside is not asset-protected so much as *story* protected; (c) 4 consecutive EPS misses into a binary print 8 days away; (d) the largest risk cannot be hedged with index shorts (R² 0.11) — only with position size.

**Why not zero:** the cloud franchise is genuinely inflecting at +45% with the strongest third-party validation (Apple/Qwen) any China asset has received; downside is floored by 1.6x book, RMB99–162B net cash and the Ant stake; insiders bought $15M personally at lower prices; and the de-escalation catalyst (quick-commerce losses halving by October) is live and measurable.

**Risk contribution check:** 1.5% × 40% vol ≈ **0.6% portfolio volatility** — acceptable. At 2.5% it becomes 1.0%, which is the ceiling given ~90% idiosyncratic variance.

**If the book must be decisive in one action:** take 1.0% now and accept the print risk, accepting that a −20% adverse reaction costs 20bp of portfolio. That is within most risk budgets and avoids the regret of missing an asymmetric setup. I prefer the staged path.
