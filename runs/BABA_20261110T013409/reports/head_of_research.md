**Investment Thesis**

BABA is two companies in one ticker: a genuinely inflecting #1 China cloud/AI franchise (cloud +34–45% YoY depending on segment definition, external +40%, Apple selected Qwen for iPhone AI in China) attached to a mature commerce franchise being sacrificed to a quick-commerce subsidy war and a RMB 380B AI capex program. The financial consequence is negative free cash flow (−RMB 51B FY26; four straight negative quarters), ROIC of 4.3% versus a ~10–12% cost of capital, and a depreciation wave not yet in the P&L. At ~16x normalized earnings and 1.6x book, $105 is a *fair* price for that uncertainty — not a margin of safety — and the unresolved US §1260H/NS-CMIC designation path caps how much of it anyone should own. We hold, permit only small staged entries, and let the 19 Nov print and the FCF line decide the next move.
