# RIVN — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**Rivian Automotive, Inc.** | Consumer Cyclical / Auto Manufacturers | Price $10.93 (Nov 20, 2026) | Mkt cap ~$15.9B | Report date: Nov 20, 2026

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **WEAK (improving at the margin)**

**Negatives**
- **CEO/Chairman duality.** Robert "RJ" Scaringe is Founder, CEO **and** Chairman (confirmed in the Q3'26 earnings-call participant list and proxy language). No independent lead director has been identified in the sources reviewed. Founder-run is defensible at a growth-stage company, but combining Chair + CEO with no disclosed lead director concentrates power over an already-founder-led board.
- **Disclosure-quality track record.** The **$250M securities class action settlement** (*Crews v. Rivian*) received final court approval **May 20, 2026**, resolving claims Rivian misled investors about vehicle production costs around the 2021 IPO ($60M in plaintiff fees awarded). This is a *resolved* but material historical governance failure — the single largest black mark on the record.
- **CFO vacuum at the worst possible moment.** CFO **Claire McDonough departed Oct 30, 2026** ("to pursue a new opportunity") after ~6 years; **Derek Mulvey (VP Finance) is only *interim* CFO** with no permanent successor announced as of the Oct 29 Q3 call. This lands mid-R2 ramp and mid-cash-burn, precisely when capital-markets credibility matters most.
- **Board interlocks / related-party dynamics.** **Amazon holds ~17%, has a board seat, and accounted for ~$1.6B of 2026 revenue** — a shareholder-customer-director triangle that requires careful related-party disclosure.

**Positives (real, and why this isn't "Critical")**
- **Dual-class structure has been eliminated** — 7.8M Class B shares (10 votes each) retired/converted to one-vote-per-share. Scaringe's voting power fell to roughly **7.6%** (further diluted by a divorce settlement). The Wikipedia claim of a founder "veto" is **outdated/incorrect** as of the latest filing — flag it as a data error, not a live risk.
- Institutional capital validation from **Volkswagen (software JV) and Uber**, plus a conditional **DOE** facility, implies diligence access and some external governance pressure.
- Independent directors with genuine tech credentials (e.g., Aidan Gomez, Cohere CEO) — though automotive manufacturing depth on the board is unverified from sources reviewed.

**Gap:** The DEF 14A was not retrievable via the filing tool (SEC 403 error). Board independence ratios, committee composition, and executive comp metrics are **unverified** — recommend obtaining the 2026 proxy directly.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Single-plant concentration + un-built second plant.** R1T/R1S, the EDV line, and the **R2 ramp all run through Normal, Illinois**. One fire, weather event, labor action, or critical supplier outage halts the entire company. The Georgia plant — the only structural fix — is gated behind a **conditional DOE loan whose first draw slips to early 2027**. This is the highest-severity operational risk on the book.

2. **R2 launch execution against credit-inflated margins.** Q3'26 revenue rose 38% to $2.16B and deliveries jumped 58% QoQ, yet **automotive gross profit did not follow** — Rivian is absorbing launch costs before second/third shifts deliver leverage, and 8% gross margin leans on ~$80M of regulatory credits. Cash burn was **$1.12B in Q3** against **$7.1B liquidity** (no near-term debt maturities). Guidance is now 67,000–70,000 deliveries with lower capex — a narrowing margin for error.

3. **Key-person and bench-depth risk.** Scaringe is simultaneously founder, Chair, CEO, and the public face of the autonomy narrative. The **CFO seat is filled on an interim basis only**, and the COO (Javier Varela) is relatively new. Three of the four most critical seats (CEO, CFO, Chair) carry concentration or transition risk at once.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **MODERATE**

- **Product-safety narrative is the live vulnerability.** A ~115K-vehicle NHTSA probe on a *suspension component* — following a recall that itself stemmed from an improper *repair* procedure — feeds a "quality and service-execution" narrative that is harder to repair than a price cut. If PE26-004 escalates to a recall, expect amplified coverage given the R2's mass-market positioning.
- **Bear-case amplification is loud.** Coverage in the past 30 days includes "Rivian Stock Falls Off A Cliff," "Rivian Was a Blockbuster IPO and Lost Most of Its Valuable… $150 billion dream shrink," and repeated Lucid-comparison cash-burn pieces — the stock is **~51% below its Dec 2025 peak of $22.45** and at/near a 52-week low ($10.82 intraday Nov 20). **Short interest hit an all-time high in share count at end-October (~12% of shares out)** — a crowded short base that amplifies both good and bad headlines.
- **Positives partly offsetting:** UBS upgrade to Buy ($17 PT), raised guidance, R2 conversion exceeding expectations, Amazon EDV expansion (and possible Walmart), and the Dec 9 Autonomy & AI Day as a narrative catalyst. The Amazon van **exclusivity has ended**, which is strategically positive (TAM expansion) even as it removes a demand backstop.
- **No material ESG/environmental controversy, consumer boycott, or executive-misconduct scandal identified.** The "EV" brand carries political-polarization risk as a category, not a company-specific one.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Rivian's non-financial risk is dominated by **regulatory and subsidy dependency** (conditional DOE loan, vanishing credit revenue, EV policy whiplash, open NHTSA probe) layered on **an unhedged single-plant operating model** and a **governance bench in transition**. These risks do not appear on the balance sheet, but they are the transmission mechanism through which the $7.1B liquidity buffer gets consumed faster than guidance implies. Financial risk (cash burn, dilution) is well covered elsewhere; the non-financial layer is what makes the equity a high-beta, policy-contingent story rather than a manufacturing ramp.

Mitigants that keep this from "Critical": no active SEC/DOJ enforcement, the dual-class structure is **gone**, the largest securities litigation is **settled and closed**, and multiple strategic partners (VW, Uber, Amazon, DOE) have underwritten the business after diligence.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **CFO departure + interim-only successor (Oct 30, 2026), with no permanent CFO named.** Escalate to management: who is accountable for the R2/Georgia capital plan and for the next equity/debt raise? An interim CFO during a cash-burn inflection is a classic precursor to guidance reset or a surprise capital raise.
2. **NHTSA PE26-004 open on ~114,922 vehicles** — request the ODI resume and Rivian's response; model a forced-recall scenario and the warranty accrual impact.
3. **Q3 gross margin is credit-dependent** (~$80M of credits on an 8% gross margin), and management itself expects most credit revenue to vanish by year-end. **Verify ex-credit automotive gross margin** — if negative, the "margin expansion" thesis is a policy artifact.
4. **DOE $4.5B loan is conditional with first draw in early 2027.** Any federal-level hostility to ATVM or a missed construction milestone forces either a dilutive raise or a capex pullback. This is a binary, unhedged policy risk to the post-R2 growth story.
5. **~51% drawdown from the Dec 2025 peak with short interest at an all-time high (~12%)** — elevated probability of reflexive, headline-driven volatility; confirm no undisclosed liquidity/going-concern language in the next 10-Q.
6. **Amazon = ~17% owner + board seat + ~$1.6B of 2026 revenue.** Confirm related-party terms and whether EDV pricing is at arm's length; monitor for Amazon stake reduction (an overhang risk).
7. **One open-market insider buy vs. sustained CEO/officer selling** — set an alert for any *acceleration* of Scaringe sales outside a 10b5-1 plan, particularly post-Dec 9 Autonomy Day.
