### Investment Memo: CHTR — 27 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate at $147.27 | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $140 (−5% from current $147.27); scenario band $95–$230
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Charter is a genuinely essential, cash-generative broadband franchise in structural decline, financed with ~$109B of debt against a ~$17–20B equity stub — a levered call option on a melting ice cube. The bear case (subscriber erosion, 4.2x leverage, broken pricing power) is real but, after a −76% drawdown, fully discovered and largely priced; the bull case ($3B of cost self-help, 22–30% FCF yield, capex rollover) is equally real but was substantially paid out by the +44% six-week squeeze off the $102.48 October low. At $147 the discount no longer exceeds the estimation error: this is a watchlist name with a mechanical entry plan at $105–110, not a purchase today.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector UNATTRACTIVE — cable is a net broadband share donor (−233k pro forma subs in Q3 alone; New Street sees no cable growth this decade); consolidation/cost extraction is the only lever. Macro UNFAVOURABLE — 10Y 5.13%, 30Y 5.47%, bear-steepening, December hike consensus; a 100bp WACC rise implies a 50–70% equity hit. Own CHTR as a rates trade, not a cable trade.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM-HIGH — zero foreign exposure, but concentrated US legal risk: FCC ACP National Verifier letters ($49.4M peer repayment precedent, DOJ accelerating qui tams), securities class action naming CEO and CFO, KSF fiduciary probe, CPUC-style conditional approvals. $108.6B of debt leaves minimal absorptive capacity for an adverse outcome.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD — moat is narrow and narrowing (management's own $6.3B impairment concedes it); probability-weighted IV ~$218 but inside a $119–$386 error band — "a discount smaller than your own estimation error is not a margin of safety." Buy price ~$105.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: SELL — Rule of 40 = 7.5; capex +32% since 2022 against +0.5% revenue; zero margin expansion; the disrupted incumbent, not a compounder.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL — top-decile value (+2σ) colliding with bottom-decile momentum (−47.7% 12-1) and worst-in-cohort volatility (55–60%); textbook value-trap configuration that underperforms over 6–12 months.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH — BB+/Ba1/BB+ (sub-IG at all three), 4.2x net leverage, EBIT/interest only 2.5–2.9x, current ratio 0.37, tangible equity −$74B, Altman Z 0.602. A −20% revenue scenario breaks coverage to ~1.3x.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH — interim CFO since 15-Oct during a transformational integration; impairment booked one quarter after closing the $34.5B Cox deal; repeat breach (4.9M accounts). Offset: CEO and three directors bought ~$3.8M open-market at the lows.

**Bull Case**

- Deep value with real cash: ~5x EV/EBITDA, 22–30% FCF yield; the market is capitalizing ~12%/yr perpetual decline — too harsh if costs land; Spectrum Mobile compounds at +15% (13.5M lines) and capex has peaked ($3.2B vs $3.6B y/y).
- Optionality: spectrum/asset monetization, active liability management ($1.5B tenders), consolidation premium, and insider buying at the lows; credit affirmed stable at BB+.

**Bear Case**
- Structural share loss with broken pricing: penetration −260bp y/y, ARPU −1.6%, pro forma revenue −1.5% and EBITDA −4.0%; KeyBanc models ~3M internet losses through 2028; FWA/fiber attack both ends of the market.
- Leverage meets the wrong rate regime: $109.4B net debt at 4.2x, interest ~$5.4B ≈ 137% of trailing FCF; every +100bp of refi cost removes ~$1.1B (~28% of FCF); a second Fed hike breaks the refinancing window → macro bear case $85–95.
- Capital-allocation credibility: ~$36B destroyed repurchasing stock at ~$560 average; impairment one quarter post-Cox signals overpayment; interim CFO; super-voting control insulates the strategy from challenge.

**Key Risks**

2. Cost program slips a quarter, or annualized FCF falls below ~$2.5B against $5.4B of interest.
3. Downgrade below BB+/Ba1 or spread widening closing the refi window before the 3.5x target is reached.
