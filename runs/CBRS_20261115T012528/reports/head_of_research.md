### Investment Memo: CBRS — Cerebras Systems Inc. — 15 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (Speculative) | Conviction: Low–Medium
**Price Target**: $94 probability-weighted (−14% vs. current $108.72); scenario range $35–$160
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Cerebras is a genuine frontier-technology franchise — the only commercial wafer-scale AI engine, the fastest revenue growth in its peer set (+75.7% FY25, 174.6% 3-yr CAGR), and a $26.1B RPO worth 1.06x its market cap — but it is a sub-scale, GAAP-unprofitable price-taker whose gross margin collapsed from 44.6% to 20.0% in two quarters while the "core margin" narrative excludes rented third-party compute. The stock has already fallen 72% from its IPO-day peak, yet still trades ~14–16% above our probability-weighted intrinsic value in a rate regime (10Y 5.13%, 30Y 5.47%, Fed hiking) that is maximally hostile to a 20-year-duration equity. The operating story is intact; the price already pays for much of it. This is ownable only as a sized option on the bull case — not as a core holding.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector ATTRACTIVE ($151B→$443B accelerator TAM by 2030, hyperscaler capex +81% in 2026, inference mix-shift favors the architecture) but late-cycle; company position AVERAGE — first on growth, last on margins (GM 39% vs ~59% peer median). Macro UNFAVOURABLE: ~90% of value is terminal value; the 68bp 10Y move since IPO explains most of the de-rating; a 25% Section 232 tariff sits on its TSMC-fabbed core input. Watch 10Y/2Y as the leading indicators for the stock.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. The MBZUAI amendment (Ex-10.1) delivers hardware to a redacted third-country facility, explicitly not-US/not-UAE, with Cerebras as exporter of record — a textbook BIS diversion/end-use exposure on 38% of revenue. Plus an active willful-copyright class action and 7+ plaintiff firms circling post-IPO.
- **Value**: SELL/AVOID. FY25 "profit" of +$237.8M was a $383M non-operating gain (operating income −$145.3M). Probability-weighted FV ~$91; requires $65–68 for margin of safety. FCF −$1.43B in 9M'26.

- **Quantitative Signals**: SELL. 5/100 on all four scoreable factors (value, momentum, quality, low-vol); 113.8% vol, −73.7% max drawdown, negative book equity. Discounted from Strong Sell only for the 126-session sample.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH, split explicitly: solvency LOW (net cash +$5.9B, D/E 0.16x, no covenants) vs. equity risk HIGH-to-CRITICAL (burn accelerating: −$119.6M → −$476.7M → −$838.6M by quarter; DSO 53→109 days; inventory days 19→79; +5.5% dilution in two quarters).

- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (not Strong Short). Earnings quality 19/100; the 27-pt GAAP-vs-"core" GM gap is the single most decision-relevant fact — it quarantines the cost of rented compute. Beneish M 5.374 (caveated). Critically: **no fraud alleged, no solvency trigger** — $7.4B gross cash removes the funding wall that a strong short requires.

**Bull Case**
- $26.1B contracted RPO (~30x forward revenue) with OpenAI, Microsoft, AWS, Oracle — converts on schedule into a 3x revenue FY27.
- Q4 delivers GAAP-margin recovery toward 35%+ as owned capacity replaces rented compute; the neocloud discount re-rates to proprietary-silicon multiples.
- Disinflation + term-premium unwind takes the 10Y toward 4.3%; a 20-year-duration equity re-rates to $150–165 on macro alone.

**Bear Case**
- The margin "inflection" is a non-GAAP construct; structural GM is 20–28% and the 2028 consensus ($7.6B, 8.6x current) is a TAM-slide extrapolation.
- 72% of revenue sits in two counterparties (MBZUAI 38%, OpenAI 34%); either is a −34/38% revenue event, and MBZUAI carries binary export-control risk.
- Further dilution: ~11%/yr share growth plus a probable capital raise to fund 700MW, priced well below the $185 IPO.

**Key Risks**
BIS action on the third-country delivery structure; OpenAI/MBZUAI loss or repricing; Q4'26 (≈Feb 2027) GAAP GM <30%; 10Y >5.60%; equity raise below $100; warranty-reserve charge validating Culper; securities-litigation escalation.
