---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — **TRANSITIONAL, LEANING RISK-OFF (hawkish re-acceleration)**

The regime has inverted versus the 2024–25 easing cycle. The Fed cut from 5.33% (Aug-2024) to 3.63% (May–Aug-2026), then **hiked 25bp in September 2026 to 3.75–4.00% — the first hike in three years** — and held on 28-Oct-2026 with two dissents. Headline CPI has re-accelerated to **3.82% YoY**, with the 3-month annualised run-rate at **~5.7%**, driven by an energy/commodity impulse (headline sits ~105bp above core). The long end has repriced violently: the 10Y went **4.45% (29-May) → 5.31% peak (5-Oct) → 5.10–5.13% now**, and the 30Y is at **5.47%** — a term-premium/fiscal-supply problem, not just a growth problem. Critically, the **2Y at 4.74% sits 86bp above the effective funds rate**, i.e. the market is pricing *further hikes*. 13-Nov PPI printed hot and dragged the Nasdaq down 362 points.

Real activity is fine — Q3-26 GDP grew **~3.4% SAAR** (+2.1% YoY), unemployment **4.1%**. This is not a recession tape; it is a *discount-rate tape*. That is the worst possible combination for a long-duration, high-multiple, newly-public growth equity.

**Cerebras was listed into the exact wrong half of the cycle.** It priced at $350 on 14-May-2026 when the 10Y was 4.45%; it now trades at $108.72 — **-65% from its IPO close, -72% from its intraday high of $386.34**, and -58% from its Aug-12 close high of $262.06. The rebound off the 5-Nov low ($81.71) is +33%, but that is a sentiment bounce, not a macro repair.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Cerebras is close to the maximum-rate-sensitivity equity profile that exists: **1.85 beta, ~9.7x forward revenue, GAAP-unprofitable, ~90% of value in terminal value, capex-hungry own datacenter build ("we are behind schedule" — CEO, 13-Nov), and demand that is itself debt-financed by its customers.** It faces a hawkish Fed, a 5.1% 10Y, a 5.47% 30Y, sticky 3.8% headline CPI, energy-cost inflation in its own COGS, a 25% import tariff on its core input, and a strengthening dollar.

The offsetting case is real and should not be dismissed: Q3 revenue **+70% to $230.9M**, cloud/services **+272%**, adj. EPS **+$0.12 vs -$0.14 expected**, guidance raised, and a **$26.1B backlog** = 1.06x market cap. But **none of that is macro** — it is execution. Macro is a pure multiple headwind, and it is the binding constraint on the shares today.

---

### 5. The Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BULL (disinflation + term-premium unwind)**

Consensus and positioning are now bearish on both AI capex durability and duration. The surprise is a **fast unwind of the inflation impulse**: if the energy/commodity shock that drives the 105bp headline-over-core gap reverses — entirely plausible given core is still only ~2.7% — headline CPI falls from 3.82% toward ~2.5% by Q1-2027. The Fed's September hike is then validated as a one-and-done, the two Oct dissents flip dovish, and the **term premium that took the 10Y to 5.31% compresses back to ~4.30%**.

For CBRS, that is not a 10% move. A 80bp decline in the 10Y on a 20-year-duration equity plus a shift in AI-capex financing conditions (the debt-funded third of 2027 hyperscaler capex becomes fundable again) could re-rate the stock from **9.7x to 14–15x forward revenue — roughly $150–165**, a ~40–50% upside move driven by *macro alone*, with the raised guidance and $26.1B backlog as pure operating leverage on top.

**The symmetric bear tail** is a 30Y through 6% as fiscal supply overwhelms demand; that closes the datacenter debt window, defers backlog conversion *and* compresses the multiple simultaneously — the outcome that produces a sub-$70 print.

**Bottom line for the investment memo:** the operating story is intact and improving; the macro is actively hostile and fully explains the drawdown. Position sizing should reflect that CBRS is, for now, a leveraged bet on the long end of the US Treasury curve rather than on wafer-scale inference. Watch the **10Y (5.13%) and the 2Y (4.74%)** as the primary leading indicators for this stock — ahead of any earnings release.
