**Scale context:** Q3 FY27 revenue **$115.8B, +103% YoY**; Q4 FY27 guide **$130.0B ±3%**. Critically, *"NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook"* — management has effectively zeroed the single largest geopolitical tail risk in the forward model. Any licensed-China revenue is therefore pure optionality, and any *reinstatement* of it is a headline event.

**The Singapore problem is the analytical crux.** Through Q2 FY26 NVDA billed **22% of revenue** through Singapore ($10.16B), disclosing that "customers use Singapore to centralize invoicing while our products are almost always shipped elsewhere."
