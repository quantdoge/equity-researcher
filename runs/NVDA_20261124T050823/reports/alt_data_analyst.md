### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (fundamentals Bullish / governance-risk Bearish — the two are not yet reconciled)

The most important alt-data observation is a **decoupling**: NVDA beat by 7.6% on Nov 17 and still fell to $125.53 by Nov 20 — a **-47.5% drawdown from the Oct 6 high of $239.24**, then **+31.5% in one session** on a personnel decision. Nothing in the demand data changed across that round trip. That is a stock trading on **event risk**, not on the fundamentals that alt data measures.

- **Demand/activity alt data: Bullish and improving** — 100% beat rate, expanding surprise magnitude, datacenter-specific hiring, rising supplier commitments, $1T order book, fast product cadence, zero-China guidance conservatism.
- **Attention alt data: Bearish/decaying** — baseline Trends interest down ~40% from year-ago, meaning rallies lack organic retail sponsorship and the current spike is already fading (100 → 55 → 25).
- **Governance/legal alt data: Bearish and unresolved** — live DOJ probe, open internal investigation, Dec 1–2 testimony, congressional hearings. Binary, and not in consensus estimates.
- **Valuation context:** ~$3.98T on ~$10.7 annualized EPS ≈ **~15x** — the market has already applied a large governance/compliance discount.

**Actionable framing:** Alt data does *not* support shorting the demand story. It also does not support chasing the +31% pop, because the attention signal that would sustain it is decaying and the legal calendar is front-loaded into the first week of December. **Asymmetry favors owning the demand signal, sizing for the Dec 1–2 headline, and adding on any testimony-driven re-test toward the $125–145 capitulation zone.** A clean Dec 1–2 with no escalation flips this to **Bullish**, and the Feb 24 guide (consensus +12.9% QoQ) is beatable on current activity data.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:** three of six channels have real gaps — **patent filings unavailable** (API failed twice), **web traffic unquantified**, **social sentiment unquantified** (proxy noise only). Job-posting read is qualitative with no time series. Google Trends lags ~1 week and therefore **misses the Nov 23 reinstatement entirely**. The news sentiment score (+1.0) is a demonstrable artifact and was overridden by manual reading.

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly and ~5–7 days stale; news sentiment is keyword-based and structurally mis-scores legal/governance coverage; hiring, traffic and social feeds are search proxies rather than licensed feeds (Thinknum/SimilarWeb/Reddit API would materially upgrade this). Alt data is a **leading indicator, not a definitive one** — and in this specific case the dominant near-term driver (a live federal investigation and a Dec 1–2 deposition) is a **non-alt-data, binary event** that no amount of demand-side signal can forecast.
