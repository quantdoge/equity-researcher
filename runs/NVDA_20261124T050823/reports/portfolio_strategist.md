## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration.** NVDA alone is 36% of the semis peer group; the top three are 67% (HHI ~2,015). If the book already holds TSM, AVGO, AMD or MU, NVDA is *additive to an existing overweight*, not diversification:
- **NVDA–TSM 0.71** (verified) — the tightest pair. TSMC is simultaneously NVDA's sole leading-edge supplier *and* a 0.71-correlated holding. That is one position expressed twice.
- NVDA–MU 0.53, NVDA–AMD 0.45, NVDA–AVGO 0.43. Cap the **whole semis sleeve at 10–12%**, of which NVDA ≤2.5%.

**Genuine diversification insight.** NVDA's correlation to **MSFT is only 0.22** — far below its 0.70 to SPY. If the portfolio owns the *customers* (MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN/META) rather than the supplier, NVDA is a statistically diversifying leg even though both are levered to the same capex dollar. **But do not run both at full weight** — the 0.22 correlation is a statistical artifact of differing factor exposures, not economic independence. Choose the supplier or the buyers.

**Factor exposure — this is the problem.** NVDA adds: **+high beta (2.44), +top-quintile quality, −bottom-quintile momentum, +bottom-quintile low-vol.** In a regime with a 5.13% 10Y and a Fed that has resumed hiking, that is close to the worst possible factor bundle: long-duration quality at maximum beta with broken momentum. Against SPY at 0.70 correlation and 2.44 beta, NVDA is functionally a **2.4x-levered market position with a semiconductor-cycle overlay**. Current momentum is −3.96% on the 12-1 construction, which *hides* the crash; true trailing 12-month return is ~−20%.

**Macro fit.** Dollar +4.7% in ten weeks (translation headwind), AI credit spreads widening ("junk-like" compensation), and the sector on track to absorb more credit in 2027 ($612B) than the entire IG+HY market supplies ($470B). NVDA has no refinancing risk — but its *customers* do, and that is the transmission channel to the order book.

**Hedging note.** At post-crash implied vol, protective puts are expensive; a stop-loss discipline is the cheaper hedge. If an overlay is required, express it at the portfolio level (short SPY futures / index put spread) rather than single-name options.
