**Investment Thesis**
GM is a statistically cheap, policy-dependent cyclical: 6.2x 2026E earnings, 1.19x book, ~4.5x 2026E adjusted EBIT, and a $20bn buyback shrinking the float ~6–8%/yr. The cheapness is real but *explained* — earnings are set by tariffs, courts, the pump price and a live NHTSA probe rather than by management, and a ~$10bn GAAP/adjusted gap plus two consecutive kitchen-sink Q4s mean reported earnings quality is poor. The asymmetry favours patient accumulation (EV +12.6% on strategist scenario weights), but the next 3–6 months carry the file's highest-probability negative catalyst: a third consecutive Q4 charge on 26-Jan-2027.
