## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.5% of portfolio (starter), hard ceiling 3.0%**

**Kelly arithmetic:**
| Scenario | Prob. | Price | Return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base — multiple re-rates to 7x on $14–15 EPS | 55% | ~$100 | **+30%** |
| Flat — macro pressure offsets EPS delivery | 28% | ~$78 | +1% |
| Bear — SAAR <15.5M / third Q4 charge / recall escalation | 17% | ~$58 | **−25%** |

EV = (0.55 × 30) + (0.28 × 1) + (0.17 × −25) = **+12.6%**. Odds ≈ 1.2:1. Full Kelly ≈ **~19%** — which is absurd for a 34.7%-vol single name with a live regulatory tail, and is precisely the number that tells you *not* to trust the raw output. Applying **1/8 fractional Kelly** for volatility, idiosyncratic concentration (R² 0.21) and the L87 tail gives **~2.4%**; discounting further for the unavailable Value Researcher second opinion and a broken momentum factor gives **1.5%.**

**Why 1.5% and not 3–5%:**
- Five independent risk flags: hostile macro (High), earnings-quality gap (High), product-safety tail (High), momentum break (tape), and zero insider buying.
- Per my framework, **multiple risk flags caps the position at 0.5–1.5%**; GM escapes the "pass" bucket only because the balance sheet is not impaired (BBB/stable, ~net-cash automotive on an estimated basis, $27.8bn cash) and the downside is a *de-rating*, not a solvency event.
- **Risk contribution:** 1.5% × 34.7% vol ≈ **0.52% of portfolio volatility.** At the $67 stop, max loss ≈ **0.20% of portfolio.** That is a risk-budget line item, not a conviction position.

**Path to 3.0%** (add only on evidence, in this order of value): tariff/USMCA relief that removes cost uncertainty → L87 closed without an expanded mandatory recall → two quarters of gross margin ≥15% → Q4 print without a third consecutive charge.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss: $67 hard (−13% from $77.09, ≈ −0.4σ).**
Not a tighter stop — 34.7% volatility will whipsaw anything closer. $67 is ~5.0x 2026E EPS and ~1.03x book; below that, either the macro bear case (SAAR <15.5M) or a fresh charge event is being priced, and the thesis is no longer intact regardless of the multiple.

**Thesis invalidation — exit on ANY of these, independent of price:**
1. **Third consecutive Q4 kitchen-sink charge**, or Q1/Q2 gross margin back below 10% with no tariff relief (Short Analyst's #1 trigger; Fin Risk's #1 red flag).
2. **NHTSA L87 upgraded to an Engineering Analysis**, a mandatory recall materially above 1M units, or any indication of DOJ interest in GM's pre-recall knowledge. (Nonfin Risk: *"the single most under-priced non-financial risk at GM today."*)
3. **U.S. SAAR below 15.5M for three consecutive months** — confirms affordability, not competition, is setting GM's pricing.
4. **GAAP operating income goes negative on a trailing basis**, or the GAAP/adjusted gap *widens* beyond ~$10bn.
5. **Buybacks continuing above $85 while capex/revenue stays below 9%** — capital return at the expense of the product cycle.
6. **GM Financial liquidity below ~$20bn** (currently $28.0bn, down from $35.0bn in two quarters) or ABS delinquency acceleration — the true tail risk is a funding-market event, not an auto event.

**Trim triggers (scale out, don't exit):**
