## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **0.5% initial, 1.25% maximum**

**This is a venture-option position, not a core holding.** Sizing logic:

**Kelly check.** On the Value Researcher's own distribution, adjusted to $28.58: 0.15(+145%) + 0.40(−20%) + 0.45(−76%) = **−20.5% expected return → Kelly says zero.** To justify any position I must shade bull probability above ~30%. Shaded to 35/35/30: **+21% expected return → Kelly fraction ≈ 0.21/1.45 ≈ 14% of a full-Kelly bet**, and half-Kelly for estimation error gives ~7% of *risk capital*, which on a 105% vol asset translates to a low-single-digit portfolio weight. **The option is real but thin.**

**Volatility budget check.** 0.5% × 105.6% vol = **0.53% standalone portfolio vol**; beta contribution 0.5% × 3.73 = **1.9% of market beta**. A 1.25% max position contributes 1.3% vol and 4.7% beta — the outer limit I would tolerate in a name with a −78.5% realized drawdown.

**Why not pass.** Three reasons: (a) the Short Analyst — the most adversarial, most rigorous voice — declines to short at $28.58; (b) EV/FY28 sales of 6.5x on a 3-billion-subscriber addressable base is a failure-discount multiple, not a growth multiple; (c) $2.80B cash + $1.0B non-dilutive Japan funding + 2032 maturities means **no solvency trigger inside the investment horizon** — the bear case is a slow dilution grind, not a zero.

**Why not 3–5%.** Governance 3/10, an active class action naming the CEO and CFO/CLO, 17.2% dilution in nine months, and a guidance record of 7 consecutive misses averaging −81%. That is disqualifying for a conviction-weighted position regardless of price.
