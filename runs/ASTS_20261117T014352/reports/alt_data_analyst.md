Rising brand search on bad news = capitulation interest.

The operating improvement is real but ~3 quarters from being provable, and the next earnings print is ~3.5 months away with consensus ($75M Q4 revenue) already above the implied guidance path.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:**
- **Two data sources failed outright:** patent filings (API error, 2 attempts) and web traffic (domain unresolved). No patent signal at all.
- **Job-posting data is a backward-looking web-search proxy with no time series** — I can characterise hiring *mix* (production-heavy, consistent with the ramp) but not *acceleration*.
- **Google Trends is measuring investor attention, not consumer demand** for a pre-revenue-commercial wholesale business. Treat search data here as a sentiment read, not a demand read.
- **Duplicate/stale earnings-calendar entries** (two future events both carrying $75.02M revenue estimates) make Q4 consensus somewhat uncertain.
- **No direct consumer app, retail traffic, or store-ranking data exists** for this business model — the standard consumer alt-data toolkit is structurally inapplicable.

**What would move me to High:** confirmation of nationwide intermittent service activation before year-end 2026, a quantitative job-posting time series showing sustained ramp through Q1 2027, and clean resolution of the class action without a restatement.

**Data lag note:** Google Trends is weekly and normalised per-keyword (not comparable across keywords); news sentiment is keyword-count based over 14 days and is currently distorted by boilerplate legal notices; the Q3 operational data is ~5 weeks stale. Alt data is a leading indicator — not a definitive one.
