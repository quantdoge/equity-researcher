# NON-FINANCIAL RISK ASSESSMENT — CMG (Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc.)
**CIK 0001058090 · Sector: Consumer Cyclical / Restaurants · ~3,000+ corporately operated stores, ~130K employees · Newport Beach, CA**
*Assessment window: Oct 2025 – Oct 2026*

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate** (eroding — heavy C-suite churn in a crisis window)

**Evidence:**
- **CEO tenure <2 years at top.** Scott Boatwright succeeded Brian Niccol (departed to Starbucks) *during* the crisis-prone 2024–25 period; the top team is unseasoned relative to the shock it is managing. No evidence of CEO/Chair duality surfaced, but full board-independence verification was **not possible** (proxy data unavailable) — flagged as a data gap.
- **Revolving C-suite.** CFO Jack Hartung retired (Mar 2025, replaced by Adam Rymer); Chief Accounting & Administrative Officer **Jamie McConnell resigned Aug 2025**; CHRO→Chief Legal Officer transition announced earlier. **Five 8-Ks filed in 2026** (Jun 17, Jul 8, Jul 29, Aug 5, Sep 14) — an elevated cadence consistent with ongoing leadership/event disclosures beyond routine ops.
- **Positives:** non-dual structure appears intact; company invests in talent pipeline (Apprentice program targeting a leader in every restaurant by 2027; ~25K employees in debt-free education via Guild); long-tenured institutional holders (BlackRock ~10%) provide oversight pressure.

**Net:** Governance is not the binding constraint, but the combination of a fresh CEO + new CFO + new CAO steering through a food-safety and litigation storm is a genuine governance/oversight concern.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary
- **No active SEC/DOJ enforcement identified** in EDGAR search; however the enforcement full-text search was **unavailable** (gap — cannot rule out open DOJ/state AG inquiries).
- **Criminal precedent is live context:** record **$25M DOJ criminal fine (2020)** for the 2015–18 E. coli/norovirus failures — any repeat contamination triggers the same federal tail.
- **Pending litigation:** federal injury/class action filed **Aug 5, 2026** (case 0:26-cv-03514-KMM-SGE) referencing E. coli O26 amid the 2026 outbreaks; more customer suits are likely if case counts climb.
- **Labor/enforcement history:** NYC DCWP **$20M settlement (2022)** under the Fair Workweek Law (~13K workers) — with *post-settlement non-compliance allegations* by 32BJ/SEIU; **$7.75M NJ labor-standards settlement**; **NLRB found merit** to ULP charges that Chipotle denied raises to unionized workers (Delta Twp., MI, 2024, with ~6 open charges at that store); Kansas crew ULP complaint; the sole US Chipotle union **disbanded Apr 2026 without a contract**.
- **No data-privacy/GDPR/CCPA enforcement or cyber breach traced** in this window.

## 3. Insider Activity Analysis
- **Elevated Form 4 filing cadence: 30+ insider filings over the past 12 months**, clustered Feb 10/17 (6), Jun 12 (9), Aug 10–25 (7), plus singles in May, Sep, Oct 2026 — consistent with routine **10b5-1 plan selling** by multiple officers (typical post-transition pattern), not with accumulation.
- **Direction/buy-sell detail could not be retrieved** (EDGAR document pages blocked 403). **Gap:** no confirmation of net-buying vs. selling; given the cluster density, assume officer selling — recommend an endpoint-level Form 4/Form 144 review. **No anomalous buying signal observed.**

## 4. Top Operational Risks
1. **Food-safety recurrence across a fresh-produce supply chain (CRITICAL).** Summer 2026: FDA traceback of a **salmonella outbreak linked to jalapeño lots** (MN alone: 110 cases; reports of 345+/400+ ill across 27–32 states, 36 hospitalized); Chipotle pulled jalapeños and switched growers. The company has been forced into a **reactive Palantir-based food-safety platform build (WIRED, Sep 2026)** — an admission that prior QA/HACCP controls failed. The 2015–18 precedent: multi-quarter traffic collapse, >$300M in fines/settlements/sales damage. Ingredient-level concentration (a single common lot hit multiple states/retailers) is the core exposure.
2. **Labor-compliance failure at scale.** Wage-and-hour class actions, Fair Workweek violations, and NLRB adverse findings across ~3,000+ stores / 130K hourly workers — a structural compliance-control problem, not a one-off (post-settlement re-violation allegations are the tell).
3. **Fresh/half-formed leadership bench at the top + store-level GM churn.** Two years of C-suite turnover (CEO, CFO, Chief Accounting Officer) exactly when operational discipline is being stress-tested; new Apprentice program is the mitigation but unproven.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **HIGH**
- "**A summer of outbreaks**" framing (WIRED) is a direct replay of the 2015 narrative that defined the brand for years; nationwide jalapeño-pull news (AP, Aug 4) and pending injury litigation keep the story live.
- **Investor-confidence erosion visible:** CMG down ~15% y/y, "selling outruns the restaurant group," Oct 5 selling drop of 4% vs. dipping peers; margin-compression coverage despite +9.3% Q2 revenue.
- **Mitigants:** this outbreak is *better handled than 2015* — cooperative with MN health officials, prompt removal, and (unlike 2015) the source appears at least partly shared with other concepts. TD Cowen's analysis notes a McDonald's-type event can be contained to ~1 quarter; the brand's menu-innovation momentum (4× cadence, market-share gains) is intact. But Chipotle's specific history means the market gives it **zero benefit of the doubt** — that trading behavior is visible in the tape.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**
(Justified by the confluence of a live food-safety crisis with litigation tail, a documented pattern of labor-compliance failures, elevated insider-filing activity, and leadership churn — despite adequate governance fundamentals and no confirmed active SEC/DOJ matter.)

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation
- 🚩 **Multi-state foodborne-outbreak recurrence with federal criminal-liability tail** — first since the 2020 DOJ resolution; monitor case counts, CDC/PulseNet updates, and FDA traceback conclusions; a finding of *inadequate process* (vs. contaminated raw material) converts this to CRITICAL.
- 🚩 **Pattern of labor non-compliance post-settlement** — NYC DCWP/SET and NLRB recidivism risk invokes heightened AG/City enforcement and bills as a governance-control weakness.
- 🚩 **Elevated, direction-unverifiable insider Form 4 activity** coinciding with a 15% share decline — warrants a dedicated insider-trading deep-dive before underwriting.
- 🚩 **Entire C-suite regenerated within 24 months while the company responded to its worst operational event since 2015.**
