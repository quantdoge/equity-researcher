**Prepared: October 5, 2026 | Analyst: Macro Research**

## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: TRANSITIONAL — biased risk-off for premium consumer-discretionary growth equities.**

The U.S. is in a "sticky-inflation, higher-for-longer" regime. The Fed's easing cycle that ran from September 2025 (4.33% → 3.63%) has effectively **paused at ~3.75%** (Fed funds effective, Sep 2026), even as the long end keeps climbing: the **10-year Treasury traded at 5.24% on Oct 1, 2026** after testing 5.29% on Sep 30 — roughly 90bp above its April 2026 low (4.26%). The curve has bear-steepened (2s10s +34bp, no longer inverted), which is classic term-premium/fiscal-supply pressure, not a recession signal.

Real growth remains decent but is cooling: **real GDP +2.2% annualized in Q2 2026** (24,408.0B chained 2017$) versus a ~3.9% pace in mid-2025. The labor market is softening — **unemployment 4.2% (Sep 2026)**, up from 3.9–4.0% in early 2025 — and the low-to-middle-income consumer (CMG's marginal guest) is trading down. Inflation is not beaten: **headline CPI ~3.1–3.4% YoY and core PCE +3.0% YoY (Aug 2026)**, both stuck well above target. A strong dollar (broad index ~120.3) and food-away-from-home inflation of ~3.4% complete a picture that is **moderately hostile to a 30-40x-earnings QSR growth stock**.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

The macro backdrop is a net headwind today: a ~5% 10-year is repricing long-duration consumer growth, sticky ~3% inflation keeps the cost base rising into a price-sensitive guest, and the low-income consumer CMG's fourth guest depends on is weakening. The drawdown (CMG −26% y/y vs. flat-to-down S&P) shows the market already agrees.

**Mitigants that keep this from "highly unfavourable":** (1) debt-free balance sheet — no refinancing or rate-expense exposure; (2) US-dominated revenue — zero currency risk; (3) comps inflecting positive (+0.5% Q1 2026) off value-led traffic; (4) ~2.2% real GDP still supports new-store economics. Watch items: unemployment trajectory toward 5%, core PCE re-acceleration, and whether 10Y holds above 5.2%.

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise

**Bear case (probability skew risk): "Term-premium shock + consumer rollover."** If long yields break decisively above 5.5% (fiscal supply, no Fed rescue while core PCE ≥3%) concurrent with unemployment drifting toward 5%+, QSR traffic weakens further, CMG comps re-enter negative territory, and the multiple (already de-rated ~26%) compresses again toward the 20s on earnings. Watch the 10-year's reaction to the next CPI print and FOMC guidance — a 10Y hold above 5% into year-end 2026 with a labor-market softening print is the setup that pressures CMG most.
