### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **SUB-INVESTMENT GRADE (crossover / BB-equivalent profile)**
*No public rating or CDS spread was available from any tool — rating assigned on fundamentals (flagged data gap).*

The credit sits in an unusual place: **leverage is genuinely low, but earnings quality is poor and coverage on a GAAP basis is negative.** FY2026 net debt of $969M was only **0.78x** $1,241.5M EBITDA — a solid high-yield profile. But FY2026 **EBIT was –$36.5M**, giving **interest coverage of –0.23x** (tool: –0.67x). The company only services interest because **D&A of $1,278M — largely amortisation of Zynga acquisition intangibles — is added back.** EBITDA/interest is **7.7x**, which is the more honest measure for this asset base and is adequate.

The balance sheet is not the problem; the P&L is. **Retained earnings are –$7.45B** and **tangible book equity is negative (–$1.2B)** — goodwill and intangibles of $4.90B exceed total equity of $3.67B. That is a large impairment cushion sitting on top of a business that has now posted GAAP net losses for five consecutive years.

**Near-term refinancing is the single clearest credit event:** short-term debt jumped from **$30M (Mar-26) to $629M (Jun-26 and Sep-26)** — reclassification of the ~$600M 3.70% senior notes due **April 2027**, now roughly five months out. The curve (as of 11 Nov 2026) shows **2Y 4.74% / 10Y 5.13% / 30Y 5.47%, 2s10s +39bp, no inversion**. Refinancing at ~6% unsecured for a loss-making, event-dependent issuer implies **~$15M/yr of incremental interest** on that tranche alone, and more on the 4.950% 2028 notes when they come. This is manageable, not existential — cash of $1.67B covers the 2027 maturity 2.7x.

The low beta is the **most dangerous number in this report.** Statistical beta says TTWO is defensive; economically it is a **binary event stock** whose price is currently pinned to a single launch on 19 November 2026. The historically low realised drawdown (–27.7%) reflects a three-year uptrend into that event, not resilience. A disappointing launch or a third delay would produce a drawdown far outside anything in this sample — **historical VaR materially understates tail risk here.**

**Verdict on the –20% case: the company survives comfortably.** EBITDA still covers interest 2.4x, cash of $1.67B covers the $629M April-2027 maturity outright, and there are no dividends or buybacks consuming liquidity. This is the key finding — **the balance sheet is stress-resistant to an ordinary demand shock.**

**The scenario that actually hurts is the binary one:** a third GTA VI delay past 19 November. Street FY2027 revenue of ~$9.23B against guidance of $8.0–8.2B net bookings means roughly **$2.3–2.6B of the forward revenue line depends on this one title landing on schedule.** A slip reverts the model to the –20% to –30% case *and* re-rates the equity — the credit would survive (EBITDA ~$0.4B, interest covered), but the equity would take the damage, not the bondholders. **This is an equity-risk story, not a credit-risk story.**

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

Reasoning: leverage is low (0.78–1.26x), Piotroski is 7/9, FY2026 delivered a **$676M FCF swing** (–$214.6M → +$461.5M), cash covers the only near-term maturity 2.7x, and the –20% revenue stress is survivable without covenant or liquidity strain. Offsetting: five years of GAAP losses, negative tangible equity of $1.2B, negative EBIT coverage, a $629M refinancing into a 5%+ rate environment, and extreme single-title concentration. **No liquidity or solvency crisis is visible; the risk is concentrated, dated and binary.**

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Refinancing wall in ~5 months.** $629M of short-term debt (April 2027 notes at 3.70%) must be refinanced at ~6%. Rates at 10Y 5.13% / 30Y 5.47%. Cash covers it, but the interest burden ratchets permanently higher.
2. **Negative tangible book equity (–$1.2B).** Goodwill + intangibles of $4.90B exceed total equity of $3.67B. A write-down would not touch cash but would gut the already-marginal book-basis Z-Score.
3. **Cash is falling into the launch.** $1,989M → $1,827M → $1,674M over two quarters; H1 FY2027 FCF –$337M; FY26 working capital drain –$1,007M and H1 FY27 –$934M. Burn reverses post-launch, but the company is financing the biggest launch in its history off the balance sheet.
4. **Binary event risk on 19 Nov 2026.** ~$2.3–2.6B of forward revenue is launch-dependent. Beta of 0.58 and a –27.7% max drawdown **understate** true tail risk; historical risk measures are not valid across this event.
5. **Dilution.** Shares 175.1M → 187.1M (+6.9% over two years); SBC $305M (~4.6% of revenue). Piotroski F7 = 0.
6. **Negative EBIT interest coverage** in every recent period. The company cannot cover interest from operations — it relies on non-cash amortisation add-backs.
