# Take-Two Interactive (TTWO) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $224.64 | Market Cap $42.0B | EV $42.6B | Report date: ~Nov 10, 2026**

## 1. Growth Story

Take-Two is nine days from the largest product launch in entertainment history: **Grand Theft Auto VI ships November 19, 2026** (PS5/Xbox Series X|S), with ~$751M in console pre-orders already booked two weeks out (Sensor Tower). The thesis is not the $70 disc sale — it is that GTA VI becomes a decade-long *platform*: GTA Online 2 with recurrent consumer spending, a first-of-its-kind Netflix content partnership extending the IP beyond gaming, plus NBA 2K and Zynga mobile feeding a ~$200B global games TAM. Take-Two is a company that converted a 2013 title (GTA V) into >$10B of lifetime revenue; the market is currently paying 4.9x forward sales for the sequel.

- **Revenue CAGR:** 17.4% FY22→FY26 (but distorted — FY23's +52.6% was the Zynga acquisition; organic growth was flat in FY24, +5% FY25, then +18% FY26).
- **Margin trajectory is the real story:** operating margin improved ~2,000bps in two years to -1.6% GAAP. GAAP gross margin understates economics — $1.28B of annual D&A (mostly Zynga intangible amortization) sits in cost of revenue. On an adjusted basis gross margin is ~70%.
- **Rule of 40 = 25.1** (18.2% growth + 6.9% FCF margin) — below 40, but this is the *pre-launch* trough. FCF swung +$676M YoY to positive in FY2026 on working capital build ahead of launch.
- **Forward consensus:** FY2027 rev $8.68B / EPS $7.06; FY2028 $9.22B / EPS $10.12; FY2030 $10.26B / EPS $11.73. Note FY2027 GAAP EPS guide is only $0.55–0.75 — the gap to $7.06 consensus is entirely intangible amortization + SBC.
- **Valuation:** 4.9x FY2027E sales, **16.3x FY2027E EV/EBITDA**, 13.4x FY2028E, **31.8x FY2027E EPS**, 22.2x FY2028E. For a franchise at this scale entering its monetization ramp, those are undemanding multiples.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **TAM:** Global games ~$200B, split console/PC premium ~$50B, mobile ~$100B, plus in-game spend. Take-Two's FY2026 revenue of $6.66B = **~3.3% share** of the global market.
- **Penetration path:** GTA V (2013) sold 200M+ units and generated >$10B lifetime revenue — over 30% of it from GTA Online recurrent spend in years 3–10, not launch. If GTA VI follows a similar arc into a >$10B lifetime asset, plus GTA Online 2, NBA 2K (~$2B/yr), and Zynga mobile, revenue plausibly reaches **$11–13B by FY2031–32** (~11–14% CAGR), i.e. share rising to 5%+.
- **Differentiation:** Rockstar is arguably the only studio globally that can launch a $1B+ *opening week* product. The moat is brand, development talent, and the live-service economy — not technology. NBA 2K is a quasi-monopoly (licensed NBA simulation); Zynga gives free-to-play distribution reach peers lack.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Nov 19, 2026 — GTA VI global launch.** The single biggest re-rating event. Positive reviews + no server meltdown → estimate revisions up.
2. **Feb 8, 2027 — Q3 FY2027 results.** Consensus models **$3.42B revenue / $3.97 EPS in one quarter** (vs. ~$1.5B normal). This is the first hard datapoint on launch sell-through and attach.
3. **GTA Online 2 / recurrent spend disclosure (CY2027 H1).** The actual EBITDA driver; watch the monetization roadmap and Shark Card-equivalent design.
4. **Netflix partnership content rollout.** Exclusive GTA 6 content — validates a transmedia TAM expansion beyond the console installed base.
5. **FY2028 guidance (May 2027).** Tests whether management holds its "record net bookings in FY2026 and FY2027" multi-year framing — and whether FY2028 grows ex-GTA.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, catalyst-dependent)

The setup is unusually favorable: the biggest entertainment launch ever is nine days away, GTA VI is *selling faster than any product in history*, forward multiples are 4.9x sales / 16.3x EBITDA / 32x FY2027 EPS, and the stock is **down ~12% over the past year** (52-week range $183.86–$265.94). The market has already de-risked the delay cycle (two delays: Nov 2025 and earlier) and is now pricing "restrained guidance" — which is precisely the conservative-guidance posture that historically precedes beats.

I stop short of Strong Buy for three reasons: (1) **management reiterated rather than raised** FY2027 bookings of $8.0–8.2B, while consensus already sits at $8.68B — the bar has shifted from "guidance" to "beat guidance"; (2) the **last two quarters were both EPS misses** (-45% and -70%), breaking a previously strong beat pattern (57% beat rate, +20.8% avg surprise); and (3) the true margin driver — recurrent spend on GTA Online 2 — won't be visible for 4–8 quarters, leaving a plausible "sell the news" air pocket between launch and monetization.

**Position sizing:** build now, add on post-launch volatility. The 12-month risk/reward is roughly +27% to consensus ($285) vs. -20% downside to the Oct 2026 low ($184) if launch reception disappoints.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Launch execution risk (acute, 9 days).** Pre-orders are already the demand signal; a server outage, technical failure, or mixed reception has no offsetting upside to absorb it.
2. **Concentration risk.** FY2027 revenue is effectively a GTA VI binary. Post-GTA VI pipeline is opaque — no GTA VII this decade, and Rockstar's 5–7 year dev cycles create a visible FY2029–31 air pocket (consensus revenue growth decelerates to ~+6% in FY2028 and +2% in FY2029).
3. **NBA 2K and mobile deceleration.** Already dragging Q2 FY2027 bookings; mobile advertising softness hits Zynga.
4. **Recurrent-spend timing.** Launch revenue is low-margin (royalties, platform fees ~30%, physical); the 70%+ margin mix arrives with online monetization, possibly later than consensus.
5. **Guidance that doesn't move.** With $8.0–8.2B reiterated into the biggest launch in history, any Q3 booking south of ~$3.5B reads as a miss even if the game is a hit.
6. **M&A impairment legacy.** FY2025's $4.48B net loss (large Zynga goodwill write-down) is a reminder that capital allocation beyond Rockstar has destroyed value; book value is only $18.95/share (P/B 11.9x).
7. **Data caveat:** the revision-momentum tool flagged a -19.35% "downgrade," but it compares the FY2031 estimate ($9.46, only 4 analysts) against the prior FY2030 figure ($11.73) — a period-mismatch artifact, not a true cut. Treat the Nov 2026 PT reductions ($262–$295 from $280–$313) as the real revision signal: downward-sloping but still well above spot.
