## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT (leaning constructive, conditionally)**

- **Bull cluster (3 agents, high conviction):** Growth, Alt Data, Sector. They agree on *one thing only* — **demand into 19-Nov is materially above plan.** That is well-evidenced, corroborated across three independent data sources, and I weight it heavily.
- **Bear cluster (4 agents):** Non-Fin Risk (HIGH), Short (SHORT), Geo Legal + ESG (regulatory tail), Macro (negative skew). They agree on a *different* thing — **the quality of the earnings, the terminal value of the recurrent-spend annuity, and the alignment of insiders are all worse than the price implies.**
- **The two clusters do not actually contradict each other.** The bulls are right about the launch. The bears are right about FY2028+. The stock is a launch trade wrapped around a structural-value question.

**Conflict resolution — applying precedence rules:**
1. **Fraud veto does NOT trigger.** The Short Analyst ran Beneish (−2.99, low risk), receivables (FY26 AR −13.5% vs rev +18.2% — clean), and accruals (negative, conservative) and **explicitly declined to allege manipulation.** The bear case is valuation + concentration, not integrity. So the short does not get veto power — it gets a **size cap.**
2. **Non-Fin Risk HIGH + insider asymmetry ($57M sold / $0 bought)** is the single most decision-relevant soft signal. Insiders with access to real-time pre-order data chose not to buy. That is a legitimate discount to any "unanimous bull" narrative and caps conviction at Med-High, not High.
3. **Macro's bear case is the one I cannot dismiss.** $160–170 *with bookings at the top of guidance* is a coherent, non-tail mechanism (discount-rate, not fundamentals). It sets the stop-loss, not the direction.
4. **Value gap acknowledged.** Without a Value Researcher, the valuation verdict rests on absolute multiples only: 34x trailing EBITDA / 90x P/FCF / 11.9x P/B on a business with negative ROIC in all four years — versus 13.4x FY28E EV/EBITDA and 22.2x FY28E EPS. ## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **2.0% target, 3.0% hard cap, 1.0% starter**
