**Investment Thesis**

ASTS is a genuinely important technology — the only proven broadband-grade direct-to-device satellite system, with AT&T, Verizon and Vodafone pre-contracted, FCC authorization for a 248-satellite constellation in hand, and a real ~3-billion-subscriber partner TAM — attached to a financial structure that cannot support its own valuation. At ~84x TTM EV/revenue with −$1.8B of trailing FCF, a 0.16 Altman Z-score, +17% annual dilution, and a $4.1B convert stack struck at $79.57–$149.20 (deep out of the money at $26), the funding model is circular: solvency depends on issuing equity into an 82% drawdown. Even granting the consensus bull case and valuing it like its mature peers implies roughly $14/share — the easy downside has been taken, but there is no edge at $26; the position, if any, is a catalyst-gated option, not an investment.

**Bull Case**
- Real, FCC-legitimized TAM: ~$3.5B 2030 consensus revenue is only ~$0.10/month per partner subscriber — an astonishingly low bar on 3B contracted subscribers.
- Balance sheet bought time: ~$2.8–3.2B cash, no maturities before 2031, low-coupon converts, $1B non-dilutive Japan funding; manufacturing constraint demonstrably behind them (production through BlueBird 50).
- Convex right tail: at 0.17x the July convert strike with β ~3, any disinflation-driven duration rally (10Y → ~4%) plus one launch proof point could produce a 100%+ re-rating in two quarters; strategic-bid optionality from MNOs/hyperscalers.

**Bear Case**
- Funding circularity now repriced: −$1.8B TTM FCF, $4.1B converts that must be cash-settled if the stock stays below $79.57, and the next raise must be equity sold into an 82% drawdown (15–20% dilution per year through 2028).
- Structural competitive inferiority: ~0.5% of Starlink's orbital scale, third-party launch dependency (the exact risk that already broke the model), MNO buyer power capping gross margin at 40% vs 65–77% at mature peers.
- Credibility deficit is documented, not alleged: 0-for-7 earnings record, target slipped from late-2026 to mid-2027, serial raises after "sufficient capital" messaging — the literal center of a live fraud class action — and insiders monetized ~$600M while shareholders absorbed the collapse.

**Key Risks**
1. **Execution gates outside company control** — New Glenn launch of BB 14/16 (Jan-27) and full FCC commercial authorization (800MHz); either failing delays revenue without reducing the ~$1.5B/yr burn.
2. **Dilution/debt spiral** — a >10% equity raise or converts struck below $40 accelerates the per-share value destruction that is the primary bear mechanism.
3. **Macro tail** — a forced Fed path to 5%+ keeps the convert window shut and funds the constellation at $20–25/share; reverse tail (violent disinflation) is the bull scenario and is outside management's control.
4. **Litigation escalation** — SEC follow-on, restatement, or auditor change converts a disclosure overhang into a fraud event.
5. **Squeeze risk to any short** — 23% of float short in a retail-heavy, β-2.9 name; sizing discipline is the only protection.
6. *Data gaps flagged*: FY 10-K and DEF 14A were unretrievable (related-party/TRA terms unverified); convert maturity conflict (2031 vs 2034) unresolved; pure-play peer multiples unavailable.
