## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **INVESTMENT GRADE (High Quality)**

Howmet is a deleveraging story with genuinely fortress-like credit metrics. Ratings sit solidly investment grade and are trending up: **Moody's Baa1** (upgraded from Baa3, Aug-2024), **S&P BBB+**, and **Fitch A-** (two-notch upgrade to A- on 13-Feb-2026, citing "conservative capital allocation and strong deleveraging momentum"). Net debt has fallen in a straight line — $3.49B (2022) → $3.23B (2023) → $2.91B (2024) → **$2.31B (2025)** — while EBITDA has nearly tripled (2021 $853M → 2025 $2,388M). Net leverage is now **0.97x**, down from 3.17x in three years.

The Altman Z-score reads **2.64 ("grey zone")** but this is a **methodological artifact, not a warning**: the tool uses *book* equity in X4 (0.92). On a market-capitalization basis (X4 ≈ 17.8x), the Z-score exceeds **12**, deep in the safe zone. Piotroski F-Score is a **perfect 9/9**, confirming simultaneous improvement in profitability, leverage/liquidity, and efficiency.

**Conclusion: HWM survives a 20% revenue collapse with ease.** Even in the severe case, net leverage stays ≤2.3x, interest coverage stays near 5x, and FCF remains positive and covers the dividend. Management would simply pause the $755M buyback — the primary discretionary outflow — to protect the balance sheet. There is **no plausible near-term covenant or liquidity breach** at this leverage profile.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **LOW**

Balance-sheet risk is among the lowest in cyclical industrials: sub-1x net leverage, 13.5x interest coverage, 9/9 Piotroski, rising IG ratings, expanding FCF, and no refinancing cliff. The principal risks are **market/valuation risk** (35% vol, 1.17 beta, −36.5% max drawdown, thin ~1.4% FCF yield leaving little valuation cushion), not solvency risk.

## 6. Key Red Flags & Caveats

- **Valuation/expectations risk, not credit risk:** At ~$258 the stock has re-rated violently (from ~$170 in Aug-2025); a 1.4% FCF yield prices in sustained high-teens growth. Any aerospace-cycle disappointment (e.g., OEM build-rate cuts, engine durability issues) could trigger a 30%+ drawdown despite intact fundamentals.
- **Inventory-heavy working capital:** Quick ratio 1.09 vs current 2.13 — a large ~$1.85B inventory position is the main liquidity sensitivity if demand rolls over.
- **Cyclical/aerospace concentration:** Revenue is tied to commercial aerospace build rates and spares; the 2020-style demand shock is the true tail risk.
- **Data gaps flagged:** (1) Sortino ratio not provided by tool. (2) Full debt maturity schedule and revolver capacity not directly confirmed. (3) Market-cap/price inconsistency — profile shows $84.7B/$212.97 while latest price (2026-02-20) is $257.86 implying ~$104B; FCF yield reported on the lower cap overstates the current yield. (4) Yield-curve tool returned an out-of-sequence as-of date.

**Net:** A high-quality, de-risked balance sheet attached to a high-volatility, richly-valued cyclical. Credit risk **Low**; equity market risk **Moderate-High**.
