**Investment Thesis**

Tempus has built a genuine two-engine asset — share-gaining oncology diagnostics plus a higher-margin, prospectively generated clinical-genomic data library monetised to pharma — and the operating inflection is real and corroborated by five independent agents (organic growth ~23%, gross margin 40.6%→64.8% in four years, first positive adjusted EBITDA and first positive operating cash flow in Q3'26). But the *security* is a 2.4–3.6 beta, 82–93% vol, long-duration asset trading at ~7.9x FY26E sales with no earnings-based floor, heading into (a) a fully-priced December hike with the 10Y at 5.13%, (b) the 1-Jan-2027 CMS CLFS reset (up to −15% on 700+ tests), and (c) a live class action attacking the consent foundation of its highest-margin segment. The correct expression is a small, staged, volatility-budgeted position — not a conviction weight.

**Bull Case**
- Operating inflection is real and broad-based: organic ~23% growth vs 8–10% market, gross margin +24pts in four years, first positive adjusted EBITDA ($25.6M) and OCF, FY26 guide $1.60B/+25.8% with ~$65M adj. EBITDA.
- The data asset compounds with every test; pharma validates it with nine-figure, multi-year contracts (BMS through 2031, Merck, GSK, >$1.1B neuro contract). At 7.9x sales it trades at less than half the multiple of slower-growing MRD peers.
- Convex macro payoff: if the 6.5% PPI proves to be tariff pass-through and the 10Y retraces ~80bp, the duration math alone is worth roughly +50–70% before any operating leverage. Balance sheet carries no forced-financing event.

**Bear Case**
- Valuation has no floor: probability-weighted intrinsic value ~$34; negative EBITDA, negative tangible equity, ~12%/yr dilution plus $200M/yr SBC — the "profitability" narrative rests on SBC add-backs larger than the EBITDA and ~$114M of un-itemised non-operating gains.
- *Farrier* is a binary against the crown jewel: an adverse class-certification or de-identification ruling impairs the >$430M, +33%, highest-margin segment with no hedge available.
- A 2.4–3.6 beta into a hiking Fed with the 10Y at 5.13% and the CY2027 CLFS reset landing 1-Jan-27 — the next 12 months are a discount-rate and reimbursement air-pocket; −30% to the low-$40s is a base-case rate-shock outcome, not a tail.

**Key Risks**
1. *Farrier* class certification or injunctive relief requiring consent re-papering across 70+ data partners (structural impairment — exit trigger).
2. December FOMC hawkish surprise / 10Y sustainably above 5.50% → further 25–35% de-rating.
3. CMS CLFS 2027 reset worse than management's "pricing upside" mitigation.
4. Organic growth decay masked by serial M&A; FY27 guide below ~20% → multiple compresses to 4–5x sales.
5. The ~$114M non-operating income line: if it proves to be a Pathos-related fair-value mark, the related-party concern becomes the story.
6. Goodwill impairment ($823M vs $500M book equity) from Ambry/Personalis; continued dilution from a Personalis deal issued at a depressed multiple.
7. Data gaps flagged: non-operating income itemisation, ~$170M Q3 cash outflow, exact Class B voting power — all require 10-K/DEF 14A verification.
