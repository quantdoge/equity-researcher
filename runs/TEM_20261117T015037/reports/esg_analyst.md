# ESG ANALYSIS — TEMPUS AI, INC. (NASDAQ: TEM)

**Sector:** Healthcare / Medical-Healthcare Information Services · **Market cap:** ~$11.5B · **Price:** $64.76 · **Beta:** 3.6 · **Employees:** ~3,800 · **IPO:** June 2024 (short public track record)

---

## 1. ESG Materiality Map

Tempus is an AI/data platform monetising **genetic and clinical patient data** (45M de-identified records claimed; ~2M images; ~1M matched clinical-genomic records). The materiality map is unusually lopsided:

| Factor | Materiality | Transmission to cash flow / cost of capital |
|---|---|---|
| **S — Data privacy & consent (genetic)** | **Critical** | Directly gates the Data & Applications segment ($108.1M in Q3'26, +33% y/y, 26% of revenue, highest-margin line). Injunctive relief = revenue-model risk, not just a fine. |
| **S — Product safety / clinical validity** | High | FDA CDx status is a moat; a validation failure or mislabeled test destroys clinician trust and reimbursement. |
| **G — Controlled-company structure (30:1)** | **High** | Minority holders cannot check founder strategy, M&A, or related-party dealings. Justifies a governance discount. |
| **G — Disclosure credibility** | High | "AI-washing" securities suit attacks the multiple, not the operations. |
| **S — Talent / SBC dilution** | Medium | 3,800 employees, heavy equity comp; dilution is a shareholder-value transfer. |
| **E — Carbon / physical climate** | **Low** | Labs + cloud compute; no carbon-intensive assets, no CBAM exposure, no stranded-asset risk. |
| **E — Lab waste / biohazard** | Low | Routine, well-regulated, immaterial to valuation. |

**Conclusion: this is a Social-and-Governance story, not an Environmental one.** Any ESG scoring framework that weights carbon heavily will mis-rate TEM.

---

## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **LOW**

- Asset base is laboratories, offices and cloud compute — no Scope 1-intensive operations, no upstream fossil exposure, no CBAM-relevant exports.
- Principal environmental footprint sits in **Scope 3** (data-centre energy for model training, sequencing consumables, biohazardous waste streams). These are cost lines, not liability lines.
- **Gap:** No verified Scope 1/2/3 disclosure or science-based target identified in available materials. Immaterial to the thesis, but it caps any "Leader" ESG rating.
- Physical climate risk: minimal — no agricultural, coastal or water-stress-dependent assets.

---

## 3. Social Risk Assessment: **HIGH**

**The single dominant issue is genetic data consent.** Tempus acquired Ambry Genetics (Feb 2025, $600M) and, per consolidated class actions, compelled transfer of Ambry's genetic database — allegedly hundreds of thousands of individuals — then used it to train AI models and disclosed it to 70+ pharma counterparties (AstraZeneca, BMS, Pfizer, GSK, Incyte, Servier and others). Suits were consolidated as *Farrier et al v. Tempus AI* (N.D. Ill., April 15, 2026), 21 counts, alleging Illinois GIPA, California/Florida/Georgia/Michigan/New York/West Virginia consumer-protection violations, plus negligence, conversion and unjust enrichment. A **third** proposed class action followed in Illinois federal court.

- **Core allegation cuts at the product claim:** that DNA "cannot be de-identified" because it is inherently an identifying biomarker. If accepted, Tempus's de-identification-based consent architecture — the legal foundation of the Data & Applications business — is contestable.
- **Mitigants:** Tempus holds FDA companion-diagnostic approval for xT CDx (tumor-only, May 2026) — a genuine quality/regulatory validation; published peer-reviewed work (*Nature Medicine*, PRISM2); no known labour, safety, or union controversies; no product recalls identified.

---

## 4. Governance Quality Score: **3 / 10**

**Rationale — structural control plus disclosure questions:**

1. **Dual-class, 30:1.** Class A = 1 vote; Class B = **30 votes** (per SEC DEF 14C / EX-4.2). Founder/CEO Eric Lefkofsky holds voting control via Class B and Gray Media entities. Minority shareholders have effectively no ability to alter strategy, board composition, or M&A.
2. **CEO/Chairman duality.** Lefkofsky is Founder, CEO **and** Chairman (confirmed in the Nominating & Corporate Governance Committee charter, which seats him as a committee *observer*). Boardroom Alpha grades the board **C-**; the 2026 slate has 9 nominees with Lefkofsky explicitly non-independent.
3. **Weak shareholder-accountability cadence.** The 2026 proxy's **first-ever** say-on-pay vote occurs in **2027**, and the Board recommends a **triennial** frequency — i.e., shareholders get a compensation referendum once every three years, at a controlled company where the vote cannot bind.
4. **Related-party / interlock exposure.** Tempus lists **Pathos AI** among strategic collaborators; Lefkofsky is reported to be affiliated with Pathos (chairman). *This is the highest-priority diligence item — I could not verify the exact terms from filings within budget and flag it as unconfirmed.* Any founder-affiliated counterparty in a data-licensing deal warrants scrutiny of pricing and exclusivity.
5. **Disclosure credibility.** A June 2025 securities class action alleges Tempus branded itself an AI company "despite having little history of generating significant revenues from AI solutions" (AI-washing); the stock fell **19.2% ($12.67/share)** on the disclosure event.
6. **Mitigants:** PwC as independent auditor; separate Audit, Compensation and Nominating/Governance committees; a lead-independent-director provision in the governance guidelines; PSUs tied to multi-year revenue growth and relative TSR (a genuine long-term alignment feature); and the data-licensing contracts (e.g., Recursion amendment, BMS through 2031) are arm's-length with real cash economics.

## 5. ESG Controversies (with financial impact estimates)

| Controversy | Status | Estimated financial impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Genetic data privacy class actions** (*Farrier*, N.D. Ill., + 2 more) | Consolidated Apr 2026; active | **GIPA statutory damages: $2,500/negligent and $15,000/willful violation.** Theoretical exposure across hundreds of thousands of records runs into the billions; realistic settlement range **$50M–$300M** (0.5–3% of market cap). **The material risk is not the cheque — it is injunctive relief** barring data disclosure without written consent, which would impair the $430M+/yr Data & Applications run-rate and its premium multiple. |
| **"AI-washing" securities class action** | Filed Jun 2025, N.D. Ill. | One-day market-cap loss of ~19%. Settlement typically 1–2% of market cap (**$30M–$80M**), largely D&O-insured; reputational cost to the "AI company" multiple is larger. |
| **Guardant Health v. Tempus AI** (D. Del.) | Tempus's motion to dismiss **denied** | IP/trade-secret exposure on liquid biopsy; potential injunction or royalty. Unquantified. |
| **Data-partner concentration / consent scope** | Structural | 70+ pharma data licensees. Any consent re-papering requirement raises fulfilment cost and could trigger contract renegotiation across the book. |

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure

- **United States (primary):** HIPAA (business-associate obligations); **state genetic-privacy statutes** — Illinois GIPA (private right of action, the live threat), California CMIA, plus Florida, Georgia, Michigan, New York, West Virginia consumer-protection regimes; state comprehensive privacy laws; FDA (LDT/CDx pathway — currently a *tailwind* given xT CDx approval); FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.
- **EU (secondary, rising):** GDPR Article 9 special-category data; **EU AI Act** — most clinical AI tools classify as **high-risk**, with binding obligations from **August 2, 2026**. Low revenue exposure today (US-centric), but a hard compliance cost for any European expansion.
- **Not applicable / low:** CBAM, EU CSRD (below thresholds / US-only), SEC climate-disclosure rule (stayed; emissions immaterial anyway), no ESG-linked debt covenants identified.

---

## 7. Overall ESG Rating: **ESG RISK** (Laggard tier)

Not a broad-based deterioration — a **single concentrated, business-model-level controversy** (genetic data consent) sitting on top of a **controlled-company governance structure** with no meaningful shareholder check. Environmental performance is unremarkable and immaterial. A conventional ESG score would likely under-penalise the privacy issue and over-penalise carbon; our rating weights the former.

---

## 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEGATIVE** (valuation discount, not a disqualifier)

**For the thesis:**
- **Apply a 10–20% governance-and-litigation discount** to any DCF/EV-multiple. The 30:1 structure and CEO/Chair duality permanently cap the re-rating ceiling — this stock will not earn a governance premium regardless of operating performance.
- **Reframe the privacy suits as a revenue risk, not a legal-reserve risk.** Model an injunctive-relief sensitivity on Data & Applications (26% of revenue, the highest-margin segment). A settlement under $300M is absorbable against an $11.5B cap; a consent re-papering mandate across 70+ data partners is not.
- **Diligence the Pathos AI relationship** before sizing a position — unverified founder-affiliated counterparty in the core data business.
- **Monitor:** (i) ruling on class certification in *Farrier*; (ii) any motion for injunctive relief; (iii) Guardant IP outcome; (iv) whether the 2027 say-on-pay passes with meaningful dissent — the only lever minority holders have.
- **Offsets that argue against an outright exclusion:** FDA CDx approval, peer-reviewed clinical validation, and contracted multi-year data economics (BMS through 2031, Recursion through ~2029) suggest the consent architecture is defensible, not fraudulent.

**Net:** ESG is a **position-sizing and discount-rate input**, not a veto. Risk-tolerant holders can own TEM with an explicit governance discount; ESG-mandated or governance-screened mandates should exclude on the dual-class/board-grade combination alone.
