# TEM (Tempus AI) — Forensic Short-Seller Analysis

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **MEDIUM** (not fraudulent — but cosmetically managed)

This is not a fraud. There is no accruals blow-up, no receivables bomb, no inventory rot, no going-concern cliff. But *reported profitability is engineered*, and the quality of the revenue mix is deteriorating. Two separate layers of adjustment are required to reach the "we're approaching profitability" narrative:

**Layer 1 — Stock comp larger than the EBITDA loss.** FY2025: EBITDA **–$118.5M**, SBC **$124.7M**. "Positive adjusted EBITDA" exists *only because SBC exceeds the entire EBITDA loss*. SBC is 10% of revenue and ~20x the implied adjusted EBITDA.

**Layer 2 — Non-operating gains are doing the heavy lifting in 2026.** Deriving other income/expense from operating income vs net income:

| Quarter | Op income | Interest | Implied non-operating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1'26 | –$84.7M | –$14.3M | **–$26.9M** |
| Q2'26 | –$75.9M | –$10.3M | **+$91.8M** |
| Q3'26 | –$56.9M | –$6.7M | **+$48.7M** |
| **9M'26** | | | **≈ +$113.7M** |

Q2'26 reported "EBITDA" of +$45.1M and net income of +$5.6M sit alongside an **operating loss of –$75.9M** — meaning roughly **$95M of non-operating income is embedded in reported EBITDA**. Strip it and clean EBITDA is approximately **–$50M** (Q2) and **–$28M** (Q3). The single most important open question in this name is *what that line item is*. I could not itemise it (no 10-K returned; the 10-Q endpoint returned only an exhibit). Candidates: remeasurement of Ambry contingent consideration, or a mark-up of Tempus's equity stake in **Pathos AI** — a company its CEO co-founded. Both are non-cash; the second would be a related-party fair-value exercise in which Tempus is marking its own asset.

**Cash flow reality:** 9M'26 OCF **–$71.6M**, FCF **–$107.5M**. FY2025 OCF –$218.1M (including a –$178.5M working-capital drag); FCF –$245.4M. The Q3'26 OCF turn positive (+$9.2M) is a genuine but thin first step, and FCF is still negative.

## 2. Key Red Flags

**a) Revenue deceleration is severe and structural.** YoY growth: **82.98% → 36.12% → 21.56% → 23.14%**. The 80%+ prints were Ambry consolidation (closed Nov 2024). Post-anniversary run-rate is a **~22% grower** priced at 7.9x EV/Sales. Serial M&A (Ambry, and now the announced **Personalis** deal) means organic growth is never cleanly disclosed — every year gets a bolted-on acquisition. This is the core of the bear case: **you cannot tell what the business actually grows at, and that is likely the point.**

**b) Data-licensing revenue is decaying and partly paid in customer equity.** From the Recursion amendment (Ex-10.1, filed 2 Nov 2026): annual licence fees are **$22M (yr 1) → $32M (yr 2) → $14M for each of yrs 3–5**, with a floor of only **$4M in cash** and up to **$10M payable in Recursion Class A stock at Recursion's sole discretion**. So the flagship "AI data" contract drops >50% after year two, and 71% of it can be settled in the counterparty's own devalued paper. The amendment also caps the number of records Recursion may access. **Revenue recognised against illiquid customer stock is a classic revenue-quality tell.**

**c) Related-party revenue concentration.** The Apr-2025 AstraZeneca/**Pathos** expansion calls for **$200M in data licensing and model-development fees** — and Lefkofsky is a **co-founder of Pathos AI**. Add Recursion, GSK and Merck, and the entire Insights franchise rests on a handful of counterparties, at least one run by an entity the CEO co-founded and in which Tempus holds equity.

**d) Receivables — I checked, and it's mostly innocent.** The screener flags AR +106.7% vs revenue +83.4% (FY2025). Two-thirds of that is an Ambry consolidation artifact (only ~2 months of Ambry AR in FY2024). DSO: 79 days (Q3'25) → 90 (Q2'26) → **84 days (Q3'26)** — elevated but improving. Not a channel-stuffing story. **This flag is weaker than the screener implies.**

**e) Inventory: clean.** Inventory days 42.7 → 42.9 → 44.9 → **39.8** — falling, no demand signal.

**f) Gross margin is *expanding*, not eroding.** 40.6% (FY22) → 53.8% → 55.0% → **62.7%** → **64.8%** (Q3'26). This is real pricing/mix improvement. **It is the single strongest datapoint against the "moat erosion" thesis**, and I weight it accordingly.

**g) Balance sheet is thin, not broken.** Cash $637M vs debt $1.43B → **net debt $841M**. Book equity **$500M** against goodwill & intangibles **$823M** → **tangible equity ≈ –$322M**. Retained earnings –$2.53B. No near-term maturity wall, ~4+ years of runway at current burn — **no liquidity catalyst.** Implied leverage on adjusted EBITDA is meaningless (infinite).

**h) Dilution is continuous.** Shares 162.1M (FY24) → 174.3M (FY25) → **181.3M (Q3'26)**, +12% in 18 months, on top of $125M/yr SBC.

**i) Anomaly worth a footnote check:** total debt rose from $1.335B (Q3'25) to $1.432B (Q3'26) while quarterly interest expense *fell* from $15.4M to **$6.7M**. Debt up, interest down — that needs an explanation (capitalised interest? derivative/amortisation mix? swap?) that I could not resolve.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

- **Core NGS diagnostics** competes with Foundation Medicine (Roche), Guardant, Natera, Exact Sciences, NeoGenomics, Caris. Reimbursement (CMS/MolDx) — not competition — is the real margin sword.
- **Ambry (germline/hereditary)** is a lower-margin, semi-commodity lab business; the genetics space has already bankrupted Invitae. This is the segment carrying the revenue growth and dragging the quality of it.
- **"AI" is a narrative, not a disclosed P&L line** — the subject of a 2025 AI-washing securities class action alleging Tempus "branded itself as an AI company despite little history of generating significant revenues."
- **Data-licensing moat is shallower than advertised**: de-identified, non-exclusive datasets sold to a handful of pharma buyers, against Datavant/Flatiron/TriNetX/Aetion — and the Recursion amendment shows *per-record access caps and a step-down in fee*, i.e. the buyer is pricing data access *down*, not up.

**Net: mild-to-moderate erosion in the labs, a genuinely improving gross margin overall, and a data moat that is narrower and lumpier than the equity story requires.**

## 6. Short Thesis Summary — and the Other Side

**The bear case:** A ~22% top-line grower (decelerating from 83%), burning ~$107M FCF in 9M'26, carrying net debt of $841M against negative tangible equity, priced at **7.9x EV/Sales** and — most damningly — **~22x consensus *2030* EPS of $2.95**. GAAP "breakeven" is manufactured by ~$114M of 2026 non-operating gains plus a $125M/yr SBC add-back. Growth is bought via serial M&A that obscures organic trends; the highest-multiple segment (data) has a related-party counterparty, a contract that steps *down* >50% after year two, and payment terms permitting settlement in customer equity. The CEO has sold $50M+ into strength, including at $80+. Target: de-rate to 5.0x FY2027E sales (~$1.95B) = 9.75B EV → **~$49/share (–24%)**; harsh case 4.0x → **~$38 (–41%)**.

**The bull case is genuinely strong, and I will not understate it:**
1. **Gross margin has risen 24 points in four years** (40.6% → 64.8%) — that is real operating leverage, not accounting.
2. **Revenue more than doubled in two years** ($532M → $1.27B → ~$1.57B) and **Q3'26 OCF turned positive** — the cash inflection is real.
3. **No liquidity catalyst.** $637M cash, no near maturities on the converts, ~4+ years of runway. Shorts have no forced-financing event to point to.
4. **The prior short thesis partly failed.** Spruce Point's 50–60% call (May 2025) has been substantially disproven by execution; the stock is *above* its level on the day of that report.
5. **Squeeze risk is severe.** Beta 3.6; single-day moves of **+24% (19 Aug 2026)** and **+15% (17 Sep 2026)** on 16–23M volume; a crowded, widely-publicised short. This is how short sellers get carried out. Note also the stock is already **~24% off its 3 Nov high of $84.47** — the easy part of the de-rating may be behind us.
6. **Altman at market cap is 2.72** (grey, not distress); accruals are negative; receivables and inventory are clean.

**Verdict on the thesis:** This is a **valuation and governance short, not a fraud short.** There is no accounting blow-up to be found — I looked and it isn't there. What's there is a richly-priced narrative stock whose profitability optics rely on two layers of adjustment and a related-party data contract that is shrinking, and whose CEO is a persistent seller.

## 7. Contrarian Rating: **SHORT** (moderate conviction — *not* Strong Short)

**Not "Strong Short"** because: accruals are clean, cash conversion is ~1.0x, gross margin is expanding, there is no liquidity wall, and the demonstrated 15–24% single-day squeeze behaviour on a beta-3.6 name makes a headline short a poor risk-adjusted expression.

**Not "No Edge"** because: at 7.9x EV/Sales and ~22x *2030* EPS, for a ~22% decelerating grower with negative FCF, negative tangible equity, ~12% annual dilution, a decaying related-party data contract settled partly in customer stock, and $50M+ of CEO selling into strength — the asymmetry favours the bears on a 12–18 month horizon.

**Expression I'd recommend:** defined-risk (puts or put spreads struck 15–25% below spot, 6–12 months), sized small, rather than a naked short. **Catalysts to watch:** (1) itemisation of the ~$114M of 2026 non-operating income in the next 10-K — if it is a Pathos-related mark, that is the real story; (2) Ambry/Personalis organic growth disclosure; (3) the Recursion fee step-down to $14M and any further equity-settled licensing; (4) CMS reimbursement changes; (5) any goodwill impairment against $500M of book equity.

**Data gaps, stated honestly:** no 10-K was retrievable, the 10-Q endpoint returned only an exhibit, so the non-operating income line is *inferred, not verified*; sector-peer multiples were unavailable (the endpoint returned mega-cap pharma, not the relevant comps — Guardant, Natera, Exact, NeoGenomics), so all multiples above are my own EV/Sales and consensus-derived calculations; insider sale prices for two lots are approximate from press descriptions rather than parsed Form 4 XML.
