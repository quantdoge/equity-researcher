## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — but the split is informative, not noise**

The eleven reports cluster into three near-unanimous blocks and one genuine disagreement.

**Block A — The operating inflection is real (High agreement: Sector, Growth, Alt Data, Fin Risk, Short).**
Revenue +23% organic, gross margin 40.6%→64.8% in four years, first positive adjusted EBITDA ($25.6M), first positive OCF (+$9.2M), FCF improved from −$128M to −$5.5M across four quarters. **Even the short analyst concedes this** — "gross margin has risen 24 points… that is real operating leverage, not accounting."

**Block B — There is no solvency event (High agreement: Fin Risk, Value, Short, Macro).**
$637M cash, zero short-term debt, 0.00% converts due 2032, secured facility repaid. Nobody is calling a default or a financing wall. **Dilution, not default, is the transfer mechanism** (~12% annual share growth + $200M/yr SBC).

**Block C — The risk stack is dense and uncured (High agreement: Non-Fin Risk, Geo-Legal, ESG, Value).**
Live class action against the highest-margin segment; founder super-voting control; executive-wide selling with zero offsetting purchases; related-party revenue. Three independent agents rated this **HIGH** without coordinating.

**The genuine disagreement — valuation (Split).**
Growth says 7.9x FY26E sales is cheap versus NTRA at 18.1x and GH at 19.8x. Value says probability-weighted value is $34. Both are internally sound; they differ on the exit multiple and the bear weight. Value assigns 30% to a $12 bear case, which is what drives its negative verdict — and that bear case *is* the 2027 CLFS cut plus sustained 5%+ long rates, i.e. Macro's base case for the next 12 months, not a tail.

### Conflict resolution (applying framework precedence)

1. **Legal/privacy risk takes precedence over the growth thesis.** The Farrier suit does not seek a fine; it seeks to invalidate the consent architecture underpinning Data & Applications (>$430M run-rate, +33% growth, highest margin). This is a **binary, non-diversifiable, un-hedgeable** risk. It cannot be sized away with a wider stop.
2. **Fraud risk is *cleared*.** The short analyst did the forensic work and found the earnings cash-backed: accruals −1.18%, Beneish M-Score's "high risk" reading attributable to Ambry intangibles and 83% growth (six of eight variables point clean), receivables and inventory clean, DSO improving (90→84 days). The Altman "distress" score is an accumulated-deficit artifact (Z=2.72 at market cap). **I do not apply a fraud discount — I apply a governance-and-litigation discount.**
3. **Macro wins on timing, not on thesis.** The near-horizon driver is the discount rate, and there is a fully-priced binary event on 9-Dec. Entry should be staged across it, not placed in front of it.
4. **Alt Data vs Short resolve on horizon.** Both agree fundamentals improve and valuation is full. Alt Data itself says the edge lands in the Feb-2027 print. That is a scheduling instruction, not a contradiction.

**Net:** the *business* is a buy; the *security* is a small, staged, volatility-budgeted option. That is the only synthesis consistent with all eleven inputs.
