# SECTOR BRIEF — Tempus AI (TEM)

---

## 1. Sector Overview

TEM competes in precision oncology diagnostics — comprehensive genomic profiling (CGP), MRD/ctDNA monitoring, germline testing — plus monetisation of linked clinical-genomic data to pharma ("Data & Applications"). The addressable diagnostics pool is roughly **$19.5B in 2026 growing ~8–10% p.a.**, while the broader precision-oncology market (therapeutics included) is ~$133B. The market is **structurally fragmented**: the three leaders (Tempus, Natera, Guardant) are projected to hold only **~28% combined share in 2026**, implying a low HHI (~sub-1,000) and no pricing oligopoly. Barriers are moderate — the LDT pathway keeps capital intensity below FDA-PMA diagnostics, but payer coverage, guideline (NCCN) inclusion, and multi-modal data scale are durable moats once achieved. Porter's Five Forces: **rivalry HIGH, buyer power HIGH** (Medicare CLFS administers price; commercial payers use prior auth), supplier power MODERATE (Illumina sequencing), new entrants MODERATE-HIGH, substitutes MODERATE (small panels, IHC, academic labs).

**Cycle position:** mid-to-late **growth phase**, not mature — unit volumes are compounding 20%+ for share gainers even though *price* is deflationary. The critical swing factor is the **CY2027 CMS Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule reset (PAMA round 2): up to 15% cuts on ~700+ of ~1,200 tests effective 1-Jan-2027 absent Congressional action.** This is a sector-wide ASP event landing in the next two quarters.

---

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **Volume-led CGP and MRD penetration.** TEM's MRD test volumes grew **+300% YoY** in Q3'26; CGP attach is rising as biomarker-linked ADC/bispecific launches require broader panels. Diagnostics revenue +20% YoY on ~20%+ volume.
- **Pharma data licensing is the highest-value, highest-margin leg.** Data & Applications revenue reached **$108.1M in Q3'26 (+33% YoY)**, re-accelerating, backed by multi-year deals (BMS, Moderna, Merck, AstraZeneca, GSK). This is recurring, software-like revenue on data already generated by the lab — incremental margins well above the 64.8% blended gross margin.
- **Regulatory overhang has lifted.** FDA's LDT rule was **rescinded/vacated (ACLA v. FDA)**, removing a multi-year compliance and re-filing cost burden from lab-based test developers — disproportionately helpful to scaled LDT operators like TEM.

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **PAMA/CLFS 2027 reset = direct Medicare price cuts.** Up to **15% reductions on 700+ tests from 1-Jan-2027**. Management guides to "2027 pricing upside" (new codes/ADLT), so this is partly offset, but the sector's pricing power is structurally weak and headline ASP risk is real.
- **Payer scrutiny and bad-debt risk.** Prior authorisation intensity, growing high-deductible/uninsured exposure, and sequencing cost deflation that gets competed away rather than retained — gross margin gains (60.7% → 64.8% YoY) must be defended, not assumed.
- **Lumpy, concentrated demand from life-science budgets.** Pharma R&D budget discipline and pressured NIH funding make data-licensing and trial-matching revenue volatile and top-10-customer concentrated. Combined with **intense MRD/early-detection competition** from Natera and Guardant — both well capitalised and growing 30%+ — the sector is in a share-shift war, not a rising-tide market.

## 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG** (high-beta strong)

**Rationale for the positive call:**
- **Growth leadership:** ~23–26% organic vs a 16.3% peer median and an 8–10% market — clearly taking share in a fragmented market.
- **Gross margin at parity** with the diagnostics peer group (62.7% → 64.8%) despite a much larger owned-lab footprint than software peers.
- **Only company in the set with a genuine two-engine model:** diagnostics volume (share-gaining) plus higher-margin data licensing (+33%, accelerating), which no pure lab peer replicates at this scale.
- **Inflection validated:** first positive adjusted EBITDA and four consecutive quarters of operating-margin improvement; the deceleration cycle appears broken.

**Rationale for the "high-beta" qualifier:**
- Still **GAAP loss-making** (-$245M FY25), SGA of $232M/quarter, and **beta 3.61 — the highest in the comp set**, so it is the sector's most fragile holding if the 2027 CLFS cuts or a pharma data-budget pause hits sentiment.
- **Valuation is a growth-premium debate, not a discount:** 7.9x FY26E sales is below the 15.2x peer median but above ILMN/DOCS — it is *cheap only if* 25%+ growth and the EBITDA ramp hold.
- Stock is **~16% below its Sept-2026 high ($85)** after a 30% six-month run — the market is already pricing reimbursement risk.

## 6. Sector Rating: **ATTRACTIVE** (3-year view) / **NEUTRAL** (next 12 months)

The structural case is attractive: a ~$20B diagnostics pool growing high-single/double digits, low concentration leaving room for share gainers, large AI/data-monetisation optionality, and regulatory clarity post-LDT rescission. **But the next 12 months are a reimbursement air-pocket** — the CY2027 CLFS reset lands 1-Jan-2027 with up to 15% cuts on the majority of molecular tests, and peer multiples (15.2x median sales) leave little room for an ASP miss. Recommended posture: **own the share gainers on reimbursement-driven drawdowns** (TEM, NTRA), avoid the volume-losing tools exposure (ILMN), and treat the profitable healthcare-IT names (VEEV, DOCS) as the defensive ballast in the same thematic basket.
