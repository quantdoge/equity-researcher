# NXT — Nextpower Inc. (formerly Nextracker) | Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $91.13 | Market cap $13.8B | EV $12.9B (net cash >$1.0B, no debt) | Fiscal year ends March**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Nextpower is transitioning from the world's leading solar tracker vendor into an integrated utility-scale power-technology platform — trackers (NX Horizon), foundations (NX Earth Truss), electrical BOS, inverters/power conversion (NX PowerMerge, 1.1 GW framework), and now battery storage plus AI-data-center firm power via the Prevalon acquisition. The core tracker business is generating record bookings and a **>$6B backlog** on the back of a 2026–27 US solar pull-forward (OBBBA requires placed-in-service by Dec 31, 2027 to capture the ITC), while storage/power conversion/content-per-project expansion is the mechanism that bridges the post-2027 policy air pocket. Critically, the stock has round-tripped from **$156 (May 2026) to ~$78 (Sept) and back to $91** — the market is currently pricing a demand cliff, not the platform expansion.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **Core tracker SAM:** global utility-scale tracker market roughly $8–10B today, growing high-single to low-double digits with global GW installs; NXT is the #1 global share holder (~30%+, higher in the US).
- **Content-per-project expansion** is the real lever: trackers → foundations + eBOS + inverters + software. eBOS bookings topped $100M in a single quarter (record), foundations set records, and NX PowerMerge already has **>2 GW in backlog**. This can roughly double revenue per MW served without new customers.
- **BESS TAM:** management sizes ex-China BESS demand at **up to $35B by 2030 ($15B US)** — a market as large as the entire tracker TAM, entered for ≤$365M via Prevalon (6+ GWh deployed, 1.3 GW firm contracts for AI/hyperscaler data centers).
- **Geographic runway:** Zimmermann added 15 countries → **68 countries total**; international is the structural offset to a US post-2027 slowdown.
- **Penetration:** storage and complementary products were **~22% of Q2 revenue**, up from a rounding error two years ago — the platform thesis is already visible in the numbers, not just the slide deck.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Nov 16, 2026 — Capital Markets Day (Las Vegas):** first formal long-term growth/financial framework post-rebrand. Highest-impact near-term re-rating event; stock is already moving on sentiment (Jefferies "franchise pick" drove +15.7% on Nov 2).
- **Late Jan 2027 — Q3 FY27 results:** guide implies only ~10% revenue growth with margin 1–2 pts below Q4; a beat + initial FY28 framing is the key test of the "no cliff" narrative.
- **Prevalon/BESS revenue conversion:** 1.3 GW firm data-center supply contracts converting to bookings/revenue through FY28; hyperscaler order announcements.
- **Power conversion ramp:** first NX Power Conversion shipments done; 1.1 GW framework across five projects scaling into FY28.
- **Policy milestones:** FEOC/45X guidance, safe-harbor clarity, and 2027 placed-in-service rush (216–240 GW of US utility-scale solar reportedly safe-harbored — a multi-year installation tailwind for trackers).
- **Buyback cadence:** ~$162M repurchased in Q2 alone against a $13.8B cap, with >$1B cash and zero debt.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends ("solar tracker"):** spiked to 100 in April 2026 and again to 81 in early June — coincident with the stock's $156 peak — then collapsed to single digits by October. Retail/news attention has fully normalized; consistent with the de-rating but **no leading signal of demand recovery**. Treated as noise, not edge.
- **Job postings / hiring:** thin via proxy, but the rebrand to Nextpower, board additions (Sarah Slusser, Michael Alvarez), and engineering postings around NX PowerMerge/Earth Truss support the platform build-out.
- **Company-level leading indicators are the strongest alt-data here:** **record quarterly bookings, backlog >$6B (≈1.4x FY27 revenue)**, and 45X/tariff rebates of $99M in the quarter. Backlog is the highest-quality forward signal and it is accelerating, not decelerating.
- **Analyst PT trajectory:** nine PT cuts in Oct 2026 (post-print) but the **median target ~$126 (~+38% vs spot)**, with the range $101–$150 — the Street is trimming numbers, not abandoning the thesis. PT cuts + unchanged above-market targets is typically a sentiment trough, not a fundamental one.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

**What the market is implying:** at $91, EV of $12.9B ÷ (15x EV/EBITDA × 21% margin) implies revenue of ~$4.1B — essentially **zero growth beyond FY27**. A 12x multiple and 22% margin implies ~$4.9B, i.e. only ~two years of growth. Meanwhile the company has >$6B of contracted backlog, record bookings, entry into a $35B BESS TAM, net cash, and 100% beat rate. That asymmetry — not the 19x P/E — is the investment case.

**Why Buy and not Strong Buy:** (1) Rule of 40 of 35 is below the 40 threshold after two years of gross-margin compression (33.7% → 32.6% → 31.1% in Q2); (2) the Q3 guide of ~10% growth signals a genuine air pocket; (3) ~40% of adjusted EBITDA in Q2 came from 45X vendor rebates and tariff recoveries — a policy-contingent earnings stream; (4) 2028 is a policy blind spot that requires the storage/data-center story to actually deliver. I would size the position for a **2–3 year hold with a re-underwrite at the Nov 16 CMD**.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Post-2027 US demand cliff (existential to the multiple):** OBBBA ties the ITC to placed-in-service by Dec 31, 2027; the 5% safe harbor ended Sept 2, 2025. If 2028 US utility-scale installs roll over, backlog conversion stalls and the multiple compresses further regardless of BESS traction.
2. **Margin quality:** 45X rebates/tariffs contributed ~$99M against $250M adj. EBITDA. Solar 45X phases down late this decade; gross margin has already slipped ~260bps YoY. Mix shift into storage/eBOS/foundations (lower gross margin than trackers) structurally dilutes the P&L.
3. **Execution/integration risk:** two acquisitions in one year (Prevalon ≤$365M, Zimmermann ~€230M) expand headcount and geography (68 countries) with no track record in BESS manufacturing — a harder, warranty-heavy, working-capital-intensive business than steel structures.
4. **Competition:** Array Technologies, GameChange, PVH, and low-cost Chinese trackers pressuring international pricing; Fluence/Tesla/Sungrow in BESS. Nextpower's moat is scale + software + balance sheet — real, but not unassailable in commodities-like hardware.
5. **Concentration & cyclicality:** utility-scale project concentration (a few large IPPs/EPCs), beta 1.93, and 50% peak-to-trough drawdown in five months demonstrate how violently this de-rates on policy headlines.
6. **Key-man / narrative risk:** the rebrand and platform expansion are tightly bound to Dan Shugar; a credibility gap between CMD targets and delivery would be punished hard.

---

**Bottom line:** A best-in-class operator in a structurally growing market, now trading at ~19x forward adjusted EPS with PEG <1, a $6B backlog, zero net debt, and record bookings — while the market discounts a 2028 cliff that storage, power conversion, and 68-country distribution are specifically designed to bridge. The Nov 16 Capital Markets Day is the swing factor: long-term targets that credibly frame FY28+ growth turn this into a re-rating candidate; vague targets leave it a policy-beta trade. **BUY, accumulate into the CMD, with risk controls for policy headlines.**
