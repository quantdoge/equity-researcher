## Financial Risk Assessment: NXT (Nextpower / Nextracker Inc.)

**Sector:** Energy — Solar (utility-scale tracker & software) | **FY End:** March 31 | **Latest FY:** FY2026 (filed 2026-05-19)

---

### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **INVESTMENT GRADE (balance-sheet quality basis; unrated)**

NXT carries essentially **no debt**. Total debt at 3/31/2026 was $52.9M against **$1.095B of cash** — a **net cash position of $1.042B**. Long-term debt is zero. Interest expense of $2.6M on $716M of EBIT implies **265x coverage**, which is effectively a non-metric; solvency risk from leverage is negligible.

The correct framing is not "can they service debt" but "is the balance sheet being used productively." At $137M–$190M market cap-implied equity (see data note), net cash is 5–8% of market value — a genuine cushion but not the whole story.

**Caveat:** No public credit rating or CDS spread was available — this rating is derived from fundamentals, not agency opinion. Flagged as a data gap.

**Two scores require interpretation, not just reporting:**

- **Z-Score of 2.08 is misleading.** The x4 term uses *book* equity (1.34) and x2 is negative because of a **$1.97B accumulated deficit** inherited from the pre-IPO capital structure (retained earnings went from −$3.07B in FY24 to −$1.97B in FY26 — rapidly healing). Substituting market cap (~$19B) into x4 against $1.74B of liabilities gives x4 ≈ 10.9, which would place NXT firmly in the **safe zone (Z > 5)**. Treat the grey-zone reading as a mechanical artifact, not a distress signal.
- **F-Score of 4 is real but reflects deceleration, not distress.** Points lost: ROA declined (16.0% → 14.4%), accruals negative (CFO $556M < NI $586M), share count rose (143.5M → 148.0M), gross margin fell (33.7% → 32.6%), asset turnover fell (0.93 → 0.87). This is the profile of a high-growth company normalizing post-peak margin — not a company in trouble.

### 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — Revenue −20%

Modeled off FY2026 actuals ($3,559M revenue, 32.6% gross margin, $342M SG&A):

| Revenue | $3,559M | $2,847M | $2,314M |

| SG&A (10% flexed / 10% flexed) | $342M | $308M | $308M |
| D&A | $31M | $31M | $31M |
| **EBIT** | **$697M** | **~$590M** | **~$416M** |
| EBITDA | $747M | ~$620M | ~$446M |
| Interest Coverage | 266x | ~226x | ~160x |
| Net Debt / EBITDA | −1.39x | −1.68x | −2.34x |
| FCF (est.) | $512M | ~$450M | ~$300M |

**Conclusion: NXT survives a 20% revenue decline without any financing action, and survives a 35% decline comfortably.** Net cash *increases* in the downside because working capital releases. There is **no refinancing cliff** (zero long-term debt), **no covenant risk**, and **no liquidity risk** — cash and equivalents alone cover 94% of current liabilities.

The downside scenario that *would* matter is not a demand slowdown but a **gross-margin shock** — e.g., Section 232/AD-CVD tariffs on imported steel components, or loss of IRA/45X transferability economics. A 500bp gross-margin compression at flat revenue costs ~$178M of EBIT (26% of operating income) — more damaging than a 20% volume decline, and it flows directly to FCF. **This is the scenario to underwrite, not the revenue one.**

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

**Split assessment — this is the key judgment:**
- **Solvency / credit / liquidity risk: LOW.** Net cash, 265x coverage, 2.45x current ratio, self-funding FCF. Among the strongest balance sheets in the solar equipment space.
- **Market / equity risk: HIGH.** 64.9% vol, 1.93 beta, 51% max drawdown.
- **Business-model concentration risk: HIGH** (see red flags).

Blending these, MODERATE is the fair label — but investors should understand the risk is **almost entirely in the equity price and end-market cyclicality, not in the capital structure.**

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Geographic / policy concentration:** **77% of FY2026 revenue came from U.S. projects**, 23% international (per FY26 10-K). U.S. solar demand is a direct function of IRA tax-credit policy, interest rates (project finance), and interconnection queues. A policy reversal is an existential-level revenue risk that no balance sheet can offset.
2. **Customer concentration:** The 10-K contains explicit disclosure tables for customers exceeding 10% of revenue and top-five concentration. Utility-scale solar is a small-buyer industry (EPCs and IPPs); top-five concentration is typically very high. **I was unable to extract the specific percentages — this is a material data gap that should be closed before sizing a position.**
3. **Tariff / input-cost exposure:** Steel-intensive product with imported components. Tariff changes hit gross margin directly and quickly — the margin shock modeled above.
4. **Margin and ROA deceleration:** Gross margin 33.7% → 32.6%, ROA 16.0% → 14.4%, asset turnover 0.93 → 0.87. Three consecutive F-Score points lost on efficiency. Growth is being bought with working capital and dilution.
5. **Dilution:** Share count 143.5M → 148.0M (+3.1%) with no buyback offsetting it (repurchases of just $395K in FY26 vs $552M in FY24). SBC was $119M in FY25.
6. **Goodwill & intangibles of $567M** = 24% of equity, 14% of assets — grew from $266M in FY24 via acquisition. Impairment risk if the platform-expansion strategy disappoints.
7. **Backlog is the mitigant, not a red flag:** backlog exceeded **$5B at FY26 year-end** (reported above $6B in a more recent quarter). This substantially de-risks the next 4–6 quarters of revenue and is the single strongest argument against the −20% scenario materializing near-term.

---

**Bottom line:** NXT is a **balance-sheet-fortress, high-beta cyclical**. It cannot be killed by leverage, liquidity, or a demand downturn — it can be hurt badly by a gross-margin shock from tariffs or by a U.S. policy reversal, and its equity will draw down 40–50% in any broad risk-off move given a ~1.9 beta and 65% volatility. Underwrite the equity as a policy-and-margin-sensitive cyclical, not as a credit story.
