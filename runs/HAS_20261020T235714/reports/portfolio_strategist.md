## 2. Consensus Assessment: **MEDIUM — a "good business, full price" split**

Two clean camps:

- **Fundamental-momentum camp (Growth, Alt Data, Short-rejection, Macro, Sector):** earnings are real, cash-confirmed, accelerating, and cheap on 11–15x forward EBITDA/EPS. No forensic objection survives.
- **Valuation-and-risk camp (Value, Quant, Fin Risk, Non-Fin Risk, Geo/Legal):** the price already discounts the turnaround; the equity has no book cushion; Q3's 75.9% gross margin and 49.2% Wizards segment margin are likely cyclical peaks that management itself says compress in 2027; the stock trades *above* the sell-side mean target after an 11% gap.

**Resolution of the three live conflicts:**
1. **Growth's PEG vs Value's "no margin of safety."** Growth is arithmetically right but its denominator (forward EPS) embeds peak MTG margins; the company has explicitly flagged 2027 Wizards margin compression. I weight the Value view on *sizing* and the Growth view on *direction*. There is no disagreement that the business improved — only about what to pay.
2. **Buyback doubling vs zero insider purchases.** Company capital deployment is real and a positive tell; the ~$54M of insider sales with $0 of buys is a modest negative (likely 10b5-1, but CEO/CFO sales at $95–101 pre-print are unflattering). Net: neutral-to-slightly-negative, worth a monitoring line, not a veto.
3. **Short analyst's "no edge" is the most valuable input.** It removes the only thesis-killer scenario (fraud / channel stuffing) and gives us a hard downside anchor of **$85 — which coincides exactly with Value's buy zone ($85–88) and the six-month base low ($85.37)**. That convergence defines the whole trade structure below.

**Agreement level on the two things that matter: High.** (a) MTG concentration is the dominant risk; (b) ~$85–88 is the level where risk/reward flips.

HAS is the *right* name in a *neutral* sector — but adding it increases aggregate consumer-discretionary exposure into a weakening consumer. **Rule: do not hold HAS alongside another collectibles/hobby-cycle name** (that would be a levered bet on one fashion cycle, not a diversifier).

Risk must be controlled by position size and fundamental stops, not index hedges.

**Factor exposure added:** **+ momentum** (12-1 +10.1%, top-quintile, now extended at a 52-week high), **+ earnings-revision**, **− quality** (ROIC 0.28%, F-score 4/9, negative GAAP EPS), **− value** (27x book, 3.3x sales), **+ low-beta-but-high-total-vol** (an unusual and slightly contradictory combination). **Liquidity:** $15.4B cap, ~1.5M ADV, tight spreads. A 1.5–3% position in a typical mid/large portfolio is trivially exitable; no slippage constraint on the stop.
