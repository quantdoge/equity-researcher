### Investment Memo: T (AT&T Inc.) — October 22, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (starter position only) | Conviction: Low
**Price Target**: $26.00 (+18.7% from current $21.91; ~+24% total return incl. 5.1% dividend yield)
**Time Horizon**: 12–18 months

**Investment Thesis**

AT&T is a verified cash machine trading at 7.2x earnings, 8.1x P/FCF and a 12.4% FCF yield — a 25–40% discount to Verizon and T-Mobile on every cash-flow multiple — with a credible FCF ladder ($18B→$19B→$21B, 2026–28) funded by the capex rollover and a working fiber-convergence engine. The discount exists for real reasons: ~2% structural growth, $174B of debt with the weakest interest coverage in the peer group, headline EPS flattered by disposal gains, and an unquantified lead-cable liability. We initiate small: the 5% yield pays us to wait, but broken momentum, downward estimate revisions, and a dividend yield now at parity with the 10Y mean we buy weakness and confirmation, not strength.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector:** Mature 3-player oligopoly (~$336–357B market, ~3.8% CAGR); industry revenue growth sub-2% but capex intensity rolling over (22.9% of revenue vs 26.9% in 2022) drives a sector-wide FCF inflection. T leads carriers on EBITDA margin (43.5%) but has the worst 3-yr revenue CAGR (−1.6%). Macro is a ceiling, not a threat: 10Y at ~5% with bear-steepening; T's 5.1% yield now at parity with the 10Y — the historic yield floor is gone. CPI 3.7% favors T's pricing power; 2.2% GDP / 4.2% unemployment supports subscription demand. Term-premium shock (20% odds) = −12% to −16% on yield repricing alone.
- **Geopolitical & Legal:** Geopolitical exposure LOW and improving — ~97% US revenue; Mexico sale ($2B) closes Q1'27. Domestic legal stack heavy: lead-clad cable liability (no reserve; third-party estimates $2B–$60B industry-wide) is the key unquantified overhang; $177M breach settlement + $13M FCC settlement now largely cap privacy exposure; FCC posture currently favorable but partisan/oscillating; Lumen fiber deal (if signed) faces antitrust review. Rating: MEDIUM.

Structural case: ~6% pre-tax ROIC ≈ cost of capital; debt-funded buybacks at 5-yr highs (76% of FCF). Yet an outright short pays ~5% carry against a 13% FCF yield — unattractive.

**Bull Case**
- **Verified cash at a discount:** 12.4% FCF yield, dividend covered 2.4x, Beneish/accruals confirm the cash is real; 8.1x P/FCF vs 9.4x (VZ) and 11.3x (TMUS).
- **FCF inflection + convergence:** capex falling ~$1B/yr through 2028 while fiber attach climbs past 41%; buybacks on a 7x multiple are a powerful EPS lever; Q4'26 print (late Jan) could confirm $18B+ FCF and 2027 guide.
- **Compression already done:** −22% from highs with Starlink fears priced after two gap-downs; median target $26.50; any 10Y break below 4.50% re-rates the whole complex — rate sensitivity cuts both ways.

**Bear Case**
- **Structural value trap:** ~2% growth, ~6% ROIC at/below WACC, $20.8B annual capex treadmill; cheap for years, and CMCSA at 4.1x shows the floor is lower.
- **Earnings quality:** FY25 "profit doubling" was a disposal gain; Q3'26 operating margin compressed to 12.7%; the Street's own model has EPS declining through 2028.
- **Uncapped tails:** lead-cable liability unquantified and unreserved; Fitch Negative Watch on $174B of debt; yield-parity with the 10Y removes the valuation floor (shock scenario −12% to −16%).

**Key Risks**
1. **Lead-cable remediation (binary):** any disclosed reserve >$2B or state AG/EPA action re-rates the equity — the one risk that takes the position to zero.
2. **Credit:** downgrade to BBB−/Baa3 or adverse Fitch Watch resolution; refinancing $174B at wider spreads mechanically compresses coverage toward ~3.6x.
3. **Dividend/FCF:** guidance below $18B (2026)/$19B (2027) or cover <1.5x; a dividend cut is an automatic exit.
4. **Convergence stall:** attach stuck at ~41% or internet net adds <400k for two quarters breaks the growth leg.
5. **Rates/competition:** 10Y through 5.50%; Starlink direct-to-cell and cable MVNOs (taking 50%+ of industry adds) compress growth and multiple.
6. **Data integrity (flagged):** reported $6.58B buyback vs only 0.4% share-count decline needs 10-K reconciliation; EBITDA does not reconcile to EBIT+D&A.
