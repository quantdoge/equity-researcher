## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **LOW**

GRMN is one of the cleanest balance sheets in technology hardware: net cash of ~$2.5B, no interest expense, current ratio 3.6x, Altman Z 5.3, F-Score 7, and a 4-year track record of rising FCF ($0.54B → $1.36B). Dividends + buybacks (~$0.9B in FY25) are fully covered by FCF, and the dividend is growing (2025 payout ≈ 40% of net income). The dominant risks are **market/valuation risks, not credit risks**: a 2.5% FCF yield means the equity price already discounts strong growth, and 35%+ volatility means meaningful drawdown exposure — but financial distress is not a credible scenario.
