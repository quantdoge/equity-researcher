# GARMIN LTD. (GRMN) — Value Investment Analysis
**Price: $283.54 | Market Cap: ~$54.9B | FY2025 (ended Dec 27, 2025)**

---

## 1. Business Quality Assessment

**Moat Rating: WIDE (segmented)**

Garmin is not one business but five focused franchises (Fitness, Outdoor, Aviation, Marine, Auto), and three of them hold genuinely durable advantages:

- **Aviation (Wide moat):** Dominant supplier of certified avionics for general aviation. FAA/EASA certification cycles create a decade-long, regulation-buttressed switching cost — pilots and OEMs do not casually swap flight decks. This is an installed-base business with a sticky aftermarket and a competitive oligopoly. FY25 aviation revenue grew ~16%.
- **Fitness/Outdoor (Narrow-to-Wide):** The premium wearable franchise. Garmin has carved out the "serious athlete" niche above the Apple Watch, with a 40-year GPS/engineering heritage, ~500 SKUs, and a data/ecosystem advantage (Garmin Connect, Connect IQ). Pricing power is demonstrated by 58.7% gross margins and a mid-30% price-point premium to mass-market wearables. Still, this segment competes with Apple, Google/Samsung, Huawei, Coros, and Whoop — the moat is brand + ecosystem, not structural exclusivity. Fitness grew +42% in FY25; it is the engine of the current boom.
- **Marine (Wide-ish):** Integrated chartplotter/sonar/radar ecosystem with high switching costs once boats are wired. FY26 growth ~14%.
- **Auto (Narrow):** Declining legacy PNDs partially offset by OEM infotainment; least attractive segment.

**Moat trajectory:** Widening over the past 3–5 years. The wearable flywheel (device → ecosystem → subscription/replacement cycle) and aviation installed base have compounded; the company has gone from ~$4.2B revenue (2020) to $7.2B (2025). ROIC has held 15–20%+ throughout — the hallmark of a moated business.

## 5. Investment Verdict: **HOLD**

This is a superb business — wide moats, 20% ROIC, net cash, compounding dividends — but at $283 it is priced for perfection. The value discipline is: *a fair price is not a buy.* The market has re-rated GRMN from 15x (Jan 2024) to 33x trailing earnings in under three years; the easy money from multiple expansion has been made.

- **Buy zone:** Below ~$230 (≈ 21x forward earnings / ~25% discount to mid-intrinsic) — a pullback from wearable-cycle digestion or tariff noise would create the required margin of safety.
- **Do not chase** at $283 despite bullish analyst targets (street range $289–370); these embed the continuation of super-normal fitness-wearable growth.

---

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Valuation/multiple compression:** 33x trailing / 27x forward embeds high expectations; any growth normalization (Q2'26 revenue already decelerating to +11%) could trigger a de-rating toward the mid-20s multiple.
2. **Wearable competition:** Apple's Watch Ultra, Coros, Suunto, and Chinese entrants attack Garmin's premium niche; fitness (+42% in FY25) is the profit engine and the most contested segment.
3. **Consumer cyclicality:** Fitness-device demand is discretionary and was super-charged by the post-COVID health boom; a demand air pocket (as seen in FY22: −2.5% revenue) would hit FE and AO margins hardest.
4. **Tariff/geopolitical exposure:** Manufacturing concentrated in Taiwan; April 2025 tariff shocks knocked the stock >20% in days, and October 2025 saw a similar air pocket. Component and freight cost swings pressure the ~59% gross margin.
5. **Aviation regulatory dependency:** Certification-driven cadence is a moat, but any slowdown in FAA approvals or OEM production (Boeing/Airbus/DAHER/Cirrus schedules) delays the high-margin aviation backlog.
6. **FX:** Substantial EMEA/APAC revenue reported in USD; a stronger dollar is a headwind, and a Swiss-domiciled structure adds tax/regulatory complexity.

---

**Bottom line:** A world-class franchise trading at a full-to-rich price. Patient owners should hold what they own, harvest the 1.5% growing dividend, and wait for a meaningful pullback to accumulate. New capital is better deployed elsewhere until GRMN offers a margin of safety.
