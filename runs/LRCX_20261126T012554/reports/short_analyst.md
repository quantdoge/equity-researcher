# LRCX — Forensic Short-Seller Analysis

**Lam Research Corp. | Semiconductors / Semi-Cap Equipment | Price $185.01 | Mkt cap $231.4B | Net cash $3.70B**

**Context that must frame everything below:** LRCX peaked at **$438.03 on 30 June 2026** and trades at $185.01 today — **−57.8%**. It fell 33.7% between Oct 6 and Nov 4, 2026 alone, including a −16.4% gap on Nov 5 (31.5M shares). This is not a name where the market is complacent. Any short thesis must clear the bar of "what is still not in the price."

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment

**Rating: MEDIUM (deteriorated in FY26, but the deterioration has already reversed)**

The FY26 annual data looks genuinely alarming on first pass: accruals ratio **+5.98%** (warning band), cash conversion **80.6%** (vs. 115% in FY25), earnings quality composite **36/100**, and a Beneish M-Score in the "likely manipulator" zone. That combination is normally a red-alert cluster.

**But the quarterly sequence dismantles most of it.** The entire annual cash-conversion shortfall is concentrated in two quarters and has already mean-reverted:

| Quarter | Net income | Operating CF | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 FY26 (Sep-25) | $1,569M | $1,779M | 113% |
| Q2 FY26 (Dec-25) | $1,594M | $1,480M | 93% |
| Q3 FY26 (Mar-26) | $1,826M | $1,141M | **63%** |
| Q4 FY26 (Jun-26) | $2,277M | $1,457M | **64%** |
| **Q1 FY27 (Sep-26)** | **$2,753M** | **$3,498M** | **127%** |

Last quarter LRCX converted 127% of net income to operating cash and *released* $535M of working capital. TTM cash conversion is **89.7%** and TTM accruals are **3.6%**, not 6.0%. TTM FCF is **$6.54B** (77% of net income), on a business with 40% operating margins.

This is the single most important finding of my work: **the FY26 "low-quality earnings" signal was a fiscal-year-end working-capital timing artifact, not a manipulation.**

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

**Red flag 1 — Receivables vs. revenue (real, but mostly timing).** FY26 revenue +26.0% vs. receivables +58.1% — a textbook channel-stuffing signature, and it flagged in two consecutive years. However, days sales outstanding tells the true story: 59.4 (Q4 FY25) → 62.1 → 59.5 → 64.4 → **72.3 (Q4 FY26)** → **62.6 (Q1 FY27)**. DSO spiked at the June year-end and reverted within one quarter. The red flag is authentic but *non-persistent*; a genuine channel-stuffing pattern does not unwind this cleanly while revenue accelerates 54%.

**Red flag 2 — Shareholder returns exceeded free cash flow.** FY26 buybacks $3.85B + dividends $1.27B = **$5.12B vs. FCF of $4.89B (104.7%)**. Cash fell from $6.39B to $5.58B partly to fund it. LRCX repurchased heavily at what turned out to be peak prices — the stock averaged far above $185 through FY26 (June 2026 alone traded $370–$438). Value-destructive capital allocation at cycle peak. Real, and it is the one thing management got demonstrably wrong.

**Red flag 3 — Insider selling into strength.** CEO Timothy Archer sold 30,000 shares at **$300** (≈$9.6M) around 10 Sept 2026, retaining only ~61,400 options; further CEO sale filed 23 Oct 2026. CLO Harter sold at $222.66 in March. 25 Form 4s in six months. **Mitigant: these are Rule 10b5-1 pre-scheduled plans, not discretionary dumps** — low signal by construction. Direction of travel is negative, but it is not the "insiders fleeing" tell.

**Red flag 4 — China concentration.** China was **35.1% of Q1 FY27 revenue**, down from 42–43%, and management guides it below 30% as export restrictions bite. Roughly a third of the revenue base is subject to unilateral policy risk that management does not control. This is the highest-variance item in the model.

**Not a red flag — inventory days are falling:** 196.1 (FY24) → 166.3 (FY25) → **135.6 (FY26)**. Falling inventory days during accelerating revenue is a demand-strength signal, not a demand-weakness one. I looked for the classic pre-downturn inventory build and it is not there.

## 3. Beneish M-Score and Accruals Analysis

**M-Score: −1.683** ("HIGH RISK — likely manipulator," threshold −1.78). This is a **false positive, and I can show why from the component decomposition:**

| Index | Value | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| DSRI | 1.254 | ⚠️ Receivables outrunning sales — the year-end timing item above |
| SGI | 1.260 | ⚠️ Pure sales-growth artifact — mechanically penalises fast growers |
| **GMI** | **0.965** | ✅ **< 1.0 = gross margin IMPROVED** (44.6%→50.5% since FY23) |
| AQI | 1.023 | Neutral |
| DEPI | 1.056 | Neutral |
| SGAI | 0.929 | ✅ Overhead growing slower than sales |
| **LVGI** | **0.874** | ✅ **Leverage FALLING** |
| TATA | 0.0598 | ⚠️ Accruals — but 3.6% on a TTM basis |

Three of the eight indices (GMI, SGAI, LVGI) point *away* from manipulation. The score is triggered by the two indices that are structurally upward-biased for a hyper-growth company (SGI, DSRI). Beneish himself warned the model degrades on high-growth firms. **I assign this low evidentiary weight.** Corroborating checks: Piotroski **F-Score 8/9** (the sole miss is F4, accruals quality — the same artifact), Altman Z **5.40 (Safe)**, and revenue accelerating (+22% → +24% → +30% → **+54%** YoY across the last four quarters). Manipulators do not usually post improving gross margins, deleveraging, and accelerating cash collection simultaneously.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

**Moat: INTACT and arguably widening near-term; eroding only at the long tail.**

*Bull-side evidence (which I must credit):* Gross margin expanded every year — 44.6% (FY23) → 47.3% → 48.7% → **50.5%** (FY26) → **52.3%** (Q1 FY27). Operating margin hit **40.0%**. Foundry reached **46% of systems sales**, a record, diversifying away from memory. Pricing power is *improving*, which is the opposite of commoditisation.

*Genuine threats:*
- **Customer concentration:** the buy-side of this market is 4–6 firms (Samsung, SK hynix, Micron, TSMC). One memory capex pause re-rates the whole thesis. HBM/AI memory is the marginal driver of the current cycle.
- **China tool self-sufficiency:** NAURA and AMEC are scaling in etch/deposition specifically — LRCX's core. This is a slow-burn share loss compounded by export controls shrinking the addressable market. Management's own sub-30% China guide quantifies the bleed.
- **Three-player oligopoly discipline:** AMAT and Tokyo Electron are rational, but share shifts in deposition/etch are lumpy and customer-specific.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary

**The forensic short thesis fails. The cyclical short thesis is late.**

The bear case that *was* compelling at $438 — peak margins, peak multiple, peak memory capex, 35% China concentration, buybacks at the top — has largely been paid out. The stock is down 58%. Meanwhile, the fundamentals are still accelerating: Q1 FY27 revenue +54.4% to $8.22B, and **Q2 FY27 guidance is $8.60B ±$400M, implying roughly +61% YoY** — that is *acceleration*, not the deceleration that typically precedes a semi-cap downturn. Operating margin is 40%. Net cash is $3.7B. TTM FCF is $6.5B. Valuation is **27.6x TTM**, but **~19x FY27E ($9.66)** and **~15x FY28E ($12.12)**. Shorted at $185, you are leaning against accelerating guidance, expanding margins, falling leverage and a fortress balance sheet.

What would still make a credible short? A genuine **memory capex rollover** — that is the real variable, and it is not yet visible in inventory days, DSO, or guidance. If DRAM/HBM orders stall, peak EPS of ~$9.66 gets cut toward $6 and 15x trough EPS puts a floor near **$90–105** (another 45% down). That is a *macro/timing* bet on the silicon cycle, not a forensic one — and it is now the consensus trade after a 58% drawdown, with VIX at 28 and semis broadly de-rated. Crowded, late, and with unbounded squeeze risk (beta 2.01) against a company still guiding +61%.

**Explicitly, the bull case is stronger than my bear case.** The honest read: this is a well-run, cash-generative, no-fraud oligopolist that got absurdly overvalued and has now partially corrected. The best short entry was $400+, not $185.

## 7. Contrarian Rating

# NO EDGE — Reject Short Thesis

*Forensic integrity: no manipulation case exists. Beneish is a growth-artifact false positive; accruals and cash conversion deteriorated for exactly one year-end and reversed to 127% the following quarter.*

*A cyclical short has a coherent logic but no forensic edge at $185 after −58%, against +61% guided growth and 40% margins.*

**What I would monitor to re-engage short:** (1) two consecutive quarters of *falling* DSO-inclusive backlog/guidance below the prior quarter; (2) inventory days turning back up above 160 while revenue decelerates; (3) memory customer capex cuts announced; (4) cash conversion below 80% for two consecutive quarters *without* a working-capital explanation. None of those are true today.

**Options note:** if an investor insists on expressing a bear view, a defined-risk structure (put spreads below $150) dominates a naked short, given beta 2.01 and the post-crash reflexivity.

**Confidence: Medium-High on rejecting the forensic short; Low on directional timing.**
