**Investment Thesis**

**Bull Case**
- **Secular intensity step-up, not just a cycle**: HBM stacking, GAA, 3D NAND/DRAM and hybrid bonding raise etch/deposition dollars per wafer; WFE >$200B in 2027 with DRAM leading — Lam's highest-torque exposure, confirmed by share gains.
- **Peak earnings may be a plateau**: at 15.3x FY28E with a $25B buyback and 40% margins, even modest multiple normalization toward 20x forward yields $240+; macro bull tail (10Y back below 4.75%) re-rates toward $280–300.
- **The drawdown has done the work**: −57% with *accelerating* fundamentals, capitulated retail attention, and a management-authorized floor is the cleanest dislocation profile in large-cap semis.

**Bear Case**
- **Policy double-hit**: Affiliates Rule snaps back Jan 10 + MATCH extends to spares/service → China 35%→15%, EPS −20–25%, multiple to ~18x → **$130–150**.
- **2027 is the top**: WFE reverts to ~$150B in 2028 → revenue ~−25%, EPS ~$8, ~$120 (−35%); full memory rollover puts trough floor at **$90–105**.
- **Macro keeps tightening**: a second hike in March with 10Y at 5.75% compresses all long-duration semis; LRCX's 2–3.3 beta makes it the worst place to hide.

**Key Risks**
1. China export-control escalation (Jan 10, 2027 and NDAA language) — binary, unhedgeable, 35% of revenue.
2. Memory customer capex rollover (SK hynix/Micron/Samsung = ~46% of systems revenue).
3. Multiple compression resuming if 10Y breaks 5.40% — watch as bear-confirmation level.
4. Execution tells: guidance decelerating sequentially on Jan 27, or DSO back above 72 days.
5. Data gaps carried: exact S&P notch, debt maturity ladder, DEF 14A/10-K legal text unverified; peer multiples dated; sell-side PTs stale.

**Panel disagreement flagged**: Value (don't initiate below $160) vs Growth (buy at 15x FY28) is an unresolvable bet on whether 2027 WFE is plateau or peak. That disagreement is precisely why this is a **half-size starter, not a conviction position**.
