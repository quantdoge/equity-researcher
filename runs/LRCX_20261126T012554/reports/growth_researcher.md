## LRCX — Lam Research Corporation | Growth Analysis

**Price $185.01 (Nov 25, 2026 close) · Market cap $233B · Beta 2.01 · Down ~40% from ~$310 peak (Oct 12, 2026)**

---

### 1. Growth Story

Lam is the purest-scale play on the *intensity* of materials-engineering — etch and deposition — per wafer, and AI is now the mechanism raising that intensity: HBM4/DRAM stacking, 300+ layer NAND, 4F²→3D DRAM, gate-all-around and hybrid bonding all consume more Lam tools per wafer. Management raised CY2026 WFE to the **mid-$150B range** (from $135–140B) and guided **preliminary CY2027 WFE above $200B, with DRAM leading**, and just printed the first-ever 40% operating margin. The stock has de-rated ~40% on AI-capex digestion fears with **no company-specific setback** — a multiple compression against rising estimates.

### 3. TAM & Penetration

- WFE TAM: **~$150B CY2026 → >$200B CY2027 → likely $250B+ by 2030.** Lam's served SAM (etch + deposition + single-wafer clean) is roughly a third of WFE, call it **$50–65B**, plus a growing **advanced-packaging (TSV/hybrid bonding)** SAM and a ~$6B installed-base services annuity.
- **Memory is 39% of systems revenue in FY26 and guided to surge in FY27** — the highest-torque exposure to the DRAM/HBM capex wave of any large-cap semicap.
- Optionality: **Aether** platform targeted at **$2.5B revenue by decade-end**; four new TSV/bonding positions won across multiple customers in advanced logic.
- Competitive moat: co-development lock-in with the three leading memory makers, ~50%+ share in NAND string-stack etch, and a service installed base that stabilizes through cycles.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Jan 27, 2027 — Q2 FY27 print:** guide $8.60B / $2.33 EPS / 52.5% GM. Watch the first formal CY2027 WFE number; a raise above $200B re-rates the group.
- **$25B buyback (announced Nov 5–6, ~10% of market cap)** — direct EPS accretion and a management-implied floor; dividend now $0.33/qtr (0.7% yield).
- **DRAM/HBM4 capacity orders** (SK hynix, Micron, Samsung 2027 capex) and 4F²/3D-DRAM architecture decisions.
- **Advanced packaging awards** (hybrid bonding, TSV) — Lam's fastest-growing SAM extension.
- **2027 NAND upgrade cycle** as layer counts cross 300+ (lagging leg of the cycle, still ahead).

### 7. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (accumulate; Strong Buy trigger = CY2027 WFE raised above $200B)

Highest-quality growth franchise in semicap at ~15x FY28 EPS, 100% beat rate, +19% revisions, and a $25B buyback into a news-free drawdown. I stop short of Strong Buy on one reason: the cycle. **Key risks:** (1) **2027 as the WFE blow-off top** — if WFE peaks at $200B and falls to $150B in 2028, revenue could slide ~25% to $30B with EPS near $8, implying ~$120 (−35%); (2) memory-price inflation destroying end demand (Qualcomm already warned) and Goldman/Citi flagging memory weakness; (3) customer concentration and China/export-control tail risk; (4) NAND (the lagging leg) failing to materialize; (5) beta 2.01 — expect 40% drawdowns as a feature, not a bug; size accordingly.
