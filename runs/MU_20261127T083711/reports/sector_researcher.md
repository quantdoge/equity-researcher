## SECTOR BRIEF — Micron Technology (MU) | Semiconductors: Memory (DRAM/NAND/HBM)

**Data as of 25–26 Nov 2026. Price $601.25, market cap $680.3bn.**

### 1. Sector Overview
The AI build-out has converted memory from the historic "boom-bust commodity" into the tightest supply/demand squeeze in the industry's history. TrendForce puts global DRAM revenue growth at +59.5% q/q in 2Q26, with HBM consuming ~23% of DRAM wafer capacity in 2026 rising toward ~30% in 2027, cannibalising conventional DRAM supply. The market remains a tight oligopoly — Samsung (39.4% DRAM revenue share in 2Q26), SK hynix and Micron account for the overwhelming majority of supply, with CXMT (conventional DRAM revenue +99.3% q/q in 2Q26) and Huawei (HBM) as the only credible new entrants. Micron's FY26 (Sep-26) results show the extreme: revenue $133.2bn (+256% y/y), gross margin 80.7%, operating margin 74.6% — margins now above NVIDIA's. The sector is **late-cycle boom**: absolute demand still exceeds supply (2027 DRAM/HBM capacity is reportedly fully booked/sold), but the rate of change in pricing has begun to decelerate.

### 2. Key Tailwinds
- **Sold-out forward book:** Memory capacity for all of 2027 is reportedly booked, and SK hynix has said it is sold out of DRAM and next-gen HBM through 2029. HPE expects memory scarcity into at least H2 2028. This gives unprecedented revenue visibility for a memory maker.
- **HBM mix shift + pricing:** HBM blended ASPs are forecast +121% y/y in 2027 (KB Securities), and 2027 DRAM contract prices +40%. Because HBM takes ~3x the wafer area per bit of commodity DRAM, each HBM ramp tightens conventional DRAM too.
- **AI-driven demand inelasticity:** Hyperscaler capex is price-insensitive relative to memory's share of system BOM; AI reportedly absorbs ~70% of DRAM supply in 2026, structurally raising the sector's earnings floor versus prior cycles.

### 3. Key Headwinds
- **Pricing inflection risk in 2027–28:** Jeff Pu has cut every quarter of his 2027 DRAM price forecast (+6%, +3%, 0%, 0%) and his first-ever 2028 base case has DRAM prices **halving**; BNP Paribas downgraded Micron on expectations ASPs decline from ~mid-2027. NAND is already soft (TrendForce sees NAND prices −20%).
- **Capacity response:** DRAM capacity is forecast +50% between 2026 and 2028; SK hynix plans 100-layer 3D DRAM for 2030–31. Every past memory peak has been created by the industry's own capex.
- **China supply + policy risk:** CXMT scaling conventional DRAM and Huawei developing domestic HBM is a structural threat to the tail of the market; export controls and China revenue exposure add political volatility.

**Read-across:** Micron is the fastest-growing name in large-cap tech and now the most profitable on a margin basis, yet trades at ~8x trailing earnings vs ~30x+ for logic/foundry. The market is explicitly discounting mean reversion of peak memory earnings — and MU has already fallen ~50% from its June 2026 peak of $1,211 (still ~5x its Sep-2025 level of $118).

### 5. Micron's Competitive Position: **Strong (cyclically) / Average (structurally)**
- *Positives:* Third-largest DRAM supplier and the fastest share gainer (+3.7pp in 3Q25; conventional DRAM revenue +65.5% q/q in 2Q26); HBM qualification with NVIDIA and US-based supply chain are strategic assets under export-control regimes; highest margins in the sector today; cheapest multiple in semis.
- *Negatives:* Sub-scale vs Samsung and SK hynix in absolute capacity, no EUV-based DRAM leadership guarantee, NAND exposure (~facing 20% price declines) is a drag, and its margin lead is a function of price, not structural cost advantage — it is the most operating-levered name into any ASP downturn.

### 6. Sector Rating: **NEUTRAL (peak-cycle)**
Earnings momentum is exceptional and 2027 is largely contracted, which argues for *Attractive*. But three things cap the rating: (i) gross margins at 87% are unsustainable against a 2028 price-halving base case; (ii) industry capex is rising 50% through 2028, historically the reliable trigger of the next down-cycle; (iii) the sector's own equity market has already de-rated memory to ~8x while paying ~30x for AI logic — meaning the reward for being right on near-term earnings is small relative to the risk of being wrong on the cycle turn. **Tactically attractive into 2027 contract resets; structurally a late-cycle sector.**
