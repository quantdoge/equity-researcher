# Micron Technology (MU) — Alternative Data Signal Report
**As of:** November 25–26, 2026 | **Price:** $601.25 | **Market cap:** $680B | **Beta:** 2.37
**Context:** MU is **-50.4% from its June 22, 2026 peak ($1,211)**, including -14% on Nov 20 and -33% over the past month. This is the single most important fact for interpreting every signal below.

---

## 1. Consumer Demand / Attention Signals — **Deteriorating**

**Google Trends, "Micron" (US, 12-m):** Peak = 100 (week of Jun 21, 2026 — exactly the price top). Current = 24; prior four-week avg ~21.7 vs 18.3 a month earlier.
- Retail search interest is **-76% from peak**, but ticked **+19% mom off the September low** — consistent with capitulation/CNBC-driven attention on the crash, not new demand.
- Classic blow-off-top signature: attention and price peaked together in June, both have since decayed.

**Google Trends, "DRAM prices" (US, 12-m):** Peak = 100 (week of Jun 28), collapsed to **2–6 today** (~-97% from peak; four-week avg 3.5 vs ~17 in August).
- This is the more damning signal. The *supply-scarcity narrative itself* has lost its audience. When a cyclical's core narrative stops being searched, it usually means the marginal buyer has already been converted and the topic is stale — often coincident with, or just before, the pricing peak.

**Web traffic (micron.com):** ⚠️ **Data gap** — SimilarWeb returned only comparative page stubs, no monthly visit time series. No usable traffic trend. (Note: micron.com is a B2B product/spec site; traffic is a poor demand proxy for this name regardless.)

**Read:** For a component supplier, consumer "demand signals" mostly measure *investor attention*, not memory bit demand. On that measure, the signal is unambiguously bearish-to-neutral.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **Tracking ABOVE near-term estimates — but decelerating**

**Next print: FQ1-27, Dec 23, 2026.** Consensus EPS **$38.25**, revenue **$61.75B** vs company guidance of **$61.5B ± $1.5B** — i.e. consensus sits *above guidance midpoint*, which is unusual and means the bar is already elevated on numbers even though the multiple has collapsed.

The 100% beat rate is now a *lagging* comfort — the stock fell 33% in a month *through* record results, proving beats no longer move it.
- Sequential EPS implied growth: $33.42 → $38.25 = **+14.5% q/q** required, versus +33% last quarter. Deceleration is already in consensus.
- I would expect a *modest* beat (low-to-mid single digits) on the scarcity backdrop, but guidance conservatism is Micron's standard practice and a beat is unlikely to be rewarded durably.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH** (with a near-term beat-and-fade risk skew)

**The reasoning, in order of signal quality:**

1. **Leading (bearish, decisive):** "DRAM prices" search interest -97% from peak; distributor/partner expectations of early 2027 price peak; Citi peak call; tariffs; competitor capacity (SK Hynix next-gen DRAM, two Chinese entrants). Attention *and* pricing narrative have both rolled over.
2. **Cross-check (bearish):** The de-rating is **sector-wide, not idiosyncratic** — SK Hynix (SKHY) is **-43% from its Sept 22 peak ($195 → $111)**, with both names down ~13-14% on Nov 20. Paradoxically, the market is repricing the entire memory cycle, which means no company-specific alt signal can offset it. MU has de-rated *harder* (-50%) and *earlier* (peaked June vs SK Hynix September) than its closest peer.
3. **Coincident (bullish, but lagging):** Record revenue, 100% beat rate, HPE scarcity into 2028, HBM pricing not yet caught up, monthly contract repricing in sellers' favour. These describe the quarter that just happened and the one about to be reported — they are the *last* legs of a cycle, not the first.
4. **Sentiment (contrarian-neutral):** News positive on valuation, social polarising on "peak," retail search interest exhausted. No capitulation volume signature yet.

**Framing for the desk:** I would not short into the Dec 23 print — a scarcity-driven modest beat plus depressed positioning is a genuine squeeze setup, and Tepper's 13F adds are a real institutional tell. But as a *leading indicator*, this alt-data set says the memory pricing cycle is transitioning from shortage-driven expansion to a peak/decay phase, and the 50% drawdown is a rational discount of that, not a mispricing. **Near-term print: at/above. Forward 2–4 quarters: Below.**

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM** (not High)

**Why not High:** Two of four core business-activity feeds failed or produced no series (patent API error; job postings, social, and web traffic were all search proxies with **zero quantitative time history**). Google Trends measures investor attention, not bit demand — for a B2B memory supplier it is a *sentiment* input, not a demand input. News sentiment is a keyword counter that systematically underweights the tariff/pricing-peak negatives. There is also no direct proprietary data here on DRAM spot/contract pricing, inventory weeks, or hyperscaler HBM order books — the variables that actually drive MU.

**What would change the call:** (a) DRAM/HBM contract price data showing Q1-27 up double-digits q/q → upgrade to Neutral/Bullish; (b) a fresh leg down in "DRAM prices" search *and* a Micron guidance reset on Dec 23 → high-conviction Bearish; (c) 13F/Option data showing institutional accumulation continuing through the drawdown (Tepper is one data point, not a trend).

**Lag warning:** All alt data here lags 1–4 weeks. Google Trends is 1–2 weeks stale; FMP news up to 24h; SK Hynix/MU price data through Nov 25 close. Earnings event Dec 23 is ~4 weeks out — positioning, not fundamentals, will likely dominate until then.
