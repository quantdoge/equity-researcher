## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: SUB-INVESTMENT GRADE (speculative / high-yield profile)** — not formally rated; no public rating or CDS spread available.

ASTS is a pre-profit, capital-intensive satellite buildout with **negative EBITDA, negative gross profit on a TTM basis, and negative interest coverage**. It is funded almost entirely by equity and convertible issuance rather than operating cash flow. The credit is *not* near-term distressed — $2.80B of cash against $8.8M of short-term debt and no maturities before 2032 — but the structure is structurally dependent on continuous capital-markets access. That is a financing-risk profile, not an operating one.

The mitigating features are genuinely strong: **no financial maintenance covenants** (convertible notes are covenant-light), **no refinancing cliff** (tranches due 2032 / 2034 / 2036), and $2.8B of liquidity. The aggravating features are equally real: **$4.12B of gross debt**, TTM FCF of **–$1.80B**, and an equity currency that has fallen 80% in six months, which is precisely when they next need to raise.

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis

### Scenario A — Revenue –20% (base downside)
TTM revenue $142.9M → $114.3M (–$28.6M). At the Q3-26 incremental gross margin of ~40%, gross profit falls ~$11.4M. Opex (~$680M TTM) is fixed — no plausible cost take-out in a satellite buildout. Result: TTM EBITDA –$655M → **–$667M**, TTM FCF –$1.80B → **–$1.81B**. **Runway stays ~1.5 years.**

**Key conclusion: a 20% revenue decline is close to immaterial to solvency.** Revenue is not the binding constraint — capex and capital access are. The equity is a $7.8B claim on a company whose revenue is $143M; the downside sensitivity is to the *financing tape*, not the P&L.

### Scenario B — Funding gap (the real risk)
If capex holds at ~$1.55B/yr through 2027–28 and revenue growth stalls, cumulative funding need is ~$3.6B against $2.8B of cash — a **$0.8–1.0B gap**, and management will want a buffer well above that. Call it **$1.5–2.0B to raise in 2027–2028.**

At $26.02, raising $2.0B in equity means **~77M new shares, +26% dilution** on top of an already 2.5x-inflated share count (54.4M in 2022 → 300.1M in Q3-26). Converts are the alternative, but at 102% vol and –80% drawdown, terms would be far worse than the 1.625% / 2.25% coupons they got in 2026. The yield curve does not help: **2Y 4.74%, 5Y 4.93%, 10Y 5.13%, 30Y 5.47%, 2s10s +39bp (not inverted)** — long rates are elevated, so any straight or hybrid debt refinancing prices at 5%+, which on a negative-EBITDA credit means secured/structured terms or nothing.

### Scenario C — Conversion shortfall
$4.1B of mostly convertible debt against a stock 80% below its peak. If the shares never recover to conversion prices, these become **cash repayment obligations in 2032/2034/2036** rather than equity. That converts a currently "equity-like" liability into a genuine $4.1B cash claim — the single largest structural risk in the capital structure. Interest expense, already $83M TTM and ~$121M annualised, would compound the problem if refinanced at market rates.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Not Critical — there is no 2026/2027 maturity, no covenant, and $2.8B of cash. But **High** is warranted on four independent grounds: (1) negative EBITDA and –$1.8B annual FCF; (2) ~1.5-year runway requiring a $1.5–2B raise in a hostile equity tape; (3) debt/equity at 1.90x and rising with interest coverage at –8.8x; (4) 102% volatility, 2.6x beta, and an 82% drawdown that has impaired the equity currency precisely when it is needed.

**What would change the rating:** Upgrade to Moderate on evidence of positive EBITDA, capex normalisation below ~$1B/yr, or a completed equity raise that extends runway beyond 2029. Downgrade to Critical on a failed/withdrawn capital raise, a covenant introduced on new debt, or cash falling below ~$1.2B.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **–$1.80B TTM free cash flow vs $2.80B cash — ~1.5-year runway.** The most important number in this report. They must raise capital in 2027–2028.
2. **Debt doubled in nine months**, $2.24B → $4.12B, via the Feb-2026 $1.0B 2.25% notes (due 2036) and Jul-2026 $1.15B 1.625% notes (due 2034). Net debt swung from –$97M to +$1.32B.
3. **Negative interest coverage (–8.8x) and rising interest cost** ($83M TTM → ~$121M annualised). Coverage cannot turn positive until EBITDA does, which needs revenue in the $700M–1B range — 5–7x current TTM.
4. **Extreme dilution:** 54.4M shares (2022) → 154.5M (2024) → 256.0M (2025) → 300.1M (Q3-26). **+453% in four years.** Piotroski F7 (no dilution) scores zero.
5. **Altman Z of 0.61 (FY25) / ~0.16 (Q3-26) in the distress zone** — reported for completeness, but mis-specified for a pre-revenue buildout. Do not over-read it.
6. **Convertible overhang:** $4.1B of equity-linked debt that likely stays out of the money at $26. If the stock doesn't recover, this becomes a $4.1B cash claim in 2032–2036.
7. **Q2-2026 outsized operating charge:** operating loss –$297.6M vs –$149.4M in Q1 and –$168.7M in Q3. Cause not established from the data — needs verification.
8. **Valuation leaves no room:** EV ≈ $9.1B on TTM revenue of $143M → **~64x EV/revenue**, on a company burning $1.8B a year.

**Offsets (genuine):** revenue ramp is real — Q3-26 $42.3M, +187% YoY; TTM revenue +16x vs FY24. Gross margin is improving sequentially (24.9% Q1 → 25.2% Q2 → 40.2% Q3-26). Liquidity ratios are exceptional (current 13.65x, cash ratio 12.42x). No near-term maturities. No covenants.

## 7. Data Gaps & Caveats (flagged explicitly)

- **Market cap discrepancy:** company profile reports **$10.66B**; 300.1M diluted shares × $26.02 = **$7.81B**. The profile figure implies ~410M shares. I have used **$7.81B**; if the profile's figure is correct, FCF yield improves to ~–16.9% and P/S falls to ~40x — but the runway and leverage conclusions are unaffected.
- **Gross profit inconsistency:** FY2025 COGS ($33.0M) is *lower* than Q4-2025 COGS alone ($91.3M) — internally impossible. The TTM gross margin of –5.8% is therefore distorted by the Q4-25 charge and should not be read as a run-rate. The 2026 quarterly trend (40.2% in Q3) is the better guide.
- **No Sortino ratio** was returned by the volatility tool — estimated qualitatively only.
- **No credit rating and no CDS spread** available. Credit assessment is inferred from the financials.
- **No peer benchmarking** performed — ASTS has no listed comparable at this stage; sector peers in Communication Services are not economically analogous.
- **Debt maturity schedule** was inferred from press releases and 10-Q references, not from a full maturity table. Confirm against the latest 10-Q before relying on it.

---

**Bottom line:** ASTS is a **High** financial-risk credit — an adequately liquid but structurally cash-negative capital-intensive buildout whose survival depends on continued access to equity and convertible markets, now at an 80% lower share price than six months ago. The revenue trajectory is improving and the near-term maturity wall is absent, which is why this is not a Critical rating. But the 1.5-year runway, –$1.8B annual burn, –8.8x interest coverage, and 1.90x and rising leverage mean the next capital raise is the single event that determines whether this is a going concern or a restructuring.
