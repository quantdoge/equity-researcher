**Investment Thesis**

ASTS is a technologically genuine but commercially sub-scale challenger in an attractive sector whose economics accrue disproportionately to its better-capitalized, self-launching competitor. The equity is a levered, ~18-year-duration option on 2029–2031 fleet economics that must clear three sequential gates in the next 12 months: a Q4 print it has missed seven quarters running, a 45-satellite launch cadence it does not control, and a $1.5–2B capital raise into a tape down 80%. The optionality is real — but it can be bought later, at the same or lower price, after confirmation. Paying for it today is a negative-expectation trade.

**Bull Case**
1. **Sector + distribution locked**: >30% CAGR market; wholesale revenue-share with AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone and Google means near-zero CAC and software-like incremental margins once the fleet flies.
2. **Valuation asymmetry**: at ~$7.8B cap, the market pays for 2028 and gets 2029–2035 free; cash covers ~30–40% of cap; fresh targets cluster at $43–56 (+65–115%).
3. **Convex catalysts**: confirmed launch cadence + first high-margin service revenue re-rates violently; a macro regime shift (10Y back to 4.4%) is mechanically worth +15–18% on duration alone; short-interest and 2.85 beta amplify any squeeze.

**Bear Case**
1. **Financing reflexivity**: −$1.8B annual burn forces a $1.5–2B raise into a stock down 80% with widening credit spreads — dilution at ~4x the historical cost per dollar, the primary per-share value leak.
2. **Launch dependency**: the binding constraint is outside company control — 45 satellites slipped to mid-2027, New Glenn already lost BlueBird 7, ~13 launches needed in 2027, and the in-house answer (Blacklift) is an unproven rocket flying no earlier than late 2028.
3. **Competition + credibility**: Starlink D2C is commercially live with hundreds of satellites and owned launch economics; Amazon absorbed Globalstar's spectrum. ASTS is 0/7 on EPS, under litigation over capital disclosure, and its insiders sold the top quartile of the 2026 range.

**Key Risks**
- *To the avoid stance*: launch success re-rating; a hyperscaler/strategic stake (Google already an investor); short squeeze; macro term-premium compression. These are why conviction is Medium, not High — and why we would not short this name.
- *To the equity*: Q4'26 print miss (~1 Mar 2027); a dilutive raise; slippage beyond Q3 2027; anchor-MNO loss or dual-sourcing; adverse FCC/Senate action; class-action overhang; convert shortfall turning $4.1B into cash claims in 2032–36.
