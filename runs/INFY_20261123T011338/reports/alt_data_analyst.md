**Verdict:** Revenue likely in line to a hair above (deal flow + consulting jobs support it); **EPS at risk of a third straight penny miss** on margin/pricing. Low-quality "in-line" — asymmetric downside on any guidance reaffirmation commentary.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (with a negative tactical tilt into Q3)**
The signals genuinely conflict, and the conflict is informative:
- **Positive leading indicators:** $5.2B TCV, AI revenue compounding +29% q/q, October US consulting-jobs surge, buyback support, healthy site engagement.
- **Negative coincident/price indicators:** guidance cut to 1–2%, two straight EPS misses, –37% attention decay, institutional selling (Allspring –49%), securities investigation, retail over-bullishness.

Translation: the *demand* story is stabilizing, but the *earnings* story has not yet inflected. Alt data supports the "overlooked AI beneficiary" thesis on a **6–12 month horizon**, but does **not** support an above-consensus January print. Price action corroborates — stock rolled over from $12.93 (Oct 22) to $12.03, –7%, on **declining volume** (7–12M shares vs 20–40M during the Oct re-rating): drift, not accumulation.

**Positioning implication:** Neutral into the Jan 13 print; the asymmetry favors waiting for evidence that TCV converts (sequential revenue +3% or better) before treating INFY as an AI winner rather than an AI victim.
