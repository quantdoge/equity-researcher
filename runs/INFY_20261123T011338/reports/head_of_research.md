**Investment Thesis**
Infosys is the highest-quality operator in a structurally challenged sub-industry: fortress balance sheet (net cash ~$2.8B, Z-score 4.57, 37.8% ROIC, 113% FCF conversion), honest books (Beneish M-score −2.74), and an 8.6% shareholder yield. But agentic AI is deflating the core unit of value — the billable hour — and no agent found evidence Infosys can outgrow it: FY27 guidance cut to 1–2% CC (three cuts in seven months), FY27E EPS *below* FY26, and ~86% of sequential growth coming from AI while the 89.5% non-AI core shrinks. The 40% de-rating from 2021 highs was a correction from a bubble multiple to a fair one, not a bargain: five independent valuation anchors (Value IV $12.15, Growth $11–13.50, Short, Quant, sell-side median ~$11.75) converge at or below spot.

**Bear Case**
- **AI cannibalization**: GenAI deflates price-per-unit-of-work faster than volumes grow — revenue and EPS shrink even as "AI revenue" headlines surge (FY27E EPS already below FY26).
- **Credibility cascade**: third consecutive EPS miss at Q3 FY27 (13-Jan) or FY28 guidance below 1% breaks the "honest operator" floor; −9.6% and −6.5% single-day reactions show the skew.
- **Policy + capital-return squeeze**: DOJ consent decree / H-1B fee reimposition hits onsite economics while the 111%-of-FCF payout forces a buyback cut, removing the only technical support. Bear case: $8.30 (−31%).
