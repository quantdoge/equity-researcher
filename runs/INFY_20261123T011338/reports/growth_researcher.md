# INFY — Infosys Limited | Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $12.03–12.27 (ADR) · Market cap ~$48B · Net cash ~$2.8B · Data through Q2 FY27 (Sept 2026)*

---

## 1. Growth Story

Infosys is a ~$20B-revenue global IT services incumbent that is attempting to re-platform its labor-arbitrage model onto an AI-delivery model (Infosys Topaz / Topaz Fabric). The disruptive tension is real but runs *against* the equity: AI revenue is compounding fast (10.5% of revenue, +29% QoQ, ~$2.2B annualized), yet total revenue grew just **1.5% YoY in constant currency** and management **cut FY27 guidance to 1–2% CC** from 1.5–3.0%. This is the classic innovator's-dilemma setup — the new business is growing, but it is being sold partly *in place of* the old one, not on top of it.

- **5-yr revenue CAGR 7.9%, but 3-yr CAGR only 3.4%** — the deceleration is the story, not the history.
- **Rule of 40 = 23.1 — a clear fail** (needs 40). Growth +4.6% and FCF margin +18.5% describes a cash cow, not a compounder.
- **Margins have structurally eroded**: gross margin −470bps and operating margin −420bps since FY21 (34.9%→30.2%; 24.5%→20.3%). Flat for four years, not recovering.
- Consensus implicitly assumes **re-acceleration** to 5–6% by FY29–30 — an unproven bet on AI revenue becoming additive. FY26–FY30 implied revenue CAGR: **4.0%**; implied EPS CAGR: **2.4%**.
- Earnings quality is high (FCF conversion 113% of net profit), but **Q2 FCF fell 15.7% YoY**.
- **Shareholder yield is the real support**: FY26 dividends $2.13B + buyback $2.01B = **$4.14B (8.6% of market cap)**, though that was **111% of FCF**. Diluted shares fell 3.8% YoY (4.15B→4.00B).
- Track record is deteriorating: **two consecutive EPS misses** (Jul-26 −4.8%, Oct-26 −4.8%) after 5 straight beats; beat rate 71% but momentum has flipped negative.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- Global IT services spend is ~$1.4–1.5T; Infosys at $20.2B holds **~1.4% share** — superficially a huge runway.
- But its *serviceable* market (application services, consulting, managed infrastructure in North America/Europe) is growing only **mid-single digits**, and client budgets are being reallocated *internally* toward GenAI pilots rather than net-new outsourced spend.
- The critical TAM nuance: AI expands the transformation budget but **compresses revenue per unit of work** (fewer engineers per outcome, outcome-based pricing). The addressable revenue pool per client IT dollar may *shrink* even as the technology TAM grows — Accenture cut FY26 local-currency growth to 3–4% from 3–5%, confirming the read-across.
- Capture math is unfavorable in the near term: AI revenue +$131M QoQ out of +$153M total sequential growth = **~86% of all sequential growth came from AI**, implying the non-AI core ($4.6B/qtr) is flat-to-shrinking. Until AI is clearly additive, penetration gains don't translate into company growth.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q3 FY27 results — 13 Jan 2027**: does the 1–2% CC guide hold, and does AI revenue cross ~12–13% of the mix? Two consecutive misses make this a credibility event.
- **AI mix inflection (H1 CY27)**: AI at 10.5% of revenue growing 29% QoQ. A sustained mix above ~15% *with* total growth re-accelerating to 4%+ would be the first genuine re-rating trigger.
- **Large-deal TCV conversion**: $5.2B TCV in Q2 with 62% net new and a >$2B seven-year contract — the strongest leading indicator in the print, but the market has stopped paying for TCV until it converts to revenue.
- **Capital return**: interim dividend ₹26/share (+13% YoY); next buyback decision likely at the April 2027 results (last was $2B). A new buyback plus a falling share count would put a hard floor under the multiple.
- **M&A optionality**: ~$370M of acquisitions already deployed in H1 FY27 — bolt-ons for AI capability are the most plausible path to additive growth.
- **Sector read-across**: Accenture's next guide and US client IT budgets (H-1B cost inflation, tariff uncertainty) will drive the whole complex.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends (US, "Infosys") — bearish.** Weekly interest fell from a 55–60 average in H1 2026 (Feb peak 76) to **25–36 in Sept–Nov 2026**, roughly −40%. For a B2B firm this is a weak signal, but the direction corroborates the demand malaise and fading retail/enterprise mindshare.
- **Large-deal TCV — bullish.** $5.2B, 62% net new, incl. >$2B/7-yr. The one unambiguous positive leading indicator.
- **AI revenue trajectory — bullish on slope, ambiguous on substance.** +29% QoQ, 8.2%→10.5% of revenue. Risk: partly a reclassification of existing digital work, and it is deflating adjacent revenue lines.
- **Job postings — neutral/gap.** LinkedIn ~1,500 open roles, Indeed ~4,870 US listings, headcount 328,594. No clean YoY trend available (data gap); the absence of aggressive hiring is consistent with a "cost-takeout, not expansion" posture.
- **Sell-side drift — mixed/improving at the margin.** Targets collapsed from $18–20 (Jan-26) to $10.40–13 (Oct-26) — a brutal revision cycle — but the **two most recent actions were raises** (BMO $12→$13; TD Cowen $9→$11 on 26-Oct). Median target ~$11.2 sits *below* spot, so there is no analyst cushion.
- **Price action — broken.** −20.5% YoY and −37% from the Dec-2025 close high of $19.35; the Sept-2026 low was $10.27. Absolute downtrend, though the stock has reclaimed its 50-day area into November.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

**Valuation is undemanding — 15.0x FY27E EPS, 8.9x EV/EBITDA, 12.9x P/FCF (7.8% FCF yield), net cash, 4.4% dividend yield, 8.6% total shareholder yield — and that is precisely the problem: the market has correctly stopped paying a growth multiple because this is no longer a growth business.** On my framework it fails TAM-capture, fails Rule of 40 (23 vs 40), fails margin trajectory, and fails forward-estimate direction (FY27E EPS *below* FY26 actual). The one legitimate bull thread — AI revenue at $2.2B annualized growing 29% QoQ, funded by the strongest deal book in years, with 3.8% annual share shrinkage — is real optionality, not yet a growth curve. I cannot underwrite a Strong Buy on 1–2% CC growth and declining EPS. Equally, a 111%-of-FCF capital return, net cash and a mid-teens multiple cap the downside, so I won't call Sell. **Hold; revisit toward Buy if two consecutive quarters show total CC growth ≥4% with AI mix ≥15% and stable 21%+ operating margin.** Fair value band $11–13.50.

## 7. Key Risks

- **AI cannibalization risk (the dominant risk)**: if GenAI deflates pricing per unit of work faster than volumes grow, revenue *shrinks* even as AI revenue "surges." ~86% of sequential growth already coming from AI, with the core flat, is the early fingerprint of this.
- **Guidance/credibility risk**: FY27 cut from 1.5–3.0% to 1–2%, two straight EPS misses, and the possibility of FY28 guidance below the +3.2% consensus.
- **Margin risk**: 20.3% FY26 operating margin sits at the low end of the 20–22% guide; any further AI investment, wage inflation, or pricing concessions pushes toward the floor.
- **Capital-return sustainability**: returning 111% of FCF while FCF declines 15.7% YoY is not a durable strategy; a buyback pause would remove the main technical support.
- **Competitive threat**: Accenture, TCS (better scale/pricing power per S&P), and *in-house enterprise AI* — clients using foundation models directly to reduce outsourced headcount entirely.
- **Macro/policy**: US client discretionary spend, H-1B/immigration cost inflation, USD/INR (reported 1.3% vs CC 1.5% growth shows FX is already a headwind), and concentration in BFSI.
- **Data gaps**: no quantitative job-posting trend series; peer multiples (ACN, TCS) not pulled in this run — relative valuation not independently verified.
