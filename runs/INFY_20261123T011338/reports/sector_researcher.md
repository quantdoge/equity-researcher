# SECTOR BRIEF — INFOSYS LTD (INFY)
### Sector: Technology / IT Services & Consulting | Fiscal year ends March

---

## 1. Sector Overview

The global IT services industry is a **~$1.56 trillion** market (Gartner 2026E) that is decelerating hard relative to the rest of tech: IT services spend is forecast to grow **+4.4% in 2026** vs **+15.2% for software** and **+10.8% overall IT spending**. This is the defining feature of the current cycle — AI infrastructure and software are absorbing budget share, while discretionary services work is being cut or deflation-priced. The industry is highly fragmented (INFY holds roughly **1.3%** of global IT services; low HHI), with the Indian export cluster — a **>$280bn** industry where INFY ranks **#2 behind TCS** — representing the low-cost delivery benchmark. Competitive intensity is rising at exactly the moment the traditional labour-arbitrage pyramid is being compressed by generative AI: management itself notes AI is simultaneously "giving us the growth" and driving "compression" through "competitive intensity." The sector is in a **late-mature, structurally re-rating phase** — not a cyclical trough that mean-reverts on its own.

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **AI-led deal renewal cycle is pulling through large contracts.** Almost **100% of $50m+ deals now include AI** components (management, Oct-2026). This creates a re-bid opportunity across installed bases and supports large-deal TCV even as run-rate revenue lags.
- **Total IT wallet still expanding fast at the top of the funnel.** Gartner sees worldwide IT spending at **$6.15trn in 2026 (+10.8%) rising to $7.35trn in 2027 (+15%)**, and AI spending alone up **49.5% in 2026** — an enormous pool of adjacent work (data platform, cloud, agentic AI build-outs) that services firms can capture share of.
- **Cost-arbitrage + cash-return resilience in the incumbents.** Indian-tier delivery costs and benign INR (INR ~87/USD) support margin defence; INFY's operating margin of **20.3%** is roughly **420bps above the IT-services peer median**, funding buybacks/dividends through the downturn.

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **Growth has fallen to near-zero — guidance cut twice.** INFY cut FY27 constant-currency growth to **1–2%** (from 1.5–3%), below the **2.03%** consensus, and ADRs fell **up to 7.6%** on the print. Peers (HCLTech, Wipro) trimmed simultaneously — this is sector-wide, not company-specific.
- **AI-driven price deflation is compressing the revenue model.** Per-unit pricing on coding, testing and BPO is falling faster than volumes rise; clients are reallocating saved budget to AI infrastructure vendors rather than incremental services spend. IT services growth of **4.4%** vs **15.2%** software growth quantifies the share shift.
- **US immigration and geopolitical cost shocks.** A proposed **$103,265 H-1B petition fee** (currently blocked in court) has already altered behaviour — Infosys cut H-1B lottery registrations **~91%**, TCS **~95%**, Cognizant **~100%** — forcing costly onshoring/localisation. Add client budget caution from the Iran conflict, higher energy costs and persistent inflation.

## 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG** (with a growth caveat)

- **Margin leadership is structural, not accounting noise.** 20.3% operating margin is **420bps above the peer median** and the best among pure-play IT services peers, sustained through four years of decelerating revenue (20.3–21.1% band) — evidence of pricing discipline, offshoring mix and pyramid leverage rather than a cyclical peak.
- **Scale + brand:** #2 in a $280bn export industry, top-3 global IT services brand, ~328,600 employees — enables participation in the $50m+ mega-deal market where AI content is now mandatory.
- **Capital returns and balance sheet** cushion the de-rating; INFY has remained profitable and cash-generative (Q1 FY27 net income **+12% to ₹77.8bn**, a beat), i.e. the earnings base is intact even as the top line stalls.
- **The caveats:** (i) growth of **4.6%** trails the peer median by 240bps and guidance of 1–2% is the weakest in the cohort; (ii) margin trend is drifting down (23.0% FY22 → 20.3% FY26), so it is defending, not expanding; (iii) **CEO transition** underway (insider appointment) adds execution risk mid-transformation; (iv) ADR is down **~22%** over the past ~13 months ($15.92 → $12.38) — the market is pricing a structural, not cyclical, problem.

Verdict: **the highest-quality operator in a structurally challenged sub-industry.**

## 6. Sector Rating: **UNATTRACTIVE**

IT services is losing budget share to AI infrastructure and software (4.4% vs 15.2% growth), pricing is deflating on generative-AI-driven productivity gains, and the three largest Indian exporters have all cut guidance. This is a **structural re-rating, not a cycle to buy the dip** — the sector's core product (labour-hours) is being repriced downward by its own technology. INFY deserves a quality premium within the group for its 20%+ operating margin and cash generation, but a sector that grows 1–2% with compressing pricing cannot support a re-rating until AI-led revenue visibly outruns AI-led deflation.
