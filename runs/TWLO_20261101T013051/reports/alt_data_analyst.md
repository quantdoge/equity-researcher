### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **In-Line (revenue soft, EPS above)**

Next catalyst: **Q4'26 report, Feb 11, 2027** — consensus EPS $1.54, revenue $1.604B.

**Supporting an EPS beat:**
- Beat rate **7/7 (100%)** over seven quarters; average surprise **+14.1%**
- Surprise magnitude shows **no decay**: +19.0 → +13.3 → +16.8 → +8.1 → +18.1 → +11.4 → **+12.2%** (Q3'26)
- Q3'26: EPS $1.65 vs $1.47 est (+12.2%); revenue $1.5596B vs $1.5152B (+2.9% beat, +19.9% YoY)
- Low hiring + record FCF → cost line remains controllable; consensus $1.54 already embeds sequential EPS *decline* from $1.65, an easy bar

**Supporting a revenue miss/reset:**
- Guidance is a **deliberate decel**: organic growth 16% (Q3) → **11–12% guided (Q4)**, a 400–500bp step down
- Consensus $1.604B = only **+2.8% QoQ** vs +4.0% QoQ in Q3 — already haircut, so the beat bar is now *higher*, not lower
- Alt-data demand signals (search, web traffic) show **no offsetting acceleration** to surprise to the upside

**Verdict: In-Line overall.** EPS tracking **above**; revenue tracking **in-line to slightly below**. The 100% beat streak is the strongest single alt-signal in this dataset — but it has been an *expense/FCF* beat generator, not a demand beat generator, and the Q4 setup confirms that pattern.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (growth-bearish / EPS-bullish)

The alternative data does **not** support a demand re-acceleration thesis. Search interest is at 12-month lows, web traffic is decelerating, hiring is restrained, and Segment search demand has effectively flatlined — all consistent with management's own 11–12% Q4 guide rather than contradicting it. Conversely, the persistent 100% beat streak, record FCF, low hiring intensity, and raised 2027 targets point to **continued EPS/FCF beats independent of revenue**.

**Actionable framing:** alt-data argues **against paying up for an AI-driven growth re-rating** (no leading indicator supports it), while supporting the **margin-compounder / FCF story**. The Q4 print is most likely an **EPS beat with in-line-to-soft revenue** — the same pattern that just produced a –15% reaction. Watch for trajectory change in Google Trends and twilio.com traffic in Dec–Jan as the earliest tell of a guide-up.
