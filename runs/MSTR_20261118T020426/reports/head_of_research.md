**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**
- **The engine runs backwards**: mNAV ~1.0× makes every issuance dilutive; sats/share −8.4% in 6 months; $21.2B raised YTD bought 1,666 net BTC in Q3. The wrapper now destroys per-share value by construction.

- **Macro bear branch (~40%)**: a second hike takes BTC to $50–55k → equity NAV ~$68–84 at par and −35–45% on the price, with the dilution spiral as a non-linear terminal branch.

**Key Risks to the Sell Call** (how this is wrong)
1. **BTC re-rating** — the dominant risk; this is a levered call, and sitting out a crypto recovery is the explicit cost of the call.
2. **Premium re-inflation** — retail can return faster than fundamentals heal; >1.5× mNAV restores accretion and invalidates the dilution thesis.
3. **Rates roll over** — soft Nov CPI prices out the Dec hike, resets STRC's coupon lower, and lifts both NAV and carry.
4. **Strategic events** — software-stub sale, common buyback at a discount, clean litigation resolution, or Nasdaq-100 retention would each force a re-rating.
