# SECTOR BRIEF — Strategy Inc. (MSTR)

**Data as of 18 Nov 2026 | Price $96.50 | Mkt cap $38.9B basic / $43.9B fully diluted**

> **Classification caveat:** MSTR is filed as *Technology / Software – Application*, and the auto peer screen returned mega-cap semis (NVDA, TSM, AVGO) — analytically useless here. Economically, 99% of MSTR's market value sits in 850,849 BTC. I have benchmarked against the **Bitcoin treasury / digital-asset-treasury (DAT)** sector as the primary arena, with crypto-market infrastructure and analytics software as secondary comps.

---

## 1. Sector Overview

The digital-asset-treasury sector is **~15 months into a violent de-rating**. There are now **179 listed DATs across 30 countries holding 1,288,519 BTC (~6.1% of the 21M supply, ~$100B)** as of Sept 2026, and the 50 largest have shed **~$83B of market value since July 2025**. Bitcoin sits near **$64,300** (down ~50% in 13 months), ETF flows are negative, and the sector's governing metric — **mNAV** (market cap ÷ coin value) — has collapsed from 2–3× in the 2024/25 premium era to a **0.93×–1.32× 52-week range**. That single change flips the business model from accretive to dilutive: below 1.0×, every share issued to buy Bitcoin transfers value *away* from existing holders. It is a highly fragmented, essentially zero-barrier industry whose only true moat — cheap access to equity capital — has been withdrawn by the market.

---

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **Scarce, institutionalised supply base.** DATs collectively control 6.1% of all Bitcoin that will ever exist, and MSTR alone holds **66% of all DAT Bitcoin** (850,849 coins) — a position no new entrant can replicate. Bitcoin's fixed supply plus a possible Fed pivot remain the sector's structural call option.
- **Capital-stack innovation is extending the runway.** MSTR has built a perpetual-preferred complex (**STRK, STRF, STRD, STRC**) alongside converts, giving it lower-cost, longer-duration funding than the convert-only 2021 structure. Senior claims total **$19.7B** and it still added **485 BTC on 15 Nov 2026**.
- **Index inclusion was defended — for now.** MSCI decided in **January 2026 not to exclude** DATs, preserving passive demand for the largest names; BTC Treasuries exposure is also increasingly wrapped into ETF-of-ETF and SMA products.

---

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **The mNAV flywheel has inverted.** MSTR's headline mNAV is **1.07×**, gross **0.80×**, and **1.25×** on a net-of-senior-claims (CEBE) basis — versus multiples of 2–3× in 2024. Preferred-funded purchases now produce what practitioners call **"phantom growth"**: gross BTC/share rises while common-equity BTC exposure per share (124,080 sats) stays flat. Dilution has been ferocious — **shares outstanding 100.2M (2021) → 294.0M (2025), +193% — while revenue fell 6.6%.**
- **Spot Bitcoin ETFs are a strictly superior substitute.** IBIT-type vehicles deliver identical coin exposure at roughly **1.0× NAV for ~25bp**, with no leverage, no dilution, no refinancing risk and no $19.7B claim stack ahead of you. Every basis point of ETF-fee compression makes the DAT wrapper harder to justify; ETF outflows are simultaneously the sector's cause and effect.
- **Leverage + index-exclusion overhang.** MSTR is **underwater on its stack** (average cost ~$75,578 vs ~$64,300 spot, ~15% unrealised loss), and the senior stack consumes **305,664 BTC (35.9%)** of the treasury; **break-even CEBE Bitcoin price is $23,106** — 64% below spot, beneath which the common's coin exposure goes negative. Against that, the MSCI review remains open and **removal risk resurfaced in November 2026** — a forced-seller event independent of valuation. MSTR has also **sold Bitcoin for the first time**, the clearest signal the accumulation model has broken.

| Dimension | Assessment |

| Per-share accretion engine | **Weak** — mNAV at/below 1.0× makes issuance dilutive; +193% shares for −6.6% revenue; CEBE flat |
| Operating business | **Weak** — +3.0% growth, −13.3pp gross margin over 4 yrs, breakeven operating income; subscale vs PLTR |
| Balance-sheet resilience | **Average** — $19.7B senior claims but long-dated perpetuals; break-even BTC $23,106 gives real headroom, though cost basis is underwater |

**Rationale:** MSTR remains the *category king* of a category the market is repricing downward. Its scale and liquidity are genuine and defensible; its **product, however, is dominated by the ETF** on cost, purity and counterparty risk, and its historical edge — issuing equity above NAV — is exactly what has disappeared. A dominant share of a shrinking premium pool is not a moat. Positioning: average franchise, negative momentum.

## 6. Sector Rating: **UNATTRACTIVE**

**Cycle position: late-stage de-rating / post-bubble capitulation, ~15 months in.** The DAT sector has gone from premium-scarcity to discount-glut: 179 issuers, near-zero barriers to entry, one dominant substitute (spot ETFs), a shut equity-capital window, and ~$83B of destroyed value among the top 50. Porter's Five Forces is maximally hostile — **very high** new-entrant threat, **very high** substitution threat, **high** rivalry for the same marginal allocator dollar, and rising supplier power from convertible/preferred buyers. Precious little of that is MSTR's fault; almost none of it is in MSTR's control.

**What would change the call:** a durable return of mNAV above ~1.5× (restoring the accretion flywheel), a Bitcoin recovery that puts the stack back above the ~$75.6k cost basis, and clarity that MSCI excludes DATs from index review. Absent all three, the sector is a **levered, fee-laden, dilutive proxy for an asset you can buy directly at par** — and that is a structurally unattractive place to hold equity.
