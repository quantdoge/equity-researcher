**Why medium:** Two of five feeds failed (patents, social), the web traffic data is a *search-result proxy* rather than API-level SimilarWeb monthly visits, and news sentiment rests on only 4 PR-wire articles. Google Trends and the earnings-surprise series are the high-quality pillars here and both are consistent with each other.

**Data limitations & lag:**

- **Ticker collision:** "RBA" also = Reserve Bank of Australia. Rate-hawkish headlines (Bullock, Sept 17–18) polluted the news feed and must be excluded from any RBA sentiment read.
- Earnings calendar shows a **Nov 9, 2026** date with Q2'26 revenue reported inconsistently across vendors ($1.30B company release vs. $1.66B MarketBeat) — revenue-level consensus is less reliable than EPS consensus.

**Bottom line:** Alt data is a leading indicator, not a verdict. It says the Nov 9 print likely delivers a *smaller* beat than RBA's history implies, with take rate — not volume — as the swing factor, and the tape has already voted bearish. Watch for a take-rate stabilization comment as the single most important tell.
