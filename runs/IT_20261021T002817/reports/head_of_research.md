**Investment Thesis**

Gartner is a high-quality, cash-generative franchise (70% gross margin, ~$1.2B FCF, ~160% FCF conversion, negative accruals) caught in a structural re-pricing of its business model: the market believes generative AI commoditizes syndicated research, and contract value (CV) growth of ~1.7–2.5% — against 3.7% CPI and shrinking client count (−4.5%) — is the live evidence. The stock has already absorbed the punishment (−72% peak-to-trough) and recovered 75% off the June low to a fair-to-full ~15x FY26E adjusted EPS. What remains is a genuinely bimodal name: if CV re-accelerates above 3–5% on AI-governance demand, a re-rating toward $275–290 follows; if CV stalls below 2% or turns negative, the multiple compresses toward $150–165. At the current price, above the sell-side median target and days before a binary Q3 print, the risk/reward is symmetric — not actionable.

Rising real yields (10Y TIPS 2.80%) and a +3% USD are mild headwinds; the multiple damage is largely paid. **Bull Case**
- **CV inflection confirmed**: two consecutive quarters of acceleration, a channel check above consensus, and AI-governance/EU AI Act demand could push CV back to mid-single digits — a re-rating to 17–20x on $16–17 EPS yields $275–300.
- **Financial engineering floor**: ~$2B/yr of buybacks against a ~$14B cap is a ~13% annual share-count shrink, manufacturing double-digit EPS growth even at flat revenue, funded by a ~9% FCF yield.
- **Last-man-standing franchise**: >50% share, 16x nearest pure-play rival, Magic Quadrant network effects, conferences compounding +15% at ~60% contribution margin, and AI complexity *raising* demand for credentialed judgment.

**Bear Case**
- **AI substitution is already in the numbers**: CV ~2%, client count −4.5%, growth manufactured by price on a shrinking base — the classic late-stage subscription signature. If CV turns negative, there is no valuation floor before ~$150.
- **Margin cliff in 2027**: the 2026 margin recovery (22.6% in Q2) is cost cuts masking a declining top line; you can only cut once, and FY24/FY25/FY26 earnings are distorted by one-offs in both directions.
- **Governance/litigation overhang**: a validated class action on the exact metric anchoring valuation, a CLO exit mid-case, and buybacks executed at $300–450 into a decelerating core (~$1B+ of value destroyed) — a negative-equity balance sheet with no cushion if the narrative breaks again.

**Key Risks**
1. **Q3 print (3 Nov)** — binary event; the stock has gapped ±20%+ on two of the last three prints. Watch CV growth, net new CV, and client count — not the EPS beat.
2. **CV <2% for two quarters or client count <12,500** — confirms structural decline; exit.
3. **Feb-2027 FY27 guidance** — the event that caused a −21% day in Feb-2026; pattern of disappointing guides not yet broken.
4. **Legal escalation** — derivative suit naming directors, SEC inquiry into AI disclosures, or evidence the CLO departure relates to disclosure controls.
5. **Buyback pause** — removes the sole EPS engine and the price floor.
6. **Macro bear tail** — Warsh-Fed hikes into 3.7% CPI, 10Y through 5.5% → $150–165 retest.
