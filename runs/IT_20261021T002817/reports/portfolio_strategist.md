## 2. Consensus Assessment

**Agreement level: SPLIT** — medium agreement on business quality, low agreement on entry timing and valuation.

### What the agents agree on

1. **Gartner is a high-quality cash generator, not a fraud or solvency risk.** The Short Analyst’s forensic work is explicit: earnings quality is high, accruals are negative, receivables are shrinking, and the Beneish M-score shows no manipulation signature. Fin Risk confirms leverage is manageable and there is no near-term maturity wall.
2. **The stock is no longer expensive on cash flow.** At ~15–16x FY26E adjusted EPS, ~12–14x EV/EBITDA, and a 7.4%–9% FCF yield, the easy “short the AI casualty” trade is gone. The Short Analyst rejects a fresh short.
3. **No agent recommends chasing at $219.64.** Value and Growth both say HOLD with buy zones of ~$170–180. The stock is above the median sell-side target (~$189–200) after a +75% rebound from the June low.
4. **The Nov 3 print is a binary near-term event.** Alt Data is bullish on the fundamentals into the quarter, but explicitly warns the entry point is poor. The stock has moved ±20% on two of the last three prints.

### Where the agents disagree

- **Near-term vs. structural:** Alt Data sees a beat-and-raise setup; Sector, Value, Growth and Macro see a decelerating, AI-threatened subscription franchise whose terminal value is being repriced.
- **Valuation vs. risk:** Value sees a 13% discount to intrinsic value; Non-Fin Risk sees HIGH governance/litigation risk and Fin Risk sees 60%+ volatility. The discount is real but not large enough to compensate for the risk stack.
- **Quality vs. momentum:** Quant likes value and quality but flags negative 12-1 momentum and the worst-in-class low-vol score. This is a value/quality stock with event-stock risk.

### Conflict resolution

The **Non-Financial Risk HIGH rating and the Geo Legal MEDIUM rating take precedence over the Value/Growth HOLD views** when sizing. Fraud risk is low, but litigation and disclosure-control risk are live and directly attack the metric — contract value — on which the entire thesis depends. The Value Researcher’s own conclusion supports this: a 13% discount is not enough. The Alt Data bullish signal is real but tactical; it does not change the portfolio-level risk budget.

**Aggregated conviction: ~4.5–5.0 / 10.** Good business, fair-to-full price, high idiosyncratic risk, no margin of safety, binary catalyst. This is a small-position-or-wait name, not a core holding.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### Recommended sizing

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Target position size** | **1.0% of portfolio** |
| **Maximum position size** | **1.5%** |
| **Initial tranche at current price** | **0–0.5%** — do not initiate a full position at $219.64 |
| **Conditional initiation** | **0.5% after Q3 confirms CV ≥2.5–3%** and FY26 guide is intact |
| **Add-to target** | **1.0–1.25%** on a pullback to $180–190 or on CV >3% with a credible FY27 guide |
| **Existing holders** | **Hold; cap at 1.5%.** If above 1.5%, reduce to 1.5% into strength. |

### Rationale

- **Kelly logic:** The probability-weighted intrinsic value is ~$253, implying ~15% upside. The bear case is ~$129, or −41%. With 62% annualised volatility, the edge is thin relative to the dispersion. A half-Kelly allocation for a 1–1.5% edge-to-risk ratio is roughly **0.5–1.0%**, not a 3–5% position.
- **Risk budget:** A 1% position with 62% volatility contributes ~0.62% of portfolio volatility. A 25–27% stop-loss on a 1% position costs ~0.25–0.27% of portfolio — acceptable for a diversifying, low-correlation name, but not for a larger bet.
- **Liquidity:** ~1.2M shares/day (~$260M notional). A 1% position in a $1B portfolio is ~$10M, under 5% of ADV. In a multi-billion portfolio, scale entries over 3–5 days with VWAP/limit orders.
- **Governance/litigation cap:** Non-Fin Risk HIGH and Geo Legal MEDIUM justify a hard cap below what pure valuation would suggest. The CLO departure and class action are not priced as a specific reserve; a derivative suit or adverse disclosure could compress the multiple again.

**Bottom line:** 1.0% target, 1.5% max, 0.5% starter only after confirmation. At $219.64, new capital should **pass** unless the investor is willing to own a 0.5% “event stub” into the print.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

### Sector concentration

- FMP classifies IT as “Technology,” but economically it is **information services / research & advisory**, not semis or hardware. The correct peer set is FDS, MORN, VRSK, FORR, CLVT, not NVDA/AAPL/MSFT.
- If the portfolio is already overweight **data & analytics** (FDS, MORN, VRSK), adding IT increases a correlated information-services exposure: IT–FDS correlation 0.68, IT–MORN 0.65, IT–VRSK 0.58. In that case, keep IT at the low end (0.5–1.0%) or pass.
- If the portfolio is overweight **mega-cap tech/semis**, IT does **not** add semiconductor beta. Its correlation with SPY is only 0.07 and beta is 0.31. It adds idiosyncratic AI-sentiment risk, not broad tech beta.

### Factor exposure

The low beta is misleading: R² is 0.005, so market beta explains almost none of its risk. Diversification benefit is limited in an AI/growth scare because IT’s drawdowns are narrative-driven, not market-driven.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

### Stop-loss

- **Hard stop:** closing price below **$160–165** (approximately −25% to −27%). This is below the August breakout base (~$170–180) and inside the macro bear-case zone of $150–165.
- Use a **closing basis**, not intraday, given 62% volatility and repeated 20%+ single-day gaps. Position sizing — not the stop — is the primary risk control.
- For a 1% position, a stop at $165 risks ~0.25% of portfolio. That is acceptable. Do not widen the stop to “give it room”; if the thesis is right, it should not retest the June panic zone.

### Thesis invalidation conditions

Exit or reassess immediately if any of the following occur:

1. **CV growth falls below 2% for two consecutive quarters**, or turns negative.
2. **Client count falls below ~12,500** — the current 12,775 is already down 4.5% y/y; further attrition confirms price-led growth on a shrinking base.
3. **FY27 revenue guided down more than 3%**, or management withdraws the CV/guidance framework.
4. **Operating margin fails to hold ~20% in 2027** — the current margin recovery is cost-cut-driven; a 2027 margin cliff would break the EPS story.
5. **Buyback is paused or net leverage exceeds ~2.5x** — the buyback is the main EPS engine; a pause removes the floor.
6. **Adverse legal/governance development:** derivative suit naming directors, SEC inquiry into AI-related disclosures, auditor change, or evidence that the CLO departure relates to disclosure-control disagreements.
7. **AI-attributed contract-value churn is disclosed** — e.g., renewal rates or seat counts materially deteriorating because of LLM substitution.

### Trim triggers

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| **$240–250 with no CV >3% or FY27 guide** | Trim one-third |
| **$255–265** (≈90% of base-case intrinsic value of $286) | Trim half |
| **$290–300** | Exit/avoid unless CV >5% and FY27 guide supports a higher terminal value |
| **Position exceeds 1.5% of portfolio** | Rebalance to 1.5% |

### Event-risk protocol

- **Before 3-Nov:** no new full position. If already holding, consider hedging or trimming to 0.5–1.0% if the position is above target.
- **After 3-Nov:** re-underwrite within 48 hours. The key numbers are CV growth, net new CV, client count, and FY26/FY27 guide — not the EPS beat.
- **Feb-2027:** the highest-variance event. Initial FY27 guidance at this meeting took the stock down 21% in Feb-2026. Size the position so a −20% gap is tolerable.
