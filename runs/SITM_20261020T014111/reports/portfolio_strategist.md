## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — but split on price, not on business**

**High agreement (12/12) on fundamentals:** SiTime owns a real, narrow-but-widening moat; revenue is genuinely accelerating; the Renesas deal is strategically sound; gross margin (~63%) is real. Even the forensic short-seller explicitly refused to short the business.

**High agreement (9/12) on risk:** Geopolitical HIGH, non-financial HIGH, market/volatility HIGH, governance WEAK. These are stacked, not independent.

**Critical cross-agent conflict I must resolve:** Value Researcher's $300–350 fair value uses a **10% WACC**. Macro Researcher independently establishes that a 3.08-beta stock at a 4.99% risk-free rate implies a **cost of equity of 16–18%**. These two reports are internally inconsistent — re-running Value's own DCF at a WACC consistent with its beta produces a fair value *materially below* $300–350. Meanwhile the Growth case relies on FY2028 revenue of $1.735B, a figure supported by only **3 analysts** with a 2x dispersion in the field (one model has FY27 revenue at $720M vs consensus $1,353M).

**Resolution: the valuation bears win the argument on the numbers.** Even after a 45% drawdown, SITM is not cheap on any conservative discount rate. But the bulls win the argument on *what gets repriced* — the de-rating is a rates event, not an order-book event, and rates events reverse.

**Thesis weight by domain relevance:** Growth (8) and Alt Data (6) are the domain experts on the operating story. Value (8) and Macro (8) are the domain experts on the price. They do not conflict factually — they conflict in time horizon. That produces a *small* position, not a large one.
