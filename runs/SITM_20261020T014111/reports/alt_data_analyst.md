### Data Limitations
1. **Patents (failed x2)** and **web traffic (failed)** — 2 of 6 framework pillars are empty; R&D-intensity and demand-velocity trends are unverified.
2. **Social sentiment unavailable** — no crowding/positioning read into a high-beta, 30%-drawn-down name.
3. **Job postings are a level snapshot, not a trend** — the positive read rests on absolute openness (~17% of headcount), not acceleration.
4. **Google Trends is event-driven noise** for this B2B model; treated as non-informative rather than as a demand negative.
5. **Inorganic quarter** — Q3 consensus embeds an acquisition; alt data calibrates poorly against a step-up of this size. Treat the Above-consensus call as directional, not a magnitude forecast.

**Bottom line:** Business-activity alt data (test-capacity and NPI hiring) and the 7-for-7 beat record point to a Q3 delivery at or above plan on Nov 4, while the 33% drawdown was a beta-amplified sector de-rating rather than a fundamental deterioration — a favourable setup *if* the Renesas contribution consolidates at the guided pace.
