**Investment Thesis**

SiTime is a genuine narrow-moat franchise — ~80–90% share of MEMS precision timing — riding two real secular drivers: quartz→MEMS substitution (~5–10% penetrated of a $6.8B market) and rising timing content per AI system. Operating momentum is exceptional and *accelerating* (+61% FY25 → +127% YoY Q2'26), the books are forensically clean, and the Renesas deal creates a full timing platform. But the equity is a 3.08-beta duration asset trading at ~18x FY26E sales and ~62–71x FY27E GAAP earnings with negative FCF after $114M of stock comp — there is no margin of safety even after the drawdown. The position is a race between operating momentum and a hostile discount-rate regime, with ~62% of revenue and effectively 100% of wafer supply concentrated in the highest-risk geography in semiconductors.

**Bull Case**
1. **Substitution + content intensity**: MEMS is ~5–10% penetrated in a $6.8B timing market; CED growing ~181% with each SerDes/optical generation raising timing content per rack. If FY28 consensus ($1.74B / $20 EPS) is even roughly right, today's price is ~34x FY28 earnings — a PEG near 1.
2. **Renesas completes the platform**: +$300M of ~70%-GM revenue, 10,000 customers, clock-IC capability, path to $1B revenue two years early. Q3 print (Nov 4) is the first full consolidation — a structurally easy beat-and-raise setup given the 7/7 record and conservative guidance culture.
3. **Asymmetric rate optionality**: if September's 3.72% CPI proves the peak, a Fed pause plus a 60–70bp fall in the 10Y re-rates the highest-beta duration name in semis by 35–50% with no change to the fundamental story.

**Bear Case**
1. **Valuation offers no cushion**: ~18x FY26E sales, 62–71x FY27E GAAP EPS, 0.28% FCF yield, negative FCF after SBC. At a beta-consistent discount rate, DCF fair value sits below the price; the hawkish-escalation path (10Y >5.5%) mechanically implies $350–400, and de-rating to 15x sales on a 20% revenue miss implies ~$5.6B market cap (−60%) *with the company still solvent*.
2. **Proven cyclicality + AI concentration**: revenue fell −49% in 2023; GM swung 64.5% → 51.6% → 63%; ~64% of revenue is now an AI-capex derivative. One hyperscaler digestion quarter or a soft Nov 4 guide is amplified ~3x by beta.
3. **Stacked, correlated non-financial risks**: unproven mega-integration in a 441-person org, doubling DSO, one-way insider selling, and 62% of revenue / ~100% of wafers in the Taiwan-China corridor — these are not independent; any one escalation compounds the rest.

**Key Risks**
Rates shock (10Y >5.50% — the dominant channel); AI-capex air pocket (CED growth <60% invalidates); Renesas integration/attrition failure; DSO >65 days or another >$30M working-capital drag; gross margin <60% as legacy Renesas mix rolls in; Taiwan Strait / export-control escalation; continued SBC dilution and convert conversion overhang; insider selling resuming below $600. **Data gaps to close before sizing up**: 10-K/10-Q full text (customer concentration, legal proceedings, convert terms, securities-portfolio duration); the organic/inorganic split of Q2–Q3 growth; actual TPD revenue vs the $300–450M estimate.
