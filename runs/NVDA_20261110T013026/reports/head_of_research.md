**Investment Thesis**
Twelve specialists unanimously agree this is an elite franchise — wide and widening moat, 77% ROIC, net cash, revenue accelerating to +106% — yet they split almost perfectly on the security at $178.67: Growth says Strong Buy (19x FY27 EPS), Value says Hold (probability-weighted IV $169, a 6% premium), Macro says Unfavourable (bear-steepener de-rating regime). Overlaying a binary governance/legal tail (DOJ-track Megaspeed probe, board investigation of the CEO, no succession plan) caps conviction and position size. The business is not impaired; the price is fair, not cheap; the multiple — not earnings — is the risk variable.

**Bull Case**
- Growth is accelerating, not decaying (+106% y/y, FY27E +90%), supply-constrained, with a 100% beat record and upward estimate revisions — while the multiple has compressed to ~19x FY27 / 11x FY28, cheaper than AVGO and AMD on EV/EBITDA.
- Moat is widening (CUDA + rack-scale integration + annual cadence; ~80–85% accelerator share), economics are elite (60–66% operating margin, 77% ROIC, net cash), and zero-China guidance makes any export easing free upside.
- Positioning is washed out (−26% from peak, record capitulation volume, search interest at lows): a clean board-probe outcome plus a held $108B/74%-GM guide on 17 Nov is a re-rating catalyst toward $235–260.

**Bear Case**
- Binary legal/governance tail: a DOJ criminal finding on diversion, an adverse board-probe result, or a Huang departure with no successor hits earnings, multiple, and key-man premium simultaneously — and is unresolvable before earnings.
- Macro de-rating: Fed hiking into a credit-financed $1tn capex cycle; a single AI-credit accident (HY 3.4% → 6%) drives the double-whammy of estimate cuts plus multiple compression — two independent agents converge on $100–110.
- Earnings-quality drift is the slow-burn tell: OCF/NI 40% in Q2, WC absorbing 18.6% of incremental revenue, GAAP inflated by ~$32.6bn of non-operating equity marks, FY26 GM −3.9pp — the first credible cracks in the scarcity-rent thesis.

**Key Risks**
1. DOJ/BIS enforcement or board-probe finding adverse to Huang (thesis invalidation).
2. Hyperscaler capex digestion / AI-credit accident cutting FY27–28 estimates.
3. Cash-conversion deterioration (receivables/inventory outgrowing revenue 2+ quarters; OCF/NI <50%).
