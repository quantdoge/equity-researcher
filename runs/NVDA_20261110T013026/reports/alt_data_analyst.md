Consensus $108.8B is barely above the guidance midpoint; several sell-side previews run **$111–113B / EPS $2.53–2.60** vs. calendar consensus $2.47 — i.e., **estimate revisions are drifting up, not down**, and management's guide implies +12% QoQ / ~+90% YoY.

Sell-side commentary frames the business as **supply-constrained, not demand-constrained** — which means alt demand proxies (search, traffic) are structurally uninformative here and should not be read as a miss signal.

**However:** the market is no longer trading the print. The de-rating is sentiment- and headline-driven, and a beat may not be rewarded.

### 4. News Sentiment Score & Summary — **−0.50 (Negative) | 20 articles, 6 negative / 2 positive / 12 neutral**

Timing note: the keyword model badly understates the tone — most "neutral"-tagged stories are the scandal itself.

**The entire news flow is idiosyncratic governance + national security, not demand:**

**Read-through:** this is a *key-man + regulatory tail risk*, not a fundamental demand shock. It is unresolvable before Nov 17 and caps multiple expansion regardless of the print.

**Macro sentiment overlay — the regime flag:** Google Trends "AI bubble" spiked to **100 (12-month high) in the week of Oct 25**, settling at 34. The narrative has shifted from "how big is AI" to "is AI a bubble." This is the single most important alternative data point in the report.

### 7. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (with a bearish sentiment tilt)**

Fundamental alt data is intact and slightly constructive (100% beat rate, accelerating surprises, rising revisions, supply-constrained framing, healthy hiring). But **every forward-looking sentiment and attention series is deteriorating** — brand search at 12-month lows with no pre-earnings ramp, consumer GPU interest −65% off peak, "AI bubble" fear at a 12-month high, news tone −0.50 and dominated by an unresolved board investigation and a DOJ-referred export-control story.

Net: **fundamentals are not the problem; the multiple is.** The print is likely a beat-and-raise that trades on the governance headline, not the numbers. Asymmetry favors patient buyers *only* after either (a) a favorable conclusion to the board probe, or (b) a genuine sentiment washout — which social data says has not yet occurred.

### 8. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM-LOW**

**Why not higher:** patent filings unavailable (2 failed attempts), web-traffic proxy returned unrelated domains, no app-store or geospatial reads exist for a B2B chipmaker, hiring data has no time series, and headline-keyword sentiment is a crude proxy that mis-tags the scandal. Google Trends measures attention, not orders, and has poor predictive power for Data Center revenue.

**Data lag:** Google Trends is weekly through ~Nov 1; news is real-time through Nov 9; the fundamental anchors are the Aug 26 print and Oct 28 guidance. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one — treat this as a sentiment-and-positioning read, not a revenue forecast.
