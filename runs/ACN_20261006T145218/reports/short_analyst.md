## 7. Contrarian Rating: **No Edge** (event-driven tactical short at best)

The engineering-economics of a short here are poor for a new position: the multiple-compression trade (30x → 13x, -68%) is done; the company throws off enough cash to defend the balance sheet for years; and shorting into a 148%-cash-backed earnings stream at 13x requires the impairment cycle and bookings to worsen materially. A fresh outright short meaningfully risks being "right about the business, wrong about the entry."

**Conditional short triggers worth respecting (not pre-committed):**

Absent those, the correct positioning is **underweight/long-duration avoidance, not a short**. The honest verdict on the underlying franchise remains bearish on fundamentals — but as of today's tape, **No Edge**.
