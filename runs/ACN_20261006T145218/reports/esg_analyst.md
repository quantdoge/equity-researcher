## 2. ENVIRONMENTAL RISK ASSESSMENT: **Low**

- **Carbon intensity:** Minimal direct footprint (FY2019 baseline ≈ ~91k tCO₂e Scope 1+2; since cut materially). Scope 3 (air travel, supply chain, and delivered cloud/data-center energy) dominates and is the honest exposure.
- **Transition risk:** Low direct — no material carbon-tax (CBAM/Ireland carbon tax) exposure; EU ETS exempt as a services provider. Upside as a decarbonization consultant.
- **Physical risk:** Low for own assets (leased offices); minor through client supply-chain consulting exposure.
- **Targets:** Net-zero Scope 1+2 by 2025 and full-value-chain by 2040, SBTi-validated — among the best in IT services. Main caveat: audited Scope 3 progress is de-emphasized in newer reports, so verification depth is a minor gap.
- **Bottom line:** E is not a value risk for ACN; it is a modest marketing/ESG-credential strength.

---

## 3. SOCIAL RISK ASSESSMENT: **High**

S is where ACN's value is made and where its liabilities sit:

- **Discrimination/pay litigation:** **(a)** 2026 DOJ settlement of **$25M** — the federal government alleged the federal-services business considered **race and sex in hiring and promotion** (one of the first major DEI-enforcement settlements; reputational multiplier far exceeds the dollar amount). **(b)** Gender-pay class action (2019, headline $2.5T claim) dismissed, but the related ERISA 401(k)-matching parental-leave suit settled for **≈$302.9M in 2021–22** — one of the largest ERISA settlements on record. Pattern: compensation systems repeatedly litigated.
- **Workforce reduction optics:** ~19,000 jobs eliminated (announced Mar-2023, rolled through FY2024) for "efficiency" while CEO pay stayed in the ~$28M+ band — union/shareholder friction and attrition spikes.
- **AI labor disruption:** 799k-person model faces structural AI displacement; CEO Julie Sweet has made AI fluency a promotion requirement — a deliberate but risk-laden reskilling bet.
- **Supply chain / communities:** Long-running criticism over operating for Gulf-state clients and surveillance-adjacent work; moderate, reputational only.
- **Positives:** Historically elite D&I employer (45% female managing-director target; US ethnic-minority representation goals), strong safety record for an office workforce, and best-in-class training.
- **Bottom line:** Scale + repeated EEO/pay settlements + layoff/AI churn = **High** — the controlling ESG risk factor.

## 4. GOVERNANCE QUALITY SCORE: **7 / 10**

**Strengths:** Separate CEO (Julie Sweet) and board chair (no duality); board overwhelmingly independent non-executives with deep global-operations diversity; strong audit/risk/compliance infrastructure; clean anti-corruption record; transparent earnings calls and structured capital allocation (note $4.99B senior-notes issuance adds modest leverage).
**Weaknesses:**

1. **Tax domicile optics:** Irish plc structure is legal but a perennial governance-reputation drag; Pillar Two top-up forces effective tax to ~15% from 2024 — removes most of the arbitrage but invites ongoing scrutiny.
2. **Pay-for-performance alignment:** Equity plan dissents at AGMs (low-single-digit % votes against) during mass layoffs signal weak alignment between comp and workforce outcomes.
3. **Compliance control gap:** The DOJ $25M settlement indicates EEO controls in the federal business did not keep pace with stated D&I values — a governance, not just social, failure.
4. **Structural concentration:** Founder-free, widely held register is a positive; long-tenured insider CEO line (20+ years at firm) is a minor independence drag.

Score held at **7/10** (strong process, dinged on tax optics, comp alignment, and the DOJ compliance failure) — versus a hypothetical 8–9 for a clean-scoring services peer.
