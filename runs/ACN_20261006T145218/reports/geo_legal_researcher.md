## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**1. Middle East / Iran–Israel war escalation — Probability: High (materializing) / Impact: High**
The Iran war has already hit Q3 FY26 Middle East revenue by **~$400M**, driven bookings down ~2–3% in local currency, and triggered a share drop of >17% on guidance cuts (Reuters). FY27 organic growth guide of only 1–3.5% reflects this drag (Seeking Alpha). Accenture has material direct operations in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Israel — further escalation, sanctions on Gulf states, or project cancellations would compound the hit.

**2. US federal-contractor enforcement & national-security scrutiny — Probability: High / Impact: High**
Serving the FBI, DoD and RSA means ACN is in the crosshairs of the DOJ's **Civil Rights Fraud Initiative** (settled DEI allegations for **$25M** in 2025 — $11.6M restitution + interest — under False Claims Act exposure) and now the **Oct 2026 FBI data-breach incident**, where an Accenture contractor was removed over exposure of personal data of thousands of FBI employees (Reuters). AFS's ~$10B federal book makes suspension, debarment, contract-rescission or follow-on FCA litigation a material downside scenario.

**3. AI-driven price deflation & US–China decoupling of IT services — Probability: Medium-High / Impact: Medium**
AI is compressing IT-services pricing ("AI deflation") — Accenture's own FY27 guidance is 1–3.5% organic growth — while US tariffs, H-1B tightening and US-China tech decoupling pressure the India/China delivery model. This is structural margin, not headline risk, but it shapes multi-year growth.
