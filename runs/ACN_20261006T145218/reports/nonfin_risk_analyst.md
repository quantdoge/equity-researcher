## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Cybersecurity breach and client-data custody — LIVE.** An **Accenture contractor was removed from the FBI** (Oct 2026) over a "damaging" breach exposing sensitive personal data of thousands of bureau employees. Combined with the **2021 LockBit ransomware breach** (~6TB exfiltrated), this is a pattern: the company whose flagship practice is *cybersecurity services* has now been a repeat breach victim and the cause of a client data compromise at a national-security agency.
2. **US government client concentration / procurement cuts.** Pentagon's post-DOGE $5B consulting-term cuts explicitly target firms like ACN; federal contracts were previously a growth cushion and are now a contraction risk.
3. **AI-driven business-model disruption and pricing deflation.** FY27 organic growth guidance is only **1–3.5%**; analysts flag AI deflation compressing revenue-per-employee and client pricing power ("canary in a coal mine"). Talent pyramid (~800k staff, heavy offshore delivery) creates attrition, wage, and immigration-compliance risk at scale.
