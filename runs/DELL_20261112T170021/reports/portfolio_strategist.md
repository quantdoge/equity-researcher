### 2. Consensus Assessment: **LOW / SPLIT** — a genuine two-camp standoff

**The split is not noise; it is a methodology disagreement, and it is the whole question.** Value discounts *trailing* FCF (currently depressed by a working-capital drag) and gets $285. Growth capitalises *forward* EPS (currently at a possible cyclical peak) and gets $434–570. Neither is wrong; both are incomplete. Midpoint of a blend of the two credible anchors is roughly **$390–430**, i.e. ~5–15% above spot.

**Where all eleven agents actually agree (this is the real signal):**
1. **Gross margin compression is structural**, not cyclical — 23.8% → 22.2% → 20.0% → ~19.4% LTM, with a 17.75% trough. Every single agent flags it as the swing variable.
2. **No fraud.** Beneish, accruals, auditor, and restatement checks are clean across four independent agents. The Short Analyst explicitly declines to allege manipulation.
3. **Credit is safe; the equity is not.** BBB+, 1.24x net leverage, refinancing cliff removed — but 2.6 beta, 88% vol, negative book equity.
4. **The AI server business is a pass-through with no pricing power.** Value, Sector, Growth, Short, and ESG converge on this independently.
5. **24-Nov is binary.** Four agents name it as the determining event.

**Where they truly conflict:** what multiple to pay for earnings whose durability is unknown. I resolve this **in favour of the bear case on duration and the bull case on price**: the earnings *are* real and under-modelled (85.7% beat rate is the hardest data in the file), but they are not *permanent*, and paying a compounder multiple for them is an error the market has already partly corrected.

**Two cross-report data corrections I am applying:**
- **Short Analyst's valuation case is ~25% overstated.** It uses a $502 price (27-Oct); at $373, EV/EBITDA is **14.5x not 19x**, FY27E P/E is **14.4x not 19.4x**, EV/Sales **~1.5x not 1.8x**. A third of the bear's valuation argument has already been paid out.
- **Macro's $250–280 bear target rests on a stale forward EPS.** At 15x the current $25.92 consensus you get **$389**. Getting to $250–280 requires a multiple reset *and* an EPS cut. That is a real scenario but it is not "the 15x area on forward EPS" — it is 15x on a *broken* forward number.
- **Backlog conflict resolved:** $95B (Q2 FY27) is correct; the $51.3B used in two reports is stale. **FY27 guide conflict unresolved:** $165–169B (company-confirmed) vs $192–194.6B (Growth's figure). I use **$165–169B for margin-of-safety purposes** and treat the higher number as the post-Q2 raise.
