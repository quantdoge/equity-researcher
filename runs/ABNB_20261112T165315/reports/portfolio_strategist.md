## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — but a clean, three-way split**

This is not noisy disagreement; the agents divide along consistent lines:

- **Business momentum — HIGH AGREEMENT (bullish).** Sector, Growth, Quant, Short and Alt Data all concede the franchise is genuinely reaccelerating: revenue +19%, GBV +21%, 41.5% GAAP op margin, net-cash, share count shrinking. The forensic short explicitly declines the trade. **This is the strongest consensus in the pack**, because it is the one backed by primary-source Q3 filings.
- **Valuation — HIGH AGREEMENT (negative).** Value (IV $170), Quant (Value 25/100, P/E 45.4 vs peer median 24.6 = **+85% premium**), Growth (won't pay up), Macro (~30x fwd), Short (~29x 2027E). **Nobody underwrites upside from here on multiple expansion.** Mean analyst PT ~$199 is only ~9% away — that is not a mispricing.
- **Risk — HIGH AGREEMENT (elevated).** Geo Legal, Nonfin, ESG and Macro independently land on HIGH, and they converge on *the same mechanism*: third parties can revoke ABNB's "inventory" (supply) with no asset to defend. Governance blocks any corrective response.

**Resolution of conflicts:**
- *Growth BUY vs. Value HOLD* → The Growth case is about the **earnings trajectory**; the Value case is about the **payoff**. Both can be true. Growth's own numbers reconcile to Value's: consensus already fades to +12.2% (2027) and **+7.9% by 2030** — the DCF's fade assumption is the consensus assumption, so there is no informational edge in the bull case.
- *Short "NO EDGE" vs. Geo Legal/Nonfin "HIGH"* → Different questions. Short tested **integrity** and found none; the risk agents are testing **terminal value**. Integrity risk is low; structural-supply risk is high. Both stand.
- *Sector ATTRACTIVE vs. Geo Legal HIGH* → Sector rated the **sub-sector**; the risk sits at the **company/jurisdiction** level. Net: good neighbourhood, dangerous address.

**My aggregation:** Business quality **8/10 · Valuation 3/10 · Risk 3/10.** Great company, full price, fat left tail with a governance structure that prevents remediation.

This is explicitly an **underweight** relative to what the earnings momentum alone would justify — the discount is for the regulatory/terminal-value risk that no agent could price.

**Explicitly rejected: a 3–5% "high conviction" weight.** That framework tier requires both high conviction *and* low risk. ABNB has the opposite risk profile, and Value's price work removes the return.

**Fundamental thesis-invalidation triggers (any two → exit regardless of price):**
