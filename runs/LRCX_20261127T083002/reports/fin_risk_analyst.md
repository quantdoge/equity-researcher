### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **INVESTMENT GRADE (strong)**
**Rating: Investment Grade. Probability of financial distress: negligible over the next 3 years.**

LRCX is in a **net cash position** — $5,579M cash vs. $4,122M gross debt → **net debt of –$1,458M**. Gross leverage is **0.47x EBITDA**, net leverage **–0.17x**. Interest is effectively covered by interest income (FY26 interest expense reported as $0; net interest income positive), so interest coverage is *de minimis* rather than merely comfortable. Corroboration: S&P upgraded LRCX to **'A' from 'A-'** on AI-driven scale, projecting adjusted FOCF >$8.5B in FY27 and ~$12B in FY28 (senior unsecured previously BBB+).

The binding test is empirical: in the **FY2024 downcycle revenue fell 14.5%** (17,427 → 14,905), operating margin held at 28.6%, and LRCX still produced **$4.26B FCF** with net cash intact and ~24x interest coverage. The company's cost structure survives a real downturn without touching the balance sheet.

**A 20% revenue decline does not threaten solvency on any metric.** EBIT stays $5.7B, leverage rises to 0.68x — still investment-grade by a wide margin. Even a 50% collapse leaves leverage at 2.1x with $5.6B cash. The only genuine casualty is **shareholder returns**: dividends + buybacks were **$5,122M (105% of FCF)** in FY26, so a downturn forces a buyback cut — a capital-allocation decision, not a credit event. Working capital would *release* cash in a downcycle (FY24 WC was +$360M inflow vs –$1,914M drag in FY26), cushioning FCF.

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **LOW** (solvency) / **HIGH** (market risk)
**Blended: LOW-to-MODERATE financial risk.** Balance-sheet risk is close to the floor for a cyclical: net cash, 0.47x gross leverage, 5.40 Z-score, 8/9 F-score, 2.63 current ratio, 'A' rating. The residual risk in LRCX is *cyclicality of revenue and multiple*, transmitted through a 2.35 beta — not through the liability side.

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **China concentration (highest-severity operating risk):** China was **35% of September-quarter revenue**, guided *"meaningfully lower"* in the December quarter as domestic spending normalizes. A geopolitical/export-control step-change is the most plausible trigger for a >20% revenue shock, and it can arrive non-linearly.
2. **FCF conversion deterioration:** FCF/net income fell to **67%** (FY26) from 101% (FY25); FCF margin 29.4% → 21.1%. Driver is a **–$1,914M working-capital drag** (receivables +58% y/y to $5,340M vs revenue +26%). Piotroski F4 (accruals quality) is the sole failed test. Period-end DSO ≈ 84 days vs 67 days FY25 — largely a strong-exit-run-rate artifact (68 days on average receivables), but this is the metric to monitor for a demand-quality signal.
3. **Capital returns exceed FCF:** $5,122M returned vs $4,891M FCF; cash fell $812M. Sustainable at peak earnings, not through a trough — buyback capacity is the shock absorber, and its removal is itself a downside equity catalyst.
4. **Valuation leaves no margin of safety:** ~32x FY26 EPS ($185.01 price / $5.76) and a **2.1% FCF yield** price in sustained AI-driven growth. Combined with beta 2.35 and 60% vol, de-rating risk dominates credit risk.
