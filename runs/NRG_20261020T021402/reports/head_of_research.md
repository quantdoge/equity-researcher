### Investment Memo: NRG — October 20, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: **Medium**

**Investment Thesis**
NRG is a genuinely scarce asset — ~25 GW of dispatchable generation bought at ~$920/kW against $1,500–2,000/kW replacement cost, bolted to the #1 US competitive retail book (~8M customers) — but it is a **leverage story dressed as a growth story**. At ~4.3x pro-forma net leverage (above Fitch's 3.5x downgrade trigger), with $162M of cash and ~1.4x GAAP interest coverage, the equity is a levered stub on a data-center demand supercycle, discounted at a 10Y yield above 5%. The 47% drawdown has already removed the AI premium; at ~95% of base-case intrinsic value, the stock offers no margin of safety into a binary earnings event 11 trading days away. Wait for the print.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**

- **Guidance credibility is breaking**: FY26 midpoint requires **H2 EPS of ~$5.92 (+57% YoY)** after a −33% H1; consensus already forecasts below the floor; two straight misses driven by exactly the deal costs (interest, D&A) that will persist.
