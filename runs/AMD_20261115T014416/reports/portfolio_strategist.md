## 2. Consensus Assessment

**Overall agreement: MEDIUM — but the split is *quality vs. price*, not bull vs. bear on the company. This distinction is the whole investment decision.**

- **Near-unanimous on the business (HIGH agreement, 9 of 11 agents).** Data Center +97%, GM 51.7%→53.9% GAAP / 56% non-GAAP, operating leverage real, balance sheet pristine, share gains genuine (Google Cloud EPYC host, OpenAI/Meta 6GW). Even the Short Analyst could not find a fraud — M-score −2.761, F-score 8/9, falling DSO. **There is no accounting, solvency, or franchise-integrity dispute in this file.** That is unusual and it removes the category of risk that normally justifies a hard pass.
- **Near-unanimous on the entry price (HIGH agreement, 8 of 11).** Value (FV $295), Fin Risk (valuation "Critical"), Quant (value 4/4 worst), Short (19x sales on unanalysable revenue), Macro (no multiple cushion), Alt Data (frothy), ESG (neutral, no offset), Nonfin (insider selling at highs). **No agent argues the stock is cheap.** The bull case is entirely a forward-EPS argument — 20x FY2028E — resting on estimates with **~2× dispersion** ($105.9–205.7B FY28 revenue range).
- **Contested view — the growth trajectory.** Growth sees FY27 $96.4B (+85%). Quant correctly flags the +30.5% "revision momentum" Growth cites as a **fiscal-year roll-forward artefact, not a same-period revision**. I discount that input. The genuine revision evidence is only the PT cluster — which is *relative* and already stale.
- **Contested view — the AI moat.** Value/Short say AMD is a price-taking second source *paying customers in equity*; Growth/Sector say 6GW anchors de-risk the roadmap. **I side with Value on the economics** (warrants = revenue purchased with equity; true GM on OpenAI-attributable revenue is unanalysable) **and with Growth on the volume** (the revenue is real and contracted). Net: real revenue, understated cost, and ~10% dilution the per-share math must absorb.

**Conflict resolutions applied:**
1. **No fraud override needed** — Short's forensic work clears the name. Nonfin's "revenue quality" flag is an *earnings-quality* haircut, not a disqualifier.
2. **The warrant dilution (Nonfin/Short) overrides Growth's per-share math.** Growth's "$880 bull / 25x FY29 EPS $35.19" ignores up to ~10% incremental shares. Haircut that bull case to roughly **$790–800**.
3. **Fin Risk's FCF collapse outranks Growth's EPS acceleration.** Revenue +45% with OCF/NI at 0.49x and inventory +45% is the single most important new fact in the file. It is the classic pre-writedown signature — and AMD already ran this exact play in Q2'25 ($800M MI308 charge, GM 54%→39.8%).
4. **Macro's real-yield argument overrides the sell-side PT cluster.** Targets set in a $449–489 tape are not a defence against a 50bp real-yield shock.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### **1.0% of portfolio — starter only. Hard cap 2.5%.**

**Rationale (Kelly-framed):** My probability-weighted expected return is only modestly positive with enormous dispersion: ~40% bull ($790–880, +50–65%), ~40% base ($450–520, −3% to −16%), ~20% bear ($290–400, −25% to −45%) → **expected value ≈ +5%**, against a 76% annualised volatility and a −37.7% realised one-year drawdown. Kelly on an edge that thin, with that variance, produces a fraction of a percent. **The framework's "0.5–1% or pass" band applies, and I take the top of it — not the 3–5% the growth case would justify.**

**Three independent reasons to cap small:**

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector / concentration — the binding constraint.** AMD's correlations (252d) are **0.66 to MU, 0.62 to AVGO, 0.48 to NVDA, 0.66 to SPY**. If the book already holds NVDA + AVGO + MU, a 1% AMD position does not diversify anything — it stacks a fourth layer of the same AI-capex factor. **Rule: total AI-compute sleeve (NVDA + AVGO + MU + AMD + TSM) should not exceed ~12% of the portfolio; AMD's share of that sleeve should not exceed 15%.** If the sleeve is already full, fund AMD from NVDA, not from cash.

**The one genuine diversification argument:** AMD's 0.48 correlation to NVDA is the lowest pair in the complex, and AMD is *structurally long* NVDA's stumble (the 19% relative outperformance in the week to 13 Nov is the mechanism). **Owning AMD against a larger NVDA position is a relative-value trade inside AI compute** — that, not absolute upside, is the most defensible portfolio role for this name. Size it as a hedge leg, not a core holding.

**Factor exposure — directionally negative for most books.** AMD is a large positive loading on **momentum** (12-1 +127.8%), **growth**, and **beta**; a large negative loading on **value** (P/E 201.5x trailing, P/FCF 129.7x, P/TBV ~31x) and **low-volatility** (76.2% vol, worst in peer set). Adding AMD increases unintended momentum/growth tilt and *reduces* portfolio quality-of-earnings. **If the book is already momentum-heavy (it likely is, given 2026's tape), this position should be sourced from another momentum name.**

**Correlation to existing risk factors:** 0.66 to SPY means AMD is, for practical purposes, a 3.6x levered S&P position with idiosyncratic AI-capex risk layered on top (R² only 0.447 — two-thirds of the vol is stock-specific). It is **not** a diversifier; it is an amplifier.

**Liquidity:** none. $874B cap, 15–50M shares/day. No capacity constraint at any institutional size.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Hard stop-loss: $398 (close basis).**
Anchored to the 5 Nov low of $404.40 — a decisive break confirms both the momentum failure and the de-rating regime that Fin Risk and Macro describe. **−25.5% from $534**, or **−26bp of portfolio** on a 1.0% position. That is a tolerable loss for a 1% starter. *Trailing version: once the position is above +25%, raise the stop to breakeven; above +40%, trail at 20%.*

**Thesis invalidation — exit fully on any one of these (fundamental, not price):**
1. **Cash conversion:** OCF/NI below 0.7x for **two consecutive** quarters, or inventory days above **220** (now ~199). This is the highest-priority trigger — Fin Risk and Short both flag it, and AMD has a demonstrated $800M/800bp-GM precedent.
2. **Helios/ramp:** Q4'26 revenue below **$16.0B**, or non-GAAP gross margin below **54%**, or any disclosed deferral of an OpenAI/Meta 6GW tranche.
3. **Policy:** any named AMD distributor or ODM channel inside a BIS diversion enforcement action; OR the 15% China revenue-share arrangement voided **with** licence loss; OR §232 expanded to cover a broader set of AMD-sourced components.
4. **Macro transmission:** HY OAS above **3.75%** *and* a hyperscaler 2027 capex guidance cut >10% in the same quarter. (Macro's early-warning chain: HY OAS → 10Y TIPS >3.10% → Dec FOMC size → capex revisions → USD >125.)
5. **Dilution:** confirmation of the reported *second* hyperscaler warrant at 160M shares — that would push total potential dilution toward ~20% and break the per-share math outright.

**Trim triggers (take risk off into strength):**
- **Trim 1/3 at $650** — ≈100% of the sell-side median and above my dilution-adjusted bull case's midpoint.
- **Trim another 1/3 above $720** — >90% of the dilution-adjusted bull case (~$790–800).
- **Mechanical rebalance:** if appreciation pushes the position above **2.5%**, cut back to 2.0% regardless of price. With β 3.56, letting this run is how a 2% position becomes 6% of book risk.
- **Time stop:** if neither tranche 2 nor tranche 3 conditions are met within **two quarters**, reduce to 0.5% and reassess. No carry means no reason to hold dead capital.

**Optional hedge (for books where 3.56 beta is unacceptable):** beta-match the 1% position with ~3.5% notional SPY or SMH short, or buy a 6-month 90% put collar funded by selling a 120% call. Given 76% vol, options are expensive — the collar, not the outright put, is the right structure.
