# FORENSIC SHORT ANALYSIS — ADVANCED MICRO DEVICES (AMD)

---

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **MEDIUM**
*(Not High, not Low — and emphatically not Fraudulent)*

I went looking for a fraud and did not find one. What I found instead is a clean balance sheet attached to a business whose *reported* growth is being part-funded by its own equity and whose cash conversion turned sharply negative in the most recent quarter. That is an **earnings-quality** problem, not an accounting-fraud problem, and the distinction matters enormously for position sizing.

The single most important number in this file: **GAAP EPS $1.46 vs. non-GAAP EPS $2.09 in Q3'26 — a 43% gap.** That $1.04B quarterly bridge ($523M SBC + ~$520M intangible amortisation) is annualising near **$4.2 billion** of real economic cost that AMD asks you to ignore. At 115x GAAP earnings, you are not paying for GAAP earnings.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

### 🚩 Flag 1 — Q3'26 cash conversion collapsed to 0.49x
| $M | Q4'25 | Q1'26 | Q2'26 | Q3'26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 10,270 | 10,253 | 11,536 | **13,377** |
| GAAP op. income | 1,752 | 1,476 | 1,990 | 2,832 |
| GAAP net income | 1,511 | 1,383 | 2,297 | 2,428 |
| Operating cash flow | 2,600 | 2,955 | 2,366 | **1,202** |
| **OCF / NI** | 1.72x | 2.14x | 1.03x | **0.49x** |
| Capex | (222) | (389) | (808) | (648) |
| **Free cash flow** | 2,378 | 2,566 | 1,558 | **554** |
| Δ Working capital | (533) | +456 | (730) | **(3,357)** |

A record revenue quarter with record operating income produced **$554M of FCF** on a $876B market cap — a 0.25% annualised FCF yield in the quarter of the *beat*. Q3'26 accruals flipped to **+$1.23B** (9.2% of revenue) after three consecutive negative-accrual quarters. TTM conversion is still 1.20x, so this is a *trend* break, not a level break — but the trend is the thing that precedes problems.

### 🚩 Flag 2 — Inventory build of $3.5B in nine months, ahead of an unproven ramp
Inventory: $7.92B (Q4'25) → $8.05B → $8.47B → **$11.46B (Q3'26)**, +~$3.0B in the September quarter alone, +45% in nine months and up roughly 60–75% YoY against revenue +44.7%. Inventory days (165 in FY25, ~169 in Q3'26) are flat, which is the *only* mitigating fact.

This is the highest-conviction bear data point in the file because **AMD has already demonstrated exactly how this ends.** In Q2 FY2025, an $800M inventory/related charge for MI308 China export restrictions took GAAP gross margin from ~54% to **39.8%** and GAAP operating income to **negative $134M** in a single quarter. The company has a demonstrated, recent, ~800bp gross-margin air-pocket mechanism, and it is now carrying 45% more inventory than it was then. If the MI450/Helios ramp slips one quarter — and this is a product that has already been re-sequenced — $11.5B of inventory re-prices.

### 🚩 Flag 3 — Circular / vendor equity financing of revenue (the Chanos trade)
AMD issued **OpenAI a warrant for up to 160 million shares at $0.01**, vesting in tranches tied to *purchases of AMD Instinct GPUs*, under a 6-gigawatt deployment agreement (8-K, Sept 2025). At $534, that is **~$85 billion of equity consideration against ~9.6% dilution** — transferred to a counterparty that must raise capital in order to pay AMD.

Strip away the structure: **AMD is paying a rebate in stock to a customer to buy its product, and booking the top line at full price.** GAAP gross margin does not reflect this. At a $13.4B quarterly revenue base growing toward $96B in 2027 consensus, the question "what is the *economic* gross margin on OpenAI-attributable revenue?" has no clean answer in the financials. That is the definition of an unanalysable revenue stream, and unanalysable revenue should not command 19x sales.

### 🚩 Flag 4 — Debt raised into a cash burn, buybacks halted, dilution resumed
Total debt went **$4.28B → $8.92B in one quarter** (+$4.64B) while OCF was $1.20B and FCF $0.55B. Net cash of $0.81B flipped to **net debt of $1.51B**. AMD borrowed to hold cash while burning working capital.

Simultaneously: buybacks were $221M (Q1'26), $207M (Q2'26), **$0 (Q3'26)** — versus $1.32B in FY25 and $4.11B in FY22 — while diluted shares rose **1.636B → 1.661B (+1.5% in nine months)** plus a 9.6% warrant overhang. SBC runs **~$2.0B TTM (4.4% of revenue, 26% of GAAP net income)**. Capital allocation is now: pay employees in stock, stop buying back stock, borrow to fund inventory, and hand 160M shares to a customer. Read that sequence twice.

### 🚩 Flag 5 — Segments outside Data Center are shrinking
Data Center $8.5B, +97% YoY = **64% of revenue in one segment, concentrated in a handful of AI labs** (Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, OCI named in the release). Client & Gaming **−8% YoY**; Gaming **−40%** to $773M; Embedded +29% on a base that is still below the 2022 Xilinx level. This is no longer a diversified semiconductor company — it is a leveraged, concentrated bet on AI-lab capex, wearing a diversifier's costume.

### 🚩 Flag 6 — Goodwill & intangibles = 59% of book equity
$40.57B of $68.39B equity. Tangible book $27.8B → **P/TBV ≈ 31.5x.** From the $49B Xilinx deal, roughly $40B of the purchase price remains unamortised/unimpaired. No impairment has been taken, and none is currently indicated at these enterprise values — but there is zero margin of safety beneath the stated equity.

### ✅ What I could NOT find (credit where due)
- **No receivables red flag.** DSO fell from ~98 days (FY24) to ~63 days (Q3'26); AR grew *slower* than revenue. This is the opposite of channel stuffing.
- **No auditor change, no restatement, no related-party self-dealing disclosed, no management control deficiency** — the SOX 302 certification from CFO/CEO is clean, signed Nov 3, 2026.
- **No aggressive capitalisation signal.** Capex is only 4.6% of revenue; D&A is tracking amortisation, not being stretched.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

| Threat | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **CUDA software lock-in** | High | ROCm 10 just shipped. NVIDIA has a 15-year developer moat and an annual cadence. AMD is competing on price/performance and *paying* to do so. |
| **Price-based share capture** | High | Data Center +97% is being achieved partly via 160M warrants to OpenAI. Share gains bought with equity are rented, not owned. |
| **Balance sheet asymmetry** | Medium-High | NVDA at $3.93T can outspend AMD (0.88T) 4.5:1 on R&D and can subsidise customers indefinitely. |
| **Customer credit quality** | High | The end-demand is AI labs raising capital at private marks. Their capex is a financing decision, not a budget line. |
| **Gross margin trajectory** | ✅ **Mitigating** | GAAP GM 51.7% → 53.9% YoY; non-GAAP 56%. Pricing power is *currently* intact — the bull case's strongest pillar. |

Consensus now models AMD revenue of **$96.4B in 2027, $136B in 2028, $181B in 2029, and $242.7B in 2030**. For scale: that 2030 number means **AMD in 2030 ≈ NVIDIA's entire revenue today.** That is not a forecast; it is a hope with a spreadsheet attached.
