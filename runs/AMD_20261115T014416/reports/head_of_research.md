**Investment Thesis**

AMD is a genuine, structurally advantaged #2 in the strongest semiconductor upcycle on record — Data Center +97% YoY, Q4 guided to $17B (+66%), gross margin inflecting through 56% non-GAAP, with anchor 6GW commitments from OpenAI and Meta and a pristine balance sheet. But the stock at $534 prices in flawless execution: ~116x TTM GAAP earnings, 19x sales, 0.8% FCF yield, sitting ~80% above the value team's base-case DCF ($295) and at ~88% of its *bull* case. The deciding facts are new and negative: cash conversion collapsed in Q3'26 (OCF/NI 0.49x, FCF margin 4.1%, inventory +45% to $11.5B) against a Fed that has restarted hiking with 10Y real yields at 2.86% — the worst possible mix for a 2.5–3.6-beta, long-duration asset. Own a little, with rules; do not own a lot.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is attractive but *late-cycle* — WSTS models $1.5tn (2026) → $1.9tn (2027) sales; hyperscaler AI capex +~50% to $1.2–1.3tn in 2027, funded increasingly with debt at record-tight credit spreads (HY OAS 3.20%). The macro skew is unfavourable for the *multiple*: Fed hiking again (3.75–4.00%, December hike likely, 50bp possible), 10Y real yield 2.86% (+79bp since June), USD +3.4% in two months, 25% Section 232 tariff in force. Macro's bear scenario (50bp hike → HY OAS 4.5% → capex re-underwriting) maps to $290–350.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH risk, direction of travel negative. ~22% of revenue in China; TSMC single-source is an existential tail; the 15%-of-China-AI-revenue-to-USG arrangement is legally unprecedented and now sits inside the Neal/Wyden congressional probe. Mitigants: China Instinct already excluded from guidance (~$800M downside pre-framed), Adeia IP suit settled to a licence, no antitrust/FCPA exposure.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL. ROIC 5.5% vs ~10% WACC fails the core test; narrow moat (x86 real, AI accelerator none — CUDA is NVIDIA's); warrant structures mean AMD is *paying for share with equity*. Base DCF $295, bull $604; only $210–240 offers a margin of safety.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (high conviction). Revenue $34.6B FY25 → $52B FY26E → $96B FY27E; Rule of 40 ≈ 70; 7/7 beats, +5.2% avg surprise; 20x FY28E EPS, PEG ~0.8. Caveat: Quant caught Growth's "+30.5% revision momentum" as a fiscal-year roll-forward artefact — genuine revision evidence is thinner than presented, and FY28 revenue estimates span ~2x ($106–206B).
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL — factors in open conflict. Momentum 1/4 (+128% 12-1), but value and low-vol both 4/4 worst (76% vol, β 3.56 1y OLS); Sharpe 2.07 is not statistically significant (t ≈ 1.17). A realised −37.7% drawdown in five weeks proves the de-rating risk is not hypothetical.

The risk is the multiple plus deteriorating cash quality: FCF margin 25.0% → 13.5% → **4.1%** over three quarters, inventory +45% to $11.46B (DIO ~199), and an unexplained +$4.6B debt raise in one quarter. A −20% revenue scenario keeps the credit investment-grade but implies −45% to −65% on the equity.

The bear case is valuation + capital intensity, not accounting: GAAP vs non-GAAP EPS gap of 43% (~$4.2B/yr of SBC + amortisation bridged out), OCF/NI 0.49x, and revenue part-funded with the company's own equity via warrants. **Bull Case**
1. **Real, accelerating share gains**: Data Center +97% YoY and guided to +66% in Q4; 6GW OpenAI/Meta anchors, Google Cloud EPYC host win, server CPU share mid-40s% vs a shrinking Intel. FY27E $96B is contracted-and-ramping, not speculative.
2. **Margin inflection with operating leverage**: GAAP GM 53.9% / non-GAAP 56%, operating margin 7.4% → 21.2% in eight quarters; at 20x FY28E EPS and PEG ~0.8, growth investors are not obviously overpaying *if* the ramp delivers.
3. **Fortress balance sheet, clean forensics, competitor turmoil**: net cash, 47x coverage, no fraud signal anywhere; AMD is the structural beneficiary of NVIDIA's CEO scandal/diversion probe (+19% relative in one week).

**Bear Case**
1. **Valuation with zero cushion**: 116x TTM EPS, 19x sales, 0.8% FCF yield, ~31x tangible book; price is ~88% of the value team's *bull* case. A 50bp real-yield shock plus HY OAS widening to 4.5% maps to $290–350 (−35–45%).
2. **Cash conversion broke while revenue accelerated**: FCF margin 4.1% in the "beat" quarter, inventory +45% with DIO ~199 — and AMD has already shown how this ends (Q2'25: $800M charge, GM 54% → 39.8% in one quarter).
3. **Equity-subsidised revenue and insider behaviour**: ~10% (possibly ~20%) warrant dilution to customers, 43% GAAP/non-GAAP gap, $239M of CEO selling with zero buys, buybacks halted while debt doubled — capital allocation is moving the wrong way at the top of the tape.

**Key Risks** (with monitoring triggers)
1. **Macro transmission**: December FOMC 50bp; HY OAS >3.75%; 10Y TIPS >3.10%; any hyperscaler 2027 capex cut >10%.
2. **Helios/ramp miss** (Feb 2, 2027): revenue <$16.0B or non-GAAP GM <54% or any OpenAI/Meta tranche deferral.
3. **Working capital**: OCF/NI <0.7x for two consecutive quarters, or inventory days >220.
4. **Dilution**: confirmation of the second hyperscaler warrant (~160M shares) — breaks the per-share math.
5. **Policy**: AMD channel named in a BIS diversion action; the 15% China arrangement voided *with* licence loss; §232 expansion; Taiwan tail risk.
6. **Competitive**: CUDA/ROCm gap persists; custom ASICs (TPU/MTIA) cap merchant share before AMD reaches 20%.
