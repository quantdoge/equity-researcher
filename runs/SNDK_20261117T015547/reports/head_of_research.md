**Investment Thesis**
Sandisk is a fortress-balance-sheet NAND pure-play enjoying the most violent scarcity windfall in memory history (85% gross margin, $88.9B contracted backlog, $12.8B customer collateral) — but every dollar of that margin is a shortage rent with a disclosed expiration date: supply outpaces demand from H2 2027. At $809 the market has already discarded peak earnings; our probability-weighted work says it has not discarded enough. This is a cycle-timing call, not a fraud call — the cash is real, solvency risk is nil, and the bear case is valuation, not viability.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: A barbell — superb nominal macro (AI capex, scarcity pricing power) against a hostile discount-rate macro (10Y at 5.13%, Fed hiking, CPI 3.82%); the 66% drawdown maps ~1:1 to the real-yield backup. Sector Neutral with negative skew: record pricing and a 4–5% 2026 supply deficit, but sequential growth decelerating hard (158%→51%→23%→13%E) and the supply response is funded, disclosed and dated to H2 2027.

Normalization math: 15x on $2–3B mid-cycle earnings = $30–45B market cap, −73% to −82%. Timing is the whole game; the turn is a 2027 event.

**Bull Case**
- The contract architecture breaks the historical bust template: ~50% of FY27 and ~two-thirds of FY28 bits pre-committed (95% investment-grade), $88.9B RPO and $12.8B collateral convert a demand shock into a timing issue.
- Structural AI demand plus enforced supply discipline: 2027 NAND capacity ~40% below 2022 as wafers shift to DRAM/HBM, and 5.5%+ financing costs paradoxically enforce capex restraint — the shortage could run past the dated flip and violently re-rate a 3.6x forward P/E.
- HBF (High Bandwidth Flash) is a free option on a new, non-commodity memory tier; first customer-qualification news in 2027 re-rates the multiple.

**Bear Case**
- Fixed-COGS leverage cuts both ways: wafers are purchased (PP&E just $705M), so ~100% of ASP movement drops to gross profit. A 50% ASP decline takes quarterly gross profit from ~$9.4B to ~$3.9B; take-or-pay commitments and $3.0B of inventory (170 days) add impairment risk on top.
- Consensus "E" is an extrapolation, not an estimate: FY28 EPS of $291 assumes a 67% net margin persists; memory estimates are routinely cut 60–80% within two quarters of a turn. On mid-cycle EPS of $37–47, today's price is ~17–22x normalized — not 4x.
- Behaviour at the top is confirming: ~$9.5B of buybacks at $1,100–2,300, a $31B Japan capex commitment, a peak-cycle 8-for-1 split, and $105M of insider sales against zero purchases.

**Key Risks**
*To the SELL call (what makes us wrong):*
1. Right-tail extension — LTAs and capacity discipline push the flip past 2028 while a Fed pause delivers simultaneous EPS upgrades and multiple expansion (Macro's bull scenario exceeds the June high).
2. Squeeze mechanics — a $5B/quarter buyback bid, 3.5% short interest and the 20-Nov split liquidity event can produce violent countertrend rallies.
3. Mid-cycle reset higher than modelled — if consolidation and QLC substitution hold normalized GM at 45–50%, $809 is defensible.

*To any residual position:*
1. H2 2027 supply flip colliding with a hawkish Fed and a hyperscaler capex guide-down.
2. Antitrust conduct remedies and/or Section 232 expansion to NAND.
3. Correlated counterparty failure inside the 8-customer backlog (the collateral is exposed to the same AI-capex event as the revenue).
4. Kioxia single-site supply shock (seismic/financial). 5. Post-split retail-flow volatility; the 4-Feb-2027 print is the next hard catalyst.
