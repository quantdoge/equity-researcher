## SNDK (Sandisk Corporation) — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment

**Prepared:** Nov 2026 | **Sources:** FY2026 Form 10-K (filed 8/17/2026), Q1 FY2027 Form 10-Q (filed 10/30/2026), White House/EY/CBP Section 232 guidance, Bloomberg/CNBC/SCMP geopolitical feeds. *Note: SEC.gov and IR-hosted PDFs returned HTTP 403; filing content was obtained via indexed full-text renderings and XBRL taxonomies.*

### Company Snapshot (risk-relevant)
- NAND flash pure-play, spun off from Western Digital 2/21/2025; Milpitas, CA; ~11,100 employees across **33 countries**.
- **82% of FY2026 net revenue was international.** Headcount by region: **74% Asia-Pacific, 19% Americas, 7% EMEA** (10-K FY2026).
- **All NAND wafers are sourced from Flash Ventures** — three JVs with Kioxia in Japan (49.9% owned; ~80% of capacity sits in Kioxia-owned fabs; all co-terminate 12/31/2034). Committed to pay Kioxia **$1.2B in 2026–2029** for manufacturing services/supply continuity.
- Q1 FY2027: revenue $11.03B, net income $8.10B, **RPO $88.9B**, **$12.8B of customer collateral** held at third-party FIs (accessible only on customer default).

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**① U.S. tariff & export-control regime volatility — Probability: HIGH (already in force) | Impact: HIGH**
- A **25% ad valorem Section 232 duty on certain advanced computing chips and "derivative products" took effect 1/15/2026** (Presidential Proclamation 1/14/2026; CBP CSMS #67400472; EY Global Tax News). SSDs/embedded flash bundled into servers and AI systems are directly in scope of "derivatives."
- SanDisk imports from Japan (wafers) and ships back-end output from Malaysia/China into the U.S. — it is structurally a **tariff-exposed importer** with limited U.S. manufacturing (PP&E only ~$705M).
- Export-control policy is in open flux: **BIS suspended the Affiliates Rule on 11/12/2025** as part of a U.S.–China arrangement, while Commerce Secretary Lutnick is under Senate scrutiny over chip export controls (Bloomberg, 11/12/2026) and an unprecedented **Xi–Trump–Putin summit** is scheduled (CNBC, 11/15/2026). Rules can re-tighten with little notice; the 10-K flags "evolving trade policies, tariff regimes, and trade wars" as risk factor #1.

**② China retaliation and JV/customer exposure — Probability: MEDIUM–HIGH | Impact: MEDIUM–HIGH**
- **48% equity interest in the Unis Venture**, which markets and sells SanDisk products in China (partner: Unissoft Wuxi Group, Tsinghua Unigroup lineage) — equity-method, one quarter in arrears. This is both a revenue channel and a compliance vector.
- Precedent risk: Beijing's 2023 CAC ban on Micron products and its habit of impeding U.S. semiconductor deals via SAMR. China merger/antitrust posture moved **materially interventionist in 2025–26** (Slaughter and May).
- New **MOFCOM draft Special Provisions on Protecting Chinese Trade Secrets (10/26/2026)** and China's 2026 supply-chain security/counter-extraterritoriality rules raise the cost of maintaining China operations and complicate IP/tech transfer (SCMP; Freshfields).

**③ Japan single-site supply concentration (Kioxia/Flash Ventures) — Probability: LOW–MEDIUM | Impact: VERY HIGH**
- Effectively **100% of NAND wafer supply from one alliance in one country**. Earthquake/seismic, power, or Kioxia-specific financial stress is an existential supply shock; SanDisk bears ~50% of fixed costs *regardless of volume* and must fund ~50% of capex when JV cash flow is short.
- ¥/USD volatility is live: Finance Minister Katayama warned against "one-sided and rapid" yen moves (WSJ, 11/12/2026). Yen weakness inflates JV cost translation and the JPY-denominated commitments.
- Mitigant: JV terms locked to 12/31/2034, and the $88.9B RPO backlog gives unusual forward visibility.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights
- **Litigation: no material pending matters disclosed.** FY2026 10-K Item 3 "Legal Proceedings" (p.38) and 10-Q Part II Item 1 appear routine; no DOJ/SEC/FTC investigation disclosed. *Gap: full legal-proceedings text could not be retrieved (SEC 403) — recommend direct EDGAR review before publishing.*
- **Legacy securities class action resolved:** SanDisk LLC settled for **$50M**; claim deadline has passed (sandisksecuritieslitigation.com; Daily Journal). Historical — relates to pre-spin NAND disclosures.
- **IP:** prior ITC §337 action *Memory Technologies v. Sandisk* (337-TA-1034) closed 2017; continued patent docket activity per Justia, none material.
- **Tax/structural:** current income tax payable **$1.51B** + non-current **$431M**; multi-jurisdiction exposure (JP, MY, SG, CN, IN, GB). Flash Ventures cost-sharing and the WDC separation create **transfer-pricing and spin-off tax indemnity** exposure — a genuinely underappreciated tail risk.
- **Trade compliance:** dual exposure to BIS/EAR (U.S.) and MOFCOM dual-use/export-control lists (China).
- **Regulatory posture:** no consumer data-privacy regime of consequence (hardware), but the 10-K newly flags **"risks associated with the use of artificial intelligence in our business operations."**

## 4. Compliance Red Flags
1. **China JV governance** — a 48% minority stake in a distribution JV with a Tsinghua Unigroup-linked partner, in a jurisdiction actively criminalizing unauthorized transfer of trade secrets. Highest FCPA/anti-corruption and IP-leakage watch item. Confirm whether the JV books revenue that flows through consolidated sales (product-concentration risk factor suggests it is material).
2. **Tariff-origin/transshipment scrutiny** — Japan-to-Malaysia-to-U.S. routing under a 25% Section 232 duty invites CBP origin and valuation challenges; the 10-Q explicitly flags "the impact of tariffs, trade policies and related governmental actions."
3. **NBM contract structures** — $88.9B RPO supported by **$12.8B of third-party customer collateral** (not on balance sheet; released only on customer default/breach). This is a novel, opaque credit-support architecture that will attract auditor and, potentially, SEC scrutiny if counterparties are AI-neocloud or China-linked.
4. **Shanghai subsidiary in run-off** — residual held-for-sale assets and divestiture accounting create disclosure/impairment friction.
5. **Unis-related receivables** disclosed as a customer-concentration item — verify collection and sanctions screening of end customers.

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale:** Litigation and regulatory *enforcement* risk is genuinely low — clean legal docket, no investigations, a clean post-spin capital structure. But **geopolitical/structural risk is High**: 82% international revenue, ~100% of wafer supply from a single alliance in a single country, direct exposure to a live 25% Section 232 tariff on semiconductor derivatives, a China distribution JV in a jurisdiction turning more hostile to foreign IP holders, and a U.S.–China policy apparatus in visible disarray (Lutnick scrutiny, suspended Affiliates Rule, three-way summit). The $88.9B backlog and Kioxia lock-up to 2034 offset this materially — but those same contracts convert a supply or tariff shock into a contractual-liability event rather than just a demand miss.

*Note: a Chinese export-control move on Japan-based capacity or a re-tightening of BIS rules would push this to **Very High** quickly.*
