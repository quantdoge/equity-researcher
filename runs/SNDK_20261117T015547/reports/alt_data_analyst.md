Peak-cycle multiple compression, ~2–3 quarters ahead of the fundamental data. Alt data confirms current-quarter strength; it cannot confirm the out-quarter.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why medium, not high:** the price/attention/social signals are high-quality and mutually corroborating (multiple independent feeds, same inflection). The demand-side signals are directionally clear.

**Data limitations and lag — read before using:**
- **Google Trends "SanDisk" conflates the company with consumer SD cards**, so it measures retail/DIY attention and speculative interest far more than enterprise NAND demand. It is a *sentiment* proxy here, not a revenue proxy — enterprise is now the growth engine ($4.8B data center vs. consumer cards).
- **"NAND flash price" trends series is unusable post-August** (zero-filled). Substituted TrendForce/NAND Research contract-pricing commentary, which is *monthly and reported with a 2–4 week lag*.
- **Web traffic is only sandisk.com** — enterprise/cloud procurement does not transact through the website, so this over-weights consumer.
- **Patent data unavailable** (USPTO API failed twice) — R&D-intensity signal missing entirely.
- **Hiring data is a search proxy**, not counted requisitions; no YoY baseline.
- **News sentiment score of +1.00 is a keyword artifact** and should be disregarded as a directional input. I weight the underlying headline content instead.
- **Social sentiment is search-proxy only** — no quantitative message-volume or bull/bear ratio.
- **Split mechanics:** 8-for-1 effective ~Nov 19–20; all prices above are pre-split and not comparable to post-split quotes.
- **Earnings event risk:** Nvidia's print is imminent and is the dominant near-term driver of SNDK — an exogenous factor alt data cannot forecast.

**Thesis in one line:** Fundamentals at a genuine cyclical peak, leading indicators six months past peak, multiple already down 66% but arguably still not at cycle-trough valuation — alt data says **no urgency to catch this knife until attention and surprise cadence stabilize; watch for Google Trends troughing and the Feb 4 guide as the confirmation points.**
