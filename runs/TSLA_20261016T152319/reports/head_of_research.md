### Investment Memo: TSLA (Tesla, Inc.) — October 16, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL — do not initiate; reduce/exit existing positions | Conviction: **Medium** (fundamental case High, timing Medium due to squeeze risk)
**Price Target**: **$300 base case (−16% from current $358.40)**, range **$260–330**; upside **$440–490** only if autonomy revenue *and* auto margins inflect simultaneously
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Tesla's core auto business is being competed to zero margin — FY25 revenue fell for the first time in history (−2.9%), operating margin halved to 4.6% (Q2'26 print: 1.4%), and returns (ROIC 4.8%) are now below WACC — yet the stock trades at ~332x trailing earnings and ~0.5% FCF yield, capitalising ~$1.4T on unproven robotaxi/Optimus/energy optionality. The bull thesis is real but is precisely what the price already embeds, and its critical path runs through the regulators investigating the company (DOJ criminal probe, NHTSA 3.2M-vehicle engineering analysis, Cybercab self-certification audit). With the 10Y at ~5% and rising, the macro, legal, governance, and valuation tapes all point the same direction: risk-off.
