## 1. Credit Risk Assessment: **INVESTMENT GRADE (Fortress Balance Sheet)**

Tesla's leverage is minimal by any measure: debt/equity of **0.10x**, debt/assets of **0.06x**, and a **net cash position of ~$35.7B** (cash + short-term investments of $44.1B vs. total debt of only $8.4B). Net debt/EBITDA is **−3.0x**. Interest coverage of **12.9x** is comfortable (EBIT $5.6B vs. interest expense $0.34B). By all computed metrics the company is solidly investment-grade — leverage is *lower* than that of key peer Toyota (D/E 1.05x, net debt/EBITDA 3.96x). The credit risk here is not solvency — it is the **concentration of earnings in a deteriorating, price-cutting automotive business** and the risk that prolonged margin destruction erodes EBITDA coverage quality over time.

**Altman Z-Score:** **2.45 (grey zone)** — *but this is an artifact of the book-equity formulation.* The tool's x4 uses book equity ($82.9B), not market cap ($1.4T). Substituting market value of equity yields Z ≈ **17+ (deep safe zone)**. Bindery: on a market basis Tesla has zero distress risk; on a pure accounting basis it sits in the grey zone because book equity understates the equity cushion.

**Piotroski F-Score:** **5/9 — Moderate** (not weak). Failures: ROA not improving, leverage not being reduced, share dilution ongoing (SBC), and no asset-turnover improvement — all consistent with the 2025–26 profitability slide.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Profitability collapse is real and ongoing.** Operating income fell from $13.7B (2022) to $4.4B (2025), and Q2 2026 operating margin printed just **1.4%** — down from 16.8% at the 2022 peak. Q1'26 and Q2'26 operating income ($0.9B / $0.4B) are running ~75% below the FY2025 run-rate. This is price-war-driven margin destruction, not a one-off.
2. **Stock-based compensation = ~75% of net income** ($2.8B SBC vs. $3.8B NI in FY2025). Earnings quality is weak; cash conversion from stated profits is heavily diluted by non-cash compensation, and share-count creep (2.9% YoY share growth) offsets per-share gains.
3. **Valuation detached from fundamentals.** Trailing P/E ~370x, EV/EBITDA ~117x, FCF yield 0.49%. The equity carries no earnings cushion against disappointment.
4. **Revenue is now shrinking** (−2.9% YoY in FY2025 after flat 2024) — the growth narrative that justified the multiple has stalled without offsetting margin gains.
5. **Beta ~2.3 / vol 59%** with a −54% historical drawdown — this is a leveraged play on market direction (and Elon Musk key-man/concentration risk) in a stock that has already demonstrated severe tail behavior.
