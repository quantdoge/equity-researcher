### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk 1 — US-China tech/trade decoupling lands on Tesla's near-shore model** (Prob: High ~65%; Impact: High, hits both China revenue and US import economics)
The Nov 2025 US-China trade deal explicitly **excluded EVs, batteries, and chips from tariff relief** (NBC Washington). Washington has already denied Tesla/GM tariff-relief requests on Chinese content (Reuters), and Tesla has suspended orders for lower-cost China-imported Model 3s in the US until ~March 2027 (GuideAuto). Tesla is uniquely exposed on both sides of the rivalry: Shanghai is its highest-volume plant, yet Chinese-made cars feed US/EU markets and now carry tariffs.

**Risk 2 — China demand erosion + regulatory dependence** (Prob: High ~70%; Impact: Medium-High, ~22% of revenue)
BYD and domestic price wars are compressing Tesla's China share even as FSD gains approvals. China's autonomous-driving regime requires data localization and state cooperation for FSD deployment — a geopolitical control point. Any escalation (e.g., battery/mineral export controls, retaliation over Taiwan) directly hits Shanghai output and China earnings.

**Risk 3 — CEO-anchored political backlash hardens across Europe/Canada** (Prob: High, ongoing; Impact: Medium-High, Europe is the bulk of "Other" ~28%)
Musk's DOGE/Trump alignment triggered boycotts, "Tesla Takedown" protests, arson and showroom vandalism across Europe and Canada; European sales plunged ~49% YoY by May 2025 (CNBC/Reuters), with Germany down ~76% at the trough. This is a demand/sovereign-customer risk (fleet purchases, incentives, charging subsidies) rather than a classic expropriation risk, but it persists structurally.

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **NHTSA FSD probe escalated** — preliminary evaluation (Oct 2024, 2.4M vehicles) upgraded to an **engineering analysis covering ~3.2M vehicles** (Mar 2026) after 9 FSD-linked crashes; December 2025 information request flags FSD's failure to detect camera degradation (sun glare/visibility). Recall risk is the base case, not the tail case (Fast Company/Reuters).
- **DOJ criminal investigation** into Autopilot/FSD marketing — centered on potential **securities and wire fraud** (Reuters, May 2024; ongoing).
- **SEC v. Elon Musk** — lawsuit over untimely disclosure of his Twitter/X stake (filed Jan 2025); **Musk lost his motion to dismiss (Feb 2026)** (Reuters/CNBC). Separate SEC scrutiny of Musk's xAI time-allocation and related-party dealings.
- **Governance/related-party exposure** — Tesla's ~$2B investment in xAI (Jan 2026) and the proposed 2025 "CEO Performance Award" (tied to market cap and xAI milestones) heighten conflict-of-interest and 14a/16b litigation risk.
- **EU/India trade friction** — 9% EU duty on China-built Teslas (on top of 10% standard) forced European Model 3 price hikes; India's ~110% import duty neutered entry (225 sales in 2025).
- **Ongoing** — CA DMV deceptive-advertising claims over "Full Self-Driving," Autopilot wrongful-death/product suits, Grünheide environmental litigation (expansion only preliminarily approved, construction at own risk), and US EV tax-credit ($7,500) rollback reducing demand.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

- **CEO conflict-of-interest architecture**: Musk heads DOGE while his company sits under DOJ/NHTSA/SEC investigations and pursues $2B related-party xAI investments — high-risk optics for enforcement, procurement, and shareholder litigation.
- **China data sovereignty**: FSD requires data localization + CCP cooperation; any miss is an AML/data-privacy/national-security tripwire with severe consequences (mirrors the broader autonomous-driving data crackdown).
- **Marketing-claims exposure**: "FSD/Autopilot" representations are the factual basis of both the NHTSA recall analysis and the DOJ fraud probe — representation risk is live.
- **No material FCPA/AML flags identified** (direct-sales model, no dealer channels), though India/China licensing decisions imply corruption surface that bears monitoring.

---

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH** (borderline Very High)

Tesla operates a genuine tri-pole footprint (US ~50% / China ~22% / Europe+ ~28%) with active exposure on every axis: a recall-stage NHTSA probe, a live DOJ fraud investigation, SEC litigation against its CEO, US-China tariff exclusion from Chinese content, domestic-China share erosion to BYD, and sustained political/brand backlash in Europe and Canada. The principal mitigant is scale and cash generation, but the concentration of regulatory jeopardy — safety, securities, and related-party — plus dual-sided trade-war exposure justifies a **High** rating with continued escalation risk.
