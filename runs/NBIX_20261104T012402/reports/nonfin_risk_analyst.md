## Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. (NBIX)

**Snapshot:** US biotechnology, San Diego; ~2,500 FTE; market cap ~$16.8B; beta 0.36. Three marketed CNS/rare-disease assets — INGREZZA (valbenazine, tardive dyskinesia), CRENESSITY (crinecerfont, classic CAH), VYKAT XR (diazoxide choline, Prader-Willi hyperphagia; acquired via Soleno, $2.9B, April 2026). Q3 2026 net product sales >$1.1B (+41% YoY); INGREZZA FY26 guidance $2.9–2.925B.

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate, trending Weak**

Evidence:
- **Board classified, minimal refreshment.** 2026 AGM seated only three Class III nominees, all management-recommended (no contested/withhold signal disclosed). Boardroom Alpha grades the board **C+** and 1Y TSR at **-1.9%**.
- **Two former CEOs on the board.** Founder **Kevin C. Gorman, Ph.D.** (18.8 yrs tenure, former CEO) is formally **not independent** and stood for re-election in 2026. **Gary A. Lyons** (33.8 yrs tenure, joined Feb 1993, also a former Neurocrine President/CEO) is classified independent — legally defensible, substantively strained at 33+ years. Average tenure is extreme; genuine independent refreshment is limited to recent additions (e.g., Johanna Mercier, 5.5 yrs).
- **No CEO/Chairman duality** — a positive; the chair role sits with a non-executive director, and CEO Kyle Gano (appointed Oct 2024, internal promotion from Chief Business Development & Strategy Officer) does not chair. Succession was orderly and pre-announced (CEO-elect May 2024), a governance positive.
- **Compensation/dilution:** say-on-pay passed on an advisory basis; 2023 PRSUs paid at 125%; management sought **+4,000,000 shares** to the 2025 Equity Incentive Plan. Repeated equity-pool expansions at a company with heavy SBC are a slow dilution/alignment concern, not (yet) a red flag. Clawbacks, anti-repricing, ownership guidelines exist — protective features.
- **Auditor:** Ernst & Young ratified for FY26 — no auditor turnover or disagreement signal.
- **Caveat:** the DEF 14A and 10-K were not retrievable via the filing tool (403/not found), so committee composition, related-party transactions, and the full legal-proceedings note could not be independently verified. Chair/independence detail is drawn from proxy-derived third-party summaries.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

- **No SEC enforcement actions or DOJ matters** returned in EDGAR full-text search for the issuer — a clean enforcement record as of this review.
- **INGREZZA patent litigation: resolved, but with a conditional tail.** All ANDA suits were settled (Nov 2023) with four generic filers permitted US entry **March 1, 2038, "or earlier under certain circumstances."** The accelerators (at-risk launch, invalidity, volume triggers) are the residual risk; the 2038 date removes near-term cliff anxiety but not optionality risk. Additional infringement suits against Teva were filed historically, indicating a persistent filer.
- **IRA Medicare negotiation — unresolved and material.** INGREZZA carries ~$2.4B Medicare Part D spend and has been repeatedly flagged as negotiation-eligible, with a possible small-biotech exception. CMS published the IPAY-2028 selected-drug list on 27 Jan 2026; **I could not confirm from the source PDF whether valbenazine appears on it** — this is the single most important unverified regulatory datapoint and should be checked before any position is sized.
- **VYKAT XR post-marketing safety → live FDA interaction.** Company confirms active dialogue with FDA on surveillance. Spectrum of possible outcomes: label/boxed-warning change, REMS, prescribing restrictions, or withdrawal petition (see §5).
- **No disclosed FDA warning letter, CRL, or manufacturing consent decree** found in the period reviewed. Pending pipeline approvals are largely 2027 catalysts rather than near-term binary events.

### 3. Insider Activity Analysis (past 12 months): **Net selling, zero open-market buying**

- **CEO Kyle Gano:** a continuous, 12-transaction sale record from 2022 through Jan 2026 with **no open-market purchases at any point**. Largest recent: **16 Jan 2026 — exercised 36,400 options at $35.99 and sold all 36,400 shares at $132.70 (~$4.83M)**; prior **Jan 2025 — 65,000 shares at $141.50 (~$9.2M)**. Post-transaction direct holdings fell to ~140k shares against a 144,940-share option award granted Feb 2026 — i.e., the CEO's retained outright equity is modest relative to annual grants.
- **Sept/Nov 2026 Form 4 cluster** (~12 filings): predominantly annual equity awards and tax-withholding dispositions, not discretionary conviction signals.
- **Interpretation:** programmatic 10b5-1-style monetization is normal for biotech executives and is *not* by itself a fraud indicator. But at NBIX the pattern is one-directional across four years and multiple price levels, which weakens any "insiders are signalling undervaluation" read. There is **no insider buying to offset** — treat insider sentiment as neutral-to-negative, not supportive.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Single-product revenue concentration.** INGREZZA at ~$2.9B of an ~$3.9B run-rate means one molecule, one mechanism (VMAT2), one payer category carries ~three-quarters of enterprise value. Any label constriction, competitive VMAT2 entrant, TD market-share shift, or pricing action transmits almost directly to equity value. Diversification (CRENESSITY, VYKAT XR) is real but immature.
2. **Acquired-asset integration and diligence quality (Soleno/VYKAT XR).** A $2.9B all-cash deal (34% premium, announced 6 Apr 2026) closed months before post-marketing deaths surfaced. Management states it "conducted extensive diligence on the safety profile, including adverse event data" — if that diligence missed a signal visible in FAERS, the board's M&A oversight and the reliability of its risk reporting become the issue, not just the drug. Watch for impairment charges and for any disclosure of pre-close safety data.
3. **Key-person and execution dependency in a commercial rebuild.** A first-time CEO (since Oct 2024) is simultaneously running a three-product launch machine, a $2.9B integration, and a Phase 2 pivot into Alzheimer's (NBI-'567 initiated Oct 2026) and schizophrenia/MDD. Clinical readouts in 2027 are binary (muscarinic agonists carry class-wide tolerability risk). CMO/API dependence for valbenazine and specialty-distribution dependence for CRENESSITY/VYKAT were **not verifiable** from the retrieved filings — an acknowledged gap.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **Elevated**

- **The dominant controversy is VYKAT XR.** As of Aug 2026 the Prader-Willi Syndrome Association posted a safety notice; clinicians reported **seven deaths and ~100 serious adverse events** in FDA's adverse-event system (swelling, respiratory and cardiac complications) since launch. A **former FDA regulator has publicly called for withdrawal**, while other experts urge caution pending causality. BMO flagged that "increased physician caution around prescribing and risk of regulatory scrutiny could limit uptake." The stock fell ~2% on the disclosure — a muted reaction that likely understates tail risk rather than dismissing it.
- **Pricing/political exposure.** A Q4 2026 report reportedly called for a ~90% price cut on tardive-dyskinesia drugs; INGREZZA's economics rely on ~90% of patients paying ≤$10 out of pocket, i.e., a patient-support/gross-to-net structure that is a standing target for payer and political attack. Expect continued ICER-style and congressional scrutiny regardless of clinical merit.
- **General press flow is otherwise strongly positive** (KINECT-PRO data, NEI congress, CAH education programs) — the company retains substantial scientific goodwill, which provides a buffer.
- **Litigation expectation:** product-liability suits in PWS are plausible and, given the patient population's elevated baseline respiratory/cardiac risk, defensible — but defence costs and discovery (including internal diligence files) are the real exposure.

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **Moderate**

Rationale: clean enforcement record, orderly CEO succession, no fraud indicators, ANDA risk pushed to 2038, and a genuinely productive R&D organisation. Offsetting: a stale, founder-anchored board; four years of unidirectional insider selling with no buying; and — decisively — the VYKAT XR safety signal sitting on top of an already concentrated revenue base. **This is a Moderate rating with asymmetric downside**: it becomes **High** on any FDA label restriction/withdrawal action, on a confirmed causal link in the PWS deaths, or on evidence that the Soleno diligence process suppressed or misread a known safety signal.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **VYKAT XR mortality signal (highest priority).** Seven deaths, ~100 SAEs, a public withdrawal call from a former FDA official, and an open FDA dialogue. Escalate for: FDA action letters, label changes, REMS, or securities litigation alleging the risk was known pre-close.
2. **Diligence-credibility question on Soleno.** Management's assertion of "extensive diligence… including adverse event data" sits awkwardly against a post-marketing signal emerging within months. Request the board's M&A risk-review record and any 10-K/10-Q disclosure of pre-acquisition safety data.
3. **IRA exposure unverified.** Confirm whether valbenazine is on CMS's IPAY-2028 selected list and whether Neurocrine qualified for the small-biotech exception. An unquantified price-setting mechanism on the largest revenue line is an unacceptable research gap.
4. **Board entrenchment.** Non-independent founder-director standing for re-election plus a 33.8-year "independent" director — withhold-vote and proxy-advisor risk at the 2027 AGM; monitor for shareholder activism given -1.9% 1Y TSR against +41% revenue growth.
5. **Insider selling asymmetry.** Twelve consecutive sales by the CEO with zero purchases — not evidence of wrongdoing, but it removes the strongest counter-signal an analyst normally relies on when a safety controversy breaks.

**Disclosed data gaps:** 10-K risk factors, DEF 14A committee/related-party detail, CMO/supply-chain concentration, cybersecurity incident history, and the CMS IPAY-2028 list could not be retrieved within budget (SEC 403/formatting failures). Conclusions above rest on proxy-derived third-party summaries, Form 4 records, EDGAR full-text enforcement search, and trade press; they should be re-verified against the primary filings before publication.
