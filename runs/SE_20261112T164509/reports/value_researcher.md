# Sea Limited (NYSE: SE) — Value Investment Analysis

**Price:** $73.44 (Nov 12, 2026) | **Market cap:** ~$44.9B | **EV:** ~$37–40B | **52-wk range:** ~$71 – $196
Stock is **−62% from its Sept-2025 peak ($196.50)** and −33% since July 2026. The de-rating is recent and specific: an October collapse (−30% in two sessions) on the announced **"Sea Cloud AI" enterprise platform** plus a potential **Monee IPO**, followed by a Q3 EPS miss.

---

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat Rating: **NARROW**

Sea is three businesses of very different quality bolted together:

| Segment (Q3'26) | Revenue | YoY | Adj. EBITDA | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shopee (e-commerce) | $6.10B | +41.3% | $269M | 4.4% |
| Monee (fintech/lending) | $1.60B | +63.9% | $298M | 18.6% |
| Garena (gaming) | $840M | +28.6%* | $458M | 54.5% |

\*Bookings — the honest metric — grew only **+5.9%**.

**Shopee — the real moat (Narrow, arguably Wide regionally).** #1 GMV position in Southeast Asia, two-sided network effects, an owned logistics network (SPX) that is a genuine cost advantage, and now proven monetization: core marketplace revenue +50.6% (take rates and ads rising). Gross orders +25.5%, GMV +23.3% — take-rate expansion, not just volume, is driving revenue. That is pricing power. **But the moat is narrowing at the margin**: TikTok Shop took share from ~61% to ~56% between 2023 and 2025, and holds >60% of Indonesian beauty GMV. Brazil is a capital-hungry growth market with the same competitor. Shopee's 4.4% EBITDA margin is thin — this is a scale business, not a toll bridge.

**Garena — a melting ice cube with a great margin.** Free Fire is ageing: QAU flat at 671.7M, paying users +4.1%, bookings +5.9%. The 54.5% EBITDA margin is wonderful but the revenue growth here (+28.6%) is deferred-revenue accounting, not franchise growth. Games are hit-driven; I assign **no durable moat**, only a temporary one on Free Fire's brand/community.

**Monee — no moat, and the quality problem.** Loans outstanding +60% to $12.6B (of which $11.2B on-book), revenue +64% but EBITDA only **+15.5%**. Growth is being bought with margin and balance sheet. NPLs 90+ days rose from 1.1% → **1.3%**. This is a credit book, not a moat — it is cyclical, capital-consuming, and untested through a SEA consumer downturn.

**Sea Cloud AI — outside the circle of competence.** Announced October 2026, this is a capital-intensive entry against AWS/Alibaba/Tencent. No evidence of advantage. This is the single largest reason the stock halved.

**ROIC vs. cost of capital — the decisive test, and Sea fails it today:**
| Year | ROIC | ROE |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 11.7% | 12.5% |
| 2024 | 5.3% | 5.2% |
| 2023 | 2.0% | 2.3% |
| 2022 | −14.5% | −28.4% |

Against a WACC of ~11% (beta 1.56, EM exposure), Sea has only just reached its cost of capital in the last twelve months. Buffett's hallmark of a moated business — *consistently* ROIC > WACC — is not yet present. The trajectory is right; the record is four years old and one year long.

## 2. Financial Quality Score: **6 / 10**

| Metric | FY2025 | TTM (Q3'26) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $22.94B (+36.4%) | $30.32B |
| Gross margin | 44.7% | 45.8% |
| Operating margin | 8.1% (from −11.9% in '22) | 7.8% |
| Net income | $1.578B | $1.768B |
| Operating cash flow | $5.03B | — |
| Free cash flow | $4.51B | — |
| **Owner earnings** (NI + D&A − capex) | **$1.44B** | ~$1.6B |
| Cash / Debt | $12.79B / $3.33B | net cash |
| ROIC | 11.7% | ~12% |

**Positives:** Revenue CAGR of 39% over five years with accelerating growth (36% → 43% in the latest quarter). Gross margin stable-to-rising through a competitive war — a real sign of scale. Operating leverage is dramatic and genuine (operating margin −11.9% → +8.1% in three years). Net cash balance sheet. Gross margin of 45% on $30B of revenue is not something a weak business sustains.

**Where the score gets hit:**
- **FCF is misleading.** 10% FCF yield ($4.51B / $44.9B) overstates distributable cash badly. Owner earnings are only **$1.44B** — FCF conversion of 286% is driven by working capital and by **not expensing the fintech loan book build**. On-book loans are growing ~$2–4B/year; a meaningful slice of that is shareholder capital, not operating cash. True economic earnings are closer to **$1.6–2.0B** — a ~4% owner-earnings yield, not 10%.
- **Dilution is real and offsets buybacks.** 612M basic vs. **654M diluted** — 6.8% annual dilution. The company spent **$997M repurchasing 11.1M shares at an average of ~$89.84** (22% above today's price) and basic share count still barely moved. That is a ~$1B cheque written to employees, dressed up as capital return.
- **SBC is a genuine cost**, ~$700–800M annually, and is why "adjusted EPS" ($0.94) sits far above "GAAP EPS" ($0.77) in Q3'26.
- **Margin mix is deteriorating.** Group adjusted EBITDA grew only 28.9% on 43.4% revenue growth. High-margin Garena is being diluted by low-margin lending and e-commerce growth.

## 3. Valuation Analysis

**Current multiples (on $73.44):**
- P/E (TTM GAAP diluted $2.77): **26.5x**
- P/E (TTM adjusted EPS ~$3.30): **22.3x**; forward 2027E adjusted (~$4.30): **~17x**
- EV/EBITDA (~$3.0B GAAP TTM): **~12.5x**; on adjusted EBITDA (~$3.4B): **~11x**
- P/B: 3.7x | P/S: 1.5x
- Owner-earnings yield: **3.2%** (FCF yield of 10% is not economic)

The sector-peer screen returned a grab-bag of US retailers (Amazon, Home Depot, Tesla) with a median P/E of 24.6x — a meaningless comparison for a SEA platform. The correct comps (Meli, PDD, Coupang, Grab) were not available from the data feed; I've flagged that as a gap rather than leaning on a bad screen.

**My own DCF** (adjusted EBITDA → FCF, SBC expensed, capex > D&A, loan-book equity funding deducted, 11% discount rate, 3.5% terminal):

| | 2027E | 2028E | 2029E | 2030E | 2031E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue ($B) | 40.8 | 48.9 | 56.8 | 64.2 | 71.2 |
| Adj. EBITDA margin | 11.5% | 12.5% | 13.0% | 13.5% | 13.5% |
| Unlevered FCF ($B) | 1.6 | 2.5 | 3.4 | 4.3 | 4.9 |

PV of 2027–31 FCF ≈ $11.7B; PV of terminal value ≈ $40.4B → **EV ≈ $52B**, plus net cash (conservatively $2B after haircutting cash that funds the loan book) → **equity ≈ $54B ≈ $83/share**.

Sanity checks bracket this:
- **Bear ($50–58):** TikTok Shop keeps taking share, NPLs rise through a consumer cycle, cloud AI burns $1B+/yr, EBITDA margin stalls at 9%. Implies ~20x depressed EPS.
- **Bull ($115–135):** Cloud AI is shelved or proves modest, Shopee margin reaches 8%+ of GMV-equivalent, Garena stabilizes, Monee IPO crystallizes value, buybacks get disciplined.
- **Central intrinsic value: ~$80–85/share.**

**What the current price implies:** ~$73 discounting roughly 15% EBITDA growth with no margin expansion — i.e., the market is pricing in competitive erosion and capital misallocation. That is not an irrational price. It is also not a gift.

**Margin of safety:** at $73.44 vs. $83 central IV, you are getting a **~12% discount**. My requirement is 25–30%. **A buy would need ~$58–62.**

## 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Good, with a fresh and material reservation**

**Strengths:** Forrest Li is a founder-chairman-CEO with meaningful ownership and a 15-year track record of winning in brutal markets (Shopee came from behind to pass Lazada). The strategic pivot from growth-at-all-costs to profitability in late 2022 was executed decisively and honestly — operating margin went from −12% to +8% in three years. That is one of the better capital-discipline pivots in EM tech. Disclosure is solid, segment reporting is transparent, and they voluntarily flag deferred-revenue adjustments.
