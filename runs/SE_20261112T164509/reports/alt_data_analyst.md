**Critical divergence.** The core Indonesian franchise is *not* deteriorating — Shopee search is up 8% YoY, the 4-Oct line hit 100 (9.9 promo), and SeaMoney's SPayLater is up 35% YoY, the single strongest signal in the dataset. That is credit-led GMV and take-rate expansion, not a demand break.

**But Brazil is the crack.** Brazil Shopee interest has decayed from a ~92 run-rate through mid-2026 to ~70 now (-25% YoY), and the trend is persistently down since a 91 peak in early June. Brazil is the market consensus leans on for the 25% GMV growth guide and for multiple expansion. Alt data says the fastest-growing geography is decelerating into Q4 — a leading indicator that is *not* yet in consensus.

**Competition check:** "Temu" interest in Indonesia is ~flat YoY (81 vs 86.5, -6%), so PDD is **not** gaining search share. The competitive-intensity narrative driving the October selloff is real in *spend* terms but is not visible as competitor demand capture. This argues the pressure is self-inflicted subsidy/logistics spend, i.e., a margin issue, not a share-loss issue.

*Data caveat:* US-geo "Shopee" trends collapsed 100→19; that is a meme/viral artifact in a non-operating market and should be discarded.

Structural cost inflation from subsidies plus IDR weakness means the revenue beat converts to an EPS miss. Q4'26 (due ~2-Mar-27) setup: **revenue beat likely, EPS miss likely** — the same pattern for the fourth consecutive quarter. Brazil deceleration is the incremental risk to the *size* of the revenue beat.
