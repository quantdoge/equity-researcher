## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Investment Grade (indicative, unrated)**

Sea Limited carries no public credit rating from major agencies (the S&P "A-" reference in search results pertains to SEA SpA, an unrelated Italian entity). On fundamental credit metrics, however, the company screens as **Investment Grade**:

- **Net cash position:** Cash and short-term investments of $12.79B versus total debt of $3.33B, implying net cash of ~$9.46B (Net Debt/EBITDA of **-3.55x**).
- **Interest coverage:** **55.5x** (operating income / interest expense), leaving enormous headroom even in a severe downturn.
- **Debt/Equity:** **0.26x**; **Debt/Assets:** **0.11x** — very low leverage for a consumer-cyclical retailer.
- **Piotroski F-Score: 8/9 (Strong)** — positive ROA, positive operating cash flow, improving ROA, low accruals, lower leverage, positive equity change, improving gross margin and asset turnover. The only miss is F7 (share count increased, i.e., dilution).
- **Altman Z-Score: 1.529 (Distress zone)** — this is the one conflicting signal. The low score is driven by **negative retained earnings (-$6.58B)** and a high asset base relative to book equity. For a high-growth, accumulated-deficit tech/consumer platform, the Z-score is structurally distorted and should not be read as a literal bankruptcy probability. The net-cash balance sheet and 55x interest coverage contradict a true distress reading.

**Conclusion:** Credit risk is **low**. The company has ample liquidity, minimal leverage, and strong cash generation. The main credit caveat is the absence of a public rating and the Altman Z-score flag, which warrants monitoring but is not a near-term solvency concern.

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis: 20% Revenue Decline

**Key takeaways:**
- Even with a severe 20% top-line decline and **no SG&A cuts**, the company remains **profitable** (operating income ~$0.60B) and **cash-generative** (FCF positive).
- Interest coverage remains **~18x**, far above any covenant threshold.
- The balance sheet stays in a **net cash** position (~-$9.8x Net Debt/EBITDA).

- **The real downside risk is valuation/market risk, not solvency.** A 20% revenue decline would likely trigger a much larger equity drawdown (given the 53% vol and 1.16–1.56 beta), but the company would survive comfortably.

## Data Gaps & Caveats

- **No public credit rating or CDS spread** was available for Sea Limited; the S&P "A-" reference is for SEA SpA (unrelated).
- **Debt maturity schedule** is not fully broken out; only short-term ($1.70B) and long-term ($0.51B) are visible. The implied ~$1.12B "other debt" is unverified.
- **Altman Z-score** uses book equity (not market cap), and for a company with negative retained earnings, it is structurally biased downward. Treat as indicative only.
- **Beta discrepancy** (1.16 vs 1.563) — likely different lookback windows; both indicate above-market sensitivity.
- **FCF yield** uses the tool's approximate market cap ($46.87B); on the actual $44.95B market cap, yield is ~10.0%.

**Bottom line:** Sea Limited is a **financially resilient, net-cash company** with strong liquidity and cash generation. Its credit risk is low. The **high market risk** (volatility, drawdown, beta) and the **Altman Z-score distress flag** are the primary risk considerations. A 20% revenue decline would pressure earnings but not solvency — the company would remain profitable, cash-generative, and net-cash. The key watch items are dilution, the accumulated deficit, and equity-market volatility.
