### Investment Memo: TEM — 3 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $60 (24% downside from current $78.48)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

---

**Investment Thesis**

Tempus is a genuinely improving, category-leading precision-oncology and data business — but its equity is priced for an outcome it has not yet earned. Two independent valuation methods (DCF, sum-of-parts) converge at $43–45 against a $78.48 price; probability-weighted value is ~$58–62. The bull case rests on a data-licensing flywheel whose legal premise (de-identified genetic data) is under direct attack in the *Farrier* GIPA class action, while a hawkish-Fed, rising-real-yield regime mechanically compresses a 224x EV/adj.-EBITDA, beta-3.6 asset. This is a good company and a bad stock at this price.

---

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro UNFAVOURABLE — 10Y at 5.13%, real yields +40bp to 2.60%, Fed hiking (Sept +25bp); ~21% CAPM-implied required return against ~$65M FY26 adj. EBITDA on a $13.9B market cap. Sector attractive but barbelled: TEM's data moat is best-in-class, yet its 62.7% gross margin sits *below* the diagnostics-peer median and headline +83% growth was M&A-inflated (organic ~22–23%).
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM risk, entirely domestic — negligible foreign/sanctions exposure, but the 21-count *Farrier* GIPA suit (7 states) attacks the consent basis of the 71%-gross-margin Data & Applications segment; CMS algorithm-test reclassification and the 2026 PAMA cycle are pending.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL. Base-case intrinsic $43–45 (two methods converge), probability-weighted $54. 9.1x EV/FY26 sales, 224x EV/adj. EBITDA, negative tangible book, zero years of ROIC above WACC, ~$210M/yr SBC rendering "positive adj. EBITDA" economically hollow.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY on a 3–5yr view. Gross margin +24pts in four years; >$550M YTD data bookings vs ~$430M segment run-rate; 5-for-5 beats (avg +66%); 8.9x FY26 sales vs NTRA ~21x and GH ~17x. The strongest input against this memo's verdict.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL. Top-decile momentum (+95.7% 12-1) against bottom-quintile value, quality and low-vol. Alpha +19.7% but R² of 0.16 on a sub-2-year sample — a trend vehicle, not factor-validated.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. Interest coverage −3.1x; tangible equity −$334M; four-year cumulative FCF −$892M; Altman Z −0.65. Mitigants are real: $755M cash, 3.1x current ratio, cheap 0.75% converts not due until 2030 — survival is liquidity-funded, not operations-funded.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. 30:1 Class B voting, CEO/Chair duality, Nevada reincorporation, audit-chair pedigree concerns; **~$97M of CEO selling with zero open-market insider buys in 12 months**; EDNY CID and Ohio AG subpoena status unknown and unresolved.
- **ESG**: ESG RISK. Governance 3/10. GIPA statutory damages of $2,500–15,000/violation imply a theoretical $1.25–7.5B tail (realistic settlement $50–300M), plus injunctive risk that could force consent-based re-papering of the data estate.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL, negative tilt. Revenue-beat magnitude compressing four straight quarters (+1.3→0.8→0.7→0.5%); search interest −60% y/y while Natera is flat; earnings-day volume −35%. Good news is priced.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (moderate conviction) — explicitly a valuation/quality case, *not* fraud. Growth collapsed 85%→23%; DSO 65→92 days; Q3'26 "FCF inflection" was ~100% working-capital release. Downside $40–50 at 4–5x FY27 sales.

**Bull Case**
- **Data flywheel converts**: bookings already exceed the segment run-rate; two or three more BMS/AstraZeneca-scale deals would double the segment and add ~1,500bp of blended margin at near-zero cost.
- **Relative mispricing**: less than half Natera's and roughly half Guardant's revenue multiple for ~8pts slower growth, with positive adj. EBITDA already delivered — plus genuine strategic scarcity/takeover floor.
- **Rate-pivot convexity**: if September's hike proves terminal and real yields fall 75–100bp, a 3.6-beta, top-decile-momentum name could retest $110+.

**Bear Case**
- **Valuation**: price is ~45% above convergent intrinsic value and above the street median target; you must underwrite flawless execution to 2030 (19% CAGR, 25% EBITDA margin) to break even.
- **Model-level legal tail**: an adverse *Farrier* ruling or any pharma partner pausing licences imperils the highest-margin revenue line, not just a quarter.
- **Earnings quality and governance**: decelerating organic growth, receivables outpacing revenue, SBC-flattered profitability, negative tangible equity — under a 30:1 founder-controlled structure selling stock, not buying it.

---

**Key Risks**
1. *Farrier* motion-to-dismiss denial / class certification on the "DNA cannot be de-identified" theory — the kill switch.
2. Pharma counterparty (AZ, BMS, Merck, GSK) pausing or narrowing data licences — a faster tell than the docket.
3. CMS algorithm-test final rule / PAMA 2027–29 rate-setting; MRD coverage decisions.
4. Organic growth below 18% for two consecutive quarters — no valuation support below that.
5. Personalis close: dilution, integration, and a failed/repriced shareholder vote.
6. Macro: CPI re-acceleration forcing the Fed toward 4.75–5.00% and the 10Y through 6% — a 40–50% de-rating scenario for this duration profile.
7. EDNY CID / Ohio AG escalation from unknown to active.

**Portfolio Construction Note**

**Pass — 0% new capital; if held, reduce to ≤0.75% or exit.** Kelly fraction is negative (−0.47) under every evidence-supported probability set; there is no derivable long case at $78.48. At 80% annualised volatility, even a 0.75% position contributes ~0.6% of portfolio vol, and TEM's 0.52 correlation with NTRA/GH/ILMN makes it a redundant, levered second unit of any existing diagnostics sleeve. It also consumes rate-sensitivity budget in the wrong regime. Five unresolved binaries (GIPA ruling, CMS rule, Personalis vote, EDNY/Ohio status, Feb-23 print) are too many for a sub-1% position. **Conditional re-entry:** <$58 starter (0.5%) only after a benign GIPA MTD ruling *and* a clean Feb print; <$48 to 1.0–1.5%; 2.5–3.0% only on two quarters of SBC-adjusted, ex-working-capital positive FCF — at which point the debate moves from multiple to arithmetic. Trim half at $85–90; exit fully above $95.

**Dissent on record**: the Growth analyst's BUY is credible and trajectory-based; this memo resolves the conflict on price, not on company quality. If *Farrier* resolves favourably and the stock reaches the high-$40s, the verdict flips.
