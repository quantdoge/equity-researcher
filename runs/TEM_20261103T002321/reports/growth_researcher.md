# Tempus AI, Inc. (NASDAQ: TEM)
### Growth Investment Analysis — Price $78.48 (post-Q3'26, Nov 2, 2026)

---

## 1. Growth Story

Tempus is building the **operating system for precision medicine**: a closed-loop platform that runs ~1/3 of US oncology NGS testing (xT/xR), feeds every result into the world's largest multimodal clinical + molecular + imaging data library, and resells that library to pharma as data licensing — a business with >80% incremental margins and a genuine data network effect that no competitor can replicate by spending money alone. FY2025's 83% growth was acquisition-inflated (Ambry Genetics), but the *real* story is the mix shift: Data & Applications grew 33% in Q3'26 to $108.1M with **>$550M of data licensing bookings signed year-to-date** against a ~$430M annualized segment run-rate, while MRD volume grew 300% to ~12,000 tests from a near-zero base in a multi-billion-dollar TAM.

Correction to the bear case: the "competitors are bigger" argument is being eroded. **Natera reported ~296,700 oncology tests in a quarter vs. Tempus's ~12,000 MRD tests** — Natera is 25x larger in MRD. But Natera's entire market cap ($51.8B) rests on that one assay; Tempus monetizes the *same* patient relationship across diagnostics **and** a data library Natera cannot sell. Tempus's differentiation is the **longitudinal multimodal dataset** — a compounding asset, not a feature.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

*(High-conviction buy on a 3–5 year view; not a Strong Buy because the near-term setup is crowded.)*

**Why Buy:** Gross margin +24pts in four years proves the model scales. The data-licensing flywheel is producing >$550M of annual bookings — a leading indicator that is not yet in the revenue line — and it is a moat built on proprietary longitudinal multimodal data, exactly the kind of compounding asset that deserves a premium. The company just crossed into positive Adjusted EBITDA a full two years earlier than the 2025 consensus expected, guided up, and beat by 66% on average for five straight quarters. At **8.9x FY26 sales vs. NTRA at ~21x and GH at ~17x**, the market is pricing Tempus as a lab company when it is increasingly a data company.

**Why not Strong Buy:** the 83% headline growth was purchased, not earned — organic growth is ~25% and decelerating (36% → 22% → 23% sequential). Consensus now models deceleration to 15.6% by 2030. The stock is at **$78.48 vs. a street average target of ~$76** (Goldman Neutral $75, UBS Neutral $70, Redburn Neutral $67) — it has already run past the sell side after a 92% move off the July low. GAAP operating losses are still ~-$280M TTM and SBC runs ~$200M/yr.

**Position-sizing note:** beta of 3.61 is not a rounding error. This name drew down **-60% ($103.25 → $40.77) in nine months** while revenue grew 26%. Own it in size appropriate to a venture-like risk profile, not a core holding.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Execution / organic growth deceleration.** Strip Ambry and growth is ~25% and slowing. If Data & Apps slips below ~25%, the multiple has no support — data licensing is lumpy and contract-timed.
2. **Competitive threat in MRD.** Natera is ~25x larger (~296,700 vs. ~12,000 tests/qtr) with entrenched reimbursement and a decade of clinical evidence. Guardant and Foundation Medicine (Roche) pressure tissue CGP. Tempus's MRD is an *option*, not yet a business.
3. **Dilution and capital structure.** Shares 162M → 181M (+12%) in two years; SBC + payroll taxes $50.8M in Q3 alone. Interest expense ~$15M/qtr from Ambry financing. FCF was -$245M in 2025; the $65M Adjusted EBITDA guide flatters a business still burning cash.
4. **Segment drag.** Hereditary (Ambry) growing +3% dilutes blended growth and caps margin expansion — the acquisition bought scale but also a low-growth liability.
5. **Reimbursement / regulatory.** PAMA rate pressure and CMS coverage decisions for MRD are binary and outside company control.
6. **Data-licensing concentration.** A handful of pharma counterparties (Merck, Moderna, BMS, AZ, GSK) drive bookings. One de-prioritized program or an AI-driven shift in pharma data strategy would hit the highest-margin line first.
7. **Valuation.** 138x 2028E EPS and 27x 2030E EPS. The market already discounts a lot of the good news; the stock is above most published targets.
