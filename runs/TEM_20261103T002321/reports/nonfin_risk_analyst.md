# NON-FINANCIAL RISK ASSESSMENT — TEMPUS AI, INC. (NASDAQ: TEM)

**Snapshot:** Healthcare / Medical-Healthcare Information Services · Chicago, IL · 3,800 FTE · Market cap ~$13.9B · Price $78.48 · Beta 3.61 · IPO June 14, 2024 · Q3'26 revenue $411.5M (+23% YoY), first positive adjusted EBITDA quarter, FY26 guide ~$1.6B revenue / ~$65M adj. EBITDA.

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **WEAK**

Evidence, in order of severity:

- **Extreme dual-class control.** Class A carries 1 vote; **Class B carries 30 votes per share** (per the company's DEF 14C reincorporation filing), and founder **Eric Lefkofsky holds all Class B**. He is simultaneously **CEO, Chairman, and 10% owner**. Public shareholders cannot meaningfully influence the board, compensation, or M&A — even as his economic stake has been sold down. This is one of the most lopsided vote structures in US large-cap healthcare.
- **Triple concentration of power with no counterweight.** Lefkofsky is also the company's principal dealmaker (AstraZeneca, Merck/Moderna, BMS, Pathos, Personalis) and a prolific venture investor outside TEM (Lightbank; Pathos AI). There is no independent chairman, and the board has not elected the Nasdaq "controlled company" exemption *yet* — a stated option, meaning committee independence standards could be relaxed at any time.
- **Delaware → Nevada reincorporation (completed Aug 7, 2025 via certificate of conversion).** A "DExit" move that reduces shareholder litigation rights, books-and-records access, and fiduciary exposure. Coming immediately after a 2025 securities class action is filed, this is a shareholder-unfriendly sequencing choice.
- **Audit committee pedigree is a red flag.** Per Spruce Point, the **Audit Committee chair is the former CEO/Chairman of InnerWorkings** — a Lefkofsky-founded company that restated results and disclosed a material weakness shortly after his departure. The **CFO is a Groupon accounting alum**; the **CAO** came from Surgalign/RTI Medical (multi-period restatement, SEC revenue-recognition fraud allegations involving others). Groupon restated revenue ~50% at IPO and reported a material weakness; Echo Global Logistics (also Lefkofsky/Keywell) likewise. This is a *pattern*, not an isolated data point.
- **Compensation/alignment:** Heavy use of adjusted EBITDA (a metric Spruce Point alleges is flattered by ~$25.6M LTM of non-cash Google Cloud debt-principal netting against opex) plus TCV-based disclosure with non-binding opt-ins. Incentives are tied to metrics management defines and aggressively redefines.

**Verdict: Weak.** A 30:1 vote structure, CEO/Chair duality, Nevada reincorporation, and an audit chair drawn from a prior Lefkofsky restatement story leave shareholders with essentially no governance recourse.

## 2. Regulatory & Legal Risk Summary: **ELEVATED / HIGH**

**Active litigation (this is the core of the thesis risk):**

1. **Genetic privacy class action — *Farrier et al. v. Tempus AI* (N.D. Ill.).** Consolidated April 15, 2026; **amended consolidated complaint filed Aug 24, 2026**. Alleges violation of the **Illinois Genetic Information Privacy Act (GIPA)** and statutes in seven states (CA, FL, GA, IL, MI, NY, WV). Core allegation: as a condition of the **$600M Ambry Genetics acquisition (Feb 2025)**, Tempus compelled disclosure of genetic data on hundreds of thousands of patients, used it to train AI models, and licensed it to **70+ pharma partners** (AstraZeneca, BMS, Pfizer, GSK, Incyte) in deals alleged to total **$1.1B**. **21 counts** including negligence, unjust enrichment, fraudulent concealment, conversion, invasion of privacy, breach of fiduciary duty. Plaintiffs argue genetic data **cannot be de-identified** because DNA is inherently an identifier — a direct attack on TEM's entire data-licensing business model. Seeks damages *plus an injunction* barring data sharing absent written consent. GIPA is a statutory-damages statute; exposure could be material and is not reserved at scale on the balance sheet.
2. **AI-washing securities class action (N.D. Ill.), filed June 12, 2025.** Class period Aug 6, 2024 – May 27, 2025 (the day before the Spruce Point report). Names Tempus, the CEO and officers. **Motion to dismiss filed Sept 22, 2025; no ruling located as of this review** — status unresolved, discovery/insurance cost accruing.

**Investigative matters — status unknown, escalate:**
3. The 2024 IPO registration statement disclosed a **Civil Investigative Demand from the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of New York (received March 4, 2024)** and a **subpoena from the Ohio Attorney General**. EDNY healthcare CIDs typically concern anti-kickback/False Claims Act or billing practices. **I could not verify current status in the filings available to me** — this is a material disclosure gap, and "no news" is not evidence of closure.

**Regulatory approvals — net positive, but creates dependency:**
- **FDA PMA approval for xT CDx** (648-gene NGS), with a **tumor-only label expansion granted May 29, 2026** — first lab with both tumor-only and tumor-normal CDx approval. Commercially meaningful (pricing uplift from 2027).
- **EU IVDR Class C certification for xR RNA sequencing (Oct 26, 2026)** — EU access secured; note IVDR compliance raises ongoing QMS/notified-body burden.
- **ARPA-H award up to $9.5M** (Sept 2026) — low risk, reputational positive.
- EDGAR full-text enforcement search for TEM returned **no SEC enforcement actions**.

**M&A regulatory:** **$1.5B Personalis (PSNL) acquisition** announced July 20, 2026. HSR waiting period **expired Oct 2, 2026 — but only after a pull-and-refile**, a signal of staff scrutiny and a timing variable. Closing now hinges on the PSNL shareholder vote (late 2026/early 2027).

## 3. Insider Activity Analysis: **UNANIMOUSLY NEGATIVE**

**Over the trailing ~12 months to Oct 29, 2026: ZERO open-market insider purchases. Every reported transaction is a sale.**

- **CEO/Chairman Lefkofsky**, via four entities under a **Rule 10b5-1 plan adopted March 8, 2026**, sold at least **~$97M** across 2026: 166,250 sh @ $57.48; 250,000 @ $42.06; 132,591 @ $49.61; ~250,000 @ $68–70; 200,000 @ $80.57; 250,000 @ $77.47; 250,000 @ $70.36 (Oct 27). Reported holdings fell from **37.1M shares (Aug 18 filing) to ~12.1M (Oct 27)** — a step-change reduction, not routine trimming.
- **Broad-based executive selling:** CFO Rogers (48,842 sh @ $55.21; 11,529 @ $55), CLO Polovin (30,075 sh), CAO Bartolucci (multiple), CEO Diagnostics Schoenherr, and **CEO of Data & Apps Ryan Fukushima (~172k shares Aug–Sep 2026)** — the last is notable because Data & Applications is the 71%-gross-margin growth engine.
- **Directors Doudna, Epstein, West** sold small lots ($12k–$207k), consistent with RSU tax withholding rather than conviction selling — I do not treat these as signals.
- **Interpretation:** sales cluster at $70–$85 and again at $42–$50, i.e., programmatic and price-insensitive (consistent with 10b5-1). But a plan adopted **March 8, 2026** — months before the Ambry privacy complaint was consolidated — combined with total absence of buying at the $42 July low, is a poor alignment signal for a founder-led story stock.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Business-model concentration in licensed de-identified data — legally contested.** Revenue growth is increasingly dependent on licensing patient data to ~70 pharma partners (Data & Applications +33% YoY, >71% GM). The Ambry litigation's core claim is that this data *cannot* be de-identified. An adverse injunction would impair the highest-margin segment, not a peripheral one.
- **Counterparty and revenue-quality concentration, with related-party circularity.** Pharma partners (AZ, BMS, Merck, GSK, Pfizer, Recursion) are both customers *and* collaborators/investees. The **Sept 15, 2026 Recursion master-agreement amendment** shows TEM accepting up to **$10M of a $14M annual fee in Recursion's own stock** — non-cash consideration and a receivables/impairment channel. Spruce Point separately flags $300M of non-binding "opt-ins" and $22.4M of "unlikely milestones" inside reported TCV.
- **Key-person and integration risk.** A $13.9B, beta-3.6 company is functionally inseparable from one 55-something founder who controls the board, sets the AI narrative, and personally fronts every major pharma relationship. Layer on simultaneous integration of Ambry (2025) and the pending $1.5B Personalis/MRD deal, plus lab-capacity, NGS reagent supply, and cloud/compute cost inflation.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **HIGH**

The reputational fault line is **"the company that sells your DNA."** Coverage in the *Chicago Tribune/Cancer Letter*, Law.com, HIPAA Journal, and consumer legal-marketing sites frames TEM's data business as genetic-data monetization without consent — a narrative that is unusually durable because it is intuitive to patients and regulators alike. This strikes directly at hospital/lab referral relationships (the diagnostic funnel) and at state AGs (note the Ohio subpoena history).

Secondary: the **AI-washing** label. Only ~$12.4M (≈2% of 2024 revenue) came from "AI applications" despite an AI rebrand pre-IPO — a credibility gap that invites both short reports and securities suits. Founder association with **Groupon (-95% from IPO, ~50% revenue restatement)** is a persistent overhang on management credibility.

**Countervailing positives:** FDA CDx approval, IVDR certification, expanded BMS/Moderna/Merck collaborations, and ARPA-H selection are genuine third-party validations that partially offset the narrative. Brand damage is currently *litigation-and-press driven*, not yet customer-loss driven.

---

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Justification: no confirmed enforcement action, antitrust clearance obtained, and FDA/EU approvals advancing (which argues against *Critical*). But the combination of (a) a 30:1 founder-controlled vote structure, (b) a 21-count genetic-privacy class action targeting the core margin pool, (c) an unresolved AI-washing securities suit, (d) unexplained-silence EDNY/Ohio AG investigative history, and (e) ~$97M of founder selling with zero insider buying is a genuinely poor non-financial risk profile for a name trading at a premium multiple.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **EDNY Civil Investigative Demand (Mar 2024) and Ohio AG subpoena — confirm current status immediately.** Unresolved healthcare-fraud-adjacent investigations are the single most under-priced item here. Demand 10-K/10-Q "Legal Proceedings" disclosure and ask management directly on the next call.
2. **Ambry/GIPA injunction risk.** Ask for: number of class members, statutory damages estimate, insurance coverage, whether any pharma partner has amended data-licensing terms or paused renewals since Aug 24, 2026.
3. **Insider selling asymmetry.** ~$97M of CEO sales under a plan adopted March 8, 2026, and holdings reportedly down from 37.1M to ~12.1M shares, with **no purchases at $42**. Require a 10b5-1 plan review and clarity on remaining Class B/voting control and any pledging or 10b5-1 modification.
4. **Revenue-quality verification.** Reconcile TCV against contracted backlog; quantify the Google Cloud debt-principal netting benefit to adjusted EBITDA; quantify non-cash (stock-settled) license revenue such as the Recursion arrangement.
5. **Governance remediation vacuum.** 30:1 Class B, CEO/Chair duality, Nevada reincorporation, audit chair from a prior Lefkofsky restatement. No sunset provision identified — control is permanent absent his volition.
6. **Personalis integration + shareholder vote.** A failed or repriced vote would be a confidence shock; watch for PSNL dissent and any revised terms.

**Data gaps (honest):** No 10-K or DEF 14A was retrievable via the filing tool ("No 10-K found"); current board roster, committee independence, related-party transaction schedule, and up-to-date legal-proceedings notes could not be verified from primary filings. The 10-Q return surfaced only an exhibit (Recursion amendment). Findings on governance composition and TCV quality rely partly on Spruce Point's May 2025 short report and should be re-confirmed against the FY2025 10-K before publication.
