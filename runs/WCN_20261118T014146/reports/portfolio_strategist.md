5. **🛑 Succession (per Nonfin Risk):** an unplanned CEO departure. Mittelstaedt has had one unexplained leave (2019) and there is no visible heir on an 8-person board.

**Trim triggers (take profits, do not exit):**

**Hard monitoring obligations:**
