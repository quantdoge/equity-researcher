## FORENSIC SHORT ANALYSIS — WASTE CONNECTIONS, INC. (WCN)

**Data note:** Financials are current through Q3 FY2026 (filed 2026-10-22); the 10-K text endpoint returned no filing, so risk-factor language is unreviewed. I have used the profile price of $181.50 (≈251.9m diluted shares, ≈$45.7bn market cap) as the valuation anchor; price data shows a $156–$194 range over the last 12 months, so valuation conclusions are sensitive to the exact mark. All ratios below are my own calculations from reported statements.

### 2. Key Red Flags Found

**Flag 1 — The "adjusted" earnings bridge is the acquisition engine being excluded (material).**
FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS $4.17 vs. adjusted EPS $5.17: a **$1.00/share (24%) gap**. The add-backs are amortisation of acquired intangibles and impairments — i.e. WCN's headline metric excludes *the cost of the assets it must keep buying to grow*. Industry-standard presentation (WM and RSG do the same), so this is not deception; but it means the "quality compounder" is priced on a number that omits its own input cost.

**Flag 2 — Serial impairments treated as non-recurring.**
Q4 2024: **$601.6m** of impairments "primarily related to the early closure of a landfill" → operating loss of $(199.2)m. Q3 2025: $63.5m of contract/permit/environmental write-downs. 9M FY2026: **$139.9m** of impairments and other items. That is **>$800m in under two years**, roughly 70% of a full year's net income. An early landfill closure at a company whose thesis is landfill scarcity is a direct capital-allocation failure, not noise.

**Flag 3 — Negative and shrinking tangible equity.**
Goodwill + intangibles **$10,382m = 48% of total assets**. Tangible book equity **≈ −$2.5bn (−$9.9/share)**. Equity *fell* from $8,230m to $7,884m during 9M FY2026 despite $875m of net income — buybacks and dividends outran earnings.

**Flag 4 — Capital returns funded with debt at a negative spread.**
9M FY2026: FCF $1,077m; dividends $266m + buybacks $944m = **$1,210m returned** (>100% of FCF), with acquisitions on top. Net debt rose to **$9,662m**. Total debt is up **87% since 2021** ($5.2bn → $9.8bn) against EBITDA up 69%. After-tax cost of debt ≈3.8% versus an adjusted earnings yield of ~3.1% at 32x — the buyback is **EPS-accretive only through growth, dilutive on a static basis**. Buying back stock at 32x earnings with borrowed money is the opposite of the value-creation narrative.

**Flag 5 — Growth is price, not volume.**
Q3 FY2026: revenue +7.3%; acquisitions contributed $53m (~2.2 pts). Organic ≈5.2%, composed of ~5.5% core pricing + 1.3% surcharges **offset by ~1.2–1.6% volume decline**. Landfill volumes are only "improving 70bp sequentially" off a weak base. A model that raises price 5.5% while shedding volume is borrowing from future growth; it works until it doesn't.

**Flag 6 — Revenue deceleration.** +17.2% (2022) → +11.2% (2023) → +11.2% (2024) → **+6.1% (2025)**, with consensus at +5.9% (2027) and +5.2% (2028). Partly re-accelerated in FY2026 (Q1–Q3 +6.4%/+6.4%/+7.3%), but this is commodity/E&P-driven — i.e. *cyclical* revenue being capitalised at a defensive-compounder multiple.

**Flag 7 — Working capital has turned into a drag.** Change in WC: +$12.5m (2023) → −$209m (2024) → **−$264m (2025)**. Receivables +9.8% vs revenue +6.1% in FY2025; DSO 39.8 → 41.2 days. Small, but directionally wrong. SG&A also rose to 10.1% of revenue from 9.6%.

### 3. Beneish and Accruals — Detail

M-Score of −2.71 sits 0.93 below the manipulation threshold. TATA of −0.063 (negative total accruals to assets) is a *quality* signal, not a warning. Accruals ratio has been negative in all four years (−6.9%, −7.6%, −8.1%, −6.3%). **A forensic short on the numbers is not available here.**

**A necessary correction:** the Altman Z-Score of **1.39 ("distress")** is a **false positive**. It is driven by x1 = −0.038 (negative working capital — structurally normal for waste, where customers prepay and payables fund operations) and x4 = 0.63 using *book* equity against $13.6bn of liabilities. Substituting market capitalisation ($45.7bn/13.6bn = 3.35) gives **Z ≈ 3.02 — the safe zone**. Interest cover is 5.2x on EBIT and ~9.0x on adjusted EBITDA; net debt/adjusted EBITDA is 2.9x. This company is not distressed, and any short thesis resting on the Z-score is broken.

---

### 4. Competitive Threat Assessment: **Low — moat intact and widening**

This is where the bear case materially weakens. Gross margin has expanded *every* year: 40.6% (2021) → 39.9% (2022) → 40.9% (2023) → 41.8% (2024) → **42.4% (2025)** → **43.1% (Q3 FY2026)**. Adjusted EBITDA margin reached **34.0%**, up 50bp underlying. Pricing power is real and demonstrated, not asserted.

The structural protections are genuine: landfill permitting is a decades-long, near-impossible barrier; post-collection disposal is a natural local monopoly; route density compounds; the customer base is diversified across residential/commercial/municipal/industrial. There is no competitor commoditising this. **The threat to the equity is not the business — it is the multiple.**

### 5. Management & Governance

- **Ronald Mittelstaedt (CEO/co-founder)** language is promotional — "differentiated growth strategy," "another top-to-bottom beat," "record return of capital." On the evidence, he has *delivered*: 16 consecutive double-digit dividend increases, guidance raised twice in FY2026. Promotional but not a credibility gap.
- **Insider selling:** 25 Form 4s in the trailing 12 months — a dense, continuous pattern consistent with systematic 10b5-1 programmes. **The filing metadata does not disclose direction and I could not confirm any open-market purchases**, so I flag this as suggestive, not established. Present it as: *no visible insider buying at $181 while executives sell on a schedule.*
- No auditor issues, no related-party red flags evident, no restatements. Ontario-domiciled/US-listed structure is worth noting for tax/index purposes but is not a red flag.

### 6. Short Thesis Summary — Is There a Credible Bear Case?

**There is a valuation bear case, and only a valuation bear case.**

The thesis: WCN trades at **~16.5x EV/adjusted EBITDA, ~40x GAAP EPS, ~32x forward adjusted EPS, ~2.9–3.1% FCF yield** for a business growing organic revenue ~5% (price-led, negative volume) and EPS ~8–10%. It is priced for perpetual mid-single-digit growth plus margin expansion, while carrying 2.9x leverage, $10.4bn of goodwill (48% of assets), negative tangible equity, and a recurring impairment habit of ~$400m/year. Its FY2026 beats are leaning on commodity-linked and E&P revenue — the *cyclical* part of a defensive narrative. If core pricing normalises toward 3–4% as CPI cools, revenue growth halves and the multiple has no support.

**Downside maths:** at 14x EV/EBITDA (roughly WM's current level) on ~$3.3bn adjusted EBITDA, equity value is ~$36bn ≈ **$145/share, −20%**. At 15x, ~$158 (−13%). Against ~$210 (+16%) at 19x, the risk/reward is roughly **1:1**. That is not a short; it is a coin flip with negative carry.

### 7. The Other Side — the bull case is stronger

I will not manufacture bearishness where the data refuses to support it. WCN has *expanding* margins, *negative* accruals, **2.2x cash conversion**, capex tracking D&A, a 7/9 Piotroski, a 0.48 beta, an essential-and-contracted revenue base, a genuinely durable permitting moat, a founder-CEO who has under-promised and over-delivered for a decade, and it just raised guidance twice. The alleged "aggressive capitalisation" and "channel stuffing" angles find **zero** support in the numbers. And it is a low-volatility, dividend-raising defensive in a market that pays up for exactly that — meaning borrow is cheap, the crowd is long, and there is no identifiable catalyst to force a re-rating.

### Contrarian Rating: **NO EDGE — Reject Short Thesis**

To be precise about what I am and am not saying:

- **Not a short.** No fraud, no accruals anomaly, no moat erosion, no distress, no catalyst, symmetric risk/reward, negative carry. Shorting a clean-book defensive compounder purely because it is expensive is how short sellers lose money.
- **Not a buy at this price either.** 16.5x EV/EBITDA and 32x adjusted earnings embed ~5.5% pricing power and sustained margin expansion in perpetuity, while leverage rises and buybacks are funded with debt at a spread that is negative on a static basis. The margin of safety is absent; a de-rating to peer multiples is a −13% to −20% event with no accounting fraud required.
- **The one thing I would monitor** as a genuine thesis-changer: a quarter where core solid-waste pricing drops below ~4% while landfill volumes stay negative — that would break the "pricing power funds the growth" flywheel and, with 2.9x leverage and $1bn/year of buybacks, would turn the multiple from expensive to indefensible quickly.
