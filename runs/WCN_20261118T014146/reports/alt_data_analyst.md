Against:
- **Q4 is the seasonal weak spot and the only miss in the sample** — Feb 2025 printed $1.16 vs $1.20 est (−3.3%).

**Verdict: In-Line.** Beat bias from the track record and fresh raise, offset by a demanding Q4 comp and no alt-data upturn. Risk is asymmetrically **to the downside on Q4 specifically**.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL**

Brand search sits 14–15% below its annual average, hiring shows no step-up, social and web traffic are unavailable or inert, and the last seven days of news sentiment flipped to zero on an insider-driven 8% drawdown. The Q3 pop (+9.6%) has already capitalised the good news; alt data says the next leg needs volume, and volume is the one line item management flagged as soft.
