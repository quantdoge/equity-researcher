### Investment Memo: WCN — Waste Connections, Inc. — 18 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (pass on new capital above $165; accumulate on weakness) | **Conviction**: Medium
**Price Target**: $170 (-6% from $181.50 close, 17-Nov-2026); fair value today $145–165
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
WCN is the best-run operator in one of the highest-quality sub-sectors in industrials — a widening permitting moat, best-in-class ~34% EBITDA margins, CPI-escalated contracted revenue, and forensically clean books — but four independent valuation methods (DCF, peer multiples, macro de-rating, growth-adjusted) converge on $145–165 against a $181.50 price. Nine of eleven domain reviews land on Hold/Neutral; none recommend buying here, none recommend shorting. The equity is a long-duration, fully-priced compounder with an unquantified California environmental tail — own it at the right price, not at this one.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector rated *Attractive* — oligopolistic disposal (>60% of landfill capacity held by top three), 5–6% industry pricing, landfill volumes inflecting positive. WCN is best-in-class: 34.0% Q3'26 adj. EBITDA margin vs WM 31.0%/RSG 31.8%; 11.7% 5-yr revenue CAGR. Macro is split: 3.8% CPI is a *revenue driver* via escalators, but the 10Y at 5.13% / 30Y at 5.47% is hostile to a ~30x multiple; macro bear case $140–150.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: *Low risk.* ~88% US / ~12% Canada (estimated), no sanctions/FCPA/EM exposure; largest identified litigation is a $1.4M settled class action. PFAS/leachate and landfill-permit outcomes are the watch items that could migrate the rating to Medium.
- **Value**: *Hold.* Wide, widening moat; base intrinsic value $150–160 vs $181.50 → ~-15% margin of safety. Buy below $150, accumulate $120–135. Expected return ~6–7%/yr — below opportunity cost. ROIC incl. goodwill only 9.7%: the moat is real but acquisition-financed.
- **Growth**: *Hold.* Rule of 40 = 19; revenue decelerating 11%→11%→6.1%→~4.4% (FY27E); PEG ~3.4x. Upgrade trigger at $160 or FY27 guidance framing >2027 as the RNG/AI-pricing payoff year.
- **Quantitative Signals**: *Neutral, 44/100.* Last or tied-last on 3 of 5 factors vs the waste comp set; only earnings revisions support (+7.75% on 2030E). Realized vol 28.9% — highest of the trio, failing its defensive mandate. **Data integrity flag: the SPY series is contaminated (impossible negative correlations); all vs-SPY beta/alpha/Sharpe discarded.** Beta vs WM 0.962 (R² 0.545), alpha ~+2%; market beta ~0.5.
- **Financial Risk**: *Moderate.* A-/A3/BBB+, 3.02x net debt/EBITDA in-band, no maturity cliff (next $500M due Dec-2028), 5.2x coverage, 17.6% revenue cushion before a 4.0x breach. **Altman Z of 1.39 is a false positive** (book-equity artifact; market-adjusted Z = 3.11). The genuine flags: negative tangible equity (-$2.5B), goodwill 48% of assets, and dividends + buybacks at 109% of FCF — buybacks are debt-funded at the margin.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: *Moderate*, with one escalated item: **Chiquita Canyon** — CA AG suit (12 causes, per-violation penalties + disgorgement + open-ended abatement fund), EPA Finding of Violation Oct-2026, DTSC exposure $25K/day, no disclosed ceiling, after ~$613M and ~$110M of charges in consecutive years. Social pillar genuinely elite (<10% turnover, incidents -30%); environmental risk High and concentrated in Chiquita; disclosure below peers (no net-zero, no CDP, Scope 3 gap, a 19%→15% emissions-claim restatement). Estimated unaccrued Chiquita exposure $100–500M.

- **Short / Contrarian View**: *No edge — reject the short.* Beneish -2.71, negative accruals four years running, OCF/NI 2.24x, capex ≈ D&A: there is no fraud thesis here. Downside $145–158 (-13% to -20%) vs upside ~$210 (+16%) ≈ 1:1 — expensive, not broken. The thesis-changer to monitor: core pricing below 4% with landfill volumes still negative.

**Bull Case**
- Best-in-class compounding machine: the only large-cap with a structurally rising operating margin (17.2% FY22 → ~20.5% Q3'26), 5.5% core price covering cost inflation, and FY27 framework of double-digit adjusted FCF/share growth.
- Fortress cash economics: FCF/NI 115%, 16 consecutive double-digit dividend hikes, IG balance sheet with no near-term maturities, and decades of tuck-in M&A runway at disciplined 6–8x EBITDA.
- Inflation is a tailwind, not a cost: CPI-linked escalators reprice the book upward into 2027 renewals while landfill volumes and special waste (+7%) inflect positive; RNG and AI-pricing are unpriced options post-2027.

**Bear Case**
- No margin of safety: 43.5x trailing / ~32x forward adjusted EPS, a 24% P/E premium to the peer median, most expensive in its comp set on P/FCF — the apparent EV/EBITDA "discount" to WM is a leverage artifact (WM 4.5x vs WCN 3.0x net debt/EBITDA).
- Price-led growth masking volume decay: solid waste volumes -1.2%, revenue growth has halved since 2022, and recent beats lean on cyclical E&P/commodity revenue being capitalised at a defensive multiple.
- Duration, debt-funded returns, and an unexplained tape: ~$1.4B of FY26 capital returns exceed FCF; the 17-Nov **-8% reversal on 4.4x average volume remains unattributed** (an open information gap, possibly 2027 cost absorption), and Chiquita carries an unquantified tail on an already-closed asset.

**Key Risks**
1. **Multiple compression** — de-rating from 32x to 24–25x on unchanged earnings implies $140–150 (-17–23%); the dominant risk.
2. **Chiquita Canyon escalation** — accrual >$250M, abatement-fund/disgorgement language, or physical expansion of the reaction zone.
3. **Flywheel break** — core pricing below ~4% with negative volumes ends the margin-expansion narrative that justifies the multiple.
4. **Balance-sheet drift** — net debt/EBITDA >4.0x or payouts >110% of FCF sustained; goodwill impairment on $10.4B of intangibles.
5. **Key person** — founder-CEO with a 2019 leave and abrupt 2023 transition history; thin bench, 8-person board.
6. Diesel/fleet capex inflation, M&A multiple inflation at peak deal activity, E&P cyclicality.
