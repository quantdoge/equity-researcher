### Investment Memo: IP — 24 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $26 (−10% from current $28.98); post-distribution this maps to roughly a $22–23 RemainCo stub plus the DS Smith ADS value. Total-return outlook is worse — the $1.85 dividend is likely to reset to ~$0.75–1.00.
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

IP is a sub-par-return cyclical at a fair price with a solvency-shaped left tail. Blended intrinsic value of ~$27 sits *below* the $28.98 price, the 6.4% dividend is unfunded by ~$900M per year and is arithmetically certain to be cut, the balance sheet screens in the Altman distress zone (Z = 1.03), and a live DOJ criminal antitrust probe hangs over the product generating ~two-thirds of revenue. The sequential operating inflection (PS NA operating profit $204M → $427M → $460–500M guided) and permanent industry capacity removal are genuine — but they are cost-recovery-driven, already priced, and more efficiently expressed through PKG or SW. The 7-Dec DS Smith spin simplifies the equity story, yet the undisclosed post-spin capital structure makes the stock un-underwritable until the Form 10 lands.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector NEUTRAL — ~4.0m tons (~10%) of NA containerboard capacity permanently removed, +$100/ton realised in H1'26 and an $80–140/ton September round, but demand growth only ~0.7%/yr to 2028 and cost-push absorbs the price. IP's ~3% adjusted operating margin vs 8.8% peer median (14.0% at PKG) is structural, not cyclical. Macro UNFAVOURABLE — bear steepener (10Y 5.13%, BAA ~6.5%), CPI 3.8%, hawkish Fed, flat industrial production: the worst mix for a levered cyclical carrying $1.96B of short-term debt.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. DOJ criminal grand jury (N.D. Ill.) subpoenas to IP, SW, PCA and Cascades over containerboard pricing; the parallel civil class action survived a motion to dismiss in Sept'26. Unquantified and unaccrued. Russia fully exited (2023); post-spin IP becomes a NA pure-play, cutting geographic risk to Medium — the antitrust overhang remains the dominant, un-diversifiable risk.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD, no margin of safety. No moat; ROIC below WACC through the cycle (12.2% → 8.3% → 3.4% → −11.0%). Blended IV ~$27; bear $15, bull $40. Buy price $21. Dividend cut of 45–60% is near-certain on a 40–50%-of-FCF payout policy.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: HOLD, fails the growth screen. Organic revenue negative, organic Rule of 40 ≈ −4, no penetration runway at 25–30% of NA capacity. "Special situation, not a compounder." The ~$39.50 consensus PT is pre-spin and must be restated down — treat headline "36% upside" as false.
- **Quantitative Signals**: SELL, composite 2.8/10. Momentum −18% (worst of set), 44.1% volatility (~2.5x the market with R² of only 0.10), deteriorating quality. The only positive factor is value (7.0) — the classic value-trap factor. The +30% "revision upgrade" is a period-roll artifact on a one-analyst estimate; rejected.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. TTM FCF +$75M vs $980M dividends; current ratio 1.01; working capital −95% to $0.12B; short-term debt doubled to $1.96B against $815M cash; Altman Z 1.03. Capex ≈ D&A — there is no discretionary spend left to cut. A 20% revenue decline implies ~10x leverage and covenant stress.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. CEO/Chair duality through the most complex period in company history; acquire → impair ($2.3–4B) → divest within ~20 months; insider buying stopped after 1-May-26 despite multi-year lows (three independent directors did buy ~$4.3M Jan–May'26 — a signal that has faded, not reversed).
- **ESG**: Neutral. SBTi-validated targets credible; the spin sheds EUDR/EU ETS/CSRD drag. Governance — capital allocation — not carbon, is the differentiating weakness. ESG does not move the rating.
- **Alternative Data**: Neutral with a bullish tilt, low confidence. Three consecutive EPS beats, three quarters of sequential revenue growth, and the June/September price rounds land in the Q4'26–Q1'27 P&L. Offset: maintenance-only hiring, bearish social sentiment, and two dead feeds (Google Trends, patents).
- **Short / Contrarian View**: NO EDGE. This is real value destruction, not fraud — the "clean" Beneish M-score is a false negative manufactured by impairments, and I concur with the desk's rejection of it. The thesis is known and largely priced after −40%; the sequential inflection and the spin are positive catalysts a short cannot absorb. Flip-to-short triggers: PS NA missing the $2.27B EBITDA guide, post-spin leverage >3.5x, or price rollover alongside falling volumes.

**Bull Case**
- Supply discipline delivering real price: ~10% of NA capacity permanently closed plus three increase rounds; IP guides $900M–1.1B of incremental pricing benefit landing through Q1'27.
- Self-help inflection: PS NA operating profit more than doubled sequentially with $250–350M of cost-out underway; a mid-cycle $2.8–3.2B EBITDA supports $32–40.
- Spin unlock: the 7-Dec distribution removes a loss-making EMEA perimeter ($73M Q3 operating loss, $1.38B impairment), targets $2B of debt reduction, and leaves a cleaner NA pure-play candidate for re-rating toward SW's 7.75x EBITDA.

**Bear Case**
- Balance sheet and dividend: FCF ≈ zero against a $980M payout, Altman Z 1.03, $1.96B of short-term debt refinancing into a 6.5% BAA market with the 10Y at 5.13%.
- Legal tail: criminal probe plus treble-damages civil exposure on ~two-thirds of revenue; a co-defendant plea converts this from a valuation question into a solvency question.
- Capital-allocation record: $2.3–4B of DS Smith impairments within 24 months and retained earnings down $6.3B; a price-led recovery into falling volumes reverses the moment industry discipline cracks.

**Key Risks**
1. DOJ escalation or a Smurfit Westrock/PCA plea — the asymmetric tail; monitor weekly.
2. Dividend cut deeper than 50%, or a cut denial paired with new debt/equity issuance.
3. Post-spin capital structure: net debt >$6.8B or >3.5x retained EBITDA kills the deleveraging narrative — the single largest analytical gap, unverifiable until the Form 10/8-K.
4. Containerboard price rollover before Q1'27 with volumes still negative.
5. Spin execution: UK regulatory conditions, stranded costs, the 19.9% retained-stake overhang.
6. Input-cost inflation (OCC, energy, diesel) eroding realised price; management itself guides 2027 as a "slog."
