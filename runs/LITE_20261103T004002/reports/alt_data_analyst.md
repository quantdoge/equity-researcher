### Key Takeaways for the Investment Memo
1. **Do not trade LITE on Lumentum alt data any more.** Model it as 4.5 × MRVL plus a deal-probability curve. Any LITE "demand signal" thesis must be expressed through MRVL, or through the spread.
2. **The 11.3% arbitrage discount is the mispricing.** Either (a) the market is over-pricing deal-break risk given both boards approved and strategic logic is strong, or (b) it correctly discounts a bump/renegotiation fight from LITE holders calling $1,132 a "trash offer." Dig into the vote mechanics and break fee — that's the next research step.
3. **Consensus revenue for Feb-11 ($1.48B, +17.3% QoQ) sits below the trailing +25% QoQ cadence** — worth ~5–8% upside if the capacity ramp holds. This will be *MRVL's* problem soon, but it supports the strategic case and therefore a higher bump.
4. **Google Trends collapse (−90% off May peak) despite 80%+ revenue growth** signals the AI-optics theme is fully consensus, not emerging. Expect no narrative tailwind — only execution.

**Data limitations:** Google Trends is a poor proxy for B2B optical components (supply-chain interest ≠ product demand) and has a ~1–2 week lag; trend series end Oct 25, 2026 and therefore exclude the Nov 2 announcement spike. Patent filings unavailable (API error). Web traffic unavailable (no quantitative SimilarWeb series). Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one.
