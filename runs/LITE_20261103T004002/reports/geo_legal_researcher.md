### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**① China indium phosphide (InP) & critical-mineral export controls — Probability: High (>70%); Impact: High**
InP is the core material for Lumentum's high-speed AI lasers. China has throttled InP export licences since Feb 2025; indium prices rose from ~$250/kg to ~$805/kg (Reuters 6/11/26; Asia Times 9/4/26). LITE is **sold out through 2028 despite quadrupling output**, and has paid **AXT $87M in deposits to reserve six years of InP wafer supply** (MarketWatch 8/7/26) — a single-supplier, China-based chokepoint. Lumentum VP of Operations Ian Croston (8/26/26): *"Have we seen issues with China? We have them all the time. We have to work around."* Beijing tightened gallium, germanium, rare earths and InP through 2026; China's Sept 15, 2026 rules also permit exit bans for export-control violations.

**② Merger approval risk — China SAMR (and US HSR) — Probability: High for delay/conditions (~60–70%); Block ~20–25%; Impact: Very High**
Closing needs US and Chinese regulatory clearance plus both shareholder votes (Street Insider 10/30/26; Morningstar 10/29/26). The agreement embeds **a $3.2B regulatory termination fee payable by Marvell to Lumentum, a $5.8B general termination fee, and a $2.9B reverse fee owed by Lumentum** — the size signals the parties themselves price material regulatory failure risk. Precedent is adverse: SAMR never cleared MaxLinear/Silicon Motion (2022–23), which collapsed. A ~19-month expected timeline (to H1 CY2028) exceeds typical HSR/EU review and points squarely at SAMR. Any Chinese retaliation over US export controls lands directly on this deal.

**③ US-China truce expiry (Jan 10, 2027) + tariff exposure in Thailand/Mexico — Probability: Medium-High; Impact: Medium-High**
The truce/tariff suspension was extended from Nov 10, 2026 to **Jan 10, 2027** (CNBC 9/24/26; Barnes Richardson). A breakdown would likely tighten China's mineral licensing (compounding ①) and re-escalate tariffs. Lumentum's 10-K explicitly flags *"higher tariffs and other trade restrictions between the U.S. and other countries, including China and Thailand"* — i.e. ~56% of revenue sits in tariff-exposed manufacturing/ship-to jurisdictions.

- **Active transaction**: merger agreement signed Nov 1, 2026 (LITE 8-K/425 filings); Delaware target; shareholder litigation related to the Transaction is expressly listed as a risk in the 425 filing.
- **Regulatory perimeter**: US (HSR/antitrust, plus potential CFIUS-adjacent scrutiny given AI-infrastructure optics), China SAMR (required), and other jurisdictions not fully disclosed in available sources.
- **Legal Proceedings (10-K Item 3) and Note 16 Commitments & Contingencies exist but could not be retrieved** — the filing-text tool returned "No 10-K found for LITE" and direct SEC fetch was not executable within budget. **Gap: no confirmation of whether ordinary-course IP/patent or commercial litigation is pending.**
- **FY26 GAAP net loss of $6.9B ($92.96/diluted share) on $3.01B revenue** (Lumentum FY26 results release) — an extraordinary loss relative to revenue that requires verification of whether it reflects a non-cash impairment/valuation charge; not confirmed as a legal contingency.
- Customer concentration: 10-K fragment references a single customer at **11.4%** of net revenue in one of FY24–FY26 — concentration risk that also amplifies sanctions/export-control exposure to any single counterparty.
- Lumentum publishes a Global Trade compliance commitment covering US export control, sanctions and import laws; no FCPA/AML enforcement action surfaced in research.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags
- **Export-control licensing dependency (amber)** — dual-use InP/compound-semiconductor flows in both directions (China-origin material inbound; US-origin technology outbound). Highest-probability compliance failure vector; already causing paperwork, lead-time and cost friction.
- **China counter-sanctions/counter-extraterritoriality regime (amber)** — new tools (Apr-2026 State Council regulations; draft trade-secret rules) could restrict LITE's China/HK entities or personnel. HK exposure of 17.2% is the transmission channel.
- **No FCPA, AML, sanctions-evasion or data-privacy action identified (green)** — but absence of evidence is partly due to the unavailable legal-proceedings text.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**
Drivers: 26.7% Greater China revenue, ~80% international, a single-country chokepoint in its most critical input (InP) with a sole-source AXT deposit arrangement, and a pending $115B merger whose consummation hinges on SAMR clearance with an adverse precedent and a $3.2B regulatory break fee. Mitigants: US-domiciled, genuinely global manufacturing (Thailand, Mexico, Japan, EMEA), sold-out demand through 2028 providing pricing power to absorb input inflation, and explicit management attention to supply diversification.

**Key monitoring triggers:** SAMR filing/acceptance and review clock; Jan 10, 2027 truce deadline; any Chinese InP licence interruption or AXT shipment delay; MFN/tariff changes on Thailand or Mexico; shareholder vote dates; any securities or merger-objection suit post-announcement.
