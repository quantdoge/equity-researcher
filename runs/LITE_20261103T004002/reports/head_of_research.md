### Investment Memo: LITE — November 3, 2026

---

**Investment Thesis**

On Nov 2, 2026 Marvell agreed to acquire Lumentum in a **fixed-ratio, uncollared all-stock deal — 4.500 MRVL shares per LITE share, ~$115B EV**. LITE is no longer an optical-components equity; it is a claim on 4.5 Marvell shares trading at a 12.7% gross (~8% annualized) discount, with a $3.2B reverse break fee (~$35/share) cushioning the exact failure scenario that matters most. The underlying business is genuinely exceptional — +83% FY26 revenue, 51% gross margin, sold out through 2028 — but those returns now accrue to Marvell's share price. The correct trade is to **harvest the spread and the break-fee-protected optionality, sized as a merger-arb position, not to own LITE as an AI-optics growth story.**

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector rated **Attractive** — AI-optics oligopoly, supply (not demand) constrained, 1.6T ramp early; LITE leads its true peer set (COHR, FN, CIEN, AAOI) on every growth and margin metric. Macro rated **Unfavourable** — Fed hiking (3.75–4.00%), 10Y at 5.13%, CPI reaccelerating, crude >$100, AI-duration complex de-rating. Post-deal, LITE's macro exposure *is* Marvell's.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH**. 26.7% Greater China revenue; sole-source China InP chokepoint (AXT, $87M deposits). **China SAMR approval is the binding deal risk** — assessed at ~60–70% delay/conditions, **20–25% outright block**, with adverse precedent (MaxLinear/Silicon Motion). US–China truce expires Jan 10, 2027. Break fees ($3.2B regulatory / $5.8B general / $2.9B reverse) confirm the parties themselves price this risk.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **No margin of safety standalone.** Disciplined DCF $403–489; normalized range $450–650 vs $684 spot. Narrow cyclical moat, ROIC 8.3% below WACC, 0.5% FCF yield. The only "discount" available is the merger spread — i.e., MRVL at ~$152 vs $171 spot.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **Exceptional and validated.** FY26 +83%, FY27E +110%; gross margin 18.5% → 51.0% in eight quarters; Rule of 40 = 93; 7/7 beats; FY28E ~19x EV/EBITDA, PEG ~0.6. But the fixed ratio caps the equity's ability to re-rate independently.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral (37.5/100).** Sole factor support is momentum — and it is stale (12-1 lookback excludes the −18.6% Nov 2 gap). Value, quality, low-vol all worst-in-peer-set; β 2.96–3.58, ~99% vol, ~85% idiosyncratic variance. No quantitative basis for a fundamental Buy.
- **Financial Risk**: **Credit fortress, equity fragile.** Net cash +$1.07B, 24x interest coverage; survives a −40% revenue collapse from cash. But risk is transferred to shareholders: 0.5% FCF yield, +24% share count in a year, $1.6B debt reclassified current, working capital absorbing the ramp. The risk is multiple compression, not solvency.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** 41.6% of revenue from two customers; **~30 Form 4s with zero open-market insider buys** (10b5-1 mitigant noted); merger-objection litigation already filed (Brodsky & Smith); 19-month binary regulatory horizon. Enforcement record itself is clean.

- **Alternative Data**: Fundamentals **bullish** (capacity-ramp hiring, sold-out channel checks, next-quarter consensus revenue ~5–8% below the trailing +25% QoQ cadence); equity signals **non-actionable** — price is a pure function of 4.5 × MRVL. Google Trends −90% off peak: the theme is fully consensus, no narrative tailwind remains.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Explicitly not a fraud short.** The −$6.94B FY26 GAAP loss is a non-cash below-the-line charge; Beneish (−6.57), Altman Z (−2.99), ROE (−149%) are contaminated artifacts. Normalized accruals are negative, cash conversion >100%. The real bear case is cyclical: peak-cycle margin being capitalized as franchise, capex doubling at the top, insiders distributing into the rally.

**Bull Case**
1. **Defined-edge arb**: unanimous board approvals, 12.7% gross spread, ~8% annualized to close, plus a $3.2B reverse break fee and sale-of-company optionality (shareholder dissent cuts both ways — a bump is plausible).
2. **Fundamentals support completion**: the strongest optical franchise in the peer set, accelerating, with consensus estimates likely still too low — this de-risks the vote and supports MRVL's currency.
3. **Convex macro upside**: if crude breaks <$85 and the 10Y retraces, MRVL recovering toward $250 implies ~$1,125 conversion value — a +60–65% path with break-fee downside protection.

**Bear Case**
1. **SAMR**: a 20–25% block probability on a binary, dated, uncontrollable event. Break state = standalone value $450–650, i.e., **−20% to −35%**, with no collar.
2. **Uncollared MRVL beta for ~18 months**: post-close beta 4.63, 105% vol; MRVL already fell 32% in three sessions on AI-capex jitters; macro tape is hostile to exactly this duration exposure.
3. **Peak-cycle fundamentals**: 51% GM is shortage pricing; 41.6% customer concentration; FY24 (−23% revenue, 18.5% GM) proves the air-pocket risk; insiders sold the top and bought nothing.

**Key Risks**
1. SAMR block or remedy requiring divestiture of the EML/InP business (thesis-ending).
2. LITE or MRVL shareholder vote failure (public "trash offer" dissent makes this non-trivial).
3. MRVL de-rating below ~$120, which breaks both the spread math and the deal's strategic logic.
4. US–China truce lapse (Jan 10, 2027) transmitting into export controls or SAMR retaliation.
5. InP/AXT supply interruption forcing a guidance cut — damages the break-state standalone value the downside case rests on.
6. **Data-integrity caveat**: the exact composition of the ~$7.2–7.8B FY26 non-cash charge is unverified (10-K text inaccessible; analysts attribute it to convert equitization / deferred-tax items). All agree it is non-cash and non-operating — but automated screens (Z-score, P/E, EV/EBITDA) must not be trusted on this name. Sell-side PT dispersion ($806–$1,328, opposite ratings same day) confirms no consensus on fair value.
