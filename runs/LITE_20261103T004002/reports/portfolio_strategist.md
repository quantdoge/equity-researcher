**Why not larger.** Kelly logic applied honestly: p ≈ 0.78 close, b = +13%/−20% → naive Kelly f* ≈ 44%, which is meaningless for a **single, unrepeatable, binary event with model-estimated probabilities** (Kelly assumes repeatable independent bets). The binding constraint is therefore **risk-of-ruin, not edge**: at 1.5% weight, a total deal break with a −25% move costs **~38bp of portfolio**. That is the entire permitted loss on this name. At 3%+ unhedged it costs 75bp+ for a thesis we cannot control — unacceptable for a non-diversifying, 99%-vol, 85%-idiosyncratic position.

**Why not smaller / why not pass.** Unlike a pure speculative binary, there is a **contractual backstop**: both boards unanimously approved, the fixed ratio guarantees 4.5 MRVL shares on completion, and a **$3.2B regulatory reverse break fee payable by Marvell to Lumentum (~$35/share)** partially cushions the exact break scenario we fear most. Expected value at MRVL flat is positive (~+6–7% over 18 months on my scenario weights: 68% close / 10% bump / 22% break). A small, ring-fenced position is correct; zero is leaving a defined-edge trade on the table.

**What we are explicitly NOT doing:** buying LITE as an AI-optics growth position. Growth's thesis is right, but the returns accrue to **MRVL's share price**. If the mandate wants that growth, the efficient expression is MRVL directly — and LITE's only incremental merit is that it buys MRVL at **$152.09 vs $171.41 spot (11.3% cheaper)**, with deal risk attached.

## 5. Entry Strategy — **Trade the spread, not the price. Scale in on widening, not on weakness.**

The instrument is a spread; its price moves with MRVL, not with LITE's merits. Therefore:

1. **Do not buy on a price dip caused by MRVL falling** — that is not a discount, it is the same trade getting no better (spread unchanged) with more beta. Only a **wider spread** improves the risk/reward.
2. **Tranche it on spread, defined as `1 − LITE / (4.5 × MRVL)`:**
   - **≥12% (today: 12.70%) → open 1/3 of target** (≈0.35% unhedged).
   - **≥16% → add to 2/3.**
   - **≥20% → complete to full target.** A 20%+ spread on a signed, board-approved deal with reverse break-fee protection is where the odds genuinely compensate for SAMR.
3. **Legitimate reason to defer the remainder:** wait for **SAMR filing acceptance / Phase-I clock start** (Q1 CY2027) and the **LITE/MRVL shareholder vote dates**. Timeline risk is the market's main complaint; each procedural milestone mechanically compresses the spread. Pure arbs should let the clock prove itself before the second tranche.
4. **Do not average down after a break or a vote failure.** The thesis fully resets to standalone value, which is a different (and worse) trade at $684.
5. **Hedged pair:** open the full 3% gross immediately at the 12.7% spread — the hedge removes the beta, so tranching on price is unnecessary; tranche only on spread.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss — spread-based, not price-based.** A price stop is inapplicable: LITE's price will fall whenever MRVL falls, with no change to the thesis. Use:
- **Spread breach stop: gross spread > 25–30% → EXIT.** At that level the market is pricing near-certain breakage; you are being told something that diligence has not surfaced.
- **Absolute drawdown stop (unhedged sleeve): if MRVL < $120 (−30% from $171.41), halt and reassess** — at that point Marvell's own currency has repriced enough to threaten the deal's strategic and accretion logic, and the mark-to-market on the unhedged position is ~−30%.
- **Emotional-discipline stop: never add after a single-day move >15%** in either name (2-Nov printed −18.6% on 15.1M shares vs ~4.5M ADV — the book is thin on event days).

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any one of these, same day:**
1. **SAMR blocks, or conditions approval on divestiture of the EML / InP laser business** — this is the specific remedy that destroys the transaction's entire purpose.
2. **US HSR second request escalating without a remedy path, or EU Phase II referral** (secondary, but a timeline breaker).
3. **LITE shareholder vote fails** (public "trash offer" dissent makes this non-trivial), or either board terminates.
4. **US–China truce lapses at 10-Jan-2027 with retaliatory action explicitly targeting the transaction** — geo-legal's transmission channel into SAMR.
5. **AXT/InP supply interruption or licence denial** that forces a guidance cut — this destroys the break-state standalone value the whole downside case rests on.
6. **Insider distribution continues post-announcement with no open-market buys** — the existing pattern is the one behavioural signal that should cap any add.

**Trim / profit-taking:** exit the position when the **spread compresses below ~3%** and material regulatory steps remain. The remaining ~3% no longer pays for residual binary risk over months of dead capital — take the ~9–10% and redeploy. On the unhedged leg, also trim if MRVL rallies >40% from $171 (the 4.5× conversion value then embeds a windfall that is pure MRVL beta, not deal edge).

**Hard time stop:** the outside date is **1-Nov-2027, extendable to Aug-2028**. If the deal is not closed by the extension deadline, exit regardless of spread — capital cannot remain hostage to an open-ended process.

**Post-break playbook (pre-committed):** if the deal breaks, do **not** re-enter LITE on the same thesis, but **pre-authorise a standalone re-entry for the growth sleeve at ≤$500/share** — that is inside value's normalized $450–650 range and below growth's ~$850–1,000 bull case, i.e., the first price at which declining fundamentals meet a real margin of safety.
