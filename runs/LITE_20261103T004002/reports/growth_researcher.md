## LITE — Growth Investment Analysis
*Data as of the Nov 2, 2026 close ($684.40). One week old — and that week changed the nature of the security.*

---

### ⚠️ Threshold Finding First
**Lumentum is being acquired.** On Nov 2, 2026, Marvell (MRVL) announced a **$115B all-stock merger at a fixed exchange ratio of 4.500 MRVL shares per LITE share** (~$1,132 at announcement, a 24% premium). Expected close: **H1 CY2028**; outside deadline Nov 1, 2027, extendable to Aug 2028.

At MRVL's $171.41 close, the deal is worth **4.5 × $171.41 = $771.35 per LITE share**. LITE at $684.40 trades at a **~12.7% discount** to that — roughly an **8% annualized return** to a mid-2028 close, with a **$5.8B break fee / $3.2B regulatory reverse break fee** protecting downside.

**Implication:** the extraordinary fundamental growth below now accrues to *Marvell's* share price. LITE equity is a capped, fixed-ratio merger instrument — not a standalone hypergrowth stock. Fundamentals drive the *combined* entity's re-rating, which is the actual return lever.

---

### 1. Growth Story
Lumentum has become the critical optical layer of the AI datacenter. Its EMLs, CW lasers, pump lasers and optical circuit switching are the bottleneck components converting copper-bound compute clusters into 800G/1.6T optical fabrics — and the demand inflection has been violent and sequential, not lumpy: revenue rose **+21%, +22%, +25%, +26% quarter-over-quarter** across the last four quarters. Gross margin has gone from 18.5% (FY24) to 51.0% GAAP (Q1 FY27) as capacity utilization and mix shift to AI optics, and management is guiding Q2 FY27 to **~$1.6B revenue at 45–46% non-GAAP operating margin** — a margin structure this company has never remotely approached.

### 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration
Datacenter optical interconnect is the tightest supply chain in AI. Industry sizing puts pluggable optics at roughly **$25–30B in 2026** expanding toward **$70–90B by 2030** as 800G gives way to 1.6T and 3.2T, with additional pools in optical circuit switching (OCS) and co-packaged optics (CPO). Lumentum's serviceable slice — EMLs, high-power CW lasers, pump lasers, ROADMs, OCS — is the highest-value, lowest-competition layer: indium phosphide laser fab capacity is genuinely scarce and takes years to build.

Lumentum is running at ~$3B against a component SAM I'd estimate at **$12–18B today growing to $35–45B by 2030**. At FY30 consensus revenue of $20.7B the company would hold roughly 12–15% of a much larger pool — aggressive but not implausible given current share gains and 15%-of-revenue capex being poured into capacity. The real constraint is **supply, not demand**.

- **Product/ramp catalysts:** 1.6T transceiver volume ramp, OCS deployments, CPO laser attach, and new InP/laser capacity coming online through FY27–FY28.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

Split the verdict honestly:

- **The business: Strong Buy.** This is a genuine frontier-innovation franchise hitting an 82% two-year revenue CAGR with gross margin up 3,300bps, a 93 Rule-of-40 score, 100% beat rate and upward revisions. On FY28 estimates LITE trades at **6.9x EV/revenue and 19x EV/EBITDA** (EV ~$68.5B) and **29x FY28 earnings** — a PEG near **0.6**. For this growth, that is cheap.
- **The security: Buy, not Strong Buy.** The fixed 4.5x exchange ratio **caps your upside at Marvell's equity** and removes LITE's ability to re-rate independently. You are being paid ~12.7% gross / ~8% annualized to hold MRVL through a 15–19 month regulatory gauntlet, cushioned by unusually large break fees. That's an attractive risk-adjusted trade, but it's a merger-arb return, not a disruptive-growth return.
- **If you want the growth thesis cleanly, own MRVL.** Post-close it is the category leader in AI connectivity, and it is currently trading at a 32% discount to where it valued LITE three days earlier.

### 7. Key Risks
1. **Deal/structural risk (largest).** MRVL fell from $251.65 to $171.41 in three sessions (−32%). LITE holders now wear that volatility with **no price floor**. The $684 price is a bet on MRVL, not Lumentum.
2. **China SAMR / antitrust.** A combined MRVL+LITE would dominate merchant AI optical components. China approval is the single biggest closing risk; the $3.2B reverse break fee exists precisely because both sides see it.
3. **Hyperscaler concentration and capex digestion.** A handful of buyers (NVIDIA, Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta) drive the ramp. AI capex is the most consensus-crowded assumption in markets; one digestion quarter would break the sequential-growth narrative that justifies 11x forward sales.
4. **Execution on capacity.** Capex is 15% of revenue. Yield/qualification stumbles on new InP and packaging lines would compress a 53% gross margin fast — and that margin is the entire margin-expansion thesis.
5. **Competition.** Coherent, Innolight, Eoptolink and Fabrinet attacking from the module side; Broadcom and Marvell itself pushing CPO, which could disintermediate pluggable laser suppliers over time.
6. **Cyclicality is proven, not theoretical.** FY24 revenue fell 23% and gross margin collapsed to 18.5%. This is a capex-cycle business wearing a growth-stock multiple. The risk isn't 2027 — it's the FY2029–2030 air pocket after this build-out completes.
7. **Accounting opacity.** The −$6.9B FY26 non-operating charge and the enormous GAAP/non-GAAP gap (diluted EPS $3.54 GAAP vs $4.57 non-GAAP in Q1) require scrutiny before sizing a position.

---

**Bottom line:** The growth thesis is validated emphatically — this is one of the best fundamental inflections in technology right now, and the market's 40% drawdown has nothing to do with it. But the merger has converted the equity into a capped, ratio-fixed arbitrage with an ~8% annualized return and meaningful regulatory tail risk. **Buy the spread, or buy MRVL for the growth.**
