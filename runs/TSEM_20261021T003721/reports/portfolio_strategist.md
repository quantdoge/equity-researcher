**Factor exposure — the decisive problem.** TSEM is simultaneously: **short value** (47x fwd P/E, P/B 7.2, P/FCF negative), **short quality** (ROIC 6.3%, bottom-quintile ROE 7.6% and GP/assets), **short low-vol** (88% vol, 42% DD, worst of peer set), and **long revisions/momentum** (+37% forward EPS). **Cash-flow quality.** Negative FCF (FY25 −$41M; ~−$100M TTM ex-WC) with capex at 110% of operating cash flow means the position cannot be underwritten on any yield metric. There is no cash-flow cushion if the cycle turns — the "fortress" protects solvency, not the equity.

Here it is a vice: the idiosyncratic tail includes a **binary ITC exclusion order**, which is the one risk that does *not* diversify within a semiconductor book (GF, TSM and every US-facing customer is exposed to the same event).

**Liquidity:** adequate — 4.29M shares on the 20-Oct down day, $20.7B cap. Exiting a 2% position is not a concern; exiting it *during* an ITC headline would be.
