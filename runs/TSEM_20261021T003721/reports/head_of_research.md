### Investment Memo: TSEM — 21 October 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (do not initiate; reduce existing positions to ≤1.0%) | Conviction: **Medium**

**Investment Thesis**

Tower Semiconductor is a genuinely improved business wrapped in an uninvestable price. It has become the merchant foundry of record for silicon photonics — SiPh revenue +270% y/y to a ~$680M run-rate, **$1.3B of contracted 2027 SiPh revenue with $290M of customer prepayments**, record 30% gross margin, and a fortress net-cash balance sheet — but the stock at ~47x 2027E earnings and ~11x sales with **negative free cash flow** already prices in full delivery of a 2028 model ($3.6B revenue, ~32% net margin) that rests on 1–3 analysts and assumes a margin structure the company has never achieved (~15% net margin is its operating-history ceiling). Our probability-weighted value is ≈$138; the asymmetry is negative at spot, and three un-priced tail risks — a live ITC import-ban action, 25% Section 232 tariffs on a ~42% US revenue base, and a re-hiking Fed — compound the valuation problem.

But macro is **unfavourable**: 10Y at 4.99%, CPI re-accelerating (3.72%, +0.65% m/m), Fed back to hiking — the −42% drawdown from the June peak is duration repricing, not earnings deterioration, and it may not be finished.

**Bull Case**

- **Operating leverage has arrived**: GM 23%→30%, Q2'26 operating income +67% y/y, Q3 guide +32% y/y; if GM ≥32% and Japan 300mm capacity lands (2027–28), 2028 EPS >$10 is reachable and today's price is ~17x that.
- **Balance sheet + strategic optionality**: ~$1B net cash funds the ramp internally; Intel previously agreed to pay $53/share for a far weaker asset — takeout optionality is a genuine floor against the bear case.

**Bear Case**
- **The price requires fantasy margins**: ~32% net margin by 2028 vs a ~15% all-time operating-history ceiling, modeled by 1–3 analysts; depreciation from ~$920M/yr capex lands in COGS exactly as those margins must appear. Probability-weighted value ≈$138.
- **Binary legal/tariff tails**: an ITC exclusion order on accused SiGe/RF flows would hit the growth engine and the ~42% US revenue base simultaneously; 25% tariffs already in force are likely under-modeled.
- **Macro de-rating risk on a 47x asset**: Fed hiking into sticky 3.7% CPI with the 10Y near 5%; a second hot CPI print plausibly compresses the multiple another 25–35% *with fundamentals intact*. Insider/institutional distribution at the top corroborates.
