**Investment Thesis**
First Citizens is one of the best-capital-allocated banks in America — a family-run compounder that turned the FDIC-assisted SVB acquisition into a mechanical EPS machine: $18.5B of Purchase Money Note retired in 2026 alone, a share count down 27% since 2022, and a fresh $1.2B buyback (~4.5% of cap). But after a +8.2% post-earnings gap to an all-time high, the price (~11.6x 2026E, 1.4x TBV) sits only ~7% below central intrinsic value. The one corroborated fundamental crack is funding quality: ex-PAA NIM has fallen four straight quarters to 2.99%, now *below* the 3.01% cost of interest-bearing liabilities, with growth funded by Direct Bank/brokered deposits and allowance coverage thin at 0.91% of loans. Fair price for a very good franchise — not a buy price.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**
