**Investment Thesis**
CrowdStrike is the strongest franchise in security software — revenue accelerating for four straight quarters to +28.3%, record $419M net new ARR, ARR of $6.26B (+27%), GAAP operating breakeven, ~30% FCF margin, and a first-mover AI-agent security category already at $100M ARR. **Bull Case**

- Platform + AI-category optionality: Falcon Guardian at $100M ARR in year one; Zscaler adds the #1 SSE franchise, >$10B combined ARR, claimed $1B revenue synergies and pre-synergy FCF/share accretion — at ~4% penetration of a $250B+ TAM.

**Bear Case**
- Valuation offers zero margin of safety (34× ARR, 160× P/FCF) precisely when a hiking Fed and a 5.47% 30Y are compressing duration assets; the sector's own history (PANW, CHKP margin compression) shows platform wars cost profitability.
- The Zscaler deal is a 60–70%-probability-delayed, ~16–19% dilutive, growth-dilutive acquisition with a one-sided break fee — and the equity has already voted (−8.9% on announcement).
- Structural: seat-pricing compression from agentic AI, Microsoft E5 bundling capping endpoint pricing, SBC at 22.8% of revenue, $228M of insider selling against zero buying, and an unclosed SEC file.

2. Rates: 10Y sustained >5.5% with HY OAS widening beyond ~475bp removes the credit cushion.
3. SEC escalation (subpoena/Wells notice) — the highest-value monitoring item on the board.
4. A second systemic Falcon content-update incident — low probability, existential severity.
5. EU sovereignty procurement hitting EMEA, the growth engine.
6. Thesis-level: net-new-ARR guidance below +20% or NRR below 110% breaks the flywheel thesis — exit regardless of price.
