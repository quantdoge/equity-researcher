## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT on direction — but unusually HIGH consensus on the risk map**

**Agreement level: LOW on recommendation, HIGH on facts.** This is the most useful thing in the file. Every desk independently converged on four propositions:

1. **The business is genuinely excellent.** Growth, Value ("narrow moat but widened over 3 years"), Sector ("STRONG position"), and Short Analyst all agree. The forensics desk — the one paid to find fraud — found **none**. This removes the tail scenario that would force an immediate pass.
2. **Customer concentration is the #1 risk.** Nonfin (>70% one end customer), Fin Risk (two customers at 20% each), ESG, Value, and Short Analyst all name it as the dominant risk. Five independent confirmations.
3. **Volatility/position sizing is the binding constraint, not conviction.** Quant, Fin Risk, Macro, and Value all say so explicitly.
4. **Gross margin is structurally declining, not cyclically.** 76.4%→75.7%→73.3%→72.3%. Growth, Sector, Short, and Fin Risk agree this is a permanent mix consequence of winning the switch business.

### Conflict resolution

**Value ($140-200 FV) vs. Growth (10.6x 2027E sales, PEG 0.42).** These are not factual disagreements — they are **discount rate and terminal value disagreements**. Value uses a 14% WACC appropriate to β=3.86; Growth uses forward multiples. The $140-to-$300+ spread *is* the entire investment question. **Resolution:** neither is wrong. Value is right that at a 14% WACC there is no margin of safety; Growth is right that no negative estimate revision has occurred through a 46% de-rating. **Macro breaks the tie**: with the real 10Y at 2.92% and the Fed hiking, the 14% WACC is the more defensible input *today*. This caps size; it does not veto the position.

**Short Analyst (no fraud, quality HIGH) vs. Nonfin Risk (HIGH).** Both stand. Accounting is clean; customer concentration is real. **Resolution:** the fraud tail is removed (so no automatic pass), while the concentration risk remains and caps the position at ~3%, not 5%.

**Macro (unfavourable) vs. Alt Data (bullish).** Correct on different variables. Macro governs the *multiple* over 3-6 months; Alt Data governs *earnings* over 12-24 months. **Resolution:** the macro desk's own framing is the right synthesis — "much of the rate damage is already in the price, so further downside requires an AI-capex cut or another leg in real yields." That is an argument for a small, staged position, not for zero or for full size.

**My override:** I discount the Quant desk's −3 composite. Its value/momentum/low-vol scores are arithmetic restatements of the drawdown that has already happened, and its own author flags the 252-day Sharpe (+1.12) as a data artefact and the revision score as statistically meaningless (n=5 analysts, FY2030 horizon). I also discount the Growth desk's "high conviction" label — it is a fair characterization of the fundamentals, not of the risk-adjusted opportunity.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### **Initiate at 1.5% of portfolio. Target 3.0%. Hard cap 3.5%.**

**Why not 3-5% (the standard high-conviction bucket):**
- β **4.13**, σ **103.8%**. A 4% position carries **4.15% of standalone annualized portfolio volatility** — roughly a third of an entire equity book's total risk in a single name where **one end customer is >70% of revenue** and 67% of variance is idiosyncratic (R² vs SPY = 0.33).
- Kelly logic: with p(success) ~55-60% but an outcome distribution ranging from −45% (Macro bear, $120-140) to +45% (analyst median $300), the edge is real but the *odds* are wide. Kelly with wide outcome dispersion mandates a **fractional** stake, not a full one. Half-Kelly here lands near 2-3%.
- The Macro desk gives a specific −35% to −45% downside path **with earnings intact**. A 3% position absorbs −1.2% of portfolio on that path — tolerable. A 5% position absorbs −2.0% on a single rates headline. Not tolerable.

**Why not pass:**
- Growth **+154% YoY, accelerating**, with a Q4 guide implying +170%. Not a single negative estimate revision through the drawdown.
- **10.6x 2027E revenue / 8.2x 2028E**, PEG 0.42, and the analyst PT cluster (**$290/$300/$315**) sits **+37% to +49%** above spot.
- **Forensics cleared.** No manipulation, no restatement, no auditor change, negative accruals for four years.
- **$1.38B net cash, zero debt, Altman Z 6.86.** Even a −40% revenue scenario survives 100+ quarters. There is no solvency path to zero.
- Retail attention at a 12-month low; positioning is washed out. Alt Data's divergence — improving fundamentals, collapsed sentiment — is historically the best entry setup for β>3 names.

**Sizing by risk budget (the operative math):** targeting ALAB contributing ≤1.5% of portfolio annualized volatility implies a ~1.4% weight at 104% vol. Allowing a 3% weight accepts ~2.4% standalone risk contribution after the 0.33 R² — appropriate for the highest-conviction name in a speculative sleeve, unacceptable as a core holding.

**Mandate override:** If the portfolio already carries >20% in AI infrastructure/semis, or if the mandate requires a margin of safety on intrinsic value, the action is **PASS**. There is no version of this that is a value investment at $211.60.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration — the primary constraint.** ALAB is the *same sleeve* as CRDO and MRVL, not a separate position. Verified 252-day correlations:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ALAB** | 1.00 | **0.71** | **0.68** | 0.59 | 0.44 | 0.57 |

At 0.71 to CRDO and 0.68 to MRVL, ALAB is a near-substitute for existing connectivity exposure. **Rule: cap the total "AI connectivity" sleeve (ALAB + CRDO + MRVL) at 5.0%.** If the book already holds 2% CRDO, ALAB's cap drops to 3.0% on a combined basis — meaning a 2% ALAB weight, not 3.5%.

**Factor exposure — this is an anti-factor position, and that must be deliberate.** ALAB is simultaneously: bottom-quintile value (**446.6% P/E premium** to peer median: 173.4x vs 31.7x), negative 12-1 momentum (−10.2%), and worst-in-peer-group low-volatility (β 4.13, −62.8% MDD). Its only positive factor is **quality** (ROE 16.1%, accruals −0.065, ROIC inflecting from −18.4% to +12.4%). Adding ALAB therefore *increases* a portfolio's short-value, short-momentum, high-beta tilt. **Do not pair it with other high-multiple, unprofitable growth names.** The correct pairing is quality/cash-generative or low-beta exposure as an offset.

**Correlation & diversification:** 0.57 to SPY but R² of only 0.33 — two-thirds idiosyncratic. ALAB is **not a diversifier**. It is a levered derivative on AI capex sentiment with a rates overlay. In a 10% SPY drawdown, β 4.13 mechanically maps to a **~40-50% ALAB decline**.

**Liquidity:** ~4-5M shares/day at $211 ≈ **$900M notional** — a 3% position in a $5B portfolio is ~1 day's ADV. Build across 2-3 sessions, limit orders only, avoid the first and last 30 minutes. Volume spikes are *downside* spikes (11.98M shares on the −20% Nov 5 session vs ~3M average) — a distribution tape.

**Valuation cross-check:** current multiples are 173x P/E, 27.9x P/B, 163.7x EV/EBITDA, 134.8x P/FCF, **0.74% reported FCF yield** — and roughly **0.29% ex-SBC**. Any position must be underwritten on forward earnings, never on current cash yield.

*Why not a tighter stop:* at 104% annualized volatility and a ~$14 daily ATR, a −24% stop is ~1.5 sigma of *annual* noise. A tighter stop guarantees being stopped out on volatility, not on thesis. This is why fundamental triggers are primary.

### Thesis invalidation — **any two = reduce to zero; any one of #1-#3 alone = cut to 0.75%**

1. **Gross margin below 70.0% in any quarter.** The Short Analyst's moat-break line, not a mix line. Current: 72.3%, guided ~72%. 400bp already gone from the Q1'26 peak.
2. **Second-source award or in-sourcing at the >70% end customer.** The binary, existential event — explicitly identified by Nonfin Risk and Short Analyst as the single trigger that converts a P&L hit into a revenue step-function.
3. **Revenue growth below +50% YoY, or any QoQ decline.** Q4 guide is +27% QoQ; anything negative is a regime change, not a wobble.
4. **DSO above 60 days, or AR growth >2x revenue growth for a second consecutive quarter.** DSO has already run 17→28→39→45→45 days; AR +563% YoY against revenue +154%.
5. **Inventory days above 130** (currently 113, up from 99). Combined with #4 this is the channel-stuffing / obsolescence signature.
6. **A BIS rule extending export controls to connectivity silicon, or to re-exports via Singapore.** 53% of Q3'26 billing is Singapore, 25% China. This is a *revenue* event, not a discount-rate event.
7. **2027 hyperscaler capex guidance cut below +30%** (currently +54% consensus). The demand-side kill switch.
8. **Effective tax rate normalizing toward ~20% without an EPS offset.** Consensus FY2027 EPS of $7.16 embeds a ~0% rate funded by pre-IPO NOLs; normalizing takes it to ~$5.75 (−20%) and the multiple from 29.5x to ~37x.
