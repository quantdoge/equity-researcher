### 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **Low** (but disclosure-deficient)
- **Model:** Fabless. TSMC fabricates all ICs; ASE and Amkor assemble/test. No fabs, no process chemicals, no remediation liabilities, no environmental fines or spills disclosed in Item 3 (Legal Proceedings) of the FY25 10-K.
- **Estimated footprint (analyst estimate, not disclosed):** Scope 1+2 likely **<5,000 tCO₂e**; Scope 3 upstream (wafers, OSAT, substrates, logistics) dominant at perhaps **40–60ktCO₂e**. Implied intensity (~25–35 tCO₂e per $m revenue) is among the lowest of any hardware sector.
- **Climate transition risk:** Minimal direct. Indirect positive/negative: products *increase* AI datacenter power draw systemically (Scope 3 downstream "financed emissions" of its customers' AI buildout) — a reputational and customer-ESG-scrutiny vector, though per-bit power efficiency is a core selling point.
- **Disclosure failure is the real E risk:** no GHG inventory, no targets, no third-party assurance. As hyperscaler customers (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta) cascade CSRD-driven Scope 3 data requests and their own supplier codes onto the value chain, ALAB's failure to quantify emissions is a **commercial qualification risk** on new design wins, not a compliance-cost risk.
- **Physical climate:** Taiwan concentration at TSMC is the tail risk (earthquake/typhoon/water). Uninsured-event probability low per year, but a single event is a multi-quarter revenue shock given 14.9% Taiwan-billed and 100% foundry dependence.

### 3. Social Risk Assessment: **Medium**
- **Human capital (primary):** 756 employees at FY25 against ~$2bn FY26 revenue — extreme revenue-per-employee (>$2.5m) and intense reliance on a few hundred engineers. Headcount is being scaled by M&A (aiXscale Photonics GmbH, ~60 Pliops staff, new Tel Aviv/Ramat Gan/Haifa design centres, Bengaluru, Markham, Burnaby). Integration/retention of scarce SerDes/DSP talent is the material S item; heavy stock-based comp is the retention mechanism and is also a margin/governance cost.
- **Product responsibility:** ISO 9001:2015 certified; JEDEC reliability qualification; "break the part" stress testing. No recalls, no product-liability actions, no customer quality controversies found. Strong on this axis relative to disclosure norms.
- **Supply-chain labour:** REACH/RoHS/conflict-minerals compliance is asserted at the supplier-policy level only. No RBA/Sedex audit disclosure, no Tier-1 OSAT labour standards transparency. Unverifiable rather than adverse.
- **Diversity/community:** No EEO-1 or diversity data published. Board and executive team are male-dominated (CEO, President/COO, Chair all male), which is a common proxy-advisor flag for newly public tech.
- **Social licence:** No community opposition, no union activity, no labour disputes on record.

### 4. Governance Quality Score: **5 / 10** — *mechanically sound, disclosure-immature, concentration-heavy*

**Positives (+):** Single class of common stock — **no dual-class**; every share one vote (DEF 14A: 171.3m shares, no preferred issued). Chair (Manuel Alba) separated from CEO (Jitendra Mohan) and non-executive. Founder ownership is modest (Mohan 4.5%, Gajendra 4.3%, Alba 1.1%; all directors+officers 10.4%) — no control block. PwC auditor, SOX 404(b) attested, no restatements, no non-reliance 8-Ks. Say-on-pay 2026: **111.5m For vs 3.6m Against (~97% support)** — no compensation revolt. Non-employee director pay is equity-weighted with a $750k cap.

**Negatives (−):** **Classified (staggered) three-class board** with 3-year terms — only 3 of ~9 directors up annually, entrenching management at a company with a 54% drawdown from its June-2026 high. **No evident board-level ESG/sustainability oversight or ESG-linked compensation.** Disclosure posture is the minimum (10-K risk factors only). **Insider selling of ~$787m in 12 months with zero open-market purchases** (CEO ~$77m in April 2026, Chair ~$79.7m, COO $30.8m in Aug 2026) — largely 10b5-1, but at 88x trailing GAAP earnings the signal value is negative for alignment. **Amazon warrant** granting a >70% customer equity in up to $6.5bn of purchases through 2033 is a shareholder-dilutive, related-party-flavoured commercial arrangement that belongs on the governance watchlist. Serial M&A with no disclosed integration/ROIC framework. Capital allocation discipline untested — FY2025 was the first year of GAAP operating profit.

### 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure
- **US export controls / BIS (HIGH):** China-billed revenue 25.1% of Q3-26 ($146.7m). The 10-K explicitly flags "trade restrictions and export controls with respect to China and Chinese customers" and "regulatory risks... levying fines, restricting or delaying our product exports." Transshipment/diversion enforcement is the live risk given Singapore (53%) and Taiwan (14.9%) billing through manufacturing partners. **This is ALAB's single largest ESG-adjacent regulatory exposure.**
- **Section 232 semiconductor tariffs:** under investigation/phase-in; direct cost pass-through risk on imports of parts and modules.
- **California SB 253/261 (HIGH relevance, underappreciated):** HQ in San Jose and FY2026 revenue crossing $1bn puts ALAB in scope for mandatory Scope 1–3 emissions and climate-risk disclosure. It currently has **no inventory to report** — a genuine, near-term compliance gap.
- **Conflict minerals (Dodd-Frank 1502):** asserted but not evidenced via a filed CMR.
- **EU CSRD:** currently **out of scope** — EU billings are marginal and the aiXscale GmbH subsidiary is far below the €150m EU-turnover threshold; watch item for FY2028+.
- **CBAM:** **not applicable** (semiconductors outside covered goods).
- **ESG-linked debt covenants:** none — no material debt; equity-funded balance sheet ($1.36bn equity FY25). No greenwashing litigation exposure (no ESG claims to attack).
- **SEC climate rule:** stayed; not operative.

### 7. Overall ESG Rating: **LAGGARD** *(disclosure & third-party scores)* / **AVERAGE** *(underlying governance mechanics)*
No ESG report, no emissions data, no targets, no board ESG committee, severe-band third-party rating and index exclusion, and social/board-diversity reporting at the minimum. Offsetting: single share class, separated Chair/CEO, clean audit history, ~97% say-on-pay support, no controversies. Net: a **disclosure laggard with tolerable governance fundamentals** — not an "ESG Risk" name in the scandal sense.

### 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEUTRAL** (ESG is not the thesis; two non-financial risks should be priced)
- **Not a driver either way.** ALAB neither earns a green premium nor warrants an exclusion on ESG conduct. There is no stranded-asset, emissions-cost or litigation overhang. ESG-adjusted cost of capital is essentially unchanged.
- **But two non-financial risks are thesis-relevant and are *not* ESG-labelled in the sell-side debate:** (i) **China export-control/transshipment exposure at 25% of billings** — a BIS tightening is a direct revenue event, not a discount-rate event; (ii) **single-customer dependence at >70% end-customer / 53% of billings**, compounded by the Amazon warrant — a governance risk masquerading as a commercial one.
- **Watch items that would change the call:** a credible sustainability report with GHG inventory and Scope 3 supplier data (would lift the rating to Average and de-risk hyperscaler qualification); board de-staggering or ESG oversight mandate; a sustained halt in insider selling; conversely, any BIS action touching Singapore/Taiwan transshipment, or a quality/field-failure event at the top customer, would move this to **Negative** on both ESG and fundamental grounds.
- **Positioning:** own the AI-connectivity growth story on its merits; do not underwrite governance or regulatory risk at a 88x trailing P/E without a haircut for concentration and export-control optionality.

**Confidence: Medium-High** on governance, concentration and regulatory facts (primary filings); **Low** on environmental and social metrics — because the company discloses none, and I have deliberately not substituted vendor ESG scores for primary data.
