## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis (Revenue -20%)

Base: Rev $1,556.2M, GM 73.9%, opex $752.8M (SG&A $226.6M + R&D $526.3M), EBIT $397.1M.

| Scenario | Revenue | GM | EBIT | Margin | FCF (est.) | Solvency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: -20% rev, GM held** | $1,245.0M | 73.9% | **$167.3M** | 13.4% | ~$250–300M | No issue |
| **B: -20% rev, GM 65%** | $1,245.0M | 65.0% | **$56.5M** | 4.5% | ~$200–250M | No issue |
| **C: -40% rev, GM 60%** | $933.7M | 60.0% | **-$192.6M** | -20.6% | ~-$10M (≈breakeven) | Survives 100+ qtrs on cash |

**Conclusion: a 20% revenue decline is a P&L event, not a solvency event.** The $1.38B net cash cushion and near-zero capex intensity (5.9% of sales) mean no liquidity crisis is reachable in this cycle. Operating leverage cuts both ways, though — opex at 48.4% of revenue is largely fixed, so EBIT margin collapses ~13pts per 10% of revenue loss.

**The real downside is multiple, not default.** A de-rating to 12x EV/S (still premium) implies ~$109/share (-48%); 8x EV/S implies ~$75/share (-65%). That is the dominant risk to a shareholder — not covenant breach.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Severe customer concentration (verified, FY2025 10-K).** Two customers at **20% each** of FY2025 revenue; third-party analyses put **~82% of revenue across four buyers**. The loss of one hyperscaler program is a step-function revenue event, not a gradual erosion — and it is the specific catalyst that turns Scenario B into Scenario C.
2. **Priced for continuation of hypergrowth.** Q3'26 revenue rose **+49% QoQ / +154% YoY** — a comp that cannot be repeated indefinitely. At 22.9x EV/S, any deceleration triggers simultaneous estimate cuts and multiple compression (the -56% drawdown is this mechanism in action).
3. **SBC dilution.** $236.9M LTM (15.2% of revenue); diluted shares 184.1M vs basic 174.1M. Piotroski F7 = 0. Reported FCF overstates distributable economics by ~3x.
4. **Working-capital build.** Inventory +77% QoQ and 113 inventory days; receivables +48% QoQ. Either genuine ramp-ahead or channel stuffing/obsolescence risk if AI capex pauses.
5. **Non-trivial tail risk from beta.** At 2.9–3.9x beta, a macro drawdown produces a 60–80% equity loss mechanically.
6. **No positive credit catalysts to offset** — there is no rating, no CDS, and no debt; the credit profile simply cannot improve further, so all risk is equity-side.
