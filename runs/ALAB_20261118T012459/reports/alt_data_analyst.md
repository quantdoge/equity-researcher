### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH (positive-divergence call)**
The signals split cleanly and the split *is* the insight:
- **Fundamental/operational alt data (hiring composition, Scorpio run-rate, Orion/Hyve ramp, GM above guide, +49% QoQ) = bullish.**
- **Attention/flow alt data (Trends at 12-m low, Reddit capitulation, in-line guide) = bearish — but these are sentiment artifacts, not demand deterioration.**
- The **negative alt data has already been fully paid for** in a 56% de-rating, while the **positive alt data is not in the price**. That asymmetry — not the tape — is the trade.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
**Why not High:** two of six feeds failed (patents, web traffic), social is proxy-grade only, job postings have no time series, and there is **no near-term catalyst** (next print ~3 months out) so the divergence can persist. Beta 3.86 means the sector selloff can overwhelm the fundamental signal.

**Data limitations & lag:** Google Trends is weekly and ~2–5 day lagged, and conflates retail speculation with demand for a B2B vendor. Patent and web-traffic data are **entirely missing** — the R&D-intensity question is unanswered. Earnings-surprise history reflects non-GAAP EPS. Guidance midpoint vs. consensus compares a GAAP-based guide to a possibly non-GAAP consensus. News sentiment is keyword-based, not semantic. **Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one.**

**What would flip this to Bearish:** a Q4 guide cut, hiring shift toward G&A/ops-only (retrenchment), gross margin slipping below 72%, or Scorpio run-rate commentary softening. **What would raise confidence to High:** two consecutive months of rising job postings, patent-filing series recovery, and a positive estimate revision cycle into the Feb 9 print.
