## Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Astera Labs, Inc. (NASDAQ: ALAB)

**Snapshot:** Fabless AI-connectivity semiconductor company (Aries retimers, Taurus, Leo CXL controllers, Scorpio fabric switches). IPO 20 Mar 2024. 756 employees. CEO/co-founder Jitendra Mohan; President & COO/co-founder Sanjay Gajendra. Market cap ~$37.0B at $211.60; beta 3.86. FY2025 revenue $852.5M (+115%); TTM (Q3'26) $1.56B; Q3'26 revenue $584.9M (+153.7% YoY), GAAP gross margin 72.3% (down 390 bps YoY). Stock is 54% below its 30 Jun 2026 high of $483.02.

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE (on watch / trending weaker on alignment)**

Evidence:
- **Board structure:** Classified board (three classes; Class II — Gajendra, Barratt, Hurlston — stood for election in 2026). Classified boards entrench management and delay shareholder recourse. Audit, Compensation and Nominating committees are chartered as fully independent per Nasdaq tests, and the non-employee director compensation policy was refreshed Oct 2026 ($85k cash retainer, $365k initial / $220k annual RSU, $750k cap) — conventional and not outsized.
- **Board talent appears credible:** Barratt (ex-Atheros CEO), Hurlston (public-company CEO), Lazar, Mayer, plus Dyckerhoff and Alba (Sutter Hill). No evidence of a rubber-stamp board.
- **Founder concentration:** Co-founder is CEO and a director; co-founder is President/COO and a director. Whether the Chair is independent is **unverified** (proxy text inaccessible — see gaps).
- **Shareholder dissent:** At the 2026 annual meeting, 171.3M shares were entitled to vote, 141.4M represented; Gajendra received 98.4M for / **16.8M withheld (~14.6% of votes cast)** — a meaningful protest vote against a founder-director, unusual at this scale.
- **Compensation:** CEO Mohan was paid ~$2.2M in 2025 — modest cash for a $37B company, i.e., alignment is via equity, which is appropriate but means insider *selling* behavior is the more telling signal.
- **CFO turnover:** Michael Tate notified retirement 4 Feb 2026, effective 2 Mar 2026, remaining as Strategic Advisor to the CEO to 1 Sep 2026; Desmond Lynch appointed CFO. Orderly and disclosed via 8-K, but a CFO exit at a hypergrowth company with a six-month advisory tail warrants monitoring of the FY26 10-K internal-control disclosures.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary — **MODERATE**

- **No SEC/DOJ enforcement history:** EDGAR full-text enforcement search returns **zero** actions naming ALAB. No disclosed material litigation or investigations in filing metadata.
- **Export controls (primary exposure):** Q3'26 revenue by billing address was **Singapore 53.0%, China 25.1%, Taiwan 14.9%, United States 1.0%**. With ~25% China-billed revenue, ALAB sits directly inside tightening US BIS semiconductor/AI export rules (Jan 2026 BIS licensing rule; 2026 de minimis/foreign-direct-product-rule expansion). Singapore-heavy billing adds end-use/transshipment compliance risk. Any rule change affecting AI accelerator-adjacent connectivity content is a direct revenue risk.
- **Customer equity arrangement:** Feb 2026 Amazon performance-based warrant — up to **3.26M shares at $142.82**, vesting on up to **$6.5B of purchases** of Scorpio/signal-conditioning products. Disclosed via 8-K Items 1.01/3.02 and contractual filings. Creates $18.1M contra-revenue in Q3'26 (vs $1.9M a year earlier) and a milestone-driven revenue incentive; expect scrutiny of revenue-recognition and milestone attribution.
- **Data privacy/IP:** No GDPR/CCPA or IP-litigation actions found; IP risk is structural (competitors Marvell, Credo, Broadcom) rather than active litigation.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Customer concentration is extreme and worsening.** Top three end customers were **~86% of FY2025 revenue** (vs ~80% FY2024) and **one end customer was >70% of FY2025 revenue**. The largest *direct/invoiced* customer was **53% of Q3'26 revenue vs 27% a year earlier**; customers at 10%+ of revenue have represented 77–90% of quarterly revenue for six straight quarters. A single hyperscaler's architecture decision (e.g., moving fabric switching in-house or to a competitor) is an existential-scale event.
- **Sole-source supply chain and Taiwan concentration.** TSMC fabricates **all** ICs; ASE and Amkor handle assembly/test. Fabless with no second source on leading-edge nodes, plus 14.9% Taiwan-billed revenue and cross-Strait geopolitical exposure, means no business-continuity substitute for a Taiwan disruption or an allocation squeeze on CoWoS-class capacity.
- **Hypergrowth execution and margin quality.** Sequential growth of +49% (Q3'26) and guidance implying +27% to $730–750M tests forecasting, inventory and quality systems at a 756-person company. Gross margin has compressed 390 bps YoY on **higher inventory-related charges and manufacturing overhead**, and Amazon warrant contra-revenue is scaling — a mix/execution signal worth tracking.
- **Key-person dependency:** strategy, customer relationships and technical credibility are concentrated in two co-founders; there is no evidence of a deep bench behind them. CFO seat also changed hands in March 2026.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **LOW to MODERATE**

Product narrative is strong (Scorpio became the largest product family; $1B+ ARR exit targeted; Hyve Solutions/Orion rack-scale expansion in Nov 2026; BMO conference messaging on revenue-per-accelerator above $1,000/XPU). No recalls, product-safety events, ESG controversies or consumer-backlash exposure — ALAB sells B2B into hyperscalers, so brand risk is concentrated in customer trust rather than public sentiment. The real reputational vector is **narrative, not conduct**: a 54% drawdown inside five months, P/E ~88x on TTM GAAP EPS, "AI bubble" framing, and a live sector backdrop of governance noise (an Nvidia board/CEO investigation reported in Nov 2026) that can re-rate the whole complex. Sell-side remains bifurcated (multiple upgrades and a downgrade within weeks), which amplifies headline sensitivity.

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Drivers: (i) single end customer >70% of revenue plus a 53% largest invoiced customer; (ii) $787M of insider selling with zero insider buying; (iii) single-source TSMC supply with Taiwan/China-weighted billing (78% of Q3'26 revenue from Singapore, China and Taiwan); (iv) a customer equity warrant that both dilutes and ties $6.5B of demand to performance milestones; (v) a 3.86 beta equity in a sentiment-driven end market.

Not **Critical**, because: no SEC/DOJ enforcement history, no restatements or auditor changes found, no material litigation disclosed, credible independent directors, modest executive cash compensation, and continued large insider equity ownership.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **>70% of FY2025 revenue from one end customer**, with the largest invoiced customer at 53% of Q3'26 — request customer-level contract terms, cancellation/consignment rights and design-win durability.
2. **$786.7M insider sales / $0 open-market purchases LTM**, including same-day, same-size CEO and COO blocks at $340.36 and $53M of director (Sutter Hill) selling — confirm 10b5-1 adoption dates and whether any plans were modified during the window.
3. **Amazon warrant on up to $6.5B of purchases** — assess revenue-recognition timing, milestone definitions and whether the $18.1M contra-revenue is properly presented; watch for pull-forward incentives.
4. **~25% China-billed revenue and 53% Singapore billing** — verify export-licensing controls, end-use screening and distributor/contract-manufacturer sell-through visibility (also a channel-stuffing watch item: ALAB invoices manufacturing partners, not end customers).
5. **14.6% withhold vote on co-founder/COO Gajendra** — test proxy-advisor recommendations and any compensation or related-party items behind the dissent.
6. **CFO transition (Feb–Mar 2026) with a six-month CEO-advisor tail** — monitor the FY2026 10-K for any material weakness or control remediation disclosure.
7. **Classified board plus founder-CEO and founder-COO board seats** — confirm Chair independence and whether any super-voting class exists.
