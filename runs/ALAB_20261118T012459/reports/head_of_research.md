### Investment Memo: ALAB — 18 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (speculative, position-sized) | Conviction: Medium — *high on the business, low on the entry point*
**Price Target**: $290 (+37% from current $211.60), the post-reset analyst cluster; intrinsic value at a risk-appropriate 14% WACC is ~$179
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Astera Labs is the highest-quality franchise in the AI connectivity niche — revenue accelerating (+93% → +104% → **+154% YoY**, Q4 guided +170%), 72%+ gross margins, a debt-free balance sheet with $1.38B net cash, and forensic accounting that our short desk explicitly cleared. But it is also one customer away from a step-function revenue event (>70% of FY25 revenue from a single end customer), carries a ~4 beta in a hiking cycle with real 10Y yields at cycle-tight 2.92%, and trades at a price where even a generous DCF finds no margin of safety. The 56% drawdown has de-rated the *multiple* while leaving *estimates untouched* — that asymmetry justifies a small, tranche-built position, not a full one.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector attractive (hyperscaler capex +77% guided for 2026, $1.3T+ by 2027; ALAB growing 3.3x peer median with the group's highest gross margin). Macro unfavourable — Fed hiking, real 10Y ~2.92%, HY OAS +69bp to 334bp. Per our macro desk: *the next 20% move is a rates-and-credit event, not a revenue event*.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. ~95% of revenue Asia-billed; China ~25–30% in an active decoupling cycle; 100% TSMC sole-source; Section 232 tariffs live. No active litigation — risk is concentrated and binary, not diffuse.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD/AVOID for value buyers. DCF at 14% WACC = ~$179; buy zone <$140. ~87x TTM earnings, ~0.3% ex-SBC FCF yield. A wonderful business at a demanding price.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY, high conviction. 10.6x 2027E sales, PEG 0.42, 100% beat rate, 2030E EPS revised +24.5%, Scorpio >$1B run-rate, zero downward revisions through a 56% drawdown.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL (net −3). Worst-in-group low-vol (β 4.1–5.0, σ ~104%, −63% max DD), negative momentum, bottom-quintile value; quality the only positive. We discount the signal — it largely restates a drawdown that has already occurred.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE, split — solvency/liquidity LOW (Altman Z 6.86, 8x current ratio, no debt); equity risk VERY HIGH. Watch: SBC at 15.2% of revenue, WC drag −$311M, inventory +77% QoQ (113 days), DSO 17→45 days.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. One end customer >70% of revenue; largest invoiced customer 27%→53% in a year; $787M insider sales vs $0 buys; Amazon warrant ties up to $6.5B of purchases to equity.
- **ESG**: NEUTRAL — disclosure laggard (no GHG data, Sustainalytics severe-band) but clean governance mechanics (single class, separated Chair/CEO, ~97% say-on-pay, no controversies). Not thesis-driving.
- **Alternative Data**: BULLISH divergence — hiring rotated into optics/switches/pricing (not retrenchment), GM above guide, while retail attention sits at a 12-month low. Positive signals not in price; negative already paid for.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: NO EDGE — fraud thesis *rejected*. Negative accruals four straight years, cash conversion >100%, management under-promising. The surviving bear case is concentration + multiple, not evidence.

**Bull Case**
- Fastest growth in the sector (+154% and accelerating) at a PEG of 0.42 — the drawdown compressed the multiple, not the estimates
- TAM expanding faster than share risk: $20B+ merchant scale-up switching by 2030, sub-10% penetrated; Orion/Hyve extends ALAB from chip vendor to rack-scale subsystem supplier
- Fortress balance sheet + clean forensics remove the fraud and solvency tails entirely — even −40% revenue survives 100+ quarters

**Bear Case**
- >70% single-customer concentration: one in-sourcing or second-source decision is an existential event, not a margin event
- Structural gross-margin decline (76.4% → 72.3%) as lower-margin switches become the majority of revenue
- Macro asymmetry: a December 50bp hike + OAS through 450bp takes the stock to $120–140 (−35–45%) *with earnings intact*

**Key Risks**
1. Second-source/in-sourcing at the dominant customer (binary)
2. GM below 70% — moat break, not mix
3. China export-control escalation hitting ~25% of billings / Singapore transshipment rules
4. Tax-rate normalisation cutting FY27 EPS ~20% (consensus embeds ~0%)
5. Working-capital deterioration (DSO 45 days, inventory 113 days) signalling channel risk
