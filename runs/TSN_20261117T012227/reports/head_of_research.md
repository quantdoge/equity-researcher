**Investment Thesis**
Tyson is a clean-accounting, cash-generative but structurally under-earning commodity protein processor — ROIC of 2.6% against a ~7% cost of capital, a 2.0% operating margin, and a 5.7% gross margin that proves the absence of pricing power — whose one genuine moat (Prepared Foods) is carrying the P&L while beef loses money at scale. After a +26.7% run off the October low and a +8.7% post-earnings pop, the stock now trades at 50x GAAP earnings, ~18x *trough* adjusted earnings, ~12x trough EBITDA, a ~14% premium to base-case intrinsic value (~$56), and *above* the most recent sell-side target ($56, Piper). Eight specialist agents produced zero Buy recommendations: the recovery option on the FY2028–29 beef cycle is real, but at $63.95 you are paying full FY2029 value in FY2026 for a company whose own guidance calls for *lower* revenue and flat-to-down operating income in FY2027.

**Bear Case**
