### 1. Consumer Demand Signals

**Google Trends — "Tyson Foods" (US, 12-mo): NEGATIVE but low-information**
Current weekly interest 28–32 versus a 12-month average of ~45 — roughly 30% below baseline, and the lowest readings of the year. Critically, the August spike (86 → 100, week of Aug 9–16, 2026) is *not* demand: it coincides with the **Aug 13, 2026 beef network restructuring announcement** (Joslin, IL and Eagle Mountain, UT facility closures). Corporate-name search tracks news events, not sell-through. Read this as "news flow has gone quiet," not "demand has collapsed." Low weight.

**Google Trends — "Jimmy Dean" (branded demand proxy): POSITIVE**
12-month average ~72. Last four weeks: **100 (Oct 25), 86 (Nov 1), 81 (Nov 8)** vs a trailing 8-week base of ~69–73 → **+25% sequential acceleration** into the Thanksgiving/holiday window. Year-over-year is roughly flat-to-slightly-down (81 now vs 87 in the comparable 2025 week), so this is *acceleration off a low base*, not share gains in isolation. Directionally consistent with management's claim that the **branded portfolio outgrew the category by 150bps** and that Prepared Foods margin expanded to **12.6%**, generating >50% of operating profit. Two independent sources agreeing is the strongest signal in this dataset.

**Google Trends — "chicken breast price" (category pricing/inflation proxy): SHARPLY NEGATIVE**
Collapsed from 100 (late May 2026) to **14–18 in November** — an ~85% decline in consumer price-check interest. Consumers search price when prices are high or spiking; this decay implies **retail chicken price deflation**. Implication: the Chicken segment's profitability (currently intact, "margin normalization") is being defended on cost, not price. Expect **chicken segment revenue per lb headwind** in FY27. This is the most under-appreciated alt-data finding here and it is bearish for the top line.

**Web traffic (tyson.com): NO DATA** — SimilarWeb proxy returned only competitor-listing pages, no monthly visit series. Gap; not recoverable within budget.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **IN-LINE to modestly BELOW consensus on the number, but tracking ABOVE on surprise risk**

Consensus for the next print (Q1 FY27, ~2027-02-01): **EPS $0.952, revenue $14.16B**.

Alt-data read vs. the prior quarter:
- **Above** on Prepared Foods (branded search acceleration + retailer pull for Jimmy Dean/Hillshire into holidays) — the highest-margin segment, >50% of operating profit.
- **Below** on Chicken (price deflation signal) and **materially below** on Beef, where cattle costs are not cooperating. USDA/ERS forecasts five-area direct steer at a **record $247.21/cwt in 2027** vs $244.15 in 2026 — i.e., *no cost relief in FY27 at all*. Herd rebuilding has begun (beef replacement heifers +3% to 3.8M head; 2026 calf crop −2% to 32.5M), so the beef tailwind is an **FY2028+ story**, exactly as guidance implies.
- Guidance already reset: FY27 comparable sales **−3.5% to −5.5%**, adjusted operating income **$1.70–2.15B**. Q4 beef volumes fell **−12.7%** with segment operating loss widening to **$171M**.

**Surprise history (7 quarters): 85.7% beat rate, average surprise +11.9%.** Q4 FY26 was the exception — $0.61 vs a $0.65 Zacks consensus (**−6.2% miss**), against $1.15 a year ago. Note the data-quality conflict: our calc feed shows a $0.533 estimate (a "beat"), while Zacks ran $0.65. **The miss is the correct read; the estimate series in our feed is stale.** The bar for Q1 FY27 has now been cut hard, which historically is exactly where this name beats.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL**
*(Tactical lean: mild positive into the Feb-2027 print; structural bias: negative on FY27 revenue)*

**The bull case from alt data:** branded demand (Jimmy Dean) accelerating +25% sequentially into the holiday quarter, corroborated independently by Prepared Foods' 12.6% margin and +150bps category outperformance; sentiment maximally negative and demonstrably being absorbed by price; a record 85.7% beat rate off a freshly-lowered bar; zero retail crowding.

**The bear case from alt data:** chicken price deflation is real and broad, capping the one segment that is currently carrying earnings; cattle at record $247/cwt in 2027 means zero beef cost relief inside the forecast horizon; corporate search interest at 12-month lows.

**Positioning read:** the stock has already run **+26.7% off the Oct 1 low of $50.47** and +8.7% post-print — alt data supports the *fundamental* stabilisation but **does not support chasing at $63.95**, which sits ~6% below the May 2026 high of $68.26. Alt data does not justify new money here; it does argue against shorting a name where sentiment is this washed out and the guide is already reset.

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly and renormalised to 100 per window, so cross-period level comparisons are approximate. News sentiment is keyword-count based and is distorted by single-day event clusters — a −0.714 score on an earnings day says more about article volume than tone. SimilarWeb, Thinknum/LinkedIn and Reddit proxies returned no quantitative series; no satellite, card-panel or retail-traffic data was available for this name. **Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one** — here it is directionally supportive of a stabilising Prepared Foods franchise set against a structurally impaired Beef segment, which is a *hold*, not a *buy*.
