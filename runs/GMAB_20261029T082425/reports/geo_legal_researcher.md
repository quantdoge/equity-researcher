## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**Risk 1 — US drug-pricing and tariff regime (Section 232 + MFN). Probability: High. Impact: Medium.**
On **April 2, 2026** the White House signed a Section 232 proclamation imposing a **100% ad valorem tariff on patented pharmaceuticals and APIs**, effective **July 31, 2026** for large companies / Sept 29, 2026 for smaller manufacturers, with tiered relief: **15% for EU, Japan, South Korea, Switzerland/Liechtenstein, UK**; 0% for companies with an executed **HHS MFN pricing agreement**; 20% transitional for approved onshoring plans (escalating to 100% on April 2, 2030) (White House proclamation, Apr 2 2026; Clinical Leader/Beroe, Apr 15 2026; EY Global Tax Alert 2026-0787). GMAB sits in the **15% EU bucket** by origin, but the *incremental* risk is that its 2027 launch cohort (petosemtamab, Rina-S) will face **MFN-conditioned pricing** — i.e., launch-price deflation tied to a political, not clinical, process. Partly mitigated: GMAB has a US manufacturing site (Brooklyn Park, MN) and US CMC via ProfoundBio/Seattle, supporting an onshoring designation. **Open question not resolved within budget: whether GMAB appears on the 17-company Annex III list.**

**Risk 2 — Royalty-stream legal/contractual erosion on DARZALEX. Probability: Medium (structural, ongoing). Impact: High.**
J&J unilaterally reduced the royalty split in 2020 (Halozyme SC-delivery cost-sharing claim). GMAB fought this through **two arbitrations plus an appeal and lost all three** — the appeal arbitrator (Jan 2024) held the Faspro claim should have been raised in the first arbitration, exhausting GMAB's royalty claims (Fierce Pharma, Jan 23 2024; Global Arbitration Review). Consequence: DARZALEX royalties step down structurally and will **terminate at patent expiry early next decade** — the single biggest long-dated risk to the equity story, and one that is legal in nature, not commercial.

**Risk 3 — Denmark/US political friction (Greenland) + EU trade retaliation spillover. Probability: Low-Medium. Impact: Low-Medium.**
2026 saw an active **"Greenland crisis"** with the US pressing Danish sovereignty claims and open tariff threats against Denmark, before de-escalation (Wikipedia/Greenland crisis; PIIE). Direct operational impact on GMAB is minimal (Denmark is a small share of revenue), but a Danish-domiciled, US-listed company is a visible target in a broader US–EU trade escalation, and Danish export-control modernisation (**Executive Order 365/2026**, aligning with EU dual-use rules) adds compliance overhead for a company with increasing China-based R&D.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

- **Arbitration (resolved, adverse):** Two Janssen arbitrations + appeal, all lost by 2024. No further recourse. Treat as permanently impaired royalty economics rather than contingent upside.
- **No material *active* litigation disclosed** in available 2025/2026 interim disclosure; the standard 20-F risk factors (IP enforceability, third-party infringement) apply, and Rina-S enters a **crowded FRα ADC field (AbbVie's Elahere)** where IP and commercial litigation is a live industry pattern. Genmab also **discontinued two ADCs (GEN1160, and a second) from the $1.8bn ProfoundBio deal**, which raises impairment/portfolio-pruning scrutiny but not legal risk (BioSpace).
- **Regulatory posture — generally favourable:** FDA approved an sBLA in Q1-26 removing the 24-hour hospitalisation recommendation for 3L+ r/r DLBCL (EPKINLY), a meaningful label-expansion win; three positive Phase 3 readouts (petosemtamab LiGeR-HN1, Rina-S RAINFOL-02, epcoritamab DLBCL) set up **2027 launches** and therefore 2027–28 HTA/payer reviews in the US, EU (EU HTA JCA) and Japan.
- **Medicare/IRA:** DARZALEX has **not been selected in the CMS negotiation cycles announced to date**; as the post-approval clock runs, later-cycle selection becomes a growing tail risk given its >$16bn run-rate. *(Directional risk; not a current selection.)*
- **EU-specific:** EU HTA Regulation, the EU Pharma Package (data-protection reform) and CSRD sustainability reporting all apply as a Danish/EU issuer; Dutch (Utrecht) and Danish works-council/consultation obligations now matter post-Merus.
- **M&A integration:** **Merus N.V. (closed Dec 2025)** — $77m of H1-26 integration charges, $90m guided for FY26. Adds Netherlands ops, Biclonics/BIZENGRI IP estate, and integration-execution risk.

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **China nexus (ProvenBio/Genmab Suzhou)** — an R&D and potential CMC footprint in Suzhou inside an ADC platform that will manufacture US commercial product. This is the clearest **BIOSECURE-Act / data-security / 100%-tariff-tier** exposure in the story and deserves a specific question on the call.
2. **Third-party/CRO and investigator-site bribery risk** across global trials — FCPA/UK Bribery Act exposure is generic but real for a company scaling commercial ops in the US, EU and Japan simultaneously; no disclosed enforcement action identified.
3. **GxP/data-integrity and pharmacovigilance inspections** across newly acquired Merus and ProfoundBio entities — no red flag found, but integration is recent.
4. **No sanctions, AML, or export-control violations identified.** No FCPA/DOJ/SEC enforcement found in my sweeps.

---

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale:** Operations sit almost entirely in **low-risk jurisdictions** (Denmark, Netherlands, US, Japan) with strong rule of law, no sanctions exposure, and no live material litigation or government investigation. That caps the downside. What holds it at Medium rather than Low is **concentration risk of a legal/policy kind, not an EM kind**: (i) ~two-thirds of revenue rides on a single royalty contract whose terms GMAB has already litigated and lost, with a hard patent-expiry cliff next decade; (ii) ~55% US revenue exposure into an actively protectionist US pricing/tariff regime (Section 232 + MFN) timed exactly against its 2027 launch cycle; and (iii) an unhedged China R&D footprint inherited through the ProfoundBio deal.

**Key monitoring items:** Annex III listing status; any HHS MFN pricing agreement (0% tariff upside vs. price-concession downside); CMS negotiation list for DARZALEX; petosemtamab/Rina-S launch pricing in the US and EU HTA outcomes; further ADC discontinuations from the ProfoundBio book.
