# FINANCIAL RISK ASSESSMENT — GENMAB A/S (GMAB)
**Biotechnology | Denmark | Market cap ~$35.2bn | FY2025 (period end 31-Dec-2025)**

⚠️ **Structural data caveat first:** FY2025 statements are reported in **USD**; FY2024 and earlier in **DKK**. The company also closed the **~$8.0bn all-cash Merus acquisition on 12-Dec-2025**, funded with cash on hand plus **~$5.5bn of new non-convertible debt**. Both facts make naive year-over-year ratio trends (including the Piotroski inputs) unreliable and are central to the risk read.

---

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **INVESTMENT GRADE (lower rung / unrated)**

Leverage jumped from essentially net-cash to 2.78x net debt/EBITDA in a single quarter, but debt service is comfortably covered and cash generation is large and positive. Not distressed; not a fortress anymore either.

**Critical adjustment:** reported interest coverage of 20.5x is **not the run-rate**. FY2025 interest expense was only $61.0m against $5,426m of debt because the acquisition debt was drawn in mid-December. At the current ~5% marginal cost (10Y UST 4.99%), **forward interest expense ≈ $270m**, giving true **EBIT coverage ≈ 4.7x** and **EBITDA coverage ≈ 4.9x**. That is the number to underwrite.

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis (revenue –20%, i.e. –$744.6m)

Because cost of revenue is only 8.3% of sales while R&D (~$1.56bn, ~42% of revenue) and SG&A ($602m) are effectively fixed, the operating leverage is severe:

| Scenario | EBIT | EBITDA | Net debt/EBITDA | Interest coverage (on $271m run-rate) | FCF |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base FY2025 | 1,266.0 | 1,337.1 | 2.78x | 4.7x | +1,150 |
| **–20% rev, no cost action** | 497.1 | 568.2 | **6.5x** | **1.8x** | ~+250 to +300 (still positive) |
| **–20% rev + 15% opex cut** | 821.5 | 892.6 | 4.2x | 3.0x | ~+500 |
| –30% rev, no action | 155.7 | 226.8 | 16.4x | 0.6x | negative |

**Liquidity holds in every case:** $1,711m cash + $500m RCF = $2,211m covers $273m ST debt and ~$1,151m of non-debt current liabilities; cash/current liabilities = 1.20x, and FCF stays positive even at –20% revenue. **This is a coverage/leverage story, not a liquidity story.**

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating — **MODERATE**

*Not Low:* leverage was created by debt-funding an $8bn acquisition, tangible equity is **negative**, 74% of the asset base is acquired goodwill/intangibles exposed to binary clinical outcomes (petosemtamab), and the equity has drawn down 62% in under three years at 48% vol.
*Not High:* $1.15bn of FCF, $1.7bn cash plus an undrawn $500m facility, 2.78x net leverage, and coverage that survives a 20% revenue shock with FCF intact.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Partial-year interest expense masks true coverage** — 20.5x reported vs ~4.7x run-rate. 2026 guidance/consensus built on the former will be wrong.
2. **Negative tangible book value (–$3.6bn); goodwill/intangibles = 162% of equity.** An impairment on the Dec-2025 Merus assets would hit reported equity directly.
3. **Refinancing cliff, unquantified:** $5,001m long-term + $273m short-term raised Dec-2025. **No debt maturity schedule available** — the single most important missing input for credit work.
4. **Revenue concentration:** economics remain dominated by DARZALEX royalties from a single partner (J&J). A –20% royalty/price shock is a realistic single-event path to the stress case above.
5. **Growth deceleration:** revenue CAGR 2022–24 was ~21.8% (DKK 14.5bn → 21.5bn) but FY2025 implies ~11–12%; profitability declined year-on-year as D&A and interest stepped up.
6. **Altman Z reads "Distress" (1.70) on book equity but "Safe" (~4.2) on market cap** — a 2.5-point spread driven purely by the x4 definition. I would not rely on either reading.
