## 1. Macro Environment Summary — "Re-acceleration / tightening resumed" (risk-off for duration, risk-on for idiosyncratic catalysts)

The US macro regime shifted materially in the last two months. The Fed **raised** the funds rate 25bp on 16 Sep 2026 to **3.75–4.00%** — the first hike since 2023 — then **held on 28 Oct 2026** on a 10–2 vote with dissents toward further tightening, as headline CPI re-accelerated to **3.7% y/y** (from 3.2% in March) and *broadened*. The long end has repriced violently: the 10Y went from 4.45% in May to a **5.31% peak on 5 Oct** (now **4.99%**), and the 30Y sits at **5.37%** — a bear steepener driven by term premium and inflation risk, not growth optimism. The 2Y at 4.65% sits ~75bp above the mid-point of the funds range, i.e. the market is pricing *more hikes*, not cuts. Growth itself is fine (real GDP +2.2% q/q annualised in Q2 2026, unemployment 4.2%), so this is a **stagflation-lite / policy-error-risk tape**, not a recession tape. The curve is positively sloped (+34bp 2s10s), so the classic inversion recession signal is absent; the stress is in real yields and the discount rate.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact (GMAB)

Genmab's economics are a **USD royalty annuity on DARZALEX (≈80% of royalty revenue) plus an owned/co-developed commercial and pipeline tail** (EPKINLY, petosemtamab, rina-S). DARZALEX Q3 2026 net sales were **$4,239m, of which $1,710m ex-US → ~60% US**. Guidance is in **USD** and assumes **USD/DKK 6.2**; with EUR/DKK pinned near 7.46 by ERM II, implied spot USD/DKK ≈ **6.66**, i.e. ~7% weaker DKK than assumed. Guidance context: H1 2026 royalties $1,708m, Darzalex H1 sales $8,171m (+20.6% y/y), 2026 adjusted operating profit guided to **$1.07–1.39bn** (raised).

The stock is currently trading on clinical catalysts, not on macro — which is precisely why the macro matters now: a de-rated multiple on a stock that has already re-rated is where the damage would come from.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL (with an unfavourable tilt on rates/policy, offset by FX)**

Justification: Genmab is not rate-*fragile* (net cash, profitable, no refinancing wall) — so this is not a 2022-style biotech funding squeeze. But it is rate-*sensitive* in valuation (long-duration assets at a 5% 10Y / 5.37% 30Y), and it carries concentrated US drug-pricing exposure through Darzalex royalties. The FX channel is currently a genuine tailwind (~7% DKK weakness vs. the guided rate). Net: macro is not the driver of the thesis right now, but it is a persistent ~10–15% multiple headwind rather than a tailwind.

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **Bear: "The 6% long bond"**

Inflation (3m annualised 4.6%, broadening per the October Fed statement) forces the Fed to follow September's hike with two more, taking the funds rate to 4.50–4.75% by mid-2027. Term premium and deficit-financing pressure push the **10Y through 5.75% and the 30Y above 6.25%**; the ECB is dragged into its own hiking cycle and EUR/USD breaks to 1.05. Simultaneously, the MFN regime is implemented aggressively and the 1 Jan 2027 MFP list bites US oncology net prices. In that world Genmab faces: (a) a 150–200bp higher discount rate on a 2027–2035 pipeline NPV, (b) ~60% US royalty base under price pressure, and (c) no FX offset (a stronger dollar helps costs but a weak euro area compresses ex-US royalty translation). A **25–35% de-rating** is plausible even with petosemtamab data fully intact — a purely macro-financial drawdown that would look like a buying opportunity only after the long end stabilises.

*Bull mirror case (lower probability near term):* if the September hike proves the last and CPI rolls to ~2.5% by Q2 2027, the 10Y back to 4.2% plus three 2027 launches would compound multiple expansion on top of the clinical re-rating.
