## 1. Growth Story
Genmab is in the middle of converting from a **royalty-collecting antibody licensor** (Darzalex/J&J, Kesimpta/Novartis) into an **integrated oncology commercial company** — and in the last ten days that thesis de-risked violently. Three consecutive Phase 3 wins in October 2026 (Rina-S in platinum-resistant ovarian, epcoritamab in 1L DLBCL, and petosemtamab in 1L PD-L1+ head & neck squamous cell carcinoma) culminated in the **LiGeR-HN1** readout on Oct 28, where petosemtamab + pembrolizumab cut the risk of death by **50% vs. pembrolizumab alone** (OS HR ≈ 0.50, both primary endpoints met, no new safety signals). The stock rose **+28.3% on 29.3M shares (~20x average volume)** — its largest single-day move on record and an all-time high.

The $8B all-cash acquisition of Merus (closed Dec 2025) is what brought petosemtamab in-house. That deal looked expensive at the time; ten months later the LiGeR-HN1 data has plausibly justified it on a single indication.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

*(Not Strong Buy — the entry point, not the thesis, is the constraint.)*

**Why the thesis is a Buy:** Three independent Phase 3 wins in ten days is a rare, fundamental re-rating event. Management has now de-risked the single largest criticism of the stock — that Genmab was a royalty shell facing a Darzalex cliff with no proprietary growth engine. It now has three. Consensus 2030 revenue of $7.76B embeds effectively **zero** contribution from the $7–11B of unmodeled peak sales sitting in petosemtamab, Rina-S, and 1L epcoritamab. Forward multiples (5.9x EV/S on 2028E, 18x 2030E EPS) are cheap for a company with ~20% revenue CAGR and ~47 Rule of 40.

**Why not Strong Buy:** The stock **gapped +28% in one session and now trades at $57.29 against a pre-data median PT of ~$42.** Every model in the market is behind it. 2026E P/E is 48.6x on EPS of $1.18, and the near-term estimate line is *declining* (FY26 EPS $1.18 vs FY25 $1.54) — the Merus deal, interest expense, and integration costs compress near-term earnings even as the long-term value rises. A PEG of ~1.75 on 2026 numbers is full. Post-gap digestion is the base case for 1–2 quarters.

**Position sizing guidance:** Build a core position now; add aggressively on any pullback toward **$45–50** (the pre-gap level, which should become support as PTs are revised upward). The 2027–2030 setup justifies a full position; the next 90 days argue for patience on adds.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Darzalex royalty concentration (~50% of revenue).** Darzalex faces eventual LOE erosion in the early 2030s. The entire bull case depends on petosemtamab/Rina-S/epcoritamab replacing ~$2B of royalty income before it decays. If launch execution slips to 2029+, there is a genuine revenue air-pocket 2031–2033 that consensus does not model.

2. **Merus integration & balance sheet.** $8B all-cash closed Dec 2025 turned a net-cash company into **~$3.9B of net debt** (EV $39.6B vs market cap $35.9B). Interest expense already at $61M. Goodwill impairment risk if petosemtamab underdelivers on label or uptake.

3. **Regulatory & confirmatory-trial risk.** LiGeR-HN1 is a first-interim-analysis win — final OS analysis at ASCO 2027 must hold. FDA could require the full dataset before approval. HNSCC is a **small market ($2.5B today)**; a $3–4B peak sales estimate requires both dominant share *and* premium pricing in a small population.

4. **Competitive intensity in HNSCC.** Merck's pembrolizumab is the incumbent partner *and* potential competitor in combinations; other EGFR-directed and bispecific programs are close behind. Genmab's deal with Merck for the combo gives Merck visibility into the asset.

5. **Guidance credibility / near-term EPS compression.** Two misses in the last three quarters (−89%, −42%). FX (DKK functional, USD reporting) will keep reported numbers noisy — the same artifact that broke this screen will confuse other investors.

6. **Momentum reversal.** +28% in a day on 20x volume invites profit-taking; the stock is above every stale price target, and an index/quant-driven unwind is plausible regardless of fundamentals.

7. **Data gaps in this analysis:** no short interest, no institutional ownership data, no peer-relative multiples for pure-play ADC/bispecific comps (sector peer tool returned mega-cap pharma only — LLY, JNJ, ABBV — which are not valid comparables). Consensus estimates used are pre-Oct-28 and therefore **understate forward revenue**; treat the 2026–2030 revenue line as a floor, not a forecast.
