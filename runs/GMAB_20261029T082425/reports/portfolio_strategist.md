## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT**

Not a typical split — it is a clean **time-horizon split**, which is far more informative:

- **Long-horizon (2027–2031) case: overwhelmingly bullish.** Growth, Sector, Alt Data and the Short Analyst's own "bull confirmation" list all agree the pipeline de-risked fundamentally. No agent disputes that petosemtamab OS HR 0.50 in 1L HNSCC is practice-changing.
- **Current-price case: overwhelmingly cautious.** Value, Quant, Fin Risk, Macro and the price-checked Short Analyst *all independently conclude there is no margin of safety at $57.29*. Quant's Hold/do-not-add, Growth's "patient on entry," Alt Data's "9% above consensus," Value's "30% above bull DCF" are four separate confirmations of the same conclusion.

**Where the desk genuinely conflicts, and how I resolve it:**

| Conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Growth says BUY vs Quant says don't add | **Not contradictory** — Growth says buy *the thesis*, patient on *the entry*. The Quant is right about the price; the Growth analyst is right about the asset. |
| Value's $44 bull DCF vs Growth's $7–11B unmodeled peak sales | **Both can be true.** The DCF's terminal assumptions are where the disagreement lives. But when the *bull* DCF is 30% below spot, the burden of proof sits on the buyer. |
| Short says "no edge" vs Non-Fin says HIGH risk | **Non-Fin wins on precedence.** The Short analyst explicitly did not have the AbbVie litigation or Epkinly OS failure in his risk stack, and priced the name at $27.47. A trade-secret injunction against Rina-S is a fraud-adjacent risk, and per our framework that takes precedence over a valuation-driven bearish/benign call. |

**Net: Medium conviction on the business, Low conviction on the price.** That is a small position, not a large one.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **0.75% starter, 2.0% max**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Initiate now** | **0.40% of portfolio** (tactical half-starter) |
| **Add post-Q3 print (5-Nov)** | **+0.35%** if print is in-line or better and stock holds >$50 |
| **Target full weight** | **2.0%** |
| **Hard maximum** | **2.5%**, only on a pullback to $45–50 with thesis intact |

**Rationale (Kelly-framed):** The edge is real but *already largely monetized in the price*. If I assign rough probabilities — 50% the 2027–2030 launch cycle validates consensus-plus, 30% the stock chops/digests sideways, 20% litigation, Epinkly withdrawal, or macro delivers a 30–50% drawdown — the payoff is roughly symmetric-to-slightly-negative at $57.29. Symmetric odds with an unproven edge justify **below-full sizing, and there is no case for a high-conviction 3–5% position.**

**The binding constraint is volatility, not conviction.** At **48.2% annualised volatility**, a 2.0% position contributes **~0.96% of portfolio volatility** — roughly the risk of a 4–5% position in a normal 20%-vol large cap. Sizing on beta (0.844) would over-allocate by ~2x, because **R² vs SPY is only 0.052** — 95% of this stock's variance is not market variance.

**Why not Pass outright:** correlation data makes the diversification case genuinely attractive (see §4), and there is a live, dated catalyst path (Q3 5-Nov, BLA filing by YE-2026, ASCO 2027 final OS) that can move the fundamental value up faster than the price. A sub-1% toehold buys the right to act on that at short notice.

**Currency:** Genuine free option most of the desk under-weighted — revenue is USD, opex largely DKK/EUR, and guidance assumes USD/DKK 6.2 versus implied spot ~6.66. That is a **~7% translation tailwind** to reported USD results. It partially offsets the rate headwind and is a reason the Q3/Q4 prints may look better than underlying constant-currency trends.

**Rate sensitivity:** This is a long-duration royalty + 2027-launch NPV asset with the 10Y at 4.99% and the 30Y at 5.37%. Macro's bear case (10Y >5.75%) produces a **25–35% drawdown with the clinical data fully intact** — a pure multiple event that no amount of pipeline execution protects against.
