# Macro Assessment: Alphabet Inc. (GOOG)

**As of 29 October 2026 | Price $321.89 | Mkt Cap $3.94T | Beta 1.20**

---

## 1. Macro Environment Summary — **TRANSITIONAL (hawkish reflation)**

The regime has flipped from the 2024–25 easing cycle into a **reflationary tightening bias**. The Fed *raised* rates 25bp on 16 September 2026 to **3.75%–4.00%** — the first hike since 2023 — under Chair Kevin Warsh, and held at the 28 October meeting on a 10–2 split. Headline CPI has re-accelerated from **2.39% YoY (Jan 2026) to 3.72% YoY (Sep 2026)**, while growth holds at ~2.2% and unemployment sits at 4.2%. The result is a **bear-steepening curve**: the 10Y has risen from 4.44% (30 Jun) to 4.99%, having touched **5.31% on 5 October**, and the 30Y is at 5.37%.

Critically, this is **not** a recession-pricing environment: HY OAS at **3.18%** (27 Oct) is only 53bp off its 2.65% September tights and far below its long-run median. This is a **discount-rate shock in an otherwise healthy economy** — the worst combination for long-duration growth equities, and the best for nominal revenue.

**Net:** the *operating* macro backdrop (nominal growth, full employment, easy credit, double-digit ad market) is genuinely favourable. The *financial* backdrop (5% long bond, Fed hiking, stronger dollar) is hostile to the multiple. Alphabet's price action — **+31% over 12 months but −19% from the 13 May peak of $398.54** — is precisely this tug-of-war.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL**

Fundamentals are macro-tailwind-supported, and the balance sheet is immune to the rate cycle (net cash, no refinancing wall). But the two dominant macro forces right now — a **5% 10-year yield** and a **2.9% dollar rally** — both hit Alphabet directly: one through the discount rate on a ~40x earnings multiple, the other through translation of half its revenue. These roughly cancel the nominal-GDP tailwind. **Downgrade to Unfavourable if the 10Y sustains above 5.50% or the dollar extends another 4–5%.**

## 5. The Macro Scenario That Would Materially Surprise

**Bear (under-priced): "The 2027 Hawk."** Consensus (and the FOMC's own SEP) assumes inflation rolls over and the Fed peaks at 4.00–4.25%. The surprise would be CPI holding above 3.5% into 1H27 — driven by tariffs, midterm-year fiscal expansion after the November 2026 elections, and term-premium concerns about deficits absent a credible fiscal anchor. In that world the Fed goes to **4.75–5.00%**, the 10Y clears **5.75%**, and the 30Y tests 6%. Consequences for GOOG: multiple compression of a further **15–20%** *despite* 20%+ revenue growth; the AI capex program ($80B+ in H1 alone) gets re-underwritten at a far higher cost of capital; and the SpaceX-style mark-to-market losses on the $11B+ portfolio become a recurring earnings feature. Note that HY spreads at 3.18% are offering essentially **zero compensation** for this outcome — credit is the most mispriced asset in the complex.

**Bull counterpoint (less likely to surprise, but high torque):** if AI-driven productivity shows up in 2027 unit labour costs, inflation breaks below 2.5% quickly, the 10Y returns to ~4.2%, and Alphabet — with 25% revenue growth and a structurally larger ad market — re-rates hard to new highs. The asymmetry favours owning the name *after* duration stabilises, not before.

---

**Bottom line for the equity thesis:** Alphabet's earnings are a play on **nominal GDP and the AI capex cycle**; its *multiple* is a play on the **long bond**. Right now the macro winds are favourable to the former and hostile to the latter, and the 29 October price action confirms the market is trading the discount rate, not the revenue line. Watch the 10Y and the broad dollar as the two variables that matter most — not the Fed Funds rate, which is nearly irrelevant to a net-cash company.
