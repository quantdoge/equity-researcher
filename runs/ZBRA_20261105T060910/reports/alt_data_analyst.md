## Business Direction

**Channel/competitive check:** no evidence of inventory stuffing or channel distress in the data. No negative news flow on pricing pushback beyond normal customer commentary on price increases. Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

## 5. Earnings Preview Signal: **Tracking IN-LINE to modestly ABOVE consensus (high variance)**

**The quantitative setup is unusually favourable:**
- Beat rate **85.7%** (6 of 7 quarters); average surprise **+11.8%**
- But surprise magnitude is **decaying fast**: +45.6% (Q2'26) → +12.8% (Q1'26) → −0.2% (Q4'25) → **+1.2%** (Q3'26). The Q3 beat was the narrowest in the series, and management attributed it to *extra memory supply availability* — a one-off fulfillment enabler that is not repeatable if that supply is already committed, and which may sit on the balance sheet as inventory ahead of the 15% price increase (October) and the January 4, 2026 price increase. Watch for a **pull-forward dynamic** that helps Q4 revenue but steals from Q1'27.
- **The bar has been deliberately reset.** Q4 adjusted EBITDA margin guided to ~19–20%, with the explicit framing that Q4 is the "baseline" for 2027 and full-year lands ~23.5%; the higher base absorbs a restructuring/M&A charge (~$35–40M) and the tariff refund benefit rolling off. Zebra's history inside its own guide is near-unblemished, so this reset has the shape of **self-imposed conservatism ahead of a low hurdle** — which is a *positive* for the Feb 11 print even as it is negative for the reported 2027 margin line.
- Consensus revenue of ~$1.64B for the Feb print sits **above** the implied trajectory from Q4 guidance (~$1.50–1.52B on ~13–15% growth off a ~$1.32B year-ago base). Either consensus embeds ~$120–140M of Elo/FX contribution not in my model, or there is **a revenue estimate that needs trimming**. I flag this as an unresolved tension — treat the $1.64B consensus figure with skepticism.

**Alt-data verdict vs. estimates:** demand-side alt data (search decay, traffic −5%) argues the *organic growth rate is decelerating into year-end*, while the company's order book and the 2027 logistics refresh cycle argue the opposite. **Net: in-line on revenue, tilted above on EPS due to a soft guide.**

## SEO/Concentration Risk In The Setup

Zebra's full-year memory headwind was raised from $120M (August) to **~$145M**. This is the single largest swing factor and it is **not represented in any of the demand-side alt data above** — a genuine blind spot in the signal set.

## Bottom Line

**Overall Alt Data Signal: NEUTRAL** (Bullish demand / Bearish margin — they cancel at the EPS line)

**Confidence: MEDIUM.** Demand-side reads (company order commentary, 85.7% beat rate, reset bar, customer win PR) are high-quality and consistent. The margin trajectory is a cost pass-through problem with a known and worsening exogenous input. But the *quantitative* alt-data panel is thin: patent data failed, hiring has no trend, web traffic is single-source, and social is low-volume. I would not size a position on the demand alt data alone.

**What to do with it:**
1. **The demand signal is confirming, not leading.** The 2027 logistics refresh cycle is a consensus-visible, management-supplied narrative already partly in the 17× FY'27 multiple. No differentiated edge here.
2. **The only differentiated alt signal is the margin/pricing pass-through.** Track DRAM contract pricing (TrendForce monthly) and any shift in Zebra's price-increase cadence. If DRAM decelerates in H1'27 while the 15% price increase sticks, gross margin expands *mechanically* against a sandbagged EBITDA baseline — that is the bull case, and it is a 2027 story, not a Feb 11 story.
3. **The Feb 11 print is a low-bar event.** EPS beat probability is high (soft guide, deliberate conservatism, 86% historical rate); revenue is the risk (organic decelerating vs. a firm consensus number). Asymmetric setup favours owning the print, not the year.

**Signal changes that would move me:** (a) a second consecutive quarter of sub-2% EPS surprise → the beat machine is broken, downgrade; (b) DRAM contract price QoQ turning negative → upgrade to Bullish; (c) "barcode scanner"/"RFID" search recovering above yr-ago while traffic MoM turns positive → genuine organic re-acceleration, upgrade; (d) any evidence of order cancellations post-price-hike → downgrade to Bearish.

**Data limitations:** Patents unavailable (2 failed attempts). Hiring trend unavailable (level only, no history). Web traffic single-source, unverified. Google Trends are category keywords, not company-specific, and contaminated by consumer/retail traffic in a B2B procurement business. News sentiment is keyword-based and demonstrably blind to guidance content. Social volume too low for statistical significance. All alt data lags reported financials by one quarter minimum.
