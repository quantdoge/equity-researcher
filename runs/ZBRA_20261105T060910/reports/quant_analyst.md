## 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

1. **Momentum reversal risk — HIGH.** +35.7% 12-1 with a 36.5% trailing max drawdown and 51% annualised vol. The 2026 EPS estimate (+34%) is a cyclical recovery year; the estimate path decelerates to +5.7% in 2027. Momentum names that stall on a decelerating revision slope de-rate fast.
2. **Factor crowding / consensus long.** 10 consecutive upward price-target revisions with no dissents is itself a crowding signal. There is no bearish estimate in the tape to absorb bad news.
3. **High idiosyncratic risk.** R² 0.213 vs SPY — beta 1.74 understates true risk. Position sizing should use the 51% vol figure, not the beta.
4. **Gross-margin and ROA deterioration.** F-score F8 = 0 and F3 = 0; gross margin −250bps y/y; net margin 7.8% vs 10.6%. Revenue grew +8.3% but profit fell — negative operating leverage.
5. **Reporting-lag risk.** Multiples are computed on FY2025 financials against a late-2026 price. The GAAP P/E of 40.6x vs adjusted ~21x is a large enough gap that screens using GAAP will wrongly classify ZBRA as expensive.
6. **Liquidity — NOT a concern.** $15.7B cap, 51.2M diluted shares, adequate float.
7. **Small-sample caveats:** the earnings-revision score rests on an n=2 FY2029 estimate; the peer group (n=4–5) is too small for statistically meaningful percentile ranks — treat all "sector rank" figures as ordinal context, not z-scores.
