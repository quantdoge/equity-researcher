# Geo/Legal Risk Report — Life Time Group Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: LTH)

## Company baseline
- **Identity:** Life Time Group Holdings, Inc. — premium health/fitness club operator ("athletic country clubs"), plus Life Time Digital and spa/media operations.
- **Source:** FMP `company` / `profile-symbol` (ticker LTH); investor site (ir.lifetime.life) and WSJ (Nov 2024: ~177 clubs in US & Canada as of Sept 30, 2024).
- **Footprint:** ~177–190+ clubs, essentially **100% US + Canada** (Canada presence concentrated in Ontario/Greater Toronto). ~52,000 employees, ~850k members (Q2 2025). Consumer Cyclical sector, Chanhassen, MN HQ.
- **Secular context:** US/Canada-only premium consumer leisure. This is a **reset-risk-light profile** — no emerging-market, sanctions, expropriation, or currency-repatriation exposure exists.

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Sovereign-Class Risks

1. **US state & federal consumer-regulatory divergence** — Probability **Medium**, Impact **Low–Medium**.
   Fitness membership auto-renewal, cancellation-fee, and "negative option" practices are being actively policed by state AGs and the FTC. Precedent: **NY AG secured ~$3.97M from Xponential Fitness (June 2026)** for misleading franchise/consumer practices; the FTC’s 2021–2022 auto-renewal Rule and commercial-surveillance rulemakings raise the bar for member-facing subscription products.

2. **Health/wellness data privacy as a regulatory wedge** — Probability **Medium**, Impact **Low–Medium**.
   Life Time Digital collects health, fitness, biometric, and nutrition data and partners with **Apple Fitness+**. The FTC has explicitly signalled health-data and commercial-surveillance enforcement; CCPA (they operate in California) and PIPEDA (Canada) apply. A data breach or unfair-data-practice enforcement is the highest-probability material regulatory event.

3. **Social/cultural-policy exposure (state-level, sub-sovereign)** — Probability **Low**, Impact **Low**.
   August 2024: the **Missouri Attorney General’s office opened an investigation** and issued subpoenas involving Life Time over a locker-room (transgender member) incident. This is reputational/political rather than financial material, but signals the brand can be pulled into state-level culture wars.

No meaningful exposure to Russia-linked assets, China-trade frictions, or emerging-market currency ↔/repatriation controls.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Active/Recent matters:**
- **Wage-and-hour (FLSA) class actions** — recurring theme. **Sept 2025 Bloomberg Law:** federal judge gave initial approval to a **$1.25M class-wide settlement** of a Life Time wage-and-hour dispute. (Older, same strand: 2016 DOL finding of ~**$976k back wages + damages to ~15,909 employees** in 26 states; group-instructor FLSA suits from 2016.)
- **TCPA (telemarketing) class actions** — historical settlement **$10–15M** (2015–2017, FaegreDrinker/FindLaw) over unsolicited automated texts. Closed, but marks the company’s consumer-communication control breach.
- **EEOC pregnancy-discrimination** — settled 2017 for **$86,000** + relief (EEOC.gov).
- **State AG inquiry** — Missouri AG subpoena (Aug 2024), social/political angle above.
- **Routine exposure** — 10-Q standard disclaimer: "Litigation or other proceedings incidental to the normal course… including investigation fees." FY2024 published results note **~$1.3M of "unusual legal settlements or judgments"** (PR Newswire, Jan 2026) — i.e., modest, absorbable.

Overall litigation posture: a steady drumbeat of **consumer and wage class actions**, all settled or contested in low-to-mid single-digit $MM bands. No DOJ/SEC criminal-imputation, no securities fraud, no anti-competition blockbuster.

## 6. Overall Geo/Legal Risk Rating: **LOW**

**Rationale:** LTH's legal profile is a workaday US/Canada consumer-services construct. There is effectively **no geopolitical/country risk** (no EM operation, no sanctions, no repatriation, no expropriation). The genuine risks — health-data privacy, consumer-membership regulation, and wage/labor class actions — are **Low in severity**, mostly recurring at fractional $MM to low-$MM settlements, and addressable through compliance investment. The Missouri political controversy adds noise, not material legal downside.

**Watch-list:** (1) FTC/state rules or an action touching Life Time Digital’s health data; (2) any fresh member-facing auto-renewal enforcement; (3) escalation of a wage claim beyond the $1.25M settled scale. Any of these would lift the rating to Medium-High, but none is on the current balance sheet as material.
