### Investment Memo: LTH — 2026-08-23

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $54 (≈ +20% upside from $45.11)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Life Time is the clear leader in premium "athletic country club" wellness — a genuine pricing-power compounder (FY25 revenue +14.3%, net income +139%, EBITDA margin 26.4%) that is beating and raising consistently. But the stock is up ~70% YTD and now trades at ~27x trailing earnings and ~21x FY25 GAAP EV/EBITDA with negative free cash flow, lease-heavy leverage (Altman Z 1.81), and ~$1.45B of insider/sponsor selling into strength — a quality business at a rich price with no margin of safety. We'd hold existing positions and buy only on a pullback toward the $38–41 area; chasing $45 is a momentum trade, not an investment.

**Bull Case** (3 bullets)
- **Pricing-power compounder**: +14% revenue with 26.4% EBITDA margin and comp-center +9.1% — growth is coming from affluent mix/price, not volume; expanding margins and falling (company-defined) net leverage.
- **Re-rating + catalysts**: 12–14 record club openings (6–7 in Q4 2026), interest-expense tailwind from repricing, and upward estimate revisions (consensus ~$56) — FMP levered DCF implies $57.91 (+28%).
- **Clean earnings quality**: 2.33x OCF/NI conversion, negative accruals (cash-backed income), Piotroski 7/9 — the "expensive" multiple is on real, rising earnings, not accounting.

**Bear Case** (3 bullets)
- **Rich valuation, no FCF**: 27x trailing P/E, ~21x FY25 EV/EBITDA, negative FCF (−$21M FY25); earnings yield 3.7% vs 10-yr ~4.5–4.7% — the multiple has done as much work as fundamentals (+70% YTD).
- **Leverage + macro**: lease-inclusive net debt/EBITDA 8.25x, Altman Z 1.81, beta 1.50; a rate hike / consumer softening compresses the multiple *and* strains a capital-hungry model (capex ~30% of revenue).
- **Insiders selling + litigation**: ~$1.45B sponsor/insider sales into strength and an active child-safety lawsuit + discrimination complaint are asymmetric governance/reputational overhangs.

**Key Risks**
1. **Rate/refinancing shock** — sticky core PCE (3.3%) re-opening hike risk; LTH is the most rate-sensitive name in its peer set.
2. **Valuation compression** on any growth/guidance stumble (the entire bull case is momentum + revisions; value/quality-of-capital are subpar).
3. **Child-safety / brand-trust litigation** escalation (Kid's Academy, CAIR) — reputational and liability tail risk.
4. **FCF remains negative** past the 2027 build-out if new-club yields disappoint.
5. **Data-quality caveat** — FY25 FMP statement reclassification (LT debt / interest expense) means leverage figures should be cross-checked against the 10-Q before sizing.
