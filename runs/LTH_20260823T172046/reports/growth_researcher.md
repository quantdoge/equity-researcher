# Growth Analysis: Life Time Group Holdings (NYSE: LTH)

**Company:** Life Time Group Holdings, Inc. — luxury "athletic country club" operator (fitness/wellness/lifestyle), formerly LTF Holdings, SPAC IPO Oct 2021. ~185–190+ clubs across the U.S. and Canada, with >45,000 employees.

---

## 1. Growth Story

Life Time is the premium end of a **K-shaped economy** trade-up thesis: affluent consumers are spending more on holistic health, including high-margin dues, medical (GLP-1), and in-club entertainment (pickleball, co-working, café, spa). Management is deliberately scaling up — **15% larger club format, record club openings — while expanding pricing power (average monthly dues up 12.3% to ~$245/mo) and mix rather than chasing raw membership count**. This is organic, same-brand growth (comparable-center revenue up 6–7% even after 1.3% member-count growth), not serial acquisitions, which is the highest-quality growth profile in fitness/consumer wellness.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- **US health & fitness club market ≈ $45–47B (2025)**, forecast to ~$71.5B by 2035 (~5.2% CAGR); global club market ≈ $131B growing ~8% (IBISWorld / Custom Market Insights / Mordor).
- Life Time's $3.0B revenue ≈ **~6.5% share of U.S. club spend** — already a large premium player, but the samer SAM extends to broader wellness, healthy-aging, medical (GLP-1), digital (Life Time app + L·AI·C AI companion), supplements, and ~25+ live athletic events.
- Its true opportunity is **dues/ARPM upside in a K-shaped economy**, not club-count alone. With 822k center memberships (+1.3%) but **revenue per member +11.7% to ~$3,531**, Life Time is capturing wallet share from fewer households at higher price points — a compounding mix shift.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Record 2026 CTB class:** 12–14 new clubs (biggest-ever, ~1.2M sq ft, ~double prior years), with 6–7 opening in **Q4 2026** — largest near-term kicker. *(co: wear company guidance / press)*
- **28 new clubs by end-2027** with larger, financially stronger formats (Business Journals).
- **Interest expense tailwind:** 2025 term-loan repricing + interest-rate swap cuts interest expense; FY2026 net interest ~$36–60M declining → direct EPS lever.
- **GLP-1 / medical-wellness + AI digital monetization:** L·AI·C AI health companion, larger in-center spend (in-center = 28.7% of revenue and rising).
- **Analyst target upgrades:** Morgan Stanley raised to **$53** (from $39), RBC to **$60** (from $50); recent estimate revisions are upward (Simply Wall St, CNN).

## 5. Alternative Data Signals (Non-Financial)

- **Footprint expansion is real and accelerating** (14 openings scheduled, largest wall~ in history; multi-city California/San Diego push per CoStar).
- **Macro same-store momentum:** comparable-center revenue +6.3–7.3% guide for 2026 on top of +11% comp growth in 2025 — demand holding even as consumer sentiment softens elsewhere.
- **Premium recovery:** record U.S. gym membership **~81M (2025)**, up from 77M, with the expensive tier growing (ABC Fitness, IMMCG, NIHCA).
- **Earnings beats:** Q2 2026 EPS of $0.45–0.48 beat ~$0.37 consensus — indicates conservative guidance and positive estimate-revision trend.

---

## 6. Growth Verdict: **Buy**

Durable double-digit organic growth (~11–14% forward), superb pricing power, growing EBITDA margins, and real operating leverage. This is not cheap (~27x forward adjusted P/E, ~15x EV/EBITDA) and the stock trades ~near its 52-week high, but for a high-quality, capital compounder with accelerating unit expansion and a margin/interest tailwind, the premium is defensible. I'd trim to "Buy" rather than "Strong Buy" because free cash flow is negative during the build-out and the multiple already prices in much of the upside. Accumulate on near-term pullback.

---

## 7. Key Risks

- **Execution / real-estate risk:** 14 concurrent ground-up constructions is the largest in company history — any slippage in openings, rents, or "competition among your own (nearby clubs" hurts the cadence.
- **Capital & leverage:** heavy capex (~$8-3~$92M 2026) keeps FCF negative; a consumer recession or credit stress on the development pipeline could compound pressure.
- **Membership-dependence:** member-count growth is only +1.3% — growth leans heavily on price (dues +10–30) and spend intensity, both rate-sensitive if the K-shaped economy softens.
- **Competitive entry:** premium fitness is attracting capital (Equinox; Planet Fitness at the cheap end competing for areas/real estate and pressuring club-level economics is an intensifying competitive backdrop).
- **Valuation:** already ~$45 vs mid-$54 analyst target — limited margin of safety; forward P/E ~27x prices in strong execution.
