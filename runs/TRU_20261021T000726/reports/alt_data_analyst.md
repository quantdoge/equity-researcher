## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH** (with a consumer-funnel caveat)

The thesis rests on one dominant, genuinely under-appreciated signal: the mortgage credit-score regime change. TRU is pricing VantageScore 4.0 at $0.99 through Dec 2028 and *free* when pulled alongside FICO, against a FICO that has raised prices ~100% and is now in visible distress (workforce cut, stock halved). Lenders switching mortgage score providers is a multi-year, high-margin revenue and share shift that consensus Q3 numbers cannot yet reflect — it is a 2027+ earnings driver but a live 2026 sentiment driver (hence the +28% move). Supporting: +5.9% MoM site traffic, dense product cadence, unbroken beat streak.

Counterweights: the consumer-direct funnel is in outright decline on search data; the revenue beat cushion is shrinking quarter by quarter; and the stock has already re-rated 28% in three weeks into the print, so expectations — not fundamentals — are now the risk.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Strengths: clean 7-quarter surprise series, 50 weeks of Google Trends, 30 days of article-level sentiment, full price history.
Limitations: (1) **Patent data unavailable — two failed pulls**; (2) **social sentiment unavailable** — no retail signal at all; (3) web traffic is a qualitative SimilarWeb page-scrape, not a monthly visit series, and only one MoM delta is legible; (4) job posting counts are Glassdoor/careers-page proxies with no time series, so "trend" cannot be established — only a level; (5) Google Trends at weekly US granularity is noisy and the March/June "TransUnion" and "free credit report" spikes look event-driven (likely breach/promo coverage), which distorts peak-to-current comparisons. Alt data here is a leading indicator on the *consumer* segment only; the mortgage-driven B2B upside is inferred from news flow, not measured.

**Bottom line:** Alt data supports an in-line quarter with a likely modest EPS beat, wrapped in a materially improved structural narrative. The trade is not the Oct 27 print — it is VantageScore mortgage adoption compounding through 2027–28. Weakest measured link: consumer-direct search demand (–14.5% MoM, below year-ago).
