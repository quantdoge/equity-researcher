**Where the 11 agents agree (high confidence):**

- **The regulatory/policy event is the dominant swing factor and it is binary.** Sector, Geo Legal, Non-Fin Risk, ESG and Short all independently identify the same thing: FHFA converting tri-merge into a two-bureau or exception-based regime. Nobody can underwrite the outcome.
- **Cheap vs peers, not cheap vs cash flows.** Every valuation-capable agent reaches this: 13.5x EV/EBITDA is the cheapest in the group (EFX 14.4, SPGI 19.0, VRSK 19.8, MCO 23.0, FICO 23.7) — but base-case DCF value is ~$66.
