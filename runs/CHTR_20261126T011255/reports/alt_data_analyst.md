### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — **Slightly Negative, but Improving Sequentially**

**Google Trends, US, 12-month (weekly):**

| Keyword (signal) | Jul–Aug peak | Oct avg | Latest 3 wks | vs Oct | vs Nov'25 LY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "cancel Spectrum internet" (**churn intent**) | ~83–100 | 64.3 | **56.7** | **−12%** | +23% |
| "Spectrum internet deals" (**gross-add intent**) | ~60 | 35.5 | **44.0** | **+24%** | +9% |
| "Charter Spectrum internet" (**brand interest**) | ~85 (100 on Jul 19) | 52.5 | **50.7** | −3% | −4% |

**Interpretation — this is the most useful signal in the dataset.** Churn-intent search volume peaked at 100 in late July and has decayed steadily (91→83→75→61 by Sept, now 47–57). Historically a 3–6 week leading proxy for disconnect activity, this points to **Q4 disconnect pressure easing materially vs the summer**. Critically, gross-add intent ("deals") bottomed at a 12-month low of 24 in late Oct and has recovered to 44 — **above year-ago levels**.

**But two caveats:** (a) churn intent is still **~23% above Nov-2025**, so this is *mean reversion from a bad summer*, not a healthy funnel; (b) brand interest is flat-to-down YoY, suggesting no demand inflection from the new operating model/promo push yet.

**Web traffic:** ❌ **Data gap.** SimilarWeb proxy returned only competitor-list fragments (spectrum.com vs xfinity.com, Oct 2026) with **no monthly visit series**. Cannot quantify traffic MoM. Material gap for an ISP.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal (Q4'26, ~Jan 29, 2027): **Tracking Modestly ABOVE Prior-Quarter Trend**

- **Surprise history: 28.6% beat rate (2 of 7), avg surprise −48.4%** — but that average is destroyed by the impairment quarter. **Ex-charge, CHTR is a chronic modest misser: avg ≈ −1.6%** (range +6.8% to −9.6%). Do not read the headline −48% as operational decay.
- **Alt data vs last quarter:** churn intent −12% MoM and gross-add intent +24% off the trough both argue the **−233k sub loss worsens no further, and likely improves** in Q4. That is the single metric management and the short book are watching.
- **Bull case validation:** the 10.9% Nov 10 rally (+$13.17) was driven by the **$2B annualized cost-savings operating model**, not by the print. Those savings are *promised, not evidenced* — my alt data is consistent with the promise but does not confirm delivery.
- **Risk to the preview:** 45% short interest means the beat/miss reaction is asymmetric and positioning-driven, not fundamentals-driven. GAAP optics stay ugly.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (tactical positive tilt)**
Consumer demand is stabilizing off a trough rather than recovering; the balance sheet ($110B debt vs $20B equity) dominates any fundamental upside; positioning and cost-cut optimism are driving price, not demand data. The honest read: **churn is getting less bad, gross adds are no longer deteriorating, and the short base is enormous — but brand interest is flat YoY and no operational evidence of the $2B savings exists yet.**

**Relative check:** CMCSA +15.1% (Oct 20→Nov 25) vs CHTR +33.8% — CHTR is running **~2.2x the cable complex**, i.e. much of the recent move is **idiosyncratic squeeze/valuation re-rating, not sector demand**. Note the Nov 24–25 leg came on only mildly elevated volume (3.7M vs ~2.3M avg), whereas the catalyst days (Aug 19 Cox close: 11.0M; Nov 6 print: 6.5M) carried the real conviction. **Weaker volume confirmation on the latest leg is a caution flag.**

### 7. Confidence Level: **LOW–MEDIUM**
- **Medium:** news sentiment, positioning/short interest, capital-markets activity, earnings surprise history (all hard data).
- **Low:** consumer demand — Google Trends is a 3–6 week lagged proxy, the series is noisy (weekly swings of 20+ points), and brand/deals keywords are imprecise for a subscription business where churn happens off-portal.
- **Missing entirely:** quantitative web traffic (SimilarWeb), patent trend (tool failure), real-time social volume/tone. Two of six framework pillars are dark.

**Bottom line for the memo:** Alt data supports "**deterioration has stopped, recovery is not proven**." The investable signal is the *asymmetry* — improving churn intent + 45% short interest + 3.3x forward P/E against $110B of debt. The next print's internet net adds are the binary. Key data limitation: I could not verify subscriber funnel health with web traffic or real-time social data, and the Google Trends series is a leading proxy only — **not a definitive measure** of net adds.
