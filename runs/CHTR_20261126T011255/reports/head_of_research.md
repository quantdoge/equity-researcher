**Investment Thesis**

Charter is a levered bet on stopping a structural decline, not a compounding franchise. The post-Cox entity carries ~$109B of debt (4.1x net leverage, 2.65x EBIT coverage) against a ~$20B equity stub — the thinnest 15% of the capital structure — while pro forma broadband losses accelerate (−233K internet subs in Q3'26), revenue and EBITDA shrink, and a hiking Fed pushes the long end against a 15–20-year-duration equity. The bull case is real but narrow and conditional: a credible $3B cost program, capex falling toward ≤$11B, and mobile attach growth could drive an FCF inflection — but zero of eleven specialist agents recommend buying at $147, and the two most constructive (Value, Growth) both say the asymmetry only works **below $110–115**. We sell/avoid here and define the re-entry zone rather than chase a 44%-off-the-low squeeze.

**Bull Case**
- **Self-help arithmetic**: $3B cost program + capex ≤$11B could lift FCF toward $7–9B against a $20B equity stub — deleveraging that could retire the market cap in ~3 years; probability-weighted IV $220–240 (+50–60%).
- **Mobile attach + rural builds**: the one genuine growth vector, with convergence economics and a 60% attach ceiling vs 42% today.
- **Positioning asymmetry**: 45% short interest, insider buying near current prices, improving churn-intent data, and a PE-takeout/Comcast call option mean a merely "less bad" Q4 print could squeeze violently.

**Bear Case**
- **Accelerating structural decline**: −233K pro forma internet subs (vs −171K a year ago), revenue −1.5%, EBITDA −4% — negative operating leverage in an unattractive sector.
- **Leverage × rates**: $109B debt at 4.1x with 2.65x coverage into a hiking Fed and 5%+ 10Y; a −15–20% EBITDA path at the current multiple values equity near zero. Quant confirms the value-trap factor structure.
- **Unquantified tails**: ACP/FCA exposure, Dec 16 MTD ruling, further impairment of $103.8B intangibles, and governance that prevents minority holders from forcing discipline.
