### Investment Memo: BA — 28 October 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: High (with a flagged, legitimate minority dissent)
**Price Target**: $130 (23% downside from current $168.18)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Boeing is a fortress franchise wrapped around a broken income statement. The $745bn record backlog (~12 years of sold-out duopoly demand) is real and is the only thing keeping this from a distress story — but it converts to cash through a production ramp that has now slipped twice in 2026, into a company earning ~0% operating margin, where interest expense ($2.77bn) exceeds operating profit, tangible equity is −$13.4bn, and free cash flow has been negative in 5 of 7 years. At $168 the market is pre-paying for a 2028–29 recovery that must clear a gauntlet of binary risks — FAA discretion, a DOJ NPA cliff in May 2027, an unsigned China order, and a hiking Fed compressing the value of the longest-duration backlog in the sector. This is a rates-and-execution problem, not a demand problem — and neither is in Boeing's control.

Sector rated ATTRACTIVE — but returns are accruing to GE Aerospace (19.1% op margin) and GD, not the capacity-constrained OEM. Sector attractiveness ≠ BA attractiveness.

**Bull Case**
- **Contracted demand at record levels**: $745bn backlog (7.7x revenue), MAX 7/10 certified, 777X first delivery 2027 — every increment of rate is highly incremental margin on a fixed cost base; FCF inflection to ~$10bn by 2029 is mechanically credible *if* rates hold.
- **Working-capital and deleveraging optionality**: delivering down $84.7bn of inventory could release cash faster than earnings suggest; $8.3bn of debt already retired in 1H26 with Fitch at Positive outlook.
- **Cheap macro convexity**: a US–Iran deal taking WTI $108→$70 pulls the December hike, drops the 10Y toward 4.50%, and re-rates BA on both earnings and multiple; a signed China 200-jet order is a further $50–60bn list-value catalyst. Consensus target $265 sits 58% above spot.

**Bear Case**
- **The arithmetic is broken today**: negative TTM operating income and FCF, interest 2.7x operating profit, $5.5bn equity vs $54bn debt — one FY24-style quarter erases the equity cushion and forces a fourth dilution (shares already +37% since 2020).
- **Execution credibility is impaired**: the 737 ramp has slipped twice in two quarters, the market now discounts announced rates rather than believing them, and estimate cuts for Q4'26/H1'27 are mechanically baked in.
- **Stacked binary tails**: FAA rate cap/quality escape, DOJ NPA breach (May 2027), China order collapse — each independently capable of a 30–40% de-rating; Macro's bear path (Iran escalation, WTI >$130, spreads 1.45%→2%+) puts the $140s or lower in play.

**Key Risks**
1. 737 rate below 40/month or a third push-out of 47/month (highest-velocity fundamental trigger)
2. Any new FAA cap, certificate action, or quality escape on 737/787/777X
3. DOJ NPA breach before ~May 2027 — criminal re-open on a recidivist posture
