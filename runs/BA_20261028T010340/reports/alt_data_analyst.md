## Alternative Data Signal Report — The Boeing Company (BA)

**Snapshot:** $168.18 (27 Oct 2026), −11% on the day, −33% from the 23 Jan 2026 high ($252.15). Volume 46.8M vs. ~6M trailing average — a 7–8× capitulation spike. Market cap ~$133B. Sector: Industrials / Aerospace & Defense.

---

### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — Neutral / Not Applicable
Boeing is a B2B/durable-order business; consumer-style demand proxies carry limited weight, but attention data is still informative.

- **Google Trends ("Boeing", US, 12m):** 12-month average ≈52. Latest four weekly readings: 50 → 49 → 39 → **43** (18 Oct, pre-earnings). Trend is **declining** and now ~15–17% below the 12-month mean. The two extreme prints (98 on 5 Jul, 100 on 19 Jul) are event-driven search spikes, not sustained interest, and should be excluded from trend fit.
- **Interpretation:** Fading retail/consumer attention into the print — no demand "buzz" cushion ahead of a negative catalyst. This is a rear-view window (data ends 18 Oct), so it does not capture post-earnings search behavior.
- **Web traffic / app data:** Not meaningful for this business model; omitted rather than fabricated.

### 2. Business Activity Signals — Mixed, tilted Negative on Execution
- **Deliveries (hard alt-data proxy):** Q3 2026 = **161 jets**, with a record 737 MAX quarter. MAX output has moved **38 → 42/month**, targeting 47/month.
- **The critical negative:** Management now expects the **47/month rate in early 2027** (previously guided for around now / as recently as May), and the step to **52/month is pushed to H2 2027**. Two independent sources (Reuters, Wichita Business Journal) confirm the timeline slippage. This is the single most important alt-data finding: **demand is not the problem — the supply ramp is.**
- **Hiring trend — flat/tepid:** ~803 US postings on Indeed, skewed to fabrication/material handling, procurement, and systems/software engineering in ones-and-twos. No evidence of an aggressive engineering or production ramp in headcount. A hiring build-out is what you would expect *ahead* of a rate increase; its absence corroborates the delayed ramp.
- **Patents — data gap.** The USPTO PatentsView feed failed twice (malformed response). No patent trend available. Noted as a limitation, not a null signal.
- **Geospatial/supplier activity:** Not available in this environment.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **TRACKING BELOW consensus**
Next catalyst: **26 Jan 2027 (Q4/FY26)**.

- Two consecutive **misses**: Q3-26 EPS −$0.34 vs −$0.26 est (−33%); Q2-26 −$0.76 vs −$0.28 (−171%). The 57% 8-quarter beat rate is misleading — it is inflated by a one-off +2,362% print in Jan-26 and the sample is small.
- Forward setup is worse than the trailing numbers suggest: a slower MAX ramp means **Q4-26 and H1-27 delivery counts** will run below the schedule embedded in sell-side models. Guidance for 52/month slipping from "2027" to "H2 2027" mechanically removes deliveries that 2027 FCF estimates depend on.
- Offsetting positive: **record $745B backlog** and Y/Y revenue growth, with positive cash flow in the quarter. So this is an *estimate-cut* signal on timing, not a demand-destruction signal.

**Net: Below consensus on near-term (next 1–2 quarters) revenue, EPS and free cash flow.** Expect downward estimate revisions on the 2027 ramp assumption.

### 4. News Sentiment Score — Strongly Negative
- **Score: −0.50** (20 articles/14 days; 4 positive keyword hits vs. 12 negative). Aggregate tone: **negative**.
- Dominant narrative is uniform and one-sided: "plummets 11%," "shares plunge," "why did Boeing stock crash," "Q3 loss wider than expected," "lower production rates and missed delivery targets."
- The only genuinely positive items are the backlog ($745B) and positive cash flow — both framed as secondary to the production disappointment.
- **Assessment:** This is *fresh, high-quality* negative sentiment, not stale drift. It typically pressures the stock for days-to-weeks post-catalyst and raises the bar for the next print.

### 5. Social Sentiment — Mixed / Retail Contrarian (Low Confidence)
Reddit/WallStreetBets proxy shows classic dip-buying language: *"Boeing is too big to fail, so buy it while it's down,"* *"Boeing is undervalued,"* *"Boeing All In."* Threads also reference a prior crash headline and union/CEO commentary. Tone is **retail-contrarian bullish** — a crowd that has repeatedly bought the dip and been wrong (one visible post: 850 shares held 18 months for +$250). Low signal quality; treat as a sentiment *headwind indicator* for retail-heavy flow, not a thesis.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH (near-term)**
| Signal | Direction |
|---|---|
| Search interest | Declining, below 12m average |
| Deliveries/production ramp | Improving but **timeline slipping** |
| Hiring | Flat — no ramp build-out |
| Patents | No data |
| News sentiment | −0.50, sharply negative |
| Earnings surprise momentum | 2 consecutive misses |
| Backlog (demand) | Record $745B — structurally positive |
| Social | Retail dip-buying, low quality |

**The core divergence:** alt data shows *demand* at a record and *output* rising — but the rate of change is decelerating versus what was promised. Boeing's problem is now, and has been, **execution cadence**. The market had been pricing a faster 2027 ramp; alt data (delivery counts, 47/month slippage, absence of hiring build) says that ramp is late by two-plus quarters. That argues for a lower-for-longer EPS/FCF path and further estimate cuts.

Structurally the $745B backlog is a real, durable call on future revenue — this is not a broken-franchise setup. But that is a 2028+ story, and it does not protect the next two quarters.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
**Strengths:** Delivery and production-rate data are hard, specific, and independently corroborated by multiple sources; earnings surprise history is quantitative; price/volume reaction is unambiguous; news sentiment is current and dense (20 articles in 14 days).

**Limitations and lag:**
- **No patent data** (API failed twice) — the R&D-intensity leg of the framework is missing.
- **Job posting data is a qualitative proxy only** (Indeed/LinkedIn page scrapes, no time series) — cannot establish acceleration vs. prior quarters.
- **Google Trends ends 18 Oct**, nine days before the catalyst; it does not capture post-earnings search behavior.
- **No app-download, web-traffic, or geospatial data** applicable to this business.
- **Earnings surprise sample is only 7 quarters and distorted** by one extreme outlier; treat the 57% beat rate with skepticism.
- **No consensus revision data or forward estimate levels available** — the "below consensus" call is inferred from guidance slippage, not from a measured delta versus the current consensus book. That is the weakest link in this report and should be validated against a live estimate feed before sizing a position.

**Bottom line:** Alt data supports a *tactical bearish / estimate-revision-down* posture into the 26 Jan print, with the important caveat that the demand backdrop (record backlog, rising deliveries) caps how negative this should be read. Signal is about timing of the recovery being pushed right, not about the recovery failing.
