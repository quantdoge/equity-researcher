## 3. Composite Quant Signal: **SELL**

BA scores at or near the bottom on **four of five** factors. It is expensive (P/B 23.5, negative FCF), losing momentum (−10.4%), operationally low-quality (ROIC 7.2%, GP/Assets 2.6%), and exhibits high realised volatility (36%) with a deeply negative Sharpe (−1.05) and Sortino (−1.60). The single positive is the Piotroski F-Score of 7, flagging a genuine year-over-year *fundamental turn* (positive ROA, positive operating cash flow, lower leverage, margin/turnover improvement) — but this is an "improving-from-a-trough" score, not evidence of a healthy business today.

The bull case is entirely a **far-dated recovery optionality** story: consensus has BA back to ~$7.67 EPS by FY28 (≈22x today's price) and ~$14.56 by FY30 (≈11.5x). That is real, but on the *quantitative* evidence as of today, the stock fails the factors. **Signal: Sell** (aggressive/contrarian accounts may hold a small recovery option, but the factor composite does not support accumulation).

## 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

- **Momentum-reversal / value-trap risk:** −10.4% 12-1 momentum plus depressed earnings is the classic value-trap signature. F-Score 7 offers some support that it isn't a pure trap, but the trend remains down.
- **Quality distortion via leverage:** Reported ROE of 41% (and 302% in FY24) is an artifact of a thin/negative equity base (BVPS $7.16), not profitability. GP/Assets of 2.5% and ROIC of 7.2% are the honest quality reads — both weak.
- **Negative FCF / balance-sheet fragility:** P/FCF of −68.3 and negative-to-marginal free cash flow mean dividends/buybacks are unavailable; valuation leans on recovery that must be financed.
- **High beta (1.24) + 36% vol + 33% drawdown:** amplified downside in any market/industrial-cyclical selloff; poor Sortino confirms the pain is concentrated on down days.
- **Alpha estimate unreliable:** R² of 0.205 means ~80% of BA's return is idiosyncratic — the −67% alpha is directionally negative but statistically noisy; do **not** cite it as precise.
- **Estimate-revision data artifact:** any model relying on the raw +26% revision score will be misled; the real near-term revision direction is down.
