# STLA — Stellantis N.V. | Financial Risk Assessment

**As of:** data through 2026-11-03 · Price $4.72 · Market cap $13.7B · Sector: Consumer Cyclical / Auto Manufacturers (NL-domiciled, EUR reporting)

**Bottom line:** STLA is a post-crisis balance sheet, not a going-concern question. FY2025 was catastrophic (net loss €22.4B, negative EBITDA), but €31.3B of cash and a visibly improving 2026 (H1 operating income +€1.39B vs Q4-25 −€20.7B) mean near-term default risk is low while **solvency quality is poor**. This is a high-risk equity with impaired credit, not a distressed credit event.

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **Sub-Investment Grade (Distress-adjacent)**

FY2025 (EUR, stat. to 2025-12-31): revenue €153.5B, **gross profit −€4.2B**, operating income **−€22.2B**, net income **−€22.4B**, EBITDA **−€15.25B**, EPS −€7.75. This reflects the Feb-2026 reset: a ~$26.5B writedown, US tariff/mix deterioration, and inventory clearing. Goodwill+intangibles fell €54.4B → €44.9B, retained earnings €77.3B → €53.3B, equity €82.1B → €54.0B.

Leverage is not the problem — **earnings are**. Total debt €45.9B (+23% y/y) against €31.3B cash gives net debt €15.8B, only 0.24x assets. The failure mode is coverage: EBIT/interest of **−17.5x**, and net debt/EBITDA is *not meaningful* because EBITDA is negative (the tool's −0.96x should be disregarded).

Normalising to the 2026 run-rate materially improves the picture and is the fairest credit read:
- 9M-2026 EBIT ≈ €1.18B (H1 €1.39B, Q3 −€0.21B); 9M interest ≈ €0.91B → **coverage ≈1.3x** (EBITDA/interest ≈5.6x)
- Net debt €15.8B / annualised 9M EBITDA ~€6.8B → **≈2.3x**

Direction of travel is positive but the absolute cushion is thin: 1.3x EBIT coverage leaves no room for a demand shock, and at 5%+ market rates (10Y UST 5.13%) refinancing €45.9B of debt is a material and rising cost. I could not retrieve agency ratings or covenant terms — **inferred** positioning is low-BBB/BB+ territory post-writedown.

**FCF sustainability is the weakest link.** FCF was +€12.3B (2023) → **−€7.1B (2024) → −€16.2B (2025)**, a cumulative **−€23.3B** two-year burn. Critically, the company returned €7.7B (div+ buyback) in 2024 and €2.3B in 2025 *while* burning cash — funded entirely from the balance sheet. Management has now suspended the dividend (confirmed Feb-2026) and authorised **up to €5B of issuance**, i.e. dilution is the chosen recapitalisation lever.

## 4. Downside Scenario — Revenue −20%

Base: 2026E revenue ~€160B (9M €119B annualised), EBIT ~+€1.5B, EBITDA ~€7B, capex ~€10.5B.

Revenue −20% (−€32B) at a ~25% contribution margin ⇒ **−€8.0B EBIT**, taking EBIT to **≈−€6.5B**.
- Interest coverage → **negative**; net debt/EBITDA → **n.m.** on EBITDA ~€0.5B
- FCF: EBIT −6.5 + D&A 7.0 − capex 10.5 (pre-cut) − interest 1.8 ⇒ **≈−€11.8B**, partly offset by +€2–3B working-capital release (inventory €22.2B) ⇒ **≈−€9 to −€10B**
- Liquidity: €31.3B cash − €13.3B ST debt = €18B available ⇒ **~1.8 years of runway**; with the €5B authorised issuance, ~2.3 years
- Altman Z would fall well below 0.5; ratings almost certainly cut to BB or lower

**Survival verdict: yes over a 24-month horizon.** Self-help levers are real: dividend already at zero (+€2.3B), buybacks halted, capex cuttable by 20% (+€2.1B), inventory liquidation, and €5B of authorised issuance. The binding constraint is the **€13.3B short-term debt wall plus €30.2B long-term maturities refinancing into a 5%+ curve** — that is where a liquidity event would originate, not in operations.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Metrics land in the *Critical* band on accrual and leverage screens (Z 1.03, F 3, FCF yield −119%, negative EBITDA), but a €31B cash position, suspended dividend, improving 2026 operating trend, and €5B of committed issuance capacity prevent a Critical designation. Risk is concentrated in **earnings quality and refinancing**, not imminent insolvency. Treat as equity risk, not credit risk.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **FY2025 operating loss €22.2B / net loss €22.4B** — largest in company history; negative EBITDA €15.25B.
2. **Two-year FCF burn of €23.3B** with €10B returned to shareholders over the same period — capital allocation was pro-cyclical.
3. **Z-Score 1.03 (distress) and F-Score 3 (weak)** — no profitability or leverage-improvement points scored.
4. **€44.9B goodwill/intangibles = 83% of equity** — tangible equity only €9.1B (≈€3.14/share); a second impairment round would push equity toward the €40B area.
5. **Working capital €1.8B and quick ratio 0.74** — no operational buffer; inventory-funded liquidity.
6. **Refinancing cliff into a 5.13% 10-year**: €13.3B ST + €30.2B LT debt, while prior coupons were struck at far lower rates.
7. **Dividend suspended; €5B issuance authorised** — dilution overhang, and a signal management sees the balance sheet as the constraint.
8. **Coverage of ~1.3x EBIT** on normalised earnings offers no absorption capacity for a demand shock.
9. **Price −81% from peak and still volatile at 53%** — no technical support; negative Sharpe in both windows.

## 7. Data Gaps (explicit)

- **Currency mismatch:** financials are **EUR**, market cap/price **USD**. The FCF yield (−118.9%) is cross-currency; directionally correct, magnitude approximate.
- **No FY2026 cash-flow statement** available — I could extrapolate operating cash flow only from interim income statements. 2026 FCF is unquantified.
- **Quarterly labels are inconsistent** — the 2026-06-30 row (€81.6B revenue) appears to be cumulative H1, not a discrete Q2; I treated it as such.
- **Not available:** credit ratings, CDS spreads, covenant definitions/thresholds, full debt maturity ladder by year, pension obligations, captive-finance (Leasys) cash segregation, and Q3-2026 cash flow. These would materially refine the liquidity runway estimate.
- **Peer set unusable:** the sector screen returned mega-cap consumer names (AMZN, HD, MCD, BABA); only **TM** is a genuine auto comparable and is used above. No European auto comps (VW, Renault, GM, Ford) were retrievable.
