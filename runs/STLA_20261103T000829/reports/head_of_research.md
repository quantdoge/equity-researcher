**Investment Thesis**

Stellantis is the auto sector's most acute stress case: a 14-brand legacy OEM whose FY2025 reset (−€22.4bn net loss, −2.7% gross margin, −€16.2bn FCF) destroyed two decades of apparent value creation and exposed a business earning far below its cost of capital. The bull case is a self-help turnaround — North America inflecting, cost cuts landing, positive industrial FCF promised for 2027 — priced at a distressed 0.25x book / 0.08x sales. That cheapness is real but not investable yet: the recovery exists only in guidance, the macro regime (5.13% 10Y, Fed hiking, sentiment at 48.1) is actively hostile, and the equity carries correlated balance-sheet, legal, and governance risk. Right thesis, wrong moment: this is a **watchlist name with defined re-entry triggers, not a position.**

**Bull Case**
1. Distressed valuation (0.25x book, ~0.08x sales, EV/2027E EBITDA ~3x) with the depreciation base reset by €22.2bn of charges — 2027 EBIT mechanically inflects off a near-zero base.
2. North America (Ram/Jeep) is a genuine profit pool and is inflecting: Q3'26 revenue +1% YoY, AOI positive, guidance reaffirmed twice; $13bn US investment and 11 launches rebuild the franchise by 2027–28.
3. Regulatory relief (EU 2035 softened, US EV rollback) plus hybrid sweet spot favours STLA's multi-energy platforms; EUR/USD translation is a reported-earnings tailwind.

**Bear Case**
1. The recovery is not in any reported line item: Q3 AOI margin 0.7%, ~$4.5bn quarterly cash burn, brand-level demand decelerating into Q4, consensus FY26 EPS (€0.45) vs 9M actual (~€0.02) — estimate cuts are near-certain.
2. Balance sheet: €27bn net debt, coverage ~1.3x, €13.3bn short-term debt refinancing into a 5%+ curve, €44.9bn goodwill against €9.1bn tangible equity — a second impairment or a €3–5bn rights issue (25–35% dilutive) is a live tail.
3. Macro/structure: Fed hiking into a broken consumer, ~€1.0bn tariff drag, Chinese competition compressing Europe/LatAm, Fiat chip stoppages through December — with correlated governance, legal, and disclosure risk on top.

**Key Risks** (to the SELL call, i.e. upside risks): a single credible margin beat could re-rate the stock 40–60% in days from washed-out positioning; tariff relief or USMCA resolution is a direct EPS catalyst; sovereign/family backing caps true downside. **To the long side**: equity raise, second goodwill impairment >€10bn, an FY2026 full-year net loss, NA share slipping back below 7.9%, adverse SDNY/SEC development.
