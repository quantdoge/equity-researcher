### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — Negative (deteriorating sequentially)

Google Trends, weekly, indexed 0–100. I ran brand-level queries because STLA's revenue is brand-driven, not corporate-name driven — corporate keyword search is mostly news/noise.

| Keyword (geo) | Q2'26 avg | Q3'26 avg | QoQ | Last 4 wks (Oct) | Δ vs Q3 | Δ YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jeep** (US) | 94.3 | 87.4 | **−7.3%** | 75.8 | **−13.3%** | ~−3% |
| **Ram 1500** (US) | 90.5 | 82.8 | **−8.5%** | 75.5 | **−8.8%** | ~+7% |
| **Peugeot** (FR) | 88.0 | 85.4 | −3.0% | 79.5 | **−6.9%** | **−8%** |
| **Fiat** (IT) | 66.8 | 71.5 | +7.0% | 72.5 | +1.4% | **+9%** |
| Stellantis (US, corporate) | 55.0 | 38.3 | −30% | 31.5 | −18% | −13% |

**Read:** This is the single most important alt-data finding. Jeep and Ram — the two brands that generate the bulk of North American profit — have rolled over for three consecutive months and are now **~24% below their April–August peaks**. The deceleration is *accelerating* into Q4 (Jeep −13% vs. Q3 average), which is precisely the wrong direction for a company whose Q3 beat was powered by North American improvement. Peugeot France is now down 8% YoY, corroborating the "Europe drove in the opposite direction" narrative from the Q3 call. Fiat Italy is the only green shoot (+9% YoY) — and that is the brand Reuters says is chip-constrained through December, so it can't convert.

**Caveat:** Auto search interest is seasonally spring-weighted (peaks Apr–Jul), so QoQ declines are partly seasonal. The signal I weight is the **YoY and trajectory** comparison, plus the fact that Jeep's Oct level (76) sits at the bottom of its 12-month range.

**App data:** Not applicable — STLA has no material consumer app funnel (Free2move/Leasys are B2B-adjacent). No signal.

### 2. Business Activity Signals — Neutral, leaning negative

- **Job postings (proxy, not quantitative):** ~766 Stellantis postings on Indeed. The mix is telling: overwhelmingly blue-collar and plant/logistics roles (truck driver, millwright, pipefitter, parts distribution, wastewater operator, dyno cell operator) plus a small number of engineering reqs (Design Systems Engineer – Transmissions). **There is no visible acceleration in the white-collar/SDV/software hiring that would signal a forward growth investment cycle.** Hiring reads as maintenance of industrial operations under a cost-cut regime — consistent with the $70B turnaround / cost-savings narrative, not with volume expansion.
- **Patents:** USPTO PatentsView returned an error on three attempts (Stellantis, Stellantis NV, FCA US LLC). **Data gap — no R&D intensity signal available.** Do not infer R&D deceleration from this; it is a tool failure, not an observation.
- **Web traffic:** SimilarWeb public data yielded only a ranking (stellantis.com #18,614 globally, #5 in "Motorsports," Sept 2026) with **no historical visit series**. No trend signal extractable. Data gap.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **Tracking BELOW consensus**

- Q3'26 (reported 28 Oct): revenue **$49.66B vs $46.96B est (beat)**; EPS **−$0.01 vs $0.2505 est (huge miss)**. Metrics improved YoY, guidance reaffirmed.
- Q4/FY'26 consensus (25 Feb 2027): **EPS $0.1116 on $46.28B revenue** — i.e., the Street already models a sequential revenue decline from Q3.
- **History is close to unusable as an anchor:** beat rate 50%, average surprise **−23.4%**, with quarterly swings from −104% to +63%. Estimate quality is extremely poor; I place low weight on the surprise model.
- **Alt-data verdict:** brand search demand declining QoQ across Jeep, Ram and Peugeot into the Q4 period implies **volume risk to the downside**, partially offset by price/mix and cost savings. I expect Q4 revenue **at or modestly below** the $46.3B consensus and EPS at the low end / below $0.11. This is a "don't trust the reaffirmed guidance to mean upside" signal — guidance was reaffirmed *despite* softer underlying demand pull.

### 4. News Sentiment Score and Summary — Mixed, headline score is a false positive

- **Negative:** **$4.5B cash drain in Q3** (CNBC) — the FCF inflection is a 2027 promise, not a current fact; **chip shortage halting Fiat output in Italy, possibly through December** (Reuters, 2 Nov — the most recent item and the most under-priced); **Europe deteriorating** while NA improves; raw-material inflation and European pricing pressure; stock −62% over one year.

### 5. Social Sentiment — Weakly constructive, low information content

No quantified Reddit/StockTwits scoring available (tool returns web-search proxies, not sentiment counts). What surfaced is narrative: retail coverage frames STLA as a deeply beaten-down value/turnaround name ("down a staggering 70% over three years," "bouncing off a fresh 52-week low"). Positioning appears washed out. **I treat this as a contrarian-positive but low-conviction input** — it is chatter, not data.

**Price/volume confirmation:** $8.20 (28 May) → $4.72 = **−42%**; 52-week low **$3.92** (23 Oct); post-earnings **+14%** on **119.5M shares (~4.8× normal volume)**. That is a genuine capitulation-and-reversal volume signature. Valuation is distressed: market cap $13.7B, ~0.17× book, ~0.07× sales (third-party figures, unverified).

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH (tactical / near-term)**

The alt data does **not** confirm the post-earnings rally. Every demand-side probe — Jeep, Ram, Peugeot, corporate search — is decelerating sequentially into the Q4 reporting period, and the one region carrying the earnings beat (North America) shows the sharpest search rollover. Add the fresh, under-priced Fiat chip shortage and the $4.5B quarterly cash burn, and the 14% relief rally looks technical rather than fundamental. This is a **bearish near-term signal on a structurally cheap, washed-out stock** — i.e., a case for fading the pop or waiting for a better entry, *not* a short thesis against a 0.17× book value.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM (would be Low-Medium)**

- **High-confidence:** Google Trends series (clean, current, four independent brand probes all agreeing on direction).
- **Medium:** earnings-preview read — undermined by STLA's extraordinarily noisy estimate history.
- **Low / absent:** patents (API failure ×3), web traffic (ranking only, no series), job postings (binary search proxy, no counts), social sentiment (qualitative only).
- **Known lags:** Trends is weekly but reflects *consideration*, not orders — 1–2 quarter lead on volume, and search intent does not distinguish brand interest from shopping intent. Q4 industry incentives, dealer inventory and chip supply will dominate the actual print.

**Bottom line:** Search demand says Q4 volumes soften; the tape says the market has already decided the turnaround is working. Those two are in conflict, and I would not pay up ahead of 25 Feb 2027 on alt-data evidence this weak on the demand side.
