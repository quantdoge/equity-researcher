### 2. Business Activity Signals — **Positive on hiring, unresolved on R&D**
- **Hiring: expanding.** ~5,400 listings surfaced (Indeed), including GTM/deployment roles (Technical Deployment Lead, $275–380K), **finance-systems production support** (classic post-IPO SOX/controls buildout), and research operations. Signals enterprise go-to-market ramp, not just research.
- **Patents: no data.** USPTO PatentsView proxy failed on two attempts. Anthropic's IP is weights/trade-secret oriented, so patent counts would be a weak signal even if available — noting the gap rather than a conclusion.
- **The real capacity signal is in the S-1, not alt data: $895.4B of compute purchase commitments.** That is the binding constraint on revenue recognition, and it is the single most important number for the Q4 print.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **Tracking modestly ABOVE consensus (low confidence)**
First public report: **~3 Mar 2027** (Q4-26). Consensus: **revenue $20.98B, EPS $0.0125**.

- Base: Q3-26 revenue **$16.8–17.0B, +47–50% QoQ** (from $11.5B); 9M $33.24B, ~+1,200% YoY; Q3 operating loss just **$567M** (near breakeven).
- Consensus implies **+23% QoQ** — i.e. a >50% halving of the growth rate. Alt data (+10% search, +9% traffic, aggressive hiring) does **not** corroborate that sharp a deceleration. **Revising-estimate direction: up**, in my view.
- **Counter-signal:** "Claude Code" search interest is **down 18% QoQ** (30.4 → 24.8), flat at 22–26 since August. It has still doubled its Nov-25 baseline, but developer/API traction — the highest-multiple revenue line — is **not accelerating**. This is the one alt-datapoint arguing against an upside print.
- Note: EPS estimate of $0.0125 implies continuing near-breakeven; the $104B net loss in the S-1 is largely non-cash.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **Bullish**
Operating demand, web traffic, hiring, and revenue trajectory all point one way; the S-1 growth rate is extraordinary and the Q4 bar looks low. The bear case is a **price-and-psychology** case, not a fundamentals one — capital intensity ($895B commitments), a 13.4x annualised revenue multiple on ~$84B run-rate, and a hostile sector tape. **The divergence between constructive alt data and a -23% drawdown from the $111.50 open is the trade.**

### 7. Confidence Level: **Medium-Low**
Why not higher: (a) zero earnings-surprise history — no base rate for this management team; (b) Google Trends is a weak proxy for an enterprise/API-weighted revenue base; (c) only 1–2 alt-data periods since the S-1 update, so QoQ comparisons rest on ~6 weekly observations; (d) patent and social feeds unavailable/contaminated; (e) **no true AI-lab peer exists in the sector table** (only mega-cap semis/hardware like NVDA, AVGO, MSFT), so relative-multiple validation is impossible.

**Data limitations:** Trends/web-traffic are lagged 1–4 weeks; the Oct SimilarWeb figure predates the IPO; hiring counts from Indeed are inflated by reposts (5,400 listings vs 5,300 FTEs is not a clean count); two feeds (patents, social) failed. Alt data is a leading indicator here — it tells you demand is intact, not what multiple the market will pay for it in a sector selloff.
