## 1. Growth Story
Anthropic is the fastest-scaling enterprise software company ever recorded: revenue went $41M → $386M → $4.585B → **$33.2B in the first nine months of 2026**, with Q2 and Q3 revenue nearly tripling sequentially. The moat is code/agentic work — Claude Code and Opus 5.5 have cut cost-per-task 40% per release, and demand has expanded faster than price has fallen (Jevons paradox in practice). This is a deliberate land-and-expand machine: ~11,900 customers spend >$100K/yr, and **net revenue retention is ~900%**.

## 3. TAM and Penetration
The constraint on ANT is not demand — it is **compute and capital**. Global software spend is ~$1.1T; knowledge-worker labor is a $30T+ pool being repriced by agents. If frontier AI software is a ~$1.5T market by 2030, consensus 2030 revenue of $384B implies **~25% share** — aggressive but defensible for the #2 lab with enterprise leadership. Penetration today is trivially low: 11,900 six-figure customers, top-5 concentration down to ~25% of revenue, and **~half of revenue now flowing through cloud marketplaces** (AWS, Google, Azure) — a distribution unlocked by partners rather than built head-on.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts
- **Mar 3, 2027 — first public earnings (Q4/FY26).** Revenue est. $20.98B. The single biggest event: first audited test of 85% gross margin durability. High impact.
- **Comming months — index inclusion.** ~6% float; S&P/Nasdaq rules actively under review. Fast-track inclusion = forced passive demand.
- **~Mid-May 2027 — 180-day lockup expiry** (another 35% tranche after FY26 results). A supply shock, and your best entry point.
- **Model cadence** — Fable 5.x / Opus 6 successor releases; each 25–45% cost-per-task cut historically *accelerates* revenue (Opus 4.5 lifted QoQ growth 48% → 132%).
- **Vertical expansion** — Life Sciences research team now hiring; Claude Code enterprise rollout still early.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (accumulate; not yet Strong Buy)
A genuine frontier-innovation compounder with the best growth-plus-retention combination available in public markets. I am not going Strong Buy for four specific reasons: (1) FY2030 EPS was revised **down 6.0%** — negative momentum at the moment of listing; (2) coverage is **1–2 analysts**, so consensus has no information content; (3) the stock broke issue price immediately ($83 → $97 → **$86**, −25% from its IPO-day high of $115) with a ~6% float; and (4) the Q3 operating-loss reversal shows the margin path is lumpy. **I would size this at half a position now and add into the mid-2027 lockup expiry or on confirmation of >50% gross margin plus positive operating income at the March 3 print.**

## 7. Key Risks
1. **⚠️ The $637B compute commitment is the whole risk in one number.** Two-thirds non-cancelable, split across Broadcom ($161B), SpaceXAI ($120B), Google (≥$115B), Amazon (≥$110B), Microsoft (≥$45B) — against **$4.6B of FY25 revenue**, with $265.6B in fixed/minimum guarantees. Revenue is consumption-based with **no customer lock-in**; the payments arrive whether customers do. Operating leverage cuts both ways.
2. **Model commoditization.** OpenAI, Gemini, xAI, Meta. Frontier leadership rotates on ~9-month cycles, and cost-per-task deflation (−40% per release) is a double-edged sword — it expands demand but permanently erodes pricing power.
3. **Gross-margin definition and volatility.** The S-1 explicitly warns margins will fluctuate. 40% → 85% in one year, and the full-compute-cost basis still shows negative gross profit in FY25. Do not underwrite 85% as steady-state.
4. **Concentration and litigation.** 2025 revenue was 76% from six-figure accounts; a $1.5B copyright settlement already hit FY25, and IP litigation risk is structural.
5. **Float and flow distortion.** ~6% float, thin coverage, lockup supply, and possible index-driven forced buying create a price that may not reflect fundamentals in either direction.

**Bottom line:** the growth is real and the retention is extraordinary; the balance sheet carries a fixed-cost bet that requires flawless demand execution. Buy the dislocation, respect the commitments, and let the March 3 print tell you whether 85% gross margin is a trend or a quarter.
