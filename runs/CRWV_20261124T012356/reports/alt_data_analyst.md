### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (bifurcated)**
- **Operational/fundamental alt data: Bullish.** Hiring is commissioning-focused and unbroken, backlog +138%, Forge traffic exploding, five straight revenue beats, institutional accumulation.
- **Attention/sentiment alt data: Bearish.** Category search interest at 12-month lows, brand interest purely event-driven, social gauge bearish with record engagement, momentum structurally broken (−65% from May peak, sector-wide).
- These offset. The bullish evidence is *lagging-but-real* (contracted demand); the bearish evidence is *coincident and reflexive* (financing cost, crowding, sentiment). Nothing in this dataset argues for adding before the Feb 25 print, nor for exiting a backlog-backed name at −65%.

### 7. Confidence Level: **LOW–MEDIUM**
Data-quality caveats: (a) web traffic, hiring and social "trends" are search-scraped single snapshots, not time series — no MoM/quarterly direction is verifiable except forge.coreweave.com and Google Trends; (b) patent API failed; (c) news sentiment is keyword-count based and demonstrably rally-skewed; (d) Google Trends brand series is contaminated by ticker/stock chatter, and the "GPU cloud" collapse may partly reflect index normalization rather than demand; (e) only ~6 quarters of post-IPO history, so beat-rate statistics (60%, +12.45% avg surprise) are statistically thin; (f) Q4'26 consensus EPS showing an unusual +$0.66 QoQ improvement is unexplained and should be verified against the actual estimate-revision file.

**What would move me to Bullish:** a verifiable monthly traffic/job-posting series showing acceleration into Q4, or a Q4 print where revenue beats >5% while EPS losses narrow. **What would move me to Bearish:** evidence of backlog conversion slippage (commissioning hires stalling) or renewed acceleration in interest expense.

*Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. All figures above are pre-print and unverified against company filings.*
