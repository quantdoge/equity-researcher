**Investment Thesis**

CoreWeave is the clear #1 merchant GPU cloud with genuinely extraordinary contracted demand ($132.5B backlog, +138% YoY; revenue +168% YoY, five straight beats). But it is economically a **capital-intensive equipment lessor, not a software company**: reported 68% gross margin excludes depreciation of the very machines generating the revenue (true margin ~16% including D&A), ROIC has never exceeded WACC, and cumulative FCF since 2023 is roughly −$32B. With $66.2B of debt (doubled in nine months), EBIT of ~zero against ~$3.4B annualized cash interest, ~80 days of cash runway, and a 2027–28 maturity wall colliding with a hiking Fed, the equity is a **levered call option on credit availability** — priced at roughly the probability-weighted mean of two very wide tails. No specialist in this packet recommends buying at $48.33. This is a "pass/avoid" verdict, **not a short recommendation**: 104% volatility and a demonstrated +22% single-day melt-up make short-side risk/reward poor.

**Bull Case**
1. **Contracted demand is real and converts**: $132.5B largely take-or-pay backlog (~10x FY26E revenue), 4.6 GW contracted power, revenue re-accelerating (+168% YoY, +42% QoQ), top-customer concentration improving (67% → 30%), and the A3-rated SPV securitization channel is open and deepening.
2. **Macro reversal optionality is extreme**: a disinflation print → Fed pause → 10Y toward 4.3% and 2032 notes from 16% back to ~10% restores primary-market access and could plausibly **double the equity ($90–110)** — the +22% session on 23 Nov demonstrates the snap-back violence.
3. **Asset-life upside breaks the bear case**: re-contracting older GPUs at higher short-lease prices, plus Rubin-class longevity — if economic life is 8 years not 5–6, steady-state D&A falls to ~50–60% of revenue and operating margins inflect hard.

**Bear Case**
1. **Financing, not demand, kills it**: $66.2B debt at a marginal cost that has doubled, ~$4.2B annualized interest guided for Q4, ~80-day cash runway, and a 2027–28 maturity wall into a hiking Fed with $13.4B of AI-developer debt competing in the same 2027 queue. A failed DDTL draw or forced equity raise in the $30s starts a dilution doom loop.
2. **Accounting duration**: true gross margin is ~16% including D&A; depreciation must roughly double on the current asset base; a 4-year GPU life adds ~$10.7/share of annual pre-tax cost. GAAP operating margins should be expected to *deteriorate* as the fleet ages.
3. **No moat, no governance recourse**: commodity GPU-hours squeezed by NVIDIA upstream and hyperscalers downstream (who are also the largest customers); ROIC never above WACC; insiders extracting billions with 10:1 voting control and an active fraud class action — public shareholders have no mechanism to protect themselves if backlog conversion slips.
