## CoreWeave, Inc. (CRWV) — Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment

**Snapshot:** US-domiciled AI "neocloud" (HQ Livingston, NJ; IPO 28 Mar 2025; ~$26.9bn market cap, beta 7.7). A single-product, single-supplier, dollar-denominated US business that has just begun a mid-2026 push into South and Southeast Asia — precisely as Washington tightened the rules governing that geography. Geographic risk here is therefore **not** a function of foreign revenue share; it is a function of *where the GPUs sit* and *who can reach them*.
