### Investment Memo: CHRW — October 29, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
*(Gated, 1% speculative starter permissible for risk-tolerant accounts — see Portfolio Note)*
**Price Target**: $150 (+18% from current $127.60) — ~20x FY27E EPS of $7.44, below the strategist's probability-weighted $158 to reflect unresolved litigation
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
CHRW is the best-run broker in a structurally mediocre industry, trading at a fair — not cheap — price while carrying two unpriced binary risks. The franchise is genuinely strong: 14 consecutive quarters of volume share gains vs. the Cass index, best-in-class 29% net-revenue operating margin, clean books (Beneish −2.84, negative accruals), and a freight cycle that has inflected. But the stock's 39% de-rating is rational: an essentially unaccrued ~$711M judgment sits against $279M of tangible equity, a unanimous Supreme Court ruling has re-opened the brokerage liability model, and management is funding a $5.8B acquisition with debt into that same liability regime. Normalized owner earnings (~$5.90/sh) put fair value at $118–136 — roughly where the stock trades. Upside requires peak-cycle earnings to hold and both binaries to break favourably; that is a bet, not an underwrite.

**Key Findings by Domain**

Rate/fuel inflation is currently *paying* CHRW (AGP/shipment +17%, Global Forwarding margin 36.6%), but a 4.99% 10Y and re-accelerating 3.7% CPI cap the multiple. A Middle East ceasefire is a live −15–20% EPS scenario (forwarding windfall + spot deflation + fuel-surcharge reversal all unwind at once).
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH.** The Lipe verdict ($604M, ~$711M with interest; 23% apportioned but joint-and-several exposure to the full amount) against only ~$155M of insurance and an "immaterial" accrual. *Montgomery v. The RXO deal adds HSR/antitrust execution risk through H1'27.

Quality is real (ROIC 22.9%, cycle FCF conversion >150%) but the moat is narrow, not wide — 2023's −65% net income proves the earnings stream is cyclical.

**Bull Case**
- The multiple has already reset (30x → 17x FY27E) while fundamentals improved; even a deal-risk haircut supports $164–179 if FY27 EPS of $7.44 is delivered.
- 14 straight quarters of share gains + margin leadership + $300M of credible RXO synergies (~30% of current operating profit) = the consolidator wins the upcycle.
- A deal break is paradoxically bullish: CHRW keeps its balance sheet, resumes buybacks, and the litigation overhang gets the balance sheet's full capacity.

**Bear Case**

- Pro forma leverage lands at ~4x into a fading rate cycle, with the revolver cliff in Nov'27; synergy delivery (~176% of RXO's EBITDA) has no precedent in broker integrations.
- Net revenue margin keeps compressing — Q4'26 gross profit growth <10% with revenue >20% would confirm permanent spread erosion, breaking the FY27 consensus.

**Key Risks**

2. RXO financing terms / pro forma leverage in the proxy; HSR second request; shareholder vote.
3. Freight-rate fade through 2027 (spot comps flatten from Q1'27) exposing the pass-through nature of current "growth."
