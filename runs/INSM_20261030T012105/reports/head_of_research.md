### Investment Memo: INSM (Insmed Incorporated) — 2 November 2026

**Investment Thesis**

Insmed has executed one of the best rare-disease launches on record — BRINSUPRI, the first and only approved therapy for non-CF bronchiectasis, went from $28M to $400M/quarter in five quarters, with 9M'26 revenue +267%, gross margin now above peer median at 85.1%, and losses narrowing 71% — yet the stock is −47% from its December 2025 high on non-commercial events. The catch: at ~13x forward revenue and a probability-weighted intrinsic value of $108–112, the market price already reflects fair value, so the entire return depends on 2027–2030 estimates proving too low (FY26 consensus sits *below* the low end of guidance). This is a high-quality operating story at a full price, with real but non-thesis-breaking governance and policy overhangs — correctly expressed as a small, staged, event-gated position, not a full-conviction buy.

Macro is Neutral-to-hostile: Fed hiking (3.75–4.00%), 10Y ~5.00%, bear-steepening compresses long-duration DCFs, but INSM is demonstrably an execution story (rose +15% on the Q3 beat with rates at 5%). The −80pp relative performance vs XBI over 12m is idiosyncratic, not macro.

Revenue is re-concentrating into the US (~87–90% of 2026E), making INSM a US-policy beta story: Section 232 tariffs (BRINSUPRI is non-orphan, unlike exempt ARIKAYCE; ~$200M gross-margin exposure at a 15% duty) and MFN pricing, with no Insmed MFN/onshoring deal identified. No material litigation. Binary FDA event: ARIKAYCE sNDA PDUFA 28-Jan-27.

**Bull Case**
- **Category creation still compounding**: BRINSUPRI +29% QoQ in Q3'26, guide raised twice to $1.35–1.4B, FY26 consensus below the guidance floor; Japan (launched 19-Oct) and EU are effectively free upside at 0.5% of franchise revenue.
- **Operating leverage inflecting**: gross margin 79.4% → 85.1% (above peer median), SG&A flat at ~$250M/qtr while revenue ~quadrupled; first positive operating quarter in Q2'26; credible path to 2027 cash-flow positivity without new capital.
- **Priced-in optionality is ~zero**: the market capitalizes roughly 2029 consensus and assigns nothing to TPIP (>$6B peak, four Phase 3s), the ARIKAYCE label expansion (PDUFA 28-Jan-27, >$1B potential), or the gene-therapy pipeline.

**Bear Case**
- **No valuation cushion**: 13x forward revenue with IV = spot; bear case −50% vs bull case +55–60% is roughly symmetric risk/reward.
- **Price durability, not volume, is the real risk**: $88k/yr in a prevalent, non-fatal disease invites G2N erosion, step-edits, IRA negotiation on the small-molecule ~7-year clock (~2032), and MFN/tariff exposure — with EU launch already deferred on policy.
- **Concentration + deceleration + overhangs**: 76% of revenue from a five-quarter-old molecule; QoQ growth decelerating (44% → 49% → 29% → ~16% implied Q4); opex still ~100% of revenue; unexplained CFO exit, disclosure suits, serial dilution.

**Key Risks**
1. ARIKAYCE CRL on 28-Jan-27 (~25% of revenue; plausibly −20–30% equity impact at this multiple)
2. Any BRINSUPRI safety signal or label change — non-negotiable exit
3. Gross-to-net/payer deterioration; persistence unobservable (no IQVIA data — the two variables the thesis rests on are the two we cannot see)
4. MFN/Section 232 action naming non-orphan launch drugs; no accommodation in place
5. Governance escalation: investigations converting to accounting allegations, or a second CFO departure
6. Macro tail: stagflationary term-premium shock (10Y >5.75%) → 25–35% de-rating regardless of execution
