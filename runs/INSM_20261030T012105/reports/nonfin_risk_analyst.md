## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

- **No SEC enforcement actions or DOJ matters** returned by EDGAR full-text search. Clean on formal enforcement.
- **ARIKAYCE sNDA — PDUFA Jan 28, 2027** (Priority Review). Seeks **full approval** and expansion to newly diagnosed / recurrently infected MAC lung disease. This is the single largest binary regulatory event in the near term: converting from accelerated to full approval de-risks the franchise; a CRL or narrow label would hit a ~$460–470M revenue base. AdCom risk not disclosed in retrieved sources.
- **ARIKAYCE carries a boxed warning** (increased respiratory adverse reactions) — permanent label-driven commercial and liability exposure.
- **BRINSUPRI safety labeling**: dermatologic adverse reactions (rash, dry skin, hyperkeratosis), plus URI, headache, hypertension. Real-world persistence/adherence is the key post-approval risk; a label update or safety communication would damage the core growth engine.
- **Sequelae of the Dec 17, 2025 CRSsNP failure**: brensocatib's Phase 2b BiRCh study missed primary *and* secondary endpoints; program discontinued. No regulatory consequence, but see litigation below.
- **International**: Japan launch of BRINSUPRI (Oct 19, 2026); EMA product information exists, indicating EU approval. Multi-jurisdiction launch cadence raises pharmacovigilance and pricing/reimbursement complexity.
- **IRA pricing exposure (structural)**: brensocatib is an **oral small molecule**, making it eligible for Medicare negotiation ~7 years post-approval versus 11 for biologics — a medium-term gross-to-net cliff that is underappreciated in peak-sales estimates.
- **Litigation**: **Multiple securities firms (Pomerantz, Bronstein Gewirtz & Grossman, Schall) opened investigations into potential securities fraud** tied to pre-Dec 17, 2025 statements about the brensocatib CRSsNP opportunity. These are investigations, not yet confirmed filed complaints with a defined class period — but a formal class action is a live possibility requiring monitoring.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Extreme revenue concentration in a five-quarter-old launch.** BRINSUPRI = **$400.1M of $524.4M** Q3 2026 revenue (~76%), up 29% QoQ on top of 49% QoQ in Q2, with guidance raised twice in six months to $1.35–1.4B. A single quarter of demand softness, gross-to-net deterioration, or channel inventory correction would be read as a franchise-level break. Forecast credibility is now itself a risk factor.
- **Third-party manufacturing and supply chain.** Insmed relies heavily on outsourced CMOs for ARIKAYCE (a complex liposomal inhalation suspension) and for BRINSUPRI scale-up. Liposomal and inhalation fill-finish capacity is scarce and single-sourced in places; a site qualification failure, FDA 483, or contamination event would be supply-limiting with limited ability to substitute. Rapid multi-country launch (US, Japan, EU) compounds release-testing and serialization complexity.
- **Key-person dependency and internal-control bandwidth.** Insmed is the "Will Lewis story" — an 11+ year CEO who is also Chair, executing four parallel Phase 3 PALM studies (PAH, ILD, PPF, IPF — the company's largest clinical effort ever) while running a two-product global commercial organization and replacing its CFO. Over-extension is a genuine execution risk, as is institutional knowledge loss at the CFO seat during a $14B peak-sales build-out. **No cybersecurity incident history was retrievable — noted as a data gap, not a clean bill of health.**

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: MODERATE

- **The core reputational asset is intact**: "people-first" rare-disease positioning, first-in-class/first-in-disease narrative, strong patient-advocacy ties in NTM/MAC and bronchiectasis. No product recalls, no manufacturing scandals, no ESG controversies surfaced in review.
- **Principal reputational vulnerabilities:**
  1. The **Dec 2025 CRSsNP miss** abruptly ended a multi-year "win streak" narrative and drew plaintiff-firm attention. The reputational frame has shifted from "unbroken execution" to "one franchise, one shot."
  2. **Pricing/political exposure**: high-cost rare-disease and now broad-population bronchiectasis pricing invites payer, congressional, and IRA scrutiny — the higher BRINSUPRI's revenue climbs, the larger the political target.
  3. **The unexplained CFO exit** plus a **~25% drawdown from May highs** feeds a "what do they know" narrative that is being actively amplified by securities-investigation press releases.
- Sell-side sentiment has cracked (an October downgrade), and the Q3 EPS miss ($0.46 loss vs. $0.34 expected) alongside raised guidance reinforces a "spending to grow" perception.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE** (upper end)

The company has no enforcement history, no fraud indicators in the public record, a substantially independent and recently strengthened board, programmatic rather than opportunistic insider selling, and a genuine clinical/regulatory franchise. Offsetting: single-product revenue concentration in an unproven launch, a binary FDA decision within one quarter, live securities-fraud investigations, unexplained C-suite turnover, CEO/Chair duality, a staggered board, and a structural IRA pricing overhang unique to an oral small molecule.

**Not Critical** — there is no evidence of accounting irregularity, going-concern stress, or control failure. **Not Low** — the CFO exit plus plaintiff-firm activity plus ~76% revenue concentration form a coherent cluster that deserves premium scrutiny.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **CFO departure (Oct 30, 2026) with no disclosed successor or reason**, seven weeks before a Jan 28, 2027 PDUFA date and mid-launch. Escalate to management: confirm whether the departure involved any disagreement over accounting, internal controls, disclosure, or guidance. *This is the highest-priority question in the file.*
2. **Securities-fraud investigations (Pomerantz, BG&G, Schall)** arising from pre-December 2025 brensocatib CRSsNP disclosures. Confirm whether a complaint has been filed and the defined class period; a formal filing converts this from Moderate to High on the legal axis.
3. **Revenue concentration / guidance cadence**: 76% of revenue from a product launched five quarters ago, with guidance raised twice in two quarters. Stress-test channel inventory and gross-to-net; watch for a Q4 2026 "beat-and-raise" that front-loads 2027 expectations.
4. **ARIKAYCE PDUFA (Jan 28, 2027)** — binary event on a maturing franchise; confirm whether an advisory committee is scheduled (not disclosed in retrieved sources).
5. **Governance structure**: Chair/CEO duality combined with a **classified board** limits shareholder recourse precisely when the above issues are crystallizing. Watch whether the Hudson/Koppikar appointments precede a de-staggering proposal.
