## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (fundamentals: High agreement / price: Low agreement)

Three distinct clusters, and the split is diagnostic:

**(a) Business momentum — HIGH CONSENSUS (5/5 relevant agents agree).** Growth, Sector, Alt Data all independently confirm the same thing, and critically **the short analyst concedes it too**: "the launch is objectively working… gross margins expanding… no recognition games." An accounting forensics agent finding *negative accruals* and flat DSO is the strongest possible validation that the revenue is real. There is no dissent anywhere in the file that BRINSUPRI is a genuine commercial success.

**(b) Valuation — CONTESTED.** Value's probability-weighted IV of $108–112 lands *on* the spot price; Growth's Street-anchored $180 implies +61%; Short's base case $105–120 brackets the spot. Two of three independent valuation approaches converge at **$105–120**. I weight Value highest here on methodology (probability-weighted scenarios with bear assigned a 30% weight vs Growth's implicit near-zero).

**(c) Risk overhangs — CONTESTED, and this is where I override.** Non-Fin Risk raises two items the bullish cluster underweights: (i) the **CFO exit one day after the Q3 print with no disclosed successor and no stated reason**, and (ii) **live securities-fraud investigations**. Applying the fraud-risk precedence rule: I asked whether the Short Analyst — the designated forensic skeptic — could substantiate an accounting problem. He explicitly could **not** (SGI/DSRI inflation mechanical, TATA negative, DSO stable, leverage falling, no auditor change, no restatement, 302 certifications clean). The investigations are **stock-drop disclosure suits tied to the Dec-25 CRSsNP program failure, not accounting fraud**. Conclusion: this is a *governance and disclosure* penalty, not a thesis-killer. It caps size; it does not veto the position.

**Net: aggregate conviction = MEDIUM.** Business quality is high; the price offers no discount to value; risk is elevated and single-sided (76% of revenue from a five-quarter-old launch, with a binary FDA event 11 weeks out).
