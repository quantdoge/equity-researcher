**Why not High:**

These are the two most objective, non-sentiment series in the framework, and their absence means the business-activity read rests on soft, qualitative job-posting observation only.

- Job postings and social sentiment are **web-search proxies with no time series**, so I can describe a state, not a verified trend.

**Data lag caveat:** Google Trends data ends ~Oct 18, 2026 and the most recent weekly points are typically revised upward by Google; the Q3 news window is 10 days stale relative to the last print. The Feb 18, 2027 catalyst is ~3.5 months out — far enough that these signals should be re-run in January, particularly branded search and Japan prescription-tracking data, before acting on the earnings preview.

**Actionable framing:** Alt data supports the *revenue trajectory*, not necessarily the *stock*. This is a case where alt data says the business is fine — the debate in the share price is about TPIP pipeline value and duration, which alternative data cannot see.
