### Investment Memo: CVNA — October 30, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (Reduce / do not initiate) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $57 (−17% from $68.30) | Entry band for re-engagement: $48–52
**Time Horizon**: 12 months

**Investment Thesis**

Carvana is a genuinely excellent operating company — the turnaround is real, the share gains are independently verified by alternative data, and the balance sheet is the strongest in five years. But at $68 the price discounts flawless execution of the 3M-unit plan: even hitting the 2030 consensus perfectly (~$60B revenue, ~$5.4B EBITDA) yields ~1% annualized at a generous 15x exit multiple, while gross margins are already compressing, the 10Y is 5.05% with further hikes priced, and an open SEC subpoena plus an 84% family-controlled governance structure impose a structural, unfixable multiple discount. This is a valuation call, not a quality call — and explicitly *not* a short recommendation in a 3.55-beta name with real squeeze risk. Note: the Growth desk dissents (BUY), and that disagreement is material to conviction.

Offsets are real: 4.2% unemployment, tariffs lifting new-car prices (trade-down tailwind), Manheim firming +2.2% YoY. **Bull Case**
1. The only scaled share-gainer in a flat $1T+ market — +39% units, brand search taking share from CarMax, and a credible path where the market prices barely half of management's 3M-unit ambition.
2. Structural unit economics: 20.6% gross margin (~2x CarMax), 10% adj-EBITDA margin with FY26 guided to the upper end of $2.7–3.0B, net leverage ~1.3x, positive FCF three years running.
3. Rates asymmetry: if the September 2026 hike proves terminal and the 10Y retraces toward 4.4%, a 3.55-beta name re-rates violently upward (+50% scenario) off real earnings.

**Bear Case**
1. Priced past perfection: probability-weighted FV $57; even flawless 2030 execution at a 15x exit ≈ 1% annualized; at a CarMax-like 12x the exit EV is below today's.
2. Growth quality is decaying: GM down 170bps YoY to ~18.9%, GPU −$365/unit, earnings dominated by mark-to-model loan-sale gains, cash conversion 74% — and management drops quarterly guidance after Q4'26.
3. Compounding tails: higher rates hit finance GPU, warehouse costs and the multiple simultaneously; SEC escalation or an adverse Nov-4 ruling is unreserved; governance discount is permanent while Class B control persists.

**Key Risks** (and what would change the call)
- GPU/margin normalization; a credit event in auto ABS (record subprime delinquencies) hitting funding and gain-on-sale together; adverse legal outcomes (SEC escalation, summary judgment/class certification, top-5 state license action); dilution overhang; forced selling from pledged shares.
- **Data integrity flags**: share count unresolved (688M basic vs 1,121M diluted → market cap $47B–$77B, EV/EBITDA anywhere from 17x to 37x); vendor EBITDA fields corrupted; Form 4 direction inferred. Verify against the 10-K/10-Q before acting on any single multiple.
- **Upgrades to the thesis**: two consecutive quarters of GM ≥19%, SEC de-escalation, or price ≤$52. Downgrades: GM <18% for two quarters, adj-EBITDA margin <9% with units >35%, or any CNL write-down.
