Directionally this says the *category* intent pool is softening while Carvana's *brand* intent is rising — i.e., share gains inside a tougher market. High index volatility on a low-volume term; treat as directional only.

**App/download and satellite data:** not available in this dataset — gap.

**Synthesis:** Consumer demand alt-data is the strongest part of this thesis. Brand search at an all-time high, up high-teens YoY, and taking share from CarMax. This is consistent with the reported ~40% retail unit growth and is *not* what a decelerating demand curve looks like.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:**
- **Google Trends is contaminated by investor interest.** The 100 peak in Aug 2026 and the Feb–Mar step-up coincide with stock events (index-inclusion chatter per Reddit), not necessarily shopping intent. Brand search conflates "carvana stock" with "carvana cars" — this materially weakens the headline YoY read.
- **Category proxy is deteriorating** ("buy used car online" at annual lows) and CarMax is flat-to-down YoY — the demand pool is shrinking.
- **Web traffic data is stale/undated**; **job postings have no trend series**; **patent API failed**. Three of six feeds are single-snapshot or missing.
- **Highest-importance alt-data source (transaction-level unit data, app store ranks, satellite lot inventory) is unavailable** — for an auto retailer, actual listing/inventory counts and day-supply trends would be far more decisive than search interest.
- Social sentiment is a **web-search proxy**, not a quantitative score, and the Reddit posts span mixed vintages.

**Bottom line for the memo:** Alt-data supports the *growth* leg of the CVNA story with better-than-usual conviction (relative search-share gain is a genuine, hard-to-fake leading indicator), but it offers **no support for the margin/EPS leg**, and the 19% drawdown from April highs despite record KPIs tells you the market has already moved the debate to multiple and earnings quality. Watch for Q4 margin commentary and any deceleration in the CVNA/CarMax search ratio — that ratio breaking back below ~1.0 would be an early warning that the share-gain engine is stalling.
