### 2. Key Red Flags Found

1. **2026 gross margin is flattered by a non-recurring item.** Q2'26 GM jumped **+710bp to 65.6% — of which 390bp was "net tariff refunds."** That is ~$64M of gross profit (~$0.09–0.11/share after tax) run through the margin line. Full-year adjusted GM guidance of 60.5–61% explicitly bakes in an 80bp benefit from it. Consensus FY26 EPS of $1.307 is ~7–8% inflated by a government refund, and management *raised guidance on the back of it*.
2. **Growth is effectively a single-country story.** Q3'26: total +24%, but **Greater China +61%**, APAC +40%. Americas was ~+18% and, per channel commentary, materially slower. My estimate: **~85–90% of the $413M YoY revenue increase came from Greater China + APAC.** Greater China is ~34% of H1'26 revenue and ~44% of Arc'teryx sales.
3. **Inventory outgrowing cost of sales.** FY2025 inventory **+32.6%** vs COGS **+20.4%**. Inventory days: 186.6 (FY22) → 191.8 → 193.2 → **212.8 (FY25)** → ~232 on TTM COGS at Q3'26. Partly a pre-winter ski/Arc'teryx build, but the trend is unhelpful.
4. **EPS trajectory is back-end loaded on margin expansion that is peaking.** Consensus: revenue +16.1% (2027), +14.6% (2028) — but EPS +26% and +22%. That gap requires continued gross-margin expansion *from an already-guided ~61%*, a level unusual for a business with ~1/3 wholesale mix and rising DTC capex (capex $65.9M → $225.5M, 2021→2025).
5. **Structural capitalisation.** Goodwill + intangibles **$5.0B = 46% of assets and ~70% of equity**, legacy of the 2019 ANTA-led LBO. Altman Z was **1.813 (grey zone)** on FY25.
6. **Dilution.** Diluted shares 489.5M (pre-IPO) → 589.4M (Q3'26), ≈+20%; Piotroski F7 = 0. The data feed reports SBC = $0, which is a **data gap** — share count creep proves real economic SBC exists.
7. **Reduced-disclosure structure.** Cayman-incorporated, Finland-headquartered, ANTA-controlled. Annual filing retrieval (10-K/20-F) **failed on both attempts** — an honest gap in my work, but it flags a foreign-private-issuer-style disclosure regime with less granular segment/related-party visibility than a US domestic filer.

### 3. Beneish & Accruals Detail
Indices: DSRI 1.007 (no receivable deterioration — good), GMI 0.961 (margin *improving* → pushes score down), AQI 0.903, SGI 1.267 (high growth, the main upward driver), DEPI 0.976, SGAI 1.008, LVGI 1.056, TATA **−0.03**. **Verdict: no accounting-forensics case.** De-leveraging is genuine: net debt $6.18B (2023) → $0.44B (Q3'26); interest expense $401.7M → ~$50M annualised. That alone explains a large part of the 2023→2026 EPS swing, and it is legitimate, one-time-in-nature financial engineering rather than operating improvement.

### 4. Competitive Threat Assessment: **REAL BUT SLOW-BURNING**
- Arc'teryx is a genuine premium asset with pricing power — GM 52.5% → 57.6% → ~59.3% is *expanding*, i.e. **no moat erosion visible yet.**
- But the moat is increasingly **rented from Chinese consumer sentiment**, not owned. Arc'teryx in China is a status brand in a market that has punished aspirational/luxury pricing (2025–26 luxury down-trading). A shift in Chinese preference for domestic brands, a "daigou"/grey-market price reset, or a tariff/geopolitical shock hits both revenue *and* the highest-margin mix simultaneously.
- Winter-sports exposure (Salomon/Atomic) adds weather variance; wholesale restocking has been a one-time tailwind that guidance now says fades.

### 5. Management & Governance Red Flags: **MILD**
- **25 Form 4s in 2026**, heavily clustered (13 between 1 May and 18 May 2026, plus June/September), from multiple reporting-person CIKs — a cadence consistent with **systematic post-lockup selling by insiders and pre-IPO holders**. **No insider purchases observed.** Direction cannot be confirmed from the metadata returned, so I flag this as an inference, not proof.
- ANTA's controlling stake is a double-edged sword: distribution/JV synergy, but a **sell-down overhang** and related-party revenue that is not fully visible in my dataset.
- Guidance raised three times in 2026 — promotional cadence is high, and one of those raises leaned on a tariff refund. "Beating and raising" against self-set bars is not the same as beating independent expectations.

### 6. Short Thesis Summary — is there a credible bear case?

**Yes, but it is a valuation/concentration short, not a fraud short.**

The bear math: strip the ~$0.09–0.11 of tariff refunds → FY26 EPS ≈ $1.20 (24.8x). Consensus 2027 $1.643 = **18.4x**. If China growth mean-reverts from +61% to +15% while tariffs and DTC fixed costs cap gross margin near 61%, 2027 EPS could land ~$1.40 and the multiple de-rate to 15x → **~$21, i.e. −30%**. That is the credible downside. Note the stock has already travelled this path once: **$37.65 (6 Aug) → $26.18 (17 Sep) = −30% in six weeks**, then back to $32.83. Real volatility, real downside reflex.

**The other side of the trade is strong, and I will say so plainly:** earnings quality is genuinely good (accruals negative, cash conversion 171%, Beneish −2.46), margins are expanding, the balance sheet has been transformed (net debt/EBITDA ~0.3x; retained earnings turned positive at +$203.6M for the first time), receivables are clean, and short interest is only **2.70% of float (14.94M shares, mid-Oct 2026)** — the trade is *not* crowded, so there is no squeeze fuel but also no consensus bearishness to exploit. A 20%+ grower at 18.4x 2027E is not an egregious multiple. Beta 1.97 means a short carries heavy market-beta risk.

**What would flip me to an active Short:** (a) two consecutive quarters of Greater China growth below +25%; (b) inventory days above ~240 excluding the seasonal build, or DSO rising; (c) FY27 gross-margin guidance flat-to-down; (d) a confirmed ANTA stake sale; (e) evidence the tariff refunds were booked without a matching prior-period charge (i.e., earnings management rather than a true refund).

### 7. Contrarian Rating: **NO EDGE (Watchlist — Conditional Short)**

I cannot underwrite a short here. The forensic screen is clean, the cash flow is real, the receivables show no stuffing, the balance sheet is de-risked, and the multiple is defensible against 20%+ growth. The genuine bear points — **China/APAC concentration (~88% of incremental growth), one-off-flattered 2026 margins, margin-dependent 2027–28 EPS estimates, inventory build, insider selling cadence** — are real but are *risks*, not *falsifications*. They constitute a watchlist with defined triggers, not a Hindenburg report.

**Verdict: Reject as a forensic short. Hold as a tactical short candidate contingent on the China deceleration trigger above.** The most honest summary of my work: *this is a well-run, cash-generative, China-concentrated growth company trading at a full-but-not-absurd multiple — the danger is geographic, not accounting.*
