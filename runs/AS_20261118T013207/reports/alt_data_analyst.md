### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **TRACKING ABOVE (modestly)**

Setup into the 23-Feb-2027 print:
- Consensus: **rev $2.507B (+19.3% YoY), EPS $0.40 (+29% YoY).**
- Q3-26 actual: rev $2.169B (+23.5% YoY), EPS $0.42 vs $0.34 est.; FY26 revenue growth guide **raised to 25–26%**, with margin and EPS guidance also raised.
- 9M-26 revenue = $5.75B. Hitting the midpoint of the 25–26% FY guide implies Q4 of ~$2.54B — **~1.3% above consensus**, and consensus Q4 growth (+19%) is a visible deceleration from the +23–32% delivered in each of the last four quarters.
- Guidance has ratcheted **20–22% → 25–26%** over 2026, and beat rate is **7/7 (100%) with average surprise +59%**.
- Alt data corroborates rather than contradicts: traffic +40% MoM and rising brand search into the holiday window.

I'd characterise this as **above consensus but not a wide gap** — the beat is more likely to come from margin/EPS (avg surprise +59% vs. only ~+5–6% revenue surprise) than from a large revenue blowout. Revenue beats have been consistently 4–6%; the EPS beats are far larger.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**

Driven by (a) a genuine sequential inflection in Arc'teryx demand off a summer trough, (b) Salomon search at the highest level since June and up ~40% YoY, (c) arcteryx.com traffic +40% MoM in October, and (d) evidence this is **relative market-share gain** versus HOKA rather than sector-wide recovery. Primary risk: the signals are unmeasured in **Greater China**, which is the highest-margin and most volatile part of the story — this is the single largest blind spot in the file.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:** the bullish case rests on two data families only (brand search + web traffic). App-download, satellite/geospatial, transaction-panel, and quantitative hiring data are all absent; social sentiment is a dead feed; patents are a dead feed. SimilarWeb figures are search-snippet estimates, not a full monthly series, so only one clean MoM delta exists per domain. US-only geo coverage omits China/EMEA, where a large share of Amer's growth and nearly all of its margin expansion is being generated. Price data terminates on the print date, so I have no post-earnings drift to confirm or refute the signal.

**Net:** the direction of the alt data is clear and it is the right direction — demand is re-accelerating into Q4 and the tape has not re-rated for it. Sizing conviction should reflect that this is a two-source read with a China-sized hole in it.
