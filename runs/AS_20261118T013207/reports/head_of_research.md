### Investment Memo: AS (Amer Sports, Inc.) — 18 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $34 (~12% upside from current $30.24)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

---

**Investment Thesis**

Amer Sports is a genuinely high-quality compounder — 25–26% guided FY26 growth, ~1,000bps of five-year gross-margin expansion, a transformed balance sheet (net leverage 0.31x, BBB−), clean earnings (Beneish −2.46, 171% cash conversion), and a fresh $1bn buyback — trading at 22.7x forward / 18.4x FY27E earnings, roughly a PEG of 1.0. The problem is that this quality is already approximately priced: our value work puts fair value at ~$31, the macro regime (10Y at 5.13%, USD +4% in eight weeks, record-low sentiment) is compressing exactly the kind of high-beta, long-duration multiple AS carries, and the growth engine is dangerously concentrated — ~85–90% of incremental revenue is coming from China/APAC under a Chinese controlling shareholder, in a boycott-prone market, with tariff-refund-flattered 2026 margins and zero insider buying in twelve months. This is a good company at a fair price with a fat left tail: hold, add on weakness, respect the triggers.

---

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: AS sits entirely on the winning side of the premium/mass bifurcation — premium technical brands compound 25–30% at 57–63% gross margins while Nike/VF retrench, releasing share. Sector niche rated Attractive, conditional on Arc'teryx brand heat (Lululemon is the mortality template). Macro is a *multiple problem, not an earnings problem*: reflation with a hawkish paused Fed, 10Y 5.13%/30Y 5.47%, dollar rally threatening to flip the embedded ~200bp FX tailwind into a headwind. Negative skew; macro bear case is $22–24 even on in-line earnings.
- **Geopolitical & Legal: HIGH.** ~95% Asia sourcing (27% China) sits under a new June-2026 Section 301 forced-labour track covering up to 16 countries; ~25–28% of revenue is in politically sensitive, boycott-prone Greater China (the Sept-2025 Tibet fireworks incident drew a ~600m-view backlash and an official probe); an active securities class action; and a dense PFAS/UFLPA/EU Forced Labour compliance calendar through 2027. Q2-26's 390bp margin lift from tariff refunds proves policy — not just brand — moves this P&L.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value: HOLD.** Narrow-but-widening moat; ROIC inflecting 0.8% → ~12%; FCF conversion >100%; net debt cut $6.2bn → ~$0.45bn. But blended DCF fair value is ~$31 (base $30/bull $42/bear $21), falling to ~$28 at the 11% WACC a 1.97 beta implies — zero margin of safety at $30.24. Buy below $24, sell above $42.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth: BUY (high conviction).** Revenue re-accelerating (guide raised 20–22% → 25–26%), 7/7 beat rate with +59% avg surprise, FY26–30E EPS CAGR ~22.6% at 18.4x FY27E (PEG ~1.0), ~4–5% share of a $150–200bn serviceable market with Salomon Softgoods as the next leg. Own caveat: US Arc'teryx search interest collapsed post-April 2026 — growth is being carried by China/DTC.
- **Quantitative Signals: NEUTRAL, tilt negative (−0.6σ).** Bottom-half of the comp set on value, momentum *and* quality (last on ROE and GP/assets); highest beta in peer set; 12-1 momentum −16.2% in an intact downtrend of lower highs. Trailing multiples are stale artifacts (39.8x P/E vs 23.1x FY26E), and with ~1.8 years of history no statistic is robust (alpha t ≈ −1.2). Screens Sell on factor ranks; forward earnings power holds it at Neutral.
- **Financial Risk: MODERATE** (credit LOW / equity HIGH). Survives a −35% revenue shock without external financing (cash $668m ≈ 1.5x net debt; ICR 12.9x; FCF ~breakeven even in the severe case). The real risk is equity-side: 40–47% vol, −37.4% max drawdown, and $5.0bn of goodwill/intangibles (46% of assets) as the impairment tail. Ignore the book-equity Altman Z of 1.81 — market-cap-adjusted Z ≈ 4.2, safe zone.
- **Non-Financial Risk: HIGH.** Zero insider purchases in 12 months against CEO sale of 500k shares (~$17.2m) and the CSO exiting ~78% of direct holdings; ANTA deploying ~$1.8bn into a competing 29% Puma stake while AS trades 28% off its high; controlled-company governance with ANTA nomination rights over 5/11 directors; FPI disclosure regime (no 10-K/10-Q/proxy) limiting related-party monitoring; CEO doubling as interim Salomon/Outdoor head.
- **ESG: AVERAGE / thesis-neutral.** Genuine environmental leadership (SBTi-validated −90% absolute Scope 1–3 targets, 86% renewable electricity, ePE PFAS transition largely pre-incurred) offset by the Tibet values-credibility failure and the China brand-trust concentration — a low-probability, high-severity tail, not a cash-flow drag. Governance merits a small multiple discount.
- **Alternative Data: BULLISH.** Arc'teryx US search interest roughly doubled off its summer trough into November; Salomon search at its best since June (+~40% YoY); arcteryx.com traffic +40% MoM in October — and this is share gain, not sector beta (HOKA searches flat-to-down). Consistent with Q4 tracking ~1–2% above consensus, with the beat more likely in EPS than revenue. Confidence Medium: two data families, and China — the highest-margin region — is a blind spot.
- **Short / Contrarian View: NO EDGE — reject as forensic short.** Earnings quality is clean (negative accruals, DSO improving, no receivables stuffing), short interest only 2.7% of float. The credible bear case is valuation/concentration: strip ~$0.09–0.11 of tariff refunds → FY26 EPS ~$1.20; if China mean-reverts and FY27 lands ~$1.40 at 15x, that's ~$21 (−30%). Defined triggers, not falsifications.

**Bull Case**
- **Durable 20%+ compounding at a discount to itself**: 25–26% growth, PEG ~1.0, 18.4x FY27E, estimates rising through a −28% de-rating — sentiment, not fundamentals, has done the damage; the analyst cluster ($42 median) is the re-rating path.
- **Self-reinforcing fundamentals**: margin mix shift (DTC >50% of sales, Arc'teryx DTC +32%), SG&A leverage, ROIC toward mid-teens, net cash, and a $1bn buyback (~5.6% of shares) putting a mechanical bid under the stock into year-end.
- **Alt data inflecting into the catalyst**: US brand demand re-accelerating into Q4 against consensus that models a deceleration (+19% vs +23–32% delivered); a fifth consecutive beat-and-raise on ~23 Feb 2027 breaks the de-rating narrative.

**Bear Case**
- **China is the whole story and the whole risk**: ~25–28% of revenue, ~44% of Arc'teryx sales, ~85–90% of incremental growth — in a market with +0.4% retail sales, nationalist backlash precedent, and a Chinese controlling shareholder with conflicts of interest (Puma stake, related-party distribution).
- **Margin quality is flattered**: 390bps of Q2's 710bps GM gain was non-recurring tariff refunds; 2027–28 consensus EPS implicitly requires continued expansion from ~61% GM — a level that invites both mean-reversion and competition (ON at 62.8% GM is attacking directly).
- **A fair price meets a hostile tape**: zero margin of safety at $30, highest beta in the peer set, negative momentum, insider selling with no offsetting buys, and a rates/FX regime explicitly working against the multiple.

**Key Risks**
1. Greater China deceleration below +25% for two consecutive quarters (the short-desk trigger and the value thesis's axle)
2. Tariff regime escalation (Section 301 forced-labour action) hitting 95% Asia-sourced COGS; refund benefits lapping in H1-27 comps
3. Multiple compression: 22.7x forward at beta ~1.5–2.0 in a 5%+ 10Y world — a shift to 16–18x is a 25% drawdown on unchanged earnings
4. Governance event: ANTA sell-down, related-party expansion, or further insider liquidation
5. Inventory build (+33% YoY, ~232 days) converting to markdowns if sell-through slows; goodwill impairment ($5.0bn) on any Arc'teryx stumble
6. Arc'teryx brand-cycle mortality (the Lululemon template) — sector team's flagged #1 structural risk

**Portfolio Construction Note**

Size this as a **high-beta satellite, not a core holding**: 40–47% annualised vol, beta ~1.5–2.0, and a demonstrated −30%-in-six-weeks drawdown capacity argue for a **starter position of 0.75–1.0% NAV maximum** (half a normal single-name weight), funded from low-beta consumer staples rather than adding to discretionary beta. Do **not** pair with DECK/ONON — correlations of 0.59–0.65 mean no diversification. Execution discipline: hold the starter through the Feb-2027 print; **add toward 1.5% only below ~$26** (near the Value team's $24 buy zone and the September $26.10 low, where the risk/reward finally offers a margin of safety); **trim above ~$40** into the analyst-target cluster. Hard review triggers — any one of: two quarters of China growth <+25%, FY27 gross-margin guidance flat-to-down, inventory days >240 ex-seasonal, confirmed ANTA stake sale, or insider selling resuming post-lockup — move this to Sell regardless of price. The China blind spot in our alternative data and the FPI disclosure gap mean position size must carry the residual uncertainty that research cannot close.
