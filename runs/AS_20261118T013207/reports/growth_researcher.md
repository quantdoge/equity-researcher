## Amer Sports, Inc. (NYSE: AS) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $30.24 | Mkt cap ~$17.6B | EV ~$17.0B (net cash $594M) | As of Q3'26 print, 17-Nov-2026*

### 1. Growth Story
Amer Sports is a **premium-brand platform consolidator** — Arc'teryx (technical apparel), Salomon (trail footwear/softgoods), and Wilson (racquet sports) — converting category-leading authenticity into a direct-to-consumer, China/APAC-led compounding machine. Revenue has re-accelerated every year since IPO (17.8% → 23.2% → 26.7% → ~25-26% guided FY26) while gross margin climbed **+1,020bps in five years to 59.3%**, the signature of a brand portfolio with genuine pricing power. The stock has de-rated ~28% from its Feb-2026 high ($42.76) purely on multiple contraction — estimates have gone *up* every quarter — creating the classic Wood-style disconnect between durable growth and sentiment.

### 3. TAM and Penetration
At ~$8.2B revenue, AS holds only **~4-5% of a ~$150-200B serviceable market** in premium outdoor apparel, athletic/trail footwear, and racquet sports. The three growth engines each have long runways:
- **Arc'teryx:** premium outdoor/apparel SAM ~$40-50B, growing high-single digits; still <$4B revenue, with China/APAC store count early-stage and a 16% omni-comp proving brand heat where it lands.
- **Salomon Softgoods:** the newest and largest incremental TAM — trail running and "sportstyle" footwear inside a ~$150B global footwear market. This is the 2027-2030 margin/scale story management just showcased at its Annecy investor day.
- **Wilson Tennis 360:** riding a multi-year global tennis participation boom; mid-to-high single-digit grower but the weakest margin link (6.1% adj op margin, **-210bps YoY**).

Long-term algorithm (5+ yrs off FY26 base): group revenue CAGR low-double-to-mid-teens, **+30-70bps adj op margin per year**, Tech Apparel and Outdoor Performance both **mid-teens CAGR**. That is a credible path to ~$16-17B revenue by 2031 at ~18% adjusted operating margins.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts
1. **$1B buyback / ~$200M executed before 31-Dec-2026** — first-ever capital return, signals FCF inflection; a mechanical bid under the stock into year-end.
2. **Q4'26 + FY2027 guidance, ~23-Feb-2027** (consensus $2.51B rev / $0.40 EPS) — a fifth consecutive beat-and-raise would break the de-rating narrative.
3. **Salomon softgoods ramp through 2027** — the freshest growth lever; watch for S/LAB and sportstyle sell-through and whether the Feb-2026 "investment cost" margin drag rolls off.
4. **Ball & Racquet margin recovery** — 210bps of segment margin erosion is the single easiest source of group upside; Wilson Tennis 360 scaling fixes it.
5. **Greater China + APAC store expansion** (+30% growth) — new Arc'teryx/Salomon doors and Tmall/DTC mix; LA28 Olympics (Wilson/baseball) is the multi-year brand catalyst.

### 5. Alternative Data Signals
- **Google Trends, "Arc'teryx" (US): a genuine warning.** Interest peaked ~93-94 in April 2026 and 87 in May, then collapsed to **12-25 from July through November**. Reported +26% growth is therefore being carried by China/APAC and DTC, not US brand heat. This is the one data point that argues against a Strong Buy.
- **Inventories +16% vs revenue +24%** — supportive; lean, not overloaded.
- **Job postings:** modest (~16 open Indeed roles; LinkedIn skewed to distribution/production, not growth hiring) — neutral, consistent with a company scaling retail rather than a hyper-growth land-grab.
- **Analyst price targets are falling while estimates rise** (Goldman $48→$43, list now clustered $38-43; Barclays high at $50, UBS cut from $62). Median PT **~$42.50 (+41%)**. Multiple compression, not fundamental deterioration.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, short of Strong Buy)
The core thesis is intact and getting better: **accelerating top line, 1,000bps of five-year gross margin expansion, net cash, a $1B buyback, a 100% beat rate, and estimates rising — against a stock down 28%.** At 22.7x forward earnings for ~23% EPS CAGR (PEG ~1.0) and 2.1x EV/Sales, the market is pricing in a meaningful deceleration that management's own multi-year algorithm does not support. I'd want Q4 confirmation plus stabilization in US Arc'teryx search interest to upgrade to Strong Buy; equally, a re-rating toward the $42-43 analyst cluster is the base case over 12 months. Position sizing should respect the China concentration.

### 7. Key Risks
- **China dependence (~28% of revenue, +30% growth):** Chinese consumer slowdown, anti-Western-brand sentiment, or FX/regulatory shifts would hit the fastest-growing and highest-margin region. This is the dominant risk and the reason the multiple sits where it does.
- **Multiple + consumer cyclicality:** beta 1.97; a premium outdoor category is discretionary and the 2026 de-rating shows how fast sentiment reverses.
- **Ball & Racquet profitability:** adj op margin 6.1% and falling — Wilson promotional intensity could offset group margin expansion.
- **Salomon investment costs:** Feb-2026 sold off precisely on this; brand-building spend may cap 2027 margin leverage.
- **Execution risk on DTC/retail:** rapid owned-store and e-commerce mix shift (>50% of sales) raises fixed-cost and inventory risk; capex is holding FCF margin at ~8-9%, keeping Rule of 40 below 40 until FY28.
- **Governance/insider overhang:** founder/PE-sponsor sell-downs (Aug-2026 -7.6% on insider liquidations); leadership turnover (new Arc'teryx CEO Haselden, Atomic president transition).
- **Tariffs and sourcing:** US tariffs on China/Vietnam-sourced footwear and apparel could compress gross margin faster than price increases can offset.
