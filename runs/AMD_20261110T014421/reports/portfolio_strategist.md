## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (directional agreement: LOW)

Two coherent, internally consistent camps — and the split is not about the business, it is about the price:

- **The business camp (Growth, Sector, Alt Data, Short-on-accounting, Fin Risk-credit):** revenue accelerating, margins expanding, accounting demonstrably clean, balance sheet a fortress, supply locked for 2027. Six of eleven agents independently corroborate this. **Whichever way we size AMD, we are not worried about fraud or solvency.**
- **The price camp (Value, Macro, Geo Legal, Quant-value/vol, Nonfin):** 109x TTM earnings, 0.85% FCF yield against a 5.13% risk-free rate, 22% revenue under a revocable licence, +$3.4B working-capital build, 78% vol, beta unstable, insiders selling with no buying.

**Conflict resolution.** Per framework, fraud/forensic risk takes precedence over value opinion — and the forensic assessment clears AMD unambiguously. That removes the one class of risk that would justify a hard pass. What remains is a pure **price-vs-expectations** debate, and it is decided by a single variable: whether AMD delivers FY27 revenue of ~$95B (a 2.1x step-up from the $45.4B TTM run-rate). Note the estimate quality: 31 analysts model 2027, only 10 model 2030, and 2027 EPS dispersion spans $10.83–$22.81 — a **2.1x range masquerading as consensus.** That is an unusually wide distribution for a $830B company, and it is the reason this must be sized as a *convex, thesis-contingent* position rather than a core compounder.

**Where the camps actually agree (high-confidence synthesis):** (1) nothing here is broken; (2) the multiple has no cushion; (3) gross margin is the scoreboard — every agent converges on GM as the thesis variable; (4) a 20–35% drawdown is routine, not a tail.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.0% initial, 2.5% hard cap** (target 1.5–2.0% on confirmation)

**Volatility-budget math first, because it dominates.** At 78.2% annualised vol, a 1.0% position contributes ~0.78% of portfolio volatility standalone; a 2.5% position contributes ~1.95%. In a portfolio running 12–14% vol, a 2.5% AMD position consumes ~15% of the entire risk budget from a single name. That is the binding constraint — not the conviction score.

| Beta reliability | 2.49 reported vs 3.60 regression (R² 0.445) | **Size on vol, never on beta** |
| Event risk | Jan 10 2027 truce expiry; Feb 2 FQ4 print | Stage entry |

**Kelly check.** Probability-weighted 12-month distribution I assign: 45% bull (+40%, FY27 delivered and Street extends), 35% base (−5%, FY27 slips one year), 20% bear (−30%, capex digestion or real-yield shock). Expected return ≈ **+10%**, σ ≈ 78% → raw Kelly f\* = μ/σ² ≈ 16%. Half-Kelly 8%. **Then apply a ~85% haircut for estimation error** (2.1x EPS dispersion, single-variable 2027 assumption, beta instability) and concentration constraints → **~1.2–2.0%.** Kelly says the bet has genuine positive edge; it does not say to take it large. **1.0% starter / 2.5% cap is the honest output.**

Distribution note for the PM: there is a **55% probability of a non-positive 12-month return** under my weights. This is a position you own for the right tail, sized so the left tail doesn't hurt.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Correlation (252d, verified):** QQQ **0.801** · MU 0.665 · SPY 0.664 · AVGO 0.610 · NVDA 0.517.

This is the report's least comfortable finding. **AMD's highest sector correlation is with Micron (0.665), not NVIDIA (0.517), and its highest overall correlation is with QQQ (0.801).** AMD is currently trading as a high-beta memory/cyclical + levered growth-index proxy — *not* as a clean AI-compute derivative. Consequences:

- **It does not diversify an AI-semi book.** If the portfolio already holds NVDA, AVGO or MU, adding AMD concentrates rather than diversifies. AMD+MU should be treated as **one risk bucket**.
- **It does not hedge a broad-market book** (SPY 0.664, QQQ 0.801). It is additive beta, not offsetting beta.
- **Total AI-capex complex cap: 15% of portfolio**, no single name above 5% (NVDA, AVGO, MU, TSM, AMD + AI-infrastructure-adjacent holdings). If the book is already at ~12%, this is a 1.0% addition, not a 2.5% one.
- **Funding:** if scaling above 1.0%, fund from MU or AVGO where overlap is highest — do not fund from cash.

**Factor exposure — this is the most important portfolio-level point.** AMD adds a *pure anti-factor* tilt:
- **Negative value** (4/4 worst in semis; 123x P/FCF)
- **Negative quality** (ROE 6.9% vs peer 28–76%; GP/Assets 0.223 vs 0.254–0.742)
- **Positive momentum** (top-ranked, but *maturing* — alpha decayed 80.1%→13.7%)
- **Negative low-vol** (5/5 worst; 78% vol)

Adding AMD tilts the portfolio **simultaneously toward high-beta and high-momentum — the most crowded trade in the market** (Quant's crowding flag). If the current book is quality/value-tilted, a 1.0% AMD position is an acceptable, deliberate barbell. If the book is already growth/momentum-heavy, **this is a pass on portfolio-fit grounds regardless of the fundamental case** — that is a firm condition, not a caveat.

**Sector concentration:** Semis are attractive (Sector Researcher) but the sector is a *single-theme* trade — nearly all incremental revenue traces to a handful of hyperscaler capex budgets. AMD carries 2–3x the sensitivity of that theme. Position accordingly.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Price stop-loss: hard exit on two consecutive closes below $395** (below the Nov 5 low of $402.10, ≈ −22% from entry).
With a 1.0% starter, a stop-out costs **−0.22% of portfolio** — tolerable. *Do not* set a tight stop: 78% vol means daily σ ≈ 4.9%; anything inside −15% is noise, not signal. **Do not attempt a beta hedge** — a 2.49-to-3.90 beta spread means any hedge ratio will mis-size by up to 50%.

**Thesis invalidation (exit regardless of price):**
1. **Any hyperscaler 2027 capex guidance cut or deferral** (MSFT/META/GOOGL/AMZN/ORCL). This is the primary sell signal for the whole complex per the Sector Researcher.
2. **FY27 consensus revenue revised below $90B** (from $95.4B) — the extrapolation breaking.
3. **Gross margin below 50% for two consecutive quarters** — pricing-collapse confirmation (Short Analyst's monitor; Value's thesis-breaker in reverse).
4. **Inventory days through 200, or cash conversion below 1.0x** (currently ~171 days, 1.78x). The Q3 OCF collapse to $1.20B on a $3.36B WC drag is the single most important new operating signal in the whole pack — **watch this before anything else.**
5. **MI308 licence revocation, or the 10 Jan 2027 truce lapsing into escalation** — trim 50% immediately; do not wait for the revenue to show up in the P&L.
6. **Formal BIS/DOJ diversion or sales-practices investigation naming AMD**, or a goodwill impairment ($40.6B, 59% of equity).

**Trim triggers (sell into strength — this is not a buy-and-hold):**
- **$640** (≈ sell-side median PT, +26%): trim one-third of the position.
- **$700–725** (top of the PT range): trim to half.

- **Time-based:** if by Q2 2027 the FY27 revenue trajectory is not tracking >$85B, cut to 0.5% or exit — pay for evidence, not for patience.

**Macro overlay trigger (trim, not exit):** 10Y nominal through 5.50% or 10Y real through 3.25% → reduce position by one-third. Per the Macro Researcher, a 100bp real-yield shock is worth 20–30% of fair value on a 2.49-beta, 16x-sales name, *with fundamentals intact*. This is a discount-rate trim, not a thesis break — and it should be executed before the price does it for you.

**Rebalance discipline:** quarterly back to target. Reassess Beta 2.49 vs 3.60 — if regression beta stays above 3.0, keep the cap at 1.5%, not 2.5%.
