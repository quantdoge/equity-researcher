# SPCX — SpaceX: Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $159.31 | Market cap ~$466B (2.93B diluted sh.) to ~$2.1T (fully diluted incl. Musk award/convertibles) | IPO 12-Jun-2026**

---

## 1. Growth Story

SpaceX is not an aerospace company — it is a **reusable-launch platform that has compounded into a global broadband utility**. Falcon 9/Heavy reset the cost curve for orbital access, and Starlink has now eclipsed launch as the primary revenue engine (~$11.8B), turning a project business into a subscription business with telecom economics. The next leg is Starship: full reusability collapses $/kg again, unlocking Starlink V3 capacity, direct-to-cell, Starshield national-security work, and — eventually — orbital AI/data infrastructure. Revenue is inflecting *now*, not in 2030: Q3'26 revenue of $14.58B was up 86% sequentially and 13% above consensus. This is the rare pre-profit franchise where the S-curve is visibly steepening.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Mid-December 2026 — full 180-day lockup expiry.** Tranches already rolled off Aug 21, Sep 10, Sep 25, Oct 10 (~912M shares freed). The drawdown from $225 to $105 is largely this supply overhang; clearing it is historically a bottoming event, but it is the single largest near-term risk.
2. **25-Feb-2027 — Q4/FY2026 results.** Consensus Q4 revenue $21.74B (vs Q3's $14.58B); first full-year print plus 2027 guidance — the key validation of the 147% growth step-up.
3. **Starship cadence & orbital refueling (2027).** Commercial payload ramp and NASA Artemis HLS milestones; each successful flight de-risks the $/kg step-down.
4. **Starlink V3 + direct-to-cell commercial launch (2027).** T-Mobile rollout converts a zero-revenue option into a subscription tier.
5. **Starshield / Golden Dome contract awards (2027 budget cycle).** Government revenue is the highest-margin, most durable layer of the model.
6. **S&P 500 index inclusion (2027)** — likely once seasoning and profitability criteria are met; mechanical demand catalyst.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **STRONG BUY** *(high-risk / long-duration capital only)*

The setup is textbook disruptive-innovation compounding: accelerating revenue, expanding gross margin, a 121% forward CAGR against a >$1.5T TAM, 100% beat rate, and a sell-side revision cycle that is still chasing the numbers. At $159.31 the stock is **29% below its post-IPO high**, and on 2028E it trades at **2.3x sales and 6.9x EV/EBITDA** on basic shares — cheap for a business growing triple digits. The moat (reusability cost curve, launch cadence, vertically integrated satellite manufacturing, spectrum) is not replicable on a five-year horizon.

**Valuation reality check:** trailing P/S is **25x on 2.93B diluted shares but ~112x on a fully diluted ~13.2B share count** — the reported $2.1T market cap. Against A&D peers at 17–27x EV/EBITDA (GE 26.8x, RTX 19.5x, LMT 16.7x), SpaceX's 108x trailing multiple is a 4–6x premium that **only** works if the 2026–28 ramp is delivered. This is a position to size at 1–3% of a growth portfolio, entered in tranches, not a core holding.

## 7. Key Risks

- **Estimate credibility.** A 121% three-year revenue CAGR on an $18.7B base is without precedent at this scale. The 2026E revenue of $44.1B implies ~$29B of incremental revenue in twelve months; Q3 run-rate ($58B annualized) supports it, but one Starship or Starlink V3 slip breaks the model.
- **Valuation/dilution ambiguity.** The gap between $466B and $2.1T market cap reflects a very large fully diluted share count (Musk performance award, 2024 diluted shares touched 9.96B). At full dilution, 2026E P/S is ~48x — a multiple that requires perfection. Further SBC ($1.95B in 2025) and lockup supply are real overhangs.
- **Cash burn.** FCF was **−$13.95B** in 2025 on $20.7B capex; cumulative 2024–25 burn ~$19.3B. Financing risk rises if capital markets tighten or Starship cadence disappoints. Note the GAAP net loss (−$4.94B) versus press reports of ~$8B "profit" — adjusted EBITDA excludes heavy Starship development and depreciation; the gap matters.
- **Concentration/key-person.** Elon Musk's dual role across Tesla, xAI and political exposure introduces governance and headline risk; the CEO is the strategy.
- **Competition.** Amazon Kuiper, Blue Origin/New Glenn, and Chinese launch providers are scaling; subsidized national champions could compress Starlink pricing in international markets.
- **Regulatory/spectrum.** FCC, ITU, and national-security approvals gate direct-to-cell and Starshield revenue; geopolitical restrictions can remove entire countries.
- **Post-lockup supply.** With ~912M+ shares already freed and the full unlock in December 2026, expect continued volatility; the $104.83 August low is the reference downside level.

---

**Bottom line:** SpaceX is the highest-quality exponential-growth asset in public markets and the clearest "innovation moat" available to growth investors. The data supports buying the post-IPO drawdown — but only with strict position sizing and tolerance for 40–50% drawdowns. The 25-Feb-2027 print and the December lockup clearing are the two events that will determine whether the next leg is up.
