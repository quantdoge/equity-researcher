## 4. Key Quant Risk Flags

1. **Sample size — severe.** Only ~88 trading days since the 2026-06-12 IPO. Every "252-day" statistic (vol, beta, alpha, Sharpe, momentum) is computed on ~4 months and is **not statistically reliable** — alpha's t-stat and beta's standard error are wide. Treat all annualized figures as provisional; true 12-month factor behavior is unknown.
2. **Extreme valuation / factor crowding into growth.** EV/EBITDA 108x, P/B 11.3x, negative P/E and P/FCF — bottom of sector. Any de-rating risk is high.
3. **Momentum reversal risk.** +9% "momentum" masks a violent post-IPO boom-bust (peak $225.64 → $107.01). Momentum readings on new listings are unstable and prone to whipsaw.
4. **Leverage/quality gaps.** Loss-making (net income −$4.94B in FY2025), ROIC −4.03%, F-score 5/9 (moderate). Operating cash flow positive ($6.79B) is the one quality bright spot; accruals ratio −12.7% is genuinely favorable.
5. **Revision signal is distant-horizon.** The +170% revision is for FY2028 — not a near-term catalyst; near-term estimates unavailable in the feed.
6. **Market-cap data conflict.** Profile shows ~$2.10T (implying ~13.2B shares) vs valuation ratios' ~$466B (2.926B diluted shares × $159.31). Diligence the share count — valuation multiples depend on it.
7. **Post-IPO lockup overhang.** Recent listing; scheduled lockup expiry could pressure the float. Liquidity is adequate (ADV in tens of millions of shares) so liquidity is not a primary flag.
8. **Peer comparability imperfect.** No pure-play publicly listed peer; A&D comparables (GE, RTX, BA, LMT) are legacy defense/aero businesses — valuation and quality ranks should be read directionally, not precisely.
