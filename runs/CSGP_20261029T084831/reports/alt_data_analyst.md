## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **Tracking BELOW consensus**

The most important alt-data read is the **net new bookings collapse**: **$37M vs $69M in Q2 and $84M a year ago (-56% YoY)**, blamed on "economic headwinds." Bookings are the leading indicator for a subscription business; revenue lags bookings by 4–8 quarters.

Triangulated with the alt data:
- Traffic down 5–12% MoM across all portals and search interest down YoY → **no volume tailwind to offset a bookings slowdown**.
- Q4 guidance of $976–996M implies ~10% growth vs 14% in Q3 and 18% in Q2 — management has already begun guiding the deceleration. Given $37M of annualized bookings, **FY2027 revenue consensus is at risk**; I'd expect high-single-digit or below.
- **EPS is a different story.** 7/7 beats, +20.3% avg surprise, and $350M+ of cumulative cost actions make a *further EPS beat on 23-Feb-2027 (consensus $0.49) plausible*. But the surprise magnitude is compressing (32% → 13.6% → 12.8% → 12.0%), and the beat is now cost-driven rather than demand-driven. Quality of beat is deteriorating.

**Net: revenue/booking tracking below; EPS likely in-line-to-above but low quality.**

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH**

Weight of evidence:
1. **Bookings -56% YoY** — the single cleanest leading indicator CoStar discloses (High conviction).
2. **Traffic down 5–12% MoM at all three portals; search interest down YoY at all three** — demand-side confirmation (Medium-High).
3. **Homes.com growth is price-driven, not audience-driven** — fragile; a 267-rep sales floor (from 655) plus a 58% ASP increase is exactly how you trade long-term share for near-term EBITDA.
4. **Cost-cut-driven EPS beats are masking a decelerating top line**, which is why a "beat-and-raise" produced -14%.
5. Offsetting bull case (why not outright short-signal): 100% beat history, $200M+ incremental savings identified, residential EBITDA inflecting positive, LoopNet +13%, CoStar product +8%. The margin story is real — it's just not a growth story, and at 14x sales-implied multiples the market had been paying for growth.

**Asymmetry:** alt data argues estimate cuts to 2027 revenue/bookings, further multiple compression, and a likely "beat the EPS, guide revenue down" pattern repeating on 23-Feb-2027. Any re-rating requires evidence of booking re-acceleration — which today's traffic and search data do not support.

## 7. Confidence Level: **Medium**

**Reasons for only Medium:**
- Web traffic figures are SimilarWeb *search-snippet* MoM percentages (-12.4%, -10.7%, -5.5%), not a verified monthly series — directionally reliable, magnitude not.
- Job-posting and social data are search proxies with no historical time series, so I can state level and composition but not a quantified trend.
- **Patent filing trend unavailable** (USPTO feed returned empty payload twice) — a genuine gap in the business-activity leg.
- Google Trends for "Homes.com"/"Apartments.com" conflates consumer intent with brand-name news/marketing noise and is seasonally distorted (spring leasing peak); single-week prints are noisy (CoStar's 65 print in Sept-26 is an outlier).
- Nothing in the alt data captures enterprise churn or renewal cohorts — the very thing that will determine whether $37M of bookings is a trough or a trend.

**Data lag note:** All signals are 14-day-to-3-week stale relative to today; alt data is leading but imprecise. The bookings figure is company-reported (Q3, ended 30-Sep-26), not independent.
