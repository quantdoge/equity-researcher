**Investment Thesis**
CoStar is a genuinely superior CRE data franchise (75% gross margins, ~43% commercial segment EBITDA margins, 90%+ retention) whose consolidated economics have been diluted by a $3B+ Homes.com bet and $5.4B of cycle-peak M&A. The margin inflection is real — Q3-26 adjusted EBITDA doubled to $229M and residential swung positive — but it is being purchased with a 56% collapse in net new bookings ($37M vs $952M revenue) and a 59% cut in the Homes.com salesforce, meaning the recovery is cost-financed, not demand-led. At 14.5x FY26E EBITDA with only a ~21% discount to intrinsic value, the market already pays for most of the repair, while the two risks that matter most — antitrust forced data-portability (MDL 3192) and AI erosion of the data moat — sit outside every valuation model in this stack.
