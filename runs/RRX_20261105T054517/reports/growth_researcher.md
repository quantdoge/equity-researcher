# Regal Rexnord (RRX) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $173.38 | Mkt Cap $11.5B | EV $16.1B | 52-wk range ~$127–$247 (-27% from June peak)**

---

## 1. Growth Story

RRX is a transformed industrial motion-and-power franchise that has spent three years digesting the $5B Altra acquisition and is now emerging with **structurally higher gross margins (28% → 37% since 2021)**, falling leverage (2.9x), and — critically — a brand-new growth S-curve in **data center power**. The company entered the e-Pod modular power module market in early 2025 and has already booked **~$735M of e-Pod orders** that begin shipping in early 2027, a defined path to **$1B of data center sales within two years** (from a base where data center was a rounding error). This is not a hyper-growth innovation platform — it is a **margin-expansion + deleveraging compounder with a data center call option priced at a cyclical multiple** (~11.7x forward adj. EBITDA vs. Eaton 23.9x, Parker 21.1x).

Management expects **1–1.5 points of 2026 sales from e-Pod alone**, with the real conversion landing in 2027. Backlog is **up ~50% YoY** — the clearest forward signal in the entire story.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4 2026 earnings (~Feb 3, 2027)** — first clean FY2027 guide, the year e-Pod ships in volume. Highest-impact near-term event.
- **e-Pod order-to-revenue conversion** — $735M backlog converting largely through 2027; watch for quarterly order announcements and whether the $1B data center target gets *raised*.
- **Portfolio simplification / divestitures** — Industrial Motors & Generators being sold for ~$400M; remaining Industrial Systems businesses under strategic review. Proceeds → debt paydown below 2.5x, unlocking buybacks (zero repurchases in 2025 is a coiled spring).
- **Tariff (IEEPA) normalization** — FY26 adj. EBITDA margin guidance cut to 21.3% *excluding* tariff refunds. Any resolution is pure upside to a beaten-down margin narrative.
- **Automation & Motion Control momentum** — 24.5% growth and 13% order growth ex-data-center suggests the short-cycle industrial trough is behind.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (moderate conviction — GARP, not a compounder)

**Why Buy, not Strong Buy:** by any strict disruption standard, RRX **fails the growth screen**. Rule of 40 of 13.4 is far below 40; organic revenue is negative over two years; GAAP earnings are thin (FY25 GAAP EPS $4.22 vs. $9.61 adjusted — a **$5.39/share gap** from Altra amortization); and Q3 2026 posted a **GAAP net loss of $12M despite an adjusted beat**. The core ~70% of the business is a short-cycle industrial that grows with GDP.

**Why still Buy:** the risk/reward has flipped. At $173 you pay **13.7x FY27E adjusted EPS and 12.9x FCF** for a business with:
1. A **quantified, backlogged** new growth engine ($735M e-Pod orders → 2027 revenue), 
2. **900bps of gross margin expansion** already proven,
3. Leverage cascading from 2.9x → ~2.2x with $400M of divestiture proceeds and zero buybacks so far,
4. Order growth (+13% ex-DC) that contradicts the price action.

**Fair value: $195–215** (17–19x FY27E adj. EPS of $12.68, still a discount to Parker/Eaton). That's ~12–24% upside, and the catalyst path is unusually well-dated. The re-rating — not the growth rate — is the bet.

## 7. Key Risks

- **Tariff/margin execution (highest probability):** EBITDA margin guidance already cut to 21.3%. If tariffs bite and e-Pod carries structurally lower margins as a new entrant, the "margin expansion" thesis stalls and the multiple stays stuck in the low teens.
- **Data center competitive threat:** e-Pod competes against **Eaton, Vertiv, Schneider, ABB, Delta** — entrenched incumbents with hyperscaler relationships and scale. RRX entered in 2025. This is a genuine innovation-moat risk: no proprietary lock-in yet.
- **Adjusted-vs-GAAP illusion:** $500M of D&A (mostly acquisition amortization) and $349M of interest expense mean reported earnings are a fraction of "adjusted." A skeptical market can punish this indefinitely — it has for six months.
- **Leverage:** $4.6B net debt at 2.9x with $349M interest expense consumes ~25% of EBITDA. Rate-sensitive; constrains buybacks if divestitures slip.
- **Cyclicality:** ~70% of revenue is short-cycle industrial. A macro rollover would overwhelm the e-Pod ramp in 2027.
- **Thin forward coverage:** 1 analyst estimates 2030. Consensus visibility degrades materially past 2028 — 5-year DCF inputs are effectively unverifiable.
- **Sentiment/technical:** the stock is in a downtrend, making lower highs since June. Two consecutive post-earnings gaps down show sellers control the tape; don't expect an immediate re-rate before the February 2027 guide.

---

**Bottom line:** RRX is a real-world growth story masquerading as a value trap — a $735M backlog in a brand-new data center franchise sitting inside a 12.9x P/FCF industrial. I'd be a buyer at $173 with a 12–18 month horizon, sized as a **quality-GARP position, not a Cathie Wood core disruptive holding**. The February 2027 guidance is the proof point: if FY27 revenue prints with a $7B+ handle and e-Pod shipments begin on schedule, this re-rates toward $210+.
