### 2. Business Activity Signals
- **Job postings: Stable-to-expanding, low-resolution.** Employer-specific listings skew toward **go-to-market and federal expansion** — Federal Account Manager (McLean, VA, **$202K–$302K**), Commercial Account Manager (Bethesda, MD, **$254K–$380K**), Regional Enterprise Sales Director, Corporate Strategy Manager. High-comp public-sector quota roles signal continued confidence in federal/regulatory-driven displacement demand. Caveat: LinkedIn "9,000+" and Indeed "3,942" counts are **keyword matches** (jobs *requiring* Nutanix skills at other firms), not Nutanix headcount itself — not usable as a growth rate. No time series available. Headcount 8,350 per profile.
- **Patents: DATA GAP.** USPTO PatentsView feed failed twice (API/parse error). R&D intensity trend is **unmeasurable** for this name. Note this removes our cleanest forward R&D signal.
- **Bookings/backlog (third-party channel analysis):** The most important business-activity read is **negative**: independent analysis characterizes the Q1 beat as **drawn from backlog while backlog is being spent faster than refilled**, with **declining bookings and fewer new customers**, putting FY28 growth expectations at risk. Management *reiterated* (did not raise) the annual revenue forecast despite the 15% ARR growth and a 15% EPS beat. Direction: **Deteriorating.**

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **In-Line on the headline, BELOW on quality**
- **Statistics favor another beat:** 7/7 beat rate (100%), avg surprise **+17.9%**, and the last four quarters accelerated (19.9%, 22.5%, 32.1%, 27.3%). Q2 FY27 consensus is $0.65 EPS / $827.7M revenue (+14.5% YoY).
- **Alt data contradicts the beat quality:** revenue upside funded by backlog conversion, not new bookings; new-customer adds down; search interest −25%; guidance unchanged. The gap between "beat rate 100%" and "bookings declining" is the single most important alt-data inference in this report — **the beat pattern is decelerating in substance even as the optics hold.**
- Net: model a likely mechanical EPS beat, but revenue guidance risk skewed to the downside into FY28.

### 4. News Sentiment Score and Summary
- **Score +0.714 (20 articles, 30d; 12 positive / 2 negative) — headline "positive," but treat as UNRELIABLE.** The keyword classifier mislabels articles (e.g., "Nutanix stock drops after earnings beat and CEO succession news" scored positive).
- **Actual narrative is bifurcated:** fundamentals positive (record margin, 15% ARR growth, raised profit outlook, "top-ranked growth stock" framing, "3 key takeaways for long-term investors"); catalyst negative (**surprise CEO exit**, unchanged FY sales guidance, "temporary dip in bookings," PT cuts, "spending its backlog faster than it refills it," "worst day in over a year"). Several analysts explicitly are **not** panicking on the CEO exit — a genuine bull-bear debate rather than one-sided negativity.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH** (near-term)
Weighting: bookings/backlog deterioration (highest conviction, fundamental) > search-interest decay (−25%, medium) > event-driven negative catalyst with PT cuts > partially offset by federal GTM hiring, record margins, and an unbroken 100% beat record.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Data quality is mixed: two feeds returned nothing (patents, social/forum-level sentiment), two are search proxies without time series (web traffic, job postings), and the news-sentiment classifier is demonstrably mislabeling. The bookings/backlog read is the load-bearing signal and it comes from third-party interpretation of call commentary, not from our own primary data.

**Data limitations & lag**
- Price feed returned an offset 2025 window alongside the 2026 window; analysis uses the 2026 series. Data as-of Nov 25, 2026 close.
- Google Trends ends **Nov 15, 2026** — the Nov 24 earnings/search spike is **not** in the series; expect an artificial rebound next print.
- "VMware alternative" search proxy was **unusable** (intermittent zero-fill), so the VMware-displacement demand thesis could **not** be validated with alt data.
- Patent filings unavailable (2 attempts); social sentiment unavailable at thread level (1 attempt).
- Search interest for a B2B infrastructure vendor is a **noisy proxy** for enterprise procurement — it tracks news flow more than pipeline. Do not treat the −25% as a revenue forecast.
- Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. The stock is already −44% from its $82.42 52-week high, so a meaningful portion of the negative signal may be priced in; the bull case now rests on a credible CEO successor and a bookings re-acceleration that alt data **cannot yet confirm**.
