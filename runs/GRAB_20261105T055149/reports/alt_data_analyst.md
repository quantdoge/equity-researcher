- 9M26 actual revenue = $955M + $997M + $1,112M = **$3,064M** → implied Q4 revenue = **$1,086–1,136M** (midpoint $1,111M).
- **Consensus Q4 revenue sits at $1,149M** — i.e. **~1.1% above the top end** and **~5.8% above the midpoint** of the company's own raised guidance.
- Precedent: Q3 consensus revenue was **$1,156M** vs actual **$1,112M (−3.8% miss)**. Consensus has been running ~4% too hot on revenue for two consecutive quarters.

**Read:** Q4 revenue likely prints **below consensus** unless there is a late-December promotional surge. Implied Q4 growth is **+20% to +25% YoY** vs Q3's **+27%** — a 2–7pt deceleration that the flat October demand signals corroborate. EBITDA is more likely to be **in-line/above**, given management's credible record and the record $237M Q3 EBITDA (+73% YoY) with all three segments profitable for the first time.

**Surprise history:** beat rate **71.4%** (5/7), average magnitude **+33.4%** — but the streak **broke in Q3'26**: EPS **$0.0066 vs $0.02 est (−67%)** plus the revenue miss. Surprise momentum has turned negative.

**Tactical conclusion:** the mispricing signal is now ~60–70% closed. The alt-data set supports holding/accumulating on pullbacks rather than chasing $3.36, with the specific, non-consensus risk being a **Q4 revenue miss against a stale $1.149B consensus** even as EBITDA beats. Watch Indonesia search interest as the key leading indicator — a recovery there is the next leg's precondition. Secondary catalysts/risks: **GoTo merger speculation** (GoTo CEO Patrick Walujo's resignation) and the durability of the 8% commission cap.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why medium, not high:**
- Google Trends is a **news-confounded** proxy for this company — earnings coverage, regulatory headlines and driver strikes all move the series and can't be cleanly separated from organic demand. Two of my "downtrend" readings (ID 91 in Aug, VN 100 in Sep) are almost certainly event-driven base effects.
- **Three material gaps:** no app-download/ranking time series (the best consumer-demand metric for a super-app), no quantifiable job-posting trend, no patent data (API failure). My business-activity read is effectively unevidenced and I have flagged it as such rather than filling it in.
- **No in-quarter data at all for Q4 yet** — Google Trends through Oct 25 is the only forward-looking demand input, and Q4 is ~60% of the way through with the crucial holiday/12.12 promotional period still ahead. 12.12 and Ramadan timing will dominate Q4/Q1 seasonality in ways this dataset cannot see.
- News/social sentiment is backward-looking and fully priced after a 24% three-day rally.
- Mitigating strength: the Q3 results and guidance are hard, reported facts, and the consensus-vs-guidance revenue arithmetic is precise and independently verifiable — that is my highest-confidence single finding.
