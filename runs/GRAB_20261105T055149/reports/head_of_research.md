**Verdict**: HOLD — speculative accumulate only as a small, staged position | **Conviction**: Medium-Low

**Investment Thesis**
Grab is a genuinely inflecting business — Q3 2026 delivered +27% revenue, record adjusted EBITDA of $237m (+73%), all three segments profitable for the first time, and raised FY26 guidance — wrapped in a low-quality earnings structure and a regulator-capped take rate. The market has already punished it (−48% from the Sept-2025 high) for risks that are real: Indonesia's 20%→8% commission cap is a regional template, ~49% of TTM net income is non-operating, SBC ≈ 90% of net income, and the CEO now holds ~74.9% of votes on ~4% of economics. At $3.36 the risk/reward is balanced, not compelling: the operating inflection and fortress balance sheet earn a small position; the earnings-quality gap, regulatory ceiling, and governance entrenchment deny it a large one.

**Bull Case**
1. **The inflection is real and GAAP-measurable**: operating income −$54m (FY24) → +$299m (TTM), all segments profitable, guidance raised, share at 55% and gaining — all achieved *after* absorbing the 8% cap, undercutting the "structurally impaired" narrative.
2. **Fortress balance sheet plus alignment**: ~$3–4bn net cash, BB upgrade, $500m buyback remaining, and a founder who put $30m of personal capital in at the lows.
3. **Coiled multiple**: ~6x FY28E EBITDA with a double-barrelled macro unwind available — oil −21% off peak and the 10Y off its high; a ~60bp discount-rate retracement is worth ~20–30% on this duration.

**Bear Case**
1. **Regulators set the take rate**: the 8% cap is a template, four-wheel extension is a live −10% FY27 EBITDA tail, and both consolidation (GoTo) and bolt-ons (foodpanda) are legally foreclosed — the external-growth leg is impaired.
2. **Earnings quality**: adjusted EBITDA is ~2.7x GAAP operating income; cash conversion is 43% and deteriorating; incremental growth is increasingly debt-funded consumer credit provisioned at *falling* coverage.
3. **Governance + macro discount is permanent**: 74.9% voting control removes any activist or takeover path, while a 5.13% 10Y, strong USD, and record-low IDR keep the multiple capped regardless of execution.
