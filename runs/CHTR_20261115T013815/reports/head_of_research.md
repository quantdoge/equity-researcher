**Investment Thesis**

Charter is a genuinely cheap, genuinely broken, genuinely levered asset. The core broadband franchise is in structural decline — accelerating subscriber losses, falling ARPU, negative organic revenue — and carries ~$104B of net debt against a ~$20–22B market cap, making the equity a levered call option on stabilization. But the bear case is now priced: at ~4.8–5.4x EV/EBITDA with a ~23% FCF yield, the market capitalizes permanent decline, while three dated, verifiable catalysts (capex falling to ≤$11B in 2027, $2B opex cuts plus $1B+ Cox synergies, deleveraging toward 3.5x) offer a credible path to FCF nearly doubling. The discount is wide enough to buy a small piece; the leverage is high enough that the piece must be small.

**Bull Case**
- **Deep value with dated catalysts**: ~4.8x EV/EBITDA, ~23% FCF yield; capex ≤$11B in 2027 + $2–3B cost-out takes PF FCF toward $9–11B — ~7–9% of net debt retired annually and a violent re-rating off a ~$21B cap.
- **Asymmetric rate optionality**: a 90bp drop in the 10Y (5.13% → ~4.25%) historically delivers a 0.7–1.0x cable multiple re-rating — worth +85–120% on this levered stub. HY spreads at 320bp keep refi access open.
- **Positioning washed out**: −76% peak-to-trough, then +35% off the low on rising volume; sector-best margins, mobile lines +15%, and zero geopolitical exposure.

**Bear Case**
- **Structural decline, accelerating**: pro forma internet −233k in Q3'26 (vs −171k a year ago), revenue −1.5%, EBITDA −4%, residential ARPU −1.8%. FWA and fiber are permanent share-takers; the $6.33B impairment is management's own admission.
- **Leverage into decline**: 3.93x, FCF/interest 1.2x, tangible equity −$77B. The bear case is not a drawdown — scenario value $0–40.
- **Governance/execution**: interim CFO through the largest integration in company history; $35B of buybacks at $300–500 average prices; a second impairment on the $97.6B goodwill/intangible balance is plausible.

**Key Risks**
CFO vacancy unresolved past ~Apr-2027 (control-environment failure); second franchise impairment; 2027 capex guided above $13B (kills the FCF thesis); leverage >4.25x (S&P downgrade trigger); ACP clawback or new securities filings; state rate regulation spreading beyond NY/CA; DSO >27 days (revenue quality); 10Y sustained above 5%.
