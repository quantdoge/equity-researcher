## CHTR — Alternative Data Signal Report
*As of Nov 13, 2026 (last close $138.39; mkt cap $18.7B)*

**Context that frames every signal below:** CHTR is down ~68% from its May-2025 high ($427) and ~32% YoY. Apr-24-2026 (−25% on 13.3M shares) and Jul-25-2025 (−18%) were distribution events. Q3'26 (Nov 6) showed a **GAAP EPS of −$20.50 vs $8.94 est** — a $6.33B franchise impairment — alongside **−233k internet subscribers**, pro-forma revenue −1.5% YoY, and a $2B cost-cut program tied to Cox integration (11M passings / 5M customers added).

**Interpretation:** The most useful signal here is *relative*, not absolute. Spectrum's branded search interest held roughly flat through Sep–Nov while Comcast's Xfinity search interest collapsed ~80% — an unusual divergence that suggests Charter is capturing a larger share of broadband "shopper" intent right now. However, the high bounce rate and shallow pages-per-visit confirm these are **price-shopping, churn-prone visitors**, not high-intent gross adds. Critically, this alt signal **does not corroborate a broadband turnaround**: search interest is stable, not inflecting, which is consistent with continued (if perhaps moderating) subscriber losses rather than net adds.

Read: **steady hiring** in field/network functions supporting Cox integration — no evidence of a hiring freeze despite the $2B cost program. Direction: flat.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **In-Line to Below**
Next print: **Jan 29, 2027**, EPS est. $9.51 (vs $10.34 prior-year actual, −8% YoY). Historical beat rate is **28.6% (2 of 7)**, but the average is distorted by the Q3'26 GAAP impairment. Ex-charge, CHTR is roughly a coin-flip printer that misses slightly more often than it beats. Current alt data — flat brand search, unresolved subscriber bleed, no measurable demand acceleration — points to **no upside surprise in subscriber/revenue lines**; the $2B cost program is the only credible EPS offset and is already in consensus. Verdict: **In-Line to modestly Below.**

### 5. Social Sentiment (Reddit/WSB proxy): **Contrarian bullish, low quality**
Tone has shifted from despair to valuation debate: *"priced for bankruptcy"* ($19B cap vs $100B+ debt), *"one of the cheapest multiples of all US stocks,"* *"potential rerate," "something weird is going on with $CHTR."* This is classic post-capitulation value-chatter — supportive at the margin, but retail narrative, not a fundamental leading indicator.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **Neutral** (with a modest constructive tactical tilt)
The leading indicators split cleanly. **Supportive:** favourable Spectrum-vs-Xfinity share-of-search shift, stable web traffic, active deleveraging, washed-out sentiment and positioning, and a sharp post-earnings reversal. **Against:** the primary demand variable — subscriber growth — is still materially negative, search interest is flat rather than improving, and legal/impairment overhangs are unresolved. Alt data argues the *rate of deterioration* may be slowing; it does not yet argue for a turn.

### 7. Confidence Level: **Low-to-Medium**
Every consumer/business feed here is a **web-search proxy, not a measured time series** — none has actual month-over-month counts. The patent feed failed outright. Google Trends keyword matching is noisy ("Spectrum" is a generic term; "Xfinity internet" may reflect Comcast-specific events rather than category demand). SimilarWeb traffic figures are unverified snippet estimates with ambiguous column attribution. Reported EPS data mixes GAAP impairment with adjusted consensus, and earnings calendar dates are irregular. **Treat this as a directional, leading-indicator overlay — not a substitute for the reported subscriber and FCF numbers.**
