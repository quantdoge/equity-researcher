# Investment Memo: LIME — August 23, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $47 (+15% upside from $41.06)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Lime (Neutron Holdings, Inc.) is the dominant global shared-micromobility operator — ~27% category share, ~230 cities/29 countries, real operating profitability (2025 adj. EBITDA $218M, FY26 guide $265–285M; 2025 FCF +$104M) and ~30% revenue CAGR — whose IPO eliminated a pre-listing ~$846M debt wall and converted ~$700M of converts to equity. That quality is real, but the stock has re-rated +64% from its $25 IPO in seven weeks, sits above the $39.4 consensus price target, and on owner-earnings DCF is only fair-to-slightly-rich ($39–60 intrinsic range vs $41). We rate **HOLD**: a legitimate operator, but no margin of safety at $41, a 7-week statistical track record, and a December-2026 lockup overhang — buyers should wait for a pullback toward $33–38 rather than chase the tape.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Cooling-but-positive US regime (Q2'26 GDP +1.5%, Fed on hold at 3.50–3.75%, 10Y 4.55%, CPI +3.4%) is a *negative* for a capex-heavy, discretionary-demand business via financing cost, but the pre-IPO refinancing wall is now gone (debt cleared at listing; $200M undrawn revolver). Sector is post-shakeout and consolidating around Lime as the only scale operator after Bird's Chapter 11 (Dec 2023) and Helbiz's exit; permit-gated markets give a narrow but contestable moat. (macro_researcher, sector_researcher)

Hardware sourced from China/Cambodia/Vietnam against active Section 301 tariffs; recurring rider-injury/product-liability docket plus an $8.5M misclassification settlement; post-IPO securities-class-action and a disclosed internal-controls **material weakness** are watch items. **Bull Case**
- Category leader with ~27% share (37% US) and ~30% organic revenue CAGR now at the profitability inflection (op. income +$70.4M 2025; FCF +$104M; FY26 guide $265–285M EBITDA) — the rare "survivor of the shakeout."
- Balance sheet reset at IPO removes the single biggest bear point (~$846M debt wall → no long-term debt, $200M revolver, ~$339M cash); recurring subscription mix (28% and rising) + Uber distribution (~14% of revenue, near-zero CAC) build real switching costs.
- On adj-EBITDA basis the stock is not stretched: EV/EBITDA ~12x (2025) / ~9.6x (2026E) vs ~30% growth (Rule of 40 ≈ 41); exit-multiple check at 15x FY26 EBITDA → ~$63/share.

**Bear Case**
- No margin of safety: owner-earnings DCF (base FCF $103.8M, 11.5% WACC) → ~$47/share; at 13% WACC → $39 (−5% vs spot). The +64% post-IPO run has priced in execution; consensus PT ($39.4) is *below* the current quote.
- GAAP economics are unproven — net loss −$59.3M (2025), depreciation switched to a friendlier straight-line method, Q2'26 operating profit −69% and FCF negative; "profitability" rests on a non-GAAP metric that deletes the business's single largest real cost (fleet wear).
- Structural permit risk is existential — Paris/Madrid/Prague precedents can erase revenue city-by-city; plus China-linked tariff exposure, rider-injury litigation tail, a disclosed internal-controls material weakness, and a ~Dec-2026 lockup/unlock float overhang.

**Key Risks**
1. **Permit/regulatory revocation** (highest) — single-city decisions destroy fleet value overnight; NYC UL certification is now a fleet-wide operational gate.
2. **Uber concentration** — 21.9% owner + board seat + 14.3% revenue; a renegotiation or integration exit is a double-sided risk (and releases Uber's staggered lockup).
3. **GAAP-vs-adjusted gap & depreciation policy** — straight-line 5-yr fleet life may understate true replacement cost; adjusted metrics overstate owner earnings.
4. **Tariff/supply-chain** — China/Cambodia/Vietnam hardware under active Section 301 tariffs; battery/rare-earth concentration.
5. **Data scarcity** — only 37 trading days and *no* FMP financial statements; beta/Sharpe/Altman-Z/Beneish/ROIC/runway are uncomputable or statistically meaningless, so the risk picture is materially incomplete.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
Treat as a **satellite/tactical** holding, not core: cap at **1.0–1.5% of NAV** (liquidity is the binding constraint — ~350k sh/day ≈ $8–16M/day traded, and ~$10M ≈ a full day's float). It adds **factor crowding, not diversification** (small-cap growth/consumer-cyclical, overlapping Lyft/Uber) and is effectively market-uncorrelated in its short life. Conviction framework should weight **risk/short 30%**, quant 20%, growth 15%, value 15%, macro 10%, with the short/risk signals carrying veto power. (portfolio_strategist)
