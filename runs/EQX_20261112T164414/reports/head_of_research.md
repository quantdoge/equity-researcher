### Investment Memo: EQX — November 12, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — with a Reduce/Underweight bias (do not add; new money waits for defined triggers) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: **$11.00** (~6% upside from current $10.39) — probability-weighted; scenario range $8.25–$16.50
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
EQX is a transformed ~1.1Moz gold producer with a genuinely repaired balance sheet (net debt $1.4B → net cash, $1.2B liquidity) and record revenue and EBITDA — but it remains a high-cost (AISC ~$2,000–2,100/oz vs. ~$1,785 industry average), high-beta (2.2–2.5 vs. SPY, 1.15 vs. GDX) gold proxy whose shareholders have captured essentially none of the greatest gold bull market in history: operating cash flow per share went from $1.13 (2021) to $1.11 (2025) while gold rose 89%, with five consecutive negative-FCF years and +310% dilution. At $10.39 the market is paying fair value for ~$4,000 gold with zero margin of safety, into a hawkish Fed and a negatively-skewed Feb-17 print, while ~15–33% of output sits in sanctions-exposed Nicaragua. There is no fraud and no solvency risk — but also no stock-specific edge (1-yr alpha vs. GDX −16.8%, R² 0.85).

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector rated Attractive (record AISC margins, 12% earnings yield, cheapest vs. S&P in 20 years) but late-cycle — prefer low-cost majors over EQX within it. Macro Neutral with negative skew: 10Y TIPS at a 2.66% cycle high, a 78%-priced Dec-9 hike, gold −27% from peak at ~$4,013, WTI ~$100 (+43%) inflating AISC. Every *marginal* macro variable is moving against the stock; BRL/MXN weakness partially cushions USD unit costs.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH**, improving. Nicaragua (15–18% of revenue; live OFAC escalation, state seizure precedent) plus Mexico (Los Filos 18-month suspension, permit freeze; June-26 land agreements signed) put roughly a third of revenue in jurisdictions where the state, not courts, determines outcomes. A Brazilian court blocking the CMOC mineral-rights transfer threatens the $115M contingent payment due Jan-2027.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Moat **NONE** — a price-taker with no cost advantage. Critical correction: post-Orla share count is ~1.16–1.25B, so market cap is **~$12.1B, not the stale $6.5B** in vendor screens; the "cheap" headline multiples were a share-count artifact. Corrected EV/EBITDA ≈ 8x LTM and 5.7x 2027E — but ~10.5x on mid-cycle ($3,500) gold, i.e. a *premium* to its own history once earnings normalize.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **HOLD**. +97% TTM revenue is acquisition-financed: revenue/share +19% against +130% share count in six quarters; Rule of 40 = 22; gross margin compressing 50.9% → 39.2% → 33.7% sequentially; consensus itself models EPS peaking in 2028. Upgrade trigger: two quarters of per-ounce margin expansion with flat share count; downgrade: Q4 <870koz or AISC >$1,800.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **SELL** — worst-in-cohort value (5/100) and quality (5/100), ROIC 6.0% vs. ~9% WACC (economic value destruction), 65% vol, raw 12-1 momentum −21%, and near-zero two-year alpha vs. GDX: a levered beta certificate, not a mispricing.
- **Financial Risk**: **MODERATE**. Credit risk genuinely low — net cash, ~4x interest coverage through a −20% gold shock, no maturity wall. But market risk is high (−54% drawdown Feb→Jul 2026), FCF quality poor (~1% yield; $640–700M/yr capex consumes nearly all operating cash flow), and working capital deteriorating (current ratio 0.93). The real break point is gold ~$2,450, not the balance sheet at current prices.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH**. Two mergers plus CEO *and* Chair turnover inside 13 months (the ex-Orla team now runs the company — a de facto reverse takeover of management); Los Filos is a repeat-failure social-licence problem; Q4 needs a record ~258koz. Meaningful offset: unambiguous insider buying (Beaty ~C$9.7M in Aug-26 after ~US$18.2M earlier; zero selling into the drawdown).
- **ESG**: **AVERAGE / Neutral**. Zero fatalities and zero significant environmental incidents in 2025; majority Tier-1 jurisdiction mix post-Brazil exit. But Los Filos represents ~$300–380M/yr of forgone EBITDA (the largest ESG-attributable loss in the file), there is a fresh serious-injury OHS probe at Valentine, and governance scores 5/10 after serial M&A and CEO churn.

- **Short / Contrarian View**: **NO EDGE** — accounting is clean (Beneish −2.64, negative accruals, shrinking receivables). The bear case is capital allocation, not integrity: cumulative FCF −$735M (2021–25) and 45% of Q2-26 EPS from non-operating items. But net cash, a rising dividend, and a 2.5 beta make a naked short a widow-maker into any gold re-acceleration.
