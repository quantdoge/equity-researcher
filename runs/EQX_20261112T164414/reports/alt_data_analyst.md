### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: ⚠️ **BELOW consensus**

This is where the alt data earns its keep. Four independent signals converge:

1. **Guidance cut to the floor.** On the Q3 call (Nov 10), management guided FY26 production to the **low end of 870–920 koz**, citing ramp-up challenges at two Canadian mines and **pit instability at Mesquite**. That is a negative revision the sell-side has not yet fully flowed through.
2. **Q4 requires a record quarter.** YTD output is 611.5 koz; hitting even the low end needs **~258.5 koz in Q4** — an all-time record. Most of that is a calendar effect (full quarter of the Orla mines ≈ 269 koz at Q3 rates); the genuine swing factor is **Greenstone recovery and Valentine grade**, both of which are currently underperforming. Per third-party analysis, a 30-koz miss ≈ **7¢/share**.
3. **The metal is working against them.** Q3 average GLD ≈ 391; Q4-to-date average ≈ **374 (−4.4% QoQ)**, with a further **−6.6% in the last three sessions**. Consensus Q4 revenue of $1.18B (+13% QoQ) requires volume +18% *after* that price drag — versus the ~+9% volume implied by guidance. **Consensus revenue looks ~8–9% too high** on my bottom-up.
4. **Consensus EPS of $0.34 is +79% QoQ** off Q3's $0.19. That is a very high bar for a company guiding to the low end of production.

**Surprise history (7 quarters):** beat rate **57.1%**, average surprise **+16.1%** — but dispersion is extreme (−221% to +281%). A coin-flip beater with fat tails; the base rate does not rescue this setup.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH (mild)** — earnings-preview driven, not structural

The bearish call is narrow and dated to the **Feb 17 print**. It rests on a guidance floor, a record-quarter requirement against two known underperforming assets, a falling gold price into quarter-end, and a stock that *failed on a beat*. The post-earnings 4.6pp underperformance vs GDX says the market is still repricing, not finished.

**What stops me from being more bearish — and the 12-month case:**
- AISC **down 4% QoQ**, cash costs **down 9% QoQ**; at ~$3,540 spot, margins remain very wide.
- **EQX is down 44% from its March peak vs gold's 28%** — an amplification of only ~1.55x against a 2.49 beta. The miner has de-rated *less* than its beta implies, i.e., relative resilience.
- EQX/GDX ratio is **flat over two months** (0.1260 → 0.1256) — no ongoing relative bleed.
- Retail search interest at 12-month lows, dividend initiated, Barron's bottom-fishing, "gold price" searches inflecting up. Positioning is washed out.

**Net:** poor risk/reward into the print, reasonable value on a 12-month view. This is a "wait for the Q4 print and the Greenstone/Valentine grade data" situation, not a short.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM** (Low on consumer proxies, Medium-High on earnings preview)

- Google Trends is US-geo only and misses the Canadian holder base; the EQX series is noisy at low absolute levels (14–24).
- No SimilarWeb time series for the corporate domain — only a careers-subdomain snapshot.
- No quantitative social sentiment; Reddit read is qualitative and undated.
- Patent feed failed (immaterial for the sector).
- My Q4 consensus comparison is a bottom-up estimate from Trends/price proxies, **not a formal model**; per-ounce revenue implied by Q3 disclosures did not reconcile cleanly to spot, so treat the "8–9% too high" revenue gap as directional.
- Prices through Nov 12, 2026; gold has moved since.

**Bottom line:** Alt data flags a **negative earnings-preview skew into Feb 17** that consensus estimates ($0.34 EPS, $1.18B revenue) do not yet reflect. The offsetting positives — falling costs, washed-out positioning, in-line relative strength — argue for patience rather than a directional short.
