# Equinox Gold Corp. (EQX) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $10.39 | Mkt cap ≈$10.9B (1.05B sh) | Net cash ~$16M | Nov 12, 2026**

---

## 1. Growth Story

EQX has executed a deliberate roll-up strategy — Calibre (2025) then Orla Mining (2026) — transforming a sub-scale, sub-500koz producer into a ~1.1Moz pro-forma gold platform anchored by the Greenstone mine in Ontario, all while the gold price has re-rated to roughly **$4,300/oz**. The disruptive "thesis" here is not technology — it is **scale consolidation in a fragmenting mid-tier gold sector** plus an AISC of ~$1,605/oz against a ~$4,341/oz realized price, i.e. ~$2,700/oz of cash margin. The critical caveat: this is a **price-taker with no innovation moat**, and the growth has been bought with equity, not built organically.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

- Global mine supply ≈115M oz/yr (~3,600t), worth roughly **$500B annually** at current prices. EQX at ~1.1Moz holds **~1% share** — negligible pricing influence, but a long runway via consolidation.
- Real addressable pool: the **mid-tier gold consolidator set** (EV $3–30B), where scale economics in capital markets, G&A and debt cost are the actual synergy. Orla/Calibre prove EQX can transact — but at a cost of ~130% dilution in 18 months.
- **No moat in the classic sense**: no proprietary tech, no data network effect, no switching costs. The only durable advantage is **low-cost, long-life ore bodies** (Greenstone, Mesquite, Aurizona). Reserve life and AISC curve position *are* the moat — and I could not verify reserve/reserve-life figures in this data set (**gap**).

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 17, 2027 — Q4/FY2026 results.** YTD production 611,483oz vs 870–920koz guidance: needs ~259–309koz in Q4. A miss here is the single biggest near-term risk; a beat validates the "strong finish" narrative.
- **Jan–Feb 2027 — 2027 guidance** with a full 12 months of Orla (~1.1Moz pro forma). Any AISC guidance below ~$1,700/oz is a re-rating trigger.
- **H1 2027 — Greenstone operational fixes/ramp**; management flags further improvements supporting 2027 volumes.
- **Capital returns escalation.** Dividend already raised 50% (Q2'26); buyback initiated ($8.8M in Q3'26); balance sheet flipped from **$1.28B net debt to ~$16M net cash** in one year. A formal return-of-capital program is the cleanest offset to the dilution history.
- **Macro:** Fed path / real rates and central-bank buying remain the dominant swing factor for the entire thesis.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

EQX passes the *balance-sheet* and *cash-margin* tests spectacularly (net cash, ~$2,700/oz margin, ~7x EV/EBITDA vs NEM 8.8x and AEM 10.6x, 1.13x book vs peers at 3.7x) and fails the **growth-quality** test that defines my mandate: Rule of 40 of 22, ~3.5% FCF margin, 130% dilution, revenue/share up only 19%, sequential margin compression, and consensus EPS that peaks in 2028. You are being asked to underwrite a leveraged gold-price call with a management team that pays for growth in shares. **Upgrade trigger:** two consecutive quarters of expanding per-ounce margin *and* positive FCF with flat share count → Buy. **Downgrade trigger:** Q4 production below 870koz or AISC above $1,800/oz.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Gold price is ~90% of the thesis.** Beta 2.49; a 20% gold decline compresses margins from $2,700/oz to ~$2,000/oz and EPS by roughly half. Analysts already model declining EPS post-2028.
2. **Cost inflation / integration.** Cost of revenue +61% QoQ in Q3'26. Absorbing Orla's higher-cost Mexican assets into a fixed-price environment is unproven.
3. **Serial dilution & M&A discipline.** 130% share growth in six quarters with zero buyback scale-up until Q3'26. Another large all-stock deal would be read as value-destructive.
4. **Execution concentration.** Greenstone repairs and ramp are the swing factor for 2027; a repeat operational stumble hits both guidance and credibility.
5. **Jurisdictional/reserve risk** across Brazil, Mexico, US and Canada; reserve life unverified here.
6. **Coverage thinness** (1–5 analysts on out-years) means estimate revisions are low-conviction and can swing violently.
7. **Tax/earnings volatility** — Q3'26 net income of $67.5M on $433M EBITDA implies a very high effective tax/other drag that deserves scrutiny in the filing.
