**Bull Case**
1. **Demand is accelerating, not peaking**: Q4 guide $130B +91% YoY *with zero China revenue assumed*; CoWoS/HBM sold out through 2026 protects 74–75% gross margins and 2027 visibility.
2. **The valuation has already de-rated**: ~16x FY27E / ~10x FY28E for a business growing revenue triple-digits with 77% ROIC and a 3.7% FCF yield — the market is paying for skepticism, you are not.
3. **The systems moat widens**: rack-scale Vera Rubin + NVLink + networking expands content per rack; CUDA lock-in and $390B of locked supply allocation are unreplicable by ASIC challengers at scale.

**Bear Case**
1. **Disclosure risk is live and binary**: SEC probe into revenue recognition at a major customer, against $81B receivables, DSO 64, NI > OCF, and customer circular financing — a restatement breaks the quality thesis itself.
2. **$390B of irreversible commitments** written against a demand curve that must keep compounding; if capex plateaus, take-or-pay obligations convert operating leverage into an operating trap (bear case $95, −42%).
3. **Governance is impaired, not resolved**: the political mechanism that reinstated the CEO can remove him again; the board's credibility, the investigation's independence, and national-security findings all survived the reinstatement intact.
