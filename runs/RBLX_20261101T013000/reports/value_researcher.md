**Pricing note / data caveat:** The valuation feed shows a price of **~$43.0** (market cap ~$28.8B; ~670M shares) with net debt ~$0.6B (cash+ST investments $3.06B vs. $1.80B debt) → **EV ≈ $28.4B**. ### 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat Rating: **Narrow (and currently narrowing)**

**What Roblox is:** a two‑sided UGC entertainment platform. Creators build experiences in Roblox Studio; 138M users (Q3'26) consume them; Roblox monetizes via Robux purchases (bookings), pays ~1/3 out to developers (DevEx), and retains the rest. FY2025 revenue $4.89B (+36%), FY2026E revenue ~$5.8–6.0B, FY2026E bookings ~$6.9B.

**Genuine moat sources:**
- **Network effects (real):** more creators → more content → more users → more creator earnings. ~$1B+/yr paid to developers is a real switching cost for the supply side.
- **Social graph switching costs:** friends lists, inventories, Robux balances, and identity/avatar investment make migration costly for the core (younger) user.
- **Cost advantage in infrastructure:** proprietary engine + cloud tuned for massive concurrent 3D sessions; reported gross margin 78% and *rising* (74.1% in 2021 → 78.1% in 2025).
- **Intangibles (weak):** brand is strong with under‑13s — which is precisely the regulatory risk, not a pure asset.

**Why the moat is Narrow, not Wide — the decisive evidence:**
- **2026 age‑verification rollout removed ~20M DAUs sequentially.** Q3'26: DAUs 138M (**−9% YoY**), hours engaged ~34B (**−15%**), **bookings $1.697B (−12% YoY)**, with Q4'26 guided to **−10% to −15% bookings**.
- A business whose engagement can fall 15% in one year from a *self‑inflicted compliance change* does not have the durability Buffett demands. Pricing power is also unproven: monetization depends on a teenage audience's discretionary allowance and on Apple/Google's 30% (now partially liberalized) app‑store toll.
- **ROIC is negative every year (−49% to −69%)** and the company has never produced a GAAP operating profit. A moat that cannot be converted into returns on capital is, by my framework, not yet a demonstrated moat.

**The critical finding — reported FCF is an illusion.** FY2025 "FCF" of $1.35B is composed of: net loss −$1.07B **+ $1.13B SBC + $1.45B working‑capital inflow** (deferred revenue growing as bookings exploded) − $0.44B capex. Strip SBC and the WC tailwind and the business **consumed roughly $1.2B of cash**, exactly matching the GAAP operating loss. **Roblox's underlying economic earnings today are approximately zero.** As bookings growth stops, the deferred‑revenue tailwind disappears and FCF converges to Adj. EBITDA − capex ≈ $450M − $450M ≈ **$0**, on my estimates.

**Positives:** margin trajectory is genuinely improving (op margin −45% → −25%; Q3'26 Adj. EBITDA $138M vs. $46M a year ago; net loss narrowing to −$230M); balance sheet is sound (net debt only ~$0.6B, $3.06B liquidity, no dividend and no meaningful maturities pressure); revenue visibility is unusually high because revenue is recognized over an ~2‑year average user life (bookings are pre‑paid).

**Negatives:** 23% of revenue paid as stock comp; thin book equity ($375M); current ratio <1 (a deferred‑revenue artifact, not distress, but it shows the model runs on customer prepayment); capex doubling ($181M → $443M) as it builds AI/trust‑and‑safety/data‑center capacity; ongoing child‑safety litigation costs.

### 3. Valuation Analysis — **No margin of safety at ~$43**

| Multiple (EV $28.4B) | Value | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| EV / FY26E bookings (~$6.9B) | **~4.1x** | Rich for *declining* bookings; historical range 6–10x was set in a growth regime |
| EV / FY26E Adj. EBITDA (~$450M) | **~63x** | Adjusted metric that ignores $1.2B of SBC |
| EV / reported FY26E FCF (~$1.1B) | ~26x | FCF is SBC‑ and WC‑flattered |
| EV / owner earnings (FCF − SBC) | **negative** | The honest number |
| P/B | 79.6x | $0.54 book value/share |
| P/FCF (trailing) | 21.9x | Same caveat |

**What the price implies:** to justify a $28.4B EV at a value‑investor's 12x owner earnings, Roblox needs ~$2.4B of *after‑SBC* free cash flow. With SBC running ~$1.2B and underlying cash generation near zero, that requires pre‑SBC cash flow of ~$3.6B — roughly **4x** the current underlying level, i.e. bookings of ~$14–15B at a ~25% EBITDA margin within ~7 years (+12%/yr from here). That is a defensible *growth* scenario, not a conservative one, and the current trend is −12% bookings.

**My own owner‑earnings DCF (transparent, not the flawed tool output — the tool misparsed WACC as 1000%):**

- **Base case:** bookings trough ~$6.5B in 2027, then +8%/yr to ~$9.5B by 2032; Adj. EBITDA margin 22–25%; capex 8% of bookings; owner earnings ~$0.6–0.9B by 2032; 18x exit → PV **$18–24B ≈ $27–36/share**.
- **Bull case** (age checks prove transient, bookings re‑accelerate, SBC cut to ~10% of revenue, 30% margins): **$60–75/share**.
- **Bear case** (engagement structurally impaired, app‑store/regulatory friction, continued SBC): **$12–20/share**.

Probability‑weighted (30/45/25): **≈ $38/share**. Current ~$43 → **~10–15% above my central intrinsic value**. Even using the generous headline $1.35B FCF with a 3% perpetuity at 10% you get ~$19B (~$28/share).

**Analyst context:** street targets cluster at $41–55 (UBS $50, Goldman $55, BTIG $41–42, Needham $55) — i.e. consensus sees fair value roughly where the stock trades, with no upside cushion.

### 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Mixed, leaning shareholder‑unfriendly**

- **Founder‑led since 2004 (David Baszucki), dual‑class super‑voting control.** Vision and longevity are real strengths; accountability to outside owners is weak — minority holders cannot change capital allocation.
- **Capital allocation is the problem.** SBC of 23% of revenue (28–31% in 2022–24) is a wealth transfer from owners to employees, and it is the single largest destroyer of per‑share value here. GAAP losses have never been paired with SBC discipline.
- **Mitigating:** buyback began in 2026 — 4.4M shares for ~$200M in Q3'26, **$2.4B authorization remaining**. Importantly, they are buying at ~$45 rather than $138, which is better timing than most of their peers. But at ~17M shares/yr it only *offsets* dilution; it does not shrink the count.
- **No dividend** (appropriate for a reinvestment‑heavy business), no debt‑funded empire building, no large value‑destroying M&A (goodwill only $161M). Infra capex is organic and rational.
- **Verdict:** high integrity, high competence on product, **poor on per‑share economics**. In Buffett terms: an owner‑operator, not yet an owner‑*minded* allocator.

### 5. Investment Verdict: **SELL / AVOID** (do not initiate; trim if held)

Roblox is a genuinely impressive franchise inside a narrow but real network‑effect moat, run by a founder with a 20‑year track record. It is also, today: **unprofitable on a fully‑loaded basis, ROIC negative for five straight years, dependent on 23%‑of‑revenue stock comp and a deferred‑revenue float to show "free cash flow," and now posting declining DAUs, hours, and bookings.** At ~$43 (~$28.4B EV, ~4.1x declining bookings, ~63x adjusted EBITDA, negative owner earnings) the market is already capitalizing the recovery scenario in full. There is **no margin of safety** — arguably a ~10–15% premium to intrinsic value.

**Where I would get interested:** below ~$26–28 (EV ~$17–18B, ~2.5x bookings), which is roughly a 30% discount to my base‑case intrinsic value and would give me a free option on the bull case. Before that, I would want to see two consecutive quarters of **positive YoY bookings growth** and SBC falling below ~15% of revenue.

### 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

**Risks to owning it (why the stock can fall further):**
1. **Regulatory / child‑safety risk is now existential, not theoretical.** Age verification has already cost ~20M DAUs; further mandated restrictions, state AG actions, or a platform ban for under‑13s would permanently impair the funnel.
2. **Engagement decay is compounding:** hours −15% with DAUs only −9% means *intensity* is falling faster than users — a leading indicator that bookings declines continue into 2027 as revenue recognition catches up to bookings.
3. **Books are borrowed from the future:** revenue runs ~2 years behind bookings, so the reported +16% revenue growth is a lagging echo. 2027 revenue comparisons turn ugly.
4. **SBC dilution:** ~$1.2B/yr against a $28B market cap is ~4%/yr of value if not offset; buybacks currently only neutralize it.
5. **Hit‑driven content cycle** and competition from Fortnite/UGC, Minecraft, and AI‑native game creation tools — "Grow a Garden"-style viral spikes flatter one year and create impossible comps the next.
6. **App‑store economics** remain a structural tax, and infrastructure/trust‑and‑safety capex is escalating (capex $181M → $443M).

**Risks to my Sell/avoid stance (what would make me wrong):**
- Age‑check friction proves transient, DAUs recover through 2027, and bookings re‑accelerate → bull case $60–75 easily.
- Management substantially re‑bases SBC and demonstrates 25%+ Adj. EBITDA margins — that would convert this from a growth story to a genuine cash compounder and could justify today's price.

**Bottom line:** a great product, a narrow and currently eroding moat, and a price that offers no cushion. Buffett's test — "wonderful business at a fair price" — fails on both legs. **Sell.**
