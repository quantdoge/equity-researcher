# RBLX — Roblox Corporation | Growth Investment Analysis

**Price: $42.99 | Market cap ~$28.8B | EV ~$28.4B | 52-wk high $150.59 (Jul '25) — down 71%**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Roblox is the closest thing to a *3D internet infrastructure layer*: 138M DAUs, ~34B quarterly engagement hours, 78–80% gross margins, and a creator economy that produces content at zero marginal cost to the platform. The thesis has always been that a UGC platform monetizes at a fraction of its engagement value — and that advertising, rev-share, and eventually AI-assisted creation would close that gap. **That thesis is now in its first real stress test**: a mandatory facial/ID age-verification regime (rolled out Jan 2026 to fix child-safety exposure) broke the core social/chat loop for ~49% of users, driving bookings from +25% growth to **-12%**, and the stock has de-rated from ~12x bookings to ~4x.

The growth question is therefore no longer "how big is the TAM" — it is **"is the age-gate a compliance friction or a permanent network-effect break?"** The evidence, so far, leans friction.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

| Layer | TAM | Roblox position |
|---|---|---|
| Global gaming | ~$190–200B | ~3% of TAM; 138M DAU vs ~3.4B gamers (~4% penetration) |
| In-game/immersive advertising | ~$80–100B (of ~$700B digital ad) | **~0%** — rewarded video only launched Q4'26 |
| Creator/UGC economy + rev-share | ~$25B+ | Category leader |
| Education / enterprise 3D simulation | ~$10–20B early | Roblox Education, nascent |
| **Aggregate SAM** | **~$300B+** | **<3% captured** |

The white space is real and enormous — Roblox monetizes ~$0.05–0.07 per engagement hour versus YouTube/Meta at multiples of that. Every 1¢ of incremental revenue per hour on 130B+ annual hours is ~$1.3B of high-margin revenue. **But the TAM argument only compounds if the DAU base grows.** At 138M and shrinking, penetration is moving backwards — the ad lever cannot offset a user decline. This is why the next two quarters matter more than the TAM slide.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4'26 earnings — Feb 4, 2027 (highest importance).** Guided: revenue $1.47–1.58B (+4–12%), bookings $1.89–2.00B (**-10–15%**), FCF **-$105M to -$190M**. Watch: age-check compliance %, DAU trajectory, and critically the **Q1'27 bookings guide** — the first clean read on post-compliance run-rate.
- **Age-check compliance 51% → >90% (through H1 2027).** ~54M DAUs currently have restricted chat. Each 10pts of compliance mechanically restores engagement. This is the single largest identifiable recovery lever.
- **Rewarded video ads scaling (now → 2027).** Live in 1,200+ experiences; creator rev-share begins **2027**. First non-bookings revenue line; watch impressions/ARPDAU disclosures.
- **GTA VI headwind annualizing (Q1 2027).** Management explicitly baked a "modest GTA headwind" into Q4 — it laps by spring.
- **Buyback execution.** $2.4B authorization remaining (~8% of market cap); 4.4M shares repurchased for ~$200M in Q3. First capital return in company history.
- **Securities class-action ($6.7B claim) over age-verification disclosures** — any settlement/clearance removes a multiple-suppressing overhang.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** *(speculative, catalyst-dependent — not a core position)*

This is a genuine disruptive platform trading at a **~4x EV/bookings** — the lowest multiple in its public history, versus 8–14x historically, and roughly half of what you'd pay for a mature, no-growth game publisher. At $43 you are being asked to pay for terminal decline; you are being delivered 138M DAUs, 80% gross margins, an inflecting EBITDA line, a $2.4B buyback, and a 51%→90% compliance lever that is arithmetic, not speculative.

**Why not Strong Buy:** the growth engine is objectively broken right now. Bookings -12%, hours -15%, negative Q4 FCF, and 2027 consensus (+13%) looks ~8pts too high (Jefferies at +5%). The revenue growth being reported is an accounting lag that will **roll over into revenue declines in 2027–2028** if bookings don't re-inflect. Cathie's framework tolerates volatility for TAM capture — but it requires the S-curve to still be upward-sloping. That is not yet proven.

**Scenarios (FY2027 bookings basis):**
- **Bear (~30%) — network-effect break persists:** bookings -5%, 2.5x → **~$24/sh (-44%)**
- **Base (~45%) — compliance normalizes, bookings +5–8%:** 5.0x → **~$52/sh (+21%)**
- **Bull (~25%) — compliance >85% + ads scale, bookings +13%:** 7.0x → **~$85/sh (+98%)**

Positively skewed, but with a fat left tail. **Invalidation triggers to monitor:** DAU sustained below 130M on a QoQ basis; compliance stalling below 60%; Q1'27 bookings guide worse than -10%; delay of the 2027 ad rev-share.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Network-effect break (the existential one).** Chat is the core social loop. Age-gating 49% of users may have permanently pushed the most engaged cohort to Fortnite, Minecraft, or Discord. Sequential recovery in Q3 could be seasonal back-to-school noise, not healing.
2. **Revenue accounting illusion.** Revenue is recognized over a ~27-month average paying-user life. Two more quarters of negative bookings guarantee reported revenue deceleration — consensus 2027-2028 revenue will need cutting regardless of DAU trends.
3. **Estimate cut cycle not finished.** Street at +13% FY27 bookings vs Jefferies at +5%. Another guidance reset is the most likely near-term catalyst, and it's negative.
4. **Structural — not one-time — safety costs.** Infrastructure expense guided higher; OCF fell to $395M from $546M. The >90% compliance target requires ongoing facial-recognition/ID infrastructure, moderation, and legal spend.
5. **Regulatory & legal overhang.** $6.7B securities class action, plus UK Online Safety Act, EU DSA, and state AG scrutiny. Roblox is a political target; mandated product changes can recur.
6. **Competition.** GTA VI (near-term engagement headwind), Fortnite/UEFN, Minecraft, and — most dangerously — AI-native UGC platforms that collapse the cost of 3D content creation, eroding Roblox's creator-moat.
7. **Monetization ceiling on a child audience.** Ad targeting, data use, and spend are constrained by COPPA/child-safety rules — capping the very ad TAM that underpins the bull case.
8. **Dilution.** 755M diluted shares (+2% YoY) against ~$1.13B annual SBC partially offsets buyback economics.
9. **Balance-sheet optics.** P/B 79.6x and negative P/E make traditional screening useless; the stock trades purely on bookings inflection and narrative.
