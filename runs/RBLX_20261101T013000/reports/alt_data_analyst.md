### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **BELOW** — for the *forward* quarter (Q4'26)

For the quarter just reported, alt data was **In-Line to Below** and correctly tilted cautious: Trends decelerated into September, and RBLX indeed delivered the negative DAU surprise that drove a **-18% single-day move** (Oct 30: $52.46 → $42.99).

Quality of the print itself was actually decent on the numbers:
- **EPS: -$0.32 vs -$0.41 est → +26.2% beat** (largest beat in 4 quarters)
- Revenue topped consensus; **bookings exceeded the high end of guidance**
- Beat rate: **85.7% (6 of 7 quarters)**, avg surprise **+14.2%** — a persistent sandbagger
- Revenue still growing (2025: $4.891B, +35.8% YoY)

**But the market repriced the forward story, not the quarter:** DAUs -9% QoQ, bookings reported down ~12% YoY (Bloomberg), GTA VI launching into the holiday window, and structurally tighter child-safety measures that suppress the under-13 cohort. My alt data says the **Q4 setup is Below consensus risk**: search interest is 20–25% below its summer peak heading into the seasonally critical December window, which is the opposite of the pattern you want into a holiday guide.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH** (near-term, 1–2 quarters)

The composite is unusually coherent, which is what makes me weight it:

| Signal | Direction |
|---|---|
| Google Trends (rolling over since Aug, flat post-print) | **Negative** |
| Web traffic (3rd straight quarterly decline) | **Negative** |
| Job postings (safety/AI infra, not growth) | Neutral-positive |
| Earnings track record (85.7% beat rate) | Positive — but already exploited |
| News sentiment (surge in negative coverage) | **Negative** |
| Social (dip-buying retail) | Contrarian-neutral |

**The core thesis:** Roblox is two separate stories right now. The *reported* business keeps beating low bars (sandbagged guidance, cost discipline, AI content velocity). The *engagement* business is contracting for reasons that are partly **self-inflicted and structural** (safety/age-verification changes) and partly **competitive** (GTA VI into holiday). Google Trends and web traffic — my two independent demand proxies — both rolled over *ahead* of the disclosure and **have not turned back up**. That is the difference between a washed-out stock and a bottoming one.

Valuation has de-rated hard (from ~$54 pre-print to $42.98; market cap $28.8B, beta 1.47), so much bad news is priced. But alt data does not yet give me the "leading indicator has inflected" signal I'd need to be constructive into the Q4 guide.

**Actionable framing:** avoid adding into holiday-quarter guidance risk; watch Trends + app-download data for a *sustained* 3–4 week turn above ~65 as the trigger for a constructive re-rating. The first hard evidence that safety-related funnel friction is annualising (Q1'27 lap) is the more likely catalyst than any near-term print.

**Confidence Level: MEDIUM.**
- *Supporting:* Trends and web traffic are two independent sources pointing the same way, and both are confirmed by the actual DAU/bookings disclosure — the model's predictions validated against the outcome, which raises my confidence in the *framework*.
- *Capping at Medium:* no hard app-download/MAU data (the decisive variable for diagnosing new-user funnel vs. churn), no quantitative job-posting counts, patents feed failed, and the automated news sentiment score is demonstrably wrong (flagged). Also, alt data's edge is largely spent for this quarter — the event has occurred; this is now an assessment of Q4, where GTA VI's launch-date spillover effects are not observable in any dataset I have.
