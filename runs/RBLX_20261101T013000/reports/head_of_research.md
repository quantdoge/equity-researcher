# Investment Memo: RBLX — 1 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (do not initiate; existing holders reduce to ≤0.5% position) | Conviction: Medium-High
**Price Target**: $38 (–12% from current $42.99)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Roblox is a genuine franchise — 138M DAUs, an expanding ~80% gross margin, and a real two-sided creator network — trading at the cheapest multiple of its public history (~4x bookings). But four independent workstreams (Value, Financial Risk, Short, Quant) converge on the same finding: reported free cash flow is a deferred-revenue float, not economic earnings (cumulative owner earnings FY21–25 are **–$1.33B**), and guidance implies that float now reverses as bookings decline. The age-verification regime has broken the growth algorithm (DAU –9%, hours –15%, Q4 bookings guided –10 to –15%), and with no valuation floor, an unpriced regulatory binary inside 90 days, and zero insider buying through a 70% drawdown, the stock at ~blended intrinsic value offers no edge and a fat left tail. The bull case is early, not wrong — which is why this is a reduce-with-a-re-entry-ladder call, not a permanent pass.

## Bull Case
- **Compliance recovery is mechanical:** age-check compliance moving 51%→90% restores chat for ~54M users; Q3 sequential trends (DAU +12% QoQ) suggest Q2 was the trough. Bull case ~$85 (+98%).
- **Structurally superior P&L:** 79.8% gross margin and rising, Adj. EBITDA +200% YoY, advertising layer (~0% penetrated on ~130B annual hours) offers a high-margin revenue lever beginning 2027.
- **Punished valuation + real-rate tail:** at ~4x bookings (all-time low) with $3.06B cash and a $2.4B buyback, a real-rate retracement or one clean bookings inflection could re-rate the stock violently.

## Bear Case
- **FCF illusion inverts:** deferred-revenue float produced >100% of FY25 FCF; guided bookings decline mechanically reverses it — Q4 guided FCF is already negative (–$105M to –$190M).
- **Structural, not transitory, demand impairment:** hours (–15%) falling faster than DAUs (–9%) means intensity is decaying; both independent demand proxies rolled over and have not inflected.
- **No floor + binary catalysts:** P/B 79.6x, negative owner earnings, three consecutive –18%/–27%/–18% post-print gaps, and UK/Australia scope decisions inside 90 days.

## Key Risks
1. Q1'27 bookings guide (Street +13% vs. Jefferies +5%) — most likely near-term catalyst, skewed negative.
2. UK Ofcom scope inclusion of gaming platforms / Australian eSafety action — 15–25% move potential.
3. BIPA/GDPR biometric class action over facial age estimation — the identified unpriced tail.
4. Second consecutive negative-FCF quarter confirming the float reversal.
5. **To the Sell call:** compliance recovery re-inflects bookings in H1'27 (+98% bull tail); sharp real-rate unwind.
