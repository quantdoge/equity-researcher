### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Regulatory Risks

**Risk 1 — US state AG and private child-safety litigation becoming a recurring cost of doing business (Probability: ~95% of continued escalation; Impact: High)**
- Alabama ($12.2m), West Virginia ($11.1m) and Nevada settled April 21, 2026: **~$35.8m total** (Reuters; claimsjournal.com; alabamaag.gov; ago.wv.gov). Settlements impose operational remedies — e.g., **no encryption of minor communications** — which raise product cost and reduce feature parity.
- JPML consolidated child-exploitation cases into an **MDL on Dec 13, 2025** (pbglaw.com); Reuters reports **140+ federal lawsuits plus additional state actions**.
- Active sovereign plaintiffs: **Texas AG (Paxton) suit**, **Nebraska AG suit (Mar 4, 2026)**, **LA County suit (Feb 19, 2026)** which survived a motion to dismiss on age-check adequacy (iclg.com; lacounty.gov; ago.nebraska.gov). Connecticut (AG Tong) and Georgia (AG Carr, Feb 17, 2026) have opened investigations; **Florida AG has issued subpoenas in a criminal-style investigation** (bitdefender.com).
- Assessment: low existential risk of a single catastrophic judgment, but high probability of a compounding $50–150m/yr settlement-and-remediation drag plus product restrictions.

**Risk 2 — EU Digital Services Act exposure after VLOP designation (Probability of a formal adverse finding: ~40–50% within 24 months; Impact: Medium-High)**
- The European Commission designated **Roblox a Very Large Online Platform (VLOP) on Aug 31, 2026** (EU Commission; techpolicy.press), with **46.6m EU monthly active recipients** (Roblox DSA help page, 6-month period ending Aug 13, 2026). That triggers systemic-risk assessments, independent audits, recommender transparency and Commission-level oversight.
- **Dutch ACM opened a formal DSA investigation on Jan 30, 2026** (acm.nl; Reuters) focused on minor protection and dark patterns; expected ~12 months; remedies include binding instructions, fines, or periodic penalty payments. Roblox's own Q2 FY26 10-Q flags DSA fines of **up to 6% of global annual turnover** — ~$350m+ on TTM revenue.
- Assessment: Portugal/Netherlands-style national enforcement plus Commission proceedings make this a genuine multi-year margin and engineering-resource tax.

**Risk 3 — Age-verification regime change is now a revenue risk, not just a compliance cost (Probability: realized; Impact: High)**
- Roblox made **facial age estimation mandatory to access chat** (rolled out globally ~June 2026; about.roblox.com Nov 2025 announcement; CBS). Q3 FY26: **DAUs −9% y/y to 138m**, engagement hours −15% to 34bn (WSJ, Oct 29 2026); stock fell ~18% on Oct 30 (stockanalysis.com).
- Full-year FY26 bookings growth guidance was cut from 22–26% to **8–12%** on April 30, 2026 — the trigger for the securities class action. Q3 bookings reportedly fell ~12% (Bloomberg Technology).
- Regulatory catalysts still ahead: **Australia's under-16 restriction** (in force; eSafety FAQ), **UK's confirmed under-16 social media ban (Jun 15, 2026)** with Ofcom "highly effective" age-assurance standards at 16+, and a ~14-country copycat wave (orrick.com; bbc.com; ofcom.org.uk). Whether game platforms like Roblox are captured is the swing factor: inclusion ⇒ material DAU/bookings hit in UK/AU; exclusion ⇒ competitive advantage.

*Also relevant (non-top-3):* trade/tariff and US-China exposure is **immaterial** — no manufacturing supply chain, no China revenue dependence (Luobu has been effectively dormant since Dec 2021, with a reported possible Tencent-partnered 2026 relaunch, unconfirmed). **No sanctions (OFAC/EU/UN) exposure identified**, no nationalisation or currency-control risk of consequence.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **One key control is being litigated as inadequate** — multiple AGs and the LA County court treat Roblox's age-assurance architecture as not fit for purpose. Regulatory findings of inadequacy could be used as evidence in the MDL.
2. **Mandatory facial biometrics on a child-majority user base** — highest-sensitivity data category under GDPR/BIPA/COPPA; a single state biometric class action would be material and is currently absent, i.e., unpriced.
3. **Publicly reported ~51% compliance** with the age-check requirement at rollout (shattered.io) — **unverified, secondary source**; if directionally right, it is an enforcement target for ACM/Ofcom.
4. **No FCPA/AML/anti-bribery or sanctions flags identified.** Roblox is a consumer platform with no disclosed DOJ/SEC bribery or export-control matters; international expansion (Tencent JV) is the only structure with meaningful third-party/jurisdictional entanglement.
5. **Disclosure risk**: the April 2026 guidance cut sits inside a live securities class period — any further restatement or "known trend" dispute compounds both the securities case and D&O exposure.

---

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale:** The risk here is *not* classic geopolitical (no sanctions, no supply chain, no EM currency/export-control chokepoints, ~58% US revenue in a strong rule-of-law jurisdiction). It is **regulatory-legal concentration**: a consolidated MDL of 140+ suits, five+ state AG actions with criminal-adjacent Florida subpoenas, a certified securities class action, VLOP designation plus a live Dutch DSA probe carrying 6%-of-turnover fines, and a mandatory biometric age-verification regime that has already cost ~9% of DAUs and ~10 points of bookings guidance. Not **Very High** only because: (a) settlements to date are modest relative to $5.9bn revenue and $3.2bn cash; (b) the US remains the core market with predictable appellate recourse; (c) Türkiye and Russia both reversed/softened bans, showing platform value can outlast political bans.

**Monitoring triggers for a downgrade/upgrade:** an adverse MDL bellwether verdict; an ACM binding instruction or EU Commission statement of objections; inclusion of gaming platforms in the UK/Australia under-16 scope; any BIPA/GDPR biometric class action; and whether bookings guidance stabilizes post-age-check (Q4 FY26 print).
