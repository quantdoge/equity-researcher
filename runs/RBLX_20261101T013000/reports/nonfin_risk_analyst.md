### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary — **the dominant risk**

Multi-jurisdictional, accelerating, and now reaching the **core revenue model** (under-13 users ≈ 35% of verified DAUs).

- **US state AG wave (9+ states have sued).** Quantified settlements already paid: **Nevada $10M + $2.5M** (Apr 15, 2026), **West Virginia $11M**, **Alabama $12.2M** — each paired with mandated facial age estimation, ID verification, and behavioral monitoring.
- **Active litigation:** Texas (AG Paxton), Arkansas (AG Griffin, naming Discord), Nebraska (AG Hilgers), **San Diego County** consumer-protection suit (Oct 5, 2026, covering child safety *and* consumer privacy). **Connecticut** opened a formal investigation. **Florida AG issued criminal subpoenas.** Running total disclosed settlements ≈ **$35M** and climbing, before defense costs.
- **Federal/political:** EPIC and a coalition have formally petitioned the **FTC** to investigate manipulative design; **Senators Hawley and Durbin** opened a Senate inquiry into child-safety failures (~65,000 exploitation reports cited).
- **Securities/derivative:** Federal securities class action (Levi & Korsinsky) against Baszucki and CFO Chopra, class period **Oct 31, 2024 – Apr 30, 2026**; **Ohio AG moved to lead** the class on behalf of OPERS ($21.5M loss); new **derivative suit** filed in Clark County, NV (~Oct 20, 2026) alleging the board misled on age-check impact; Moore Law investor-action notice Oct 29, 2026.
- **Ex-US (structural):** proposed **EU KIDS Act** (Sept 2026 — social-media ban under 13, "digital majority" at 15, safety-by-design for games), DSA minor-protection enforcement, UK Online Safety Act, Australia's under-16 regime. This is a demographic-redefinition risk, not a fine risk.
- **EDGAR full-text search returned zero SEC enforcement actions** against RBLX — no federal securities enforcement to date.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Trust & safety remediation is now the product.** Mandated age verification broke engagement: only ~51% of users complied, driving **DAU −9% to 138M** and **engagement hours −15% to 34B** in Q3 2026, after bookings-growth guidance was cut to 8–12% on Apr 30, 2026. Moderation cost scales with content volume (AI-generated experiences reportedly +50% q/q), creating a compliance-cost/engagement trade-off with no proven stable point.
2. **Extreme key-person risk amplified by control.** Strategy, voting control, and public narrative are fused in one founder. There is no succession disclosure of note, and the board is family-linked and staggered.
3. **Infrastructure, ecosystem and new biometric-data exposure.** Roblox Cloud uptime and creator-payout economics are single-point dependencies, while deploying **facial age estimation on minors** creates a novel, litigation-attractive biometric-privacy surface (BIPA-style and state privacy statutes). Historical incidents exist (2016 breach; a later vendor-phishing/extortion event) — no material 2026 breach found, but the target profile has escalated. Competitive overlay: **GTA VI** holiday slate cited as a demand headwind.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **SEVERE**

The brand is now synonymous with child-safety controversy in mainstream press, state capitols, and the US Senate. Three single-day drawdowns in six months quantify the franchise damage: **−18.3% (May 1), −26.9% (Jul 31), −18.0% (Oct 30, 2026)**; shares are **−68% y/y** from $134.07 and ~−70% from the Oct 2025 peak of ~$142. Sell-side has turned (Jefferies to Underperform at $38). ESG/social controversy is not cyclical — it is embedded in the product's demographics, and every additional settlement re-triggers coverage. Brand recovery is now dependent on regulators, not marketing.

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH** (regulatory sub-rating: **CRITICAL**)

No fraud indicators, no restatement, no going-concern or liquidity signal, and clean Sarbanes-Oxley CEO/CFO certifications in the Q3 2026 10-Q. Offsetting that, the company faces a coordinated multistate + federal + EU regulatory campaign that has already forced a product change destroying ~70% of market value, with securities and derivative litigation layered on top and unanimous insider distribution. This is a **structural, existential-grade regulatory repositioning** risk, not a fine-and-move-on event.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Litigation/regulatory accruals are likely understated relative to the runway** — $35M paid across three states with 9+ suing, FL criminal subpoenas, a Senate inquiry, and the EU KIDS Act pending. Escalate to legal/forensics for a contingent-liability review of the Q3 10-Q.
2. **Guidance credibility event:** bookings guidance cut Apr 30, 2026, followed by a class period ending that exact date. Cross-check any 10b5-1 plans adopted/amended within 90 days of Apr 30, 2026 and Jul 31, 2026 disclosures.
3. **CEO + family-director + 20:1 control** with no independent check demonstrated during the crisis — escalate as a board-engagement/governance campaign candidate.
4. **Zero insider buying at a 70% drawdown**, including a ~$23.6M director sale at the trough — treat management's "transitory" framing with skepticism.
5. **Biometric processing of minors' facial data** — unquantified privacy-liability tail; request the vendor/DPIA posture and any state AG data-privacy inquiries beyond the San Diego action.
