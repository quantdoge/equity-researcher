## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **LOW (but NOT fraudulent)**

Roblox is a textbook case of *legal-but-aggressive* financial presentation rather than manipulation. The distinction matters enormously for how this is traded.

The single most important fact: **the entire GAAP net loss is stock-based compensation, and virtually the entire operating cash flow is a working-capital inflow from bookings.** Strip both and the business has produced essentially nothing.

| Metric | FY2025 | TTM (Q4'25–Q3'26) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $4,891M | $5,898M |
| Net income | −$1,065M | −$979M |
| Operating cash flow | $1,796M | $1,949M |
| **of which working-capital benefit** | **+$1,454M** | **+$1,450M** |
| Stock-based comp | $1,129M | $1,153M |
| FCF (OCF − capex) | $1,353M | $1,564M |
| **FCF − SBC ("owner earnings")** | **$224M** | **$411M** |
| Implied P/owner earnings | 129× | **70×** |

**81% of FY2025 and 74% of TTM operating cash flow came from working capital** — i.e., customers pre-paying bookings that haven't been recognised as revenue. This is not earnings; it is a balance-sheet financing effect that *mechanically reverses the moment bookings growth stops.*

Sensitivity — the heart of the bear case:

| Bookings scenario | WC contribution | Owner earnings | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| +20% growth | ≈ +$1.4B | ≈ +$0.4B | 70× — today's reality |
| **Flat** | **0** | **≈ −$1.0B** | Business burns cash |
| **−12% (guidance run-rate)** | **Outflow** | **Materially worse** | Deferred revenue unwinds |

The company guides Q4 2026 bookings **down 10–15% YoY**. That is the scenario that flips the sign.

Cumulative reality check: FY2021–FY2025 FCF totalled **+$2,618M** against **$3,944M** of SBC — a **negative $1.33B in cumulative owner earnings across five years** while the company reported "record" bookings every year.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

1. **Bookings inflecting negative.** Q4 2026 guided to $1.89–2.00B, **−10% to −15% YoY**, vs ~$2.01B consensus. Revenue guided +4% to +12%, a collapse from +36% in FY2025.
2. **Guidance credibility broken — twice in one year.** FY2026 bookings first guided $8.28–8.55B (Feb 2026, above the $7.87B street), cut to $7.33–7.60B (Apr/May 2026), now $7.47B midpoint. FY2026 revenue guidance cut from $6.02–6.29B to $5.87–6.14B. Stock gapped **−18% (May 1), −27% (Jul 31), −18% (Oct 30)** on consecutive reports.
3. **First-ever buyback is value-destructive theatre.** $201M repurchased in Q3 2026 against ~$1.15B of annual SBC issuance (~7%/yr dilution). At ~70× owner earnings, buying back at this multiple while issuing 7% of the float annually destroys value in both directions.
4. **Total shareholders' equity is approximately zero (−$27M).** Retained earnings −$5.72B. Altman Z-score **−0.589 ("distress")**, current ratio 0.80×.
5. **Capex intensity tripled.** $181M (FY2024) → $443M (FY2025), +145%, now 9.1% of revenue vs 5.0%. Capital being sunk into proprietary infrastructure — a rising fixed-cost base right as growth decelerates.
6. **Receivables outgrew revenue in FY2025** (+47.5% vs +35.8%) — genuine channel-stuffing signal, though DSO fell from ~70 days (Dec-25) to ~31 days (Sep-26), suggesting payout timing rather than stuffing. **Downgraded to a yellow flag.**
7. **Insider selling with zero open-market buying through a 60% drawdown.** 25 Form 4s in five months, including an 11-filing cluster on 2026-08-24. CEO David Baszucki: 0 purchases, 5 sales, 103,196 shares (~$4.29M). Director Gregory Baszucki sold 16,666 shares Sept 1, 2026. Net negative ~$4.33M over 90 days.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

**Moat is intact on product economics, eroding on monetisation and facing an existential-consequence regulatory threat.**

*Working in the bulls' favour (must concede):*
- **Gross margin is expanding**, not contracting: 74.1% → 75.4% → 76.8% → 77.8% → 78.1% → **79.8% (Q3'26)**. This is the single strongest anti-short datapoint. Pricing power is *not* eroding; the take-rate on a two-sided creator economy is holding.
- DAUs 138M, +12% YoY. Q3 2026 bookings of ~$1.92B beat the ~$1.72B consensus by 12%.
- Creator supply-side moat (Studio, DevEx) is real and self-reinforcing.

*Against:*
- **Q3's beat-and-drop is the tell.** A 12% bookings beat that produces an 18% decline means the market has stopped paying for current performance and is pricing terminal deceleration. When a stock falls on good news, the debate has already shifted to durability.
- **The litigation overhang is the real moat threat, not competitors.** Texas AG lawsuit, Nebraska AG lawsuit, Connecticut AG investigation, Mississippi AG settlement — a coordinated multi-state child-safety campaign. The age-verification rollout already caused a "steep DAU decline" per the Q1 report. Safety remediation directly impairs the engagement and social-graph loop that drives bookings. This is a business-model risk that consensus underweights because it has no clean financial line item.
- Q3 2025's +48% growth was a viral-hit-driven comp (Q2 2025 was only +20.9%). Some of the 2026 deceleration is optical — but a guided absolute bookings decline is not.

## 5. Management Red Flags

- **Metric selection:** headline KPI is *bookings*, not revenue or GAAP earnings; "Adjusted EBITDA" excludes $1.1B of SBC. Legitimate for the model, but it systematically directs attention away from the fact that no GAAP profit has ever been produced.
- **Guidance management:** two guide-downs inside one fiscal year is a pattern, not an accident.
- **Capital allocation:** initiating a buyback at ~70× owner earnings while diluting 7%/yr.
- **Insider behaviour:** consistent sales by the founder-CEO and a director with zero open-market purchases during a 60% decline. Mitigant: dollar amounts are small relative to the Baszucki stake and appear plan-driven (10b5-1). **Yellow flag, not red.**
- No evidence of related-party abuse, auditor changes, or restatements identified. Auditors signed a clean SOX 302 certification in the Q3 2026 10-Q.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary

**Credible bear case — yes. But it is a quality-of-earnings and terminal-value short, not a fraud short, and a large part of it has already been paid out.**

**The bear case:** Roblox's celebrated cash generation is a deferred-revenue illusion. On an owner-earnings basis (FCF less SBC, which shareholders genuinely bear through 7%/yr dilution), the business has earned **negative $1.33B cumulatively since 2021** and ~$411M TTM against a $28.8B market cap (70×). Bookings are now guided to *decline* 10–15%, which mechanically inverts the working-capital tailwind that produced 74–81% of all reported operating cash flow. Consensus does not model GAAP profitability until **2030** ($179M net income — 161× that year's earnings). Meanwhile equity is ~zero, the Z-score is in distress, capex intensity has tripled, and a multi-state AG campaign threatens the engagement loop itself.

**The bull case — and why I won't over-claim:** Gross margins are *expanding* to ~80%, DAUs are +12%, Q3 beat bookings by 12%, and there is $3.2B of cash against $1.9B of converts (no solvency risk — the Altman distress reading is a false positive because deferred revenue is a non-cash, customer-prepaid liability). SBC is falling from 31% to 19% of revenue
