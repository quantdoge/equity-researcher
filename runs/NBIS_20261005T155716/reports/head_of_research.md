## Investment Thesis

NBIS is the highest-beta pure-play vehicle on the AI-capex supercycle: a +450%-YoY-growing GPU cloud with a contracted, prepaid ~$40B backlog (Microsoft anchor, NVIDIA co-investment), gross margins inflecting to ~77%, and adjusted EBITDA now positive. The bull case is real — but it is already the price: at ~107x trailing / ~18x forward revenue, the market is underwriting flawless execution of a $7–9B run-rate that does not yet exist, against an operating loss at every scale achieved and a −$3.7B FCF. The risks (financial, geopolitical, governance, valuation) agree in sign and are broad; the growth case rests on forward execution. We therefore do not initiate at size, hold existing positions at reduced weight, and permit only a small, strictly-stopped speculation where the mandate explicitly carries AI-capex tilt.

## Bull Case
1. **Contracted, prepaid demand**: ~$40B committed book (~$32B firm) — Microsoft $17.4–19.4B through 2031 plus NVIDIA's $2B strategic investment — converts to revenue with an 80% beat rate; sequential growth is *accelerating* (+46% QoQ), not decelerating.
2. **Unit economics are inflecting**: gross margin 68.6% → ~77%; adjusted EBITDA turned positive (+$236M, Q2'26); ~75%-owned infrastructure and confirmed pricing power (17–21% GPU-lease price increases) support the 40%+ EBITDA-margin guide.
3. **Structurally scarce sector, first-mover position**: 3.5–4GW contracted capacity against demand that exceeds supply into 2027; full-stack AI-native cloud with NVIDIA-aligned allocation is one of the few independent Western ex-China AI-infra vehicles.

## Bear Case
1. **Valuation is untethered from reported economics**: ~107x trailing / ~18x forward revenue; the price already equals best-case 2028 value; operating losses persist at every scale reached; zero margin of safety under any conservative intrinsic method.
2. **The financing treadmill**: $10.1B debt (20x in 18 months), ~$16B/yr capex against a $530M-revenue base, ~12–14-month cash runway, $5.75B of converts (future dilution); earnings quality deteriorating (receivables 1.6x revenue; Meta "deal" is a backstop).
3. **Concentrated, binary, macro-hostile risk stack**: ~100% of the contract book in two counterparties; US export-control + Russian-legacy tail; insiders net-selling; 113% vol with demonstrated −51%/−58% drawdowns; 10Y at ~5.2% and rising de-rates a beta-1.46, long-duration equity.

## Key Risks
1. **Financing/refinancing failure** — ≤14-month runway dependent on open capital markets; a failed raise or frozen credit forces dilution or capex-cut (idling depreciating GPUs).
2. **Customer concentration & insourcing** — Microsoft/Meta are anchor and counterparty; hyperscaler self-build at renewal is the model's structural threat.
3. **Geopolitical/export-control action** — BIS licensing, OFAC/DOJ escalation (unverified Volozh inquiry), Dutch/Finnish national-security review.
4. **Governance & earnings quality** — open ICFR weaknesses (restatement risk via end-2026 catalyst), dual-class control, insider selling, non-operating earnings optics.
5. **Valuation air-pocket** — multiple compression alone can halve the stock; demonstrated on modest news (−51% June→July; −25% on a raise).
6. **Macro rates tripwire** — sustained 10Y > 5.3–5.5% or recession re-triggers the duration de-rating. **NVIDIA allocation/grid permitting slippage** constrains contracted-capacity delivery.
