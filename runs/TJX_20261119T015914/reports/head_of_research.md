### Investment Memo: TJX — November 20, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (existing holders) / DO NOT INITIATE (new money) | Conviction: **Medium-High**
**Price Target**: $125 (−11% from current $140.30)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
TJX is the highest-quality operator in a structurally attractive channel — 30.3% ROIC rising four consecutive years, net cash ex-leases, 125% cash conversion, a forensic-clean P&L, and a widening share of a counter-cyclical off-price market. But every valuation lens converges on the same answer: at ~26.5x forward earnings (a 21% premium to the peer median) with a 3.1% FCF yield, the market has already capitalised a decade of flawless execution. The margin cycle has inflected (Q3 FY27 gross margin −11bp YoY, the first decline in the series; Q4 guided *below* prior outlook), and ~$331M of one-time tariff refunds embedded in the FY27 base will mechanically depress FY28 growth. Great business, wrong price — this is a name to buy on the ladder below, not at market.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector rated **ATTRACTIVE** — off-price has taken ~950bps of visit share from department stores since 2022 and wins at both ends of a K-shaped consumer (UMich sentiment 48.1, saving rate 3.8%). TJX is the preferred vehicle: +459bps operating margin above peer median, most diversified, lowest beta. Offset: the discount-rate channel is hostile — 10Y at 5.13%, 5Y real ~2.7%, Fed hiking — which caps multiple-led upside and drove the −27% June–September de-rating. USD +3.8% in nine weeks is an unhedged translation drag on the two strongest divisions (Canada, International).

- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **MEDIUM (leaning low)**. 100% of stores in high-rule-of-law jurisdictions; zero sanctions/FCPA exposure; clean Item 3. The live risks are trade policy (Section 301 actions on Bangladesh/Cambodia/China; China exclusion expiry Jan 9, 2027) and a credible, well-telegraphed consumer class action over TJX's retention of $331M in IEEPA tariff refunds — $112M of which went to bonuses, drawing Senate-level criticism. Legal cost is small; headline and AG-inquiry risk is not.

- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **Fails every value test.** DCF and owner-earnings converge on intrinsic value of ~$85–120 (central ~$100–105); the reverse DCF requires ~14.5% FCF growth for a decade versus a 6.4% historical revenue CAGR. A de-rating to the peer-median 23.7x on unchanged EPS is ~$116 — a ~17–25% loss with zero fundamental deterioration. Buybacks at 28–32x earnings are value-dilutive — a genuine capital-allocation blemish on an otherwise excellent management record. Buy zone: below ~$105; aggressive below ~$85.

- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: Real but decelerating. Raised 7,500-store target (vs ~5,100 today) underwrites a 6–7% revenue runway; FY27E EPS ~$5.22 (+7%), FY28E ~$5.73 (+10%). But comps slowed from +5% to +4%, Q4 is guided to +2–3% (slowest plan in eight quarters), and Marmaxx (~60% of sales) is the laggard. Growth reverts to mid-single-digit comps as the base case, not the bear case.

- **Quantitative Signals**: Composite 66/100, but fragile. Quality (92/100: ROIC 30.3%, negative accruals four straight years, Piotroski 8/9) and Low-Vol (85/100: beta ~0.25–0.59, 21% vol) are the entire bull case; **Value scores 30/100 — the most expensive name in its comp set on every metric.** Note: the momentum and alpha claims in the raw quant feed were invalidated as stale-data artifacts — realised trailing-year risk-adjusted return is *negative* (alpha −9.2%), and the stock is −16% from its June high. Factor crowding (quality + low-vol is the market's most crowded pair) adds de-rating risk.

- **Financial Risk**: **LOW** — one of the strongest profiles in consumer retail. Reported $13.5B "debt" is misleading: funded debt is $2.87B against $6.23B cash = **net cash ex-leases**. 98.6x interest coverage, S&P "A", Altman Z ~6.4 on market cap, FCF $4.9B (+17%). A −30% revenue stress still leaves IG-comfortable leverage. The only vulnerabilities are valuation (3.1% FCF yield) and that 89% of FCF is committed to dividends + buybacks — a capital-return risk, never a solvency risk.

- **Non-Financial Risk**: **MODERATE**. Clean SEC/DOJ record and no securities litigation are real positives. Offsets: a non-independent Executive Chairman (former CEO, 20-year tenure) atop a long-tenured board; chronic store-level compliance dings (wage-and-hour, CPSC's then-record $13M penalty, hazardous waste); and a **one-way insider tape — ~$32M of sales clustered at $157–169 (within 5% of the all-time high) with zero open-market buys in 12 months**, including the CEO, Executive Chairman, and CFO. Programmatic, but the clustering is the strongest independent bear signal in the book.

- **ESG**: **AVERAGE / NEUTRAL**. Best-in-class Scope 1+2 progress (−44% since FY17) and mechanically sound governance (80% independent, one-share-one-vote, 92% say-on-pay). No thesis-relevant cash cost (~$5–15M/yr penalties). Structural caveat: the closeout sourcing model that creates the margin edge is the same feature that creates forced-labour traceability (UFLPA) and recall-compliance risk — no Scope 3 target, no published supplier list. No forced-selling or re-rating catalyst either way.

- **Alternative Data**: **Moderately bullish with one sharp caveat.** News tone maximally positive (15/0), holiday search ramp intact across all banners, and the +8% post-print gap is demand-endorsed. **But TJ Maxx search interest is flat over 12 weeks** — the Marmaxx problem is not visibly resolving — Ross is out-tracking TJX (+7.6% vs +2.0%), and the EPS surprise cushion has compressed to zero (Q3 landed exactly in-line). Data ends 11/8, pre-Black Friday: the decisive window is unobserved.

- **Short / Contrarian View**: **No forensic short exists** — Beneish −2.66, accruals negative four straight years, 125% cash conversion. The short is a *margin-cycle* short, and the evidence is specific: Q2 FY27's 33.4% gross margin contained 2.0pts of tariff refunds (~$0.21/sh, ~15% of that quarter's EPS); Q3 gross margin fell 11bp YoY (first decline); Q4 guided below prior outlook on fuel/freight; inventory days up 57.4 → 63.9 with inventory growing ~2x revenue. FY26's margin gains were explicitly credited to lower freight and shrink — both cyclical, both reversing.

**Bull Case**
- Structural share gainer in a counter-cyclical channel: off-price wins in good times and better in bad, and the current consumer (sentiment 48, 3.8% savings rate) is the ideal trade-down backdrop — Q3 comps +4% with International +8%.
- Elite, still-improving economics: 30.3% ROIC (4x WACC), gross margin +335bps since FY23, fortress balance sheet, $4.9B FCF funding a $3B buyback and a rising dividend.
- Visible growth runway: 7,500-store target, underpenetrated Europe/Australia/HomeGoods, and a favourable closeout supply environment from tariff-driven order cancellations.

**Bear Case**
- Valuation with no cushion: 26.5x forward / 28.7x TTM at a 21% peer premium; peer-median de-rating alone is −17% on TTM EPS, and a 5.13% 10Y with the Fed hiking removes multiple support.
- Margin inflection plus a mechanical FY28 air-pocket: gross margin rolled over in Q3, freight/fuel tailwinds have reversed, and the $331M tariff-refund benefit (~$0.19 of 9M EPS) rolls *out* of the FY28 base — EPS growth reverts to ~6–8% regardless of operations.
- Core engine stalling with insiders selling the top: Marmaxx comps decelerated to +2% (flat search interest says unfixed), Q4 comps guided +2–3%, and the entire senior team sold ~$32M near the highs with zero buys on the 17% drawdown.

**Key Risks**
1. **Multiple de-rating** in a bear-steepening curve — the dominant risk; ~79% of the stock's variance is idiosyncratic, and quality+low-vol crowding amplifies it.
2. **Tariff re-escalation** (Section 301/232) raising landed costs and thinning the closeout pipeline that *is* the moat.
3. **Tariff-refund consumer class action / state AG inquiry** — low financial cost, high headline velocity against a value-brand promise.
4. **Inventory risk**: days at 63.9 and rising into decelerating comps — watch H1 FY28 gross margin for markdown damage.
5. **USD strength** converting International/Canada outperformance into reported-EPS drag.
