## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH (moderate)**

Convergence: search momentum mildly positive, traffic strongly positive, holiday ramp intact and broad, news tone maximally positive, social washed out. The +8% gap was directionally endorsed by the underlying demand data rather than merely being a short squeeze.

**But with two clear qualifications:**
1. **Marmaxx (TJ Maxx) search interest is flat** — the largest division's problem is not visibly fixed. This is the single best reason to fade the relief rally's durability.
2. **Ross is out-tracking TJX on search momentum** (+7.6% vs +2.0%) — a mild relative-share yellow flag in off-price.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:**
- Google Trends is the only true quantitative series and it **ends 11/8/2026 — 10 days stale, missing both the earnings window and Black Friday**. The single most decision-relevant period is unobserved.
- Each keyword is normalized to its own 100 max, so **cross-brand levels (TJX vs. Ross) are not comparable** — only momentum and seasonal shape are. Share conclusions are therefore directional only.
- A 5-year request returned an identical 12-month window, so **no clean YoY and no multi-year structural trend** can be computed. 12-month seasonal comparison is contaminated by non-aligned week positions.
- Web traffic and social sentiment are **web-search snippets, not API series** — no visit counts, no scored sentiment. Job postings and patents are unusable/no-signal.
- **No store-traffic, card-panel, or geospatial data** — the highest-value alt inputs for an off-price retailer are absent.

**Bottom line:** Alt data supports the post-print move and points to a solid holiday quarter, but it does **not** confirm that the Marmaxx deceleration has been fixed, and the estimate cushion is the thinnest in seven quarters. Treat this as a leading-indicator tailwind with a 2–6 week lag, not a definitive call — and re-run search data after Black Friday, when the Marmaxx question resolves.
