## 1. Growth Story

TJX is the global scale leader in off-price retail and a structural share gainer as department stores, mall apparel, and traditional wholesale channels cede traffic. Its flexible closeout-buying model, deep vendor relationships, and “treasure hunt” format create a durable moat, while a recently raised long-term target of **7,500 stores** (from ~5,100 today) extends a visible **6–7% revenue growth runway** with 31% gross margins and ~$5B of annual free cash flow.

This is not a Cathie Wood-style frontier disruptor; it is a high-quality, cash-generative compounder. The key debate is whether 27x forward earnings is justified for high-single-digit EPS growth.
