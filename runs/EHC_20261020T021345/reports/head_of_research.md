### Investment Memo: EHC (Encompass Health) — 20 October 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (staged entry) | Conviction: **High on the business, Medium on the entry** — net Medium-High
**Price Target**: $145 (~19% upside from $121.71) | Range $125–155
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
EHC is the highest-quality compounder in U.S. post-acute care: the largest freestanding inpatient rehabilitation operator (~20–25% of national IRF beds), protected by certificate-of-need laws and the Medicare 60% rule, growing revenue ~10% (2x the peer median) at operating margins ~620bp above peers and expanding every year since 2022. Accounting is forensically clean (Piotroski 9/9, Beneish M-score −2.87, negative accruals, DSO improving) — the short desk declined to short. The counterweight is structural: ~75–80% of revenue is set by a single annual federal rule currently running ~1.4pp below CPI, and the stock enters Q3 earnings (28 Oct) up ~20% in a month with consensus at the very top of management's own guidance range.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector rated ATTRACTIVE — demographically underwritten demand, supply-constrained, EHC the clear share gainer. Macro is a grinding headwind: FY2027 IRF update +2.3% vs 3.7% CPI (a real cut), 10Y at 5.09% compressing the 10.7x EBITDA multiple, easing cycle stalled. Bear scenario $106; bull $159.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Geopolitical risk effectively nil (~100% domestic). Composite Medium: favourable 5th Circuit FCA dismissal (Nov 2025), but a $48m 2019 FCA legacy, an open CMS case-mix-redesign RFI, and MA prior-authorisation friction. Realistic sensitivity: 100–200bp of EBITDA margin, not existential.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD, accumulate <$105. Blended IV ~$140 ($125–155); price is a ~13% discount, not the 25% margin of safety required. Moat narrow but widening (ROIC 13.0%→17.6%). "A fair price is not a buy."
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY. 7-for-7 EPS beats (+9.7% avg), self-funded 6–10 de novos/yr, ~8.7% forward revenue / ~11.8% EPS CAGR, positive revisions; 23% upside to the $144–155 target cluster. PEG ~1.7 caps the "premium multiple" case — Buy, not Strong Buy.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL, quality-led. Quality top-tercile (monotonic ROIC), Value worst-in-peer-set (10.7x EV/EBITDA vs UHS 6.2x). Key flag: +32.8% 1y return vs +8.1% reversal-controlled momentum — essentially the whole year arrived in the last month; poor entry timing signal. Beta unestimable (R²=0.004 vs SPY).

Yellow flags: ~$21.7M CEO sale near highs with zero insider buying (10b5-1 status unverified — most checkable open item), and 34 of Medicare's 41 worst preventable-readmission outlier IRFs sit in EHC's portfolio.

Caveats: surprise magnitude decelerating (13%→5%), and an unexplained −4.8% high-volume distribution day on 19 Oct, seven sessions pre-print.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: NO EDGE — reject short. M-score −2.87, accruals −8.6%, OCF/NI 207%, receivables growing 3.4% vs revenue +10.5%. The honest bear case is valuation/deceleration (revenue growth faded ~12%→9.5%; Q1'26 contained a ~$0.40 margin one-off; consensus $6.11 = top of the $5.89–6.11 guide), not deterioration.

**Bull Case**
- CON/60%-rule moat + referral density drive a repeatable, self-funded algorithm: ~5–6% discharge growth + ~3–4% price/mix + operating leverage = ~12% EPS compounding at 17.6% ROIC vs ~7.5% WACC.
- Pristine earnings quality and a deleveraging balance sheet (1.74x) fund the pipeline, a 10%-growing dividend, and an expanded buyback — with FY2026 guidance already raised twice.
- Re-rating room to ~$145–150 (11.5x EBITDA) if estimates keep rising while the stock consolidates; macro bull case (10Y→4.3%) extends to ~$159.

**Bear Case**
- Single-payer concentration: FY2027 update +2.3% is a real-terms cut; MedPAC cites 17.1% and rising IRF Medicare margins — the exact condition for constraining future updates. Site-neutral/unified PAC payment (CBO-scored at $157B) is the structural tail.
- Entry risk is immediate: +20% in a month, distribution into the print, consensus at the top of guidance with a flattered Q1 base — a beat-and-lower-setup could gap the stock down 10%+ (it has happened twice in 15 months).
- Rate regime: 10Y >5% compresses a 10.7x multiple; refinancing the $2.7B stack at +125bp is a slow EPS headwind; FCF yield only ~3.5% given $736M capex.

**Key Risks**
1. Medicare reimbursement: sub-2% FY2028 update, case-mix redesign, or site-neutral expansion — hits margin and multiple simultaneously.
2. Same-store volume/price fade: same-store discharges +2.8% vs total +5.6%; net revenue per discharge +3.9% — two consecutive quarters below 2% on either breaks the algorithm.
3. Medicare Advantage denial/authorisation creep — a slow, hard-to-see margin leak.
4. Labour inflation (~60% of costs) vs an administratively fixed price.
5. Quality-of-care pattern (34 of 41 worst readmission outliers) converting into CMS penalty extension to IRFs or a licensure event.
6. Unverified insider-sale plan status; unexplained $143.7M FY2024 cash-flow/income-statement gap (likely presentation, unconfirmed — 10-K unretrievable).
