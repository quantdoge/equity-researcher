### Investment Memo: NOW — 30 October 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium-High on the HOLD call; low on near-term price direction
**Price Target**: $124 (+13% from current $109.67) | Accumulation zone $85–95 | Trim zone $145–170
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months; decisive catalyst is Q4'26 results / FY27 guide on **27-Jan-2027**

**Investment Thesis**
ServiceNow remains the strongest franchise in enterprise workflow software — 20.9% FY25 growth vs an 11.8% peer median, $29.7B RPO (+22%), ~6x the ITSM share of its next two rivals, and real AI monetisation (ACV target raised twice in 2026 to >$1.7B). But the Q4 cRPO guide of 18.5% — the first sub-20% print and the first backlog-to-revenue inversion — has exposed a genuine seat→consumption business-model transition at the exact moment the rates regime (10Y 5.05%, 2Y pricing hikes) is structurally hostile to long-duration software. The 53% drawdown has reset the stock to **fair value, not a discount**: quality is not in dispute, entry price is.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro UNFAVOURABLE — CPI re-accelerating (3.72%), 10Y at 5.05% (+50bp bear steepening in 3 months), 2Y +90bp over fed funds (hikes priced). NOW is "a rates trade with software attached" until the 10Y breaks <4.75%; a 2027 hike scenario (~35% probability) implies $80–85 on no estimate change. Sector NEUTRAL — agentic-AI repricing cycle ($234B spend exposed, seat-based vendors 21%→15%) — but NOW is the **best relative holding**: only peer still growing >20%, shallowest deceleration, ~11pp operating-margin self-help vs peers.

Watch: US federal procurement concentration (GSA OneGov amid DOGE-era scrutiny), EU AI Act enforcement from Aug 2026, and a post-selloff securities-litigation tail (plaintiff solicitations only; weak scienter basis).

At ~7.0x EV/Sales (cheapest ever), ~28.5x non-GAAP FY26 EPS, ~4.8% FCF yield — fair, with **no margin of safety**. Wide moat on switching costs; SBC at 15% of revenue is the core flaw.

Caveats: FY26 growth flattered by ~$10B of M&A (organic high-teens); the 7-quarter beat streak broke (−2% EPS miss). *(Report truncated mid-valuation; directionally positive, not relied on for the verdict.)*

**Bull Case**
1. **AI inflection**: >$1.7B AI ACV (+70%) converts to cRPO re-acceleration >20% by Q1'27; +13x consumption is real usage, and NOW owns the data/orchestration layer agents need.
2. **Rates normalise**: a benign CPI path takes the 10Y toward ~4.3%, violently re-rating a 20%-grower from 7x EV/Sales — bull case $170+. Seven of the last ten >10% down days were higher six months later.
3. **Margin self-help + capital return**: ~11pp below peer-median operating margin, 36% FCF-margin guide, and demonstrated aggressive buybacks ($2.2B in Q1'26) put a floor under the equity.

**Bear Case**
1. **Slope change, not cycle**: two more backlog undershoots put FY27 growth mid-teens and intrinsic value toward $95 — seat deflation eating the base faster than AI ACV replaces it.
2. **The 2027 Fed hike**: the 2Y already prices it; 10Y at 5.75–6.00% takes NOW to $80–85 with zero estimate change.
3. **Earnings-quality erosion**: gross margin down 550–680bp, GAAP EPS −44% YoY, net debt ~$6B — "rule of 57" is half-funded by SBC, and guidance credibility (36% FCF margin vs 22.6% delivered 9M) is exposed.
