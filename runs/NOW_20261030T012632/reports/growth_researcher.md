# ServiceNow (NOW) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $109.67 (close 2026‑10‑29) · Market cap ~$114.8B · EV ~$110.9B · Net cash ~$3.9B*

## 1. Growth Story

ServiceNow is attempting the most ambitious pivot in enterprise software: from IT service-management seat vendor to the **"Super Intelligence Control Tower"** — the governed action layer through which enterprises deploy, orchestrate, and audit AI agents across IT, security, HR, and customer service. The franchise is not broken — AI net-new ACV is tracking **$1.7B for 2026, up 70% since January** (raised twice), in‑production agentic AI consumption is up **13x YoY**, and RPO stands at **$29.7B (+22%)**. What broke on 28 October 2026 was the *narrative*: Q4 cRPO growth was guided to roughly **18.5% — the first sub‑20% print in company history** — and the stock fell **27% in a single session** on 5x normal volume. NOW is now ~46% below its June 2025 highs and trades at **~19x forward FCF and ~27x forward EPS** — the cheapest growth-adjusted multiple in its history, at a moment when its AI business is compounding 70%.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

ServiceNow's addressable market has been reframed around $250–300B (ITSM/ITOM, CSM, HR, security operations, GRC, low‑code App Engine, and now AI agent governance). Against ~$16.2B of FY26 revenue that is **~6% penetration** — still early. Two offsetting TAM dynamics matter:

- **Expansion:** the "AI Control Tower" creates a *new* category (agent identity, AI governance, non‑human identity security — hence Veza/Armis) that did not exist in the $275B framing.
- **Contraction:** Gartner estimates AI agents will **divert $234B of enterprise software spend by 2030** — much of it from exactly the seat-based workflow budgets ServiceNow sells into. ServiceNow is explicitly re-architecting contracts away from seats toward AI-consumption bundles, targeting **$30B ARR by 2030**. That is the right strategic move and simultaneously the source of the multiple collapse: nobody yet knows the net ACV per customer in an agent-priced world.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4 2026 results & FY27 guide — 27 January 2027.** The single biggest event. Needs: cRPO ≥18.5% guide, AI ACV ≥$1.7B, and FY27 subscription growth ≥17%. A "beat-and-raise with stable cRPO" is a 20–30% re-rating candidate.
- **Armis ($7.75B) + Veza integration.** First full quarter of security/IRM revenue contribution; watch whether security ACV is disclosed — that would validate the Control Tower as a second growth engine.
- **OpenAI partnership build-out.** New ServiceNow plugin for ChatGPT via Action Fabric, plus joint Security & Risk models and Codex integration in AI Workflow Factory — a distribution channel into OpenAI's enterprise base.
- **Google Cloud / Gemini Enterprise headless integration** (announced October 2026) — a template for becoming the "action layer" inside third-party AI assistants rather than competing with them.
- **World Forum (~May 2027).** Expected refresh of the 2030 $30B ARR target and disclosure of AI-pricing economics; a long-awaited "prove the model" moment.
- **Buyback capacity:** $1.84B repurchased in FY25 (vs $696M in FY24) with no dividend — an accelerating floor under the stock.
