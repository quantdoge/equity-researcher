## 1. Consumer Demand Signals

**Google Trends — the headline signal is confounded, and that matters.**
- Brand term **"ServiceNow"** (US, 12m): peak 100 (wk of May 31) → 41–50 through Sept–Oct, last 4 wks 50/43/41/46/44. That is a ~50% decay from peak and **below the prior-quarter trend**.
- But **"ServiceNow stock"** collapsed far harder: 100 (May 31) → **12–19** in Sept–Oct. Investor search interest has essentially evaporated relative to the June meme peak.

**Interpretation:** for a B2B enterprise vendor, Google Trends on the brand name is dominated by investor/retail attention, not buyer intent. The near-total collapse of the *stock* term proves the brand-term decay is attention-driven, not demand-driven. **Discount this as a demand read — it is a sentiment proxy in disguise.**

**The cleaner product-level read is "Now Assist" (the agentic AI SKU):** 100 peak (Apr 12) → trough 50–59 in August → **re-accelerated to 75 (Sept 20) → 62/56/65/64 into late Oct.** Last two weeks (65, 64) sit **above the Aug trough and above the trailing-3-month average.** This is the one genuine forward demand datapoint, and it is **modestly positive**, consistent with management raising the AI ACV target to $1.7B from $1.5B.

**Web traffic:** service-now.com legacy domain **−5.76% MoM**; signon.servicenow.com is 87.8% referral-driven (customer portal, not acquisition funnel). Low signal — one month, one source, no history.

## 2. Business Activity Signals

**Job postings — qualitative positive, no quantitative trend.** ~422 ServiceNow jobs on Indeed; LinkedIn listings skewed to **"Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer, Agentic Application"** and platform-architecture roles. The *mix* signals continued heavy R&D allocation to agentic AI rather than retrenchment. I could not obtain a month-over-month posting count, so **direction is unmeasurable** — treat as supportive colour only.

**Patents — DATA GAP.** USPTO PatentsView returned an API parse error on both attempts. R&D-intensity trend **unavailable**; no conclusion drawn.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **BELOW consensus (transitioning quarter)**

This is the crux. NOW **just reported** (Oct 28) and broke its streak:
- Revenue **$4.107B vs $4.095B** est (thin +0.3% beat); subscription revenue $3.99B.
- EPS **$1.00 vs $1.02 est → −1.96% miss.** First miss in 7 quarters.
- **Q4 cRPO guided to 18.5% CC growth, down from ~21% in Q3** — a ~250bp deceleration. This, not the EPS print, drove the collapse.

Prior-quarter history remains strong (**85.7% beat rate, +5.7% avg surprise**), but the *gradient* has decayed monotonically: +14.7% → +13.3% → +4.0% → +4.4% → **−2.0%**. The surprise tailwind is exhausted.

**Verdict: alt data tracks BELOW prior-quarter trends.** Product interest (Now Assist) is the sole counterweight, and it is too small and too early to offset a forward-bookings deceleration. Next print **Jan 27, 2027**, consensus $1.09 EPS / $4.369B — post-guidance-cut, so the bar is now lower.

## 4. News Sentiment Score & Summary

**Score: +0.625 (13 positive / 3 negative, 20 articles, 14d) — "positive." Treat with heavy scepticism.**
The +0.625 is a **keyword-counting artefact**, not a genuine tone read. The corpus is one post-earnings article burst in which "revenues rise" and "beat" get tagged positive while the actual content is bearish: *"Guides to Slower Backlog Growth,"* *"Q3 Earnings Lag Estimates,"* *"Tumbles 19% as Slower Contract Growth."* Negatives include a **SueWallSt investor investigation notice** (typical post-drawdown ambulance-chasing) and *"Debt and Interest Costs Jump: Is Cash Flow Enough?"* — a real fundamental question given the debt raise.

**Genuine news sentiment: negative/defensive.** A +0.625 score on a −27% day is a data-quality failure signal, not a bull signal.

## 5. Social Sentiment

**Reddit/StockTwits proxy: mixed-to-contrarian, no capitulation.** Dominant thread is **dip-buying and forensic defence** — "the 10-Q explains it better than the headline: cRPO…", "a good entry point?", alongside references to **−51% over 12 months** and the 5-1 split confusion. r/wallstreetbets framing ("Service NOW, now is its time") is contrarian-accumulation, not enthusiasm.

Read: **retail is catching a falling knife rather than pricing in a sustained downturn.** Historically that pattern is ambiguous-to-negative near-term — it signals the positioning flush isn't complete. Notably absent: any panic-volume "the thesis is broken" thread.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH (moderate)**

The alt-data picture **corroborates the guidance cut rather than contradicting it**:
- Forward-bookings deceleration is real and the surprise trend has flipped negative.
- Product-level AI interest (Now Assist) is stabilising off an August trough and the AI ACV target was raised — a **real offset, but second-order**.
- Attention/social data show a fatigued tape with dip-buyers, not a washed-out one; news flow is contaminated and worse than it scores.
- Hires still skew agentic-AI, so the company is **not** retrenching — this is a demand-growth-duration problem, not an execution or culture problem.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not high:** (a) the largest apparent signal — the Google Trends brand collapse — is **materially confounded** by investor attention and I have deliberately discounted it; (b) patent trend is **missing** (API failure, 2 attempts); (c) job-posting data is **non-quantitative**; (d) web traffic is a **single-source, single-month** estimate; (e) news score is a **keyword artefact**.

**What is high-confidence:** the earnings-surprise trajectory (hard data, 7 quarters) and the post-print price/volume reaction (75.3M shares vs ~13M avg, ~5.8× normal — genuine repricing, not noise).

**Data limitations & lag:** Google Trends is weekly and reflects *attention*, lagging actual bookings by 1–2 quarters at best. Web traffic and job data carry multi-week lag. Social data is a search proxy, not an API feed — no quantitative volume/tone time series. **Nothing here observes NOW's actual pipeline, RPO bookings, or enterprise IT budget data directly.** Alt data is a leading indicator and an imperfect one; this report should be weighted accordingly against the primary filings and management commentary.
