# ALNY — Alnylam Pharmaceuticals
**Price $165.04 | Mkt cap ~$22.3B (135M sh) | Net cash ~$2B (est. Q3-26) | EV ~$20.5B | No dividend**

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: NARROW (and widening, with a visible cliff)

Alnylam is the originator and clear leader of RNAi therapeutics delivered via GalNAc conjugation, with three commercially approved products and the only approved *silencer* in ATTR-CM.

**Sources of moat:**
- **Intangible assets (strong):** ~20 years of RNAi IP, the GalNAc delivery platform, and — most valuable — the HELIOS-B outcomes data (mortality + CV event reduction) that supports Amvuttra as a first-line ATTR-CM therapy. Hard to replicate; competitors need their own outcomes trial.
- **Cost/scale advantages (moderate):** in-house manufacturing, ~2,500 employees, 90% payer coverage with no step edits in Medicare FFS (~half of business). Gross margin 82% (2025), 75–78% in 2026 as product mix/royalties shift.
- **Switching costs (weak-to-moderate):** quarterly subcutaneous injection vs. daily oral stabilizers. Clinical inertia cuts both ways — it slows adoption (the 2026 problem) but retains treated patients (>80% share in ATTR-PN).

**Has the moat widened?** Commercially yes — TTR product revenue went $1.22B (2024) → $2.49B (2025) → ~$4.4–4.7B guided (2026), and Amvuttra captured ~35% new-to-brand ATTR-CM share within three quarters. Structurally, less so: **TTR is ~89% of product revenue**, and the competitive set is intensifying. BridgeBio's oral Attruby (acoramidis) is scaling, and Pfizer's Vyndamax loses exclusivity in **late 2028**, inviting generic oral stabilizers and, plausibly, commercial step edits. One genuine positive: AstraZeneca/Ionis' eplontersen **failed** CARDIO-TTRansform in Aug-2026, removing the nearest silencer rival — but the failure also raises class questions about whether deeper TTR knockdown delivers outcomes in cardiomyopathy, which matters directly for nucresiran.

**ROIC:** 24.3% (2025) vs. −13%, −26%, −68% in 2022–24. Returns now exceed cost of capital — the single most important fact in this file. This is a genuine moat inflection, not a promotional one.

**Positives:** Revenue compounding 40%+, operating margin reaching 24% in Q3-26, positive and accelerating FCF, **net cash ~$2B**, no buybacks/dividends squandering capital, and ~$6.7B of accumulated deficit/NOLs that will **shield cash taxes for years** — a real, underappreciated FCF tailwind now that the valuation allowance has been released.

**Deductions:** (a) **SBC of $348M in 2025 (~9% of revenue)** is a genuine economic cost and dilution runs ~1–2%/yr; (b) FCF conversion is distorted by working capital (2025: −$445M swing in receivables/inventory); (c) extreme historical earnings volatility; (d) reported "interest expense" (~$250–365M) is far above cash interest on $1.28B of converts, implying non-cash amortization — reported net income understates cash generation, but the disclosure quality is mediocre.

**Note on the Q3-26 blowout:** the $2.17B net income / $16.19 EPS was **not** operating performance. It was a **$1.9B non-cash release of deferred-tax valuation allowances**. Excluding it, Q3-26 EPS was ~$1.5. Any screen ranking ALNY on trailing P/E (70x) or "earnings surprise" is misreading this company.

## 3. Valuation Analysis

**Normalized owner earnings (2026E):** operating income ~$1.2B + D&A ~$56M − capex ~$60M − cash interest ~$65M, minimal cash tax → **~$0.9–1.0B**. EV/owner earnings ≈ **20–22x**.

**Intrinsic value (DCF, 10-yr, WACC 9%, terminal 3%):**
| Scenario | Assumptions | Value/Share |
|---|---|---|
| Bear | TTR peaks ~$5.5B, generic tafamidis + Attruby compress pricing from 2028; peak FCF ~$1.5B then −5%/yr | **$110–140** |
| Base | Revenue +22% CAGR to 2030 (below mgmt's 25%+), ~28% op margin, nucresiran PN 2028 / CM 2030 on time | **$250–300** |
| Bull | 30%+ margin, nucresiran succeeds, 2+ non-TTR blockbusters by 2030 | **$400–450** |

Sanity check against management's own "Alnylam 2030" targets: 25%+ revenue CAGR through 2030 implies ~$13B revenue and ~$4B operating income by 2030 — i.e., **~5.5x current EV**. Even at 60–70% of plan, the stock is cheap.

**Peer context:** trailing P/E of 70.8x vs. healthcare peer median 30.8x (+130% premium) is a *meaningless* comparison given the tax item; on normalized 2027E earnings the premium largely disappears. Sell-side targets cluster **$225–350** (Stifel $225 Hold is the low; Freedom $350), all set against a $165 price.

**Implied expectations:** at $165 the market is pricing roughly 12–15% revenue CAGR to 2030 and a mid-20s operating margin — materially below guidance, and roughly consistent with "Amvuttra stalls, nucresiran is a coin flip."

## 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Good (B+)**

- **CEO Yvonne Greenstreet** (since 2022) and CFO Jeff Poulton; deep bench, disciplined disclosure. Alnylam has met or exceeded every prior five-year goal (P5x25) — a rare record.
- **Capital allocation:** correct in kind — reinvested everything in R&D/commercial rather than paying dividends early; avoided large dilutive M&A; balance sheet fortified with converts now comfortably covered by cash.
- **Two blemishes:** (1) in 2026 management **expanded the sales force ahead of demand** and the demand did not follow — guidance was trimmed and TTR is now tracking the *low end* ($4.4B). That is a modest misjudgment of commercial ROI. (2) Guidance credibility has slipped (Q4-25 and Q2-26 both disappointed), which is precisely why the stock de-rated twice (−30% Jul-26, −25% Oct-26).
- Insider ownership is low single-digit percent; no buybacks (reasonable at this stage, though at $165 buybacks would now be value-accretive).

## 5. Investment Verdict: **BUY** (initiate modestly / scale in)

At $165 you own the category leader in a $10B+ and growing TTR market, with 24% ROIC, ~$2B net cash, a tax shield, and a platform option (nucresiran, brezosiran in Huntington's, zilebesiran) — for ~21x normalized owner earnings against a stated 25%+ growth ambition. My base-case intrinsic value of **$250–300** offers a **~35–45% discount**, satisfying the margin-of-safety test; the bear case (~$110–140) is roughly where the stock already trades, so the asymmetry is favorable.

It is not a Strong Buy because conviction in the 2030 glide path has been *empirically damaged* twice this year, and the 2028–2030 period holds a genuine competitive cliff plus a binary Phase 3. Buy in thirds: **$165 and below is attractive; add below $140; stop adding above $220; reassess if the base case is reached (~$280).**

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Amvuttra demand deceleration** — the core thesis. Q3-26 growth was 52% YoY but new-to-brand share momentum has stalled; prescriptions, not revenue, are the tell.
2. **Attruby (oral acoramidis) share gains + tafamidis LOE (late 2028)** — generic orals could trigger commercial step edits and price pressure; management's "combination is a tailwind" argument is unproven.
3. **Nucresiran is binary** — TRITON-CM readout determines the 2030+ franchise; eplontersen's CM failure lowers the class's probability of success even as it removes a competitor.
4. **Concentration** — ~89% of product revenue from TTR; a single franchise with a defined patent/competitive horizon.
5. **Guidance credibility / sentiment** — two guidance cuts in six months; further slippage could compress the multiple faster than earnings grow.
6. **Payer and policy** — Medicare dominates; IRA/step-therapy changes could hit a ~$20B-class injectable.
7. **SBC dilution** (~1–2%/yr) quietly erodes per-share value.
8. **Circle-of-confidence caveat:** intrinsic value here rests on clinical and reimbursement outcomes that cannot be underwritten with Buffett-style certainty. Size accordingly — this is a quality-compounder-at-a-discount position, not a bond-like franchise.
