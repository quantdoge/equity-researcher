**Investment Thesis**
Alnylam is the category-defining RNAi platform — 65% FY25 revenue growth, first GAAP-profitable year, 24.3% ROIC, ~$1.6–2.3B net cash, a $1B buyback — trading at a 66% discount to its October-2025 peak after two guidance-driven collapses (−28% July, −25% October). The market is pricing ~12–15% revenue CAGR to 2030 and a mid-20s margin versus a 21% consensus and a 25%+ company target; our base case values it at $250–300, and the bear case ($110–140) is roughly where the stock trades. The fraud thesis is dead (negative accruals, clean FY25 cash conversion, no SEC/DOJ action); what remains is a genuine but non-fatal credibility, concentration and pricing-power problem — so we own the platform, not the guidance, in a deliberately small, staged position.
