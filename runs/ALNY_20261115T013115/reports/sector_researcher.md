## SECTOR BRIEF — ALNY (Alnylam Pharmaceuticals)
**Sub-sector: RNAi therapeutics / rare-disease & cardio-metabolic biotech** (GICS "Healthcare" → "Biotechnology"; the broad Healthcare tag is misleading — peers must be drawn from biotech, not payers/distributors)

---

### 1. Sector Overview
Alnylam competes in the RNAi/siRNA modality niche nested inside the ~$2.4T (top-25 company sample) Healthcare complex. It is one of the few genuine growth pockets in an otherwise maturing large-cap biotech industry: FY2025 revenue growth was 65% for ALNY and 34% for Ionis, versus 9.6% (Vertex), 1.4% (Biogen) and 1.0% (Regeneron) — i.e., the legacy biotech cohort is in a patent-cliff/mature phase while the RNAi modality is still early-to-mid growth (global RNAi market ~$3.6B in 2026 → ~$9.1B by 2035, ~11% CAGR). The commercial centre of gravity is ATTR amyloidosis (TTR silencers + stabilisers), forecast to grow from a ~$6–7B amyloidosis market today to ~$35B by 2035 (~14.6% CAGR), with ATTR-CM alone projected at up to ~$64B by 2033 on diagnosis-rate expansion. The sector has shifted from monopoly to oligopoly in 24 months (tafamidis 2019 → acoramidis Nov-2024 → vutrisiran Mar-2025 → eplontersen/next-gen entrants + one-shot CRISPR), so rivalry — not demand — is now the binding constraint. Capital intensity is high (oligo manufacturing, global commercial infrastructure) but R&D/delivery-chemistry IP remains a real barrier to entry.

### 2. Key Tailwinds
- **ATTR-CM diagnosis pool expansion + outcomes evidence.** Only ~5,000–7,000 new US ATTR-CM diagnoses per year against a far larger prevalent pool; new 5-year HELIOS-B open-label-extension data (AHA, Nov-2026) show sustained mortality/morbidity benefit for vutrisiran — supportive of guideline positioning and duration of therapy.
- **Modality diversification past TTR.** Zilebesiran (hypertension, annual/semi-annual dosing), Regeneron-partnered CIDEBsiRNA (first-in-human MASLD data showed liver-fat normalisation after a single dose, Nov-2026), plus CNS/ocular — each a multi-billion TAM where ALNY retains economics or royalties.
- **Profitability inflection + capital return.** FY2025 was ALNY's first GAAP-profitable year (op. income $502M, 13.5% margin); a **$1B buyback** authorised Nov-2026 signals durable FCF — a rare signal in small/mid biotech and a support for per-share value.

### 3. Key Headwinds
- **ATTR-CM price/ share war.** Tafamidis ~$268k/yr, acoramidis ~$244k, patisiran ~$450k, vutrisiran ~$477k list. Acoramidis real-world data (HFSA, Sept-2026) positioned it favourably vs tafamidis; Intellia's one-time gene editing (nex-z) is a structural substitute threat to chronic silencers. HHI in ATTR is falling fast — top-3 share still ~85–90% but pricing power is eroding.
- **Demand deceleration / credibility damage at ALNY specifically.** Q3-2026 revenue growth slowed to **+16.7% YoY** ($1,458M vs $1,249M) from +66.8% in Q2 and +96.4% in Q1, on slower Amvuttra patient starts. Shares fell −28% (30-Jul-26) and −25% (29-Oct-26); securities-fraud investigations opened (Pomerantz, Glancy Prongay) and commercial leadership changed — an overhang on the whole RNAi complex's valuation multiple.
- **Gross-margin compression + IRA/payer pressure.** Q3-2026 gross margin **75.2%** vs 84.2% a year earlier (COGS 24.8% of revenue vs 19.2% in FY2025) on partner royalties and manufacturing scale-up; Medicare Part D redesign, inflation rebates and negotiated pricing constrain realised net price on $450k+ therapies.

### 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG** (but narrowing)
*Rationale:* ALNY is the only pure-play RNAi platform of scale, growing **~6.8x the peer-median rate**, with peer-par gross margin (81.8%), the only TTR silencer with a demonstrated mortality benefit in ATTR-CM, a self-owned oligo manufacturing base, and royalty exposure to partners' programmes (Regeneron, Novartis). It has crossed into GAAP profitability a full cycle ahead of Ionis and Sarepta, and the $1B buyback is evidence of cash generation, not just guidance. **Discounts to "strong":** operating margin (13.5% FY25, 21.6% 9M26) is still below the 19.1% peer median and far below Vertex (39.4%); gross margin is below Vertex/Regeneron/Ionis and deteriorating; and the +16.7% Q3 growth print means ALNY is transitioning from hyper-growth to high-growth with a damaged credibility premium (stock −66% from the $491 Oct-2025 peak). Position vs peers: **growth leader, margin laggard, second-tier multiple (5.9x sales already de-rated from ~15x).**

### 6. Sector Rating: **ATTRACTIVE (RNAi / ATTR-CM niche) — Neutral for large-cap biotech overall**
Structurally growing demand (diagnosis penetration), 11–15% TAM CAGRs, high barriers (GalNAc delivery IP, oligo manufacturing), and a visible profitability inflection justify an Attractive rating for the modality. The rating is capped by oligopolistic rivalry in ATTR-CM, one-shot gene-editing substitution risk, and IRA-driven net-price erosion. The alpha in this sector over the next 24 months will come from **share capture and durability of ATTR-CM patient starts**, not from multiple expansion — which is precisely the variable ALNY is currently being penalised for.
