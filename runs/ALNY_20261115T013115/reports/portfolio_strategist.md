## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (structurally bimodal)

This is not a disagreement about facts — every agent cites the same numbers. It is a disagreement about **what price already discounts them**.

- **Fundamental camp (BUY):** Value, Growth, Sector — 3 agents, medium-high conviction. Argue $165 prices ~12–15% revenue CAGR and a mid-20s margin, versus guidance of 25%+ and consensus of 21%.
- **Risk/technical camp (SELL/AVOID):** Quant, Macro, Alt Data, Non-Fin — 4 agents. Argue momentum is broken, the estimate revision cycle is incomplete, and the 340B/rate shock is dated and unreflected.
- **The bridge:** Short Analyst — "no edge at $165, no fraud, don't short here" — plus Fin Risk ("balance sheet fine, equity price is not") and Geo/Legal ("Medium, not Critical").

**Critical conflict resolution:** the *fraud* concern is **resolved against the bear** — Short Analyst's forensic work (negative accruals, 1.67x cash conversion in FY25, Beneish driven by growth not manipulation) and Geo/Legal's empty EDGAR enforcement search both clear it. The *disclosure-credibility* concern stands (7 firms investigating, two guidance cuts) but is a **multiple** problem, not a solvency or integrity problem. **The earnings-quality flag is real but non-fatal: strip the $1.87B non-cash tax benefit and Q3-26 underlying EPS is ~$1.5, not $16.19 — anyone quoting a trailing P/E of 70.8x or an "8x P/E" is wrong in opposite directions.**

The honest split: **the value is real, the timing is unproven, and the next 90 days contain a dated policy headwind (340B, 1-Jan-27) and a binary credibility event (11-Feb-27).**

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **2.0% of portfolio (initiate 1.0%, reserve 1.0%)**

**Rating band applied:** medium conviction + elevated risk → 1–3%. I land at the low-middle.

**Kelly check:** p(success) ≈ 0.55, upside +52% to base case, downside −25% to bear case → raw Kelly ≈ 38%. That is **unusable** for a single binary biotech and must be overridden. The binding constraints are:

- **Volatility budget:** 58.8% annualized vol vs. ~15–18% for a diversified book. A 2.0% position contributes ~1.2% of standalone portfolio vol — a 2.0% weight is roughly equivalent to a 6–7% position in a market-beta name. This is the real size limit.
- **Binary event risk:** R² vs SPY = 0.0175. Three >18% single-day moves in five months. Diversification logic does not protect this position; only sizing does.
- **Concentration discipline:** 87.5% of revenue from one molecule; adding ALNY to a book that already owns IONS/BridgeBio/Pfizer/Intellia doubles ATTR-CM thesis risk.

**Why not 3%+:** the 11-Feb-27 print is a genuine third-strike risk (two guidance cuts already), and the 340B rule lands 1-Jan-27 against 15–20% of AMVUTTRA volume in the same quarter. Paying up for certainty costs little when the bear case ($110–140) is near spot.

**Why not 0.5% or pass:** net cash $1.63B, FCF positive four straight quarters, ROIC 24.3%, base-case IV $250–300, and a $1B buyback authorized. The downside is bounded by a fortress balance sheet — the shareholder risk is multiple compression, not impairment.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss:** **No mechanical price stop.** At 58.8% vol with 25% gap risk on earnings, a price stop between $120–135 would be triggered by noise and would have exited the 24-Jun-24 +34% gap. Use **sizing as the stop** (max 2.0%). If a hard floor is required by mandate, use **$110** — the bear-case floor — and expect it to be hit only on a structural thesis break.

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any of these:**
1. **Third TTR guidance cut** at the 11-Feb-27 print (below $4.2B) → confirms structural penetration wall, not launch normalization. **Exit.**
2. **Gross margin below 74%** in any quarter → confirms structural loss of pricing power (trajectory: 85.6% → 81.8% → 77.3% TTM → 75.2% Q3-26).
3. **SEC subpoena or 8-K disclosing a formal SEC/DOJ inquiry** → flips legal risk from Medium to Critical. **Exit.**
4. **FDA Warning Letter** on AMVUTTRA promotion (escalation from two untitled letters) → franchise-level commercial risk on 87.5% of revenue.
5. **Nucresiran Phase 3 failure** or a pivotal design rejection → removes the 2030+ franchise.
6. **DSO above 85 days** or AR growth >1.5x revenue growth → channel-stuffing / receivables-quality signal.
7. **Combined exec insider selling without offsetting officer purchases** through a further drawdown → governance signal.

**Trim triggers:**
- **$250** (low end of base-case IV) — trim one-third.
- **$280–300** (base-case midpoint) — trim another third; reassess remaining.
- **>3.0% of portfolio** on appreciation — rebalance to target regardless of price.

**Calendar risk markers:** 1-Jan-2027 (340B rule; Q4 is first full quarter of UHC MA restriction), 11-Feb-2027 (Q4/FY26 + 2027 guidance), 2027 (nucresiran Phase 3 topline; brezosiran pivotal design).
