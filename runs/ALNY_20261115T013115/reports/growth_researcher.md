# ALNY — Alnylam Pharmaceuticals | Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $165.04 | Market cap $22.1B | EV ~$20.6B | 52-wk high $491.22 (−66% from peak) | Next earnings 2027-02-11**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Alnylam is the category-defining RNA interference platform — six approved products, 80%+ gross margins, and a technology that silences disease-causing genes at the source rather than managing symptoms. The thesis just got violently repriced: a 66% drawdown on two consecutive ATTR-CM guidance disappointments took the stock from ~7x forward sales to ~3.4x 2027 sales, while the underlying franchise still grew 65% in 2025 and 52% in Q3'26. The market is now pricing a plateau; the company's own "Alnylam 2030" plan targets 25%+ revenue CAGR through 2030 and TTR market leadership, and the nearest competitor (AstraZeneca/Ionis' Wainua) just failed in ATTR-CM. This is a platform-compounding story temporarily trading like a single-product patent cliff.

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration Opportunity

| Market | Size / Growth |
|---|---|
| Global TTR amyloidosis treatment market | **$25.4B by 2034**, 14.2% CAGR |
| hATTR (polyneuropathy) market | ~$3B in 2025, ~11% CAGR 2026–2036 |
| Alnylam TTR revenue 2026E | $4.2–4.5B |
| Hypertension (zilebesiran) | Multi-$10B; Phase 3 ZENITH CVOT running |
| MASLD/CNS/ocular (via Regeneron) | Additional multi-billion optionality |

**Penetration is the entire story, and it is early.** ATTR-CM is chronically underdiagnosed — the addressable pool in the US alone is measured in the hundreds of thousands, and diagnosis rate, not prescribing, is the binding constraint. Management discloses ~80% of new ATTR-CM starts are first-line (where Amvuttra is now positioned), >50% of new starts among experienced prescribers go to Amvuttra, and adherence exceeds 90%. That is a share-gain engine, not a demand problem — the problem is the *speed* of diagnosis funnel expansion. Alnylam is redirecting spend into diagnostic infrastructure and field force to widen the top of the funnel. The 2030 ambition — TTR revenue leadership plus 2+ new blockbuster medicines beyond TTR, 10 tissue types, >40 clinical programs — is a genuine platform TAM expansion, not a label extension.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 11, 2027 — Q4/FY2026 results** (revenue est. $1.525B): the single most important event. Does ATTR hit $4.2–4.5B, and does 2027 guidance re-establish credibility or cut again? A third cut would be thesis-breaking.
- **2027 — Nucresiran Phase 3 (polyneuropathy) topline**: next-gen silencer showed **>92% TTR knockdown** in Phase 1 vs. Amvuttra's 81% and Wainua's 70%. Launch targeted 2028 (PN) / 2030 (CM) — a best-in-class franchise extension that defends the TTR moat through the 2030s.
- **2027–2028 — Zilebesiran Phase 3 ZENITH cardiovascular outcomes trial**: a once-every-six-months injectable for hypertension. Largest un-modeled TAM in the pipeline; any positive CVOT signal is a major re-rating event.
- **2027 — Mivelsiran (ALN-APP) CNS/Alzheimer's data** and Regeneron collaboration readouts (CIDEB siRNA in MASLD showed first-in-human results Nov 2026) — validation of the "10 tissue types" platform claim.
- **Ongoing — ATTR-CM diagnosis-rate acceleration** plus first-line share data; also resolution of post-drawdown securities litigation overhang.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

**Supportive:**
- **Field force expansion**: active commercial hiring (Business Account Executive roles in South Alabama and San Francisco; Director, Global Compensation – Commercial) — consistent with management's stated step-up in diagnosis and customer-facing investment. A company cutting to the bone does not staff new territories.
- **Clinical demand quality**: >90% adherence, >50% new-start share among experienced prescribers, 80% first-line positioning — these are the metrics that matter for durability, and they are strong.

**Neutral to bearish:**
- **Google Trends "Amvuttra" (US, 5-yr)**: peaked at 97 in March 2026 and 94 in August 2026 (ESC data cycle), now 52–55 — down ~45% from peak. Reads as normalized post-launch interest rather than accelerating demand, consistent with the deceleration the company reported. Not a demand collapse, but no re-acceleration signal either.
- **Competitive datapoint is strongly positive and underappreciated**: Wainua failed CARDIO-TTRansform in ATTR-CM, effectively removing a key competitor; Pfizer's tafamidis generic challenge was pushed to 2031. The competitive field narrowed precisely as the stock fell.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

This is a high-conviction platform call available at a cyclical price. The 66% de-rating is a **growth-rate** de-rating, not a **thesis** de-rating: revenue still grew 52% in Q3'26, gross margins are 82%, the company just crossed into operating profitability (+13.5% margin), and the Rule of 40 score of 77.7 is elite. Meanwhile the competitive field narrowed (Wainua's ATTR-CM failure, tafamidis generics to 2031) and the next-gen asset is best-in-class by knockdown. At 3.4x 2027 sales and 18x 2027 EPS with a 0.88 PEG, the market is implying ~$5B of 2030 revenue — roughly 48% below consensus and 56% below the company's own 25% CAGR target. That gap is the opportunity.

**The math:** if ALNY merely earns consensus 2030 revenue of $9.7B at a 4.0x EV/sales multiple, EV = $38.8B vs. $20.6B today — ~88% upside over four years (~17% IRR) before crediting zilebesiran, nucresiran, or the Regeneron pipeline. If the 25% CAGR target holds ($11.3B) at 4.5x, upside approaches 150%.

**Why not Strong Buy:** credibility is now impaired — two guidance cuts in one year, growth decelerating 65% → 47% → 21% → 17% → 13% → 11%, and 86% of revenue from one molecule. A 25%+ CAGR target against a 21% consensus is a gap that must be *earned back* quarter by quarter. Position size accordingly; add on confirmation at the Feb 11, 2027 print.

## 7. Key Risks

- **Execution/credibility risk (highest)**: a third ATTR guidance cut in February 2027 would confirm a structural penetration wall rather than launch normalization, and would likely break the 2030 target. Watch Q4 ATTR revenue against the $4.2–4.5B range.
- **Single-product concentration**: Amvuttra is ~86% of revenue. Any safety signal, label issue, or pricing/reimbursement pressure in ATTR-CM is existential to near-term numbers.
- **Competitive threats**: tafamidis (Vyndamax) remains the first-line incumbent with protection to 2031 and vastly greater physician habit; BridgeBio's acoramidis is a direct oral competitor; Intellia's one-time gene editing (nex-z) could structurally obsolete chronic silencers late in the decade.
- **Diagnosis-rate ceiling**: growth is gated by how fast the undiagnosed ATTR-CM pool converts. If diagnosis infrastructure spending doesn't lift new-patient starts within 2–4 quarters, the deceleration narrative wins.
- **Pipeline binary risk**: the 2030 plan leans on nucresiran, zilebesiran, and mivelsiran. A Phase 3 failure in any one removes a large slice of the forward TAM and the "beyond TTR" optionality.
- **Accounting quality**: 2026 EPS is inflated by an unidentified one-time gain; GAAP net income of $314M on $3.71B revenue in 2025 (8.4% net margin) is thin, and interest expense of $253M is a real drag on a ~$1.6B net debt position.
