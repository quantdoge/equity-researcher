## Macro Report: Alnylam Pharmaceuticals (ALNY)

**Data as of 15 November 2026.** All series from FRED unless noted.

---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — Stance: **Risk-on at the index level, hostile at the discount-rate level**

The US is in a **late-cycle re-acceleration** regime, not a slowdown:

- **Growth is above trend.** Real GDP (GDPC1) rose to $24,611bn in Q3 2026 from $24,408bn in Q2 — roughly **+3.4% SAAR**, and **+2.1% YoY**. Three consecutive quarters of ~2.2–3.4% annualized growth.
- **Inflation is re-accelerating, not falling.** CPI rose to **3.82% YoY in October 2026** (index 337.476 vs 325.063), up from 3.72% in September. This is the opposite of the 2024–25 disinflation trade.
- **The Fed has shifted from cutting to a hiking bias.** Effective fed funds is **3.88% (Oct 2026)**, up from a 3.63% trough held Feb–Aug 2026. The FOMC **raised the IOER to 3.90% effective 17 Sept 2026**, held at **3.75–4.00% on 28 October in a 10–2 vote with both dissents favouring a hike**, and minutes point to another hike before year-end.
- **The long end has sold off violently.** The 10Y went from **4.45% (29 May)** to a peak of **5.31% (5 Oct)** and sits at **5.13%** today — the highest since 2007. Critically, this is a **real-rate shock, not an inflation-expectations shock**: the 5Y breakeven is only **2.30% (13 Nov)**, down from 2.54% in June. Implied 5Y real yield is ~2.6% — deeply restrictive.
- **Curve is bear-steepening, not inverting.** 2s10s at **+39bp** — no recession signal, but a rising term/inflation-risk premium.
- **Credit and risk appetite are fine.** Baa–10Y spread is **1.48% (12 Nov)**, near cycle tights. **XBI is +37.6% over the past twelve months** (109.39 → 150.55). There is no broad risk-off.

**The single most important macro fact for this stock:** ALNY is **down ~64%** over the same twelve months in which the biotech sector rose 38%. The drawdown is overwhelmingly **idiosyncratic** — Q3 revenue grew 52% but the stock fell 25% on 29 October on a "trifecta of uncertainties" (slower TTR demand, pricing/inventory, the 340B rule), and fell 28% on 30 July on a guidance cut. **Macro is an amplifier here, not the cause.**

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Justification: three of the four macro variables that matter most for a long-duration, single-franchise biotech are pointing the wrong way — **10Y at a 19-year high of 5.13%, a Fed with a hiking bias, and a firming dollar** — while the fourth (fiscal drug pricing via 340B) is a dated, quantifiable hit to the largest product starting 1 January 2027.

The rating is **not** a call on solvency or demand. It reflects that macro offers **no valuation tailwind** to offset the company's idiosyncratic problems. In a 4.4% 10Y world the market might have forgiven the AMVUTTRA guidance cut; at 5.13% it did not. Note also that high rates **erode the takeout optionality** that Oppenheimer and others now cite — a 5%+ cost of debt makes a ~$22bn strategic bid for a 2030-launch asset harder for Sanofi to justify, which removes a support that would normally exist at a 58% YTD decline.

### 5. The Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **Bear: a term-premium shock to 6%**

The consensus reads the current sell-off as an inflation story and expects the Fed to cap the long end. **The breakevens say otherwise** — 5Y BEI at 2.30% and falling while the 10Y rises. That means the long end is being driven by **real yields and term premium** (fiscal supply, Fed balance-sheet runoff, lost policy credibility with two dissents demanding hikes). If the 10Y breaks **6.0%** — roughly one more 85bp leg, well within the velocity of the last five months — the mechanical effect is severe:

- ALNY's 2030 nucresiran launch and 2027 brezosiran pivotal become substantially less valuable in present-value terms: another 85bp on a ~10-year duration asset is roughly **another 8% off fair value**, on top of what has already been lost.
- A December Fed hike, made more likely by CPI at 3.82% and rising, would confirm "higher for longer" and keep the entire long-duration biotech complex — including XBI, currently +38% — vulnerable to a sharp reversal. ALNY would be hit hardest precisely because it has already lost the confidence of momentum holders.
- Simultaneously, the 340B rule lands on 1 January 2027 against 15–20% of AMVUTTRA volume, so a macro de-rating would collide with a **known, dated, company-specific volume headwind** in the same quarter.

**Bull mirror-image (less likely, ~25%):** if labour cracks (unemployment is already 4.1% and drifting up from 4.0% in Jan 2025) and the Fed is forced to abandon the hike bias, a 10Y back to 4.4% would restore ~8–10% of PV to ALNY's pipeline plus re-open the takeout bid. That is the asymmetry worth monitoring: **the stock is now a leveraged bet on the long end, not just on ATTR-CM scripts.**
