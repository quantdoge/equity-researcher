## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT**

Not a dead heat — a **split with a bearish tilt on timing**.

- **Agreement on quality:** Growth, Quant, Fin Risk and Value all concede the underlying business is high-quality (ROIC 25.5% rising 19.0%→25.5% since 2022; best-in-class AUVs; debt-free; negative accruals). This is not a solvency or accounting story — the Short Analyst explicitly rules out fraud (Beneish −2.854, TASC −6.4%).
- **Agreement on deterioration:** Sector, Short, Nonfin and Macro all independently identify the same three problems — **margin structure reset, traffic/transaction decline, and structural cost rigidity** (premium sourcing + labor mandates + avocado tariffs).
- **The genuine disagreement is valuation vs trajectory.** Growth says 24.7x for a 25% ROIC brand with 8–10% unit growth is cheap after a 50%+ de-rate. Short says consensus 2027 EPS ($1.37) is 25–35% too high and a de-rating toward MCD's multiple implies $17–19. Both can be right: the multiple is *fair, not cheap*, and fair multiples do not protect against estimate cuts.
- **Conflict resolution — risk precedence:** No fraud flag exists, so the Short Analyst's accounting thesis does not override the Growth case. But **food-safety risk does take precedence over the growth narrative** — a repeat outbreak is the one event that can permanently impair traffic (2015 precedent: ~60% drawdown, comps −15% to −30%). That is a fat left tail sitting directly on top of an already-decelerating sales trend.
- **Decisive split:** Quant's verdict — *"quality and valuation keep this from a Sell; negative momentum and revisions block any Buy"* — is the most honest synthesis. Momentum and revisions lead price; neither has stabilized.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### **Target weight: 0.75% of portfolio — with 0.0% initiated today.**

This sits at the very bottom of the "medium conviction / elevated risk" band, deliberately below the 1–3% range, for four reasons:

1. **Kelly math is not favourable.** Upside to the $36–40 target cluster is +28% to +42%; the Short Analyst's downside math is −32% to −40% ($17–19). Odds are roughly 1:1 with a slight edge to the bear at current estimates. Kelly fraction for a 1:1 bet with a ~50/50 edge is near zero — this argues for a token/tracking position, not a core holding.
2. **Risk flags are numerous and correlated.** Food safety (repeat offender), labor regulation, tariffs, margin reset, negative revisions — five flags, and the food-safety and margin flags are *causally linked* (premium sourcing rigidity drives both). Correlated flags do not diversify within a position.
3. **Six-day binary event.** A 41%-volatility stock reporting Q3 on 28-Oct with consensus $0.29 EPS. Full-size initiation before that print is uncompensated event risk.
4. **Quality provides a floor on *survival*, not on *price*.** Debt-free with ~$1.4B FCF means no existential risk — which is precisely why 0.75% is appropriate rather than 0%. This is a "keep it on the watchlist with skin in the game" size, not an "own it" size.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Correlation — the strongest argument for holding a position at all.** CMG's 252-day correlation to SPY is **0.149**, to MCD **0.232**, to SBUX **0.346**, to YUM **0.363**. This is a genuinely idiosyncratic asset; beta 0.464 vs SPY and R² of ~0.022 mean roughly 98% of its variance is stock-specific. At 0.75% weight it contributes almost no portfolio beta and very little sector correlation — the *diversification* case is real even though the *return* case is contested.

**But that cuts both ways.** The low beta is not a low-volatility property — annualized volatility is **40.98%** with a **31.2% max drawdown** and a Sharpe of **−0.866**. At 0.75% weight, CMG contributes ~0.31% of portfolio-level volatility on a standalone basis. That is tolerable; at 2–3% it would not be.

**Sector/factor concentration — the main caution.** Restaurants sit inside consumer discretionary. The Macro Researcher's backdrop (CPI re-accelerating to 3.72%, Fed hiking, 10Y at 5.12%, sentiment at 51.7) is close to a worst-case for that sector. If the portfolio is already overweight discretionary or consumer, CMG compounds an unintended factor bet. **This position should be funded from consumer-discretionary exposure, not added on top of it.**

**Factor tilt:** CMG screens as high-quality (ROIC 25.5%) at a fair multiple (P/E 24.67 vs peer median 23.92, only a **3.1% premium**) — but with **negative momentum and negative revisions**. Buying it now is a deliberate "quality-at-a-fair-price, catching a falling knife" factor bet. That is defensible only at small size.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss: $23.00 (hard, −18% from $28.12).** Set below the $25 tranche-3 trigger and above the bear-case $17–19 zone; a break of $23 without a fundamental catalyst signals the de-rating is still incomplete. Given 41% volatility, a tighter stop would be noise-stopped.

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any of the following:**
1. **New food-safety event** — a second outbreak, an FDA warning letter, or evidence the 2020 DPA remediation commitments were breached. This is the precedence risk; do not wait for the price to react.
2. **Gross margin below 25% for two consecutive quarters** — confirms the margin structure has reset rather than temporarily compressed. Q2'26 was already 25.2%.
3. **Negative comparable restaurant sales** in any quarter — the Q1'26 "about flat" guide and Q4'25 −2.5% comps make this a live risk, not a hypothetical.
4. **FY guidance cut or a second consecutive downward EPS revision cycle** beyond the current −5.74%.
5. **Loss of investment-grade-like balance sheet flexibility** — e.g., buybacks continuing above FCF run-rate, pushing net debt materially higher.

**Trim trigger: $36–38.** This is the cluster of the most recent target revisions (Gordon Haskett $36, Raymond James $37, Stephens $38) and roughly 26x 2026E EPS. Trim 50% here. Full exit above $45 (Deutsche Bank's lowered target) unless gross margin has demonstrably recovered toward 26–27% and comps are running mid-single-digit positive — in which case hold for the 2027 EPS step-up.

**Position-level rule:** CMG must not exceed 1.25% of portfolio even on a full thesis confirmation. The food-safety tail risk justifies a permanent cap below the standard 3–5% high-conviction band.
