## NTNX — Alternative Data Signal Report
*Data as of Nov 27, 2026. Last print: Q1 FY27 (Nov 24, 2026). Next print: Feb 24, 2027.*

**Context:** NTNX beat Q1 (EPS $0.73 vs $0.609; rev $772.4M vs $762.8M; record 32.1% non-GAAP op margin; ARR $2.63B +15%) — and fell 15% to $46.30 on volume 6.8x average. Price is −37% from the Oct 12 high of $74.14. The market rejected the *quality* of the beat, not the beat. Alt data corroborates that read.

### 1. Consumer/Enterprise Demand Signals — Deteriorating
- **Google Trends "Nutanix"** (12-m, US): Peak 100 (wk of Feb 22). Q4 FY26 (May–Jul) avg ~64 → Q1 FY27 (Aug–Oct) avg ~48.5 → trailing 4 wks ~46. **≈ −25% QoQ.** Series has stabilised in the low/mid-40s — no post-earnings search bump.
- **Google Trends "Nutanix AHV"** (closest proxy to product-level deployment/purchase intent): Q4 FY26 avg ~64 → Q1 FY27 avg ~30. **≈ −53% QoQ.** Sharpest signal in the dataset; product-intent search has halved even as reported revenue grew 15%.
- **"VMware alternative"** — *unusable.* Series zeroes out from mid-July 2026 (sampling gaps). This is the key limitation: the entire NTNX bull case rests on post-Broadcom VMware displacement, and I cannot verify that displacement demand is holding.
- **Web traffic** — no quantitative data returned (SimilarWeb only gave relative comparisons). Gap.

### 2. Business Activity Signals — No usable signal
- **Job postings**: proxy returned noise. Indeed/LinkedIn "Nutanix jobs" counts (≈3.9k) are postings *mentioning Nutanix as a skill*, not NTNX headcount. Anecdotally GTM/corporate-strategy roles are live; no trend extractable.
- **Patents**: USPTO feed failed twice (API error). Gap. Note the company guided toward enterprise AI (~$20M GPU investment) — unverifiable via filings here.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **BELOW consensus**
Split verdict, and the split is the story:
- **EPS: likely still a beat.** 100% beat rate over 7 quarters, avg surprise **+17.9%**; FY27 op-margin guide raised to 24.5–25.5%, FCF raised to $875–975M. Cost/margin discipline is real.
- **Revenue/ARR/forward bookings: below.** Consensus models Q2 FY27 revenue of **$827.7M (+7.2% QoQ)**. That step-up is not supported by my data: **RPO bookings −2% YoY, NRR slipped to ~105%, fewer large deals and new logos**, and the Q1 beat was *pulled from backlog* ("spending its backlog faster than it refills it"). Management **left FY27 revenue guidance unchanged** at $3.18–3.23B despite the beat — in a beat-and-raise name, an unraised top line is itself a below-consensus tell.
- **Revision direction: negative.** BofA downgrade to Neutral, PT $78→$62, multiple compressed from 6x CY27 sales to 4x CY28 sales; other PTs cut post-print.
- **Alt-data read:** search interest −25% QoQ and AHV intent −53% QoQ line up with the bookings deceleration, *not* with a +7.2% sequential revenue acceleration.

### 5. Social Sentiment — **no current signal**
Reddit/StockTwits proxy returned only stale threads (2019–2024, e.g. VMware/Broadcom "goes brrrr" theses). No live retail read available. Gap.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH**
Two independent fact sets converge: (a) bottom-up demand proxies declining double-digits QoQ at the product level, (b) company-reported forward metrics (RPO bookings −2%, NRR ~105%, fewer logos) confirming the same. The 15% ARR growth is a *lagging* consumption of already-sold backlog. Margin strength is genuine but is a cost story, and margin beats do not re-rate a 15%-growth infrastructure name that just lost its CEO mid-AI-transition.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Reasoning: Google Trends (two correlated keywords, consistent direction) and the reported bookings/RPO/NRR disclosures are high-quality and mutually reinforcing. But **4 of 8 alt feeds failed or returned nothing** (patents, web traffic, social, job postings), and the single most important thesis-check — VMware displacement intent — is unavailable. Peer multiples were also unavailable (sector endpoint returned mega-caps: MSFT, NVDA, AVGO — not comparable to a $12.6B infrastructure software name).

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly, subsampled, and reflects *interest*, not purchase orders; the AHV series contains zero-value sampling gaps that inflate the apparent decline. Trend data lags actual bookings by roughly one to two quarters. Sentiment is keyword-derived and event-distorted. Volume/Option flow not modelled. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one — this is a *quality-of-beat* caution, not a call that the franchise is breaking.

**Actionable framing:** The Feb 24 print is a low-bar EPS setup against a high-bar revenue setup. Risk/reward hinges on whether AI-infrastructure orders (the stated reason for the CEO change and the $20M GPU investment) show up in **RPO bookings** before the backlog cushion is exhausted — watch RPO bookings and NRR, not the EPS line. On alt data alone, the mid-$30s stress case circulating is plausible before the backlog refill is evidenced.
