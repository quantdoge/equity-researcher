**Investment Thesis**

Nutanix is a genuinely high-quality franchise — 86.8% gross margin (best in its peer set by ~19pts), net cash (~$0.8–0.9B), ~29% FCF margin, negative invested capital, and real switching costs as the leading independent VMware alternative. The dispute across the team is not about quality — it is unanimous — it is about **price and the forward vector**. At ~4x FY27E sales and ~25x owner earnings (FCF less SBC), the stock is *fairly priced, not cheap*: Value's central intrinsic estimate is ~$42, roughly 10% **below** today's price. Against that sits a deteriorating forward picture (backlog-funded Q1 beat, RPO bookings −2%, NRR ~105%), an open-ended CEO vacancy with a troubling insider-selling pattern, and a hostile rate regime (10Y 5.13%, Fed hiking) that specifically punishes long-duration assets. The franchise deserves ownership; this entry point does not.
