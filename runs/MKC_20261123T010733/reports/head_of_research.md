**Investment Thesis**

McCormick is the best-run operator in a packaged-food sector in volume reset — +1.7% revenue growth vs. a −2.9% peer median, the flattest margin trajectory in the group, and the most GLP-1-resilient category (flavor) — with a 4.1% yield from a 103-year dividend record. But the equity is no longer a spice story: it is a $44.8B Unilever Foods combination now in a UK CMA Phase 2 review, which converts today's owners into a 35% economic minority with no board control, ~4.2x pro forma leverage, and −$3.7B of tangible equity. At $47.40 against a $45–50 fair-value range on *clean* TTM EPS of ~$2.47 (19.2x — the headline 16.2x is inflated by a ~$820M one-off gain), the probability-weighted return of ~+7% does not compensate for 33% realised volatility and the worst momentum in the staples group.

**Bull Case**
1. **Relative outperformance in a defensive category**: +460bp growth vs. peers, the most stable margins in the group, #1 global spice share, low private-label penetration, GLP-1-resilient mix; the sector laps easy impairment comps into FY27.
2. **Margin recovery is the re-rating lever**: gross margin has recovered 35.8% → 39.3% (Q3 FY26), restructuring savings are in flight, and full Mexico consolidation is EPS-accretive in FY27 — a credible FY27 guide could re-rate the stock toward the $55–59 analyst cluster (+15–20%).
3. **Floor + optionality**: 4.1% yield (41st consecutive increase), visible buy-side support at $43–44, ~1 turn of EBITDA below true peers — and if shareholders reject the deal, MKC plausibly re-rates *upward* as a standalone.

**Bear Case**
1. **Hollow growth**: organic ~2% with negative volumes, alt data showing demand decay in the highest-margin growth brands, and FY27 consensus requiring +30% EPS growth off the clean base while quarterly EBITDA falls (Q3: $299.5M vs. $379.7M).
2. **No balance-sheet cushion**: ~4.2x pro forma leverage, negative tangible equity, coverage down to 3.26x, FCF below dividends, and a deleveraging plan that current cash generation cannot fund.
3. **Deal overhang with adverse governance**: CMA Phase 2 (~24 weeks) with UK divestiture risk, dilution to a 35% minority with family voting control retained, integration 3x the acquirer's size, and ~100:1 net insider selling at multi-year lows.

**Key Risks**
- CMA Phase 2 requires remedies/divestitures or pushes close beyond mid-2027; loss of RMT tax-free treatment.
- Jan 28, 2027 print: FY27 organic guidance <1.5% or gross margin <38% ends the relative-growth and margin-recovery theses simultaneously.
