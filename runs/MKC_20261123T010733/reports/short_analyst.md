# MKC — Forensic Short-Seller Analysis
**McCormick & Company | Consumer Defensive / Packaged Foods | Price $47.40, Mkt Cap $12.75B**

**Context that reframes everything:** MKC trades ~45% below its mid-2023 high (~$85). On 31 Mar 2026 it announced a combination with **Unilever Foods**. Post-close, **Unilever shareholders own 55.1%, Unilever itself 9.9%, and existing McCormick shareholders only 35.0%**. The easiest part of this bear case has already been paid out.

**This is not a fraud short.** Beneish M-Score **−2.457**, below both the −2.22 and −1.78 thresholds. No accrual inflation, no revenue/EBITDA divergence of the kind that precedes restatements. I looked for the fraud case and it isn't there — I'll say so plainly.

**But the "7" F-Score is hollow.** Both quality-of-earnings legs **failed**: F3 (ROA improvement) = 0 and F8 (gross-margin improvement) = 0. The score is carried entirely by balance-sheet mechanics (F5 leverage, F6/F7 no dilution) and asset turnover. Profitability momentum is negative.

**The real quality problem: 2023 was a fluke, not a trend.** FY2023 OCF of $1,237M included a **+$269M working-capital release**; FY2022 and FY2025 both consumed working capital. Strip it out and normalized OCF is ~$950M–$1.0B, not $1.24B. Every "cash flow inflection" narrative built off FY2023 is an artifact of a one-time inventory liquidation.

## 2. Key Red Flags (with data)

1. **Organic growth is ~zero and the headline is manufactured.** Q3 FY2026 net sales +17.4%, but **organic growth only +1.9%** — the entire delta is **consolidation of McCormick de Mexico** (the Jan 2026 step-up to majority ownership). FY2025 organic was +1.7% revenue growth. Four-year revenue CAGR: **2.0%**; net income CAGR **1.1%**; EPS CAGR **1.2%** ($2.80 → $2.93 over four years). A company that has spent ~$8.6B on goodwill and intangibles has bought essentially all of its growth.
2. **Sales +17%, operating income −24.8%.** Q3 FY2026 operating income fell to **$217M**. Acquired growth is arriving with negative operating leverage — deal costs, stepped-up intangible amortization, and higher interest. Any "accretive to adjusted EPS" claim is non-GAAP scaffolding over a GAAP decline.
3. **Receivables growing 4x faster than revenue.** AR **+7.07%** vs revenue **+1.73%** in FY2025 (DSRI 1.052). With receivables at $629M, that's ~$43M of revenue sitting in the channel rather than in the bank. Modest, but it is the first year it has happened in three.
4. **Inventory days rising through a soft demand backdrop:** 98.8 (FY23) → 109.5 (FY24) → **109.3 (FY25)**. Up ~10 days in two years on 2% revenue growth. Consistent with volume weakness masked by price.
5. **Gross margin has never recovered to FY2021.** 39.5% → 35.8% → 37.9%. Still **160bp below** the pre-inflation peak on 8% more revenue. Pricing power is being shared with private label, not defended.
6. **Negative tangible book value of −$2.83B.** Goodwill + intangibles **$8.594B = 65% of total assets, 149% of book equity.** With organic growth at 1.9%, the carrying values of acquired brands rest on a knife-edge.
7. **Depreciation/amortization of only $231M against $8.6B of intangibles (2.7%).** Brand intangibles are held as indefinite-lived and **not amortized**. Capitalize a 20-year brand life and ~$250M+/yr of expense appears — roughly a third of net income. Reported EPS structurally overstates the economic cost of the acquired portfolio.
8. **Interest burden deteriorating despite deleveraging.** Interest expense $136.6M (FY21) → **$196.2M (FY25), +44%, even as total debt fell 26%** ($5.39B → $4.00B). Coverage: **7.6x → 5.7x.** With $3.1B of long-term debt to refinance at higher coupons, this gets worse before better.
9. **Working-capital tightness:** current ratio **0.70** ($2,138M vs $3,062M). Normal for the industry, but there is no liquidity cushion — cash is only **$95.9M** against $3.996B of debt.
10. **Capital returns consume the deleveraging capacity they need:** dividends $483M + buybacks $34.8M = **$518M vs $740M FCF (70%)**. Over FY2021–25, 64% of FCF went out the door while leverage stayed near $4B.

## 3. Beneish M-Score and Accruals Analysis

**M-Score −2.457 (Low risk).** Component-by-component, nothing screams manipulation, but three indices lean the wrong way:
- **DSRI 1.052** — receivables creeping ahead of sales (channel/fill signal, mild)
- **GMI 1.017** — gross margin deteriorating (consistent with the 39.5%→37.9% path)
- **DEPI 0.828** — the depreciation rate *fell 17%*; slower cost recognition flatters earnings
- **TATA −0.0131** — negative total accruals, i.e., earnings are cash-backed. This is the single most exculpatory number in the file.

**Verdict:** accruals analysis exonerates the income statement. The overstatement is in the **balance sheet and the non-GAAP presentation**, not in accruals. That's a materially weaker short.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

- **Private label is winning the price-value fight in center-store seasonings.** MKC is holding share only through promotion; hence 1.9% organic against inflation-level pricing.
- **Flavor Solutions (foodservice/industrial) is structurally lower-margin and more customer-concentrated** than Consumer — mix shift is a margin headwind, not a tailwind.
- **The moat is real but bounded.** Frank's RedHot, French's, Cholula, Old Bay are genuine brands with cultural sticking power; spice/heat is one of the few categories where private label under-penetrates. I do not think the franchise collapses.
- **The deal is the biggest competitive risk.** McCormick is a $6.8B-revenue company absorbing a **$20B-revenue** business — ~3x its own size, its largest transaction ever, for **$600M of run-rate synergies (net) by Year 3**. That synergy target is ~2.2% of combined revenue, which is at the aggressive end for food deals. **Approximately two-thirds by Year 2** is a schedule the company has never demonstrated at this scale.

## 5. Management Red Flags

- **Governance transfer, not a red flag of fraud: control.** Unilever complex will own **65%** (55.1% + 9.9%) of the combined company. MKC shareholders drop from 100% owners to a **35% minority with no control**. Whatever one thinks of the industrial logic, existing holders are being asked to accept a minority position in a business whose reporting, capital allocation and dividend policy they will no longer set.
- **Insider activity:** **25 Form 4 filings between Jul and Nov 2026**, clustered in January 2026 (around the Mexico transaction), September 2026, and **four on 18 Nov 2026 alone** — with no matching buy filings visible. Direction and dollar amounts are not resolvable from the filing metadata I could access, so I state this as a **watch item, not a proven net-sell signal.** I flag it honestly rather than dress it up.
- **Promotional tone vs. GAAP delivery.** "Accretive to adjusted EPS," "$600M synergies," "preeminent global flavor company" — while GAAP operating income fell 24.8% in the most recent quarter. Classic adjusted-metric gap.
- No auditor changes, no related-party issues, no restatements found.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary — Is there a credible bear case?

**Yes, but it is a structural/value-transfer short, not a forensic one.**

**The bear case:** You own 100% of a business doing $6.8B revenue with a cleanish balance sheet, and you are being exchanged into **35% of a $28B-revenue entity** controlled by Unilever, with lower blended margins, ~$600M of unproven synergies, no control, an integration that is 3x your own size, and a GAAP earnings trend that is currently going backwards. Organic growth is 1.9%, inventory and receivables are loosening, gross margin never recovered, tangible equity is **negative $2.83B**, and interest coverage has fallen from 7.6x to 5.7x. The combined company will carry meaningfully more leverage, and MKC's own dividend capacity gets subordinated to the integration.

**The honest other side — and it is strong:**
- **The de-rating already happened.** −45% from the 2023 high; 16x trailing EPS, ~11x FY2030E EPS of $4.43. Consumer staples at 0.67 beta with defensive cash flows.
- **No earnings manipulation.** Negative accruals, 1.22x cash conversion, Beneish −2.457. This is a *quality-of-reporting* critique, not a *fraud* critique. There is no restatement catalyst.
- **The bull case is stronger than my thesis on the numbers.** Gross margin recovering three years running (35.8→37.9%), debt down $1.4B since FY21, positive OCF every single year, no dilution in the pre-deal period, and a $600M synergy number that, if even 70% delivered, is >$6/share of pre-tax value on 268M shares. Management has integrated Cholula and FONA successfully.
- **Deal-break is a real, positive catalyst.** The transaction needs **McCormick shareholder approval**. If holders balk at being diluted to 35%, MKC re-rates upward as a standalone. My short is directly exposed to that binary.
- Altman Z of **1.582 ("Distress") is an artifact** of the tool's book-equity convention. Re-computing X4 on market value ($12.75B / $7.43B = 1.72) gives **Z ≈ 2.13 — grey zone, not distress.** Still not "safe," but I won't oversell it.

## 7. Contrarian Rating: **SHORT — modest conviction, event-driven**

Not "Strong Short." I could not substantiate manipulation, channel stuffing at scale, or an accounting time bomb. The genuine short is about **dilution, loss of control, integration risk and a negative tangible equity base financing an unproven synergy story** — and roughly half that story is already in the price.

**What would raise conviction:** Form 4s confirming sustained net insider selling post-announcement; FY2027 organic growth below 1.5%; synergy disclosure slipping past Year 2; a goodwill impairment on the brand portfolio; FY2026 combined net debt/EBITDA above 3.5x.

**What would kill the short (and I'd cover):** deal termination on the shareholder vote; organic growth re-accelerating above 3% with gross margin above 39%; FY2026 FCF above $850M funding visible deleveraging toward 2.5x.

**Position sizing:** small, paired against a staples basket rather than outright — the standalone franchise is defensive enough that naked shorting here is a beta bet, not a forensic one.

**Data gaps to note:** peer multiples not pulled; Form 4 transaction direction/amounts not resolvable from available metadata; combined-entity pro-forma financials not yet filed.
