## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate (with caveats)**

- **Concentration of power:** Stephen Squeri is *both* Chairman and CEO (since 2018) — classic CEO/Chairman duality. No named successor surfaced in searches despite Squeri being ~66; succession transparency is a genuine gap.
- **Board refreshment:** Recent appointments of two new directors (investing.com, 2026) and an active 2026 proxy season indicate ongoing refreshment; director Michael Angelakis made a $1.0M open-market purchase (Mar 2025) — directors demonstrate some skin in the game.
- **Alignment / insider ownership:** Insider holdings flat at **0.06%** (Trendlyne, Apr 2026) — extremely low for an alignment signal, though typical for a mega-cap. Institutional ownership ~95% provides external oversight.
- No related-party-transaction or insider-activity scandals; no short-form governance red flags from filings.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary — **Elevated**

- **DOJ Non-Prosecution Agreement (Jan 16, 2025):** ~$230M total to resolve a federal wire-fraud investigation (EDNY) plus CFPB civil allegations — deceptive marketing of credit card *wire products* and use of **"dummy" employer identification numbers** to help customers evade taxes. The NPA leaves criminal exposure **dormant but alive**: any breach reopens prosecution.
- **CFPB order (Jan 2025):** settled within the CFPB's end-of-administration flurry (~$206.6M across five cases) covering deceptive card practices.
- **Live AML matter (2025):** Law360 reported Amex "may face regulatory fines over its anti-money laundering compliance programs" and has been working to "identify and…" remediate deficiencies — an unresolved BSA/AML supervisory exposure with fine potential.
- **Recidivist enforcement history:** CFPB $85M refunds + $27.5M penalties (2012, CARD Act); ~$59.5M restitution + $16.2M fines for marketing/billing violations (2018); OCC $15M fine on third-party/vendor management; OFAC $430.5K sanctions settlement with American Express National Bank (2022). The pattern of consumer-protection settlements across 2012–2025 makes repeat-offender treatment plausible.
- No SEC enforcement actions surfaced in EDGAR search. *(Note: 10-K risk-factor text was unavailable via filings tool — regulatory picture built from press, DOJ/CFPB releases, and filing metadata.)*

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Cybersecurity & fraud exposure** — card network operators are high-value targets; fraud mitigation is a core competency but the attack surface (76,800 employees, global issuing/network) is immense; no major AXP breach alleged.
- **Key-person / succession concentration** — Chairman-CEO duality with no publicized succession plan.
- **Third-party and co-brand concentration** — Delta joint-venture and co-brand partnerships, plus a history of OCC censure for third-party vendor management.
- **Credit-cycle stress on collections ops** — rising card delinquency/write-off rates reported for spring 2026 signal operational strain in collections as credit quality softens.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **Moderate**

- **Platinum fee hike to $895** (from $695, 2026) is generating vocal customer backlash on social/Reddit, though strong new-card-account growth (reported ~doubled rates) shows the premium brand thesis still holds. Value-perception erosion is the watch risk, not an active boycott.
- No product recall / boycott / material ESG controversy events found in the news window.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **Moderate** *(regulatory dimension skews High)*

Material non-financial risk exists but is contained and monitorable.
