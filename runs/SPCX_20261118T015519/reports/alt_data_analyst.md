- **Web traffic −6.3% MoM is the one genuinely negative consumer datapoint**, but treat cautiously: it is a single SimilarWeb snippet, not a time series, and a maturing base installs the app/inherits referrals, so traffic growth should decelerate ahead of subscriber growth as a matter of course.

The estimate-revision direction has been persistently upward, and alt data does not contradict it.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (bullish fundamentals, bearish tape)
The operating alt-data stack — search intent re-accelerating, 1.6M net adds, 8.8% requisition rate, 2/2 beats averaging +111% — is genuinely constructive and points to a **fourth consecutive beat on 2/25**. What blocks a Bullish call is that **the stock is −37% from its $225.64 June high and −12% below its IPO-day close despite that record**, driven by mechanical lockup supply rather than demand erosion. Price is not confirming the alt data, and I will not fight a supply calendar with a search-interest chart.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
**High confidence:** earnings-surprise record, subscriber adds, Google Trends, news flow (all recent, all directly sourced).
**Low confidence:** web traffic (single snippet, no series), hiring (absolute counts with no baseline — cannot compute a *trend*, only a level), social (proxy via search, low participation).
**No data:** patent filings (feed broken ×2), app-store rankings/download velocity, satellite/geospatial.

**Data limitations & lag:** All of this is ≤2 weeks stale except the Q3 operating metrics (9/30). Google Trends is US-only and search terms ≠ revenue — "Starlink" interest includes prospective, canceling, and curious non-customers. Alt data here is a **leading indicator for demand, not a read on the AI compute segment that now drives SPCX's estimate revisions**, and it says nothing about the lockup schedule that is actually setting the price. Position sizing should respect that the dominant near-term variable is share supply, which is legally scheduled and thus not an edge.

**Actionable summary:** Alt data supports owning SPCX *into* the Feb print and does not support the −37% drawdown on fundamental grounds — but with zero informational edge on the unlock calendar or the AI segment, this is a Neutral signal, sized small, and worth revisiting as Q4 subscriber adds and post-unlock price action confirm.
