## SpaceX (SPCX) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $141.70 | Market cap ~$1.87T | Industrials / Aerospace & Defense | IPO 12-Jun-2026**

> ⚠️ **Data integrity note first.** FMP's valuation tool reports a $414.6B market cap using a 2.926B share count — that is the **pre-merger, pre-IPO weighted-average share count** from the FY2025 10-K. Post-IPO the share count is **~13.18B** ($1.867T ÷ $141.70 reconciles exactly to the profile's market cap; third-party sources put the cap at ~$1.98T). Any multiple built on $414.6B is understated ~4.5x. **All multiples below use ~$1.87T.** Separately, consensus 2028 EPS of $9.90 implies only ~8.67B shares — inconsistent with the 13.18B float-relevant count — so I anchor on net income ÷ 13.18B instead.

### 1. Growth Story

SpaceX is no longer a rocket company — it is a **vertically-integrated infrastructure monopoly** spanning launch, a 13.6M-subscriber LEO broadband constellation, and (post the February 2026 xAI merger) one of the world's largest AI compute platforms. The flywheel is self-reinforcing: Starship's ~10x-per-launch downlink advantage cheaply thickens the Starlink constellation, Connectivity throws off 45% segment operating margins, and that cash plus contracted hyperscaler compute deals fund SI capex. Q3 2026 revenue grew **161% YoY to $14.6B** with operating margin inflecting from -33.7% (2023) to **+19.2%** — the rarest thing in markets: a company simultaneously accelerating revenue and expanding margins at trillion-dollar scale.

### 3. TAM Assessment

- **Connectivity:** 13.6M subs (from 7.2M a year ago, +89%) against ~1.6B broadband households globally — **under 1% penetration**. Consumer revenue $3.1B in the quarter (+57%); enterprise/government the faster-growing $2.1B. Aviation alone: **12,000 aircraft contracted, 22M monthly users**.
- **Super Intelligence:** Contracted AI compute backlog is the revelation — Google at **$920M/month for 32 months (~$29B)**, plus a fourth capacity deal at **$1.11B/month starting December 2026**, taking SI to a projected **>$3.4B/month (~$41B annualized) by year-end 2026**. Hyperscaler AI capex is a multi-hundred-billion annual pool.
- **Space/Starshield:** Starship deflates $/kg by an order of magnitude, which *creates* elastic demand rather than just capturing share — plus NSSL, Golden Dome, and orbital data centers as wholly new TAMs. Starbase Louisiana targets "thousands of Starship flights annually."

Penetration is sub-1% in the largest vertical, which is precisely the asymmetry a TAM-first framework is built to find.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **~Dec 2026 — IPO lockup expiry (180 days from 12-Jun-2026).** The clearest *negative* catalyst; expect a supply overhang. Sized position entry opportunities likely cluster here.
- **Dec 2026 — Fourth AI compute contract begins revenue** ($1.11B/mo), converting SI backlog into reported run-rate and validating the 2027 revenue bridge.
- **25-Feb-2027 — Q4/FY2026 results** (consensus $22.8B qtr revenue); first full consolidated year and likely first formal 2027 outlook.
- **Starship cadence + Starlink V3.** Flight 14 was the first orbital *and* first revenue-generating Starship mission (26 V3 satellites, ~10x Falcon 9 downlink). Watch flight frequency and full-reusability proof.
- **Index inclusion.** Q3'26 GAAP net income of $4.4B may clear S&P 500 profitability screens; Nasdaq-100 fast-track is plausible. Passive demand at ~$1.9T cap is material.

### 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Hiring: ~1,911 (Indeed) / ~1,946 (LinkedIn) open roles**, skewed to *Starship* (Brownsville/Starbase) and — tellingly — **"Data Center (Starlink)"** and telecommunications technician roles. Physical buildout, not just R&D.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, high volatility — size accordingly)

The disruption is real and accelerating, the moat (reusability + vertical integration + owned constellation + owned compute) is about as wide as equity markets offer, margins are expanding at scale, and **contracted AI backlog materially de-risks the 2027 revenue bridge** that the consensus hockey stick depends on. Revision momentum is strongly positive: **forward EPS estimate revised +135% ($4.21 → $9.90)**, a **100% beat rate** over two reported quarters with **average surprise +111%**, and a near-universal post-Q3 target raise (Bernstein $267, BofA $275, Barclays $300, Cantor $345, JPM $500; median ~$250 — roughly +75% versus spot). Only HSBC sits at Hold with a $150 target.

I stop short of Strong Buy for three specific reasons: (1) **38.8x 2026 sales** prices near-perfection on a hockey stick whose 2028 revenue estimates span **$110B to $1.04T (10x dispersion)**; (2) **FCF is deeply negative** — FY2025 was -$13.95B and Q3'26 capex alone was $19.6B, implying $60B+ of annual burn and near-certain equity/debt raises into a post-lockup market; (3) the multiple's sensitivity is extreme — consensus 2028 at 25x earnings is only ~$2.2T (~+18%), so the return case relies on *beating* an already aggressive consensus.

### 7. Key Risks

- **Capital intensity / financing.** $19.6B quarterly capex, 84% of it SI. FCF conversion is the whole debate; dilution risk is real and recurring.
- **AI customer concentration.** SI's revenue rests on a handful of hyperscaler contracts. A crack in AI capex sentiment would hit the fastest-growing 52% of revenue and the multiple simultaneously.
- **Estimate dispersion.** $110B–$1.04T for 2028 revenue means the consensus number carries little information. Model your own case.
- **Lockup overhang** (~Dec 2026) into a stock already demonstrating 50% drawdowns.
- **Space segment deterioration.** Operating income swung from +$259M to -$7M as Starship R&D rose to $1,225M; if Starship slips, this drags longer.
- **Competition:** Amazon Leo/Kuiper entering service 2026, AST SpaceMobile in direct-to-cell, Chinese LEO constellations — though all remain far behind 13.6M subs.
- **Governance/key-man:** Musk-controlled dual-class structure, political entanglement, and single-point-of-failure leadership risk.
- **Execution:** Starbase Louisiana's 2029 target and "thousands of flights annually" are unfunded promises at this stage.

---
**Bottom line for the memo:** SPCX is the single best pure-play expression of disruptive innovation available in public markets — sub-1% penetration in broadband, a contracted AI backlog scaling toward $41B annualized, and margin expansion proving the flywheel works. Own it as a long-duration, modestly-sized position; use the December lockup, not the analyst targets, as the entry trigger.
