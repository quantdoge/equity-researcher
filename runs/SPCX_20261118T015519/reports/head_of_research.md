**Investment Thesis**

SpaceX is a genuinely exceptional *operating* business — vertically integrated across launch, a 13.6M-subscriber broadband constellation earning 45% segment operating margins, and now a hyperscale AI compute platform — sitting on a net-cash, investment-grade balance sheet. The listed *security*, however, is a different proposition: ~58x TTM sales, −$39B TTM free cash flow, headline profits flattered by depreciation lag and a related-party consolidation, a charter engineered to leave public holders permanently disenfranchised, and a supply/rate shock arriving in three weeks. The company will not fail; the equity can lose half its value without the company being remotely distressed. Six independent methods on this desk reached "no margin of safety"; none produced a defensible fair value above $99.

**Bull Case**
1. **The AI backlog is contracted, not hoped for.** Google ~$29B over 32 months plus a fourth deal at $1.11B/month from December takes Super Intelligence toward ~$41B annualised; Q1'27 guidance of $33–37B in a single quarter implies FY27 revenue >$140B — if delivered at 30% margins, every bear DCF on this desk is wrong.
2. **Starlink is a real annuity with unpriced options.** +89% subscribers at 45% segment margin, <1% penetration, direct-to-cell on owned 800MHz spectrum, 12,000 aircraft contracted, Starship V3 thickening the cost moat.
3. **Balance sheet and flows can defy valuation for a long time.** $51B net cash, triple IG ratings, HY OAS 334bp (cheap debt funding), potential S&P 500 inclusion, and momentum sponsorship (SPCX is Nvidia's largest holding; Tepper, Druckenmiller, BlackRock adding) — flow can outrun fundamentals well beyond $141.70.

**Bear Case**
1. **Arithmetic.** $141.70 prices the fantasy case ($155B+ 2031 FCF). Base IV $53, bull IV $99; six methods, six conclusions below spot.
2. **Earnings quality.** Q3 profit is substantially a depreciation-and-acquisition artifact; FCF is −$39B TTM and worsening; runway ~2.3 years forces repeated capital raises into a post-lockup market; ARPU erosion in the core franchise.
3. **Calendar collision.** Dec-8 unlock (up to ~797.6M shares) meets Dec-9 FOMC (91–100% hike odds, 50bp tail) on a 25-year-duration asset with real yields rising. Macro's non-consensus bear: **$85–95 with excellent operating news throughout.**
