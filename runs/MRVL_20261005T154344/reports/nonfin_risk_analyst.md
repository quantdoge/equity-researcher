1. **Single/few-customer concentration in custom AI silicon.** The Google deal alone is reported at up to **$120B cumulative revenue over ~6 years** — existential dependence on one hyperscaler program. 2. **Foundry/supply-chain concentration.** Fabless model concentrates manufacturing in TSMC-class foundries; AI-chip competition for co-packaged optics/chiplets capacity (peers: NVDA, AVGO, TSM) leaves little slack if allocation shifts.
3. **Key-person and leadership transition.** Chairman/CEO Murphy is the face of the AI strategy; the **8-K language confirms CFO Willem Meintjes' departure** ("...Willem has been an integral part of Marvell, and his…"), creating CFO transition risk into a period of hypergrowth and tight EPS scrutiny.
