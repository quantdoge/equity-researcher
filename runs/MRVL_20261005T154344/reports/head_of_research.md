### Investment Memo: MRVL (Marvell Technology, Inc.) — October 5, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate above ~$235; accumulate into drawdown zones | Conviction: **High** on the split (elite business / wrong price); Medium on timing of re-rating
**Price Target**: Accumulation/fair-value zone **$150–235** (14–45% below spot ~$272); bear floor $135–175; bull re-rate $300–360 only after multiple compression
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Marvell is a genuinely elite AI-infrastructure compounder — #2 custom-AI silicon (behind Broadcom), #1 data-center interconnect, +42% FY26 revenue inflection, NVIDIA's $2B strategic stake, and a $94B custom-silicon TAM — with an investment-grade balance sheet that survives stress. The investment decision is made by price and earnings quality, not franchise quality: at ~89x trailing P/E, ~169x FCF and ~2x peer-median multiples, the market prepays five years of flawless hyperscaler execution, while FY26 GAAP profits carry a ~$1.3B non-cash one-time credit and receivables/DSO blowouts trip a Beneish manipulation flag. Own it if held; buy only the air pockets, not the spot.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**
