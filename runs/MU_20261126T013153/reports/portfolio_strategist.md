## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT**

Not a split on *facts* — the agents overwhelmingly agree on the facts. A split on **what to do about them**.

**Genuine consensus (high agreement, 9 of 11 agents):**
- The **balance sheet is a fortress** and removes solvency/dilution risk (Fin Risk, Value, Short, Growth, Macro all concur). This is the single most agreed-upon point in the file.
- **80.7% gross margin is a cycle artifact**, not a structural floor (every agent that touched it — Sector, Value, Growth, Short, Quant, Fin Risk — says the same thing in different words).
- **The equity is not a fraud** and is **not shortable at $601** (Short Analyst explicitly; corroborated by clean accruals and cash conversion).
- **$601 is not a "cheap" entry** on any through-cycle lens — Value's $505 PWV and Short's $345 base bear bracket the downside; the 8x P/E is peak EPS.

**The real disagreement — and it is a genuine 4x dispersion, not noise:**
- **Growth** says buy the transformation at 3.4x forward; **Value** says the forward estimate is fiction and 90% of it should be discounted.
- **Macro** says the distribution is *bimodal* (bear −50% more, bull +40% in weeks); **Alt Data** says the near term skews to a lower guide; **Short** says both are right and therefore there is no edge.

**How I resolve it (agent-weighting by domain relevance):**

1. **The risk agents outrank the valuation agents on *sizing*.** Non-Fin, Geo Legal, and Alt Data all flag *dated, binary, near-term catalysts* (14-Nov antitrust ruling already landed; 9-Dec FOMC; 17-Dec print; 27-Jan-27 China export-truce expiry). Valuation does not set the entry date when the catalyst calendar is this dense — it sets the *cap*.

2. **The fraud pillar is absent.** The Short Analyst — the agent explicitly paid to find the problem — came back with "no edge" and validated the accounts. This **removes the tail-risk case for a zero position** and is why I do not recommend Pass.

3. **The Value Analyst's $505 is the binding constraint on size, not on direction.** He is not calling for a short (he grades it Hold, not Sell, precisely because $90B of FCF and net cash make an outright short reckless). PWV 16% *below* spot with a 25/45/30 distribution means **the expected edge is roughly zero to slightly negative** — which, under Kelly, argues for a *minimum* position, not a full one.

4. **The insider signal is corroborated twice and is the strongest single bear data point.** Non-Fin and Short independently derived the same $250–330M net sale / zero purchase pattern, with the CEO averaging ~$950. Insider revealed preference is the highest-quality evidence in the file because it is *behavioral, not modelled* — and it says management does not believe current margins are durable.

**Net synthesis: a high-quality balance sheet attached to a maximum-cycle earnings stream, trading roughly at fair value on a through-cycle view, with a dated negative catalyst calendar and a broken tape.** That is a **small-starter** setup, not a conviction buy and not a pass.

**Why 1.0% and not 0.5% or 3.0%:**

- **Why not larger (the beta argument is decisive).** MU's beta is **3.53 (252d) / 3.25 (504d)**, volatility **91.8%**, and R² is only **0.379** — two-thirds of its variance is idiosyncratic. **A 3% notional position contributes the risk of a ~10.5% SPY-equivalent holding.** In contribution-to-risk terms, 3% of MU is a large-cap core position, not a satellite. Sizing on notional here would be a serious error. At 1.0%, MU contributes ~3.5% of portfolio risk — appropriate for a single-thesis, single-cycle bet.
- **Why not 0.5%.** The fortress balance sheet (net cash $33.2B, 937x coverage) and the fact that the stock has *already* pre-crashed 50% mean the permanent-capital-loss scenario is remote. A 0.5% position is too small to matter if the bull case (Growth's 3.4x forward, re-rating to $1,000+) plays out. You need enough weight to be paid for the research.
- **Why not Pass.** Three independent agents (Short, Fin Risk, Value) confirm no fraud and no solvency risk, and the Short Analyst explicitly rejects the short at this price because "the bear case is correct and already priced." When the designated bear says stand aside rather than short, a zero position forfeits the convex upside.

**Kelly logic:** with PWV (−16%), a 25% probability of +66% and a 30% probability of −58%, edge ≈ −0.5% on a per-bet basis and the true estimate dispersion is 4x wide. Kelly with an edge indistinguishable from zero says **bet the minimum that preserves optionality and information rights.** 1.0% is that number.
