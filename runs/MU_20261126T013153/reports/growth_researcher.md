# Micron Technology (MU) — Growth Investment Analysis
**Price $601.25 (Nov 25, 2026) · Market cap ~$687B · EV ~$649B · Beta 2.37**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Micron has been structurally re-rated from commodity DRAM merchant to AI-memory infrastructure supplier. FY2026 (ended Sep 3, 2026) revenue exploded **+256% to $133.2B** with gross margin reaching an unprecedented **80.7%** (from -9.1% in FY23) as HBM and high-density server DRAM pricing cleared at scarcity levels. The stock, however, has round-tripped: **-50.4% from its $1,211 June peak** to $601, as BNP Paribas cut its target to $775 (from $1,700) on Nov 25, warning that "the risk of an oversupply scenario is mounting" for 2027. The entire thesis now rests on one question: **are 80% gross margins a new structural floor for AI memory, or the most extreme peak in memory-cycle history?**

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration

- **AI memory TAM**: HBM ~$35B (2025) → consensus $100B+ by 2028–2030; total DRAM TAM potentially $250B+ by 2030 as AI accelerator memory content per system rises 3–5x per generation.
- **Micron's position**: one of only three scaled DRAM/HBM suppliers globally (with SK Hynix ~$789B mcap, Samsung). Historically ~20–25% DRAM bit share; HBM share has been the swing factor, with capacity reported sold out through 2027.
- **Penetration**: On FY2026 revenue of $133B against a ~$200B DRAM+NAND market, Micron has already captured an outsized share — **the penetration argument is largely spent at current pricing**. Upside now comes from TAM expansion (HBM4/HBM4E content per GPU, SOCAMM, data-center SSD), not share gains.
- **Moat**: HBM is a packaging + yield + qualification moat, not a fabless-style moat. It is real but contestable — Samsung's HBM4 qualification is the single biggest threat to the 80% margin structure.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Dec 23, 2026 — FY27 Q1 earnings**: consensus $38.25 EPS / $61.75B revenue. The single most important event; a miss or soft guide validates the oversupply thesis. (>1 month)
- **HBM4 / HBM4E qualification & ramp for NVIDIA Rubin** — volume pricing negotiations for 2027 contracts set the margin trajectory. (Q4'26–Q1'27)
- **Capacity additions**: Idaho ID1, New York megafab, Hiroshima, Singapore HBM advanced-packaging plant — supply arriving is both the growth engine and the glut risk. (2027–2028)
- **Capital return step-up**: with $59B FCF and ~$38B net cash, a materially larger buyback or dividend initiation would signal management confidence in margin durability. (any quarter)
- **Post-downgrade estimate reset**: the sell-side is mid-revision; the shape of FY27 EPS cuts determines whether "3.4x" becomes 6x or 10x. (ongoing)

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, cyclical caveat)

The setup is genuinely asymmetric: **6.0x EV/EBITDA, 11.7x P/FCF, 8.1x trailing P/E, ~3.4x FY27 consensus EPS, net cash, flat share count, 100% beat rate.** You are buying the third of three global AI-memory suppliers at a multiple that already discounts a severe cycle downturn. If HBM4 pricing holds through 2027, the stock re-rates violently; if it doesn't, downside is cushioned by net cash and a 45% drawdown already absorbed.

But I will not call this a Strong Buy, and I will not treat it as a Cathie Wood-style multi-year compounder. **80.7% gross margin in a commodity memory market is not a sustainable equilibrium — it is a supply signal.** History (2018, 2022) is unambiguous: memory margins at extremes mean-revert, and the equity tops before the income statement does. This is a **cyclical growth trade with a structural overlay**, sized accordingly.

**Staged entry**: initiate now, add on confirmation at the Dec 23 print, and require HBM4 2027 contract pricing to hold before sizing to full weight.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **2027 oversupply (highest risk)** — BNP's call; Samsung HBM4 qualification plus industry-wide capex would compress pricing and gut the 80% margin. A 50% EPS haircut to ~$37 still leaves 16x — not a fire sale.
2. **Margin mean reversion** — gross margin fell from 45% (FY22) to -9% (FY23) in one year. The same amplitude exists on the way down.
3. **Customer concentration** — NVIDIA and a handful of hyperscalers drive the HBM book; a single architecture slip or dual-source decision is material.
4. **Estimate integrity** — FY2027–FY2030 consensus ($277B→$393B revenue) is implausible in its smoothness and does not appear cycle-adjusted. Do not underwrite to it.
5. **Capex treadmill** — $30.7B capex (and rising) must be financed through the cycle; FCF of $59B at peak margins can evaporate.
6. **Volatility** — beta 2.37, 50% drawdown in five months. Position sizing is a risk control, not a detail.

**Bottom line:** The business transformation is real and the quarterlies prove it (+256% revenue, 44% FCF margin, 100% beat rate). The stock's collapse says the market believes the transformation is priced at a cycle peak. At 3.4x forward earnings with net cash, I side with the transformation — **but I underwrite to a normalized margin, not to 80%.**
