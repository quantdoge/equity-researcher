**Investment Thesis**
Micron is a fortress balance sheet (net cash ~$33B, 937x interest coverage, Z-score 5.13) wrapped around the most extreme peak-cycle earnings stream in memory history — FY26 revenue +256% to $133B at an 80.7% gross margin, ~20 points above any prior industry peak. The 8x trailing P/E is not a bargain; it is the market correctly refusing to capitalize peak EPS. The books are clean (cash conversion 1.06x, negative accruals — the short thesis was explicitly rejected), but the people with the best visibility into 2027 pricing sold ~$330M of stock within 15% of the top and have bought nothing on a 50% drawdown. Own a little because the downside is capped and the option is convex; own only a little because the earnings are a cycle artifact.

**Bull Case**
- 75% of 2027 supply already sold, HBM4 ASPs reportedly +121% YoY, 2027 HBM sold out — the "peak" could be a plateau; FY27 EPS of $150+ makes $601 a 4x forward multiple that resolves violently upward.
- Structural change is plausible: HBM absorbs 30% of DRAM wafers by 2027, starving commodity bits; the only US-domiciled memory maker captures a supply-security premium and CHIPS funding.
- Fortress balance sheet removes the ruin scenario that made prior cycles catastrophic; $59B FCF funds capacity, buybacks, and survival through any trough without dilution.

**Bear Case**
- 80.7% gross margins have never persisted in memory; the supply response ($50B/yr industry capex, Samsung HBM4, CXMT) lands 2H27–28. Normalized EPS ~$16–37 implies fair value of $140–500.
- Insider revealed preference: ~$250–330M sold into $950s, zero bought at $601 — management does not underwrite current margins.
- Dated negative catalysts: antitrust answer due 7-Dec, FOMC 9-Dec, FQ1 print 17-Dec, China truce expiry 27-Jan — any one can reprice the stock double digits in a session (twice demonstrated in November).

**Key Risks**
