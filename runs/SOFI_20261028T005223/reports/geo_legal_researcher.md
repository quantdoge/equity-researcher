## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**① US financial-regulatory posture & supervisory intensity — Probability: High / Impact: Medium-High**
As a **bank holding company** (SoFi Bank, N.A. via OCC charter; Fed consolidated supervision), SOFI is directly exposed to shifting US regulator priorities. 2026 has seen a *recalibration* of the landscape: CFPB restructuring, an Executive Order (May 2026) directing a federal shift on fintech regulation with rulemakings due ~Nov 2026, and the OCC's "debanking" review. Direction of travel is currently *deregulatory/innovation-friendly* (a tailwind), but the **two-sided risk** is abrupt reversal, new fair-lending/BSA-AML expectations, or conditions on its charter (the OCC's original approval explicitly required SoFi to **stay away from crypto**).

**② US student-loan policy volatility — Probability: High / Impact: Medium**
SoFi's founding and largest lending vertical is student-loan refinancing, which is highly sensitive to federal program changes. The **SAVE plan was terminated (March 2026)** and IDR plans (SAVE/PAYE/ICR) are being phased out by July 2028. Policy whiplash cuts both ways (can spur refi demand as borrowers lose federal protections, or suppress it), creating **volume/credit-mix uncertainty** and headline risk for the Lending segment (~a large share of profit).

**③ Latin America expansion & FX/political exposure — Probability: Medium / Impact: Low-Medium**
The Non-US segment (+152% to $249M) is concentrated in LatAm via Technisys/Galileo. Exposed to **BRL/MXN/ARS currency volatility**, Brazil–US trade friction (new US tariffs on Brazilian goods in 2026), and data-localization/regulatory divergence. Absolute dollar impact is small today but is the fastest-growing geographic vector.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights
- **Securities class action:** SoFi has faced shareholder suits alleging misrepresentation (Stanford/FRT settlement trackers list a SoFi Technologies securities settlement paying out in Q1 2026 — reported at ~$200M; *verify exact figure in 10-K Note on contingencies*, which I could not retrieve — SEC direct fetch was blocked). **Confirm size/status in filing.**
- **Underwriter/IPO litigation:** **SoFi Securities LLC** is named as a defendant alongside Robinhood in a complaint (SDNY) tied to 2025 IPO underwritings — a contingent, likely immaterial but recurring exposure.
- **Data breach:** A **late-2025 breach** exposed customer names and dates of birth; consumer class actions and breach-related MTD activity reported. **Data-privacy/enforcement risk (GLBA, state privacy laws) is a live compliance item.**
- **Legacy consumer-finance settlements:** Prior CFPB-adjacent class settlements (e.g., 2016 ~$2.4M) show a pattern of consumer-protection litigation.
- **Charter conditions:** OCC national bank charter carries ongoing compliance obligations (BSA/AML, fair lending, crypto restrictions).

## 4. Compliance Red Flags
- **Bank-holding-company supervision** → full BSA/AML, OFAC sanctions-screening, and fair-lending (ECOA/HMDA) obligations; any lapse draws MRAs/consent orders.
- **Data privacy / cybersecurity** — post-breach exposure; potential state AG and regulatory inquiry.
- **Crypto guardrails** — charter-embedded restriction limits product expansion (strategic constraint).
- **Third-party/vendor risk** (Galileo/Apex/Technisys serve other institutions) → BaaS "sponsor" contagion risk if partners fail (cf. Synapse-style failures).

## 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM-HIGH**
*(Framework: sits between Medium and High.)*

**Rationale:** Geographic diversification is minimal (93% US), so classic geopolitical/sanctions risk is **Low**. But the company carries **elevated, structural regulatory risk** as a regulated bank holding company, a **policy-dependent** student-lending core, **active securities and data-breach litigation**, and a fast-growing LatAm segment adding FX/jurisdictional complexity. The **regulatory sub-score is High**; the **geopolitical/EM sub-score is Low-Medium**.
