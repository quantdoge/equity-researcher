### Investment Memo: SNDK — 26 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (speculative accumulation permitted for suitable mandates) | Conviction: **Low**

**Investment Thesis**
Sandisk is a pure-play NAND price-taker exiting the most extreme earnings peak in memory history (85% gross margin, $6.58 EPS in a single quarter) just as a contracted 2027 supply wave (+25–30% bits) meets cut Chinese CSP orders and a hawkish Fed at a 5.13% 10-year. The market has already done most of the work — a −75% drawdown leaves the stock at 4.7x trailing EPS, 2.6x FY27 consensus, 12.6% FCF yield with net cash and a $88.9–93.9B LTA backlog — so this is neither shortable (forensic view: cash is real, fraud thesis fails) nor ownable at size (normalised P/E ≈ 52x on mid-cycle earnings; the balance of evidence says EPS has peaked). The correct posture is to hold with tight risk rules, adding only on contract-price confirmation.

**Bull Case**
- **Valuation asymmetry**: 2.6x FY27 consensus EPS, 12.6% FCF yield, net cash; buybacks at this price retire ~6% of float per $5B, mechanically lifting mid-cycle EPS.
- **Contracted downside protection**: $88.9–93.9B of RPO/LTAs with price floors covering ~50% of FY27 and ~2/3 of FY28 bits — a mitigant no other NAND pure-play has, if enforceable.
- **HBF optionality**: co-authored the open High Bandwidth Flash standard; a design win converts NAND from commodity to qualification-locked content and re-rates the multiple.

**Bear Case**
- **The cycle has turned**: 85% GM is an all-time peak; contract prices go negative 1Q27 with 25–30% bit supply growth against cut Chinese orders. This business lost money in FY23, FY24 and FY25 — mid-cycle earnings power is the honest base rate.
- **Weakest structural position in memory**: no fabs (Kioxia can reclaim the transfer-price spread), no DRAM/HBM hedge, smallest of five suppliers, beta 5.65.
- **Alignment is broken**: unidirectional insider selling at the top, none at the bottom; $9.5B of buybacks incinerated at ~2.5x today's price.

**Key Risks**
1. Q1 CY27 contract settlements worse than −10% QoQ (Jan–Mar 2027) — the gating datapoint.
2. DSO >60 days or OCF turning negative — the forensic tell that peak earnings are being marked.
3. Renegotiation/impairment of LTA price floors — removes the sole bear-case mitigant.
4. Policy shock: Section 232 data-center exemption review, BIS rules, memory-chip tariffs (47% Greater China revenue).
5. Kioxia transfer-price reset or capex escalation without matching offtake.
6. Estimate risk: 8x forward-EPS dispersion means "cheap" is unverifiable.
