# AVGO — Financial Risk Assessment (as of FY2025, ended Nov 2, 2025)

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment: **Investment Grade**

Broadcom carries a true investment-grade profile: **A- (S&P) / BBB+ (Fitch)** (Moody's ~Baa1). S&P recently *upgraded* on AI momentum and projects adjusted leverage falling from ~2.0x (FY24) to ~1.2x (FY25) and toward ~0.8x. The credit story is fundamentally healthy despite a large absolute debt stock carried from M&A (VMware, legacy Broadcom).

**Why investment grade:** FY25 revenue +24% YoY, operating margin ~40%, and the debt load is *declining* in absolute terms even while cash grows — that is a deleveraging trajectory, not a leveraged blow-up.

No refinancing cliff: cash ($16.2B) covers short-term debt ($3.2B) ~5x, and AVGO demonstrated active capital access in FY25 ($4.5B new notes, $2.5B tender, frequent taps).

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **Moderate**

- **Credit risk: Low** — IG-rated, deleveraging, 8x coverage, resilient to a 20% demand shock.
- **Liquidity risk: Low** — 1.7x current ratio, cash >5x near-term debt, deep capital-markets access.
- **Market risk: High** — beta ~1.9–2.0, 52% vol, 41% max drawdown, FCF yield below the risk-free rate.
- **Composite: Moderate** — the balance sheet is fortress-like for its leverage; the risk lives in the *equity* (valuation + cyclicality), not the obligations.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Altman Z "distress" reading (tool artifact)** — driven by book-equity x4; market-cap Z is ~12. Do not misread as bankruptcy risk.
2. **Goodwill & intangibles = $130.1B, ~76% of total assets** ($171.1B) — heavy VMware-era acquisition premium; an impairment would hit book equity but is non-cash and does not threaten solvency.
3. **Negative spread: FCF yield 1.56% vs 10Y 4.99%** — the stock prices in years of flawless AI execution; any growth stumble triggers the high-beta downside above.
4. **Elevated leverage vs mega-cap semiconductor peers** — NVDA/AMD/TSM are extreme net-cash; AVGO carries $65B debt, so its coverage (~8x) is strong but *structurally thinner* than its sector cohort.
5. **Concentration risk** — revenue increasingly tied to a small set of custom-AI accelerator customers (design-flex wins); a single top-tier customer pause is the 20% scenario in section 4.
