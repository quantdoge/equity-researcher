**Investment Thesis**

Broadcom is the elite #2 franchise of the AI silicon supercycle — ~70% share of external hyperscaler custom-ASIC co-design, dominant 800G/1.6T Ethernet switching, and a ~$20B margin-durable software annuity — with extraordinary growth (AI revenue guided from $13B FY25 → ~$56B FY26 → ~$115B FY27) backed by a ~$73B backlog and 7/7 beat-and-raise quarters. The problem is not the business, it is the price: at 74x trailing P/E, 64x P/FCF and a 1.56% FCF yield *below* the 4.99% 10-year, the market capitalizes the maximum of the AI distribution with no margin of safety, two years of a receivables anomaly, and high geopolitical/regulatory overhang. Hold existing exposure under a hard cap; initiate only on proven FY27 execution at a flat price or a genuine derating toward the $240–300 band.

**Bull Case**
1. **Customer-backed demand visibility**: ~$73B backlog, a 5th XPU customer, OpenAI/Anthropic/Apple programs, and gigawatt-scale orders de-risk the $115B FY27 AI guide; company and hyperscalers are co-investing, insulating demand from single-vendor capex cycles.
2. **Elite, margin-expanding economics**: GM 67.8%→69.1%, ~40% OM, FCF conversion 116%, IG credit with disciplined deleveraging ($49B net debt, falling) — and chip efficiency is a *sales* driver on customers' power budgets.
3. **Execution tape + macro tailwind**: 7/7 beat-and-raise quarters, rising estimate revisions, easing rates, soft USD, no recession — growth, not multiples, has driven the 230% run since Jan-24 and can continue to.

**Bear Case**
1. **Valuation compression**: 74x trailing P/E, 1.56% FCF yield vs 4.99% risk-free — reverting to 45x peer median at flat earnings is ~−40%. The Dec-25 (409→330) and Jun-26 (493→355) sell-offs on *good* news preview the mechanics.
2. **Concentration + receivables anomaly**: ~3 hyperscalers drive the AI book; AR has grown 2–4x faster than revenue for two years (DSRI 1.55) and inventory days are rising — the early signature of a ramp running ahead of payable demand (or lengthening terms). A modest 10–20% FY27 miss lands fair value at $210–280.
3. **Eroding edges and a far-from-neutral state**: Google designs its own TPU architecture; Amazon/Microsoft/Meta run in-house programs; Marvell and Nvidia compete for the same capex; China (~20% rev) plus EU/SAMR VMware actions and a weakening enterprise brand for the software franchise.

**Key Risks**
1. AI guidance miss / hyperscaler capex digestion pause (the Dec-9 print and FY27 $115B guide are the pivot).
2. Multiples as the swing factor: β≈2 and 44–52% vol → −30% to −40% in any 20% market drawdown.
3. Two-front VMware regulatory squeeze (EU interim measures; SAMR fines/behavioral remedies) on the ~$20B software annuity.
4. Customer concentration: loss or de-prioritization of a single anchor hyperscaler = the −20% revenue scenario.
5. China export-control escalation and rare-earth input controls; key-person succession; $7.6B/yr SBC dilution; receivables anomaly persisting a third year.
