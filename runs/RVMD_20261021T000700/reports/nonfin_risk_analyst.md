### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: **LOW**

- **Zero EDGAR full-text enforcement hits** for RVMD. No DOJ, FDA warning letter, or OCIE matter identified.
- **Constructive regulatory momentum**, not friction: FDA approval of **RASONQUE (daraxonrasib)** in metastatic PDAC (Aug-2026) — first-in-class RAS(ON) inhibitor; **positive CHMP opinion 15-Oct-26** (EU authorization imminent); **Breakthrough Therapy Designation 14-Sep-26** for 1L PDAC + gem/nab-paclitaxel; Orphan Drug Designation.
- **Litigation — RVMD is plaintiff, not target:** RVMD alleges Erasca's ERAS-0015 infringes **U.S. Patent No. 12,409,225** and involves trade-secret misappropriation. Erasca shares fell ~48% on this; Erasca and its CEO/CFO now face securities class actions. Residual risk to RVMD is **counterclaim risk** (invalidity/unenforceability, discovery exposure of internal R&D records) plus management distraction — not damages.
- **Pricing/reimbursement** is the real regulatory tail risk: first-in-class oncology launch into CMS/IRA and EU HTA scrutiny.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Extreme single-asset concentration and binary clinical risk.** A ~$42B equity value rests almost entirely on daraxonrasib, which has roughly **eight weeks of commercial history**. The 1L PDAC opportunity (BTD, RASolute 309) and zoldonrasib (RASolve 308 in 1L NSCLC, first patient dosed 20-Oct-26) are the growth story, and each is a binary readout. RASolute 302's "unprecedented" OS (13.2 vs 6.7 months; 60% reduction in death risk) is now the hurdle — any replication shortfall is a step-function de-rating.
2. **Demonstrated trade-secret/IP security failure.** RVMD's own Erasca allegation asserts a **third party misappropriated RVMD trade secrets**. Whether or not Erasca is ultimately liable, this is evidence that proprietary know-how left RVMD's perimeter. For a company whose entire moat is chemistry and translational know-how, insider-threat/egress controls and invention-assignment hygiene should be audited.
3. **Scale-up execution: 883 employees absorbing global Phase 3 + a US/EU commercial launch.** Simultaneous first-patient dosing in RASolve 308 (global) and RASolute 309, a 1L PDAC BTD program, EU launch preparation, and $2.1–2.2B of guided 2026 GAAP opex — with a finance/legal/compliance function sized for a clinical-stage company. This is the classic control-gap window for disclosure controls, CMC/supply qualification, and promotional-compliance (sunshine act/off-label) risk. **Key-person risk** is acute around Goldsmith (Chair+CEO) and Kelsey.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **LOW-to-MODERATE, predominantly positive**

- **Strong tailwind:** FDA approval of a first-in-class pill for a cancer with ~3% five-year survival, NEJM publication, "practice-changing" framing in clinical media. Brand equity is currently excellent.
- **Watch items:** (a) **Price/access backlash** — "cancer centers race to access" headlines signal supply/access friction that can flip into pricing criticism; (b) **expanded-access/compassionate-use criticism** — patient-forum discussion exists about a trial ending in Oct-2026 without continued drug supply, a recurring reputational flashpoint for oncology launches and a potential media/advocacy issue; (c) real-world tolerability signals post-approval; (d) the Erasca fight could be reframed as a large-cap suppressing a smaller innovator.
- ESG: no meaningful environmental controversy; the risk sits in **governance and social (access/pricing)**, not E.

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

No fraud indicators, no enforcement history, no accounting irregularity flags, clean auditor, 98% say-on-pay, offtake-strong regulatory standing. That base case is genuinely clean. It is held back from "Low" by four things: (i) an **$42B valuation on a single asset with weeks of revenue history**; (ii) **CEO/Chairman duality** on a staggered board; (iii) **unidirectional insider selling into record prices**; and (iv) **evidenced trade-secret leakage**. Stock behaviour confirms the fragility: +29% (7-Jan-26), **−17% (26-Jan-26)**, +42% (13-Apr-26), −7% (16-Oct-26) — a name where idiosyncratic news, not beta, sets the price. (Note: the 6-Oct-26 decline was a **sector-wide** biotech selloff, not company-specific.)

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Insider selling cluster with zero buys** — obtain 10b5-1 adoption dates for Kelsey ($18.7M), Goldsmith ($18M), Kim ($3.6M), Anderson ($2.0M). Confirm no sales occurred while in possession of non-public RASolute/AACR data.

3. **Trade-secret exfiltration allegation** — request management's account of the third-party access vector and remediation; treat as an operational-security incident, not merely litigation.
4. **Capital structure overhang** — $2.2B raised April 2026 ($1.7B common + **$500M convertible notes due 2033**); $3.9B cash at 6/30/26 against $2.1–2.2B 2026 opex. Runway is adequate but the convertible creates future dilution/refinancing sensitivity tied to the same single-asset thesis.
