## Revolution Medicines (RVMD) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Pricing as of Oct 20, 2026 close: $196.55 | Market cap $42.1B | ~214M shares | Net cash ~$3B | EV ~$39B*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Revolution Medicines has crossed the hardest chasm in biotech: on **Aug 26, 2026 the FDA approved Rasonque (daraxonrasib)** — the first RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor — for previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer, just **135 days** after the Phase 3 RASolute-302 data that showed median OS of **13.2 vs 6.7 months** (HR 0.40, p<0.0001) versus chemo. That is not an incremental drug; it is the first targeted therapy to meaningfully extend survival in the most RAS-addicted of all common cancers, and it opens a pipeline-in-a-pill opportunity across RAS-mutant solid tumors (PDAC 1L/adjuvant, NSCLC, CRC) via elironrasib (G12C) and zoldonrasib (G12D). The company has gone from pre-revenue science project to launch-stage commercial oncology platform in under seven months.

### 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration Opportunity

- **2L metastatic PDAC (approved today):** ~67k US incidence, ~80% advanced, roughly 30–35k eligible 2L patients/yr; global incidence >500k. At ~$150–180k/yr US pricing, the 2L PDAC TAM alone is **~$5–8B US / $8–12B global**. Single-digit penetration today.
- **1L metastatic PDAC (the real prize):** 2–3x the 2L population. Phase 1/2 showed **47% ORR monotherapy and 58% ORR in combination**; RASolute-303 is a global registrational Phase 3 already enrolling, and the 1L combination holds **FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation**. Success roughly doubles the PDAC TAM.
- **Adjuvant/resected PDAC:** separate RASolute trial — an earlier-line, curative-intent expansion.
- **Beyond pancreas:** ~30% of all solid tumors are RAS-driven. KRAS-mutant NSCLC is larger than PDAC in patient volume; G12C (elironrasib) and G12D (zoldonrasib, with ESMO Oct-2026 combination data) broaden the franchise. This is how $7.5B of 2030 revenue becomes a **$15B+ peak-sales** argument. Penetration of the entire addressable RAS-mutant population is currently ~0%.

**Differentiation:** daraxonrasib is a *multi-selective* RAS(ON) inhibitor that hits G12D/G12V/G12R/G13/Q61 — the variants that actually dominate PDAC (~90% RAS-mutant, only 1–2% G12C). It is first-in-class, first-approved, and owns the only randomized Phase 3 survival win in the class. That is a durable 2–3 year commercial and data lead.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Nov 4, 2026 — Q3 print: first Rasonque revenue** (consensus ~$24M). The single most important near-term event: does launch uptake confirm the 2027 >$1B consensus? Watch also net cash (~$3.0–3.2B expected) and any FY26 opex re-guide.
- **H1 2027 — RASolute-303 (1L PDAC) enrollment completion / interim signals.** The largest single value driver; a positive 1L readout would be worth multiples of the 2L opportunity.
- **2027 — Adjuvant PDAC and NSCLC expansion cohorts** advancing to registrational design; G12D (zoldonrasib) and G12C (elironrasib) combination data readouts.
- **Late 2026–2027 — Ex-US regulatory filings/approvals (EMA, Japan)** and NCCN guideline inclusion (already expected post-ASCO plenary) — guideline placement is what converts a launch into a standard of care.
- **Ongoing — payer coverage/access decisions and IQVIA script trends** as the leading indicator of whether consensus's steep 2027 ramp is real.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (not Strong Buy)

The thesis is intact and the valuation is not demanding: at **5.2x 2030E revenue and 14.5x 2030E EPS**, the market is paying roughly consensus and nothing for 1L PDAC optionality, NSCLC expansion, or the G12D/G12C franchise. For a first-in-class, first-approved, practice-changing oncology asset with a 2–3 year competitive lead, that is a discount, not a premium. The +165% EPS revision and the broadening sell-side coverage support further multiple expansion.

Why not Strong Buy: (1) the stock has already **5.3x'd off the June-2025 low** and +287% over 12 months — much of the 2L de-risking is priced; (2) the next leg requires **RASolute-303 (1L) success**, a binary, readout-gated event still 12–18 months out; (3) single-product concentration with commercial execution unproven — the Nov 4 print is the first real test; (4) the reward/risk to the $260 median target (~+32%) is good but not asymmetric enough to justify maximum conviction given a credible $179 bear case.

**Scenarios (my estimates):** Bear (1L fails, 2L peaks $2–3B, competition erodes) **~$100–120**; Base (2L PDAC peak $4–5B, modest NSCLC) **~$220–250**; Bull (1L PDAC + NSCLC/CRC, peak $10–15B) **~$350–400**. Skew is favorable, with the bear case roughly -45% and bull +90%.

### 7. Key Risks

- **Clinical binary risk (largest):** RASolute-303 in 1L PDAC. 1L patients are less pretreated but combination tolerability in a frail, jaundiced population is genuinely uncertain. A miss here removes roughly half the long-term TAM and would compress the multiple to a 2L-only franchise.
- **Competitive threat — pan-RAS molecular glues:** **Erasca's ERAS-0015** showed 57% unconfirmed ORR in PDAC (Phase 1) and is widely framed as a potential best-in-class challenger; it also suffered a trial patient death (-35% on the news), which cuts both ways — it validates the class's toxicity hurdle and shows how fragile these assets are. Amgen (pan-KRAS), Verastem, Frontier, and Quanta are preclinically active. Daraxonrasib's lead is real but not unassailable; tolerability (rash, GI, edema) is the axis on which it could lose share.
- **Launch execution & reimbursement:** steep $1.2B→$2.5B→$4.6B consensus curve assumes rapid 2L penetration, broad payer coverage at high price points, and global rollout. Any script-trend or gross-to-net disappointment in the first two quarters would hit both estimates and the multiple simultaneously.
- **Financial:** FY26 opex $2.1–2.2B against ~$0 revenue; ~$3B net cash buys ~2 years. A **second equity raise is plausible in 2027** despite the $2.1B April 2026 raise — dilution risk is live and the April raise is why share count expanded from 190M to ~214M.
- **Single-asset concentration / IP:** the equity is effectively a levered bet on one molecule plus its backup franchise. Composition-of-matter on the tri-complex/cyclophilin-A mechanism has been challenged in the RAS space before; any IP invalidity or interference proceeding is a tail risk.

Conclusion should be re-underwritten after the **Nov 4, 2026** print.
