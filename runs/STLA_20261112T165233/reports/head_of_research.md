### Investment Memo: STLA (Stellantis N.V.) — 13 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL — exit core positions, commit no new capital. *(Carve-out: special-situations/distressed mandates may retain a ≤0.5% stub as an explicitly sized call option.)* | **Conviction: Low**

**Price Target**: $3.80 (−3% vs. current $3.93) — 12-month probability-weighted: 30% dilution/fallen-angel break (~$1.50), 45% muddle-through burn (~$3.50), 25% credible trough + macro relief (~$7.00). **The verdict is driven by the shape of the distribution, not the point estimate**: a fat, unhedgeable, management-controlled left tail (dilutive raise) against upside that requires proof.

**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Stellantis is a cash-flow failure in progress wearing deep-value clothing. EBIT covers 0.79× interest; three-year cumulative FCF is −€34.6bn; net debt has risen 8.6× in two years to €27bn; Altman Z is 0.83 (distress). The 0.19× price-to-book is not a margin of safety when €46bn of the €62bn book is goodwill and intangibles and the operating business is consuming the balance sheet through a €13.2bn nine-month working-capital outflow. The equity is a levered call option on a 2027 FCF inflection and a rates/oil reversal — a legitimate distressed trade, not an investable core holding.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Hostile on 6 of 8 channels. Fed hiking (10Y 5.13%, real 2.66%), SAAR 17.2M→16.0M, WTI ~$100, €1.6bn guided 2026 tariff cost. Sector late-cycle: flat ~91.8m units, weak pricing, China at ~70% utilisation exporting deflation. STLA was worst-in-peer-set on all three FY25 margin metrics; 9M-26 recovery (10.6% GM, 0.8% OM) narrows but does not close a ~4pt gross-margin gap to peers.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. Live confrontation with a G7 government over Brampton (PM Carney: "full force of the law"; C$220m+ at stake); USMCA/25% tariff exposure; *Harman* securities class action naming two successive CFOs and the Executive Chairman. Mitigants: UK diesel case largely won, CAFE easing, no FCPA/sanctions flags.
- **Value**: HOLD/AVOID. No moat; ROIC 20.6% (2023) → −22.2% (2025) → 0.8% (TTM). Probability-weighted IV of ~€10.7 has an honest range of €0.8–€32 — an admission of ignorance, not a valuation. The entire case is 0.19× book / 0.63× tangible book. Would buy only below €2.75 (~$3.18).
- **Growth**: SELL. ~0–2% revenue CAGR; Rule of 40 deeply negative; even 2030 consensus recovers to only ~half of 2023 operating profit. No innovation moat; China effectively exited. Explicitly a credit/special-sits asset, not a growth asset.
- **Quantitative Signals**: SELL — textbook value trap: top quintile on one static metric (P/B), bottom quintile on momentum, quality, and low-vol. Beta 1.02 is a statistical fiction (R² 0.074 — 93% idiosyncratic); vol ~54%; Sharpe −1.7. - **Financial Risk**: HIGH, bordering CRITICAL on cash flow. Interest coverage 0.79×; net debt/EBITDA 4.11×; one notch above high yield (S&P BBB− negative). A −20% revenue shock → negative EBIT, ~61× leverage, fallen-angel status, and an equity raise that existing holders cannot stop-loss. Mitigant: €29.7bn cash buys ~18 months.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. **Bull Case**
1. **Convexity from a bombed-out base.** A macro reversal (WTI $100→$70, 10Y 5.13%→4.2%, real yields →2.0%) mechanically re-rates a 0.064×-sales equity 50–100% with no change to the strategic plan. Consensus is positioned for the bear case, which is largely in the price.
2. **Self-help is early but real.** €6bn cost programme (≥40% implemented by end-2026), Q3-26 AOI +50bps YoY with North America +340bps, guidance reaffirmed, positive industrial FCF targeted for 2027. Jeep/Ram and a #1 Brazilian franchise (25.6% share) are genuine assets.
3. **Strategic floor.** €9.8bn market cap against €42.7bn liquidity; brand disposals (Maserati, Alfa), breakup or consolidation interest are live options. CAFE easing and the UK diesel win remove two cost overhangs.

**Bear Case**
1. **Solvency trajectory.** Coverage below 1×, Z-score 0.83, leverage 4.1×, and a refinancing wall into wider spreads. The #1 risk is a dilutive equity raise at 0.19× book — management-controlled, gapping through any stop.
2. **Demand data deteriorating faster than the P&L.** Record Jeep inventory overhang, EU share erosion, chip-disrupted Italian output, €1.6bn tariffs, and a SAAR rollover into a tightening cycle.
3. **Credibility deficit.** The charge-after-reassurance sequence, class action, CFO churn, and absent insider buying mean every future guidance number will be discounted — including the 2027 FCF promise on which the entire thesis rests.

**Key Risks**
- **Dilution / equity or hybrid issuance** — permanent per-share impairment; the single largest risk (Value, Growth, Fin Risk all flag it first).
- **Rating migration below investment grade** — fallen-angel forced selling narrows the exit.
- **USMCA/tariff escalation** (guided €1.6bn is an assumption, not a ceiling) and **adverse Canadian adjudication** on Brampton with retaliation risk to Windsor.
- **EU CO₂ costs becoming recurring** (~€2.5bn) plus a second forced-EV reinvestment cycle mid-decade.
- **Upside risks to a SELL call**: macro-reversal squeeze, faster cost delivery, breakup/activist event. These are why conviction is Low, not High.
