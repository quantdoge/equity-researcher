# Stellantis N.V. (STLA) — Alternative Data Signal Report
**As of ~13 Nov 2026 | Price $3.92 | Market cap $11.4B | 258,668 employees**

**Context:** STLA has fallen from ~$10.7 (Nov-2025) to $3.92 — **−63% in 12 months**, vs **GM +23%** and **Ford −6%** over the identical window. This is not a sector de-rating; it is company-specific. The path includes a −24% gap on 2026-02-06 (FY2025 results: $26.3B net loss), −8.9% on 2026-07-30 (Q2 miss), +13–15% on 2026-10-28 (Q3 beat), then a **−17% fade in eight sessions into 12-Nov**. Alt data explains the fade.

**The single most important datapoint:** Jeep carries **~1.9× the industry's days' supply** — the highest of any major US brand, alongside Ram (152) and Dodge (149). Management has guided December inventory back to Dec-2025 levels (Jeep 130, Ram 115), meaning **Jeep must shed ~39 days in one quarter**. That is only achievable via elevated incentives and/or production cuts — both margin-negative into Q4/Q1. Jeep search interest is fading exactly when the brand needs traffic, and the seasonal Q4 comparison is now *worse* than last year's 77–84 range.

Offsets: Ram 1500 volumes **+73% YoY** in Q3; Fiat Topolino sold out initial US allocations; heavy marketing cadence (Marvel *Avengers: Doomsday* tie-in, NASCAR return for Dodge in 2027) — brand spend is elevated but not yet converting to Jeep sell-through.

## 2. Business Activity Signals

- **Hiring: negative/contracting.** Indeed shows ~683 Detroit / ~1,372 Sterling Heights postings — but this is a static snapshot with no time series, and it sits against indefinite layoffs (~1,300 Belvidere), ~2,200 Brampton workers on layoff since Dec-2023, and Windsor production paused the weeks of Oct 19, Oct 26 and Nov 2. Footprint is shrinking, not investing.
- **Patents: data gap.** USPTO PatentsView returned an API error on both attempts ("Expecting value: line 1 column 1"). **No R&D-intensity read available** — a genuine hole, though for a company in retrenchment (non-core brands flagged for "cuts, closures or divestitures") patent growth is not the operative variable.
- **Divestiture activity (revealed strategy):** Brampton assembly sold to defense firm Roshel; Canadian PM Carney calls it "unacceptable" and threatens legal levers; Unifor (>9,000 workers) voted **97.3% to authorize strike**, negotiations at impasse. €6B Value Creation Program (40% by 2027) is real but is being delivered by subtraction.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **Tracking Below consensus (Q4/FY26)**

Next print: **25-Feb-2027 (FY2026)**, consensus EPS **$0.111**, revenue **$45.8B** — a knife-edge, effectively breakeven.

- **Tracking below on volume/margin:** 169-day Jeep overhang, chip shortage cutting **Fiat output in Italy potentially through December**, European share loss (**18.6% Q3 vs 19.1% Q2**, −50bp q/q), European pricing pressure, Unifor strike risk, and **€4B cash burn already in Q3**.
- **Tracking better on cost/self-help:** Q3 EPS −$0.01 beat, revenue $42.6B beat, guidance reaffirmed, positive industrial FCF targeted for 2027.
- **Historical reliability is poor:** 57% beat rate, and surprise magnitudes of −66%, −62%, −48%, +63%, +86%. Average surprise is meaningless here; dispersion is enormous. Consensus estimates also conflict across vendors for the same quarter (−$0.07 vs −$0.15 vs +$0.097 for Q3), which lowers conviction.

## 5. Social Sentiment — **Bearish, low and falling participation**

Stocktwits sentiment reads **Bearish** with **"Low" message volume and participation**. Mention volume **~55/day, down 44% vs three months ago** — retail attention is evaporating. Reddit is split between value-trap theses ("Why is STLA so cheap?", "Mid Term Play") and capitulation ("literally sinking...until new CEO"). **The contrarian fuel is gone:** retail has stopped arguing, which historically precedes continued drift rather than a bottom.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH**

Demand-side data (inventory 1.9× industry, Jeep search interest fading, European share erosion) and business-activity data (layoffs, plant sales, strike risk, chip disruption) are deteriorating *faster* than the reported financials because the inventory overhang converts to incentive spend and production cuts with a one-to-two quarter lag. The Oct-28 +15% rally was a relief trade on cost self-help; the subsequent −17% underperformance says the market re-read the demand data. At $3.92 the stock is deeply distressed (0.35× the revenue multiple implied a year ago), but **alt data does not support the "cheap optionality" case** — the operating deterioration is still in front of the P&L, not behind it.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Strengths:** Inventory/days'-supply (Cox Automotive, Sept-2026) and European share data are hard third-party numbers, not proxies; Google Trends, social and news sentiment are mutually corroborating; peer divergence is unambiguous.
**Limitations:** (a) patent tool failed twice — no R&D read; (b) job postings are a static search proxy, no time series; (c) SimilarWeb returned rankings only, no MoM visit series; (d) social data is keyword-proxy, not quantified; (e) inventory data is ~6 weeks stale and one vintage conflicts (Marklines shows Jeep at 102 days vs Cox 169) — I weight Cox as the more recent and internally consistent with management's Dec-2025 base of 130 days; (f) sell-side EPS estimates disagree by >100% for the same quarter. **The Feb-25 FY26 print is ~3.5 months away, so alt data cannot resolve the catalyst — only its direction.**

**What would change the call to Neutral/Bullish:** Jeep days' supply falling below ~130 by the December reading; European share stabilizing at 19%+; Google Trends "Jeep" recovering above year-ago 78; a ratified Unifor agreement removing strike tail-risk. **Watch days' supply above all else** — it is the cleanest, highest-frequency leading indicator for this name.
