### 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** — high agreement on quality, low agreement on price/timing

- **Consensus bullish:** AXON is a genuinely superior franchise — category leader, high switching costs, 30%+ growth, 40% ARR growth, 126% NRR, $15B+ contracted pipeline.
- **Consensus bearish:** The *current* equity is not cheap enough to compensate for 23%-of-revenue SBC, near-zero economic FCF, 60% volatility, legal/regulatory overhangs, insider selling, and a hostile rate environment.
- **The split:** Growth and Sector say “own the compounder”; Value and Quant say “not at this price”; Macro and Alt Data say “not at this moment.”
- **Resolution:** The quality case is real, but the **burden of proof is on FCF conversion and legal clean-up**, not on revenue growth. Until then, this is a watchlist name, not a core position.

### 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

**Base case: 0% — PASS on new capital at $313.**

If the portfolio mandate requires exposure to the theme, or the fund already owns it:

|---|---:|---|

| **After two quarters of positive FCF + legal clarity** | **2.5–3.0%** | If AXON proves cash conversion and resolves the overhangs, the quality deserves a larger position. |

**Why not 3–5%?** That sizing is reserved for high-conviction, low-risk ideas. AXON is high-quality but high-risk: 60.5% annualised vol, 1.27 beta, 64% max drawdown, binary legal/regulatory headlines, and a management team that just cut guidance and FCF in the same quarter. The Kelly criterion does not support a large position when the probability-weighted value is roughly equal to the current price.

### 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector:** FMP classifies AXON as Industrials/Aerospace & Defense. That is wrong economically. AXON is public-safety technology / vertical SaaS + hardware. Do **not** count it as defense exposure or as an A&D prime diversifier. Its true peers are MSI, TYL and OSIS.

**Correlation (252-day):**
- AXON–MSI: **0.08**
- AXON–TYL: **0.32**
- AXON–SPY: **0.32**
- AXON–QQQ: **0.23**

The low correlation to MSI/TYL looks like a diversification benefit, but it is partly an artefact of AXON’s idiosyncratic collapse. It adds **single-stock volatility**, not reliable sector diversification.

**Factor exposure:**
- High beta (1.27), very high vol (60.5%), negative momentum, expensive vs. own history, low GAAP quality, high SBC/dilution.
- If the portfolio is already overweight high-multiple software, govtech, or high-beta growth, AXON adds unintended duration/factor concentration.
- If the portfolio is underweight long-duration growth and can tolerate 60% vol at a small weight, a 0.5% starter is defensible.

**Position-sizing math:** A 1% AXON position contributes roughly **0.6% portfolio volatility** on a standalone basis. A 2% position contributes ~1.2%. That is the practical cap until volatility normalises or fundamental risk falls.

### 5. Entry Strategy

**Do not buy the full position at $313.** The stock is in a post-guidance-cut information vacuum, with sell-side targets still converging and the next fundamental catalyst on **23-Feb-2027**.

**Preferred staged entry:**
1. **0% now.** Wait for either price or proof.
2. **0.5% starter** only if:
   - price falls to **≤$250**, or
   - Q4 results show the ~$130M federal Dedrone revenue converting and FCF turning positive.
3. **Add to 1.5–2.0%** at **≤$225–235**, assuming no new accounting/legal shock.
4. **Add to 2.5–3.0%** only after two consecutive quarters of positive FCF and evidence that SBC is trending below 15% of revenue.

**Do not chase above $350.** At that level the risk/reward is worse than the current setup, and the position would be relying on multiple expansion rather than fundamental delivery.

### 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss / review levels:**
- For any position initiated near $313: **weekly close below $240** (−23%) triggers a formal review and likely exit.
- For a position initiated at $225–235: **close below $185** (−18–20%) is the hard stop.
- Because volatility is 60%+, use weekly closes, not intraday stops. A 15–20% drawdown is normal noise for this name.

**Thesis invalidation — exit or cut to zero if any two occur:**
1. FY2027 revenue growth guided below **20%**.
2. Another federal Dedrone deployment slips, pushing the $130M revenue beyond FY2027.
3. FCF remains negative through FY2027, or management cuts FCF guidance again.
4. SBC stays above **20% of revenue** with no credible reduction path.
5. Net debt / adjusted EBITDA rises above **2.5x**.
6. The securities investigation becomes a filed 10b-5 class action with a credible restatement/accounting allegation.
7. The ALPR class action produces an injunction against a core growth product.
8. US 10Y yields break above **5.75–6.0%** while growth decelerates.
9. Founder-CEO Patrick Smith departs without a credible succession plan.

**Trim trigger:**
- Trim at **$400–425** — roughly 90% of the Growth Researcher’s $475 base case — or if the position exceeds 2% of the portfolio through price appreciation.
- Also trim on any 10b-5 filing if the alleged class period covers the current guidance period and the company does not vigorously dispute it.
