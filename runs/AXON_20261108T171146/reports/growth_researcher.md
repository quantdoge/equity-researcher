## AXON — EQUITY GROWTH RESEARCH
*Pricing as of Nov 6, 2026 close: $313.00 | Market cap ~$25.4B | ~64% below the Aug-2025 peak of $870*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Axon is no longer a stun-gun company — it is building the vertically integrated operating system for public safety: TASER 10, Axon Body 4/5 sensors, Fleet, Axon Evidence/Fusus cloud, Draft One AI report-writing, and now drones and counter-drones (Dedrone, plus a $1.2B Skydio investment making Axon the exclusive US/Canada public-safety distributor). Sold as multi-year bundled subscriptions, revenue has compounded ~34% annually from $863M (2021) to $2.78B (2025) with **zero acquisition-led financial engineering** at the top line, and the software annuity — $1.75B ARR, +40% — is now the engine. The thesis just got repriced: a Q3'26 federal-timing miss plus a $1.2B drone bet cut the stock from ~$870 to $313 and the multiple from ~15x to ~7x forward sales, creating the first genuine valuation reset in AXON's public history.

### 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

Management sizes the opportunity at **$159B**, and — the single most important statistic in the deck — Axon currently captures **less than 1% of the average customer's public-safety wallet**. Wallet-share expansion, not new-logo growth, is the compounding mechanism: every agency that adopts TASER 10 also buys cameras, evidence storage, real-time crime center software, AI drafting, and now airspace security on one contract.

Reality check: the standalone body-worn-camera market is only ~$3.9B today (→$4.2B by 2032). The $159B figure depends on Axon *creating* categories — drone-as-first-responder, counter-UAS, robotic security, AI-native records — plus international, where penetration remains low single digit. That is a Cathie Wood-style TAM: enormous, self-defined, and only partly serviceable this decade. Bull case is not "win a $4B camera market"; it is "own the subscription layer of a $159B public-safety stack."

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 23, 2027 — Q4'26 results + first FY2027 guide.** Q4 guided to $990M–$1.01B revenue (re-acceleration to mid-20s% off a soft comp) and $245–250M adj. EBITDA. The whole debate resolves on one number: does the ~$130M of slipped federal Dedrone revenue land in 2027?
- **Dedrone / DHS counter-UAS IDIQ (through FY2027).** DHS selected Dedrone under a ~$1.5B IDIQ and has already placed first orders. Task-order flow is lumpy but asymmetric — each award lands as pure incremental Platform Solutions revenue.
- **Skydio distribution ramp (H1 2027).** $1.15B of new notes funded the $1.2B stake; exclusive US/Canada public-safety channel for Skydio drones. Revenue contribution vs. dilution/carry cost is the swing factor.
- **Inventory normalization after Q1 2027** (~$100M build) → FCF inflection. A swing to positive FCF would repair the Rule-of-40 story faster than any revenue beat.
- **Draft One / AI monetization** (AI revenue reportedly +700% in Q1'26) layered onto a $1.75B ARR base — highest-margin ARPU lever in the model.

### 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends ("Axon body camera," US, 5-yr):** flat-to-soft. Interest ran 22–36 through 2025, spiked to 100 in early March 2026 (news-driven, not demand-driven), then decayed to 19–25 by Oct–Nov 2026 — below year-ago levels. **Mildly negative**, consistent with the deceleration narrative; treat as sentiment, not order flow.
- **Hiring:** Indeed shows ~190–480 live Axon postings including Professional Services Managers and body-worn-camera program roles — continued go-to-market build-out, **neutral-to-positive** (no quantitative time series available).
- **Contract flow (best signal):** federal/state awards in Oct 2026 covering **22,000+ body cameras, TASER 10s, counter-drone systems and Skydio drones**; DHS IDIQ selection mid-Oct. This directly contradicts the "demand is breaking" interpretation of the Q3 miss — it is a *timing* problem, not a *demand* problem.
- **Price/volume capitulation:** 3.6M and 5.2M shares traded Nov 5–6 (vs. ~800k average), new 52-week low. Two-year low on climax volume often marks the sentiment trough in high-multiple compounders.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, size for volatility)

At $313 the market is paying **~7.3x EV/FY26E sales** and **~30x FY2027E adjusted EPS** for a business growing 30%+ with 40% ARR growth and ~25.5% EBITDA margins. For context, AXON historically cleared 12–15x sales. Back-of-envelope: 8.5x EV/$4.81B FY2027 revenue ≈ **$475/share base case (+50%)**; 11x bull case ≈ $600; 5.5x bear ≈ $300. You are being paid to underwrite a company that is still compounding, at a multiple that implies growth dies in 2027.

Why not Strong Buy: 22.8% SBC, negative Q3 FCF, two guidance-related misses in five quarters, and a management team that just chose to spend $1.2B on a drone equity stake while free cash flow was negative. Buy, not back-up-the-truck — yet.

### 7. Key Risks

1. **Execution/lumpiness risk (highest):** Federal Dedrone deals are large, discrete, and slip. A second push into 2028 would be a credibility event, not a modeling event.
2. **Competitive threat:** Flock Safety is expanding from ALPR into the broader surveillance/AI platform — the direct fight for the "public safety OS"; Motorola Solutions (V200, Vigilant) defends incumbency; defense primes and Anduril-class players contest counter-UAS, where Axon has the least moat.
3. **Dilution & capital structure:** diluted shares 78.6M (2024) → 100M (2025); ~$1B converts plus $1.15B new notes. Adjusted EPS excludes a stock-comp expense that is nearly a quarter of revenue.
4. **Cash conversion:** FY2025 working capital drain of −$482M, $136M capex, Q3'26 negative FCF. "High growth" and "low cash generation" can coexist for a while — but not indefinitely at 30x+ earnings.
5. **Mix-driven margin erosion:** gross margin down ~150bps from the 2023 peak as hardware (Dedrone +106%) outgrows software. If Platform Solutions dominates, the multiple should compress structurally.
6. **Regulatory/privacy/AI-liability:** ALPR and drone surveillance face municipal pushback (and Flock-Axon privacy scrutiny); AI-generated police reports carry evidentiary and liability exposure.
7. **International execution:** the incremental TAM requires winning outside the US, where Axon's channel and brand are far weaker.
