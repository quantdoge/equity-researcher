## Rubrik, Inc. (NYSE: RBRK) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $110.00 | Market cap ~$22.6B | EV ~$21.1B | Cash + ST investments $1.75B (7/31/26)*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Rubrik has re-positioned from a backup/recovery vendor into the control plane for **cyber resilience and now agentic AI security** — a rare position where the data-protection install base becomes the trust layer governing what autonomous AI agents are allowed to touch, and how they get rewound when they misbehave. The franchise is compounding fast *and* levering: revenue +38% with FCF margin of ~19%, and the new Rubrik Agent Cloud / Agent Identity products (MCP support Sept 2026, Claude Code integration, "Project Hourglass" GSI alliance with Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro) open an identity + AI-governance TAM that did not exist in the original model. This is one of the few software names where growth is *accelerating in normalization terms* while profitability inflects simultaneously.

### 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

Management frames data security / cyber resilience as a **~$60B TAM expanding toward $100B+ by 2030**, driven by (a) the on-prem backup → cloud/SaaS conversion (still early — 89% cloud mix of *subscription* ARR but a large legacy maintenance base remains to convert), (b) identity resilience, and (c) the genuinely new **AI agent operations / agentic governance** layer. Rubrik's $1.66B ARR against even a $60B base market is **<3% penetration** — the runway claim is credible.

TAM-expansion credibility test: the $100K+ customer cohort is only ~3,084 accounts against a multi-hundred-thousand-enterprise global base. Every 500 net new $100K customers at ~$250K average ARR is ~$125M of incremental ARR — the model monetizes best-in-class via land-and-expand, with net revenue retention supported by data volume growth (the consumption tailwind is structural, not cyclical).

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q3 FY27 earnings (~early Dec 2026)** — third consecutive "raise all metrics" is the base case given the revision pattern; first real read on Agent Cloud contribution and Q3 guidance credibility.
- **Agent Cloud / Agent Identity monetization disclosures** — GA timing, pricing model (per-agent or per-tool-call), and any ARR disclosure would be the single biggest re-rating event; a new product line with no comparable in the comp set.
- **GSI channel ramp (Project Hourglass)** — Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro deploying agentic resilience; GSI-sourced revenue typically shows up 2–3 quarters after alliance signing.
- **Material-rights roll-off** — as the $14.2M→$4.7M headwind annualizes away, reported growth converges to the ~43% normalized rate, mechanically lifting the headline number.
- **FY28 guidance (March 2027) + index inclusion** — profitability inflection (non-GAAP operating margin positive and expanding) keeps RBRK on the radar for large-cap index migration.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction; not yet Strong Buy)

The operating model is elite by any Cathie Wood-style filter: 38% growth, 80% gross margins, Rule of 40 of ~55–68, 100% beat rate, expanding ARR contribution margins, and a genuinely new TAM (agentic AI governance) where incumbents have no product. It trades at **~12x EV/FY27E sales and ~60x EV/FY27E FCF versus CrowdStrike at ~48x trailing sales** — a striking relative discount for comparable growth and superior margin trajectory.

**But the multiple is not free.** Reverse-engineering the price: $21B EV against consensus FY31 revenue of $3.33B and a 27% FCF margin implies ~$900M FCF, which at 35x gives ~$31B EV — only ~+50% over five years (~8%/yr). The stock discounts the *base case* well. The bull case requires revenue above $4B by FY31 on Agent Cloud/identity, which at 30% FCF margins yields roughly a double. **That optionality — not the current numbers — is what you are underwriting here.** Buy in scale, size for 20%+ volatility, and add on any retreat into the $90s.

### 7. Key Risks

- **Deceleration is real and broadening.** Revenue growth 48% → 38% → consensus ~28%; **adjusted net new Cloud ARR +20%** (vs 39% cloud ARR growth) and $100K+ customer growth of +23% (down from 30%+) are the first genuine slowdown data points in the model. If adjusted net new cloud ARR falls below 20%, the 12x sales multiple compresses fast.
- **Competitive intensity.** Cohesity+Veritas as a scaled consolidator, Veeam, Commvault and Dell PowerProtect in data protection; CyberArk/Palo Alto/SailPoint in identity; Microsoft bundling. Rubrik's differentiation is architectural (immutable, SLA-backed recovery + single control plane), but the backup market is a price-war market and Microsoft can subsidize.
- **Agent Cloud is unproven.** No disclosed ARR, no pricing standard, and hyperscalers/platform players (Microsoft, Anthropic, Google) could absorb agent governance into their own control planes.
- **Stock-based compensation** at ~$73M/quarter (~17% of revenue) is real dilution; GAAP losses persist, book value is negative (-$2.64/sh) from the convertible structure.
- **Guidance optics risk.** Material-rights and consumption-based revenue create quarter-to-quarter noise; a single "raise but narrow" print could trigger a 15–20% drawdown given positioning is now crowded long.
- **Execution at scale:** headcount expansion into identity/AI while simultaneously defending the core migration cycle is a two-front war for a 3,797-person company.
