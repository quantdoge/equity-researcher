**Investment Thesis**
Rubrik is a genuinely high-quality cyber-resilience franchise — 33% subscription ARR growth, 80% gross margins, clean accounting, fortress balance sheet — trading at a price that already discounts a decade of near-flawless execution. Four independent valuation frameworks (prob-weighted DCF $88, 8x FY28E sales $86.50, short-side bear case $75, macro real-yield de-rating $65–75) converge on intrinsic value 18–32% below spot. The disagreement across the team is not about the company — it is about the price, and the structural evidence (decelerating ARR, NRR erosion, SBC exceeding FCF, +69bp in real yields) outweighs the cyclical evidence (beat cadence, stale consensus into the Nov-30 print). We own zero and express patience through a pre-authorised buy ladder.

Rubrik is the growth leader (38–39% underlying vs 21.7% peer median) but bottom-quartile on GAAP profitability. Macro **Unfavourable**: this is a discount-rate problem, not a demand problem — 10Y real yield 2.20%→2.89%, 10Y nominal 5.13%, ~1.5% FCF yield = negative equity risk premium; ~2x Nasdaq beta in risk-off tape. A real-yield retrace to ~2.4% is the highest-quality entry catalyst available.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Low risk.** No sanctioned-market revenue, no active litigation/investigations, closed patent history. Exposure is domestic: US federal/FedRAMP budget cycles, EU data-sovereignty compliance drag, and tail risk as a Tier-1 state-actor target.

**Bull Case**
- Category leadership in the fastest-growing defensible software budget: 33% ARR growth, >119% NRR, 80% GM, subscription ARR contribution margin inflecting (+460bps YoY) — fastest margin ramp in the comp set.
- Agent Cloud / identity governance is a genuinely new TAM with no incumbent product; GSI alliance (Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro) and Nov-2026 multi-cloud launch cluster feed FY28 upside.
- Nov-30 print likely beats stale consensus (7/7, avg +8.5% revenue surprise); a beat *plus* FY28 guide ≥25% plus real-yield retracement re-rates the stock to $140–160.

**Bear Case**
- Price vs value: 12.5x FY27E / 10.2x FY28E sales, ~64–69x EV/FCF, ~580x FCF ex-SBC — vs IV cluster $75–90. De-rating to 8x FY28 sales = $75 (−32%) with no change in the business.
- Leading indicators are decelerating on every front: ARR 33% < revenue 38%, NRR 119% from ~130%, $100K+ customers +23%, H2 implied ~20%, gross margin −220bp QoQ to 78.4%.
- FCF quality is optics: SBC of $329M (25% of revenue) exceeds $253M FY26 FCF; owner earnings negative; share count 154M→205M in two years — compounded by a macro regime (real yields 2.89%, Fed hiking bias) that specifically punishes 1.5%-FCF-yield duration assets.

**Key Risks**
1. **Nov-30 guidance cut** — a "beat but narrow" print triggers 15–20% drawdown given crowded positioning (documented Aug-27 pattern).
2. **Real yields >3.25%** — direct de-rating trigger to $65–75; December FOMC collides with earnings window.
3. **Hyperscaler/Microsoft bundling** compressing pricing power and gross margin (first crack already visible).
4. **Customer-data breach or backup-integrity failure** — existential brand event (15–25% of market cap); two own-incidents on record. Exit at any price.
5. **Dilution/SBC persistence** — no path to positive FCF ex-SBC if SBC stays >22% of revenue in FY28.
6. **Governance** — further Greylock/Lightspeed-affiliated related-party transactions = sell-side trigger.
7. **Data gaps carried forward**: FY26 geographic revenue split, ~$1.3B implied lease obligations, clean software-peer multiples, DEF 14A board detail (EDGAR blocked) — pull manually before circulation.
