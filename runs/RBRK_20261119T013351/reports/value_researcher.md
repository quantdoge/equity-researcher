## Equity Value Research — Rubrik, Inc. (NYSE: RBRK)

**Price $110.00 | Market cap ~$22.6B | EV ~$22.0B (net cash ~$0.6B) | Sector: Infrastructure Software | IPO: April 2024**

---

### 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat Rating: **Narrow (unproven durability)**

Rubrik has reinvented itself from an on-premises backup appliance company into a cloud-native **data security / cyber-resilience platform** (Rubrik Security Cloud + the newly launched Agentic AI layer). The economics of the transition are visible and impressive: gross margin has gone from **53.4% (FY23) → 62.1% → 70.0% → 80.1% (FY26)** as revenue shifted to subscription.

**Sources of moat:**
- **Switching costs (strongest leg):** Data protection is mission-critical infrastructure with immutable retention policies, compliance requirements, and years of recovery runbooks baked in. Ripping out a backup/recovery system is a high-risk, multi-quarter project. Dollar-based net retention has historically run in the ~120% range, and the company now has **3,084 customers with >$100K subscription ARR, +23% YoY**.
- **Intangible/brand:** Rubrik is the reference vendor for "cyber resilience" post-ransomware — a genuine category position, reinforced by a GSI alliance (Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro).
- **Not a network effect business.** No demand-side scale economics.

**Where the moat is thin:** the underlying market (backup/data protection) is brutally competitive — **Microsoft (native Azure/AWS backup), Dell PowerProtect, Veeam, Cohesity/Veritas, Commvault, Druva**. As incumbents bundle protection into broader security or infrastructure suites, Rubrik's premium depends on maintaining a technical lead in recovery speed and AI-agent security — a lead that is not clearly a decade-long asset. **Verdict: Narrow moat, and I cannot yet demonstrate it has widened.**

**Positives:** Revenue growth has *accelerated* (4.7% → 41.2% → 48.5%), gross margins are best-in-class, free cash flow turned decisively positive, and the balance sheet holds ~$1.75B of cash against $1.13B of convertible notes. Rule of 40 = ~29% ARR growth + 20% FCF margin ≈ **49**, which is genuinely top-decile SaaS.

**The two things that keep this out of the top tier:**
1. **SBC is 25% of revenue.** FY26 FCF of $253M *only exists because* $329M of stock compensation is added back. **Owner earnings (FCF less SBC) ≈ −$76M.** Reported "profitability" is largely a compensation-funding choice, not economic profit. Share count has gone from 154M to ~206M in two years.
2. **FCF quality leans on working capital.** $148M of FY26 operating cash flow came from deferred revenue growth — a real benefit while bookings compound, but one that *reverses* if growth decelerates. Accumulated deficit is $3.19B and book value is negative.

### 3. Valuation Analysis — **~25% above my intrinsic value**

Management raised FY27 guidance to **revenue of $1.685–1.693B (~28% growth)** and **subscription ARR of $1.88B (+29%)**. ARR growth is decelerating (33% → 29%) and "adjusted net new Cloud ARR" grew only 20% — the leading indicator is softening while the multiple has expanded.

At $110 the market pays **13.0x EV/FY27E revenue, 11.7x EV/ARR, ~64x EV/FY27E FCF, and a 1.5% FCF yield.**

I built a 10-year owner-FCF DCF (10% discount rate, 3% terminal growth) across scenarios:

| Scenario | 10-yr FCF CAGR | FCF in yr 10 | Intrinsic value/share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear (competition bites, decel to high-teens) | 12% | ~$1.1B | **~$51** |
| Base (solid execution, margin expansion) | 20% | ~$2.1B | **~$90** |
| Bull (agentic-AI re-rating plays out) | 25% | ~$3.2B | **~$128** |

Probability-weighted (30/45/25): **~$88/share.**

**Reverse-engineering the price:** to justify $22B of enterprise value, Rubrik must compound FCF at **~22–23% annually for a full decade**, reaching roughly **$2.5B of FCF on ~$7–9B of revenue** by FY2036. That is achievable — but it assumes near-flawless execution, sustained 20%+ revenue growth for ten years, and roughly doubling of today's FCF margin, all against Microsoft and Dell. Paying the *bull case* price for the *base case* business is the definition of no margin of safety.

*(Caveat: the peer-multiple tool returns a meaningless −61.8x P/E against a 30.8x peer median because RBRK has no GAAP earnings; my sector-peer list is dominated by mega-cap semis. I could not source reliable clean comps for CRWD/PANW/ZS/NET within budget — a genuine gap in this analysis.)*

### 4. Management Quality — **6.5 / 10**

- **Founder-led:** Bipul Sinha (co-founder, CEO, Chairman) retains meaningful equity; VC/PE sponsorship (Lightspeed, Greylock, Bain) is typical of the class.
- **Execution and guidance discipline are excellent:** nine consecutive quarters of beating and raising. The CFO sets conservative guidance — a genuinely shareholder-friendly trait.
- **Capital allocation:** no buybacks (correct), no dividend (correct), bolt-ons (Laminar) rather than empire-building. Convertible debt used sensibly.
- **The marks against:** (a) SBC at 25% of revenue is one of the highest in large-cap software — shareholders are funding the income statement; (b) **111 insider transactions in the last 90 days, ~$95M of sales and zero purchases.** Much is likely pre-scheduled 10b5-1 post-IPO diversification, but at $110 with the stock up 150% off the April low, insiders are unambiguous sellers. Watch closely; do not ignore.

### 5. Investment Verdict: **SELL / AVOID at $110** (business quality: high; price: full-to-excessive)

This is a *valuation* verdict, not a quality verdict. Rubrik is a legitimately good business transitioning into a very good one: 80% gross margins, ~30% ARR growth, positive and growing FCF, and a net-cash balance sheet. But by the standards I apply:

- ❌ **Owner earnings are negative** once SBC is treated as the real cost it is.
- ❌ **No margin of safety** — $110 vs. ~$88 intrinsic value; the market is discounting a decade of 22%+ FCF compounding.
- ❌ **Moat unproven** and facing the most predatory competitor in software (Microsoft).
- ❌ **Momentum-driven price action** ($43.81 in April → $136 in October → $110 now) driven by the "agentic AI" narrative rather than fundamentals.

**For existing holders with a growth mandate:** this is a reasonable **Hold** in a *small* position sized for 40%+ drawdowns. **For a Buffett-style value portfolio: do not initiate.** My entry zone — where the risk/reward finally compensates — is **~$60–70 (≈20–25% discount to base-case value)**, which is where the stock traded as recently as March–May 2026. If ARR growth holds ≥30% and SBC falls below 18% of revenue, I would raise the intrinsic value and consider buying that range.

### 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Multiple compression.** At 13x EV/sales, any deceleration below ~25% growth compresses the multiple violently — a de-rating to 8x sales is a ~40% drawdown with no change in the business.
2. **Competitive bundling.** Microsoft/Dell can price data protection as a feature, not a product. This is the single largest threat to the moat and to gross margins (already down to 78.4% GAAP).
3. **Dilution.** ~4–5% annual share growth from SBC permanently skims per-share value; the FY25 RSU overhang shows how violent this can be.
4. **FCF quality.** Deferred-revenue-driven working capital is a growth artifact; a bookings slowdown would hit reported FCF harder than the P&L.
5. **Narrative dependence.** The Agentic AI/Claude Code positioning is early-stage; if enterprise AI security budgets disappoint, the story premium evaporates.
6. **Convertible debt / negative equity** ($1.13B notes, −$520M book equity) limits flexibility if a downturn arrives.
7. **Insider selling** at scale with no offsetting purchases offers no signal of management confidence in the current price.

**One-line summary: a wonderful growth business at a price that already assumes it becomes a wonderful *and* much larger business — a pass for the patient value buyer, and a "do not chase" for everyone else.**
