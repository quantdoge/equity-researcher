## Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Rubrik, Inc. (NYSE: RBRK)

**Snapshot:** $22.6B market cap · $110.00 · Technology / Software–Infrastructure (data security & cyber recovery) · IPO Apr-2024 · 3,797 FTE · CEO & Chairman Bipul Sinha · FY ends Jan-31 · Q3 FY27 results due **Nov 30, 2026** (imminent catalyst)

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE** (negative tilt — controlled company)

**Evidence supporting:**
- Founder-operator model: Sinha (CEO) and Nithrakashyap (CTO) are co-founders with large retained equity; CEO holds ~5.5% directly. Equity-heavy pay aligns with multi-year TSR.
- Lead Independent Director in place (John W. Thompson); board includes VC-affiliated directors (Asheem Chandna/Greylock, Ravi Mhatre/Lightspeed, Enrique Salem/Bain Capital Ventures) and independent directors (e.g., Yvonne Wassenaar).
- Clean record: no restatements, no SEC/DOJ actions, no accounting scandals identified. C-suite stable apart from CRO transition.

**Evidence against:**
- **CEO/Chairman duality** — Sinha holds both roles; the proxy itself defends the combined structure rather than separating it.
- **Dual-class control:** Class B carries ~20 votes/share; founders plus Lightspeed/Greylock hold dominant voting power. Minority holders have limited ability to influence board composition or block related-party actions. Effectively a controlled company.
- **Compensation optics:** CEO total pay ~$140M in the IPO year (mega-grant), normalizing to ~$1.8M in 2026 — a large one-off that complicates pay-for-performance read-through.
- **CRO turnover (Feb-2026):** Brian McCarthy departed; Jesse Green promoted internally. Mitigated by internal succession, but notable in a sales-led model.
- Two of four co-founders (Arvind Jain, Soham Mazumdar) have exited to run other companies — reduces bench depth but also reduces founder-fracture risk.

*Caveat: board independence ratio could not be verified — the DEF 14A was not retrievable through the filing tool.*

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary — **LOW**

- **SEC enforcement / EDGAR full-text search: zero hits** for RBRK. No DOJ, FTC, or antitrust matters identified.
- No pending regulatory approvals (no FDA/FCC-style gating); no GDPR/CCPA enforcement actions surfaced.
- Structural exposure is ordinary-course: data-privacy regimes (GDPR/CCPA and emerging US state laws), FedRAMP/public-sector procurement compliance, and SEC cybersecurity-disclosure obligations — the latter is *heightened* for Rubrik because it sells security and has itself disclosed a breach.
- **Capital-structure item:** $1.0B convertible senior notes (2030) issued 2025; **$1.15B principal outstanding as of Apr-30-2026**. Not a regulatory matter, but a dilution/refinancing overhang worth monitoring.
- No confirmed securities class action identified; searches were inconclusive rather than exculpatory.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Competitive intensification in data protection.** Cohesity's 2026 combination with Veritas (~$1.6B ARR scale) plus Veeam, Commvault, Dell, and Microsoft/Google/AWS native tooling compress Rubrik's differentiation. Rubrik is simultaneously expanding *toward* the hyperscalers (Azure Cosmos DB, BigQuery, AWS, GitHub/Azure DevOps protection announced Nov-2026) — a strategy that depends on partners who also compete.
- **Technology-disruption narrative (AI).** A live bear thesis is that agentic AI and cloud-native backup commoditize the category; management has had to publicly "push back hard on AI disruption risk." Rubrik's counter is Agent Cloud / Project Hourglass (Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro, AHEAD, Trace3, WWT) — partner-dependent, still early.
- **Key-person and execution concentration.** Growth thesis rests on Sinha (CEO/Chair) and Nithrakashyap (CTO); both are also the largest individual sellers. Public-sector/education concentration is called out in the FY2026 10-K as a continuity risk, exposing Rubrik to federal budget and procurement cycles. Gross-margin/cash-flow contraction (Q2 FY27 revenue beat at $427.3M vs. ~$396.3M consensus, yet shares fell ~12% on cash-flow margin compression) suggests scaling costs are outrunning the model.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **MODERATE**

- **The core reputational asymmetry:** Rubrik sells trust in data recovery, so any compromise of *its own* systems is disproportionately damaging. It has two on record: the **2023 GoAnywhere MFT zero-day (CVE-2023-0669)** exposure, and a **2025 disclosure** that its security team found anomalous activity on a server containing log files, with compromise of "access information." Neither appears to have caused lasting brand damage, but the pattern is a standing credibility risk.
- **Sentiment is currently constructive:** 30 of 31 analysts rate Buy (avg. target ~$118), stock +~100% from May-2026 lows of ~$55, record high $136 on Oct-28-2026. Press coverage is dominated by product launches, not controversy.
- **Volatility is high and headline-driven:** −12% on Aug-28 (cash-flow margins), −14% across Oct-30 to Nov-2 (sector-wide cyber selloff after Fortinet flagged slowing product revenue, plus rate/AI-financing fears), −11% on Nov-5. No company-specific misconduct identified in any of these moves.
- No ESG controversy, product recall, or consumer-backlash dynamic of note. Zero short-seller reports found.

---

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

Low regulatory/legal risk and a clean enforcement record are offset by a controlled-company governance structure, an unusually persistent insider-selling pattern with no offsetting purchases, rising competitive intensity, and the elevated reputational bar that comes with being a security vendor that has itself been breached. Nothing here is disqualifying; the risk is concentrated in governance/alignment rather than in legal or operational failure.

---

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Insider selling with zero purchases over 24 months (~$262.6M sold).** CTO sales on Nov-2 and Nov-4 at $117–118 preceded an 11% drop to $105.74 by Nov-5. Likely 10b5-1 and not evidence of impropriety — but the *optics* around a pre-earnings window (Nov-30 print) warrant a disclosure check on plan adoption dates.
2. **Controlled-company structure:** ~20:1 Class B votes plus combined CEO/Chair. Minority shareholders cannot meaningfully check board decisions; activist or takeover pressure is structurally blunted.
3. **Security vendor with its own breach history** (2023 GoAnywhere; 2025 server/access-information compromise). Any future incident is a direct hit to the product's core promise.
4. **Cash-flow margin contraction despite revenue beats** — monitor ARR/billings quality, RPO disclosure, and capitalized commissions for signs of growth-at-all-costs. No accounting irregularity found; this is a watch item, not an allegation.
5. **CRO turnover (Feb-2026)** in a sales-led growth model, alongside two of four co-founders having exited.
6. **$1.15B convertible notes (2030)** — dilution and refinancing overhang to model alongside the equity story.
