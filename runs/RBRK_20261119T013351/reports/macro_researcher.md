# RBRK — Macroeconomic Environment & Company Impact Assessment
**As of 19 November 2026** | Rubrik, Inc. (NYSE: RBRK) — Technology / Software-Infrastructure, US$22.6B cap

## 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Risk-Off / Transitional (hawkish reflation)**

The regime is not a conventional late-cycle slowdown. It is a **fiscal-supply and real-yield shock layered on a still-expanding economy**. Three facts define the tape:

1. **Inflation has re-accelerated, and the Fed is hiking again.** CPI is running **3.82% YoY (Oct 2026)** — up from 2.65% in Dec 2025 — with the last three months annualising at **~5.7%**. The FOMC **raised** 25bp on 16 Sept 2026 to 3.75–4.00% and **held** on 28 Oct 2026 with the explicit line that "inflation remains elevated." The effective funds rate is 3.88% (Oct) versus a 3.63% trough in mid-2026: the easing cycle is over and partially reversed.
2. **The long end is selling off for real-yield reasons, not inflation-expectation reasons.** The 10Y nominal is **5.13%**, but the 10Y TIPS real yield has risen from **2.20% (30 Jun) to 2.89% (17 Nov), +69bp**, while 10Y breakevens *fell* from 2.38% (28 Oct) to **2.19% (18 Nov)**. This is the single most important observation for RBRK: the market is repricing the *discount rate* (term premium, issuance, hawkish policy), not inflation. Inflation expectations are actually improving — but that does not help a long-duration equity.
3. **The curve is bear-steepening, not signalling an imminent recession.** 2Y 4.74% / 5Y 4.93% / 10Y 5.13% / 30Y 5.47%, 2s10s **+39bp**. Critically, the **2Y sits ~86bp above the effective funds rate**, i.e. the market is pricing roughly three-to-four further hikes. Growth, meanwhile, is intact: **real GDP +2.1% YoY** (Q3 2026 SAAR +3.4%) and unemployment at **4.1%**.

Against that, credit is beginning to flinch: **HY OAS has widened from 2.63% (28 Aug) to 3.43% (17 Nov)**, ~80bp, though still historically tight. And the dollar has strengthened **+3.8% since 11 Sept** (broad index 122.65). Finally, an **AI-complex deleveraging** is running in parallel — the Nasdaq fell **5.52% on 5 Nov 2026** (~$2.5T of value) on AI-infrastructure economics, and a roughly **$1T software de-rating** has been underway since February on AI-disruption fears (SEG SaaS Index −25.7% in Q1 2026 alone). RBRK is caught in both the rate shock and the AI-complex unwind.

Roughly 85–90% of its intrinsic value sits in 2031+ cash flows. That structure makes it one of the most rate-sensitive equities in the market, on top of an AI-narrative overlay.

## 5. Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BULL: the fiscal-dominance trade unwinds**

**Consensus is now aggressively hawkish** (2Y implying ~85bp more hikes). That makes an *upside* rates surprise the higher-convexity outcome.

The Fed pauses in December and signals a hold; the 10Y falls to **4.30–4.50%** and the **10Y real yield retraces from 2.89% to ~2.40%**. In that world RBRK's 33% ARR growth is re-rated rather than de-rated: a move from ~13.7x to **17–18x ARR** on a rising ARR base implies a share price of **$140–160 (+30–45%)**, coinciding with early-December Q3 FY27 results that have beaten for ten consecutive quarters. A weaker dollar (broad index back to ~118) adds another 2–3 points of reported growth.

**The bear tail worth insuring:** if real yields break above 3.25% and the market begins pricing a 5%+ terminal rate, the same duration math takes RBRK to **8–9x ARR (~$65–75, −30–40%)** without any change to the operating story.

**Event risk inside 30 days:** the December FOMC and Rubrik's Q3 FY27 print (**both expected in the week of 7–11 December**) land together — an unusually tight collision of the two variables that dominate this stock.

## Bottom Line for the Investment Memo

RBRK's macro problem is a **discount-rate problem, not a demand problem**. A hawkish, real-yield-led bear steepening (real 10Y 2.20%→2.89%) plus an AI-driven software de-rating has compressed the multiple while the franchise compounded ARR at 33%. The balance sheet is a genuine relative strength — $1.15B of zero-coupon converts maturing 2030 means no refinancing exposure to a 3.43% HY market. But with a ~1.4% FCF yield, a negative equity risk premium versus the 10Y, and ~2x beta to a fragile Nasdaq, the stock is a levered bet on the real-yield path. **Macro risk rating: Unfavourable.** We would treat any further 30–40bp rise in real yields as a direct de-rating trigger, and any retracement toward 2.4% as the highest-quality entry catalyst available.
