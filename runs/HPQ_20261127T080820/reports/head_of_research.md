### Investment Memo: HPQ — November 27, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (do not initiate at $31.15; scale in below $27.50) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $34 (~+9% from $31.15; probability-weighted IV $34.80, range $28–42)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
HPQ is a narrow-moat, cash-generative hardware franchise caught at the worst point of a memory-cost downcycle: revenue "growth" of +9.5% is ASP pass-through masking a −22% unit market, while gross margin sits near a decade low. The cash engine is real — $3.19B FCF (11% yield), 164% cash conversion, a raised 4% dividend, and 25% share-count shrink in five years — but at 9.4x FY27E EPS the stock trades within ~9% of intrinsic value and *above* the median analyst target after a 76% run off the February low. This is a bond proxy, not a compounder: the return case is cyclical (memory mean-reversion + PS margin recovery), and that case requires two macro variables to break HPQ's way at once.

**Bull Case**
- **Memory mean-reversion is a coiled spring**: DRAM/NAND rollover in CY2027 converts the 40%-of-BOM headwind into a tailwind; PS margins recover toward the 5–7% target and FY28 EPS ~$3.56 becomes credible — the same operating leverage that crushed FY26 works in reverse.
- **Cash machine at a discount**: 11% FCF yield, 8.2% total shareholder yield, dividend just raised, 9.4x forward EPS — value investors get paid to wait for the cycle.
- **Relative competitive strength**: +9.3% Q4 revenue in a −22% unit market, AI-PC mix ahead of industry, and whitebox consolidation favors scaled OEMs.

**Bear Case**
- **Margin pincer with no pricing power**: gross margin at a decade low, PS margin at 3.0%, and the FY27 guide embeds a recovery the cost curve does not yet support; IDC/Gartner see the shortage persisting through 2027.
- **Structural erosion**: supplies annuity declining ~5%/yr with EU Right to Repair adding regulatory risk; 5-yr revenue CAGR −0.9% — the "growth" is inflation.
- **Fragile support structures**: tangible equity −$8.2B, 73% of FCF committed to capital returns while FCF is guided down — the buyback is the only thing holding EPS, and it breaks first in a prolonged squeeze.

**Key Risks**
1. Memory costs stay elevated through CY2027 (IDC/Gartner base case) → gross margin <19%, FY27 EPS cut toward $2.80–2.90 (Barclays $23 scenario).
2. Hawkish Dec 9 FOMC + 10Y >5.5% → a sub-Treasury 4% yield drives income holders out; bear case retests $24–25 (Macro assigns ~30% probability).
3. Tariff escalation vs. the China production shift-back; incremental duties are a multi-hundred-million-dollar gross exposure on a 5% margin.
4. Inventory write-down on the $12.3B (+44%) position if component prices reverse — the classic OEM double-hit.
5. Execution/governance: Walmsley's Jan 4 start with no sector background, withheld FY27 guidance, and the Feb 23, 2027 print as the first real test.
