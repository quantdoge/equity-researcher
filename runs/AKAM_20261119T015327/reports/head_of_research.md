### Investment Memo: AKAM — 19 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $75 (−18% from current $91.70)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Akamai is no longer the asset-light CDN/security compounder its multiple still implies — it has voluntarily converted itself into a levered, sub-investment-grade AI compute lessor whose $14B contracted backlog is concentrated in one non-exclusive counterparty (Anthropic, $11.6B) requiring ~$5.5B of largely irreversible capex to serve. True economic FCF is already negative (TTM FCF $211M less $526M SBC ≈ −$315M), gross margin has eroded 860bp in five years, ROIC (~5%) sits below WACC, and the depreciation cliff lands in 2027–28 ahead of the revenue. At $91.70 the market is paying full price for flawless execution; our probability-weighted value is ~$83 and base-case intrinsic value is $70–75.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro **UNFAVOURABLE** — real 10Y ~2.94%, Fed re-hiking (50bp December risk), USD +4%: the worst regime for long-duration, capital-intensive equity. Sector **NEUTRAL** — the only edge/CDN peer with materially deteriorating gross margin; growing at one-third the peer median; delivery melting, security decelerating to high single digits, CIS (+34%) the lone bright spot.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **MEDIUM** — genuinely clean on litigation/enforcement; China (Jun-26) and Russia (2022) exits already executed; no non-US country ≥10% of revenue. Residual: EU digital-sovereignty compliance costs, EU–US Data Privacy Framework under CJEU appeal, securities-disclosure risk if Anthropic-linked 2027 guidance slips.
- **Value**: **SELL**. Base case $70–75 — below price, i.e., *negative* margin of safety. The 14x non-GAAP P/E is an illusion (excludes $709M D&A + $459M SBC — the costs that matter in a hardware business); GAAP P/E ~41x, EV/EBITDA ~12.6x, FCF yield 1.6%. Reverse DCF demands ~$2B of owner earnings vs $341M delivered in FY25. Entry: <$65.
- **Growth**: The lone dissent — speculative BUY, half-size. >$14B of signed commitments; consensus +20% (2027E) and +31% (2028E) revenue; ~3.6x 2027E EV/sales undemanding *if* backlog converts at acceptable margin. But Rule of 40 ~10–22, one guidance cut already, and the whole case rests on a single lever: conversion economics.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Value 6.5/10 is the best factor; Quality 3/10, Low-Vol 2/10, Revisions 3/10 (5 of 6 recent analyst actions were target cuts). Realized vol ~69%, beta 1.42 with R² 0.09 — an idiosyncratic event-driven security, not the defensive 0.66-beta holding some assume.
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH**. Fallen angel (S&P BB+, two cuts in 2026; Moody's Baa3); net leverage 4.3x rising toward >5x pro forma; reported 20x interest coverage is a capitalization artifact (true EBIT cover ~2.4x); S&P projects >$1B cumulative negative FCF through 2029; convertible refinancing ladder while junk-rated.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH**. Concentration is the defining risk: $11.6B (up to ~$20B) from a non-exclusive, cash-burning private counterparty vs $4.4B revenue; the MSA was signed with the data-processing addendum still unnegotiated. Governance adequate (independent chair, $1-salary CEO paid in equity) but buyback-then-lever sequencing is a capital-allocation red flag. Offset: CEO Leighton's multi-million-dollar open-market purchases near the lows.
- **ESG**: AVERAGE, negative momentum — and here ESG risk *is* margin risk: clean-energy share 59%→52%, PUE 1.40→1.48 (above German EnEfG thresholds), electricity +23%, all while reaffirming "100% renewable by 2030." Permitting/community opposition is already a disclosed revenue-timing issue.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL. Beat streak intact (7/7) but amplitude compressing sharply (+13.4% → ~3% avg last four); Q4 revenue slippage pre-announced; sector de-rating (FSLY −40% on a beat). Bright spot: Nov-18 +8.3% breakout on the Microsoft Edge partnership on above-average volume — early accumulation.
- **Short / Contrarian**: Books honest (Beneish −2.80, negative accruals four years); economics broken. Capex 57% of FY26 revenue vs 19.5% in FY25; D&A on track to overtake EBITDA by 2028 absent ~2x revenue; DSO deteriorating (66→78 days); buybacks stopped dead at the lows after $800M near the highs.

**Bull Case**
1. **Contracted, not hoped-for**: >$14B signed multiyear commitments; 2027 CIS guide of $1.1–1.3B (>2x) and +17–22% total growth is largely pre-sold. If the Q4 slip is timing, the Feb-2027 print is a trough into a steep ramp.
2. **Asymmetric re-rating**: at ~3.6x 2027E EV/sales, successful conversion supports $130–150+; Street bull targets reach $150–175.
3. **Validation signals**: CEO buying near the lows, the Microsoft partnership, and Anthropic choosing Akamai's edge confirm a real, hard-to-replicate asset (4,000+ locations).

**Bear Case**
1. **Value-destruction math**: ROIC ~3–5% vs ~8–9% WACC; true economic FCF negative; D&A (~$1.3B+) overtakes EBITDA by 2028 — GAAP losses are plausible even if the plan "works."
2. **Concentration + contract quality**: one non-exclusive counterparty, funded by Akamai's own competitors, underwrites the entire capital budget; renegotiation strands $5.5B of capex.
3. **Financing trap**: junk-rated, >5x leverage, negative FCF through 2029 — an equity raise at depressed prices (the very dilution the 1% converts were meant to avoid) is a live 2027 outcome, with macro stacked against it.

**Key Risks** *(to the SELL call — what makes us wrong)*
- CIS revenue lands on the guided path through H1 2027 at better-than-feared margins → sharp re-rating.
- A second anchor AI tenant, or Anthropic expansion beyond $11.6B, de-risks concentration.
- The CFO's 2028 operating-leverage inflection materializes; strategic/takeover interest in a sub-$14B-EV irreplaceable edge network.

**Portfolio Construction Note**

PASS for non-holders (0.0%): expected value ~$83 vs $91.70 is a negative edge — no margin of safety is being offered. Existing holders: REDUCE to ≤0.5% (at 69% vol, even 0.5% contributes ~35bp of risk) — the position is no longer the security it was underwritten as. Re-entry requires BOTH gates: price ≤$70 AND evidence — two consecutive quarters of CIS revenue in-guide, FCF inflection with capex intensity declining, and Anthropic concentration de-risked via disclosure or a second tenant. Hard stop $77 on any residual position; do not initiate through the Feb-18-2027 print.
