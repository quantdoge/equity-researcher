# Sector Brief — Akamai Technologies (AKAM)
**Industry:** Software – Infrastructure / CDN, Edge Compute & Application Security | **Mkt cap:** $13.2B | **Price:** $91.70 | **Beta:** 0.66

---

## 1. Sector Overview

AKAM competes at the intersection of three converging markets: **content delivery (CDN)**, **cloud/edge infrastructure**, and **application & DDoS security**. The core CDN market is ~$28.5B in 2026, forecast to grow at a ~10% CAGR to ~$62.7B by 2034, but the *addressable* growth is concentrated away from classic caching: CDN-security is only ~$6.0B in 2026, while edge compute and AI inference are growing fastest off a small base. The market is **moderately concentrated but structurally fragmenting** — Akamai (~$4.2B revenue), Cloudflare (~$2.2B) and Amazon CloudFront hold roughly a third of CDN spend combined, yet Cloudflare is the identified reverse proxy for **84.1% of websites** versus Akamai's **2.3%** (W3Techs, Jul-2026), a stark illustration of how much of the *volume* growth has migrated to the challenger and to hyperscaler-bundled offerings. Barriers are high on network/capex and enterprise trust, but low for hyperscalers who already own the infrastructure and can bundle CDN near-free with compute.

**Cycle position: bifurcated.** Legacy media delivery is **late-cycle/declining** (AKAM delivery revenue −7% in Q1'26, ongoing price deflation). Security is **mid-cycle** (decelerating to high-single digits as the market matures). Edge compute/AI inference is **early-cycle** but capital-hungry — hence AKAM's $2.5B convertible note raise closed 12-Nov-2026. Input costs (data-centre power, colo, fibre, GPU capacity) are inflating, and that is visible directly in AKAM's P&L.

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **Security attach and consolidation.** AKAM's security segment reached **$2.243B in FY25, +10%**, now its largest line (~53% of revenue). Enterprise buyers are consolidating WAF/bot/DDoS/API vendors, favouring platforms with a network footprint and clean enterprise references.
- **Edge compute inflection.** Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS) grew **+34% YoY in Q3'26** (after +40% in Q1'26) — the fastest-growing large line in the company and the primary reason AKAM can guide FY26 revenue to **$4.445–4.550B (+5.6% to +8.1%)** versus FY25's +5.4%. AI inference demand at the edge is a genuine multi-year runway.
- **Structural shift to recurring, high-retention enterprise contracts.** ~82% of AKAM revenue is now security + compute, i.e. subscription-like and less traffic-volatile than delivery, supporting a beta of 0.66 and durable cash generation (~$1.26B FY26E EBITDA).

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **Share loss at the front of the funnel.** Cloudflare's 84.1% share of identified reverse-proxy deployments and 62.0% share of the top-1,000 sites means AKAM is defending an installed base rather than winning greenfield. Cloudflare's +29.5% FY26E growth is coming out of the same budget line.
- **Gross-margin erosion from mix and capex.** AKAM gross margin has fallen from **63.3% (FY21) → 54.7% (FY25) → 55.0% (Q3'26)**, with cost of revenue up **+17.5%** in both FY25 and Q3'26 against ~5–6% revenue growth. Operating margin compressed **605bp YoY to 9.7%** in Q3'26; 9M26 diluted EPS fell **−20% YoY** on +5.8% revenue. Compute growth is margin-dilutive, not accretive.
- **Hyperscaler price competition and commoditisation of delivery.** AWS CloudFront and Azure bundle CDN into compute contracts at near-zero incremental price, structurally capping pricing power in the segment AKAM is trying to defend — the reason delivery revenue is shrinking outright.

## 5. AKAM's Competitive Position: **AVERAGE**

**Strengths:** Deepest enterprise footprint at the high end — **22.5% share of the top-1,000 sites** and 17.8% of the top-10,000, versus only 2.6% overall. That inversion (winning where traffic and security budgets concentrate) is a real moat. It is also one of only two peers in the set generating a double-digit GAAP operating margin (14.9% vs 3.9% median), and the balance sheet was just pre-funded with $2.5B of 1.00% converts — cheap, low-coupon capital for capacity.

**Weaknesses:** Growth is roughly **one-third of the peer median** (5.4% vs 15.2%), it is losing the deployment-volume war decisively to Cloudflare, and its margin structure is going the wrong way as compute revenue displaces high-margin delivery. The −43% drawdown from the May-2026 peak (versus −18% for NET) shows the market repricing the AI/edge narrative it briefly awarded the stock.

**Verdict:** A well-run, cash-generative incumbent in a structurally challenged core, funding a credible but margin-dilutive pivot. Not a share gainer; a defensible cash compounder at the right price.

## 6. Sector Rating: **NEUTRAL**

The end-market grows ~10% and the security/edge adjacencies faster, which justifies participation. But pricing power is being eroded by hyperscaler bundling, the growth is capital-intensive with a visible margin cost, and the competitive gap to Cloudflare is widening rather than narrowing. Attractive for profitability and cash return characteristics; unattractive for growth and multiple expansion. **Neutral.**
