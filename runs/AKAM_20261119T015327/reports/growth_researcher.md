# AKAM — Akamai Technologies: Growth Analysis
*Price $91.70 | Market cap $13.2B | EV ≈ $19.2B | Nov 18, 2026*

## 1. Growth Story

Akamai is mid-transformation from a melting CDN business into a **distributed AI inference and security platform**. In 2026 it signed **>$14B of multi-year AI cloud commitments**, anchored by a **seven-year, $11.6B agreement with Anthropic** (with up to ~$9B of optional purchases, implying a contract that could approach $20B) requiring roughly **$5.5B of capex**, with service revenue starting **2H 2027**. That contract is ~2.8x current annual revenue — the single largest re-rating lever in the company's history — and it validates Akamai's differentiated asset: ~4,000 edge locations and owned network embedded close to end users, which is exactly where low-latency inference is migrating. However, the *reported* financials today still look like a 5% grower with compressing margins, so this is a **back-end-loaded, contract-visible inflection story, not a demonstrated one**.

3-yr revenue CAGR **6.4%** (2022→2025); 6-yr historic range 4.5–10.5% — a clear deceleration. Gross margin has fallen **~860bp since 2021** as lower-margin compute revenue displaces high-margin delivery, and capex rose from $458M (2022) to $820M (2025) with FCF down 16% YoY. **Share count discipline is a genuine positive**: diluted shares 165.8M (2021) → 147.0M (2025), ~$3.0B of buybacks over four years.

**Forward indicators are mixed:** 100% EPS beat rate over 7 quarters (avg +6.5% surprise), yet **Q4'26 revenue guidance of $1.16B came in 3.5% below consensus** and FY26 EPS guidance was cut 2.2%; "revision score" shows +19% (but on 2030-dated, 4-analyst estimates — low signal). More telling: **price targets were slashed post-Q3** (UBS $148→$100→$90; Piper $158→$120; RBC $85) while bulls hold $150–175. Median target ~$120–125, still ~30% above spot.

## 3. TAM & Penetration

- **AI inference / neocloud**: the market Akamai is now competing for is measured in the hundreds of billions by 2030. Its own contracted backlog (>$14B) is a *floor*, not the TAM. At $9.3B revenue in 2030 (consensus), Akamai would hold low-single-digit share of that market — a modest but highly credible penetration assumption.
- **Security**: ~$622M/quarter (~$2.5B annualized, ~55% of revenue), growing 9% — API security, WAF, Guardicore segmentation, plus the **LayerX** acquisition ($205M, May 2026) for AI-usage/browser security. Multi-hundred-billion TAM, low penetration.
- **Delivery/CDN**: structurally declining; management is deliberately trading volume for price/margin discipline. This is the drag that masks the growth story — strip it out and Security + CIS are growing high-single to double digits.
- **Penetration math that matters**: $5.5B capex against $11.6B+ of contracted revenue over seven years implies ~2.1x gross contract-to-capex — thin by neocloud standards (CoreWeave-style backlogs run far higher multiples), which is why margin recovery is the crux.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q4/FY2026 print — ~Feb 18, 2027**: first formal 2027 guide. Consensus already demands **+20% revenue**; the number and the capex/margin framing will re-rate the stock either way.
2. **CIS revenue recognition ramp — 2H 2027**: "two of the first three large deals are now generating revenue"; deployment delays already pushed revenue into 2027 (management: "no loss of revenue, just pushes to next year"). First real proof point of the AI backlog.
3. **Financing event for the $5.5B capex — next 0–3 quarters**: debt raise (S&P has already downgraded on debt-funded capex), customer prepayments, or equity/strategic issuance. Reports of an Anthropic equity stake (~5%) make dilution a live variable. **This is the highest-variance catalyst.**
4. **Additional AI contracts**: a "$14B signed" pipeline with a third large deal pending; each new anchor tenant de-risks the capex.
5. **LayerX/Guardicore security cross-sell** — incremental, steadier re-rating fuel; AI-driven threat detection is a credible upsell into the existing 100k+ customer base.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Google Trends** ("Akamai", 5-yr): spiked to 100 in the week of **Sept 20, 2026** (Anthropic deal coverage), then collapsed to **35–40** by November — retail attention has fully deflated. Contrarian-positive on sentiment, but confirms no momentum support.
- **Job postings**: listings (~75 active) now include **data center construction/account management roles** — consistent with physical buildout, supporting the capex/ramp claim rather than contradicting it.
- **Price action as data**: stock peaked at **$165.45 (May 13, 2026)** — a +27% single-day gap on May 8 — and has round-tripped to $91.70, **-45% from the high**, including a -14% post-Q4'25 and -9% post-Q3'26 reaction. The market is pricing *execution risk on the transformation*, not disbelief in AI demand.
- **Supply chain commentary**: third-party hardware/data-center delays are the recurring miss driver — a real, monitorable constraint.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY (speculative, position-sized)**

The valuation is genuinely undemanding for contract-backed acceleration: **~3.6x 2027E EV/revenue, ~9.5x 2027E EV/EBITDA, 13.3x 2027E EPS, 9.7x 2028E EPS** — for a business with >$14B of signed multi-year commitments and a 20–30% consensus growth path. Downside looks contained near the low-$70s (where Q3 traded), and the 2027–28 revenue is largely pre-sold rather than hoped-for. That asymmetry justifies owning it.

But it does **not** earn Strong Buy. Rule of 40 of **22** means current growth quality is poor; gross margins are down 860bp; FCF is shrinking exactly when capex is quintupling; and the entire thesis depends on a **single customer concentration** plus flawless, debt-funded execution that has already slipped once. This is a "believe the contracts, underwrite the balance sheet" trade — I'd size it at roughly half a core position and add on evidence of CIS revenue actually landing in 2027.

## 7. Key Risks

- **Customer concentration / contract risk**: Anthropic is a colossal share of the forward story; a scope reduction, renegotiation, or a shift to a rival's capacity would gut the thesis.
- **Financing & leverage**: $5.5B of capex on top of ~$6B existing net debt, already prompting an S&P downgrade. Debt-funded buildout raises interest expense and could force dilutive equity — note management repurchased $800M of stock in 2025 while preparing this spend.
- **Margin dilution**: compute is structurally lower-margin than CDN. If AI revenue scales at 30–40% gross margins, EPS estimates for 2028–2030 (which assume ~24% net margins) are unreachable, and this becomes a low-return capital story.
- **Competitive intensity**: hyperscalers (MSFT, ORCL/OCI, AMZN) and neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius) are adding capacity at extraordinary rates; edge inference may commoditize before Akamai's cost curve matures.
- **Execution/supply chain**: power, space, and third-party hardware timing have already caused guidance cuts; management's "temporary" framing needs to be verified against 1H 2027 bookings.
- **Structural decay of delivery** offsetting security/CIS growth, keeping reported growth in the mid-single digits for several more quarters — a long wait for the re-rate.
