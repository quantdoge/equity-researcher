Hiring is concentrated in manufacturing/packaging scale-up and HBM design, consistent with management's capex-increase guidance. **Direction: Up, moderate confidence.**

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **IN-LINE, with decelerating upside**

Next print **28 Jan 2027**: consensus revenue **$89.44B** (+14.4% QoQ from $78.21B), EPS $8.22.

The critical alt-data finding is the **collapse in the second derivative of pricing**:

| Period | DRAM/NAND contract price change QoQ |
|---|---|
| Q4'25 → Q1'26 | +80–90% (Counterpoint) |
| Q2'26 | +58–63% DRAM / +70–75% NAND |
| Q3'26 | ~+20% (reported ASPs) |
| **Q4'26 (guide)** | **+10–15% DRAM (TrendForce, 30 Sep)** |

Prices are still rising in absolute terms — but the *rate* has compressed from ~60% to ~12% in two quarters. Against a +14.4% QoQ revenue consensus, that leaves little room for another +9.6% revenue beat like Q3. **Revenue: in-line to modest beat. Beat magnitude likely well below the +71% EPS blowout of Q2.**

Surprise history: 5 of 6 quarters beat (83% beat rate, avg +24.7%), **but** the revenue series is far cleaner than EPS — revenue beat 4 of 5 (avg +5.6%), while the reported **Q3 "EPS miss" of −34% directly contradicts record operating profit and the +9.6% revenue beat**. I treat the EPS estimate series as unreliable (probable ADR share-count / FX conversion artifact post-July listing) and anchor on revenue.
