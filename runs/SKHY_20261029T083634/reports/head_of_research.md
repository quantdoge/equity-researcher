**Investment Thesis**

SK hynix is the best vehicle to own the most violent memory up-cycle in history: ~50% HBM share, HBM4 first-mover, >50% of 2027–28 output under long-term agreements, an 80% operating margin, ₩100T (~6.5% of market cap) of shareholder returns, and a net-cash balance sheet that can absorb a full 2023-style downturn without external financing. At 4.7x 2027E earnings the market is already pricing a hard margin rollover — so the cheapness is partly real, partly artifact. The correct way to own it is small, time-boxed, and trigger-driven; the wrong way is as a long-horizon compounder, or stacked on top of existing Micron exposure, or — ideally avoid — through this ADR at a 33–40% premium to the Korean line.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: DRAM oligopoly (HHI ≈2,700) at peak earnings — Q3'26 op margin ~80%, revenue +328% YoY, LTAs give visibility no prior cycle had. But the second derivative of pricing has collapsed (+60% QoQ → +12% guided Q4). Macro is earnings-favourable (no recession signal; 2s10s +34bp) but valuation-hostile: Fed re-hiking, 10Y ~5%, real yields ~2.6%, won +12% (FX translation headwind). Sector attractive **for 12–18 months only**; first sequential DRAM price decline = downgrade trigger.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH.** ~88% of revenue sits in the US–China conflict axis. ~⅓ of DRAM output (Wuxi/Dalian) runs on US licences renewed **each December** — the next renewal is the single biggest near-term event risk. Section 232 "phase two" may use a wafer-output test that Indiana's *packaging* plant fails. HBM routes Korea→TSMC→US: an unhedgeable chokepoint. Live DRAM price-fixing class action (treble damages, MTD pending) plus two HBM-focused NPE suits.
- **Value**: Narrow moat. 4-yr avg ROIC 13.7% ≈ WACC; 4-yr FCF conversion 52%. Trailing multiples are meaningless at peak margin; normalised P/E is 25–46x. The ADR at ~$160 trades at a **~40% premium to parity ($114)** — non-arbitrageable, uncompensated.
- **Growth**: Genuine — 50% HBM share, HBM4 mainstream from Q4'26, 83% beat rate, Rule of 40 = 72. But consensus itself models margins decaying to the low-50s and revenue growth to +17% by 2028. TAM growth is real; the revenue *level* is cyclically inflated (2026 revenue exceeds the forecast memory market — pure ASP).
- **Quantitative Signals**: No investable signal — 78 sessions only, alpha is noise (t≈0.07). Robust findings: beta ~3.5, vol ~102%, momentum decaying (−11.8% last 21 sessions), **0.81 correlation to MU**.
- **Financial Risk**: Split rating. Credit/liquidity LOW (net cash, A3/A−/BBB+, Z-score 3.89, survives a 2023 replay with >10 years of cash). Market risk HIGH (−34.6% drawdown in its first six weeks; 29% capex intensity at cycle peak).
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Union formed Aug 2026 with two wage votes decided by <25 votes — strike risk into a sold-out book. ~$500bn cluster capex announced before board approval; chaebol related-party dealings; insider activity unverifiable (FPI data gap).
