## 1. Business Quality Assessment

**Moat rating: NARROW** (and narrower than the current margins imply)

| Evidence for a moat | Evidence against |
|---|---|
| HBM oligopoly: 3 credible suppliers; SK hynix ~50%+ HBM share; HBM4 in mass production, mainstream from Q4'26 | Commodity DRAM/NAND is the majority of revenue and is priced at the margin |
| Qualification cycles + co-development with NVIDIA create real switching costs | 2023: gross margin **−1.6%**, operating margin **−23.6%**, ₩9.1tn net loss |
| LTAs covering **>50% of 2027–28 capacity** — genuine revenue visibility | The cure for high prices is high prices: Samsung, Micron and CXMT are all adding capacity |
| Record ROIC (32.5% in 2025) | **4-yr average ROIC = 13.7%** (7.7%, −9.0%, 23.6%, 32.5%) — roughly WACC. Through-cycle ROIC is the only honest moat test, and it fails |

The HBM franchise is real, but it sits inside a capital-intensive commodity business whose economics are set by the cycle, not by pricing power. An 80% operating margin is not a moat — it is a **standing invitation** to competitors, and the industry is already responding. **Moat has likely widened over 5 years (HBM), but not enough to change the through-cycle character of the business.**
