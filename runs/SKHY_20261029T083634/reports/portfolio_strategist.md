### 2. Consensus Assessment

**Overall: SPLIT on action, HIGH agreement on facts.**

Three findings command unanimous or near-unanimous assent — these are the load-bearing conclusions:

1. **Consensus (11/11): This is peak-cycle earnings, not a compounder.** Every agent independently reached this. The only disagreement is timing (Sector: 2028–29; Alt Data: second derivative already breaking; Short: ~Q3 2027 HBM; Growth: "by construction non-sustainable").
2. **Consensus (Geo, Non-Fin, ESG, Fin Risk, Macro): The China licence regime is the dominant un-hedgeable risk.** ~⅓ of DRAM output runs on permits renewable each December at Washington's discretion. Nothing in the fundamental case prices this.
3. **Consensus (Value, Quant, Alt Data, Short — four independent derivations): The ADR is 33–40% over the Korean ordinary line, and it is not arbitrageable.** This is the single most important finding in the pack and the one the equity research has under-weighted: **it is a pure, uncompensated drag specific to SKHY that does not exist in 000660.KS.**

**Where they genuinely split — and how I resolve it:**

- *Growth (Buy) vs. Short (no edge, structurally expensive on normalised earnings).* Resolved in Growth's favour on a 12–18 month horizon, Short's favour beyond it. Short did the most valuable work in the pack: he proved the accounting is clean (which removes the one risk that would force a pass) and proved the cheapness is an artifact of peak margins. Both can be true. **Horizon is the discriminator, so the position must be time-boxed.**
- *Quant (Neutral) vs. everything else.* Quant's own caveat governs: 78 observations, alpha standard error ±169%. I give it near-zero weight on direction and high weight on two things it measures robustly: **beta ~3.5 and 0.81 correlation to MU.**
- *Macro (Neutral) vs. Growth (Buy).* Macro wins on the multiple, Growth wins on the earnings. Net: the earnings are real and the discount rate is hostile, which is precisely the definition of a name that goes sideways violently.

No agent advocated a full-size position. Two agents (Short, Quant) explicitly recommended against acting on their own analysis. That is a signal in itself.

### 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

**Initiate at 1.5% of portfolio. Hard ceiling 2.0%. Combined memory exposure (SKHY + MU + any NAND) capped at 4.0%.**

Rationale:

- **Kelly is constraint-bound, not edge-bound.** Payoff ~+45% (median PT $237.5) vs ~−42% (bear case) from $164.70, at roughly 55/45 odds → full Kelly ≈14%, half-Kelly ≈7%. That number is unusable: it ignores the −60/−70% equity tail Fin Risk and Quant both flag, ignores the ADR premium, and ignores the 0.81 MU correlation. The binding constraint is not edge, it's **risk contribution.**
- **Volatility math.** At 1.5% weight and ~100% annualised vol, SKHY contributes ~1.5pts of standalone portfolio volatility — roughly **12–13% of a 12%-vol portfolio's entire risk budget** from one position in one cycle. At 2.0%, it's ~17%. Above that, a single Korean licence decision or one DRAM price print becomes a portfolio-level event.
- **Haircut for the wrapper.** A 35–40% ADR premium that compresses to 20% costs ~14% with zero fundamental change. That is a real, quantifiable expected loss that must be sized against. **Where the mandate permits Korean local shares, express the thesis in 000660.KS instead and capture the premium rather than paying it.**
- **Asymmetry test.** Full position loss (not plausible — net cash, no solvency catalyst) at 1.5% = −1.5% of portfolio. Realistic bear case −50% = **−0.75% of portfolio**. Acceptable. At 4%, it's −2.0% — not acceptable for a late-cycle cyclical.

This is a **tactical cycle position, not a core holding.** It does not qualify for a 3–5% high-conviction slot because conviction is Medium, two structural risk flags are rated High, and the wrapper is mispriced against you.

### 6. Risk Management Rules

**Price discipline (portfolio limits, not technical stops — 102% vol makes tight stops pure noise):**
- **Warning level $131** (22 Oct low): halve the position and re-underwrite. Not an automatic exit.
- **Hard stop $124** (the 29 Jul listing-day low): exit the position. Loss ≈ −0.37% of portfolio at 1.5% weight.
- **Trim zone $200 → $214**: trim one third at $200 (+21%, the consensus PT floor), another third at $214 (90% of the $237.5 median PT). Let the final third run only while the fundamental triggers below stay green.
- **Premium rule:** if the ADR premium widens beyond 45%, stop buying; if it compresses toward 20% while KRX is flat, treat the loss as mechanical and do not add.

**Thesis invalidation — exit on ANY of these, regardless of price. These supersede the price stop:**
1. **Any sequential decline in DRAM contract pricing** (Sector's explicit sector-downgrade trigger). This is the highest-quality signal in the pack.
2. **Any LTA renegotiated downward**, or 2027–28 contracted coverage falling below 50%.
3. **An adverse BIS decision at the December 2026 Wuxi/Dalian licence renewal** — or any quantity condition materially tighter than the Dalian 2026 precedent.
4. **Section 232 "phase two" finalised with a wafer-output-based exemption test** (i.e., Indiana's packaging/R&D spend not credited).
5. **HBM4 yield or qualification loss at NVIDIA** such that Samsung compresses SKHY's allocation.
6. **Inventory days turning up.** Already +35% in H1'26 — this preceded both prior downcycles.
7. **The first *sequential decline* in the rate-of-change of operating margin** — the mechanical peak marker.

**Portfolio hedge (optional, for books already long AI semis):** because beta 3.5 × 1.5% weight ≈ 5.25% SPY-equivalent, a book carrying NVDA + TSM + MU alongside this should consider selling ~3–5% notional index (SOXX/SPY) futures against the sleeve rather than shrinking SKHY further. **Do not buy puts on SKHY**: at ~100% implied vol, 20%-OTM protection costs more than the position's expected carry — Short Analyst reached the same conclusion.

The four independent derivations of the ADR premium — Value, Quant, Alt Data and Short, using four different methods — are the most differentiated, actionable finding in the pack. **Nothing in the fundamental research is a reason to pass; the wrapper and the cycle are reasons to be small and time-boxed.**

- **If the mandate can hold Korean shares:** **buy 000660.KS, not SKHY**, and capture the premium instead of paying it. This single decision is worth more than any of the entry-timing refinements above.
- **If it is a tactical or benchmark-relative book:** **initiate at 1.5%, ceiling 2.0%**, scale in thirds, hard stop $124, and exit on the first sequential DRAM price decline without waiting for the price stop.

**Conviction, stated plainly: MEDIUM.** Eleven specialists, unanimous that the cycle is peaking, unanimous that the geopolitics are un-hedgeable, and four independently unanimous that the security I am being asked to buy is the wrong wrapper for the thesis.
