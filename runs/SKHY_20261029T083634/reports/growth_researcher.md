# SK hynix (SKHY) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $164.70 (Oct 28, 2026) · Market cap ≈ $1.16T · Net cash ≈ ₩98.7T (~$74B)*
*Note: FMP per-share fields for this ADR are on an inconsistent share count (7.06B diluted vs. ~688M actual). I therefore built all multiples off aggregate KRW statements + total market cap, at ~₩1,333/USD (implied by the $75B ≈ ₩100T return plan).*

---

## 1. Growth Story

SK hynix has converted memory from a commodity into a co-designed AI subsystem. HBM4 is now mainstream (Q4'26), the company holds roughly 50% of HBM revenue and an estimated 60–70% of NVIDIA's Rubin HBM4 allocation, and it has signed **Long-Term Agreements with ~10 customers covering more than 50% of 2027–2028 production capacity** — a contractual structure that has never existed in this industry. Revenue compounded 25% over 2021–25 but the real inflection is now: revenue ran ₩52.6T → ₩79.3T → ₩104.6T across Q1–Q3'26 (+328% YoY in Q3) with a **76% then 80% operating margin**, and management expects demand to exceed supply *through 2030*. This is the single most disruptive margin structure in semiconductors today.

## 3. TAM Assessment

- **HBM**: ~$40–50B in 2026 → $100B+ by 2027 (Micron), with SK hynix projecting **~30% annual AI-memory market growth through 2030**. HBM consumes ~3x the wafer capacity per gigabyte of standard DRAM — structurally tightening all DRAM.
- **Total memory**: $203B (2026) → $403B (2033). AI already absorbs **~70% of DRAM supply** in 2026; all three suppliers' 2027 capacity is sold out.
- **The critical read-through**: SK hynix alone will book ~$269B of revenue in 2026 — *more than the entire forecast memory market*. That gap is pure ASP inflation, not unit volume. Penetration opportunity is genuinely huge (physical AI, robotics, enterprise inference are pre-inflection), but the current revenue base embeds a price windfall that will mean-revert. TAM expansion is real; the *revenue level* is cyclically inflated.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Late Jan 2027 — Q4'26 results & 2027 guide.** HBM4 to surpass HBM3E in both volume and revenue; first full-year visibility on LTA pricing. Highest-impact event.
- **H1 2027 — HBM4E qualification & ramp.** Samples already shipped; 2027 pricing negotiation could deliver another ASP step-up (HBM4 launched with >50% price increases).
- **Ongoing 2026–27 — Return of capital.** ~₩100T (~$75B ≈ **6.5% of market cap in one year**), including the ₩40T ($29B) buyback-and-cancel announced Aug '26 plus a Kioxia-gain special dividend. Mechanical EPS accretion.
- **Q4'26 — M15X at maximum HBM4 capacity**; Yongin/M17 first-fab timelines provide the 2028 supply answer investors are debating.
- **NVIDIA $500B AI-memory LOI (to 2030)** converting into binding multi-year contracts — the single biggest de-risking event for the 2029–30 earnings stream.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

**Supportive:**
- **Job postings**: 9,000+ SK hynix roles listed in the US on LinkedIn; ~64–75 SK hynix America openings (System-in-Package Architect, ADK Engineer, Staff DRAM Engineer) — consistent with packaging/HBM roadmap build-out and the Indiana fab.
- **Pricing power confirmation**: HBM4 prices raised >50%; DRAM and NAND both up sequentially with management calling demand unresolvable through 2030.
- **LTAs stack up**: 2027 DRAM/HBM capacity sold out across all three suppliers.

**Contradicting:**
- **Google Trends, "HBM memory" (US)**: spiked to 100 in late June 2026, then collapsed to **3–5 by October** — *below* the 5–16 pre-spike baseline. Retail attention is fully exhausted. Contrarian at the extreme, but it also marks hype-cycle completion.
- **Price action**: IPO'd July 10 at ~$168; range $124.78 (Jul 29) → $199.87 (Sep 9) → $164.70 today. The stock is **below its IPO price despite 328% YoY revenue growth** — that is multiple compression, and it happened while the +26% five-day drawdown in mid-October was driven by CXMT and 2029-oversupply chatter, not by results.
- **Analyst PTs**: 8 initiations at $200–330 (Barclays $330 cut to $300; Needham $200 raised to $235; Wolfe $200 → $250; Cantor $300). Median ~$240 = **+46% upside**, and the revision direction is net higher.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (not Strong Buy)

The rare case of a genuine secular growth asset priced as a melting ice cube. You are buying the #1 AI-memory franchise at **4.7x 2027E earnings and 2.9x EV/EBITDA**, with ₩98.7T net cash, half of 2027–28 output contractually committed, and 6.5% of the market cap being returned in twelve months. Consensus estimates are *rising*, not falling (+57% revision score), and price targets sit ~46% above spot. Cathie-style framing: TAM is compounding ~30% and the moat (co-development, yield, qualification cycles) is real.

Why not Strong Buy: **an 80% operating margin in a capital-intensive commodity industry is, by construction, non-sustainable**, and consensus itself models operating margins decaying from ~75% toward the low-50s by 2028 and revenue growth collapsing to +17%. At 4.7x forward, the market is not paying for growth — it is betting the earnings stream breaks. Until the 2028–29 supply response is quantified and the NVIDIA LTAs are binding, this stays a high-conviction Buy, not a conviction-monoculture Strong Buy. **Add on capitulation; the risk/reward is roughly 3:1 up.**

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Price mean reversion (primary).** Revenue in 2026 exceeds the entire forecast memory market — it is ASP-driven. A price normalization of even 30% takes operating margins from 80% to ~50% and halves EPS.
2. **2029 supply wave.** Korean industry voices and Musk ("China will be making a LOT of RAM in 2028") point to a "red ocean" in 2029–30; Samsung, CXMT and Yongin/M17 capacity all land in that window.
3. **China / CXMT.** New high-density server DRAM architecture announced Oct '26 and HBM3E risk production with 2027 mass-production potential. Analysts doubt yield scale, but it is the cheapest bear catalyst to re-trigger.
4. **Samsung share recapture.** Samsung's annual DRAM wafer capacity (~8.18M) already exceeds SK hynix's (~6.66M); HBM4 qualification at NVIDIA could compress SK hynix's 50–70% allocation.
5. **LTA protection is partial.** Contracts "reflect market conditions" — they cover volume, not fixed pricing, so they dampen but do not prevent a downcycle.
6. **Capex / FCF conversion.** M15X, Yongin, M17 and the US fab will bring a depreciation step-up and could shrink FCF conversion exactly as returns ramp.
7. **Extreme volatility & customer concentration.** Beta 2.39; a newly listed, thin ADR line; revenue tied to a handful of hyperscalers whose capex is itself being questioned in the current AI-trade selloff.
