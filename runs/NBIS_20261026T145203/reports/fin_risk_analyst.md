## Financial Risk Assessment: Nebius Group N.V. (NBIS)

### 1. Credit Risk Assessment
**Rating: Sub-Investment Grade**

While the balance sheet strength (Liquidity) is currently strong, the company's operational profile presents a significant credit risk.
*   **Solvency:** The Net Debt/EBITDA (2.32x) and Debt/Equity (1.08x) suggest moderate financial leverage, which is manageable for a technology company, provided it can achieve profitability.
*   **Debt Serviceability (Critical Weakness):** The **Interest Coverage ratio is severely negative (-10.31x)**, indicating the company is currently unable to cover its interest expenses with its operating income. This is a primary immediate concern.
*   **Distress Indicators:** The **Altman Z-Score is 1.1**, placing NBIS firmly in the **Distress zone** (Z < 1.81). This indicates a high probability of bankruptcy or significant solvency issues in the medium term if operational losses persist.
*   **Qualitative Strength:** The **Piotroski F-Score is 8**, suggesting that, despite operational losses, the underlying accounting structure is strong and demonstrates positive momentum in several areas (e.g., positive ROA and positive change in equity).

### 4. Downside Scenario Analysis: 20% Revenue Drop
A 20% drop in revenue presents a critical challenge for a company already operating at a -112.53% operating margin.
*   **Impact on Cash Flow:** Since the company is already heavily cash-negative (FCF of -$3.68B), a 20% revenue drop would significantly accelerate the cash burn rate, reducing the duration of the current cash runway.
*   **Solvency Risk:** While the current liquidity buffers (Current Ratio of 3.08x) are robust enough to absorb some shocks, they are not infinite. A revenue drop would likely force a reduction in OpEx or, more likely, necessitate a capital raise.
*   **Equity Value:** Given the extreme Beta (3.847) and high volatility (114.72%), a 20% drop in revenue would likely result in a far greater than 20% decline in stock price, creating a substantial downward spiral risk if investor confidence is lost.
*   **Conclusion:** The company has sufficient *liquidity* to withstand a cyclical downturn, but its structural *operational* weakness makes it highly susceptible to prolonged downturn scenarios.

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: High
The combination of operational distress (negative FCF, negative Interest Coverage, Z-Score in the Distress Zone) and extreme market risk (Beta of 3.847, 114% volatility) necessitates a "High" risk rating. The strong balance sheet acts as a buffer but does not mitigate the core operational and execution risks.

### 6. Key Red Flags
*   **Negative Interest Coverage:** The inability to cover interest payments is a severe warning sign of financial distress.
*   **Negative FCF and Operating Margin:** The company is operating at a massive loss, creating immense pressure on its ability to scale and generate returns.
*   **High Market Sensitivity:** The Beta of 3.847 means external market forces can trigger severe losses disproportionate to the overall market.
