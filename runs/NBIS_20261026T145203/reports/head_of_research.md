### Investment Memo: NBIS (Nebius Group N.V.) — October 2026
*Data as of 26 October 2026 close: $158.93 | ~$40B diluted mkt cap (~$49B fully diluted incl. converts) | Next catalyst: Q3 earnings, 10 Nov 2026*

**Verdict**: HOLD — do not initiate at spot; conditional BUY at ≤$125 or on post-Q3 confirmation | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $165 (+4% vs spot) — probability-weighted intrinsic value (25% bull ~$300 / 45% base ~$155 / 30% bear ~$65). The $289 analyst median embeds a bull-case weighting we do not underwrite.
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Nebius is the best-operated asset in the neocloud complex — fastest growth in the peer set (+479% FY25; ARR $3.0B, +598% YoY), cheapest capital (~2.3% debt cost vs CoreWeave's 8.3%), $46B of contracted backlog substantially pre-funded by ~$9B of customer prepayments, and the steepest margin-improvement trajectory in the group. But at $159 the security prices in base-case perfection: it trades at probability-weighted fair value with zero margin of safety, has never earned its cost of capital, burns -$3.7B of FCF, and must raise ~$20–25B into a macro regime that is actively tightening *because of* the AI buildout. This is an excellent company and a fully-priced, financing-fragile security. Own it at a discount, not at spot.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector **Neutral** — demand structurally exceeds supply (~50%+ CAGR, power scarcity = pricing power), but the sector has entered a financing-driven consolidation where the marginal dollar flows to funding capacity, not to every participant (CRWV -38% in October, distressed bonds). Macro **Unfavourable**: Fed hiked to 3.75–4.00% (16 Sep) with a second hike guided for December; real yields +51bp since mid-August; HY OAS +73bp in six weeks to 3.38%. The AI buildout is now the Fed's core inflation driver — the boom raises the cost of capital for the companies funding it. NBIS is the relative winner (best capitalised) in an absolute headwind.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium-High**. ~80% of ~$40B commitments rest on two US hyperscalers. Vineland, NJ (300MW, on the critical path of the Microsoft contract): >$1M DEP fine for 62 unpermitted generators, two stop-work notices, resident class action — the most concrete quantified contingency in the package. GPU export-control "taint" exposure across six jurisdictions; residual Russia/Yandex tail (Volozh de-listed from EU sanctions March 2024 — mitigating, not eliminating).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **SELL/AVOID at spot**. Base-case intrinsic ~$140–165 today (from $175–205 in 2028, discounted); bear $40–70; bull $300+. ROIC negative in every year of the modern entity (-2.7% → -12.1% → -6.2%). Clean trailing EBITDA ~$160M → EV/EBITDA ~290x; FY25 headline net income (+$101.7M) was manufactured by non-operating gains against a -$596M operating loss. Moat is "narrow at best" — capacity, not pricing power. Re-entry zone $100–115.
