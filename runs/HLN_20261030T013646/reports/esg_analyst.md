## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **LOW**

- **Carbon:** Total footprint across all scopes **2,645 kt CO₂e** (2025); Scope 3 ≈ **2,529 kt** (2024) — i.e. ~96% of footprint, Scope 1+2 ~62 kt. Intensity ≈ **240 tCO₂e per £m revenue** (~0.024 kg CO₂e per £ revenue) — an order of magnitude below pharma/chemicals peers. Even at an internal shadow price of $100/t, total footprint cost ≈ **$265m (~1.6% of revenue)**, and most sits with suppliers.
- **Targets:** net-zero **2040** (value chain); net Scope 1+2 **−100% by 2030 vs 2020 (95% absolute)**; Scope 3 **−42% by 2030 vs 2022**. Progress 2025: **Scope 1+2 −55%**, **Scope 3 −13%**; **80% of packaging recycle-ready**; virgin petroleum-based plastic down (target −10% by 2025, −⅓ by 2030). Scope 3 trajectory is the weak spot — 5 years into an 8-year target at −13% (company claims "on track").
- **Regulatory-driven capex:** EU PPWR (recycled content, recyclability, labelling) and UK Plastic Packaging Tax/EPR are the main environmental cost lines; Haleon has publicly flagged "serious concerns" about PPWR timings — i.e. compliance cost is real but manageable vs. revenue.
- **Physical climate/water:** Modest; manufacturing footprint includes water-stressed geographies (India, LatAm) — site-level, not thesis-level.
- **Environmental controversies:** None material found. No spills/major pollution enforcement identified.

**Verdict:** No stranded assets, no carbon-tax cliff, no remediation liabilities. Sustainalytics recognises Haleon as **"Industry Low Carbon Leader"** (2025).

## 3. Social Risk Assessment: **MEDIUM**

- **Product safety (elevated):** **Gas-X Extra Strength Softgels voluntary nationwide recall (4 June 2026)** — 4 lots, contamination traced to a machine coolant leak (diluted propylene glycol); 120ct/72ct bottles distributed Apr–May 2026. Small revenue impact (<£10m est.), but a **GxP/quality-control failure** at a US site and a marker of operational-control risk in a portfolio of hundreds of OTC SKUs. This is the single most repeatable social risk for the franchise (Panadol/Voltaren/Theraflu scale makes one bad lot reputationally expensive).
- **Human capital:** **January 2026 operating-model restructuring** under "Win as One" — six Operating Units, new Chief Growth/Chief Transformation Officer roles, new CFO (Dawn Allen); press reports of **hundreds of UK roles and potentially thousands globally** eliminated. Retention/culture risk during demerger-plus-restructure; severance charges and possible industrial-relations friction in European sites.
- **Safety metrics:** Lost-time reportable injury & illness rate **0.05 per 100,000 hours** (employees; similar for contractors, 2025) — genuinely strong for a manufacturer.
- **Diversity:** UK median gender pay gap **7.0%**, mean hourly **6.0%** — better than UK national average, no red flag.
- **Access & marketing:** "Empowered 50m people annually to access improved health" (listing-era responsible-business goal, stated as achieved in 2025); health-inclusivity programme. Marketing-to-children risk is limited (adult OTC).
- **Supply chain labour:** Long ingredient/packaging supply chain across EM; EU CSDDD readiness is the future test (see §6). No modern-slavery or supplier-labour controversy surfaced in my search window.

## 4. Governance Quality Score: **7 / 10**

**Positives:** UK premium-listed governance architecture — **separate Chair/CEO** (Banga/McNamara), requirement that **≥half the board excluding the Chair be independent NEDs**, one-share-one-vote (**no dual class**), annual say-on-pay, credible committee structure, **ESG qualifier embedded in the Performance Share Plan** (aligns sustainability with long-term incentive), orderly Chair succession (Banga, 1 Jan 2026, £757,103 fee; Alan Stewart Senior Independent Director).

**Deductions:**
- **Zantac indemnity asymmetry.** Haleon may be required to indemnify **GSK and/or Pfizer** for OTC Zantac liabilities — a legacy structure negotiated at demerger in which the listed vehicle carries a contingent liability it did not generate, with limited disclosure granularity. That is a shareholder-rights/disclosure quality issue, not merely a legal one.
- **Board refreshment intensity:** Chair, CFO and operating model all changed within ~18 months of listing-era leadership → execution and institutional-memory risk.
- **Remuneration optics:** restructuring-driven margin delivery is flowing into incentive outcomes while investment ("quality of growth") is questioned by brokers — comp metrics skew to adjusted operating margin, which can under-reward reinvestment.
- No evidence of anti-corruption/bribery enforcement; Belgian competition fine (below) is a conduct-culture marker.

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure

- **UK (domicile):** TCFD-aligned climate disclosure already mandatory; UK ISSB (S1/S2) endorsement will raise Scope 3 assurance burden. UK Plastic Packaging Tax + EPR.
- **EU (large revenue base):** **CSRD** applies to a non-EU parent group with EU turnover >€150m from **FY2028** (report in 2029) — first full statutory sustainability statement with limited assurance, a real reporting-cost step-up. **EU CSDDD** (value-chain due diligence, phased to ~2029 for this cohort) — supplier data in EM is the hard part. **PPWR** (recycled content/recyclability/labelling) — the most cash-relevant rule. **EUDR** (forest-derived packaging/pulp) — explicitly called out in Haleon's own 2025 report; supplier compliance burden. **Green Claims Directive** — substantiation risk on "recycle-ready"/"sustainable" marketing.
- **US:** Haleon is an FPI filing 20-F; **the SEC climate-disclosure rule does not bind it** and is in any event stayed — so no US climate-reporting exposure. US product-liability and FDA cGMP inspection exposure dominate instead.
- **Debt:** no ESG-linked bond covenants identified (recent activity is conventional note tenders, e.g. 2027 3.375% notes) — so no coupon step-up risk. **Positive.**

## 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEUTRAL** (slight positive tilt)

- **Not a thesis driver either way.** Carbon/water/physical-climate risks are immaterial to cash flows (~240 tCO₂e per £m revenue; ~1.6% of revenue even at $100/t). There is **no ESG-linked cost-of-capital penalty** and no green-premium catalyst.
- **The only ESG item that can move valuation is Zantac.** The demerger indemnity to GSK/Pfizer is the residual ESG/G tail; the April 2026 Delaware dismissal of 80,000+ cases is materially de-risking, but the **H2 2026 MDL/Daubert decision** is the swing factor. Until resolved, expect a modest persistent discount.
- **Watch items that would change the call to Negative:** (i) an MDL revival or a plaintiff verdict; (ii) a second product-safety/QC failure in 12 months (pattern risk after Gas-X); (iii) Scope 3 progress stalling below the −42%/2030 glidepath, triggering target reset/criticism; (iv) restructuring-related attrition or labour disputes in EU works councils.
- **Positive optionality:** standard-setting governance (independent chair, one-share-one-vote, ESG in PSP) plus sector-leading carbon intensity makes HLN eligible for mainstream ESG-screened mandates — a modest, durable demand-side support rather than a re-rating catalyst.
