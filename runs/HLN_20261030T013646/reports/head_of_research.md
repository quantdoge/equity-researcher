### Investment Memo: HLN (Haleon plc) — 1 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium-High
**Price Target**: $9.25 per ADR (+8% from current $8.57)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Haleon is the highest-margin franchise in consumer health (63.4% gross / 21.9% operating) trading at the group's cheapest cash-flow multiple (~6.7% FCF yield, ~15.5x FY26E P/E) — but it is a 2.5% organic grower whose price-led growth engine is running out of fuel just as a real-yield shock compresses the multiple on low-beta duration assets. The October guidance cut (FY26 organic to ~2.5%) and 14% one-day de-rating have brought the stock to ~7% below our $9.25 intrinsic value estimate: close enough to fair value that there is no margin of safety to buy, yet with clean accounting, a £500m buyback and an under-priced respiratory recovery skewing the next two quarters positively, no case to sell either. Eight of eleven agents land on hold/neutral/no-edge; none recommends buying at $8.57.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro unfavourable (−1/−2): the +47bp 10Y real-yield shock is the binding constraint on a 0.25-beta, 2.5x-levered compounder; pricing (+2.1%) now runs ~160bp below CPI (3.72%) — a classic staples squeeze. Sector neutral: category volume-starved, private label gaining; HLN is best-in-class on margins (+11.9pp GM vs peer median) but second-slowest grower. Post-Kenvue absorption, HLN is the last large listed pure-play — scarcity/M&A optionality.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Medium with a High tail. China Centrum import suspension already crystallised — CEO sees no fix through 2027; Middle East energy/freight cost shock; Section 232 tariff scope for consumer health unsettled; residual Russia presence. Zantac is an indemnity/contribution dispute (not primary liability) against a backdrop of mass dismissals and capped industry settlements.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: IV ≈ $9.25 probability-weighted (bear $6.85 / base $9.05 / bull $11.50), cross-checked at $9.4–9.9 on earnings. Narrow moat; ROIC 9.6% vs ~7.5% WACC; FCF/NI 121–155%. At $8.57 there is no 20–30% margin of safety. Buy ≤$7.00; trim >$11.00.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: Fails a growth mandate: Rule of 40 = 16, EPS CAGR ~5.4%, volume/mix only +0.7%. Thesis is self-help margins + buybacks, now cracked by the guidance cut. Key catalysts: FY26 results (late Feb 2027) and Kenvue/Kimberly-Clark close (strategic-bid optionality).

Management guided off backward-looking Jul–Sep destocking — Q4-26/FY27 carry restocking upside against a reset bar. Caveat: US-only data for a company ~60% non-US; respiratory is ~11–13% of revenue.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: Reject short — no edge. Cash conversion 140%, Beneish −2.457, margins rising on falling revenue; the GLP-1 bear narrative cuts in HLN's favour (+24% sales lift in GLP-1 destination sets). Watch-items only: DSO creep 44→70 days and 78% intangible balance sheet.

**Bull Case**
- Best-in-class economics at a 25–35% peer discount: 6.7% FCF yield, ~15.5x FY26E earnings, scarcity value as the last listed consumer-health pure-play post-Kenvue (P&G/Reckitt/Nestlé are logical buyers).
- Under-priced near-term earnings skew: early/severe flu season + retailer restocking vs a de-risked ~2.5% bar, with a £500m buyback under the tape and a 71% historical beat rate.
- Clean, self-correcting fundamentals: Piotroski 9/9, negative accruals, +510bp operating margin since 2022, leverage down from ~4x at demerger, payout ~63% of FCF.

**Bear Case**
- Structural growth exhaustion: +0.7% volume/mix, price below CPI, private label at ~24% unit share and gaining; oral care (+8.8%) is carrying the portfolio on price alone — 2.5% may prove a ceiling, not a trough.
- China Centrum suspension with no remediation path through 2027, Middle East cost inflation, Section 232 tail, and Russia reputational overhang — the items that already caused the largest one-day fall on record.
- Macro stacked against the multiple: a real-yield-led bear steepener compresses low-beta duration assets; a BoE+Fed double-hike scenario plausibly takes HLN toward 12x earnings (~250–270p / ~$6.70–7.20).

**Key Risks**
1. **Zantac indemnity** — uncapped, both GSK and Pfizer have served notice; 11th Circuit Daubert ruling (H2-2026) or Delaware appeal could revive exposure. Binary tail; provision unverified (gap).
2. **Second guidance cut** — if flu season peaks early and collapses as in 2025-26, and oral-care volume stays negative, the 4–6% medium-term algorithm is unreachable and the multiple de-rates again.
3. **Intangible impairment** — £25.6bn (79% of assets) at carve-out values; non-cash but sentiment-resetting.
4. **Competitive escalation** — a synergy-funded Kimberly-Clark/Kenvue attacking the same shelf.
5. **Rates/FX** — refinancing drag and a mechanical ~2% annual ADR leak if USD strength persists.
