### 0. The Setup in One Line
HLN fell **-14% on 2026-10-29** (10.00 → 8.57, 56.8M shares vs ~15M average) after cutting FY26 organic growth guidance to **~2.5% from 3–5%**, blamed on **"soft cold & flu consumption leading to lower orders"** — while every forward-looking alt-data source I can find says the 2026-27 cold & flu season has started **early and is running hot**. That divergence is the entire investment question.

**The signal is almost entirely respiratory.** Theraflu's seasonal ramp began mid-August 2026 (16 → 24 → 28 → 37) and is now tracking **~5 weeks ahead of the 2025-26 season** (37 is a level 2025 did not reach until Nov 23). This is corroborated by independent channel checks, not just search:
- **Reuters (Oct 20, 2026):** *"Early US flu season, current strain may signal risk for younger people"*
- **CDC:** 2026-27 season officially began **Oct 4, 2026** — earliest start in five years
- **Walgreens 2026-27 Flu Index / ABC News (Oct 2026):** **~20% YoY increase in cold & flu medicine sales**, "double-digit YoY increases"

**This is the sharpest contradiction in the dataset.** Management blamed weak cold/flu consumption, but the softness was a *Jul–Sep sell-in/destocking* phenomenon driven by the abrupt collapse of the 2025-26 season (Theraflu search fell 100 → 12 between Dec 21 and Jun 28). Consumption at retail is now inflecting up hard.

**The one genuinely bearish read:** Oral Health delivered **+8.8% organic in Q3 — yet Sensodyne search interest is flat-to-up-marginally, and the Apr–May 2026 spike to 100 was a transient anomaly (not a demand trend).** Price-led growth with no volume confirmation in the category that supplies ~30% of revenue. Q1 2026 had +3.7% price against **-2.7% volume/mix**; the price lever is largely spent, and search does not support a volume recovery in oral care. Centrum is likewise flat headed into its normal Nov–Feb seasonal ramp.

⚠️ *Google Trends "Panadol" US data is low-volume and unreliable (not a US brand); flag as directional only. UK/EU/EM geo pulls would be more informative and are the key gap here.*

- **Web traffic (haleon.com): unavailable** — SimilarWeb returned only third-party comparison pages, no monthly series. Corporate site traffic is a poor proxy here anyway; brand sites (sensodyne.com, theraflu.com) would be the right target and are the main missing feed.
- **Capital return as a flow signal:** new **£500m buyback (announced Oct 29)** on top of an existing programme — easily FCF-covered, and a meaningful bid under the stock during a de-rating. Supporting, though it failed to arrest the 14% drop.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **ABOVE consensus (modestly) — respiratory-led, timing-shifted toward Q4 2026 / FY27**

- **FY26 is now effectively de-risked.** Guidance was reset to ~2.5% after a +2.6% Q3. The bar is low and the bad news is public and priced.
- **Q4 2026 / H1 2027 carry upside risk** to the newly-lowered bar: Respiratory is roughly 11–13% of revenue and the only category with a positive leading indicator. Early-season demand + retailer under-ordering implies a **restocking catch-up that has not yet been modelled**.
- **Historical surprise record supports the upside skew:** **71.4% beat rate over 7 quarters.** Excluding the 2024 anomaly (+109% on a clearly erroneous £0.11 estimate), average surprise is **~+3.2%**, with beats of +3.6%, +3.4%, +5.2%, +9.1%. Haleon habitually guides conservatively and delivers low-single-digit beats. ⚠️ *Revenue estimates in the vendor calendar are unreliable (7.52bn actual vs 6.40bn "estimate" in Q3-26) due to ADR/semi-annual reporting misalignment — use direction, not levels.*
- **Offsetting negative:** lack of oral-care volume/price confirmation caps how much beat there can be, and FX/EM remain headwinds.

**Net: I would characterise the next two quarters as set up for a modest positive surprise against a reset bar, driven by a single identifiable variable (flu season severity/timing) that is currently the least-modelled item on the sell side.**

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH (contrarian) — with a near-term caveat**
The aggregate of *leading* indicators (early flu start, Theraflu +42% YoY, Walgreens channel sales +20% YoY, UBS corroboration) points to a respiratory recovery that the company just guided against and that JPM's Underweight case explicitly depends on not happening. The guidance cut is grounded in **backward-looking sell-in (Jul–Sep destocking)** — precisely the kind of data alt investors should fade. Technical support from a £500m buyback and a 14% de-rating lower the bar.

**Caveat that keeps this honest:** the flat oral-care search curve says the other ~30% of the business grows on price, not volume. This is a **tradeable 2–3 quarter earnings-momentum call, not a broken-out structural re-rating story.** Sequentially, Q4 restocking is a timing hope, not a certainty.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Strong, multi-source, mutually corroborating evidence on the respiratory inflection (search + epidemiological + retail POS + sell-side). Marked down for: (a) US-geo-only Trends data for a company with ~60% of revenue outside the US; (b) no brand-level web traffic or POS panel access; (c) patents feed dead; (d) the search-interest → revenue transmission chain is indirect and respiratory is only ~11–13% of the P&L.

**Data limitations:** Google Trends is US-only here and measures interest, not units or price; keywords are brand-level not SKU-level; the 2025-26 season's abnormal shape distorts every YoY comparison; web-traffic and patent feeds failed; vendor earnings-calendar revenue estimates are internally inconsistent. **Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one.**

### 8. What Would Confirm / Break the Thesis
- **Confirm:** Theraflu/flu-search stays >40 through November; Walgreens/CDC data keep printing early-season records; a respiratory sell-in inflection appears in the Q4 statement; oral-care volume turns positive. Watch for sell-side PT cuts to stall (JPM already at 295p).
- **Break:** Flu season peaks early (Dec) and collapses as in 2025-26, producing a second consecutive destocking year; retailers structurally de-stock cough/cold; oral-care volume stays negative — in which case 2.5% growth is a *ceiling*, not a floor, and the 4–6% medium-term target goes unreachable.
