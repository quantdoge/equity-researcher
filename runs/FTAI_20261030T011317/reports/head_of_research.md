**Verdict**: HOLD (no new capital) | Conviction: Medium-Low

**Investment Thesis**
FTAI is a genuinely impressive operating business — 43–76% revenue growth, CFM56 aftermarket share up from <2% to ~14%, ROIC at 20.4% and rising, plus a free option on AI data-centre power ($1.465B genset order) — wrapped in an unacceptable financial structure: five consecutive years of negative FCF, an $812M gap between net income and operating cash flow, 10.3x debt/equity, and a live securities-fraud class action aimed at the core EBITDA line. At $196 the stock trades above our $150–170 intrinsic value anchor, reached via a +17% single-day earnings gap, with the 2027 guide ($2.3B EBITDA) embedding $450–750M from a Power segment that has delivered zero units. The business is real; the earnings quality and the price are the problem.
