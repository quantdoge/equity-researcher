## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**#1 — Medicaid State-Directed Payment (SDP) cap. Probability: High. Impact: High.**
CMS proposed rule **CMS-2449-P** (July 2026, comment period closed ~20 Jul 2026) would **cap Medicaid state-directed payments at Medicare rates** (LeadingAge; HHAeXchange; Coral Health Advisors). TipRanks explicitly attributes DaVita's sharp selloff to "a proposed federal crackdown on Medicaid state-directed payments." This is the single largest identifiable threat to the equity — it compresses supplemental Medicaid revenue across a multi-state footprint, and DaVita has meaningful Medicaid/dual-eligible mix in California, Florida, Texas and New York.

**#2 — CY2027 ESRD PPS rate cycle. Probability: Certain (event). Impact: Medium-High.**
CMS's CY2027 proposed rule (91 FR 38784, ~Aug 2026) proposes a base rate of **$299.55** (ANNA comment letter), with comments due 24 Aug 2026; the final rule is expected late Oct/Nov 2026 — i.e., **pending as of this memo**. Context: CY2026 delivered only a ~1.9% proposed update on $6.9B of facility payments (Applied Policy). Kidney Care Partners has repeatedly flagged that updates lag the cost basket (e.g., 1.8% proposed for CY2025). Management already guides only **1–2% full-year revenue-per-treatment growth** (TipRanks) — thin against wage inflation. Structural negative: **oral-only phosphate binders/calcimimetics folding into the bundle** (effective 2025) transfers drug margin risk onto DaVita.

**#3 — Brazil political/trade shock & LatAm FX. Probability: Medium. Impact: Low-Medium (portfolio-level).**
Brazil faces a **2026 runoff election with a right-leaning Congress**, which FTI flags as raising policy and business risk; simultaneously **25% US tariffs** on most Brazilian goods (since 22 Jul 2026) and PIIE's warning of a US "financial autonomy" squeeze create FX and capital-flow volatility. DaVita has been *adding* risk here (Brasnefro acquisition from Fresenius, CADE-approved with divestitures). Earnings translation and repatriation risk are real but immaterial to consolidated EPS (<5%).

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Regulatory posture — adversarial and multi-front:**
- **California AB 290** (targeting dialysis industry profits via charitable-premium-assistance restrictions): the **9th Circuit struck down key provisions in April 2026** (CalMatters/RWCPulse) — a *material win*. But the underlying political coalition (SEIU-UHW) has repeatedly returned via ballot initiative (Prop 29, defeated 2022), so this is a **recurring, not resolved, risk**.
- **Marietta Memorial Hospital v. DaVita (SCOTUS 2022)** — DaVita **lost**; the Court held group health plans may cap dialysis benefits, legitimizing out-of-network steering. This structurally pressures commercial RPT and validates payer differentiation.
- **Medicare Advantage / IKC**: DaVita's risk-based integrated-care growth raises risk-adjustment coding scrutiny; MA rate pressure is cited as a current headwind (Seeking Alpha, Oct 2026).

**Litigation history — chronic, large-ticket FCA exposure (a structural red flag for a serial acquirer of physician practices):**
- **$450M** — medication-waste whistleblower settlement (Bloomberg Law)
- **$350M** — kickbacks to induce patient referrals (Corporate Crime Reporter)
- **$270M** — HealthCare Partners FCA/whistleblower resolution (Becker's)
- **≈$895M** — earlier kidney-treatment fraud settlement (CorpWatch)
- **DOJ/USAO subpoenas** over the charitable premium-assistance/"dialysis aid" arrangements (Boston USAO, Law360) — the theory behind AB 290, still live nationally
- **Antitrust**: legacy criminal no-poach matter (United States v. DaVita), plus FTC-mandated divestitures (2017) as the price of past M&A

**Data privacy / cyber (Compliance-relevant):**
- **March–April 2025 ransomware intrusion** affecting **>915,000 individuals**, with medical/identity/prescription data exfiltrated → **$15M** class settlement in principle (Law360; classlawdc; LinkedIn, 2026)
- **$3.8M** settlement over third-party pixel/web-tracking disclosures (wiretap/HIPAA theory) — a second, distinct privacy failure mode

## 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale.** Geographic risk is genuinely low: ~90% US revenue, no Russia/China/sanctions exposure, and an international segment that is growing but still <10% of sales. However, the **regulatory and litigation sub-components are High**: single-payer concentration in Medicare, an active Medicaid SDP rulemaking that has already moved the stock (down ~25% from a $247.49 52-week high to ~$178.79 on 2 Oct 2026), a pending CY2027 PPS final rule, and a two-decade pattern of nine-figure False Claims Act resolutions. The 9th Circuit's AB 290 ruling is the clearest near-term positive; the SDP cap and CY2027 final rule are the clearest near-term negatives.

**Watch items (next 1–3 quarters):** (i) CY2027 ESRD PPS **final** rule, expected late Oct/Nov 2026; (ii) CMS-2449-P SDP finalization; (iii) Q3 2026 results **29 Oct 2026** — guidance on Medicaid mix; (iv) any new DOJ subpoena disclosure in the FY2026 10-K (Feb 2027); (v) Brazilian runoff outcome and BRL trajectory.
