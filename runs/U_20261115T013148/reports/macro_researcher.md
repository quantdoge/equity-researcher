## MACRO ENVIRONMENT ASSESSMENT — UNITY SOFTWARE (NYSE: U)

**Data as of 15 November 2026. All macro series from FRED; company data from Q3'26 results (reported 5 Nov 2026).**

---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — "REFLATION WITH A TERM-PREMIUM PROBLEM" (Risk-on, but rates-led)

The regime is **not** a recession scare and **not** a disinflationary easing cycle. It is a reflationary boom colliding with a hawkish Fed and a violent repricing of the long end. Real GDP reaccelerated to **+3.4% in Q3'26** (vs +2.2% Q2, +2.5% Q1), unemployment has *fallen* to **4.1%** from 4.5% a year ago, yet headline CPI has **re-accelerated to 3.82%** from 2.65% in December 2025 — a **+117bp reversal**. The Fed, having cut to a 3.63% trough in June–August 2026, has **resumed hiking** (3.75% Sep → 3.88% Oct). The 10-year Treasury has backed up **~75bp since 29 June** to 5.13%, and the 30-year sits at **5.47%**. The 2s10s curve has dis-inverted to **+39bp** — a bear-steepening driven by term premium, not by growth optimism being priced out.

Critically, **credit is wide open**: HY OAS is **320bp**, only 54bp off its September tights and in the tightest decile historically. This is the single most important macro fact for Unity, because the company is the subject of a reported **~$25B take-private approach from Silver Lake and Saudi Arabia's PIF** (Reuters, 5 Nov 2026). LBO viability depends on financing markets that are currently functioning well even as base rates climb. Equity indices confirm risk-on: S&P 500 at 7,562, Nasdaq at 25,136.

**For a 2.11-beta, 23x EV/EBITDA long-duration software asset, this is a genuinely two-sided environment.** Growth and credit are tailwinds; discount rates and the dollar are headwinds.

### 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

Unity's revenue mix is the crux: **Grow (advertising/Vector) is now ~70% of revenue**, with Vector up **126% YoY** and Strategic Grow **+90%**. Q3'26 delivered $622M revenue, first-ever GAAP profit ($122M NI), 41% adj. EBITDA margin, **$220M FCF** in the quarter, and **$2.65B cash**. This mix creates a very specific macro signature.

**Key insight:** Unity's *growth is idiosyncratic, not macro*. Vector's +126% is driven by AI model improvement and ROAS gains, not by a cyclical ad upcycle. That gives genuine insulation on the operating line — but **zero insulation on the multiple**, because the stock is being priced as a long-duration M&A optionality story where the discount rate and the LBO financing market do all the work.

### 4. MACRO RISK RATING: **NEUTRAL**

Not favourable, not unfavourable — genuinely balanced, with an asymmetric structure:

**Why not Unfavourable:** GDP at 3.4%, 4.1% unemployment, and credit spreads at 320bp are three independent confirmations that the recession signal has *not* fired. Unity has $2.65B of cash and positive FCF — the classic rate-sensitive-company failure mode (refinancing) does not apply. The M&A bid is a hard floor argument.

**Why not Favourable:** A hiking Fed, a 5.13% 10Y, a 5.47% 30Y, and a firming dollar are unambiguously hostile to a 2.11-beta, ~23x EBITDA, FX-exposed growth asset. Headline inflation at 3.82% caps how far the multiple can expand.

**Verdict:** the *operating* macro is a tailwind; the *discount-rate* macro is a headwind. They roughly cancel — but the $25B take-private bid tilts the risk/reward positively **conditional on credit spreads staying below ~400bp**.

---

### 5. The Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BEAR: "The Refinancing Window Slams"**

**The market is complacent about one thing: it is treating 320bp HY spreads as permanent while simultaneously watching the 10Y rise 75bp in four months.** Those two facts are inconsistent. If headline CPI at 3.82% forces the Fed to validate the 86bp of hikes already priced into the 2-year — taking Fed funds to ~4.50% by mid-2027 — the 10Y likely clears **5.75–6.00%** and HY OAS widens from 320bp toward **450–500bp**.

The transmission to Unity is not gradual, it is binary:

1. **The $25B Silver Lake/PIF bid dies.** A ~$25B LBO at 5.13% base rates already requires an unusually large equity check; at 6.00% with 450bp spreads the math does not close. The take-private premium (~$55/sh) evaporates instantly.
2. **The multiple de-rates** from ~23x to ~15x EV/EBITDA — roughly a **$32–35 stock** on unchanged forward estimates.
3. **FX compounds it** — a further 5% dollar rally under restrictive Fed policy clips another 2–3pts off reported revenue growth.

**Downside path: $30–35 (-35% to -43%) on no change to the operating business.** The Feb 2026 precedent is instructive — the stock lost 68% peak-to-trough in seven weeks when the rate path repriced. Beta 2.11 is not a theoretical statistic for this name.

**The mirror-image bull surprise** — my lower-probability case — is that growth at 3.4% with the Fed only needing one or two more hikes lets term premium compress; a 10Y back to 4.60% plus a raised bid to $60–65/sh would take the stock to **$65–70**. But the bear case is the one that is *underpriced*, because credit spreads are currently signalling "all clear" while the rates market is signalling "trouble ahead."

---

**Bottom line for the investment memo:** Unity's macro exposure is a **pure discount-rate and M&A-financing exposure**, not an earnings-cycle exposure. Watch two numbers and ignore almost everything else: the **10Y Treasury** (currently 5.13%) and the **HY OAS** (currently 320bp). Growth, employment, and Unity's own AI-driven momentum are all supportive; the entire macro risk sits in whether 5%+ long rates and a hawkish Fed keep the leveraged-buyout door open for a $25B take-private.
