# UNITY SOFTWARE (NYSE: U) — Forensic Short-Seller Analysis

**Price $52.81 · Market cap $23.4B · Ent. value ~$23.1B · (fully diluted ~498M sh = $26.3B)**
**Note on data:** the stock closed $18.23 on 2026-02-27 after two single-day crashes (−24% Jan 30, −26% Feb 11). It has since re-rated ~190% to $52.81. I am analysing a stock that has already *won* its bull case, not one at a depressed multiple. That materially changes the short setup.

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **MEDIUM** (improving from Low; **not** fraud risk)

Genuine, verified inflection. Q3'26 was the first GAAP-profitable quarter since the 2020 IPO: revenue $622.1M (+32%), GAAP net income $121.6M, adj. EBITDA $256.6M (41%), record FCF $220M. Seven consecutive quarters of accelerating growth. This is real, and any short must start by conceding it.

But quality is **materially lower than the headline "adjusted" numbers imply**:

| Q3'26 | $M |
|---|---|
| Adjusted EBITDA | 256.6 |
| less SBC | (62.8) |
| less D&A (mostly acquired-intangible amort.) | (84.9) |
| **≈ GAAP operating income** | **100.6** |

Essentially **the entire $156M gap between "adjusted EBITDA" and GAAP operating income is stock comp plus amortisation of intangibles from the ironSource/apploved acquisitions.** No "one-off" add-backs to strip — but neither is any of it free.

**TTM owner-earnings math (Q4'25–Q3'26):** OCF $622.3M − capex $16.5M = FCF $605.8M. Subtract TTM SBC (~$326M, using ~$90M Q1'26 estimate — see data gap below) → **SBC-adjusted owner earnings ≈ $280M. That is 84x market cap, or ~94x fully diluted.** FCF yield on a genuinely cash basis is ~1.2%.

## 2. Key Red Flags Found

1. **Mix has inverted toward adtech — the multiple is wrong.** Q3'26: Strategic Grow (advertising) $432M (+90%), Strategic Create (the actual engine software) $165M (+11%). Grow is now **~73% of revenue**; Create ~27%. Management guides FY26 Create growth at just **9%, "with similar annual growth thereafter, primarily from ARPU improvements."** The durable-moat asset is a 9% grower; the 90–105% grower is a performance-ad network with no structural moat.

2. **2027 comparable wall.** Q4'26 guidance: total ~$688–696M (+~48%), strategic +65–67%, Grow +105–107%. For 2027 to show even 30% growth, Q4'27 must print ~$900M. **The company has step-changed its own base, so 2027 faces the hardest comps in its history**, plus non-strategic Grow goes to exactly zero. Deceleration is mechanically likely regardless of execution quality.

3. **Receivables outrunning revenue.** Net AR $833.5M at Q3'26 vs $600.1M a year earlier (**+38.9%**) while revenue grew +32%. DSO = **122 days**, up from 116 days at Q4'25 and Q3'25. Software-typical is 60–75. The annual screen flagged this too: FY25 AR +12.2% vs revenue +2.0%. Growth is booked faster than it is collected.

4. **Convertible dilution is accelerating and hidden in "adjusted" numbers.** Diluted weighted shares jumped **437.9M → 498.2M in a single quarter (+13.8%)**. Basic shares are up ~9% in seven quarters (~5%/yr structural SBC dilution). Management reports adj. EPS $0.44 vs GAAP diluted $0.25 — a **76% premium**. Fully diluted equity value is $26.3B, not $23.4B. Every "cheap" multiple is ~12% too cheap.

5. **Serial impairment: the capital-allocation indictment.** Q1'26 alone carried **$279M of impairments** (that's the COGS spike to $351.6M / 30.8% GM that quarter, versus ~$112M normal). Goodwill+intangibles fell $3.82B → $3.43B in one quarter. Cumulative retained earnings now **−$4.39B**. Unity paid ~$4.4B for ironSource in 2022, and by 2026 has wound down the ironSource Ads network as "non-strategic" and sold Supersonic. Over $1.5B has been written off. **The market is currently paying a premium multiple for the second iteration of the exact strategy that destroyed the first.**

6. **Engine moat is eroding.** GDC 2026: Unreal 42% vs Unity 30%. Godot has gone from ~1% to ~8–11% of new Steam releases. Unity's ARPU-led Create strategy is precisely the lever (runtime fees, 2023) that triggered a developer revolt and a >$10B market-cap collapse. Developer trust is a one-time-use asset and it has already been spent once.

7. **Data-integrity gap (flagged, not alleged).** Q1'26 quarterly SBC is reported as **$0** against FY25 SBC of $385M (~$96M/qtr). This is almost certainly a feed/reporting gap rather than an actual zero, but it means my TTM SBC figure is an estimate (~$326M). If actual SBC runs near FY25's run-rate, SBC-adjusted owner earnings are lower still (~$240M → ~98x).

## 3. Beneish M-Score & Accruals — **do not over-read these**

- **Beneish M-Score: −2.956 ("Low risk").** But this is **mechanically uninformative** here. TATA is −0.1208 *because* net income was negative while OCF was positive. Loss-making companies automatically score "clean" on the accrual component. DSRI 1.099 and SGI 1.020 are the only genuinely informative components and both point the wrong way. **As profitability turns positive, the accrual shield disappears and the M-score will rise. The low score is a lagging artifact, not evidence of clean accounting.**
- **Accruals ratio: −0.1208 (2025), −0.145 to −0.146 each year since 2022 — "ok."** Same caveat: negative accruals are an artefact of GAAP losses, not cash-generation superiority.
- **Cash conversion −1.05 / earnings quality score 0/100** — meaningless until NI is positive. Post-Q3'26, conversion (OCF $224M / NI $121.6M = 184%) looks strong.
- **Altman Z: 0.096 — "Distress."** Book-equity based and inappropriate for an asset-light software firm, but it correctly signals the balance sheet has **no accumulated buffer**. Mitigants: $2.65B cash, ~$327M net cash, and only ~$6M/qtr interest on $2.3B debt (low-coupon converts). Risk: the 2026 Notes (~$557M) mature Nov 2026 and the converts are now in the money, which is what drives the 498M diluted count.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment: **HIGH and rising**

- **Grow/Vector (73% of revenue)** competes with AppLovin (~$5B+ revenue, structurally superior margins and a two-year AI head start), Meta, Google and TikTok. Vector at +126% is **catch-up, not leadership** — it is a re-skinned ironSource/Luna ML stack. Ad networks are arbitrage businesses; margins and growth are competed away. Adtech deserves a **low-teens EBITDA multiple, not a software multiple**, and Unity is being awarded the latter.
- **Create (27% of revenue)** — Unreal owns AA/AAA; Godot owns the rising indie cohort; Unity is squeezed in the middle. Create's 9% growth is ARPU-driven, i.e. price, not adoption.
- **Structural:** the best talent and the most valuable IP (the engine standard) is the *slower*-growing half. That is an inverted business model relative to the valuation.

## 5. Management Red Flags: **moderate**

- **Capital allocation track record is genuinely poor** — ~$4.4B ironSource, >$1.5B impairments, the 2023 runtime-fee own-goal, the 2024 "portfolio reset," the 2026 Supersonic divestiture and ironSource Ads wind-down. Each was announced as strategic; each was reversed.
- **Promotional framing:** heavy reliance on "adjusted EBITDA" (41% margin) and "adj. EPS" ($0.44) versus GAAP ($0.25), plus "strategic revenue" as a bespoke non-GAAP growth metric that excludes exactly the businesses being wound down. Definitional churn is a classic pattern.
- **Insider filings:** 30 Form 4s in 2026, densely clustered on vesting dates (2026-05-15 ×8, 05-28 ×5, 08-26 ×3) and continuing through the post-rally period (Aug/Sep/Oct/Nov). **Direction is not available in this dataset — I will not assert net selling.** But note the absence of any visible open-market insider buying during the February collapse to $18. Positives: the 2026 proxy shows "enhanced pay-for-performance and stricter dilution controls adopted."
- **Positive:** CEO Matthew Bromberg's execution on cost discipline is real — adjusted S&M fell $22M to 12% of revenue, cloud-cost leverage is genuine, and the first GAAP profit is a legitimate milestone.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary — is there a credible bear case?

**Yes, but it is a quality-and-valuation short, not a fraud short.** There is no accounting fraud here; the cash is real, the profit is real, and the inflection is real. The bear case is:

> Unity has re-rated ~190% off its February 2026 low to **~10.7x TTM revenue (~9.9x 2026E of ~$2.37B, ~7.4x 2027E of $3.16B) and ~84–94x SBC-adjusted owner earnings**, for a business whose revenue mix has shifted to 73% performance advertising, whose durable engine franchise grows only 9% and is losing share to Unreal and Godot, which dilutes holders ~5%/yr plus a 13.8% one-quarter convertible step-up, whose receivables are growing 39% against 32% revenue, and which in 2027 laps the steepest base in its history with non-strategic revenue going to zero. The Vector hypergrowth is a commoditised catch-up product in a business Unity already bought, broke and is now dismantling. **Every 2027 consensus dollar assumes flawless execution of the strategy that has failed once already.**

**Catalysts:** Q4'26 print (Feb 2027) where guidance of +65–67% sets an impossible bar to keep beating; first evidence of Grow/Vector deceleration; Q1'27 comps against +17%; a bad-debt or receivables provision; the 2026 Notes refinancing; further impairment if Grow's intangible carrying value ($3.24B of goodwill/intangibles against $3.53B book equity) is tested.

**The other side — and it is strong.** I will not pretend otherwise:
- Multiples on *forward* numbers are not extreme: ~7.4x 2027 sales and ~15x annualised adj. EBITDA ($1.17B run-rate) for a 30%+ grower. That is defensible, not bubble.
- Consensus 2027 revenue of **$3.16B may be too low**, not too high: Q4'26 alone is a ~$2.75B annualised run-rate, and Grow is guided +105–107%.
- Record FCF ($220M/qtr), $2.65B cash, net cash position, SBC falling as a % of revenue, real cost discipline, 82–85% gross margins (up from 74%), and a first GAAP profit.
- Beta 2.11, heavily narrative-driven, structurally squeezed — an expensive and fast-moving stock to be short. This has squeeze characteristics.
- If Vector is genuinely taking share in performance advertising from AppLovin, the "commodity adtech" framing is simply wrong and the stock is cheap.

**Honest verdict on the thesis: credible but not high-conviction.** The forensic case for *fraud* is essentially absent (Beneish low-risk, accruals fine, cash real, auditor steady). The case for *overvaluation and mix-deterioration* is real but requires the growth to break, and the last three quarters have been beats.

## 7. Contrarian Rating: **SHORT (moderate conviction) — valuation & mix-quality, NOT forensic/fraud**

**This is a poor candidate for a Chanos-style fraud short and a reasonable candidate for a valuation-and-quality short.** Do not size it like a fraud conviction.

- **Sizing:** small (¼ to ½ normal short weight). Not a core short.
- **Timing:** the stock is in momentum hands with a Q4 guide it can plausibly beat. Shorting into Q4'26 (Feb 2027) is high-risk. The far better risk/reward is **shorting into 2027 H1 as the comparables go vertical** and the first Grow deceleration print appears.
- **Instrument:** consider put spreads rather than stock — the 2.1 beta and squeeze risk make naked shorting unattractive; defined-risk structures capture the same thesis.
- **Invalidation / stop-out:** Grow growth below ~40%, or Vector below ~60%, would actually *validate* the thesis rather than kill it — so the real invalidation is **Vector holding >70% growth AND Create reaccelerating above ~15% AND SBC-adjusted owner earnings annualising above ~$1.2B.** Two of those three together and the short is wrong; cover.
- **Where I would concede defeat entirely:** if Create reaccelerates and the mix shifts back toward software while SBC-adjusted FCF keeps compounding at 50%+, the "adtech commodity" framing breaks and this is a legitimate long. Monitor Create growth (currently 9%, guided ~4–6% in Q4) as the single cleanest tell on moat erosion.

**Bottom line:** the transformation is real, the accounting is not fraudulent, and the forward multiple is not absurd — but the quality of the earnings is lower than the adjusted figures present, the moat has moved to the wrong half of the business, and 2027 comparables are brutal. Short the *narrative*, not the *books*.
