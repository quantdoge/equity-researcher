## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** — but the split is instructive

**High agreement on the business.** Ten of eleven usable reports concede the inflection is real and verified: first GAAP profit ($122M), 41% adj. EBITDA margin, $607M TTM FCF, net cash, seven quarters of accelerating growth. Even the short analyst states plainly that "the cash is real, the profit is real, the inflection is real."

**Total disagreement on the price.** Targets span $30 (macro bear) → $45–55 (value IV) → $61 (consensus/growth). The disagreement is not about facts; it is about whether 16–20x forward adj. EBITDA (≈84–94x SBC-adjusted owner earnings) is fair for a business that is now 73% performance advertising.

**Resolution of conflicts (my weighting rules):**
- **Quant's SELL is discarded.** Its price basis is wrong by 65%; a factor model output on a mis-stated price carries no weight. This is the single most important data-integrity catch in the stack.
- **Short's quality critique outweighs Growth's "cheap on 2027" argument** on *earnings quality* (SBC ≈ 95% of FY25 FCF, 13.8% one-quarter diluted-share step-up, DSO 122 days) — but the Short's fraud thesis is explicitly absent, so this is a sizing input, not a veto.
- **Non-Fin + Geo Legal's governance/deal-process findings outrank Sector's M&A "floor" argument.** A reported bid from a party whose principal sits on the target's board, with no disclosed special committee, is not a floor — it is an option, and options can expire.

**The one thesis the whole stack actually agrees on:** at $52.81, U is no longer an operating story. It is a **binary event on a $55/share bid reported at ~4% above the current price.** Alt Data, Macro, and Value independently converge on this.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **0% new capital** (Kelly edge is negative)

Run the merger-arbitrage arithmetic, which is what the market is now pricing:

| Scenario | Price | Return |
|---|---|---|
| Deal at rumoured $55, no bump | $55.00 | **+4.1%** |
| Talks lapse → pre-leak price | $42.46 | **−19.6%** |
| Talks lapse + rate/multiple de-rating (Macro bear) | $32–35 | **−34% to −39%** |
| Value researcher's own standalone IV | $45–50 | −5% to −15% |

Breakeven probability of completion = 19.6 / (19.6 + 4.1) = **83%.** The report is of *talks held "in recent weeks"* (Reuters, 5-Nov) — not a signed agreement, not a filed merger proxy, not a disclosed special committee *and* an unresolved 18%-of-revenue China divestiture that must be cleared as a condition. A 4% spread cannot pay for that.

**Kelly:** edge = negative expected value → size = 0.
**If a mandate requires core mid-cap software beta exposure:** cap at **0.5%** as an event stub, funded from the existing software sleeve, not new risk budget.
**Full position size if acquired properly on a reset:** 2.0–2.5% (high conviction on the franchise, permanently elevated risk on the business model).

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector / business-mix:** Reported sector is Application Software, but the exposure is **ad-tech and gaming, not software**. Grow (ad-tech) ≈ 73% of revenue; Create ≈ 27% and growing 4–6%. Any portfolio already holding APP, META, GOOGL or RBLX is **doubling an ad-monetisation cluster**, not diversifying.

**Correlation (252d):** U–SPY **0.21**, U–APP **0.42**, U–TTWO 0.34, U–RBLX 0.32, U–ADBE 0.22. On daily returns U looks beautifully diversifying — but that statistic is exactly what makes this dangerous. Low correlation is not low risk here; it is **jump risk**. U gapped +15% on 51M shares on 5-Nov and fell −24%/−26% on single days in Jan/Feb 2026. Daily-return models will systematically understate the loss distribution.

**Factor exposure:** 69.6% annualized vol (≈3–4x a typical large-cap software name), beta unstable across windows (**1.02 / 1.70 / 2.11**), R² ≈ 0.10. A 1% position contributes roughly 0.7% of portfolio volatility on its own; a beta-hedged or factor-neutral construction will mis-size it badly, and 90% of its variance is un-hedgeable. **12-1 momentum is −1.0%** (bottom of the software peer set) even after a +40% one-month surge — the stock is either recovering from or still inside a broken trend, depending on the window you choose. This is a name you size by **loss-given-failure**, not by beta.

**Crowding:** sell-side consensus is "Strong Buy" with a $61.29 average PT; StockTwits 61% bullish; WSB theses live. The marginal buyer has already bought. Nobody is left to be surprised by good news.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**For existing holders (the more urgent decision):**
- **TRIM TRIGGER: $54.50 → SELL 100%.** That is 99% of the rumoured bid. Do not hold for a bump; a bump requires a second bidder, and there is no disclosed one.
- **HARD STOP: $42.00** (below the 4-Nov pre-leak close of $42.46). A close below that level means the market has abandoned the deal premium entirely, and the stock reverts to standalone fundamentals — Macro's $32–35 or Value's $40s.
- **Do not add on the Q4 print (10-Feb-27)** even if it beats. Guidance of +105–107% Grow has already set a bar that a beat cannot clear; that is a sell-the-news setup, not an accumulation setup.

**Thesis invalidation (fundamental, position-independent):**
1. **Grow growth below 40% YoY** in any quarter, or Vector below 60% — the single load-bearing assumption in every bull model.
2. **Create guided to zero or negative growth** (currently +4–6%) — confirms permanent moat damage.
3. **AppLovin litigation escalation:** current TRO was denied 2-Oct-26; any *preliminary injunction* restricting Ad Quality SDK data collection would threaten the Vector signal itself — exit immediately.
4. **China sale blocked or repriced**, with a further impairment.
5. **SBC above 12% of revenue for two consecutive quarters** — kills the GAAP-profitability quality argument.
6. **HY OAS above ~400bp** (Macro's tripwire) — the LBO door closes and the deal premium evaporates mechanically.

**Portfolio-level rule:** no more than 2.5% of NAV at any price, and total ad-tech cluster (U + APP + any GOOGL/META overlap) capped at the sleeve limit. Given 0.42 correlation to APP, treat a U position as half-sized against an existing APP holding.
