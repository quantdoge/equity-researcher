# Unity Software (NYSE: U) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $52.81 | Market Cap $23.4B | EV ~$22.6B | Beta 2.11 | ~4,412 employees**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Unity has completed one of the more dramatic business-model pivots in software: it stopped being a decelerating game-engine subscription company and became an AI-driven mobile advertising network. **Unity Vector** — the machine-learning UA/monetization engine rebuilt from scratch to compete with AppLovin's Axon — grew **126% YoY in Q3'26** and has turned Grow Solutions into 73% of revenue and effectively all of the growth. The company just posted its **first GAAP-profitable quarter since its 2020 IPO** (net income $122M, 20% margin) while guiding Q4 strategic revenue up 65–67%. This is a genuine fundamental inflection, not a multiple-driven re-rating — though the stock has already tripled off its Sept-2024 lows of $16.70.

## 3. TAM & Penetration

Two stacked markets, and Unity is early in both:

- **Real-time 3D / engine & dev tools:** SAM ~$6–8B. Unity is the world's most widely deployed game engine (majority of top mobile games). Create at ~$660M/yr implies low-single-digit penetration, but growth (+4–6%) shows the post-runtime-fee trust repair is slow.
- **Mobile in-app advertising / user acquisition:** TAM >$100B globally, with mobile gaming UA alone tens of billions. Grow at ~$1.8B annualized (Q4'26 guided $515–520M ×4 ≈ **$2.06B run-rate**) is a low-single-digit share against AppLovin (~$5–6B revenue) and Google/Meta.

**The structural advantage is the flywheel, and it is real:** more creators build on Unity → more games supply first-party ad inventory and first-party behavioral data → the model trains better → advertisers get better ROAS → more games choose Unity. Unlike a third-party mediation network, Unity owns the engine telemetry, which is why adjusted EBITDA margins hit 41% with no meaningful third-party traffic-acquisition cost. **Penetration math:** if Vector reaches a $3B run-rate by 2028 and Create returns to high-single-digit growth, Unity exceeds the $4.6B 2030 consensus revenue by a comfortable margin.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts (3–5)

1. **Q4'26 earnings — Feb 10, 2027.** Consensus $694.5M revenue, adj. EPS $0.33; guidance implies +65–67% strategic growth and $290–295M adj. EBITDA. Delivery would mark the **first full year of GAAP profitability**.
2. **2027 guidance** — the first year with clean strategic-only comps and the first real test of Vector's durability at scale. This is the single biggest swing factor.
3. **Vector crossing a $2B+ annualized run-rate** and possible disclosure of advertiser/ROAS retention metrics — validates the "not just a beta bump" thesis.
4. **Create reacceleration** — Unity 6 migration, Unity Spark / AI-assisted content tools, subscription price/mix. Guided at only +4–6%; every point here is nearly pure margin and free optionality.
5. **Capital allocation** — $2.65B cash and improving FCF open the door to buybacks, convertible retirement, or accretive tuck-ins (the Supersonic divestiture shows new discipline).

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (constructive, not yet Strong Buy)

**The bull case arithmetic:** EV $22.6B against 2027 consensus revenue of $3.15B = **7.2x EV/S**, and against ~$1.42B of 2027 adj. EBITDA ≈ **16x EV/EBITDA** — for a business growing revenue ~30% with a 41% EBITDA margin and a forward Rule of 40 near 58. AppLovin trades at roughly 12.3x NTM EV/EBITDA but 12.8x sales; Unity is materially cheaper on revenue and growing faster off a smaller base. If Unity simply holds ~8x 2027 sales, that's ~$60/share — consistent with the $61.29 consensus PT (+16%).

**Why not Strong Buy:** the stock has already repriced 3x from the lows and is up ~33% since August. The thesis is now dangerously concentrated — **Grow is 73% of revenue and ~100% of growth**, riding a single product that must sustain triple-digit growth into brutally difficult 2027 comps against a larger, more profitable, litigious competitor. Create, the "moat," is growing 4–6% while search interest declines 40%. At 9.9x forward sales and beta 2.1, the margin for error is thin. **Accumulate on weakness; the risk/reward at $45–48 is markedly better than at $53.**

## 7. Key Risks

- **Vector durability / comp lapping (highest risk).** +126% YoY cannot persist. Q4'27 laps a +105–107% comp; any deceleration to sub-30% Grow growth would break the multiple. There is only ~3–4 quarters of clean evidence.
- **AppLovin competition.** Axon is the incumbent leader with superior margins and scale; Unity is the challenger with a legal dispute attached. This is a share fight, not a rising-tide market.
- **Create stagnation.** +4–6% growth plus decaying search interest suggests the runtime-fee era permanently damaged developer trust and the Unity 6 upgrade cycle is muted.
- **Ad-cycle cyclicality.** Mobile UA budgets are discretionary and procyclical; ad-market fear has already produced 10% single-day drawdowns in the name.
- **Estimate data quality.** Consensus EBITDA for 2027 ($1.59B) implies a 50% margin and the EPS series is internally inconsistent ($2.82 for 2028 → $0.23 for 2029). Treat consensus EPS/EBITDA as unreliable; anchor on revenue and the company's own adj. EBITDA guide.
- **Dilution.** SBC of $385M is ~16% of revenue and ~1.6% of market cap annually — real shareholder cost even as GAAP profitability arrives.
- **Valuation/volatility.** 9.9x forward sales, 55x trailing FCF (33x on 2026E), beta 2.1. Sharp compression on any guidance wobble.
