**Bull Case**
- **Vector is a real product cycle**: +126% YoY with ~90% of growth from existing advertisers (network-effect signature), Q4 guided +105–107%, ~$2B+ annualised run-rate, trained on proprietary engine telemetry no rival can replicate.
- **Operating leverage proven in cash**: GAAP profit, 41% adj. EBITDA margin, $220M FCF in the quarter, $2.65B cash, SBC declining — FY2027 consensus $3.16B revenue may be too low, not too high.
- **Strategic scarcity with a live bid**: a reported $25B approach from credible buyers with open financing markets (HY 320bp); a signed deal — or a bump/bidding war — takes the stock through $55–60.

**Bear Case**
- **Negative-skew event risk**: +4% to the rumoured bid vs −20% (pre-leak $42.46) to −39% (macro de-rating, $32–35); a conflicted bidder-on-board process with no disclosed guardrails can fail or reprice.
- **Earnings quality below headline**: ~84x SBC-adjusted owner earnings, DSO 122 days, one-quarter +13.8% diluted-share step-up, Create growing 4–6% with creator mindshare eroding — the moat is in the slow half of the business.
- **2027 comp wall + competitive squeeze**: lapping +105% Grow comps against AppLovin (76% op margins, superior ML scale, litigation attached), with beta 2.1 and a documented −68% drawdown when rates repriced in early 2026.

**Key Risks**
1. Deal talks lapse or are blocked (CFIUS/China) — deal premium evaporates mechanically; tripwire: HY OAS >400bp.
2. Grow growth below ~40% / Vector below ~60% in any quarter — invalidates every bull model.
3. AppLovin litigation escalation to a preliminary injunction restricting Ad Quality SDK data collection — hits the Vector signal itself.
4. China divestiture stalls or reprices → further impairment against $3.24B goodwill/intangibles.
5. SBC >12% of revenue for two consecutive quarters kills the GAAP-profit quality argument.
6. Long rates: 10Y clearing 5.75–6% compresses the multiple toward ~15x EBITDA (~$32–35) on unchanged fundamentals.
