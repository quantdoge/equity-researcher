### Investment Memo: ALNY — 30 October 2026

**Verdict**: BUY | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $215 (+24% from current $174)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Alnylam is the only profitable RNAi platform company, growing product revenue +52% YoY, yet trading at ~4x 2026E sales and ~18x 2027E EPS after a 65% de-rating — the market is pricing the TTR franchise as a melting ice cube while the underlying business is still compounding. The de-rating is idiosyncratic (XBI +29% TTM), driven by two guidance cuts and genuine ATTR-CM share loss to BridgeBio's oral Attruby — but the bear case has largely been paid out, competitors are weakening (Wainua failed in CM; tafamidis generics held to 2031), and the pipeline (zilebesiran, brezosiran) is unpriced optionality. What is *not* fully priced is the governance discount: zero insider buying through a 50% drawdown, an active securities class action, and earnings quality that is 75% SBC-masked FCF — which is why this is a starter position, not a core holding.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Biotech sector in strong up-cycle (XBI +29%, $221B licensing, patent cliff) — ALNY's fall is company-specific. The business is macro-resilient (non-cyclical demand, US manufacturing, net cash), but the *equity* is a long-duration asset in a hawkish regime (Fed 3.88%, 10Y 5.05%, bear steepening) — a persistent 10–20% multiple headwind. A disinflation-driven 10Y retrace to ~4.3% is the single biggest bull catalyst (~30% probability, 30–45% re-rating).
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium risk.** Genuinely clean geopolitically (no China/Russia/sanctions/FCPA). The risk is domestic: ~80% US revenue colliding with MFN/IRA/340B Part B cuts, plus an active securities class action (class period Feb 12–Jul 30, 2026; 5+ plaintiff firms; lead-plaintiff deadline Dec 8, 2026).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Narrow moat, *narrowing* — GalNAc platform is real, but TTR is ~89% of product revenue with a visible expiry (CoM early 2030s; successor nucresiran not launching in CM until ~2030). Financial quality 6/10. Refuses to pay for pipeline.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **BUY.** Rule of 40 ≈ 78; PEG ~0.68; 26.8x 2026E → 10.5x 2030E EPS. Reverse-DCF: 2.5x 2030 sales ⇒ ~$206; 3.5x ⇒ ~$283. ~2:1 up/down. "Scale-in, not back-up-the-truck."
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral** (high dispersion, partially defective feeds). Quality top-quartile (F-Score 7/9, accruals −4.2%, cash-backed earnings); value bottom-quartile; momentum negative — classic "quality at a still-unproven price."
- **Financial Risk**: **Moderate** — split personality. Balance-sheet risk LOW (net cash, >50x cash interest coverage once the Blackstone royalty imputed interest is stripped, zero refinancing cliff, 10+ yr stress runway). Equity risk HIGH (58% vol, −65% max drawdown, two ~−28% single-day gaps). A 20% revenue shock is survivable on cash basis.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH — the only HIGH in the stack.** Zero insider open-market purchases through a 50% drawdown; CEO + CCO sold ~$10.7M at $310–363 in Jan–Mar 2026, months before the July guidance cut. Guidance credibility breached twice; FDA misbranding notice on an Amvuttra page. No fraud evidence; structure sound; conduct signals weak.
- **ESG**: **Average / Neutral.** Environmentally immaterial. The real vectors are S (Amvuttra's $477k list price makes it a probable 2028 IRA negotiation selectee — model explicit terminal net-price erosion) and G (2026 disclosure failure + a prior OIG pricing-data settlement show controls lagging revenue scale).
- **Alternative Data**: **Soft bearish.** Search interest ~20% below 12-mo trend, hiring flat (~2.7% open-req rate), news −0.14 skewed by legal boilerplate. No signal supporting a re-acceleration — but explicitly "no new deterioration."
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **NO EDGE — no short.** No manipulation (negative accruals), no moat erosion (competitors weakening), no balance-sheet stress, no goodwill to impair. The short that worked ($400→174) is over; residual quality arguments (SBC, tax-benefit profits, royalty margin leak) are disclosed, not concealed.

**Bull Case**
- **Valuation dislocation**: 4x sales / 18x 2027E EPS for 45%+ growth with 82% gross margins and net cash — the market is discounting terminal TTR decline that current data (+52% product revenue) does not show.
- **Competitive position improving, not eroding**: Wainua's CARDIO-TTRansform failure removes the most credible RNA competitor in CM; Pfizer's tafamidis generic defense holds pricing umbrella to 2031; ALNY is the only profitable name in the RNAi set.
- **Free optionality**: zilebesiran (hypertension — tens-of-billions TAM, Roche-partnered) and brezosiran (83% mHTT reduction, pivotal 2027) are ascribed ~zero value at this price.

**Bear Case**
- **ATTR-CM share loss is real and conceded**: management guides TTR to the *low end* of $4.2–4.5B, citing demand below expectations *despite* expanded selling — the signature of oral Attruby displacement, with nucresiran's bridge not arriving until 2030.
- **Governance/credibility deficit**: two guidance cuts in 12 months, zero insider buying at $174 versus heavy selling at $310+, and a class action covering exactly that window — a warranted valuation discount.
- **Earnings quality**: Q3's $2.17B "profit" was 86% a one-time tax benefit; SBC equals ~75% of FCF (true FCF ~$117M, ~195x EV); gross margin down ~830bp structurally (royalty stack) — the growth is real but less profitable than it screens.

**Key Risks**
1. **Policy/pricing**: MFN, 340B Part B final rule, UHC Medicare Advantage restriction, and probable Amvuttra IRA selection (list due Feb 2027) compressing US net price on ~80% of revenue.
2. **Commercial**: a third consecutive guidance miss (Q4 print, **Feb 18, 2027**) or US Amvuttra turning flat/negative QoQ — the named trigger that converts this into a value trap.
3. **Pipeline**: TRITON-CM enrollment expansion (1,750→2,250) hints at smaller effect size; any slip beyond 2030 leaves the franchise unbridged against gene editing.
4. **Legal escalation**: class action surviving motion to dismiss or an SEC inquiry widens the governance discount to a veto.
5. **Rates**: 10Y term-premium blowout (30Y >5.60%) de-rates long-duration biotech regardless of fundamentals.
