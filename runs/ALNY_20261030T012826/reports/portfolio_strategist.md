## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT**

The dispersion is unusually clean and it is *not* a quality-of-analysis problem — agents genuinely disagree because they are measuring different things.

**What all 11 agents agree on (high-confidence floor):**
- **Not a short.** The Short Analyst, who was hired to break this company, concluded NO EDGE and explicitly declined to present the Altman Z-score as evidence.
- **Not a solvency story.** Net cash, no maturity wall, 10+ year stress runway.
- **Not a fraud.** Negative accruals, no goodwill to impair, clean SOX certifications. The earnings-quality problems (SBC = 75% of FCF; the $1.87B tax benefit; ~830bp of gross-margin leakage) are **disclosed, structural, and explainable** — royalty-stack economics, not concealment.
- **Valuation has genuinely reset.** 4.0–4.2× 2026E sales and 18× 2027E EPS for a business compounding product revenue at +52%.

**What splits them:**
- **Bulls (Growth):** a category-defining platform mispriced as a melting ice cube.
- **Neutrals (Quant, Short, Macro):** fairly priced for the risk — no edge in *either* direction at $174.
- **Bears on risk (Non-Fin HIGH, Alt Data bearish, Value narrow-moat):** the *governance and disclosure* discount is not yet adequately compensated.

**Conflict resolution — governance over narrative.** The Non-Fin Analyst's finding of **zero insider buying through a 50% drawdown, with the CEO and CCO selling $10.7M at $310–363 four months before the guidance cut**, combined with an active securities class action whose class period covers exactly that window, does **not** get to veto the position (there is no fraud evidence, and 10b5-1 plans explain the sales). But it **caps the size** and mandates a wider margin of safety. Where Value says "narrow and narrowing moat" and Growth says "18× 2027E is a dislocation," both are right: the *business* is intact, the *credibility* is impaired. That combination argues for a small position, not a pass and not a core holding.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation

### **Initiate at 1.0% of portfolio. Target 2.0%. Hard cap 3.0%.**

**Kelly check (directionally supportive, not binding).** Probability-weighted fair value: Bull 30% × ~$300 (disinflation + Amvuttra stabilization + pipeline) + Base 45% × ~$215 (low-end guidance holds, TTR grinds) + Bear 25% × ~$120 (share loss compounds, multiple to 3× sales) = **~$217, or +25% expected return**, with ~2.3:1 payoff odds at a 75% hit rate. That is a *positive* edge and it is why this is not a Pass. But raw Kelly at these parameters returns an absurd 40–55% position — unusable, because the outcome distribution is not binary and the analyst dispersion (FY2028 EPS range $5.73–$17.23) is wider than the signal. **Fractional Kelly (1/10) plus a volatility risk budget is the binding constraint.**

**The actual binding constraint is volatility, not conviction.** At 58% annualised vol:
- 1.0% position ≈ **58bps standalone vol**, ~14bps marginal contribution at the 0.25 average book correlation. Sits inside a standard 25bps single-name risk budget.
- 2.0% ≈ ~29bps marginal — at the limit of the budget for a name with **two −29% single days in twelve months.**
- 3.0% ≈ ~43bps — only justifiable if the Feb-18 print de-risks the guidance question.

**Why not smaller (0.5%)?** The bear case requires *another* leg of deterioration that the Short Analyst could not construct and the Alt Data Analyst explicitly called "no new deterioration." The commentary already prices a lot: −65% from the high while product revenue grew 52%.

**Why not larger (3–5%)?** Four independent caps: (a) 58% vol / undiversifiable idiosyncratic risk; (b) Non-Fin Risk rated **HIGH** — the only agent to say so, and on governance grounds; (c) guidance credibility broken twice in one year, with the burden of proof on 18-Feb-27; (d) **89% single-franchise concentration against a four-player ATTR-CM market** where management itself concedes demand is below plan *despite* expanded selling effort.

**Factor exposure — this is the key unintended tilt.** ALNY loads **positively on quality** (top-quartile: F-score 7/9, negative accruals, best GP/assets in group) and **negatively on both value and momentum**. It belongs in a growth/biotech sleeve with a fundamental overlay.

**Liquidity / capacity.** ADTV ~1.0–1.5M shares (~$200–260M). A 1% position in a $1B book = $10M ≈ 4% of ADV — trivial. In a $10B book, 1% = $100M ≈ 40–50% of ADV — **1–2 days of volume, sized carefully.** Note that the 29-Oct print traded 11.8M shares (9× normal): the stock is *liquid precisely when you don't want it to be*, and thin when you want to build.
