# Alnylam Pharmaceuticals (ALNY) — Value Investment Analysis
**Price: $174.00 | Market cap: $23.3B | EV: ~$21.7B (incl. ~$1.6B net cash) | Date of analysis: post-Q3'26 (Oct 29, 2026)**

**Framing note:** ALNY is a commercial-stage RNAi platform company, not a classic Buffett franchise. The *commercialized* business can be valued with reasonable confidence; a meaningful slice of the market price rests on binary clinical outcomes (nucresiran, brezosiran) that are outside any strict circle of competence. I therefore value the franchise conservatively and treat pipeline optionality as upside I refuse to pay for.

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat Rating: **NARROW** (and narrowing in the core franchise)

**The genuine moat:**
- **Intangible assets:** Alnylam holds the foundational RNAi patent estate plus the GalNAc conjugate delivery platform — the industry's most validated liver-targeting delivery technology. Replicating 20+ years of chemistry, manufacturing and regulatory know-how is genuinely hard.
- **Regulatory/orphan economics:** GIVLAARI and OXLUMO are quasi-monopolies in tiny populations (Q3'26 combined $142M, growing 12%) with 82% gross margins.
- **Protocol entrenchment:** quarterly subcutaneous Amvuttra in a chronic, progressive disease creates mild switching friction.

**Why it is not a Wide moat:**
- **Single-franchise concentration:** TTR is $1,150M of $1,292M quarterly product revenue — **89%**. This is a one-asset company with a pipeline attached.
- **Moat direction is wrong.** ATTR-CM is now a contested market: Pfizer's tafamidis (entrenched, oral, facing generic entry), BridgeBio's acoramidis, and — the structural threat — Intellia's one-time CRISPR gene editing (nex-z). A functional cure destroys a chronic-dosing franchise. Alnylam itself conceded this quarter that **Amvuttra ATTR-CM demand growth is below expectations despite expanded selling effort**.
- **Pricing power is being tested by payers, not by competitors:** management flagged potential 340B reimbursement changes and a more restrictive UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage policy — both capable of delaying new patient starts. A wide-moat business does not depend on payer policy for its unit growth.
- **A dated moat:** Amvuttra's composition-of-matter protection runs to roughly the early 2030s. The bridge is nucresiran (ATTR-CM launch ~2030). Notably, Alnylam just **expanded TRITON-CM enrollment from 1,750 to 2,250 patients** to "ensure adequate study power under conservative estimates of outcome event rates." That is management telling you the successor asset is not a layup.

**Verdict:** A real technology moat wrapped around a franchise with a visible expiry date and rising competition. Narrow, not wide.
