# ALNY — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, Inc.** | Healthcare / Biotechnology | Cambridge, MA | ~$23.3B mkt cap | $174.00 (Oct 29, 2026 close) | ~2,500 FTE

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE** (structure strong, conduct signals weak)

**Positives**
- **Independent Chair / CEO separation.** Michael Bonney (former Cubist CEO) chairs the board; Dr. Yvonne Greenstreet is CEO (since Jan 2022). No power concentration.
- Board carries genuine domain expertise: David E.I. Pyott (ex-Allergan), Amy Schulman, Benjamin Cravatt, Charles Sigal — majority independent, majority-independent committees.
- Compensation consultant (Pay Governance) is independent; pay structure is performance/TSR-linked (CEO credited with ~100% stockholder return through the Amvuttra launch).
- Orderly founder transition: Nobel laureate Phillip Sharp (scientific founder) retired from the board May 2025 without disruption.

**Negatives**
- **Insider alignment is one-directional.** Every reported 2026 transaction is a sale. Zero open-market purchases by any officer or director in the trailing 12 months (see §3). This is the single clearest conduct red flag.
- **Selling concentrated into pre-collapse prices.** Sales clustered at $310–$363 in Jan–Mar 2026, four months before the July 30 guidance cut and a ~50% YTD decline — timing that sits squarely inside the securities class period.
- **Guidance credibility breach.** Management affirmed FY26 TTR guidance, then cut it by $200M mid-year; two sell-side analysts explicitly said the cut arrived earlier than expected and that IQVIA script data did not foreshadow it. Whether or not it was actionable, the episode damaged disclosure credibility and board oversight of forecasting is now a live question.
- Only **one C-suite change at the top in 4 years** plus a 50% stock decline raises equity-retention/attrition risk among the executive bench, all of whom were selling at much higher prices.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Single-franchise concentration.** TTR (Amvuttra + Onpattro) *is* the company — FY26 guidance $4.2–4.5B against ~$5.2B total revenue. The July miss revealed that early Amvuttra ATTR-CM uptake was **"pent-up demand" that has now normalized**; Q3 reiterated guidance only by pointing to the **low end of the range**. One commercial forecast error wiped ~$12B of market value in a day.
- **Competitive displacement in ATTR-CM.** Four approved mechanisms now compete (Vyndamax, Attruby, eplontersen, Amvuttra). The July 9 eplontersen HELIOS failure created class-wide read-through uncertainty about efficacy in patients on background therapy — a scientific question about RNAi/ATTR-CM that Alnylam does not control, with results presented Aug 2026 and still overhanging the category.
- **Platform/CMC + delivery dependency.** Complex oligonucleotide chemistry with concentrated third-party API/drug-product supply, plus intrathecal administration for the CNS pipeline (brezosiran) — a delivery channel with limited clinical infrastructure and its own safety/tolerability profile. Any single-source supplier or CMC deviation is a high-severity, low-frequency event for a company with no diversified revenue base.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **ELEVATED**

- **Investor-side damage is material.** Stock down >50% YTD (from ~$398), erasing the entire 69% gain of 2025; characterized in the financial press as "*why are investors treating it like a broken biotech*" and "*the overhang [won't] pass easily*" (Jefferies). ~93% institutional ownership means the shareholder base is sophisticated, litigious, and currently dissatisfied — 5 hold / 16 buy / 2 strong buy is a *deteriorating* consensus.
- **Legal-advertising overhang.** Serial plaintiff-firm press releases naming the stock recur roughly weekly, functioning as a persistent negative news drip with retail investors.
- **Product/brand exposure.** Amvuttra carries a vitamin A–lowering warning requiring supplementation and ocular monitoring; the consumer-facing "Play by Play" athlete campaign (Sep 2026) increases promotional visibility at exactly the moment FDA has already flagged a company webpage as misbranding. A single high-profile patient adverse event in the ATTR-CM population would compound the commercial problem.
- **Mitigant:** the October 23 brezosiran Huntington's data (up to 83% mHTT reduction, 2027 pivotal) is a genuine reputation restorer and shifts narrative toward the platform.

---

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Rationale: governance *structure* is sound and there is **no evidence of fraud, restatement, SEC enforcement, or internal-control failure** (CEO/CFO certifications clean in the Q3 10-Q; no material weakness disclosed). But the combination of (a) two discrete −25%+ disclosure shocks within 90 days, (b) an active securities class action with an expanding plaintiff coalition, (c) a demonstrated breakdown in guidance credibility, (d) total dependence on one commercial franchise facing four-way competition, and (e) uniform insider selling with zero insider buying, places this well above a Moderate rating. It stops short of Critical only because the underlying business is growing (+52% product revenue y/y), GAAP-profitable, and the legal exposure is likely D&O-absorbed.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **🚩 Securities class action with class period Feb 12 – Jul 30, 2026** — five-plus firms soliciting. Watch for a consolidated complaint and any *SEC subpoena* disclosure in the next 10-K/10-Q; a formal SEC investigation would re-rate this to Critical.
2. **🚩 Guidance-credibility event.** Affirmed FY guidance → $200M cut in one quarter, with sell-side noting IQVIA script data did not signal the shortfall. Directors should be asked directly whether demand-visibility data was available earlier than disclosed.
3. **🚩 Zero insider purchases through a 50% drawdown**, with the CEO and CCO selling ~$10.7M combined at $310–363 in the Jan–Mar window. Escalate as a sentiment and potential-scienter issue, even though plans are 10b5-1.
4. **🚩 Quality-of-earnings artifact.** Q3 GAAP EPS of $16.26 vs. $2.19 consensus was driven by a **~$1.87B one-time income tax benefit** (valuation-allowance release); *operating income actually declined* despite 52% product growth. Headline profitability is not operating profitability — do not let this mask margin erosion from competition.
5. **🚩 FDA misbranding determination on an Amvuttra promotional webpage** — isolated, but combined with an aggressive athlete-led DTC campaign, promotional compliance bears watching.
6. **🚩 Single-franchise commercial concentration** with guidance already trending to the low end; Q4 2026 Amvuttra run-rate is the single most important non-financial variable in the thesis.
