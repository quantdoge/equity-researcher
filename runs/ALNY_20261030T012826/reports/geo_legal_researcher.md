# ALNY — Geopolitical, Regulatory & Legal Risk Assessment

**Company:** Alnylam Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ: ALNY) — commercial-stage RNAi biotech, HQ Cambridge, MA; ~2,500 FTE; market cap ≈$23.3B (company profile). Six marketed products; the **TTR franchise (AMVUTTRA/vutrisiran + ONPATTRO) is now ~85–88% of product revenue** — a single-franchise concentration that magnifies any policy shock.

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**Risk 1 — US drug-pricing regime: MFN + IRA + 340B Part B cuts (PROBABILITY: High / IMPACT: High)**
- EO 14297 (May 12, 2025) set MFN price targets; manufacturers had until **Nov 8, 2025** to negotiate with HHS (Jones Day; HHS/CMS). Alnylam is **actively lobbying on MFN pricing and IRA implementation** (Legis1).
- April 2, 2026 executive order converted 12 months of Section 232 pressure into **enforceable trade law with a default 100% pharma tariff rate** (Clinical Leader; White House proclamation).
- CMS proposed rule would cut **Medicare Part B reimbursement for 340B-acquired drugs** (survey: hospitals buy ~33.4% below ASP) — directly attacks AMVUTTRA's buy-and-bill economics; management flagged the final rule as imminent. A **more restrictive UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage policy** is an additional 2027 volume/pricing risk (Q3'26 call).
- With ~80% of revenue in the US and AMVUTTRA already the growth engine, a 10–15% US net-price reset is a multi-billion-dollar NPV event.

**Risk 2 — Tariffs and supply-chain fragmentation (PROBABILITY: Medium / IMPACT: Medium)**
- Section 232 duties of up to 100% on imported patented drugs and active ingredients from **July 31, 2026** (Mallory Group; White House). Alnylam's US fill/finish in Norton mitigates finished-goods exposure, but **imported raw materials/oligonucleotide inputs and EU/Asia CMOs** remain in tariff scope. Management has not quantified tariff cost.

**Risk 3 — Ex-US pricing compression and launch-price deflation (PROBABILITY: Medium-High / IMPACT: Medium)**
- Q3'26 ex-US TTR growth (+28% y/y) was **partly offset by launch price reductions** in Europe/Japan — a structural sign that ex-US ATTR-CM economics are being negotiated downward. Japanese biennial price cuts and EU Pharma Package/HTA-joint-assessment reform add medium-term drag. MFN implementation could also provoke EU/Japanese counter-measures.

*Notably absent: no material China/Taiwan exposure, no Russia sanctions exposure, no meaningful emerging-market currency-control or expropriation risk.*

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Securities litigation — the live issue.** On **July 30, 2026**, Alnylam cut FY26 TTR sales guidance by ~$200M ("normalization of growth in second line volume after satisfying pent-up demand"), and the stock fell **28.31% to $205.48**. A **securities class action has been filed** on behalf of purchasers from **February 12, 2026** onward (Howard Smith complaint, July 2026), and multiple firms (Bronstein Gewirtz & Grossman, Glancy Prongay, Kessler Topaz) are investigating. Allegation pattern: guidance issued during the class period was not supported by underlying ATTR-CM demand. A second, separate drop followed the Q2 disclosure; subsequent Q3 (Oct 29) results showed TTR revenue +59% y/y but management said full-year TTR guidance ($4.2–4.5B) would land **at the low end**.

**IP litigation.** Alnylam sued **Moderna and Pfizer separately (D. Del., March 2022)** over LNP/mRNA patents; those claims largely resolved against Alnylam (non-infringement outcomes reported), materially reducing offensive upside but also limiting damages exposure. Peer mRNA/LNP disputes closed with a **$2.25B settlement in March 2026** (Arbutus/Moderna) — a reminder that platform-IP risk is live industry-wide, and Alnylam's siRNA chemistry remains an infringement target as its pipeline broadens into CNS.

**Regulatory posture.** Constructive: FDA approved AMVUTTRA for ATTR-CM (March 2025), and **brezosiran** (Huntington's) showed up to 83% mHTT reduction with a pivotal trial planned for **2027 pending regulatory alignment** — note the novel intrathecal/CNS regulatory pathway is an approval-timeline risk. **Nucresiran** TRITON-CM is enrolling with a CM launch targeted ~2030. Parallel EU/Japan filings are the norm for the franchise.

**Accounting flag (non-legal but governance-relevant).** Q3'26 net income of $2,167.3M includes a **$1,871.5M income-tax benefit** — i.e., earnings quality driven by a non-cash item (consistent with a valuation-allowance release), not operations. Footing: 9M26 revenue $3,916.2M; cash $1,754.3M + $1,832.9M marketable securities; $362.8M converts due Sept 2027 (10-Q).

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating

### **MEDIUM** (geopolitical: **Medium**; legal/regulatory: **Medium-High**)

**Rationale:** Alnylam has an unusually clean *geopolitical* profile for a company of its size — essentially no China, Russia, or emerging-market exposure, a US-based manufacturing base being expanded, and no sanctions or anti-corruption baggage. The risk is almost entirely **domestic policy and securities-law**, not foreign politics: ~80% US revenue concentration colliding with the most aggressive US drug-pricing regime in decades (MFN, IRA, 340B Part B cuts) while a class action tests whether the February 2026 guidance was misleading. Ex-US exposure (~20%) is confined to high-rule-of-law jurisdictions (Germany, Japan), where the risk is price deflation rather than political instability.

**What would move the rating to High:** (1) finalisation of the 340B Part B rule at full magnitude plus an adverse UHC Medicare Advantage policy, compressing AMVUTTRA net price; (2) Section 232 duties applied to Alnylam's imported inputs/CMO supply without exemption; (3) consolidation of the securities suits into a complaint that survives dismissal, inviting SEC scrutiny of the guidance process.
