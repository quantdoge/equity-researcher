# ALNY — Alnylam Pharmaceuticals: Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $174.00 | Market cap $23.3B | EV ~$21.8B (net cash ~$1.5B) | Shares ~134.7M diluted**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Alnylam is the platform monopolist of RNA interference — the only company that has taken a Nobel-winning mechanism from chemistry to three approved drugs and now to a ~$5.5B revenue base, with 80%+ gross margins and operating leverage that has finally turned the P&L profitable. The investment debate is no longer "does RNAi work" but "how durable is the TTR franchise" — and 2026 delivered two ~25-30% single-day collapses (July 30 and Oct 29) on an Amvuttra/ATTR-CM guidance cut and the pushout of next-gen nucresiran's ATTR-CM launch to 2030. The stock now trades at **~4.0x 2026E sales and 18x 2027E EPS against ~46% and ~26% revenue growth** — a growth multiple that has been re-rated as if the franchise is in terminal decline, when revenue is still compounding over 50%.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

- **ATTR amyloidosis** (wild-type + hereditary cardiomyopathy): the single largest RNAi opportunity, with an addressable US/EU patient pool in the tens of thousands and tafamidis having established a multi-billion-dollar market. Alnylam guides 2026 total TTR revenue of **$4.2-4.5B** — so penetration is still early in a market where the incumbent standard of care is oral and Alnylam's is quarterly subcutaneous.
- **Hypertension (zilebesiran)** is the real second leg: an siRNA dosed twice-yearly for uncontrolled hypertension, partnered with Roche. If it works, the TAM is measured in tens of billions — this is the program that justifies a platform multiple, and it is not in the current price.
- **CNS/neuro (brezosiran for Huntington's, ALN-APP for CAA/Alzheimer's)** and **hemophilia (fitusiran)** provide free optionality; brezosiran Phase 1 showed robust target engagement with a pivotal planned for 2027.
- The RNAi platform itself is the moat: a validated delivery chemistry (GalNAc) with a decade-long patent/KnowHow lead, a pipeline of >10 clinical programs, and partnership economics with Regeneron, Roche and Sanofi that fund R&D externally. This is a genuine data/chemistry network effect — each program de-risks the next.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 18, 2027 — Q4/FY2026 results + 2027 guidance** (biggest near-term re-rating event; management stated "on track" for 2026 in October).
- **Amvuttra US TRx/NBRx trajectory and international ATTR-CM label rollouts** (Japan, China, EU) through 2027 — the proof point against the bear case.
- **Nucresiran TRITON-CM (2,250 patients, enrollment expanded)**: any signal that the 2030 launch date could pull forward; interim data would be a major positive.
- **Zilebesiran Phase 3 KARDIA program** readouts (2027-28) — the largest unpriced TAM.
- **Brezosiran pivotal initiation in 2027** (Huntington's) and ALN-APP Phase 2 data — pipeline re-rating optionality.
- **Competitive read-throughs**: any safety/efficacy stumble from BridgeBio's Attruby, Pfizer's tafamidis, or Intellia's one-time CRISPR (nex-z) would reverse the class-wide discount.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (not Strong Buy)

The setup is a classic Wood-style dislocation: a category-defining platform, 80% gross margins, Rule of 40 of ~78, first year of profitability, net cash, and a stock down ~65% from its $491 high, now at 18x 2027E EPS and 4x sales. A reverse-DCF shows the market is discounting severe TTR erosion: consensus 2030 revenue of $10.5B at even 2.5x sales plus net cash implies **~$206/share**, and 3.5x implies **~$283**. Downside: if TTR erodes toward $5B and the pipeline stalls, ~$120-130 is the floor — roughly **2:1 up/down with a wide distribution**. I stop short of Strong Buy because three things are genuinely broken: (a) growth deceleration is steep (65% → 47% → 26% → 19%), (b) nucresiran's 2030 ATTR-CM launch removes the 2027-29 bridge that the bull case depended on, and (c) management has cut guidance twice in 2026, so the burden of proof now sits with the Feb 18 print.

**Sizing/timing discipline:** this is a scale-in, not a back-up-the-truck. Add into the Feb 2027 guidance print; a demonstrated Amvuttra re-acceleration or any zilebesiran/brezosiran data would justify moving toward Strong Buy.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Competitive erosion in ATTR-CM** — the core threat. BridgeBio's oral Attruby (acoramidis) is taking share with an oral convenience advantage; Pfizer's tafamidis owns the installed base; Intellia's one-time CRISPR nex-z, if it clears its liver-safety issues, could structurally obsolete chronic RNAi dosing in CM.
2. **Class-wide efficacy doubt** — the failure of Ionis/AstraZeneca's Wainua (eplontersen) Phase 3 raised the question of whether TTR knockdown translates into cardiomyopathy outcomes, dragging Alnylam's read-across despite its own positive HELIOS-B. This is sentiment risk that can reprice the whole franchise.
3. **Single-franchise concentration** — TTR is ~85-88% of product revenue. There is no diversification cushion if CM share slips.
4. **Pipeline/execution risk** — nucresiran (2030) and zilebesiran (Phase 3, 2027-28 readouts) are now the entire long-term thesis; both are years from revenue and both could miss. TRITON-CM enrollment was expanded to 2,250 patients, which reads as a signal the event rate or effect size is smaller than hoped.
5. **Gross margin compression** — 85.6% (2024) → 81.8% (2025) → ~75% (Q3 2026) as royalty stacks and Amvuttra mix weigh; the "scale leverage" narrative is weaker than headline growth implies.
6. **Guidance credibility and estimate risk** — two misses and a $200M TTR cut in 2026; the $10.5B 2030 consensus looks optimistic and is vulnerable to further downward revision.
7. **Financial/optics** — ~$330M annualized interest expense on convertible debt, ~3.7M dilutive shares, and a 2026 tax-benefit-inflated EPS that flatters screens and could confuse valuation work.
