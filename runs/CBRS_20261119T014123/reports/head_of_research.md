### Investment Memo: CBRS — 19 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL / AVOID | Conviction: Medium (High on risk, tempered by how much is already priced)
**Price Target**: $60 (–36% from current $94.15)
**Time Horizon**: 12–18 months (spans Q4'26 print ~Feb-2027 and the 6-Apr-2027 BIS licence cliff)

**Investment Thesis**
Cerebras has real technology and real, accelerating demand — $26.1B of contracted backlog (~27x revenue), a Q4 core guide of +87%, and marquee logos (OpenAI, Microsoft, Oracle, AWS). But the equity asks investors to underwrite a P&L whose integrity is contested by six independent workstreams: GAAP gross margin collapsed to ~20% vs. a "core" 41–49%, FY25's GAAP profit was a ~$390M non-operating warrant gain, DSO tripled to 109 days, SBC runs at ~93% of revenue, and depreciation lives were nearly doubled four months pre-IPO. Layered on top: existential single-counterparty risk (OpenAI), a BIS authorisation that expires 6-Apr-2027, an admitted data-centre build delay, and the most hostile macro configuration possible for the longest-duration asset in the AI complex. The growth is genuine; the business model is not yet proven, and the price still requires execution the company has not demonstrated.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Macro UNFAVOURABLE — 10Y real yield +80bp to 2.89%, bear steepener, Fed hiking, strong USD; roughly half the –70% drawdown is factor de-rating. Sector ATTRACTIVE (~24% growth, inference ASIC share 15%→40%), but CBRS's position within it is AVERAGE: 39% GM vs 69.5% peer median, –28.5% op margin, a share-taker in a narrow niche.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH — G42/Core42 export authorisation auto-expires 6-Apr-2027; MBZUAI Amendment No.1 routes hardware to a redacted third-country "storage facility" with CBRS as exporter of record; live CFIUS national-security agreement; eight plaintiff firms circling (lead-plaintiff deadline 8-Jan-2027).
- **Value**: SELL — IV $30–40/share. Reverse DCF demands ~45% long-run operating margins (no precedent in hardware); GAAP GM 44.6%→14.2%→20.0% through 2026; FY25 "profit" non-operational. Fortress balance sheet (~$5.9B net cash) is runway, not value.
- **Growth**: BUY (lone dissent) — RPO 27x revenue and growing, Q4 core guide +87%, 2.6x FY28E sales post-de-rating; TAM-first math (1.5–2% share of a ~$500B 2028 inference market) is achievable *if* execution holds.
- **Quantitative Signals**: SELL — bottom sector quintile on value, momentum, quality, and low-vol; the "+198% revision" was a period-mismatch artifact. Caveat: n=32 trading days — not yet statistically quantifiable.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH (equity-impairment, not credit) — no default path (net cash, zero interest expense), but FY25 FCF –$393M, Q3'26 FCF –$839M, ~6 quarters of runway at current burn → 2027–28 equity raise likely.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH — depreciation-life extension pre-IPO (highest-priority forensic flag), ~$150M insider sales at $195–209 with zero buys, CEO on record 13-Nov: "we are behind schedule," circular capacity arrangement (renting its own systems back).
- **ESG**: ESG RISK — Governance 2/10: 20:1 dual-class (~99.2% insider voting), two unremediated ICFR material weaknesses, ~86% of FY25 revenue from ASC 850 related parties.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL — web traffic +19.5% m/m confirms cloud +272%; revenue beats 4/4. But the stock surrendered its entire +11% earnings gap in three sessions on 3x volume (distribution), Google Trends –90% from peak, retail 73% bullish — no one left to convert.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: Credible and fresh — DSO 38→109 days while AR grew 3.5x revenue, inventory +130% QoQ, GAAP/"core" revenue wedge ~17%, true 9M cash burn ≈ –$1.85B. Fairly noted: the obvious part of the trade has paid (–80% from high); this is a PASS, not a short recommendation.

**Bull Case**
- Backlog converts: OpenAI 750MW ramps on schedule, Microsoft becomes a real second hyperscaler, FY27 revenue triples to ~$3B with core GM ≥47% — at 2.6x FY28E sales the stock re-rates violently.
- Term-premium unwind: breakevens at 2.19% say the 10Y's 5.13% is fiscal-supply-driven; a rally to ~4.25% plausibly delivers +60–90% on a 1.85-beta, –70%-drawdown name with no change in fundamentals.
- Inference inflection: wafer-scale's latency edge wins the exact workload growing fastest, and capacity constraints (not demand) are the binding limit — a pricing-power option.

**Bear Case**
- Margin structure is broken: ~20% GAAP gross margin and capital-intensive cloud mix mean terminal economics never support a $16.6B EV; fair value $30–40.
- Binary external dependencies: OpenAI's capital allocation and a BIS licence expiring 6-Apr-2027 are both outside management's control; either failure is a step-function, not a glide.
- Dilution: –$1.25B burned in one quarter; a 2027–28 equity raise into a contested P&L, with litigation (8-Jan-2027 deadline) and export-structuring overhang compounding the discount.

**Key Risks**
1. OpenAI concentration / schedule slip (backlog cancellation rights) — *both directions*.
2. BIS non-renewal of G42/Core42 authorisation or enforcement touching the MBZUAI structure.
3. Q4'26 print: core GM <44% or GAAP GM <25% invalidates the recovery narrative.
4. 10Y real yield breaking higher (30Y >5.75%) takes CBRS toward $60–70; an equity raise accelerates impairment.
5. Culper depreciation/wafer-durability claims validated → warranty cost + hyperscaler trust damage.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
0.0% core weight. For mandates permitting pre-profit holdings, a rules-gated 0.50% venture sleeve maximum, scaled in thirds only after: (i) Q4'26 shows core GM ≥47% *and* GAAP GM ≥30%, (ii) DSO falls below ~85 days with RPO ≥$26.1B, (iii) the G42 authorisation survives 6-Apr-2027. Hard ceiling 1.5% until GAAP GM >30%; never 2.0%; no averaging down. Do not short — squeeze asymmetry and 73% retail bullishness argue against it. Critically, CBRS's correlation to SMH is only 0.47: it is not a semis position but a third, uncorrelated idiosyncratic risk on the same "AI capex funded by cheap duration" theme the book already owns. If you want the term-premium-unwind trade, express it in SMH or NVDA — more liquidity, no licence cliff, no certification risk.

**Dissent flag**: Growth and the sell-side ($170–$240 targets) versus the quality cluster is a 5x disagreement on fair value — treat position sizing accordingly. The single highest-information event is the Q4'26 print (~Feb-2027); the single highest-risk date is 6-Apr-2027.
