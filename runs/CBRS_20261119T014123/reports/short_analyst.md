### 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **LOW**

Score 19/100 (composite). The headline profitability does not exist.

**FY2025 is the core exhibit.** CBRS reported net income of **+$237.8M** and was widely described as profitable. But:

| FY2025 | $M |
|---|---|
| Operating income (EBIT) | **−145.3** |
| Net income | **+237.8** |
| Unexplained non-operating gap | **+383.1** |
| Operating cash flow | **−10.1** |
| Free cash flow | **−392.8** |

A company with a **−$145M operating loss** reporting **+$238M** of net income is being carried by a non-operating item. Third-party S-1 analysis attributes ~**$390.75M** of the FY2025 profit to a one-time, non-cash gain. The mirror-image confirmation sits in FY2024: operating income **−$101.6M**, net income **−$481.6M** — a ~$380M non-operating *loss*. Two consecutive years where a ~$380–390M non-operating swing, not operations, determines the sign of net income. That is derivative/warrant fair-value remeasurement, not earnings.

This is not a one-off. Q2 2026 disclosed a **$149.4M customer warrant asset** tied to the G42 Warrant (exercised April 2026), with **$44.3M and $46.3M recognized** in the P&L across Q2/Q3. CBRS habitually routes non-cash warrant remeasurement gains through income, then lets the headline "GAAP" number stand.

Cash conversion ratio **−4.2%**; FCF conversion **−165%**. Quality earners convert at >100%.

**Q3 2026 was a record revenue quarter and a cash catastrophe:** revenue $210.8M, operating loss −$224.1M, **FCF −$838.6M in a single quarter**, and a **−$432.3M working-capital drain**.

### 2. Key Red Flags

**(a) Receivables — the strongest fresh signal. DSO nearly tripled.**

| Quarter | Revenue | AR | DSO (days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q4'25 | $171.4M | $71.5M | **38** |
| Q1'26 | $193.4M | $96.8M | **46** |
| Q2'26 | $180.1M | $164.1M | **83** |
| Q3'26 | $210.8M | $252.8M | **109** |

AR +254% over nine months vs revenue +73%. Note the annual screen missed this (FY2025 DSRI 0.28 — collections genuinely improved in 2025). The deterioration is entirely a **2026 post-IPO phenomenon**, concentrated in the two quarters where growth was most vigorously marketed. Receivables growing 3.5x faster than revenue is the textbook signature of push-pull revenue recognition.

**(b) Gross margin collapse — 40%+ to 20%.**
FY2025 GAAP GM 39.0%; then Q1'26 **44.6%** → Q2'26 **14.2%** → Q3'26 **20.0%**. Management reports "core gross margin" of **41%** for Q3 — a **21-point gap** to GAAP. A 21-point adjustment to cost of revenue is not a rounding convention; it is the difference between a hardware business with pricing power and a hardware business selling at near-cost.

**(c) GAAP/"core" revenue divergence.** Q2 2026 "core total revenue" was **$209.9M** against GAAP **$180.1M** — a ~$30M (17%) wedge. When revenue itself is adjusted upward by 17%, the top line is being managed.

**(d) Stock-based compensation ≈ 93% of revenue.** 9M 2026 SBC **$541.7M** vs revenue **$584.3M**. Layered on top: **$416.7M** of cash outflow in Q2 2026 booked as "share repurchase" — that is RSU tax withholding, a real cash drain sitting in financing activities, *outside* the −$1,435M of reported 9M FCF. True cash consumption 9M 2026 ≈ **−$1.85B**. Do not read the "buyback" as conviction.

**(e) Inventory days rising** 57 → 79 days in 2026; inventory +130% QoQ in Q3 against +17% revenue.

**(f) Revenue deceleration is unambiguous** — YoY growth 110.3% → 94.4% → 74.3% → **55.3%**, and Q3 came in *below* consensus.

**(g) The export-control exhibit.** Amendment No. 1 to the MBZUAI purchase order (executed 18-Sep-2026) is the most troubling document in the file. It substitutes inference systems for a training+inference cluster where: delivery location is a **"storage facility"** (redacted); **"ownership, title, risk of loss and control"** transfer on delivery **"notwithstanding any installation/acceptance requirement"**; the equipment is expressly **"not intended to be supplied... nor made available for use or enjoyment in the United Arab Emirates"**; and **Cerebras serves as exporter of record**. Recognizing revenue on title transfer to a storage facility, with end-use deliberately outside the customer's own country, is both a revenue-recognition question and a live BIS/DOJ export-control exposure — the precise structure prosecutors charged in the Aug-2025 and Mar-2026 AI-chip diversion cases. New BIS restrictions took effect **20-Nov-2026**, two days after the latest price.

### 3. Beneish M-Score and Accruals

**M-Score: 5.374 — "HIGH RISK — likely manipulator"** (threshold −1.78). | TATA **0.1066** (accruals 2x the 0.05 warning line).

I will *not* over-read the M-Score. Three of its eight indices are mechanically inflated by the capacity buildout rather than by manipulation: **AQI 18.28** (assets $1.11B → $2.33B → $11.94B as IPO cash and PPE landed), **DEPI 2.644**, **LVGI 0.668**. The verdict rests on the defensible components: **TATA 0.1066** (accruals), **GMI 1.083** (margin deterioration — corroborated decisively by the Q1→Q3 GAAP GM path), and **SGI 1.757** (high-growth manipulation risk).

**Accruals ratio: 0.1066 (warning).** Note the true accrual burden is *worse* than reported: FY2025 accruals are inflated by the $390M non-cash gain, and 2026 accruals are understated because $542M of SBC is added back as non-cash while being a genuine economic cost.

**Altman Z-Score 0.327 "Distress" — I reject this.** It is an artifact. The annual balance sheet shows negative book equity (−$578.7M) because redeemable preferred sat in liabilities; the restated Q4'25 quarterly shows **+$1.35B** equity. Post-IPO: **equity $9.16B, cash $7.36B, current ratio 5.3x, net cash ~$5.9B**. There is no solvency story here. Any short thesis predicated on distress will lose.

**Piotroski F-Score 5** (Moderate): fails on positive operating CF, accrual quality, gross-margin improvement, and asset turnover.

### 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

**Customer concentration is the existential risk, and it is sovereign.** FY2024: G42 = **85%** of revenue. FY2025: MBZUAI = **62%**, G42 = **24%** → **~86% from two Abu Dhabi state-affiliated entities**. The $24.6B backlog — the entire bull case — is overwhelmingly contracted with the same cluster. This is not a commercial demand curve; it is a sovereign capex programme that can be rescheduled, repriced, or unwound by policy.

**Moat erosion signals:** gross margin contracting ~20 points while revenue grows 55% means the incremental business is being won on price, not on wafer-scale differentiation. Against Nvidia's CUDA ecosystem, annual release cadence, and effectively unlimited capacity investment, Cerebras sells a single-architecture inference niche. Management is now spending **$1.0B of capex in nine months** to buy capacity it must then fill at 20% GAAP gross margin. Genuine diversification is occurring (cloud revenue +281%), but from a UAE base, and at deteriorating unit economics.

### 6. Short Thesis Summary

**Yes, a credible bear case exists — and it is fresh, not stale.** On 12-Nov CBRS reported a "record" quarter and the stock rose 11%; under the surface, DSO went 83 → 109 days, GAAP gross margin was 20%, inventory jumped 130% QoQ, working capital consumed $432M, and single-quarter FCF was **−$839M**. The quarter was purchased with credit terms and near-cost pricing. Meanwhile FY2025's "profit" was a $390M non-cash gain, the current quarter's P&L still carries warrant remeasurement credits, SBC runs at 93% of revenue, and 86% of revenue plus essentially all of a $24.6B backlog sits with two UAE state entities — one of which is now taking delivery of a redacted-destination cluster into a storage facility with Cerebras as exporter of record.

Consensus requires revenue of **$3.05B in 2027 (+226%)** and **$7.47B in 2028 (+145%)** from a business decelerating through 55% and reporting negative gross-margin-adjusted cash flow. A 2027–28 equity raise looks probable (cash fell $1.25B in Q3 alone; ~6 quarters of runway at that rate), which is the dilution catalyst.
