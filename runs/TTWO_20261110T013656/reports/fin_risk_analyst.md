## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

### Rating: **SUB-INVESTMENT GRADE (BB area) — but a strong credit within that band**

TTWO carries ~$2.96B of gross debt against $1.99B of cash. On the reported FY2026 figures, leverage is genuinely low: **net debt/EBITDA of 0.78x** (using $969M net debt) or **1.14x** if the $1.41B balance-sheet net-debt figure (incl. lease liabilities) is used. S&P's stated expectation (leverage below 2x in FY2026, below 1x thereafter) is consistent with a BB-band issuer with a stable outlook.

The critical nuance: **TTWO does not currently earn an operating profit.** FY2026 operating income was **–$108.6M** on $6.66B of revenue, and Q2 FY2027 was **–$117.5M**. So "low leverage" is low only because EBITDA is dominated by **$1.28B of non-cash D&A** (mostly amortization of the Zynga acquisition intangibles). On an EBIT basis, interest coverage is **negative (–0.23x)**; on an EBITDA basis it is a healthy **7.7x**. The credit is therefore serviceable but entirely dependent on the cash-EBITDA bridge holding.

**Verdict:** Solvency risk over a 12–24 month horizon is low. The credit does not breach; the **equity** is what carries the risk (see §5).

Applied to FY2026 base ($6,656M → **$5,325M**). Note the company is **already ~16% down YoY** in Q2 FY2027 ($1,497M vs $1,774M), so this scenario is not hypothetical — it is roughly the *current run-rate trough* extended for a full year.

**Conclusion of the scenario:** TTWO **survives a 20% revenue decline without a covenant breach or liquidity event.** Cash of ~$2.0B plus a positive-to-flat FCF profile covers $2.96B of debt with only $30M due inside twelve months. Leverage optically stretches to ~3.7x and interest coverage thins to ~2x EBITDA, which would pressure the BB rating — but this is a *ratings* problem, not a *solvency* problem. **The binding constraint is not the balance sheet; it is that a 20% revenue decline with no GTA VI revenue would compress a $38.8B market cap far harder than it compresses the credit.**

**Note:** the –20% scenario understates the true tail. The genuine tail risk is **GTA VI slipping out of FY2027 entirely** — management's FY2027 guidance of **$8.0–8.2B net bookings is a ~25% step-up that assumes the Nov 19, 2026 launch lands in-period**. That single assumption is the entire forward earnings case.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating

# **MODERATE**
*(Credit: Moderate-Low · Liquidity: Low · Market/Valuation: High)*

**Rationale.** This is a **balance-sheet-fortified, cash-flow-positive, GAAP-loss-making, single-catalyst company.** The pieces that usually kill a company — leverage, liquidity, refinancing — are all in good shape and improving. The pieces that kill the *equity* — negative operating margins, thin FCF yield, a 32x EV/EBITDA multiple, and total dependence on one product launch — are all present and elevated. Leverage has fallen from 1.92x to 0.84x debt/equity in one year and cash has more than doubled; that is real de-risking and should be recognised.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **~$6.2B of cumulative goodwill/intangible write-downs in three years** (GWI $8.93B → $2.72B). The $12.7B Zynga acquisition has been substantially destroyed in value. FY2025 alone carried a ~$3.7B below-the-line impairment (net loss $4.48B vs operating loss $0.74B). Management's capital allocation record on M&A is the single largest governance-level risk.
2. **Persistently negative operating income** — FY2023 through Q2 FY2027 with only one marginally positive quarter (Mar-2026, +$10M). Six of the last seven quarters posted operating losses.
3. **Revenue is already declining into the launch** — Q2 FY2027 revenue –15.6% YoY, with operating losses *widening* ($–117.5M vs $–97.9M). There is no cushion if GTA VI slips.
4. **Dilution:** shares +5.8% YoY (175.1M → 185.3M); SBC running at 4.6% of revenue. F-Score flags this explicitly. Adjusted for $305M SBC, FCF falls from $462M to ~$156M and the **true FCF yield is ~0.4%, not 1.2%**.
5. **Valuation offers no downside protection:** ~32x EV/EBITDA and 31x market cap/EBITDA on a company with negative operating margins. Any launch disappointment is absorbed entirely by the multiple.
6. **Working-capital volatility is violent:** –$1,007M and –$1,243M swings in the last two years. OCF quality is unstable and a single bad WC quarter can flip FCF negative.
7. **Negative EBIT interest coverage** means the credit is serviced from D&A, not profit. Any step-down in the amortization base (further impairments) mechanically shrinks reported EBITDA.
