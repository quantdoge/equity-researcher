## TTWO — Alternative Data Signal Report
**As of:** November 9, 2026 (post-FQ2'27 print) | **Price:** $207.40 | **Market cap:** $38.8B

**Framing note that governs everything below:** this is not a routine quarter read. GTA VI launches **November 19, 2026 — 10 days out**. Every alt-data channel available is dominated by launch-window demand, and the stock has fallen *into* the catalyst: −22% from its July 7 high ($265.94), −19% YTD from $256.03, and it printed its 2026 low ($183.86 intraday) on **October 30** — the same week Google Trends began inflecting sharply higher. That divergence is the core of this report.

### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — **Strongly accelerating**

**Google Trends, "GTA 6" (US, weekly, 5-yr series):**
| Week | Interest |
|---|---|
| Sep 20 – Oct 4 | 16–21 (trough) |
| Oct 11 / Oct 18 | 27 / 28 |
| Oct 25 | 34 |
| **Nov 1** | **62** |

The pre-launch ramp is ~2x the September trough in three weeks and now matches the August 23 spike (62). The only higher print in five years is **93 in the week of June 21, 2026** — the preorder-open/trailer event (preorders opened June 25 at $99). Search interest in prior launches typically peaks *at* release, not before it, so the current 62 with 18 days still to run is a leading indicator pointing at a new all-time high in the uncaptured November data.

**Third-party preorder tracking (quantified, and the single best signal here):**
- **Sensor Tower: 6.08M preorders as of October 13**, ≈$594M gross
- **~6.45M digital preorders by ~October 20**, ≈$630M net of returns, **74% PlayStation share**
- **DFC Intelligence: ≥$1B in preorders**, >10M units if mix skews to premium editions
- Amazon sell-through characterised as "incredibly fast"

Direction: **positive and steepening**. Note the ASP lever: $99 base pricing (~+40% vs the prior-gen $69.99 norm) means bookings upside can arrive on *unit* numbers that merely meet expectations — an under-modelled revenue kicker.

**Web traffic — low confidence, largely unusable.** Only SimilarWeb meta-snippets were returned, no numeric monthly series. Two soft reads: take2games.com out-trafficked 2k.com as of June 2026; rockstargames.com described as −3.1% MoM (pre-hype period, stale). I would not act on this channel.

### 2. Business Activity Signals — **No usable signal (tool failures)**

- **Patent filings: no data.** The USPTO PatentsView feed errored on both attempts (`Expecting value: line 1 column 1`). Low analytical loss: TTWO's moat is owned IP and content pipelines, not patentable R&D — this channel is structurally uninformative for a game publisher.
- **Job postings: no trend available.** The proxy returned generic aggregator pages (Indeed showing "9 jobs" against the *corporate* entity, which excludes Rockstar/2K studio-level listings). No time series, no direction. The one qualitative note: postings skew toward DevOps/ServiceNow/audit/internal-systems — consistent with a company in **post-development wind-down and integration mode**, not a hiring surge. I cannot quantify this, so it stays out of the signal weighting.
- **Launch-day readiness (inferred, not measured):** guidance implicitly assumes supply availability; PS5 at $649.99 and PS5 Pro at $899.99 are elevated hardware price points that could cap attach rate on the console side.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **TRACKING ABOVE** (for FQ3'27, reported ~Feb 2, 2027)

Company guidance for the launch quarter (Oct–Dec 2026): **net bookings $3.35–3.50B**, GAAP diluted EPS $1.30–1.44. FY27 bookings reiterated at **$8.0–8.2B**.

Versus consensus (revenue $3.42B / EPS $3.95): **consensus sits almost exactly at guide midpoint** — the booking bar is not stretched, and management explicitly said preorders are "far above initial expectations" while nonetheless *holding* rather than raising the FY target. Guide midpoint already represents roughly +94% YoY and exceeds the entire $2.99B booked in Apr–Sep 2026 combined. With 6.45M+ paid preorders locked before launch, the probability skews to the **upper half or above** the $3.35–3.50B range.

Three caveats that keep this from High confidence:
1. **EPS consensus is not comparable to guidance** — $3.95 consensus is clearly an adjusted basis against a $1.30–1.44 GAAP guide (Zynga intangible amortisation distorts GAAP). Headline "EPS miss" risk is an accounting artefact, not a demand signal. Watch bookings.
2. **Surprise history is noisy and has deteriorated:** beat rate 57% (4 of 7), avg surprise +20.8%, but the last two prints were EPS misses of **−45% (Aug '26)** and **−70% (Nov '26)** — both while bookings were roughly in line/above. The Nov 9 print itself: EPS $0.29 vs $0.96 est, revenue $1.497B vs $1.623B est.
3. **Revenue is guided short of prior consensus** (Reuters: "projects Q3 bookings below estimates") — that gap, not the search data, is what drove the −3.5% reaction on 2.9x normal volume.

20 articles in 14 days, 4 positive-signal, 0 negative, score 1.0. I discount this materially: the keyword method scored "Reports **Lower** Revenue Ahead of GTA VI Launch" and "Take-Two projects third-quarter bookings **below** estimates" as *neutral*. Qualitatively the coverage is wall-to-wall GTA VI with a distinctly constructive undertone — "unprecedented levels of preorders to power 'historic year'," "Pre-Orders Are Far Above Initial Expectations," and a visible "guidance is too low" thesis (Motley Fool: *"Take-Two's Forecast Might Be Too Low… Buy Before Nov. 19?"*). Net: **mildly positive, not euphoric** — appropriate for a name that has de-rated, and notably absent the blow-off tone that marks a top.

The prevalence of "priced in" fatigue *after* a 22% drawdown is a contrarian-positive tell: retail positioning is not crowded long.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**

Demand-side alt data is accelerating on every channel that actually produced data — search interest up 3x off the September trough into the launch window, preorders compounding at ~370k units/week and already >$630M, premium ASP, and management language ("far above initial expectations") that is unusually specific for this management team. Simultaneously, price is −22% off the July high, −3.5% on the print, and consensus revenue sits at *guide midpoint* rather than above it. Alt data is running ahead of both the guidance bar and the share price.

### 7. Confidence Level: **Medium-High**

High confidence in the *demand* signal (two independent quantified third-party datasets plus Trends plus company commentary all agreeing). Medium confidence in the *trade expression*, because: (a) the discrete catalyst is 10 days away and therefore fully visible to the market — this is not an information edge, it is a magnitude edge; (b) the debate that matters for the stock is FY28 durability and Q4'27 bookings of $1.51–1.86B, which no alt dataset here addresses; (c) the stock's 2026 low on Oct 30 could equally reflect console-installed-base constraints that preorder data won't reveal.

**Data limitations and lag — read before using:**
- **Google Trends lags ~5–7 days and the series terminates the week of Nov 1, 2026.** The final pre-launch surge and launch day — the highest-signal weeks — are **not in this dataset**. Search interest measures attention, not purchases.
- Trends is **US-only** (tool defaults to US geo); no global series available, which matters given 74% PlayStation skew and overseas mix.
- **Preorder figures are third-party estimates** (Sensor Tower, DFC), not company-disclosed; Sensor Tower under-counts physical retail and their Oct 13 vs Oct 20 snapshots differ in methodology (~6.08M vs ~6.45M).
- **Patent feed failed twice — no data. Job posting proxy returned no usable series. Web traffic channel returned no numeric data.** These three gaps are noted rather than filled; I have not substituted guesswork.
- No app-store ranking data was retrievable; TTWO's mobile titles (Zynga) are materially smaller than console and are not the driver here.
- **News sentiment score of +1.00 is a keyword artefact** and should not be cited as evidence of sentiment strength.
- Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. Here it argues the *magnitude* of the GTA VI quarter is being underestimated; it does not resolve whether that outcome is already embedded in a $38.8B market cap.
