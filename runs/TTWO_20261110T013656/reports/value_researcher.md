## Take-Two Interactive Software (TTWO) — Value Analysis
**Price $207.40 (Nov 9, 2026) | Market cap ~$39.0B (188M diluted sh) | Net debt ~$1.4B | EV ~$40.4B**
FY ends March 31. GTA VI launches **November 19, 2026** (10 days out).

---

### 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **Narrow overall, Wide within Rockstar**

| Segment/label | Economics | Moat source | Durability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rockstar (GTA, Red Dead) | Elite — ~100% incremental margin on catalog | Intangible asset (the strongest brand in interactive entertainment) + network effects in GTA Online + switching costs (social graphs, in-game assets) | **Wide** |
| 2K (NBA 2K, WWE, Civilization, Borderlands) | Good — annuity-like sports cadence | Exclusive NBA/NBPA licensing, brand, installed community | **Narrow** |
| Zynga / T2 Mobile | Poor — treadmill of user-acquisition spend | None demonstrable | **None** |

**Evidence:** FY2026 net bookings ~$6.7B with **84% from recurrent consumer spending** — that is the signature of a business with installed-base lock-in, not a hit-by-hit publisher. GTA V has sold on the order of 200M+ units over 12 years and still ranks among the top recurring revenue contributors a decade later; GTA VI's **$99.99 price point** is the company unilaterally resetting industry price — real pricing power. NBA 2K is a licensed near-duopoly with an exclusive NBA deal.

**Moat trend over five years: narrowed in aggregate.** Pre-Zynga (FY2022) the company earned $418M of net income on $3.5B of revenue with 13.5% operating margins. Today revenue is ~90% higher but the consolidated business has produced **negative cumulative FCF of roughly –$15M across FY2022–FY2026** and four straight years of GAAP operating losses. Translation: the Rockstar franchise asset strengthened; the *per-dollar-of-capital* quality of the enterprise deteriorated materially because ~$12.7B of shareholder money went into mobile.

**Circle of competence:** Yes. The unit economics are legible — content investment → install base → high-margin recurring monetization — though title-level hit probability is inherently not forecastable.

### 2. Financial Quality Score: **5 / 10**

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue ($B) | 3.50 | 5.35 | 5.35 | 5.63 | 6.66 |
| Gross margin | 56.2% | 42.7% | 41.9% | 54.4% | 57.2% |
| Operating margin | 13.5% | –21.5% | –21.4% | –13.1% | –1.6% |
| Net income ($M) | 418 | –1,125 | –3,744 | –4,479 | –298 |
| FCF ($M) | 99 | –203 | –158 | –215 | **+462** |
| ROIC | 13%(pre-deal) | –9.2% | –12.4% | –11.8% | –1.7% |

- **Revenue quality: good.** ~17% CAGR FY22→FY26; FY27 guided to $8.0–8.2B net bookings. Growth is annuity-heavy (84% recurrent), not one-off.
- **Cash conversion: historically poor, inflecting.** Four consecutive years of negative FCF followed by +$462M in FY2026, with FY2027 guided to **CFO > $1.0B and capex ~$290M → ≥$710M FCF**. FY2026 owner earnings (NI + D&A – capex) = **$817M**, versus reported net loss of –$298M — the GAAP/cash gap is amortization of acquired intangibles ($618M guided for FY27) and impairments.
- **Balance sheet: adequate, not fortress.** Cash $1.99B vs. debt $2.96B (net debt $1.41B, ~1.4x FY27 EBITDA). Equity only $3.51B because **goodwill + intangibles were written down from $12.3B (FY23) to $2.7B** — a $9.6B acknowledgment of value destroyed. Retained earnings –$7.36B.
- **Dilution/SBC: a real cost I do not ignore.** SBC ~$305M/yr (~4.6% of revenue); diluted shares 175M → 189M in two years. Free cash flow overstated by roughly $1.6/share annually if SBC is treated as expense.

**Why not higher:** a business that has consumed cash for four years, destroyed ~$6B of book equity on an acquisition, and only now converts ~7% of revenue to FCF does not earn an 8+.

### 3. Valuation Analysis

**Trailing multiples are meaningless here** (P/E –128x, EV/EBITDA 31.7x, FCF yield 1.2%) — FY2027 contains only ~4.5 months of GTA VI, while launch marketing is expensed up front. FY27 guidance: EPS $0.55–0.75, non-GAAP EBITDA $993M–$1,053M, FCF ≥$710M.

**Normalized earnings power (my estimate, FY2028 = first full GTA VI year):**
- Net bookings $11.5–12.5B (vs. $8.1B FY27; consensus expects ~+94% December-quarter bookings)
- Gross margin ~57%; opex ~$4.5B → operating income ~$2.0–2.3B; EBITDA ~$3.0–3.4B
- EPS ~$8.00–9.00; **FCF ~$2.2–2.6B** (~20% FCF margin, in line with the FY2021 GTA V-cycle peak)

At $207 the market pays ~12x FY28E EBITDA and ~16x FY28E FCF — but only if the launch works.

**DCF (WACC 8.5–9.5%, terminal growth 3%):**

| Scenario | FCF path | Intrinsic value |
|---|---|---|
| Bear (weak launch, modest online monetization; FCF ~$1.5B) | –20% vs. base | **~$125–140** |
| Base (bookings ~$11.5B, FCF $2.4B by FY29) | management model | **~$225–245** |
| Bull (blockbuster + strong GTA Online; FCF $3.1B) | +30% vs. base | **~$300–330** |

**Base-case intrinsic value ≈ $235/share** (probability-weighted, ~$225). Current $207 is ~12–15% below that — **inside the range, but short of the 20–30% margin of safety I require**, and the distribution is unusually wide because a single product event 10 days away determines whether the bear or bull case is live.

Sell-side targets ($262–$313, BofA at $368) cluster 30–75% above spot; they are capitalizing the bull case. Peer multiples were unavailable from the data feed (the sector screen returns megacap semis/software, not gaming comps) — an honest gap; the nearest reference points are EA (being taken private at a high-teens EBITDA multiple) and Nintendo/Roblox, none of which change the conclusion.

**Implied expectation at $207:** bookings roughly double from $8.1B to ~$11.5B with no stumble, and ~$2.4B of FCF is delivered by FY2029. That is plausible, not conservative.

### 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Good operator, flawed allocator**

**Strengths:** Strauss Zelnick has run the company since 2007 with a founder-operator orientation, heavy equity-linked compensation, and a long record of candor. He executed disciplined buybacks in the 2019–2021 window and built Rockstar/2K into the industry's premier IP portfolio. Prior to 2022, capital allocation was genuinely shareholder-oriented (no empire building).

**Blemishes, weighted heavily:**
1. **Zynga, $12.7B (2022), at roughly 7x sales near the peak of mobile gaming.** It was followed by impairments that cut goodwill and intangibles from $12.3B to $2.7B. This is the single largest destroyer of value in company history and it is the reason ROIC has been negative for four years. I judge management's M&A discipline as unproven-to-poor.
2. **Two GTA VI delays** (Fall 2025 → May 2026 → Nov 19, 2026) after public expressions of confidence — a credibility cost.
3. **No dividend and effectively no buybacks since FY2022**; shareholders have been paid in dilution (+8% share count in two years).
4. Post-GTA VI cash flow raises the risk of another large, price-insensitive acquisition — the pattern to watch.
