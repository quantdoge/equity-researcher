**As of 24 November 2026 | Data: FRED, USDA ERS, company filings**

## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Late-cycle tightening in a bear-steepening rate regime — "risk-off in duration, not in growth."**

The dominant macro fact for November 2026 is that the easing cycle is over and has partially reversed. The effective fed funds rate troughed at **3.63% (April–July 2026)** after 175bp of cuts from the 5.33% peak, and has since risen to **3.88% (October 2026)**. Headline CPI has re-accelerated to **3.82% y/y (October 2026)** from roughly **2.70% y/y a year earlier** (Nov-2024 → Nov-2025: 316.528 → 325.063). With policy at 3.88% against 3.82% inflation, the **real fed funds rate is approximately zero** — policy is no longer restrictive, which is precisely why the long end is repricing.

The yield curve has bear-steepened violently: the **10Y is at 5.13% (24 Nov 2026)**, up ~69bp from 4.44% on 30 June 2026, with an intra-October peak of 5.28%. The **30Y sits at 5.47%**, 34bp above the 10Y, and **2s10s is +39bp** — no inversion, and no recession signal from the curve. This is a term-premium/fiscal-risk steepener, not a growth scare.

Growth is fine: **real GDP rose 2.07% y/y in Q3 2026** ($24,611B chained), with ~3.4% annualised QoQ momentum. **Unemployment is 4.1% (October 2026)**, down from 4.5% a year earlier — the labour market is tightening again, not loosening. This is a "no landing" configuration: decent growth, sticky inflation, rising long rates, tightening labour.

For a Consumer Defensive protein processor, this is an unusual mix. TSN's demand is defensive, but its **input costs are labour and livestock**, both of which are inflating, while its **output price (food CPI at +2.5% y/y, September 2026; food-at-home +2.2% in August)** is running ~130bp *below* headline. That is the core macro squeeze: cost inflation in excess of category pricing power.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

**Interest rates — mildly negative, not acute.** TSN guides **FY2027 net interest expense of ~$360M** (FY2026 ~$365M) against FY2026 adjusted operating income of **$1.98B** — interest is under 20% of operating profit. Fitch affirms **BBB/F2, Stable**, with leverage ~2.7x and EBITDA ~$3.4–3.8B; the company is deleveraging. So the risk is not solvency — it is the **discount rate**. A 70bp rise in the 10Y compresses the multiple on a business with mid-single-digit growth, and management explicitly uses a **cost-of-capital hurdle for every business unit**, so a 5%+ long end raises the bar for the value-added/prepared-foods reinvestment story. Beta is 0.374 — the equity will not de-rate like a long-duration growth name, but neither will it get a falling-rate tailwind.

**Inflation — negative, via a cost/price scissors.** TSN has 124,000 employees and unemployment has fallen 40bp in a year; wage inflation is re-accelerating. Meanwhile food CPI at 2.5% caps pass-through. The gap between input inflation (labour, packaging, cattle) and the 2.5% food CPI is a margin tax. Note the asymmetry: headline CPI at 3.8% actively **hurts TSN's core low- and middle-income customer** through a real-income squeeze, pushing trade-down from beef toward chicken and private label — volume-positive for chicken, price-negative across the portfolio.

**The cattle cycle is the single largest macro variable — and it is adverse but inflecting.** Beef production fell **4.6% in 2026** after **−3.6% in 2025**, with the US herd at its smallest level in decades and cattle prices near records. TSN's beef segment lost **$654M in FY2026**, and FY2027 guidance is **−$300M to +$100M**. This is a biological cycle, not a rate cycle — it will not respond to Fed policy, but it is the dominant swing factor in earnings.

**Trade policy — mixed, with an improving input skew.** The expanded beef import quota at lower tariff rates took effect **1 September 2026**, which lowers lean-beef input costs for TSN's grinding operations and modestly compresses the beef cutout. Offsetting: retaliatory tariff risk on US pork/poultry exports, and an active **DOJ beef-price investigation** plus direct political pressure on packer margins from the administration's grocery-price agenda. That is a policy-driven margin risk that no macro model captures.

**Currency — a fresh headwind.** The broad dollar is up **~4.8% since 9 September 2026** (117.88 → 123.60) and ~3.3% since end-July. TSN's export and international operations (Mexico, Brazil, Thailand, China/Asia pork) face both translation drag and reduced export competitiveness at exactly the point where beef volumes are contracting. Every 1% of broad USD strength is a direct topline and competitiveness cost.

**Guidance context:** FY2027 sales guided **−3.5% to −5.5%**, adjusted operating income **$1.7–2.15B** (vs $1.98B), with lower chicken profitability and improved beef. Shares rallied ~10% on 16 November 2026 to **$62.95 (23 Nov)**, +24.7% off the 1 October low of $50.47 — the market is already paying for the beef recovery.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL (leaning unfavourable)**

Rationale for neutrality rather than "unfavourable": TSN's defensive demand profile, 0.374 beta, BBB balance sheet and modest interest burden insulate it from the rate shock that is damaging long-duration assets. Chicken and prepared foods benefit from consumer trade-down in a real-income squeeze.

Rationale for the unfavourable lean: (i) the **rate path has flipped from tailwind to headwind** and the long end is repricing on fiscal/term-premium grounds that TSN cannot influence; (ii) **food CPI at 2.5% is structurally below input inflation**, capping pricing power; (iii) a **record cattle cycle** keeps the largest segment loss-making through FY2027; (iv) **USD strength since September** hits exports into a contracting beef-supply year; (v) policy/DOJ risk on packer margins is elevated and rising.

## 5. Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise (Bull)

**A disinflationary commodity-and-rates reversal in H1 2027.**

Consensus (and TSN's own guidance) assumes sticky inflation, a Fed on hold or hiking, a 5%+ 10Y, and cattle prices staying near records. The non-consensus outcome is a **joint rollover in energy/commodity inflation that drags headline CPI from 3.82% back below 2.5% by mid-2027**, forcing the Fed to reverse the 25bp of recent hikes and re-open the cutting cycle — pulling the 10Y from 5.13% back toward 4.40%.

For TSN this would be a double-barrelled re-rating that is not in the $1.7–2.15B FY2027 operating income guide. First, the discount-rate effect: a ~70bp retracement on a 0.374-beta defensive name with a mid-teens earnings base would support a 1.5–2.0x multiple turn. Second, the fundamental effect: any faster-than-expected moderation in fed cattle prices (USDA already cut its 2026 fed-steer forecast by $5.75/cwt to $245.35) would push the beef segment from the guided **−$300M to +$100M** band toward the upper end or beyond, against a $654M FY2026 loss base — and expanded import quotas from 1 September 2026 amplify that pass-through. Add a weaker dollar (reversing the +4.8% since September) and export volumes recover into the chicken/pork complex.

**Why it's a genuine surprise:** the market is currently positioned for the opposite (bear steepener, sticky inflation, "high beef prices are here to stay"). The asymmetry is favourable: at $62.95 the stock has already rallied 24.7% off the October low on beef-recovery optimism, so the easy money is made — but a macro disinflation shock would validate FY2028 consensus that does not yet exist, while the bear case (10Y through 5.50%, consumer recession, cattle prices sticky) is largely reflected in guidance already.

**Key risk to this scenario:** the DOJ beef-price investigation and political pressure on packer margins could cap the benefit of any input-cost relief by preventing spread expansion — a reminder that TSN's 2027 outcome may be driven more by Washington than by the Fed.
