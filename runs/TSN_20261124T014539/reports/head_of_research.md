### Investment Memo: TSN — November 24, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $65 (+3% from current $62.95); ~6–7% total expected return incl. the 3.3% dividend
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Tyson is a cyclical margin-recovery trade, not a growth story — and the market has already paid for most of it, re-rating the stock ~25% off the October low on beef-inflection optimism. The FY27 setup is a mix swap, not an earnings addition: beef improves ~$550M while chicken and pork give most of it back, leaving guided adjusted operating income flat-to-down ($1.7–2.15B vs $1.98B). Downside is cushioned by a repaired balance sheet, 7.5% FCF yield, a raised dividend, and genuine cycle optionality into FY28 — but at $63 the risk/reward is balanced, not compelling.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro NEUTRAL leaning unfavourable — easing cycle reversed (10Y at 5.13%), food CPI (2.5%) running below input inflation, USD strengthening. Sector NEUTRAL: beef in deep trough (herd rebuild just beginning, relief 2028+), chicken rolling over from peak. TSN's problem: it owns the trough while its chicken cushion deflates.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM. ~86% US revenue anchors risk, but ICE/labor enforcement is an acute operational exposure, trade policy now cuts *against* beef economics (300K MT tariff-free imports), and antitrust has become a recurring P&L tax ($358M/$698M booked against sales in FY26/FY25). Walmart = 19% of sales.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: *Value agent report missing — synthesised from quant/short/sector inputs.* Genuine asset/cash-flow value (1.26x book, 13.4x P/FCF, 11.8x trough EV/EBITDA falling to ~8.4x FY27E) but no earnings value: 49x trailing GAAP, ~7.7x tangible book. Cheap for a reason; value is contingent on a recovery current ROIC (4.3%, below cost of capital) does not yet evidence.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: HOLD. Fails every growth test (Rule of 40 = 6.6, 4.6% 5-yr revenue CAGR, FY27 revenue guided down 3.5–5.5%). The FY27–29 EPS ramp (+9%/+25%/+11%) is margin normalization off a trough, dependent on an exogenous cattle cycle.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL (composite ~51). Value (52) and revisions (70, but FY29-dated = low confidence) offset by bottom-quartile quality (28: ROE 2.6%). R² of 0.03 means beta estimates (-0.32 to 0.37) are unusable — TSN is ~97% idiosyncratic risk.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE, deteriorating. Balance sheet genuinely improved ($2B debt repaid, 2.8x net leverage, $1.66B FCF) but the income statement is fragile: 1.95% operating margin, Q4 FY26 EBIT covered interest only 0.22x, ~8% revenue decline eliminates EBIT. Goodwill/intangibles of $14.9B = 84% of book equity.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Weak governance (family holds ~72% of votes on 22% economics against 88% shareholder opposition), new securities-fraud investigations on the Sept guidance cut (re-affirm → $1B notes → cut one month later), unresolved child-labor probes. Mitigant: insiders are net buyers — incoming CEO bought $1M at $51.50.
- **ESG**: LAGGARD. >$267M cumulative antitrust settlements, greenwashing settlement with 5-year climate-marketing ban, water/wage penalty pattern, and a live DOJ *criminal* antitrust probe — the unquantifiable tail risk. Not thesis-breaking near-term, but caps any re-rating and excludes ESG-screened buyers.
- **Alternative Data**: NEUTRAL, mild positive tilt. Jimmy Dean search interest +22% YoY into the holidays supports the Prepared Foods margin story; consumer food-price anxiety collapsing; 85.7% beat rate into a low Feb bar. No alt-data edge exists on the real swing factor: chicken pricing.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (moderate conviction). Earnings quality is clean (no fraud, negative accruals) but the $880M GAAP-to-adjusted gap (80% of GAAP operating income) makes adjusted EPS a promotional construct; dividend is 155% of GAAP earnings; downside math $48–54 on re-rating. Concedes cycle optionality and insider buying argue against high conviction.

**Bull Case**
- **Beef inflection with self-help**: heifer retention (+3%) is the first genuine rebuild signal; closures remove 10–15% of beef capacity; each $500M of beef recovery ≈ 25% of group earnings, with a path to $5+ EPS by FY29.
- **Quality assets carrying the trough**: Prepared Foods ($1.3B+ segment income, 12.6% margins, Jimmy Dean momentum) and structurally reset chicken economics fund the dividend through the cycle.
- **Fortified balance sheet + aligned insiders**: $2B deleveraged, $1.66B FCF, dividend raised, buyback at ~$55, and the new CEO bought $1M of stock at $51.50 weeks before taking the job.

**Bear Case**
- **The recovery is a swap**: FY27 guides chicken down to $1.3–1.6B (from ~$1.9B) and pork down — CFO's own words: headwinds "could outweigh the controllable improvement." Q4 FY26 (3.7% gross margin, net loss) is the exit run-rate, not the past.
- **Earnings quality and hidden leverage**: GAAP EPS $1.28 vs adjusted $3.44; $14.9B goodwill (84% of book equity) against a three-year-loss-making beef segment invites impairment; dividend uncovered by GAAP earnings.
- **Priced-in optimism**: +25% off the low, sell-side targets cluster only 5–10% above spot, JPMorgan downgraded post-print. The easy money on the beef trade is made.

**Key Risks**
1. **Double-dip**: chicken rolls over while beef still loses >$300M — EPS back toward $2 (short analyst's central scenario).
2. **DOJ criminal antitrust probe** and the securities-litigation cluster — uncapped, unquantifiable, and a direct hit to the multiple.
3. **Labor/immigration enforcement** disrupting plant throughput; child-labor escalation.
4. **Covenant/goodwill event** if the Q4 run-rate persists: FCF turns negative near −10% revenue; impairment would cut book equity by up to 84%.
5. **Feb 8 Q1 FY27 print** — seasonally weak quarter, and the only miss in the recent sample was FQ1.
