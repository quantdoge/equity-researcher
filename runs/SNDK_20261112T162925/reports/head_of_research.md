**Investment Thesis**
Sandisk is a solvency-safe, fab-light NAND pure-play earning genuinely spectacular but unsustainable peak-cycle economics (85% gross margin, $5.7B quarterly FCF), trading at 4.2x FY27E earnings because the market has *already* refused to capitalise those earnings. The bull case rests on a $93.9B contracted floor-price revenue book covering ~50% of FY27 and ~67% of FY28 bits, contract prices still rising +15–20% QoQ, and HBF optionality. The bear case — mid-cycle margin normalisation cutting EPS 50–70% — is real but largely priced; what is *not* fully priced is the governance and supply-structure risk (one-sided insider selling, single-source Kioxia wafers, off-balance-sheet JV capex). The asymmetry at 4x forward earnings modestly favours a small long; nothing about this name favours a large one.

**Bull Case**
- **Contracted floor-price book**: $93.9B minimum expected revenue across 8 customers, >50% of FY27 bits — the near-term cash flow the bears discount is largely already locked; contract prices still rising QoQ with shortages forecast into 2027.
- **Valuation already prices the bust**: 4.2x FY27E, 3.3x FY28E, ~18% annualised FCF yield, $4.9B net cash, and a 100%-of-FCF return policy retiring ~16–25% of the float annually — buybacks at 4x earnings are enormously accretive even on flat EPS.
- **Fed-pause asymmetry + HBF optionality**: breakevens at 2.20% say the bond market views this inflation as a supply shock; a pause snaps the multiple back (plausible double from here per Macro), and HBF design wins in CY2027 are a free option on a new AI-inference memory tier.

**Bear Case**
- **85% gross margin has no floor**: every prior SNDK cycle ended in losses (FY23: 7% GM, −$2.1B); supply response is funded ($31B Kioxia JV) and lands 2027–28 exactly as supply normalises; mid-cycle EPS of $17–44 against today's price = −30% to −75%.
- **Management behaviour contradicts the supercycle narrative**: $132M sold into the top with zero purchases through a 66% drawdown, plus $9.5B of buybacks at ~$1,450–1,600 — peak-cycle capital allocation from a team that has run this cycle before.
- **Structural fragility**: single-source Japan wafer supply with repricing risk, 47.5% Greater China revenue inside a live export-control/tariff regime, YMTC share gain and patent-war tail risk, and a 60%-of-mix consumer/Edge book facing demonstrated demand destruction.

**Key Risks**
1. **NAND contract price rollover** — the Nov 5 session (−22% on 3–4x volume) is the template; two consecutive QoQ declines = exit.
2. **Kioxia repricing / JV economics reset** — the most under-appreciated risk; ~$15B implied share of JV capex is off balance sheet and unquantified.
3. **Rates/beta** — beta 3.9–5.65 with a hiking Fed; a 10Y at 5.75–6% implies a 40–50% de-rating *even with earnings rising*.

5. **Policy shocks** — Section 232 applied to NAND/SSD derivatives, China export controls naming NAND, or a YMTC patent suit.
6. **Earnings quality drift** — DSO 54.9 days (+42% in 3 quarters), cash conversion down to 78.5%, $5.0B goodwill (28% of equity).
