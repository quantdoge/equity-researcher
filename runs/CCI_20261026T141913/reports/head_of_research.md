### Investment Memo: CCI — 26 October 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (do not initiate at current levels) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $92 (~+4% from current $88.17; ~9% total return incl. the 4.8% yield)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Crown Castle is finally the business it should always have been: a pure-play US tower REIT with ~40,000 irreplaceable sites, ~70% EBITDA margins, 3% contractual escalators, and $2.9B of FCF on $182M of capex after the $8.5B fiber/small-cell exit. The problem is the price, not the asset: at $88 the stock trades *at* its intrinsic-value midpoint, at a premium to the faster-growing, less-levered American Tower, with the weakest balance sheet in the tower group — and the SpaceX demand catalyst that drove +28% in 12 sessions is a Bloomberg report, not a signed lease. Eleven of eleven specialists declined to recommend a buy here; the disagreement is only about what price makes it one ($72–76 on weakness, or any price up to $95 on a confirmed SpaceX master lease).

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is a mature cash-cow (US tower growth ~−1% to +1% organic), not a growth story — stock selection is a balance-sheet question, and CCI screens worst of the towers. Macro is the swing factor: 10Y TIPS real yield at 2.64% (+71bp since May) discounts a long-duration, levered REIT whose 4.8% yield no longer beats the 4.99% 10Y. Bear scenario (oil shock → real yield ~3.10%) implies $74–78.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Geopolitical risk low (100% domestic); legal concentration is the issue — a $3.5B DISH claim now adjudicated inside a Chapter 11 estate, ~93% of rent from three carriers, and a contested FCC siting-preemption docket. Rating: Medium.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Intrinsic value $85–92 (midpoint ≈$88) via AFFO cap, DDM and private-market cross-checks. Price = fair value; no margin of safety. Wide moat on assets, but ROIC ~7.4% ≈ cost of capital, and the fiber decade destroyed ~$10B. Buy trigger ≤$72; trim >$100.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: Real organic growth is 3.8% ex-DISH and decelerating; AFFO +11% y/y is driven by lower interest expense, not acceleration. FY27 consensus EBITDA ($3.04B) requires ~850bp of margin expansion on ~1% revenue growth — a fragile estimate. SpaceX is the only genuine growth-algorithm changer; unconfirmed and partly priced.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Composite ≈ −0.3σ (Neutral, sell tilt). Worst 12-1 momentum in the cohort (−14%); vol 38.2% and max drawdown −28% (~2x peers). Platform beta/alpha outputs are corrupted (β 0.047, R² 0.0003) — use β ≈ 0.96 and discard the "+13.5% alpha."
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Interest coverage 2.17x (half of AMT), S&P CreditWatch Negative (~7.1x adjusted vs a 6.0x threshold), negative book equity (−$1.6B), dividend ~1.0x covered by true pro-forma FCF. A 20% revenue shock breaches IG coverage and forces a dividend suspension. Note: leverage figures vary 6.3x–7.7x across agents depending on lease/pro-forma treatment — direction, not precision, is what matters.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: Moderate (upper end), path to High. Entire C-suite turns over within ~18 months (new CEO Sep-25; CFO and COO depart Feb/Mar-27), Delaware Chancery litigation over the Elliott cooperation agreement, and **zero open-market insider buying into a 12% drawdown**.
- **ESG**: Average. Genuinely clean E&S post-divestiture (Scope 1&2 carbon-neutral, no EU regulatory perimeter), but governance scores 4/10 — three CEOs in two years, $18.2M pay-for-failure. A discount-rate input, not a thesis driver.
- **Alternative Data**: News sentiment +0.67 with surging volume confirms the turn — but the signal is now *coincident with price*, not leading. Retail attention absent; the move is institutional/event flow.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: No forensic smoking gun (accruals are genuinely conservative); outright short rejected against funded, mechanical deleveraging. The only defensible expression is relative: **short CCI / long AMT**, capturing the EV/EBITDA premium, growth gap and coverage gap.

**Bull Case**
- Funded, mechanical deleveraging: $8.5B proceeds, ~$7B debt paydown, quarterly interest already falling ($249M → $177M); AFFO beat-and-raise cadence intact into 2027.
- SpaceX/Starlink fourth-carrier catalyst: an 80,000-site hunt where CCI's dense top-50-market footprint makes it the highest-beta winner; a signed MLA lifts the growth algorithm from ~4% toward 7–8%.
- Rate optionality: 97% fixed-rate debt and CPI-matching escalators mean any decline in real yields re-rates the multiple with no income-statement damage.

**Bear Case**
- Priced at fair value with negative reported growth (site rental −4.5% y/y), decelerating organic billings, and a premium multiple to a better AMT.
- Fragile equity cushion: ~2.2x interest coverage, ~1.0x dividend coverage, negative book equity, CreditWatch Negative — one adverse event (DISH recovery shortfall, churn above the ~$240M guided, FY27 EBITDA <$2.8B) forces a dividend event.
- Concentration and succession risk: three carriers = 93% of rent (T-Mobile 42%), a bankrupt fourth tenant, and an unproven C-suite mid-transition.

**Key Risks**
1. Real yields break above ~2.90% → mechanical de-rating toward $74–78 (highest-probability, highest-impact).
2. SpaceX bypasses macro towers (direct-to-cell) — near-term catalyst loss *and* long-dated terminal-value impairment in one stroke.
3. Second dividend reset or a debt-funded buyback (short analyst's explicit red line).
4. Disclosure/control failure during CFO transition before the FY2026 10-K is signed.
