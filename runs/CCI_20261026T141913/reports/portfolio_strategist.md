### 2. Consensus Assessment: **HIGH agreement on direction, SPLIT on terminal value**

**Not one of eleven agents recommends buying CCI at $88.** That is the single most important output of this exercise. The dispersion is entirely about *why* and *what price*:

- **High-conviction consensus (Value + Fin Risk + Quant + Sector):** quality assets, full valuation, over-levered balance sheet, negative momentum. Three of these four are the agents with the most rigorous, primary-source-grounded work.
- **Contested:** Growth and Alt Data see a genuine demand inflection (SpaceX 80,000-site talks, AFFO +11% y/y, guidance raise). Value and Quant argue that inflection is already in the price.
- **Resolving the momentum conflict:** Quant's −14.0% 12-1 and Alt Data's +28% in 12 sessions are *not* contradictory — the 12-1 window deliberately excludes the last 21 trading days, and the entire rally sits inside that exclusion. **Read: a violent event-driven reversal inside an intact downtrend.** Unconfirmed reversals of this type are not positionable at the point of maximum extension.
- **Conflict precedence applied:** Fin Risk (HIGH) and Non-Fin Risk (C-suite churn, no insider buying) override Growth's optionality case, because the leverage is the *transmission mechanism* that converts any operational shock into an equity event. Per the framework, balance-sheet risk outranks narrative upside.

### 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis — **Poor on all three axes**

**Sector / sub-sector concentration.** Measured correlations (252d, my calculation): **CCI–AMT 0.886, CCI–SBAC 0.721**, versus CCI–DLR 0.266 and CCI–EQIX 0.210. Tower REITs are effectively *one security*. Any existing AMT or SBAC holding means a CCI position is a duplication, not a diversification — a 2.0% CCI sleeve beside a 3% AMT sleeve is a 5% single-factor bet carrying ~35% volatility, i.e. ~1.7% of total portfolio risk from one US sub-sector with a three-customer rent roll.

**Factor exposure — all four additions are unwanted.**
- *Low-vol / drawdown:* 38.2% vol and −28.2% max drawdown vs peers at −14% to −16%. This is a 2x-vol REIT.
- *Momentum:* bottom quintile (−14.0%), worst in cohort.
- *Quality:* ROIC 7.4% — roughly at cost of capital — with 2.17x interest coverage. (Factor screens on P/E 86.6x, ROE −27.2%, F-score 4/9 are divestiture artifacts and should be discarded, but the *direction* — leverage up, equity down — is genuine.)
- *Duration:* this is the real exposure. A 4.81% dividend yield against a 4.99% 10Y and 2.64% real yield means **the income sleeve can earn more, risk-free, with none of the 6.3x leverage.** There is no yield premium to compensate for equity risk. CCI is a levered long-duration bond proxy trading *through* the risk-free rate.

**Correlation caveat — do not use the regression output.** β = 0.047 with R² = 0.0003 is impossible for a $38.6bn REIT (AMT returns a *negative* beta from the same feed). This is a date-alignment defect. Use β ≈ 0.96 from the company profile for VaR and hedging; do **not** cite the +13.5% "alpha," and do not treat CCI as a market-neutral diversifier.

**The relative-value tell.** Short Analyst's framing is the sharpest portfolio insight in the packet: on 2027E EBITDA, CCI trades ~22.3x versus AMT ~21.75x — paying a premium for slower organic growth (3.8% vs 5.1%), 1.3 turns more leverage, and a third of the interest coverage. **If the mandate wants US tower exposure, own AMT, not CCI.** That is a cleaner expression of the same thesis with less balance-sheet risk.
