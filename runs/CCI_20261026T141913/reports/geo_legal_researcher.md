# Crown Castle Inc. (CCI) — Geopolitical, Regulatory & Legal Risk Assessment
*As of late October 2026 (post-Q3'26 results, 21-Oct-2026)*

## 1. Geographic Risk Map

CCI is now effectively a **single-country, single-line-of-business** company: after closing the sale of its Fiber Solutions business (to Zayo) and Small Cell business (to EQT/Arium) on **1-May-2026 for $8.4–8.5bn**, it is a pure-play US macro-tower REIT with ~40,000+ towers (company profile; investor.crowncastle.com/strategic-review-results; SEC 8-K 1-May-2026).

| Geography | Revenue exposure | Risk level | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | ~100% of site rental revenue (only operating geography) | Low geopolitically / Medium regulatory | No foreign operations, no EM, no currency-repatriation or sanctions exposure. Risk is domestic policy + counterparty, not sovereign |
| International | 0% (no operations; de minimis incidental) | n/a | Divested international assets years ago; the 2026 fiber/small-cell sale removed the last non-tower revenue stream |

**Effective concentration is by *tenant*, not country** (3Q26 annualised site rental revenue, Inside Towers citing company data): **T-Mobile 42%, AT&T 28%, Verizon 23%, other ~7%** — i.e. ~93% from the Big Three US carriers. DISH (formerly a growth tenant) has now been **terminated and removed from guidance** (Q1'26 10-Q summary; StockTitan).

## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Policy Risks

**① DISH Wireless / EchoStar bankruptcy and $3.5bn claim dispute (Highest)**
- CCI-affiliated lessors sued DISH Wireless in D. Colo. (No. 1:25-cv-03756) on **20-Nov-2025** for declaratory relief, **amended 30-Jan-2026** to add breach of contract under the Nov-2020 MLA; DISH denies breach and is running the same defence it used against American Tower (Wireless Estimator; EchoStar 2025 Annual Report; JWTC Q1'26 note).
- CCI has **terminated the DISH agreements**, asserts **>$3.5bn owed**, holds **~$165m of related net assets**, and has excluded DISH from 2026 guidance (Q1'26 10-Q summary). DISH Wireless Chapter 11 cases continue (DBS side emerged 1-Oct-2026); the wireless exit **slipped to December 2026** as tower companies press a combined **~$7.6bn** claim (CCI $3.5bn, AMT $2bn, SBA) — CCI also filed to **disallow an $8.86bn DISH intercompany claim** (17-Jul-2026) (WSJ Pro Bankruptcy; BankruptcyData; Wireless Estimator).
- *Probability of continued litigation/impairment noise: High (~80%). Impact: Medium* — largely reserved/removed from run-rate, but recovery value on the claim is uncertain and delays emerge from Chapter 11.

**② US federal/local wireless-siting policy (two-sided)**
- **Upside:** FCC **WT Docket 25-276** NPRM (Sept-2025, "Build America Agenda") proposes to streamline/preempt state and local siting authority, tighten "concealment element" definitions and shot clocks — directly accretive to tower modification velocity (fcc.gov DOC-414414A1; BBK Law; FCC 25-67).
- **Risk:** ~4,000+ comments filed; NLC/NACo/USCM/NATOA filed joint reply comments opposing preemption (15-Jan-2026; natOA). Adoption would almost certainly trigger **local-government litigation**, and a change in FCC leadership/policy could reverse it. CCI's returns depend on Section 6409/shot-clock enforcement remaining intact.
- *Probability of pro-tower order: ~60%; of ensuing litigation: High. Impact: Low–Medium.*

**③ Carrier capex cycle, tariffs, and direct-to-device substitution**
- **Steel/aluminium/copper tariffs of ~50%** (as of Apr-2026) raise US build costs and carrier capex efficiency, which historically correlates with tower leasing/churn decisions (Tax Credit Advisor; AGC). Cushman & Wakefield estimates ~6% CRE material cost inflation.
- **SpaceX/Starlink:** reported $8bn 800 MHz acquisition and a push for **80,000 US cell sites** is a potential new-tenant catalyst (CCI shares jumped on 22-Oct-2026 reports of lease discussions — Benzinga, Investopedia), but AT&T's Stankey has publicly framed satellites as replacing "less-used macro sites" at the network edge — a long-dated disintermediation risk (Wireless Estimator, 22-Oct-2026).
- *Probability of near-term revenue benefit from SpaceX: Low–Medium; of long-run substitution pressure: Low but rising. Impact: High if realised.*

*(No US–China, Russia-sanctions, export-control or EM-currency exposure of any consequence: 100% domestic revenue base.)*

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights
- **Primary regulator:** FCC (siting/pole attachment/Section 6409), plus ~thousands of state/local zoning authorities; REIT tax status under IRC (IRS) — no pending challenge identified.
- **Litigation:** DISH/EchoStar contract + declaratory action (above) is the dominant matter. Ordinary-course property-tax valuation appeals (e.g. *Crown Castle Towers 06-2 LLC* dockets) are routine and non-material (Pullman & Comley docket note).
- **Divestiture tail risk:** municipal franchise/ROW agreements for the sold fiber and small-cell assets typically require **municipal consent to transfer**; local governments are being advised to **resist blanket transfer approvals** and to demand insurance, indemnity and cost reimbursement — leaving potential residual indemnity/consent disputes for CCI (PublicCEO, May-2026).
- **No DOJ/SEC/FTC investigation or securities class action specific to the 2025–26 period was identified.** Historical securities class actions (2020) and prior derivative matters were resolved (Rosen/Bragar reminders; Cravath bio). *Gap: SEC.gov direct fetches returned HTTP 403, so the FY2025 10-K risk-factor and legal-proceedings notes could not be read in full; the FY2025 10-K was filed 23-Feb-2026 (accession 0001051470-26-000016).*
- **Operating backdrop:** 3Q26 site rental revenue $966m (**-4.5% YoY**), net income $283m, AFFO/share $1.24 (+10.7%), FY26 guidance raised; 2026 organic site-rental billings contribution of **-$105m**, driven mainly by ~$240m of DISH/Sprint non-renewals; net leverage ~6.3x (Q3'26 release summary; Zacks; Seeking Alpha).

## 4. Compliance Red Flags
**None material.** No sanctions, AML, FCPA or export-control exposure given the domestic-only footprint. Post-divestiture, customer-data/privacy (CPNI-type) exposure transferred with the fiber business, materially reducing cyber/privacy perimeter. Watch items: (i) tower physical-security/cyber obligations under DHS/CISA critical-infrastructure guidance; (ii) REIT qualification and 90%-distribution compliance; (iii) indemnification covenants given on the divested fiber/small-cell entities.

## 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**
Geopolitical risk proper is **LOW** (100% US revenue, no sanctions or currency exposure). The Medium rating is driven by *domestic* policy and legal concentration: a bankruptcy-adjacent counterparty dispute worth ~$3.5bn asserted, a customer base where three carriers are 93% of rent, and siting rules that hinge on a contested FCC preemption proceeding. Mitigants: contracted long-term leases with 5–7% escalators, pure-play tower focus post-divestiture, raised 2026 guidance, and a potential new hyperscale tenant (SpaceX) that could offset DISH churn.
