### Investment Memo: CHTR — 11 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (Reduce / Do Not Initiate — explicitly *not* a short) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $115 (−14% from current $134.06); risk-adjusted fair-value range $85–155
**Time Horizon**: 12–18 months; re-entry gate realistically April 2027 (Q1-27 print)

**Investment Thesis**
Charter is a structurally shrinking, hyper-levered cable incumbent whose equity — roughly 14–16% of a ~$127B enterprise value — has become a call option on cable EBITDA stabilising and on the long end of the Treasury curve. The optically distressed multiples (3.3x forward P/E, ~5.5x EV/EBITDA, ~24% FCF yield) are a leverage artifact, not a margin of safety: on an unlevered basis the enterprise trades at ~15x FCF, which is fair, not cheap, for a business with negative revenue growth and broken pricing power. The bull case (capex cliff + $3B of savings driving FCF to $8–9B by 2028) is real and datable — but the first evidence gate is two quarters of improving net adds, and nothing in Q3-26 supports it yet.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Cable broadband is late-cycle and ex-growth; FWA/fibre substitution is accelerating (CHTR lost 233k pro forma internet subs in Q3-26, *widening* YoY). Macro regime is maximally hostile: CPI re-accelerating to 3.8%, Fed dots at 4.00–4.25%, 10Y ~4.9–5.1%, 30Y 5.5%. Every 0.1x of EV/EBITDA ≈ 15% of market cap — CHTR is now a levered bet on the 10Y, not on broadband.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: ~100% domestic, so no FX/sanctions risk — but rated **HIGH** overall: active SDNY securities class action (ACP/subscriber disclosures), May-26 breach class action, Liberty Broadband investor probe, newly disclosed franchise non-compliance allegations, and CPUC-style conditionality as the template for deal approvals.
- **Value**: Moat narrow and narrowing (penetration −2.3pts, ARPU −1.4%). "Cheapness is a leverage illusion." Own DCF: base ~$98–190, bear $50–90, bull $300+. Worst capital-allocation record in large-cap media (~$35B of debt-funded buybacks at $450–650/share).
- **Growth**: Fails every growth gate (Rule of 40 = 7.5). HOLD as an asymmetric option only; buy trigger = two consecutive quarters of improving pro forma net adds + ≥$400M realized savings — expected mid-to-late 2027.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Composite SELL — only value scores positive (trap-prone); momentum −25.8% (worst in peer set), vol 59%, Sharpe −1.19, R² vs SPY 0.001. The "+73.75% revision upgrade" is a data artifact; genuine revisions are negative (Wolfe to Underperform, Wells Fargo PT $101).
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. 4.0–4.8x leverage against an S&P downgrade trigger tightened to 4.25x *with EBITDA falling*; EBIT coverage 2.65x; $477M cash vs ~$109B debt; tangible equity ≈ −$77B. A −20% revenue scenario wipes out the equity. Mitigants: 11.1-year weighted debt life, no maturity wall, F-Score 7/9.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Interim CFO during peak integration and a $6.33B impairment; ~$295M insider selling vs ~$3.8M buying with zero purchases since May; Cox holds 27.9% + the chairmanship; realized vishing breach; dual-merger integration risk.
- **ESG**: Laggard (MSCI BB; Sustainalytics ~33.8 High). ~80% a governance/consumer-protection/cyber story; ~$300M+ cumulative conduct penalties. Model as a 5–8% fair-value haircut; uninvestable for ESG-constrained mandates.
- **Alternative Data**: BEARISH — search demand flat-to-down vs baseline, loss rates widening YoY, Q4 consensus inflated mechanically by Cox consolidation. Constructive reads (FWA search cooling, retail "priced for bankruptcy") speak to a valuation floor, not a demand inflection.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: NO EDGE. Accounting is clean (Beneish −2.65, accruals −7.2%, cash conversion 3.2x) — this is not a fraud short. No maturity wall before 2030, 15–20% short interest, and the bear case is fully consensus. Do not short at $134; tactical short only above ~$220–240.

**Bull Case**
1. **FCF inflection**: capex rolls from $11.7B toward <$8–9B by 2028 while $2B opex + >$1B Cox synergies land → FCF of $8–9B against an $18B market cap; buybacks at a 24%+ yield would compound equity extremely fast.
2. **Rate reversal**: if core disinflation holds and the 10Y returns toward 4.2%, the multiple recovering to ~5.5x roughly *doubles* the equity toward ~$35B before any operating credit.
3. **Optionality already paid for by the market**: 12.5M mobile lines growing 15%+, 59M passings of irreplaceable network, P/B 0.9x, and take-private/consolidation scarcity value.

**Bear Case**
1. **Structural substitution**: pricing power has broken — ARPU is falling *while* losses accelerate; the $6.33B franchise impairment six weeks after closing Cox is management's own admission of overpayment and diminished terminal cash flows.
2. **Leverage meets a hawkish Fed**: ~$109B of debt repricing into a 5.5% 30Y; a derating from 4.8x to 4.0x EV/EBITDA eliminates essentially all equity value.
3. **Unproven self-help + governance vacuum**: Q3 pro forma opex *rose* into a revenue decline; savings are a target, not a result; interim CFO, insider selling, and an active litigation stack compound execution risk.

**Key Risks**: 10Y sustained >5.5% (macro kill-switch); downgrade of senior unsecured to sub-IG (threshold 4.25x — live); a second impairment against the $97.6B goodwill balance; Q4-26 net adds worse than −233k; adverse SDNY MTD ruling or FCC/multi-state AG action on the breach; franchise outcomes in CA/NY; squeeze risk cuts both ways (+13.5% in four sessions into this print).
