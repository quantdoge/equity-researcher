## SE (Sea Limited) — Sector Brief: Emerging-Market E-Commerce & Digital Financial Services
*Classification note: FMP tags SE as "Consumer Cyclical / Specialty Retail," a generic bucket that lumps it with Tesla and McDonald's. The economically relevant sector is **Southeast Asian/EM platform e-commerce + digital financial services**; peers below are the true platform comparables.*

### 1. Sector Overview
Southeast Asia's platform e-commerce reached **$157.6bn GMV in 2025, +22.8% YoY** (Momentum Works), after $128.4bn (+12%) in 2024 — growth has *re-accelerated*, not decayed. The market has consolidated violently into a three-horse race: Shopee (~**53%**), TikTok Shop (~29% incl. Tokopedia) and Lazada (~12–15%) together captured **98.8%** of platform GMV in 2025 versus 84% a year earlier — one of the steepest concentration curves in global retail. Critically, the sector has completed its transition from subsidy-funded land-grab to **monetisation**: take rates are at all-time highs and Shopee's ad revenue grew ~70% YoY, which is why sector operating margins are inflecting. The sector sits in the **late-growth / early-monetisation phase** of its cycle — unit economics are turning positive while penetration is still low, the most favourable phase for platform economics.

### 2. Key Tailwinds
- **Durable regional GMV growth with re-acceleration:** 2025 GMV +22.8%; Thailand +51.8%, Malaysia +47.6%, Vietnam/Philippines/Singapore >20%. Indonesia (~37% of regional GMV, ~$58bn) slowed to ~2.2% but is lapping the Tokopedia rationalisation and Bukalapak marketplace exit — a cyclical trough, not structural decline.
- **Consolidation → pricing power.** With top-3 share at 98.8%, competitive subsidy intensity has fallen. Take-rate expansion plus ad/commission monetisation drove SE's group operating margin from **−11.9% (2022) → +1.7% (2023) → +3.9% (2024) → +8.1% (2025)** on a 44.7% gross margin — a textbook scale-economics inflection.
- **Adjacent profit pools compounding:** Monee's loan book grew to **$9.9bn at Q1'26 (+71% YoY)** with Q2'26 revenue **+58.9%** to $1.4bn; Garena remains a high-margin annuity (Q2'26 adjusted EBITDA at **56.3% of bookings**). E-commerce logistics + payments + credit vertical integration raises barriers and lifetime value per user.

### 3. Key Headwinds
- **TikTok Shop is the real threat, not legacy Lazada.** TikTok Shop's SEA GMV **doubled to $45.6bn in 2025** (~71% of its global $64.3bn); combined with Tokopedia it reached **~65.7% of Shopee's regional GMV**. SEA is now TikTok's most important commerce region outside China — a well-capitalised, content-native competitor with different economics.
- **Consumer-credit cycle risk.** A loan book compounding ~70%+ in emerging markets into a decelerating macro (Indonesia ~2% e-commerce growth, soft consumption) is the classic setup for rising provisions/NPLs; SeaMoney provisions are the single largest swing factor in the earnings model, and funding costs are USD/rates-sensitive.
- **Regulatory, tariff and FX friction.** Indonesia's Permendag 31/2023 (social-commerce ban, $100 import floor), Brazil's cross-border import duties on Shopee's fastest-growing market, and shifting Vietnamese/Thai seller-tax rules all raise compliance cost and cap cross-border take rate. Continued Lazada strength in Singapore and Temu/PDD's push into EM add price competition.

### 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG**
- **#1 in all six core SEA markets**, ~53% regional share (up from 52%), $127bn global GMV (+27%), 13.9bn orders — the only at-scale platform in the region with a *rising* share.
- **Only large peer combining 30%+ growth with margin expansion** — the scarce combination in this sector; Q1'26 GMV +30.2% to $37.3bn with FY26 guidance of ~25%.
- **Multi-engine model** (Garena cash generation → Shopee reinvestment → Monee credit attach) creates a funding flywheel pure-play Lazada/TikTok cannot replicate without outside capital.
- **Caveats that keep it from "dominant":** Indonesia — the largest single market — is where growth is slowest and TikTok/Tokopedia is strongest; Monee's credit book is unproven through a downturn; and margin still lags AMZN/PDD/MELI by 300–1,300bp.

### 6. Sector Rating: **ATTRACTIVE**
Top-3 concentration of 98.8%, GMV growth in the low-20s%, and a demonstrable take-rate/monetisation inflection make this a rare EM internet sector where **both the top line and the margin are expanding simultaneously**. The discount to Attractive-as-a-sector is competitive: TikTok Shop's 100% GMV growth means share gains are no longer free, and consumer-credit exposure adds a macro beta the sector did not carry three years ago. Within it, SE is the best-positioned incumbent — 3x peer-median growth, median gross margin, and a credible 300–500bp operating-margin convergence path.
