**Investment Thesis**

Sea is the dominant digital platform of Southeast Asia (Shopee ~53% regional share and *rising*, #1 in all six core markets) with revenue **accelerating** — +36.4% FY25 → +46.6% Q1'26 → +48.0% Q2'26 — while the stock has halved on a guidance sentence, not a fundamental event. At $88.47 the market is pricing 17.6x EV/EBITDA, 1.7x forward sales and ~15x restated FCF for a business growing 3x its peer median with $3.0–9.5B net cash and a demonstrated ability to swing FCF by $6.5B in three years. The de-rating is macro- and margin-narrative-driven (Fed hiking, 10Y at ~5%, WTI +46%, deliberate reinvestment), creating a 3:1 upside/downside asymmetry — provided Monee's unseasoned $11.1B consumer-credit book (88% of book equity, provisions +71.5% YoY) seasons normally. That provision line, not e-commerce demand, is the risk being underwritten.

**Bull Case**

2. **Latent margin engine:** ad-led take-rate expansion (ads +70%) toward a 2–3% EBITDA/GMV target = ~$3B+ of Shopee EBITDA not in the price; management has already proven it can flip the profitability switch (2022→2025).

**Bear Case**
1. **Monee credit cycle:** $11.1B book (+63% YoY) = 88% of book equity, provisions +71.5% outpacing revenue +69%, ~1% NPL90+ flattered by vintage math, in four EM jurisdictions tightening consumer-credit rules — into a hostile EM rate/FX backdrop. This is seven of eleven agents' independently identified #1 risk.
2. **Structural margin compression:** four quarters of negative YoY operating leverage despite take-rate hikes; TikTok Shop ($45.6B GMV, doubling) attacks the highest-monetisation markets while Indonesia mandates fee cuts — pricing power is weaker than the moat narrative.
3. **Broken tape + governance overhang:** negative momentum, lower highs, ±19% event gaps, insider selling into weakness with zero buys, 5.5–7%/yr dilution, and a 57.7% founder voting bloc that caps the terminal multiple.
