### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Supporting quality:** Google Trends series are high-frequency, direct-to-consumer, and geographically matched to Sea's actual revenue base (ID + BR). Earnings surprise history is hard data across 7 quarters.

**Material limitations:**
- **No app-download, MAU, or GMV data** — the single biggest gap. Shopee is app-first, and mobile app rankings would be the highest-fidelity demand signal. Google Trends captures brand interest, not transacting users.
- **Web traffic feed returned nothing quantitative** for shopee.com.
- **Patent feed failed outright**; job-postings data is qualitative web-search proxy only.
- Google Trends is **campaign-sensitive** — 10.10 and double-digit sale days spike brand search without proportional GMV; the +19.6% Q4 read rests on only two weekly observations and should not be extrapolated.
- **Free Fire search is an imperfect bookings proxy** — Garena monetisation depends on in-game event cadence and top-up mechanics, not search interest. The −31% reading is a warning, not a forecast.
- News sentiment is contaminated by unrelated Equinor articles; social sentiment is effectively unavailable.
- Garena/SeaMoney disclose little segment granularity pre-earnings, so mix shift is inferred rather than observed.

**Bottom line:** Alt data supports **revenue upside risk into the Nov 10 print** and argues the selloff is margin-narrative-driven rather than demand-driven — but the gaming decay and Brazil softness are real, and without app-level data I cannot underwrite the demand signal beyond medium conviction.
