## Genmab A/S (GMAB) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price ~$44.8 | Market cap ~$28.1B | EV ~$31.8B | As of late October 2026*

### 1. Growth Story
Genmab is transforming from a high-margin DARZALEX royalty vehicle into a proprietary commercial oncology company. The October 2026 Rina-S Phase 3 win — PFS hazard ratio of 0.29 and OS hazard ratio of 0.43 in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer — materially de-risks the post-2029 DARZALEX cliff and validates Genmab’s ADC platform. With EPKINLY inflecting, petosemtamab’s pivotal head-and-neck readout imminent, and a capital-light royalty base still growing near term, Genmab offers a credible path from ~$4.4B of 2026 revenue to $8B+ by the early 2030s. The market is only beginning to price that transition.
