## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (agreement on facts: HIGH · agreement on price: LOW)

This is an unusually clean split, and the split is *informative rather than confusing* — because the disagreement is almost entirely about **what is already in the price**, not about the business.

**What all eleven agents agree on (this is the durable core):**
- ~64–70% of revenue is one royalty (DARZALEX) that management itself guides to "decline materially in 2029."
- The balance sheet inverted in 12 months: **net cash ~$1.2B → net debt $3.7B**, tangible equity **negative**.
- Interest coverage collapsed to ~3x run-rate; ~$445M/yr at ~8.2% is now a permanent claim on the cash flow the Merus deal was meant to replace.
- ROIC halved 22.5% → 11.1% and the Piotroski F-Score is 4/9.
- The clinical news (Rina-S, petosemtamab) is real and material, not promotional.

**Where they split:** Growth/Alt-Data/Sector read those catalysts as the start of a new franchise and price it at FY28E 16.6x. Value/Short/Fin-Risk read the same catalysts as optionality that is now fully paid for, sitting on a levered, time-limited annuity.

**Conflict resolution.** My framework says risk flags take precedence over optimism — but only *genuine* risk flags. Here the short analyst explicitly disclaims fraud ("there is no fraud case against GMAB"; clean accruals −0.017, cash conversion 123%). So this is **not** a value-vs-fraud collision that forces a veto. It is a **quality-of-moat and price-of-optionality** collision, which is a *sizing* problem, not an exit signal. Two flags do override into the sizing decision:

1. **Fin Risk `HIGH`** — sub-IG, ~3x coverage, negative tangible book. That removes the "fortress balance sheet" argument the bull case implicitly leans on and caps position size regardless of conviction.
2. **Geo/Legal `MEDIUM` with a binary in it** — an injunction against the Rina-S ADC platform would impair precisely the asset the re-rating is built on. Binary legal risk gets a haircut, not a pass.

**Net: the bull case is credible; the entry price is not.** Conviction aggregates to **medium-low at $44.86** and **medium at ≤$38**.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration.** GMAB is *not* a substitute for large-cap pharma defensiveness. If the book is already overweight healthcare via ABBV/AMGN/REGN/VRTX, GMAB adds a **royalty-plus-oncology-ADC** sleeve — differentiated in *mechanism* (partner-controlled royalty annuity + two binary Phase 3 assets) but not in *factor*. Count it against the healthcare band, and note a book already holding ABBV carries **dual Genmab exposure** — AbbVie is simultaneously Genmab's epcoritamab partner and its trade-secret litigation adversary. That is a correlated tail, not diversification.

**Correlation — the single best feature of this name.** My matrix confirms the quant analyst: **GMAB vs SPY 0.28**, vs large pharma **0.24–0.36** (AMGN 0.24, ABBV 0.24, REGN 0.30, VRTX 0.35, ARGX 0.36) — materially *below* the 0.47–0.56 that those peers run against each other. GMAB is a genuine diversifier inside healthcare. It is also uncorrelated with the book's rate-sensitive core, which is why a small position earns its keep.

**Factor exposure — this is the caution.** Measured beta (0.79) is the **highest in the comp set** and badly understates risk: with R² = 0.08, ~92% of variance is idiosyncratic, so a beta-based risk budget will systematically under-size this name. In factor terms GMAB adds:
- **Momentum** (12-1 +23.1%, best of 7) — a *doubling* if the book already runs a momentum tilt. It is the most crowded factor in the name right now.
- **Negative quality/low-vol** (ROIC 11.1%, F-Score 4/9, vol 36.4%, max DD −30.7%, both worst in set).
- **Duration** — 2030+ pipeline NPV. With the 10Y at 4.99% and the Fed hiking, this is the longest-duration asset in the book and will de-rate on *multiple*, not fundamentals, in a rate shock.
- **Currency, mildly helpful** — USD royalties against a DKK/EUR cost base; USD/DKK 6.6554 vs 6.3436 is a translation tailwind for DKK-reported earnings, and makes the ADR a partial natural USD hedge.

**Portfolio-level stress.** Macro's bear scenario (WTI >$110, 10Y to 5.75%) took GMAB from ~$34 to ~$24 in H1-2026 **while the pipeline improved**. That 20–30% duration de-rate is the realistic portfolio risk, and it is *not* captured by the 0.79 beta. A 1.5% cap means a full de-rate costs ~45bp of portfolio — tolerable. A 4% position would cost ~120bp on a non-fundamental event, which is not.
