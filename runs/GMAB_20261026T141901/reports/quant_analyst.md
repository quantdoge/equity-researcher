2. **Alpha not statistically significant.** R² = 0.080, t(α) ≈ 1.1. One year of data, no multi-year confirmation. Do not size on the 39.5% alpha.
3. **Quality decay is measurable, not narrative.** ROIC halved (22.5% → 11.1%) over three years; gross profit/assets lowest in the comp set; F-Score 4/9. If the FY26E EPS of $1.18 (below trailing ~$1.54) is right, earnings are *contracting* before re-accelerating.

5. **Thin, long-dated estimate coverage.** Revision signal rests on 5 analysts forecasting FY2030. Near-term breadth (FY26E: 7 analysts) is too thin for robust revision inference.
6. **Currency/data-integrity caveat.** Genmab reports in DKK; the trailing series mixes DKK (FY24 net income 7,844m) with apparent USD figures (FY25 963.8m). Cross-period ROIC/accrual trends are directionally indicative only — **not verified**.
7. **Factor crowding / low liquidity unverified.** Only 23–36% correlation to peers means low crowding risk *within* this set, but ADR liquidity and volume data were not obtained — treat as a gap.
