## Geopolitical & Legal Risk Assessment — Burlington Stores, Inc. (BURL)

**As of:** late November 2026 (Q3 FY2026 results due Nov 24, 2026) | Market cap ≈ $14.8B | ~83,300 employees

**Threshold finding:** BURL is a **single-country revenue story with a multi-country cost story**. It earns ~100% of revenue in the United States (45 states + Puerto Rico, per company profile/10-K description), so classic geopolitical revenue risks — expropriation, currency controls, sanctions on operations — are near-zero. The entire geopolitical risk surface is transmitted through **imported merchandise sourcing and US trade policy**, which in 2026 is the most volatile variable the company faces.
