**Investment Thesis**
Burlington is a well-run, honestly-reported #3 off-price retailer with the group's largest self-help runway (7.3% operating margin vs. 11.9% at TJX/ROST; 1,242 stores vs. a 2,000 target), trading at 19.8x forward earnings after a 37% drawdown. Probability-weighted intrinsic value (~$240–255) sits essentially at the price: there is no pessimism left to harvest, and no margin of safety. We wait for either a comp inflection or a better entry.
