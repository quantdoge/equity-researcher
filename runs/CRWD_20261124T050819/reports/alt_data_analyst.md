**Supporting confidence:** Earnings surprise series and guidance are primary-source, exact, and directly from the Nov 23 release. ARR/net-new-ARR/pipeline are the highest-quality leading indicators available for this business, and they trend unambiguously.

**Limiting factors (material):**

2. **Social sentiment — no quantitative data**, aggregator links only.
3. **Web traffic — qualitative comparison only**, no MoM visit series; low relevance for enterprise B2B anyway.

6. **News sentiment (+0.80) is a keyword artifact**, demonstrably mislabeling negative headlines as positive. I would not trade on it.
7. **Signal entanglement:** earnings and the Zscaler announcement landed on the same day; no clean decomposition of market reaction is possible.
8. **Peer context unusable** — sector pull returned megacap semis/software (AAPL, NVDA, MSFT), not security comparables.

**Bottom line:** Alt data supports the view that CRWD's *business* is accelerating faster than consensus models — a genuinely bullish operating signal with a five-quarter track record of consensus underestimation. But every data source here is silent on the one variable now driving the stock: whether a $43.9B all-stock Zscaler integration preserves the growth-and-margin profile that earned the multiple. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one — and in this specific case, the deal risk is outside its field of view.
