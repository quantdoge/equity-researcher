**Investment Thesis**

CrowdStrike is the best-operating franchise in cybersecurity — five consecutive quarters of ARR acceleration to $6.26B (+27%), record net-new ARR (+58%), FCF margin at 30.9% and GAAP operating breakeven reached — but it is priced for perfection at ~35x sales / 118x FCF / ~437x SBC-adjusted FCF, in a macro regime (real yields +57bp, Fed hiking, VIX 36) that is actively repricing exactly this kind of duration asset. The $43.9B all-stock Zscaler acquisition converts a clean organic compounder into a multi-year integration and antitrust story with ~18% dilution. The result: an excellent business whose 12-month risk/reward at $209 is no better than balanced — own less of it, add only on deep drawdowns or deal de-risking.

**Bull Case**
- Only scale security vendor accelerating: ARR +27% (5th straight quarter up), record net-new ARR +58%, 85.7% beat rate with expanding surprises, record Q4 pipeline — consensus still under-forecasting.
- Platform + AI-agent optionality: 6+ module adoption at 52%, Guardian $100M ARR in year one, Nvidia/OpenShell and Anthropic distribution; ZS creates a >$10B ARR platform bought at a 2.7x multiple discount with $1B/$500M synergy targets and claimed immediate FCF-per-share accretion.
- Bulletproof balance sheet: $4.4B+ net cash, 30.9% FCF margin expanding, GAAP breakeven reached, FCF positive even in a −20% revenue shock; all-stock deal structure avoids the debt market entirely.

**Bear Case**
- Zero margin of safety: 35–37x sales, 118x FCF, ~437x SBC-adjusted FCF; every independent intrinsic anchor (DCF $65–155; FY31 consensus capitalised at 25x ≈ 36–40% of today's cap) sits far below the price.
- Hostile duration regime: real yields +57bp with anchored breakevens, Fed hiking into 3.8% CPI, credit +113bp, USD +4.9%; at 1.43 beta, a 20% index drawdown implies ~−29% here. Bear path: multiple compresses to 20–22x → $120–140.
- Deal + governance overhang: ~18% dilution for a −4% GAAP op-margin asset, 20–25% antitrust delay/remedy risk, all-stock reflexivity (a falling CRWD cheapens its own currency), an open SEC rev-rec probe, and zero insider conviction buying into the drawdown.
