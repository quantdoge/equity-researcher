**FN at 0.78 with Coherent is not a diversifier of an AI-optics book — it is a higher-beta expression of the same trade.** Quant's crowding flag is confirmed by my own measurement. *Rule: aggregate AI-optical-hardware exposure (FN + COHR + LITE + CLS + AAOI + merchant-optics names) should be capped at 8% of portfolio, with FN inside that cap at ≤2.5%.*

**Factor exposure — this is a deliberate anti-factor position.** FN scores 2.0 on momentum and 1.5 on low-vol; it has −57% one-year alpha and a −0.17 Sharpe. Adding it tilts the portfolio *away* from momentum and low-vol and *toward* growth and earnings-revision. That is acceptable only if the book is not already growth/revision-heavy. **If the portfolio is momentum- or quality-tilted, FN is a genuine factor diversifier; if the book is already an AI-growth book, this doubles a bet you already own.**

**Market-beta hedging will not work.** R² vs SPY is 0.47 (Quant measures 0.22 — either way, low). Roughly half or more of FN's variance is idiosyncratic. **Index puts or SPY shorts provide poor protection; risk must be managed at the position level.**

**Liquidity:** ~0.8–1.5M shares/day (~$280–500M notional), spiking to 2.4–7M on earnings. At 1–2.5% of a normal-sized book there is no exit constraint. Not an issue.

### 6. Risk Management Rules

**Hard stop-loss: $289 (−15.4% from $341.64).** Deliberately set below the 23-Oct intraday low of $305.63 so that a routine retest does not stop you out. Given 83% vol (~5.2% daily σ), this is ~3 daily sigmas — tight enough to cap damage, loose enough to survive noise. *Practical adjustment: if $305 is breached on heavy volume, pre-emptively reduce by half rather than waiting for $289, because this name gaps.*

**Thesis invalidation — exit on any two of the following:**
1. **GAAP EPS declines y/y for two consecutive quarters while revenue grows >20%** — the tax/cash-decoupling thesis becoming permanent rather than transitional. (This is the single most important trigger. Q1 FY27 was already +7.9% GAAP vs +50.7% revenue.)
2. **Gross margin below 11.5%** — breaks the structural floor and confirms negative pricing power.
3. **Any top-4 customer disclosed below 10%** — implies a lost program; a Cisco or NVIDIA program loss is a 16–20% revenue event (Fin Risk's −20% scenario: operating income −31%, net income −47%).
4. **Capex >10% of revenue for four consecutive quarters while FCF stays negative and total borrowings exceed ~$300M** — the "borrowing to buy back stock while burning cash" pattern escalating.
5. **Inventory days above 100 without matching revenue acceleration** — the obsolescence/impairment setup (71 → 91 → ~95 already).
6. **1.6T ramp slippage, or a disclosed CPO design win at a hyperscaler that excludes merchant pluggables** — the Short Analyst's terminal-value argument arriving early.
7. **Reinstatement of ≥19% duties on Thai-origin goods under a durable statutory basis without customer pass-through** (Geo Legal's "move to Very High").

**Trim triggers:**
- **$430–450 (top of Value's range): trim one-third.** This is also the level at which the stock would be ~25x FY27 GAAP — full value.
- **$500+: trim one-half.** The stock traded $515 intraday on 9-Oct, so this is reachable, not theoretical.
- **Re-rating discipline:** if FY27 consensus EPS stays at ~$19.71 but the multiple exceeds 25x non-GAAP, the position has outrun its own thesis.

**Review cadence:** re-underwrite after every 10-Q, specifically on (a) the GAAP/non-GAAP spread, (b) OCF/NI, (c) inventory days, (d) customer concentration disclosure. Geo Legal must verify the FY26 10-K Item 3 litigation footnote (blocked at 403) before Tranche 2 — the Casix RMB 400M (~$56M) Chinese appellate claim surfaced by ESG is currently unaccrued and unverified.

### 7. Final Portfolio Action: **INITIATE (1.0%)** — not Pass, not Add

**Rationale in one paragraph.** Fabrinet is a genuinely excellent operator (10.0% operating margin — best in EMS, on a 12% gross margin, with a pristine net-cash balance sheet) riding a demand environment that is supply-constrained through 2027. It is also, at $341.64, priced slightly *above* base-case intrinsic value, carrying 57% customer concentration, 83% volatility, a 2.95 beta, a 0.78 correlation to Coherent, a permanently higher tax rate under Pillar Two, a first-ever debt drawdown alongside negative free cash flow, $35M of one-way insider selling with zero buys, newly created CEO/Chair duality, and all high-volume manufacturing on a flood-exposed Thai estate. The bull case is real but already partly in the price; the bear case is real but not existential. **A business this good, with a balance sheet this safe and a tape this unstable, earns a starter position and a pre-committed plan — not a conviction weight.** The expected value at this price does not justify 3–5%; the survivability and demand visibility do not justify a pass. **1.0% now, 2.5% ceiling, most of the capital reserved for $255–290 or for proof that the cash conversion problem is a capex cycle rather than a permanent feature of the model.**

**What would move me to 3–5% (Overweight):** price ≤$255 *with* revenue guidance intact — that is ~12–13x forward non-GAAP for a 40%+ grower with net cash, and at that point the asymmetry inverts decisively in the buyer's favour.

**What would move me to Exit:** triggers 1 + 2 in Section 6 firing together, i.e., GAAP earnings going backwards while gross margin breaks 11.5% — that would confirm the price-taker diagnosis at exactly the wrong point in the cycle.

**What would move me to Pass entirely (from the outset):** if the existing portfolio already carries ≥5% in AI-optical names (COHR/LITE/CLS/AAOI), FN's 0.78/0.62 correlations mean this position adds concentration, not return. In that case pass and express the view through the existing COHR or CLS holding instead.
