# Macroeconomic Assessment — Fabrinet (FN)

**As of 5 November 2026** | Cayman-domiciled; manufacturing concentrated in Thailand | Optical/electro-mechanical EMS for AI datacenter, telecom, industrial laser, auto & medical OEMs | Market cap $12.24B, price $341.64, beta 1.35

---

## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional — "hawkish reflation."** This is not the 2024–25 easing cycle. The Fed **cut to 3.63% by mid-2026 and has since reversed, hiking +25bp in September and +25bp in October to a 3.88% effective rate (Oct-2026)**. Headline CPI has re-accelerated to **3.72% y/y (Sep-2026)**, with the Jul–Sep quarter running ~4.6% annualised. Growth is *not* slowing — Q3-2026 real GDP grew **~3.4% SAAR** — and unemployment has drifted *down* to 4.2% from 4.5% in Nov-2025.

The result is a **bear steepener**: the 10Y has backed up ~87bp from 4.44% (30-Jun) to a 5.31% peak (5-Oct), now ~5.0%, with a 30Y at 5.47%. Term premium, not growth fear, is driving the long end. Credit is calm (Baa–10Y spread 1.54%, tighter than August's 1.69%), so this is a **discount-rate and policy shock, not a credit event**. Equities are risk-on where earnings are visible and risk-off in duration.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

Fabrinet's economics: **USD revenue, THB/RMB cost base, ~12–13% gross margin, no material debt, one dominant end-market (AI/datacenter optics)**. That combination makes it unusually levered to *demand and FX* and unusually insulated from *funding costs*.

| Macro variable | Impact | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **AI/datacenter capex** (the dominant driver) | **Strongly positive** | Q1 FY27 (Sep-26) revenue $1.47B, **+51% y/y**, EPS $4.34; Q2 guide $1.60–1.65B. Moody's sees hyperscaler capex of ~$785B in 2026 → ~$1T in 2027; JPM/Goldman ~$1T+. 800G/1.6T transceiver demand is the transmission channel. |
| **Higher long rates (10Y ~5%)** | **Negative (multiple only)** | ~26x trailing / ~17x FY27E run-rate. Every 50bp on the discount rate compresses a high-beta growth multiple ~8–12%. But **zero refinancing risk** — no material debt; the P&L is not rate-sensitive, only the multiple. |
| **Fed re-hiking / hawkish pivot** | **Negative, indirect** | Raises the hurdle rate for debt-funded AI buildouts (Goldman: ~⅓ of 2027 capex debt-funded). If credit markets balk at AI issuance, the 51% growth rate is the first thing to gap down. |
| **USD strength / THB weakness (33.76)** | **Positive** | Baht −3.9% vs USD since June is a direct **margin tailwind** on THB-denominated labour and overhead at a 12% gross margin — a 3–4% FX move is worth 100–150bp of gross margin if unhedged. Offsets the 19% Thai tariff drag. |

| **Credit conditions (spreads 1.54%, tight)** | **Positive** | Benign for customers' ability to fund equipment orders; no Fabrinet leverage to reprice. |

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL**

Justified asymmetry: **the earnings engine (AI capex, +51% growth, FX tailwind) is powerfully positive; the policy backdrop (re-hiking Fed, 5% long bond, hot CPI, tariff litigation) is a genuine headwind — but a headwind to the *valuation multiple*, not to cash flows.** A debt-free, cash-generative, 50%-growing compounder is the *least* rate-fragile way to own AI infrastructure. I would not call this Favourable, because the discount-rate regime caps multiple expansion and a hawkish Fed is precisely the trigger that could crack capex sentiment; and not Unfavourable, because demand, FX, and credit are all working in Fabrinet's favour.

## 5. The Scenario That Would Materially Surprise (Bear)

**Consensus assumes the Fed's hikes are a brief hawkish pause and the 10Y settles near 5%.** The under-priced scenario is a **term-premium/fiscal shock rather than a growth shock**: CPI holds above 3.5% into Q1-2027 on tariff pass-through and tight labour, the Fed delivers two more hikes to ~4.4%, and the 30Y pushes to ~6% on deficit-supply concerns. In that world, **growth never breaks, but the discount rate does.**

**Transmission to FN:** a 10Y at 5.6–5.8% would compress high-beta AI hardware multiples 25–30% even with estimates intact — FN at 17x FY27E re-rating to ~13x implies roughly **$250–265 (−23% to −27%)** with no change to a single revenue dollar. The tell-tale signal to watch would be *hyperscaler debt issuance spreads widening while capex guidance stays intact* — that combination precedes multiple compression by a quarter or two.

**Bull-ish offsetting surprise:** a resolution of the Section 232 derivative/component dispute that exempts Thailand-assembled optical modules, combined with the baht holding at 33–34, would add an estimated 150–250bp to gross margin and remove the single largest policy overhang on the name.
