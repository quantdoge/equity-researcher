## Fabrinet (FN) — Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $341.64 | Mkt Cap $12.24B | EV $11.51B | Net cash ~$728M | FY26 rev $4.64B**

---

### 1. Growth Story

Fabrinet is the dominant outsourced optical packaging and precision manufacturing partner for the AI datacenter interconnect build-out — the "picks and shovels" behind 800G/1.6T transceivers, and increasingly behind co-packaged/near-packaged optics. It is not a component designer; it is the process-technology moat that hyperscaler optics supply chains cannot easily replicate at scale, with deep, sticky programs at Nvidia, Cisco/Acacia and the leading merchant module vendors. FY27 revenue growth has *re-accelerated* to ~42% (Q1 actual +51%), yet the stock has been cut in half on a sector-wide optical de-rating plus a structural knock: at ~12% gross margin, Fabrinet captures AI optics **volume but not pricing power**.

### 3. TAM and Penetration

The AI datacenter optical interconnect market is the most violent growth pocket in hardware. Optical transceiver TAM is on a path from roughly $20B+ toward $40–50B+ by 2030 as cluster scale moves 400G → 800G → 1.6T and optics intensity per GPU rises each generation. Fabrinet's *serviceable* market is the outsourced portion of that — a minority of the value but a majority of the manufacturing complexity and yield risk, which is exactly what OEMs outsource.

Penetration today is early: 1.6T is just beginning to ship in volume, and **co-packaged optics (CPO/NPO)** — where optical packaging capability becomes the gating technology — is a second, larger wave not in current numbers. A $12B market cap against a $40B+ SAM with Fabrinet as the default precision-optics partner is a favorable setup. FY27 consensus of $6.59B is only ~11% of the long-term TAM.

Differentiation: proprietary process engineering, ultra-high-yield optical alignment at scale, Thailand manufacturing concentration (cost + tariff posture), and 20+ year relationships. Competitors (Foxconn/FIT, Jabil, Sanmina, Celestica, Chinese ODMs like Innolight/Eoptolink in-house) are capable but not equivalently qualified on the hardest optical assemblies.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q2 FY27 results — ~February 1, 2027.** Guidance: revenue $1.60–1.65B (+41–46% YoY) and non-GAAP EPS **$4.77–4.92 vs $4.43 consensus** — a guide ~10% above the Street. Highest-probability re-rating event.
- **1.6T transceiver volume ramp through CY2027** — the single biggest revenue lever; any pull-forward commentary moves the stock double digits.
- **Capacity conversion:** $158.7M of Q1 capex (vs $45.3M a year ago) is building the ramp. Watch capex-to-revenue conversion and any capex *peak* signal — a capex rollover with revenue intact is the bull case for FCF.
- **CPO/NPO program awards** — a new architecture win would extend the growth runway past 1.6T and lift the multiple.
- **Buyback execution:** authorization raised to **$637.3M** (~5.2% of market cap) — meaningful support at a 17x forward multiple.
- **Analyst PT resets** post-selloff (targets are stale: Northland $800, Barclays $739 vs B.Riley's $364 on Nov 3).

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

The disconnect is unusually wide. Fabrinet is growing revenue **42–51%** and EPS ~48%, with seven straight beats, an FY30 estimate revised up 19% in three months, and a guide 10% above consensus — trading at **17.3x FY27 non-GAAP EPS and 14.2x FY28, or 1.75x FY27 EV/sales**. PEG is roughly **0.4x**. Peers Celestica (32.4x P/E, 22.9x EV/EBITDA) and Jabil (26.5x P/E, 14.3x EV/EBITDA) trade at richer multiples on *slower* growth. The stock has de-rated 54% while estimates went *up* — the definition of multiple compression, not deterioration.

I stop short of Strong Buy for one reason: **the 12% gross margin is a structural verdict on bargaining power**, and the widening GAAP/non-GAAP gap (Q1 GAAP EPS +7.5% vs non-GAAP +48.6%, a ~$54M delta) needs explaining before I'd underwrite a premium multiple. This is a cyclical, concentrated, capital-hungry manufacturer riding an extraordinary wave — buy it as a high-beta AI-optics position, sized accordingly, not as a core compounder.

### 7. Key Risks

- **Customer concentration (the #1 risk).** A handful of programs (Nvidia, Cisco/Acacia, Ciena) likely exceed 10% each. A single program shift or in-sourcing decision is a step-function revenue hit.
- **Pricing power / margin structure.** Gross margin has *declined* every year since FY23 (12.71% → 11.99%). If the mix shifts toward lower-margin system-level/rack assembly for Nvidia, revenue grows while earnings quality erodes — the market is already paying for this fear.
- **Capex and FCF drought.** FY26 FCF was **$3M** on $254M capex; Q1 FY27 OCF was just $21.3M against $158.7M capex. Sustained overbuild would leave the equity dependent on continued revenue acceleration to justify the spend.
- **Cyclicality / AI capex air-pocket.** FY28 consensus decelerates to +19.5%. If hyperscaler optics orders pause, the "17x is cheap" argument becomes "17x on peak earnings."
- **Competition and in-sourcing.** Chinese ODMs are vertically integrating optical assembly; Foxconn and Celestica are scaling AI manufacturing. Fabrinet's moat is real but not unbreachable.
- **Concentration of manufacturing in Thailand** — FX (baht) and single-geography operational/tariff exposure.
