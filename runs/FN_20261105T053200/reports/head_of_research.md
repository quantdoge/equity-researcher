### Investment Memo: FN (Fabrinet) — 5 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $315 (~8% downside from current $341.64); fair-value range $250–420
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Fabrinet is the best-run optical EMS in the world — top-of-class 10% operating margin on a 12% gross margin, net cash, riding a supply-constrained AI-optics upcycle with revenue +51% and seven straight beats. But the stock at $341.64 sits *above* base-case intrinsic value (~$300), not below it: the "cheap at 17x" case rests on non-GAAP EPS, while GAAP earnings grew just 7.9% last quarter as the Pillar Two tax reset and collapsing cash conversion quietly re-based the P&L. This is a fair price for an excellent but narrow-moat, price-taking manufacturer at peak cycle momentum — own it small, buy it lower, and let the cash-flow statement prove itself before sizing up.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Demand genuinely supply-constrained through 2027 (800G production 40–60% below demand); FN grows ~1.6x peer median with best-in-group margins. Sector is mid-to-late expansion — peak momentum, rising 2027–28 digestion risk. Macro is neutral: the earnings engine is strong but a 5%+ 10Y caps the multiple; Macro's rate-shock case alone implies $250–265 with no estimate change.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH** — 77% of revenue billed to North America, produced almost entirely in Thailand, into a tariff regime whose legal basis was struck down in Feb-2026 and is being rebuilt; Pillar Two has permanently ended the Thai tax holiday; US–China export controls gate the core demand pool. No active litigation found, but the 10-K Item 3 footnote is unverified (403-blocked).
- **Value**: Base-case DCF ~$300 (bear $200–230, bull $420–450). No margin of safety at $341.64. Buy zone ≤$240–255. Narrow moat: 19% ROIC but zero pricing power, four straight years of gross-margin decline.
- **Growth**: Revenue +42% FY27E, +51% latest quarter, 7/7 beats, FY30 EPS revised +19% in 90 days, Q2 guide ~10% above consensus. At 17.3x FY27 *non-GAAP* EPS the stock is optically cheap (PEG ~0.4) — the strongest bull argument, and entirely dependent on which EPS you believe.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Composite 4.3/10 (Neutral). Value 5.0, Quality 6.5, Revisions 6.5 vs Momentum 2.0, Low-Vol 1.5; last on Sharpe in the peer set. De-rating, not deterioration — but momentum must inflect (~$410 reclaim) before fundamentals get paid.
- **Financial Risk**: Solvency **LOW** (net cash ~$678M, Z-score 4.12, survives a −50% revenue shock net cash). The weak link is cash conversion: FY26 FCF $3M on $473M net income; five-quarter cumulative FCF ≈ −$134M; inventory days 71→95.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **MODERATE** — ~$35.2M of one-way insider selling vs $0 buys (CEO, CFO, COO, four directors, sold into the $600–700 peak); CEO/Chair duality created Oct-2025; top-4 customers ~57% of revenue on cancellable programs.
- **ESG**: Average conduct, **negative** implication — the 12.5% Thai tariff is a live margin item against a 12% gross margin with a 15-year relocation lock-in; 2011 flood precedent (>$100M, ~20% of revenue) and the new Navanakorn campus sits on historically flooded ground; Casix RMB 400M (~$56M) claim unaccrued.
- **Alternative Data**: **BULLISH** on fundamentals — Building 10 adds ~$3.5B capacity, ops hiring, beat cadence trending up, news sentiment +0.867; retail attention fully washed out (Trends 100→10). Alt data predicts another beat ~1 Feb; it does not predict the stock will be rewarded for it.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: Moderate-conviction **SHORT** — not fraud (DSRI clean, receivables fine) but earnings quality is LOW: GAAP EPS +7.9% on +50.7% revenue, effective tax 5.5%→20%, $54M/quarter of non-GAAP add-backs, negative FCF funded by a first-ever debt draw while buying back stock. Consensus FY28–30 requires a gross margin (~15.5–16%) FN has never earned.

**Bull Case**
- AI-optical demand is supply-constrained through 2027 with two speed generations (1.6T, 3.2T) ahead; FN is the qualified merchant capacity of choice — Q2 guide is ~10% above the Street.
- Pristine net-cash balance sheet truncates the left tail: even a −50% revenue scenario leaves the company profitable and cash-rich, releasing working capital.
- Positioning is clean: −54% from the peak on rising estimates, retail washed out, $637M buyback (~5% of cap) underneath; if GAAP/non-GAAP converges, 17x forward for a 40% grower re-rates violently.

**Bear Case**
- Reported growth is partly a tax artefact: at a normalized ~20% rate, FY26's $13.05 EPS is ~$11, and the stock is ~22x GAAP forward, not 17x — consensus implicitly assumes margin expansion this cost-plus model has never delivered.
- Cash conversion has collapsed (FCF/NI 0.6% FY26, negative since) on a working-capital and capex binge now funded with the first debt in years; a depreciation cliff ($254M+ capex vs $68M D&A) lands on a 12% gross margin.
- Concentration and trade: 57% of revenue in four cancellable programs, one flood-prone country, and a tariff/tax regime actively repricing against it — the downside is a 25–30% multiple event even with estimates intact.

**Key Risks**
1. Single-program loss at Cisco (~20%) or NVIDIA (~16%) = immediate 16–20% revenue event.
2. GAAP/non-GAAP spread widening further (tax normalization, equity-stake marks) — the credibility gap is the de-rating trigger.
3. Inventory at ~95 days in fast-rotating optics = obsolescence/margin-impairment setup if orders pause.
4. Durable reinstatement of ≥19% Thai tariffs without pass-through; Casix claim; Thai flood/political tail.
5. CPO/LPO architecture shift re-basing the merchant pluggable value pool in FY2028–30 — precisely the window terminal multiples assume.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
**Initiate 1.0% now, 2.5% hard ceiling** — this is a starter, not a conviction weight. Expected value at $341.64 is modestly negative (~$316); the balance sheet and demand visibility argue against a pass, the math argues against 3–5%. Scale in: +1.0% at $255–290 (where Value's buy zone and Macro's rate-shock target converge), +0.5% only on confirmation (reclaim of ~$410, or two quarters of FCF/NI ≥70%). Hard stop $289 (below the 23-Oct low); trim one-third at $430–450. **Critical fit rule:** FN correlates 0.78 with COHR and 0.62 with CLS — it is a higher-beta expression of the AI-optics trade, not a diversifier. Cap aggregate AI-optical hardware at 8% of the book; if the portfolio already holds ≥5% in COHR/LITE/CLS/AAOI, pass entirely and express the view through the existing position. Index hedges won't work (R² ~0.2–0.5); risk is managed at the position level. Re-underwrite every 10-Q on the GAAP/non-GAAP spread, OCF/NI, and inventory days; verify the unaccrued Casix claim in the 10-K Item 3 footnote before Tranche 2.
