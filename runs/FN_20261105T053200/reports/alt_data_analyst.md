### 1. Consumer Demand Signals
**Note on applicability:** FN is a B2B contract manufacturer for optical/EMS hardware — it has no consumer funnel. Consumer-style alt data here proxies *investor/retail attention*, not revenue. Weight accordingly.

- **Google Trends, "Fabrinet" (US, 12m):** Baseline roughly **doubled** from late-2025 (~8–14) to Apr–Jun 2026 (30–64), peaking at **100 in week of 16 Aug 2026** (Q4 FY26 print). Since then: **sharp decay — 7–14 through Sep/Oct**, i.e. back to 2025 levels. *Trend: deteriorating from peak; retail attention fully normalized.* Data ends 25 Oct, so it does **not** capture the 2–3 Nov earnings pop.
- **Google Trends, "Fabrinet stock":** Low-volume series (many zero/threshold-clipped weeks) → low reliability. Same shape: event spikes only (16 Aug = 100), then collapse to **6–10 by mid/late Oct**. Crucially, **no pre-earnings retail accumulation** ahead of the 2 Nov print — the beat-driven +15% move on 3 Nov (2.37M shares, ~2.4× normal) was institutional/estimate-driven, not retail momentum. *Signal: neutral-to-negative on attention, mechanically not fundamental.*
- **Web traffic (fabrinet.com):** SimilarWeb shows #758,240 globally / #2,666 in Investing (Sep 2026); month-over-month change ~flat (0.15% vs comp). B2B brochure site — **no informational value**. *Signal: neutral / not usable.*
- **End-market read-through:** Every earnings-call article attributes growth to **data-center demand (+78% YoY in Q1 FY27)** and AI optical programs. That is the real "demand" variable, and it is described as accelerating — but this is company-disclosed, not independent alt data.

### 2. Business Activity Signals
- **Capacity expansion (strongest business-activity signal):** **Building 10 in Thailand is nearing completion and expected to add ~$3.5B of annual revenue capacity** — a step-change, comparable to more than two current quarters of revenue. Management explicitly accelerated manufacturing expansion for AI optical programs. This is the clearest forward-capacity signal in the dataset.
- **Job postings:** Indeed shows **~32 US openings**, concentrated in **Machine Operator (12), QC Inspector (9), Process Technician (7)** plus SMT/box-build roles in Santa Clara. Profile is **direct labour / operations, not engineering or sales** — consistent with ramping volume, but a rounding error against 21.5k global headcount (bulk of manufacturing in Thailand). *Trend: mildly positive, but no time-series → cannot claim acceleration. Low confidence.*
- **Patents:** **Data gap** — USPTO/PatentsView feed returned an error on two attempts. Structurally low value anyway: as an EMS provider, FN's IP resides largely with customers (NVIDIA-adjacent OEMs), so patent counts are a poor R&D proxy here. *Signal: unavailable.*

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **ABOVE consensus**
- **Beat rate 7/7 (100%)** over the last seven quarters; **avg EPS surprise +3.46%**, and the *magnitude is trending up*: +0.38% → +3.55% → +3.07% → +4.49% → **+7.61%** (Q4 FY26) → +3.09% (Q1 FY27).
- **Revenue beats are equally persistent**: +1.9% → +3.0% → +4.7% → +5.2% → +2.2% → +3.1% → **+4.1%** (Q1 FY27: $1,474.5M vs $1,417.1M est), with revenue **+51% YoY** and results *above the company's own guidance range*.
- **Estimate revision direction is steeply upward:** consensus EPS has compounded ~13–14% sequentially for seven straight quarters (2.47 → 2.64 → 2.82 → 3.26 → 3.56 → 3.81 → 4.21 → **4.85**). Q2 FY27 consensus now **$4.85 EPS / $1.63B revenue** vs $4.34 / $1.47B delivered (+12% / +10% q/q).
- **Alt-data cross-check:** capacity additions (Building 10), operations hiring, and AI/data-center demand narrative all point the same way — **toward another beat**, and the historical pattern implies roughly a **+3–5% revenue / +3–8% EPS beat** is the base case. **Caveat:** the beat is priced. Post-print coverage includes "Fabrinet Beat Every Estimate. Its Stock Still Fell 11%" — a classic sell-the-news/second-derivative setup where *direction* of surprise no longer drives the stock.

### 4. News Sentiment Score and Summary
- **Sentiment score: +0.867** (14 positive / 1 negative / 16 articles, 14-day window) — **very positive, near the top of the range.**
- **Composition warning:** the window is dominated by the 2–3 Nov earnings cluster ("double beat," "strong guidance," "record revenue +51%," "data center +78%"). The single negative is the 28 Oct "Drops 19% in a Month: valuation and margin concerns."
- **Narrative tension worth flagging:** (a) a **new global minimum tax** kept GAAP EPS growth far below the 51% revenue growth; (b) "growth comes at a cash cost" — capex/capacity build is consuming cash; (c) the stock fell **~37% peak-to-trough** (intraday $515 on 9 Oct → $311.81 on 22 Oct) *before* a clean beat. Sentiment has flipped positive but the underlying debate (margins, tax, valuation) is unresolved.

### 5. Social Sentiment
- **Reddit/WSB (low volume, low confidence):** FN is framed as an **AI optical/800G transceiver play** — "NVIDIA's supplier of 800G transceivers already… $500M annual run-rate," and appears alongside CLS/STRL as a value/quality pick. **Tone: constructive, not euphoric.** No squeeze/meme dynamics, no negative threads surfaced. ### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**
The demand-side alt data (capacity build, ops hiring, 100% beat rate, upward revision cycle, strongly positive news flow) is unambiguously consistent with continued fundamental acceleration. Retail attention has cooled and social volume is thin, which **removes a froth risk** rather than signaling deterioration.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
**Why not high:** (a) patent feed failed twice — a genuine gap; (b) job-posting data is a static snapshot with no time series and covers a trivial slice of a Thailand-centric workforce; (c) no proprietary web traffic or app data exists for this business model, so the "leading indicator" toolkit is structurally thinner for FN than for a consumer name; (d) Google Trends here measures *retail attention*, which has already proven to be a lagging/noisy proxy and is event-spike-dominated; (e) news sentiment is heavily clustered on a single week's coverage.

**Key limitation / lag:** price data through 4 Nov 2026 and trends through 25 Oct 2026; commentary indicates a further ~11% drawdown *after* the beat. So the alt-data picture is fundamentally bullish while the tape is digesting valuation, margin mix, Pillar Two tax drag and heavy capex. **Alt data is a leading indicator of fundamentals — not of multiple compression.** The signal says "another beat is likely on ~1 Feb"; it does not say the stock will be rewarded for it.

**What would change the call:** any evidence of data-center order deceleration, capacity ramp slippage at Building 10, further GAAP EPS/tax erosion, or news sentiment turning negative outside the earnings window. Average sell-side PT ~$611–677 (19 analysts) vs $341.64 spot indicates consensus is *already* discounting a large re-rating — dispersion is wide (targets from $364 to $739), which is itself a sign of unresolved debate.
