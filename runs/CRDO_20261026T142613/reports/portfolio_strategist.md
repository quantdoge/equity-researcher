The reconciliation is arithmetic: CRDO must roughly **3x revenue to ~$5.0B by FY29** inside an AEC market that is only ~$4.5B growing 12.5% CAGR. That requires the optical thesis (>$600M FY27) to arrive on schedule. It has not yet appeared in a reported quarter.

Value's case is conditional on nothing. When one thesis requires an unproven ramp and the other requires only the status quo, **the burden of proof sits with the bull.**

Adding it deepens exactly the tilts (long duration, short volatility) that the Macro agent says are being repriced.

**Positive fit consideration:** the fortress balance sheet (net cash $744M, no debt, 7.4x current ratio) means CRDO *cannot* be a solvency casualty — it is a pure duration bet, which is a cleaner risk to size than a leveraged credit story.

## 7. Final Portfolio Action: **PASS** *(at $148.73 — with a 0.5% rules-based starter permitted; conditional re-entry framework above)*

**Rationale in one line:** a genuinely excellent, debt-free, high-quality growth franchise whose *business* the desk should want and whose *security* is priced above its base-case intrinsic value in the worst macro regime for long-duration equity in two decades — so own the theme through a smaller position, not this one at this price.

**Three findings that decided it:**
1. **The bear case is a de-rating thesis that has largely been paid.** −51% from the June high, EV/S down from 17x to ~10.7x forward, and the Short Analyst — whose entire mandate is to find the bear case — declines to short at $147. That removes the "sell it all" option.
2. **But the green shoots are unverified.** The October rally to $220 failed at a lower high. Beat magnitude has decayed from +43.9% to +2.6%. Gross margin has compressed ~430bp in two quarters. Consensus requires an H2 revenue step-up that has never appeared in a reported quarter. **The burden of proof is on the bull, and the proof arrives 2-Dec-26.**
3. **Concentration makes it un-sizeable.** With one customer at 43% of revenue and 57% of receivables, and top-2 at 61%, this is not a diversified semiconductor position — it is an options-like claim on two procurement decisions, wrapped in a 3.4 beta and 100% volatility. That profile is capped at 1–3% of capital regardless of conviction.

**Alternatives if the desk wants AI-connectivity beta:** prefer CRDO over ALAB on valuation (17x vs 37x EV/S, 59x vs 119x P/E, higher margins) — but **reduce total thematic weight**, because 0.69 correlation means adding one name is not diversifying. If the book is long AI infrastructure broadly, CRDO should be the funded short candidate *above* $210, not an addition below $150.

**Re-entry triggers (any one):** (a) price ≤ $110 with GM stabilization; (b) post-2-Dec confirmation of GM ≥65% + optical ≥$150M/quarter + FY27 guide ≥$2.4B; (c) 10Y <4.5% and HY OAS <300bp; (d) a credible fifth hyperscaler named, breaking the 61% top-2 concentration.
