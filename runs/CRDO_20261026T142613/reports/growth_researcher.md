# CRDO — Credo Technology Group | Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $148.73 (Oct 26, 2026) · Market cap ≈$28.0B · Net cash ≈$0.8B · Sector: Semiconductors*

## 1. Growth Story

Credo owns the interconnect bottleneck of the AI cluster. Its proprietary SerDes/DSP architecture powers Active Electrical Cables (AECs) where it holds an estimated ~70% of the **merchant** market — energy-efficient copper links that are cheaper and lower-power than optics inside the rack. The disruptive step-change is that Credo is becoming a **millimeters-to-kilometers connectivity platform**: AEC + optical DSPs + zero-flap optics + silicon-photonics PICs (via the Dust Photonics acquisition) + SerDes IP licensing. That converts a single-product cable story into a multi-vector franchise as clusters scale to 1.6T and beyond.

The stock, however, has de-rated ~51% from its June 2026 peak ($302.52 → $148.73) on fears that optics displaces AEC at 1.6T, that competition is arriving, and that 4 customers = ~84% of revenue. **The growth has not yet broken; the multiple has.** That divergence is the entire investment question.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

- **AEC:** Credo ~70% share of the merchant AEC market; this is the near-term cash engine (~$1.5B+ of FY27 revenue).
- **Optical:** the strategic TAM expander. Optical DSP + SiPh PIC + zero-flap optics take Credo from a ~$3–5B cable niche into the much larger optical connectivity and DSP pool (management framing: three >$100M product lines inside year one).
- **Retimers + SerDes IP:** high-margin attach, licensing optionality.
- **Penetration:** at ~$2.5B FY27 revenue against a data-center high-speed-connectivity SAM plausibly in the **$15–25B range by 2028–30** (my estimate — I could not retrieve a company-published TAM number), Credo is mid-single-digit penetrated. The moat is SerDes/DSP IP reuse across copper *and* optics — the one architecture that wins regardless of which medium the hyperscalers pick. That optionality is the core of the bull case.
- **Why Credo and not a peer:** vertical SerDes IP ownership, a decade of DSP tuning, and system-level (not component-level) selling into reference designs.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q2 FY27 earnings — Dec 2, 2026.** Consensus $531.8M revenue / $1.29 EPS. *The* catalyst: must show the H2 optical inflection begin (Q3/Q4 need to average ~$745M).
2. **1.6T AEC design-win / hyperscaler architecture decisions** (next 1–2 quarters) — the direct answer to the "optics kills AEC" bear case.
3. **Optical revenue disclosure against the >$600M FY27 target**, including first SiPh PIC shipments from Dust Photonics.
4. **Customer diversification** — any fifth hyperscaler ramp materially reduces the 84%/4-customer concentration.
5. **OFC (March 2027)** — usual venue for 1.6T/optical roadmap and competitive reveals.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, 2–3 year horizon)

This is a Rule-of-40 score of 236 in FY26 and ~145 even in the "decelerating" quarter, trading at **~10.7x forward revenue and ~24x forward EPS with 85% growth and 68% gross margins**. PEG ~0.3. The market has moved from pricing perfection to pricing AEC obsolescence — and that is a bet against a company that is simultaneously selling the optical replacement. Median analyst target ~$270 (range $235–$310) implies ~60–85% upside.

I stop short of **Strong Buy** for one reason: the earnings-revision and price-target trajectory is *negative*, and FY27 requires an H2 optical step-up (+47% H/H) that has not yet appeared in a reported quarter. Cathie-Wood-style, I want to own the platform shift — but I want the Dec 2 print to confirm the optical ramp began. **Accumulate into weakness; add size on confirmation.**

## 7. Key Risks

- **Execution risk on the H2 ramp (highest).** FY27 revenue requires ~$745M/qtr in H2 vs $532M guided for Q2. If optics slips a quarter, consensus revenue and the whole PT stack cut — the −20% reaction to a beat shows the fragility.
- **Customer concentration — 4 customers = ~84% of revenue.** A single hyperscaler in-sourcing AECs or re-architecting to optics would be severe, not merely painful.
- **AEC displacement at 1.6T.** Copper reach limits are real; length/power advantages erode as optics cost curves fall. Management's answer is optical — but that is a lower-margin, more competitive market (Broadcom, Marvell, Astera Labs, MACOM, plus in-house ASIC teams).
- **Margin compression is already visible.** GAAP operating margin 35.7% → 25.2% QoQ; GAAP GM −370bps. Non-GAAP GM has *plateaued* at 68% after years of expansion — the scale-leverage story is stalling as mix shifts to hardware.
- **Non-GAAP quality.** Q1 FY27 GAAP opex $188.4M vs non-GAAP $95.2M → **~$93M/qtr (~$373M/yr, ~20% of revenue)** of SBC + acquisition amortization. Diluted shares 168M → 194M. Non-GAAP EPS overstates owner earnings.
- **Cycle dependency.** Revenue is levered to a handful of AI capex budgets; beta 3.25 and a 51% drawdown in four months are the practical expression of that.
