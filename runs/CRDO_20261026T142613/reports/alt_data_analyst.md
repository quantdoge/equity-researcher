**Actionable framing:** the Dec-2 print is a **binary on guidance, not on the quarter.** Watch for (a) FY27 revenue guide vs. the ~$532M implied run-rate, (b) whether management introduces a new large customer to address concentration, and (c) a re-acceleration in ticker search above ~65 in the two weeks pre-print — historically the only clean attention-based tell I have.
