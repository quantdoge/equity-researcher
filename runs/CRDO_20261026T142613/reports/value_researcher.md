# Credo Technology Group (CRDO) — Value Investment Analysis

**Snapshot:** Price ≈ **$148** (Oct 2026); Market cap ≈ **$27.7B**; Net cash ≈ **$1.14B**; EV ≈ **$26.6B**; Diluted shares ≈ 194M; Beta **3.25**. Stock is **~52% below its June 2026 all-time high of $308.67**. *(Note: profile/ratios and price history initially disagreed on price; web verification confirms today's ~$148 is correct and the June $272–308 prints were the peak of a subsequent drawdown.)*

---

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **Narrow**

Credo designs high-speed connectivity silicon for AI/cloud datacenters: **Active Electrical Cables (AECs)**, **SerDes chiplets/IP**, optical DSPs, and retimers. It is a genuine beneficiary of AI-cluster scale-out — content per rack is rising, and Credo holds a leading position in AECs built on proprietary SerDes/DSP.

**Evidence the moat is real (but narrow, not wide):**
- **Technology leadership** in low-power SerDes and AEC — hard engineering, real know-how, high gross margins (64–68%).
- **ROIC of ~21%** (FY2026) now clears an estimated ~12% WACC — a positive signal.

**Evidence it is *not* a durable Buffett moat:**
- **Customer concentration** in a handful of hyperscalers — pricing power flows toward the buyer, not Credo.
- **Margin compression is already appearing:** gross margin fell to **64.5%** in Q1 FY27 from **68.2%** the prior quarter; operating margin fell to **25.2%** from **35.7%** — driven by DustPhotonics acquisition amortization, higher opex, and competitive pricing.
- **Competition is intensifying** (Marvell, Broadcom, Astera Labs, in-house/hyperscaler solutions) in a fast-moving, cyclical end-market.
- Only **~2 years of profitability** after years of losses — the durability of returns is unproven through a down-cycle.

Verdict: a high-quality *growth* franchise, but the competitive position is a moving target — **Narrow moat at best.**

## 3. Valuation Analysis — **No margin of safety**

- **Trailing P/E ≈ 51x** (TTM NI $538M); **~59x** on FY26 GAAP EPS; **EV/EBITDA ≈ 52x**; **P/FCF ≈ 68x**. Peer median P/E ≈ 44x — CRDO already trades at a **~35% premium**, on top of far higher growth.
- **Forward (FY2027E, ~$2.2B rev, guided Q2 $532M):** ~28–30x non-GAAP EPS. Still rich for a cyclical semi with compressing margins.

**Intrinsic value (own DCF, WACC 12%, 10-yr, 3% terminal):**
- **Base case** (FCF ~$550M FY27E, 30–35% growth fading to 12%): **≈ $95–100 / share**.
- **Bull case** (AI super-cycle sustains 40% growth for 5 yrs): **≈ $195–200 / share**.
- **Bear case** (growth normalizes to ~15% and margins stay compressed): **≈ $55–65 / share**.

At **$148**, the stock sits between base and bull — i.e., the **price already capitalizes a highly favorable, multi-year AI scenario.** To earn a 25% margin of safety on the base case I would need **~$70–80**. A business trading *above* my base intrinsic value gets no new capital from me. *(The platform DCF tool returned a broken, non-usable output — terminal value zeroed — so the above is my independent build.)*

## 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Mixed / Prudent-to-Watch**

- CEO **William Brennan**; Chairman **Lip-Bu Tan** (deep semiconductor pedigree) — credible operator-investor base.
- **Capital allocation:** net-cash discipline is good; the **DustPhotonics** optical acquisition (the ~$122M goodwill/intangibles on the FY26 balance sheet) is a strategic vertical-integration bet but is *currently margin-dilutive* — execution unproven.
- **Yellow flags:** aggressive **SBC dilution**; **heavy insider selling (~$74M over 90 days)**; minimal buyback ($19M) and no dividend. Founder/insider selling into strength warrants monitoring.

## 5. Investment Verdict: **Hold — Avoid for New Capital** (not a value buy)

This is an excellent *growth* business that a disciplined *value* investor should not buy at today's price. Even after a 52% drawdown, CRDO trades **~50x trailing earnings and above my base-case intrinsic value (~$100)**, with a **narrow, unproven moat, customer concentration, visible margin compression, and generous SBC dilution.** There is **zero margin of safety** — the core Buffett requirement.

- **New money:** **Avoid / wait.** I would get constructive near **$80–100** (base-case MOS) or on clear evidence that gross margins have stabilized and customer concentration is diversifying.
- **Existing holders:** **Hold** the quality but size it as a high-volatility (beta 3.25) growth position, not a value compounder; trim on strength toward the bull case (~$200).

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis
1. **Customer concentration** — a handful of hyperscalers drive revenue; loss/share-shift is a step-function risk.
2. **Margin compression & competition** — Marvell/Broadcom/Astera/in-house designs could compress the 64–68% GM and 25–35% op margins further (already trending down).
3. **AI capex cyclicality** — revenue is levered to a hyperscaler spending cycle that can inflect sharply; beta 3.25 cuts both ways.
4. **Valuation risk** — at ~50x earnings, any growth deceleration triggers severe multiple compression (already seen: −52% from peak).
5. **Dilution & insider selling** — 14% SBC/revenue plus ~$74M of insider sales.
6. **Geopolitical/China exposure** and acquisition-integration (DustPhotonics) execution risk.

**Bottom line:** Wide growth, narrow moat, full price. A fine company — but not a value investment at $148. Patient capital waits for either a much lower price (~$80–100) or demonstrated margin durability. *A great business bought at a fair-to-rich price is still not a buy.*
