**Bull Case**
1. **Fastest grower in semis at a discount to its only true peer**: +206% FY26 / +115% latest quarter — 6.3x the peer median — with +390bp GM and +1,300bp OM vs median, yet cheaper than ALAB (17x vs 37x EV/S; 59x vs 119x P/E).
2. **Second engine arriving**: >$600M FY27 optical target (zero-flap optics, SiPh PICs, optical DSPs — three >$100M lines) converts a $4.5bn cable niche into the broader optical connectivity pool; the SerDes/DSP IP wins regardless of whether copper or optics prevails at 1.6T.
3. **Fortress balance sheet + coiled multiple**: $744M net cash, no debt; median analyst PT ~$270 (+80%); a macro turn (10Y <4.5%) re-rates the stock 50–70% on unchanged estimates.

**Bear Case**
1. **Concentration is existential, not material**: one customer = 43% of revenue and 57% of receivables; top-2 = 61%. One procurement or in-sourcing decision is a step-function revenue event — it has happened before (−47% in a session, Feb 2023).
2. **Margin inflection has turned**: GAAP GM 68.2% → 64.5%, guided 62.9–64.9%; operating margin 35.7% → 25.2%; the stock fell 20% *on a beat*. Operating leverage is reversing exactly as opex ramps for 1.6T/optical.
3. **Valuation above base-case IV in a hostile regime**: ~51x TTM earnings vs ~$95–100 base intrinsic value, with the 10Y near 5% and the Fed still hiking; a 20% revenue miss plausibly produces a 50–70% equity decline (multiple compression dominates earnings).

**Key Risks**
1. Customer concentration / dual-sourcing or in-sourcing at a top-2 hyperscaler (highest severity).
2. Failure of the H2 FY27 optical ramp (+47% H/H embedded in consensus) — Dec-2 print is the binary.
3. Structural gross-margin erosion below 63% (mix vs. pricing discrimination is the Dec test).
4. Macro: 10Y >5.5% + HY OAS >500bp triggers the AI-credit-accident scenario ($90–100); hyperscaler capex is increasingly debt-financed at 15yr+ tenors.
5. Geopolitical: Section 232 scope expansion, TSMC single-source, HK revenue routing.
6. Goodwill impairment ($1.37bn = 50% of equity) + working-capital build (inventory/AR growing 2.5x revenue) + SBC dilution (18% of revenue).
