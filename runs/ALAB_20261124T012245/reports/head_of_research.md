**Investment Thesis**

Astera Labs is a genuinely excellent franchise — the pure-play on AI rack-scale connectivity, growing +154% YoY with 72% gross margins, a fortress balance sheet ($1.38B net cash, zero debt), and a 7-for-7 beat record — that has de-rated 54% from its June high because the **discount rate repriced (10Y at 5.13%, real yields ~3%), not because demand missed**. But excellence is not the question; price and durability are. The stock trades at a ~59% premium to a credible owner-earnings intrinsic value (~$140), carries 105% volatility and a 4.1 beta, depends on **one end customer for >70% of revenue and one foundry for 100% of wafers**, and its Chair, both founders, CFO and GC have sold ~$489M of stock with zero purchases — through the drawdown. That is a sizing problem, not a direction problem: the correct expression is a small, risk-controlled position or none.
