### Investment Memo: SNDK — 27 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (avoid for new money; trim existing) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $65 (−12% from current $73.61) | **Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Sandisk is a no-moat, pure-play NAND producer at the most extreme margin point in its history (85% gross margin vs. 7% three years ago), one week after TrendForce formally called the cycle turn — NAND contract prices declining from 1Q27 with 2027 bit supply +25–30%. The optically "cheap" 8x P/E is a cycle-position indicator, not a value signal: on mid-cycle earnings of ~$3.30–$6.30 the stock trades at 12–22x, i.e. fair-to-full. Three independent agents (Value, Quant, Short) ran the same normalization and landed at fair value of **$65–75 — exactly the current price**, meaning zero margin of safety against a bear/bull dispersion of $20/$144. Conviction is Medium, not High, because the stock is already −75% from peak and much of the downgrade is priced.

**Key Findings by Domain**

Sector unattractive: NAND late-cycle peak-to-contraction; spot −20% since mid-Oct; 512Gb wafer −32% QoQ; Chinese CSP enterprise-SSD order cuts; YMTC structural deflation. BIS export-control drift toward high-capacity eSSD is the upgrade trigger to Very High. Litigation (SSD data-loss class action, legacy IP) is modest in dollars.

**Bull Case**
- AI storage demand is structural: enterprise SSD is the tightest part of the complex; $93.9B of floor-priced NBM contracts (>50% of FY27 bits) could make this downturn historically shallow.
- Fortress balance sheet + $10.5B buyback authorization at depressed prices is mechanically accretive and puts a floor that didn't exist in prior cycles.
- HBF opens a new inference-memory TAM by 2029–30 currently worth ~nothing in the price; any hyperscaler design win is a re-rating event.

**Bear Case**
- Mean reversion of an 85% gross margin no memory maker has ever sustained: a 30pt give-back on ~$50B revenue removes ~$15B of gross profit — more than the entire net income.
- 2027 supply glut (bit supply +25–30%, capex +26.7%) colliding with Chinese CSP order cuts and YMTC share gains; SNDK is the most exposed pure-play price-taker.
- Hawkish-2027 macro scenario (Fed to 4.75–5.00%, 10Y to 5.75–6.00%) compresses earnings *and* multiple simultaneously → $30s.

**Key Risks**
1. NBM floor contracts renegotiated or force-majeured in a downcycle — destroys the only structural bull argument (never stress-tested).
2. Receivables/inventory build (DSO 85 days, inventory days 170) proves to be channel sell-in, confirmed by two quarters of rising DSO on falling revenue.
3. Tariff/export-control escalation on Japan-origin NAND or China retaliation against U.S. memory suppliers.
4. Kioxia JV disruption (single wafer source; seismic; 2034 renewal).
