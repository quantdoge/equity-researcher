## SNDK (Sandisk) — Macroeconomic Assessment
*Data as of 27 Nov 2026. Prices split-adjusted (8-for-1 split effective after close 19 Nov 2026).*

---

### 1. Macro Environment Summary — **Transitional, tilting risk-off**

The US is in a **reflation / "no-landing" regime with a hawkish turn in monetary policy**. Real GDP grew 1.05% q/q in Q3 2026 (~4.3% annualized) versus 0.55% in Q2, unemployment has *fallen* to 4.1% from 4.5% a year ago, and nominal PCE is running +6.4% y/y. Growth is not the problem.

Inflation is. Headline CPI is **3.82% y/y (Oct 2026)** and the 3-month annualized run-rate is **~5.7%** — a fresh acceleration, not a fading base effect. Core CPI (ex food/energy) has re-accelerated from a ~2.7% six-month run-rate to **~3.75% annualized over the last three months**. Because headline exceeds core, a meaningful part of this is an energy/food supply shock on top of firming core — the worst mix for a central bank.

The Fed responded: the effective funds rate bottomed at **3.63% (Feb–Aug 2026)** and has since been raised to **3.75% (Sep) and 3.88% (Oct)**. With CPI at 3.82%, the **real policy rate is now roughly zero** — down from ~+1.3% a year ago. The Fed is behind the curve, which is why the long end has repriced violently: the **10Y is 5.13% (27 Nov), up ~75bp from its 4.38% June low** and near the 5.31% October peak. The curve has **bear-steepened** (2s10s +39bp, 5s30s +54bp; 30Y at 5.47%) — a term-premium/fiscal signal, not a growth signal. Meanwhile the **broad USD index has rallied +4.9% since 9 September** (117.9 → 123.6).

Translation: **liquidity is tightening, the discount rate is rising, and the dollar is strengthening — while a commodity-technology cycle rolls over.**

### 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

**a) Policy rates & the discount rate — NEGATIVE (high conviction).** SNDK is the textbook long-duration asset: a terminal-value-heavy AI-infrastructure story with a **reported beta of 5.65** (inflated by the Feb-2025 Western Digital spin, but directionally real). A 75bp rise in the 10Y mechanically cuts a 20x multiple to ~16x with no change in earnings. This is precisely what has happened: earnings have surged (triple-digit revenue growth), yet the stock is down 75% and now screens as "one of the cheapest in tech." **That is a pure multiple-compression event, driven by the rate shock.** 30Y at 5.47% also raises the hurdle rate on the Kioxia JV and fab capex commitments, and raises refinancing costs for a recently spun-off entity with an unproven standalone capital structure.

**b) Inflation / cost structure — NEGATIVE, partially offset.** NAND manufacturing is power- and materials-intensive; 3-month headline inflation running near 5.7% annualized is a direct input-cost headwind (electricity, ultrapure materials, freight, labour in Japan/Malaysia). Against that, the current NAND shortage has given SNDK genuine **pricing power** — Q4'26 contract prices still rising 8–13% and the company has locked substantial future supply into long-term contracts. Net: **pricing power is currently outrunning cost inflation, but both are peaking simultaneously** — and cost inflation lags pricing, so margin risk turns adverse in H1 2027.

**c) Growth — POSITIVE.** 4.3% annualized real GDP plus AI data-centre buildout is exactly SNDK's demand driver (enterprise SSD, QLC NAND). This is the one unambiguous tailwind, and it is why earnings are at records even as the stock collapses.

**d) Currency — NEGATIVE.** The broad dollar is up 4.9% in ten weeks and 2.7% since June. SNDK sells predominantly outside the US (China, Taiwan, Japan, Europe). A stronger dollar (i) is a translation headwind on ~two-thirds of revenue, (ii) raises the local-currency price of a USD-denominated commodity, accelerating the demand elasticity that TrendForce is already flagging, and (iii) historically coincides with weaker memory pricing. Every 1% of broad USD strength costs roughly 0.5–0.7% of revenue.

**e) Trade policy / tariffs — NEGATIVE (two-sided).** The **25% Section 232 tariff on certain semiconductors and derivative products, effective 15 January 2026**, applies to advanced chips and articles containing them, with carve-outs. SNDK's front-end output sits largely in Japan (Yokkaichi) with back-end assembly in Malaysia/China, so US-bound product is exposed. Fresh tariff threats in late November hit memory names again. Net effect: higher landed cost and demand destruction outside the US; a modest offsetting tailwind to US contract pricing. On balance a margin and volume drag.

**f) Commodity/cycle — NEGATIVE and the dominant near-term variable.** TrendForce (19 Nov 2026) now forecasts **NAND contract prices turning down in Q1 2027** (as much as −5%), with the market shifting from undersupply to oversupply in 2027 as **Chinese cloud service providers cut enterprise SSD orders**. This is the "macro" shock that actually matters for SNDK — a sector-level demand shock arriving while the Fed is tightening. Note the reflexive loop: tighter US policy → stronger dollar → weaker Chinese/EM demand → NAND price declines → earnings downgrades → high-beta multiple compression.

### 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Six of seven macro vectors are negative. The single positive (US/AI growth) is already fully reflected in record earnings and is now being offset by the market discounting a 2027 price rollover. The specific danger is **the combination, not any one factor**: a Fed that must tighten into an energy-led inflation shock while a cyclical pricing peak hits a 5.6-beta equity. SNDK is effectively a leveraged short position on the inflation/term-premium regime.

**What would change this to Neutral:** a durable move in the 10Y back below ~4.75%, broad USD back under 119, and evidence that Chinese CSP order cuts are one-quarter inventory digestion rather than a capex downcycle.

---

### 5. Surprise Scenario to Watch — **BEAR: "Hawkish 2027"**

*The consensus is that NAND rolls over in 2027 but the Fed eases into it. The surprise is the opposite.*

If the energy/food shock embedded in the 5.7% three-month headline run-rate bleeds into core — services inflation is already back to ~3.75% annualized — the Fed's 3.88% policy rate is roughly zero in real terms and will need to rise. A move to **4.75–5.00% by mid-2027** would push the 10Y toward **5.75–6.00%** on term premium, exactly as NAND contract prices post low-single-digit quarterly declines and SNDK's fixed-price contracts come up for renegotiation.

The arithmetic is brutal: earnings fall perhaps 25–30% from peak *and* the multiple drops to 8–10x, which implies a share price in the **$30s** — another ~50%+ downside from $73.61. With beta at 5.6 and the stock already down 75%, this is not a tail; it is the path of least resistance if core CPI prints 0.4%+ monthly.

*The offsetting bull case — Chinese order cuts prove transient, AI inference demand extends the shortage into 2028, and a Fed pause at 4.00% sends the 10Y back to 4.40% — would produce an equally violent 2x re-rating. But the macro regime currently points one way.*
