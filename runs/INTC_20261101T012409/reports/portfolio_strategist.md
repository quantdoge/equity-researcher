## 2. Consensus Assessment: **HIGH agreement — bearish**

**9 of 11 agents** are negative-to-neutral-bearish. The lone bull (Growth) explicitly frames itself as "speculative, position size for binary foundry proof, not the base case." Critically, two of the most rigorous agents converge from opposite directions:

**Resolving the one contested view — Growth vs. Value:** Growth is *descriptively* right (DCAI +78%, GM +200bps, beat history) but *prescriptively* wrong on price. A better company at 3x base-case intrinsic value is still a bad investment. Growth itself concedes the foundry has $1.6B annualized external revenue against a $350B TAM and negative FCF. That is an **option**, and options must be priced and sized as options — not as core equity. Per the conflict rule, the valuation gap and the four independent risk flags outrank the operating inflection.

**Two data-integrity cautions carried forward:** (a) the "85.7% beat rate / +530% surprise" is a penny-estimate artifact — Alt Data correctly flags 4 of 7 quarters had sub-$0.02 bases; (b) the "+45.5% estimate revision" is a period-mismatch artifact (FY30 vs FY29), not a real 3-month revision. Neither supportive signal is valid.
