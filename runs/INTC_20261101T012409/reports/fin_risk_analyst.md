This is a **beta-2, 70-vol single stock** — market risk is the dominant risk here, not credit. A garden-variety 20% index drawdown maps to roughly a **40%+ decline** mechanically. That is not theoretical: on Oct 29, 2026 the stock fell **-21% in one session** (from $92.07 to ~$72.85 after hours) on weak Q4 guidance, and has since drifted to $65.76 — **~29% below the October peak and ~53% below the 3-year high**. The 1.17 Sharpe reflects an enormous prior run (IPO-era $37 → record), not low risk; the same trade that produced that Sharpe produced the 53% drawdown.

**Interpretation:** the equity is priced as a foundry-turnaround call option. Downside participation is asymmetric and violent; position sizing, not fundamentals, is the primary risk control.

## 4. Downside Scenario — Revenue -20%

Take FY2025 as the base: revenue $52.85B → **$42.3B**.

| Line | Base FY2025 | -20% Revenue | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | 34.8% | **~24-26%** | D&A ($11.7B) and fab fixed costs are ~immovable; unit absorption collapses |
| Operating income | -$2.2B | **-$5.5 to -$7.0B** | Operating leverage works both ways |
| EBITDA | $14.4B | **~$9-10B** | Protected by D&A/impairments |
| Operating cash flow | $9.7B | **~$0 to $2B** | OCF ran 18.3% of sales; working-capital release partly offsets |
| Capex | -$14.6B (2025); **guided up toward ~$20B** for 18A/14A ramp | **-$14 to -20B** | Committed, strategically non-discretionary |
| **FCF** | **-4.95B** | **-$14 to -20B** | |
| Cash & investments | $37.4B (FY25) + $23B equity | **~$55-60B pro forma** | |
| Cash runway at stress burn | — | **~3 years** before new capital | Also absorbs Fab 34 minority buyback |
| Net debt/EBITDA | 0.64x | **~1.0-1.2x** (net cash-like on pro-forma cash) | Still far from covenant stress |
| Interest coverage | 2.4x | **negative** | The real casualty |

**Conclusion on the downside:** a 20% revenue shock does **not** create a solvency or liquidity event — the $37.4B cash pile, the 2026 $23B equity raise, only $2.5B of short-term debt, and a manageable ~$4.25B of maturities across 2026-27 mean there is no refinancing cliff. It creates an **earnings and credibility event**: coverage goes negative, FCF burn approaches $20B/yr, and the IG rating comes under review — likely forcing either another large equity raise (further dilution) or a capex cut that delays 14A. **The risk is dilution and strategic retreat, not default.**

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE** (credit: low-moderate; equity: high)

Split verdict, deliberately: **balance-sheet risk is low** (0.64x net leverage, 2.0x current ratio, IG rated, no near maturities); **business-model risk is high** (four consecutive years of negative FCF totalling **-$44.6B**, negative operating income, negative interest coverage on an operating basis, 72% volatility). They net to Moderate because cash and capital-market access are, for now, buying the time the turnaround needs — and Q3 2026 (DCAI +78%, 15.3% GAAP operating margin, $5.1B OCF) is the first hard evidence the plan is working.

**What would move this to High:** an IG downgrade to sub-investment grade; FCF still negative in FY2027; another equity raise; or evidence the 14A node slips.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Structurally negative FCF — four years running.** -$9.6B, -$14.3B, -$15.7B, -$4.9B (FY22→FY25). Cumulative **-$44.6B**. FCF yield -1.55%. The foundry buildout consumes cash faster than the franchise generates it.
2. **Interest coverage is below 1x on an operating basis (-2.03x).** The 2.43x EBIT figure relies on non-operating income; D&A of $11.7B is flattering EBITDA-based leverage.
3. **Serial dilution — the silent credit risk.** Shares +18% 2022→2025, then a **$23B equity offering in 2026** (~+8-10% more). Piotroski flags it (F7 = 0). Per-share value is being transferred to bondholders and new holders.
4. **Dividend eliminated** ($6.0B in 2022 → $0 in 2025) — a cash-preservation signal, and a permanent income-investor exodus.
5. **Guidance shock / disclosure risk.** Q4'26 guide of $15-16B vs ~$17B consensus triggered a -21% day; management now guides the PC slowdown "continuing at least through the first half of next year." Forecast reliability is impaired.
6. **Customer/segment concentration in the recovery thesis.** The entire bull case rests on DCAI (+78%) and external foundry (+58%) — the two most competitive, least contracted parts of the business, against NVDA/TSM/AMD.
7. **Equity risk >> credit risk.** Beta 2.05, vol 72%, 53% max drawdown, -29% off the October high. Beta-2 exposure to a market drawdown is the largest single loss channel.
