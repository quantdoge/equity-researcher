Bullish for durability; **bearish for near-term multiple expansion**, since the marginal retail buyer is not present.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **Tracking ABOVE consensus (revenue); EPS unreliable**
Next catalyst: **Q4/FY26 print, ~5 Mar 2027** — consensus **EPS $1.17, revenue $2.790B**.

- Last quarter (10 Nov 2026): revenue **$2,347M vs $2,204M est (+6.5%)**, **+17.2% YoY**; EPS **$0.41 vs $0.811 → –49% miss**. Guidance **raised**: FY26 revenue to **$8.4–8.6B** (from $8.2–8.5B) and adjusted FCF lifted. Stock **+7%** on the release — the market clearly trades EMBJ on revenue/backlog/cash, not EPS.
- **Revenue beat rate: 6 of last 7 quarters.** **EPS beat rate: only 57%** with a wildly unstable magnitude (+388%, –104%, +90%, –45%, +100%, –49%) — EPS is polluted by FX, tax and tariff credits. **Do not use EPS surprises to position.**
- **Skew: positive on revenue.** Q4 consensus implies only **+5.2% YoY** against a Q3 run-rate of **+17%** and Q4 being Embraer's seasonally strongest delivery quarter. Blockbuster backlog conversion supports upside to the top line; the risk is margin mix (see below), not volume.
- **Key nuance (bear point, per independent commentary):** most of the recent margin improvement came from **extraordinary credits and tariff relief, not core operations**; Commercial Aviation margins remain structurally weak. So revenue should beat while **margin quality disappoints** — a "good quarter, sceptical reaction" risk.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH** (but largely reflected)
The *business activity* data — backlog ($35.7B, 4 straight records, +14% YoY), deliveries (+6.5%, record Q3), broad-based order flow (ANA, Azorra, ABRA, Farnborough 28), raised guidance — is unambiguously positive and **accelerating**. That is the highest-quality alt data in this run and it points one way.

The offset is **positioning, not fundamentals**: stock at a 52-week high (+27% YTD, +82% off Jun-2025), Zacks #1, analyst PT $92.50 (~12% upside pre-split), fading retail search, and inactive social. **The alt-data edge here is thin — the good news is in the price.** The differentiated read is that revenue should beat again in Q4 while margin *quality* disappoints, i.e. asymmetric risk sits in the reaction, not the print.

**What would change this to Bearish:** a 4Q26 book-to-bill below 1.0, failed conversion of the $35.7B backlog into deliveries (supply chain), or reversal of the tariff relief that is currently flattering margins.
