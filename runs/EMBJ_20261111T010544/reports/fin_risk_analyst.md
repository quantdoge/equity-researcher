**Survivability verdict: high.** Even in a 20% revenue shock Embraer does not face a solvency event:
- Cash of BRL 14.4bn ($2.6bn) covers a −1.5bn annual burn for **~9 years**, ~3.5 years even against a severe 4bn burn.
- Only BRL 577m of debt matures near-term; no refinancing is *required* into a downturn.

The binding constraint in the downside is **P&L and covenant optics (coverage <1x)**, not liquidity. A 20% drop would likely trigger a ratings outlook revision to Negative (BBB/Stable today) but not a downgrade to sub-IG absent multi-year pain. **Downside equity risk is severe** — a −20% revenue scenario plausibly implies 40–50% equity downside on a 0.5x Z-score and 1.6 beta, even with the balance sheet intact.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Altman Z-Score 1.18 "Distress" is a false positive — but not meaningless.** The x2 term (retained earnings) is exactly **0**, evidencing an accumulated deficit, and x4 uses book equity (0.42), not the far higher market value. On a market-cap basis Z would be comfortably higher. Read it as "thin accumulated equity cushion," not "bankruptcy candidate."
2. **Goodwill + intangibles BRL 14.9bn = 21% of assets and 71% of book equity.** Tangible book equity is only ~BRL 6.0bn (~$1.1bn, **7% of market cap**). A single impairment (Eve/eVTOL, Defense programs) could wipe out a third of reported equity and hit the Z-score and any book-leverage covenant.
3. **Interest coverage ~3x on gross interest.** Thin for IG; would go below 1.5x in a −20% revenue scenario. Verify the net-interest and lease-interest split — this is the single most important missing disclosure.
4. **EBITDA data inconsistency (data-quality flag).** Reported `ebitda` fields (2025: 3,966; 2024: 6,265) do not reconcile to EBIT + D&A (4,754 / 5,634). Net-debt/EBITDA can read 0.12x, 0.88x, or 1.05x depending on definition. Leverage conclusions hold across all variants, but precision here is limited. Similarly, balance-sheet `net_debt` (4,174) ≠ debt − cash (471); likely unconsolidated lease/Eve debt.
5. **Working-capital reversal risk.** BRL +2,182m WC inflow drove 2025 OCF; a normalizing WC cycle plus continued inventory build (17.4bn) could compress FCF materially in FY2026.
6. **Macro/tariff and FX exposure.** US import tariffs already cost management ~2.6pts of Q2'26 adjusted EBIT margin. BRL-reported results inflate the cost base against USD-denominated aircraft pricing — reported margins are FX-noisy and unreliable as a trend guide.
7. **Customer credit risk.** BRL 4.8bn receivables plus Embraer's role as an aircraft financier ties balance-sheet quality to airline counterparty health in a downturn.
8. **Tool-output error to disregard:** the 13.98% FCF yield is a currency-mismatch artifact; true FCF yield is **~2.6%**.

**Data gaps:** no covenant details, no CDS spreads, no gross/net interest split, no undrawn revolver size, no debt maturity ladder beyond current/non-current, no pension or lease schedule, no genuinely comparable peer set (the sector tool returned US mega-caps — SPCX, GE, RTX, CAT — not Airbus/Bombardier/Textron), and no FY2026 full-year statements (audited base is FY2025; Q2'26 used only for qualitative momentum).
