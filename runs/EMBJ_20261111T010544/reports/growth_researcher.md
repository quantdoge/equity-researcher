# EMBJ — Embraer S.A. | Growth Investment Analysis
*Note on data: fundamentals in filings are BRL-denominated while the share price and the analyst estimate set are USD. The screen-level multiples returned by the data feed (P/E 7.8x, EV/EBITDA 3.9x, P/B 0.7x) are FX-garbled and should be ignored; I rebuild valuation on a company-level USD basis (~5.45 BRL/USD). Embraer also changed its ADS ratio on 30-Oct-2026 (ERJ→EMBJ symbol change 3-Nov-2025), so raw per-share history has a corporate-action seam. EV/enterprise-level math below is ratio-invariant.*

## 1. Growth Story
Embraer has transformed from a cyclical regional-jet maker into a three-engine compounding platform: **Commercial (E2 family), Executive (Praetor/E Phenom), and Defense (C-390 Millennium)**, each now winning share against larger OEMs that are distracted by their own narrowbody problems. The proof is in the order book — a **record $35.7B firm backlog (+14% y/y) = 4.2x forward revenue**, including a record $5.6B services backlog — which converts 2026–2029 revenue visibility from "estimate" to "schedule." The re-rating driver is no longer volume alone but **margin mix**: services and defense share gains are lifting adjusted EBIT margin to 10.9% in 3Q26 (from 8.6%) while free cash flow inflects (adj. FCF ex-Eve of $628M in a *single* quarter vs. FY26 guidance of ≥$700M, raised from ≥$400M).

## 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration
- **Commercial (<150 seats):** the sub-150-seat replacement market is Embraer's core SAM — a multi-hundred-billion-dollar 20-year delivery pool as ageing E1s, CRJs and older narrowbodies retire. E2 family has **540 orders as of Oct-2026**, with 2026 momentum from ABRA, Binter, Luxair and ANA — i.e., penetration is *just beginning* outside Europe/Brazil.
- **Executive aviation:** ~$25–30B annual industry billings; Embraer is #2–3 with ~160–170 deliveries/yr and a Praetor 600E/E Phenom refresh cycle (Flexjet first delivery, Sep-2026) — key to gross-margin repair.
- **Defense (C-390):** medium multi-mission airlift replacement (C-130H fleets) in NATO/Europe, a 200+ unit pool; 33 firm KC-390 orders with deliveries now reaching Uzbekistan (Sep-2026). This is the highest-multiple, lowest-competition leg (only meaningful Western competitor is the elderly C-130).
- **Services:** installed base of ~1,900 E-Jets + ~1,600 executive jets is an annuity; services is the highest-margin, most under-appreciated growth engine and grows regardless of OEM delivery cycles.
- **Eve (EVEX, ~73% owned):** free option on eVTOL, certification targeted 2027.

Penetration read: with backlog at 4.2x revenue the company has **already sold ~4 years of output**; the incremental TAM question is no longer "can it fill the line" but "can it price and deliver at 12%+ EBIT margin."

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts
1. **Q4/FY26 results + first FY2027 guidance — 5-Mar-2027.** Consensus Q4 revenue $2.79B / EPS $1.17. A 2027 guide of >$9.5B revenue with an 11%+ adj. EBIT margin would force estimate upgrades; this is the single largest re-rating event in the next 90 days.
2. **4Q26 backlog & delivery release (late Jan-2027).** Watch (a) backlog holding above $35B, (b) commercial deliveries landing in the 80–85 band — delivery *count* is the credibility test after two years of guidance that was raised on revenue but not on output.
3. **C-390 European/NATO order campaign (2027).** A multi-unit order from a major European air force (beyond Netherlands/Austria/Czech/Hungary/Portugal/Sweden/Slovakia/Lithuania) would shift mix toward defense and support a higher multiple.
4. **Eve type certification / entry into service (targeted 2027).** Binary; a certification slip removes optionality, a certification win crystallizes a business the market currently values near zero inside EMBJ.
5. **E175-E2 launch decision / US scope-clause evolution (2027–2028).** The E2 has been locked out of the US by pilot scope clauses; any relaxation — or an Avelo-scale E195-E2 US order — opens the largest regional aviation market and is the biggest un-priced upside.
6. **Post-ADS-ratio-change liquidity/index effects (Q1-2027).** A cleaner ADS structure can broaden the US holder base.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals
- **Backlog as leading indicator: bullish and unambiguous.** Six consecutive all-time-high backlog prints ($31.6B 4Q25 → $32.1B 1Q26 → $35.7B 3Q26). Backlog is a signed-contract metric, not sentiment.
- **Revenue beat cadence: bullish.** 6 of 7 quarters beat consensus revenue, typically by 3–8%. Management guides conservatively.
- **Hiring: modestly bullish.** LinkedIn shows 11,000+ Embraer engineer/engineering-adjacent postings in the US; Indeed Florida (Melbourne/Space Coast) shows 30+ active Embraer roles tied to product development and prototyping — consistent with capacity expansion, not a cost-cutting posture. (Caveat: LinkedIn counts are inflated/noisy; treat as directional only.)
- **Google Trends (US, 5y): cautionary/neutral.** Interest peaked ~57 in Feb-2026 and has decayed to ~31–37 by Oct-Nov 2026 even as the stock made new highs — retail attention is fading into the rally, a mild negative breadth signal, not a demand signal (Embraer sells to ~100 airline and government buyers, not consumers).
- **Sell-side attention: bullish but lagging.** Coverage is universally positive (one aggregator shows 14 buy / 0 hold / 0 sell) and targets ($81–104) sit at or below the current price — targets are chasing, not leading. That's typical mid-stage, not a top signal, but it caps near-term upside surprise.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (not Strong Buy)
The structural thesis is intact and arguably still under-appreciated: **a record 4.2x-coverage backlog, an under-levered balance sheet, FCF inflecting from ~5% to ~8%+ margin, and three independent growth engines with genuine competitive moats** (E2 fuel efficiency/noise; C-390 as the only modern Western medium airlifter; services annuity). My reverse-DCF check is supportive: at a 12x terminal EV/EBITDA, today's $15.6B EV embeds only ~$1.3B of EBITDA. On consensus 2030 revenue of $11.7B at a realistic 14% adj. EBIT margin (+D&A), EBITDA reaches ~$2.0B → ~$24B EV, or **+55% over four years (~11–12% IRR)** before dividends. That is a good, not spectacular, return.

Why not Strong Buy: the stock is up ~4.7x from Jan-2024 ($17.55 → $82.50) and now trades at **27x FY26E and 21x FY27E earnings with a Rule-of-40 of only ~20**; growth decelerates to ~12% in FY26–27 (Q4-26 consensus is only +5% y/y); guidance was raised on revenue and cash but **not on deliveries or margin**, which tells you the constraint is industrial, not demand; and the Street's average target ($92.50–104) implies only 12–26% upside — insufficient asymmetry for Strong Buy. I would upgrade to Strong Buy on either (a) a pullback below ~$65 (≈17x FY27E) or (b) a 2027 guide above $9.8B revenue with an 11%+ margin, which would put consensus 2030 numbers in play.

## 7. Key Risks
- **Tariff/political (highest near-term risk):** US tariffs on Brazilian-origin imports directly tax E2/Praetor pricing into Embraer's most profitable market; mitigation via US (Melbourne, FL) footprint is partial. Escalation compresses margin or cedes US share to A220.
- **Industrial execution:** two years of unchanged delivery guidance (80–85 commercial, 160–170 executive) signals supply-chain and GTF engine-availability friction. A delivery shortfall would break the backlog-conversion narrative and hit the multiple hardest, since it is the one thing the market is paying 21x forward for.
- **Guidance credibility on margin:** FY26 EBIT margin guidance was left at 10.0–10.6% despite a 10.9% 3Q — either conservatism (bullish) or a signal of 4Q cost/mix pressure. The 8-Mar-2027 print resolves it.
- **Competitive:** Airbus A220 owns the 100–150 seat segment and can price aggressively; COMAC/ARJ21 plus a potential Boeing NMA/Embraer-scope response could compress E2 pricing power. E175-E2 remains structurally locked out of the US absent scope-clause relief — the largest pool of regional demand is currently unaddressable.
- **FX and accounting:** BRL-denominated cost base against USD revenue; a stronger real compresses margins, and reported EPS is already noisy (two ~-45% quarterly misses) partly on non-operating FX items.
- **Valuation risk:** at 13x EV/EBITDA and 21x FY27E, the stock discounts flawless execution; any air-pocket in aerospace sentiment (Boeing/GE read-throughs) transmits quickly to a $15B-cap name with a concentrated institutional holder base.
- **Eve drag & governance:** Eve cash burn is a recurring FCF headwind (hence Embraer's "ex-Eve" FCF framing), and the Brazilian government's golden share remains a structural M&A/control overhang.
