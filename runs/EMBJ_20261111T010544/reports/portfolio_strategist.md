## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** — operating consensus bullish, valuation consensus bearish

The disagreement is **not** about the company; it is about the price.

**Agreement (7 of 9 agents, high quality evidence):** Demand is exceptional and verified — $35.7bn backlog (+14% y/y), 4 consecutive records, 6/7 revenue beats, deliveries +6.5%, guidance raised on revenue and FCF. The turnaround is real. Balance sheet is IG and under-levered. **No agent argues the business is broken.**

**Agreement (4 of 9, high conviction):** The price already discounts that. Value, Short, Alt Data and Growth converge on the same conclusion from different methods — **current $82.50 embeds flawless execution**, sits 15–20% above base intrinsic value, and above most published targets ($65 UBS, $67 MS, $81 Scotiabank; only JPM $104 and alt-data $92.50 above).

**Where they genuinely conflict:** Growth says ~11–12% IRR is "good, not spectacular" (i.e., fair compensation); Value says it's negative expected return. I resolve in Value's favour *on the base case* but note Growth's IRR is roughly at cost of equity — so this is **zero edge, not negative edge**. The resolution matters for action: neither justifies new capital, but neither demands an exit from existing holdings.

**Precedence rule applied:** Value's SELL and the Short Analyst's "NO EDGE" outweigh the bullish operating reports, because they address *price paid* — the only variable not already in the price. The tariff/ITAR risk flagged by Geo-Legal is a genuine binary and takes precedence over Sector's benign framing of "policy risk with binary outcomes."

**Why 1.5% and not 3–5%** despite "medium-high" operating conviction:
- **Volatility-scaled cap.** 44.9% annualized vol vs ~16% for a typical large cap. A 1.5% position contributes ~0.67% of portfolio volatility — equivalent to a ~4% position in a normal-vol stock. Sizing on *risk*, not capital, is the binding constraint.
- **Beta.** Confirmed 1.62 (not the 0.72 in the profile data). 1.5% × 1.62 = **2.4% of portfolio beta** — you get S&P downside exposure equivalent to a 2.4% index position.
- **Kelly.** Base case ≈ fair value → edge ≈ 0 → Kelly fraction ≈ 0. The bull case ($88, +7%) and bear case (−40%) are roughly symmetric in magnitude but the bear has 20–25% probability on a Brazil fiscal accident. Positive sizing is only defensible at a discount, not a premium.
- **Hard structural caps.** Golden share veto (permanent governance discount), ~59% revenue on a revocable executive-branch tariff exemption, ITAR leverage over the fastest-growing segment. These are *binary* risks that no amount of backlog offsets — hence a small absolute cap regardless of conviction.

**The factor exposure nobody named: EMBJ is not an A&D holding — it is an EM/Brazil country-risk proxy.** Its low correlation to defense primes but 1.62 beta to the market means the true factor loadings are (a) high-beta cyclical, (b) BRL/USD, (c) Brazil sovereign risk premium. **Test the book against those three buckets, not against "Industrials."** If the portfolio already holds Brazil or LatAm EM exposure, EMBJ double-counts the fiscal tail risk that Macro flagged as a 20–25% probability event — and that is the reason to hold the cap at 1.5% rather than 2.5%.

**Also note:** ~59% US revenue means EMBJ is *not* an EM-revenue play — it is a **USD-revenue / BRL-cost arbitrage**. That is genuinely attractive while the real is weak, but it inverts if BRL strengthens, and it is the same variable that drives the Brazil risk premium. FX help and rate harm come from one source.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss:** **$68** (−17.5% from $82.50) for any legacy position; **$55** (−17% from a $66 entry) for new tranches. Note 44.9% vol means a 17% move is under 1 standard deviation annually — **this must be a thesis stop, not a noise stop.** Do not set it tighter than 15%.

**Thesis invalidation — any ONE of these = exit regardless of price:**
1. **Delivery guidance cut** below 80 commercial / 160 executive — this is the credibility test; guidance has been unchanged for two years while revenue guidance was raised twice. A cut breaks the backlog-conversion story and is the single highest-multiple-destroying event.
2. **Backlog falls below $33bn** or book-to-bill <0.9 in any quarter.
3. **US aircraft tariff exemption revoked** (Section 232/301/IEEPA successor) — sell-side models a full 50% rate as −55–60% to 2026 earnings. Binary; do not wait for the print.
4. **Adj. EBIT margin below 9.0% for two consecutive quarters** (vs 10.0–10.6% guide) — confirms Q3's 10.9% was tariff relief and extraordinary credits rather than core operations.
5. **FY26 FCF below $400m** against the ≥$700m guide — destroys the cash-inflection leg.
6. **USD/BRL >6.00 with the BCB forced to hike** — Brazil fiscal accident; hedges flip from tailwind to mark-to-market loss.
7. **New FCPA/DOJ/CVM action**, or ITAR/end-use block on C-390 exports (UAE, India, Sweden/Netherlands).
8. **ROIC still below 10% at FY27** — the moat thesis is disproven; reclassify as a value trap.

**Trim trigger:** the framework's "90% of intrinsic value" is **already breached** at $82.50 against a $72 base. So trim on strength instead: **reduce ⅓ above $88** (bull case) and the balance above **$92.50** (street consensus top). For existing holders who bought at $30–55, Value's advice to ring the register into strength is well supported — this is a 15–20% premium to fair value, not a discount.

**Governance/ESG overlay:** apply a **+50–100bp cost-of-equity add-on** permanently for the golden share. Flag as **non-compliant for Article 8/9 funds** with defence exclusions or strict governance screens — the exclusion is structural, not transient.

## 7. Final Portfolio Action: **PASS** (new money) / **HOLD–TRIM** (existing)

**For a portfolio without EMBJ: PASS.** No initiation at $82.50. The operating story is genuinely excellent and verified — record backlog, revenue beats, IG balance sheet, real FCF inflection — but at 15–20% above base intrinsic value, 34x forward earnings with a Rule-of-40 of ~20 and ROIC at cost of capital, **you are being asked to pay a premium for a share-gainer.** Zero edge, not negative edge: the ~11–12% reverse-DCF IRR is roughly cost of equity. There is no asymmetry at this price and no reason to fund it today.

**For a portfolio already holding EMBJ: HOLD and TRIM into strength.** The thesis is intact and not deteriorating — do not exit on valuation alone while backlog and FCF are inflecting. But this is now consensus and fully priced, and the risk stack is unusual: 1.62 beta, 44.9% vol, a revocable tariff exemption on 59% of revenue, ITAR leverage on the growth leg, and a 20–25% Brazil fiscal tail. Reduce above $88; keep only a 1.5% residual core.

**Set a hard watch-list re-entry at $64–68**, and a *conditional* re-entry at current levels only if the 5-Mar-27 FY27 guide proves >$9.8bn revenue at 11%+ margin.

**One-line rationale:** Embraer is a better company than it was in 2022 and a worse investment at $82.50 — the backlog is real, but so is the premium; wait for the price, or wait for the proof.
