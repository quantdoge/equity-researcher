# Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Embraer S.A. (NYSE: EMBJ)

**Snapshot:** Brazilian aerospace & defense; mkt cap ~$15.1B; price $82.50 (10-Nov-2026); ~21,100 employees; CEO Francisco Gomes Neto (since May 2019). Price +54% since 10-Jun-26 ($53.51 → $82.50). Q3-26: revenue $2,347M (+17.2% y/y), record backlog, guidance raised to $8.4–8.6B revenue, 10.0–10.6% adj. EBIT margin, ≥$700M adj. FCF ex-Eve — but Q3 EPS *missed* consensus.

---

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **ADEQUATE** (structurally strong, structurally disenfranchising)

**Positives**
- Dispersed control since 2006 restructuring — **no controlling shareholder**; single class of common shares; **100% tag-along**; Novo Mercado (highest B3 governance tier).
- Board of 9–11 (8 independent), plus separate **Fiscal Council** reporting directly to shareholders — a genuine check US peers lack.
- Functioning committees: Strategy & Innovation; **Audit, Risk & Ethic**; Personnel & ESG. Internal Audit reports to the Audit/Risk/Ethic committee, not management.
- CEO appears separated from the chair role (Gomes Neto is President & CEO only); chair identity not independently verified — **low-confidence gap**.
- Nov-2026 LTIP proposal: share-settled awards capped at **5% of fully diluted capital**, 10-year grant window, **3-year minimum vesting**, **directors ineligible**.

**Negatives**
- **Golden share** held by the Brazilian federal government: veto over name/logo, military programs, third-party tech training, spare-parts discontinuance, change of control, bylaw amendments — plus prior approval of any tender offer. Embraer is partly a instrument of Brazilian industrial/defense policy.
- **Voting caps are severe**: no shareholder/group may vote >5% of shares; foreign holders collectively capped at 40% of votes present; 35%+ stake barred without government approval. This structurally entrenches incumbent management and makes the company effectively **unacquirable and un-activistable**. Takeover premium optionality is ~zero.
- LTIP dilutes up to 5% and **excludes cash-settled/phantom awards from the cap** — a compensation loophole.
- **FCPA legacy (2016):** $205M global resolution — $107.3M DOJ criminal penalty, $98.2M SEC disgorgement/interest, ~$20M Brazil — for bribery via a US subsidiary and third-party sales agents (Dominican Republic, Saudi Arabia, Mozambique, India). Three-year independent **monitorship closed ~mid-2020** after certification. Remediation appears complete; no new enforcement action surfaced in this window.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary — **Elevated, mostly trade policy rather than enforcement**

- **No active SEC/DOJ enforcement action found** for EMBJ in this window (regulatory-actions search returned empty; note this tool's coverage is limited, so treat as *absence of evidence*, not proof).
- **US–Brazil tariffs are the dominant regulatory variable.** The 2025 50% tariff hit Embraer exports; aircraft secured a carve-out, and the residual **10% tariff on Brazilian aircraft was subsequently removed**, restoring zero-tariff parity with Canada. As of 7-Oct-2026, non-exempt Brazilian goods still face a +25% Section 301 layer. Management has quantified tariff cost at **~$2M per aircraft**, with order delays/cancellations as the stated risk. **This relief is discretionary executive-branch action and is reversible without notice.**
- **Airworthiness directives — clustered and one emergency:** FAA/ANAC **Emergency AD 2026-08-52 (20–21 Apr 2026)** on the entire **Legacy 450/500 and Praetor 500/600** fleet (pitch trim actuator; uncommanded pitch / reduced controllability — an "unsafe condition" with immediate inspection). Also an AD on EMB-505 Phenom 300 (1-Jun-2026), a proposed AD on ERJ 190-300/-400 (E2) for **stringer/splice-fitting cracking**, and a hydraulic-locking proposed AD. Several hundred aircraft affected.
- Defense export campaigns (C-390 to Sweden 4+7 options, Netherlands/Austria 9, Uzbekistan, India with a proposed local assembly line) attract **offset, industrial-participation and intermediary** scrutiny — historically Embraer's exact corruption vector.
- No securities class action identified.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Single-source propulsion (Pratt & Whitney).** The E2 family depends solely on the PW1900G, which suffered a multi-year global GTF durability crisis. Grounded E2s are now down to ~5 and heading to zero, with full engine availability expected by year-end 2026 and P&W named Embraer's 2026 Supplier of the Year — i.e. **de-escalating but structurally unhedged**. Every GTF shop-visit queue becomes an Embraer delivery miss; Embraer cannot re-engine an aircraft.
- **Reversible trade policy.** The US is the core market for both commercial and executive jets. A zero-tariff position rests on an exemption, not a treaty. A single policy reversal re-imposes ~$2M/aircraft and threatens order deferrals against a record backlog.
- **Ramp execution and quality throughput.** Committing to 80–85 commercial + 160–170 executive deliveries against a record backlog, while absorbing clustered ADs (including a fleet-wide *emergency* AD on the highest-margin Praetor/Legacy line), points to **manufacturing/QC and supplier-conformance strain**. Also **key-person risk**: Gomes Neto has been the turnaround architect since 2019, and the Q3 call listed both a Head of IR and an EVP Financial & IR — suggesting recent turnover in the CFO/IR function (low confidence, worth a check).

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment — **Low-to-Moderate, with two live threads**

- **Commercial momentum is a reputational asset, not a liability:** record backlog, Abra Group (20 E195-E2 + options to 45), Wheels Up (6 Phenom 300E), IndiGo talks for ~100 regional jets, C-390 wins across Sweden/Netherlands/Austria/Uzbekistan. Embraer is currently the "clean" alternative to Boeing's quality crisis — valuable goodwill.
- **Thread 1 — safety perception.** An *emergency* AD is the most urgent form of safety regulation and carries media amplification disproportionate to its engineering scope. If the pitch-trim actuator issue traces to a manufacturing-process root cause rather than a supplier lot, the "quality" narrative that currently favors Embraer could invert.
- **Thread 2 — Eve Air Mobility (EVEX).** Embraer-controlled and consolidated: $356M cash, ~$55M quarterly burn, certification targeted **Dec-2028**, shrinking order book. Embraer's guidance is explicitly "FCF **w/o Eve**" — an admission of drag. Funding/dilution and a certification slip are live reputational and financial overhangs on the parent.
- FCPA history is 10 years stale and monitorship-closed; not a current reputational overhang. **ESG/social controversy: none material identified.**
- Note high headline beta: −12% on 8-May-26 (5.6M shares), −22.6% around weak 2026 guidance, +7% on the Nov-26 raise. Sentiment is fragile and event-driven.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

Strong formal governance, no controlling shareholder, monitorship closed, propulsion crisis resolving, tariffs currently favorable, and no active enforcement. Offsetting: a politically veto-holding state, hard voting caps that nullify shareholder voice, a clustered AD record touching the top-margin franchise, and a trade position resting on revocable discretion.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Emergency AD 2026-08-52** (Apr-2026) covering the *whole* Praetor 500/600 + Legacy 450/500 fleet for a flight-control actuator, alongside a Phenom 300 AD and E2 cracking AD. **Ask management directly: is the root cause a supplier lot or a manufacturing/QC process?** This is the single most under-priced item in the file.
2. **Insider filing direction unverified (EDGAR 403).** Three Form 4s on 27-Apr-2026 need confirmation as awards vs. sales into the rally. Do not accept "no insider selling" without primary verification.
3. **Tariff exemption is discretionary.** Model the exemption as a policy *grant*, not a structural right; size the exposure to US-bound commercial and executive deliveries.
4. **Defense-export intermediary risk** on India/Sweden/NATO C-390 campaigns given the 2016 FCPA facts (third-party agents, five countries). Request the compliance program's third-party due-diligence metrics — a second resolution would be materially worse than 2016.
5. **Governance disenfranchisement**: 5% voting cap + 40% foreign-vote cap + golden share. Any thesis predicated on strategic action, break-up or activism is not investable.
6. **LTIP with 5% dilution** and cash-settled awards outside the cap (EGM 30-Nov-2026) — watch for creeping dilution beyond the stated ceiling.
