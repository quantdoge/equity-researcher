**Investment Thesis**

Embraer is a genuinely better business than it was three years ago — record $35.7bn backlog (4.2x revenue), investment-grade near-net-cash balance sheet, FCF inflecting toward ≥$700m, and a defense/services mix shift lifting margins. But at $82.50 the market has already paid for all of it: the stock trades ~15–20% above base intrinsic value, at ~34x forward earnings with ROIC (~9–11%) only straddling its cost of capital. The core tension: **operating momentum is unambiguously bullish; valuation and a stack of binary policy risks (revocable US tariff exemption on ~59% of revenue, ITAR leverage over defense, 20–25% Brazil fiscal tail) argue for patience.** This is zero edge, not negative edge — no reason to fund a purchase today, no reason to dump a working position.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector:** Sector rated ATTRACTIVE — record industry backlog, mid-to-late upcycle on demand, early on margin recovery. EMBJ grows ~3pts above the A&D peer median with above-median operating margin (10.9% vs 8.5%) but ~830bps below GE Aerospace. Macro is NEUTRAL with negative skew: US 10Y at ~4.9–5.1% and 30Y at 5.47% attack both the discount rate and customers' financing capacity for the backlog. BRL's 10% depreciation is a margin tailwind *and* a symptom of the same fiscal distress that drives the risk — an unstable equilibrium.
- **Geopolitical & Legal:** MEDIUM risk. ~59% of US-touching revenue rests on a *discretionary* executive-branch tariff exemption (a full 50% tariff was modeled at −55–60% to 2026 earnings); ITAR/export controls give Washington a second lever over the fastest-growing defense segment. 2016 FCPA settlement ($205m) is resolved with monitorship closed; no live enforcement. Golden share and E175-E2 scope-clause blockade are structural.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value:** SELL, high conviction. Currency-consistent multiples: P/E ~34–44x, P/B 4.1x, P/FCF ~22x, FCF yield 4.6%. DCF base ~$63–72 vs $82.50 price. Above every published analyst target except JPM. Intangibles = 71% of equity; buy zone $48–62.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth:** BUY, medium conviction. ~16% revenue CAGR through 2027E, six consecutive record backlog prints, FCF margin inflecting 5%→8%+, reverse-DCF implies ~11–12% IRR — "good, not spectacular." Upgrade triggers: <$65 or FY27 guide >$9.8bn revenue at 11%+ margin.
- **Quantitative Signals:** Value screen #1 in sector but largely an FX artifact (BRL financials vs USD price); currency-normalized it's ~2 of 6 — cheap-ish, not anomalous. Momentum sector-best (+73% 1Y); Quality weak (ROE 9.2%, deteriorating ROIC); Low-Vol worst-in-sector (44.9% vol, 1.62 current beta). Earnings revisions positive (+11%).
- **Financial Risk:** MODERATE. Credit is solidly IG (BBB/Baa2, Fitch upgrade Sep-26), net debt ~0.1–0.2x EBITDA, no maturity wall — survivability in a −20% revenue shock is high (~9 years of cash runway). But equity risk is high: 45% vol, 1.62 beta, ~3x gross interest coverage, 188 days of inventory, and a working-capital-driven FCF stream that can reverse (Q1-26 OCF was −R$2.0bn).

**Bull Case**
- Record $35.7bn backlog (4.2x revenue) plus ~$23bn of options converts multi-year revenue from estimate to schedule; defense backlog +56% y/y.
- Quasi-monopoly in US scope-clause regional jets plus the only modern Western medium airlifter (C-390) — two franchises with no credible competitor.
- FCF inflection (≥$700m FY26E vs ~$390m FY25) with an IG, ~0.1x-levered balance sheet; FY27 guide (5-Mar-27) could force estimate upgrades if >$9.8bn revenue at 11%+ margin.

**Bear Case**
- Priced for perfection: 15–20% above base IV, above nearly all Street targets, +4.7x since Jan-2024; even the bull case ($88) offers only ~7%.
- Binary policy risk: the US tariff exemption covering ~59% of revenue is revocable discretion, not a treaty right; ITAR can gate the defense ramp.
- Cash quality and margin quality are softer than headlines: FCF leans on customer pre-payments, Q3 margin was flattered by tariff relief/extraordinary credits, and FY25 EBIT fell 46% while the stock doubled — Q4 2025 (revenue +1.6%, GM −190bp) is the template for a bad quarter.

**Key Risks**
1. Delivery guidance cut below 80 commercial / 160 executive — the single highest-multiple-destroying event (guidance unchanged for two years while revenue guidance rose).
2. US tariff exemption revocation (Section 232/301/IEEPA successor) — modeled −55–60% earnings impact; do not wait for the print.
3. Brazil fiscal accident: USD/BRL >6.00 forcing BCB hikes — hedges flip from tailwind to mark-to-market loss (~20–25% probability per Macro).
4. GTF/Pratt engine availability capping the E2 ramp; a second emergency AD inverting the "clean alternative to Boeing" narrative.
5. Working-capital reversal: R$19bn inventory (28% of assets) and PDP-funded FCF could turn cash flow negative, as in Q1-26.
6. FX noise in reported EPS (Q3-26 adj. net income −66% q/q on a revenue beat) triggering sentiment-driven de-ratings in a 1.62-beta stock.
