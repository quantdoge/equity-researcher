## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **Investment Grade**

REGN carries a fortress, net-cash balance sheet. External ratings confirm this: **S&P 'BBB+' (affirmed Aug 2025, stable outlook)** and **Moody's 'Baa3'** — solid investment grade. Internally the metrics are far stronger than the rating implies:

- **Net cash of ~$5.9B** (cash & ST investments $8.61B vs total debt $2.71B) — net debt/EBITDA of **−1.01x**.
- **Interest coverage of ~82x** (EBIT $5.27B / interest $43.8M) — the interest burden is immaterial.
- **Debt/equity 0.09, debt/assets 0.07** — minimal leverage.
- Only **$1.99B of long-term debt, zero short-term/current debt** — no refinancing cliff.
- **Altman Z-Score 4.44 (Safe zone)**, comfortably above the 2.99 threshold even using book equity.

**Conclusion: effectively a AA-quality balance sheet wrapped in a BBB+ rating.** Default risk over any realistic horizon is negligible.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **LOW**

The balance sheet is a genuine fortress: net cash, minimal debt, zero near-term maturities, ~82x interest coverage, a 4.13 current ratio, $13.6B working capital, and investment-grade ratings. Cash generation ($4.1B FCF) is strong and comfortably self-funding. Financial solvency risk is about as low as it gets in large-cap biotech.

The **residual risk is business/earnings risk, not balance-sheet risk** — concentration in EYLEA and Dupixent, and a decelerating top line (+1% in FY2025), create high *equity* volatility (37% vol, −58% drawdown). This is priced as a quality-compounder-deceleration story, not a credit story.
