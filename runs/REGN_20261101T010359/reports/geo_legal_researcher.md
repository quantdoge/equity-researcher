*As of the most recent filings available: Q3 2026 10-Q (filed 30-Oct-2026); FY2025 10-K (accession 0000872589-26-000008).*

### 1. Geographic Risk Map

Regeneron reports **US product sales directly** (EYLEA/EYLEA HD, Libtayo US, Praluent US, Evkeeza, ARCALYST) and books **ex-US economics through profit-share collaboration revenue** — Bayer records EYLEA outside the US and Regeneron records its share of ex-US profits; Sanofi records Dupixent globally (Regeneron shares global profits, with the majority of Dupixent volume outside the US). This structural feature means reported revenue overstates US concentration vs. true end-market exposure.

Q3 2026: product sales $1,805.3M vs. collaboration revenue $3,000.5M — **~59% of revenue is now collaboration-based**, i.e. heavily tied to Sanofi/Bayer ex-US performance (SEC 10-Q, 30-Oct-2026, via StockTitan summary).

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**(1) US drug-pricing and trade policy — highest-probability risk (Probability: Very High / Impact: Medium-High)**
- **April 23, 2026:** Regeneron signed a **most-favored-nation (MFN) pricing deal** with the White House — Medicaid price reductions across current and future drugs, Praluent cut from $537 to $225 via TrumpRx, plus a commitment to supply/provide gene therapy capacity in the US (White House fact sheet, Apr-2026; Reuters, 23-Apr-2026; PBS).
- **Section 232 tariffs:** A proclamation effective **31-Jul-2026** imposes a **100% ad valorem tariff** on patented pharmaceutical imports. Critically, only companies with an MFN agreement **entered into before 2-Apr-2026 and listed in Annex II are exempt**; companies in **Annex III** (17 named companies) face the 100% rate unless they secure a company-specific **onshoring agreement** (White House proclamation, Apr-2026; Crowell, Arnold & Porter, BIS Federal Register notice 13-May-2026). **Regeneron's MFN deal post-dates the 2-Apr-2026 cutoff**, so its Annex II/III status is the single most under-appreciated policy risk for a company that manufactures biologics in **Ireland** for US supply. *Unverified in the sources retrieved — priority diligence item.*
- **IRA Medicare negotiation:** EYLEA/EYLEA HD and Dupixent are repeatedly identified as top-spend negotiation candidates, with CMS's 2028 cycle (selected Jan-2026) covering 15 Part B and D drugs (CMS; JMCP analyses).

**(2) EYLEA franchise erosion / biosimilar-driven revenue concentration (Probability: High / Impact: High — legal-geopolitical hybrid)**
Aflibercept competition is now structural: Amgen's **Pavblu** holds ~30% of the non-HD aflibercept market (~9% of the total market) after litigation losses for Regeneron; **Celltrion settled and may launch 31-Dec-2026**; a **February 11, 2026 settlement dismissed a previously disclosed patent suit** (SEC 10-Q footnote; FiercePharma; Biosimilars R&R, Feb-2026). EYLEA US sales fell 42% in 2025 vs. 2024 (10-K). Biosimilar entry timing is set by US courts and BPCIA outcomes — a legal process with direct revenue consequences.

**(3) Alliance-partner and cross-border dependency (Probability: Medium / Impact: Medium-High)**
With ~59% of Q3 2026 revenue from collaboration lines, Regeneron's P&L is levered to **Sanofi (France)** and **Bayer (Germany)** — both exposed to EU pricing/reimbursement reform, EU geopolitics and FX. Mitigant: the **October 2026 Sanofi amendment** added four Regeneron-invented antibodies with a **$1.0bn upfront and up to $7.0bn in milestones**, deepening rather than diversifying the relationship.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags
- **Government price reporting integrity (ASP/AMP/Medicaid Best Price)** — the DOJ FCA theory is precisely the control-regime that MFN/Medicaid commitments now expand. Highest-priority compliance area; expect continued state AG activity.
- **MFN/TrumpRx agreement terms** — new contractual undertakings on Medicaid pricing and direct-to-consumer channels; failure-to-perform risk is political as well as legal.
- **IRA negotiation participation** — mandatory; non-compliance carries excise-tax exposure.
- **No evidence found** of Russia-sanctions, FCPA/bribery, or AML exposure in the retrieved record; China exposure is partner-mediated and small.
- Data-privacy: US/EU clinical and genomics data (Regeneron Genetics Center) — GDPR-heavy but no disclosed enforcement action.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical/Legal Risk Rating: **MEDIUM**

**Rationale:** Regeneron's geographic footprint is unusually benign for a global biopharma — a US-domiciled company whose ex-US exposure runs through blue-chip OECD partners (Sanofi, Bayer) rather than emerging markets, with negligible Russia/China/sanctions exposure. The risk is not geopolitical instability but **concentrated US policy risk** (MFN pricing, IRA negotiation, and unresolved Section 232 tariff classification of its Irish manufacturing) plus a **live DOJ False Claims Act case** and a newly consolidated **Dupixent MDL**. Any single one is manageable; their convergence in 2026–2028 compresses pricing power and adds legal tail risk precisely as the EYLEA franchise erodes.

**Two diligence gaps to close:** (i) Regeneron's Annex II / Annex III classification under the Section 232 pharma proclamation, and whether an onshoring agreement has been filed with BIS; (ii) the quantum and reserve status of the DOJ FCA matter and the MDL 3180 claim count.
