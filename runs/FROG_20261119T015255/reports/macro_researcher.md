## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: TRANSITIONAL — "late-cycle re-tightening," with a stark split between real-economy resilience and an increasingly hostile discount-rate regime.**

The defining feature of this cycle is that the Fed's 2025 easing campaign has been reversed. The effective fed funds rate fell from 4.64% (Nov 2024) to 3.63% by mid-2026, then the FOMC **resumed hiking** — 3.75% in August, 3.88% in September/October — with the target range now 3.75%–4.00%, held at the October meeting on a split vote. Street consensus expects **one further hike by year-end**, then a prolonged pause. The reason is unambiguous: inflation has re-accelerated in H2 2026. CPI went from 332.6 (June) to 337.5 (Oct) — a **5.7% three-month annualised run-rate** — lifting headline YoY back to **3.82%**. Crucially, the labour market is *not* cracking (4.1% unemployment, down from 4.5% a year ago) and real GDP grew **~3.4% annualised in Q3**. This is inflation pressure arriving with full employment, i.e. the Fed has no room to look through it.

The bond market's response is the single most important macro fact for FROG: a **bear steepener**. The 10Y has risen **69bp since 30 June** (4.44% → 5.13%, peaking at 5.31% on 5 Oct) while the curve sits at 2Y 4.74% / 10Y 5.13% / 30Y 5.47% — a **+39bp 2s10s spread**. No inversion means no classic recession signal, but a rising term premium means a rising *discount rate* — the direct enemy of a stock trading at ~20x sales. Credit is still accommodating (HY OAS 3.43%) but has widened **78bp since 1 September**, an early-warning signal worth watching. The broad dollar has firmed **+3.8% in two months** (118.21 → 122.65).

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL (negative skew)**

**Fundamentals are Favourable; the valuation regime is Unfavourable. The two net to Neutral, but the asymmetry is to the downside.**

The operating environment for JFrog is arguably the best in its history — AI-driven artifact growth, cloud mix inflecting (46% → 57% in a year), RPO +60%, and Gartner's 15% IT-spend growth for 2027. None of that is threatened by a 3.8% CPI print.

But the macro *price* of those cash flows has deteriorated materially. The Fed is hiking into full employment, the 10Y has repriced 69bp in under five months, and the term premium is rebuilding. FROG's multiple — 19x sales and 99x earnings — is the most rate-sensitive instrument in the software complex. The stock has so far *ignored* this, returning +137% over twelve months; that divergence between a rising discount rate and an expanding multiple is precisely what makes the position fragile. Note the template: on **20 February 2026** FROG fell **24% in a single session** (from ~$50 to $37.75 on 15M shares). Duration cuts both ways.

Net: **hold the fundamental view, discount the multiple.**

## 5. The Surprise Scenario (Bear, ~30% probability)

**"The Fed has to hike twice — and the AI multiple breaks."**

The 5.7% three-month annualised CPI run-rate is not a one-print anomaly; it is a trend that began in July. If it persists, the December hike becomes two hikes (to 4.25–4.50% by Q1 2027), the 10Y clears the 5.31% October high and tests **5.50%**, and — critically — HY OAS widens from 3.43% toward 5%, turning the "soft landing" narrative into a "policy error" narrative.

In that world the AI trade de-rates before it de-earns. Long-duration software at 19x sales compresses 30–40% with no change to estimates. FROG, with a 1.9% FCF yield against a 5.5% risk-free rate, a 1.31 beta, and a consumption-based revenue model that becomes a liability the moment customers start optimising AI spend, is the highest-torque expression of that trade. A retest of the February 2026 lows (~$34) — a ~70% drawdown — is the arithmetic outcome of a 4-turn de-rating plus a modest estimate cut.

**The mirror-image bull case** (worth ~25% odds): the 2026 tariff/energy impulse rolls off, CPI falls to ~2.5% by spring 2027, the Fed pivots to cuts, and the 10Y retreats to 4.20%. A 90bp fall in yields, applied to a 20x-sales compounder growing 30%, would add roughly 20% of pure multiple expansion on top of organic growth — the mechanism that produced the +137% trailing year in the first place. **Either way, FROG's next 12 months will be decided more by the 10-year Treasury than by its NDR.**
