### Investment Memo: FN (Fabrinet) — November 13, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD — new money may initiate a *starter position only* (≤1.25%), with adds gated on the February print | **Conviction: Medium** on the business, **Low-Medium** on the equity at $328
**Price Target**: $400 base (range $270 bear / $520 bull), +22% from $327.98
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months, with a mandatory re-underwrite at FQ2'27 earnings (~Feb 1, 2027)

**Investment Thesis**
Fabrinet is a best-in-class optical contract manufacturer riding the AI datacenter build-out — revenue accelerating four straight quarters to +51% YoY, a 7/7 beat rate, and a $3B capacity build that only makes sense against booked demand. The stock has already been de-rated 56% on multiple compression, not demand. The entire debate is one number: whether FY27 EPS power is ~$19 (consensus non-GAAP) or ~$13 (GAAP run-rate with a normalized ~29% tax rate and D&A catching up to capex). At $328, the stock is cheap on the bull math (16.6x) and fully valued on the bear math (~26x) — so position size, not direction, is where the edge is.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector ATTRACTIVE — AI optics mid-to-late upcycle, 800G mainstream, 1.6T ramping; FN is the best operator in the group (10% op margin, ~2x EMS median, 2.3x sales). But macro is UNFAVOURABLE: Fed hiking (4.00%), 10Y at 5.10% (+65bp since June), 30Y 5.47%. FN is being de-rated by the yield curve, not its end market; a 10Y >5.75% is the scenario where debt-financed hyperscaler capex math breaks and FN falls another 35–45% on no bad news.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH risk. ~All manufacturing in Thailand under a 19% US reciprocal tariff and Tier-2 transshipment classification. The FCC's draft ban on Chinese transceivers is a genuine **binary on 53% of revenue** — share-shift tailwind if scoped to finished Chinese models, direct hit if scoped to PRC-origin content (FN runs China operations via Casix). Clean litigation record.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD. Narrow moat, 18.8% ROIC, net cash — but intrinsic value ~$400 (base) means an 18% discount, short of the 25–30% margin of safety required for a price-taker with 59% customer concentration and ~zero FCF. Buyer at $280–300.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (high conviction). +36→39→45→51% sequential acceleration, 100% beat rate, PEG 0.40, 9.2x FY30E EPS; 2–3:1 upside/downside asymmetry. Withheld Strong Buy solely over the unexplained GAAP/non-GAAP gap.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL. Momentum −33% (12-1), beta 3.0 with R² 0.28 (72% idiosyncratic), alpha −6.5% — high beta without compensation. Cheapest profitable name in the optical/EMS set on forward multiples.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE (split). Solvency LOW — net cash $871M, Z-score 4.12; a −20% revenue stress *improves* FCF to ~$658M via working-capital release. But cash conversion broke: $473M FY26 net income → $3M FCF; inventory +76% vs revenue +36%.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Top-3 customers = 46.7% of revenue, no long-term contracts. Insider flow $17M sold / $0 bought; CEO liquidated ~43% of his direct stake (10b5-1 status unverified). CEO/Chair duality, classified board, Cayman domicile.
- **ESG**: NEUTRAL/AVERAGE. Low carbon intensity, best-in-class safety; the under-priced E risk is a repeat 2011-style Thai flood during a capacity ramp. Governance 5/10.
- **Alternative Data**: Fundamentals bullish (beats, hiring mix skewed to production, capacity commitments, positive regulatory backdrop); sentiment/momentum bearish — retail attention at 12-month lows, tape repeatedly sells good prints (−19% on an August *beat*). Contrarian-constructive, not a timing signal.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT, moderate conviction — *not* fraud. Core finding: consensus FY27 EPS of ~$19.71 is ~55% above what 11.8% gross margin plus a normalized tax rate produces (~$12.70); D&A under-earned 7.3x vs capex; FCF negative four quarters. Capped by accelerating revenue, pristine balance sheet, and discretionary (stoppable) capex.

**Bull Case**
- Demand is real and accelerating: datacenter +78% YoY, Q2 guide +43%, capex tripling *into* booked multi-year programs — the strongest non-financial corroboration available.
- Policy tailwind: China+1 re-routing plus the FCC rule positions FN's Thailand base as the default ex-China optical assembly destination; every point of Innolight/Eoptolink share that migrates is available to FN.
- Downside floor: net-cash balance sheet, ~18% below base intrinsic value, and working capital that self-liquidates in a downturn — the Fin Risk stress case shows FN *strengthens* through a 20% revenue decline.

**Bear Case**
- Earnings quality: FY26 EPS was flattered by a ~0% tax rate; Q1 FY27 GAAP tax hit ~29% and net income grew only +11% on +51% revenue. The non-GAAP guide implies a ~70% jump over GAAP run-rate — unresolved.
- Growth without cash: FCF negative four consecutive quarters, gross margin down four straight years (12.71%→11.79%), SG&A leverage exhausted at 1.8%, depreciation wave still ahead.
- Structure: 47–59% customer concentration with NVIDIA ~28%, beta 3.0/85% vol in a hiking cycle, insiders selling, and CPO as a long-dated architectural threat defined by its largest customer.

**Key Risks**
1. NVIDIA/Cisco program loss, in-sourcing, or dual-source award (existential; flagged independently by five agents)
2. February 10-Q: GAAP→non-GAAP tax reconciliation and gross margin trajectory — the verification gate
3. Depreciation cliff as ~$600M+/yr capex enters service against a 12% gross margin
4. FCC rule scope (PRC-content capture), Thailand tariff/transshipment enforcement, flood concentration
5. Macro: sustained 10Y >5.75%; IG OAS breaking >1.30% (currently 0.89%)

Initiate **1.25% now, hard cap 2.5%**, funded *from* any existing AI-optics sleeve — FN correlates 0.81 with COHR and 0.72 with LITE; it is not a diversifier. Add 0.75% only on FQ2'27 delivering revenue ≥$1.63B **and** GM ≥12.5% **and** a benign tax reconciliation (or at ≤$295); final 0.50% on two quarters of positive FCF or disclosed concentration <50%. Exit on any two of: two consecutive QoQ datacenter declines, GAAP GM <11.5%, NVIDIA/Cisco dual-sourcing news, negative FCF into FY28 with debt >$300M, adverse FCC scope, or 10Y >5.75%. Trim ⅓ at $450, ⅓ at $520–560. Use limit orders; do not initiate within 5 days of a print.
