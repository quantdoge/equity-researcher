## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (leaning constructive, but not actionable at full size)

There is **high agreement on the business, low agreement on the earnings number, and outright disagreement on the price.**

**Consensus (8+ agents agree):**
- Demand is real and accelerating — data center +78% YoY, four quarters of acceleration, 100% beat rate, $3B capacity build.
- Customer concentration (~47–58% across top 3–4 names) is the **single largest destroyer of value** in the file. Non-Fin, Geo Legal, Value, Growth, Short and Fin Risk all independently flag it.
- Gross margin is structurally capped and drifting down (12.71% → 11.79%, four straight years). No pricing power. Multiple will never re-rate to component-owner levels.
- Balance sheet is pristine and insolvency is not a coherent risk.
- The de-rating is a **multiple event, not a demand event**.

**Genuinely contested — and this is the whole trade:**
| Question | Bull (Growth, Alt Data) | Bear (Short, Value) |
|---|---|---|
| FY27 EPS power | $17.90–19.71 non-GAAP | **$12.70** at current GM + normalized tax |
| Is capex negative-FCF "growth" or "stranded"? | Growth — against booked programs | Stranded — D&A under-earned 7.3x, margin cliff |
| Is the FCC rule a tailwind? | Yes — share shifts to Thailand | **Binary** — FN has PRC footprint too |

**Conflict resolution (my job):** The Short Analyst's tax-normalization finding is the highest-quality differentiated insight in the entire stack — it is corroborated independently by Value ("FY26 EPS flattered, normalized ~$11.5 not $13.05"), Growth ("excluding a *tax charge* from non-GAAP is aggressive"), and Non-Fin ("Pillar Two a live GAAP drag"). Three agents converging on an earnings-quality defect outranks the Growth Researcher's forward multiple, **because the forward multiple is computed on the number in dispute.** The Growth Researcher itself concedes this is "the #1 item to verify before sizing up."

**However** — the Short Analyst is shorting a stock already down 56% with accelerating revenue and net cash. That is a low-quality short. The correct synthesis is neither Buy nor Short: it is **a small, staged long with a hard size cap and an explicit verification gate.** The equity is cheap on the bull's math and expensive on the bear's; with 85% volatility, position size — not direction — is where the edge is.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.25% of portfolio now, maximum 2.5%**

**Kelly logic:** Edge is genuine but the odds distribution is bimodal, not normal — outcome is roughly binary on whether FY27 EPS is $13 or $19 and whether hyperscaler capex holds. Binary outcomes argue for **fractional Kelly (¼ to ⅓)**, not full.

| Input | Value | Effect on size |
|---|---|---|
| Intrinsic value (Value, base) | ~$400 (+22% vs price) | Positive — small edge |
| Bull case (Growth, FY30 at 2.0x EV/S) | ~20% IRR | Positive |
| Bear case (Short: 26x on $12.70 / Macro: −35–45%) | −35% to −55% | **Dominant negative** |
| Realized vol 85.3%, beta 3.03 | 4x market | **Severe size constraint** |
| FCF = $3.0M on $473M NI | Quality flag | Negative |
| Customer concentration 47% | Binary tail risk | Negative |
| Macro (10Y 5.10%) | Multiple headwind | Negative |

**Sizing ladder:**
- **Day 1: 1.25%** — a starter, sized so a −55% drawdown costs **~70bp of portfolio**. Survivable and re-enterable.
- **+0.75%** only if FQ2'27 (Feb 1) delivers: revenue ≥$1.63B, **gross margin ≥12.5%**, and a GAAP→non-GAAP reconciliation showing the ~29% tax rate is a step-up in the ongoing rate rather than a discrete charge.
- **+0.50%** only if FCF turns positive for two consecutive quarters OR customer concentration disclosed below 50%.
- **Hard cap 2.5%.** Do not breach it regardless of price action — at beta 3.0 a 2.5% position carries the risk budget of a 7.5% market-beta position.

**Why not 3–5%** (the high-conviction band): that band requires low risk or resolved earnings quality. FN has neither — it has 85% volatility, an unresolved $6/share EPS question, and a 47% customer concentration. **Why not pass:** the de-rating is nearly complete on the bear math, the balance sheet has no solvency risk, forward estimates are still rising, and the asymmetry the Growth Researcher documents (+20% IRR base / −24% bear on FY30 revenue math) is real.
