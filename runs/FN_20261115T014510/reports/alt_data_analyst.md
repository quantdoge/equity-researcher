## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH** (fundamentals) / **NEUTRAL-BEARISH** (sentiment & momentum)

**Net: Constructive, contrarian Bullish.** Alt data shows a company whose physical business is accelerating (record revenue, $3B capacity build, production hiring, benign/positive regulatory backdrop) while every attention and sentiment proxy has reset to 12-month lows. That is the classic alt-data setup for a positive surprise — but it is *not* a timing signal, because the same data cannot tell us whether the de-rating reflects (a) transient AI-capex indigestion, or (b) the market correctly discounting negative FCF, margin dilution from new program ramps, and China-part regulatory exposure. Note the tape has repeatedly sold good prints (Aug 18: −19% on a +7.6% EPS beat) — an earnings beat alone has not been sufficient to move the stock.

**What would confirm the bullish read:** FQ2'27 revenue >$1.63B with gross margin stable, capex guidance peaking, and FCF turning positive. **What would break it:** a guidance miss or evidence hyperscaler transceiver orders are pausing ahead of the FCC rule.
