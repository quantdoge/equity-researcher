## 1. Growth Story

Fabrinet is the invisible assembly layer of the AI optical interconnect build-out: it manufactures the 800G/1.6T transceivers, optical engines and precision photonics that link GPU clusters for Nvidia, Cisco and now Amazon directly. The thesis just got *better*, not worse — Q1 FY27 (Sep-26) revenue grew **51% YoY to a record $1.47B**, with data center revenue +78% YoY and **+16% QoQ**, which decisively reverses the single datacom dip (−1% QoQ vs Nvidia) that triggered a −19% one-day crash in Aug-2026.

The stock, however, has de-rated **−56% from its $746 May-2026 peak** to 16.6x FY27E EPS and 1.67x FY27E EV/sales while estimates rise. That gap between accelerating fundamentals and a collapsed multiple is the entire opportunity.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

**Core TAM — AI data center optical interconnect.** Industry estimates put the optical transceiver/module market in the ~$20–25B range today, compounding at 20%+ toward $40–50B by 2030 as clusters scale from 800G to 1.6T and 3.2T. Every GPU rack adds optics content super-linearly — scale-out, scale-up, and DCI layers all convert to fiber.

- **Data center** = $777M/qtr (53% of revenue), annualizing ~$3.1B, growing 78%. If the AI optics module TAM is ~$25B in CY27, FN's data-center revenue (~$3.6–4.0B FY27) implies **~15% of module value** — but a far higher share of *leading-edge module assembly*, where yields and 1.6T active-alignment capability gate supply.
- **Communications infrastructure** = $444M/qtr (30%), +35% YoY — telecom/DCI upcycle layering on top.
- **Auto/industrial/medical** = $253M/qtr (17%), +20% — a genuinely counter-cyclical third leg (lasers, sensors, medical).

**Adjacency optionality:** co-packaged optics (CPO) and silicon photonics. The bear case is that CPO disintermediates pluggables; the more likely outcome is that CPO *increases* required precision packaging, and Fabrinet is positioning to assemble those engines. Consensus FY30 revenue of $11.3B (25% 4-yr CAGR) requires no share-gain heroics — only that FN holds share in a market growing ~20%.

**Moat:** 21,500 employees, vertically integrated optical process engineering (crystal growth, thin-film coating, precision/active alignment), Thailand cost base with BOI tax incentives, and a 25-year qualification history on the highest-complexity modules. This is a *manufacturing* moat — switching costs are real but it deserves an EMS multiple, not a software multiple.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q2 FY27 earnings — ~Feb 1, 2027.** Guide: $1.60–1.65B revenue, $4.77–4.92 non-GAAP EPS (+44% YoY). Consensus already at $1.627B/$4.87 — the bar is *in line*, so the swing factor is whether data center re-accelerates again QoQ and whether guidance beats.
- **1.6T transceiver volume ramp through CY2027.** 1.6T modules reportedly carry richer gross margin than 800G; a mix shift is the single most credible path to breaking the 12% GM ceiling.
- **Nvidia Rubin / Spectrum-X photonics cycle.** FN's Nvidia content resets with each networking platform generation; new program wins would restore the growth premium.
- **Hyperscaler-direct diversification.** Amazon became an ~11% customer in FY2026 alongside Cisco (~20%) and Nvidia (~28–30%) — direct hyperscaler relationships reduce single-customer dependence and carry better visibility.
- **Capacity coming online.** New Thailand footprint funded by the FY26/Q1 FY27 capex burst should convert to revenue in FY27–28 — a utilization/operating-leverage catalyst with a visible timeline.
- **$637.3M buyback authorization** (~5.4% of market cap), upsized by $103M in Nov-2026 — a floor under a de-rated stock.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction; Strong Buy trigger below)

The market is pricing Fabrinet as if the Nvidia slowdown were structural. The evidence says it was a program transition: datacom fell 1% QoQ in Q4 FY26, then data center revenue grew **16% QoQ / 78% YoY** the very next quarter, with Q2 guidance implying +43.5% YoY. Meanwhile the stock fell another 35% *after* that resolution — from $483 post-crash to $315 on Nov 2 — on a broad AI-hardware rotation plus two B.Riley target cuts ($598 → $431 → $364, "sidelined on valuation").

**What you are buying at $328:** 16.6x FY27E EPS and 1.67x FY27E sales for 42% forward growth, 9.2x FY30E EPS four years out, and a net-cash balance sheet. **Implied math:** EV $11.0B today. Deliver consensus FY30 revenue of $11.3B at even 2.0x EV/sales → EV ~$22.7B, **~20% IRR**; at 2.5x → ~27% IRR. Downside if AI optics growth stalls at ~$7B revenue and 1.2x sales → EV ~$8.4B, roughly **−24%**. That is a favorable 2–3:1 asymmetry on a name with a 100% beat rate and a capacity expansion that only makes sense if demand is real.

**Upgrade to Strong Buy if:** (a) Q2 FY27 data center revenue grows another mid-teens % QoQ, and (b) gross margin inflects above 12.5% on 1.6T mix. **Downgrade to Hold if:** a second consecutive datacom/Nvidia decline appears, or gross margin breaks below 11.5%.

I am not going to Strong Buy today for one reason that should not be glossed over: **the GAAP/non-GAAP gap is unusually wide and unexplained.** Q1 FY27 GAAP EPS was $2.86 but non-GAAP was $4.34 — a $1.48 gap, with GAAP net income $103.5M vs non-GAAP $157.4M. GAAP tax expense appears to have been ~$42M (a ~29% rate) versus a non-GAAP tax line of just $6M, against a historical tax rate near zero due to Thailand BOI holidays. Excluding a *tax charge* from non-GAAP is aggressive; if tax incentives are structurally expiring rather than this being a discrete item, GAAP EPS power is materially lower. **This is the #1 item to verify in the 10-Q before sizing up.**

## 7. Key Risks

- **Customer concentration (existential).** Nvidia ~28–30% of revenue, Cisco ~20%, Amazon ~11% → top three >55%. A single Nvidia program loss, or Nvidia in-sourcing/dual-sourcing optics, cuts the thesis at the root. This is a concentrated bet on one customer's roadmap, not a diversified compounder.
- **CPO technology disruption.** If co-packaged optics replaces pluggable modules faster than FN can pivot to assembling CPO engines, the addressable content per port could compress. Management's positioning is credible but unproven at scale.
- **Structurally thin gross margin.** 12% and *declining* for three straight years (12.71% → 11.99%), with FX (Thai baht) and annual merit increases as persistent headwinds. FN has essentially no pricing power; there is no 70%-margin software leverage hiding here. Operating leverage is the only earnings lever, and it is nearly exhausted at 10% op margin.
- **Capex / FCF trap (the classic EMS risk).** FY26 FCF was $3M; Q1 FY27 FCF was meaningfully negative. If AI infrastructure digestion arrives while $250M+/yr of new capacity is still ramping, FN faces stranded assets, utilization-driven margin compression, and ROIC collapse. The Rule of 40 of 35.8 is the honest read of the current cash reality.
- **Tax / earnings-quality risk.** As above — an unexplained ~29% GAAP tax rate versus a historically near-zero rate. Unresolved.
- **Momentum and estimate-revision risk.** Two consecutive B.Riley target cuts, a broken chart (−56% from the high, lower highs since June), and the stock now trading as an AI-beta proxy — it will remain violently correlated to Nvidia headlines regardless of its own execution.
- **Competition.** Coherent, Lumentum, Innolight/Eoptolink, Foxconn, Jabil and Delta all chase the same 1.6T sockets; Chinese vendors are aggressive on price at 800G.
