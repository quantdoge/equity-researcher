# SECTOR BRIEF — CoreWeave (CRWV)
### Neocloud / AI Compute Infrastructure · As of 2 Nov 2026 · Price $50.15 · Mkt cap $27.7B

---

## 1. Sector Overview

CoreWeave is classified GICS "Technology — Software & Infrastructure," but its economic comparable set is the **AI neocloud complex** (Nebius, IREN, Applied Digital, Lambda, Crusoe) competing with hyperscaler AI capacity (Azure, AWS, OCI) for GPU-accelerated compute. Demand is genuinely explosive: CoreWeave's revenue backlog compounded from **$15.1B (Dec'24) → $66.8B (Dec'25) → $99.4B (Mar'26)**, and FY25 revenue grew **+168% to $5.13B**. The sector has, however, entered a distinct **financing-constrained shakeout phase**: the constraint has shifted from demand to the cost and availability of capital. AI-infrastructure credit has repriced sharply — Oracle's $10B bond sale and Applied Digital's admission that 800MW remains unfinanced both triggered sector-wide selloffs in the first week of November. The sector is roughly **3 years into a 5–7 year capacity buildout** — early-growth on volume, but late-cycle on capital intensity and multiple expansion.

## 2. Key Tailwinds

- **Contracted, multi-year demand visibility.** $99.4B backlog vs ~$10B annualised revenue = **~10x coverage**, materially de-risking near-term revenue. Backlog grew 6.6x in 15 months.
- **Structural supply scarcity in power, shells and NVIDIA allocation.** Barriers are physical (grid interconnection, 3–5yr lead times), not just financial — entrenching incumbents who already hold contracted capacity.
- **Inference is a larger, stickier TAM than training.** Inference workloads are recurring and less lumpy, supporting higher utilisation and longer contract terms than one-off training clusters.

## 3. Key Headwinds

- **Cost of capital repricing is the single dominant risk.** CoreWeave's interest expense reached **$640M in Q2'26 alone (~$2.5B annualised)** against $1.47B quarterly EBITDA — absorbing ~44% of EBITDA. Sector-wide bond spreads have widened through 2026.
- **Memory/component inflation squeezing unit economics.** HBM and DRAM pricing (SK Hynix, Micron) is running at records, and 2026 capex guidance of **$31–35B** is being funded into a rising-rate environment. Gross margin has compressed **74.3% (FY24) → 71.7% (FY25) → 65.9% (Q2'26)**.
- **Customer concentration and hyperscaler self-supply.** Microsoft has historically been >50% of CoreWeave revenue; meanwhile AWS Trainium, Google TPU and Microsoft's own fleet are structural substitutes that cap pricing power at renewal.

## 5. Target Company's Competitive Position — **Strong (operationally) / Below-average (financially)**

**Rationale for Strong:**
- Clear neocloud scale leader — $10.3B revenue run-rate vs NBIS $0.53B, IREN $0.71B, APLD $0.61B.
- Best-in-class EBITDA conversion: **57% EBITDA margin** and $1.47B quarterly EBITDA, versus materially negative operating margins at every pure-play peer.
- Exit-velocity revenue growth (+168%) with ~10x backlog coverage and 2,189 employees — capital efficiency per head is exceptional.

**Rationale for the caveat:**
- **Beta of 7.67** and a 60% drawdown from $124.82 (1 Jun'26) to $50.15 — the equity is behaving as a levered proxy on AI credit, not as an operating business.
- Depreciation ($1.39B/qtr) plus interest ($640M/qtr) = **~$8.1B annualised fixed charges** against a $31–35B capex programme; the funding gap is the thesis risk.
- Gross margin is deteriorating as older GPU vintages depreciate — the core question is whether contract escalators outrun obsolescence.

## 6. Sector Rating: **NEUTRAL** (demand Attractive / capital-cycle Unattractive)

The sector's fundamental demand signal is the strongest in the market — a $99B backlog does not lie. But the sector is in the **unwind of a financing-driven upcycle**: gross margins are compressing across every operator (CRWV −8pts, ORCL −6pts in a year), the marginal dollar of capacity is being funded at rising spreads, and equity markets have begun discriminating violently between self-funding hyperscalers and debt-funded neoclouds. We would characterise this as **Attractive demand meeting an Unattractive capital cycle**, netting to Neutral. For CRWV specifically, the operating franchise is the best asset in the neocloud tier; the balance sheet and the beta are the reason the equity trades at an all-time low. **Sector position favours owners of capital (MSFT, ORCL, NVDA, memory) over renters of capital (CRWV, NBIS, IREN, APLD) until credit spreads stabilise.**
