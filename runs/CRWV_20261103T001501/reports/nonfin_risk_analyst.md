## CoreWeave, Inc. (NASDAQ: CRWV) — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
**As of Nov 3, 2026 | Price $50.15 (all-time low $49.46, Nov 2) | Mkt cap ~$27.7B | Beta 7.67**

Context: CRWV is down ~64% from $137.98 (May 6, 2026) and ~73% from its June 2025 peak. This is not a drift — it is a re-rating of credit and disclosure credibility, and it is visible in non-equity markets: unsecured notes (coupon 9–9.75%) now yield **>15%**, and CDS costs >10%/yr — a level S&P Global Market Intelligence describes as credit distress. S&P rates CRWV **B+**, four notches below investment grade.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

- **No SEC/DOJ enforcement action found.** EDGAR full-text enforcement search returned zero results for CRWV; no short-seller accounting report against CoreWeave is corroborated in my sources. (I explicitly flag a narrative circulating that mentions a short-seller report, delayed 10-K, and SEC/DOJ subpoenas — **that record belongs to Super Micro (SMCI), not CoreWeave**. Do not import it into a CRWV thesis.)
- **Securities class action (active, material):** *D.N.J. Case No. 2:26-cv-00355*, filed Jan 2026, class period prepurchase of the Mar-28-2025 IPO; lead-plaintiff deadline was Mar 13, 2026. Alleges false/misleading statements on capacity delivery — tied to **weather-related third-party data center delays** that forced the Nov-2025 guidance cut. Company says claims are "without merit" and will defend vigorously. Multiple firms (Rosen, Kessler Topaz, Berger Montague, Levi & Korsinsky, Glancy) are soliciting; **Moore Law opened a fresh investigation in Oct 2026** centered on disclosure of borrowing-cost risk. Derivative/§11 exposure likely.
- **Forward regulatory exposure:** energy/power permitting and local opposition to data-center siting; potential AI-compute export controls; debt-covenant disclosure scrutiny (some loans let lenders **withhold cash if a customer contract goes wrong**).

### 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Single-customer concentration (existential).** The **top customer is ~71% of revenue**; three largest customers = **72%** of Q2-2026 revenue. The 10-Q itself warns of "risks of non-payment and non-performance" by these few counterparties. Losing or renegotiating one contract impairs both the revenue base and the contract-backed loan collateral.
2. **Third-party supply chain / build execution.** CoreWeave does not build its own shells: it leases capacity from third-party developers (e.g., Galaxy Digital's Helios campus). Delays — including weather — already forced a 2025 guidance cut and are the factual core of the class action. ~**$43B of the $104.2B backlog is due within two years, much from capacity not yet built.**
3. **Key-person and liquidity-path dependency.** Value rests on three former commodity traders; the CEO is publicly attributing the collapse to "irrational" markets. With **$35.6B debt principal + $16.3B leases**, interest running ~$2.6B/yr, and H1-2026 CFO of $3.7B against $14.1B equipment spend, **capital access — not demand — is the binding constraint**. Cyber posture was not disclosed in my sources (gap).

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **HIGH**

CRWV has become the **public proxy for the AI-credit bubble**. Satya Nadella publicly described renting AI capacity to a few big customers as "not a business"; **Elon Musk warned >200 neoclouds could collapse** and stated his companies no longer buy from neoclouds. Debt prices are crashing on the secondary market and national business press is running "who is right about CoreWeave" pieces. The CEO's internal memo dismissing the market as irrational, once leaked, hardened the narrative. Offsetting: the expanded **CrowdStrike partnership** (Nov 2026) and JPMorgan's Sept upgrade show the commercial franchise is still signing logos. Reputation risk here is **self-reinforcing with financing cost** — negative press raises spreads, which raises the required return, which pressures the equity.

---

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH** (upper end; Critical on a credit trigger)

Governance is weak and insiders have sold $4.1B with no buys; disclosure is under class-action attack; customer concentration is extreme; and the third-party build model has already missed. The counterweights — $104.2B backlog, 2.4x adj-EBITDA interest coverage, $15.6B liquidity vs $10.6B due by end-2027, Nvidia's ~7% stake and $6.3B committed purchases through 2032 — are real but are **financing- and counterparty-contingent**. Note the circularity: Nvidia is supplier, shareholder, and customer, while circular financing reportedly backs ~25% of Nvidia's forward revenue — CRWV sits inside that loop.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **🚩 Unchecked Rule 10b5-1 boxes on the Sept 30, 2026 Form 4** for all five transactions — possible non-plan sales into a collapse, in an active class period.
2. **🚩 $3.3B of founder selling with zero insider purchases since IPO**, and an audit chair who is a net seller — no insider is signalling confidence at a −73% drawdown.
3. **🚩 71% single-customer revenue concentration** plus loan covenants that let lenders withhold cash on contract underperformance — a correlated, non-diversified failure mode.
4. **🚩 Liquidity path dependent on continuous capital markets access** while unsecured bonds yield >15% and CDS signals distress.
5. **🚩 Related-party financing** (ex-Magnetar executives in "strategic financing"; Magnetar as lender-holder-seller).
6. **🚩 Controlled-company/CEO-Chair structure** with a three-director outside board — no internal check on the founders who are also the sellers.
