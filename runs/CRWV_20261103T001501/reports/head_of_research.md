### Investment Memo: CRWV — November 3, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL / DO NOT INITIATE (exit existing positions on strength) | Conviction: **Medium-High** — high on negative expected value; tempered by genuinely bimodal tails

**Investment Thesis**
CoreWeave is the neocloud scale leader with the strongest contracted demand signal in the market — $104B+ backlog (~12.6x annualized revenue), +112% growth, 57% EBITDA margins — wrapped in the worst capital structure in the AI complex: $51.6B+ debt, negative EBIT interest coverage, ~3.4 months of cash runway, bonds at ~16% YTM and CDS ~1,000bp. Demand is not the binding constraint; the cost and availability of capital is. The equity is a levered call option on continued refinancing access, trading ~35% above probability-weighted fair value, while insiders have sold $4.1B and bought nothing since the IPO.
