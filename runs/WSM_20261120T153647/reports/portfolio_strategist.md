## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (agreement on *what* it is; disagreement on *what to pay*)

**Genuine consensus — High agreement, high confidence:**
1. **This is a top‑decile business.** All 12 agents converge: 40% ROIC, 46% gross margin, 18% operating margin vs 6.4% peer median, net cash, ~100% cash conversion, 25% share-count reduction. No fraud, no restatement, no solvency risk. Independently corroborated in my checks (EV/EBITDA 16.2x, FCF yield 3.9%, beta 1.27).
2. **The stock is priced for a trajectory the disclosures do not support.** Value, Growth, Quant, Short, Alt Data and Sector all independently land on "priced for perfection." My peer check confirms WSM trades at a **2.3% P/E premium to a peer median of 24.15x** (8 names) — i.e., the premium is not the issue; the *absolute* multiple against a ~6% grower is.
3. **The margin gain is the entire earnings story** and part of it is provably non‑recurring ($29.3M Q3 refund; $197.8M total claim).

**Genuine disagreement — resolve explicitly:**
- **Contested view A: is the 46% gross margin structural or cyclical peak?** Sector (+structural, direct‑sourcing moat) vs Short (7 pts above 10‑yr average of 40.1%). **Resolution:** the *level* is defensible; the *rate of change* is over — management guides FY26 operating margin **flat** at 17.5–18.5%. Either way, no further multiple support comes from margin.
- **Contested view B: is the de-rating finished after −15%?** Sell‑side says no (10 PTs, mean $259.70, +18.9% upside); Value says it has barely started ($165 FV, −24%). **Resolution:** the sell‑side notes set targets *on peak, refund-flattered multiples* and were largely published before Nov 19. I weight Value.
- **The fraud-override test (my standing rule):** the Short analyst was explicitly asked to find fraud and **declined** — "if this were being pitched as an accounting‑fraud short, I would reject it." Management disclosed refunds in bold and *excluded* them from guidance. **There is no override trigger.** But the earnings-quality flag is real and is confirmed by management's own numbers, so I use **~$9.40 normalized EPS, not $10.29 TTM GAAP** — which makes the multiple 23.3x, not 21.2x.

**Net:** quality conviction **+2**; price/setup conviction **−2**. They cancel, and the residual is negative because a −24% valuation gap is a *present* risk while a housing thaw is a *dated-uncertain* option.
