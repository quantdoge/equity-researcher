**However, the YoY read is softer.** Versus the equivalent 2025 weeks (Pottery Barn 57 → 64 as Thanksgiving approached; West Elm 28 → 32; WS 62 → 100 in Black Friday week), the 2026 ramp is starting from a **lower base** — roughly flat-to-down ~5–10% YoY for Pottery Barn and **meaningfully down (~20%) for West Elm**. West Elm is the most housing-turnover-sensitive banner, and its search interest is the weakest of the three.

**Web traffic (SimilarWeb snippets):** williams-sonoma.com **+18.1% MoM** (strong); potterybarn.com **-3.66% MoM** in October (soft). Divergence again favors the flagship, cautions on the largest-volume banner.

**Signal: Mixed / mildly positive** — flagship accelerating, big-ticket banners lagging.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **Tracking IN-LINE to slightly BELOW (revenue); modest EPS beat**

- **Surprise momentum is decaying hard:** 7/7 beats over 7 quarters (avg +4.6%), but the trajectory is 10.5% → 5.1% → 4.3% → 4.8% → 6.6% → **1.0% → 0.0%**. The beat is nearly exhausted.
- **Revenue has flipped to misses:** Q3 FY26 revenue $1,991M vs $1,998M est (-0.4%); Q4 FY25 missed by -2.4%. Consensus now models Q4 FY26 revenue of **$2,501M, ~+6.1% YoY** — an acceleration versus the +5.8% just delivered and versus two consecutive revenue misses.
- Alt-data does **not** corroborate +6.1%. Trends show a normal-but-not-stronger holiday ramp off a lower YoY base, Pottery Barn traffic down MoM, and an industry promotional backdrop that is unusually deep (major retailers advertising "up to 70–80% off" furniture pre-Black Friday). Deep promo = units over AUR, margin risk.
- Offset: structural direct-sourcing margins, +11.7% EPS growth, buyback support. **EPS beat of low-to-mid single digits is more likely than a revenue beat.**

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL** (with negative skew on revenue)

Demand is intact and the flagship is accelerating, but there is **no evidence of the re-acceleration embedded in the +6.1% Q4 revenue consensus**. The most informative combination is: decaying EPS surprise + two revenue misses + weaker YoY search base in the highest-ticket banners + deep industry promo — against a stock that has already de-rated 16%. Expectations are reset but not washed out at ~28x forward.

**Asymmetry:** revenue miss risk > EPS miss risk. Watch Black Friday/Cyber Monday trends prints (first available ~1 Dec) and December PB/West Elm search as the decisive test.

## 7. Confidence Level: **Medium**

**Data limitations (material):**
- **Google Trends 5-year request returned the identical 12-month series** — the API appears capped at ~12m, so no clean multi-year YoY was obtainable. My YoY comparisons are **week-misaligned approximations**, not precise.
- **The single most important data point is missing:** trends data ends week of **8 Nov**; Black Friday (27 Nov 2026) and Cyber Monday — which drive the majority of Q4 — are not in the dataset. The holiday read is a *pre-season* read only.
- Web traffic figures are **search-snippet estimates**, not SimilarWeb API monthly series; ±directionally only.
- Patent tool errored; social sentiment returned the wrong company; job postings proxy yields listing pages with no quantitative YoY counts.
- News sentiment is keyword-based and event-clustered around the 11/19 print.

*Alt-data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. This report should be paired with channel checks and promotional-intensity tracking through December.*
