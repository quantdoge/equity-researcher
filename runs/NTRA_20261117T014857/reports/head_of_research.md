**Investment Thesis**
Natera is the clear category leader in MRD/cfDNA oncology diagnostics — revenue +34% at 65% gross margins, operating margin inflecting (−13.4% → −8.6% in three quarters), seven consecutive 10–15% revenue beats, and a fortress balance sheet (~$1B net cash, no funded debt). The problem is the equity, not the business: at ~14x TTM EV/revenue the price already pays for the $6B-2029 target in full, while a 5.13% 10Y compresses exactly the long-duration, GAAP-unprofitable profile NTRA occupies, and an unresolved $292.5M Guardant judgment plus an abrupt, unexplained CEO exit add a persistent governance discount. The −34% drawdown removed the premium, not the overvaluation.
