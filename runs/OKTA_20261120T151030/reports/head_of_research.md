### Investment Memo: OKTA — 20 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (do not initiate; existing holders reduce) | Conviction: **Medium** — high conviction the price is wrong, tempered by a binary earnings event in 7 trading days and a genuine, unresolved AI-agent bull case
**Price Target**: $160 (~21% downside from current $201.82); fair-value range $135–185
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Okta is a genuinely good business — 77% gross margins, net cash, real switching costs at the identity control plane, clean accounting — trading at a bad price. The stock has re-rated from $63 to $202 on an AI-agent identity narrative, two +30% earnings gaps, and S&P 500 inclusion, while reported growth *decelerated* to ~10%. At 10.6x EV/sales, 154x GAAP earnings, and a 0.9% SBC-adjusted FCF yield against a 5.07% risk-free rate in a hiking cycle, the market is capitalising a TAM that management itself calls "very early." Three independent methods (DCF, discount-rate transmission, factor scoring) converge on negative expected return; no agent on the desk underwrites $202.

**Bull Case**
- **Agent identity is a real new S-curve**: non-human identities scale with machine count, not seats; dozens of AI-agent deals closed; cRPO/RPO growing faster than revenue means the 10% headline understates momentum. FY28 guide >11% with disclosed agent ARR forces a re-underwrite.
- **Self-funding margin machine**: GM +790bps in four years, GAAP op margin −59%→+5% (~13% latest quarter), ~28% FCF margin, net cash, opportunistic buybacks at $80–90. Forensically clean.
- **Strategic scarcity**: the only neutral, cross-cloud identity control plane; cheapest multiple in cyber on P/B, P/FCF, EV/EBITDA; credible takeout optionality for a platform consolidator.

**Bear Case**
- **No margin of safety anywhere**: 10.6x EV/S on ~10% growth; the sell-side's own FY31 model implies an ~9% CAGR. Every desk price anchor ($110–185) is below spot.
- **Structural deceleration with a Microsoft ceiling**: growth 56%→11.8%→~10% guided; Entra bundled in E5 caps price; seat-based revenue is doubly exposed if AI compresses white-collar headcount even as the agent narrative inflates the stock.
- **Duration + event asymmetry**: Fed hiking into a 5%+ 10Y against a 3% FCF yield; Dec-1 earnings with two prior +30% gaps — the bar is now "beat *and* raise materially."

**Key Risks** (to the SELL thesis — upside risks)
- **Dec-1 print**: cRPO ≥14%, first disclosed agent ARR, or a raised guide → a third consecutive +30% gap. This is why conviction is Medium, not High, and why the expression is "avoid/reduce," not short.
