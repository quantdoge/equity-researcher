## SECTOR BRIEF — Application Software (CRM / Salesforce)

### 1. Sector Overview
Application software is a ~$1.44tn global spend pool in 2026, growing **+15.1% YoY** (Gartner, revised *up* from 14.7%) within total IT spend of $6.37tn (+14.2%); 2027 IT spend is forecast +15% to $7.35tn. The sector is **large, growing, but structurally mid-disruption**: headline spend is accelerating on AI budgets while the *revenue model* underneath seat-based SaaS is being repriced. Gartner estimates **$234bn of enterprise application spend is at risk from agentic AI**, and Bloomberg puts $280bn of "probabilistic" software spend at greatest exposure with **100–900bps of potential margin compression**. The market is fragmented (no vendor above ~20% share in CRM; top-5 combined <50%), with high switching costs that AI-native entrants are now attacking at lower build cost (e.g. Dreamteam's $53m raise to take on Salesforce/HubSpot). Net: demand is healthy, pricing power is the contested variable.

### 2. Key Tailwinds
- **IT budget expansion, not contraction.** Software spend +15.1% in 2026, and Gartner has revised 2026 forecasts *upward four times* (10.8% → 13.5% → 14.2%). AI is additive to budget, not purely cannibalistic — yet.
- **Margin unlock is real and sector-wide.** Every scaling app-software name expanded operating margin over 5 years: CRM +1,799bps, WDAY +981bps, NOW +938bps, INTU +723bps. Disciplined hiring + cloud cost optimisation is durable, and CRM has been the single biggest beneficiary.
- **Hybrid/consumption pricing is being adopted early.** ~57% of hypergrowth SaaS firms have already moved to hybrid pricing (BCG), and outcome-based pricing is projected to reach $1.05tn by 2035 — vendors that reprice first defend revenue per customer as seats are swapped for agents.

### 3. Key Headwinds
- **Seat-based pricing is the exposed flank.** Agentic AI lets a CIO swap 10–20 of 100 seats for consumption/outcome pricing (CIO.com) — a direct volume headwind to the per-user model that drives CRM, WDAY and HUBS economics.
- **AI COGS inflate cost of revenue.** Salesforce now caps employee AI token spend at up to $1,000/month after some spent "thousands per month" — evidence that AI features carry real variable cost and pressure the 77–89% gross-margin structure the sector is valued on.
- **Multiple compression + reliability risk.** On 17-Nov-2026 CRM fell 9.45% (biggest one-day drop since May 2024) on tech outages and fears software is "particularly vulnerable" to AI misuse — a sector-wide de-rating signal, not a company-specific one. CRM is **-46% from its Dec-2024 close peak ($362.58)** and **-26% YTD 2026**.

### 5. Target Company's Competitive Position — **Average** (strong franchise, weak momentum)
*Rationale:* CRM owns the #1 position in a fragmented CRM market and has the best margin trajectory in the sector, best-in-class gross margin, and a genuine platform bundle (Slack, Tableau, MuleSoft, Agentforce). But it is **losing relative momentum on every growth metric** — 9.6% latest-year growth vs 13.5% median, 14.3% 5-year CAGR vs 17.3% median — and is priced at parity with faster-growing peers. Its beta (1.24) is above NOW/INTU/MSFT, so it offers no defensive offset. Positively, Agentforce is monetising and Slackbot-driven premium upgrades tripled; negatively, the Aug-2026 earnings pop (+22.6% single-day gap) fully retraced within three months and the company is cutting jobs again — the market is not paying for the AI narrative at the application layer.

### 6. Sector Rating: **Neutral**
Spend growth (+15%) argues for constructive exposure, but the risk is *concentrated in exactly the segment CRM occupies*: seat-based application software facing agentic repricing, with $234bn of spend and up to 900bps of margin at stake. The attractive returns sit in AI infrastructure (MSFT, ORCL at 11.3x/5.2x on accelerating growth) rather than the application layer. Within applications, growth leadership (NOW, HUBS) is worth paying for; CRM is a **margin-and-capital-return story, not a growth story**, and at 3.9x P/S for 9.6% growth it is fairly-to-fully valued until Agentforce converts into reported revenue acceleration.
