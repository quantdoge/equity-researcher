**Investment Thesis**

IDEXX is a genuinely elite franchise — ~40% share of a ~$10–13B veterinary diagnostics oligopoly growing 9–11% annually, with 50%+ ROIC, 62% gross margins and rising, ~100% FCF conversion, and clean accounting (negative accruals, Beneish −2.43). Growth is now driven by diagnostic intensity per practice (+6% revenue/practice) decoupling from shrinking clinic traffic (−1% to −2% visits). The problem is entirely the price: at ~45x FY26E EPS and a 2.2% normalized FCF yield, the stock capitalizes ~17% perpetual FCF growth against ~10% delivered, in a macro regime (10Y 5.13%, real yields +85bp, Fed hiking toward 4.25–4.50%) that mechanically compresses long-duration multiples. Wonderful business, wrong price — own it lower.

**Key Findings by Domain**

Firming USD is a ~1.5–2pt revenue headwind (~40% international).

The live issue is exclusive-dealing: FTC consent order in force, a new veterinarian antitrust suit pending, and a 2024 litigation accrual ($0.56 EPS) proves these can be material.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **SELL.** Base-case DCF ~$385 ($340–430); even the bull case ($529) sits below market. Price implies 17% FCF growth for a decade vs 10.2% demonstrated. Normalized FCF (capex≈D&A) ~$1.19B. Re-entry interesting at $400–450.

Bear case is valuation + deceleration, a slow grind, not an event. Upgrade trigger: diagnostic premium to visits <800bp for 2 quarters, or gross margin down YoY.

**Bull Case**

**Bear Case**
- The multiple, not the franchise: −33% to $452 on mere reversion to the 30x peer median; macro bear path (10Y 5.75–6.0%) implies −25–30% with fundamentals intact.
- Instrument placements −19/−20% organic front-run recurring revenue by 4–6 quarters; the growth algorithm leans on price/utilization into a shrinking visit pool while Zoetis cuts prices.
- Mars/Antech vertical integration (labs + VCA/Banfield clinics) plus antitrust scrutiny of the live contract template is a slow-moving, structural moat threat.
