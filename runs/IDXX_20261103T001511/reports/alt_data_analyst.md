## 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **In-Line** (EPS bias modestly above; volume bias below)

So I expect another EPS beat driven by **utilization/consumables and instrument base**, not traffic — a lower-quality beat.
- Verdict: **In-Line** on the print; **Below** on the volume line if Q4 traffic deteriorates further.

## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL**
## 7. Confidence: **MEDIUM**

**Why Neutral and not Bullish:** the company-specific alt data (testing frequency, senior-pet cohort, instrument placements, lab hiring, 7/7 beat record) is genuinely constructive — but the *underlying demand substrate* is contracting (−1 to −3% visits, wellness worst), pricing power is visibly at a ceiling (Zoetis cutting prices is a leading tell for the whole animal-health complex), and retail/discount channels (Tractor Supply–VIP Petcare ~2,700 sites, Petco Vetco ~1,600 clinics/wk) are structurally siphoning exactly the low-acuity wellness visits IDEXX depends on. At ~44x forward EPS after a +20% single-session re-rating, the stock now requires beats to continue; alt data supports *in-line*, not acceleration.

**Key debates to watch into the Feb print:** (1) does Q4 same-store clinical visit decline widen beyond −1.7%? (2) does diagnostic revenue per practice hold at +6% as Zoetis/competitors cut price? (3) any slippage in instrument placement — the one alt signal (lab hiring) that would flag it first.

**Data limitations:** no patent data (API error), no usable social data, web traffic estimate unverified and possibly contaminated by portal traffic, Google Trends is a retail-facing proxy for a B2B diagnostics business, and all alt reads here predate the 2 Nov guidance reset — the *next* 4–6 weeks of visit and traffic data are the actual test. Alt data is a leading, not definitive, indicator.
