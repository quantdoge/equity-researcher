## IDEXX Laboratories (IDXX) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $671.64 | Market cap $52.9B | EV $55.1B | Data as of post-Q3'26 print (Nov 2, 2026)*

### 1. Growth Story
IDEXX is the installed-base monopoly of veterinary diagnostics: ~$4.7B of revenue anchored on in-clinic analyzers and reference labs that generate high-margin recurring consumables, now entering a genuine product-led innovation cycle. The thesis changed materially in 2026 — **diagnostic revenue is decoupling from vet clinic foot traffic** (U.S. same-store visits ~-1%, yet CAG recurring diagnostics grew 11% organic), meaning growth is now driven by test *frequency and menu expansion* rather than visit volume. The inVue Dx AI cellular analyzer (pathologist-level cytology in 10 minutes) and the Cancer Dx oncology panel convert that dynamic into a razor/razor-blade upgrade cycle with 20% of Cancer Dx volume coming from practices that were *not* prior IDEXX lab customers — i.e., share capture, not just price.

### 3. TAM Assessment
Global veterinary diagnostics: **$9.7B (2026) → $22.1B (2034), ~10.8% CAGR**; U.S. $2.4B→$3.7B (9.1% CAGR); U.S. veterinary oncology diagnostics ~$1.0B growing 8.6%. Third-party estimates are wide ($5–11% CAGR), but all point one direction. Critically, IDXX's $4.7B revenue *exceeds* the narrow "diagnostics" market definition because it captures consumables, instruments, reference-lab services and software — the true served market is the diagnostic spend per clinic, which is still low-penetration. Management's own KPI (revenue per box of $3,500–5,500, 5,500 inVue Dx placements targeted in FY26, Cancer Dx in ~7,500 practices) implies years of menu-driven attach remaining. Penetration runway >> current share.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts
- **Feb 1, 2027 — Q4/FY2026 print + initial FY2027 guide.** Fourth consecutive guidance raise would validate the ~9–10% algorithm and force estimate revisions upward.
- **inVue Dx placement cadence** vs. the 5,500 FY26 target; the Fine Needle Aspiration menu addition expands consumable pull-through per installed box (highest-leverage recurring revenue driver).
- **Cancer Dx international rollout + panel expansion** — still North America-weighted; each new assay added to the panel is pure incremental margin.
- **CoVetAI integration** (acquired Sept 16, 2026 — AI ambient listening/clinical workflow) creates a software recurring-revenue attach and stickiness layer; deal costs already trimmed FY26 margin guide to 32.1–32.3%.
- **Any inflection in U.S. clinic visit volumes** — currently -1%; a return to flat/positive would remove the single biggest bear argument overnight.
- **Capital deployment:** ~$1.2B/yr buyback plus bolt-on M&A in vet software/AI.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD** (Accumulate below ~$600)
The business thesis is intact and improving — accelerating growth, expanding gross margin (58.8%→63.4%), a real AI-enabled product cycle, and 100% beat consistency. But the *stock* thesis is now fully priced after a 21% one-day gap:

- **45.5x FY26E EPS, 40.1x FY27E, 11.2x EV/sales, 36.5x EV/EBITDA, P/FCF 51.4x** for a ~10% grower → **PEG ~3.5**
- At $671.64 the stock sits **at the mean of recent analyst targets ($620–$740; mean ~$674)** — the 27% discount TIKR flagged in July has closed
- Fair value on FY28E EPS of $19.03 at 35–40x = **$666–761**, i.e. ~0–6% annualized from here
- Price-target trajectory has been *downward* all year (Barclays $850→$800→$710; UBS $750→$730→$640→$620→$627) even as estimates rose — a classic sign the multiple, not the earnings, is the debate

I'd want to own this into the Feb 1 print only on a pullback toward the $575–600 zone (≈38–40x FY27E), which restores 25%+ upside to a $750–800 target. Not a Strong Buy — the growth rate, while durable, is a 10% algorithm, not a 25% one, and 45x forward leaves no room for a utilization stumble.

### 7. Key Risks
- **Utilization/price dependence:** with clinic visits -1%, growth leans on diagnostic frequency and pricing — a soft consumer or clinic-labor constraint could break the decoupling thesis.
- **Valuation compression:** 45x forward on a high-single-digit grower; a de-rating to 30x is a ~30% drawdown with no fundamental change.
- **CEO transition execution:** Mazelsky → Mike Erickson completed May 2026, mid-launch-cycle; unproven capital-allocation and guidance credibility record.
- **Competitive intensity:** Zoetis, Mars/Antech and in-house reference labs pushing point-of-care and oncology menus; price competition on consumables would hit the 63% gross margin.
- **M&A integration and margin optics:** CoVetAI costs already lowered the FY26 operating-margin outlook; further deals risk dilution and distraction.
- **Tariffs/FX:** international is the fastest-growing region (21% reported in Q1'26) and thus the most FX- and trade-exposed.
