**Investment Thesis**

Zoetis is a high-quality, narrow-moat franchise — 70.5% gross margin, 38% operating margin, ~28% ROIC, 85% FCF conversion — that is simultaneously (a) genuinely cheap at 10.4x EV/EBITDA / 6.5% FCF yield after a 57% two-year drawdown, and (b) genuinely still deteriorating, with FY26 guided to the first organic revenue decline in its history (−3% to −1%) and US companion animal down 11%. All eleven workstreams converge on the same answer: **cheap, not fraudulent, but not yet investable at this price** — the stock sits at ~90% of base-case intrinsic value, which is where our discipline says *trim*, not *initiate*. The asymmetry only turns in our favour below $67 (reward:risk ~2.4:1, ~8% FCF yield) or on proof of a companion-animal inflection.

**Bull Case**
- **Valuation has already capitalised the damage**: 10.4x EV/EBITDA, 6.5% FCF yield, ~35–56% discount to any comparable set; the market implies roughly zero perpetual growth for a franchise still compounding owner earnings at ~8%.
- **Diversification is working**: livestock +11–23%, international +6% organic, diagnostics +12% — over 30% of the portfolio is growing, and the balance sheet (1.76x net leverage) lets Zoetis buy pipeline assets through the downturn.
- **Insider conviction + pipeline optionality**: directors buying with personal capital, Lenivia/Portela mAb launches rolling out globally, 12+ pipeline candidates, and an EUA for screwworm — the pipeline is free at this multiple.

**Bear Case**
- **The pricing model is broken, not cycling**: parasiticide price competition, generic erosion on Cerenia/Convenia, Key Derm −18% US, and alt data showing genuine category demand contraction — with Apoquel (2030) and Simparica (2032) LOEs capping the terminal value.
- **EPS is engineering, not growth**: net income guided down 5–9%; a shrinking share count and SG&A cuts are masking operational decline — and the buyback that props it up is debt-funded (181% of FCF returned).
- **Macro sequencing is the worst possible**: real-rate shock + re-accelerating CPI + record-low consumer sentiment + a stronger dollar, against a company whose pricing power is already impaired; the bear path is $50–55 (−30/−35%).

**Key Risks**
1. Third guidance cut or negative FY27 organic guide (5-Nov print is a genuine binary — four −9% to −21.5% single-day gaps in 24 months).
2. Gross margin below 69% — the last proof of pricing power — or US Key Derm deteriorating past −20%.
3. Section 232 tariff classification of animal health (un-priceable, not in consensus).
4. Librela: new FDA/EMA label action, or the securities class action surviving motion-to-dismiss (discovery into internal safety communications).
5. Channel-stress confirmation: DSO >65 days or inventory >$2.6B.
6. Leverage path: net debt/EBITDA >2.5x or a forced buyback halt.
