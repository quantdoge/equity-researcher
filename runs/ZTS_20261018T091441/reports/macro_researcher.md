## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Risk-off / late-cycle tightening — and specifically a *real-rate shock*, not a growth shock.**

The regime has changed character over the past six months. The Fed finished an easing cycle at 3.63–3.64% (Jan–Aug 2026) after cutting from 5.33%, but the effective funds rate ticked **back up to 3.75% in September 2026** — the first reversal. The curve now prices roughly **+100bp of further tightening**: 2Y at **4.77%** vs. fed funds at 3.75%. Critically, this is *not* an inflation-expectations story: 5Y breakevens are **2.33–2.37%** and falling since May. The entire move is **real yields and term premium** — 10Y TIPS went from **1.91% (1 May 2026) to 2.78–2.95% (Oct 2026)**, roughly **+90bp of real rate in five and a half months**. 30Y at 5.37% with 10Y at 5.10%.

Underlying that: **CPI re-accelerated**. September CPI +0.65% MoM (≈8% annualised), August +0.40%; 3-month annualised **≈4.6%** versus **3.72%** headline YoY. Real fed funds is now **≈0% (3.75% vs 3.72% CPI)** — policy is neutral against an accelerating inflation rate. Hence the hiking repricing.

Growth is *not* the problem: Q1 2026 +2.5%, Q2 2026 +2.2%, unemployment 4.2%. Credit is also not the problem: HY OAS **3.23%**, tight in absolute terms, though +60bp off the 2.63% low on 31 Aug 2026 — an early crack. What *is* broken is the **US consumer**: University of Michigan sentiment **51.7 (Aug 2026)**, down from 79.0 in Jan 2024 and near record lows.

**Interpretation: this is a stagflation-lite / higher-for-longer re-emergence. Long-duration equities get hit on the discount rate while discretionary consumer volumes soften simultaneously — the worst possible combination for a consumer-facing, terminal-value-heavy compounder like Zoetis.**

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

ZTS is a **~$9.1–9.3B revenue** (2026 guided) animal-health franchise, roughly 60% companion animal / 40% livestock, with approximately 40% of revenue outside the US and a beta of 0.74. It has already de-rated savagely: **−58% from $188 (Oct 2024), −71% from its 2021 peak, −44% over the past twelve months alone**, with two guidance cuts in 2026 (revenue $9.68–9.96B → **$9.12–9.32B**; adj. EPS $6.85–7.00 → **$6.15–6.25**), both blamed on US companion animal volumes **−11%** on softer end-market demand and pet-owner **price sensitivity**. At $78.42 and $6.20 mid-point EPS, ZTS trades **≈12.6x forward** with a **2.7% dividend yield** ($0.53/qtr).

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Rationale, in order of weight:

1. **The dominant macro force is a real-rate shock**, and ZTS is among the most duration-sensitive names in healthcare. The ~90bp real-yield move since May maps almost exactly onto the −30% further decline in ZTS from $110 (May) to $78 (Oct), on top of the fundamental cuts.
2. **Inflation re-acceleration arrives with pricing power already impaired.** This is the cruellest sequencing: ZTS needed either falling costs or intact pricing to defend margins; it has neither.
3. **Consumer sentiment near record lows** directly attacks the highest-margin half of the business (companion animal) and shows no sign of inflection.
4. **Dollar strength** adds a 1–2% reported-revenue drag and is unlikely to reverse while the front end prices hikes.

**Counterweights — what stops this being outright hostile:** growth is +2.2%, credit spreads are 3.2%, ZTS leverage is low, ~40% of revenue is non-discretionary livestock/medicated feed, the business is genuinely defensive in a recession, and a 12.6x multiple / 2.7% yield has already capitalised a great deal of bad news. At the 2 Oct low of $68.77 the stock was at 11x on cut numbers — a level that historically marks animal-health troughs. The stock is **+14% off that low** into mid-October, suggesting the market is beginning to look past the macro.

**Net: the *direction* of every macro variable that matters is currently against ZTS, but the *magnitude* of the damage done is extreme. Macro is a headwind to the thesis, not the thesis itself — the thesis lives or dies on whether US companion animal volumes trough in 2027.**

## 5. The Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise

**Bear case (higher probability than consensus): the Fed is forced to *hike* into a deteriorating consumer — a 1994/2022-style tightening restart.**

Mechanism: September's +0.65% CPI print proves not to be tariff-noise. With real fed funds at zero and 2Y already at 4.77%, the FOMC delivers 50–75bp of hikes by mid-2027. Real 10Y breaks **3.25%**, the 30Y clears 6%, and the equity risk premium compresses further. ZTS — no pricing power, consumer volumes already −11%, ~40% non-US revenue in a stronger dollar, and a 2030 patent cliff that makes its terminal value acutely rate-sensitive — sees companion animal decline accelerate to **−15%** as vet clinics cut inventory and pet owners defer wellness visits. Forward EPS prints **below $5.50**, and the market applies a **10x** multiple to a melting-ice-cube narrative: **$50–55 (−30% to −35%)**. In this scenario ZTS's beta of 0.74 is irrelevant — this is a duration and fundamentals double-hit, and the 2.7% dividend (payout ~34% of EPS) does not defend the price.

*Mirror-image surprise (lower probability, higher payoff): the Sep CPI spike is a tariff pass-through one-off; breakevens at 2.37% are already telling you inflation expectations are anchored; real yields fall 60bp by Q1 2027 and ZTS re-rates to 15x on stabilising 2027 EPS → **$105–115 (+35–45%)**.* Crypto-like asymmetry exists, but I weight the bear path more heavily because the policy reaction function, not the inflation data alone, is now the swing factor.
