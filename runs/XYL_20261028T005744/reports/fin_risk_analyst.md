## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **INVESTMENT GRADE** (BBB/Baa2, lower-medium IG)

Xylem is a low-levered, cash-generative IG industrial with effectively no near-term refinancing or solvency risk. Solvency metrics are unusually strong for the sector: net debt/EBITDA of **0.33x** at FY2025 and debt/equity of **0.18x**, with reported interest coverage of **42.2x**. Two important qualifiers, both directionally negative:

- **Leverage has roughly tripled on a net basis during 2026.** Net debt rose from $1.25B (Q1'26) to **$2.19B** (Q3'26): total debt +$834M (to $2,895M, consistent with new senior unsecured notes due 2033/2036) while cash fell from $1.48B (FY25) to **$708M** — the proceeds and balance-sheet liquidity went into **$1.24B of buybacks** in H1'26. Leverage is now ~**1.0x adjusted EBITDA** (23.9% Q3 adj. EBITDA margin × ~$8.95B revenue ≈ $2.14B) or ~**1.2x** on GAAP EBITDA. Still conservative, but the *trajectory* is releveraging into a decelerating cycle.
- **Reported interest expense is implausibly low** ($29M FY25 on $2.1B debt ≈ 1.4%; $6–7M/quarter in 2026). This is almost certainly net interest. On **gross** interest (~4.2% on $2.9B ≈ $120M), EBIT coverage is ~**10x** and EBITDA coverage ~**18x** — comfortably IG, but the 42x headline overstates it materially.

**No refinancing cliff:** new issuance is 2033/2036-dated; short-term debt $530M is 1.3x covered by cash. **Asset-quality caveat:** goodwill + intangibles are **$10.74B = 62% of total assets**, and **tangible book equity is now negative (~–$472M)**, down from +$1.15B at FY25 (equity fell $957M since Q1'26 on buybacks). Recovery value for unsecured creditors is weak even though cash-flow coverage is strong.
