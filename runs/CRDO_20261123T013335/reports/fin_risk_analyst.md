## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

### Rating: **Investment Grade quality (AAA-equivalent balance sheet), though unrated**

CRDO carries effectively **no debt**. Total debt of $25.4M (long-term $20.6M, short-term $0) against $2.06B of book equity and $1.44B of cash and short-term investments. The company is in a **net cash position of ~$1.42B**, with net debt/EBITDA of **-2.78x**.

There is no credit rating and no CDS spread because there is no material public debt — this is a genuine data gap, not a deficiency. Credit risk is **de minimis**: equity-to-assets is 90%, assets-to-liabilities 9.89x, and the company could repay 100% of its debt from cash on hand roughly 57 times over.

**Interest coverage is reported as null** because interest expense is $0 in FY2024, FY2025 and FY2026 — coverage is effectively infinite. EBIT of $475M is unencumbered by fixed charges.

Altman Z-Score of **7.78** sits deep in the **Safe zone** (threshold 2.99). Note the tool's caveat that x4 uses *book* equity ($2.06B) rather than market cap — but since book equity is already near-zero-levered, the score is robust; substituting market cap would only push it higher.

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — Revenue −20%

FY2026 base: revenue $1,335M, gross margin 68.0%, operating income $445M (33.3%), FCF $407M, net cash $1,419M.

Model assumes: 400bps of gross-margin compression (fixed-cost absorption + competitive pricing), 10% opex reduction ($464M → $418M), capex held at ~$55M.

| Scenario | Revenue | Gross margin | Operating income | Op. margin | Approx. FCF | Net cash after | Cash runway |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base (FY26A)** | $1,335M | 68.0% | $445M | 33.3% | $407M | $1,419M | n/a |
| **−20%** | $1,068M | 64.0% | **$266M** | 24.9% | ~$270M | $1,689M | n/a |
| **−40%** | $801M | 60.0% | **$63M** | 7.9% | ~$60M | $1,479M | n/a |
| **−50% (severe)** | $668M | 58.0% | **−$13M** | −1.9% | ~−$10M | $1,409M | **>100 years** |

**Conclusion: the balance sheet is not the risk.** At a 20% revenue decline the company still earns a 25% operating margin and generates ~$270M of free cash. Even at a **50% revenue collapse** — a genuinely catastrophic outcome — CRDO produces a trivial operating loss that its $1.44B cash pile covers for over a century of burn at that rate. There is **no covenant to breach, no maturity wall to refinance** ($0 short-term debt), and no scenario in which this company needs external capital.

**The real downside is equity valuation, not solvency.** The stock trades at roughly **46x trailing earnings and ~75x trailing FCF**. A 20% revenue miss would not threaten the business, but it would very likely trigger a 40–60% de-rating — precisely the kind of move already seen twice in the past 20 months. **Financial risk is low; equity-price risk is severe.**

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Extreme customer concentration (the single largest risk).** Per FY2026 10-K disclosure, the **top 10 customers accounted for ~90% of total revenue**, with two customers each above 10%. More recent quarterly reporting indicates **four hyperscalers each above 10%**, with the top three at ~34% combined. A single capex pause by one AI hyperscaler is a material revenue event, and the FY2025 drawdown (−67%) was plausibly a direct consequence of exactly this fear. This is a business risk that no balance sheet can hedge.

2. **Gross and operating margin compression at the inflection point.** Gross margin fell from 68.2% in Q4 FY26 to **64.5% in Q1 FY27** (−370bps sequentially); operating margin fell from 35.7% to **25.2%**. Revenue rose 9.6% sequentially while operating income fell 22.6%. Mix shift toward AECs (lower margin than ICs) plus hyperscaler pricing power is eroding the economics even as the top line accelerates.

3. **Persistent dilution.** Share count up ~8% annually (155M → 186M basic since FY24); diluted shares reached 194.4M in Q1 FY27. SBC of $182.6M is 13.7% of revenue. The F-Score correctly docks CRDO on F7.

4. **Working-capital intensity.** FY26 consumed $195.2M of cash into working capital; inventory rose from $90.0M to $250.8M (+179%) and receivables from $162.1M to $233.4M. Inventory now equals ~134 days of annualised COGS. Watch for obsolescence if the AI build-out pauses.

5. **Valuation leaves no margin of safety.** At ~75x trailing FCF (FCF yield 2.17%), the market is discounting years of hypergrowth. The 20% one-day fall on a 115%-growth quarter demonstrates how little room exists for any disappointment.

6. **Fiscal-year-end anomaly / data quality.** The FY26 balance sheet reports net debt of **−$1,139.5M**, while the leverage tool reports net debt of **−$1,417.8M** — the tool appears to net all short-term investments against debt while the balance sheet line is narrower. Both indicate net cash; the magnitude differs. Flagged for transparency.

## Data Gaps & Caveats

- **Market-cap / price inconsistency:** the company profile reports a price of **$99.86** and market cap of **$18.77B**, while the price history shows a last close of **$194.79** (2026-09-30). The profile data appears stale (possibly a pre-2025 quote). **FCF yield is therefore reported both ways: 2.17% on the $18.8B profile cap, and ~1.1% on a price-implied ~$36B cap.** The 1.1% figure is the more realistic current reading and makes the stock look more expensive, not less.
- **Interest coverage is null** — by construction (zero interest expense), not a data failure.
- **No credit rating or CDS spread available** — no material public debt outstanding.
- **No peer multiples gathered** — sector-relative valuation was outside the risk mandate and budget. Competitors in high-speed connectivity (Marvell, Broadcom, Astera Labs) would be the natural comparison set.
- **Z-Score x4 uses book equity**, per the tool's own caveat; treat as indicative (it understates the true score here).

---

**Bottom line:** CRDO's balance sheet is about as close to risk-free as a semiconductor company gets — net cash of $1.4B, a 10x current ratio, a 7.78 Z-score, and the demonstrated ability to absorb a 50% revenue decline without raising a dollar. **The financial risk is not in the capital structure; it is in the customer base and the multiple.** Ninety percent of revenue from ten buyers and a ~75x FCF multiple are the two things that can actually hurt an owner of this stock — and both are already visible in a 99% volatility and 67% historical drawdown.
