### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **IN-LINE (with downside skew)**
- Next catalyst: **FY2026 results, Feb 25, 2027** — consensus **EPS $0.1195, revenue $46.6B**.
- Historical surprise profile is deteriorating, not stable: **beat rate 50%**, **average surprise −27.1%**, last four quarters: **−66% miss, −62% miss, +63% beat, −126% miss**. Any single quarter is close to a coin flip; magnitude risk is large and negative-weighted.
- Management reaffirmed FY26 guide: **mid-single-digit revenue growth, low-single-digit AOI margin**. Alt data does **not** support upside to that: NA shipments −12%, inventory 130+ days, incentive spend elevated, Ram/Jeep search fading into Q4. Cost savings ("accelerating") are what's holding the story together — and cost saves are visible in filings *before* revenue strength is, so near-term beats are more likely to come from opex than from volume.
- Overriding risk: **US tariffs on cars/parts/steel reportedly stepping up to 50% on Jan 1, 2027** (FY26 headwind already €1–1.2B). No alt-data source can price that; it lands inside the Feb 2027 print.
- *Data-quality caveat*: this vendor's calendar mixes Stellantis's semi-annual EU reporting with quarterly shipment data (e.g., a "Q3" event with $87.5B revenue = H1-scale). Treat per-quarter revenue magnitudes with caution; the direction of surprises is the usable signal.

### 7. Confidence Level: **LOW–MEDIUM**

Limits confidence: (a) **patent trend unavailable** — total R&D-intensity blind spot; (b) job postings, web traffic and social are **web-search proxies with no time series** — directional only; (c) news sentiment score is **methodologically broken** (positive-tagged cash drain); (d) Reddit data **stale**; (e) no app-store or satellite data exists for this business model; (f) inventory/days-supply evidence is **month-stamped and not fully current** (May 2026 Cox data, Jan 2026 commentary); (g) an exogenous **50% tariff on Jan 1, 2027** can swamp every signal above.
