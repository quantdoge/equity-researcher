# STLA — Equity Growth Analysis (Stellantis N.V.)

*Note on data: financials are reported in EUR, the share price in USD. All figures below are flagged for currency. Latest price $4.68 (1-Oct-2026); market cap ≈$13.5bn (~€12.4bn).*

---

## 1. Growth Story

**Stellantis is not a growth company — it is a post-collapse turnaround.** FY2025 delivered the company's first annual loss as a public entity: revenue €153.5bn (−2%), gross profit **−€4.2bn**, operating loss **−€22.2bn**, net loss **−€22.4bn**, driven by **€25.4bn of write-downs** as management sharply reversed its EV strategy. The equity has repriced from ~$22 (mid-2024) to $4.68 — a ~79% decline — as the market moved from "cheap cyclical" to "broken capital allocation." The only credible bull case is **mean-reversion in North American margins (Jeep/Ram/HEMI V8 relaunch) plus cost cuts**, which is an event-driven recovery thesis, not a disruptive-innovation compounding story. Under a strict growth mandate, the thesis fails on every measurable dimension.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration Opportunity

Global light-vehicle revenue TAM is enormous (~$4tn+), but TAM size is irrelevant if the company is **ceding share**. STLA's ~€160bn revenue is roughly **4–5% of global industry revenue**, and the trajectory is down: it has effectively exited China (structurally uncompetitive), is losing European share to Chinese imports and Tesla, and is now fighting for North America profitability.

Critically, **the growth pocket of the TAM is precisely the one Stellantis just retreated from.** The €25.4bn write-down and EV scale-back is management admitting it cannot compete on battery cost or software against BYD and Tesla. The 2026 "reset" — returning the HEMI V-8 to the Ram 1500, refocusing on Jeep/Ram/Peugeot/Fiat — is a **tactical retreat to the highest-margin ICE/hybrid niches**. That is a defensible cash-generating strategy. It is the opposite of TAM capture in the growth segments (EV powertrain, software-defined vehicle, ADAS, energy management) where the next decade of auto value accrues.

**Differentiation:** essentially none in technology. STLA has brand equity (Jeep, Ram) and North American truck/SUV franchise value — real, but a mature-brand moat, not a data/network-effect moat. Tesla's market cap ($1.60tn) is **~118x** Stellantis's; the market is paying for the innovation layer, not the manufacturing layer.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **FY2026 results — 25-Feb-2027.** The single decisive event. Consensus €0.12 EPS. Management has **reaffirmed 2026 guidance and a positive industrial free cash flow target**; delivering positive FCF after −€16.2bn in 2025 would be the first hard proof point.
- **Q4 2026 cash flow / North America margin print.** Q3 2026 revenue €37.6bn (+1% YoY), net loss narrowed 90% to €453m, cumulative 2026 net profit +€217m. North America is currently supplying **more than 100% of group profit** — Europe is loss-making. Watch NA mix.
- **HEMI V-8 Ram 1500 launch ramp** — the highest-leverage volume/mix catalyst; directly attacks the NA profit pool.
- **€5bn issuance authorization** (board-approved post-write-down) — resolution could be deleveraging (bullish) or equity dilution (bearish). **This is the single largest unknown.**
- **US/EU tariff resolution** — management guides €1.0–1.2bn of 2026 US tariff cost; any relief is direct margin.
- **"Payoff by 2027" proof / Capital Markets Day** — Filosa has publicly committed to the turnaround landing in 2027. Credibility test.

## 5. Alternative Data Signals

- **Hiring (Indeed proxy):** ~300–350 Stellantis postings in Detroit/Sterling Heights, concentrated in skilled trades and plant roles (millwright, machine repair, production supervisor), plus Toledo OH activity. Signals **plants are running and NA operations are being resourced**, not shuttered — mildly supportive of the NA recovery thesis. But total headcount is down to **258,668** from ~300k+, confirming deep restructuring (cost tailwind, morale/execution risk).
- **Price/volume:** Capitulation signature. 54m shares traded 1-Oct-2026 with a +7.6% reversal off the $4.30 low. Volume has been elevated (20–40m/day) for months on declining price — persistent distribution. Stock is −58% over the 13 months shown and −79% from 2024 highs.
- **Analyst revisions — sharply negative and deteriorating.** Target trajectory: $23.50 (Jun-24) → $16.44 → $13 → $12.10 → $11.20 → $10 → $9.90 → $8 → $7.50 → **$5.20 (Morgan Stanley, Underweight, Sep-26)**; Piper Sandler *double*-downgraded to Underweight with a **$4 target** in Jul-26. Two of the last three actions were **Underweight**. Point targets of $4–$9 bracket the current price — the Street sees roughly fair value *after* an 79% decline.
- **Earnings surprises:** **25% beat rate, average surprise −48%**, including a −126% miss in the most recent reported quarter. Guidance credibility is severely impaired — a red flag for any turnaround.
- **North America concentration:** NA supplies >100% of group profit. This is a **single-point-of-failure** on US consumer demand and tariffs, not a diversified growth engine.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **SELL** *(growth-mandate portfolio)*

**On the framework I am required to apply, STLA fails every test:**
- Revenue CAGR forward of ~2.4% — below industry, i.e., modeled share loss
- Gross margin −2.7% (2025), only ~11.7% recovering vs 19% structurally in 2023
- Rule of 40 of **−12.7**
- FCF of −€16.2bn; dividend suspended; €5bn issuance authorized
- Innovation moat: **absent** in EV, software, and autonomy; capital reallocated *away* from the growth TAM
- Estimate revisions and price targets in free-fall; 25% beat rate

**Important nuance — I am not calling this a short.** At 0.08x sales and 0.25x book, with sequential improvement underway (H1 2026 operating income +€1.39bn vs −€1.55bn; Q3 loss narrowed 90%; guidance reaffirmed), the equity carries genuine **option value as a distressed cyclical**. If STLA merely achieves consensus 2028 EPS of €1.35 and re-rates to a still-depressed 6x, the stock is ~€8 (~$8.8) — roughly **+88%**. The market is discounting a high probability of failure, and that probability is not 100%.

But that is a **deep-value / event-driven trade with binary execution risk** — a fundamentally different mandate from disruptive-growth investing. It has no place in a growth portfolio, where capital should compound in businesses expanding their TAM, not defending a shrinking one.

**Revisit trigger:** two consecutive quarters of **positive industrial FCF** + NA operating margin >8% + clarity that the €5bn issuance is debt (not equity). Until then: Sell.

## 7. Key Risks

**To the downside (execution & structural):**
- **Dilution/leverage risk** — the €5bn issuance authorization post-€22bn loss is the biggest equity risk; an equity raise at $4.68 would be severely value-destructive.
- **North America concentration** — >100% of profit from one region, exposed to US consumer weakness, tariffs (€1.0–1.2bn guided), and truck/SUV cyclicality.
- **Europe is structurally loss-making** and facing Chinese import competition; no clear path to profitability disclosed.
- **Guidance credibility destroyed** — 25% beat rate, −48% average surprise; "positive FCF 2026" must be independently verified, not trusted.
- **Residual write-down risk** — €25.4bn was a big bath, but inventory, warranty (flagged in reports), and dealer-network liabilities could surprise again.
- **China effectively forfeited** — permanently cedes the world's largest and fastest-growing auto market.
- **Diluted/eroding brand equity** — a 15-brand portfolio (Maserati, DS, Lancia, Abarth, Chrysler, Dodge) is far too broad for a company with negative FCF; rationalization is likely, and carries further charges.

**To the upside (what would prove me wrong):**
- Faster-than-expected NA margin recovery on HEMI V-8 + cost cuts → 2027 EPS beats the €0.99 consensus materially
- Tariff relief or US-EU trade resolution
- Non-core asset sales (Maserati, Comau, stakes) at premiums to carrying value, funding the turnaround without dilution
- A credible 2027 Capital Markets Day that re-establishes multi-year targets and restores sell-side confidence

**Bottom line:** A legitimate deep-value restructuring candidate at ~0.08x sales — but a **failed growth thesis**. Sell on growth criteria; the recovery belongs in a distressed/event-driven book, not a growth book.
