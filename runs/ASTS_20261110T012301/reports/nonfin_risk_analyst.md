## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

**Active litigation (material):**
- **Hunter v. AST SpaceMobile, Inc.**, U.S. District Court, W.D. Tex., **No. 7:26-cv-00378**, filed ~14 Sept 2026. Claims under **Exchange Act §§10(b) and 20(a), Rule 10b-5**. Class period **4 March 2025 – 15 July 2026**. Core allegations: materially false/misleading statements and omissions concerning **(a) the sufficiency of the Company's capital and liquidity position** and **(b) the durability of its competitive lead** in satellite direct-to-cellular. Pleaded damages tied to the 16 July 2026 decline (**‑$11.30/share; close $66.31 → $55.01, ‑17.0%**).
- Lead-plaintiff deadline **13 November 2026**; at least **eight** firms soliciting (Robbins Geller, Kaplan Fox, Levi & Korsinsky, Rosen, Kessler Topaz, Bronstein Gewirtz, Schall Brown & Schwartz, Henzel). A **second action with a 13 December 2026 deadline** indicates an expanded or separate class. Litigation is in its earliest stage — no motion-to-dismiss ruling; **no reserve or insurance-recovery disclosure verified**.

**SEC/DOJ enforcement:** EDGAR full-text search returned **no** SEC enforcement action naming ASTS. Absence of a formal enforcement record is a positive, but note that follow-on SEC investigation after a 10b-5 suit alleging disclosure-control failure is a common pattern and cannot be ruled out.

**Sector/spectrum regulation (outcome-critical):**
- **Granted:** FCC authorised AST's **248-satellite** constellation and SCS operations with terrestrial mobile partners — a genuine, hard-won licence moat.
- **Adverse:** On **7 Oct 2026** the FCC granted **SpaceX approval for 15,000 direct-to-device satellites**, deferring only some requests. This is the single largest regulatory-competitive event of the year and now constrains AST's spectrum/priority position.
- **Pending:** FCC NPRM opening **~482 MHz across 13 bands** for SCS. Carriers are lobbying to **cut ~290 MHz** from the plan. Rulemaking outcomes will re-price the addressable market for every D2D operator.
- **Conditional authorisations** carry FCC buildout milestones (deploy-or-lose-halves deadlines). With the constellation now running late, milestone-compliance risk is live.

## 3. Insider Activity Analysis (trailing 12 months) — **NET NEGATIVE**

Headline screens show **insider purchases of 990,985 shares (16 transactions) vs. sales of 183,713 (7)** — a reported **net purchase of +807,272 shares (+3.5%)**. **This is misleading.** Decomposing it:

- The purchase side is dominated by **RSU/stock awards (17 Aug 2026: Avellan, CFO Johnson, COO Gupta, President Wisniewski, Bernal — grants at $0.00)** and **one** discretionary open-market buy: director **Adriana Cisneros, $619,200 on 31 Aug 2026** (a strategic-family affiliate, not an independent signal of management conviction).
- The sale side includes **~$3.06M of officer disposals on a single day (16 Sept 2026)** by the COO and CTO at ~$58.90 — i.e., management selling *after* the guidance reset.
- **The most material insider event by far is not captured as a "sale":** Avellan's **$147M–$183M prepaid forward on 2.5M shares (22 June 2026)**, three weeks before the class-action trigger date. Widely noted as his first-ever willing disposal.
- **Institutions cut holdings by 5,973,895 shares (‑3.56%) quarter-over-quarter.**
- **Short interest ~57.5M shares** — an elevated, conviction-heavy bear cohort.

**Verdict:** Reported net insider buying is an artefact of equity compensation. Economically, informed insiders were net *sellers/hedgers* into strength ahead of the bad news, while the only open-market buyer is a strategic partner-affiliated director.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Single-point launch dependency — and it has already failed.** **BlueBird 7 was a total loss**: on the New Glenn 3 mission the satellite separated and powered on but **reached too low an orbit to sustain operations and will be de-orbited**. A Blue Origin **launchpad explosion** then forced a **three-to-six month** slip, pushing initial commercial service **from late 2026 into 2027** (constellation target moved to ~45 satellites by early 2027). AST does not control its own ride to orbit; throughput is set by a third party's anomaly cadence. Now exploring vertical integration — a capital-intensive distraction during a cash-constrained build.
2. **Execution concentration at a single site with a novel, unproven production ramp.** All manufacturing is at Midland, TX, scaling to a high-cadence Block 2 build of very large arrays. No geographic redundancy; any facility, supply-chain or workforce disruption is company-wide. Composite/antenna supplier concentration is not disclosed in retrievable filings.
3. **Key-person dependency on the founder.** Strategy, capital-markets credibility, partner relationships (Rakuten, Vodafone, AT&T, Google, American Tower) and the "vision narrative" all run through **Abel Avellan**, who is simultaneously Chairman, CEO and a recent large-scale hedger of his own stake. No disclosed succession plan.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **HIGH**

- **Credibility erosion on schedule.** Repeated, public timeline slippage (2026 → 2027 service launch; constellation milestone pushed) against a marketing posture of *"the first and only space-based cellular broadband network."* That claim is now directly contested by the FCC's 15,000-satellite grant to SpaceX/Starlink Mobile — a positioning risk with retail, partner and sovereign-customer audiences alike.
- **Litigation-driven headline saturation.** A dense daily flow of plaintiff-firm press releases (Rosen, Levi & Korsinsky, Kessler Topaz, et al.) is now the dominant news vector — the narrative is being written by adversaries.
- **Market/perception damage is severe and measurable.** Peak **$133.86 (28 May 2026) → $28.98 low (5 Nov 2026) = ~78% drawdown**; ‑67% at the October one-year low. **Q3-26 double miss** (EPS ‑$0.44 vs ‑$0.41; revenue $42.3M below consensus despite ~3x YoY growth) against reiterated FY guidance is a guidance-credibility stress point.
- **Sell-side defection:** UBS cut PT $62 → $43; Scotiabank initiated/moved to **Sell** ($45.60); Piper Sandler downgrade. Institutional support is thinning (‑3.56% QoQ).
- **Offsetting positive:** the **Rakuten AST joint venture** (formed 6 Nov 2026, Japan) and the FCC 248-satellite/SCS authorisation are genuine third-party validations and evidence the technology-partner ecosystem still underwrites AST. Disaster-resilience framing is a durable reputational asset.

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **🚩 CRITICAL — Founder monetisation immediately ahead of the disclosure event.** Avellan's **2.5M-share variable prepaid forward (~$147–183M, Citigroup, 22 June 2026)** preceded the 15 July 2026 launch-delay + $1B convertible-notes disclosure by ~3 weeks. Confirm whether executed under a **Rule 10b5-1 plan adopted before the class period**, and obtain the plan adoption date. If adopted *inside* the class period, escalate to investment committee as a potential control-person credibility issue.
2. **🚩 CRITICAL — Capital-sufficiency allegation sits at the heart of the pleading.** The class action alleges AST overstated its liquidity runway while it was concurrently raising **$1.0B of 1.625% convertible notes due 2034 (initial conversion price $79.57)**. With the stock at $36.89, those converts are deeply out of the money and the company remains reliant on external capital in a rising-rate, deteriorating-equity-issuance environment. Demand a **pro-forma liquidity and covenant walk** and the **D&O insurance tower / retention**.
3. **🚩 HIGH — Total loss of BlueBird 7 plus launch-provider concentration.** A destructively failed orbital insertion and a pad anomaly at a *single* launch provider is a business-continuity event, not a schedule variance. Obtain the launch-services agreement's **failure/reflight liability terms** and the multi-provider manifest.
4. **🚩 HIGH — Board independence is structurally compromised by counterparty nominee rights** (Rakuten, American Tower) layered on CEO/Chairman duality. Verify the **majority-independent** representation the governance guidelines promise, and whether litigation committee authority has been delegated to independent directors.
5. **🚩 MEDIUM — Two overlapping class periods (13 Nov and 13 Dec 2026 deadlines)** with eight-plus competing firms; assess whether the later filing **broadens the class period** to capture the October SpaceX-FCC and November drawdown events.
6. **🚩 MEDIUM — Institutional exit (‑3.56% QoQ) against ~57.5M shares short** into a 13 Nov lead-plaintiff catalyst and a 2027-dependent commercial launch.
