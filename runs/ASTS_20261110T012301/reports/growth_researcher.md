# AST SpaceMobile (ASTS) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Price $36.89 | Market cap ≈$15.1B (~409M sh) | EV ≈$13.0B (incl. $3.2B cash, ~$1.2B converts) | Beta 2.85 | Data as of Nov 9, 2026*

---

## 1. Growth Story

AST SpaceMobile is building the only planned space-based cellular broadband network that connects to **unmodified smartphones** — no app, no dish, no new chip — via large-aperture (~223 m²) BlueBird phased-array satellites in LEO, backed by contracted MNO partners (AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, Rakuten, Google) and US Government awards. This is the highest-variance name in the space economy: revenue is compounding at triple-digit rates off a near-zero base, but the entire 2027–2030 consensus rests on one binary variable — **launch cadence**. The stock has de-rated 72% from its May 2026 peak ($133.86 → $36.89) on a lost satellite, a New Glenn failure, six straight earnings misses, and a target slip, yet the operating thesis (backlog ~$1.3B, $3.2B cash, ~$1B non-dilutive Japan funding) has arguably *improved*.

**Growth quality read:**
- **9M26 actual revenue = $88.6M** ($14.7M Q1 / $31.5M Q2 / $42.3M Q3) vs. FY26 guide of $150–200M → **Q4 must deliver $61–111M**, versus a best-ever quarter of $54.3M. Guidance is Q4-loaded and single-quarter dependent.

- **Dilution is the hidden tax**: weighted shares 82M (2023) → 154M (2024) → 256M (2025) → **~409M today**. ~5x in three years. The Firefly engine deal ($1.3B) is partly stock-paid. Per-share value creation lags enterprise value creation badly.

**Estimate revision momentum — be careful:** the tool flags an "upgrade" (+83.5%), but that compares the 2030E ($3.90) to the 2029E ($2.13) — a **period-comparison artifact, not genuine revision momentum**. The real signal is negative: Piper Sandler $100 (Jul) → $98 (Aug) → **$54 + downgrade to Neutral** (Oct 13); B.Riley cut to Neutral; Deutsche cut to Hold on the New Glenn explosion. Three of the last four rating actions were downgrades. EPS surprise history: **0 of 6 beats, average −81%**. Tell-tale pattern of sandbagging that never lands.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

- **TAM**: ~$1T global mobile services revenue; ~2.6–3B people without meaningful broadband. The D2D segment specifically is projected in the tens of billions by 2030. ASTS's edge is **broadband-grade** (video-capable) D2D vs. Starlink's initial text/voice capability — a genuine technical moat from aperture size.
- **Penetration today: effectively 0.0%.** 2030E consensus of $3.87B is ~0.4% of the global mobile market. The market is implying very little penetration, which is the bull case in one sentence.
- **Backlog ~$1.3B** (MNO prepayments + government), plus Rakuten AST JV worth up to ¥148B (~$1B) of J-LEO non-dilutive support.
- **Differentiation vs. peers**: Globalstar (Apple, narrowband), Skylo (IoT), Lynk (narrowband) are all sub-scale or niche. **SpaceX Starlink D2C is the real competitor** — superior launch economics, EchoStar spectrum, T-Mobile distribution. ASTS wins only if it gets to 45+ satellites before Starlink closes the broadband gap.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4'26 commercial coverage (year-end 2026)** — management guided to "initial intermittent nationwide coverage in key markets before year-end." The single most important near-term proof point.
- **Q3/Q4'26 report (Feb–Mar 2027)** — must show Q4 revenue ≥$61M to hold the $150M floor; consensus Q4 is $72.6M.
- **BlueBird 14–16+ launches** — 13 in orbit at Q2; SpaceX/New Glenn/ISRO manifest aimed at **~45 satellites by mid-2027** (already slipped from "early 2027"). Launch agreements cover 100+ satellites for 2027–28.
- **Rakuten AST JV close (Nov 2026)** + Japan commercial ops target by March 2029; ~$1B non-dilutive.
- **Government awards** — $43M SDA contract; >$100M of funded near-term value expected across 2026–27.
- **Blacklift first launch (late 2028)** — vertical integration via Firefly Miranda engines; removes launch dependency but is long-dated and capital-hungry.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY (Speculative — position-size accordingly)**

I upgraded to Buy on the de-rating, not on the print. At $133 (May) ASTS traded at ~25x 2028E revenue on flawless-execution assumptions; at $36.89 it trades at **7.8x 2028E revenue and 3.4x 2030E**, with **$3.2B of cash (~21% of market cap)** and ~$1B of non-dilutive Japan funding. That is a reasonable price for a company with a real technical moat, $1.3B contracted backlog, and ~0% market penetration.

The asymmetry is favorable but conditional: NT price targets cluster $54–$93 (median ~$78, +110%), while a further launch slip re-tests $25–28. **This is a launch-cadence option, not a compounder yet.** I'd own it, sized as a venture-style position, and add on evidence of 2 consecutive successful multi-satellite launches.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Launch cadence is the binding constraint (highest severity).** BlueBird 7 was lost on New Glenn (April 2026); a New Glenn explosion followed in May. Going from ~16 to ~45 satellites by mid-2027 has never been executed by this company. Miss it and the entire $591M FY27 / $1.66B FY28 consensus collapses — and 22x forward revenue becomes 60x.
2. **Competitive threat from SpaceX.** Starlink D2C has unmatched launch cadence and just added EchoStar spectrum + T-Mobile distribution. If Starlink reaches broadband-grade D2D first, ASTS's aperture advantage is stranded capital.
3. **Chronic dilution.** 5x share count in three years; $1.15B converts (July 2026) and stock-funded vendor deals. Even in a success case, per-share returns are materially lower than enterprise-value returns.
4. **Capital intensity vs. cash runway.** $1.06B FY25 capex, ~$610M in a single quarter of 2026. $3.2B is ~2 years of burn at current pace — likely requiring another raise before the network is self-funding, especially with Blacklift.
5. **Revenue quality / guidance credibility.** 0-for-6 on EPS; revenue tied to lumpy gateway hardware and milestone payments, not recurring service. Guidance has leaned on a single oversized quarter twice.
6. **Governance & volatility.** Dual-class control under CEO Abel Avellan; beta 2.85; 40%+ drawdowns twice in twelve months.

**Bottom line for the memo:** ASTS remains the purest, most technically differentiated public expression of broadband direct-to-device. The 2026 de-rating has converted an expensive story stock into a reasonably priced option on a $1T market — but the option's exercise price is *45 satellites by mid-2027*, and the company has not yet demonstrated it can pay it.
