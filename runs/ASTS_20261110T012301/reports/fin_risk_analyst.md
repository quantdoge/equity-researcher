## ASTS — Financial Risk Assessment
*Data as of Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended 2026-09-30, filed 2026-11-09); price $36.89 (2026-11-09)*

### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **Sub-Investment Grade (unrated issuer)**

AST SpaceMobile is a pre-profit satellite operator funding a multi-billion-dollar constellation build with equity and equity-linked paper. It has **no EBITDA to service debt** (TTM operating loss −$758M), so traditional coverage metrics are negative and uninformative. What keeps this out of "Distress" territory is not earnings — it's the **liability structure**: ~$4.1B of convertible notes, essentially all long-dated (2030–2034) at low coupons, unsecured, with a large cash cushion. Near-term default risk is **low**; structural credit quality is **weak**.

Critically, the company demonstrated *equity-linked market access three times in FY2026* (~$3.0B) plus a $1.15B 1.625% 2034 convertible on 2026-07-20. Access to convert capital at 1.6% is a real credit positive (equity risk transferred to holders), but it is **not** evidence of debt-service capacity.

> **Data integrity flag:** Q3'26 balance sheet reports `total_debt` of **$35.2M** against `interest_expense` of $24–26M/quarter in H1'26 — impossible. Total liabilities rose +$1,150M QoQ (3,457 → 4,607), exactly the July 2026 convertible. **True gross debt ≈ $4,143M** ($2,993M at 6/30 + $1,150M issued 7/20). The leverage tool output (D/E 0.94, net debt −$96M, net debt/EBITDA 0.26) is based on **FY2025 annual** and understates current leverage. Restated Q3'26: **D/E ~1.59x book, debt/assets ~61%, net debt ~$1.34B.**

**Z-score caveat:** 0.605 is mechanically driven by negligible asset turnover (x5 = 0.014) and accumulated deficit (x2 = −0.17). The model was built for leveraged industrials and penalizes pre-revenue capex intensity; with $2.8B unrestricted cash and no maturity before 2030, the "distress" reading **materially overstates 12-month bankruptcy probability**. It is, however, an accurate statement that the business model is not yet self-funding.

**Cash runway:** $1.8B TTM burn → **~24 months on $3.7B total liquidity**, **~19 months on unrestricted $2.8B**, assuming no burn acceleration. Capex is *ramping* (Q2'26 −$598M, Q3'26 −$295M), so this is likely optimistic. Realistically a **further capital raise is needed by mid-2028**, absent a sharp revenue inflection.

### 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — revenue −20% + capex overrun

A 20% revenue decline is **financially immaterial** (TTM revenue $143M = <1% of market cap) — the real risk vector is **funding, not demand**. Stressing the cash-flow engine instead:

| Scenario | Annual burn | Liquidity | Runway | Capital need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base (TTM run-rate) | $1.80B | $3.7B | ~24 mo | 2028 raise |
| Stress: rev −20%, GM 47%→30%, capex +30% | **~$2.3–2.4B** | $3.7B | **~19 mo** | ~$1.5–2.0B by mid-2028 |

Under stress, EBIT worsens further, coverage stays deeply negative, and the funding gap must be closed by **equity or convertible issuance**. Share count has already gone from 82M (FY23) to ~408M implied today — **~5x dilution in three years**. Another $2B raised at $36.89 adds ~54M shares (~13% dilution) — and at stressed prices far more. **This is the true downside: not insolvency, but equity holders absorbing repeated dilution with a −72% drawdown attached.** Note also the 1.625% converts are only cheap debt *because* holders get the equity upside; if the stock stays depressed, future converts price far worse.

**Covenant/refinancing:** No disclosed covenant breach, and **no maturity cliff before 2030** — genuinely the strongest element of the credit. Convert maturity walls (2030/2032/2034) matter only if the stock stays below conversion price, forcing cash repayment or refinancing at scale.

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Split the rating deliberately, because the headline number hides a real distinction:
- **Liquidity risk: LOW** — $2.8B cash, 13.7x current ratio, no near-term maturities.
- **Credit/earnings risk: HIGH** — negative EBITDA, negative coverage, Z-score in distress, FCF −$1.8B.
- **Market/equity risk: CRITICAL** — 101% vol, 2.6 beta, −77% max drawdown, serial dilution.

Near-term solvency is **not** the issue; **FCF sustainability and dilution** are. If commercial revenue does not inflect materially before 2028, this becomes a Critical-rated refinancing story. Watch the revenue line, not the cash balance.

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Persistent negative FCF at scale** — −$1.8B TTM, worsening, with capex ramping. No path to self-funding visible in reported data.
2. **Serial dilution** — ~5x share count growth since FY23; F-Score already flags dilution (F7 = 0). Convert structure defers but does not eliminate the cost.
3. **Leverage compounding** — $173M debt (FY24) → ~$4.14B (Q3'26). Three convertible raises in one fiscal year (~$3.0B) signals burn materially outpacing plan.
4. **Receivables anomaly** — net receivables jumped $27M → $667M in two quarters (4.7x TTM revenue). Possible large milestone billing, but **unexplained and unverified — flag for collection-quality/related-party review.**
5. **Securities class action** (class period Oct 2025–Jul 2026) alleging misrepresentation around the convertible offerings — legal/governance overhang, unquantified.
6. **Data quality** — `total_debt` field corrupted at Q3'26; leverage/FCF-yield tool outputs use stale FY2025 and an outdated $9.44B market cap (actual ~$15.06B).
7. **Altman Z in distress zone (0.605)** and F-Score 5/9 — weak fundamental momentum despite the growth narrative.

**Data gaps:** No public credit rating or CDS spread (unrated, unhedgeable); Sortino unavailable; convert maturity-by-tranche schedule not confirmed from primary filings (SEC fetch blocked, 403); 2030/2032 tranche coupons unverified. Peer comparison is not meaningful — the sector peer set (GOOG, META, VZ, TMUS) are profitable incumbents with entirely different capital structures; no comparable pre-revenue satellite operator was available.
