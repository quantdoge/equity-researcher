Q3 2026 (reported Nov 9) was another double-miss: revenue $42.3M vs ~$45.7M est, EPS −$0.44 vs −$0.40 est. The stock still rose 14.1% on the day on a Rakuten Japan JV and reiterated FY26 guidance. ## 1. Consumer Demand Signals

**Google Trends — "AST SpaceMobile" (US, 12-mo): severely deteriorated.** Peak interest of **100** in the week of May 24, 2026; the most recent reads are **8–18** (Oct 25: 8; Nov 1: 18). That is roughly **an −85% decline in search interest from peak**, and the series has made a lower-high sequence every single month since June (100 → 87 → 75 → 56 → 43 → 28 → 32 → 29 → 14 → 13). The lone positive tell: a **tick up from 8 → 18** into the earnings date, the first sequential increase since July.

**Category trend — "direct to cell satellite":** peaked at 72–84 (May–June 2026), now reading **0–7**. The entire D2D theme's mainstream curiosity has evaporated, not just ASTS's.

**Interpretation:** ASTS is a retail-momentum stock (beta 2.85); its rallies were driven by attention. Attention is now in **washout territory**. This is *constructively contrarian* only in the sense that expectations and retail positioning have been flushed — it is **not** evidence of underlying service demand. Note honestly: Google Trends measures *investor* interest here, not subscriber demand. ASTS has no consumer app, so app-store and download-velocity signals are **not applicable** — the real demand signal (D2D subscriber activations) is not yet externally observable, because commercial intermittent service is only *scheduled* to activate in the US "before year-end."

## 2. Business Activity Signals

**Hiring — strong and unambiguously operational (bullish).** Indeed shows **~300–362 open AST SpaceMobile roles** against **1,126 full-time employees** — roughly **27–32% of headcount open simultaneously.** The composition is the signal: CNC Machinist (42 roles), Mechanic (32), Senior Avionics Technician (15), Satellite Manufacturing Engineer, Industrial Maintenance Technician, Warehouse Associate, Inventory Control Supervisor — all **Midland, TX**. This is not speculative R&D hiring; it is **production-line and supply-chain staffing**, exactly what you'd expect ahead of a manufacturing step-change. Corroborated by delivery data: BlueBirds 8–10 in orbit and operational, 11–13 next, and **14–16 already delivered to Cape Canaveral**, with a 400,000 sq ft Midland facility targeting up to six BlueBirds/month. Management reports **13 satellites in orbit, targeting 45 by early 2027.**

**Patents — no data.** The USPTO PatentsView tool returned an error on retry; I am carrying this as a gap rather than inferring. For a company at this stage, IP breadth matters less than launch cadence anyway.

**The key read:** business activity is inflecting **up** while the stock is down 72%. The binding constraint is **launch cadence, not factory throughput** — satellites are being built faster than they're being flown.

## 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **BELOW consensus**

This is the most quantitatively damning signal.

- **Surprise record: 0 beats in 6 quarters. Average EPS surprise −81%.** Magnitudes: −114%, −159%, −144%, −44%, −16%, −9%. Repeat, serial, large misses. Estimate revisions have chased reality downward and still lag.
- **Revenue misses in 3 of the last 4 quarters** (−62%, −8.7%, −7.4%), with the one beat (Q4 2025, $54.3M vs $41.8M) coming from lumpy contract timing.
- **The Q4 2026 bar is punishing.** FY26 guidance was *reiterated* at $150–200M, but 9M actuals are only **$88.6M** ($14.7M + $31.5M + $42.3M). Implied Q4 revenue is **$61.4M–$111.4M** — a **+45% to +163% sequential step-up** off a $42.3M base. Consensus sits at **$73.6M** and **EPS −$0.40** for the March 1, 2027 print.
- Positive offset: revenue is genuinely inflecting (+**187% YoY**, +34% QoQ) and cash is fortified (~**$3.2–3.7B** after a $1.15B raise), so a revenue miss is a *dilution-of-credibility* event, not a solvency event.

**Verdict: tracking below.** The alt-data mix (elite hiring + satellite deliveries) supports the 2027 story but does nothing to close a +45–163% sequential gap in the *current* quarter.

But they are a **2027 signal**, and the next data point investors must survive is a **March 2027 print that needs $61–111M of Q4 revenue versus $42M just delivered**, from a management team with a **0-for-6 beat record**.

That is not a bullish asymmetry at $36.89. It is a **wait-for-the-miss** setup. I would look for a constructive entry **after** Q4 results confirm the revenue step-up — or if the stock holds the ~$30 area on a Q4 miss (the Nov 5 intraday low of $28.98 is the level to watch), which would signal the bad news is finally priced.

The earnings-surprise history is hard, audited data.

**Net:** Alt data is a leading indicator, not a verdict. Here it leads in two directions at once — toward a better 2027 and toward another near-term miss. Neutral, medium conviction, with the actionable clarity being *timing*: the operational data says the story is real; the surprise record says don't pay for it before March.
