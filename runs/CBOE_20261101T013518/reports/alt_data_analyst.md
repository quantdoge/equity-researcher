### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH — but with decelerating momentum**

**Confidence Level: MEDIUM**

**The bull case from alt data is real and corroborated by hard sources:** OCC September volume +37.9% YoY (642M contracts), YTD ADV +35.4%; index options +21.9% YoY; **0DTE at a record 24.2M ADV (36% of all volume)**; Cboe multi-list ADV of 15.3M, its second-best quarter ever; Cboe FX spot ADV $61.6B in September, +25.6% YoY. Extended/overnight Global Trading Hours are setting records and the new pre/post-market single-stock options sessions add incremental capture. This is a structurally growing franchise with recurring Data Vantage revenue that is *not* volume-sensitive.

**Three things keep this from High confidence:**

1. **The signal is largely already in the price.** CBOE closed at $335.53, up **+32% in one month** (from $253.26 on Sept 28) and +37% YoY, essentially at all-time highs ($340.42 on Oct 23). Alt data's bullish content is *priced*; its bearish content (October engagement softening) is not.
2. **The low-volatility regime is the key risk.** VIX search interest near 12-month lows implies suppressed hedging demand into Q4/Q1 — and Q1 2027 consensus already models a **−1.2% YoY revenue decline and −5.4% EPS decline** against a very tough March 2026 volatility-spike comp. That comp is the single largest swing factor in the next two prints.
3. **Data quality is uneven.** Google Trends is spike-distorted and unreliable on levels; web traffic and social are qualitative web-search proxies, not API-grade; patents unavailable; peer multiples unavailable (the sector-peer tool returned only Vanguard index funds — unusable). No satellite/geospatial coverage applies to an exchange operator.

**Bottom line for the investment memo:** Alt data supports a **constructive fundamental stance** — record 0DTE/index flow, raised guidance, disciplined costs, aggressive buyback — and suggests consensus Q4 revenue is ~conservative. But the incremental, not-yet-priced information points to **sequential deceleration in retail engagement and a low-VIX tape**, so I would not chase the +32% one-month move. The favorable setup is in **EPS delivery and multiple support**, not in accelerating transaction volume. Watch October/November OCC ADV prints and VIX term structure as the confirming or falsifying datapoints ahead of Feb 5.
