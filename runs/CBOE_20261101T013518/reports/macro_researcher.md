## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional — supply-shock inflation regime with a hawkish policy pivot. Not risk-off, but not risk-on either: a "high nominal, high vol-input, low equity-vol" regime.**

As of the Oct 28–30, 2026 data set, the US is in a **stagflation-lite** configuration. A 2026 oil supply disruption pushed WTI from $69.6 (Jul 6, 2026) back above $100 (Oct 27: $103.37), re-accelerating headline CPI from 3.30% YoY (Jul) to 3.72% (Sep) — with the monthly pace accelerating to +0.65% m/m in September. The Fed, under Chair Warsh, **hiked 25bp in September 2026** to 3.75–4.00% (ending the easing cycle that had taken the effective rate from 5.33% in mid-2024 to 3.63%), then **held on Oct 28 with two dissents**, and markets expect a **December hike**. Long yields have repriced violently: the 10Y is 4.98% (5.31% peak on Oct 5), the 30Y is 5.32%, and the 2s10s has bear-steepened to **+37bp** (2Y 4.61%) — i.e. the market is pricing roughly 60–70bp of *additional* hikes, not cuts.

Critically, 5Y breakevens are only **2.46%** — the market treats the oil shock as largely temporary — but they are rising (from 2.18% in early August) and the 10Y real yield is ~2.5%. Unemployment at 4.2% and Q3 real GDP +2.1% YoY (+3.3% SAAR) say growth has not broken; nominal PCE is +6.3% YoY. This is a high-nominal-growth, high-real-cost-of-capital world.

Meanwhile equity vol is *suppressed* — VIX 17.2 on Oct 29, 6-month range 14.2–22.2, only recently nudging ~19.4 in early November. Vol-selling and 0DTE structural flows are compressing index vol even as rates and commodities are volatile. That divergence is the single most important thing to understand about Cboe's macro setup.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

Cboe is a **low-beta (0.41), asset-light toll road on financial-market activity**, so its macro transmission channels are unusual — volume, not GDP, is the revenue driver.

**POSITIVE**
- **Inflation/commodity shock → volatility demand.** Higher realized vol and macro uncertainty (oil >$100, 5% long bonds) drive hedging and 0DTE index-option flow. Sept 2026 index-options ADV 6.42M vs 5.25M a year ago (**+22%**); multi-list ADV 15.3M was the second-best quarter on record. Q3 net revenue +18% YoY, adj. EPS +36% to $3.63, and management *raised* 2026 organic revenue growth to "high teens."
- **Higher short rates → net interest income.** Cboe earns meaningful income on clearing/corporate cash. The 2025 easing cycle (5.33%→3.63%) was a quiet headwind; the September hike and a December follow-up **re-warm this line item**.
- **Rate volatility** supports the futures/volatility complex and Cboe's data franchise (Data Vantage target raised to mid-teens growth) — recurring, macro-insensitive revenue now roughly a third of the mix.
- **Low beta = relative shelter.** In a 5%-10Y world, Cboe's earnings stream is barely GDP-geared; a growth scare hits it far less than cyclicals.

**NEGATIVE**
- **The discount rate is the whole story on valuation.** With the 10Y at ~5.0% and 30Y at 5.3%, CBOE — a long-duration, high-multiple compounder — has de-rated to a **~25% discount to its 5-year average P/E even after +36% EPS growth**. That is pure multiple compression, and it is the main reason the stock at $335.53 ($35.0bn cap) hasn't tracked earnings.
- **Suppressed VIX caps the highest-margin complex.** VIX futures ADV fell to 191k in Sept 2026 from 207k a year earlier (**−8%**). A mid-teens VIX regime with heavy systematic call-selling is structurally deflationary for VIX product demand; index-options growth has masked a soft volatility franchise.
- **Strong USD is a translation drag.** With DTWEXBGS at 121.3 (+2.8% since May) and ~20% of revenue from Europe/APAC (Cboe Europe, Cboe Japan, Cboe FX), reported international revenue faces a modest ~1–2pt FX headwind — small, but real and worsening.
- **Wage/service inflation** at 3.7% CPI pressures the ~$830–845m expense base (guidance was cut, so currently well controlled — this has been neutral rather than harmful).
- **Consumer-dependent retail flow.** If the oil shock pushes unemployment from 4.2% toward 4.75%+, retail options participation — a key volume source — would soften.

**NEUTRAL**
- Tariff/trade exposure is negligible directly; the channel runs through volatility (mild positive) and European volumes (mild negative).
- Refinancing risk is small (modest senior-notes stack) but new issuance at 5%+ is a long-term cost headwind.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL** (operating environment favourable; valuation environment unfavourable)

The macro backdrop is a **net positive for Cboe's P&L and a net negative for its multiple**, and they roughly cancel. High realized vol, resurgent interest income and lapping rate cuts support earnings; a 5% 10Y caps the multiple. The 8-point VIX futures decline and the FX drag keep this from being Favourable.

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise

**Bull (underpriced): the oil shock breaks as fast as it formed.** WTI back to $75–80 by Q1 2027 (supply restoration, demand destruction from a 5% 10Y), CPI rolls back to ~2.8%, the Fed cancels December and the 10Y falls to 4.2%. Cboe gets the *double* — high-teens organic revenue growth and high-teens volume from the prior vol regime, *plus* 3–4 turns of multiple re-rating off a 25%-discounted base. This is the asymmetric path; a beta of 0.41 means the market is not paying for it.

**Bear (the real risk): a stagflation trap with no vol relief.** If the Fed is forced to 4.50–5.00% and the 10Y clears 5.50% (tested 5.31% intraday Oct 5 already), real yields grind toward 2.8%+. Cboe keeps posting record volumes yet de-rates to 18–20x as the discount rate dominates — a flat-to-down stock on rising earnings. The tell would be **5Y breakevens breaking above 2.75%** while VIX stays under 16: the worst combination for Cboe is sticky inflation *without* volatility, which suppresses the VIX complex while compressing the multiple. Watch the December FOMC and the monthly CPI prints (Sep +0.65% m/m) as the two swing catalysts.
