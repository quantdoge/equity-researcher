### 1. Growth Story

Cboe is not a "stock exchange" — it is a **monopoly-like toll road on the two fastest-compounding derivatives franchises in global markets**: SPX index options and VIX. The September 2026 **25-year extension of the exclusive S&P 500 options license through 2051** removes the single largest terminal-risk on the equity story and converts CBOE into something closer to a perpetual royalty on U.S. equity risk transfer. On top of that base, management is doing something un-Cboe-like: **attacking new TAMs** — prediction markets ("Cboe Predicts," XSP binary contracts launched Q2 2026), Robinhood-distributed KPI contracts (Oct 2026), and tokenized options under the S&P partnership. The near-term re-rate driver is simpler: **2026 organic net revenue guidance has been raised three separate times, from "mid single-digit" (Feb) → "low-double-to-mid-teens" (Q2) → "high teens" (Q3)** — the most powerful revision cadence on this name in a decade.

### 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration

- **Index options (core TAM):** SPX is the world's most liquid index option. 0DTE has structurally expanded the addressable base from institutional hedgers to a daily-trading retail cohort. Penetration is still low relative to single-stock options — Cboe's **total options market share is 30.0%, but multi-listed share is only 23.5%**, meaning the growth is concentrated in the *exclusive* franchise where pricing power is unassailable.
- **Prediction markets (new TAM):** The Kalshi/Polymarket category has proven demand; Cboe's differentiated wedge is using **options infrastructure rather than binary-only payouts**, plus distribution via Robinhood. CFTC's June 2026 proposed rule on event contracts is the regulatory gate. This is optionality, not yet in numbers — size it at $0 today, but it's a genuine call option on a category that could be $10B+ in revenue industry-wide.
- **Data Vantage (highest-quality TAM):** Organic growth guidance raised to **"mid teens"** from "low teens." This is recurring, high-margin, non-volume-dependent revenue — the piece that should command a software-like multiple and de-cyclicalize the equity.
- **Tokenized options:** Explicitly named in the S&P extension as a joint innovation avenue. Long-dated, but it's the reason the license runs to 2051 rather than 2035.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q4 2026 earnings — Feb 5, 2027.** Consensus EPS $3.50 on $720M net revenue. Watch for a *fourth* guidance raise and, critically, **initial 2027 framing**. If management guides 2027 organic growth to low-double-digits rather than the ~5% consensus implies, the stock re-rates immediately.
2. **KPI contracts with Robinhood (launched Oct 2026).** First revenue evidence in Q4/Q1. Distribution through Robinhood is the cheapest customer acquisition channel in the industry.
3. **CFTC event-contract rulemaking (proposed June 2026, final rule pending).** A favorable final rule validating exchange-listed event contracts is a direct TAM unlock for Cboe Predicts.
4. **Monthly volume/RPC prints.** Index options ADV and RPC capture are the highest-frequency read on the thesis — 0DTE mix and SPX RPC per contract are the two numbers that move estimates.
5. **Tokenized options pilot** under the S&P DJI collaboration — any concrete announcement is a narrative re-rating event (crypto-adjacent multiple expansion).

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (not Strong Buy)

The business quality is arguably the best in financial infrastructure: a legally protected 25-year monopoly on the world's most important derivative, 66% Rule-of-40 on a net revenue basis, 48% FCF margins, beta of 0.41, and a fresh set of new-TAM call options. The revision cycle is unambiguously positive and consensus 2027 EPS looks too low.

What keeps this from Strong Buy is **price versus consensus**: at $335.53 the stock trades **23.6x 2026E EPS and 21.9x 2027E**, which is a **PEG of ~2.8** against the Street's ~8% forward EPS CAGR. The stock is also **above the majority of published price targets** ($300–$332 from GS, JPM, Wells Fargo); only Morgan Stanley's $450 is meaningfully higher. In other words, the easy money from the guidance raises has been made, and the next leg requires 2027 estimates to move up — a catalyst that is 3 months away and depends on volume durability through a potentially calmer volatility regime.

Position sizing implication: **own it, add on the 2027 guidance print, don't chase at $335.**

### 7. Key Risks

- **Volume cyclicality is the real risk, not competition.** Revenue is levered to volatility and 0DTE engagement. The May–June 2026 drawdown (from $369 to $226, **-35% in five weeks**) is a live demonstration of how violently this stock de-rates when volume momentum stalls. Peak-cycle EPS in 2026 makes the 2027 estimate vulnerable to a mean-reversion cut.
- **Kalshi is coming for the franchise directly.** Kalshi has filed with the CFTC for **S&P 500 perpetual futures** — a direct shot at both CME and CBOE. The 2051 exclusivity covers *options*, not perpetual futures or event contracts. Watch this filing closely; it is the one credible structural threat to the SPX moat.
- **Multi-listed options share erosion.** 23.5% multi-listed share against 30% total — Nasdaq, NYSE, and MIAX continue to compete on price in the commoditized multi-list book. Cboe's answer is to lean into the exclusive index franchise, which concentrates risk further.
- **Data Vantage regulatory risk.** Mid-teens data growth relies on market-data fee escalation; SEC market-data rulemaking is a persistent overhang on that margin stream.
- **Execution risk in prediction markets.** Binaries/KPI contracts are unproven, sub-scale, and face entrenched network effects (Kalshi, Polymarket) plus an unresolved CFTC rulebook. Assign near-zero value until revenue is visible.
- **Multiple compression math.** If 2027 EPS lands at consensus $15.31 and the multiple normalizes to 20x, downside is ~$306 (-9%); if volumes roll over and estimates cut to $14.50, ~$275 (-18%). Asymmetric to the downside near-term unless the Q4 print revises the forward curve.
