## Cboe Global Markets (CBOE) — Value Investment Analysis

**Price $335.53 | Market cap ~$35.0B | 104.5M diluted shares | Net cash ~$0.5B**

*Data note: FY2025 GAAP expense lines in the feed were internally inconsistent (SG&A of $91.5M vs. $603M prior year), so I rely on revenue, cost-of-revenue and operating-income aggregates, which reconcile. The provided DCF tool returned a clearly broken output (terminal value PV = $0), so I built the DCF myself. Most screening tools key off FY2025; because 2026 has been exceptionally strong, I lead with TTM (Q4'25–Q3'26) figures.*

---

### 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **WIDE**

Cboe is the toll bridge on the two most important risk-transfer products in global equity markets: SPX options and VIX.

- **Intangible assets / exclusive license (the core moat).** On **September 29, 2026, Cboe signed a 25-year extension of its exclusive S&P 500 index options license through 2051.** This is the single most important fact in the thesis. The prior agreement ran to 2032, which meant a large share of Cboe's enterprise value carried a contractual cliff. That cliff is now gone for a generation. Exclusivity on the world's benchmark index, in a product with 43 years of liquidity history, is close to an unassailable intangible asset.
- **Network effects.** Index options are a winner-take-most market: liquidity attracts liquidity. Q3'26 index options ADV rose **24%**, with **0DTE at a record 65% of SPX volume** — a self-reinforcing intraday ecosystem no new entrant can seed.
- **Cost/efficient scale.** Capex is ~1.5% of revenue. Operating leverage is extraordinary: TTM GAAP operating income of **$2,051M on $2,753M of net revenue (74.5%)**.
- **Where the moat is *not* widening.** Multi-listed (single-stock) options are contested: total options share was **29.1% in Q1'26 vs. 31.1% a year earlier**, with multi-listed share at ~23.5%. Cash equities are a scale-deficient business — Cboe is divesting Cboe Canada and has exited Japan/Australia.
- **Moat trend: widened.** The license extension arguably adds more durable value than any single product launch could, while management has been *shrinking* into its best business rather than empire-building.

**Verdict: Wide moat, concentrated in derivatives (~65-70% of net revenue).** This is a business I can understand, and one where the passage of time works *for* the owner.

ROIC of ~19–22% over three of the last four years against a ~8.5% cost of capital is the textbook signature of a moated business. FCF conversion above 100% means reported earnings are, if anything, conservative. Capital intensity is negligible.

**Two deductions:** (1) FY2022 was a genuine stinker — $235M of net income, a 12.4% operating margin and ROIC of 9.2%, driven by a roughly $400M impairment on the digital-asset/FTSE acquisitions. That is a reminder this is an acquisition-built company and that management has written off capital before. (2) Goodwill and intangibles are $4.45B, **86% of book equity** — book value of $48.89/share is not a meaningful asset value, and P/B of 6.9x tells you nothing useful here.

Revenue predictability is good but not bond-like: roughly 35–40% is non-transactional (Data Vantage, access and regulatory fees — Data Vantage grew 13% in Q3), while the rest is volume- and volatility-sensitive.

*(Peers shown on the same FY25 basis for comparability; the sector-median tool returned a 52x "median" off a single index-fund comparable, which is meaningless — I discarded it.)*

On FY2025 earnings CBOE screens at a premium to CME and ICE. On the much stronger TTM run-rate it screens at a discount to all three. The truth is in between: 2026 is a cyclical high-water mark in volatility and index-option volume, so I refuse to capitalize the TTM number at a market multiple.

**My own DCF (owner earnings ~$1,400M base, deliberately haircut from TTM EPS to temper the record-volume quarter):**

| Growth / 8.5% WACC, 3% terminal | Intrinsic value |
|---|---|
| 6% for 10 years | ~$334 |
| 7% for 10 years | ~$360 |
| 8% for 10 years | ~$389 |

**Base case intrinsic value ≈ $360/share (range $330–390).** At $335.53 the stock trades at a **~7% discount to base case intrinsic value — essentially fair value.** My required 25% discount to base case implies a **buy price at or below ~$270**.

Context matters: CBOE fell ~33% from mid-May 2026 ($365) to late June ($242) on valuation compression and competitive-threat fears, then round-tripped to $335 following the S&P extension and a strong Q3. **The opportunity was in June; it has been arbitraged away.** The stock is +38% off that low.

### 4. Management Quality — Good, with scar tissue

- **CEO Craig Donohue** (since 2023) is a career exchange operator and former CME executive chairman — a domain expert, not a hired-gun cost-cutter. CFO Jill Griebenow. Small, senior, specialist team (1,661 employees).
- **Capital allocation is increasingly disciplined.** Exiting Japan, Australia and now Canada to concentrate capital in derivatives is the right, unglamorous call. SG&A is being managed down (2026 adjusted opex guidance *lowered* to $830–845M even as revenue guidance rose).
- **Dividend raised every year:** $0.48 → $0.50 → $0.55 → $0.63 → $0.72 → $0.86 quarterly (~12% CAGR), yet the payout is only ~24% of earnings. Buybacks are modest ($97M in 2025, $226M in 2024) and opportunistic rather than mechanical — share count drifted from 109.1M (2020) to 104.5M, roughly 0.8%/yr.
- **Blemishes:** the 2022 digital-asset/FTSE write-off is a real M&A error, and the S&P extension almost certainly came at the price of higher royalty economics (I could not verify terms — see risks). Insider ownership data was not available to me; that is a gap I cannot close, and it is the standard disclosure I most want to see for an owner-operator judgment.

### 5. Investment Verdict: **HOLD**

This is exactly the kind of business I want to own — wide moat, 22% ROIC, 100%+ FCF conversion, net cash, negligible capex, a license locked to 2051, priced at roughly 24x trailing and 16x EV/EBITDA. The quality is not in question.

The **price** is the problem. A fair price is not a buy. At $335 I am earning the business's ~7-9% long-run compounding and nothing more, with no cushion if index-option volumes mean-revert from a record 2026 or if royalty costs step up. The margin of safety that existed in June at $242 is gone.

- **Buy** at ≤$280 (~22% discount to base-case intrinsic value)
- **Strong Buy** at ≤$250
- **Trim/reassess** above ~$410 (10% premium to the top of my range)

I would be a patient, happy buyer of this asset on the next volatility-driven panic. I would not chase it here.

### 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Cyclical peak risk.** 2026 revenue growth of "high teens" rides record index-option volumes and 0DTE (65% of SPX). A low-volatility regime, or a change in market structure, could flatten revenue fast while cost guidance was set against boom conditions.
2. **Royalty terms to S&P DJI (unverified, and my biggest analytical gap).** A 25-year extension of a crown-jewel exclusivity was not free. If the revenue-share stepped up materially, the license extension is worth less to shareholders than headline sentiment implies. I could not obtain the contract economics.
3. **0DTE regulatory/commercial scrutiny.** A product that is 65% of flagship volume invites attention from regulators and from the index provider itself.
4. **Multi-listed share erosion.** Total options share 31.1% → 29.1% in a year, against Nasdaq, NYSE and MEMX. Penny-increment pilots compress capture rates.
5. **New ventures are unproven.** KPI event contracts (SEC-approved, launching November 2026) and Cboe Predicts carry legal, jurisdictional and reputational risk — plus a competitive collision with CME. History (Cboe Digital) suggests this is where capital gets lost.
6. **Multiple compression risk.** At ~24x TTM, a re-rating to CME's ~19x EV/EBITDA on unchanged earnings is a ~20% drawdown — larger than the entire margin of safety on offer.
7. **Acquisition-built balance sheet.** $4.45B of goodwill/intangibles (86% of equity) leaves little tangible cushion if a future deal disappoints, as one already did in 2022.
