# FORENSIC SHORT-SELLER ANALYSIS — Tempus AI, Inc. (NASDAQ: TEM)

**Price $55.45 (20-Nov-26) | Mkt cap ~$10.0B | EV ~$10.9B | Beta 3.6 | Short-vol.**

---

## 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **LOW–MEDIUM (not fraudulent)**

No evidence of accounting fraud. What the data does show is a **low-quality, acquisition-manufactured earnings story**: structurally negative free cash flow, headline growth that is a roll-up artifact, and quarterly "profits" that come from below the operating line rather than operations.

**The single most important finding:** Q2-26 net income of **+$5.6M was reported as Tempus's first profitable quarter while operating income was –$75.9M.** An $81.5M non-operating gain (contingent consideration / warrant fair-value marks, Ambry earnout) manufactured the EPS beat. Q1-26 ran the other way (–$41M below the line), Q3-26 +$42M. A company whose "path to profitability" narrative swings ±$80M per quarter on mark-to-market items does not have a clean earnings base.

**Free cash flow — never positive, in any year as a public or private company:**

| | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue ($M) | 320.7 | 531.8 | 693.4 | 1,271.8 |
| Operating CF ($M) | –168.2 | –214.3 | –189.0 | –218.1 |
| FCF ($M) | –186.6 | –248.9 | –211.2 | –245.4 |
| OCF margin | –52% | –40% | –27% | **–17%** |

Cumulative 4-year FCF burn: **–$892M**. Revenue has grown 4x while operating cash burn has *increased* in absolute dollars every year except 2024. Critically, **2025 operating cash flow included a –$178.5M working-capital drain** — 82% of the entire OCF deficit. Growth here is being financed by the balance sheet, not generated by the P&L. That is the definition of low-quality growth.

Scorecard: Earnings quality composite **48/100**; cash conversion 0.89 (2025); Piotroski **F=5/9** (fails positive ROA, positive OCF, no-dilution, asset turnover).

## 2. Key Red Flags (data-supported)

**R1 — Receivables outrunning revenue, two consecutive years (genuine flag).** AR growth **+63.9% vs revenue +30.4%** (2024), then **+106.7% vs +83.4%** (2025). DSO: **64.9d (2023) → 81.5d (2024) → 91.8d (2025) → ~84d (Q3-26)**. Two straight years of red-flagged divergence is the classic channel-stuffing / aggressive-recognition signature. *Mitigant: Ambry's hereditary-testing book is billed through insurance payers, structurally lengthening collection cycles; DSO has stabilised near 84 days for three quarters rather than continuing to climb. This is a yellow flag, not a smoking gun.*

**R2 — Growth is a roll-up illusion and it is decelerating hard.** Reported YoY: +75% / +90% / +85% / +83% through 2025 (Ambry lapped Feb-25) → **+36.1% (Q1-26) → +21.6% (Q2-26) → +23.1% (Q3-26)**. Post-lap organic growth is roughly **one quarter** the advertised rate. Spruce Point's core claim — that guidance revisions reveal weakness in the *core* genomics and data businesses, not the acquired one — is supported by this series.

**R3 — No operating leverage in the cost base.** Gross margin genuinely expanded (40.6% → 62.7%; 64.8% in Q3-26). But **SG&A has been pinned at 56–61% of revenue for six straight quarters** ($212M → $232M, +$7M/qtr). Every 100bps of gross-margin gain is being re-consumed by SG&A. Q3-26 EBITDA margin: **5.0%**. The margin story is real at the gross line and absent at the operating line.

**R4 — Negative tangible equity.** Goodwill + intangibles **$822.5M** vs total equity **$500.4M** → **tangible book equity ≈ –$322M**. Altman Z = **–0.65 ("Distress")**, driven by retained earnings of **–$2.54B** and x4 (book equity/liabilities) of 0.28. *I discount this heavily: Altman is mechanically broken for post-acquisition growth companies with accumulated losses, and liquidity is fine (current ratio 2.6x, $637M cash). Flagged for completeness, not as a solvency call.*

**R5 — Serial dilution.** Weighted diluted shares 154.2M (2023) → 162.1M (2024) → 174.3M (2025) → **181.3M (Q3-26)** = **+17.6% in under three years**, before the $1.5B Personalis deal (announced 20-Jul-26, 28% premium, close late-26/early-27) is funded. Buying a $1.5B asset with **negative FCF and $841M net debt** implies further equity or convertible issuance.

**R6 — The data-privacy class action attacks the business model, not just the P&L.** *Case 1:26-cv-04246 (N.D. Cal., filed 15-Apr-26)* alleges Tempus compelled Ambry to hand over customers' genetic data without consent and that "de-identified" genetic data is inherently re-identifiable. If that premise survives, the legal foundation of the **Insights data-licensing business** (the high-margin segment driving the gross-margin expansion) is exposed to Genetic Information Privacy Act / state AG action. This is a moat-legality risk, materially more serious than a settlement cost.

## 4. Competitive Threat Assessment: **Moderate** (the weakest part of the bear case)

- **What is not eroding:** gross margin *expanded 24 points in three years*. That is the opposite of pricing-power loss. Inventory days stable/falling (42.7 → 39.8). Oncology test volume +26%, genetic disease +32%. Scale economics in the lab are real.
- **What is at risk:** (a) **Reimbursement** — CGP panel pricing, MolDX/local coverage and prior-auth pressure are the structural tax on all molecular diagnostics; (b) crowded field — Guardant, Foundation Medicine/Roche, Natera, NeoGenomics, Exact Sciences, Caris, plus Thermo Fisher and Illumina upstream; (c) the **"AI company" framing is thin** — Spruce Point estimates only ~**2% of revenue** derives from AI applications. The equity is valued as an AI platform and operates as a diagnostic lab with a data-licensing sidecar.

## 5. Management & Governance: **the strongest leg of the bear case**

- **Serial promoter with a documented public-market record.** Founder/CEO/Chairman Eric Lefkofsky (also 10% owner) built Groupon — a company whose post-IPO equity destruction is one of the defining cases of the 2011 vintage — plus InnerWorkings and Echo Global Logistics. Spruce Point's thesis is explicitly that he and his associates "promote disruptive technology companies, cash out early, and leave public shareholders with losses."
- **Alleged SoftBank JV round-tripping** to manufacture revenue/income, per Spruce Point. Unproven, but it goes directly to revenue *quality* and deserves weighting alongside R1 and R2.
- **Weakening partnerships:** AstraZeneca and Pathos AI deals characterised as deteriorating — material because pharma data licensing is the margin engine.
- **Board restatement associations** flagged by Spruce Point.
- **Insider selling is persistent and large, though partially explainable.** Lefkofsky sold ~$16.3M (238k sh) on 27-Aug-26, ~$17.6M (250k sh) on 27-Oct-26, and multiple tranches in Nov-26; ~$11.6M *net* over 90 days; numerous daily Form 4s. Much is characterised as RSU-vesting tax withholding (routine, and he retains a ~$1.06B stake — not a wholesale exit). **But a CEO/Chairman selling into strength, monthly, while the company issues equity to fund acquisitions, is a revealed-preference signal that should not be waved away.**
- **Consensus gap:** sell-side has **2027 EPS at –$0.015 and 2028 at +$0.55**. There is no GAAP profit in the forecast horizon.

## 6. Short Thesis Summary — is there a credible bear case? **Yes, a valuation-and-quality bear case; not a fraud case.**

At $10.0B cap / $10.9B EV on TTM revenue of $1,509M: **7.2x EV/Sales**, **75x 2027E EBITDA ($144M)**, ~**100x 2028E EPS**. The market is paying today for 2030 EPS of $2.95 (4 analyst estimates, only 4 covering) — a five-year-forward multiple of 18.8x that requires *every* estimate to land. Meanwhile: organic growth has halved to ~22%, SG&A refuses to lever, FCF has never been positive in four years (–$892M cumulative), shares are up 17.6% in three years pre-Personalis, tangible equity is negative, and a class action attacks the legality of the data-licensing moat. A de-rate to 4.5–5.0x sales — still a premium multiple — implies **$38–45, ~20–30% downside**.

**Why I am *not* calling Strong Short:**
1. **No accounting fraud.** Negative accruals, clean inventory, no auditor change, improving gross margins. The Beneish flag is an acquisition artifact — I will not launder it into a fraud claim.
2. **Profitability is genuinely inflecting.** EBITDA +$45M (Q2-26) and +$20.5M (Q3-26), GM 64.8%, guidance *raised* to $1.595–1.605B, interest expense down from $15.3M to $6.7M/quarter. This is the best fundamental momentum TEM has ever had.
3. **Squeeze risk is extreme.** Beta 3.6; **+47% in three sessions (18–21 Aug-26)**, +14% in a day (17-Sep-26), +18% in a day (19-Aug-26). A short here can be right on the thesis and still be carried out.
4. The data repository (multimodal clinical + molecular + imaging, with real AZ/GSK/Merck/Recursion contracts) is a genuinely scarce asset that strategic buyers value.

## 7. Contrarian Rating: **SHORT** (not Strong Short)

**Recommended expression:** small, defined-risk — long-dated put spreads or a put-overlay rather than naked short, given 3.6 beta and demonstrated 40%+ squeeze velocity. Sizing must assume the stock can trade $85 before it trades $45.

**What would break the thesis (cover triggers):** two consecutive quarters of *positive* operating cash flow; DSO declining back below 75 days; SG&A falling below 50% of revenue; Personalis funded without equity issuance; the genetic-data class action dismissed or cheaply settled.

**What would confirm it:** continued 20%-or-below organic growth into 2027; another equity raise for Personalis; a further 5+ days of DSO expansion; or any restatement/8-K disclosing an SEC inquiry into the SoftBank JV or Ambry data transfer.
