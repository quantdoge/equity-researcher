**The contested view — price, not business quality:** Growth capitalises the 2027–2030 inflection; Value discounts the 2030 terminal FCF and gets $35–40. The gap is entirely a discount-rate and terminal-margin argument. Given the macro agent's evidence that the 10Y alone moves this stock ±30%, the valuation outcome is **more a rates call than a company call**.

**Conflict resolution applied:**
