## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Sub-Investment Grade (implied), but materially de-risked in 2026**

TEM is unrated and carries no public CDS spread (**data gap** — no rating agency coverage retrievable). On fundamentals it screens as an implied **B-/CCC+** credit on the FY2025 balance sheet, improving to ~**B/B+** on the post-May-2026 capital structure.

The credit story turns on one event: in **May 2026 TEM issued $460M of 0% Convertible Senior Notes due 15-May-2032 and retired its senior credit facility**. Consequences:

- **Covenant risk essentially eliminated** — unsecured converts carry no maintenance covenants; the term facility (the only covenant-bearing instrument) is gone.
- **Interest burden collapsing**: quarterly interest expense fell $15.3M (Q4'25) → $14.3M → $10.3M → **$6.7M (Q3'26)**, a ~$27M annualised run-rate vs $70.3M in FY2025.
- **No maturity before ~2029** (nearest legacy converts), then 2032. No refinancing cliff in the next three years.
- **The residual risk is conversion, not default.** If the equity stays well below strike, ~$1.3B of converts must be cash-settled at maturity. A 60% drawdown scenario with negative EBITDA makes 2029–2032 refinancing structurally difficult.

**Interest coverage is negative and therefore not meaningful as a ratio (EBIT −$220.8M / $70.3M = −3.1x).** This is not a liquidity constraint today — cash interest is near zero on the new notes — but it confirms the business does not yet self-fund its cost of capital.

## 7. Key Red Flags

1. **$1.3B convert wall, 2029–2032.** With negative EBITDA, these can only be settled through a higher stock price or new equity. A sustained drawdown *is* a refinancing risk at TEM — credit risk and equity risk are the same risk here.
2. **Dilution is structural, not incidental.** TTM SBC $200M = **13.3% of revenue**; share count 174.3M → 181.3M (+4.0% y/y). F-score penalises it (F7 = 0). Every year of losses is paid for in shares.
3. **Goodwill/intangibles $825M = 36% of assets**, up from $85M post-Ambry. A downturn plus a −35% revenue scenario puts a material impairment on the table.
4. **Accumulated deficit −$2.39B**; book equity only $491M (22% of assets). Accounting equity is nearly exhausted — the balance sheet is equity-market-dependent.
5. **Extreme market risk:** beta 2.67–3.61 and 93% vol mean a market drawdown translates into a 2.5–3.5x amplified loss. This is not a defensive healthcare holding.
6. **Concentration in pharma data partnerships** (AstraZeneca, GSK, Merck, Recursion). A pullback in pharma R&D data budgets hits the highest-margin revenue line first.
