### 1. Growth Story

Tempus is building the "closed-loop" operating system for precision oncology: it runs the sequencing test (xT/xR ~$4,500 ADLT), captures the resulting multimodal clinical + molecular + imaging data, and resells that de-identified dataset and AI models to pharma (AZ, GSK, BMS, Merck, BioNTech, Pfizer, Moderna). Diagnostics is the customer-acquisition engine; **Data & Applications is the margin engine** (26% of Q3'26 revenue, +33% y/y, gross margin accretive). The flywheel — more tests → more data → more pharma contracts → more R&D → better tests — is genuinely proprietary and hard to replicate, and 2026 delivered proof of operating leverage: **Adjusted EBITDA swung from $1.5M to $25.6M in Q3, GAAP net loss narrowed 81% to -$14.9M, and FCF was nearly breakeven (-$5.5M)**. After a ~35% de-rating from the autumn peak on AI-sentiment compression (not a fundamental miss — Q3 beat and guidance was raised), the stock now prices the flywheel at ~6x forward sales instead of ~10x.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts (3-12 months)

1. **Personalis acquisition close — shareholder vote Nov 30, 2026; close late 2026/early 2027.** $16.25/share, **$1.5B EV** for MRD leadership. Converts TEM from "a player" to the consolidated MRD platform. Near-term binary/headline risk, long-term strategic win.
2. **The 2027 ASP step-up (the biggest single catalyst).** Management expects **~+$200/test average selling price beginning Jan 2027** as xT/xF/xR move through the ADLT pathway; sell-side work implies **~$85M of incremental annual revenue** at high incremental margin. This is the cleanest margin-lever in the model and lands entirely in FY27.
3. **Q4'26 results ~Feb 23, 2027 + first FY2027 guide.** Consensus sits at $1.99B revenue / $144M EBITDA (+24% / 7.2% margin). Given a 7-for-7 beat history and a Q4 revenue guide implying +25-26%, guidance is likely conservative; a 2027 frame above $2.0B would force the Neutral camp (GS/UBS/Redburn) to move.
4. **Data bookings conversion to revenue.** >$550M YTD bookings including BMS $137.5M/5yr; Q4 is seasonally the heaviest data quarter (pharma budget flush). Watch TCV disclosure and any new non-oncology vertical.
5. **Cardiology/neuro commercialization** — ECG-PH/ECG-MR clearances plus the 100,000-whole-genome research platform. Mostly optionality, but it re-rates the "AI platform" narrative away from pure lab-testing multiples.
6. **Dilution overhang watch:** the **$460M 0% convertible due 2032 (conversion ~$69.26)** — refi'd and de-risked the balance sheet (repaid $307.7M secured debt, cut interest expense to $6.7M/qtr), but a re-test of $69+ brings dilution math back into the narrative.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

**Why Buy and not Strong Buy:** the demand-side evidence is close to best-in-class (7/7 beats, +48.8% avg surprise, volume growth 3x the market, gross margin +2.1 pts y/y, FCF from -$128M to -$5.5M, $1.1B data TCV). The valuation has reset to a genuinely reasonable growth price: **EV/sales 6.2x FY26E, 5.0x FY27E, 4.1x FY28E; EV/EBITDA 154x → 69x → 34x → ~12x on FY30E**, versus ~10x FY26E sales at the November 2025 peak. Reverse-engineering the current $10B EV: it roughly discounts **~$3.1-3.5B of FY2030 revenue at 5-6x sales / ~20x EBITDA — i.e., consensus is the hurdle, not a heroic case.** Add the 2027 ASP step-up (~$85M, ~100% incremental margin) and Personalis MRD consolidation, and the risk/reward is asymmetric.

What keeps me off the top rating: organic growth of **23-25%** with Hereditary at +3%, Rule of 40 of only **~13-22 on an organic/FCF basis**, an **$854M cash pile against a $1.5B all-in Personalis deal**, SBC still ~$50M/qtr (~12% of revenue), and a beta of 3.6 that guarantees 30%+ drawdowns. This is a **position-sized, catalyst-dated Buy**, not a back-up-the-truck Buy.

**What would make it a Strong Buy:** FY27 guide >$2.05B with EBITDA margin >8%, or a quarter where Data & Apps grows >35% with FCF positive and Hereditary re-accelerates above 10%.

### 7. Key Risks

- **Deceleration / mix risk:** Total growth has fallen 36% → 23% over three quarters. If Hereditary stays at +3% and pharma data deals slip a quarter (data revenue is back-end loaded and lumpy), FY27 consensus of $1.99B breaks.
- **The 2027 ASP step-up is the load-bearing assumption.** If ADLT/MolDx pricing is delayed or set below the ~+$200/test expectation, ~$85M of high-margin revenue and much of the margin-expansion story disappear at once. PAMA-driven Medicare lab rate cuts are a structural headwind to the base diagnostics business.
- **Competitive intensity:** Foundation Medicine (Roche) and Guardant in therapy selection; **Natera and Exact Sciences in MRD (Natera is the incumbent to beat)**; Caris; plus Flatiron/CO, TriNetX and Aetion in pharma real-world data. Tempus's data moat is real but not unassailable, and pharma customers multi-source deliberately.
- **Execution/integration:** Ambry integration already shows strain (Hereditary +3%); Personalis ($1.5B, closing into a shareholder vote) is a second, larger integration while simultaneously scaling MRD — classic over-extension risk.
- **Balance sheet & dilution:** Negative TTM FCF (~-$150M) plus a $1.5B acquisition; $460M of 0% converts due 2032 with a ~$69.26 conversion price. Equity issuance, or a convert re-test above $69, is a live dilution path. SBC at ~$50M/qtr is real economic cost, not cosmetic.
- **Governance/insider:** founder-CEO-controlled structure and a history of large M&A — capital allocation is a key-man risk.
- **Sentiment/valuation:** just demonstrated it can lose a third of its value in two weeks on macro AI-sentiment alone; short interest and high retail ownership amplify moves in both directions.
