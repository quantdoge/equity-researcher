### Investment Memo: ARM — 12 November 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: Medium-High
**Price Target**: $160 (-21% from current $202.34)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Arm is a genuinely wide-moat franchise — 92.5% gross margin, an irreplaceable ISA ecosystem, 30M+ developers — wrapped in a security whose price has decoupled from its economics. At ~238x GAAP earnings, ~40x sales and *negative* economic free cash flow after $1.27B of stock comp, the price requires management's own five-year plan delivered on time and at full margin merely to earn bond-like returns — a negative margin of safety. Meanwhile the one line that matters, royalties, decelerated to +8% YoY (−6% q/q) just as binary legal risk (an FTC probe; a Delaware remedy seeking up to five years of suspended Qualcomm royalties) targets the exact pricing lever the multiple rests on, inside a macro regime that systematically de-rates long-duration assets. The fall from $452 to $202 is the deflation of a mania to "merely very expensive," not a margin-of-safety event — and the +22% bounce off the Nov-5 low came on declining volume with no attention support, making it a better exit than an entry.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is in a record AI upcycle but bifurcated — AI infrastructure capacity-constrained, consumer/IoT growing low-single-digits. Arm's franchise is best-in-class (GM 32pts above peer median) but growth (22.8%) and op margin (18.5%) sit *below* peer medians (31.6% / 39.9%). Macro is a near-worst-case configuration for ARM specifically: real 10Y +43bp to ≈2.68% with *falling* breakevens (a pure duration de-rating), bear steepening, +3.1% USD, 25% semi tariffs taxing the royalty base. Rate pivot is the bull tail; memory inflation squeezing device BOMs is the bear tail.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH.** 18% of revenue via an Arm China JV Arm does not control; live FTC antitrust probe (May-26) into the licensing model; Qualcomm competition complaints in US/EU/Korea; a Delaware trial seeking up to 5 years of suspended royalties ("potentially billions") — a direct attack on ~53% of revenue; $672M non-cancellable TSMC commitments + a $200M capacity advance import Taiwan sole-source risk into the growth engine.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **SELL.** Base case $45–55; even the company's own fully-delivered FY30 plan ($25B rev, 43% OM) yields only $135–165. Owner-earnings yield 0.28%; economic FCF negative after SBC. Re-entry $110–130. Wonderful franchise, wrong price.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **BUY** — the lone bull. Licensing +50%, datacenter royalties doubled, >60% hyperscaler CPU share exiting CY26, $2B AGI CPU ramp, 7/7 beats. But it concedes the near-term royalty deceleration, flags gross-margin dilution from merchant silicon, and explicitly says "not set-and-forget."
- **Quantitative Signals**: **SELL**, composite 3.0/10. Value 1/10 (last of 5 peers on all four multiples); momentum 3/10 and *deteriorating* (63-day −16.6% vs 12-1 −10.9%); bottom-decile vol (91%, β 3.81, −62% DD). Headline alpha (+68.6%) and Sharpe (1.15) don't survive scrutiny — t≈0.9, geometric Sharpe ≈0.17.
- **Financial Risk**: **MODERATE** — credit risk genuinely LOW (net cash ~$3.1B, zero interest expense, Z-score 4.04, no maturities); market/valuation risk HIGH. SBC = 135% of TTM net income; FCF ex-SBC negative. Operating break-even at an ~18–19% revenue decline; the downside is multiple compression, not solvency.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Governance WEAK — SoftBank ~90% control; CEO Haas simultaneously runs SoftBank Group International (unmanaged conflict); an $800M award vesting only at $1T–$2T market cap; **zero insider purchases vs $72M sold in 12m (~60% not under 10b5-1)** into a 54% drawdown. The Qualcomm jury finding that Arm breached its own license and leaked a termination threat contradicts the "neutral Swiss" brand.
- **ESG**: **NEUTRAL** — E leader (asset-light, negligible carbon), S average, **G laggard (4.5/10)**: 86–90% control, non-independent Chair (Son), SoftBank-linked largest customer, a $646M contract asset due from a SoftBank subsidiary. Governance discount structurally caps the multiple and limits ESG-fund demand.
- **Alternative Data**: **NEUTRAL, bearish quality tilt.** Search interest at multi-year lows (~40% below pre-surge baseline) — not confirming the bounce; rebound on declining volume; beats no longer rewarded (−13% on a clean revenue beat). Royalty deceleration corroborated in hard data.
- **Short / Contrarian**: **SHORT (moderate)** — and crucially, *no fraud* (Beneish −2.48, negative accruals, Altman 4.04). A valuation short with an earnings-quality catalyst: SBC exceeds entire profit, gross margin is eroding on CSS mix, and the FTC is targeting the pricing lever. Warns of catastrophic squeeze risk (3.94 beta, 50% six-week swings).

**Bull Case**
1. **The AI datacenter inflection is real**: datacenter royalties doubled YoY, >60% Arm-based hyperscaler CPU share exiting CY26, AWS Graviton commitments +20x, AGI CPU ramping with a $2B FY27–28 target and POs through CY2027 — the highest-royalty-rate pool in the portfolio.
2. **Licensing +50% is a leading indicator** of FY28 royalty re-acceleration; Armv9/CSS mix shift raises take per chip structurally; a 7/7 beat record (+8.1% avg) reflects conservative guidance.
3. **Rate-pivot convexity**: breakevens at 2.20% say the bond market believes inflation normalizes; an H1-27 Fed pivot could retrace the real 10Y toward ~2.10% — +30–45% of pure multiple expansion on a washed-out high-beta name. SoftBank take-private optionality (~90% owner) caps downside in a melt-up.

**Bear Case**
1. **The royalty engine decelerated** — +8% YoY (from +22%), −6% sequentially — while growth is manufactured in lumpy, pull-forwardable licensing. The most corroborated fact in the packet, and the one the bull thesis must invert.
2. **Negative economic FCF**: SBC of $1.27B (23% of revenue, 135% of net income) makes the "cash machine" a presentational artifact; GAAP op margin compressed 25.3% → ~14% TTM despite hyper-growth; gross margin eroding on CSS/merchant-silicon mix.
3. **No margin of safety plus binary legal risk**: 238x earnings / 40x sales / 0.28% owner-earnings yield demands FY30 perfection at a premium multiple, while the FTC probe and the Delaware remedy target royalty-rate expansion and the royalty line itself — the two things the valuation depends on.

**Key Risks** *(to the SELL call — what forces a re-rating up)*
1. **Royalty re-acceleration >15–20%** with GM ≥91% at the Feb-3-27 print — the Short analyst's own thesis-breaker.
2. **A fast Fed pivot** — a +30–45% multiple-expansion tail on a 3.94-beta name; squeeze risk is severe and naked shorts are dangerous.
3. **AGI CPU ships on time at margin**, validating the $25B FY30 plan early and forcing a re-rating.
4. **SoftBank take-private** at a premium, or AI-flow persistence — valuation is never a catalyst and the stock can go much higher on flows alone.
5. *(Holder downside):* an adverse Delaware remedy or FTC consent order capping royalty rates; Arm China rupture; further SoftBank share pledges; RISC-V acceleration. A −20% revenue scenario collapses EPS toward zero and compresses the multiple simultaneously — a −60% double-whammy with no earnings floor.

**Disagreement & data-quality flags**
- **Genuine disagreement**: Growth (BUY) vs Value (SELL) is a terminal-value philosophy gap, not reconcilable with data. Per the risk framework, the *what-you-pay* case governs sizing — and Growth's own caveats concede the near-term.
- **No fraud**: Short and Quant independently exonerate the accounting. This is a valuation problem, not a legal one — harder to fix, impossible to litigate away.
- **Gaps**: SEC/EDGAR returned 403 across agents (figures rely on 20-F-derived datasets, mirrors and press); no peer forward multiples; alt-data patent/web-traffic/quant-social feeds failed; the "+71.3% earnings revision" is a spurious FY31-vs-FY30 horizon artifact (excluded); the sector-aggregate endpoint returned a single-company sample (unusable).
