Adding MRVL does not diversify an AI-semi book; it **levers it**. If the book already holds NVDA+AVGO, MRVL adds a 3.5-beta amplifier to the same underlying factor (hyperscaler capex).

**Factor exposure — this is a negative-quality, high-beta addition.** Against the peer cross-section, MRVL is **momentum rank 1/6 and revisions 1/6, but quality 6/6 (worst) and low-vol 6/6 (worst)**. A position here increases growth/momentum tilt and *degrades* portfolio quality — the opposite of what 90% vol and a late-cycle macro regime argue for.

**Correlation/diversification illusion:** R² vs SPY is only **0.25**. That sounds like diversification, but it means **75% of the risk is idiosyncratic** — socket wins/losses, SAMR, deal terms. You cannot hedge this with index shorts, and standard portfolio-beta budgeting *understates* the risk. Size for the idiosyncratic tail, not the beta.

**Macro overlay — the worst configuration for this specific asset.** Real 10Y ~2.84% with a term-premium-driven bear-steepener is precisely the regime that compresses long-duration, high-beta equity. And the deal *worsens* MRVL's own duration (absorbing ~$35B of LITE net debt = ~$2.0–2.3B incremental annual interest at 5.13%/3.44% spreads). MRVL is the single most macro-exposed name in the sector.
