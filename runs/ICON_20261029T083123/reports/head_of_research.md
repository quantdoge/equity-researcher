**Investment Thesis**
Icon Energy is a sub-scale, over-levered, 86.4%-insider-controlled dry-bulk owner whose genuinely favourable 2026 freight environment (BDI ~2,652, H1-26 revenue +18%, net income +43%) is structurally captured by the Yorkville $20M equity line and the controller's senior convertible preferred rather than by public shareholders. Eleven of eleven reporting agents are negative; there is no dissent.

**Bear Case**

3. **Existential tail stack**: third Nasdaq deficiency/delisting, OFAC designation of a vessel or manager, charterer default, or a Series A preferred dispute each independently threatens the common.
