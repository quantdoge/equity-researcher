# Alnylam Pharmaceuticals (ALNY) — Value Investment Analysis
**Price: $150.57 | Market cap: $20.2B | Net cash: ~$2.3B | EV: ~$17.9B | Date of analysis: post-Q3 2026**

---

## 1. Business Quality Assessment

**Moat rating: NARROW (platform), but the franchise moat is narrowing, not widening.**

Alnylam is the pioneer and clear leader in RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics, with GalNAc-conjugate delivery IP, 20+ years of clinical know-how, manufacturing scale, and five approved products. FY2026 revenue will be ~$5.2B (+40%), anchored by AMVUTTRA (vutrisiran), which crossed $1B/quarter in Q2 2026 in the ATTR-CM indication approved March 2025.

Evidence for a moat:
- **Intangible assets:** dominant RNAi patent estate and a delivery platform replicable only at great cost. Partnerships with Regeneron, Sanofi, Novartis monetize it.
- **Efficient scale / first-mover** in ATTR-CM: >50% of new patient starts among experienced prescribers; adherence >90%; ~80% of ATTR-CM starts are first-line, where the company is focused.
- **Competitive win:** AstraZeneca/Ionis' Wainua **failed** CARDIO-TTRansform in ATTR-CM, removing a rival; tafamidis generics pushed out to 2031.

Evidence the moat is **not** widening:
- **BridgeBio's Attruby (oral acoramidis)** is scaling ($222M in Q2 2026) — an oral alternative attacking Alnylam's twice-quarterly injectable convenience advantage.
- **Pricing power is being contested:** 340B pressure and gross-to-net adjustments. Q3 2026 included ~$17M of favorable inventory and ~$10M of favorable GTN — i.e., reported growth was partly non-recurring. Guidance was cut twice in 2026 (TTR now $4.2–4.5B, tracking the **low end**).
- **Intellia's nex-z** (one-time gene editing for ATTR-CM) is the structural threat that caps the terminal value of the franchise regardless of current share.
- **No pricing power in the traditional Buffett sense** — this is a reimbursement-priced, physician-discretion product.

This is a high-quality *platform* business inside a competitive, capital-intensive, politically-priced end market. It is not a See's Candies.

Strengths: genuine inflection to profitability (FY2025 was the first GAAP profit year); operating margin has gone from −84% (2021) to +22%; FY2026 FCF likely ~$600M; **net cash of $2.3B** ($16.85/share, ~11% of the market cap); no dividend, and a newly authorized **$1B buyback** (~$500M to be executed by YE2026).

Weaknesses — and they matter:
- **Gross margin has fallen ~9 points YoY** (84.2% → 75.2% in Q3). Third-party royalty obligations plus 340B/GTN pressure. Management has not framed this as transitory. FCF conversion is being permanently taxed.
- **Stock-based comp ~$330M/yr (~6% of revenue)** — real cost, real dilution (~138M diluted vs. 131M basic).
- **Interest expense is anomalously high** ($84M in Q3 alone on ~$1.26B of debt) — this reflects non-cash imputed interest on royalty/financing obligations. Cash economics are better than GAAP suggests, but obligations are real and sit in the $4.39B liability stack.
- **Receivables at $1.08B** (68 days DSO) — watch for channel/GTN quality.
- Accumulated deficit of **−$6.7B** before the 2025 inflection. This business consumed capital for two decades.
