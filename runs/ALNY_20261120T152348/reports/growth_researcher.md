# ALNY — Alnylam Pharmaceuticals: Growth Investment Analysis

**Price $150.75 | Mkt Cap $20.2B | EV ~$18.6B | 52-wk range $152–$480 | Beta 0.27**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Alnylam is the platform owner of RNA interference therapeutics and has just converted 20 years of R&D into a commercial engine: AMVUTTRA's ATTR-CM approval turned a ~$1B rare-disease business into a $5B+ franchise in under two years. The stock has collapsed ~69% from its $480 peak on a July 2026 guidance cut and softer AMVUTTRA momentum — but the *underlying* franchise is still growing 52% in net product revenue, gross margins are ~80%, and the stock now trades at **2.9x 2027E revenue and 18.5x 2027E EPS**, roughly 2.2x consensus 2030 revenue. The market is pricing in a near-term franchise peak; the pipeline (nucresiran, zilebesiran, brezosiran, mivelsiran) says otherwise.

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration

**ATTR-CM is the prize.** ~300–400K diagnosed patients globally with wild-type and hereditary ATTR cardiomyopathy; only ~25–30% are on any disease-modifying therapy today. AMVUTTRA is the only RNAi approved for ATTR-CM and the only therapy with a demonstrated reduction in cardiovascular death, hospitalizations and urgent heart-failure visits. Q3 2026 TTR revenue was **$1.15B** (AMVUTTRA $1.13B, ONPATTRO $20M), up 59% YoY — annualizing ~$4.6B against a TAM that peaks well above $10B.

**Penetration today: still low single digits** of the diagnosed ATTR-CM population. The 2026 guidance cut (TTR revenue reset to $4.2–4.5B from ~$4.4–4.7B) reflects *slower conversion*, not a smaller market — a distinction the market is not making.

**Adjacent TAMs the platform opens:**
- **Nucresiran** (Phase 3, ATTR) — next-gen, faster knockdown, potential best-in-class; also a pipeline-within-a-franchise.
- **Zilebesiran** (hypertension, Roche-partnered) — a **$30B+** global antihypertensive market; a twice-yearly injectable is a genuine paradigm shift.
- **Brezosiran** (Huntington's) and **mivelsiran** (Alzheimer's/CAA) — CNS RNAi, option value not in consensus.
- **Rare disease** (GIVLAARI, OXLUMO): $500–600M 2026E, +10%.

**Differentiation:** Alnylam owns the GalNAc conjugate chemistry and the manufacturing know-how. Ionis and Arrowhead are the only credible RNAi peers; the Wainua (AstraZeneca/Ionis) CARDIO-TTRansform failure in 2026 removed the most direct competitive threat and validates Alnylam's clinical bar.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q4/FY2026 results — Feb 11, 2027.** Consensus $1.52B revenue / $1.72 EPS. The critical test: does AMVUTTRA re-accelerate sequentially off Q3's $1.13B, or does the guided $4.2–4.5B TTR range imply a Q4 *decline*? This is the single most important number in the thesis.
- **2027 guidance (same call).** Market needs to see TTR growth re-accelerate into the high-20s% rather than the ~15% implied by the cut.
- **Nucresiran Phase 3 data (2027).** Positive readout would defend the franchise against BridgeBio's Attruby and extend exclusivity well into the 2030s.
- **$1B buyback execution.** Board authorized Nov 2, 2026; ~$500M to be repurchased by year-end 2026 with the balance in 2027. At $150/share this retires ~5% of shares — a real signal that management views the stock as mispriced.
- **Zilebesiran Phase 3 (hypertension) milestones** — the largest un-priced TAM expansion; Roche partnership derisks the cost.
- **Five-year HELIOS-B data (Nov 9, 2026)** showing sustained benefit — supports label expansion and duration-of-therapy arguments.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (high conviction, high volatility)

**The valuation disconnect is extreme.** At $18.6B EV against $9.2B of 2030 consensus revenue, the market is capitalizing ALNY at **2.0x 2030 sales** — for a business with 80% gross margins, positive FCF, and a monopoly position in RNAi. Applying a 4x terminal EV/Sales multiple implies the market expects revenue of only ~$4.7B — i.e., **zero growth beyond 2026**. Even a 2027E-based framework (2.9x sales, 18.5x EPS) sits at a 40–60% discount to large-cap biopharma peers growing a third as fast.

**Why not Strong Buy:** the hypergrowth phase is genuinely over (+65% → +46% → +18% → +15%), guidance credibility took two hits in 2026, and gross margin is compressing fast (Q3 2026 GM 75.2% vs 84.2% a year ago — royalty and profit-share stack on AMVUTTRA plus gross-to-net pressure). The Q3 "beat" was a tax artifact, not operating leverage. I need to see one clean quarter of AMVUTTRA re-acceleration before upgrading.

**Why Buy now:** the bear case is fully priced. The buyback is a hard floor signal, the competitive landscape improved materially in 2026, and the pipeline optionality (zilebesiran, nucresiran, CNS) is being valued at roughly zero.

---

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Execution / guidance credibility.** Two resets in 2026 (Q4'25 miss, July guidance cut). If Q4 2026 AMVUTTRA revenue *declines* sequentially, the "peak franchise" narrative becomes consensus and the multiple compresses further.
2. **Gross margin erosion.** Q3 2026 cost of revenue rose 83% YoY against 17% revenue growth. The Sanofi royalty stack, 340B pricing pressure, and geographic mix could push GM below 75% structurally — this would break the "platform leverage" thesis.
3. **Competition.** BridgeBio's Attruby (acoramidis) is taking share in ATTR-CM on an oral/pricing advantage; Pfizer's tafamidis remains the incumbent default (generic entry now 2031, which protects *tafamidis*, not Alnylam). Slower-than-expected conversion of the diagnosed pool is the real risk.
4. **Pricing / access.** 340B reform and IRA Medicare negotiation could reach AMVUTTRA in the late 2020s; the guidance cut was partly attributed to access dynamics.
5. **Concentration.** ~79% of revenue is TTR. A single-franchise company deserves a discount — but not a 2x-sales discount.
6. **Pipeline binary risk.** Nucresiran Phase 3 failure in 2027 would be a severe blow, though partly offset by the franchise's existing label.

---

### Bottom line
This is the rare case where a Cathie Wood-style platform thesis and a deep-value entry price coincide. The innovation (RNAi, GalNAc, CNS delivery) is real and defensible; the TAM (ATTR-CM + hypertension + CNS) is enormous; the growth rate has decelerated but remains ~46% this year on a Rule-of-40 score of 78. The market is pricing a franchise peak that the alternative data — rising brand interest, an expanding TTR field force, and a $1B buyback — does not support. **Buy, sized for volatility; add on any Q4 print that shows AMVUTTRA re-accelerating.**
