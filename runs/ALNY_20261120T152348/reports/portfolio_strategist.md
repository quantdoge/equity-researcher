## 2. Consensus Assessment: **HIGH agreement on facts / SPLIT on action**

**High consensus (11/11 agents, near-zero dissent) on the FACT PATTERN:**
1. The **science and platform are not the problem** — 7 approvals, clean accounting, $0 goodwill, best-in-class balance sheet, no fraud (Short Analyst looked and explicitly found none).
2. **Commercial conversion is deteriorating** and guidance credibility is impaired — two cuts, two −25%+ single-day gaps, CCO gone.
3. **Concentration is extreme** — 79–87% of revenue in one molecule, in the one indication attracting two well-capitalised rivals.
4. **The stock is cheap on normalized numbers** (median PT +82%; Short Analyst's bull case +113%) *because* of #2 and #3 — not by mistake.

**The genuine split — and how I resolve it:** Growth says BUY now; Alt Data says the Feb 11 print has a 4–7% revenue miss embedded. **These are not contradictory.** One is a 3-year statement, the other an 11-week timing call. The synthesis: the long-run risk/reward is favourable, but the **single largest near-term catalyst is skewed negative and is deliberately unresolvable in advance**. That argues for a real but small position now, with size withheld until Feb 11.

**Where I overrule an agent:** Growth argues the market is "pricing a franchise peak that the data does not support." Alt Data — the only agent with *leading* indicators — shows brand search interest decaying *faster* than reported demand. Leading beats coincident. I therefore discount Growth's urgency (not its valuation work) and treat Feb 11 as a near-term negative skew. Conversely, I overrule any bearish read from the **Altman Z-score (−0.02, "Distress")** and the **~9x trailing P/E**: both are artifacts (accumulated deficit; $1.87B one-time DTA release). The Short Analyst's rejection of both is correct and I adopt it.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.5% of portfolio now; hard cap 3.0%**

**Rationale — three independent sizing methods converge on "small":**

- **Volatility risk budget.** At **49.8% annualised vol**, a 1.5% position contributes ~75bp of portfolio vol; 3.0% contributes ~150bp. Against a 10–12% portfolio vol target, **3% already consumes 13–15% of the entire risk budget in one name.** That is the binding cap.
- **Diversification reality (the decisive number).** **R² vs XBI = 0.077** — **92% of ALNY's variance is idiosyncratic.** You get almost *no* biotech factor beta, only standalone binary event risk. Correlation to SPY is 0.14. This is not a "sector exposure" buy; it must be underwritten as a **single-asset venture bet**, which caps size regardless of how attractive the mean is.
- **Kelly logic.** Scenario set ($84 / $150 / $321) is roughly symmetric with a modest upward skew and enormous dispersion. A half-Kelly allocation on a wide-dispersion, 50%-vol, negative-momentum, credibility-impaired name with an adverse 11-week catalyst is **1–2%**, not 4%.

Plus the decisive risk-management constraint the other agents surfaced: **an unknown here is binary and 11 weeks away** (Q4 print + 2027 guidance + HHT/obesity readouts all on Feb 11). Sizing as if that event has already resolved would be a process error.

**Build:** 1.0–2.0% band; **1.5% is the recommendation** for a diversified multi-strategy book. **Pass entirely** if (a) the portfolio is already >25% healthcare / >10% small-mid biotech, or (b) the mandate prohibits pre-revenue-catalyst single-asset concentration. **Downsize to 0.75%** if the fund already owns BBIO or Ionis (competitive overlap).
