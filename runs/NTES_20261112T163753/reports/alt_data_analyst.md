**Read:** Two distinct signals. (a) *Attention decay* across the corporate brand and the flagship Western title — Marvel Rivals' post-launch anniversary curve has fully rolled over, implying softer international/console contribution and a weakening marketing tailwind. (b) *Sea of Remnants* shows classic promo-driven spikes without retention — bearish for the "new IP execution" thesis the sell-side is focused on.

**Critical caveat:** US-geo Trends is a **poor proxy for core monetisation** — the vast majority of NetEase revenue is China-domestic (FWJ, Naraka, Eggy Party CN). This series mostly tracks ADR-investor attention plus Western title engagement, both of which thin out absent a catalyst. Treat as direction, not magnitude.

### 3. Earnings Preview Signal — **Tracking BELOW consensus**
- **Nomura:** Q3 revenue RMB 29.634bn, **+4% YoY, −2% QoQ, ~3% below consensus** — headline "A Quiet Quarter: High Base and Weak New Game Performance."
- Consensus EPS **$2.44** is the **highest bar of the year** and requires a **+38% QoQ step-up** off Q2's depressed $1.77 — a demanding sequential hurdle just as alt data softens.
- Surprise history: **57% beat rate, avg +1.5%**, but the last four quarters contain **two −23% misses** (−23.7% Aug-26, −22.7% Feb-26). Fat left tail, no directional edge from history; the Q2 miss was driven by investment losses, not operations (revenue actually beat by 2.3%).
- **Alt-data vs prior quarter:** consumer attention down 35% QoQ, new-IP awareness decaying, revenue guided-model below street. Alt data corroborates the cautious Nomura view rather than contradicting it.

### 4. News Sentiment — Score **+1.0** (6 articles/14d, 6 pos / 0 neg) — **but low information content**
Composition is the story: four of six items are **mechanical dividend/quant screener listicles** (SCHD-inspired 4-factor, "Top 10 High Growth Dividend," "Top 25 Attractive Dividend"). These are formulaic inclusions, not fundamental enthusiasm. Only one genuine analyst item: *"NetEase Q3 2026 Preview: Mixed Feelings Heading Into The Print — maintain Hold, neutral rating"* (Nov 2). Real editorial tone is **neutral-to-cautious**; the +1.0 is an artefact.

### 5. Social Sentiment — **Low volume, mildly bullish, stale data**
Reddit proxy surfaced a mix of 8–9 year-old archived threads and one recent bullish post ("NetEase keeps ripping while Alibaba and Baidu bleed"; Q1-26 EPS beat, gaming rev +6.9%, gross profit +14.8%). No measurable retail crowding, no euphoria, no capitulation. Effectively **no signal** — retail attention to this ADR is thin.

**Price/positioning context:** peaked $133.67 (Jul 20), fell 16% to $112.10 (Oct 29), then rallied +15.6% in seven sessions to $129.65 (Nov 9) before fading −5% over two days on elevated volume (1.54M shares Nov 11). A pre-earnings run-up that is already unwinding — consistent with expectations getting ahead of a soft quarter.

### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH** (tactical, into the Nov 19 print)
Leading indicators uniformly point to a below-consensus quarter: decaying consumer attention across brand and titles, promotional rather than organic traction on the new IP, Nomura's model 3% under street, and an elevated sequential EPS hurdle. This is a *tactical* call — the structural picture (net cash, buybacks/dividend, beta 0.77, stock already −8% off July highs) is not impaired.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
Supporting: consistent directional agreement across three independent consumer series, plus a named sell-side model below consensus.
Limiting: (i) US Google Trends is a weak proxy for a ~China-only revenue base — the single biggest weakness in this analysis; (ii) **patent data unavailable** (API failure, 2 attempts); (iii) **web-traffic data unusable**; (iv) hiring data is aggregator noise; (v) headline sentiment is screener-contaminated; (vi) no China-domestic alt data (Baidu/Douyin indices, CN app-store ranks) available in this toolset.

**Lag note:** Google Trends is 1–2 weeks lagged and ends Nov 1; the last two weeks of the quarter's demand signal are unobserved. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a verdict — the risk to this call is that Q3 upside comes from margin/opex discipline (Q2 gross margin 76.1%) or investment gains reversing the Q2 loss, neither of which alt data captures.
