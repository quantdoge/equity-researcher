**Investment Thesis**

All eleven substantive agents independently converge on the same fact base: a 21% ROIC, zero-net-debt, 154% FCF-conversion business trading at ~10–12x ex-cash earnings with an 8.5–8.9% FCF yield and ~29% of its market cap in net cash. The market is pricing roughly 3–4% perpetual FCF growth from a company that has compounded EPS at ~20% over five years — that is the mispricing. The catch is equally clear: this is a 7%-growth quality compounder, not a share-gainer, and the discount exists for a political tail (discretionary NPPA licensing, dormant HFCAA, founder concentration) that cannot be underwritten away, only sized for and partly truncated via the HKEX line. Returns must come from margin mix, capital return, and the free optionality on Ananta (15 Jan 2027) — not from re-rating of growth.
