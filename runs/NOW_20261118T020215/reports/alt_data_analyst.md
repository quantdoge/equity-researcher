## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BEARISH** (near-term, 1–2 quarters)
The tell: **alt data does not corroborate a -38% fundamental collapse.** It corroborates a ~2pt forward-bookings deceleration plus a waning-attention trend that was visible in search data months earlier. Layoffs are margin-accretive. AI consumption metrics are genuinely inflecting. What alt data *cannot* price is the market's new willingness to pay a much lower multiple for seat/workflow-model software in an agentic AI world — and against that, a decelerating cRPO is a bearish confirmation, not a neutral one.

**Trade framing:** estimates still cuttable into the Jan 27 print; do not front-run the "cheap" narrative until cRPO stabilizes or an AI ACV reacceleration shows up in the *next* guide.

## 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**
- **Strengths:** dense, recent, multi-source; hard guidance/ACV numbers cross-checked across company PR, SEC filing, and trade press; clear surprise-trend decay.
- **Gaps/limitations:** patent data unavailable (2 failed pulls); web traffic and job postings are web-search proxies, not Thinknum/SimilarWeb series; Reddit direct fetch 403'd (search snippets only); no app-store metric exists for this business model; Google Trends is a poor proxy for enterprise software demand and may partly reflect the ~30k employee base's own search behaviour.
- **Lag:** alt-data feeds lag ~2–4 weeks; the demand damage (if any) from an AI-agent substitution cycle would not appear in these series before the Q1'27 print at the earliest.
- **Alt data is a leading indicator, not a verdict.**
