**Bull Case**
- Valuation reset vs. intact franchise: 5.8x FY26E EV/S, ~16.5x reported forward FCF, net-cash-generative, 20% growth, RPO +22% — the de-rating (~60%) overshoots the fundamental deceleration (~200–300bp).
- AI is monetizing, not cannibalizing (so far): AI ACV +70% to a $1.7B target, consumption +13x, large-deal metrics accelerating (19 deals >$5M, +135% y/y). If the April 2026 consumption-pricing transition holds revenue-per-customer, NOW is the governance toll-road on enterprise agents, not a displaced seat vendor.
- Disinflation-shock optionality: if CPI breaks toward ~2.5% by mid-2027, the Fed's September hike reverses, the 10Y retraces toward ~4.2%, and a 20% grower at a trough multiple re-rates 40–60% with no change in fundamentals. Credit (90bp IG) says the catastrophe isn't happening.

**Bear Case**
- cRPO 21.5%→20.5%→**18.5% guided** is the thesis: forward bookings decelerating ~300bp in three quarters, with FY27 consensus (~+18.6%) likely still optimistic. A print <15% cc converts a sentiment story into a structural one.
- Margin reset may be permanent: GM −4.4pp to 70.5%, first EPS miss in seven quarters, inference costs migrating into third-party cloud opex — a consumption model has real marginal cost that the seat model never did.
- SBC treadmill + deteriorating capital allocation: SBC 15% of revenue, share count up despite $1.84B of FY25 buybacks, $6B of new debt funding goodwill now at 104% of equity. On owner earnings the "cheap" multiple is ~27x, not 16x.

**Key Risks**
1. cRPO prints <15% cc or FY27 subscription guide <15% → thesis broken, exit (27 Jan 2027 is the binary).
2. Gross margin <76% in any quarter → structural reset confirmed.
3. Formal DOJ/SEC action on Q3 growth commentary, or a metric-definition change in cRPO → disclosure-credibility event.
4. Goodwill impairment against the $13.6B intangible book; net debt/EBITDA >2.5x.
5. Churn in regulated verticals traceable to the June 2026 security incident.
6. Macro mirror-image: 10Y term premium running to ~6% takes NOW to $65–70 on duration math alone.
