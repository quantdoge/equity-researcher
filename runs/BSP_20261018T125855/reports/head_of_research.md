### Investment Memo: BSP — 18 October 2026

**Verdict**: SELL | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $40 (−15% downside from current $47.07)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

Bending Spoons is a genuinely excellent operator — an AI-enabled roll-up that converts distressed consumer software into cash at a 41.9% EBITDA margin — but the stock is not the business. Organic growth has decelerated 13% → 6% → 3% with a 97% like-for-like NRR, meaning the compounding engine is the capital markets, not the customer; that engine is financed with ~$5.7B of floating-rate BB− debt at ~1.9x EBIT interest coverage against −$5.0B of tangible equity. At ~40x TTM EBITDA and ~15x sales on a 7.6%-and-falling ROIC, the equity requires flawless execution of ever-larger deals into a rising real-rate regime — and the market's own clearing print says so: Berkshire, post-diligence, paid $33, 30% below spot. Notably, even our one bullish agent's own valuation tops out at $37.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Late-cycle reflation with a real-rate shock — 10Y TIPS +87bp since May while breakevens *fell*, the most hostile combination for long-duration equity; BSP is the most rate-sensitive name in its cohort on both the asset (terminal-value DCF) and liability (floating debt; +100bp ≈ $57M ≈ 4–5% of EBITDA) sides. HY OAS at 323bp is the model's enabling condition and its kill switch (>500bp breaks it). Sector neutral: roll-up economics favour scaled consolidators, but median peer organic growth is ~2% and BSP carries the group's lowest gross margin (65.6%) and highest leverage despite the best execution (94.7% growth vs 1.9% median).
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM. Revenue is shifting to the US (49% → 62%), moving risk toward US litigation: ~1,810 Eventbrite CIPA demand letters and 500 AAA arbitration demands with no accrual; an unquantified Sony/Warner/Universal v. Vimeo copyright case in Milan; ~$26M of Italian VAT-credit guarantees; serial merger-review exposure (Miro clearance pending Q4). No fraud or sanctions flags.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL/AVOID. No moat (narrow, narrowing): ROIC 11.6% → 8.5% → 7.6%, below WACC. Intrinsic value ~$20–25 central; even the bull case ($37) is below spot. EV/EBITDA 73.6x FY25, FCF yield ~1%, tangible book −$2.5B. The one genuine asset is an integration capability that must be re-purchased every deal — a treadmill, not a moat.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (medium) — the lone dissent. 19.3x 2027E P/E, PEG 0.3, Rule of 40 >130, expanding gross margins, positive revisions. But growth is explicitly "bought, not earned": consensus FY27 net income implies a 33.6% net margin the company has never printed, and it requires closing and cleanly integrating 1–3 more $1B+ assets at rising rates.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL, low conviction. Value bottom decile, Quality bottom third, Low-Vol bottom 2%; only Earnings Revision is strong (top decile, +79%). All risk statistics are built on ~74 trading days — 121% annualised vol, 40% drawdown, regression beta of 0.178 at R²≈0 is uninformative.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. TTM net debt/EBITDA 4.87x (gross 5.8x), EBIT/interest 1.88x falling toward ~1.6x forward, current ratio 0.64x, cash/short-term debt 0.98x. A −20% revenue scenario breaks coverage to 0.63x and pushes leverage to 7.6–8.9x — covenant-breach territory that freezes revolver access precisely when the −$761M working-capital deficit needs it. H1'26 interest consumed 97.5% of H1 operating cash flow.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Founders hold 82.71% of votes (5:1 dual class) with Chair/CEO combined; ~20 acquisitions in 36 months with two integrations in flight and Miro awaiting antitrust; lock-up expiry ~late Dec 2026/Jan 2027 into a thin float; the Berkshire placement at a ~13% discount raises related-party-terms questions worth reading in the 6-K.
- **ESG**: LAGGARD (governance 3/10). Social risk is endogenous — the 75%+ post-acquisition layoffs *are* the margin engine; Remini biometric and WeTransfer privacy exposure; no 20-F or ESG report until 2027; CSRD compliance an unpriced FY27 cost. Not thesis-breaking for generalists; likely exclusion by ESG-mandated EU funds on governance grounds.
- **Alternative Data**: BEARISH (tactical). All five tracked products' search trends fell 15–27% QoQ into the Q3 print; Evernote web rank deteriorating; the Berkshire attention spike is retail sentiment, not product demand. Pattern warning: Q2 beat EPS by 89% and the stock fell 16.5% — beats don't pay here; only organic quality will.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (moderate) — quality-of-earnings and multiple compression, *not* fraud (negative accruals, R&D fully expensed, no smoking gun). Margins are borrowed: R&D 8.3% + S&M 10.3% of revenue vs ~70% at peers; adjusted-vs-GAAP gap $141M/quarter; EPS swung by a volatile tax line. "The multiple is the arbitrage" — BSP buys revenue at 2.3–2.7x ARR and the market marks it at 8–10x the same day.

**Bull Case**
1. **Elite execution is real**: revenue $387M → $1.31B in two years, gross margin *expanding* 61% → 66%, 41.9% TTM EBITDA margin, ~$597k revenue/employee, deals underwritten to 25% unlevered IRRs.
2. **2027 math is cheap if delivered**: 13.4x 2027E EV/EBITDA, 19.3x P/E, PEG 0.3, top-decile revisions — two clean Airtable/Miro integrations and the multiple argument holds.
3. **Berkshire's $930M (~4%)** validates the model and funds the pipeline, with credit markets wide open (HY 323bp, S&P upgrade to BB−).

**Bear Case**
1. **Organic decay**: 3% LFL growth falling, 97% NRR, Remini/Splice declining, portfolio-wide demand proxies down 15–27% QoQ — every quarter's multiple depends on finding another $1B+ deal.
2. **Leverage into a hiking cycle**: 1.9x coverage on floating BB− debt, 78% of assets goodwill/intangibles — one impairment wipes the entire $1.26B book equity.
3. **Valuation asymmetry**: ~40x TTM EBITDA vs IV estimates of $20–37; de-rating to a still-generous 20x EBITDA is −57% with no change in operations; the smartest buyer paid $33.

**Key Risks**
Q3 print (12 Nov) showing organic <2% · lock-up expiry ~Jan 2027 · Miro blocked/terminated · HY OAS >500bp · CIPA claims broadening into cross-portfolio class action or adverse Vimeo/Milan ruling · Italian tax assessment · AI-native substitution of the acquired asset base · extreme gap risk (four >15% daily moves in 76 sessions — stops will not protect).
