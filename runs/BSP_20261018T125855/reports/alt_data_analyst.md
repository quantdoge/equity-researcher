### 3. Earnings Preview Signal: **BELOW consensus on organic quality** (headline revenue likely In-Line)

Consensus carries a 100% beat rate / +89% avg surprise, so the bar is set for a beat that has already proven not to matter. Guidance and organic quality are the swing factors.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM-LOW**
Strong internal consistency across five product trends and two independent traffic proxies. But: Google Trends is a share-of-search index, not absolute volume; search interest is a *poor* revenue proxy for a business model that grows by raising prices on captive users (price hikes can lift revenue while usage decays — precisely the divergence flagged here). Unresolved gaps: patents, social sentiment, app-store download/ranking data, and absolute web visits all unavailable; SimilarWeb read is rank-only. Earnings date discrepancy (Nov 6 vs Nov 12) should be confirmed. **Adjacent risk:** Nov 6-ish also sits near the IPO lockup horizon — supply overhang not quantifiable from these feeds.

**Bottom line for the memo:** alt data says the Berkshire headline is masking a shrinking underlying user base. Fade the pop; require organic net-revenue-retention disclosure before paying 10x forward sales.
