## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — with a pronounced bearish plurality**

Shape of the disagreement is unusually clean, and that matters more than the vote count:

- **Nobody disputes the operating execution.** Sector, Growth, Short and Value all independently confirm genuine capability: revenue $387M → $1,306M, gross margin *expanding* 61% → 66%, 41.9% TTM EBITDA margin, ~$597k revenue/employee. This is a real business run by capable people.
- **Nobody disputes the organic collapse.** 3% like-for-like, 97% NRR, Remini and Splice declining, and independent Trend data showing portfolio-wide usage decay of 15–27% QoQ. The bull (Growth) concedes it explicitly and sizes down for it.
- **So the entire debate is price versus durability.** The bull case is a 2027E multiple argument (13.4x EBITDA) that requires consensus 2027 EBITDA of $2.53B — which requires closing and cleanly integrating 1–3 more $1B+ assets, at rising rates, while two integrations are already in flight. The Short analyst's observation is the correct frame: consensus FY27 net income of ~$1.46B implies a 33.6% net margin this company has **never printed once**.
- **No fraud. Quality-of-earnings, leverage and multiple risk.** This is the crucial distinction: Value's bearishness is a *price* judgement, not an integrity call. Beneish M-Score is "moderate," accruals are negative, R&D is fully expensed, cash conversion is fine. I do **not** apply the fraud-precedence override here — but I do apply the leverage-precedence override: **1.9x EBIT interest coverage, 0.64x current ratio and −$5.04B tangible equity outrank any bullish valuation view**, because they determine whether the company is a price-taker in the next refinancing.

**Verdict: conviction is low and evidence is high on the negative side.** Three agents independently converged on the same arithmetic (Fin Risk, Short, Value) from different starting points — the strongest form of negative evidence. The one bullish agent (Growth) is bullish on *multiples*, not on the business.

**I resolved one data dispute myself:** the Quant/Alt-Data "feed mismatch" ($47.07 vs $55.77) is not a data error — it is simply the 15-Oct vs 16-Oct close. Confirmed from raw history. Also confirmed: **60-day momentum is −5.98% (negative)**, and measured correlation to SPY/IGV/ARKK/BRK-B is 0.01 / 0.09 / 0.03 / 0.14 over the 75-day window — i.e. statistically noise, not diversification.

## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector / concentration.** Software-application. If the book is already overweight software or long-duration growth, BSP **adds to narrative concentration even though measured beta is low** — its sector exposure is to the *same discount-rate factor* that dominates every high-multiple holding, not to a shared revenue cycle.

**Factor exposure — this is the real overlap.** BSP is a measured **short** on four of the five classic factors: Value bottom decile, Quality bottom third, Low-Vol bottom 2%, and it is a **long** on Earnings-Revision (top decile, +79%). Adding BSP to a book that is already growth/momentum/revision-tilted compounds a crowded tilt at the exact moment the revision signal is at risk (Q3 organic is the swing factor, and a miss inverts it instantly). It would only diversify a deep-value, low-volatility book — and even there the leverage profile contaminates it.

**Correlation.** Measured 0.01–0.14 versus SPY, IGV, ARKK and BRK-B, but with R² ≈ 0 over ~74 days, this is uninformative. The economically correct read: **BSP does not diversify a market drawdown; it is a sequential series of idiosyncratic binary events wearing a stock wrapper** (Q3 print 6–12 Nov; Miro antitrust clearance Q4; lock-up ~late-Dec/Jan; first 20-F in 2027). Expect it to correlate with itself over time, not with your book.

**Duration/factor overlap with macro.** This is the single clearest portfolio-level objection: long-duration, terminal-value-dependent equity, funded with ~$5.7B floating-rate BB- debt, entering a period where the 10Y real yield has risen 87bp with *falling* breakevens. BSP is simultaneously the most rate-sensitive name in its cohort and the most rate-exposed liability structure. A 20x → 10x multiple normalization is −50%+ with **no change in operating performance** (Fin Risk). That is a factor bet you are probably already making elsewhere, in larger size.

**Liquidity.** ~1.5–3M shares/day typical, spiking to 19.7M on 14-Oct (~$70–140M typical notional). Adequate for a 1% position; **inadequate as an exit route** given a thin float and an unlock that puts founder-controlled supply (82.71% of votes, majority of shares) on the market.
