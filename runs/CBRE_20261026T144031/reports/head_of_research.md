## Investment Thesis

CBRE is the strongest franchise in commercial real estate services with the **weakest margin trajectory in its peer group**. Three independent workstreams — Sector, Short, and Value — converge on the same arithmetic: gross margin has collapsed from ~22% (2021) to 15.0% (FY2025), operating profit has fallen for three consecutive years while revenue compounded +32%, and ROIC (~6.6%) now sits at or below WACC. The roll-up is adding scale, not value. Against that, the bull case is narrative: a 13.7x FY27E core multiple, a 15% EPS growth path, and AI-disintermediation fears reversing. With probability-weighted fair value (~$126) essentially at the market price, a hostile rate regime (10Y 4.99%, Fed re-tightening), and an unresolved 49-metro antitrust class action carrying a treble-damages tail, there is **no margin of safety**. This is a franchise to own at $100, not at $124.

## Key Findings by Domain

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector NEUTRAL — the recovery is real (US transaction volumes +20–30% YoY, availability down 130bp to 23.1%, eight straight quarters of positive absorption, ~$326B TAM growing ~7% CAGR) but hostage to the 10-year. Macro UNFAVOURABLE: the Fed **hiked** 25bp to 3.75–4.00% in Sep-26 (first increase since 2023), CPI re-accelerated to 3.72%, the 10Y sits at 4.99% after peaking at 5.31%, and the curve is bear-steepening — the worst configuration for cap-rate-driven transaction revenue. Management itself flagged rates "beginning to weigh on transaction activity"; the stock fell 6% on an EPS *beat*. The micro is outperforming; the macro is de-rating it.

- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM. Geography is genuinely diversified (56% US / 14% UK / 30% RoW) and the cumulative penalty record is remarkably light ($7.4M over 26 years). But risk is *concentrated*, not diffuse: the June 2026 *FitFactariDC v. CoStar et al.* class action (N.D. Ill.) names CBRE plus four peers in a hub-and-spoke lease-data price-fixing conspiracy across 49 metros — it attacks the core brokerage information model. **Class certification is the single event that moves legal risk to High.** Secondary: GSA's 50% federal portfolio cut; the Gulf data-centre pipeline is two-sided Middle East exposure (project risk vs. sovereign capital deployment).

- **Value**: HOLD. Narrow moat (scale and ~95% GWS renewal rates create switching costs; brokerage has none — talent walks). Base intrinsic value $115–125; at $123.67 the market already prices ~10% owner-earnings growth plus 4% terminal off a cash-flow base that has *declined* since 2021. P/FCF 31x and EV/EBITDA 17.8x are the richest in the services peer set while earning a *lower* ROIC than JLL. Accumulate below $100; aggressively below $88. No dividend; $4.1B of buybacks 2021–25 (share count −11.4%) is a genuine positive.

- **Growth**: BUY — the lone constructive fundamental voice. The real engine is infrastructure/data centres (+80% local-currency in Q3'26; infrastructure services +50% TTM to $4.5B, targeting ~$7B by 2027); 57% of revenue is recurring. Base case $170 on 19x FY27E ~$9.00 core EPS. **I discount this view**: the $170 target requires a multiple *re-rating* from 13.7x to 19x — i.e., AI-narrative repair, which no agent can falsify or schedule — and the analyst concedes Rule-of-40 = 16, four years of margin compression, and increasingly inorganic growth.

## Bull Case

1. **Infrastructure/data centres are a genuine structural grower** (+80% LCY; $4.5B→~$7B targeted 2027) and, with 57% recurring revenue, are converting CBRE into a less cyclical compounder — 13.7x FY27E core EPS is cheap if the 15% 2027 guide lands.
2. **Transaction-market torque**: volumes +20–30% drop through at 40%+ incremental margins when the 10Y stabilises; mortgage origination (+1% YoY) is the coiled spring; buybacks at a 52-week low are mechanically accretive.
3. **Sentiment washout**: sell-side targets of $169–192 and rising long-dated estimates against a stock down ~29% from its February high leave a large gap; if AI-disintermediation fears (−25% in two sessions in Feb-26) prove misplaced, this is a valuation-and-narrative repair trade.

## Bear Case

1. **Margin arithmetic**: gross profit fell in dollars in FY25 (−13.5%) on +13.4% revenue; CBRE is the *only* major peer whose operating margin fell; −171bp over three years; ROIC ≈ WACC. Growth is being bought with low-margin pass-through revenue and debt-funded M&A.
2. **Hostile, worsening macro**: a re-tightening Fed, 3.72% CPI, and a ~5% 10Y with a bear-steepening tail (10Y >5.5%, cap rates +75bp) implies **$95–105**. The highest-margin revenue line (capital markets) is the most rate-elastic.
3. **Balance-sheet and legal tails**: 3.18x leverage, goodwill exceeding book equity, 95-day DSO, and a 49-metro antitrust class action with treble damages and a pattern of prior price-fixing settlements.

## Key Risks

1. **Class certification** in *FitFactariDC v. CoStar et al.* — thesis invalidation; exit trigger.
2. **10Y sustained above 5.50%** — transaction book rolls over; bear case activates.
3. **FY26 gross margin <14.0% / operating margin <3.0%** — confirms mix dilution is structural.
4. **Goodwill impairment or net debt/EBITDA >3.75x** — no equity floor beneath the stock.
5. **Advisory organic revenue down >10% YoY** — AI disintermediation visible in the numbers.
6. **FY27 core EPS guide below +10%** (11 Feb 2027) — collapses the forward-multiple thesis.
7. **Earnings-quality credibility**: widening GAAP-vs-"core" gap; CoStar litigation reserves undisclosed.
