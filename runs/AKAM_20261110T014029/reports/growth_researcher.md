# AKAM — Growth Investment Analysis
**As of Nov 9, 2026 · Price $80.29 · Market cap ~$11.6B · EV ~$16–17B (est.)**

---

## 1. Growth Story

Akamai is executing one of the most violent business-model pivots in large-cap tech: from a ~5%-growth CDN/content-delivery stalwart into a contract-backed **AI inference and cloud infrastructure** provider. The anchor is an **$11.6B, seven-year Anthropic CPU-workload commitment**, which took multi-year CIS bookings from ~$2.8B (Q2'26) to **>$14B** in one quarter and drove management's first explicit long-range targets: **CIS revenue of $1.1–1.3B in 2027 (more than doubling on a $450M-ish 2026 base), total 2027 revenue growth of +17–22% cc, and >$7B of revenue in 2028** — from signed deals only, excluding a further **$9B Anthropic option**.

The catch defines the investment: this growth is being bought with capital, not earned with margin. 2026 capex is guided to **$3.46–3.66B (≈80% of revenue)**, funded with convertible debt that has pushed the company to a **BB+ (junk) S&P rating**. Revenue growth is inflecting; free cash flow, GAAP earnings, and ROIC are all moving the wrong way simultaneously. That is why the stock is **–50% from its May 2026 high of $161** and –29% from the $112 200-day average despite the largest contract win in company history.

## 3. TAM and Penetration

- **TAM:** Global cloud infrastructure is >$400B growing ~20%; AI inference specifically is the fastest-growing slice and plausibly $150–250B by 2030. Edge security (Akamai's other leg) ~$50B+.
- **Penetration:** Akamai's CIS is running at **~$436M annualized** — a rounding error versus TAM. Even the **2028 target of >$7B** total company revenue implies <2% share. The runway is not the constraint.
- **The real constraint is the moat.** Akamai's advantage here is *contracted capacity plus a globally distributed edge footprint and power/colocation secured in advance* — not proprietary silicon, not a data network effect. Management's own fully-ramped large-deal economics: **~$2.2B annual revenue at ~75% adjusted EBITDA margin (which excludes the D&A that matters), ~30% non-GAAP operating margin, and only ~15% ROIC** — and it concedes the Anthropic deal carries the **lowest-return pricing** in the book. That is a capital landlord's return profile, not a disruptor's.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Feb 18, 2027 — Q4'26 print (highest stakes).** Must show: the $30M of revenue that slipped from Q4 into 2027, delivery of a Q4 capex quarter of up to **$2.6B (including $1.7B of memory pre-purchases)**, and 2027 CIS framework intact. This is a binary-ish event.
- **Q1–Q2 2027 — CIS conversion proof.** Quarterly CIS must track toward $275–325M/qtr to hit the $1.1–1.3B year. Any deceleration below ~30% breaks the 2027 model.
- **H1 2027 — margin trough and turn.** Management guides non-GAAP margins bottoming ~1pt below current (~24%) as colocation costs front-run revenue. Visible inflection = the re-rating catalyst.
- **2027 convertible maturity / financing.** $2.2B of 0% converts raised in Nov 2026; further raises risk dilution. Conversely, a credit stabilization (or upgrade path off BB+) would remove the biggest de-rating driver.
- **Anthropic $9B option + warrants.** Exercise would add nearly another full book of business; the attached warrants are a dilution overhang.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD** (accumulate only on execution proof)

The setup is genuinely asymmetric. At ~**3.5x EV/2026E revenue, ~3.0x EV/2027E revenue and ~12x 2026E non-GAAP EPS**, the market is capitalizing Akamai as a ~5% grower and assigning **almost no value to the $14B signed CIS book**.

Run the bull math: $7B+ 2028 revenue at a 30% non-GAAP operating margin = ~$2.1B, less ~$300M interest, taxed → **~$9.50–10.00 EPS**, which is ~$145–150 at 15x. That is exactly where Evercore ($150), Raymond James ($170) and KeyBanc ($175) sit — **+80% upside**. The bear case (CIS slippage, memory cost overruns, leverage forcing equity issuance) is a $55–65 stock, **–30%**.

**Why not Buy today:** (1) guidance has been cut twice in two quarters — the ramp is *not* fully within management's control; (2) 2027 EPS is **flat** while leverage, D&A and interest all rise, so there is no earnings cushion if revenue slips; (3) a **BB+ junk rating** with negative FCF means the financing risk is now existential-adjacent rather than theoretical; (4) the ~15% ROIC on the anchor deal is a thin spread over WACC, so this growth is **not value-creating at high confidence** — it is value-creating *if* pricing improves on later contracts and capacity turns up on schedule.

**What flips this to Buy (and then Strong Buy):** Q4'26/E1'27 capex landing inside the guided range with no new raise; CIS tracking ≥$275M in a quarter; non-GAAP margin trough confirmed in H1'27 with sequential expansion in H2'27; and a stable/improving credit outlook. Absent those, the stock is a levered call option on one counterparty's compute appetite — own it small, not large.

## 7. Key Risks

1. **Single-customer concentration (the dominant risk).** Anthropic anchors the $14B book at the *lowest-return pricing* in the portfolio. A slowdown, in-sourcing decision, or renegotiation by one counterparty invalidates the entire 2027–28 model.
2. **Execution and supply chain.** Already $30M of revenue slipped Q4→2027; colocation/power delays cited; only ~70% of hardware procured. $1.7B of memory pre-purchased at what may be cycle-peak pricing.
3. **Balance sheet / funding.** ~$9B debt, BB+ (two downgrades in 2026 plus one on the Anthropic deal), cash and securities down to $4.2B, buybacks paused, 2027 converts maturing. Equity issuance at $80 would be severely dilutive.
4. **The D&A cliff.** $3.5B+ of annual capex against ~$766M current D&A run-rate means depreciation and interest will compress GAAP EPS well into 2028. GAAP EPS already ~–15% in 2026.
5. **Competitive squeeze.** Hyperscalers (AWS/Azure/GCP), neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius) and Nvidia's own stack can undercut on price; Cloudflare/Fastly compete in the edge-security franchise that actually funds the dividend-less build.
6. **Structural CDN decay.** Delivery still shrinking (–3% cc in Q3, after –5% and –8%) and gross margin has fallen ~870bp since 2021 with no floor confirmed.
7. **Narrative/valuation whipsaw.** Two >20% single-day drawdowns in 18 months (Feb 2025, and the May→Nov 2026 –50% round trip). Expect continued 30%+ swings around prints.
