## AKAM — Akamai Technologies | Value Analysis
**Price $80.32 (Nov 9, 2026) | Market cap $11.58B | 144.2M shares | 52-wk range $77.37–$165.45**

---

### 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **NARROW, and narrowing**

Akamai historically possessed a genuine wide-moat asset: a globally distributed edge network (efficient scale), deep customer integration in content delivery (switching costs), and a security franchise (WAF, bot management, Zero Trust) layered on top. That legacy franchise is now in structural retreat:

| Metric | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | TTM 9/26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | 63.3% | 61.7% | 60.4% | 59.4% | **54.7%** | ~53% |
| Operating margin | 22.6% | 18.7% | 16.7% | 13.4% | **14.9%** | ~12% |
| ROIC (reported) | — | 9.0% | 7.0% | 5.6% | **5.3%** | ~4% |
| ROE | — | 12.0% | 11.9% | 10.4% | **9.1%** | ~8% |
| Revenue growth | 8.2% | 4.5% | 5.4% | 4.7% | 5.4% | 6.2% |

ROIC has fallen below any reasonable WACC (~9%) and has done so for three consecutive years. **This is the single most damning fact in the file.** A moated business earns returns *above* its cost of capital; Akamai no longer does. Pricing power is visibly gone: gross margin is down ~860bps in four years as revenue mixes toward lower-margin compute/colocation, while delivery (CDN) is in secular decline and security has decelerated from high-teens growth to **+9%** (Q3'26, $622M).

The future business model is now something different entirely. In Q4 2026 Akamai signed an **$11.6B, seven-year contract with Anthropic** for 75–80MW of dedicated compute, requiring **~$5.5B of capex front-loaded** ($1.7B in Q4'26, $3.1B in 2027, $0.7B in 2028), and granted Anthropic a **5% equity stake**. Akamai is converting from an asset-light CDN/security platform into a debt-funded compute landlord with a single dominant counterparty. Efficient-scale economics are replaced by commodity infrastructure economics.

**Moat verdict: Narrow (residual: security brand + edge footprint). Direction: narrowing materially.**

**The honest owner-earnings number:** GAAP EBIT ~$520M TTM + D&A $755M = **~$1.28B of GAAP EBITDA** — after expensing SBC. Against a ~$19B enterprise value that is **~14.8x GAAP EBITDA**. Adding back SBC to reach "adjusted EBITDA" ($1.8B) flatters the multiple to 10.5x, but SBC at $526M/year is genuine compensation and buybacks are the only thing preventing dilution — they are a cash cost, not an accounting artifact.

Akamai repurchased ~$4B of stock over five years at prices averaging well above today's $80 — including **$924M in FY2025 near $90–100**. That capital is gone. Diluted shares did fall from 160.5M (2022) to 144.2M, but the same period saw net debt rise from roughly zero to $7B+. **Value transferred from balance sheet to sellers of shares.**

### 3. Valuation Analysis

**Current:** Price $80.32; market cap $11.58B; pro forma net debt ~$7.4B (incl. leases + new converts) → **EV ≈ $19.0B**. GAAP P/E 31x; forward non-GAAP P/E 12.5x.

**DCF (my own, WACC 9.5%, terminal g 2.5%)**

*Base case — legacy business, Anthropic economics at cost of capital:*
- Legacy cash EBIT (GAAP EBIT + intangible amortization, SBC expensed): ~$700M → NOPAT ~$555M
- Capitalized at 9.5% less 4% growth = **~$10.1B EV** for the legacy franchise
- Anthropic contract NPV: $11.6B revenue / 7 yrs = $1.66B/yr; ~$300M/yr cash opex → ~$1.36B EBITDA; less ~$917M/yr depreciation (depreciating $5.5B over 6 yrs) on a 7-year contract → after-tax cash ~$1.25B/yr; PV at 9.5% ≈ $6.4B vs $5.5B capex → **NPV ≈ +$0.9B**, assuming full-term performance and no residual value
- **Total EV ≈ $11.0B − net debt $7.4B = equity ~$3.6B ≈ $25/share**

*Bull case — contract executes flawlessly, revenue accelerates to 17% (2027) and mid-teens thereafter, margins recover, re-rating to 11x adjusted EBITDA:*
- 2029 revenue ~$5.8B, adjusted EBITDA ~$2.4B × 11x = $26.4B EV − $6.5B net debt = **~$138/share**

The bull case is real — which is why 22 analysts carry a $132 average target. But the spread between $25 and $138 is not an analytical range; **it is an admission that I cannot underwrite this business.** The variance is driven by three things I cannot diligence: (a) one AI lab's seven-year willingness and ability to pay, (b) 75–80MW of data-center execution and power costs, (c) junk-rated financing costs on $5.5B of front-loaded capex.

**What the current price implies:** ~$19B EV for a business generating $211M of true FCF. Even on generous adjusted EBITDA, you are paying ~10.5x for a company with negative real free cash flow through 2027, junk credit, 12% SBC, and declining core margins. **There is no margin of safety at $80.** The 12.5x forward P/E is a value trap: 2027 EPS must absorb ~$900M of incremental depreciation (~$4.50/share pre-tax) and ~$200M+ of new interest (~$1.00/share) before Anthropic revenue ramps — the revenue is back-end loaded while the costs are front-loaded.

**Intrinsic value estimate: $45–65/share (base case ~$55).** Current price is at or above the top of my range.

### 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Weak (deteriorating)**

- **Capital allocation has been poor and is now aggressive.** ~$4B of buybacks at $90–130 — a $3B+ destruction of shareholder capital. Now pivoting to $5.5B of debt-funded capex on a single seven-year customer contract, while accepting a **junk rating (BB+)** and gifting a 5% equity stake to a customer.
- **Guidance credibility is weak.** FY2026 revenue guide $4.44–4.465B with non-GAAP EPS *declining* to $6.47–6.68 (from ~$6.90 in FY2025); Q4'26 revenue guided to $1.16B vs. $1.20B consensus. Non-GAAP operating income fell 12% and 11% YoY in Q2'26 and Q3'26.
- **Founder-led (Dr. Tom Leighton, co-founder/CEO)** is a genuine positive — deep technical ownership. But technical brilliance has not translated into capital discipline, and the insider signal is absent (no meaningful recent insider buying that would offset a 51% drawdown).
- **Transparency:** the Anthropic deal was announced with "no impact to 2026 revenue guidance" and a $1.7B capex increase — a mismatch that obscures near-term FCF. That is not owner-operator communication.

### 5. Investment Verdict: **SELL / AVOID**

This was, until recently, a narrow-moat business earning roughly its cost of capital and trading at a full price. It is now a **leveraged, capital-intensive infrastructure bet with a single dominant counterparty, junk credit, accelerating depreciation, and negative real free cash flow through 2027** — and it still trades at ~15x SBC-inclusive EBITDA.

The three Buffett tests it fails outright:
1. **Moat/returns:** ROIC 5.3% and falling — below WACC for three years. Not a moated compounder.
2. **Margin of safety:** intrinsic value $45–65 vs. $80.32 price. You are paying a premium, not a discount.
3. **Circle of competence:** the thesis now rests on a seven-year AI-lab compute contract and 80MW of data-center buildout. I cannot underwrite that with confidence, and a business I cannot value is a business I do not own at any price.

**What would change my mind:** (a) evidence that the Anthropic contract is take-or-pay with investment-grade-style credit support and that incremental returns exceed 12% pre-tax; (b) genuine FCF recovery (OCF minus all capex > $700M) alongside stable gross margins; (c) debt reduction back toward 2x EBITDA; (d) a price near $50–55, giving a true 20–30% discount. Absent those, patience is the correct position.

### 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

**Risks to being bearish (i.e., I'm wrong to sell):**
- The Anthropic deal is a beachhead; follow-on AI compute contracts at far better incremental economics could make Akamai a legitimate third cloud. Revenue guidance of 17% growth in 2027, if delivered, re-rates the stock violently.
- $11.6B of contractual backlog is real and large relative to a $11.6B market cap — if it is take-or-pay, the downside is protected.
- Security remains a $2.4B+ franchise growing high-single-digits with high switching costs; a sum-of-parts view could unlock value.
- Beta 0.66, deep value screens, and 22 analysts at a $132 target — heavy short-squeeze/re-rating potential.

**Risks to owning it (why I won't):**
- **Counterparty concentration:** Anthropic is an AI lab whose seven-year commitment depends on continued funding and demand; a slowdown or renegotiation would strand $5.5B of dedicated assets.
- **Obsolescence:** compute assets built for 2026 workloads may be economically obsolete well before seven years; residual value is assumed to be zero in my model.
- **Refinancing risk:** BB+ with watch negative, $2.2B of new converts just issued, $3.1B of 2027 capex to fund. Equity issuance or further downgrade is a live possibility.
- **Dilution:** 5% stake to Anthropic, plus convertible overhang, plus $526M/yr SBC.
- **Secular decline in delivery** (~40% of revenue) with no cost offset — the legacy cash engine funding the pivot is shrinking.
- **Depreciation cliff in 2027–28** will compress GAAP and non-GAAP EPS precisely when management needs credibility.
