## AKAM — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
*As of 9–10 Nov 2026 (latest close $80.29; market cap ~$11.5B on 147.2M shares)*

**Context for everything below:** AKAM has round-tripped from an intraday high of $165.45 (13 May 2026) to $77.84 (5 Nov) — a ~53% drawdown. The proximate cause is not misconduct but a capital-allocation decision: on 24 Sep 2026 the company announced an $11.6B, seven-year agreement with Anthropic requiring ~$5.5B of capex (~$1.7B in Q4 2026, ~$3.1B in 2027, ~$0.7B in 2028). Shares fell ~15% on 25 Sep on 31.9M shares (vs ~3M normal), and again after Q3 (5 Nov). This reshapes the non-financial risk profile.

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate** (not Strong)

Positives (verified):
- **Independent Chair.** Dan Hesse (board since 2016, Chair since 2021) is independent; all directors other than Dr. Leighton are independent. CEO/Chair duality is *not* a problem here.
- **Shareholder rights improved** at the 13 May 2026 AGM: charter/bylaw amendments grant a **special meeting right at 25%** ownership — a genuine pro-shareholder change.
- All nominees elected; PwC ratified (111.4M for); say-on-pay approved.

Negatives / watch items:
- **Founder-CEO entrenchment and succession vacuum.** Tom Leighton, co-founder, CEO since 2013 and age 69 per the 2026 proxy, has no disclosed successor. For a company whose strategy now hinges on a single AI-infrastructure bet, this is a live key-person exposure.
- **Soft say-on-pay support.** ~102.4M for vs. 111.4M for on the auditor ratification implies roughly **high-single-digit to ~10% opposition** — not a crisis, but a signal on pay.
- **Equity dilution creep.** Shareholders approved adding **8,000,000 shares** to the 2013 Stock Incentive Plan (≈5.4% of 147.2M shares out) plus a new 2026 ESPP — layered on top of $2.2B of convertible notes issued 9 Nov 2026.
- **~46% support for a political-spending disclosure proposal** (51.7M for / 61.3M against) — an unusually high protest vote indicating a restive, ESG-sensitive holder base.
- **Capital-allocation governance is the core issue.** Two rating downgrades in 2026, then a cut to **speculative grade (S&P 'BB+' from 'BBB-', 9 Nov 2026; Moody's also acted)** to fund one private counterparty's build-out. The board endorsed a strategy that equity and credit markets have both voted against.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: **Low–Moderate**

- **No SEC enforcement actions or DOJ matters** found in EDGAR full-text search (empty result set).
- **Litigation:** *SunStone Information Defense v. Akamai* (2:26-cv-00353, E.D. Tex.) — NPE patent suit filed 1 May 2026, **closed 12 Aug 2026**. Low severity, no disclosed material charge. Legacy Limelight patent disputes are long resolved (settled in Akamai's favour).
- **No pending regulatory approvals** (no FDA/FCC/EU-style gating events). Antitrust exposure on AI-infra deals is a theoretical, not evidenced, risk.
- **One contract-level compliance gap worth escalating:** the filed Anthropic Master Services Agreement (effective 5 May 2026, Exhibit 10.1 to the Q3 10-Q) states the parties will negotiate the **Data Processing Addendum and Privacy Statement only *after* the effective date**. A deal of this scale — AI workloads, enterprise and potentially regulated data — beginning with privacy/DPA terms unsigned is a governance and GDPR/CCPA exposure that should be cured and confirmed.

### 4. Top Three Operational Risks

1. **Single-customer concentration in the entire growth plan.** The $11.6B Anthropic agreement underpins 2027 cloud-infrastructure revenue guidance of $1.1–1.3B and the >$7.0B 2028 revenue target. The counterparty is a private, cash-consuming AI lab, the MSA is expressly **non-exclusive** (Anthropic may contract elsewhere), and it includes a "Customer Competitors" schedule constraining Akamai's other business. Counterparty funding, contract durability and renegotiation risk are now the dominant drivers of equity value.
2. **Execution/capex physics: $5.5B of build vs. a 12-month track record.** Management already cited **"supply chain and data center delays"** shifting revenue out of Q4 2026, and Q3 showed revenue +6% y/y while **GAAP EPS fell 21%** on infrastructure cost pressure. Risks: GPU/power/colocation supply concentration, construction slippage, and the classic build-ahead-of-revenue trap (stranded capex if Anthropic ramps slower than contracted).
3. **Platform reliability and key-person dependency.** Akamai is internet-critical infrastructure; the public status feed shows recurring events (e.g., a service disruption 14:13–14:34 UTC on 30 Sep 2026; Mumbai/Chennai performance degradation 27 Oct 2026). These are minor, but the tail risk is asymmetric — a global edge/DNS outage would hit hundreds of enterprises simultaneously. Separately, the network and the strategy both run through a 69-year-old founder-CEO with no named successor.

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **Moderate–High (elevated)**

- **Credibility damage with the buy-side is the real issue.** A 2026 guidance trim (August), deployment-delay-driven revenue slippage, and a 53% drawdown have moved the narrative from "AI winner" (Barron's upgrade, Oct) to "AI capex value-trap" (multiple Sell/neutral notes; consensus "Hold" across 24 analysts; a published ~$70 fair value). Recovery now requires flawless execution against sceptical holders — the hardest reputational position to be in.
- **Cost of capital damage is tangible:** losing investment-grade status raises future funding costs and narrows the investor base (some mandates exclude split-rated/junk issuers), which mechanically pressures the multiple.
- **Customer-side brand remains intact.** Security revenue +9% ($622M), cloud infrastructure +34%, and product launches (Akamai Inference, Agent Sandbox) read as credible. No product recall, safety controversy, or consumer backlash found.
- **ESG/political-spending sensitivity** (46% support for a disclosure proposal) means the holder base will punish perceived governance missteps.
- *Gap: no social-sentiment or ESG controversy-score data was retrievable; this assessment rests on news flow and market reaction.*

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Not because of integrity failures — there is **no evidence of fraud, no enforcement action, no restatement, and the board is independently chaired**. It is High because three risk vectors stack simultaneously: (a) an existential **counterparty concentration** in a private AI lab, (b) **execution risk on $5.5B of debt-funded capex** that already has a documented slippage record, and (c) **severely impaired credibility with both equity and credit markets** (junk-rated, down 53%). Governance is the weakest *contributor* only in the capital-allocation and succession dimensions.

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Counterparty concentration:** >$7B 2028 revenue target dependent on one non-exclusive agreement with a private, loss-making counterparty. Request: contract term sheet on minimum commitments, take-or-pay provisions, and termination/credit support.
2. **Privacy/DPA not executed at signing** of the Anthropic MSA (Exhibit 10.1) — confirm the DPA and Privacy Statement have since been finalized; unresolved, this is a GDPR/CCPA and enterprise-contract exposure on AI workloads.
3. **Two downgrades to speculative grade in a single year** (S&P to 'BB+', 9 Nov 2026; Moody's action) on debt-funded capex, plus $2.2B convertible issuance — capital-allocation governance warrants board-level challenge.
4. **Insider conviction gap:** ~$8.2M sold, zero bought, through a 53% drawdown; CFO/COO/GC/CHRO among sellers. Recommend verifying 10b5-1 adoption dates predate the Anthropic deal, and monitoring for any accelerated selling.
5. **Guidance reliability:** August trim + Q4 revenue slip + GAAP EPS −21% y/y. Any third consecutive slippage on cloud-infrastructure delivery should be treated as a control/forecasting-quality issue, not merely a timing issue.
6. **Succession:** no disclosed plan for a 69-year-old founder-CEO mid-way through the largest capital program in company history.
