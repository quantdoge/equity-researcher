# AKAM — Financial Risk Assessment
**As of:** market data 2026-11-09 (close $80.29) · latest financials Q3 2026 (period end 2026-09-30)

## Executive Summary

Akamai is mid-transformation from a high-margin, cash-generative CDN/security business into a capital-intensive AI cloud infrastructure provider. That pivot has broken the company's two historical risk anchors: **positive free cash flow** and **low leverage**. Gross debt has risen from $4.53B (FY2023) to $9.45B (Q3 2026), capex guidance has jumped to **$3.46–3.66B for FY2026** versus $820M in FY2025, and the equity has de-rated 52% from its May 2026 peak. Liquidity itself remains adequate ($3.52B cash); the risk is *structural and forward-looking*, not a near-term solvency event.

**Piotroski F-Score: 5/9 (Moderate).** Weaknesses: no ROA improvement, no leverage improvement, no gross-margin improvement, no asset-turnover improvement. Ironically, asset turnover *fell* because $3.9B of liabilities were converted into assets that have not yet produced revenue.

**Credit deterioration signals (all confirmed):**
- The November 2026 8-K amendment **cut the secured subsidiary debt and liens basket from 10% to 3.5% of consolidated total assets** — lenders tightened collateral terms. This is a lender-imposed constraint, a classic credit-deterioration marker.
- $3.5B of upsized zero-coupon converts closed May 2026 (two $1.75B tranches, 2030 and 2032) — the largest financing in company history, and the stock has since halved.
- No dividend, but $1.4B of buybacks across FY25/H1'26 have now stopped entirely (Q3'26 repurchases: $0) — cash is being redirected to capex.

TTM revenue $4.39B → $3.51B (−$878M). Decremental margins are severe because the new cost base is largely fixed (depreciating data centre assets, network, R&D).

**Conclusion:** a 20% revenue decline **eliminates EBIT entirely** and pushes leverage to 18–20x. The company would be forced to halve capex — writing off the strategic rationale for the entire $3.5B raise. Cash of $3.52B against ~$1.8–2.0B annual burn gives roughly **1.8–2.0 years of runway** before external financing is required again, *and* they would be returning to market having already exhausted the converts and with the secured-debt basket cut to 3.5% of assets. In this scenario the rating moves to **Critical**.

**Concentration risk amplifies this:** the capex case rests on large contracted AI compute arrangements (a reported ~$11.6B Anthropic-linked deal and a ~$3.4B compute contract). These are single-counterparty dependencies layered onto a $4.4B revenue base — a repricing or cancellation is itself the −20% scenario.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Rationale: liquidity and interest coverage are genuinely sound today, which rules out "Critical." But four independent measures all deteriorated in three quarters — leverage (4.26x → 6.70x), Z-score (2.16 → 1.71), FCF (+$699M → −$2.0B annualising), and covenant terms (secured basket cut 10% → 3.5%). The balance sheet is being levered against assets that will not generate revenue for years, in a business whose operating margin already fell from 20.0% (Q3'25) to 13.3% (Q3'26) and whose gross margin fell ~430bps YoY.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. 🔴 **FCF cliff.** FY2026 capex guidance $3.46–3.66B vs $820M in FY2025, implying ~$2.5B of Q4 capex. FY2026 FCF ≈ **−$2.0B**; forward FCF yield ≈ **−17%**.
2. 🔴 **Leverage doubling.** Gross debt $4.53B → $9.45B in under three years; net debt/EBITDA 3.3x → 6.7x. Incremental debt has funded assets, not earnings — EBIT is *lower* than in FY2023 ($496M TTM vs $670M).
3. 🔴 **Lender-imposed covenant tightening.** Secured debt/liens basket cut from 10% to 3.5% of consolidated total assets (Nov 2026 8-K) — a credit signal, not a refinancing convenience.
4. 🟠 **Convertible maturity wall with no equity escape valve.** $1.15B due 2027 (sitting in current liabilities), $1.1B 2029, $1.75B 2030, $1.75B 2032. With the stock at $80 and conversion prices far above spot, these settle in **cash**, not shares.
5. 🟠 **Margin compression while revenue grows.** Revenue +6.4% YoY in Q3'26 but operating income fell from $211M to $149M. D&A is compounding as capex capitalises.
6. 🟠 **Extreme price volatility / breach of risk budget.** 52% annualised vol, 1.24 realised beta, −52% max drawdown, Sharpe 0.071. Any risk-parity or vol-targeted mandate will be forced to size this near zero.
7. 🟡 **Goodwill/intangibles at $3.94B = 77% of book equity** ($5.15B) — a write-down in a downside scenario would eliminate much of the equity cushion underpinning the Z-score.
8. 🟡 **Buyback halt and single-counterparty AI concentration** — the two supports for the equity story are now the two largest sources of uncertainty.
