---

**Investment Thesis**

Akamai has converted itself from a cash-generative, narrow-moat CDN/security franchise into a **junk-rated (BB+), negative-free-cash-flow, single-counterparty AI datacenter landlord**. The $11.6B, seven-year Anthropic contract — funded with ~$5.5B of debt-financed capex and a 5% equity warrant at admittedly the *lowest-return pricing in the book* (~15% ROIC) — arrives just as legacy delivery shrinks 3–7%/yr, gross margin has collapsed ~900bps since 2021, and ROIC (5.3%) sits below WACC for a third straight year. There is no fraud here (Beneish −2.80, accruals −0.093, cash conversion 3.4x) — this is a capital-allocation and business-model problem. At $80, the market is capitalizing low-margin hosting revenue as software; intrinsic value on SBC-expensed economics is ~$55. The honest offset: $14B of *signed* commitments against an $11.6B market cap means the option is nearly free — but you cannot size a position on one customer, no FCF, and a negative Kelly.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Neutral. ~80% of AKAM's risk is idiosyncratic, 20% macro (strong USD −1pt on the 50% international book; 5.13% 10Y caps the multiple at ~12x). The CDN/edge/security sector is bifurcated: security and AI-inference grow 11–23%, legacy delivery is commoditizing. AKAM is the **slowest grower (−9.1pp vs peer median) and lowest-gross-margin name (−24.1pp)** in its true competitive set, albeit one of only three GAAP-profitable. Bull scenario: fast disinflation → $100–110 on multiple alone.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium.** Clean litigation, no Russia/China, no country ≥10% of revenue. The risk is direction of travel: EU CADA sovereignty rules targeting non-EU cloud providers, export-control dependencies on the Anthropic build, and an unexecuted **Data Processing Addendum on the $11.6B MSA** (GDPR/CCPA exposure — escalated by two independent agents).
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **SELL.** Moat narrow and narrowing; ROIC 5.3% < WACC; SBC $526M exceeds GAAP net income; ~$4B of buybacks at $90–130 destroyed; net debt ~$7.4–8.3B pro forma. DCF base ≈ **$55**; bull ≈ $138. The spread is an admission the business is now underwritable only as an option.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **HOLD.** The inflection is real: CIS bookings $2.8B → >$14B in one quarter, 2027 guide +17–22%, >$7B revenue by 2028. But guidance has been cut twice in two quarters, 2027 EPS is *flat* while D&A and interest rise, and FY26 FCF ≈ **−$2.0B**. Growth is being bought with capital, not earned with margin.
- **Quantitative Signals**: **Neutral, tilt Sell.** #1 of 3 on value (13x EV/EBITDA), #3 on momentum (−11.8%) and quality — the empirical signature of a **value trap**. Realised vol 68%, beta 1.49, max drawdown −52%, R² ~0.10 (90% idiosyncratic — beta-based sizing fails here). The "+17% revision upgrade" flag is a calculation artifact; true broker revision direction is negative.
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Net debt/EBITDA 4.3x → **6.7x** in nine months; gross debt $9.45B; Altman Z ~1.17–1.71 (distress boundary); **lenders cut the secured basket from 10% to 3.5% of assets**; FY26E FCF −$2.0B (forward FCF yield ≈ −17%); $1.15B of converts due 2027 settle in cash at $80 spot. A −20% revenue scenario eliminates EBIT entirely and pushes leverage toward 18–20x.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Not integrity — independence, no enforcement, independent chair — but stacked vectors: existential counterparty concentration, execution risk with documented slippage ($30M already slipped Q4→2027; only ~70% of hardware procured), **12 insider sales / ~$8.2M and zero open-market buys through a 53% drawdown**, and no succession plan for a 69-year-old founder-CEO mid-way through the largest capital program in company history.
- **ESG**: **Average, negative momentum.** Best-in-class disclosure (externally assured, first exchange-executed VPPA) but decarbonisation running backwards: intensity +37%, clean-energy share −7pp, PUE 1.40 → 1.48, 2030 target progress regressing 47% → 42% — just as the energy bill for the Anthropic build lands. Neutral to the thesis, but an under-priced cost-of-capital risk on a newly junk-rated issuer.
- **Alternative Data**: **Neutral, leaning bearish.** Google Trends −32% QoQ, Stocktwits mentions −67% — the *narrative* has fully unwound while the *contracts* remain intact. Revenue surprises compressed to ±0.8%; the market has stopped paying for beats (Q3 beat → −9.4%). Alt data does not corroborate the +9.6% YoY acceleration consensus needs in Q4.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **SHORT (not Strong).** Deterioration is already in the cash flow statement, not a forecast. Withheld "Strong" because there is no fraud catalyst, $3.5B cash remains, and a stock with 25 moves >5% in a year can carry a short out on a stretcher.

**Bull Case**
1. **$14B signed book vs $11.6B market cap.** If Anthropic performs and pricing improves on follow-on contracts, 2028 revenue >$7B at ~30% non-GAAP margins → ~$9.50–10 EPS → $145–150 at 15x (+80%).
2. **Security is a real franchise.** $2.4B+, +10%, high switching costs — a SOTP case exists if the legacy delivery drag is ever excised.
3. **Macro asymmetry.** A fast disinflation re-rates the multiple ~25% on an unchanged story; beta 0.66 long-run, and positioning is capitulation-level washed out.

**Bear Case**
1. **The economics don't work at spot.** IV ~$55 with SBC expensed and Anthropic at cost of capital; FY26–27 cumulative funding gap ~$2.8B on top of ~$8.3B net debt; D&A cliff hits GAAP EPS precisely when credibility is needed most.
2. **One counterparty is the entire growth story.** Anthropic is private, cash-consuming, non-exclusive, with hyperscaler alternatives; a deferral strands $5.5B of purpose-built, rapidly-obsolescing assets against $9.5B of debt.
3. **Financing is now reflexive.** BB+ with negative outlook, covenant baskets already tightened, equity at $80 — a further raise is either expensive debt or dilutive equity, and both re-price the stock down.

**Key Risks**
- **Anthropic deferral/renegotiation** — the single event that invalidates 2027–28 models (probability: medium, impact: severe)
- **Q4'26 print (Feb 18, 2027)**: capex must land inside $3.46–3.66B with no new raise; a miss is a binary negative
- **Second downgrade / further covenant tightening**; 2027 convert maturity settling in cash
- **Gross margin below 53%** — no floor confirmed; CIS decelerating (+39% → +35%) before the ramp
- **EU CADA / export controls** constraining siting of contracted capacity
