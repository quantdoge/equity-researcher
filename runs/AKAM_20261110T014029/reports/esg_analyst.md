**Sector:** Technology / Software–Infrastructure (edge cloud, CDN, security) | **Market cap:** $11.5bn | **Revenue 2025:** $4.21bn | **Employees:** ~11,832 | **HQ:** Cambridge, MA

## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **HIGH**

Akamai publishes unusually good data — and the data shows **decarbonisation running backwards** precisely as the AI buildout begins:

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Δ YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope 1 (tCO₂e) | 54 | 45 | 70 | trivial absolute |
| **Scope 2 market-based** | 112,701 | 121,001 | **173,000** | **+43%** |
| Scope 2 location-based | 243,301 | 262,401 | 322,800 | +23% |
| Scope 3 | 226,615 | 174,340 | 180,350 | +3% |
| **Gross S1+2+3** | 339,370 | 295,386 | **353,420** | **+20%** |
| **Emissions intensity (t/$ revenue)** | 0.000030 | 0.000030 | **0.000041** | **+37%** |
| Electricity consumed (MWh) | 828,534 | 873,400 | **1,073,200** | **+23%** |
| **Clean energy share** | 56% | 59% | **52%** | **−7pp** |
| **Average DC PUE** | 1.40 | 1.40 | **1.48** | **worse** |
| 2030 target progress (vs base) | — | 47% | **42%** | **regressed** |

**Read-through:** Three of the four operational levers moved the wrong way in one year — intensity +37%, renewable share −7pp, PUE +0.08. Non-renewable energy consumed rose 45% (364,313 → 527,240 MWh); they burned **more** fossil power in 2025 than the prior year while claiming a 2030 net-zero path.

**Mitigants (real, not cosmetic):** Gross footprint is still small (~353ktCO₂e — comparable to a mid-size manufacturer), 100% ISO 14001 coverage, 100% e-waste diversion, external assurance of emissions data (June 2026), and a genuinely innovative **first-ever CFTC-registered exchange-executed VPPA** (Resurety CleanTrade, Aug 31 2026) — evidence of sophisticated energy procurement capability.

**Forward risk — the core issue:** Q3 2026 cloud infrastructure revenue was $109m (+34% YoY), guided to **$1.1–1.3bn in 2027** against >$14bn of multiyear commitments (incl. a reported **$11.6bn Anthropic deal**). Energy consumption will plausibly multiply from the current 1.08 TWh base. Against a 2030 target requiring −100% of combined Scope 1/2/3, the **plan is not credible at current trajectory**. Expect the gap to widen, not close.

**Physical/transition risk:** Moderate. Colocation concentration creates grid-interconnection and power-price risk (already flagged in earnings as "rising infrastructure costs squeezing margins"), and rising EU carbon costs on non-renewable MWh.

## 3. Social Risk Assessment: **LOW–MEDIUM**

- **Attrition is low and trending up only modestly:** US attrition ex-RIF 4.3% → 5.4% → **6.4%** (2023→2025); global women's attrition 6.2%. Well below tech-sector norms — a genuine positive.
- **Workforce stability:** ~3% restructuring (~300 roles) in 2023; no evidence of union disputes, strikes, or wage controversies. FlexBase flexible-work model is a retention asset.
- **Diversity:** Reports composition metrics annually; gender/racial representation disclosed. Board is 4/9 women (Bowen, Brown, Akella, Ranganathan) — strong.
- **Product safety / systemic risk (the real S-factor):** As a security and availability provider, AKAM's "product safety" failure mode is a platform-wide outage or compromise. The 2021 Edge DNS outage is the reference case; no comparable material incident in the review window.
- **AI-era labour/ethics:** Agentic-AI governance and nonhuman-identity risk are both a product line and a social/regulatory exposure.
- Supply chain labour standards: supplier assessment programme exists; semiconductor/GPU upstream is the least-verified link.

## 4. Governance Quality Score: **7 / 10**

**Positives**
- **Single share class** — no dual-class entrenchment. (Many tech peers cannot say this.)
- **9-person board; 8 of 9 independent** (CEO Leighton the only insider). Board includes strong independent voices (former FCC/FDIC/ambassador Sharon Bowen; ex-Amazon/Microsoft infrastructure operator Tom Killalea).
- **New right for stockholders to call special meetings** approved at 99.4% — a real expansion of shareholder rights.
- **Say-on-Pay 91%** support (10.5m votes against — modest dissent, not a revolt).
- Robust disclosure architecture: GRI + SASB + S&P CSA mapping, TCFD, **externally assured** emissions, climate metrics embedded in executive compensation (per proxy/CSA cross-reference).
- PwC auditor ratified at 91%; no restatements or accounting controversies found.

**Deductions**
- **Charter amendment carried with only 58% support (47.7m against)** and required an **adjournment to solicit proxies** (itself passed with just 57%) — a board-sponsored item drawing ~42% opposition is a governance-quality red flag and indicates weak engagement on a structural change.
- **Political spending disclosure proposal won 45.7%** of votes cast despite board opposition — nearly half the shareholder base wants transparency management won't provide.
- **Director Janaki Akella re-elected with 92%** (~9.6m against / 8.5%) — the only director below 96%; committee-role scrutiny warranted.
- **Uncertainty on chair/CEO separation:** the 2026 nominee slate contains no separate executive chairman (long-time Executive Chairman George Conrades does not appear), leaving open the possibility that founder-CEO Leighton now also holds the chair. *Unverified — proxy document was inaccessible; this is the single governance fact I most want confirmed.*
- Founder-CEO duality of influence (Leighton is co-founder, CEO, and director) warrants monitoring on capital-allocation discipline given the AI capex ramp.

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure

- **EU CSRD** — AKAM's EU turnover exceeds the €150m threshold; disclosure obligations phase in for FY2028 (wave 3 timing). Akamai is already ~GRI/SASB-aligned, so compliance cost is modest, but **double-materiality assessment will surface the AI energy ramp** more prominently than management currently does.
- **EU Energy Efficiency Directive — data centre reporting (highest near-term salience).** The Commission proposed rules in **September 2026** requiring EU data centres to disclose **energy and water efficiency** KPIs. Akamai operates an extensive EU edge footprint; mandatory public PUE/water disclosure is coming, and its **PUE deteriorated to 1.48** — that number will be published and compared.
- **EU Code of Conduct for Data Centres / delegated acts** — voluntary now, likely to harden.
- **SEC climate disclosure rule** — stayed in litigation; low near-term binding risk, but Akamai already discloses voluntarily.
- **State privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA et al.) and DORA/NIS2 in the EU** — material for the security business; also a **commercial opportunity** (compliance demand drives product).
- **CBAM: not applicable.**

## 7. Overall ESG Rating: **AVERAGE — with negative momentum**

The split verdict matters more than the label:

- **Disclosure & measurement: LEADER.** Externally assured multi-year emissions inventory, SASB/GRI mapping, PUE and intensity disclosed voluntarily, 100% ISO 14001, first-of-kind exchange-executed VPPA. MSCI rates AKAM "A" (secondary source, per KnowESG).
- **Decarbonisation execution: LAGGARD.** Intensity +37%, clean energy share −7pp, PUE worsening, target progress regressing 47%→42%.
- **Governance: slightly above average (7/10)** with specific dissent pockets.
- **Standing caveat:** KnowESG reports Akamai's target as **not SBTi-validated ("Removed")** and **CDP disclosure as "not disclosed"** — secondary-source, unverified, but consistent with a self-declared target. If true, the 2030 net-zero claim lacks third-party validation, which is precisely the kind of gap that ESG-screened mandates penalise.

## 8. ESG Investment Implication: **NEUTRAL (leaning NEGATIVE on a 2–3 year view)**

**Not a reason to own or avoid on its own — but it is a live risk to the bull case, and it is under-priced.**

1. **The AI transition is an ESG transition.** The $11.6bn Anthropic deal and 2027 cloud-infrastructure guide of $1.1–1.3bn convert a carbon-light model into a carbon- and power-intensive one. That introduces: (a) direct exposure to electricity price and grid interconnection queues — already visible in Q3 guidance; (b) a widening, publicly quantifiable gap to the company's own 2030/2040 targets; (c) mandatory EU energy/water efficiency disclosure from 2026–27 that will make the deterioration comparable across peers.
2. **Cost of capital channel.** With $2.2bn of new convertible notes just issued (Nov 2026) and heavy upfront capex, the marginal cost of capital matters more than it did. ESG-linked debt covenants aren't present, but ESG-screened equity and credit demand is a real marginal buyer.
3. **Governance friction is rising, not stable.** A 42%-opposed charter amendment plus a 45.7% political-spending vote means the 2027 proxy season will see escalated stewardship engagement — a distraction and a potential overhang on any equity issuance requiring shareholder approval.
4. **Genuine offsets:** clean controversy record, low attrition, single-class shares, assured data, and demonstrated capability to execute sophisticated renewable procurement (the exchange-executed VPPA). This is a company that *can* fix the trajectory; it currently is not.

**Verdict: Neutral for the thesis.** ESG does not change the investment call, but it adds a specific, monitorable risk to the AI-capex story that most models do not capture: **the energy bill and the carbon bill for the Anthropic buildout are not yet in guidance.**

**Watch items (next 4 quarters):** (1) Does 2026 clean-energy share recover above 59% and PUE fall below 1.40? (2) Is the 2030 target re-baselined — and does management admit it? (3) Does Akamai seek **SBTi validation** and file a **CDP** response? (4) Confirmation of chair/CEO separation in the 2027 proxy. (5) Any EU EED data-centre KPI disclosure.
