### Investment Memo: CRWV (CoreWeave, Inc.) — 19 October 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (Exit if held; do not initiate) | Conviction: High
**Price Target**: $28 (~54% downside from current $61.39) — probability-weighted, wide distribution
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
CoreWeave is the commercial leader of a genuinely explosive industry — >$104B contracted backlog growing +246% YoY — but the demand thesis and the equity thesis have decoupled. The equity is a levered residual claim (~86% of EV in equity value terms sits *behind* $45B+ of net debt) on a company that has never generated free cash flow, earns ~0% ROIC, funds itself at a ~15% marginal cost of debt, and has roughly one quarter of self-funding runway. When senior credit demands equity-like returns (2031 notes at ~78c, ~14.5–16.4% YTM; CDS ~1,000bp), the junior claim has no defensible value at $61 — the stock is a call option on the refinancing window staying open, struck against a hostile real-rate regime.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector demand is excellent (neocloud revenue +223% YoY, firming GPU rental rates, power scarcity protects incumbents) — but the sector has entered a credit-gated shakeout, and CRWV sits on the fault line. Macro is hostile on three simultaneous channels: 10Y TIPS real yield 2.78% (+87bp in 5 months), 30Y at 5.37% with the easing cycle stalled, and energy CPI +20.3% squeezing a top-two input. Broad HY OAS at 3.23% confirms this is *issuer-specific rationing*, not a credit cycle — the valve that is open for everyone else is shut for CRWV. Sector P/S discount (4.5x vs ~12x peer median) is a balance-sheet discount; on EV/revenue (~8x) it largely disappears.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH. Microsoft ~67% of FY2025 revenue; active §10(b) class action with a damaging documentary trail (WSJ: delivery delays flagged ~9 months before the Nov-2025 guidance cut); data-center moratorium legislation in 15+ states and 100+ localities targeting exactly the asset class being built; BIS export-control perimeter risk given 100% Nvidia supply dependence. No active government investigation keeps it out of "Very High."
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL, intrinsic value $15–40 (midpoint ~$28). No moat, TTM FCF −$13.7B, ROIC −0.1%, capex 2.5x quarterly revenue, debt:equity 10:1. The crux: **even the flawless-execution bull case (~$45/share) is below the current price** — there is no margin of safety in any scenario.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: HOLD, the sole non-bear. Backlog ($104.2B + >$25B Q3 commitments) against ~$12.5B 2026E revenue is a category-defining position; at 8.6x 2029E EPS the upside is "generational" *if real*. But Growth's own framing is decisive: "the equity is no longer a growth story; it is a capital-structure story." Conditional Strong Buy only *after* refinancing de-risks.

(Note: the short thesis is right on mechanism but uninvestable on timing — a 98%-vol name with a $130B backlog and Nvidia backstop can double on one financing headline. Express as avoid, not short.)

**Bull Case**
- **Contracted demand at unprecedented scale**: $104B+ backlog (3x market cap) with Microsoft, Meta (~$14B), and OpenAI as counterparties; revenue ramping +113% YoY on a $10B+ run-rate; only cohort member near operating breakeven (57% EBITDA margin Q2'26).
- **Strategic backstop**: Nvidia ~10% equity holder and sole GPU supplier — preferential allocation plus a credible rescue-financing candidate; power/interconnection scarcity gives incumbents a 3–5 year non-replicable asset.
- **Reflexive re-rating**: at 8.6x 2029E EPS and a 60% P/S discount to peers, *any* successful refinancing (bonds back above ~90c) plausibly re-rates the equity 50–100% violently.

**Bear Case**
- **Distressed capital structure**: $51.6B debt (plus ~$15.9B unreconciled obligations, likely leases), 11.5x leverage, 15% marginal funding cost, CDS ~1,000bp implying ~60% directional 5-year default probability; a 30% EV decline destroys ~70% of the equity.
- **Illusory economics**: EBITDA is a depreciation artifact — full-cycle D&A (~54% of revenue and rising) plus $2.6B annualized interest turns "near-breakeven" into ~−$4.9B restated EBIT; FCF has never been positive.
- **Adverse alignment and legal overhang**: founders monetized >$3.3B while retaining 72% voting control; class action with a scienter-grade paper trail; customer concentration (MSFT ~67%) means your largest customer can renegotiate, in-source, or walk.

**Key Risks**
1. **Refinancing failure / Q4-2026 window closure** — the dominant risk; a failed or punitive raise converts liquidity stress into covenant/dilution spiral. (Bear trigger)
2. **Counterparty shock**: MSFT/OpenAI renegotiation, cancellation, or AI-lab funding stall — backlog is only as good as customer balance sheets. (Bear trigger)
3. **Depreciation catch-up**: GPU life revision toward 5–6 years instantly impairs reported EBIT and the collateral base underpinning secured debt. (Bear trigger)
4. **Upside risk to a sell call**: a clean refinancing, Nvidia/strategic equity infusion, or energy retracement pulling real yields back to ~2% → 50–80% squeeze. (Bull trigger — the fat right tail is why conviction is High, not Maximum)
5. **November 9 Q3 print**: will be judged on financing cost, D&A ratio and backlog conversion, not revenue; alt data shows asymmetric downside reaction risk. Do not pre-position.
