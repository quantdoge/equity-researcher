## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector / theme concentration — ADVERSE.** CRWV is functionally a leveraged AI-capex + power-infrastructure play mislabelled "Software–Infrastructure." Any portfolio already holding NVDA, ORCL, MSFT, AMD, or a broad AI/thematic sleeve is **adding to an existing overweight**, not diversifying. ORCL correlation of **0.61** is the highest in the peer set — CRWV will not diversify a cloud/AI book; it will lever it.

**Factor exposure — SEVERELY ADVERSE.**
- **Beta 3.48x** (provider series suggests higher). A 20% SPY drawdown maps to roughly a 65–70% CRWV drawdown; the realized −65% peak-to-trough is exactly consistent with this. Adding it raises portfolio beta materially for zero expected return premium (Sharpe 0.12).
- **Volatility contribution is non-linear.** At 97.7% annualised vol, even a 1% position contributes ~1% of portfolio volatility on its own — equivalent to a 16% position in a 6%-vol asset. Token sizing does not make it harmless.
- **Hidden long-duration/credit factor.** This is the critical portfolio-level insight: CRWV's dominant risk is **real-rate and credit-spread duration**, not equity beta. It behaves like a high-yield bond with a call option attached. A portfolio already long duration (growth equity, long Treasuries, REITs, utilities-at-a-discount) becomes **doubly exposed to the same 2.78%-and-rising real-yield shock** — imperfectly revealed by the 0.49 SPY correlation.
- **Low R² (0.22)** means index shorts will not hedge it. Idiosyncratic, single-name news (financing headlines, covenant terms, customer renegotiation) dominates return variance. It is effectively unhedgeable within a diversified book.
- **Positive screen:** adequate liquidity (95–130M shares/day in the selloff) means an exit is executable — a genuine mitigant, and the reason a holder can still act.

**Verdict: this position would be the single largest source of tail risk in a diversified portfolio while contributing negatively to expected return.** It fails the fit test independently of the valuation test.
