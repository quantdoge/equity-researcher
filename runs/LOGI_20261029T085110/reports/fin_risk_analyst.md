### 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — Revenue −20%

FY2026 structural economics: gross margin 43.1%, total opex $1,305.7M (SG&A $983.8M = 20.2% of revenue; implied R&D ~$321.9M = 6.6%). **Degree of operating leverage = 43.1% / 16.2% = 2.66x** — a 1% revenue move drives a 2.66% swing in operating income. This is the single largest financial risk in the name.

| Scenario | Revenue | Gross margin | Opex | Op. income | Op. margin | Est. FCF | Dividend coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base FY2026** | $4,861.7M | 43.1% | $1,305.7M | $788.4M | 16.2% | $976M | 4.2x |
| **① −20%, no mitigation** | $3,889.3M | 43.1% | $1,305.7M | **$370.6M** (−53%) | 9.5% | ~$420M | 1.8x |
| **② −20%, 10% opex cut** | $3,889.3M | 43.1% | $1,175.1M | $501.2M (−36%) | 12.9% | ~$520M | 2.2x |
| **③ Severe: −30%, GM −380bp, opex −10%** | $3,403.2M | 39.0% | $1,175.1M | $152.2M (−81%) | 4.5% | ~$250M | 1.07x |
| **④ Tail: −40%, GM −500bp, opex −20%** | $2,917.0M | 38.0% | $1,044.6M | **$63.9M** | 2.2% | ~$160M | 0.7x (dividend at risk) |

**Operating break-even revenue: ~$3,030M — i.e. revenue can fall ~37.7% before operating income hits zero.**

**Reading the scenarios:** in the base −20% case, net cash actually *rises* (to ~$2.1B) because FCF stays positive and capex is only 1.3% of revenue. Dividends remain covered ~1.8x and are fully covered even in the −30% case. **The buyback ($537M) is the discretionary lever and would be suspended well before any covenant or liquidity issue** — with a 79% FCF payout, suspended buybacks alone free up ~$537M of annual liquidity. Only the −40% tail scenario puts the dividend at risk, and even there $1.75B cash covers ~7 years of the current dividend.

**This scenario is not hypothetical — it already happened.** Revenue fell from $5,481.1M (FY2022) to $4,298.5M (FY2024), **−21.6%**, and the company stayed profitable every single year, with operating margin bottoming at 10.1% (FY2023) and net income never dipping below $364.6M. FY2026 revenue ($4,861.7M) is **still 11.3% below the FY2022 peak**. The balance sheet emerged stronger: net cash rose from $1.44B to $1.66B through the downturn, and gross margin *expanded* 380bp from the FY2023 trough (38.2% → 43.1%) — evidence of pricing power and mix discipline under volume pressure.

**Valuation-based downside (additional lens):** EV = $12.45B; EV/EBITDA 13.7x, P/E 20.5x on $4.80 diluted EPS. A de-rating to 10x EV/EBITDA implies ~$9.08B EV → **~$74/share, −24%** — a more likely route to loss than any solvency event.

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **High operating leverage (DOL 2.66x) into a normalising demand cycle** — 37–41% realised volatility on a *net-cash, profitable* company is the tell. Peak-to-trough revenue decline of 22% is already in the record.
2. **Piotroski F-Score 6/9 with deteriorating components** — fails on leverage, gross-margin trend (43.3% → 43.1%), and asset turnover (1.287x → 1.256x). Fundamental momentum has flattened: FY2026's 16.2% operating margin is a post-2021 high, and mean-reversion risk is real.
3. **Persistent under-investment vs depreciation** — capex/D&A has run 0.66x → 0.70x → 0.79x over three years; capex is only 1.3% of revenue. For a hardware/innovation business, running below depreciation warrants monitoring for future product-competitiveness risk.
4. **79% of FCF distributed** ($770.4M of $975.6M). Manageable only because buybacks are discretionary — but it leaves no organic buffer if FCF halves.
5. **SBC $112.4M = 15.7% of net income** (2.3% of revenue); net dilution is masked by ~3% annual buyback retirement (diluted shares 152.8M → 148.2M).
6. **Beta metric divergence (0.65 published vs 1.23 realised)** — do not size this as a defensive holding; the low published beta understates tail exposure.
7. **Thin equity risk premium** — 205bp over the 4.65% 2Y Treasury for a 40%-vol equity.
