### 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration.** LOGI is Consumer Peripherals / Computer Hardware. It is a **~$14.6B mid-cap in a $40B+ niche** — the position is unlikely to breach a sector cap on its own, but it must be aggregated with any existing PC-OEM or hardware exposure.

**Correlation (252-day, computed):**

| Pair | ρ | Read |
|---|---|---|
| LOGI–HPQ | **0.57** | Highest — the true factor twin. Any HPQ/DELL/PC-OEM holding must be netted against this |
| LOGI–DELL | 0.40 | Moderate — AI-server mix dilutes the link |
| LOGI–SPY | **0.39** | Genuinely low market correlation |
| LOGI–CRSR | 0.35 | Lower than expected; do not treat as a substitute |
| LOGI–AAPL | 0.24 | Effectively independent |

**Verdict on diversification: favourable.** The 0.39 correlation to SPY and R² of 0.15 mean **~85% of LOGI's variance is idiosyncratic** — it is a genuine diversifier at the index level. The penalty is that idiosyncratic risk is *undiversifiable within the position*, which is precisely why the size stays at 1.5–3%.

**Factor exposure impact:**
- **Adds:** Value (top-quintile), Quality (ROIC 34%, best accruals in peer set), Income (1.7% div).
- **Subtracts:** Momentum (12-1 = −0.3%, bottom-quintile) and Alpha (−3.0% annualised).
- **Net:** if the portfolio is already quality-tilted and momentum-light, LOGI adds *to* an existing tilt rather than diversifying it. **Check for >35% aggregate quality-factor loading before adding.** Conversely, if the book is momentum-heavy, LOGI is a legitimate contrarian offset.
- **Beta:** adds realised beta 1.23 — do not book it against a defensive sleeve.

**Currency.** ~60% of revenue ex-USD, USD reporting, CHF-denominated dividend. Macro flags USD +2.9% since 9 Sep as a **fresh translation headwind** — LOGI adds unhedged non-USD revenue exposure to the book.

**Macro regime fit.** In a hiking, bear-steepening, above-4%-CPI regime, LOGI's net-cash balance sheet is genuinely rate-insensitive (no refinancing wall, earns interest income on $1.75B) — that is a real relative advantage. But its ~20x multiple is long-duration and competes with a 4.65% 2Y. **Neutral regime fit: better than leveraged peers, worse than short-duration value.**

### 6. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss (price): $81.50** — just below the $82.00 intraday low of 28 Jan 2026 and roughly the 10x EV/EBITDA de-rating level (~$74) plus a buffer. At 1.5% initial size this is a **−17% stop = ~26bp of portfolio loss** — acceptable for a name with a 56% annual trading range. **Widen the stop only if tranches 2–3 are filled at lower prices.**

**Thesis invalidation — fundamental (these override the price stop):**
1. **Q3 FY27 (26 Jan 2027) gross margin below 41%** — i.e., below the bottom of the company's own guide. This converts a transitory shock into structural margin erosion.
2. **Ex-refund gross margin below 43% for two consecutive quarters.** The $61M refund inflated H1 FY27 GM to 47.2%; the through-cycle structure is 43–44%. Below 43% the "pricing power" moat claim — the entire bull case — fails.
3. **Buyback suspension.** At 79% FCF payout, $537M of annual buyback is the discretionary liquidity lever. Suspension signals management sees FCF stress the models don't.
4. **Component supplier not restored to full production by Q4 FY27.** A second outage converts a one-off into a structural supply-chain defect.
5. **Class action survives a motion to compel arbitration** *and* is followed by a securities action alleging divergent investor/consumer disclosure — this is the Non-Fin Analyst's [HIGH] escalation and the one path to a genuine re-rating lower.
6. **Revenue below $1.32B in Q3** (bottom of the $1.36–1.43B guide) — would confirm the Alt Data demand fade is real, not seasonal.

**Trim trigger: $133–137.** That is ~90% of the Value Researcher's optimistic IV ($137) and ~20% above the base midpoint ($113). At that level the position is priced for the bull case and no longer compensates for a 41%-vol, cyclical hardware model. **Trim one-third.**

**Monitoring calendar:** N.D. Cal. docket (arbitration motion) · CBP refund reconciliation vs. $61M · monthly EMEA sell-through (~40% of revenue, currently −6%) · Google Trends brand/gaming slope post-holiday · Q3 FY27 on 26 Jan 2027.
