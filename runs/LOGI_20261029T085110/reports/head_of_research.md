### Investment Memo: LOGI — 29 October 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $110 (+12% from current $98.27)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Logitech is a fortress-balance-sheet franchise (net cash $1.66B, ~12% of market cap; ROIC 34%; cash conversion 145%) trading ~13% below its intrinsic-value midpoint after a −12.1% post-earnings gap-down. But it is entering a genuine margin trough — memory-cost inflation, a $70–80M single-supplier outage, and an EMEA demand break drive a Q3 FY27 guide of −4% revenue at midpoint and 41–42% gross margin — into a hostile macro (Fed hiking, 10Y ~5%, firming USD). Quality argues against selling; cyclical and event risk argues against buying size before the 26 Jan 2027 print. Hold existing positions; new money initiates only a starter tranche with a defined scale-in ladder.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Neutral sector in a cyclical trough. The memory supercycle (DRAM +58–63% QoQ) is inflating PC ASPs ~17% and collapsing 2026 PC units >11%, transmitting to peripherals with a 1–2 quarter lag. Macro "mildly unfavourable": tariff relief fading while memory/logistics inflation escalates; USD strength flips FX from tailwind to headwind. LOGI is the sector's quality share-gainer — +10pp gross and +9pp operating margin vs peer median, 11 straight growth quarters into a contracting PC market.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: MEDIUM-HIGH. The $61M tariff-refund class action (N.D. Cal., Aug 2026) seeks disgorgement after prices were raised on 51% of the portfolio; arbitration is the likely defence. EMEA (~40% of revenue) is contracting (−6% YoY) on Middle East conflict. China-centric contract manufacturing plus a demonstrated single-supplier failure is the structural concentration risk.
- **Value**: IV $100–125, midpoint ~$113. At $98.27 the stock sits at/below the conservative end — improved entry vs the $105 underwritten, but still short of the $88–92 margin-of-safety zone. 34.1% ROIC vs ~9% WACC; 14.3x EV/FCF.
- **Growth**: Not a compounder. Rule of 40 = 26.8; 3-yr revenue CAGR 2.3%; TAM growing mid-single digits; growth decelerated to +2% cc with Q3 guided negative. A GARP/quality holding at ~16x FY27E non-GAAP EPS vs ~25x 10-yr average.
- **Quantitative Signals**: BUY (moderate). Top-quintile value + quality within hardware comps, best-in-group accruals — but bottom-quintile momentum (12-1 = −0.3%), negative alpha (−3.0%), and beta instability (1Y 1.23 vs 5Y 0.65). "Cheap and good, but out of favour."
- **Financial Risk**: LOW financial / moderate-high market risk. Zero funded debt, Altman Z 4.50, FCF $976M (20.1% margin); survives a −38% revenue decline before EBIT reaches zero. The real exposure is operating leverage (2.66x): a de-rating to 10x EV/EBITDA implies ~$74 (−24%) — that, not solvency, is the loss path.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: MODERATE, negative trajectory. Two concurrent controversies: the tariff "double-dip" narrative (kept the $61M refund after price hikes — damaging for a premium consumer brand) and a realised Clop/Oracle EBS breach (~1.8TB exfiltrated). Governance adequate: independent chair, but CEO ownership <1x pay.
- **ESG**: NEUTRAL. Environmental programme is genuinely moat-like (81% recycled plastic, 100% product carbon footprints — a B2B/CSRD sales-qualification advantage). Governance is the weak link: the 2024 founder–board war, a court-enjoined AGM, and leadership churn. No forced-labour or environmental enforcement found.
- **Alternative Data**: BEARISH (mild). Brand search −18% YoY, "Logitech G" −24% YoY, both at 12-month lows; revenue beats compressed to <1%; EPS beats carried by margin. Consistent with flat-to-negative Q3 revenue; no signal supporting upside into January.
- **Short / Contrarian**: Short thesis rejected. Beneish −2.90, accruals −8.35%, receivables and inventory days *falling*, share count shrinking. The best bear nugget — the one-time $61M refund flattering H1 margins (Q1 GM 49.5%) — supports gradual multiple compression, not collapse. Short interest ~7.9% of float is already elevated and falling.

**Bull Case**
- Fortress quality at a discount: net cash ~12% of cap, 34% ROIC, 137% FCF conversion, ~4.7% total shareholder yield, ~16x FY27E earnings.
- Transitory shock with a snap-back setup: supplier at full production by ~year-end, the $70–80M Q3 hit resolves by Q4 FY27, and memory pricing eases from 2028 as the AI-PC refresh restores PC units — GM re-expansion toward 45%+.
- Share capture and premiumisation: gaming +7%, video collaboration +12%, GM +150bps YoY in Q2 — gaining share in a shrinking market with pricing power peers (CRSR at 28.9% GM) cannot replicate.

**Bear Case**
- Margin scissors: GM 49.5% (Q1) → 44.9% (Q2) → 41–42% guided (Q3); memory locked only into H1 FY27 leaves the FY28 headwind unhedged, and Q1's print was flattered by the refund.
- Demand fade in leading indicators: search interest at 12-month lows, EMEA −6%, Q3 revenue guided −4% at midpoint, consensus EPS likely $0.15–0.25 too high into January.
- Event/litigation cluster — class action, breach, contested governance — against a stock that already round-tripped $127 → $98 and gaps double digits around earnings.

**Key Risks**
1. Q3 FY27 GM below 41% (bottom of guide), or ex-refund GM <43% for two consecutive quarters — breaks the pricing-power thesis.
2. Second component-supplier outage; memory costs persisting unhedged into FY28.
3. Class action survives a motion to compel arbitration, with follow-on securities litigation on divergent investor/consumer messaging.
4. Macro bear case (~25% probability): December hike + sticky CPI → 10Y 5.4–5.5%, USD +3–4% → drawdown toward the $70s.
5. Operating leverage 2.66x into a normalising PC cycle: −20% revenue ⇒ ~−53% operating income.

**Portfolio Construction Note**
Initiate partial: **1.5% of portfolio now** at $98.27; add +0.75% at $88–92; final +0.75% at $80–85 only if Q3 confirms GM ≥43% and supplier resolution (3.0% cap). Stop-loss $81.50 (~26bp portfolio loss at initial size); trim one-third at $133–137. Size on realised beta 1.23, not the published 0.65 — this is not a defensive holding. LOGI–SPY correlation of 0.39 makes it a genuine index diversifier, but net the 0.57 HPQ correlation against any existing PC-OEM exposure and check aggregate quality-factor loading before funding. Do not chase above $105 pre-earnings.
