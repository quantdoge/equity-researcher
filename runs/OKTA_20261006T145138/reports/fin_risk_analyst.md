# OKTA, INC. (OKTA) — Financial Risk Assessment
*Identity & Access Management (Software – Infrastructure) | Market cap ≈ $36.3B | Price $218.50*

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment: **Investment Grade (low fundamental credit risk; unrated by agencies)**

Okta has **deleveraged aggressively** and now sits in a **net cash position**. Fiscal year 2026 (ended 31-Jan-2026) marked the inflection: first positive operating income (+$153M), first meaningful positive GAAP net income (+$235M), and a 6-point year-over-year Piotroski improvement to **8/9 (Strong)**. Total debt fell from $2.37B (FY2023) to **$422M** — almost entirely a $350M short-term convertible tranche — against **$2.55B cash**, i.e. **net cash of ~$2.1B** and net debt/EBITDA of **-6.0x**. Interest coverage is **~38–65x** (EBIT $259M vs. interest expense of only $4M). The balance sheet is essentially ungeared: D/E 0.06x, D/A 0.04x.

The headline Altman Z-Score of **1.68 ("distress") is a methodological artifact and should be discounted**: the tool substitutes book equity for market value (x4), and Okta's book metrics are crushed by $5.6B of goodwill/intangibles and a $2.6B accumulated deficit. Substituting market cap (~$36B vs. liabilities of $2.7B) puts x4 at ~13x and the market-value-adjusted Z ~**8**, firmly in the safe zone. This is a balance-sheet score, not a solvency signal.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

**Balance-sheet/credit risk is genuinely LOW** (net cash, 38x+ coverage, Piotroski 8, zero refinancing exposure), and **liquidity risk is LOW-to-MODERATE** (current ratio 1.36 is adequate but not cushy; the goodwill overhang is the real balance-sheet wildcard). **Market risk is HIGH** (57% vol, beta >1, -50% drawdown, and a 2.3% FCF yield carrying heavy growth expectations). The blended rating is **Moderate** — the fundamentals have decisively to the good, but the equity carries outsized drawdown and dilution risk that a prolonged downturn would expose.

4. **Decelerating growth at a full valuation**: +12% revenue growth with only 2.3% FCF yield leaves no valuation cushion.
5. **Working capital thinness**: current ratio of 1.36 is the weak spot on an otherwise fortified balance sheet — watch receivables growth ($687M) relative to cash conversion.
