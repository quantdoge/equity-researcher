## Investment Thesis

Okta is a genuinely high-quality identity-infrastructure franchise — narrow but durable moat, 77.4% gross margin, first full year of GAAP profitability (FY26 NI +$235M), ~$905M reported FCF, ~$2.1B net cash — at a **speculative price**. At $218.50 (~12x FY27E sales, ~43x FCF, ~167x trailing P/E, +273% vs peer median P/E), the market is underwriting a decade of ~20%+ FCF compounding against 10–11% revenue growth, in a rising-rate regime (10Y @ 5.28%, Fed re-hiking) at the top of a +150% momentum re-rating (Dec-25 → now) that has already consumed the upside. **Price is the thesis, and at $218.50 there is no thesis to buy.**

## Bull Case
- **Quality inflection is real**: 77.4% gross margin (tops security peers), first GAAP profit, FCF near-doubled to $905M, Piotroski 8/9, ~$2.1B net cash — OEM-like economics with zero solvency risk.
- **AI-agent identity is genuine TAM expansion** — non-human/machine identity with no entrenched incumbent; ~30% of bookings at +40% ACV, 7/7 beats, guidance raised twice.
- **Momentum and revisions are one-sided bullish**: +34.9% 12–1mo momentum, +9.4% EPS upgrades, PT cluster $230–245, Sharpe 1.88/Sortino 3.90; 0.78 correlation with CRWD rides a buoyant cyber complex.

## Bear Case
- **Valuation offers no margin of safety**: all conservative DCFs ($104–145) sit far below market; a 10% growth stumble compresses 40x FCF toward 25–30x (25–40% drawdown) with no fundamental break.
- **The re-rating ran *against* the fundamentals**: 6 quarters of deceleration (10.6% latest), slowest growth in the security pack, Microsoft Entra bundling caps pricing/share; Okta gains no share while PANW/CRWD consolidate.
- **The trust moat is demonstrably breached, serially** — and the "profitability" justifying the multiple partly rests on a ~$550M SBC timing artifact.

## Key Risks
1. **Rate regime** — 10Y >5.5% / further hikes → 25–35% drawdown even without company-specific news; OKTA is a rate-beta proxy for AI-security.
2. **Multiple compression** — momentum unwind in a 74%-vol, 167x-P/E name after a 3.4x move; factor crowding (0.78 CRWD / 0.70 PANW) unwinds the whole basket at once.
3. **Cyber-incident recurrence** — one more high-profile breach with disclosure deficiency breaches the bull case outright.
