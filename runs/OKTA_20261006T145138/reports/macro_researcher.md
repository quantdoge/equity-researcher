# Macroeconomic Assessment — Okta, Inc. (OKTA)

*Data as of early October 2026. Sources: FRED, FMP, J.P. Morgan/enterprise commentary.*

## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: RISK-OFF for long-duration growth equities — with a narrow, company-specific exception.** The macro regime has turned decisively less friendly in the last two quarters: the Federal Reserve **resumed hiking** (+25bp in September 2026 to a 3.75–4.00% target range — the first hike since 2023) after cutting to ~3.63% in spring 2026, because headline inflation re-accelerated to ~3.0–3.1%. Critically, the **10-year Treasury has blown out to 5.28%** (Oct 2, 2026) from ~4.3% in April, driven primarily by term premium, fiscal supply and inflation risk — not by growth strength. The curve is positively sloped (+34bp 2s10s, no inversion), indicating a bear-steepening, "higher-for-longer plus term premium" regime rather than a recession signal. Real GDP growth (~2.2% YoY) is decelerating from ~2.5% toward trend, unemployment has drifted up to 4.2%, and the broad dollar has strengthened to ~121.4 — an active headwind to international revenue. This is a hostile backdrop for a ~11x-revenue, long-duration software multiple; OKTA's resilience rests almost entirely on company-specific drivers (security budgets staying defensive, AI-identity demand) rather than the macro tailwind.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL** (Favourable fundamentals, unfavourable rates)

The macro tape is *unfavourable for the asset class* (Fed hiking again, 10Y >5%, strong dollar, decelerating GDP) — this is the kind of regime that historically compresses software multiples. OKTA has decoupled so far in 2026 (+~130% off late-2025 lows) on company-specific identity-security/AI momentum, culminating in a Q2 CY2026 beat (+10.6% YoY revenue to $805M, FY guidance 10–11% growth). But that decoupling is precisely what makes the stock macro-vulnerable: its multiple embeds a market that is paying for the AI-identity story and treating security as recession-resilient. Net: macro is a headwind; company idiosyncratics are the offset — hence Neutral, with the balance of near-term risk tilted lower **only if** the bond market continues to deteriorate.

## 5. Scenario That Could Materially Surprise — **BEAR (higher probability, higher stakes)**

**"Term-premium tail-spin":** Re-accelerating headline inflation (energy-driven ~3%) forces additional Fed hikes into 2027 even as growth slows toward 1.5% — a stagflation-lite mix. The 10Y breaks decisively above 5.5–6% on fiscal-supply concerns; the positively-sloped curve steepens further. Long-duration equity multiples compress broadly, and OKTA's ~11x-sales valuation resets despite intact fundamentals — a 25–35% drawdown is plausible *even without any negative company-specific news*, because the stock's 2026 performance has made it a rate-beta proxy for the AI-security theme.

**Bull flip-side:** If the inflation impulse proves transitory (base effects roll off by mid-2027), the Fed pivots back to cutting, the 10Y falls through 4.5%, and enterprise AI-identity spend (30% of bookings, +40% ACV) keeps compounding — OKTA re-rates further with duration tailwind plus fundamentals. The asymmetry favors watching the 10Y and the September-2026 hike's follow-through as the swing factors.
