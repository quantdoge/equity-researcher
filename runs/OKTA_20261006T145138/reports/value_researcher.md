# OKTA — Equity Value Research Memo
**Okta, Inc. (NASDAQ: OKTA) | Software — Identity & Access Management | Price: $218.54 | Mkt Cap: ~$39.2B**

## 1. Business Quality Assessment — Moat: **Narrow**

Okta is the category leader in workforce identity (SSO/MFA) and, via Auth0, a top-3 player in customer identity (CIAM). The moat is real but not fortress-grade.

- **Switching costs (strong):** Identity is the control plane of an enterprise application stack. Re-platforming SSO/MFA touches every app, user, and policy; enterprises do it once in a decade. This drives the sticky revenue base and supports pricing.
- **Integration/ecosystem moat:** Thousands of pre-integrated applications and deep protocol/standards expertise take years to replicate; this is the strongest durable advantage outside the Big Three clouds.
- **Trust/brand in security:** Post-breach (2022 LAPSUS$, Oct 2023 support-system intrusion), revenue still compounded ~15-20%, evidencing stickiness — but the breaches also proved the moat did **not** extend to invulnerability.
- **Pricing power is constrained:** Microsoft **Entra ID is bundled free with E5 licensing** — the single largest long-term threat to incremental share and pricing. It caps the moat at "narrow."

**Net assessment:** Narrow, *not* wide. The moat has held over the past five years (revenue CAGR ~31%, NRR consistently >105%) but growth has decelerated from 56% (FY22) to ~11% guided for FY27 — a slowing in the high end of the S-curve, not a widening of advantage. ROIC of ~2% (FY26) versus a ~9-10% WACC confirms this is not yet a moat that earns its cost of capital — the hallmark of wide-moat businesses is absent so far.

## 3. Valuation Analysis — **Current price ~50–110% above conservative intrinsic value**

**The numbers at $218.54:** P/E 167x (FY26 GAAP EPS $1.31; ~100x+ on FY27E); EV/EBITDA 104x; P/FCF 43x on SBC-inflated FCF (≈110x on $360M economic FCF); FCF yield 2.3%; ~12x FY27E revenue; P/E at a **+273% premium** to the 44.7x peer median.

**Intrinsic value (DCF, WACC 10%, terminal growth 3%):**
- *Conservative* (normalized FCF ~$600M, 15% → 10% fade): **~$104/share**
- *Generous* (normalized FCF ~$700M, 18% → 12% fade, margin expansion to ~20% op margin): **~$145/share**
- The tool's own DCF (30% FCF growth for 10 years) implies $110B — an assumption set no conservative investor can justify against 10-11% revenue growth.

To support $218, one must underwrite ~20-25% FCF compounding for a decade — i.e., pre-paying for a ServiceNow-like margin trajectory (~20%+ operating margins, 18-20x FCF) on a company growing revenue 10%. That leaves **zero margin of safety** and embeds flawless execution for five-plus years. My required 20-30% discount to intrinsic value is not merely absent — the stock trades at a large premium to my generous case.

## 4. Management Quality Assessment — **Positive, with caveats (6/10)**

- **CEO Todd McKinnon** (co-founder) has a long, credible owner-operator track record; management navigated 2023-25 cost discipline, reached GAAP profitability in FY26, and initiated a modest buyback ($73M FY26). Guidance discipline has improved.
- **Blemishes:** Auth0 at $6.5B (~32x revenue) remains a value-destructive acquisition; SBC at ~19% of revenue is an entrenched dilution machine; the 2022-23 security breaches were operational/governance failures in a trust-critical business. No dividend, insiders own a meaningful but minority stake (founder-led, not founder-controlled).

## 5. Investment Verdict — **SELL**

A genuinely good business (narrow moat, 77% gross margin, net cash, emerging profitability, sticky install base) at a **speculative price**. Every conservative estimate of intrinsic value ($104-$145) sits far below the market price ($218.5), and the market's implied assumption — 10 years of 20%+ FCF growth against 10-11% revenue growth — is not something a value investor can underwrite. There is no margin of safety; the risk/reward is asymmetrically negative. For disciplined capital, this is a **Sell** (or at minimum do-not-initiate; trim on strength if held). Revisit on a 40-50% pullback toward the $120-140 zone.

## 6. Key Risks to the Value Thesis

1. **Microsoft Entra bundling pressure** — free identity included in E5 can compress pricing and slow new logo/expansion growth.
2. **Growth deceleration** — revenue trajectory (56% → 11%) shows a reversion trend; if FY27 FY28 land <10%, the 12x sales multiple compresses violently.
3. **Valuation/multiple risk** — the 2026 re-rating (~$86 → $218, +150% in 10 months) is momentum-driven; a growth scare or rate move could unwind half of it quickly.
4. **Security breach recurrence** — trust is the product; a third major incident undermines the brand and lengthens sales cycles.
5. **SBC dilution** — ~9% annual share-count growth quietly converts reported FCF gains into far lower per-share value.
6. **AI/CIAM disruption** — passwordless, passkeys, and platform-native identity (hyperscaler AI-agent-level authentication) could commoditize segments of the identity stack faster than Okta monetizes its "agent identity" narrative.
