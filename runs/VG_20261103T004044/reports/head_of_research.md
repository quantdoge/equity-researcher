# Investment Memo: VG — 3 November 2026

**Venture Global, Inc. (NYSE: VG) · LNG liquefaction & export · Price $12.87 · Mkt cap ~$32.2bn · Net debt ~$39.6bn · EV ~$71.8bn**

**Verdict**: HOLD — with a **speculative, starter-size BUY (≤1%)** permitted for risk-tolerant mandates | Conviction: **Medium** in the call, **Low-Medium** in any directional view
**Price Target**: **$16** (~+24% from $12.87), the probability-weighted value ($17) haircut for unquantified BP damages and the post-COD margin reset. Scenario range: bear **$7–8**, severe **$5–7**, bull **$20+**
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

---

**Investment Thesis**

VG is a genuinely elite operating asset — best-in-class margins (49% gross / 37% operating), capacity priced below replacement cost (~$1,170/t vs $1,200–2,000/t), ~30% EBITDA CAGR through 2029, 91% of 2026 volumes contracted — wrapped in a high-risk shell: 5.4x net leverage and rising, four consecutive years of negative FCF, roughly one quarter of self-funded cash runway, 97.5% founder voting control, near-universal insider selling, and unresolved arbitration with up to ~$3.7bn+ of BP damages outstanding. The equity is a long-duration, binary-outcome instrument — cheap at ~4.5x 2028E EBITDA but not cheap enough to clear a margin-of-safety bar (Value requires <$10). Expected value is positive (+25–32% to prob-weighted fair value) but the left tail is permanent capital loss, so **sizing and staging is the entire decision**, not the direction.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Operating backdrop is the best in company history — Henry Hub $2.76 feedstock vs JKM/TTF ~$33 (a ~$30 spread), decade-low EU storage. But VG monetises mainly a **fixed fee ($6.79/MMBtu Q3)**, so the spread is second-order; the 5.13% 10Y / re-hiking Fed hits the DCF directly (financing macro scored 2/10). Sector: Neutral — the 2026 window is genuinely tight but the 2027–29 capacity wave converges prices toward US marginal cost, exactly where VG's edge lives.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Overall **Medium; legal sub-rating HIGH**. Arbitration tally: 3 losses (BP, Galp, Tauron*), 2 wins (Shell, Repsol), 1 settled (Edison), **Orlen pending — verdict by YE2026**. BP quantum (≥$3.7bn sought) is the largest single unquantified exposure. Offset: CP2's FERC approval upheld (D.C. Circuit, Aug 2026). EU methane rules from Jan 2027 touch the core European market. (*Tauron single-source, unconfirmed.)
- **Value**: **HOLD; buy only <$10**. Owner earnings negative four straight years (−$6.8bn FY25, ~−$6.5bn H1'26); 5.4x ND/EBITDA with $12bn equity under $42.8bn debt. Prob-weighted intrinsic ≈ **$17** — a ~25% discount, not a fat pitch. Management: high competence, low trustworthiness.
- **Growth**: **BUY (high-variance)**. EBITDA $5.8bn → ~$19bn (2029E); trades below replacement cost; 2.5:1 upside/downside. Caveats self-acknowledged: B-grade growth quality, no FCF until 2028, 2030 EPS consensus already rolling over — a volume compounder, not a margin compounder.
- **Quantitative**: **Neutral** (risk-adjusted: avoid). Quality is real (Piotroski 7/9, negative accruals, ROIC improving) but vol is 77% — worst in peer set — momentum just reversed (−13% on 2.5x ADV), and every risk statistic rests on ~450 trading days. Beta is unusable (sign-flips; R² 1.5%).
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH**. BB-/negative outlook; debt +$7.9bn in H1'26 alone; capitalised interest masks true coverage; cash ≈ one quarter of burn → structurally dependent on open project-finance markets for 12–18 months; Altman Z ~1.0. Not Critical: 3.5x coverage, $7.3bn LTM EBITDA, no near-term maturity cliff.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH**. Founders chair the board and sit on comp/nominating committees; ~$109M net insider selling in 90 days with the stock now below every sale price; the arbitration losses stem from a board that never constrained the decision-makers. No SEC actions; IPO class action dismissed (a genuine positive).
- **ESG**: **Laggard / Negative**. Governance 3/10 (controlled-company exclusions cut the institutional buyer base); environmental exposure rising with the asset base; EU methane-intensity cap (2030) gates the highest-value market. Best-in-class safety (TRIR 0.17) is the one clear credit.
- **Alternative Data**: **Bullish for the 10 Nov print only** — the October spread blowout plus three straight beats and raised guidance skew to a beat, but the spread widening occurred in Q4 and the Oct 31 Plaquemines COD de-rates 2027 unit margins: "beat-and-fade" risk profile.
- **Short / Contrarian**: **No edge — do not short**. The fraud narrative is refuted by the cash flows (243% OCF conversion, negative accruals, two arbitration wins); the real bear case is margin erosion + D&A normalisation (~$0.30/sh) + $23.7bn committed capex — and with ~84% of float already short (unverified), that view is fully owned.

**Bull Case**
- Asset base trades below replacement cost at ~4.5x 2028E EBITDA with a fully financed 29 MTPA CP2; base-case 2029 math supports **$20+** (Morgan Stanley $22, Wells Fargo $21).
- Contracting momentum is sector-best (EnBW, TotalEnergies, Vitol, China Gas 2030, QatarEnergy talks) — 20-year SPAs converting spot windfalls into bankable cash flow.
- Record JKM/TTF spreads and EU scarcity could let VG contract CP2/CP3 at premium fees; 84% short interest is squeeze fuel on any good Orlen/BP news (the Dec-25→Mar-26 +150% run is the template).

**Bear Case**
- Balance sheet: 5.4x net leverage and rising, negative FCF into 2028, one quarter of runway, $23.7bn committed capex — total dependence on capital-markets access; a severe downside scenario (revenue −35% + arbitration award + wider spreads) puts coverage below 1x.
- Litigation: two tribunals have already found breach on the same theory; BP quantum ≥$2.5–3bn plus an Orlen loss would impair equity and potentially force a dilutive raise.
- Structural margin reset: gross margin 78.7% → 49.3% and falling as fixed-fee volumes replace spot; consensus itself models −31% EPS in 2027 and a 2029 peak; the 2027–29 LNG supply wall caps fee upside on unbuilt capacity.

**Key Risks**
1. **BP quantum award and Orlen ruling (YE2026)** — binary, correlated, and capable of moving the stock 25%+ either way.
2. **Refinancing/access risk** — 364-day revolver reliance; a high-yield window closure or covenant test is the existential pathway.
3. **Macro double-hit**: mild winter collapses JKM toward $14–16 just as the Fed hikes — numerator and denominator fall together (Macro's explicit bear: −25–35%).
4. **CP2 execution/cost inflation** in a 3.7% CPI, tariff-laden environment; Clean Air Act permit challenge unresolved.
5. **Governance/insider signal** — selling by CFO, GC, COO into strength; no independent check on capital allocation (dividend paid from borrowed cash).
6. **EU methane regulation** friction on European offtake from Jan 2027.
