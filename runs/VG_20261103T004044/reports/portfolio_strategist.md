## 2. Consensus Assessment: **MEDIUM — split on valuation, unanimous on risk**

**High agreement (4+ agents):**
- **Operating quality is real.** Growth, Sector, Quant, Short, Alt Data all concede the asset base, volumes and construction execution are genuinely elite (49% GM, 36% op margin, 243% OCF conversion, TRIR 0.17).
- **The balance sheet is the binding constraint.** Fin Risk, Value, Non-Fin, Quant and Short independently flag 5.4–5.7x net leverage, negative FCF, and near-total dependence on continuous project-finance access.
- **The litigation overhang is a permanent, not one-off, discount.** Geo Legal, Non-Fin, ESG, Value and Short all land on the same 3-losses / 2-wins / 1-pending arbitration tally, with BP quantum the largest unquantified item.
- **Insider behaviour is a red flag.** Non-Fin (~$109M sold/90d, zero bought) and Short (~$222M/24mo) agree independently.

**Genuine disagreement (the crux):**
- **Valuation.** Growth sees 4.5x 2028E EBITDA and below-replacement-cost capacity → Buy. Value sees a ~25% discount to a *wide, assumption-heavy* range, negative owner earnings, and requires **< $10** before buying. Quant sits between at Neutral. This is not resolvable with current data — it depends on whether 2028–29 EBITDA materialises and at what fee base.
- **Near-term vs structural.** Alt Data is bullish *for the Q3 print*; Macro and the 2 Nov tape (−13% on 34.6M shares) say the market is already discounting the post-COD margin reset. Both are right about different time horizons.

**Conflict resolution (per framework): risk flags outrank bullish views.** Fin Risk and Non-Fin Risk both rate HIGH, and Geo Legal's legal sub-rating is HIGH. Under the rule that fraud/severity flags take precedence over value/growth enthusiasm, the Growth Buy cannot be sized as a high-conviction position. It survives only as a **small, optionality-sized** holding — which is Growth's own conclusion ("own it, size it modestly").

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.0% of portfolio (starter), 2.0% hard maximum**

**Rationale — fractional Kelly:**
- Probability-weighted value ≈ **$17** (Value: 40% base $24 / 40% bear $13 / 20% severe $5–7) vs $12.87 = **~+32% expected edge**.
- Downside cases are **−38% (Growth bear ~$8)** to **−55% (Value severe)**; upside **+55% (Growth base $20)** to **+265% (bull)**.
- Kelly with this payoff skew ≈ 15–20% of the *risk* capital allocated to VG, but volatility is **77%** and outcomes are **binary litigation events**, so applying a 1/6 to 1/10 fractional-Kelly haircut for estimation error and event risk yields **~1–2% of portfolio**.
- The 5.4x net leverage and "one quarter of cash runway" means the severe case is a **permanent capital loss**, not a drawdown — this argues for the low end.

**Tiering:** Start **1.0%** now. Do not exceed **2.0%** total under any circumstance, because VG already stacks ~0.66 correlation to energy and consumes disproportionate portfolio volatility (see §4).

**What would justify moving to 2.0%:** Orlen resolved favourably + BP quantum contained < $1.5bn + first evidence of FCF inflection (both Value and Growth name these as gating conditions).
**What would justify Pass instead:** an adverse BP quantum award or a failed CP2 financing before entry — in which case do not initiate.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Hard stop-loss:** **$9.50** (−26% from entry). Wider than typical because 77% vol makes tight stops self-defeating; but a close below $9.50 breaks the "cheap optionality" premise and signals the market is pricing the severe case. Reassess (do not average down) at **$10.00**.

**Thesis invalidation (exit regardless of price):**
1. **BP quantum award ≥ $2.5–3.0bn**, or a new arbitration filing by a major counterparty.
2. **Orlen loss** combined with any additional litigation escalation.
3. **Net debt/EBITDA > 7.0x** or a covenant breach / failed refinancing of CP2.
4. **CP2 cost overrun or schedule slip beyond 2029**, or Plaquemines expansion FID deferred.
5. **2027 guidance showing fixed liquefaction fees below ~$5.00/MMBtu** (the fee base eroding, not just spot volumes).
6. **FCF still materially negative into 2028**, or the dividend maintained while borrowing (governance tripwire per Short/Value).
7. **Macro bear trigger:** JKM/TTF collapsing toward **$14–16 by March 2027** on a mild winter (Macro's explicit bear), which removes the CP2/CP3 premium-fee contracting window.

**Trim triggers:**
- Trim **one-third** at **$15.50–16.00** (~90% of Value's prob-weighted $17 and near consensus PT ~$16).
- Trim a further **one-third** at **$18–20** (Growth's base case, Morgan Stanley/Goldman targets).
- Trail the remainder; do not re-add above $15.

**Gating conditions before any add:** Value's and Growth's shared checklist — evidence of FCF inflection, quantified/contained arbitration, net leverage trending down, *or* genuine insider buying (Non-Fin requires this explicitly).

## 7. Final Portfolio Action: **INITIATE — 1.0% starter position, staged, 2.0% hard cap**

**The synthesis in one paragraph:** VG is a genuinely elite operating asset (best-in-class margins, below-replacement-cost capacity, 30% EBITDA CAGR, 91% of 2026 volumes contracted) wrapped in a HIGH financial-risk and HIGH non-financial-risk shell (5.4x net leverage, negative FCF, one quarter of runway, adverse BP/Galp rulings, 97.5% founder control, near-universal insider selling) at a price that is cheap on forward EBITDA but not cheap enough for the value discipline to underwrite with confidence. That blend does not support a core position — it supports a **small, catalyst-gated, option-like allocation**. One percent of portfolio today, add only on confirmation (10 Nov print, Orlen resolution, FCF inflection), never above 2%, hard stop $9.50, trim into $15.50–20.

**Do not:** short it (84% float already short — no edge, severe squeeze risk), size it as a defensive or low-beta diversifier (the 0.22 beta is meaningless), or add it to an already-overweight energy book.
