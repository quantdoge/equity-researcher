### 8. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM**

**Why not High:** (1) Commodity spread is macro, not company-specific — it's a proxy for realized pricing, not a measurement of it; (2) ~91% contracted/hedged volumes may mute pass-through; (3) news sentiment model is demonstrably broken (0 negatives on a −13% day) and 20% contaminated; (4) social sentiment unavailable; (5) patent data failed; (6) job posting and web traffic feeds are unstructured search proxies with no counts or historical baselines.

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly and normalized (Sept 2026 has a suspicious 0 reading — a data artifact, not real). SimilarWeb figures are relative-only. Commodity prices are spot (Oct 27–30), while the Q3 print covers Jul–Sep — *the largest spread widening occurred in October, i.e., in Q4, not Q3*. That timing mismatch is the single biggest risk to this call: if October's spread blowout is the real story, it shows up in **Q4 results and in Nov 10 guidance language**, not in the Q3 income statement. Alt data is leading, not definitive.
