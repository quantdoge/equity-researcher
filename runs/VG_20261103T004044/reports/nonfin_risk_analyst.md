## VG — Venture Global, Inc. | Non-Financial Risk Assessment
*Data as of early November 2026. Price $12.87; market cap ~$32.2B; IPO 24-Jan-2025 at $25 (≈–49% from offer).*

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **WEAK**

| Evidence | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Founder control / dual-class (Up-C)** | Company operates as subsidiary of Venture Global Partners II, LLC; IPO structure carries dual-class voting per the 424B4. Founders retain voting control disproportionate to economic stake — minority holders have limited ability to effect change. |
| **CEO/Chair duality** | Michael Sabel is simultaneously **CEO and Executive Co-Chairman**; Robert Pender (co-founder) is Executive Co-Chairman. Two founders occupy both chair seats. |
| **Committee independence** | Per the company's own committee-composition disclosure, **Sabel and Pender sit on the Compensation Committee**, and **Sabel sits on the Nominating Committee**. Founders setting their own pay and choosing their own board successors is a textbook entrenchment flag. Audit Committee (Orekar, Staton) appears independent — the one clean channel. |
| **Comp structure** | Heavy option-based grants (e.g., 34,722 options to director Staton, May 2026) with large low-basis option exercises ($0.79–$1.16 strikes) converting into immediate open-market sales. Incentives are being monetized, not held. |
| **Insider alignment** | Zero open-market purchases of consequence; one token buy (Sabel, 1,226 shares, ~$16k) against $100M+ of sales. Revealed preference is unambiguous. |

**Verdict:** A founder-controlled, dual-class, founder-chaired, founder-compensated board. Governance is structurally hostile to minority shareholders. Not fraud — but the checks that normally constrain management are largely absent.

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: **ELEVATED**

**No SEC enforcement actions on record** (`search_regulatory_actions` returned empty — a genuine positive).

**Active/consummated commercial arbitration — the dominant legal risk.** This is not ordinary contract noise; it is a *systemic pattern* alleging VG sold commissioning cargoes at spot prices instead of honoring long-term contracts by delaying the Calcasieu Pass commercial operation date:
- **BP — LOST (Oct 2025).** BP seeking **at least $3.7B**, potentially **>$6B** with interest/costs/fees. Largest single quantified exposure.
- **Galp — LOST (8-Oct-2026).** Breach confirmed; damages hearing 2027–28, **capped at ~$170M** (immaterial per Capital One). One arbitrator dissented.
- **Tauron (Poland) — reported loss (decision 1-Oct-2026)** per secondary sourcing; treat as *unconfirmed* pending company disclosure.
- **Shell — WON**, and VG defeated Shell's NY state-court challenge to the award (March 2026). **Repsol — WON.**
- **Edison — SETTLED** (Q2 2026) via additional cargoes.
- **Orlen — PENDING**, decision expected by year-end 2026. Binary near-term catalyst.
- **Score: 3 losses, 2 wins, 1 settlement, 1 pending.** A 3-of-9 loss rate on the same factual question suggests the commercial-operations-date strategy was legally aggressive by design — a management-judgment risk, not bad luck.

**Permitting/regulatory:**
- **FERC:** CP2 approval **upheld** by the appeals court (Aug 2026); a parallel challenge to the Cameron Parish facility was **rejected** (27-Aug-2026). FERC opened a **new scoping period for CP2/CP Express** (Federal Register, 17-Aug-2026) — expansion requires fresh environmental review.
- **Clean Air Act:** Sierra Club et al. federal suit seeking to vacate CP2's CAA permit — **status unresolved**; a permit vacatur would be a construction-halting event.
- **DOE export authorization** for CP2 is a live dependency on federal LNG-export policy, which is politically reversible.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Balance-sheet fragility against a construction pipeline.** ~**$42.8B total debt vs. $3.15B cash**, with three material-debt 8-Ks between June and September 2026 (including a $3.0B 364-day revolver closed 2-Sep-26). Short-dated revolver reliance during a multi-project build-out leaves little cushion if LNG spreads compress or a damages award lands. Capital-markets access *is* the operating risk.
- **Counterparty litigation overhang impairing the franchise.** The entire economic model rests on long-term SPAs with investment-grade buyers. Suing/being sued by BP, Shell, Galp, Orlen, Repsol, Edison and Tauron damages the core asset: willingness of blue-chip offtakers to sign 20-year contracts. Mitigants: ConocoPhillips 1 MTPA SPA (3-Oct-26) and QatarEnergy talks (22-Oct-26) show the franchise is not yet broken.
- **Execution/key-person concentration.** ~2,000 employees running three mega-projects. Founders Pender and Sabel are the strategic center of gravity; Sabel only became CEO in Sept 2023. Simultaneous scale-up of Plaquemines Phase 1 (commercial ops 1-Nov-26), CP2, an 85 MTPA-by-2029 target, **and** a new 10 GW data-center power venture with GE Vernova/Baker Hughes is a very wide span of control for a first-time public company. Plaquemines already missed cost expectations (Q2'26 revenue miss on project cost inflation).

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **ELEVATED**

- **Buyer-relations damage is now documented fact, not allegation** — three tribunals have found VG breached supply contracts. For an LNG seller whose product is *contractual reliability*, this is brand-level damage in its most important customer pool.
- **Community/Environmental:** Louisiana commercial fishers and seafood processors are **suing VG over a sediment spill** alleged to have caused millions in damage to the seafood industry; state suits were **paused in Oct 2026** procedurally (not dismissed) and remain live. Local officials have publicly criticized the company's community engagement. Environmental-group litigation (Earthjustice, Sierra Club, SELC, NRDC) keeps a steady negative-news drumbeat.
- **Market perception:** reported **~80.5% of float sold short** (Basis Report, 30-Sep-26 — third-party estimate, *unverified*; treat with caution but the direction is consistent with the insider data). A stock with this short interest and this insider selling will be a persistent target for bear research; note an earlier **IPO securities class action was dismissed 15-Jun-2026** ("permanently defeated" per Law360) — a real credit that reduces the "fraud" tail.
- VG has publicly accused Shell of waging a "three-year campaign" — combative public posture raises the risk of further escalation and discovery-driven disclosures.

---

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Not Critical — there is no SEC enforcement action, no going-concern question, no whistleblower or restatement signal, and the IPO class action was dismissed. But weak founder-controlled governance, a serial-losing contract-litigation record with multi-billion-dollar BP exposure, and near-universal insider selling into a depressed share price jointly constitute a materially elevated non-financial risk profile. These risks are **correlated, not independent**: the arbitration losses arose from the same commercial-operations-date decision that the board — chaired and compensated by the decision-makers — never constrained.

---

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **🚩 Orlen arbitration ruling due by year-end 2026** — binary event; a fourth loss would confirm a systemic contract-interpretation failure and sharpen BP's damages claim.
2. **🚩 BP damages quantum ($3.7B–$6B)** — equal to 12–19% of market cap; any award or adverse procedural ruling is a material restatement of equity value.
3. **🚩 Zero insider conviction buying; CFO, GC, COO and CAO all selling** while the stock sits below every recent sale price. Escalate to PM as a management-credibility issue.
4. **🚩 Founders on both Compensation and Nominating Committees** (Sabel/Pender) — no independent check on pay or board refresh; activist or proxy-advisor opposition is likely at the next annual meeting.
5. **🚩 ~$42.8B debt / $3.15B cash with 364-day revolver dependence** — refinancing risk meets litigation risk; these two could crystallize together.
6. **🚩 Sierra Club Clean Air Act permit challenge to CP2** — an adverse ruling would halt construction on the primary growth asset.
7. **⚠️ Reported ~80.5% short interest** — unverified third-party figure; if accurate, expect acute volatility and elevated borrow costs, and treat sell-side price targets with skepticism.

**Recommended action:** Escalate to the investment committee for a governance-and-litigation overlay before sizing. Any long position should be sized for a headline BP/Orlen outcome and should require evidence of *insider buying* or an Orlen win as a precondition. Request the full BP arbitration award and the board's related-party/indemnification disclosures.
