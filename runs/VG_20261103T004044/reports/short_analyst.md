## FORENSIC SHORT ANALYSIS — VENTURE GLOBAL, INC. (NYSE: VG)

**Price $12.87 (2 Nov 2026) | Mkt cap ~$32.2B | Net debt $32.5B | EV ~$64.7B**
Context: IPO'd 24 Jan 2025 at $25.00 → low $5.89 (Dec 2025) → high $17.46 (Mar 2026) → $12.87. **Short interest ≈ 83.8% of float.**

---

### 1. Earnings Quality Assessment: **MEDIUM** (not Fraudulent Risk)

This is where I have to break with the popular narrative. The cash-flow record does **not** support an accounting-manipulation short:

| Metric | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accruals ratio | −0.122 | −0.066 | −0.014 | **−0.072** |
| OCF / Net income | 119% | 126% | 123% | **243%** |
| Gross margin | 65.1% | 78.7% | 66.4% | **49.3%** |

Negative accruals in all four years, cash conversion far above 100%, Piotroski F-score 7/9. Earnings are backed by cash. Anyone pitching VG as an Enron is peddling a narrative the numbers reject.

---

### 2. Key Red Flags Found

1. **Collapsing gross margin — the single best bear datum.** 78.7% (2023) → 66.4% (2024) → **49.3% (2025)**. This is the effective death of the commissioning-cargo spot arbitrage that produced the 2022–23 windfall. As contracted volumes (≈115% of Henry Hub) replace spot sales, margin is structurally capped. This is *realised* pricing-power loss, not a forecast.
2. **D&A is understated.** FY25 D&A $941M on $47.3B net PP&E = **1.99%**. Normalising to 3.5–4.0% for 40-year LNG trains implies $1.66–1.89B, i.e. **$0.26–0.35/share of EPS that hasn't shown up yet** — precisely as Plaquemines exits full-year ramp.
3. **Borrowing to pay dividends.** FY25 dividends $465M against **free cash flow of −$6.80B**. Q3 2026 dividend raised 122%. Capital-return signalling while consuming capital is a promotional tell.
4. **Receivables flag (FY23, FY24):** AR +39.5% vs revenue +22.5%, then +37.4% vs revenue −37.0%. Cleared in FY25 (AR +152% vs rev +177%) — a legacy of the commissioning-period billing dispute, not ongoing channel stuffing.
5. **Working-capital deficit:** current assets $4.04B vs current liabilities $4.34B (ratio 0.93); cash $2.36B.
6. **Leverage:** total debt **$34.9B**, interest expense $1.454B (up from $584M in FY24, +149%). Two further committed projects: **CP2 Phase 1 $15.1B** (FID Jul 2025) and **Phase 2 $8.6B** (FID 13 Mar 2026) = **$23.7B of forward capex** into a softening price deck. FCF was −$11.6B (FY24) and −$6.8B (FY25).
7. **Consensus already models the cliff:** 2026E EPS $1.79 → **2027E $1.23 (−31%)**, revenue $18.9B → $17.5B. The sell side's own numbers are a bear case.
8. **Dilution:** share count 2.07B (2023) → 2.43B (2025); Piotroski F7 = 0.

### 3. Beneish M-Score & Accruals — read the components, not the headline

**M-Score = −1.315 ("HIGH RISK"), but this is a mechanical false positive.** Decomposition:

- **SGI = 2.769** — revenue more than doubled. On a company commissioning two LNG trains, this term alone drags the score below the threshold and carries no forensic information.
- **GMI = 1.346** — *this* is the legitimate signal. It equals prior/current gross margin (66.42/49.31 = 1.347 ✓) and confirms the margin erosion above.
- **TATA = −0.072** — negative total accruals, i.e. conservative, not manipulative.
- AQI 0.463, DEPI 0.464, SGAI 0.501 — all flattered by the construction-to-operations asset base shift.

**Verdict: the M-Score is not evidence of manipulation.** It is evidence of a business whose margin structure deteriorated by 29 percentage points in two years.

---

### 4. Competitive Threat Assessment

Genuine and structural. The 2026–2029 global liquefaction wave (Plaquemines itself, Corpus Christi Stage 3, Golden Pass, LNG Canada, Qatar's North Field expansion) adds roughly 40% to world capacity against inelastic near-term demand. JKM–Henry Hub spreads are the entire economic engine for uncontracted volumes, and Q2 2026 already showed the pattern: **volume +42% but lower realised LNG prices**, with revenue of $4.58B *sequentially down* from $4.60B in Q1 2026 despite the ramp. That is a company running hard to stand still. A commodity-spread merchant with 3.0× net debt/EBITDA and $23.7B of committed capex has no pricing power to defend.

---

### 5. Management & Governance — the strongest bear pillar

- **Adjudicated breach of contract, repeatedly.** Tribunals have now *found* that Venture Global breached obligations to declare Calcasieu commercial operation date in a timely manner — **BP prevailed**; **Galp** obtained a liability ruling (8 Oct 2026, damages to be assessed); **Edison** settled in Q2 2026. VG did win against **Shell** (and defeated Shell's Mar 2026 motion to vacate) and **Repsol** — I credit that fully, and it materially weakens the "corporate fraud" characterisation Shell advanced. But a company that has litigated against Shell, BP, Repsol, Galp, Edison and Orlen simultaneously has a counterparty-relations problem that is now priced as a permanent discount, not a one-off.
- **Insider selling dominates insider buying.** ~**17.5M shares sold for ~$221.8M** over the trailing 24 months, with a steady monthly Form 4 cadence from numerous distinct reporting persons through 2026 (including the CFO selling into the decline). The only notable buys were the founders' *symbolic* $2.34M each in March 2025 — a rounding error against $221.8M of sales.
- **Founder control** (Sabel/Pender) with a dual-class structure; retail-heavy, thin float. Reported beta of 0.223 is meaningless given a 3× round trip in twelve months.

### 6. Short Thesis Summary — is there a credible bear case?

**Yes, but it is a quality-and-leverage bear case, not a fraud bear case.**

The credible thesis: VG is a spread merchant being repriced as such. Peak-margin spot arbitrage (2022–23) is gone; gross margin has nearly halved; D&A normalisation will take another $0.30 of EPS; consensus already shows a 31% EPS decline in 2027; and $23.7B of committed capex against $34.9B of debt with negative FCF means external financing dependence through 2028. Every dollar of the 2026 guidance raise came from commodity price, not from anything durable.

**Where the bears are wrong:** cash flows are real (243% conversion), accruals are negative, two of the three flagship arbitrations were won, the F-score is 7, and Altman Z of 0.815 is an artefact — x4 uses book equity ($10.3B) against $43.1B of liabilities for a project-financed entity with no retained-earnings history. On consensus $11.0B 2026E EBITDA, net debt/EBITDA is ~3.0× and EV/EBITDA ~5.9×. That is not a distress valuation; it is a cyclical-mid valuation.

**And the decisive problem: 83.8% of the float is already short.** The bear case is not merely known — it is *the* positioning. That crowding is exactly what produced the $5.89 → $17.46 squeeze. Adding to that trade at $12.87 means paying borrow to own the most consensus view in the sector, with a balance sheet that can refinance $23.7B of committed projects and a stock that rips 40% on any European gas spike.

---

### 7. Contrarian Rating: **NO EDGE** (do not initiate a short here)

The forensic work *supports* scepticism but does **not** support a short at $12.87. My honest read: the margin/leverage thesis is correct and already fully owned by the market, while the fraud thesis is refuted by the cash flows.

**Conditions that would convert this to a Short:**
- Price back above **~$16.50–17.50** (EV/EBITDA >8× on 2026E, >10× on 2027E declining EBITDA) into a Q1/Q2 2027 print showing realised prices below guidance — that is a fresh, uncrowded entry with the crowd's cost basis underwater.
- **CP2 Phase 1 cost overrun or schedule slip**, which would break the sole remaining bull leg (volume growth) while capex commitments stay fixed.
- A **material adverse Galp damages award** plus any new arbitration filing, reviving counterparty risk into a refinancing window.
- Dividend continuation alongside widening negative FCF — a governance tripwire.

**Kill switches for the bear case:** sustained JKM–HH spread widening; new long-term SPAs at CP2 that de-risk the $23.7B program; net debt/EBITDA sustained below 3.0× with FCF inflection; and the founders buying in size rather than $2.34M token amounts.

**Bottom line:** I cannot sign a short-seller report calling VG a fraud, because the accruals, cash conversion and two arbitration wins say otherwise. I also cannot recommend shorting a stock that 84% of the float is already short. The rigorous call is to stand aside, keep the margin-erosion and D&A-normalisation thesis on file, and wait for the next macro-driven spike to sell into.
