- ❌ **Cost (deteriorating):** memory super-cycle is a direct tax on 19% gross margins; this is the genuine alt-data-informed bear case.

**Net:** Alt data does **not** support adding exposure ahead of the Nov 24 print. The revenue beat is likely but largely pre-announced via the backlog; the asymmetry sits in guidance and gross margin.
