**Investment Thesis**

FDXF is a genuinely good asset — North America's #1 LTL carrier with real pricing power, record service metrics, and a self-help margin story — wrapped in a bad structure: negative book equity, ~4.4x net leverage, an entrenched FedEx-conflicted board, and a 19.9% parent overhang. Two independent valuation approaches (Value's DCF/multiples: $95–110; Short's peer-anchored 14–16x FY27E EBITDA: $90–109) converge tightly on **~$100 fair value**, meaning the stock trades ~20–25% above intrinsic value after its +14.9% post-earnings pop. The asymmetry is unfavorable (−33% bear vs +19% bull; expected return ≈ −12.5%), and the next binary catalyst (Dec 2 guidance) is five weeks away. This is a name to own at $90–105, not at $126.
