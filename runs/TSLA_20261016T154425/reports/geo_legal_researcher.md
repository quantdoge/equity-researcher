## 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**1. US–China trade-war escalation / truce expiry (Jan 2027)** — *Probability: Medium-High | Impact: High*
- The US–China trade truce has a January 2027 deadline; China is simultaneously tightening export controls on rare earths and AI technologies (WSJ/CNBC, Oct 2026). Tesla sits on both sides: ~22% of revenue and its most efficient factory (Shanghai) depend on frictionless US–China commerce, while China controls the rare-earth magnets and battery inputs Tesla needs.
- Retaliation scenarios (higher EV tariffs, approval freezes on FSD/software, export bans on materials) directly attack the margin and robotaxi pillars of the thesis.

**2. Europe: structural demand decay + regulatory tariff drag** — *Probability: High (materializing) | Impact: Medium-High*
- European registrations fell ~28.5% in the first nine months of 2025 and fell a 13th straight month in Jan 2026 (−17%); Germany −48.4% in 2025 (Reuters, CNBC, Politico). Cause: Musk's political activism (AfD backing, US DOGE role), protests/vandalism, Chinese competition, aging lineup.
- The EU's 9% countervailing duty on Shanghai-built Teslas (Tesla is challenging it in EU courts) makes the Europe business structurally worse even as local Berlin capacity struggles with union and permitting friction.

**3. US autonomous-driving regulation as key risk to FSD/robotaxi monetization** — *Probability: Medium | Impact: High*
- NHTSA's open Autopilot investigation plus a new FSD visibility probe covering ~3.2M vehicles raises recall risk for the exact product (FSD/robotaxi, Austin launch June 2025) that carries most of the current valuation premium. A federal safety action or state-permit freeze in major markets (California) would impair the core growth narrative.

## 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**Active / recent litigation:**
- **Autopilot product liability (Miami):** *Benavides v. Tesla* — Aug 2025 jury verdict ~$243M (incl. $200M punitive), upheld by Judge Bloom (Feb 2026); Tesla settled a related Florida teenager wrongful-death suit (Apr 2026). A second Miami case yielded a ~$329M verdict (Sept 2025). Ongoing pipeline of FSD/Autopilot crash suits is the largest single litigation exposure.
- **Musk compensation (Delaware):** Delaware Supreme Court (Dec 19, 2025) unanimously **reversed** the Chancery rescission and fully restored Musk's ~$56B package (Oct 2026) — a major overhang resolved in Tesla's favor.
- **EU tariff challenge:** Tesla is party to the EU General Court challenge of the anti-subsidy duties on China-built EVs — outcome governs the economics of Shanghai→Europe flows.
- **9th Circuit class-certification appeal** (filed Sept 1, 2025; referenced in FY2025 10-K) re: securities/shareholder class action.

**Regulatory posture:**
- **NHTSA:** Autopilot defect investigation formally remains **open**; FSD/visibility probe (~3.2M vehicles) could force a recall. Multiple prior OTA recalls (FSD Beta 362k vehicles, Autopilot ~2M vehicles Dec 2023).
- **Emerging-market expansion:** self-driving approval granted in Slovakia (Oct 2026) shows per-country AV licensing as the gatekeeper model — a slow, fragmented path globally.
- **US fiscal policy:** EV purchase-credit phaseout legislated in 2025 reduces demand support in the ~50% US market.

## 4. Compliance Red Flags

- **CEO political entanglement → policy/brand risk:** Musk's dual role as senior US-government adviser (DOGE) creates perception-of-capture risk when Tesla interacts with NHTSA, EPA, and foreign regulators, and is the proximate driver of the European boycott. Governance concern, not a settled violation.
- **Safety-regulator materiality:** an adverse NHTSA finding on the 3.2M-vehicle FSD probe could trigger both a recall and a wave of follow-on plaintiff litigation (punitive-damage precedent now set).
- **China data/FD compliance:** FSD in China hinges on Beijing's data-localization and cross-border data-transfer approvals — a recurring operational license Tesla cannot take for granted amid the tech war.
- **FCPA/sanctions:** no material disclosed FCPA or sanctions enforcement (Russia sales suspended); exposure is moderate and indirect via China state relationships and third-party service networks — monitor only.
- **SEC:** no new active enforcement surfaced in this window, but SEC's historical scrutiny of Musk's disclosures (2018 settlement, X-stake probe) warrants monitoring given aggressive robotaxi/FSD claims.

## 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale:** ~50% of revenue sits inside an active US–China trade confrontation (22% in China directly, plus supply-chain dependence); Europe (~28%) is in a self-inflicted demand crisis compounded by regulatory tariffs; the US safety regulator is actively probing the product (FSD) on which future value rests; and Tesla carries a live, precedent-setting product-liability docket. Mitigants — Delaware win, cash generation, US domestic manufacturing depth, OTA-remediable safety fixes — keep this below "Very High," but the convergence of trade-war, EU-demand, and AV-regulation risk in a single 12-month window justifies a High rating.
