## Investment Thesis

Tesla is a deteriorating auto business — revenue −2.9% (FY25, first decline since 2020), operating margin collapsed from 16.8% (2022) to 4.6% (Q2'26: 1.4%), ROIC collapsed from 26.4% to 4.8% — still priced at ~330x trailing earnings, ~105x EV/EBITDA, and a ~0.5% FCF yield, with the entire premium resting on robotaxi/FSD/Optimus revenue that contributes essentially nothing today (robotaxi: ~380k unsupervised miles vs. Waymo's 200M+). The balance sheet is a fortress (~$36B net cash; no solvency risk), so this is not a credit or fraud call — it is a valuation and margin-quality call: the price embeds a decade of flawless AI execution while the fundamentals move the wrong way, the macro backdrop (10Y ~5%, CPI re-accelerating, Jan-2027 US–China truce expiry) is a headwind, and live NHTSA probes sit on the very products (FSD, Cybercab) carrying the multiple. Consensus across desks on "no long at $360" is high; the split is only on expression — underweight vs. small defined-risk short.

## Bull Case

1. **Autonomy/robotics optionality is real and catalyst-dense**: revenue re-accelerating (+16–26% H1'26), +45.8% estimate revisions, and a 12-month calendar of proof points (Austin robotaxi fleet expansion, FSD v15, Optimus Gen 3 external sales H2'27, Semi) — hiring data corroborates genuine buildout. If unsupervised FSD monetizes at scale, the multiple is the entry price and TAMs are multi-trillion.
2. **Energy storage is a genuine second engine peers lack**: record 46.7 GWh deployed in 2025, >100% YoY growth, riding the AI-datacenter/grid-battery supercycle — macro-agnostic growth independent of the auto cycle.
3. **US leadership restored + fortress balance sheet**: US EV share recovered to ~52% as GM/Ford retreat; ~$36B net cash removes any solvency constraint and funds the AI buildout through execution risk.

## Bear Case

1. **The margin collapse is structural, not cyclical**: GM 25.6%→18.0%, op margin 16.8%→4.6%→1.4% (Q2'26), ROIC below cost of capital, ~⅔ of net income non-operating, and the regulatory-credit crutch declining ~28% YoY — consensus expects credits to fade further.
2. **The valuation embeds a decade of flawless execution with zero margin for error**: ~330x trailing P/E, ~0.5% FCF yield, 6x Toyota's market cap on a fraction of its earnings — while price momentum is negative (−10.4%), alpha is −48% annualised, and analyst dispersion ($157–$840) signals estimation chaos, not a clean upgrade cycle.
3. **Risk stacking in a single 12-month window**: Jan-2027 US–China truce expiry (22% revenue + supply chain), re-accelerating CPI with live Fed-hike risk (10Y ~5% on a negative-FCF equity), EU demand collapse, and two active NHTSA probes on the exact products that carry the premium — any of which can trigger a −50%+ drawdown (3-yr max DD −54%).
