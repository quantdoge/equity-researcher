# Macroeconomic Assessment — Tesla, Inc. (TSLA)
**As of mid-October 2026 | Prepared by: Macroeconomy Research**

---

## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional, tilting risk-off.**

The US sits in an awkward middle zone: the Fed has delivered ~150bp of easing from the 5.33% plateau (effective Fed Funds now 3.75%, Sep 2026), growth is solid (~2.2–2.5% real GDP), and the yield curve has dis-inverted (2s10s +34bp). But the pieces that matter for a high-multiple consumer cyclical are deteriorating: **CPI re-accelerated to ~3.7% YoY (with +0.65% m/m in September)**, long-end yields are sticky near 5% (10Y 4.99%), the broad dollar is strengthening through 121.6, high-yield spreads have widened ~50bp in six weeks, unemployment has drifted up to 4.2%, and the market is openly debating **a Fed *hike*** after Chair Warsh's hawkish IMF remarks — October hike bets were only recently dialed back. Add the January 2027 expiry of the US–China trade truce and this is an environment where rate relief (Tesla's main macro tailwind) may be the thing that evaporates first.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

The only unambiguously positive macro force today is the ~150bp of policy easing already banked — and the market is now debating giving some of it back. Against that: inflation re-accelerating at ~3.7% and climbing, 10Y yields stuck near 5% on a negative-FCF, long-duration equity, a strengthening dollar squeezing international translation, widening consumer-credit spreads into a luxury-automotive buyer pool, and a January 2027 US–China tariff cliff. For a 1.92-beta consumer cyclical, that is a net headwind. Company-specific mitigants (localized manufacturing, captive financing, energy segment) blunt — but do not offset — the macro drag.

---

## 5. The Material Surprise Scenario (Bear — and it is live)

**"The Re-Tightening Fed."** Suppose tariff-induced CPI acceleration persists (3.7% → 4.5%+ by H1 2027), the US–China truce expires in January with new tariffs rather than renewal, and Chair Warsh's hawkish stance hardens into an actual **re-hike** while the 10Y breaks toward 5.5%. Consequence: USD pushes through ~125 on the broad index, HY spreads widen past 400bp, auto loan rates re-inflate just as Tesla's China/Europe price war deepens. For Tesla this compounds on three legs simultaneously — discount rate (multiple de-rates further from ~$360), demand (affordability breaks on a big-ticket discretionary), and cost (inputs + tariffs on an already margin-constrained, negative-FCF profile). A 20–30% drawdown in the stock from macro alone is plausible in that sequence. The bull mirror-image — a truce extension that lets disinflation resume, the Fed cutting toward ~3%, and the 10Y back under 4.3% — is how Tesla's optionality (robotaxi/AI, storage, Roadster) gets re-rated sharply higher. Right now, the odds lean the former way, and that is why the macro stance is risk-off-transitional rather than outright risk-on.
