## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — functionally a HOLD consensus**

**Agreement level: High on the action, Low on the reason.**

- **Zero of 11 agents recommend buying.** Value, Growth, Quant all land on HOLD/NEUTRAL at $83.48. The Short Analyst explicitly *refuses* to short. That is a rare configuration: **nobody likes it long, nobody wants it short.** The stock is fairly priced in the middle.
- **The genuine disagreement is about the Anthropic stake**, and it is material:
  - *Value/Fin Risk/Sector/Nonfin* carry it at **~$3.13B ≈ 13% of market cap** (the reported Q2 FY27 mark).
  - *Alt Data* argues it is worth **~$7B at IPO ≈ 29% of cap** (Fool/Baird/Ives estimates spanning $1.6B–$7B+).
  - **This single unresolved item is the difference between "fairly valued" and "cheap."** At 13% of cap, ZM trades ~20x core EV/FCF; at 29%, ~10x. I size toward the conservative 13% case and treat the delta as free optionality — because the alt-data confidence is only MEDIUM and the ownership percentage is unverified.
- **Conflict resolved:** Growth's "no path to 15%" and Value's "no margin of safety" both dominate the Alt Data's structural-bullish NAV case for *entry timing*. The NAV floor prevents a short; it does not create a buy at $83.
