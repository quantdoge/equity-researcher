## Zoom Communications (ZM) — Growth Investment Analysis
*Pricing as of the Nov 20, 2026 close: $83.48; market cap ~$25.7B; EV ~$17.9B (net cash ~$7.8B); next print Nov 23, 2026*

---

### 1. Growth Story

Zoom has completed its transformation from pandemic hyper-grower to a **cash-generative collaboration platform**: revenue has compounded just ~3.7% over the last two fiscal years, but GAAP operating margin has gone from 5.6% to 23.1% and free cash flow to $1.92B (39.5% margin) in the same window. The *growth* case now rests entirely on three second-derivative vectors rather than core meetings: (a) **enterprise expansion** (enterprise revenue +6.1% y/y, now 60% of total), (b) **Contact Center** (+94% y/y growth in customers with >$100K ARR, to 229), and (c) **AI Companion monetization** (MAUs more than tripled in FY26; Companion 3.0 now in GA). This is no longer a disruptive-growth stock — it is a **Rule-of-40 franchise trading at a deep-value multiple**, which is a different (and for a growth mandate, lesser) proposition.

### 3. TAM and Penetration

- Served TAM (UC&C + contact center + workspace) is roughly **$90–110B**, growing high single digits; contact center alone is a $50–60B pool heading toward $100B+ by 2030 as it shifts to AI-native.
- Zoom's ~$4.9B revenue is only **~4–5% of that TAM**, but **core meetings penetration is effectively saturated** in SMB/education — the 40% online segment is a low-growth, churn-prone base. The remaining runway is *enterprise seat expansion + phone + contact center*, which is why enterprise (+6.1%) is running ~50% faster than the company.
- Differentiation: best-in-class reliability and UX, a single platform (Meet/Chat/Phone/Contact Center/Workvivo), and now an AI layer bundled at no extra cost. The moat is **switching costs and platform breadth**, not proprietary model technology — and it is contested by Microsoft (Teams bundled into M365) and Google (Meet bundled into Workspace). Penetration upside therefore depends on out-executing free.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts

1. **Q3 FY2027 results — Monday, Nov 23, 2026** (consensus $1.49 EPS / $1.279B revenue, +4.0% y/y). Guidance, not the print, is the event; shares just fell ~10% on Nov 19 into a headline citing softer profit guidance. This is a binary 48-hour catalyst.
2. **AI Companion 3.0 monetization** — first evidence of incremental seat pricing/upsell (Business tier at $18.33 PUPM; anything above that is new ARPU). Watch for any disclosed AI-attach or ARPU metric.
3. **Contact Center scale-up** — 229 customers >$100K ARR (+94%); crossing ~$500M product-line ARR with AI-agent pricing would be the first genuine re-rating narrative.
4. **Capital allocation** — ~$7.8B net cash (30% of market cap) plus $1.9B+ annual FCF: an accelerated buyback or inaugural dividend is a plausible 2027 announcement.
5. **Enterprise mix >60% / Zoom Phone & Workvivo cross-sell** — continued mix shift supports margin durability even at 4% topline.

### 6. Growth Verdict: **HOLD**

This is a high-quality business that fails the growth screen and passes the value screen. A 43.9 Rule-of-40 score is genuinely healthy, but the composition is wrong for a growth mandate: **4.4% revenue growth + 39.5% FCF margin**, with EPS growth now decelerating to ~3%. At 9.3x EV/FCF with 30% of the market cap in net cash and a ~6% annual buyback, downside is well protected — but there is no visible path to the 15%+ growth that would justify a growth multiple. The "market-implied" 5-year scenario is only ~4–5% revenue CAGR producing high-single-digit annual total returns; that is a bond-proxy compounding profile, not a disruptive-innovation re-rating.

**What would make me a buyer:** enterprise net expansion back above 110%, any disclosure that AI Companion is lifting ARPU (not just MAUs), Contact Center ARR crossing ~$500M, or a buyback/dividend step-up of $2B+. **What keeps me out:** a Q3 guide-down on Nov 23 confirming that AI inference costs are compressing the 77% gross margin.

### 7. Key Risks

- **Competitive bundling (highest severity):** Teams inside M365 and Meet inside Workspace are free at the margin; Zoom must win on UX while competitors give the product away. This caps pricing power permanently.
- **AI commoditization + margin math:** AI Companion is priced at $0 incremental. Inference costs against a 77% gross margin create real risk of the first GM *contraction* in years — the single biggest threat to the FCF-margin half of the Rule of 40.
- **Execution risk in the pivot:** the growth vectors (Contact Center, Workvivo) are sub-scale (~2% of revenue) and must compound >50% for years just to offset a decelerating core.
- **Growth deceleration/terminal-value risk:** 4% growth with a saturated online segment invites the market to price eventual decline; multiple expansion is unlikely without a re-acceleration proof point.
- **Guidance fragility:** the Nov 19 selloff and the negative out-year revision show how little tolerance there is for a soft profit guide at 13x earnings.
- **SBC dilution overhang:** still 15.6% of revenue; improvement is real but incomplete.
