## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis — Revenue −20%

**FY2025 base:** Rev $2,790.5M | GM 55.2% | Opex $811.1M (SG&A $428.8M + R&D $382.3M) | EBIT $728.6M (26.1%)

| Scenario | Rev | GM | Opex | EBIT | EBIT margin | Δ EBIT | FCF |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base** | 2,790 | 55.2% | 811 | 728.6 | 26.1% | — | $666M |
| **A: Ordered downturn** (−20%, mgmt cuts 12%) | 2,232 | 50.0% | 714 | **402** | 18.0% | **−45%** | **~$390M** |
| **B: Severe / share-loss + price pressure** (−20%, sticky costs) | 2,232 | 45.0% | 771 | **234** | 10.5% | **−68%** | **~$265M** |
| **C: Operating breakeven** | 1,622 | 50% | 811 | **0** | 0% | −100% | ~$50M |

**Key findings:**

1. **Operating leverage cuts both ways.** A 20% revenue decline destroys **45–68% of EBIT** — roughly 2.3–3.4x the revenue shock. This is the core earnings fragility.
2. **Breakeven revenue is ~$1,470–1,620M**, i.e., **−42% to −47% below FY2025**. MPWR would need to lose *nearly half its business* before operating income hits zero.
3. **Solvency is never in question.** Even in Scenario C, FCF stays positive once capex is cut and working capital releases. Interest expense remains $0 in every case.
4. **Cash runway:** $1,256.5M of cash covers **1.55 years of total opex** at zero revenue, or **4.4 years of dividends**. Zero debt means no covenant triggers and no lender consent risk.
5. **The real damage is equity, not credit.** FCF of ~$265–390M at a de-rated multiple of 60–70x implies a market cap of $16–27B, i.e., **−60% to −76%** from $67.7B. A 20% revenue miss is a **severe equity event and a non-event for the balance sheet.**

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Valuation vs. rates — the #1 risk.** 0.99% FCF yield / ~101x P/FCF against a 5.13% 10Y Treasury. No margin of safety; a 60–70x de-rating alone costs 30–40%.
2. **Asia = 92% of FY2025 revenue** (94% FY2024, 87% FY2023), heavily via China/Taiwan distributors. Extreme geographic and channel concentration — tariff, export-control, and geopolitical exposure is the most likely trigger for the −20% revenue scenario above.
3. **FCF quality deteriorating despite record revenue.** FCF margin 31.9% → 29.1% → **23.9%**; OCF grew only 6.3% on 26.4% revenue growth; working capital a $88M drag. Growth is consuming cash.
4. **Capex tripled** ($57.6M → $146.1M → $172.0M) — if demand rolls over mid-buildout, stranded capacity converts directly into gross-margin compression.
5. **Inventory days +9.5 and DSO +2.9** while revenue grows — classic early-cycle warning of channel stuffing or end-demand softening.
6. **Gross margin −320bp from the 2022 peak** (58.4% → 55.2%) — persistent pricing/mix erosion in a company priced for perfection.
7. **SBC = 8.2% of revenue / 34% of FCF** — the GAAP-to-cash bridge is materially SBC-dependent.
8. **FY2024 earnings anomaly:** NI $1,786.7M vs EBIT $572.9M. Any model using 2024 as a base overstates ROE, ROA, and earnings growth.
9. **Beta 2.35** — a −10% S&P move implies ~−24% on MPWR; position sizing must reflect this, not the 1.73 profile beta.
