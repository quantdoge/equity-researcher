**Investment Thesis**

MPWR is a genuinely exceptional business — net cash, ~20%+ ROIC, re-accelerating revenue (+63% YoY in Q3'26, guiding ~+80%), the only analog peer compounding >25% — attached to a genuinely dangerous price. At ~77x TTM earnings and a 0.99% FCF yield against a 5%+ risk-free rate, the stock discounts a flawless decade while carrying an unremediated material weakness, a class-certification-stage securities suit, and a growth engine (42% of revenue) that one customer re-awards each GPU generation — and has taken away once before. The bull and bear cases are both honest: fair value estimates span $750–$2,250. When credible dispersion is that wide, the correct position is small, staged, and conditional — not maximal in either direction.

**Bear Case**
