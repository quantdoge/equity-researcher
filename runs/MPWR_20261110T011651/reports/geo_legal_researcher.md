### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical Risks

**① China concentration + US–China export-control/tariff escalation — Probability: High (65%) | Impact: High**
China is simultaneously ~47% of revenue and the plurality of long-lived assets. The FY2025 10-K itself warns that *"the current stagnation in China's economy has adversely impacted, and could further adversely impact, our customers, prospective customers, suppliers…"* (SEC filing, mpwr20251231_10k.htm). Layered on top: the 2026 US regime of §232 tariffs on advanced computing chips and a 25% tariff band on certain China-bound AI silicon, China's two-month extension of its tariff pause (Global Times, Nov 2026), and Beijing's willingness to activate its **2021 blocking rules and Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law** against firms complying with US measures. The tail risk is not a sales ban on MPWR's commodity PMICs — it is tariff-driven margin compression on China-origin output plus legal conflict between two overlapping regulatory regimes.

**② Taiwan Strait / supply-chain choke point — Probability: Low (5–10% for acute disruption in 12 months) | Impact: Severe**
~17% of revenue ships to Taiwan and MPWR's fabless model depends on Taiwanese foundry capacity. 2026 has seen Taiwan move to tighten advanced-IC exports to China and China impose counter-sanctions on Taiwanese entities. A quarantine or blockade scenario would impair both the Taiwan revenue stream and the wafer supply that feeds every other region. Low probability, unhedgeable, and correlated with risk ① — the two are really one tail.

**③ Policy-driven AI demand shock / Nvidia program concentration — Probability: Medium (30%) | Impact: Medium-High**
Because Enterprise Data is now the growth engine and Nvidia is the anchor customer, US export-control decisions on China-bound accelerators (H20/H200 licensing, the 25% tariff waiver regime) transmit almost one-for-one into MPWR's content per system. The stock has already shown this sensitivity: **MPWR fell 15.9% in four weeks** despite record results (Zacks, Nov 3, 2026), and Seeking Alpha characterized Q3 as "record growth and yet on sale" amid AI-sector doubts. MPWR retains the 2024-era reputational overhang of having underperformed on an Nvidia power-module program — the same issue underlying the litigation below.

### 3. Legal & Regulatory Highlights

**A. Securities fraud class action — *In re Monolithic Power Systems, Inc.*, No. 25-cv-00220 (W.D. Wash., Judge James L. Robart)** — *the most material item*
- Class period **Feb 8, 2024 – Nov 11, 2024**. Alleges MPWR misrepresented that voltage-regulator modules and PMICs sold into Nvidia GPU platforms had resolved performance/quality-control defects, that the Nvidia relationship was "irreparably damaged," and that a subsequent disclosure drove a ~30% stock decline.
- **Status: live and advancing.** Lead plaintiff filed its **motion for class certification on Sept 4, 2026**; motion is being briefed, discovery is open. Defendants have moved for judgment arguing insider stock sales negate scienter (Law360, *Monolithic Execs Say Stock Sales Debunk Investor Fraud Suit*).
- No accrual disclosed; no settlement signalled. Estimate low-to-mid nine figures gross exposure, low eight figures net of D&O.

**B. Restatement + material weakness — the *newer*, and arguably more damaging, problem**
- On **Feb 26, 2026** the **Audit Committee** concluded that the **FY2024 Form 10-K and each 2025 Form 10-Q should no longer be relied upon**, following an unintentional error in deferred income tax accounting tied to a one-time foreign tax incentive (8-K via StockTitan; virgo-pr restatement survey).
- A **material weakness in internal control over financial reporting** was disclosed in the FY2025 10-K (filed Feb 27, 2026) and **remained unremediated as of the 10-Q filed Nov 3, 2026** (stockrow filing red-flag screen). An unremediated material weakness across two consecutive fiscal years is a live SEC-comment-cycle and disclosure-controls issue.
- **Direct consequence:** Rosen Law Firm opened a **breach-of-fiduciary-duty / derivative investigation** into MPWR directors and officers, running a multi-wave solicitation campaign from **Oct 29 through Nov 4, 2026**. Expect a parallel shareholder derivative suit in Delaware or the W.D. Wash. — this is the standard sequel and should be modelled as a follow-on cost.

**C. Regulatory posture**
- Principal regulators: **SEC** (reporting/ICFR — active), **BIS** (export licensing for AI-adjacent power silicon), **CBP/ITC** (tariff classification & country-of-origin for China/Malaysia back-end), **SAMR/MOFCOM** (China — Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law, unreliable-entity-list exposure).
- **No DOJ, FCPA, AML or sanctions enforcement action identified** in this review, and no evidence of a sanctions violation. Flagging as a genuine gap rather than a clean bill of health: no public docket search was possible within budget.

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale.** MPWR combines (a) **~47% China revenue and a China-majority fixed-asset base** — an unusually deep exposure for a US-listed semi with no announced manufacturing relocation plan; (b) **~17% Taiwan ship-to concentration** on top of Taiwanese foundry dependence, giving two correlated Asia tail risks; (c) a **live federal securities class action at the class-certification stage** with a well-funded institutional lead plaintiff; and (d) a **fresh tax restatement with a still-unremediated material weakness** and a newly minted derivative-bar campaign.

**What would move the rating down:** China revenue share falling below ~40% on AI-driven US/Taiwan growth (mechanically happening now); remediation of the material weakness with clean 10-K/10-Q certification; class certification denied; concrete disclosure of non-China back-end capacity (Malaysia/Uniserve expansion).
**What would move it to Very High:** any SEC formal order or DOJ referral on the restatement; a court granting class certification; expansion of US export controls to commodity power-management silicon; or tariff action reaching China-assembled power modules.
