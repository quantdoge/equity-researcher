### Investment Memo: DT (Dynatrace) — 5 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $50 (~+7% from current $46.78)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months (thesis test dated: Q3 FY27 print 8-Feb-2027, Investor Day 16-Feb-2027)

**Investment Thesis**

Dynatrace is a high-quality, net-cash observability franchise — 81.6% gross margin, 26% FCF margin, 16x Gartner MQ Leader — caught in a three-way squeeze: organic ARR growth decelerating four straight years (32% → 16%), a hostile rate tape (10Y at 5.13%) taxing its long-duration multiple, and Datadog winning the incremental AI-native workload. The Nov-3 sell-off (−14.7%) punished an Arize-driven EPS cut as if it were a demand collapse — but flat net-new ARR ($70M, 0% YoY) is a genuine warning, and on SBC-adjusted owner earnings (~$215–250M, a ~1.5–1.8% yield vs 5.13% risk-free) the stock is roughly fairly valued, not cheap. This is a wait-for-the-print hold: re-accelerating net-new ARR re-rates it toward $60–65; a second flat quarter opens the high-$30s.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Category decelerating to high-teens with winner-take-most dynamics favouring Datadog (+32% vs DT +17.4%). DT is the quality/value end of the barbell (5.5x EV/S, 13.1% GAAP op margin, debt-free history). Macro is the dominant headwind: GDP +3.4% supports demand, but the 10Y at 5.13% with rising term premium and USD +3.1%/8wk hit DT precisely where it lives — the multiple and translation, not cash flows. Sector: NEUTRAL. Macro: UNFAVOURABLE, though much rate pain is priced after −28%.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: LOW-MEDIUM. No material litigation, no DOJ/SEC/FTC matters, nil Russia. Risk is compliance density: 32% EMEA revenue vs EU AI Act/GDPR/sovereignty procurement; session-replay wiretap class actions are a recurring nuisance, not a solvency issue. FedRAMP High still "Initial Implementation" — federal upside is option value, not booked.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD, accumulate ≤$41. Narrow moat, stable-to-narrowing; 8/10 financial quality; excellent FCF compounding but ~13–15% SBC and buyback-masked dilution. Value's $55 DCF rests on a $520M owner-earnings base the portfolio strategist adjudicated as overstated — SBC-adjusted fair value is closer to $40–48, i.e. the stock is at the top of fair range, not 15% below it.

At 5.6x EV/S vs DDOG ~15–20x (~36% cheaper growth-adjusted), with AI cohorts consuming 2x and 1,100+ AI-agent customers (+37% q/q), you are paid to wait for February. Counted once, not twice — it anchors to the same print as Value.

The risk is entirely equity: 38–49% vol, Sharpe ~0.1–0.6, two 30–48% drawdowns in 18 months. Watch unresolved debt-composition disclosure (2031 exchangeable notes) and FY26 SBC reporting anomaly.

**Bull Case**
- The Nov-3 print was an M&A accounting event misread as demand collapse: ARR guide raised, standalone cc guide held, deferred large deals land in H2, and Arize adds ~200bps — re-rating to 7x sales = $60–65 (+30–40%).
- Fortress economics with an aggressive, well-priced buyback: net cash, 26% FCF margin, ~9% of float authorized, management itself buying at $47.38.
- Structural AI-observability option: Arize + CrowdStrike partnership + 2x-consuming AI cohorts, against a relative valuation 36% below Datadog on growth-adjusted sales.

**Bear Case**
- The growth engine lost a cylinder: net-new ARR flat at $70M, NRR down to 109%, COGS outgrowing revenue, ARR growth below revenue growth — and the H2 recovery is a promise, not an observation.
- SBC-adjusted owner earnings of ~$215–250M yield ~1.5–1.8% against a 5.13% risk-free rate; buybacks largely neutralize dilution rather than retire stock.
- Term-premium shock to a 5.75–6% 10Y de-rates long-duration software another 20–25% regardless of fundamentals → $33–36.

**Key Risks**
1. **Second consecutive flat NNARR quarter (Feb 8)** — falsifies the thesis; the stock becomes a 12–13% grower at a full multiple.
2. **Arize integration without a permanent CFO** — $915M at ~36x ARR, 175bps dilution, talent-retention risk, goodwill now >52% of equity (impairment exposure).
3. **Gross margin below 80% for two quarters** — the short analyst's tripwire; signals pricing/infrastructure-cost pressure.
4. **Structural share shift to Datadog** + hyperscaler bundling/OpenTelemetry commoditising the instrumentation layer.
5. **June-2026 source-code incident second-order effects** — a customer compromise traceable to DT infrastructure breaks the trust brand.
6. **Rate/dollar**: 10Y through 5.31% or DXY extending +3% hits the multiple and reported growth mechanically.

**Portfolio Construction Note**

DT is a **high-idiosyncratic-risk, high-duration asset** — R² 0.023 renders beta hedging ineffective and ~60%+ of DCF value sits in the terminal year, making it the book's most rate-sensitive position. Size it on idiosyncratic risk budget, not beta. Given a composite conviction of ~46/100 and binary February catalysts, hold a **half-position at most**; the disciplined adds are ≤$41–42 (the bottom of the SBC-adjusted fair range) and trims >$60. Do not carry full size into Feb 8 without evidence of NNARR re-acceleration (watch Sept–Dec search intent and the CrowdStrike attach rate). Treat any further 10Y rise above 5.31% as a mechanical de-rating trigger independent of fundamentals.
