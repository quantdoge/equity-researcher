# Macroeconomic Assessment: Salesforce, Inc. (CRM)
**Prepared:** October 2026 | **Data as of:** Oct 2–6, 2026 (FRED)

## 1. Macro Environment Summary
**Stance: Transitional (soft-landing attempted, reflation risk building)**

The US economy is growing moderately — real GDP expanded at a **+2.2% annualized pace in Q2 2026** (GDPC1: $24,408B, up from $24,274B in Q1) — but the disinflationary glide path has broken down. Headline CPI is back to **~3.0–3.4% YoY (Aug 2026)**, above the Fed's 2% target, with monthly readings re-accelerating. The Fed cut the funds rate from the 5.33% peak (through 2024) to **~3.63–3.75% effective** by mid-2026, but the **September 2026 observation ticked back up to 3.75%** — a signal the easing cycle has stalled as inflation reasserts itself.

The dominant macro feature is the **bear steepening in rates**: the 10-year Treasury broke through 5% on September 14, 2026 (DGS10: ~5.0–5.3% in early October, ~80bp above the late-February low per TD Economics), driven by term-premium and fiscal-deficit concerns rather than growth strength. The 2s10s spread is **+34bp (no inversion)**, so the curve is not signaling recession — it is signaling **inflation and supply-side risk premia**. The broad USD index sits at **121.4 (Oct 2, 2026)**, up ~2–3% from mid-2026 levels. For a long-duration, high-multiple SaaS equity, this backdrop is the single biggest headwind: elevated discount rates compress recurring-revenue valuations, which is exactly what CRM's ~50% drawdown from its Dec-2024 peak ($363 → ~$164 trough in Apr 2026) has reflected.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **Neutral-to-Unfavourable**

The macro mix is a coin flip that currently leans negative for this specific asset: **modestly cutting Fed + no recession + AI demand tailwind pull in CRM's favor, but a 5%+ 10-year, re-accelerating CPI, and firm USD are directly toxic to long-duration software multiples.** CRM's strong balance sheet, FCF, and buyback capacity cap the downside that would hit levered peers, but if long rates stay at 5%+, the valuation re-rating is capped regardless of execution.

## 5. Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise: **"Base Effects Gone Wrong" (Bear) — or the Bull Case**

**Bear:** Tariff-driven CPI re-acceleration to ~3.5-4% forces the Fed to *pause-and-hold or hike* into 2027. The 10-year breaks decisively above 5.5% (term premium widens on deficit funding needs), the 2s10s steepens further, and equity multiples re-rate down ~15-25% across software. CRM — beta 1.26, still ~37% below its prior peak, with growth investors already underweight — would be among the hardest-hit mega-cap software names (multiple compression to ~4-5x EV/FCF territory), and its reported revenue growth (~8-10%) would look worse on a constant-currency basis with a stronger dollar.

**Bull (watch item):** If the Fed's pause proves temporary — CPI falls back toward 2.5% on cooling shelter/energy, the 10Y normalizes toward ~4%, and the curve un-inverts without a recession — long-duration SaaS multiples re-expand disproportionately. CRM's AI/Agentforce bundle adoption (expanded Oct-2026, driving ARR attachment) is the idiosyncratic accelerant that would convert a macro multiple re-rating into a compound re-rate. The recent ~25% bounce off the April low shows the sensitivity of this equity to even the *prospect* of a softer rate regime.

**Key monitorables:** Sept/Oct 2026 CPI prints (3.0% is the battle line for the Fed's re-pause), 10Y auction demand/term premium (5.3% breakout = bear trigger), and monthly USD index trend (121–122 resistance).
