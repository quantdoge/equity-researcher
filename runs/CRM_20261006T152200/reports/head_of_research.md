### Investment Memo: CRM (Salesforce) — October 6, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (initiate small, laddered position for fresh capital; do not sell existing) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: ~$235 base case (+3% from $228); downside $175–185 (−19% to −23%); bull ~$300+ (+32%) on agentic-AI re-acceleration. Strategic entry zone for fresh capital: $170–185.
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**: Salesforce is a wide-moat, cash-generative category leader (77.7% gross margin, 21.5% operating margin, $14.4B FCF, A+/A1 credit) that has already been repriced ~50% off its Dec-2024 peak on AI-disruption fears — the bear case is substantially in the tape. The mature seat-based core grows ~9–10%, but Agentforce/Data Cloud (~$1.2–1.5B and ~$3.4–3.9B ARR, +200–330% YoY) is a credible, underpriced re-acceleration option. At ~29x P/E and 6.6% FCF yield, the price is approximately fair value with zero margin of safety; demand momentum is cooling into the Dec 2 print and a 5%+ 10-year caps multiple re-rating. Net: a wonderful business at a fair price — HOLD and scale in opportunistically, not a conviction buy at spot.

**Bull Case**
- Agentic-AI is monetizing where peers are still announcing: Agentforce ~$1.2–1.5B ARR (+205–330% YoY), >8,000 deals, AI bundles expanded Oct-2026 — an option consensus barely prices; FY27 guide already raised twice in a year.

- Most of the bear case is already in the price: −50% from peak, short thesis rejected at this level, analyst targets ($266–315) sit 10–35% above spot; if the Fed's pause proves temporary and the 10Y normalizes below ~4.5%, long-duration SaaS multiples re-expand disproportionately (macro notes the recent ~25% bounce off April lows as evidence of sensitivity).

**Bear Case**
- "SaaSpocalypse" is not over: if AI agents replace seats rather than augment them, per-seat pricing deflates even as bookings grow; Microsoft Copilot/Dynamics bundling and AI-native entrants attack the same budget. NRR slipping below 100% converts the 6.6% FCF yield into a no-growth asset.
- Demand momentum is demonstrably cooling: Agentforce search −70% from peak, top-line tracking at/below consensus heading into Dec 2; core growth guidance (7–8% FY26) shows a deceleration floor risk; consensus FY28 EPS (~$16 on ~$51B revenue) assumes unbroken margin expansion plus buyback accretion.
- Balance-sheet reflexivity: ~$27.2B single-quarter buyback drove net debt from ~$9.8B toward ~$33B in ~6 months (interest toward $1.5–1.9B/yr); the consensus EPS bridge requires the very leverage the balance sheet can no longer comfortably support — a buyback pause would remove the price floor and likely trigger the S&P negative-outlook flag.
