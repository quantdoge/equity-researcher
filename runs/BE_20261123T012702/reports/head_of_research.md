**Investment Thesis**

Bloom Energy is a real business with a genuinely scarce product — firm, on-site power delivered in 9–12 months into a 5–7-year grid queue — and the operating momentum is exceptional (+138% revenue in Q3'26, three guidance raises, gross margin 12.4%→33.8%). But the equity at $142 prices in flawless conversion of a $20B *unaudited* backlog that the auditor recognizes as only ~$493M of RPO, through a related-party-heavy revenue base, with negative FCF in the quarter of record GAAP profit, a China-controlled single-input supply chain now at the center of a federal securities class action, and a 4+ beta into a hostile rate regime. Our own optimist's fair value ($100–115) sits below the market price: new capital is not being paid for the risk. This is a quality-and-valuation SELL, not a fraud call — the forensic models (Beneish –2.52, four years of negative accruals) actively reject the fraud thesis, and solvency is not in question.

**Bull Case**
- **Contracted scarcity with unusual visibility**: ~$20B backlog (~4.4x FY26 revenue), Oracle 2.8 GW, AEP $2.65B, Brookfield project financing; FY26 guide $4.4–4.6B (+122%) with a credible path to $7B+ in 2027.
- **Operating leverage finally inflecting**: GM 12.4%→33.8% in four years, three straight GAAP-profitable quarters, FY26 non-GAAP op margin ~22%; VRT (36% GM, 18% OM) marks where margins could go. Q4'25 proved the seasonal FCF pattern can deliver +$395M.
- **Balance sheet and policy tailwinds**: net-cash-ish with 0% converts to 2029/30; fuel cells kept the 30% 48E ITC when wind/solar lost theirs; $3.10 gas and US manufacturing are relative shelters. A fast disinflation break (CPI ≤3% by Q1'27) could drive +70–100% in six months on this beta.

**Bear Case**
- **No valuation floor**: even the optimist's fair value is below the price; ~9.4x FY26 sales vs peers at 2.7–6.3x; P/FCF >600x; the entire case rests on FY27–30 estimates with 3x dispersion.
- **Earnings quality unresolved at the worst time**: record GAAP profit and –$181M FCF in the same quarter; backlog-to-RPO ~40x; 44–74% related-party revenue; $230M/yr SBC excluded from the headline EPS; four CFOs in two years; $81M insider selling, zero buying.
- **Single-point failures everywhere**: Chinese scandium chokehold (~220t needed vs ~240t global supply) plus a live class action; one customer ~73% of a quarter's revenue; flagship projects permit-gated (Jupiter to 2029, AEP to 2030); a 4+ beta into a bear-steepening tape.

**Key Risks**
1. **AI-capex pause** — a single-digit number of hyperscaler counterparties drives the growth; a pause hits growth rate and multiple simultaneously (the Nov 5 tape: –16.4% in a day).
2. **Second disclosure event** — SEC inquiry, delayed 10-K, restatement, or CBP/EAPA origin detainer; the class period means any new adverse disclosure carries a litigation amplifier.
3. **Scandium supply interruption or MACR/FEOC credit disqualification** — physically caps the 5 GW ramp and impairs customer project IRRs.
4. **Customer renegotiation / dual-sourcing** by the ~73% related-party customer, or evidence Brookfield JV revenue accrues ahead of energization (revenue-recognition event, not just concentration).
5. **Rates/credit** — 10Y to 6% with HY at 5% puts BE at $70–85; a 10% market drawdown implies ≈–42% for BE at beta 4.2.
6. **Dilution** — 295M fully diluted vs 240M basic; converts + SBC continue to tax per-share compounding.
