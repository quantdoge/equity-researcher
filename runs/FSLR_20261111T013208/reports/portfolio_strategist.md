## 4. Portfolio Fit Analysis

**Sector concentration — adds, does not diversify.** Correlation to TAN **0.72**, NXT 0.54, ENPH 0.48. Any existing clean-energy/solar sleeve makes FSLR a doubling-down, not a new exposure. Only against a **hydrocarbons** book is it genuinely diversifying (XLE −0.16) — and even then it is not a hedge, because the driver is policy, not crude.

**Factor exposure — three unintended tilts:**
1. **Short momentum, long value.** 12-1 momentum −19.8%, revisions −10.3% (direction only; the magnitude is a period-mismatch artifact per Quant). Buying FSLR is a deliberate anti-momentum value bet — do not book it as core.
2. **Hidden long duration.** Macro's estimate: ~60+ years effective duration, ~−0.66%/bp of 10Y. FSLR is functionally a **long-duration bond proxy wearing an equity ticker.** Adding it alongside other long-duration growth compounds an exposure most books size separately.
3. **Bottom-quartile low-vol.** 53% vol, 4-of-4 worst in cohort. In any vol-targeted or risk-parity sleeve this is the single most expensive 1% you can own.

**Correlation / hedgeability.** R² to SPY is **0.12–0.16** — 85% of variance is idiosyncratic policy and tariff headlines. **Beta hedging will not work here.** Size is the only risk control available; that is precisely why the 2% cap is hard.

**Concentration within the name.** ~90% revenue in one jurisdiction, ~80% of EBITDA from one statute, one CEO since 2016, one critical mineral (tellurium, ~60% China-refined) capping nameplate at ~20 GWdc. No diversification benefit accrues *inside* the position.

**Liquidity: not a constraint** ($19.5B cap, index member, 2–9M ADV). No execution risk.
