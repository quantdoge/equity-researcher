**Investment Thesis**

First Solar is a fortress balance sheet attached to a levered bet on US industrial policy. Roughly **80% of FY26 guided EBITDA ($2.10–2.19bn of ~$2.65bn) is the Section 45X tax credit** — a real, cash-monetisable subsidy that statutorily decays 100/75/50/25/0% across 2030–33. Revenue (−23% y/y in Q3'26), shipments (guided 16–17 GW, cut twice), and backlog (50.1 → 44.0 GW) are all contracting while margins expand — the mechanical signature of rising credit intensity, not pricing power. Two independent methodologies (Value SOTP, forensic PV-of-credits) converge on **$140–150/share**, and the probability-weighted value (~$176) sits *below* spot. The accounting is clean (Beneish −2.47, negative accruals) — this is a valuation and policy-decay call, not a fraud call.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is bifurcated — global module manufacturing is in depression (JKS/CSIQ loss-making at ~$0.10/W), while the US-protected segment prices at ~3x global ASPs with a legislated floor (Section 232 MIP $0.38/W, effective 4-Dec-26). FSLR is the strongest player in the advantaged sub-segment. Macro is **Unfavourable**: 10Y at 4.88% is term-premium-driven (won't fall on a Fed pause), and FSLR has behaved like a ~60-year-duration asset (−0.66% of equity value per bp; −46% Jun→Sep 2026 as the 10Y rose 70bp). Earnings are protected by policy; the multiple is hostage to rates.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH risk.** ~90% of revenue in one jurisdiction, ~55% of unit economics from one statute. Live 10b-5 class action (*Day v. First Solar*, E.D.N.Y.) with a plaintiff-friendly history ($350m prior settlement); AMBER customs-origin flag on the SC "finishing" operation amid 249% India AD/CVD and a DOJ trade-fraud priority; tellurium (~60% China-refined) is an unhedged single-input tail risk. No FCPA/sanctions exposure.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: **SELL.** Moat is narrow and *statutorily narrowing* from 2030. Ex-credit ROIC ~6% vs ~12.5% cost of equity (2022's clean year: 2.7% gross margin). Intrinsic value ~$145; margin-of-safety entry ~$109–115. Trailing 12.7x P/E is an illusion — ~36–40x on core ex-credit earnings.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: **BUY (moderate)** — the dissenting voice. 10.3x FY26E / 7.8x FY27E, ~6–8% FCF yield, Rule of 40 = 47, PEG 0.43, perovskite pilot H1'27; targets $255–270. But concedes revenue growth is gone (FY26 −8%), backlog covers only ~2.3 years, revisions are negative, and consensus EPS *peaks* in FY29 and declines into the 45X phase-down — a "policy-margin compounder masquerading as a growth stock."
- **Quantitative Signals**: Neutral, negative tilt. Top-quartile value and quality (F-Score 7/9, negative accruals) cannot offset momentum −19.8%, negative revisions, worst-in-cohort 53–57% vol, Sharpe ~0, and −34% one-year alpha. R² to SPY of 0.12–0.16 means returns are ~85% idiosyncratic — **beta hedging does not work here; size is the only risk control.**
- **Financial Risk**: **Credit LOW** — $2.3bn net cash, 36x interest cover, 2.67x current ratio, no maturity wall; survives a 40% revenue shock without stress. **Equity risk MODERATE-HIGH**: 57% vol, −53% max drawdown, earnings policy-funded.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH (policy-driven, not integrity-driven).** Controls, board and enforcement history are clean — but 16 insider sales and **zero open-market purchases** (~$7.5m) into a 25% drawdown, the Malaysia 1.8 GW "binary decision" (impairment risk vs $5.7bn PP&E), the Swift Current warranty suit, and buyback-into-guidance-cut optics weigh on the rating.
- **ESG**: Neutral. Genuine environmental leader (polysilicon-free CdTe, >95% recycling recovery, net-zero-water India plant); the 2023 Malaysia forced-labour finding was remediated properly. Governance *disclosure credibility* is the live flaw. Tellurium availability caps scaling at ~20 GWdc — flagged as under-priced.
- **Alternative Data**: **BEARISH.** Revenue surprises deteriorating three consecutive quarters (+0.9% → −0.6% → −4.5%) while EPS beats mask it; the Blue Wave catalyst was priced in one day and erased in four (failed-catalyst pattern); 67k calls bought on 4-Nov are underwater; Google Trends at multi-year lows. Business activity (hiring, bookings) is the only constructive leg.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **SHORT (moderate).** Explicitly *not* a fraud thesis — forensics exonerate the books. The thesis: paying $17.1bn EV for a decaying annuity; SOTP $140–150 (−17% to −23%). Key warnings: 10.8% short interest makes this a crowded squeeze trap; a durable Section 232 price floor enabling above-import-parity pricing post-2029 is the single variable that kills the short.

**Bull Case**
- **Section 232 durability**: the $0.38/W floor (4-Dec-26) sits *above* FSLR's ~$0.36/W US ASP — a legislated moat for the only scaled non-Chinese supply chain; if it proves durable post-2029, ex-credit earnings of $1bn+ support $240+/share.
- **Multi-year contracted visibility**: 44 GW / $13.5bn backlog through 2030 at ~$0.31/W, 45X at full rate through 2029, net cash $2.3bn, $500m buyback — a real valuation floor.
- **Asymmetric rate sensitivity cuts both ways**: if CPI breaks and the 10Y returns to ~4.2%, the stock has already demonstrated it can travel $170→$318 in nine months; perovskite pilot data (H1'27) is a free option.

**Bear Case**
- **The subsidy expires**: 80% of EBITDA is a statute stepping down 2030–33; ex-credit gross margin has never exceeded the mid-teens — no evidence FSLR can replace the credit with pricing.
- **Volume/backlog erosion now**: revenue −23% y/y, backlog −12%, guidance cut twice in 2026, customer deferrals on 232/FEOC uncertainty; 2028 demand cliff as 45Y/48E projects must be placed in service.
- **Rate ceiling**: a 10Y move to 5.5% (barely above the 5.31% touched in October) implies $130–155 on the observed sensitivity.

**Key Risks**
1. **Policy binary (both directions)**: FEOC "material assistance" tracing (50%→85% by 2030), a future Congress re-opening 45X, or adverse customs-origin enforcement on SC finishing. Mitigant: Democratic sweep lowers near-term repeal risk.
2. **Litigation**: MTD denial in *Day v. First Solar*; Swift Current warranty discovery alleging testing deception — a bankability shock to the franchise if it lands.
3. **Tellurium supply** (~60% China-refined; announced capacity exceeds the ~20 GWdc availability cap by ~25%).
4. **Volatility/squeeze**: 57% vol, 10.8% short interest, beta instability — position sizing error is the biggest portfolio-level risk in either direction.
