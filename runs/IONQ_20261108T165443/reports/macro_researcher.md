## 1. Macro Environment Summary

**Stance: Transitional → tilting RISK-OFF.** As of 8 Nov 2026 the US is in a *re-tightening* regime, not an easing one. The Fed cut ~150bp from Sept 2025 to a 3.63% trough (May–Aug 2026), then **reversed and hiked to 3.88%** (Sept and Oct 2026) as headline CPI re-accelerated from a 2.4% Feb-2026 low to 4.2% in May and 3.7% in Sept — a classic tariff/pass-through overshoot that never fully normalised. Critically, **the 2Y at 4.74% sits 86bp *above* the effective funds rate**: the market is pricing further hikes, not cuts. Growth and labour are still firm (Q3 real GDP +3.4% SAAR, unemployment 4.1%), so there is no recession signal to rescue duration assets. The move in the long end, however, is *not* inflation-driven — 10Y breakevens are anchored at 2.23%, so the 5.13% 10Y embeds a **~2.90% real yield**, a term-premium/fiscal-supply repricing. That is the single most punishing variable for a 3.3-beta, cash-burning, long-duration pure play. Credit is starting to corroborate: HY OAS widened **+88bp (2.60% → 3.48%) since 28 Aug 2026** — still tight in absolute terms, but the direction is a rotation out of risk.

## 3. Company-Specific Macro Impact

**Negative — real discount rate (dominant channel).** IONQ's value is almost entirely terminal — ~27x FY26 guided revenue ($505–515m) with an adjusted loss of $0.29/share in Q3-26. Empirically, the stock fell **−34% while the 10Y rose 75bp** (26 Jun → 8 Nov 2026), implying an equity duration of roughly **40–45 years** — i.e. **~40%+ of fair value per 100bp of long-yield rise**. With the long end still steepening on term premium, this is the binding constraint.

**Negative — cost of equity capital / dilution.** The company funds itself with equity, not debt, so refinancing risk is minimal but **issuance risk is acute**. Negative carry, SkyWater foundry capex and GAAP margin drag mean repeated raises; each raise at $30 is ~2.5x more dilutive per dollar than at $78. A depressed share price also weakens IONQ's acquisition currency.

**Negative — tariff-driven input inflation.** CPI re-acceleration is concentrated in goods/tariff categories. SkyWater makes IONQ a *hardware manufacturer*, so components, semiconductor equipment and materials are now inflating at 3.7%+. Hardware-heavy revenue (systems vs. high-margin cloud/algorithm revenue) compresses gross margin precisely when the multiple is under pressure.

**Negative — firm dollar.** Broad USD +3.1% since early August. IONQ distributes via AWS Braket, Azure Quantum and Google Cloud and sells systems internationally (Europe, Japan, Korea); a stronger dollar is a modest translation and price-competitiveness headwind.

**Neutral — credit conditions.** HY widening to 3.48% is a tight, not stressed, level; IONQ has no material debt. The widening matters only as a *rotation signal* — high-beta/long-duration equities are the first funding source cut — and as a lead indicator the re-tightening is biting.

**Mildly positive — policy/fiscal, not monetary.** Backlog $1.2bn with RPO +$715m in one quarter is largely government and strategic-enterprise demand, which is driven by the fiscal/defence budget and tech-sovereignty policy, not the funds rate. The Treasury's proposed expansion of outbound investment restrictions (COINS Act, Nov 2026) covering quantum, AI and supercomputing is a structural tailwind: it walls off US capital from Chinese quantum rivals and entrenches domestic champions. Higher short rates also mean modestly better interest income on IONQ's large treasury balance.

**Net:** the monetary channel overwhelms the fiscal and idiosyncratic channels. Note that this is a *de-rating, not an operational deterioration* — revenue tripled (+336% YoY to $173.8m) and guidance was raised, yet the stock made a new 52-week low of $26.43 on 5 Nov 2026. That divergence is the macro discount rate at work.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **UNFAVOURABLE**

Beta 3.30, ~40–45 year equity duration, 27x sales, negative earnings, equity-dependent funding, and hardware/tariff cost exposure — against a Fed that is *hiking*, a front end pricing more hikes, a 5.13% 10Y driven by real yields and term premium, and credit spreads widening. Every major macro variable is misaligned with the business model simultaneously. The one offsetting force (strategic fiscal support for US quantum) is real but cannot outrun a ~2.9% real cost of capital.

## 5. The Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise

**Bull surprise: the re-tightening is a policy error, and the Fed pivots abruptly in H1-2027.** With unemployment at 4.1%, HY spreads already +88bp and the 2Y 86bp above funds, the market is priced for hikes into a slowing economy. If Q4-26/Q1-27 activity rolls over and CPI mean-reverts toward 2.5% (tariff pass-through proving one-off, as breakevens at 2.23% imply), the Fed would have to reverse fast to ~3.0%. A **~100bp decline in the 10Y toward ~4.1%** would, on IONQ's ~40-year empirical duration, be worth **+50–70% of upside** purely from re-rating — on top of a 336%-growth revenue base and a sold-out Superion 256 order book through 2027. That combination (falling real yields + tripled revenue + a J-curve into 2028 profitability) is the setup in which this name re-tests $70–80. The mirror-image tail is a term-premium spike pushing the 30Y to 6% and forcing an equity raise at $25 — a dilution spiral that would take the stock to the high teens. I weight the pivot scenario at ~30%, the tail at ~20%, and grind-lower consolidation at ~50%.
