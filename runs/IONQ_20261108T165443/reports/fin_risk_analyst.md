### 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: UNRATED — de facto Investment Grade on liquidity / solvency; Distress-grade on earnings quality.**

The dichotomy is the whole story. IonQ has effectively **no debt**: total debt $154.3M vs equity $5.02B (D/E 0.031), and net cash of ~$1.55B at 9/30/26, rising to **~$3.55B pro forma** for the $2.0B equity offering that closed 2026-10-16. There is **no refinancing cliff** — long-term debt is only $56.8M. Default risk over any realistic horizon is negligible.

But the *earnings* are venture-stage: LTM operating loss **−$1.28B** on LTM revenue **$380M** (−338% operating margin), and no credit rating or CDS exists to price the risk (likely unrated, sub-scale for agency coverage). Interest coverage is **not meaningful** (FY2025 interest expense = $0; Q3'26 annualised interest ~$9M against negative EBIT).

At that acquisition cadence, liquidity would be exhausted in **under 4 quarters** without the October raise. The $2.0B equity offering was effectively a refill of what M&A consumed — a serial-dilution funding model, not a self-funding one.

### 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

- **Solvency risk: LOW** — net cash $3.55B pro forma; no maturities; current ratio >4.
- **Financial/equity risk: HIGH** — 100%+ volatility, 3.6 beta, 68% observed drawdowns, negative FCF, 55.6% of assets in intangibles, 36% annual share-count growth.

The Altman Z of 0.758 must be read with care: it is driven by negative EBIT and accumulated deficit (−$1.69B retained earnings) and, critically, **x4 uses book equity, ignoring the $1.7B cash pile and $14B market cap**. For a pre-profit, cash-rich ramp story the score is structurally misleading. The F-Score of 3/9 is the more honest signal of operational quality.

### 6. Key Red Flags

2. **Cash burn acceleration** — quarterly FCF: −$83M (Q4'25) → −$159M → −$114M → **−$332M** (Q3'26); 9M'26 FCF −$605M.
3. **Gross-margin collapse** 63% → 40% → 22% as revenue mixes toward low-margin foundry/hardware. Revenue up 336% YoY but gross profit up only ~99%.
4. **Goodwill/intangibles 55.6% of assets** — $4.37B from SkyWater/ID Quantique/Oxford Ionics/Elisity; tangible equity $657M. An impairment would hit book equity, not cash.
5. **Earnings quality** — Q3'26 GAAP net income **+$564M** vs operating loss −$433M, entirely from non-cash warrant mark-to-market; do not read GAAP EPS as profitability.
6. **SBC at $524M LTM (138% of revenue)** — compensation funded by dilution, not cash flow.
7. **Altman Z 0.758 (Distress) / F-Score 3 (Weak)** — both structurally poor.
8. **Data inconsistencies (verified):** FY2025 net income −$510.4M in the income statement vs −$601.3M in the cash flow statement; market cap $14.0B per profile vs $9.14B used in the FCF-yield tool (the −3.28% tool output understates burn yield; −4.9% is the correct LTM figure).
