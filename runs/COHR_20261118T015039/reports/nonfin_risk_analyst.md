## Non-Financial Risk Assessment — Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR)

**Snapshot:** Photonics/optics supplier to AI data centers. Market cap ~$41.5B, price ~$210.86, beta 2.23. FY26 record revenue and margin expansion; Q1 FY27 revenue $2.37B. Stock has swung from ~$306 (peak, mid‑2026) to ~$170 (a ~43% drawdown in a month) and back to ~$210 — a high-beta policy-and-narrative stock.

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: ADEQUATE (positive structure, yellow-flag pay)

**Evidence supporting:**
- **Independent Chair, no CEO duality.** Enrico DiGirolamo has served as independent Chair since June 2024; Jim Anderson is CEO/board member only. Clean separation of power.
- **Board independence verified.** All four Class Three nominees are independent, no overboarding, no family ties, no non-independent directors on key committees: Corasanti (Nominating chair, ex-public-company CEO), Hatter (cybersecurity executive — Palo Alto Networks, McAfee, Intel), Skaggs (Audit & Risk chair, designated audit committee financial expert), Vij (Compensation).
- **96% prior-year say-on-pay support**; FY26 proxy: all directors FOR, auditor (EY) ratified. No restatement or audit failure disclosed.

**Evidence against / watch items:**
- **Classified board** (three-year staggered terms through 2029) — structural entrenchment, limits shareholder refresh.
- **Pay magnitude and optics:** CEO FY26 total comp **$28.3M**, only 3.9% fixed salary, 84% stock; **pay ratio 1,381:1**. A further **$50M target-value award** was disclosed via 8-K (Aug 27, 2026), plus large special FY27 PSU grants — dilution and optics risk into the **Nov 18, 2026** annual meeting.
- **"Adjusted" metrics inflate payout:** annual bonus was scored on adjusted revenue ($7,288M vs. $7,120M reported) and adjusted profit, lifting payout from ~176% to 190% of target **without explanation**. This is the single most important governance watch item — pay-outs measured on non-reportable figures that management itself defines.
- **Peer group set above Coherent's size** (ranked ~23rd percentile of chosen peers) and CEO target bonus raised 150%→165%; PSUs rely on a single relative-TSR measure. Mitigant: +611% 3-yr TSR vs. +197% peer median means incentives were clearly *earned*.

---

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: MODERATE–HIGH (policy-dependent, no enforcement)

- **No SEC enforcement actions, DOJ investigations, or EDGAR full-text hits** for COHR. Clean on active enforcement.
- **FCC / White House ban on new Chinese optical transceivers** is the dominant binary policy variable. Drafting reported Aug 2026; implementation repeatedly contested (Microsoft lobbied the White House directly; a "speed threshold and a date years away" caveat). Coherent is a *beneficiary* (share shift from Chinese module makers), and the stock moves 8–13% on headlines — meaning **policy upside is already partially in the price** and reversal risk is symmetric. Second-order risk: Chinese retaliation or export-control action against a US photonics firm with China-based operations.
- **Subsidy compliance:** $50M CHIPS Program letter of intent (June 2026) and $14M Texas Semiconductor Innovation Fund grant for InP expansion — carry milestone, reporting, and clawback conditions.
- **Litigation:** No active securities class action at plaintiff firms (Rosen's COHR page shows "no active cases right now"; prior solicitation is stale). Docket shows only a securities/commodities suit (Jan 2025) and an FMLA employment suit (May 2026) — routine, non-material.
- **No COHR-specific pending approvals**; the notable regulatory clock is third-party — **Marvell/Lumentum** (close targeted H1 CY2028, $3.2B break fee, China/antitrust clearances), which would create a better-capitalized direct competitor.

### 3. Insider Activity Analysis: NET SELLING (but a meaningful November reversal)

- **Past 6 months: ~10 open-market sales (~$9.2M) vs. 2 purchases** — net seller in dollar terms.
- Notable sellers: **CFO Sherri Luther** 1,000 sh @ $306.68 (Jul 22); **Director Lisa Neal-Graves** 2,200 sh @ $268.42 (Sep 2, *not* under a 10b5-1 plan — discretionary); a director "Enrico" sold ~$1.63M @ $305.99 plus gifted shares, cutting direct ownership ~38%.
- **Counter-signal (important):** CEO **Anderson and Chair DiGirolamo bought a combined ~$1.22M of stock on Nov 9–10**, immediately after the drawdown. Open-market cluster buying by the two most informed insiders is a stronger signal than routine diversification sales — and the chair re-entering after selling near $306 suggests prior sales were price-driven, not conviction-driven.
- **Read-through:** selling was concentrated at $268–$306 (near highs); insiders are buying at ~$190. Net positive, but the earlier discretionary selling at peak prices warrants monitoring alongside the pending $50M CEO award.

---

### 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Extreme customer concentration in a handful of AI buyers.** Two customers each exceeded 10% of FY26 revenue (reported ~20%/12%, secondary-sourced); NVIDIA holds a **$2B equity stake** plus a multibillion-dollar non-exclusive purchase commitment; and the **$20B, five-year PhotonLink LTA** (average ~$4B/yr, ~42% of current annualized revenue) is with an **unnamed** customer with **no disclosed minimum volumes, take-or-pay terms, or cancellation provisions**, ramping only in **early CY2028**. Altman's Nov 2026 warning that AI suppliers are all planning around the same few buyers describes this exposure precisely.
2. **Capital-intensive capacity bet against $11.8B of non-cancellable commitments.** Q1 FY27 capex $775.5M; **operating cash flow −$92.7M** and FCF proxy ~−$868M; debt ~$4.0B vs. cash ~$1.75B; vendor purchase commitments ~$11.8B (June 30, 2026), non-cancellable. InP capacity is being doubled on the assumption the ramp arrives on schedule. Any demand air-pocket converts "growth capex" into stranded cost plus inventory. A **delayed Texas expansion** has already been referenced.
3. **Key-person and integration risk.** CEO Anderson (since June 2024) is the architect of the margin turnaround and the AI pivot — a short-tenure, high-dependency CEO with 84% stock-based pay (retention positive, but flight risk is a thesis-level risk). The II-VI/Coherent integration and continuing portfolio reshaping add execution load.
4. *(Supporting)* **Cybersecurity/IP exposure:** the company's own annual report states it **has experienced cybersecurity incidents and some resulted in data breaches** (none material to date). For an InP/photonics IP leader, exfiltration risk is strategically asymmetric.

---

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: MODERATE

- **Positives:** Named to *Forbes America's Best Companies 2027* for the second consecutive year; strong shareholder returns; no product recalls, consumer backlash, or ESG controversy identified.
- **Negatives / watch:** (a) The **1,381:1 pay ratio** and 661% YoY CEO pay jump are the kind of data point that attracts proxy advisors and press scrutiny, with large special awards queued for the Nov 18 meeting; (b) the company is increasingly **politically entangled** — a named winner in a US–China trade crackdown, which invites retaliation narratives and headline-driven volatility; (c) **sentiment/beta risk**: a 2.23 beta and 7–14% single-day moves mean brand/reputation is now hostage to an "AI bubble" narrative it does not control.

---

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Not Critical — no enforcement action, no restatement, no short-seller attack, no fraud finding, and governance structure is genuinely sound (independent chair, independent committees, 96% pay support). But the combination of **two >10% customers plus a $20B undisclosed-counterparty LTA**, **$11.8B of non-cancellable purchase obligations financed with negative operating cash flow and $4.0B of debt**, and **binary policy dependence** means a large share of the equity story rests on assumptions outside management's control. Governance execution (adjusted-metric bonus, $50M award) adds a modest, not disqualifying, discount.

---

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Contract quality of the $20B PhotonLink LTA — HIGHEST PRIORITY.** Customer identity, minimum volumes, cancellation/termination rights, pricing resets, and prepayment/deposit terms are undisclosed. The headline should be treated as total contract value, not backlog. Escalate to read the 10-Q/8-K directly before underwriting 2028 estimates.
2. **Liquidity/commitment mismatch.** $11.8B non-cancellable vendor commitments + $775M/qtr capex + negative operating cash flow + $4.0B debt. Escalate for covenant, maturity-wall, and inventory-obsolescence review. (One sell-side note frames **inventory as "the defining number"** for Coherent — consistent with record revenue and negative operating cash flow coexisting.)
3. **Compensation measured on non-GAAP "adjusted" revenue/profit above reported figures** — a potential incentive for aggressive revenue recognition. Pair with the negative operating cash flow and rising inventory; treat as a quality-of-earnings watch item, not yet a fraud indicator.
4. **Discretionary director/CFO sales at $268–$306** (including a non-10b5-1 sale) followed by CEO/Chair buying at ~$190 — monitor for any renewed selling into strength.
5. **Cyber incident history with materiality caveat** ("none material *thus far*") — obtain incident specifics and insurance/board-oversight posture.
