## SECTOR BRIEF — COHRITE / COHERENT CORP (COHR)
*Sector: Optical & Photonics Components (GICS: IT — Electronic Equipment & Components). Fiscal year ends 30 June. Note: the FMP profile text is stale — post-II-VI merger the listed entity *is* Coherent Corp.*

### 1. Sector Overview
Coherent competes in optical/photonics components — indium phosphide (InP) and EML laser sources, high-precision optics, datacom transceivers (800G/1.6T), and industrial lasers. The sector has bifurcated: the AI datacom leg is in hyper-growth (AI optical transceiver market ~$16.5bn in 2025 → ~$26bn in 2026, +57% per TrendForce; 1.6T to overtake 800G as the primary AI back-end port speed by 2027), while the industrial laser/instrumentation leg is flat-to-down (IPG Photonics revenue +2.7%, MKS +9.6%). Structure is moderately concentrated in the *component* layer (Coherent, Lumentum, and Chinese module vendors Innolight/Eoptolink dominate transceivers) but fragmented at the module assembly layer (Fabrinet, AAOI, dozens of Asian vendors). Barriers to entry are high at the materials layer — InP substrate/wafer capacity and EML yield are the recognised chokepoints (McKinsey models 800G 40–60% short of demand through 2027 and 1.6T 30–40% short through 2029) — and low at final module assembly.

**Porter's Five Forces (datacom leg):** Buyer power **very high** (5–6 hyperscalers/NVIDIA absorb the bulk of volume — Sam Altman publicly warned in Nov-2026 that suppliers are all planning around the same few buyers); supplier power **high but favouring vertically integrated players** (InP, EML, DSP); new entry **moderate** (Chinese capital, but US policy may foreclose); rivalry **high** (share battles on 800G pricing); substitution **rising** (co-packaged optics is a multi-year threat to pluggables).

### 2. Key Tailwinds
- **AI cluster interconnect demand outrunning supply.** Sector TAM +57% in 2026; 800G/1.6T modules remain in structural shortfall through 2027–2029. COHR's AI-datacom revenue is now the swing factor, and management guides FY27 pro-forma revenue growth **>60%**, targeting a first **$3bn quarterly** revenue milestone.
- **US policy risk working *for* US suppliers.** Washington is reportedly advancing a ban on imports of Chinese optical transceivers, which lifted COHR +8%, AAOI +7%, LITE +4% on 17-Nov-2026. A ban would hand share and pricing power to Coherent/Lumentum/AAOI. (Microsoft is lobbying against — binary catalyst.)
- **Mix shift to higher-value optical content.** Optical links *between* data centres exceeded **$200m in Q1 FY27, +53% QoQ and ~4x YoY** (PhotonLink), on top of 1.6T transceiver ramps and 800G growth — content per AI cluster is rising faster than cluster count.

### 3. Key Headwinds
- **Extreme customer concentration and AI-capex sentiment fragility.** The SOX index fell 12% in a single session in Nov-2026; COHR fell from $440 (Jun-26) to $170 intraday on 5-Nov-26 — a **–52% peak-to-trough drawdown** despite record results. Beta 2.23 makes the equity a levered proxy on hyperscaler capex headlines.
- **Price competition from Chinese module vendors** (Innolight, Eoptolink) plus low-cost contract manufacturing (Fabrinet) caps transceiver gross margin structurally; gross margin at the module layer is a fraction of the component layer.
- **Industrial/industrial-laser end markets remain in a shallow down-cycle** — IPGP revenue –1.6% (FY24) then +2.7% (FY25) with a 1.3% operating margin. This drags Coherent's blended growth versus pure-play AI peers.

### 5. Target Company's Competitive Position: **STRONG**
- **Scale leadership:** $7.1bn revenue is the largest in the photonics peer set (ex-Ciena) — ~2.4x Lumentum and ~1.5x Fabrinet — spanning datacom, industrial, instrumentation and semiconductor capital equipment.
- **Vertical integration in the scarce layer:** in-house InP/EML and precision optics ownership is precisely where the 2026–2029 supply bottleneck sits, which underpins the margin trajectory (gross margin 30.9% FY24 → 37.5% FY26 → 39.1% Q1 FY27; operating margin 2.0% → 12.7% → 15.9%).
- **Execution and deleveraging:** FY26 net income swung to +$805m from $49m, interest expense down from $288m (FY24) to $190m (FY26), EPS diluted $4.12 vs –$1.84 two years ago.
- **Two blemishes:** (i) growth *rate* trails Lumentum/AAOI on a reported basis — it is not the purest AI-datacom exposure; (ii) it is not the technology leader in co-packaged optics, the medium-term substitution risk. Both justify the P/S discount to LITE but not the full 65%.

### 6. Sector Rating: **ATTRACTIVE**
Fundamentally the sector is in the **early-to-mid stages of an AI-driven capex upcycle** — 800G/1.6T demand exceeds supply through 2027+, pricing is firm, and policy may remove the low-cost Chinese competitor set. The industrial-laser leg is mature/late-cycle but is shrinking as a share of the mix, so blended growth and margins are inflecting upward. The one genuine caution is that **in valuation sentiment the sector is late-cycle**: multiples have already repriced violently (LITE –40% in a month; COHR –52% from its June peak) and the entire demand chain rests on the capex plans of ~5 buyers. Net: attractive on 12–24 month fundamentals, but with equity risk that is beta-2+ and headline-driven — position sizing matters more than direction.
