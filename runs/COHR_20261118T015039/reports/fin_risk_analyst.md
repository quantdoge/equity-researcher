## 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **Sub-Investment Grade (BB), improving**

Fitch affirmed Coherent at **BB with a Positive Outlook (Feb 2026)**, i.e. the top tier of high yield, two notches below IG. Critically, the sub-IG rating is **not** a leverage story: leverage is now ~1.1–1.3x net. It reflects (a) end-market cyclicality, (b) an aggressive, debt-and-equity-funded capex ramp, and (c) customer concentration. The trajectory is strongly positive: EBIT/interest has gone 0.49x (FY24) → 2.27x (FY25) → 5.46x (FY26) → 8.7x annualised (Q1 FY27).

**Verdict: no near-term solvency problem; the risk is funding the build-out, not servicing existing debt.** No material maturities before **Dec 2029** ($990M 5.000% senior notes) and the **July 2029** Term Loan B-3 — this is the single most important mitigant, since it gives management ~3 years to either complete the ramp or throttle capex. *(S&P/Moody's ratings and CDS spreads not retrieved — data gap.)*

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis

Base case uses management's FY27 framing (>60% pro forma growth → ~$10.2B revenue), Q1 FY27 annualised EBITDA $2.21B, and capex ~$3.3B (32%+ of sales). Starting net debt $2.79B.

| Scenario | Revenue | EBITDA (margin) | EBIT/interest | Capex | Year-end net debt | Net leverage | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base (guided)** | $10.2B | $2.45B (24%) | ~12x | $3.3B | ~$4.7B | ~1.9x | ~$2B FCF burn → likely **another equity/convert raise in FY27**; dilution overhang persists |
| **−20% (AI optics digestion)** | $8.2B | $1.27B (15.5%) | ~2.5x | cut to ~$2.0B | ~$3.6B | **~2.8x** | Revolver headroom consumed; growth capex deferred; goodwill ($7.3B) impairment likely; no covenant breach on unsecured notes, possible maintenance-covenant pressure if revolver heavily drawn |
| **−35% (cycle break / customer push-out)** | $6.6B | $0.66B (10%) | **~0.3x** | cut to ~$1.0B (maintenance) | ~$2.6B | ~4.0x | EBITDA no longer covers interest; $3–5B goodwill/PP&E write-down vs $11.7B book equity; **distress requiring emergency equity, asset sale, or covenant waiver** |

**Key asymmetry:** in a −20% world, FCF is roughly breakeven *if* management cuts capex — the burn is growth investment, not an operating bleed. But if demand rolls over *after* the $3B+ is spent, the company is left with 212 days of inventory, heavy fixed capacity, and a single-customer-underwritten asset base. That sequencing risk is what makes this "High" rather than "Moderate."

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

Not Critical: leverage is ~1.3x, coverage is 8.7x, no maturities until 2029, $2.3B+ of liquidity, and gross margins are expanding (40.6% non-GAAP, target >45%). The balance sheet can absorb a normal downturn.

High because: **FCF is deeply negative (−$1.0B FY26, −$0.87B in Q1 alone), capex is 33% of revenue and rising, working capital is absorbing cash, and the funding model already required $2.0B of equity in FY26 (+30% shares).** Combine that with 81% volatility, 2.8 beta, a 60% realised drawdown, and 82% of revenue tied to one end-market, and the *financial* risk to an equity holder is high even though *credit* risk is contained. Refinancing cost context: with the 10Y at 5.13% and 2s10s at +39bp (no inversion), a 2029 refinancing of the $990M 5.000% notes would come at a materially higher coupon.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Negative free cash flow at scale** — −$1.02B FY26, −$868M in Q1 FY27; FCF yield −2.5%. Dependent on continued capital-market access.
2. **Serial dilution** — $1.9986B common equity issued in FY26; diluted shares 154.8M → 202M (+30%). F-Score F7 = 0. Another raise is plausible if capex guidance holds.
3. **Accruals/quality gap** — OCF $79.5M vs net income $805M (10% conversion); working capital drain $1.10B. F-Score F4 = 0.
4. **Inventory at 212 days** ($3.34B) — obsolescence exposure across 800G → 1.6T → CPO transitions.
5. **Customer concentration** — two customers >10% of FY26 revenue; the capex program is effectively underwritten by one **$20B / 5-year PhotonLink LTA starting 2028**. A single counterparty's roadmap change is an existential capex risk.
6. **Segment monoculture** — Datacenter & Communications is 82% of revenue; Industrial is shrinking ($2.05B → $1.84B FY26, −10%) and offers no offset.
7. **Debt rising again** — total debt $3.54B → $4.35B in one quarter (+$800M) as equity proceeds are exhausted.
8. **Equity tail risk** — beta 2.82, 81% vol, −60.2% max drawdown; a market-level 20% decline implies roughly halving the stock.
