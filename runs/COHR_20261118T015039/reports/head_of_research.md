### Investment Memo: COHR — November 18, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (staged, small size) | Conviction: **Low** on the stock, High on the business
**Price Target**: $250 (~+19% from $210.86) | probability-weighted: Bear $110 (25%) / Base $250 (45%) / Bull $365 (30%)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months, with the Feb 3, 2027 (Q2 FY27) print as the decisive checkpoint

**Investment Thesis**
Coherent is the vertically integrated owner of the scarcest input in AI networking (InP/EML), in the strongest operating momentum of its history — revenue +49.9% YoY, gross margin up 830bp in two years, a $20B five-year LTA, and CY27 capacity sold out — trading at the cheapest forward multiple in the AI-optical peer set (20x FY27E / 13x FY28E). Against that sits a genuinely broken cash story: −$1.0B FY26 FCF, −$868M in Q1 FY27 alone, 212 days of inventory, +27% share-count growth, and an $11.8B non-cancellable commitment stack, all priced by a market in a real-rate shock (10Y TIPS 2.92%, Fed hiking). Eleven of twelve agents agree on the facts; they disagree 3x on what to pay for them ($135 prob-weighted IV vs $376–450 street). When rigorous frameworks disagree by 3x, true edge is near zero — so the correct action is a small, evidence-gated position, not a conviction one.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector ATTRACTIVE on 12–24m fundamentals (AI transceiver TAM +57% in CY26; 800G/1.6T short through 2027–29) but late-cycle in sentiment. Macro is bifurcated: operating environment the best in years (3.4% GDP, easy credit), discount-rate environment the worst (Fed hiking into 3.8% CPI, real 10Y +85bp). The stock trades on its discount rate, not its revenue — "own the earnings, respect the curve."
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: HIGH risk, currently net-favourable. US ban on Chinese transceivers is a structural share-shift tailwind (+8% on the headline); the symmetric risk is Chinese InP/gallium/germanium retaliation — a step-function impairment vs a gradual upside. 28% of long-lived assets in China; 25% Section 232 tariffs in force; active securities class action (2020–25 class period).
- **Value**: SELL. Prob-weighted IV ~$135; even the bull case ($194) is below spot. ROIC 6.1% < WACC for four straight years; FCF/net income conversion −127%. "The business is fine; the price is not."
- **Growth**: STRONG BUY. 20.3x FY27E for +90% EPS growth (PEG 0.23), contracted backlog, 7/7 beats, +21.5% FY30 estimate revisions. The FCF trough is investment into contracted demand, not margin erosion.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL, negative tilt. Quality 25/100, Low-Vol 15/100 (worst of cohort), momentum broken (−50.6% from peak), alpha −3.9% but statistically indistinguishable from zero. Note: the +21.5% "revision" is an adjacent-fiscal-year artifact; FY30 consensus rests on **two analysts** — the load-bearing assumption behind every street target.
- **Financial Risk**: HIGH. Credit contained (BB+, leverage ~1.3x, no maturities to 2029, coverage 8.7x), but FCF deeply negative, capex 33% of revenue and rising, and a second equity raise inside 12 months is the base case.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: HIGH. Governance structure sound (independent chair, 96% say-on-pay), and CEO/Chair bought ~$1.22M at ~$190 post-drawdown — the highest-quality signal in the file. But the $20B LTA counterparty is undisclosed with no take-or-pay terms, and pay is scored on "adjusted" metrics above reported figures.
- **ESG**: NEUTRAL — not a valuation driver. ~$155K all-time penalties (<0.003% of revenue). Real unpriced tails: process safety at single-site InP capacity, and CSRD/SB 253 Scope 3 disclosure.
- **Alternative Data**: BULLISH. Attention fully reset (search −62% vs 12m avg) against intact demand evidence (production hiring, NVIDIA-anchored Sherman TX buildout staffed, Zacks #1 upgrade Nov 17). Classic asymmetric setup — fundamentals up, expectations washed out.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: Fraud thesis REJECTED — DSO flat (61.8 vs 61.9 days), GM expanding, no channel stuffing. The real bear case is cash quality (accruals ~5.9% TTM, cash conversion −6%), a manufactured depreciation cliff (~$3B/yr capex vs ~$600M D&A), and the two-analyst FY30 hockey stick. At 13x FY28, the best of this trade was $350–430, not $210.

**Bull Case**
- Contracted, not forecast, demand: $20B PhotonLink LTA (~$4B/yr from CY28) + sold-out CY27 capacity + capacity booked into CY28 — unusual visibility for a component maker.
- Cheapest growth-adjusted exposure in AI optics: 20x FY27E / 13x FY28E / 8x FY28 EV/EBITDA with 15.9% operating margin vs a 7.1% peer median, and a credible path to >45% gross margin.
- Multiple embedded catalysts: Feb print (GM guide 40–42%), InP capacity doubling a quarter early, potential second LTA, FCC ban enactment, and an FY28 FCF inflection the market is not paying for. CEO and Chair bought the dip with their own money.

**Bear Case**
- Cash-negative growth: ~$0.50–0.55 of working capital consumed per incremental revenue dollar; 212 days of inventory in a 12–18-month product cycle is either a pre-build or a future write-down; dilution (+27% shares) plus likely further raises transfer per-share value.
- Truncated left tail: beta 3.6 (252d), 97% vol, R² to SPY only 0.29 — 71% of risk is idiosyncratic and unhedgeable; three independent HIGH risk ratings converge on the same commitment-stack mechanism.
- Macro hostage: at a 2.92% real 10-year with a hiking Fed, the stock fell 60% while beating and raising. A TIPS break above 3.50% compresses the multiple regardless of execution — and a −35% revenue scenario leaves EBITDA below interest expense.

**Key Risks**
1. Q2 FY27 gross margin <39–40% or datacom growth <+30% — breaks the operating-leverage leg both bull and bear cases require.
2. Second equity raise or a capex increase against negative OCF — confirms the dilution treadmill is structural.
3. Inventory days >250 with flat margins — the Short Analyst's formal "Strong Short" trigger.
4. Chinese export-licence action on InP/Ga/Ge — volume-constrained precisely as the ban hands over share.
5. PhotonLink LTA disclosure showing no minimum volumes/take-or-pay — undercuts the entire FY28+ consensus.
6. 10Y TIPS >3.50% or any hyperscaler capex guidance revision — the macro bear trigger.
