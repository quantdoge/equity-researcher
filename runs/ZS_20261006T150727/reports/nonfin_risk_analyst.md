# Zscaler, Inc. (ZS) — Non-Financial Risk Assessment
*Prepared: Oct 2026 • Price $211.30 • Mkt cap ~$34.2B • Sector: Software—Infrastructure (cloud security)*

## 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate (leaning Weak on concentration)**

- **Founder-CEO-Chairman duality:** Jay Chaudhry (age 68, founder) holds Chairman *and* CEO roles — a concentration-of-power flag. No evidence of a lead independent director arrangement surfaced; proxies unavailable via tooling (gap noted).
- **Board:** Includes strong independent voices (e.g., Charles Giancarlo, Compensation Committee Chair). No independence crises, related-party transaction alarms, or enforcement-driven board shakeups found.
- **Executive comp/alignment:** Proxy (DEF 14A) text unavailable; cannot verify pay-for-performance mechanics. CEO retains a founder-scale stake, which aligns him with long-term shareholders but also means his personal tax/10b5-1 sales move the tape.
- **Leadership stability — the real governance concern:** CFO transition within the last ~18 months (Remo Canessa retired 2025 → Kevin Rubin, May 2025) and a **CRO transition effective Oct 1, 2026** (Mike Rich departing "for personal reasons," replaced by Ross Tackett) amid a ~3% workforce restructuring during FY2026 and a severance-policy update. Concentration of strategy in the founder while revenue leadership turns over is a live execution risk.

## 2. Regulatory Risk Summary: **Moderate**

- **Export controls / China:** Zscaler wound down its China business following U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) rules restricting cybersecurity exports to China; the **FY2026 10-K and 10-Q explicitly flag U.S. export-control and sanctions exposure.** Beijing has separately directed Chinese enterprises to stop using U.S./Israeli cybersecurity software — structural loss of a TAM (~1% of revenue previously), not an isolated fine.
- **No SEC/DOJ enforcement actions, fines, or litigation found** (EDGAR litigation search unavailable; open web search returned none). Clean enforcement record to date.
- **Data privacy (GDPR/CCPA) & government contracts:** Inherent exposure as a cloud security vendor handling customer traffic/metadata; FedRAMP/public-sector business adds audit/compliance scrutiny.
- **Pending approvals:** N/A (no FDA-style gate); the material regulatory risk is geopolitical trade policy, not a single approval.

## 4. Top Operational Risks

1. **Supply-chain compromise of Zscaler's own environment:** In the **Salesloft Drift breach (June 2025, disclosed ~Sept 2025) attackers exfiltrated Zscaler customer data via a compromised third-party Salesforce tool.** For a zero-trust vendor, a breach of its own tenant is operationally and reputationally disproportionate.
2. **Sales-execution and key-person risk:** CRO transition effective Oct 1, 2026 (coinciding with FY27 ARR guidance of ~$4.40–4.43B, implying growth deceleration) plus a CFO with only ~18 months tenure and an aging founder-CEO — revenue leadership churn right at the growth inflection.
3. **Cloud infrastructure dependency:** Multi-tenant enforcement-node architecture means any data-center outage (e.g., reported SIN4/SIN5/Philippines routing issue) or capacity failure directly degrades customers' live security posture; no meaningful business-continuity testing evidence available.

## 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **High (elevated)**

- **The breach narrative is the core reputational liability** — "the security company got breached and lost customer data" is a trust-damaging story for an enterprise-security brand and invites class-action attention (none surfaced yet).
- **Negative tape over 12 months:** record ~31% one-day drop (Sept 2025 guidance shock), ~30% trailing 1-yr underperformance vs. cybersecurity peers that roughly doubled, ~9% drop on CRO exit (Sept 25, 2026), Citi publicly questioning AI-traffic monetization.
- **Offsetting positives:** Oct 6, 2026 Investor Day reaffirmed FQ1/FY27 guidance (shares surged); Guggenheim upgrade to Buy ($214 PT); Wedbush Outperform; branded AI-era positioning (Aston Martin F1 partnership) keeps the narrative alive. Reputation is recoverable but currently on the back foot.

## 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **High**

Governance is adequate but concentrated; regulatory exposure is geopolitical rather than enforcement-driven; the **High** call rests on the combination of a confirmed customer-data breach at a security vendor, sustained insider-only selling, revenue-leadership turnover during ARR deceleration, and stock-price-driven reputational pressure. Not Critical: no enforcement actions, no fraud indicators, no accounting red flags, balance sheet is a non-issue (net-cash enterprise software).

## 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Zscaler's own security breach (Salesloft Drift, 2025):** customer data taken from its Salesforce via third-party tool — requires post-hoc review of disclosure adequacy, notification scope, and any resulting regulatory/legal claims.
2. **China/export-control regime loss:** material, disclosed, and structurally permanent — validate remaining China revenue and sanctions exposure in the FY2026 10-K.
3. **CRO transition + ARR deceleration:** sales leadership change effective 10/1/2026 against FY27 guidance — monitor quota-carrying attrition and pipeline metrics next two quarters.
4. **CEO/Chairman duality + founder key-person risk** with no succession visibility — flag for board-assessment follow-up.
