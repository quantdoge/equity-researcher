**Investment Thesis**

Zscaler is a category-leading, high-quality zero-trust franchise (77% gross margin, net cash, clean accounting, operating margin improving every year) inside a structurally attractive sector — but at ~$211 the stock offers **no margin of safety against a breaking leading indicator**: FY26 calculated billings grew only ~13% vs. +25% revenue, pointing to FY27 growth regression toward the mid-teens and multiple compression on a ~40x FCF equity. With an active securities-litigation wave, a vendor-side customer-data breach, one-directional insider selling, and a binary Nov 24 earnings catalyst, the asymmetry is negative at market — HOLD, with tactical buy limits at $150–190 and a bull trigger if FY27 billings guidance comes in ≥20%.

**Bull Case**
1. **Secular demand engine**: zero-trust TAM compounding 15–17%/yr, AI-security expands the addressable surface (AI app usage +600% y/y), security is mission-critical — and the sector already de-rated ~60% peak-to-trough, creating a better entry on a category leader with 77% GM, 25% FCF margin and net cash.
2. **Clean execution**: 7/7 quarterly beats (+11% avg surprise), negative accruals (conservative accounting), improving operating margin every year, Investor Day reaffirmed guidance, upward revisions, new partnerships (IBM/Red Hat) — plus the CRO-transition overhang is at least disclosed, not hidden.
3. **De-rated multiple with optionality**: at ~10x EV/sales vs. CRWD ~59x, relative upside exists if growth converts to GAAP profit; a billings re-acceleration (Q1 is seasonally weak) toward FY27 guide ≥20% re-rates the stock toward the $260 bull case.

**Bear Case**
1. **The billings tell**: revenue +25% is being carried by pre-sold deferred revenue while calculated billings print +13%; FY27 growth regresses mechanically toward mid-teens vs. consensus ~24% — a guide-down is the catalyst risk, and history (May 27, 2026 −31%) shows this re-rates violently.
2. **Shareholder economics, not fraud**: SBC = 24.5% of revenue ≈ 96% of FCF; GAAP losses 4 straight years; ~3.4%/yr dilution; FCF margin eroding ~200bp/yr while revenue decelerates — double compression on a 40x FCF multiple.
3. **Dense risk cluster**: securities-litigation wave, vendor-side customer-data breach, revenue-leadership churn during ARR deceleration, insider selling with zero buys, platform consolidation from Microsoft/PANW/AI-native entrants — 72% annualized vol and a −49% drawdown history leave 40–50% downside if December guidance lands soft.
