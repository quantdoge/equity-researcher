## 2. Environmental Risk Assessment: **Low**
- SaaS business: no manufacturing, no CBAM/ETS-ETS exposure, minimal Scope 1. Main footprint is electricity via AWS/Azure enforcement nodes (Scope 3) + business travel.
- Reported **carbon-neutral for CY2022 (RECs + offsets)** and a stated **net-zero ambition (target ~2025)**; Zscaler also markets its zero-trust architecture as reducing customers' hardware/VPN energy footprint (positive externality).
- Weaknesses: reliance on offsets/RECs rather than deep abatement; Scope 3 cloud reporting still maturing relative to hyperscaler-grade peers.
- Third-party ESG risk score (FactSet/Sustainalytics): **Environmental 3.95** — lowest sub-score, consistent with "Low."

## 3. Social Risk Assessment: **Low** (lower end of sector)
- 8,700 mostly US/EU/India high-wage employees; no supply-chain labor exposure; positive societal externality (protecting critical infrastructure).
- Watch-items, not current crises: (a) cybersecurity sector lags on women/URM representation and ZS publishes limited DEI granularity; (b) repeated phishing/impersonation campaigns abusing the Zscaler brand (2023–2025) and a 2025-26 regional data-center routing incident (Philippines→Singapore) — trust-erosion, modest direct cost.
- Government/defense business (FedRAMP, IL-level environments) imposes a discipline that *raises* the social safety/security bar.

## 4. Governance Quality Score: **6 / 10**
- **Negatives:** Jay Chaudhry is simultaneously **founder, CEO and Chairman** (weakest independence post in the profile); a 2024 media/investor episode over a reported related-party senior hire (company subsequently codified board-approval for hires of the CEO's relatives); **open securities-fraud investigations** by multiple firms (Pomerantz, Schall, Bronstein Gewirtz & Grossman) after the May 2026 guidance-driven crash — a direct question mark on disclosure credibility (10b-class action tail).
- **Positives:** single-class common stock (no dual-class entrenchment); founder pays himself a nominal cash salary (≈$30K base) with equity-linked upside — long-term alignment by construction; annual say-on-pay; standard clawback/vesting; S&P/FactSet **governance sub-score 4.79** on a low-risk scale; company maintains a published corporate-responsibility report for a sector where many peers disclose far less.
- Net: functional but founder-centric governance with an active litigation overhang — the single largest ESG drag on the ZS thesis.

## 6. Regulatory ESG Exposure
- **US SEC climate disclosure rule:** stayed/litigated; if revived, ZS would report Scope 1/2/3 — low compliance burden given service-only model.
- **SEC cyber incident disclosure:** already in force and material for ZS (both as filer and as vendor to other filers).
- **EU CSRD:** only if EU-subsidiary/revenue nexus crosses thresholds — indirect today. **GDPR / NIS2 / DORA** apply to European operations and, more importantly, tighten *customer* procurement screens ZS must pass. **EU AI Act** touches its AI-security features (Security Autopilot, agentic-AI VP role just created).
- **CBAM: none** (services, no goods). **FedRAMP / defense** regime is the operative "ESG-adjacent" license-to-operate gate in the US.

## 7. Overall ESG Rating: **Average**
E and S are clean-to-favorable (Low risk; Sustainalytics total **18.56 = Low** risk category); G is below sector standard due to founder-CEO-Chairman duality and an active securities-litigation overhang. The governance drag keeps ZS out of "Leader" but the profile is far from "ESG Risk."

## 8. ESG Investment Implication: **Neutral** (governance-tilted)
ESG does not underwrite the AI-security demand thesis, nor does it break it — but the G overhang (litigation, founder control, growth-deceleration communication) is a genuine **risk-off that should discount the multiple** vs. better-governed peers (CRWD, PANW). Monitor: (1) outcome of securities investigations (dismissal vs. class certification), (2) any split of Chairman/CEO roles, (3) delivery vs. the stated net-zero 2025 target, (4) revival of the SEC climate rule. Steer: **Neutral** for a long thesis, *negative tilt* on governance pricing.
