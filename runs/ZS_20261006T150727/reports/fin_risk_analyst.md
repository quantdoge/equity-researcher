# Financial Risk Assessment — Zscaler, Inc. (ZS)

**Profile:** Cloud-based cybersecurity platform (Software–Infrastructure), San Jose, CA. FY ends July 31. Market cap ≈ $34.2B; price $211.30. Data as of FY2026 (period ending 2026-07-31).

## 1. Credit Risk Assessment: **Investment Grade — but on cash metrics, not GAAP metrics**

The credit story is a tale of two ledgers. **On a cash basis this is a clean credit:** net debt of **-$1.62B** (cash $3.47B vs. total debt $1.86B), trivial absolute interest expense ($11.8M on $1.86B of debt), zero short-term debt, and $852M of trailing FCF. **On a GAAP basis it fails every conventional stress metric:** negative EBIT four years running, negative interest coverage, a negative-equity-building trajectory (retained earnings -$1.25B), and an Altman Z of 0.81 (distress zone). The gap is driven almost entirely by non-cash stock-based compensation ($822M in FY26 — roughly 13× reported net loss), a common SaaS artifact, and by Altman's x4 using **book** equity ($2.6B) rather than the $34B market cap. A ratings agency keying on cash flow and net-cash balance would keep ZS investment grade; the Z-Framework read understates it.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **GAAP unprofitable 4 consecutive years** — net income -$63M (FY26), EPS -$0.39; driven by $822M non-cash SBC, but it caps refinancing options and pressures book equity (E/A only 33%).
2. **Altman Z-Score in distress zone (0.81)** — qualified by book-equity methodology, but flagged for any covenant/rating trigger test.
3. **Thin equity base & cumulative losses** — retained earnings -$1.25B; equity grows only via dilution (share count +10% over 3 years; Piotroski dilution flag).
4. **Valuation/volatility stress** — FCF yield 2.5%, 72% vol, 49% historical drawdown: severe multiple compression risk if growth decelerates.
5. **Data gaps / quality flags:** no debt maturity schedule or covenants, no credit rating/CDS data available; implied interest rate on debt ≈0.6% (implausibly low — suspect data); balance-sheet `net_debt` field ($927M) internally inconsistent with computed -$1.62B; benchmark peers (PANW, CRWD, FTNT) available but peer financial ratios not pulled this pass.

**Bottom line:** ZS passes the stress test on cash and liquidity; its vulnerabilities are accounting-quality optics, equity thinness, and extreme market volatility — not near-term solvency. Monitor: GAAP profitability turnaround, SBC dilution rate, and any shift of the $1.7B long-term debt ahead of the 4.7–5.0% yield-curve (no inversion, but elevated refinancing costs).
