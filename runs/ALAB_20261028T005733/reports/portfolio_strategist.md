It is a *derivative* of the same driver (hyperscaler capex) as NVDA, AVGO, CRDO, VRT and the neocloud complex. Owning it alongside NVDA does not diversify AI capex — it **levers it.** Net sector exposure must be checked and reduced elsewhere if this is added.

**Read:** ALAB is *not* a low-correlation diversifier (0.53–0.69 to the core book), but its correlation to NVDA is only 0.43 and regression R² to the market is 0.28 — so **~72% of its variance is idiosyncratic.** That cuts both ways: it is not a pure AI-beta proxy (mild positive for fit), but its standalone risk is enormous. **Do not pair it with CRDO** — at 0.69 they are one position, not two.

**Factor exposure — TWO UNINTENDED TILTS.** Adding ALAB increases (a) **high-beta/growth** and (b) **momentum**, while deepening a **negative value** tilt and adding the *worst-in-cohort low-vol score*. If the book is already long momentum/growth, this is pro-cyclical risk-stacking, not construction. It should be funded from another high-beta AI name, not from cash.

**Macro factor:** ~30-year empirical equity duration. Negative exposure to the 10Y, which is at 5.00% and rising with a hawkish Fed. This is a **rates bet** as much as a stock bet.

**Fit verdict: POOR on a standalone-diversification basis; ACCEPTABLE only as a small, risk-budgeted satellite funded by rotating out of correlated AI names.**
