## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT** (but the split is narrower than it looks)

**Where all eleven genuinely agree (high-quality consensus):**
1. **No fraud.** Value, Short, Fin Risk and Quant independently cleared the books. Accruals negative, cash conversion >1.0x, DSRI <1.0. Two "distress" model outputs were correctly rejected as bank-misapplication artifacts.
2. **The franchise is elite.** 30–32% ROE, 19.3% efficiency, 143M customers, 16.4% equity/assets. Nobody contests this.
3. **The de-rating was event-driven, not operational.** The −17% on 26-Oct (339M shares) was a Brazil election/BRL shock; NU printed record net income of $1.1bn nine days later.
4. **Credit is deteriorating and is not yet resolved.** This is the one finding every agent that looked at it converged on: 90+ NPL 6.5%→7.3% in two quarters, provisions +75% YoY, gross margin −440bp, risk-adjusted NIM 12.4%→12.1%.

**Where they genuinely diverge — and it is a *time-horizon* split, not a factual one:**
- **Growth + Alt Data** underwrite the 12–24 month path (earnings delivery, multiple recovery).
- **Value + Quant** underwrite the price (fair value ≈ price ⇒ no edge).
- **Macro + Geo Legal + Non-Fin Risk** underwrite the discount rate and the tail (exogenous, unpriceable).

**How I resolve it:** I give the *bearish* agents precedence on **risk sizing** and the *growth* agents precedence on **whether to own it at all** — because the risks cited (Brazil fiscal, Sec-301/Pix, card-rate cap) are exactly the risks the 16x multiple already discounts, and none of them is currently *realised*. Critically, **the Short Analyst is the highest-quality bearish voice in the set and he declines to short** — the asymmetry does not support pressing a short after a 30% drawdown. That, plus a clean forensic read, is what moves me off "Pass."

**Blended intrinsic value: $14.0–14.5 base (bull $18.5–20.0, bear $8.5–9.5) ⇒ ~+7% expected 12-month return, wide dispersion.** That is a *thin* edge — enough to own a small piece, nowhere near enough to own a large one.

## 3. Position Sizing Recommendation: **1.0% of portfolio to initiate; 2.5% hard maximum**

**Kelly logic, applied honestly.** Edge ≈ +7% to blended fair value; bull/bear span ±35%. Full Kelly on a 50.5%-vol, fat-left-tailed, single-country name would imply a position far larger than any risk committee should approve. Applying a **fractional Kelly (≈1/4) plus a hard cap for tail risk** yields 1.0–2.5%.

**Deductions that take it from the 3–5% "high quality" band to 1–2.5%:**
- **50.5% annualised vol and −34% max drawdown** — a 2.5% position carries ~1.26% of portfolio volatility from this name alone.
- **HIGH geopolitical/legal rating** with a genuine, non-diversifiable tail (OFAC/Pix, card-rate cap).
- **90%+ single-country revenue** and a −20.5% single-day political shock on record.
- **Credit cycle unproven** — NU has never run a book through a full Brazilian down-cycle; bear case is a live 25–30%.
- **Governance discount is structural** (74% voting control, CEO=Chair, FPI exemptions) — it will not re-rate away.

**Why not pass entirely:** the forensic case is clean, the multiple is 15x forward for 30% ROE, the Q4 revenue bar is conspicuously low (−1.1% QoQ after +7.2% delivered), and BofA's $10 target — the most bearish on the Street — is only ~24% below spot while the recent median target is ~$19. That is an acceptable skew for a 1% starter.

**Thesis invalidation (exit regardless of price — these supersede the stop):**
1. **Credit:** 90+ NPL >**8.5%**, or Stage 3 >**10%**, or provisions >**32% of revenue** for two quarters. Coverage (234%) buys time, not immunity.
