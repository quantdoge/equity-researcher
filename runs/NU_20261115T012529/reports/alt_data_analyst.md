### 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **BULLISH**

The distinctive feature of this setup: **company-level alt data is stable-to-improving while the stock has been repriced by a political factor outside the alt-data frame.** Brazilian search interest unchanged through an election-driven −17% drawdown, Nubank still the #1 downloaded finance app in Brazil, in-app CDN traffic +50% MoM, 3.7M net customer adds, efficiency improving, and a Q4 revenue bar set at −1.1% QoQ against +7.2% delivered. That is an alt-data/fundamentals-vs-price dislocation, not an alt-data deterioration.

**Key risks alt data does NOT cover:** (a) **90+ NPL rising 6.9% → 7.3%** — credit quality is the swing factor into a Q4 print and no alt source here proxies it; (b) BRL/FX and Selic path, which dominate USD EPS; (c) policy/tax risk under a fourth Lula term — a fat-tail that resets the multiple regardless of operating momentum.

Strengths: Brazil-geo Trends is the correct proxy and directly contradicts the bear narrative; app-download data is third-party and specific (#1 in Brazil, 48.3M); earnings data is hard-reported; the Oct 26 macro attribution is verified across multiple sources. Constraints: web traffic figures are coarse third-party estimates (±), **patent data failed entirely (two attempts)**, social sentiment is qualitative with no API backing, and the single most important forward variable — credit quality — has no alt-data proxy. Search interest also has limited information content for a 143M-customer incumbent approaching saturation in Brazil; growth increasingly comes from ARPAC and Mexico/Colombia, which Trends captures poorly.

*Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one. Signals above are as of Nov 14, 2026 and will decay fastest on the credit-quality and political axes.*
