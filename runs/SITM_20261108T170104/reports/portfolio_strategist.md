## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — but split on the wrong axis**

This is not a normal disagreement about business quality. **Eleven of eleven reporting agents agree the operating business is excellent and improving.** The disagreement is entirely about the **price of risk**:

- **Consensus on fundamentals (High agreement):** revenue tripling, 70%+ non-GAAP gross margin, ~80% share in AI-datacenter timing, a real (if narrow) MEMS moat, upward estimate revisions, consensus numbers set below the guide. Growth, Sector, Alt Data, Value and even ESG converge here.
- **Consensus on risk (High agreement):** every risk-domain agent — Fin Risk, Geo/Legal, Non-Fin, Quant, Macro — independently lands on high-volatility, high-leverage, geopolitically concentrated, earnings-quality-fragile. None of them dispute the growth; all of them dispute what it is worth at a 3.1 beta.
- **The genuine split is Macro vs. Growth.** Growth says the multiple is 50% below slower-growing AI-connectivity peers. Macro says that multiple is a *duration instrument* and the real-yield shock is the reason it is cheap. Both are right; they are describing different return horizons.

**Conflict resolution applied (per framework — risk flags take precedence):**
1. Growth's bullishness on demand **does not** override Fin Risk's leverage/earnings-quality findings. A company earning negative ROIC with negative tangible equity and SBC-add-back-dependent FCF gets sized smaller than its growth rate alone would justify.
2. Geo/Legal's HIGH rating **caps** position size independently — the Taiwan tail is unhedgeable at any fundamental valuation, so it is managed by exposure limit, not by analysis.
3. Value's "$520 base IV, range $250–900" is the honest reconciliation: **+25% to base, −40% to bear, +116% to bull.** This is a genuinely asymmetric but extremely wide-dispersion payoff — the definition of a position that gets 1.5–2.5%, not 4%.
4. **The 2023 ICFR material weakness and restatement** is the highest-severity item that is *not* a fraud allegation. Under this framework, an unresolved accounting-integrity question on a name whose reported earnings depend on ~$121M/quarter of adjustments means: **do not take a full-size position until the FY2026 audit opinion is confirmed unqualified.**

Expected-value math I am underwriting: 30% bear ($250, −40%) / 45% base ($520, +25%) / 25% bull ($900, +116%) → **EV ≈ +28%**. Real, but derived from a DCF whose terminal value drives ~70% of the answer. Full Kelly on 94% vol and that estimation error would imply a 30%+ weight; fractional Kelly (≈1/12) with a governance/accounting haircut lands at the low single digits.

## 6. Risk Management Rules

**Price-based (pre-split levels; divide by 3 post-split):**
- **Halve the position at $340** (−18%) — breaks the 5-Nov low and the base-case discount thesis.
- **Exit remaining position at $275** (−34%) — below the $280 floor of the bear case; at that level the market is telling you the terminal-value assumption has failed, and the leverage profile removes the option value of waiting.
- Note: with 94% annualised vol, one standard deviation of a *monthly* move is ±27%. **A price stop tighter than ~$300 is noise, not risk management** — which is precisely why the primary risk controls must be fundamental.

**Thesis invalidation (fundamental — these override the price stop):**
1. **FY2027 revenue guide below $1.40B** — i.e., no growth on the $1.34B Q4'26 run-rate. Kills the entire growth thesis.
2. **Organic (ex-TPD) AI/optical core decelerating below ~20% Y/Y** — the +257% headline is inorganic-flattered; the organic split is the number to interrogate every quarter.
3. **Non-GAAP gross margin below 65%** or a management statement that the 70% AI-mix is eroding — the "room to peer 58% GM" argument runs the other way too.
4. **Any second ICFR material weakness, restatement, or a qualified audit opinion.** With a 2023 precedent, this is a zero-tolerance, immediate-exit trigger — no averaging, no waiting for the call.
5. **Goodwill impairment against the $4.16B intangible base**, or convertible terms renegotiated/refinanced at a distressed price ahead of the June 2031 maturity.
6. **Entity-list designation of any top-5 customer** (Apple ~17% of revenue), **a Taiwan quarantine/blockade event**, or a BIS enforcement action touching the HK channel. These are binary and unhedgeable — exit on announcement, not on analysis.
7. **CEO/Chair separation failure combined with continued zero-insider-buying.** $43.8M sold with no open-market purchases is a live signal, not a formality.

**Trim ladder (do NOT use a flat "90% of intrinsic value" rule here — IV dispersion is $250–900):**
- **Trim 25% at $600** (~1.15x base IV)
- **Trim to one-half at $750** (~1.45x base IV)
- **Exit remainder above $900** (bull case; also the approximate convert conversion price, where latent dilution re-activates)
- **Mechanical trim** any time SITM exceeds **4.0% of portfolio** through appreciation.

**Portfolio-level circuit breaker:** if the combined **SITM + MTSI** weight exceeds 4%, or total AI-infrastructure theme exposure exceeds the mandate limit, trim SITM first — it has the higher beta (3.11 vs MTSI's lower) and the weaker balance sheet.
