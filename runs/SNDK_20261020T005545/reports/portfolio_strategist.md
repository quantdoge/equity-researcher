TrendForce's 2027 supply>demand call, the $31B capacity commitment, and the $4.5B peak buyback are textbook cycle-top markers.

**Where agents agree (high-conviction):** balance sheet is fortress (net cash, no credit risk); accounting is clean (no fraud thesis); the risk is *earnings durability and equity multiple*, not solvency. **Where they collide:** whether forward "cheapness" (7x) is real or is a peak-margin illusion. The Value/Short evidence — 84.6% GM, DSO blowout, insider distribution, cycle-peak capacity adds — is the higher-weight evidence per the framework's rule (structural/fraud-adjacent and risk signals outrank the growth narrative). **Net consensus: fundamentally fragile, tactically supported.**
