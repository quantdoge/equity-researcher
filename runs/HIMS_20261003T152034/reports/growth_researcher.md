# HIMS — Hims & Hers Health, Inc. (NASDAQ: HIMS)
## Growth Investment Analysis — Equity Growth Research
**Price: ~$29.00 | Market Cap: ~$6.5–7.5B | Sector: Healthcare (DTC Telehealth/Consumer Healthcare)**
*Data as of latest available (Q4 2026 window; Q3 2026 earnings due Nov 9, 2026)*

---

## 1. Growth Story

Hims & Hers is the largest vertically integrated telehealth-to-consumer healthcare platform in the US, and it is in the middle of the most consequential pivot in its history. After riding the 2024–25 FDA-shortage-driven compounded semaglutide boom (revenue CAGR 74% 2021–25), the FDA's removal of semaglutide from the shortage list (June 2025) legally gutted that business overnight — the stock fell from $70 to the low-$30s. Management has restructured around a **multi-source GLP-1 strategy**: a branded **Wegovy (Novo Nordisk) partnership** that shipped 125k+ prescriptions in its first six weeks, **generic liraglutide** (first-mover ANDA advantage), and its own pharmacy (Tryvium), while the core ex-GLP-1 business still grows ~30% YoY at 75%+ gross margins. The investment thesis: the compounding shock is priced in at ~$29, and the branded/in-house GLP-1 + core health stack re-accelerates to 30–40% growth with margin normalization in H2 2026 — a classic disruption-trough entry.

**Growth quality:** 3-yr revenue CAGR ~64% (2022–25); 5-yr CAGR 73.6%. Growth is **organic** (zero M&A revenue — even Tryvium is capability, not revenue). Historically consistent quarterly revenue beats (Q3'25 $600M vs $580M est; Q2'26 $753M vs $699M est). **Kaplan: growth decelerating (59%→~25%) but reaccelerating QoQ** — Q2'26 revenue +24% QoQ after a trough Q1'26 (+4% YoY, the GLP-1 transition floor).

**Margin trajectory (the red flag):** Gross margin fell from 82% (2023) to 73.8% (2025) and collapsed to 49% (Q1'26) / 64% (Q2'26) on inventory write-downs and low-margin GLP-1 mix. **Forward estimates:** 2027E rev $4.05B / EPS $0.30; 2028E $4.6B / $0.69; 2030E EPS $0.85. **Earnings revision score: +8.5% upgrade** (bullish — estimates moving up, not down).

## 3. TAM Assessment & Penetration Opportunity

- **Weight management (the accelerant):** ~100M+ US adults are obese; the global obesity-drug market is projected to exceed **$100–130B by 2030**. Management targets **100k+ new weight-loss subscribers/month** — at $300–500/month ARPU that is a $3.6–6.0B annualized revenue pool alone. Current GLP-1 book is a rounding error of that TAM.
- **Adjacent stack:** dermatology, sexual health, hair loss, mental health, cardiovascular — plus OTC/consumables. Management has repeatedly sized its combined US SAM in the **$100B+ range**.
- **Penetration today:** ~**2.6M subscribers** (Q1'26, +9% YoY) — that is low-single-digit penetration of treatable US adults, and away from the peak GLP-1 boom (2.4M, +31% YoY in Q2'25), net adds have slowed to ~50–60k/qtr. **The wedge is GLP-1 distribution; the moat is the 2.6M-subscriber base for cross-sell.**
- **Differentiation vs. peers:** first-mover generic liraglutide, branded Wegovy fulfillment at scale (125k scripts/6 weeks), owned compounding + dispensing infrastructure, Super Bowl-grade brand. Distribution defensibility vs. **Lilly Direct** (pricing vials at $85–499/mo) and **Ro/Noom/Amazon Pharmacy** is the battleground — this is a distribution-margin game, and pharma's direct-to-consumer push is the structural threat.

## 4. Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q3 2026 earnings — Nov 9, 2026:** consensus revenue **$893M** (vs $753M in Q2) — the market already models a sharp H2 ramp; a beat plus intact margin commentary re-rates the stock. *Near-term, highest-impact.*
- **Wegovy/branded GLP-1 fulfillment cadence:** shipment run-rate updates; potential additional branded partnerships (Novo expansion or a tirzepatide agreement) would structurally derisk the post-compounding model.
- **Subscriber re-acceleration data:** any print showing net adds returning toward 100k+/month in weight loss validates the "100k monthly" target.
- **FDA/tirzepatide compounding resolution:** regulatory clarity removes the last big overhang on the category and the stock's risk premium.
- **Analyst target momentum:** August 2026 cluster of raises post-Q2 — Needham $42, Canaccord $40, BofA $37, Truist $32, Morgan Stanley $28 (all vs. ~$29 spot). Further upgrades likely on Q3 traction.

## 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY**

The disruptive thesis is intact and the worst of the regulatory shock is behind it: revenue base tripled in two years, Rule of 40 = 62, the ex-GLP-1 core grows ~30% at 75%+ margins, and the branded Wegovy + generic liraglutide pivot gives it a credible path back to 30–40% growth off a $3B base. At **~2.3x 2026E sales / ~1.7x 2027E sales / ~21x 2027E EV/EBITDA / ~34x 2030E EPS**, the market is pricing a permanently lower-growth, lower-margin company — we think the valuation already embeds substantial failure. **Not a Strong Buy** because two quarters of GAAP losses, a guided-down EBITDA band, gross-margin mix compression, and slowing net subscriber adds mean H2 2026 execution must be *proven* — the Nov 9 print is the swing event. Constructive on any weakness toward the high-$20s; disciplined on a Q3 miss + guide-down.

## 7. Key Risks

- **Regulatory (highest):** FDA/state actions on compounded semaglutide and now **tirzepatide** could re-kill a recovery leg; branded partnerships mitigate but don't eliminate.
- **Margin mix compression:** GLP-1 gross margins (est. 20–35%) structurally dilute a 75%+ core; sustained mix shift + promotional pricing could cap gross margin in the mid-60s and keep EBITDA below prior expectations.
- **Competition/CPG-style war:** Lilly Direct's direct pricing, Ro, Noom, Amazon Pharmacy, and manufacturer contracting squeeze distribution margins — the moat is brand + convenience, not proprietary molecules.
- **Execution on the pivot:** Q1'26 revenue grew just +4% YoY and subscribers +9%; if the H2'26 GLP-1 ramp falls short of the ~$893M Q3 consensus, the stock re-rates down in a high-beta tape.
- **CAC/marketing fatigue:** declining search interest post-Super Bowl plus ~50% revenue SGA spend is a fragile economics loop if subscriber growth stalls.
- **Balance sheet shift:** first meaningful net-debt position (~$0.5B per EV vs. Mcap data) on a $226M capex year (2025) funding pharmacy/fleet — fine at current cash flow, a risk if losses persist into 2027.
