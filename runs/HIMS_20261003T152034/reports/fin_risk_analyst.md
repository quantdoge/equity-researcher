## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Sub-Investment Grade (speculative-grade profile)**
*No agency credit rating or CDS data available — flagged as a data gap. Assessment is inferred from capital structure and cash-flow metrics.*

HIMS completed a transformative capital-structure event in FY2025. Total debt went from **$11.3M (FY2024) to $1,120.6M (FY2025)** — a ~100x increase in one year — funded via **zero-coupon (0.00%) convertible senior notes maturing 2030 and 2032** (upsized offerings totaling ~$870M plus ~$350M), used to finance acquisitions and expansion (goodwill & intangibles rose from $156M to $474M; PP&E rose ~5x to $449M on the AP3 compounding-facility buildout).

**What this means for credit:**
- **Favorable:** 0% cash coupon → **zero interest expense** (coverage ratio is effectively unbounded today) and **no refinancing cliff** — maturities are 2030/2032, with zero short-term debt on the balance sheet.
- **Unfavorable:** The equity base is only **$541M** vs. $1,121M of debt (D/E = 2.07x). The company went from **net cash of -$209M (i.e., $209M net cash) in 2024 to +$543M net debt in 2025** — a swing of ~$750M in one year. Equity/assets of just 0.25 leaves a thin solvency cushion.

**Leverage metrics:** D/E 2.07x; **Net debt/EBITDA ~3.0x**; Debt/assets 52%; Interest coverage N/M (no interest). At 3.0x EBITDA with margins this thin, there is no headroom for the leverage to rise further without pushing into distressed territory.

## 5. Overall Financial Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale:** No immediate solvency or refinancing danger (0% coupon, 2030/2032 maturities, $577M cash, current ratio 1.9x) — these keep the risk from being "Critical." However: (1) the balance sheet swung from net cash to 3x net-debt/EBITDA in one year with only a 25% equity cushion; (2) the primary growth engine carries binary regulatory risk (compounded GLP-1); (3) the equity is extremely volatile (96% vol, 2.7 beta, -59% max drawdown); (4) F-score is weak at 4/9; (5) SBC ($135M) exceeds net income ($128M); and (6) FCF yield is only ~1%, offering little valuation support. High leverage + thin margins + regulatory concentration + hyper-volatility = **High** overall risk.

6. 🚩 **Thin margins:** 4.5% operating margin leaves almost no buffer for a revenue shock or GLP-1 mix loss.
7. ⚠️ **Buybacks while levering up:** ~$90M of repurchases in FY2025, funded alongside new debt — capital allocation prioritizes equity support over deleveraging.
