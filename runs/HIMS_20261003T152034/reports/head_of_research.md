**Investment Thesis**
HIMS is the best operator in digital telehealth — 59% FY25 revenue growth, 73.8% gross margin, the only profitable pure-play at scale — but its growth engine was a regulatory arbitrage (compounded GLP-1s) that the FDA has now closed, and management responded to the shock by levering up ~$1.1B, buying/building capacity at the cycle top, and pivoting to low-margin branded-drug pass-through. The result: negative EBITDA in both H1'26 quarters, a 30-point gross-margin collapse, a CRITICAL legal/regulatory stack (FTC suit, securities class action naming the CEO and CFO, FDA rulemaking, Novo break-up), and a stock still at 45x EV/EBITDA with ~1% FCF yield. The market has repriced much of the cliff (~55% off highs), but at $29 the long-side asymmetry is still poor: intrinsic value sits at $14–21 and leading demand indicators are collapsing into a binary Nov 9 print.

**Disagreement flag:** 9 of 11 agents are negative or risk-rated (Sector Neutral; Macro Unfavourable; Value SELL-High; Quant SELL; Fin Risk HIGH; Non-Fin CRITICAL; Legal HIGH; ESG Negative; Alt Data Bearish). Growth is a guarded BUY (Medium) and Short is "No Edge" (bear case real but largely priced). The value↔growth dispute — is the GLP-1 pivot a *disruption trough* or a *structurally lower-margin toll-taker*? — cancels on the long side but reinforces on the risk side; even the bull requires unproven execution (Nov 9) before it would act.

2. **Credible post-compounding path**: branded Wegovy partnership, first-mover generic liraglutide, owned pharmacy — Q2'26 revenue beat (+24% QoQ) suggests the trough may be behind, with long-dated OTM converts ($89.95 strike) meaning no near-term dilution.
3. **Much of the damage is priced**: down ~55–78% from highs, ~2.3x 2026E sales; strategic-M&A interest (Lilly) and insider buying at $24 caps downside narrative risk; analyst median (~$32) brackets spot.

**Bear Case**
1. **Existential legal/regulatory concentration**: FDA GLP-1 compounding restrictions, FTC/state enforcement on privacy and billing, and securities litigation naming the CEO/CFO are *live, dated catalysts* (Nov 2 lead-plaintiff deadline), not tail risk — and they sit directly on the highest-ARPU revenue line.
2. **Fundamentals are deteriorating, not just resetting**: two consecutive EBITDA-negative quarters, gross margin −30pts in 18 months, leverage to 3.0x ND/EBITDA on a 25% cushion, SBC above net income, FCF −63% — leverage and inventory (GLP-1 API) carry impairment risk.
3. **Leading indicators contradict the consensus ramp**: brand and compounding search demand are in freefall while sell-side still models ~$893M Q3 revenue and H2 acceleration — expect a print-and-guide-down or another EPS miss (beat rate 42.9% over 7 quarters).
