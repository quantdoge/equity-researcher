## 6. Overall Alt Data Signal: **NEUTRAL (with a hard negative skew on structural demand)**

**Bearish weight:**
1. VantageScore search-mindshare spikes on every regulatory date — the competitive threat is real and lender-side evaluation is live, not theoretical.
2. **myFICO search interest −18% YoY and −55% off peak** — B2C funnel contracting faster than the stock's narrative reflects.
3. 15% RIF + offshore hiring only = growth apparatus being dismantled.
4. News flow still net negative; legal overhang unresolved.

**Bullish/offsetting weight:**
1. Consensus has been cut to a bar **below every recent actual** (85.7% beat rate).
2. Capitulation signature: −27% on ~16x average volume, then +30% recovery — classic washout.
3. Product/IP output has not slowed; UWM and direct-reseller licensing show the moat being actively defended.
4. Pricing-grid parity (VantageScore onto FICO Classic's grid) actually removes FICO's *price-under cutting* vulnerability even as it removes exclusivity — a mixed, not purely adverse, outcome.

**Net:** the alt data does **not** corroborate further downside from here, but it also does **not** support a clean re-rating. The structural break in mortgage-score exclusivity is a regime change, not a sentiment blip — permanently lower terminal multiple. Near-term, positioning and a sand-bagged consensus favour the Nov 4 print.
