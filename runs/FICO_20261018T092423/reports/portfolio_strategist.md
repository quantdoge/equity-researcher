## 2. Consensus Assessment — **Diagnosis: HIGH agreement. Action: SPLIT, with zero bullish conviction.**

This is the rarest and most decision-relevant configuration I have seen: **eleven agents, not one recommends buying.** The consensus on the *facts* is nearly unanimous and unusually well-evidenced:

- The mortgage scoring monopoly was removed by a regulator, not a competitor, in a single session (**−26.5% on 16x volume, 29-Sep-26**).
- FY2026's spectacular results (**+27% revenue, ~+45% EPS**) are a **price harvest ahead of the competitive wave**, not demand.
- The balance sheet has been hollowed out by **~$4.3B of buybacks at ~2x today's price**, leaving negative equity and junk ratings — removing the flexibility to defend price.
- The distribution channel **owns the competitor**.

The only genuine disagreement is **whether $771 already discounts it**:
- **Value** says fair value is ~$810 → ~5% upside, insufficient.
- **Growth** says sum-of-parts for Software + non-mortgage Scores alone is **~$600–650/share**, i.e. the market charges almost nothing for mortgage optionality — the bull case.
- **Short** says the bear case is real but **already monetised** — no edge either way.
- **Sell-side** median target **$1,057** (range $700 BofA–$1,230 Raymond James), but every one of those was cut **20–45% in the last three weeks**. That is a downgrade cycle *in motion*, not a floor.

**My weighting:** I weight the four risk agents (Geo Legal, Fin Risk, Non-Fin Risk, ESG) at full weight on the go/no-go decision — their evidence is documentary and contemporaneous. I weight Value and Growth fully on valuation. I **discount Quant** (stale data, R²≈0) and **Alt Data** (two of six feeds unavailable). The conflict between Value (bullish quality, no MOS) and Short (bearish thesis, no edge) resolves cleanly: **both conclude the expected return is insufficient to compensate for the risk.** That is a Pass.
