### Investment Memo: FICO — October 18, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (no new capital) | Conviction: **Medium-High**
**Price Target**: $810 (~+5% from current $771) — probability-weighted intrinsic value, not a return objective
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
FICO is a genuinely exceptional business — 70% ROIC, 82–87% gross margins, >100% FCF conversion — whose crown-jewel mortgage-scoring monopoly was revoked by its own regulator (FHFA) in September 2026, converting a permanent pricing moat into a contested duopoly against a $0.99 competitor co-owned by FICO's own distribution channel. The 60% drawdown has brought the stock to roughly fair value, not below it; FY2026's spectacular results (+27% revenue, ~+45% EPS) are a price harvest ahead of the competitive wave, and the Nov 4 FY27 guidance is the binary event that determines terminal economics. Eleven specialist agents reviewed this name; none recommends buying. With a negative-equity, junk-rated balance sheet and zero insider buying through a 75% peak-to-trough collapse, the expected return does not compensate for the risk.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Macro is *unfavourable* — 30Y mortgages at 7.40% (+142bp) suppress the highest-value score vertical; a bear steepener (10Y ~5.0%) with the market pricing *hikes* removes FICO's usual easing convexity; tight spreads and improving card delinquencies keep non-mortgage volumes healthy. Sector is mid-transition from monopoly to duopoly scoring; the bureaus (TRU/EFX) are the structural beneficiaries. Sector rating: Neutral.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH.** Single-regulator dependency crystallized in one session (−26.5%, Sep 29). ~51% of revenue flows through three CRCs that co-own VantageScore. Live DOJ antitrust investigation, Florida AG subpoena, Senate/FTC referral, and a four-firm securities-investigation cluster (disclosure-timing exposure). EU AI Act classifies scoring as high-risk.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: Wonderful business at a fair price. Probability-weighted IV ~$810 (bear $450 / base $850 / bull $1,100) vs $771 — **no margin of safety**. Buy trigger ≤$600. Blemish: ~$4.3B of debt-funded buybacks at ~2x today's price.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: The growth algorithm is broken — Q3 mortgage revenue +97% on **flat volumes** (100% price, 0% demand). FY27E decelerates to +9.8%. Sum-of-parts: Software + non-mortgage Scores support ~$600–650/share, meaning the market already charges little for mortgage optionality.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Composite Neutral (−0.54). Top-decile quality (ROIC 69.6%, negative accruals), bottom-decile momentum and low-vol (68.6% annualized vol, −64% max drawdown). R²≈0.005 — no market hedge, no diversification benefit. *Data caveat: quant price feed was stale; factor scores retained, price statistics discarded.*
- **Financial Risk**: **HIGH.** Ba2/BB+ junk; gross debt +82% in three quarters to $5.6B; net leverage 4.22x against a **4.0x covenant step-down on Dec 31, 2026**; equity −$4.1B; cash $248M. A −20% revenue shock is survivable (FCF still ~2x interest) but pushes leverage to 5.5–5.9x and halts the buyback engine.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH, trending Critical.** Zero insider open-market buying through the entire drawdown; 15% RIF announced four business days after the shock degrades exactly the functions needed to defend share; disclosure-timing scrutiny on pre-FHFA statements.
- **ESG**: Average with negative trajectory. The material risk was Social/license-to-operate, and it *already converted into a financial event* — the Hawley letter (Mar 2026) preceded the FHFA ruling by six months. Environmental risk negligible.
- **Alternative Data**: Neutral with negative structural skew. myFICO searches −18% YoY and −55% off peak (B2C funnel shrinking); VantageScore mindshare spikes on every regulatory date; Q4 consensus ($10.86 EPS) is sandbagged below all recent actuals — **the print likely beats, the FY27 guide is what gets punished.**
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **No edge.** Forensics are clean (Beneish −2.67, accruals −6.8%, cash conversion 1.20x) — this is not an accounting short; it was a moat-expiration short that already paid out (−75% peak-to-trough). Re-short zone $950–1,100; cover trigger is FHFA grid language preserving FICO pricing.

**Bull Case**
- The ex-mortgage franchise is intact and underpriced: Software + non-mortgage Scores (~$2B revenue, 46%+ operating margins) plausibly support $600–650 standalone — investors pay almost nothing for mortgage retention optionality, and UWM's reaffirmation plus the Direct License Program show active, effective defense.
- The Nov 4 bar is low: 86% historical beat rate against a consensus cut below every recent actual; a ≥$2.75B FY27 guide with >70% mortgage retention forces a violent re-rating.
- Asymmetric rate convexity: the market prices hikes; a growth scare that cracks the bear steepener (10Y → ~4%, mortgages → ~6%) triggers a refi wave on a 60%-de-rated stock with 15% of the cost base already removed.

**Bear Case**
- Regulatory rent repriced to zero: VantageScore at $0.99 through 2028 vs FICO's $4.95–$10; Rocket (largest originator) defected; a unified GSE pricing grid cuts FY27 EPS an estimated 25–35% — consensus has not marked this.
- Hollowed balance sheet: 4.22x net leverage, negative equity, junk ratings, and a 4.0x covenant test arriving Dec 31, 2026 — precisely when the revenue reset would hit. No flexibility to defend price.
- Distributor conflict is structural: the three bureaus that deliver 51% of revenue own the competitor; every incentive runs against FICO, and adoption share is now individually negotiated, lender by lender.

**Key Risks**
1. FY27 guidance on **Nov 4** below ~$2.6B or mortgage retention <70% (thesis-breaking).
2. A second top-5 originator defaulting to VantageScore (cascade risk).
3. Covenant/credit event: net leverage vs the 4.0x step-down; downgrade halts buybacks.
4. Filed 10b-5 class action with SEC formal investigation (solvency-adjacent overhang).
5. Mortgage rates staying ≥7% through 2027, compounding price loss with volume loss.
