## ELV — Geopolitical, Regulatory & Legal Risk Assessment
*Elevance Health, Inc. (NYSE: ELV) | Healthcare — Managed Care | As of late November 2026*

**Headline:** ELV has essentially **no cross-border geopolitical exposure** — it is a ~100% U.S. revenue business. Its risk is therefore almost entirely **domestic political, regulatory, and litigation risk concentrated in federal Medicare/Medicaid programs and state insurance oversight**. That concentration is presently elevated and is the single most important non-financial risk in the equity story.

---

### 1. Geographic Risk Map

ELV conducts insurance operations in **all 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico**; FY2025 revenue was **$199.1B** (+13% vs. $177.0B in 2024), TTM **$201.8B** at 9/30/26. The 10-K states the largest revenue concentrations are **California, New York, Virginia, Indiana, Ohio, Georgia, Florida and Texas** ([ELV 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1156039/000115603926000013/elv-20251231.htm)). **ELV does not disclose state-level revenue percentages** — figures below are qualitative, not quantified.

**International footprint** is limited to captive service entities — Carelon Global Solutions India LLP, Philippines Inc., Ireland Ltd., Puerto Rico LLC ([SEC Exhibit 21 subsidiary list](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1156039/000115603925000010/exhibit21-20241231forform1.htm)). **There is no FCPA/bribery red flag of note**, but the India/Philippines BPO footprint creates third-party/vendor and cross-border data transfer risk (HIPAA + state privacy laws).

**Structural franchise note:** 14-state operations run under **Blue Cross/Blue Shield licenses** ("Anthem" brands). The **$2.67B BCBS antitrust settlement (MDL 2406)** received final approval **Aug 19, 2025** and eliminated the rule forcing large national employers to use the Blues plan in their HQ geography — a modest **competitive negative** for Blues incumbents' national-account moat ([bcbssettlement.com](https://www.bcbssettlement.com/); [Becker's](https://www.beckerspayer.com/payer/the-market-is-reaping-the-benefits-of-bcbs-antitrust-settlement-elevance-says/)).

### 2. Top 3 Geopolitical / Political Risks

**Risk #1 — Medicare Advantage payment compression and risk-adjustment enforcement (Probability: High | Impact: High)**
This is the dominant risk. Three independent shocks have already landed:
- **CMS intermediate sanctions (Feb 27, 2026):** CMS imposed intermediate sanctions for "substantial and persistent noncompliance with Medicare Advantage risk adjustment data" — suspending new MA-PD enrollment and marketing from **March 31, 2026**. Shares fell ~8% on disclosure; ELV **set aside $935M** to cover potential costs ([CMS notice](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/elevancehealthsanction02272026.pdf); [Becker's](https://www.beckerspayer.com/payer/medicare-advantage/elevance-sets-aside-935m-over-medicare-advantage-enrollment-threat/)). **Mitigant:** CMS confirmed on **July 13, 2026** that Elevance completed all corrective-action steps ([CMS completion notice](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/elevancesanctioncompletion07132026.pdf)) — but the $935M reserve and underlying DOJ matter persist.
- **Rate environment:** The **CY2027 MA Advance Notice** projects a net average payment increase of just **0.09%** (~$700M industry-wide) ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2027-medicare-advantage-part-d-advance-notice)). ELV responded by **cutting its 2027 MA footprint to 308 plans from 345** and exiting **181 counties**.
- **Star-ratings exposure:** ELV has sued HHS twice — **Elevance I** (~$115M at stake, 2026 ratings, N.D. Ga., fully briefed) and **Elevance II** (**$516M** at stake, 2027 ratings, D.D.C., filed 11/6/26) ([Georgetown CHIR tracker, 11/22/26](https://medicare.chir.georgetown.edu/tracking-19-new-lawsuits-over-2026-and-2027-medicare-advantage-star-ratings-with-billions-at-stake/)). Note CMS **appealed** the favorable *Clover* precedent to the Eleventh Circuit (argument Jan 2027) — relief could be short-lived.

**Risk #2 — Medicaid and individual-market funding/policy shock (Probability: High | Impact: Medium-High)**
Medicaid margins are at a **2026 trough**, with management guiding improvement in 2027 on state rate updates, and targeting ≥12% adjusted EPS growth in 2027 off a ≥$26.75 baseline ([Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/elevance-health-posts-14b-profit-q3-earnings-beat); [Healthcare Finance News](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/elevance-raises-outlook-challenging-medicaid-performance-improves)). But ELV is actively **retreating**: it will exit Medicaid markets it deems unsustainable over **12–18 months** (analysts flag Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Arkansas as failing its own test), has exited the **Ohio small-group ACA market**, and is guiding to **≥900,000 individual ACA members** by YE2026 amid **expiration of enhanced premium tax credits** ([STAT, 7/15/26](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/15/elevance-health-shrink-medicaid-portfolio-high-costs/); [Modern Healthcare](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/insurance/mh-elevance-health-ohio-small-group-aca/)). Membership fell **1.5% to 44.9M**. Policy risk is bilateral: federal Medicaid cuts/work requirements and ACA subsidy lapse both compress revenue, while state rate increases are the swing factor.

**Risk #3 — State AG and private-enforcement wave (Probability: High | Impact: Medium)**
A coordinated multi-state push on **mental-health parity, "ghost" provider networks, SUD claim denials, and PBM practices** is now hitting ELV's Carelon division directly. On **Nov 10, 2026**, Carelon Behavioral Health settled with **NY AG Letitia James**: **$2.35M** in penalties/fees plus **up to $8M** restitution (≥$10.35M total) for inaccurate provider directories and wrongful SUD claim rejections ([NY AG](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-expands-access-mental-health-and-substance-use-disorder)). Florida opened a PBM CID in June 2026 (initially CVS), and payers broadly faced 2026 state penalties for slow reimbursement and parity violations. Expect this template to spread to other AGs — low dollar impact individually, but reputational and rate-negotiation drag.

### 4. Compliance Red Flags

1. **Risk-adjustment data integrity — 🔴 Red.** "Substantial and persistent noncompliance" finding by CMS, concurrent DOJ FCA litigation, and a contested witness-access dispute. This is a control-environment issue, not a one-off.
2. **Utilization management / AI-driven clinical decisioning — 🟠 Amber.** DOJ, CMS and OIG have all signaled 2026–27 focus on **AI in MA diagnosis coding and prior authorization** ([RISE National 2026](https://www.risehealth.org/insights-articles/live-at-rise-national-2026-cms-doj-and-oig-on-the-future-of-medicare-enforcement-and-use-of-ai-in-fraud-detection/)). Suppliers of AI coding tools are a live enforcement theory.
3. **Network adequacy / directory accuracy — 🟠 Amber.** Adjudicated in New York; a state-by-state repeat-risk.
4. **Data privacy — 🟡 Yellow.** HIPAA plus a patchwork of state privacy laws; offshore BPO (India, Philippines, Ireland) raises cross-border transfer and vendor-oversight exposure.
5. **FCPA / sanctions / export controls — 🟢 Green.** No material foreign revenue; no OFAC nexus identified.
6. **Disclosure controls — 🟠 Amber.** Securities class action (2025) plus a fresh investigation letter (2026) after a sharp guidance-driven drawdown.

---

### 5. Overall Geopolitical / Legal Risk Rating: **HIGH**

**Rationale.** ELV scores **very low on classic geopolitical risk** — no Russia/China/tariff/sanctions/currency exposure, no expropriation risk, U.S. dollar revenues. But it scores **high on the risk that actually matters here: single-sovereign, single-payer-adjacent policy and enforcement concentration.** Roughly all revenue derives from U.S. regulated insurance programs and U.S. employers, exposed simultaneously to (a) an active DOJ fraud case with treble-damages tail risk, (b) a demonstrated CMS willingness to suspend enrollment, (c) ~$631M of star-ratings litigation, (d) securities class-action exposure, and (e) an expanding state-AG enforcement template. Cross-jurisdictional diversification across 50 states provides operational but *not* political diversification — federal policy and DOJ/CMS actions hit the entire book at once.

**What would change the rating:** Upgrade to *Medium* on (i) dismissal or settlement of the DOJ risk-adjustment matter below reserve, (ii) favorable/binding star-ratings relief, and (iii) 2028 MA rate improvement. Downgrade to *Very High* on DOJ intervention escalation, a renewed or expanded CMS sanction, or a multi-state AG action targeting MA risk adjustment or prior authorization.

**Gaps disclosed:** ELV does not publish state-level revenue percentages; segment/state revenue splits in this note are qualitative only. The CMS sanction PDF was not machine-readable and was characterized from the SEC 8-K and secondary reporting. Precise accruals for the DOJ matter beyond the $935M reserve are not publicly disclosed.
