**Investment Thesis**
Elevance is the best-margin franchise in managed care (4.07% operating margin vs 1.72% peer median) emerging from the 2024–25 medical-cost shock with a credible 2026 turnaround (MLR improved 70bp to 90.6%, FY26 EPS guide raised to ≥$29, ≥12% growth targeted for 2027). The de-rating from ~20x to ~13x forward has largely happened — but so has the recovery. At 91.5% of our ~$415 intrinsic value, the stock offers no margin of safety while carrying an unquantifiable regulatory tail (the CMS "CRUSH" rule reaches back to 2018; an active DOJ risk-adjustment fraud matter). Right name, wrong price: the entry point is ~$330 or the day after CRUSH is resolved, whichever comes first.

**Bull Case**
- 2026 turnaround delivered and compounding: Q3 EPS $6.99 vs $4.71 consensus, FY26 guide raised twice, 2027 setup has Medicaid rate updates, MA margin expansion and Carelon scaling all pointing the same way; consensus 2027 ($30.31) already sits 7% below management's target — upside if they simply execute.
- Valuation floor: 12.7–13.1x forward with a 1.8% dividend and ~4.9% total capital-return yield, in a sector whose rate cycle has turned favourable (+2.48% CY2027) with a structural MA penetration runway (55%→63% by 2034).
- Relative winner in the regulatory storm: least MA-exposed major to the $107B Star-Ratings/CRUSH shock; a Dec-9 Fed skip or a softened/litigated CRUSH rule re-rates the stock to 14–15x = $420–450 (+11–19%).

**Bear Case**
- CRUSH rule finalized as drafted: contract-wide RADV extrapolation retroactive to 2018 on a company already found in "substantial and persistent" noncompliance, with a parallel DOJ FCA matter — a multi-billion tail nobody can size, on a balance sheet with $4.5B of tangible equity.
- The earnings base is lower-quality than the multiple implies: FCF $3.2B vs $4.1B of capital returns, 76% cash conversion, Medicaid at −1.75% margin, and management's base rate of seven guide-downs in 25 months — the Street has already voted not to believe the 12% 2027 target.
- Structural, not cyclical, margin compression: administratively-set pricing with no pass-through mechanism, operating margin down in four of five years, and a −20% revenue scenario takes interest coverage to ~3.0x and forces buyback suspension (removing 2–3pts of EPS growth).

**Key Risks**
1. **CRUSH final rule + DOJ FCA escalation** (highest severity, unquantifiable) — retroactive MA clawbacks; exit trigger if finalized as drafted or if DOJ intervenes.
2. **2027 guidance below ~$31 at the Jan-27 print** — breaks the "2026 is the trough" narrative; consensus already at $30.31.
3. **Medical cost trend re-acceleration** — each 100bp of MLR ≈ ~$2bn pre-tax; Q4 is the seasonally dangerous quarter on a thin $2.08 consensus.

5. **Macro tail**: Dec + Mar hikes with 10Y >5.50% de-rates even "cheap" defensives toward 10x (~$300).
6. **Financial-policy tell**: buyback suspension or goodwill impairment signals FCF is worse than guided.
