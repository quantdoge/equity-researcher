**Investment Thesis**

Netflix is a genuinely high-quality franchise — ROIC 16.0% → 32.4% in three years, operating margin 17.8% → 29.5%, net leverage 0.18x — that has de-rated ~46% from its peak almost entirely on the discount rate (10Y TIPS +87bp), not on deteriorating earnings. At $70.53 the stock sits slightly *above* hand-built intrinsic value (~$65 mid) yet well *below* growth-case fair value ($88–95); the disagreement is a WACC/terminal-growth judgement, not arithmetic, and the macro regime currently favours the conservative side. The result is a quality compounder at a fair-to-full price with a poor 12-month risk/reward (full Kelly ≈ zero): too good to sell, not cheap enough to buy — and a binary earnings event in two trading days that will hand us either confirmation above $88 or a value entry near $60.

**Bull Case**
- **Ad-tier asymmetry**: ~1.5% share of a $200–250B CTV ad pool with 325M+ ad-capable households; delivering the "double to $3B" pledge at high incremental margin re-opens the growth narrative at an 18.6x multiple.
- **Margin and capital-return engine**: ~300bp/yr operating-margin expansion plus ~$9B+/yr of buybacks shrinking the float ~4% converts ~11% revenue growth into ~19% EPS growth; PEG ~1.0 against a $600B+ TAM.
- **Rate-driven re-rating optionality**: the de-rating was mechanical (real yields), so a Fed unwind — with core PCE momentum at ~2.0–2.1% annualised and breakevens *falling* — re-rates the stock 25–30% on multiple alone, with no change to any operating assumption.

**Bear Case**
- **Engagement-led decay**: content hours −21% YoY, streaming share <20%, price-led revenue growth into a saturated UCAN/EMEA base — the classic terminal move of a maturing subscription utility; FY27 guidance ≤10% justifies a 15x multiple (~$57).
- **Disclosure retrenchment + cash-conversion decay**: management is withdrawing the KPIs that would verify engagement while OCF/NI halves; buybacks at 96% of FCF (3.4x FCF in Q2) manufactured EPS near the top.
- **Duration re-rating tail**: a term-premium accident (30Y >6%) compresses the multiple toward the low $60s regardless of the print — this stock is currently a duration instrument.

**Key Risks**
1. **20 Oct print / FY27 guidance** — a second ad-revenue miss or ≤10% FY27 framing breaks the $65 floor (Wells Fargo $57).
2. **Real yields above ~3.25%** — each 100bp ≈ 13–17% of fair value.
3. **Content-cost inflation from live sports** — fixed-cost, low-margin rights erode the very ROIC that justifies the premium.
4. **Execution discontinuity** — new CCO, 1,000 layoffs, and any 2027 slate guidance issued before Rice is in seat is low-confidence.
5. **DOJ conduct probe / US film tariff** — unquantified, politically driven.
6. **Data-integrity caveat**: beta, alpha and revision-factor outputs were artifacts; 10-K/10-Q/proxy text was unavailable to three agents (litigation quantum, related-party detail unverified); off-balance-sheet content commitments — arguably the largest true claim — are unobservable.
