### Investment Memo: MU — 13 November 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD | Conviction: **Low** (widest agent dispersion on record for this team: FV estimates span $390–$1,400)
**Price Target**: $850 (+8% vs. $788.54 close, 12-Nov) — probability-weighted; scenario band **$470–$1,350**
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Micron is the strongest-franchised version of a commodity memory maker in its 48-year history — net cash $33–38B, capacity sold out through 2027, and a genuinely new contracted floor (26 SCAs through 2030, $12.9B customer deposits) — earning an 80.7% gross margin that no memory company has ever sustained. Both sides of the debate are right on different timelines: FY2027 earnings are real and contractually locked, while FY2028–29 margin mean reversion is near-certain, amplified by an $8–12B deferred depreciation wave from peak-cycle capex. The 28–35% de-rating since June is the market pricing that turn in advance, as memory equities always do; at ~$850 probability-weighted value, expected return is positive but uncompensated against 88% volatility and a −70% tail — a sizing decision, not a directional one.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Best industry structure memory has ever had (3-player DRAM oligopoly, HHI ~2,700–3,300), but 80.7% GM is ~2x any prior peak; bit supply +16–17% vs. demand in 2026, with CXMT and greenfield supply landing 2027–28 as handsets/PCs contract 15–20%. Macro: earnings are Fed-orthogonal (AI capex is cash-funded), but a hiking Fed and 10Y at ~5.1% govern the multiple on a β-2.4 name. Both **NEUTRAL (late-cycle)**.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **HIGH (lower bound)**. Taiwan is 12.7% of revenue *and* hosts leading-edge HBM fabs — correlated chokepoint. BIS Affiliates Rule cliff Jan-2027; Section 232 tariffs live; CXMT at 10% DRAM share is a state policy instrument. Positives: Netlist settled ($600M), zero DOJ/SEC/FTC actions, China revenue down to 6.3%.
- **Value**: **SELL** — FV ~$425; normalised EPS $25–30 at 15–18x; 8x *peak* EBITDA is expensive for a cyclical (5x peak = $505). "Cheap for a reason."
- **Growth**: **BUY (staged)** — SCAs + deposits genuinely truncate the left tail; 4.5x FY27E; FV $1,170–1,250. Beat magnitude decaying (+32.8% → +5.2%).
- **Quantitative**: **BUY, not Strong** — #1 of 7 semis on *both* value and quality (ROIC 69%, accruals −2.4%, Piotroski 8/9), **dead-last on low-vol** (vol 88%, β 3.31, alpha insignificant). Composite top ~37%.
- **Financial Risk**: **MODERATE, bimodal** — solvency-safe (D/E 0.04, 937x coverage, no maturity wall, survives a −60% revenue depression for ~4 years on cash) but equity-fragile (−64% on $40B normalised EBITDA; capex/D&A 3.2x queues the margin drag).
- **Non-Financial Risk**: **HIGH** — live DRAM price-fixing class action (Jun-2026) atop 600–700% cumulative price rises; **$279M net insider selling, zero buys in 90 days** (CEO sold ~$182M near the peak); CEO/Chair duality since Jan-2025; AI Data Center Moratorium Act (S.4214) is a demand-side tail.
- **ESG**: **AVERAGE / Neutral** — strong operational climate execution (100% US renewable electricity met) offset by governance regression and the Clay NY Article 78 permit litigation, which is **capex-schedule risk** on the $100B flagship, not a footnote.
- **Alternative Data**: **NEUTRAL** — fundamentals say the 23-Dec print tracks **above** $38.20 consensus (60%+ contract pricing, sold-out 2027); positioning says caution (search interest led the top by ~2 months, retail still euphoric at 75/100). The drawdown is sector-wide (NVDA −31%, SK hynix −30%), not MU-specific.
- **Short / Contrarian**: **SHORT (cycle, not fraud)** — forensic work *cleared* the company (negative accruals, 105% OCF/NI; Beneish M-score a mechanical false positive). The real flags: DEPI (slowing depreciation rate) and DSRI (receivables outpacing sales) — earnings flattered at the margin, not fabricated.

**Bull Case**
- **Sold-out 2027 + SCA floor**: >75% of 2027 output committed; $12.9B customer cash deposits are hard proof customers believe the shortage is durable; HBM share gains vs. SK hynix into a sold-out market.
- **Cash machine with a fortress balance sheet**: $59B FCF, net cash $33–38B, FQ1'27 (23-Dec) tracking above $38.20 EPS consensus; four straight $10B+/quarter revenue steps.
- **Structural re-rating optionality**: if price-band contracts scale toward 40–50% of revenue, the historical boom/bust multiple logic weakens and 8–10x on $150+ EPS = $1,200–1,500.

**Bear Case**
- **Mean reversion is certain; only timing is debatable**: 80.7% GM vs. a 90-point historical cycle range; Bernstein models DRAM **−53% in 2028**; three independent methods converge on $390–480 (−45%).
- **The depreciation wave is already funded**: $30.7B capex vs. $9.5B D&A (3.2x) means $8–12B/yr of deferred D&A lands exactly as pricing rolls — an accounting drag independent of demand.
- **Smart money is exiting**: $279M+ net insider selling with zero purchases through a 40% drawdown; memory equities de-rate 6–9 months before ASPs peak — the 28% fall on *beat-and-raise* prints is that signal firing.

**Key Risks**
1. **2028 supply cliff meets demand destruction** (CXMT 13.9% share by 2027 + greenfield fabs vs. −15–20% handset units) — the dominant risk.
2. **AI-complex sentiment beta**: −17.5% on a single Nvidia headline; β 3.31 with 10Y at 5.1%+ and a hiking Fed. Position can halve without any MU-specific news.
3. **Taiwan concentration** (revenue + leading-edge capacity correlated) and the **Jan-2027 BIS Affiliates Rule cliff**; MATCH Act cuts both ways.
4. **Antitrust escalation**: private class action is live; any *government* investigation (2002–06 precedent: nine-figure fines, criminal pleas) is a rating-changing event.
5. **Estimate/data fragility**: FY27E EPS range $138–$341 (±48%); FY26 figures, though corroborated across agents, are unprecedented and should be verified against the primary 10-K; revision feeds returned broken data.
6. **Clay NY permit litigation** — capex schedule slip on the flagship US fab, with CHIPS milestone/clawback mechanics attached.

Do not chase strength above ~$1,050 absent estimate revisions; the add zone is <$550–600 where base-case normalisation is already priced. Any downside expression should be long-dated put spreads, never naked short (2.4–3.3 beta, sold-out book). The 23-December print is the next decision gate — treat it as a sizing event, not an earnings event.

**Bottom line**: an excellent operator at a cyclical peak, de-rated but not cheap on the right denominator. Hold small, respect the gates, and let the December print — not the narrative — set the next move.
