### Investment Memo: ONON — 12 November 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (Initiate, small size) | Conviction: **Medium**
**Price Target**: $42 (~21% upside from current $34.70)
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
On Holding is the strongest-positioned listed pure play on the only growing pocket of a flat footwear market — performance running (+13% vs. +1% category growth) — with best-in-class gross margins (62.8% FY25, ~67% underlying), a record 45.7% DTC mix, net cash, and founders who bought $10.6m of stock at the lows. The stock has de-rated ~45% from highs *while EPS grew* — this is a real-rate-driven multiple compression, not fundamental deterioration, and at 16x FY28 consensus EPS / ~0.9 PEG the market is pricing a deceleration the TAM math (~1% of a $550bn market) doesn't support. The thesis is gated on earnings quality: reported margins are flattered by CHF 52.6m of tariff refunds and a 0.7% tax rate, governance is weak, and the macro backdrop is hostile to a 2.0-beta long-duration name.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector: premium performance niche *attractive* within a *neutral*, flat category; Nike/Puma ceding ~$8bn of scale that On is absorbing. Macro **unfavourable**: real 10Y ~2.93% with the Fed hiking (3.88%), UMich sentiment 48.1 — the worst backdrop for a high-beta compounder, and the primary cause of the de-rating. Mitigant: core CPI anchored at 2.5%, so a rate stabilisation is the re-rating trigger.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: **Medium risk.** ~52–58% US revenue against ~90% Vietnam footwear sourcing, in a tariff regime rewritten three times in 12 months. Tariff refunds moved Q3 GM by ~570bps — policy is a top driver of reported margin. Legal exposure immaterial ($ terms); watch CBP transshipment enforcement and the CloudTec/"Swissness" class actions.
- **Value**: BUY. Net cash, ROIC 17% and rising, GM up ~700bps in four years with genuine full-price selling power. Agent's $52 DCF was re-triangulated by the Portfolio Strategist on consensus to **~$42** (25x FY27E EPS $1.725; street median PT $40.50) — ~21% upside, not 33%. Narrow moat, re-earned seasonally; bear case (~$26–28) is near the September low.
- **Growth**: BUY. 47.9% four-year CAGR; apparel +39%, APAC +31%, DTC +34% cc. But growth has halved (30%→17%), Rule of 40 is sub-threshold (~33), and Q3's beat was margin-driven on in-line revenue. FY26 consensus EPS ($1.456) looks stale vs. ~$1.57 YTD adjusted.

**Bull Case**
- Structural share gainer in the one growing sub-segment, with pricing power proven by 66.9% underlying GM *in a promotional market* and record DTC — consensus FY29 ($5.8bn revenue, ~14% CAGR) is below what 1%→3% TAM share mechanically delivers.
- Multiple has de-rated to ~16x FY28 EPS / 16x FY26E EBITDA on consensus while fundamentals improved; a real-rate stabilisation alone recovers much of the 45% drawdown, with insider buying marking the floor.
- Operating leverage inflecting: op margin 9.1%→12.5%→~15%+ guided, with DTC mix and LightSpray as structural margin levers.

**Bear Case**
- Growth durability: 30%→17% in four quarters, wholesale softening, two guide-downs in 2026; a FY27 guide to mid-teens compresses the multiple regardless of margin quality (DECK at 8x EBITDA implies ~$20–25).
- Earnings quality: Q3 GM of 72.6% is 66.9% ex-refunds; FY25 net income flattered by a ~0.7% tax rate; FCF −43%; reported run-rate profitability is overstated.
- Exogenous macro: real 10Y at 2.93% and a hiking Fed against a 2.0-beta, 50%-vol name — cheap can stay cheap; negative 3-year Sharpe says holders have been paid nothing for the risk.

**Key Risks**
1. FY27 guidance below mid-teens or a third consecutive reset (primary thesis risk).
2. Tariff regime reversal / CBP origin enforcement on Vietnamese footwear — margin is partly a policy variable.
3. Brand-heat decay in a fad-prone category with no switching costs.
4. Governance: founder-controlled, fused CEO/Chair, untested bench, unexplained CHF 67.9m statutory loss.
5. Working-capital deterioration (DSO 47 days, inventory watch) and CHF translation headwinds.
