### 1. Consumer Demand Signals — *Mixed, improving into Q4*

**Google Trends (US, 12-mo):**

| Query | Nov-25 | Peak (Apr-26) | Oct-26 | Nov-26 | YoY Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "On Running" (brand) | 59–60 | 100 | 72–75 | **80** | **+25%** |

| "Hoka" (competitor) | 42–50 | 100 | 29–32 | 31 | **−35%** |

- **Brand-level interest is rising:** "On Running" has climbed from a July trough of 63 to 80 in the week to 1-Nov — the strongest reading since the April peak, and +25% YoY. This is the cleanest *directional* read and it is positive into the Q4 selling season.
- **Product-level queries are contracting:** "On Cloud" and "Cloudmonster" are both down ~30% YoY and still trending lower sequentially.
- **Crucially, On is not losing share:** Hoka is down ~35% YoY over the identical window, and the On/Hoka search ratio is essentially flat (1.55x now vs ~1.60x a year ago). The absolute decline looks **category-wide seasonal normalisation**, not brand-specific deterioration. On is holding relative share against its closest competitor.

**Web traffic:** on-running.com visits **+29.8% month-over-month** (SimilarWeb public data). This is the most direct read-through to On's fastest-growing, highest-margin channel — consistent with management's Q3 disclosure of DTC +25% vs wholesale +12.2%.

*Caveat:* "On Running" is contaminated by generic running/company-news queries; "On Cloud" is contaminated by cloud-computing/"on cloud nine." On's product proliferation (Cloudsurfer, Cloudboom, LightSpray) fragments product-term searches, mechanically understating demand in any single query. No app-store data was available for this name.

### 7. Confidence Level: **MEDIUM-LOW**
Driven by: (a) two of five business-activity data sources (patents, job postings) returned no usable data; (b) Google Trends keyword contamination and product fragmentation materially limit the product-level conclusions; (c) SimilarWeb figure is a search snippet with no absolute visit series, no regional split, and an imprecise window; (d) social data is qualitative and unquantified.

**Data limitations and lag:** Google Trends is weekly and re-normalised per pull — only relative direction is meaningful, and the Nov-26 series ends 1-Nov, *before* the Black Friday/holiday window that dominated last year's spike (Nov-23-25). The web-traffic reading likely carries a 2–4 week lag and cannot isolate paid vs organic or promotional vs full-price visits. All sentiment scores are keyword-based, not human- or LLM-graded. Alt data is a leading indicator, not a definitive one.
