## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

### Rating: **INVESTMENT GRADE** (balance-sheet equivalent ~BBB+/A-; not formally rated in the data provided)

On Holding carries **net cash**, not net debt. Total debt of CHF 581.7m sits against CHF 1,021.1m of cash and short-term investments, for **net cash of CHF 439.4m (−1.24x EBITDA)**. Interest coverage is 17.2x on FY25 EBIT and ~20.7x on trailing-twelve-month EBIT (CHF 516.9m EBIT vs ~CHF 25.0m interest). This is a lender's dream credit profile — and the reason it is *unrated* is simply that it doesn't need public debt.

**A structural point the headline number obscures:** total debt of CHF 581.7m versus the sum of reported short-term (CHF 138.1m) and long-term (CHF 2.8m) borrowings leaves **~CHF 441m unclassified** — almost certainly IFRS-16 lease liabilities from owned retail stores and offices. On a *financial-debt-only* basis, leverage is roughly **CHF 141m gross against CHF 1,021m cash — a 7.2x cash cover of borrowings**. There is no refinancing cliff and no plausible covenant breach path.

The one credit-negative trend: gross debt rose **+67% in FY25** (CHF 347.6m → 581.7m), which is why Piotroski's leverage test fails (F5 = 0). This is growth capex and lease expansion, not distress — but direction matters.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Free cash flow collapse.** FY25 FCF CHF 253.2m vs CHF 445.6m — **−43%** — on *rising* revenue (+30%). Operating cash flow fell 37%. The driver is a **CHF 304m working-capital swing** (+141.8m source in FY24 → −162.8m use in FY25). Not explained by inventory (DIO actually improved, 167 → 137 days), so it points to payables/accrual reversal — needs the FY25 20-F to confirm.

2. **Margin compression is being masked by a data anomaly.** Adjusted for the implausible Q3'26 COGS, Q3'26 operating margin was ~14.1% versus ~15.7% in Q3'25. **SG&A grew +21% against +17% revenue in Q3'26** — negative operating leverage at the exact moment growth is slowing. Q2'26 SG&A also outgrew sales.

3. **Growth deceleration confirmed by a guidance cut.** FY25 revenue grew 30%; Q3'26 grew 17.2% YoY; management guided to "low-20% range" growth, triggering a **−20.3% single-day crash** on 2026-08-11 — a record one-day decline to two-year lows.

4. **Return on assets deteriorating.** 12.2% (FY24) → **7.8% (FY25)**. This is a genuine Piotroski fail (F3) and the reason the F-Score sits at 7 rather than 9.

5. **Leverage moving the wrong way.** Gross debt +67% in a single year (CHF 347.6m → 581.7m) with an emerging CHF 138.1m short-term component. Immaterial today against CHF 1,021m cash — but the trend is the wrong sign (F5 = 0).

6. **Abnormally low tax rate flatters earnings.** FY25 pre-tax income (CHF 205.2m) versus net income (CHF 203.7m) implies a **~0.7% effective tax rate**, versus 10.8% in FY24. Normalising to a mid-20s rate would cut FY25 net income by roughly 20%. TTM P/E of ~21x is really ~26x normalised. The LTM net-income trajectory (CHF 15.7m in H1'25 → CHF 442.5m TTM) is not a clean series.

7. **Uncompensated volatility.** 3-year Sharpe of **−0.109** with 48% annualised vol and a −58% max drawdown. Shareholders have absorbed enormous risk for negative risk-adjusted return.
