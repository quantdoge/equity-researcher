## 2. Consensus Assessment: **SPLIT — but a resolvable split**

**Fundamentals: high agreement (~8 of 10 agents).** Sector, Value, Growth, Alt Data, ESG, Geo Legal and the insider tape all converge: this is a genuinely elite-margin, founder-led, net-cash franchise taking structural share, and the *business* is executing better than the *stock*.

**Return drivers: fundamental disagreement.** The split is not about the company — it is about **which variable sets the price**:
- **Fundamental camp** prices ONON off growth durability and margin convergence → BUY.
- **Macro + Quant camp** prices it off the discount rate and the tape → real 10Y at 2.93%, alpha −38%, momentum −6.5%, negative Sharpe → SELL.

**Resolution (precedence rules applied):**
1. **Macro/Quant win the *timing and sizing* argument, not the *direction* argument.** The de-rating is provably exogenous — EPS grew while the stock fell 45%. I cannot forecast real yields, so I must not size as if the re-rating is imminent. Nine months of tape says "cheap can stay cheap."
2. **The risk agents win the *quality-of-earnings* argument outright.** Non-Fin's governance finding and Fin Risk's FCF/tax-rate findings take precedence over the Value bull case — not because the business is impaired, but because **the headline numbers investors are underwriting are not the run-rate numbers.** Specifically: Q3 GM of 72.6% is 66.9% ex-refund; FY25's ~0.7% effective tax rate flatters net income ~20%; FY25 FCF fell 43% on a CHF 304m working-capital swing; SG&A is outgrowing revenue (Q3: +21% vs +17%).
3. **Governance is a permanent discount factor, not a fixable item.** Co-CEOs who co-Chair a dual-class board the minority cannot influence. This belongs in the discount rate and the position cap, permanently.
4. **Insider buying is the single most credible counterweight** — founders put $10.6m of personal capital in at $30.67–36.64 immediately after the August collapse. That is why this is Initiate and not Pass.

**Why the sub-3% cap survives the bull case:** 87% of ONON's variance is idiosyncratic (R² 0.126). That is *good* for diversification — genuine alpha potential — but it means a 3% position behaves like a 3% *event bet*, not like 3% of market exposure. Combined with weak governance (no lever if execution slips a third time) and a policy-dependent gross margin, the sizing must be governed by downside tolerance, not by upside conviction.

## 7. Risk Management Rules

**Stop-loss: do not use a tight price stop.** A 49%-vol name with two −11%/−20% single-day gaps (25-Mar, 11-Aug) will stop you out on noise. Use these instead:

| Rule | Level / Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard price floor** | **$26.50** (Sept-26 low, −24% from spot) | Exit. A break of the capitulation low with no fundamental repair means the bear case ($20–25) is in play |
| **Position-loss circuit-breaker** | −20% from average cost | Halve the position and re-underwrite; do not average down without a clean print |
| **Sizing drift** | Position >3.0% of portfolio from appreciation | Trim back to 2.5% mechanically |

**Thesis invalidation — any two of these = exit, regardless of price:**
1. FY27 revenue guided **below mid-teens**, or a third consecutive guide-down.
2. **Underlying (ex-refund) gross margin below 64%** — the full-price selling story is broken, and margin is the *entire* moat proof.
3. **SG&A grows faster than revenue for a third consecutive quarter** (the operating-leverage thesis inverts to permanent).
4. **DTC growth decelerates below wholesale** growth — the highest-quality demand indicator reverses.
5. **Inventory grows faster than sales** in any reported period (invalidates "healthy full-price sell-through").
6. A **CBP transshipment/origin penalty** on Vietnamese footwear, a formal greenwashing action (EU Green Claims / state AG) targeting CleanCloud or "preferred materials", or a forced-labour finding in the Vietnam chain.
7. **Founders reduce stakes**, or the 100% living-wage target is missed again.
8. **2025 statutory loss (CHF 67.9m) is not satisfactorily explained** in the FY26 20-F — this is an open diligence item and an accounting-quality tripwire.

**Trim triggers:**
- **$38 (90% of IV):** halt all accumulation; if the position has grown above 2.5%, trim to 2.5%.
- **$42 (fair value):** trim to **1.0%** — take the re-rating, keep a stub for the FY28–29 TAM compounding.
- **$47–50:** trim to **0.5%** core. Only hold through this level if FY27 revenue is tracking ≥20% *and* GM is ≥66% without refunds.

**Monitoring calendar:** Q4/FY26 results **~2-Mar-2027** (a duplicate 18-Mar listing exists — confirm the date); FY2026 20-F (for the CHF 67.9m statutory loss and the commentary on the CHF 304m working-capital swing); FY26 Impact Report (absolute Scope 3, Tier 2 renewables, living-wage 77%→100%); CBP/USTR actions on Vietnam origin; the "Swissness" and CloudTec class actions.
