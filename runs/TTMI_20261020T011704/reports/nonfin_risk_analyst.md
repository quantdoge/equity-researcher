# TTMI — TTM Technologies, Inc.
## Non-Financial Risk Assessment

**Company snapshot:** $10.4B market cap PCB/RF/mission-systems manufacturer; 18,200 employees; 24 facilities (US, China, Malaysia, Canada); three segments (A&D, Commercial, RF&S). FY2025 net sales $2.91B (+19%), operating margin 9.1%. CEO Edwin Roks (relatively new in seat); Chairman Rex D. Geveden. Beta 2.14 — high volatility. Active M&A: Epiq Solutions (closed Sep 30, 2026), Ophir Optics ($650M, announced Oct 19, 2026), $500M 6.75% senior notes (Sep 2026).

---

### 1. Governance Quality Rating: **Adequate (trending Strong)**

**Evidence:**
- **No CEO/Chairman duality.** Rex Geveden (independent, since Feb 2018; Chairman since May 2021) is separate from CEO Edwin Roks. Geveden is himself CEO of BWXT — relevant A&D expertise, though his primary full-time role elsewhere limits bandwidth.
- **Board independence and expertise look genuine,** not box-ticking: Wajid Ali (Lumentum CFO, designated "audit committee financial expert"), Julie England (ex-TI VP, RFID/microprocessor), plus A&D/defense pedigree. Independence explicitly affirmed in filings.
- **Fresh CEO transition = transition risk.** Roks is early-tenure. Continuity risk around strategy, the aggressive M&A program (Epiq, Ophir) and the Syracuse ramp falls on a new leader.
- **Governance hygiene present:** standalone Whistleblower Policy for anonymous fraud/accounting reporting; formal ethics code for CEO/Senior Financial Officers; 2023 Incentive Compensation Plan with RSU-based director/executive alignment; $100M buyback authorized through May 2027.
- **Watch item:** Repeat EBSA (benefit plan administrator) violations — 2021, 2023, 2024, and two in 2025 (~$100K each). Small dollars, but a *recurring* compliance-control pattern in benefits administration, not a one-off. Indicates back-office control weakness worth monitoring.

---

### 2. Regulatory Risk Summary

- **No active SEC enforcement or DOJ investigation surfaced** in EDGAR full-text enforcement search — a clean result and a genuine positive.
- **Historical penalty record (Violation Tracker): $15.05M total since 2000, 14 records** — largely legacy/predecessor:
  - $9.7M water pollution violation (2004, Tyco Printed Circuit Group — predecessor)
  - $5.05M False Claims Act (Telephonics: $4.25M 2017 + $0.8M 2000) — **defense contracting integrity history**, relevant given growing A&D revenue
  - ~$270K aggregate EBSA benefit-plan violations (2021–2025)
  - Minor OSHA/EPA/Scaqmd items ($5K–$7.2K)
- **Structural regulatory exposure — the real risk, not fines:**
  - **Environmental/hazardous materials:** PCB fabrication is chemical-intensive; RCRA/hazmat liability is inherent.
  - **Export controls / ITAR / FCPA:** Expanding defense and space-compute (Epiq SDRs, radiation-tolerant compute) deepens exposure to export-control and anti-corruption regimes across China/US facilities.
  - **Tariffs & US–China trade policy:** Explicitly flagged in 10-K as material to material cost and revenue. China manufacturing footprint is a live policy-risk vector.
  - **Data privacy:** December 2023 data breach exposed SSNs/health data; class-action investigation active (no confirmed settlement found). Modest but unresolved reputational/legal tail.

### 3. Insider Activity Analysis (trailing ~12 months): **Net Buyer — constructive**

- **CEO Edwin Roks purchased 10,000 shares at $111.77 (~$1.12M) on Aug 25, 2026** — an **open-market, non-10b5-1** buy, lifting direct holdings to 64,814. Conviction signal from the top, especially notable near all-time-high prices. This is the single most meaningful insider datum.
- **Routine, non-alarming selling:** Officer Daniel Boehle planned sale of 5,890 shares (~$660K) via Form 144, after June sales of 10,559 shares partly to cover taxes. Consistent with diversification/tax, not a red-flag dump.
- **Large Form 4 cluster in late June 2026** (20+ filings) = RSU vesting and tax-withholding events, not discretionary sales. Sept 2, 2026: Roks had 5,331 shares withheld for RSU tax at $115.33 — standard mechanical.
- **Net read: modest net insider *buying* by the CEO against routine tax-driven dispositions.** No insider-selling red flag.

### 4. Top Operational Risks

- **Customer concentration + hyperscale/AI demand cyclicality.** One customer ≈12% of revenue; top OEMs (defense primes and hyperscalers) are a large share of sales. A single program deferral or hyperscaler capex pause hits results disproportionately. The 91% data-center growth that's driving the story is also the concentration that creates fragility.
- **M&A integration and execution overload.** Three simultaneous strategic moves — Epiq (closed), Ophir Optics ($650M at a rich >18x forward EBITDA, announced Oct 19), and the greenfield Syracuse ultra-HDI plant (volume 2H26) — strain management bandwidth and absorb capital ($240–260M capex guided for 2026) while leverage rises via $500M new notes. Integration missteps or overpayment are the key value-destruction vector. Diligence on Ophir's customer/contract transfer not visible.
- **Geographic/supply-chain concentration and single-source inputs.** Manufacturing concentrated in US + China with Malaysia/Canada expansion still ramping; labor shortages and wage inflation flagged in both US and China. Tariff/trade-policy shifts and the Syracuse ramp timing are binary operational risks. Capacity-utilization sensitivity is high given capital intensity.

---

### 5. Reputational Risk Assessment: **Low-to-Moderate**

- **Brand standing intact.** Positioning as a strategic national-security/AI-infrastructure supplier is a reputational asset; the Syracuse domestic-capacity and defense-backlog ($1.7B backlog, $7B+ pipeline) narrative plays well with policymakers and investors.
- **Soft spots:** (a) unresolved **December 2023 data breach** class-action exposure — sensitive SSN/health data; (b) **Telephonics False Claims Act history** — any new defense-contracting integrity issue would be doubly damaging given the A&D pivot; (c) PCB fabrication carries inherent environmental-community exposure; (d) recurring EBSA compliance lapses are a minor governance-optics irritant.
- **No consumer-brand or activist/ESG controversy** in current news flow. Press tone is growth/analyst-positive, with valuation and supply-constraint caveats rather than controversy.

---

### 6. Overall Non-Financial Risk Rating: **MODERATE**

Justification: Clean governance structure (independent chair, credible board, active whistleblower policy, strong CEO insider buying) and no live SEC/DOJ enforcement offset elevated **operational/integration risk** from a three-front M&A-plus-greenfield expansion, high **customer and geographic concentration**, and genuine **trade/export-control** exposure. This is not a fraud or governance-crisis profile — it's a high-execution-risk growth story. Beta of 2.14 amplifies any operational disappointment.

---

### 7. Red Flags Requiring Escalation

1. **Simultaneous M&A + greenfield + new debt + new CEO** — compounding execution risk. Ophir at >18x EBITDA (>$650M all-cash, announced Oct 19) needs a hard look at purchase-price discipline and financing/leverage trajectory alongside the $500M 6.75% notes. **Escalate to valuation/credit team.**
2. **Customer concentration (~12% single customer, plus hyperscale/defense concentration)** combined with a 91%-growth data-center segment — a demand air-pocket would be amplified. **Escalate for scenario/stress discussion.**
3. **Recurring EBSA compliance violations (four events, 2021–2025)** — small dollars but a repeating control-failure pattern. Diligence board/audit-committee oversight of back-office controls.
4. **Unresolved 2023 data breach class action** — size of exposure unquantified; verify reserve/disclosure adequacy.
5. **US–China tariff/export-control exposure** across China fabs and expanding defense/space-compute lines — monitor policy and ITAR/export-license risk.
