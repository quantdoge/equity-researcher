### Investment Memo: TTMI — October 20, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (do not initiate at $99.10; conditional buy plan below) | Conviction: Medium
**Price Target**: $100 (~1% upside from current $99.10); credible range $85–115
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**

TTMI is a genuinely improving specialty-PCB manufacturer riding two real demand engines — AI data-center boards (Q2'26 revenue +37% YoY, record $1.0B) and defense (44% of sales, $1.7B backlog) — with forensic accounting that our short analyst could not impeach. But it is a capital-intensive, high-operating-leverage cyclical that generates **zero free cash flow at the peak of its cycle**, has never sustained ROIC above its cost of capital, and is now layering ~$1.75B of debt-funded M&A onto the balance sheet. At $99.10 the stock sits at the midpoint of every credible intrinsic-value estimate in this stack ($85–120) with a binary earnings catalyst in two days — there is no margin of safety, only a coin-flip at 85% volatility.

**Key Findings by Domain**

- **Macro & Sector**: Sector is in an early-to-mid upcycle — CCL demand +34% into 2026, AI capex >$600B, genuine high-end capacity scarcity, and a ~460bp gross-margin re-rating at TTMI that evidences real pricing power. Macro is the offset: 10Y at 5.09%, CPI re-accelerating to 3.72%, Fed on a hiking bias, copper +35% YoY — a hostile discount-rate and input-cost regime for a 2.1–3.3-beta, long-duration, copper-intensive name. Sector rated Attractive with a late-inning caveat: announced industry capex becomes competing capacity in 2027–28.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Net policy *beneficiary*, not victim. The §4873 DoD ban on China-fabricated PCBs (effective Jan 1, 2027) shifts share to the largest US fabricator; TTMI's ~15% China revenue is the exposed slice. No material litigation; clean on sanctions/FCPA. Customer concentration jumped to 55% of sales (top-10), amplifying any single prime's sourcing decision. Rating: Medium-High, skewed to share-shift/compliance-cost risk rather than existential.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: HOLD. Fair value $85–88 vs. $99.10 price. ROIC history (1.7%–9.1%) has never cleared WACC; FCF negative (-$0.7M FY25) at cycle peak with capex at ~13% of sales; trailing P/E 59x, EV/EBITDA 27.5x. The price capitalizes 2029–30 outcomes. Entry discipline implies ≤$68–70.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY (staged). Revenue +37% and accelerating, 7/7 EPS beats (avg +14.9%), FY26 guidance raised to ~$4.4B, FY28 EPS revisions +20%. On a haircut to consensus (~$4.20 FY26 / ~$6.00 FY27 EPS), the stock is ~16.5x 2027 — reasonable for 30%+ growth. Caveat: only 2 analysts model revenue; consensus margins exceed management's own 16–18% EBITDA framework.
- **Quantitative Signals**: Neutral/high-risk. Value and low-vol factors both bottom-quintile; 12-1 momentum **-15.3%** despite +34% trailing return — classic mean-reversion setup. Alpha +19% vs. SPY but beta 3.3, vol 85%, max drawdown -57.6%. F-Score 8/9 and negative accruals are the bullish threads.
- **Financial Risk**: Solvency is the strong point — on the *pre-deal* balance sheet: 1.24x net debt/EBITDA, ~7.8x interest coverage, $501M cash + $1B revolver, first maturity 2029. A 20% revenue decline is absorbed. But **pro-forma for Epiq ($1.1B, closed 9/30) + Ophir ($650M all-cash, announced 10/19), net debt moves toward ~$2.2–2.4B (~2.5–2.7x FY26E EBITDA)** — the credit profile is materially weaker than the stated metrics and under-flagged across the stack.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: Moderate. Clean governance (independent chair, no SEC/DOJ enforcement) and a constructive insider signal — CEO Roks bought $1.12M open-market at $111.77 in August. The real risk is execution overload: Epiq integration + Ophir + Syracuse greenfield ramp + new CEO, simultaneously, with rising leverage.
- **ESG**: Average; not a thesis driver. Transmission channels are water/chemical permitting at Asian fabs (seven-figure precedent) and conflict-minerals/UFLPA/export-control compliance given the 44% A&D mix. Scope 3 unaddressed — a watch item for hyperscaler qualification.
- **Alternative Data**: Fundamentals lean bullish into the Oct 22 print (perfect beat streak, aggressive RF/capacity hiring, positive deal flow); attention and price structure lean bearish (Google Trends -55% from peak, stock -26% in nine sessions). Net: high-variance catalyst, data skewed to a beat, positioning hostile.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: **Short thesis affirmatively rejected.** Beneish M -2.40, accruals -3.0%, F-Score 8/9, OCF/NI 1.65x — the numbers are real; this is a cyclical/valuation bear case, not fraud, and it has already been paid out (-55% from the $221 June peak). One unresolved flag: the **$411M receivables swing Q1→Q2'26** (DSO ~122→~65 days while revenue rose 19%) — could be a collection/prepayment, could be factoring; must be resolved from the 10-Q footnote. Re-short trigger only at $140–155.

**Bull Case**
- AI substrate/HDI scarcity holds through 2028: 15–20% revenue growth with 13–14% operating margins, FCF inflects hard as capex normalizes — fair value $120–130 (Value's bull case; Short analyst's 2027 EBITDA math agrees).
- Policy is a tailwind, not a risk: §4873 reshoring + $1.7B defense backlog + Penang "China+1" capacity make TTMI the designated Western beneficiary; defense decorrelates the equity from AI-capex sentiment.
- The de-rating is done: multiple already compressed from ~45x to ~14–17x 2027 earnings, guidance was raised post-collapse, the CEO bought at $112, and Third Point is building a position.

**Bear Case**
- FCF is ~$0 at the absolute top of the cycle (capex ~10–13% of sales) while pro-forma leverage roughly triples to ~2.5–2.7x — the growth is being purchased with debt at peak multiples (Ophir >18x EBITDA).
- FY2023 is the rehearsal: a 10.5% revenue decline swung net income negative. A 2027 AI-capex digestion + the industry's simultaneous 2027–28 capacity wave hits utilization, and a PCB maker re-rates from 22x toward 8–12x EBITDA (severe case: $21–25/share).
- The macro regime (5%+ 10Y, sticky CPI, copper +35%, 25% Section 232 tariffs) specifically penalizes this asset profile; the 2Y already prices ~90bp of hikes.

**Key Risks**
1. **Oct 22 print (2 days)**: consensus $1.25 EPS / $1.13B revenue; the real test is the Q4 guide (~$1.4B implied) and whether adj. EBITDA margin clears 17%. Binary against an 85%-vol distribution.
2. **Pro-forma leverage / FCF**: Q3 10-Q must confirm the combined Epiq+Ophir debt load and capex trajectory; another capex raise with FCF still negative confirms the bear thesis.
3. **The $411M receivables swing** — factoring vs. collection; the single highest-priority diligence item.
4. **Margin mean-reversion**: 21.1% GM / 10.9% OM are cyclical highs; operating margin was 1.9% three years ago.
5. **Concentration**: top-10 customers = 55% of sales; ~80% of revenue tied to AI + defense themes; 12% single-customer exposure.
6. **§4873 compliance execution** (IPC-1782 traceability certification) and Chinese rare-earth/gallium export controls on inputs.
