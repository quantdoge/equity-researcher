## 1. Credit Risk Assessment

**Rating: Investment Grade (solidly)**

MRVL carries modest leverage for a cyclical semiconductor. At FY2026 (ended 31-Jan-2026): total debt $4.79B, cash $2.64B, **net debt $2.15B = 0.47x EBITDA**. Debt/equity 0.33x, debt/assets 0.21x. Interest coverage on operating income is 6.6x and 16.0x on the reported EBIT line — comfortably serviceable.

Fitch rates the senior unsecured notes **BBB+** (entity page, dated 11-Mar-2026), i.e. mid investment grade. The near-term refinancing cliff has already been addressed: the **1.65% notes due 15-Apr-2026 (~$500M) were repaid**, funded by a new **$1.0B 5.300% senior notes due 2036** issued April 2026. Post-refinancing gross debt rises to ≈$5.3B and interest run-rate steps up (5.30% vs 1.65% on the replaced tranche), but coverage remains ample.

**Verdict: no covenant, coverage, or refinancing stress in the base case.**

## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis

**Base FY26:** Revenue $8.19B, gross margin 51.0%, operating income $1.338B, EBITDA $4.540B, interest $203M.

**Scenario A — Revenue −20% (AI/data-center order digestion), gross margin held, 10% opex cut:**
- Revenue → $6.56B; gross profit −$836M
- Operating income → **≈$786M** (9.6% margin)
- Interest coverage → **≈3.9x**
- EBITDA → ≈$2.08B; **Net debt/EBITDA → ≈1.04x**
- FCF still comfortably positive (~$0.7B)
- **Credit outcome: fully investment grade, no covenant pressure.**

**Scenario B — Revenue −30% with modest margin compression:**
- Operating income → ≈$0.4–0.5B; EBITDA ≈$1.8B
- Coverage ≈2.3–2.5x; **Net debt/EBITDA ≈1.2x**
- Still no liquidity or covenant stress.

**The asymmetry:** the *balance sheet* survives a 30% revenue decline without distress. The *equity* does not survive gracefully — at ~0.74% FCF yield and a ~55x+ trailing earnings multiple, a 20% revenue miss compresses the multiple violently. With beta 2.6, a fundamental reset plausibly produces a **50–65% drawdown**, in line with the observed −60.8% historical max. **This is a valuation-risk story, not a solvency-risk story.**

Additional scenario flag: goodwill & intangibles of **$13.1B = 59% of total assets**. Tangible book equity is only ~$1.2B. A structural AI-demand reset would likely trigger a material impairment, hitting book equity and book-based ratios sharply (though not cash flow or covenants).

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Earnings quality — large non-operating gap.** FY26 operating income $1.338B but net income $2.670B. The ~$1.5B pre-tax gap below the operating line (non-operating gain and/or tax benefit) is **not** operating earnings. Normalise before extrapolating. Corroborated by Piotroski's accruals-quality failure.
2. **Valuation / FCF yield.** 0.74% reported FCF yield, 0.42% ex-SBC. No cash-flow cushion in the price. Any multiple de-rating is brutal.
3. **SBC dilution.** $591M SBC (7.2% of revenue); share count still rising modestly despite $2.04B of buybacks (Piotroski dilution flag).
4. **Shareholder returns exceed FCF.** $2.25B returned vs $1.40B FCF — a ~$850M shortfall funded from balance sheet/cash-flow elsewhere. Not sustainable if growth slows.
5. **Goodwill concentration.** $13.1B intangibles vs $1.2B tangible book — impairment risk on an AI-demand reset.
6. **Market risk.** 74.7% vol, 2.58 beta, −60.8% max drawdown. Position sizing matters more than fundamentals here.
