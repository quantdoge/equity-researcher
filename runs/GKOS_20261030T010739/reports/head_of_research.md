### Investment Memo: GKOS — October 30, 2026

**Verdict**: SELL (avoid new money; reduce into strength) | Conviction: **Medium-High** on valuation, **Low** on timing
**Price Target**: $130 (−46% from current $242.48) — probability-weighted intrinsic value; re-entry zone $185–200 gated on iDose LCD clarity
**Time Horizon**: 12–24 months

**Investment Thesis**
Glaukos is the best growth story in ophthalmic medtech — 58% revenue growth, 85% GAAP gross margins, two FDA-approved category-defining products (iDose TR, Epioxa), a net-cash balance sheet, and zero forensic red flags. But the stock, after tripling in twelve months and gapping +34% on the Q3 print, now trades at ~18x FY26E sales and **above the average of the six price targets published the morning after the print**. The bears aren't disputing the company; they're disputing the arithmetic: prob-weighted intrinsic value is ~$130, the bull-case DCF ($213) sits *below* spot, and 2030 consensus earnings capitalised at a generous 30x produce less equity value than today's market cap. We decline to pay for a flawless decade in advance, with a binary Medicare reimbursement decision pending on 56% of revenue.

**Key Findings by Domain**
- **Macro & Sector**: Sector backdrop is a modest tailwind at best — scaled ophthalmic incumbents grow 5–6.5%, pure-plays are shrinking; GKOS's alpha is company-specific. Macro is neutral on demand (GDP 3.4%, U 4.2%, pricing power) but the 10Y at 5.05% (+50bp since July) is a mechanical 20–25% de-rating risk on a ~90%-terminal-value multiple. Zero debt insulates the balance sheet, not the multiple.
- **Geopolitical & Legal**: Geopolitically a ~84% US pure-play with no Russia/China exposure of consequence (LOW). The real risk is domestic regulatory: the equity story now hinges on CMS reimbursement for two J-coded drugs; new pharma-compliance surface (MDRP/AKS/FCA); $14.1M UC Regents charge plus ongoing royalty; active SpyGlass IP litigation. Rating: LOW–MEDIUM.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Value**: SELL. Narrow (widening) moat not yet proven in returns — ROIC negative four straight years, accumulated deficit −$969M. Intrinsic value ~$130/share (base $128, bull $213, bear $56); reverse DCF at 9% WACC demands ~21–23% revenue CAGR for a full decade. 0.25% FCF yield; owner earnings negative after $83M SBC. Analyst consensus targets ($225–255) are now behind the price.
- **Equity Fundamentals — Growth**: BUY on weakness only. Genuine inflection — growth accelerating +41%→+50%→+58%, GM +1,000bps in 18 months, first GAAP profit, FY26 guide raised $90M, FY27 pre-announced at $1B, 6-of-7 beat rate. But at 18x FY26E sales the market already discounts ~$2.9B of 2030 revenue (~38% CAGR). Preferred entry $200–215.
- **Quantitative Signals**: NEUTRAL. Top-quintile momentum (+101% 12-1) and real upward revisions, but bottom-quintile Value and Low-Vol, Piotroski 3/9, alpha t≈1.7 (insignificant), R² vs SPY 0.026 — beta 0.74 is unusable for sizing; true risk is 64.6% idiosyncratic vol and a −53.7% three-year drawdown.
- **Financial Risk**: MODERATE. Net cash (~$139M), 4.69x current ratio, converts retired — no solvency event in any realistic scenario. But negative EBIT/FCF every year on record, SBC 12.5% of revenue, receivables +79% vs revenue +32% (DSO 46→79 days). A −20% revenue shock doubles the operating loss; the real downside is re-rating, not cash burn.
- **Non-Financial Risk**: MODERATE (upper end). ~$28M of insider sales vs **zero open-market buys** in 12 months — COO $10.8M single-day, CFO metronomic monthly — much of it *preceding* the $1B FY27 guidance raise. Chair/CEO duality, disclosed single-source supplier dependence, 58% growth on a 1,094-person base. Clean SOX, no enforcement.
- **ESG**: NEUTRAL. Genuinely clean controversy record, 7/8 independent board, third-party-assured GHG data; offset by no Scope 3, no targets, combined Chair/CEO, 67:1 pay ratio, ~8% say-on-pay dissent. Not a valuation input; monitor product safety and healthcare compliance only.
- **Alternative Data**: BULLISH (medium confidence). Hiring skews to manufacturing scale-up and patient access; web traffic +19–89% MoM; the Q4 bar ($227M, +7.5% QoQ vs recent 14–25% cadence) looks conservative — upside skew into the 25-Feb-2027 print.
- **Short / Contrarian View**: SHORT (not strong short). Earnings quality LOW: reported FCF +$36M vs SBC −$83M → **FCF net of SBC is −$47M**; 14x GAAP-to-non-GAAP gap; DSO +33 days in two years; ~$122M Q4'25 goodwill impairment. But no fraud evidence — Beneish clean, accruals cash-honest — so this is a valuation/reimbursement short, sized small, against seven straight quarters of momentum.

**Bull Case**
- Two FDA-moated category creators inflecting simultaneously: iDose TR (re-administration approved, converting one-time procedures into recurring revenue) and Epioxa (sole epi-on CXL, +154% y/y with a permanent J-code from Jul-2026) — FY27 $1B guide implies only ~28% growth off the Q4 run-rate, arguably conservative.
- Pharma-economics mix shift: 85%+ gross margins, operating margin inflected from −41% to positive in three years; ~30% compounding through 2030 yields ~$2.2B revenue at still-low-teens penetration.
- Scarcity value: a sub-$15B asset compounding >30% with sole-source CXL positioning is a strategic target for growth-starved ALC/BLCO — a floor under the multiple, and a neutral LCD outcome is a clear re-rating event.

**Bear Case**
- Valuation arithmetic: $242 discounts ~$4B+ of 2030 revenue vs $1.64B consensus; even the bull DCF ($213) is underwater; consensus 2030 NI at 30x = ~$8B equity value vs $14.3B today.
- Reimbursement concentration: five MACs propose restricting iDose TR (56% of Q3 revenue, growing 47%) to post-SLT + two-topical-failure patients — a binary policy event the company doesn't control.
- Earnings quality and incentives: FCF is negative after SBC, ~29% dilution over five years with no buyback, and the people selling $1B of 2027 to the market sold ~$28M of their own stock in the prior twelve months.

**Key Risks** *(to the SELL call, stated honestly)*
1. **Momentum risk**: seven straight quarters of beats; a neutral LCD plus a Q4 beat could gap the stock to $280–320 before fundamentals matter. This is why conviction is Medium, not High, and why we are not recommending an outright short for most books.
2. **LCD resolution skew**: if the final LCD is benign, the single largest overhang clears and the growth camp is right for 4–6 more quarters.
3. **Estimates are stale**: consensus feeds lag the raised $775–785M FY26 guide and $1B FY27 target; the multiple is less extreme on company numbers (18x/14x) than on stale consensus.
4. **Downside triggers that would confirm the call**: adverse LCD; growth <30% in any quarter; DSO >80 days with deceleration; two consecutive GM declines; a second impairment; SBC >10% with stalled GAAP margin.
