## 4. Downside Scenario Analysis

**Scenario A — Revenue −20% (severe ad recession, 2-year duration):**
- Revenue $402.8B → **$322B**; assume operating margin compresses from 32% → ~22% (ad mix + AI capex drag) → EBIT **~$71B**
- Interest coverage still **>95x**; letter-of-the-law solvency unaffected
- OCF falls ~25–30% to ~$115B; FCF drops to **~$24B** after the $91B capex program → FCF yield ~0.6%
- Gross debt/EBITDA rises to ~0.5x; net cash stays positive (~$20–30B even without cutting capex/returns)
- Z-Score remains comfortably >2.5. **No covenant or refinancing stress** (IG issuers carry negative covenants only; no near-term cliff given $126.8B liquidity vs. $59.3B debt)

**Scenario B — Revenue −30% stress (ad collapse + cloud slowdown + capex overrun):**
- Revenue ~$282B; EBIT margin ~15% → EBIT ~$42B; coverage still **>55x**
- FCF approaches zero if capex holds at $90B — liquidity buffer ($126.8B cash) still covers debt 2x-plus; solvency intact
- Rating pressure would be theoretical only; 2 notches of downgrade from AA+ would still leave A+/AA territory

**Conclusion:** Alphabet is structurally immune to downturn-driven insolvency. The downside scenarios that matter are **earnings- and sentiment-driven** (multiple compression, FCF-yield erosion from the AI capex supercycle), not credit-driven.

## 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Piotroski F-Score down to 6/9** — F3 (ROA improvement) and F9 (asset turnover) scored zero in FY2025: the asset base grew faster than revenue as $91B of capex came online. Not alarming, but a sign of capital-intensity inflection worth monitoring.
2. **Total debt more than doubled in 2025** ($25.5B → $59.3B), including a 100-year bond — a deliberate financing decision, yet it converts a previously invisible leverage line into something the market can price.
3. **FCF yield of just 1.76%** (P/FCF ~51–57x) — the AI capex cycle is compressing free-cash-flow conversion; if capex stays at ~$90–100B and revenue growth slows, buybacks will be funded from OCF rather than surplus. This is a valuation/market risk, not a solvency one.
4. **Elevated market risk** — 31% vol, beta 1.11, 29% max drawdown: GOOG behaves like a high-beta growth name in drawdowns despite its fortress balance sheet.
5. **Regulatory tail risk (qualitative)** — antitrust remedies (DOJ search/ad-tech cases) could impair the highest-margin ad businesses; not quantifiable from the tools, but a genuine earnings-risk overlay.
6. **Data gap flagged:** per-tranche debt maturity schedule and CDS spreads not available — but given $126.8B cash vs. $59.3B total debt, no refinancing cliff can credibly exist at this leverage.
