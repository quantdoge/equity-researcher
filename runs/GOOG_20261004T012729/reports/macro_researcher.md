# MACROECONOMIC ASSESSMENT — Alphabet Inc. (GOOG)
**Prepared by: Macro Economy Research | Date: October 4, 2026**

## 1. Macro Environment Summary: TRANSITIONAL (late-cycle expansion, rates at cycle highs, risk appetite intact)

The US macro backdrop for Alphabet is a **solid-growth, sticky-inflation, elevated-rates** regime. Real GDP is growing ~2.2–2.5% annualized (Q2-2026 real GDP $24,408B, +0.55% QoQ), unemployment sits at a benign 4.2% (Sep-2026, up from 4.0% in early 2025 but stable), and the 2s10s curve has **de-inverted (+34bp)** — no near-term recession signal. Advertising and enterprise-IT budgets track nominal GDP, which is running hot (~5-6% nominal), a direct tailwind for Alphabet's two engine rooms (Search/YouTube ads and Cloud).

The complication is rates and inflation. The Fed eased ~150bp from the July-2024 peak (5.33% → 3.63% by Jan-2026) but the effective funds rate has ticked back up to **3.75% (Sep-2026)** and the 10-year Treasury has **repriced to ~5.0–5.3%** (5.24% Oct 1; 5.29% Sep 30) — near multi-decade highs on sticky inflation, heavy supply, and rising term premium. Headline CPI **re-accelerated from ~2.4% YoY (Jan-2026) to ~3.3% YoY (Jul/Aug-2026)**. This is a "no landing / re-acceleration" inflation regime, not a disinflation regime. The dollar is strong (broad index ~120.3, Sep-25-2026).

For a $4.1T mega-cap growing revenue 24%, the earnings tide is strongly positive; the valuation-discount-rate headwind is the key macro tension. Net stance: **risk-on for fundamental growth, risk-off at the margin on duration/multiple**.

**Net macro read:** The operating environment (nominal GDP growth ~5-6%, no recession, sticky-but-not-exploding inflation) is **conditionally favorable** for Alphabet's top line. The financial environment (10Y ≈ 5%, USD ≈ 120) is **unfavorable at the margin** for its multiple. The balance sheet insulates it from the rate pain that hits REITs/banks/utilities.

## 4. Macro Risk Rating: **NEUTRAL**

- Not **Favourable** because: 10Y at ~5.0–5.3% compresses long-duration multiples even for best-of-breed AI franchises; inflation is re-accelerating rather than converging to 2%; the dollar is a handbrake on international revenue; and a ~$4.1T market cap at ~35x+ forward earnings leaves little room for macro disappointment.
- Not **Unfavourable** because: 2.2-2.5% real growth with no curve inversion, a de-anchored-but-tolerant inflation regime that feeds nominal ad budgets, easing-cycled Fed with room to cut if growth wobbles, and a net-cash balance sheet to which rates are a mild *credit* positive. GOOG is a high-beta beneficiary of any risk-on continuation.
- Net: **Neutral with a constructive tilt** — fundamentals (24% revenue / 63% cloud growth) dominate the macro discount-rate headwind at current prices, consistent with the WSJ "ad and cloud growth acceleration justify the surge" framing.

## 5. Macro Scenario That Could Materially Surprise (Bear, ~30% probability)

**"Term-premium shock": the 10-year grinds to 5.75–6.0%.** With CPI back above 3% and re-accelerating, the Fed forced to pause/possibly re-hike, record Treasury supply, and the market questioning whether $190B of AI capex earns its cost of capital, the long end reprices another ~75bp. This is the sharpest knife to a mega-cap growth multiple: a 25-30% drawdown in GOOG becomes plausible *despite* flawless operating results, as cloud growth partially migrates from "AI monetization" to "capex-funded depreciation drag" in investor framing, and the strong dollar shaves another point off reported growth. Watch: weekly CPI prints, 10Y auction tails, and any Fed communication reverting to "higher for longer."

**Bull corollary (for balance):** if energy-driven disinflation resumes in H2-26, the Fed resumes cuts to ~3.0% by mid-2027, 10Y returns toward 4.3%, and cloud margins inflect upward — GOOG re-rates toward $400+ with the AI-cycle intact.
