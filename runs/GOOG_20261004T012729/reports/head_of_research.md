**Bull Case**
1. **Growth re-acceleration is real and broad:** 7 straight beats, revenue +24%, Cloud >$20B/quarter at +63%, record margins while investing — a second S-curve from a $400B base with +18% estimate revisions behind it.
2. **Full-stack AI moat:** the only company owning frontier models (Gemini), custom silicon (TPUs) and distribution (Search, Android 3B+, YouTube, Workspace); AI is widening — not eroding — the cost and data advantage, and Cloud bookings are the accelerant.
3. **Regulatory tail risk resolved toward benign:** Chrome/Android divestiture rejected, Wiz probe closed, consumer antitrust suit dismissed — most structural outcomes are now off the table, removing the anchor of the old discount, with buybacks (~$46B/yr) supporting the stock.

**Bear Case**
1. **Capex without conversion:** FCF guided to ≈zero in FY26 on $195–205B capex, and a $60–75B/yr D&A ramp by 2028 taxes operating margin and EPS at exactly the moment monetization is in question — the Rule-of-40-type derating risk (FCF margin 18%).
2. **AI disruption of the profit engine:** conversational AI intercepts query *growth* (AI-search traffic +527% YoY; organic queries −40% per third-party data); Search monetizes per query at a multiple of AI-answer surfaces. This is the one scenario that breaks the moat math.
3. **Legal + macro double-hit:** a surviving/expanding remedies outcome (ad-tech divestiture on appeal, EU non-compliance penalties up to 10% global turnover) atop a 10Y repricing to 5.75–6.0% (macro bear, ~30% prob) produces a 25–30% drawdown despite flawless operations. Value desks are correct that entry at $340 embeds near-flawless execution.
