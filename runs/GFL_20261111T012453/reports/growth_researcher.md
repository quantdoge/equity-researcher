## GFL Environmental (NYSE: GFL) — Growth Analysis
*Data as of Nov 10, 2026 close ($42.41). A pivotal week: the board rejected take-private bids.*

---

### 1. Growth Story
GFL is North America's #4 diversified environmental services platform (solid waste, liquid waste, soils/remediation) executing a dual engine: **industry-leading organic growth (7.4% in Q3'26, ~5.7% from core pricing)** plus an aggressive **M&A densification strategy** (~C$2B of annualized revenue acquired YTD'26, including SECURE Waste Infrastructure at C$6.4B EV, closed Sept 2026). The investment case is not frontier-tech disruption — it is a **FCF inflection story**: Adjusted EBITDA margins expanding ~115bps underlying, Adjusted FCF guided to >30% growth (C$1.02B in 2026), and leverage falling to the mid-3s, which should force a re-rating toward WM/RSG multiples. Critically, the board just **rejected a ~$52/share (~US$22B) PE take-private bid from Blackstone/KKR/ECP as too low, signalling it believes standalone value is $58+** — a hard, third-party valuation floor.

### 3. TAM Assessment and Penetration
- **TAM:** North American solid waste collection/disposal/transfer/recycling ≈ US$85–95B, plus liquid/industrial waste and remediation ≈ US$40–50B → **~US$130B+ addressable**, growing ~4–5% nominal (GDP+ pricing).
- **Penetration:** 2026E revenue ≈ US$5.8B = **~5% share**. WM (~US$25B) and RSG (~US$16B) are 2–4x larger; the long tail of independent haulers remains fragmented → **decades of tuck-in runway**.
- **Differentiation:** vertically integrated disposal network (post-SECURE densifies Western Canada), Canada-to-US platform, and pricing discipline. Moat is **route density and disposal assets (landfill permits = local oligopoly)**, not technology. Sustainable moat, but not an innovation moat.

### 4. Upcoming Catalysts
- **Feb 2027 (Feb 10): Q4'26 results + 2027 guidance** — first clean standalone print after the rejected bid; watch FCF conversion toward mid-40s% and leverage to ~3.0x.
- **SECURE integration & synergy disclosure** (full-year contribution in 2027) — 12–15% Adj FCF/share accretion claimed at announcement.
- **Buyback resumption / capital return**: C$2.97B repurchased in 2025 (≈15% of shares), paused during the review; a restart is a direct FCF/share catalyst.
- **Take-private optionality re-emerging**: board has effectively published a **$58 clearing level**; bidders (Blackstone/KKR/ECP at ~$52; Brookfield/IFM interested) may return at a higher price.
- **Potential rating upgrade** as leverage hits mid-3s → lower cash interest (currently ~C$455M/yr).

### 6. Growth Verdict: **BUY** (moderate conviction; not a Strong Buy)
GFL is the best *organic* grower in waste (7.4% vs WM's ~4%), with pricing power, expanding margins, and a genuine FCF inflection. At **~13.4x 2026E and ~11.9x 2027E EV/EBITDA** vs WM 23.4x / RSG 17.9x (FMP calc; likely overstated — on adjusted EBITDA WM ≈16x, RSG ≈14x), and **~22x 2026E Adjusted FCF falling to ~17x**, the discount is too wide for the growth delta. A PE consortium already validated ~$52, and the board says $58+.

**12-month fair value: $52–55** (15x 2027E EBITDA ≈ $30B EV, less ~US$5.6B net debt). Downside ~$37–38 if leverage stalls and multiple holds at 11x. Risk/reward roughly 2.5:1.

Why not Strong Buy: growth is **majority acquisition-driven** (organic Rule of 40 ≈20), the SECURE deal cost **20.8% dilution** and pushed leverage back up, and the board just **removed the near-term catalyst** by rejecting a 23% premium — execution is now the whole story.

### 7. Key Risks
1. **Execution/label risk post-rejection:** the board has publicly committed to a >$58 standalone value. Any miss now reads as "management overplayed its hand"; the stock could retest $33–38.
2. **Leverage & rate sensitivity:** mid-3s net leverage, ~C$455M cash interest, 10-yr UST near 5% with Fed hike expectations rising → refinancing costs and covenant optics.
3. **Acquisition quality:** goodwill-heavy roll-up; ROIC dilution, integration risk at SECURE, and $2B of YTD revenue bought at cycle-peak multiples.
4. **Organic volume softness:** CEO cites a "challenging macroeconomic backdrop"; 7.4% organic leans on 5.7% pricing — volumes may be flat-to-negative, making growth price-dependent.
5. **Earnings optics:** YTD'26 net loss from continuing ops of −C$409.8M and negative headline EPS screen badly and can pressure the multiple regardless of Adjusted EBITDA.
6. **Competition:** WM/Casella and RSG density advantages, plus municipal in-sourcing and pricing pushback in a soft industrial economy.
