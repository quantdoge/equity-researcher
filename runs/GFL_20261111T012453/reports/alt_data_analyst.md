The two halves disagree, and the disagreement is the finding:

- **Event & sentiment alt data (bearish):** takeover floor removed, volume-confirmed de-rating, JPMorgan Underweight on leverage, retail bearish.
- **Net:** a structurally sound operator that has just lost its bid premium, now in price discovery against a **$42 (JPM) to $59 (Barclays) target range**. The residual ~18% gap to the rejected $52 bid is a real option on a return of a bidder — but that is event risk, not an alt-data signal.

### 7. Confidence Level: **Medium**
**Why not high:** (a) no ticker-level primary alt-data feeds — Google Trends, web traffic, hiring and social are all web-search proxies; (b) **no satellite/geospatial or geolocation data**, which for a waste hauler is the highest-value alt dataset and the real gap here; (c) patents unavailable; (d) revenue estimate series internally inconsistent; (e) news sentiment tool demonstrably lagged the largest catalyst in the window.
**What is reliable:** price/volume reaction (hard data), the Q3 guidance raise, and the peer-relative move (WM/RSG up vs GFL down).

**Limitations & lag:** Google Trends is weekly and US-only, while GFL's base is US+Canada; "dumpster rental" is a national proxy that does not isolate GFL's footprint or its pricing; hiring counts are snapshot, not a trend; social data is directional at best. Alt data is a leading indicator — here it describes a healthy operating business attached to a de-rated equity, and it offers no read on the leverage question that actually drives the stock.
