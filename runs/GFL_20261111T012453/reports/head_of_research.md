### Investment Memo: GFL — November 11, 2026

**Verdict**: HOLD (PASS on new capital) | Conviction: Medium

**Investment Thesis**
GFL is a genuinely improving franchise — best-in-class organic growth (7.4%), real pricing power (+5.7% core, above CPI), and EBITDA margins (31.5%) converging on RSG/WCN — wrapped in a balance sheet and governance structure that prevent minority holders from capturing that improvement. Reported leverage (~4.85–5.2x) sits at or above the 5.00x maintenance covenant with compliance dependent on pro-forma add-backs; "Adjusted FCF" does not reconcile to cash; and a founder with ~94% of votes on ~6% economics just rejected a $52 take-private. At $42.41, the ~15–20% discount to fair value is compensation for those risks, not a mispricing.

GFL is the sub-scale #4 (operating margin 5.2% vs 18.1% peer median): a self-help story, not a sector-beta story. Macro NEUTRAL/negative tilt — the model is operationally resilient (pricing >3.8% CPI, defensive volumes, CAD-translation tailwind) but is a **levered duration asset**: HY OAS at 3.34% (cycle-tight) while the Fed hikes at 3.88%. The credit spread, not GDP, is the governing variable.

The 50% US tariff on Canadian municipal/clinical waste (Oct 30, 2026) hits GFL's Ontario→Michigan corridor — a low-single-digit % EBITDA irritant with tail risk of volume bans. **Bear Case**

2. **Cash-quality gap**: adjusted FCF doesn't trace to cash; tangible book is negative; ROIC 2.24% after a decade of roll-up M&A.
3. **Permanent governance discount + event damage**: bid floor removed, three-month technical uptrend broken, no catalyst until Feb 10 — while a credit-spread normalisation to ~450bp HY OAS delivers 25–30% downside with zero operating deterioration.
