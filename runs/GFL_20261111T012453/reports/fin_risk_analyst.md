### 1. Credit Risk Assessment — **SUB-INVESTMENT GRADE (crossover BB)**

GFL sits in the upper-tier high-yield bucket: **Moody's Ba2 corporate family rating** (upgraded from Ba3 in 2025 on debt reduction), with **S&P senior unsecured in the 'BB-' area and a '4' recovery rating (30–50% recovery)**. Not investment grade, but two notches into "junk" rather than deep distressed.

The credit story is **two very different pictures depending on which denominator you use**, and that ambiguity is itself the risk:

| Lens | Net debt | EBITDA | Leverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reported LTM (Q3'26) | ~C$11.6B | C$2,257M | **~5.2x** |
| Pro-forma run-rate (mgmt) | ~C$11.6B | ~C$3.1–3.3B | **~3.5–3.8x** |
| FY2025 (pre-SECURE, tool) | C$7,847M | C$1,999M | 3.93x |

GFL **re-levered hard in 2026**: long-term debt rose from C$7,422.6M (Dec-2025) to **C$11,671.4M (Sep-2026)**, +57%, driven by the **SECURE Waste Infrastructure acquisition (closed 1-Sep-2026, C$5,414.1M total consideration)** plus 13 smaller deals. Mitigant: ~80% of SECURE was funded with **C$4,334.8M of stock** (77.7M new shares), only C$1,079.3M cash — equity-funded M&A is credit-supportive.

**The critical issue:** the revolver covenant is **Total Net Funded Debt / Adjusted EBITDA ≤ 5.00x**. On my reported-LTM calculation (~5.2x) the company would be *in breach*. Management confirms covenant compliance at 30-Sep-2026, which is only possible because the covenant calculation permits **pro-forma, run-rate EBITDA credit for acquisitions**. Headroom therefore exists on paper but is **entirely definition-dependent** — a genuine red flag for credit quality.

Coverage: **EBITDA/cash interest ~5.5x** on FY2026E (C$2,492M / C$455M) is adequate. But **EBIT/interest is only 1.50x** (C$683M / C$456M) and the tool's operating-income-based coverage is **0.76x** — thin, reflecting the heavy D&A burden (C$1,316M) from acquired intangibles. Note cash interest steps up in 2027 (SECURE only consolidated for one month in 2026) — a full year at ~C$11.7B debt likely means **C$580–620M cash interest**, i.e. coverage compresses to ~5.2x pro forma rather than expanding.

### 4. Downside Scenario: –20% Revenue Shock

Base: FY2026E revenue C$8,065M, Adj EBITDA C$2,492M (30.9% margin), net capex C$910M, cash interest C$455M.

Waste is high-fixed-cost (routes, trucks, landfills), so decremental margins run ~45–50%; a 20% revenue loss produces roughly a **30–32% EBITDA decline**.

| Line | Base FY26E | –20% Revenue | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | C$8,065M | C$6,452M | Volume-driven |
| Adj EBITDA | C$2,492M | **~C$1,695M** | Margin → 26.3% |
| Cash interest | C$455M | C$455M | Fixed |
| EBITDA / interest | 5.5x | **3.7x** | Still serviced |
| Net capex (cut to maintenance) | C$910M | ~C$650M | Growth capex slashed |
| Adj FCF | C$1,020M | **~C$550M** | –46% |
| **Net debt / EBITDA** | 3.5–5.2x | **~6.8x** | ❌ **Covenant breach** |

**Assessment:** GFL survives a 20% downturn on *cash flow* (interest still covered 3.7x, FCF positive), but **the leverage covenant breaks at ~6.8x versus the 5.00x limit**, even before considering the loss of pro-forma acquisition add-backs. Consequences: revolver availability restricted, restricted-payment capacity eliminated (no buybacks — note C$2,967M spent on repurchases in 2025), and rating downgrade pressure.

**The real vulnerability is liquidity, not solvency.** With the current ratio at 0.58x, cash of C$85.6M, and negative C$834M working capital, GFL operates permanently on its revolver. A covenant breach that impairs revolver access converts a manageable earnings problem into a funding crisis. A **–30% scenario** (EBITDA ~C$1,370M, ~8.5x) would push toward genuine distress.

### 6. Key Red Flags

1. **Covenant headroom is definitional, not real.** Reported LTM leverage ~5.2x exceeds the stated 5.00x maintenance covenant; compliance depends on pro-forma acquisition EBITDA. This is the #1 risk.
2. **Re-leveraging into an unresolved take-private.** Debt +57% in nine months while the board runs a sale process — a leveraged buyout would layer further debt onto an already-levered balance sheet.
3. **Minimal cash buffer.** C$85.6M cash, 0.04x cash ratio, 0.58x current ratio, –C$834M working capital — total dependence on revolver.
4. **Thin EBIT coverage (1.50x)** and rising cash interest in 2027 as SECURE annualises.
5. **FCF quality gap** — reported FCF C$174.6M vs Adj FCF ~C$1,020M guided; the C$845M+ difference rests on add-backs and gross-vs-net capex definitions.
6. **Stale solvency scores** — F-score of 8 and 3.93x leverage are pre-SECURE and pre-date the largest acquisition in company history.
7. **Serial M&A integration risk** — 13 acquisitions in nine months, C$5.4B SECURE purchase price allocation still provisional.
