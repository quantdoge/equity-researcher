# Investment Memo: JPM (JPMorgan Chase & Co.) — September 26, 2026

**Verdict**: BUY (small, scale-in) | **Conviction**: Medium
**Price Target**: $370 (+8% from current ~$343; bull $400 / +17%, bear $290–220 on hard landing)

## Investment Thesis

JPMorgan is the highest-quality US banking franchise — best-in-class margins (26.0% op margin, ~23% ROTCE), the widest deposit moat, a defensive β of 0.77, and conservatively guided earnings that beat 6 of 7 quarters (85.7%, +11.7% avg surprise) into a near-term macro wind (steepened +46bp curve, re-tightening Fed lifting NII, improving card losses; FY26 NII guide raised to ~$105.5B). The honest counterweight: the market already capitalizes this quality at a cycle-top multiple (2.6x book vs. 1.2–1.5x peer band) with no margin of safety, while earnings-quality accruals, credit costs, political litigation, and Dimon succession all trend negative. Own it as quality ballast — small, patient, scaled on pullbacks — not as a growth or conviction bet, and respect the 2027 recession tail.

Mature oligopolistic sector (aggregate income −19% in 2025); JPM is the only major bank that grew revenue (+3.3% vs. peer median −1.0%) and leads margins. Bear: Fed re-hiking into decelerating growth — "overtightening into stagflation," 2027 recession tail.

The honest bear is a macro trade: 2027 recession → 30–40% earnings cut, multiple to 10–12x (~$180–220). "Not a short — but emphatically not an aggressive buy at cycle-top."

## Bull Case
- Best-in-class franchise gaining share — only major US bank to grow revenue in 2025, #1 margins, widest deposit moat, β 0.77, natural sector consolidator.
- Earnings momentum intact and under-guided — 85.7% beat rate, FY26 NII raised to ~$105.5B, +8.5% revisions, ~11% EPS CAGR, 1.8% yield + buybacks.
- Near-term macro wind — steep positive curve, rising rates ahead of deposit costs, resilient labor (4.1%), improving card losses, fortress solvency.

## Bear Case
- Cycle-top multiple, no margin of safety — 2.6x book vs. 1.2–1.5x band; hard landing re-rates to 10–12x.
- Top-of-cycle tells — accruals up 4 straight years, op income −2.9% on +3.3% revenue, debt +25%, loans +24% into rising card NCOs (3.2–3.4%), insiders distributing; 2026 acceleration is volatile markets revenue, not NII.
- Macro + governance + political tail — 2027 recession odds, succession churn, $5B presidential suit, debanking legislation, #1 fossil-fuel book.

## Key Risks
1. Macro hard landing (overtightening into stagflation) — the single largest risk, skewing above market pricing.
2. NII guidance cut (bedrock line; Q2 beat was fee/tax-driven).
3. Credit crack — card NCOs sustained above ~3.5%.
4. Governance shock — unplanned Dimon transition without a named successor.
5. Political/regulatory escalation — Trump-suit discovery, debanking legislation, AML enforcement pattern.
6. Funding-cost sensitivity from wholesale debt growth.
7. **Data gaps**: no value anchor (Value Researcher timeout); Russia exposure, Zelle status, CET1/LCR, peer multiples unverified.
